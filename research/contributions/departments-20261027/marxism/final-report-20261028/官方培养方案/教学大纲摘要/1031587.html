课程简介（中文）

课程简介：通过学习这门课程，使学生搞清什么是现代化；了解现代化的基本理论和流派；认识中国现代化与资本主义及社会主义的关系；认识中国特色社会主义现代化道路是历史的选择。同时，对于中国现代化程中遇到的各种问题及其解决的途径进行思考与探索。课程的主要内容：一、经典现代化理论概述。二、世界主要国家现代化发生的历史条件、道路等诸因素比较。通过比较揭示中国特色现代化道路及其特征形成的历史原因。三、中国特色现代化道路的历史选择。中国特色现代化道路形成的过程及其所规定的特点。主要内容涉及：1、洋务运动以来，中国现代化模式的两次转换，即：从“西化”模式到苏联模式；从苏联模式再到中国特色社会主义现代化的发展模的历史演变过程。2、中国的现代化与资本主义；3、中国现代化与社会主义等。四、中国特色现代化发展模式：全面分析回答中国特色社会主义现代化发展模式的内容和特点。五、中国特色现代化建设的历史经验。通过总结经验，思考和把握中国现代化的发展规律。认清中国现代化既不能照搬西方资本主义现代化模式，也不能照搬苏联模式，而是要走自己的路；认识中国现代化利用资本主义的必要性和必然性等。

课程简介（英文）

Course Introduction Through studying this course, students will clarify the definition of modernization, grasp the basic theories and schools of modernization, understand the relationship between China's modernization and both capitalism and socialism, and recognize that the path of socialist modernization with Chinese characteristics is a historical choice. Meanwhile, students will reflect on and explore various problems encountered in the process of China's modernization and the corresponding solutions. Main Course Content Overview of Classical Modernization Theory Comparison of historical conditions, paths and other factors of modernization in major countries around the world. The historical reasons for the formation of the path and characteristics of modernization with Chinese characteristics will be revealed through such comparison. Historical choice of the path of modernization with Chinese characteristics: the formation process and inherent characteristics of the path of modernization with Chinese characteristics. The main contents cover: (1) The two transformations of China's modernization model since the Westernization Movement, namely the historical evolution from the "Westernization" model to the Soviet model, and then from the Soviet model to the development model of socialist modernization with Chinese characteristics. (2) China's modernization and capitalism. (3) China's modernization and socialism. Development model of modernization with Chinese characteristics: a comprehensive analysis and elaboration on the content and features of the development model of socialist modernization with Chinese characteristics. Historical experience of modernization construction with Chinese characteristics. By summarizing experience, students will reflect on and master the development laws of China's modernization, clearly understand that China's modernization can neither copy the Western capitalist modernization model nor the Soviet model but must take its own path, and recognize the necessity and inevitability of utilizing capitalism for China's modernization.

教学目标

通过学习这门课程，使学生搞清什么是现代化；了解现代化的基本理论和流派；认识中国现代化与资本主义及社会主义的关系；认识中国特色社会主义现代化道路是历史的选择。同时，对于中国现代化程中遇到的各种问题及其解决的途径进行思考与探索。

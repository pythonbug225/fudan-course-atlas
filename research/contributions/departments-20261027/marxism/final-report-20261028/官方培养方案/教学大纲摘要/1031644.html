课程简介（中文）

本课程的基本内容为讲授人工智能与政党建设的理论和实践，授课内容共分三大模块。课程的第一阶段为AI for Science基础知识模块。这一部分的授课内容是让没有人工智能知识基础的选课同学能够基本把握人工智能的发展历程、底层逻辑、应用场景和发展趋势，重点是介绍和讲授关于生成式人工智能（神经网络、自然语言处理、机器学习）的主干知识和发展背景。课程的第二阶段为人工智能创新政党建设研究知识模块。这一部分的授课内容是讲授传统的政党研究与人工智能范式下的政党研究的差异，重点是帮助学生打开学科视野，充分了解人工智能扩展和创新政党建设研究的方式方法、工具手段、策略逻辑。课程的第三阶段为人工智能赋能政党建设的实践进展知识模块。这一部分的授课内容是讲授国内外出现的人工智能赋能政党建设的最新实践案例，重点是呈现政党建设在不同层次、不同领域、不同维度应用人工智能技术所带来的机遇和挑战，分析和研判这对政党组织模式、意识形态、政党活动、社会基础、政党成员和政党发展的影响。

课程简介（英文）

The core content of this course is to expound the theories and practices of artificial intelligence (AI) and party building, which is structured into three major modules. The first module is the Fundamentals of AI for Science. Designed for students with no prior knowledge of artificial intelligence, this part aims to enable them to grasp the development history, underlying logic, application scenarios, and future trends of AI. The key focus is on introducing and teaching the core knowledge and developmental background of generative artificial intelligence, including its essential components: neural networks, natural language processing (NLP), and machine learning. The second module is the Research on AI-Driven Innovation in Party Building. This part elaborates on the differences between traditional party research and that conducted under the AI paradigm. Its primary goal is to help students broaden their disciplinary horizons, and fully understand the methodologies, tools, approaches, and strategic logics through which artificial intelligence expands and innovates the research on party building. The third module is the Practical Progress of AI Empowering Party Building. This part presents the latest domestic and international practical cases of AI-enabled party building. The core focus is to demonstrate the opportunities and challenges brought by the application of artificial intelligence in party building across different levels, fields, and dimensions. It also analyzes and judges the profound impacts of such applications on the organizational models, ideology, activities, social bases, membership, and long-term development of political parties.

教学目标

《数智赋能政党建设》这门课程的教学目标包括三个方面，第一是推动选课学生了解和培养AI for Science的理念和意识，让“文科生”成长为“新文科生”；第二是通过学习和掌握人工智能资源和工具，帮助学生扩展政党建设研究的知识边界，第三是讲授人工智能应用到政党建设领域的国内外前沿实践，培养学生利用人工智能工具赋能政党建设的实践能力和水平。

教学方式

结合人工智能与政党建设这门课的三大模块特点，以及 “跨学科、理论 + 实践、技术门槛差异大” 的核心属性，采用分层递进的混合式教学模式。

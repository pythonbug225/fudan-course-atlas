课程简介（中文）

本课程为马克思主义学院硕士生、博士生的专业选修课。教学内容主要从数字劳动、数字金融、数字生命政治和数字帝国主义等多个维度探讨西方数字资本主义的发展趋势与主要特征。第一，从数众、数字工人主义和数字共同体等三个层面探讨当代西方左翼对数字资本主义的批判路径。第二，从数字劳动概念内涵、数字劳动二重性、数据商品二因素和数字劳动所有权等层面探讨数字劳动问题。第三，从数字生命政治、数字地缘政治经济和数字霸权等层面探讨数字帝国主义问题。第四，从数字货币权力、数字金融资本、比特币的产生等探讨数字资本主义金融化问题。第五，以ChatGPT为例探讨人工智能赋能数字劳动的新形态及其资本化的问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a professional optional course for master’s and doctoral students at the School of Marxism. Its teaching content mainly explores the development trends and major characteristics of Western digital capitalism from multiple dimensions, including digital labor, digital finance, digital biopolitics, and digital imperialism. First, it probes into the critical approaches of contemporary Western left-wing scholars to digital capitalism from three perspectives: the multitude, digital workerism, and the digital commons. Second, it examines the issues of digital labor with a focus on the conceptual connotation of digital labor, the duality of digital labor, the two factors of data commodities, and the ownership of digital labor. Third, it delves into the problem of digital imperialism from such dimensions as digital biopolitics, the digital geopolitical economy, and digital hegemony. Fourth, it discusses the financialization of digital capitalism by analyzing digital currency power, digital financial capital, and the emergence of Bitcoin, among other topics. Fifth, it takes ChatGPT as a typical case to explore the new forms of digital labor empowered by artificial intelligence and the problems associated with their capitalization.

教学目标

了解并认识百年大变局背景下西方数字资本主义的发展动态和前沿问题。 通过对国内外数字资本主义研究最新文献的研读，使得学生系统而深入了解当代西方左翼对数字资本主义的批判路径和前沿问题。 辨别包括人工智能在内的数字技术最新发展及其资本化的积极成果和消极因素。 通过本课程的学习，引导学生思考以数字化推进中国式现代化的有益启示。

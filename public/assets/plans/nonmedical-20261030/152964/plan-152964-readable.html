培养方案正文

学科专业简介

复旦大学国际关系与公共事务学院成立于2000年11月，前身为1923年建立的复旦大学政治学系。百年政心赓续，问道政学、作育国士。目前拥有政治学和公共管理两个国家A类学科，是国家“双一流”学科建设单位，在世界大学专业排名中稳居前列。秉承“卓越为公”理念，培养新时代具有全球视野、国家意识和专业才能的领军人才。
国际政治专业为国家级一流本科建设专业，主要方向包括：国际关系理论、大国战略和大国关系、中国对外战略和对外关系、国际安全与国家安全、全球治理与国际组织、全球发展、宗教与国际关系、美国研究、欧洲研究、亚太研究、金砖国家与全球南方等。

培养目标

我们致力于培养从事国际组织、政府部门、非政府组织及国际企业相关工作的高层次人才，同时为有志于学术研究的学生提供深厚的学术基础。
We are dedicated to cultivating high-caliber talents for careers in international organizations, government agencies, non-governmental organizations, and international businesses, while also providing a strong academic foundation for students aspiring to engage in scholarly research.

基本要求

国际组织与全球治理本科专业专注于全球治理、国际组织及国际关系的理论动态与发展应用，本专业是全英文授课的法学本科四年制学位专业。
本专业旨在培养德智体美劳全面发展，具有全球视野、扎实的理论基础和实践能力的复合型人才。毕业生应能够系统掌握全球治理与国际组织的理论和实践，熟悉国际事务运行机制，深刻理解国际规则与规范，具备分析和解决复杂全球性问题的能力。毕业生也应具备跨学科的知识储备、实践技能、国际视野、创新思维、职业素养、社会责任感和领导力，能够独立思辨、实践操作与终身学习。

The Global Governance and International Organizations undergraduate program focuses on the theoretical dynamics and applied development of global governance, international organizations, and international relations. This is a four-year Bachelor of Laws degree program taught entirely in English.
The program aims to cultivate well-rounded individuals who excel in moral, intellectual, physical, aesthetic, and labor development. Graduates are expected to have a global perspective, a solid theoretical foundation, and practical skills. They should be capable of systematically mastering the theory and practice of global governance and international organizations, familiar with the operational mechanisms of international affairs, and deeply understand international rules and norms. Additionally, they should possess the ability to analyze and resolve complex global issues. Graduates are also expected to have interdisciplinary knowledge, practical skills, a global outlook, innovative thinking, professional ethics, social responsibility, and leadership capabilities. They should be capable of independent critical thinking, practical application, and lifelong learning.

学分要求

本专业学生毕业时须满足通识教育课程（含通识教育核心课程和专项教育课程）40学分、专业培养课程49学分和多元发展路径课程的修读要求，总学分不低于132学分（含实践学分不低于20学分；含美育学分不少于2学分，其中至少在“美学和艺术史论类”或“艺术鉴赏和评论类”课程中修读1学分，并至少参与一项艺术实践活动；劳动教育不少于32学时，并满足劳动周教育要求），达到学位要求者授予法学学士学位。

留学生的通识教育课程修读要求，以及留学生的水平测试要求，参见英文版培养方案和相应修读说明。

Students are required to complete 40General Education Courses credits (including Core General Education Courses and Specific General Education Courses), 49 Major Courses credits , and satisfy the requirements of Multi-Development Track Courses. Completion of no fewer than 132 credits in total (including at least 20 Practice credits; 2 Aesthetic Education credits, including at least 1 credit in a course in ‘Aesthetics and Art History’ or ‘Art Appreciation and Criticism’ and participation in at least one practical art activity; 32 hours of Education on The Hard-Working Spirit credits and meeting the requirements for labor week education), a Bachelor of Economics degree is conferred to those who meet the requirements of the degree by Fudan University.

International students are required to complete 26 General Education Courses credits (including Core General Education Courses and Specific General Education Courses), 49 Major Courses credits, and satisfy the requirements of Multi-Development Track Courses. Completion of no fewer than 116 credits in total, a Bachelor of Law degree is conferred to those who meet the requirements of the degree by Fudan University.

课程体系与科研训练体系

春风化雨训练体系依托学院“春风化雨导师计划”面向本科轨道学生开展自选学术训练或实务训练，具体修读方式可对应参照本博融通、本硕融通轨道本科阶段项目调整后执行。
本科毕业时，本科轨道学生可将学术或实务训练项目成果直接作为毕业论文申请送审，也可以单独撰写毕业论文。

The Spring Breeze and Rain training system relies on the college's "Spring Breeze and Rain Mentor Program" to carry out optional academic or practical training for undergraduate track students. The specific study methods can be implemented with reference to the adjusted undergraduate programs of the integrated bachelor-doctoral or integrated bachelor-master tracks.

Upon undergraduate graduation, undergraduate track students can directly submit the results of their academic or practical training project as a graduation thesis for review, or they can write a separate graduation thesis.

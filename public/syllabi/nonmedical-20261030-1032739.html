说服与影响

2026春 · JCM70041.01 · 邓建国

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

无论是科学技术还是社科人文领域，就观念、产品或服务进行说服和传播是从业者必备的素养。本课程教授学生与说服和传播运动相关的基本理论和实践技能，以让他们在自己的生活和工作中成为高明且道德的说服者，同时也能识别和抵御不道德的说服。

课程简介（英文）

Whether in the fields of science and technology or the social sciences and humanities, the ability to persuade and communicate ideas, products, or services is an essential competency for professionals. This course teaches students the fundamental theories and practical skills related to persuasion and communication campaigns, enabling them to become skilled and ethical persuaders in their own lives and work, while also equipping them to recognize and resist unethical persuasion.

教学目标

教学方式

讲座、讨论和课外实践

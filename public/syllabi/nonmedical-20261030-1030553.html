翻译研究方法论

课程简介（中文）

本课程以优秀的翻译研究论文为案例，对国内外的翻译研究方法论进行全局性的综合研究，围绕翻译研究的历史与现状、学科理论与方法论、选题与写作方法等三大领域，对国内外文献进行系统分析、总结及反思，建构系统的翻译研究方法论，为翻译学博士生打下研究方法基础。课程由Lecture+ Discussion +Presentation三部分构成，学生需要阅读大量文献，参与课堂讨论并进行思考，不定期撰写小型汇报，至少主讲一到两个研究方法。期末考查，提交博士论文研究方法论章节。

课程简介（英文）

This course takes outstanding translation research papers as cases to conduct a comprehensive and holistic study of translation research methodologies at home and abroad. Centering on three major domains—the history and current situation of translation research, disciplinary theories and methodologies, as well as topic selection and writing methods—it carries out systematic analysis, summarization and reflection on domestic and international literature, and constructs a systematic translation research methodology, laying a foundation in research methods for PhD candidates in Translation Studies. The course consists of three components: Lectures, Discussions and Presentations. Students are required to read a large number of literatures, participate in and reflect on classroom discussions, write occasional mini reports, and deliver lectures on at least one or two research methods as the main speaker. For the final assessment, students shall submit the chapter on research methodology of their PhD dissertations.

教学目标

本课程主要针对博士生一年级开设的方法论课程，通过中英文论文案例分析，归纳总结国内外行之有效的翻译研究方法，让博士生接受深入系统的研究方法学术训练，旨在提高他们的学科意识、理论意识和学术规范意识，培养批判性思辨能力，增强分析问题和解决问题的能力。 This course is a methodology course designed primarily for first-year PhD candidates. Through the case analysis of Chinese and English academic papers, it induces and summarizes the effective translation research methodologies at home and abroad, and provides PhD candidates with in-depth and systematic academic training in research methods. It aims to enhance their disciplinary awareness, theoretical awareness and awareness of academic norms, foster their critical thinking ability, and strengthen their capacity to analyze and solve problems.

教学方式

This course for first-year PhD candidates in Translation Studies adopts a diverse and research-oriented teaching method system, integrating theoretical explanation and practical operation, and combining individual learning with group interaction. The specific teaching methods are as follows: Lecturing The instructor delivers systematic lectures on core translation research methodologies (e.g., action research, corpus-based study, narrative analysis), expounds on the theoretical connotation, application scope and operational steps of each method, and sorts out the development history and research status of translation studies methodology, laying a solid theoretical foundation for students. Case Analysis Taking excellent Chinese and English translation research papers as typical cases, the course analyzes how different research methodologies are applied in actual academic research, discusses the advantages and disadvantages of method application and the key points of practical operation, and guides students to summarize effective research experience from cases. Classroom Discussion Centering on the research methodologies of each chapter, students are required to read a large number of relevant literatures in advance, and conduct in-depth classroom discussions on the application dilemmas, innovation paths and practical value of research methods under the guidance of the instructor, so as to stimulate academic thinking. Guest Lectures Invite experts in the field of translation studies to give special guest lectures on specific research methodologies (e.g., Q methodology, social network analysis, experimental research), introduce the latest research progress and practical application experience of relevant methods, and expand students' academic horizons. Practical Operation Organize practical training such as simulated interviews for students, let them apply the learned research methodologies to specific operational links, and experience the whole process of method application from design to implementation, so as to improve their practical research ability. Class Presentation & Instructor Comments In the section of dissertation methodology writing, students are required to make classroom presentations on their research methodology design ideas and writing frameworks. The instructor conducts targeted comments and guidance, points out existing problems and puts forward revision suggestions to polish students' academic writing ability. Q&A Session Set up a special Q&A link, answer students' doubts about research methodology application, dissertation writing and other aspects in a targeted manner, and solve the practical problems encountered by students in the learning and research process. Comprehensive Review & Reflective Learning In the final stage of the course, conduct a comprehensive review of all translation research methodologies, and organize students to carry out collective reflection and summary on the learning process, so as to help them construct a systematic and structured research methodology system and form academic reflection awareness. Reading Guidance & Independent Study Assign required and recommended reading materials for each research methodology, guide students to carry out in-depth independent reading and literature combing, and require them to complete book reviews and other assignments, cultivating their autonomous academic research ability.

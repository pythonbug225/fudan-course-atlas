经济人类学

2026春 · SOCI60007.01 · Horace Michel Ortiz (何瑞雪)

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍当代经济人类学的主要理论、概念和研究问题。课程的第一部介绍经济人类学的五个核心概念：生产、交换、价值、信贷和债务以及货币。课程的第二部分从经济人类学的角度深入研究当代问题：全球关系、身体和身份、宗教、亲属关系、道德、消费、金融、国家和数字技术。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the main theories, concepts and research topics in contemporary economic anthropology. The first part of the course presents five central concepts: production, exchange, value, credit and debt and money. The second part of the course studies contemporary research questions from the point of view of economic anthropology: global relations, body and identity, religion, kinship, morality, consumption, finance, state and digital technologies.

教学目标

通过经济人类学理论和问题的介绍，让学生将经济人类学的研究问题与人类学研究的主要议题联系起来。让学生理解当代经济过程和理解非经济社会过程的经济方面。让学生将自己的研究与核心经济问题联系起来。让学生分析本地与全球社会经济关系。 Through the presentation of the theories and questions raised by economic anthropology, this course allows to connect the research questions of economic anthropology with the main research questions of anthropology in general. It helps students understand contemporary economic processes and the economic aspects of non-economic social processes. It helps students connect their own research with economic issues. It helps students analyze the connection between local and global processes.

教学方式

研讨会阅读任务讨论及学生研究项目研讨 Seminar discussion of reading assignments and students' research projects

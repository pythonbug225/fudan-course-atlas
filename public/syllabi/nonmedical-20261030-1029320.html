课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提高非英语专业研究生的学术英语写作能力。课程设计基于以下三点理论依据：1）中国高等教育英语语言教育从一般用途英语（EGP）向学术英语（EAP）转型，迫切需要在课程中增加学术英语课程；2）学术英语的跨学科性特点促使学生在不同学科群体中探讨学科内容，并通过对比学科差异发展自己的学科认同；3）论文写作是研究生进行学术交流基本形式，而英语是世界通行的学术交流语言。这门课程给学生介绍论文写作的基本要求，帮助学生了解论文写作的基本规范，从而研究生参与国际学术交流做好准备。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to develop non-English major graduate students’ academic English language proficiency. The course design is based on the following three rationales: 1) The paradigm shift from English for General Purposes (EGP) to English for Academic Purposes (EAP) in English language education at the tertiary level in China necessitates the inclusion of EAP courses in the curriculum; 2)The growing interdisciplinary focus in EAP encourages students to address disciplinary content within mixed disciplinary groups and develop their disciplinary identities through contrasting disciplinary differences; 3) Papers serve as one of the most important forms of academic communication for gradudate students and English is one of the common languages in academic communication. This course will introduce graduate students to the basic requirements of academic writing, including writing convention, techniques and mechanics, and equip them with the necessary skills that will enable them to publish their studies and engage actively in academic exchanges.

教学目标

General Objective: To equip students with the essential skills and strategies for producing clear, coherent, and rigorous academic texts appropriate to their disciplines, while adhering to scholarly conventions and ethical standards. Genre Awareness Develop proficiency in common academic genres (essays, reports, literature reviews, research proposals). Rhetorical Competence Craft thesis-driven arguments with evidence-based support. Master discipline-specific discourse conventions (e.g., STEM vs. humanities). Technical Mastery Apply formal academic style (precision, objectivity, hedging). Navigate citation systems (APA/MLA/Chicago) to avoid plagiarism. Critical Process Practice iterative writing stages: drafting, peer review, revision. Synthesize and critique scholarly sources effectively. Transferable Skills Adapt writing for conferences, publications, and graduate studies.

教学方式

以学生为核心，围绕学术英语写作的特点，讲练结合，一边让学生了解学术英语写作，特别是研究报告写作的规范，一边让学生结合自己的学科和兴趣从事写作实践。

课程简介（中文）

随着生物科学研究技术的发展、在组织切片或者细胞涂片上引入生物化学的酶-底物反应、免疫化学的抗原-抗体反应及分子生物学的DNA或RNA基因片段杂交反应，与组织形态学技术相结合分别形成酶组织化学、免疫组织化学和杂交组织化学，统称为现代组织化学。通过光学或电镜观察，人们能够在组织、细胞及核酸水平进行定性、定位和定量观察，使细胞形态与功能有机结合，在当今精准医学的时代背景下，现代组织化学技术已经成为生命科学和医学研究以及临床病理诊断中必须的重要技术手段。
在这门课程中我们将介绍常见的组织化学技术原理及实验过程中的基本注意事项，实验课将安排学生进行实验操作或录像演示实验步骤，并介绍最新的技术进展。

课程简介（英文）

Morphological observation on tissue sections can distinguish morphological changes in normal or pathological conditions. With the development of biological science research technology, the introduction of biochemical enzyme-substrate reactions, immunochemical antigen-antibody reactions and molecular biology DNA or RNA gene fragment hybridization reactions in tissue and cell specimens, they are related to tissue morphology The combination of technology forms enzymatic histochemistry, immunohistochemistry and hybrid histochemistry, which are collectively called modern histochemistry. Through optical or electron microscope observation, people can make qualitative, localization and quantitative observations at the level of tissues, cells and nucleic acids, and organically combine cell morphology and function. In the era of precision medicine, modern histochemical technology has become life science and medicine Important technical means necessary for research and clinical pathological diagnosis.

教学目标

我们期望通过课程学习，能让学生短时间内掌握常用的免疫组织化学技术原理，基本步骤，结果观察及实际应用等，帮助学生能在课题研究，病理诊断等工作实践中加以应用。
We hope that through the study, students will be able to grasp the common immunohistochemical technology principles, basic steps, results observation and practical applications, and help students to apply them in their research work or pathological diagnosis.

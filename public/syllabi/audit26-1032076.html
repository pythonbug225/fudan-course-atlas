课程简介（中文）

    本课程为公共卫生一级学科研究生专业选修课程，主要通过环境与健康领域的典型研究案例，介绍空气污染、气候变化与人群健康研究方法，以及暴露评价新技术、新方法在环境流行病学中的应用场景，阐明不同研究设计的环境健康研究理论、方法和实践，提升研究生分析和实践能力，为培养公共卫生科研人才提供基础。主要内容包括：绪论，空气污染、气候变化急慢性效应研究介绍及案例分析，队列研究等经典设计，时间序列研究、病例交叉研究、实验性研究方法在环境健康领域的应用等，并介绍环境流行病学研究中的暴露评价新技术和方法、应用场景、各自的优势和不足、以及不同暴露评价方法对健康效应估计的潜在影响。希望学生通过本课程学习，了解环境相关基本知识，掌握环境流行病学不同研究设计的类型、基本统计分析方法、研究设计思路、以及应用场景，能够运用本课程所学提升研究生课题的质量。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a professional elective for graduate students in the first-level discipline of Public Health. Using representative case studies in the field of environment and health, it introduces research methods for assessing the health impacts of air pollution and climate change, and explains how emerging technologies and novel approaches for exposure assessment are applied in environmental epidemiology. The course elucidates the theories, methods, and practices underlying environmental health studies across different research designs, aiming to strengthen students’ analytical and practical competencies and to provide foundational training for cultivating public health researchers. Key topics include an introduction; acute and chronic health effects of air pollution and climate change with case analyses; classic designs such as cohort studies; applications of time-series studies, case-crossover studies, and experimental research methods in environmental health; and advances in exposure assessment technologies and methods, including their application scenarios, strengths and limitations, and the potential influence of different exposure assessment approaches on health-effect estimation. By the end of the course, students are expected to understand fundamental concepts related to environmental exposures, master the major types of study designs in environmental epidemiology along with basic statistical methods, develop sound study-design thinking and an understanding of appropriate application scenarios, and apply the knowledge gained to improve the quality of their graduate research projects.

教学目标

本课程以流行病学研究设计为基础，系统从环境与健康研究的设计类型、理论基础、统计分析方法、代码实现、实践应用等方面进行授课，重视在环境与健康的知识传授过程中为学生理清研究设计全过程思路，并注重提高学生对不同研究设计类型的分析方法和特点的把握，帮助学生科研素质的提升；同时关注暴露评价方法和技术，以及先进暴露评价方法对环境健康研究的推进作用，提升学生对环境因素暴露方法和技术的认识以及应用能力。

风险投资，上市和并购

2026春 · MIB70010.01 · 孙霄汉

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在深入探讨风险投资、企业上市以及并购过程中的关键金融战略。通过分析一系列真实案例，课程将揭示这些复杂交易背后的逻辑、策略和执行要点。学生将学习如何评估投资机会、优化资本结构、以及在不断变化的市场环境中制定有效的并购策略。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to explore in depth the key financial strategies in venture capital, corporate listing and mergers and acquisitions. By analyzing a series of real cases, the course will reveal the logic, strategy and execution points behind these complex transactions. Students will learn how to evaluate investment opportunities, optimize capital structure, and develop effective M&A strategies in a changing market environment.

教学目标

通过案例研究，掌握风险投资的运作机制，包括项目筛选、尽职调查、投后管理及退出策略。 通用案例解剖，理解企业上市的全过程，学习如何进行有效的IPO估值和市场策略制定。 分析并购案例，探讨并购策略、交易结构设计、估值分析及整合计划。 培养学生运用金融衍生工具进行风险管理的能力，以及通过案例学习提升决策和分析技能。 Through case studies, students will master the operating mechanism of venture capital, including project screening, due diligence, post-investment management and exit strategies. Through case analysis, students will understand the entire process of corporate listing and learn how to conduct effective IPO valuation and market strategy formulation. Analyze M&A cases, explore M&A strategies, transaction structure design, valuation analysis and integration plans. Cultivate students' ability to use financial derivatives for risk management, and improve decision-making and analytical skills through case studies.

教学方式

主题讲授 (Lecture, 占40%)：系统讲授风险投资、上市与并购的核心理论与知识体系。 案例研讨 (Case Study, 占30%)：每周围绕关键案例进行深度讨论，鼓励学生积极参与。案例涵盖风险投资、上市与并购的不同方面，帮助学生理解其在实际商业环境中的运作方式。 企业参访 (Company Visits, 占15%)：计划组织2-3次企业参访活动，重点参访上海的科技或明星企业，了解其风险投资、上市与并购的实践经验。 分组项目 (Group Project, 占15%)：学生分组完成一个深度行业研究或创新应用方案。通过团队合作，学生将综合运用所学知识，完成一个完整的项目，培养团队合作和项目管理能力。 Lectures (40%): Systematic instruction on the core theories and knowledge of venture capital, IPOs, and M&A. Case Studies (30%): In-depth discussions on key cases each week, encouraging active student participation. Cases cover different aspects of venture capital, IPOs, and M&A, helping students understand how they operate in real-world business environments. Company Visits (15%): 2-3 company visits planned, focusing on leading technology companies in Shanghai to understand their practical experience in venture capital, IPOs, and M&A. Group Projects (15%): Students work in groups to complete an in-depth industry research or innovative application solution. Through teamwork, students will comprehensively apply their knowledge to complete a comprehensive project, cultivating teamwork and project management skills.

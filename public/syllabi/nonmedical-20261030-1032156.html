Fintech

2026春 · MF60024.03 · Charles CHANG(张纯信)

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course will explain core knowledge about the evolution of Fintech and its potential disruption, including artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain, cloud computing, metaverse, big data, and other related digital economy/digital finance topics. There are strong synergies among these technologies, enabling a broad spectrum of disruptive impact. The course will cover the fundamentals of these technologies and focus on resulting business model innovation. The class will also delve into the applications and use cases of Fintech for products and services in the finance and digital economy industries. The course is designed to be interactive with discussions involving various business use cases as well as incorporating practical insights from leading industry experts. The class highly encourages students to adopt an innovative and open-minded attitude to leverage technology in finance. The class will involve weekly assignments as well as final project that will test understanding of concepts and the student’s ability to apply these in the real-world.

教学目标

To help students understand the basics of Fintech, its applications in modern finance, and its potential impact on the finance industry and economy.

教学方式

This course requires students to carry out case studies and actively participate in class discussions and practical activities. The group project requires student groups to review and share their progress on a weekly basis.

教学内容与进度安排

Session 1 Topic: Fintech: Cloud and Metaverse Note: Overall introduction to Fintech and course expectations. What is the cloud economy and why is it core to fintech development? What is the meta/twinverse? What are the key components to make it work? Reading/Case: Quantifeed/assignments Session 2 Topic: Big Data Note: What is “Big” Data and how does it impact economic innovation? Investigate core applications, alternative data, and risks (privacy concerns, KYC, AML). Reading/Case: Vtou, Shuxing/assignments Session 3 Topic: AI Note: AI in trading, data cleaning, and automation. How is machine learning impacting financial modelling and how we access data? Reading/Case: Yewno/assignments, Hypers Session 4 Topic: Blockchain 1.0: Cryptocurrency vs Reasonable Application Scenarios Note: Description of crypto-markets, discussion of core fundamentals, core uses and application scenarios of blockchains Reading/Case: Wynn, Axoni/assignments Session 5 Topic: Blockchain 2.0: ICO and Smart Contracts Note: A paradigm shifting technology, which also leads to a number of questions: What are its fundamentals? What is the role of regulation? STO and asset-backed/cashflow-backed tokens Reading/Case: ArtPi, Brickmark/assignments Session 6 Topic: New Finance Note: What will the token economy in the future look like? What is the role of the Digital Currency? Reading/Case: Industry prospect, central bank digital currency/assignments

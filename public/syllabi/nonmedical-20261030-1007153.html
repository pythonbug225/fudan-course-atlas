课程简介（中文）

通过本课程的学习，初步了解知识产权法律基本内容，了解我国知识产权法 律制度基本框架，并对如何实施知识产权战略、促进创新型国家建设、提升 国家综合竞争实力有所理解；尤其是了解专利与专利法的基本内容，自觉地 树立专利意识，提高运用专利制度与专利战略的水平与能力。

课程简介（英文）

Through the study of this course, I have gained a preliminary understanding of the basic content of intellectual property law and the basic framework of China's intellectual property legal system. I have also developed an understanding of how to implement intellectual property strategies, promote the construction of an innovative country, and enhance the comprehensive competitive strength of the nation. In particular, I have acquired knowledge of the basic content of patents and patent law, consciously established a patent consciousness, and improved my ability and level to use the patent system and patent strategies.

教学目标

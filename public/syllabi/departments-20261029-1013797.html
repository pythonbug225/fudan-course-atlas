课程简介（中文）

不断变化的市场以及相关的计算环境正在对营销管理者的工作结构和内容产生影响。与此同时，营销领域也在迅速演变，不再仅仅基于其概念性内容。尽管许多人仍然将传统营销视为一门艺术，但新兴的营销越来越像工程学，通过有效结合艺术与科学来解决营销问题。营销人员需要的不仅仅是概念，他们还需要充分利用各种丰富数据。无论你是否从事营销研究工作，在职业生涯的某个阶段，你很可能都需要接触营销研究——无论是作为营销研究的生产者还是使用者。本课程是营销研究的简明指南。

课程简介（英文）

Ever-changing marketplaces and the related computing environment are making an impact on the structure and content of the marketing manager’s job. Concurrently, marketing is so rapidly evolving that it is no longer based on its conceptual content alone. Even though many still see traditional marketing as an art, the new and emerging marketing increasingly looks like engineering by combining art and science effectively to solve marketing problems. Marketers need more than concepts to fully make use of various and rich data available to them. Whether or not you ever work in a marketing research function, at some time in your career, you will most likely need to deal with marketing research, either as a producer or as a user. This course is a concise guide to marketing research

教学目标

The course objective is to provide the students with the required level of theoretical foundation while staying highly practical and application focused. After this course, the students should have a sound theoretical understanding as well as being able to conduct a market research project from start to finish.

教学方式

The basic pedagogical approach is to employ a mix of learning methods, including lectures, software tools, cases, and assigned readings. Weekly class sessions will be devoted to probing, extending and applying the material in the readings and the cases. We will use the “Tell-Show-Do” sequence to give you hands-on experience in using the course materials for making marketing decisions. (i) Lectures (supplemented by the text) will cover the concepts and models you need to 4 understand and apply the scientific approach to marketing. (ii) The application of these concepts to practice will be illustrated in the cases, readings, and the examples we will cover. (iii) Through the software tools available with the text, you will have hands-on opportunities to apply the concepts and models to resolve real-life marketing problems.

中国社会保障体制及其改革

2025秋 · MIB60009.01 · 丁纯

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍新中国成立以来中国城乡社会保障制度的建立、发展、改革与重构历程。重点分析中国社会保险（养老保险、医疗保险、失业保险、工伤保险、生育保险）、社会福利、社会救助、优抚安置、脱贫攻坚等制度安排与实践。

课程简介（英文）

The establishment, development, reform and reconstruction of Chinese urban and rural social security system are introduced since the founding of New China. This course focuses on the analysis of China’s social insurance (pension, health care, unemployment, work injury and maternity), social welfare, social relief, and special care and placement, anti-poverty and other institutional arrangements and practices.

教学目标

Through “China’s social security system and its reform” course, international students pursuing Master degrees can have an understanding of the basic concepts of the social security system, relevant theories and the formation, the question of the reform and trend of the social system in the world. On this basis, the establishment, development, reform and reconstruction of Chinese urban and rural social security system are introduced since the founding of New China.

教学方式

教师授课为主。

教学内容与进度安排

教学周 教学内容及预期效果 1 Concepts, theories, models and practices of the social security system 2 Overview of the Traditional social security of planned economy era 3 Exploration of the social security system reform approach during Economic reform era 4 Reconstruction of social security system, China’s social insurance, Old - age insurance 5 Medical Insurance, Unemployment Insurance, Work related Insurance 6 Maternity Insurance 7 House security, Social welfare, Social relief, Special care and placement 8 Social security system in rural China；Anti - poverty policy and its practice 9 Chinese social security system, economic development and social transformation 10 Problems of China’s social security system and its reform 11 Problems of China’s social security system reform and institutional issues in the reconstruction and improvement

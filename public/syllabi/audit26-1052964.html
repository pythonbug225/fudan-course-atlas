课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍行为经济学的核心概念、理论与应用，探讨心理因素如何影响个人和机构的经济决策。课程涵盖前景理论、参照依赖、利他与公平、跨期选择、自我控制、家庭金融及神经经济学等主题，帮助学生理解现实经济行为与传统模型的差异，并运用行为经济学分析实际问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces key concepts, theories, and applications in behavioral economics. It examines how psychological factors shape economic decision-making by individuals and institutions, and why real-world behavior often differs from traditional economic models. Topics include prospect theory, reference dependence, altruism and fairness, intertemporal choice, self-control, behavioral household finance, and neuroeconomics. The course helps students apply behavioral insights to economic and business contexts and understand how such insights can explain, predict, and influence economic decisions.

教学目标

  1. Introduce key concepts and theories in behavioral economics.
  2. Apply behavioral economics to real-world economic and business scenarios.
  3. Develop skills in incorporating psychological insights to explain economic behavior.
  4. Foster an understanding of how behavioral economics can be used to predict and influence economic decisions.

教学方式

本课程采用专题讲座形式

教学内容与进度安排

Session 1: Introduction to Behavioral Economics

- Overview of behavioral economics.

- Prospect Theory.

- Endowment Effect.

 

Session 2: Reference Dependence and Decision Making

- Loss aversion and probability weighting.

- Reference dependence.

- Behavioral Games and Inequity Aversion.

 

Session 3: Altruism and fairness

- Altruism and Gift Exchange.

- Dark Side of Incentive.

 

Session 4: Intertemporal Choice and Self-Control 

- Time preference.

- Theories of self-control and procrastination.

- Commitment Devices.

 

Session 5: Behavioral Household Finance

- Automatic enrollment and saving behavior.

- Commitment devices and their effectiveness.

- The role of defaults in financial decision-making.

 

Session 6: Neuroeconomics and Behavioral Economics

- The intersection of neuroscience and economics.

- The role of genetics and environment in economic behavior.

- Practical applications and future directions.

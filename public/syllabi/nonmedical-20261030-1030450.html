国际商事仲裁法

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

通过国际商事仲裁课程讲授，培养学生运用仲裁方法解决跨境商事争议的能力。本课程将系统介绍仲裁作为有效争议解决手段与其他争议解决方式的差异与优势，重点聚焦仲裁的基本原理、国际商事仲裁协议效力极其法律适用、仲裁条款独立性原则、仲裁庭的自裁管辖以及法院的司法审查、仲裁的主要程序问题、仲裁裁决的类型、撤销与不予执行，特别是《纽约公约》下的承认与执行问题。通过1995年中国仲裁法与2025年仲裁法修订的比较，展现中国仲裁法在涉外仲裁方面的主要变化、对实践产生的影响以及中国仲裁国际化的路径等。本课程将结合丰富的案例实践，运用比较方法进行讲授。为国家培养熟悉国际规则的涉外法治人才。

课程简介（英文）

he Chinese arbitration practice began from the 1950Th , when CIETAC andCMAC were established to resolve international commercial disputes at vacancy of arbitration laws, the first Chinese Arbitration Law was adopted in 1994 and take legal effective in 1995 and need to be revised due to the fast development of commercial arbitration practices. The main contents of this course include: A general introduction to arbitration; Determination of the validity of arbitration agreement and the applicable law governing the validity of international arbitration agreement; Arbitral tribunal and its jurisdiction; main procedural issues under Chinese Arbitration law; Arbitral awards; Determine governing law of substantive issues; Judicial review of the People’ Court to arbitral award; Recognition and enforcement of arbitral awards under 1958 New York Convention. From the above, some interesting and special issues will be addressed e.g. whether foreign arbitration institution is allowed to conduct arbitration seated in China? whether awards made outside China without foreign elements can be recognized and enforced by Chinese court under New York Convention; whether ad hoc arbitration is permitted in China and whether international investment disputes can be arbitrated by Chinese arbitration institutions, etc.

教学目标

The aim of this course is to help students understand systematically how to resolve cross boarder disputes through arbitration , the problems of current Chinese arbitration law and its implementation in practice, and the new progress of Chinese Arbitration legal system adapting the international business environment.

教学方式

Professor’s lecture combined with students group presentations

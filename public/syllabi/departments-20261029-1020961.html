课程简介（中文）

本课程分为三部分。第一部分介绍介绍随机变量的渐进性质、普通最小二乘法、广义最小二乘法、广义矩估计、极大似然估计等估计量。第二部分介绍微观计量模型，包括Logit和Probit模型，以及多元选择模型。第三部分讨论固定效应、随即效应等面板数据模型。

课程简介（英文）

The content can be divided into three major parts. In the first part, we will introduce some estimating methods such as the GLS, GMM and MLE. In the second part, we will discuss models in Microeconometrics such as the Logit and Probit, in both the binary and multinomial cases. In the third part, different models for panel data will be introduced, including the widely used fixed and random effects models and their extensions.

教学目标

After finishing the course, students are supposed to have a theoretical understanding of econometric methods such as the ordinary and generalized least squares, generalized method of moments and maximum likelihood. They should also be able to apply the aforementioned methods to make estimation and inference with both cross section and panel data.

教学方式

Lecture, discussion, homework, etc.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在向学生介绍写作的基础知识及医学情景下部分实用文体的写作方法，包括电子邮件、申请信、推荐信、简历、个人陈述、开幕致辞、发言人/参会者介绍、闭幕致辞等。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to introduce students to the fundamentals of writing as well as the writing of some practical genres in medical contexts, including emails, application letters, recommendation letters, resumes, personal statements, opening speeches, introductions of speakers/participants and closing speeches.

教学目标

Understand the general features of a typical English paragraph; Know how to write letters for practical purposes in medical settings, such as job application, recommendation letter, letter to editors of the target journal, etc.; Be able to write a personal statement, curriculum vitae (CV)/résumé, and biography (bio); Be able to handle writings related to conferences, including opening and closing speeches, and introduction of the speakers/participants.

教学方式

The teaching methodology of this course combines lectures and discussions.

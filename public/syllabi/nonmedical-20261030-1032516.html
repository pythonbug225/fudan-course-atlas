课程简介（中文）

本课程主体内容为讨论课，辅以专题讲授与学生报告等形式，意在帮助学生了解中国古代文学研究领域中的重要研究论题、理论框架、文本解读方法、论文写作方式与学缘分派。在每一次讨论课中，我们都会针对一个具体的文学时段或文体，选取相关的经典研究论著，在课前重点阅读与延伸阅读的基础上展开讨论，以帮助学生掌握中国古代文学研究的核心论著、基本知识、主流文本分析方法以及学术史发展与分派。与此同时，本课程也将引导学生了解学术写作的基本规范，学习阅读文献、打磨问题、建构有效论述的方法，帮助学生围绕各自的研究兴趣和课题，练习学术综述的写作。

课程简介（英文）

The main content of this course consists of discussion sessions, supplemented by lectures and student presentations, aiming to help students understand important research topics, theoretical frameworks, text interpretation methods, thesis writing approaches, and academic lineages in the field of ancient Chinese literature studies. In each discussion session, we focus on a specific literary period or genre, selecting relevant classic research works for discussion based on pre-class essential and extended readings. This approach helps students grasp the core research works, fundamental knowledge, mainstream text analysis methods, and the development and branching of academic history in ancient Chinese literature studies. At the same time, this course will guide students to understand the basic norms of academic writing, learn methods for reading literature, refining questions, and constructing effective arguments, and assist them in practicing the writing of academic reviews centered around their respective research interests and topics.

教学目标

掌握中国古代文学研究的核心论著、基本知识、主流文本分析方法以及学术史发展与分派。了解学术写作的基本规范，学习阅读文献、打磨问题、建构有效论述的方法，帮助学生围绕各自的研究兴趣和课题，练习学术综述的写作。

课程简介（中文）

本课程为国际政治、国际关系与外交学的专业英文博士生课程，通过教师讲授与学生阅读研讨相结合的方式，围绕当前国际安全形势与国家战略需要，系统介绍国际关系学科的基本概念，前沿理论与方法，围绕竞争与合作两种互动模式，分析战争、军备、联盟、强制外交等传统安全问题，以及气候变化，能源、互联网等非传统安全领域问题，培养学生在相关领域开展独立学术研究的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides students an overview of the field of international security. It introduces the theoretical frameworks and analytical methods of major strategic choices tackling issues ranging from war prevention and termination to peacemaking and peace maintaining such as deterrence, coercive diplomacy, reconciliation, and cooperation. It systematically introduces the basic concepts, cutting-edge theories and methods of the discipline of international relations, and analyzes traditional security issues such as war, arms, alliances and coercive diplomacy based on the two interaction models of competition and cooperation. It aims to cultivate students' ability to conduct independent academic research in related fields.

教学目标

One important goal of the course is for students to continually reflect on the core questions that animate the field of international security. Secondary goals of the course include: Building oral presentation skills through leading and participating in seminar discussions.  Sharpening concise writing skills.  Improving research and analysis skills. An original research paper is assigned to give Ph.D students a chance to improve their research and analysis skills. It will challenge students to design an interesting research design on a topic in international security, research it, and compose a paper with a coherent and compelling perspective. Achieving this requires each student carefully read all assigned course material (about 150 pages each week), participate in all seminar discussions, and complete course writing assignments. This course will be run as a seminar. Students are expected to prepare and present their answers in class. Each session will start with an introduction to the topic provided by the instructor, and then move to students’ presentations. In the end, the floor will be open for discussion.

教学方式

This is a seminar course. To get most out of the course and achieve good grades, you are highly suggested to: read the required readings carefully and prepare well for presentations and discussions; read selective readings to expand your knowledge and think thoroughly about the connections of different frameworks/approaches; actively contribute to discussions with thoughtful and diverse opinions (you are highly encouraged to challenges others’ opinions but do Not take it personally); always remember to take notes about your thoughts, comments, questions, etc; not be late for class, assignment due, and exam.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Course Introduction Week 2 Origin(s) of War Week 3 Security and Statecraft Week 4 Pure Theories of Total War: East vs. West Week 5 Theories of Limited War Week 6 Ending War Week 7 Deterrence-I: Conventional Week 8 Deterrence II: Nuclear Week 9 Compellence and Coercive Diplomacy Week 10 Alliance Formation, Management & Conflict Week 11 Defensive Realism, Cooperation & Institutions Week 12 IO, Security Community, Global Governance Week 13 Rebuilding Peace via Reconciliation Week 14 Ethnic Conflict Week 15 Domestic Politics of Grand Strategy Week 16 Understanding Strategic Behavior Week 17 Term paper writing Week 18 Final paper due.

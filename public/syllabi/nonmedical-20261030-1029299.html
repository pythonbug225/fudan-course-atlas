课程简介（中文）

英语学术语言特点（书面语语篇特征、名词化、被动结构、长句复杂句、时态、常见搭配，语篇衔接与连贯），研究选题、范围确定与原创性，文献回顾（文献检索、查阅、英语文献资料语言特征的分析和总结、文献回顾的写作技巧、引文格式和规范等），研究方法（如何在论文中表述研究思路、实验设计、数据采集等内容），导论与结论（论文导论的写法和常见误区，论文结论部分的写法（总结、进一步论述，等等）），论文摘要（摘要的基本类型、语言和语篇特点、论文标题、关键词）。 穿插的内容：学生作业或课程论文初稿、二稿的修改、点评，教师指导等，可以作为课堂专题，也可以穿插在其他内容讲解的过程中，由任课老师自行斟酌。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides assistance in English academic writing, its contents including: characteristics of academic English, organization of a research paper, strategies for textual cohesion and coherence, techniques for writing the abstract, introduction, literature review, methods section, discussion and conclusion, cover letter, and how to deal with peer-reviews and respond to letters from academic journal editors. Each topic or knowledge point is accompanied with illustrative examples that come from published papers, and supplemented with exercises that help cultivate a particular skill. The students will receive hands-on training in English research paper writing, under the teacher’s instructions, and are expected to know how to write and revise a research paper, applying all the linguistic tricks learned over this course, and know how to respond appropriately to reviewer and editor feedback.

教学目标

This course aims to improve the students’ knowledge and skill in academic English writing, and prepare them for tasks such as research paper publishing and international academic conferences. By the end of this course, the students, hopefully, will have developed a sharpened awareness of the characteristics of academic English and the organization of a research paper, acquired the ability to deploy specific linguistic strategies in research paper writing, and become cognizant of what to do in every phase of paper writing and publishing. This course also attempts to instill in the students a sense of academic integrity which underpins every plagiarism-avoiding technique.

教学方式

This course is a combination of lectures and exercises. By intermingling lectures on particular topics (e.g. characteristics of academic English, hedging, tense forms, how to write an abstract, an introductory part, a literature review) and exercises (e.g. Chinese-to-English translation, drafts of the term paper), and through case studies of a number of students’ outputs (selected from their assignments), the students should be able to improve both their proficiency in using the English language and their awareness of the structure and style of an academic thesis.

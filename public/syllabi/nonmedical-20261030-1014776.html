课程简介（中文）

全球航空业现状问题与应对策略，以及航空公司基本商业模式与收益经济学。

课程简介（英文）

Global Airlines’ Industry current issues and strategies, Basic Airline Business Models and Revenue Economics

教学目标

The aviation industry has been facing unprecedented challenges worldwide: Record levels of passenger demand, backlogs of new aircrafts, along a fierce competition to lower cost and managing changing customer preferences, demographics and global political challenges. Traditional models of Airline’s strategic positioning fail to address the magnitude of these challenges. Open innovation, systems integration and strategic human resource management are all part of a new model for airline’s global strategy. This course presents an opening segment on the current aviation industry, global landscape and solid business strategies with basics of revenue management and economics.

教学方式

In class lectures, case studies and presentation.

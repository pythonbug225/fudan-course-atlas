高温封装材料

2025秋 · EST70005.01 · 刘盼

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨聚焦高温电子封装这一前沿方向，讲解高耐温电子封装中所需的金属、陶瓷、高分子等材料的特性、制备工艺及应用。其中，就不同种类的金属烧结贴装材料展开深入探讨。同时，探讨工艺优化、性能测试、可靠性分析等高温封装材料面临的主要挑战，以及结合机器人、低空飞行器、光电等应用需求下高温封装的未来发展趋势。课程致力于培养学生明确高温封装的需求痛点，提高解决问题的能力，激发创新思维，为相关领域科研与实践打下坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the cutting-edge field of high-temperature electronics packaging, covering the properties, preparation processes, and applications of materials such as metals, ceramics, and polymers required for high-temperature electronics packaging. It includes in-depth discussions on different types of metal sintering materials. The course also addresses major challenges faced by high-temperature packaging materials, such as process optimization, performance testing, and reliability analysis, as well as the future development trends of high-temperature packaging in combination with the application demands of robotics, low-altitude aerial vehicles, and optoelectronics. It aims to help students identify the pain points in high-temperature packaging, enhance their problem-solving abilities, stimulate innovative thinking, and lay a solid foundation for scientific research and practical applications in this field.

教学目标

本课程教学目标是使学生理解高温电子封装的基本概念与分类，掌握高温封装材料的需求背景与发展趋势。学生能够掌握金属、陶瓷和高分子基的常用高温封装材料的特性与制备工艺。同时，掌握对封装材料进行工艺优化、性能评价与可靠性分析的相关方法，以提升半导体器件在各种环境下高质量应用。 The teaching objective of this course is to enable students to understand the basic concepts and classifications of high-temperature electronics packaging, and to grasp the demand background and development trends of high-temperature packaging materials. Students will be able to master the properties and preparation processes of commonly used high-temperature packaging materials such as metals, ceramics, and polymer-based materials. Meanwhile, they will also acquire the relevant methods for process optimization, performance evaluation, and reliability analysis of packaging materials, so as to enhance the high-quality application of semiconductor devices in various environments.

教学方式

课程讲义：通过自编讲义，传递高温封装材料的核心概念、分类和特性等基础知识，涵盖课程的核心内容。 案例研究：运用应用案例来讨论高温封装材料在不同行业的应用，提供实践洞察，帮助学生将理论与实践相结合。定期开展课堂讨论，鼓励学生积极参与，提出问题，通过互动式教学方式帮助学生澄清疑问，加深对知识的理解。 项目工作：通过小组作业和汇报促进团队合作和沟通技巧，学生将共同研究并汇报与高温封装相关的主题。学生将开展小型项目，探索高温封装的特定方面，这将鼓励学生自主学习和解决问题。 同伴评审：开展同伴评审活动，提供建设性反馈，增强批判性思维能力，学生将学会评估和批评彼此的工作，从而加深对学科的理解。 Course Notes: Through self-compiled notes, the core concepts, classifications, and properties of high-temperature packaging materials and other basic knowledge are conveyed, covering the core content of the course. Case Studies: Use application-oriented cases to discuss the use of high-temperature packaging materials in different industries, providing practical insights and helping students combine theory with practice. Regularly conduct classroom discussions to encourage active student participation and questioning. Through interactive teaching methods, help students clarify doubts and deepen their understanding of the knowledge. Project Work: Promote teamwork and communication skills through group assignments and presentations. Students will work together to research and present on topics related to high-temperature packaging. Students will undertake small-scale projects to explore specific aspects of high-temperature packaging, which will encourage independent learning and problem-solving. Peer Review: Conduct peer-review activities to provide constructive feedback and enhance critical-thinking skills. Students will learn to evaluate and critique each other's work, thereby deepening their understanding of the subject.

To master the properties of silver sintering materials, and the process flow of silver sintering, and to let students understand how to prepare high-performance metal high-temperature packaging structures through the synergistic optimization of materials and processes. 教学目标：理解银烧结的原理，掌握银烧结材料的特性和工艺流程，让学生理解如何通过材料-工艺的协同优化，以制备高性能金属高温封装结构。 Week 5 第5周 Course Content: Metal High-temperature Packaging Materials 3 Copper Sintering 教学内容：金属高温封装材料3 铜烧结 Course Objectives: To acquaint the principles and processes of copper sintering. To familiarize with the key points of copper sintering process, and to understand how to improve the performance of high-temperature packaging materials by optimizing process parameters. 教学目标：了解铜烧结的原理和过程，熟悉铜烧结的工艺要点，让学生理解如何通过优化工艺参数来提高高温封装材料的性能。 Week 6 第6周 Course Content: Metal High-temperature Packaging Materials 4 Composite Sintering Materials 教学内容：金属高温封装材料4复合烧结材料 Course Objectives: To understand the characteristics and advantages of composite sintering materials, to master the properties of composite sintering materials. To familiarize with the preparation process of composite sintering materials and the research and development process of composite sintering materials in high-temperature packaging. 教学目标：理解复合烧结材料的特点和优势，掌握复合烧结材料的特性。熟悉复合烧结材料的制备工艺，及高温封装中复合烧结材料的研发历程。 Week 7 第7周 Course Content: Ceramic-based High-temperature Packaging Materials 教学内容：陶瓷基高温封装材料 Course Objectives: To acquaint with the types and structural characteristics of ceramic materials. To master the properties of ceramic high-temperature packaging materials, and to select appropriate ceramic materials according to different packaging application scenarios. 教学目标：了解陶瓷材料的种类和结构特点。掌握陶瓷高温封装材料的特性，能够根据不同的封装应用场景选择合适的陶瓷材料。 Week 8 第8周 Course Contents: Polymer-based High-temperature Packaging Materials 教学内容：高分子基高温封装材料 Course Objectives: To acquaint with the types of polymer-based high-temperature packaging materials and their molecular structural characteristics. To familiarize with the processing technology of polymer-based high-temperature packaging materials and the impact of process parameters on material forming quality and packaging performance. 教学目标：了解高分子基高温封装材料的种类，以及它们的分子结构特点。熟悉高分子基高温封装材料的加工工艺，以及工艺参数对材料成型质量和封装性能的影响。 Week 9 第9周 Course Content: Group Presentation 教学内容：分组汇报 Course Objectives: Through group presentations, to deepen the understanding of the characteristics, preparation processes, and applications of different materials, and to promote the improvement of students' ability to systematize and comprehensively apply knowledge. To cultivate students' team collaboration ability, communication and expression ability, and problem analysis and solving ability. 教学目标：通过分组汇报，加深对不同材料特性、制备工艺以及应用等方面的理解，促进学生对知识的系统化和综合运用能力的提升。培养学生的团队协作能力、沟通表达能力和问题分析解决能力。 Week 10 第10周 Course Content: Process Optimization of High-temperature Packaging Materials 教学内容：高温封装材料的工艺优化 Course Objectives: To master the basic concepts and methods of process optimization of high-temperature packaging materials, and to understand the important role of process optimization in improving packaging quality and performance. To apply the principles of materials science and engineering to analyze and optimize the preparation process of high-temperature packaging materials. 教学目标：掌握高温封装材料工艺优化的基本概念和方法，了解工艺优化在提高封装质量和性能方面的重要作用。能够运用所学的材料科学和工程原理，对高温封装材料的制备工艺进行分析和优化。 Week 11 第11周 Course Content: Performance Testing and Evaluation of High-temperature Packaging Materials 教学内容：高温封装材料的性能测试与评价 Course Objectives: To master the commonly used methods and standards for performance testing of high-temperature packaging materials. To teach how to analyze and evaluate test data, to be able to judge the superiority or inferiority of material properties based on test results, to provide a basis for the selection and improvement of high-temperature packaging materials, and to cultivate students' rigorous data processing and analysis skills. 教学目标：掌握高温封装材料性能测试的常用方法和标准。学会对测试数据进行分析和评价，能够根据测试结果判断材料性能的优劣，为高温封装材料的选用和改进提供依据，同时培养学生严谨的数据处理和分析能力。 Week 12 第12周 Course Content: Reliability and Failure Analysis of High-temperature Packaging Materials 教学内容：高温封装材料的可靠性与失效分析 Course Objectives: To master the methods of reliability assessment of high-temperature packaging materials. To familiarize with the types and causes of failures of high-temperature packaging materials, and to be able to analyze failed materials to identify the root causes of failure, to provide guidance for the improvement and reliability enhancement of high-temperature packaging materials, and to cultivate problem diagnosis and solving abilities. 教学目标：掌握高温封装材料可靠性评估的方法。熟悉高温封装材料失效的类型和原因，能够通过对失效材料的分析，找出失效的根本原因，为高温封装材料的改进和可靠性提升提供指导，同时培养学生的问题诊断和解决能力。 Week 13 第13周 Course Content: Interface Issues of High-temperature Packaging Materials 教学内容：高温封装材料的界面问题 Course Objectives: To understand that the interface characteristics between different materials in high-temperature packaging have a crucial impact on packaging quality and performance.

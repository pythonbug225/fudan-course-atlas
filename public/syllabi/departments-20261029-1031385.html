课程简介（中文）

本课程为公司金融学的中级课程。通过本课程学习，学生将进一步掌握公司金融学涵盖的所有知识要点，并将学习运用课程中学到的理论来具体分析企业投融资决策，公司治理结构，企业并购重组等相关案例。

课程简介（英文）

This graduate course provides a deeper understanding of the principles and practices of corporate finance. Students will further explore the key concepts and tools used in financial decision-making within a corporation. The course covers essential topics such as time value of money, risk and return, stock valuation, financial statement analysis, capital budgeting, capital structure, payout policy and working capital management.  Emphasis is placed on the application of theoretical concepts to real-world scenarios, equipping students with the analytical skills necessary for effective financial management.

教学目标

The purpose of this course is to help the students develop deeper insights on important topics in the area of corporate finance.  After this course, they are expected to develop skills and knowledge necessary for the financial management of the corporations.

教学方式

Class discussions and case studies

教学内容与进度安排

1 The overview of the course 2 Time value of money 3 Bond and stock valuation 4 Risk and return 5 Optimal portfolio choice and CAPM 6 Capital budgeting 7 Equity and debt financing 8 Theories on capital structure 9 Payout policy 10 Advanced valuation 11 Financial analysis and planning 12 Special topics in corporate finance 13 Case studies by the students 14 Final Exam

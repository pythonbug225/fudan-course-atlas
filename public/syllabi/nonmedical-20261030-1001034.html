留学生高级汉语I

2025秋 · GECS10001.02 · 彭增安

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本大纲旨在为非中国本科生设计，通过16周、每周4课时的汉语课程，提升学生的汉语语言能力和跨文化交际能力。课程围绕家庭亲情、恋爱婚姻、男女平等、求职与工作等主题，帮助学生深入了解中国社会和文化，同时引导他们反思自身文化与中国文化的差异，促进双向文化交流。

课程简介（英文）

This syllabus is designed for non-Chinese undergraduate students, aiming to improve their Chinese language proficiency and cross-cultural communication skills through a 16-week course with 4 class hours per week. The course focuses on topics such as family ties, love and marriage, gender equality, job hunting and work, helping students gain a deeper understanding of Chinese society and culture. Meanwhile, it also guides students to reflect on the differences between their own culture and Chinese culture, thus promoting two-way cultural exchange.

教学目标

全面提高学生听说读写译的技能，尤其针对学生正式表达能力较弱的现状，重点提升他们的学术写作能力，同时提高学生的跨文化交际能力以及对中国文化的理解，促进学生对中国文化的认同。如果有华裔学生，则针对继承语教学设置特定的作业要求。

教学方式

课堂面授与课堂讨论相机和，同时通过课外作业巩固所学知识，通过社会实践加深对中国人和中国社会文化的理解。

教学内容与进度安排

Schedule about the course: Weeks 1-2: Family Ties, Love, and Marriage Lesson 1: Introduction to Chinese family structure and traditional family values • Lesson 2: Discussion on the status of love and marriage in Chinese culture • Lesson 3: Learning relevant Chinese expressions and commonly used vocabulary • Lesson 4: Group discussion: Comparing family and love-marriage concepts between China and other countries Weeks 3-4: Gender Equality and Job Hunting Lesson 1: Discussion on the concept of gender equality in Chinese society • Lesson 2: Analysis of the Chinese job market and job-hunting strategies • Lesson 3: Learning vocabulary and expressions related to professions • Lesson 4: Role-playing: Simulating a job interview in China Weeks 5-6: Language and Overseas Study Lesson 1: Understanding the current situation and challenges of Chinese language learning • Lesson 2: Discussion on overseas student life and cultural adaptation in China • Lesson 3: Learning vocabulary and practical sentences related to overseas study • Lesson 4: Case study: Success stories of Chinese overseas students Weeks 7-8: Consumption and the Internet Lesson 1: Researching Chinese consumer culture and market trends • Lesson 2: Discussion on the impact of online shopping and social media • Lesson 3: Learning vocabulary related to online shopping and social media • Lesson 4: Practical activity: Online shopping experience Weeks 9-10: Electronics and the Online Society Lesson 1: Introduction to the popularity and development of electronic products in China • Lesson 2: Analysis of the impact of the online society on Chinese people • Lesson 3: Learning vocabulary related to electronic products and the internet • Lesson 4: Debate: The pros and cons of electronic products on interpersonal communication Weeks 11-12: Language Challenges and the Art of Naming Lesson 1: Discussion on difficulties and challenges in learning Chinese • Lesson 2: Introduction to Chinese naming traditions and culture • Lesson 3: Learning methods to solve language problems • Lesson 4: Practical activity: Choosing a Chinese name for oneself Weeks 13-14: The New Generation, Festival Customs, and Food Culture Lesson 1: Discussion on the lifestyle and values of the new generation in China • Lesson 2: Introduction to traditional Chinese festivals and customs • Lesson 3: Learning about Chinese traditional food culture and vocabulary • Lesson 4: Cooking experience: Preparing traditional Chinese cuisine Weeks 15-16: Sports and Health, Living Abroad, This is China Lesson 1: Introduction to popular sports and fitness methods in China • Lesson 2: Sharing experiences and stories of foreigners living in China • Lesson 3: Summarizing learning outcomes and reviewing Chinese culture • Lesson 4: Simulated travel: Planning a travel route in China Evaluation and Feedback Weekly evaluation of students' learning progress through assignments, group discussions, and classroom performance. • Comprehensive testing after the course to assess students' Chinese proficiency and cross-cultural communication skills. • Encouraging students to provide feedback for continuous improvement of teaching methods and content.

教学参考资料

Teaching Materials Textbooks: Relevant articles from Global Times, multimedia teaching resources, simulated scenario materials, etc. • Supplementary materials: Books, films, and online resources related to Chinese culture.

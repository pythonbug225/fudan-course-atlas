课程简介（中文）

介绍自由场和相互作用场的规范量子化和路径积分，包括规范理论。将涵盖费曼图，重整化和异常。

课程简介（英文）

Introduction to canonical quantization and path integrals for free fields and interacting fields including gauge theories. Will cover Feynman diagrams, renormalization, and anomalies.

教学目标

To learn how to quantize fields using canonical quantization and the path integral method. How to compute exact correlation functions in free field theory using Wick’s theorem. How to compute correlation functions in interacting field theories, including gauge theories, perturbatively at weak coupling. Computation of perturbative correlation functions using Feynman diagrams and renormalization of divergent diagrams. Consequences of symmetries in QFT and quantum violation of classical symmetries.

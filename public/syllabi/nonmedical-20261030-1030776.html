课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在系统介绍经济学与金融学领域的现代实证研究方法，涵盖概念基础与实践应用。课程首先探讨实证研究问题的提出方式、描述性分析的作用，以及相关性与因果关系的区别。随后引入因果推断的基本概念、实验方法，并讨论使用观测数据识别因果关系所面临的挑战。 在此基础上，课程系统讲解应用研究中广泛使用的核心实证策略，包括工具变量法、双重差分法、匹配与加权方法、合成控制法以及断点回归设计。课程后半部分重点讨论如何通过稳健性检验、证伪检验、敏感性分析和外部有效性评估实证研究的可信度，并通过处理效应异质性分析与机制分析深化对实证结果的解读。 课程全程将结合具有影响力的经典实证论文，引导学生理解前沿研究者如何在实际研究中设计实证方案并论证其可靠性。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a conceptual and practical introduction to modern empirical methods used in economics and finance. It begins by discussing how empirical research questions are developed, the role of descriptive analysis, and the distinction between correlation and causation. The course then introduces causality, experiments, and the challenges of identification using observational data. Building on this foundation, the course covers core empirical strategies widely used in applied research, including instrumental variables, difference-in-differences, matching and weighting methods, synthetic control, and regression discontinuity designs. The latter part of the course focuses on assessing empirical credibility through robustness checks, falsification tests, sensitivity analysis, and external validity, as well as deepening interpretation through treatment heterogeneity and mechanism analysis. Throughout the course, students engage with influential empirical papers to understand how leading researchers design and defend empirical studies in practice.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to train students to think like empirical researchers. Rather than focusing on statistical techniques or software implementation, the course emphasizes how empirical methods are used to formulate research questions, establish causal relationships, evaluate empirical credibility, and interpret empirical findings. By the end of the course, students will be able to understand and critically assess empirical research designs, identify key sources of causal variation, evaluate the strengths and limitations of commonly used empirical methods, and apply disciplined empirical reasoning when reading or presenting academic research in economics and finance.

教学方式

The course is taught primarily through lectures combined with guided discussion of selected empirical studies. Lectures focus on conceptual understanding, economic intuition, and research design rather than mathematical derivations or software implementation. Empirical papers are integrated into each topic to illustrate how methods are applied in practice and to develop students’ ability to critically evaluate empirical research. Students are expected to actively participate in class discussions, present selected academic papers, and write research proposals. Emphasis is placed on clear reasoning, careful identification arguments, and thoughtful critique, with the goal of helping students internalize best practices in empirical research

教学内容与进度安排

 Course Introduction  Starting Empirical Research: Research Questions, Styles, and Descriptive Analysis  Causality, Experiments, and Identification with Observational Data  Core Methods:  Instrumental Variables  DID, Matching, Weighting, and Synthetic Control  Regression Discontinuity Design  Credibility Checks: Robustness, Falsification, Sensitivity, and External Validity  Deepening Understanding: Treatment Heterogeneity & Mechanisms  How Great Economists Do Empirical Work (Optional)  Paper Presentations

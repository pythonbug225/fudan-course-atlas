课程简介（中文）

授课内容包括1、现代免疫分析技术；2、现代核酸分析技术；3、生物芯片及Lab-Chip技术；4、空气中病原微生物分析技术；5、水中微生物分析技术；、6饮用水国标中生物指标检测技术。

要求学生初步掌握现代生物分析技术，尤其在环境空气和水质监测中的应用，了解我国及世界先进国家在环境微生物检测和监测中的具体技术水平。

对每一个专题，每个学生结合其兴趣，选择一个题目，完成文献检索，课堂做PPT报告。每个学生要做5-6次PPT报告，每次时间在15-20分钟。将根据文献检索的内容，PPT制作水平和表述，每次给予成绩（及格、中、良、优），并根据其平时课堂情况适当调整。

课程简介（英文）

The course content includes: 1. Modern immunoassay techniques; 2. Modern nucleic acid analysis techniques; 3. Biochips and lab-on-a-chip technology; 4. Analytical techniques for airborne pathogenic microorganisms; 5. Analytical techniques for microorganisms in water; 6. Detection techniques for biological indicators in drinking water as per national standards.

Students are expected to acquire a preliminary mastery of modern biological analysis techniques, particularly their application in environmental air and water quality monitoring, and to understand the specific technological levels employed in environmental microorganism detection and monitoring in China and other advanced countries worldwide.

For each topic, every student will select a subject based on their interests, conduct literature research, and deliver a PowerPoint presentation in class. Each student is required to give 5–6 presentations, each lasting 15–20 minutes. Grades will be assigned based on the content of the literature research, the quality of the PowerPoint presentation, and the delivery, with appropriate adjustments made according to their usual classroom performance.

教学目标

课程传授学生现代前沿生物分析技术的原理和在环境生物分析中具体应用，授课内容涵盖现代免疫分析技术，现代核酸分析技术，生物芯片技术，测序技术，空气中病原微生物分析技术，水中微生物分析技术。通过学习掌握对环境藻类及相关毒素、细菌、霉菌、病毒和寄生虫等微生物的现代分析技术，尤其在环境空气和水质监测中的应用，了解我国及世界先进国家在环境微生物检测和监测中的具体技术水平。

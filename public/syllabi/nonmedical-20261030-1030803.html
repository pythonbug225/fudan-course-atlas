课程简介（中文）

有越来越多的迹象表明，翻译是外语教学中一种实用且正当的工具，其教学法和方法的复杂程度较以往有了显著提升（W. Wills）。尽管经过了多年的英语语言训练，但总体而言，医学生们对翻译理论缺乏整体认识，对翻译技巧也缺乏深入理解。本课程旨在向学生阐明翻译理论、中英语言之间的差异以及医学文献翻译的技巧。此外，还选取期刊文章作为作业，以期让学生接触到医学科学的最新进展。

课程简介（英文）

There are a growing number of indications that translation is a useful and legitimate tool in foreign language teaching with a markedly higher degree of didactive and methodical sophistication than earlier before (W. Wills). Despite years of training in the English language, medical students, as a whole, do not have a general picture of the theory of translation and a thorough understanding of translation skills. The course is designed to enlighten students with the theory of translation, the differences between the Chinese and English languages and the skills for the translation of medical literature. Furthermore, journal articles are selected as assignments in the hope that students get access to the latest advance in medical sciences.

教学目标

The students are expected to get to know the history of translation, the definition of translation, the differences between the Chinese language and the English language, the differences between literal translation and liberal translation, the features of medical English and the skills for medical English translation. They are required to translate journal articles recently published and to interpret medicine-related video clips. Upon completion of the course, the students are expected to have improved skills in C-E and E-C translation, both in the written form and the oral form.

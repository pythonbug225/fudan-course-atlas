课程简介（英文）

This course introduces social policy from a practical perspective, covering fundamental concepts and cultivating students' thinking and practical capabilities in policy work. It comprises three integrated dimensions: ‌Historical and Dynamic Analysis of Social Policy‌: Explores the formation and evolution of social policy boundaries, along with theoretical models. ‌Core Elements of Policy Process‌: Examines decision-making procedures, policy tool selection, and implementation models. ‌Practical Simulation and Methods‌: Features classroom exercises to demonstrate policy analysis techniques and participatory consensus-building approaches—focusing on evidence utilization in policy, stakeholder engagement, cross-interviews, participatory group policy reviews, Delphi methods, and systematic literature reviews. While not centered on specific policy domains, the course references representative cases from domestic and international contexts. As a practice-oriented course, it emphasizes hands-on activities integrated throughout the teaching weeks to accommodate in-class simulations.

教学目标

课程内容涵盖科学与技术发展的早期理论、知识分类与社会秩序、情境性知识与多元主体、行动者网络理论（ANT）、新物质主义、后人类主义与赛博空间等核心主题。通过这些内容，学生将理解科技实践如何不仅反映社会文化特征，也在动态中重构社会秩序与权力关系。此外，课程将讨论实验室民族志、技术物的政治性以及大数据与算法等当代科技议题，揭示科技在全球化与地方实践中的复杂性。

教学方式

课程以研究生研讨班形式进行，强调文本阅读、课堂展示和互动讨论。学生需深入研读经典著作与前沿研究，批判性地分析理论框架与案例研究，培养跨学科的理解与分析能力。同时，课程鼓励学生结合自身研究兴趣，将理论与现实问题相结合，探索科技人类学在当代社会中的应用。本课程旨在通过科技人类学的独特视角，引导学生理解科学与技术在社会文化中的动态演变，培养其在学术研究和实际问题中运用科技人类学方法进行深度分析的能力。

Derivatives and Risk Management

课程简介（中文）

Derivatives have become a popular hedging and investment tool over the last three decades, and their concepts are required for almost every advanced finance topic. This course provides students an introduction of derivatives (forwards, futures, options). Students will first learn the basic concepts of derivatives, how to price them, and some general principles on how to use them for hedging, speculation and arbitrage. We will also invite industry experts to share with us some real-life examples of derivatives trading in both equity and Treasury markets.

教学目标

Understand the mechanics of forwards, futures and options. Understand the general features of derivative markets Get familiar with derivative products in China and globe. Learn how to determine forward/futures prices as well as values of forward/futures contracts. Learn the determinants and bounds for option prices. Understand the no arbitrage principle and call put parity. Learn basic option trading strategies.

教学方式

Lectures, Reading, Discussions, Exercises and Examination.

教学内容与进度安排

Class/Week 1 Topic: Introduction, Futures markets Content: Basic concepts of forward and future, futures markets and clearing Reading: Hull Chap 1-2 Class/Week 2 Topic: Hedging and pricing of futures Content: Hedging strategies and determination of forward and future prices Reading: Hull Chap 3,5 Class/Week 3 Topic: Interest rate futures, Swaps Content: Concepts of interest rates futures and swaps, guest talk on futures trading Reading: Hull Chap 6-7 Class/Week 4 Topic: Mechanics of option market Content: Concepts and properties of stock options Reading: Hull Chap 10,11 Class/Week 5 Topic: Trading strategies involving options Content: Common option trading strategies Reading: Hull Chap 12 Class/Week 6 Topic: Option pricing; Options on Stock Indices Content: Black-Scholes-Merton model, guest talk on option trading Reading: Hull Chap 15,17 Note: This year, we will try to arrange an onsite visit to CFFE(中金所)

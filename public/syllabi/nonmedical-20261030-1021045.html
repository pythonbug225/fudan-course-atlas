课程简介（中文）

《实验谱学》是面向本科生和研究生的英文授课中文辅助的专业课程，系统介绍了实验光谱学的基本原理、关键技术与前沿应用，特别是VUV，EUV和X射线光谱学的原理和应用，以及在当前热门的聚变以及先进制程光刻领域的重要应用。课程涵盖光谱发展历程、主要光源与分光器件、探测器原理及典型光谱系统，结合虚拟仿真实验与实际观测训练，培养学生掌握光谱分析方法，理解其在物理、核技术等领域的重要作用，提升科学素养与实验能力。

课程简介（英文）

"Experimental Spectroscopy" is a professional course taught primarily in English with Chinese assistance, designed for both undergraduate and graduate students. It comprehensively covers the fundamental principles, key technologies, and cutting-edge applications of experimental spectroscopy, with special emphasis on the principles and applications of VUV, EUV, and X-ray spectroscopy. The course highlights its significant role in current high-impact fields such as fusion energy and advanced process lithography. Topics include the historical development of spectroscopy, primary light sources, spectral splitters, detector principles, and typical spectroscopic systems. Through a combination of virtual simulation experiments and hands-on observational training, the course aims to equip students with spectroscopic analysis methods, deepen their understanding of its importance in physics, nuclear technology, and related disciplines, and enhance their overall scientific literacy and experimental capabilities.

教学目标

本课程旨在通过系统的理论讲授与前沿的实践训练，使学生能够： 掌握核心知识： 系统掌握实验光谱学的基本原理，特别是真空紫外（VUV）、极紫外（EUV）和X射线光谱学的关键技术、仪器设备（如光源、分光器、探测器）及其工作原理。 理解前沿应用： 深刻理解光谱学在重大前沿科学与技术领域（如磁约束核聚变、先进光刻技术等）中的关键作用和应用场景，了解当前的研究热点与发展趋势。 培养综合能力： 通过虚拟仿真、光谱观测实践和专题研讨，培养学生设计和执行光谱实验、分析与解读光谱数据、以及就光谱学专题进行有效学术报告与交流的能力。 塑造科学素养： 引导学生在学习过程中体会光谱学如何推动物理学发展，并理解核心技术自主可控的重要性，培养其服务国家重大战略需求的使命与担当，培养其探索精神、创新思维与科学责任感。 This course aims to enable students to: Master Core Knowledge: Systematically grasp the fundamental principles of experimental spectroscopy, particularly the key technologies, instrumentation (such as light sources, spectral splitters, detectors), and working principles of Vacuum Ultraviolet (VUV), Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV), and X-ray spectroscopy. Understand Cutting-Edge Applications: Deeply comprehend the critical role and application scenarios of spectroscopy in major forefront scientific and technological fields (e.g., magnetic confinement fusion, advanced lithography technology), and understand current research hotspots and development trends. Develop Comprehensive Abilities: Cultivate students' abilities to design and execute spectroscopic experiments, analyze and interpret spectroscopic data, and deliver effective academic presentations and communications on spectroscopic topics through virtual simulations, hands-on spectral observation practice, and thematic seminars. Shape Scientific Literacy: Guide students to appreciate how spectroscopy has driven the development of physics, understand the importance of independent control of core technologies, foster a sense of mission and responsibility to serve major national strategic needs, and nurture their spirit of exploration, innovative thinking, and scientific responsibility.

教学方式

为达成知识、能力与素养相统一的教学目标，本课程采用多元化的研究型教学模式： 前沿系统讲授： 以英语系统精讲光谱学核心知识体系，穿插中文进行关键概念阐释与答疑，确保学生对复杂原理的深刻理解。课程内容紧密结合教师团队的科研前沿，动态引入VUV/EUV/X射线光谱等领域的最新进展。 虚拟仿真与实操结合： 利用VR实验、计算机模拟等手段，突破高端光谱仪器在本科教学中难以触及的局限，让学生安全、低成本地完成如晶体光谱仪模拟等复杂训练。同时，辅以光谱观测实践和自制简易光谱仪等活动，强化动手能力与工程直觉。 问题导向的专题研讨： 围绕聚变诊断、光刻光源等国家重大需求与前沿科学问题组织专题讨论，引导学生主动查阅文献、分析案例并进行口头报告，培养其批判性思维、解决复杂问题的能力及学术交流能力。 科研反哺与价值引领： 将教师在精密光谱、EBIT物理等领域的科研成果转化为教学案例，使学生在学习专业知识的同时，切身感受“从0到1”的探索过程和突破核心技术“卡脖子”问题的科学精神与家国情怀。 To achieve the unified educational objectives of knowledge, ability, and literacy, this course adopts a diversified, research-oriented teaching model: Systematic Lecturing on Cutting-Edge Topics: The core knowledge framework of spectroscopy will be delivered systematically in English, supplemented by explanations in Chinese for key concepts and Q&A sessions, ensuring students' profound understanding of complex principles. Course content is closely integrated with the research frontiers of the teaching team, dynamically incorporating the latest advancements in fields such as VUV/EUV/X-ray spectroscopy. Integration of Virtual Simulation and Hands-On Practice: Utilizing methods like VR experiments and computer simulations to overcome the constraints of limited access to advanced spectroscopic instruments in undergraduate teaching, allowing students to safely and cost-effectively complete complex training such as crystal spectrometer simulations. This is complemented by hands-on spectral observation practices and activities like building simple spectrometers to strengthen practical skills and engineering intuition. Problem-Oriented Thematic Seminars: Organizing thematic discussions centered on national major needs and frontier scientific issues, such as fusion diagnostics and lithography light sources. This guides students to actively consult literature, analyze cases, and deliver oral presentations, cultivating their critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and academic communication abilities. Research-Fed Teaching and Value Guidance: Transforming the instructors' research achievements in fields like precision spectroscopy and EBIT physics into teaching cases. This allows students to personally experience the "from 0 to 1" exploration process and the scientific spirit behind breaking through core technology "bottleneck" issues, while learning professional knowledge, thereby fostering their scientific spirit and sense of national mission.

教学内容与进度安排

课程进度安排 第1周： 光谱学与课程导论；从简单但具有深刻内涵的现象-荧光展开微观世界的规律。 第2周： 光谱学发展史：里程碑事件与关键人物；科学精神的启示。 第3周： 光谱学物理基础：光子与物质相互作用、谱线线形与展宽机制。 第4周： 先进光源I：托卡马克与仿星器中的等离子体光源原理；VR虚拟演示及其在聚变能源中的应用背景。 第5周： 先进光源II：同步辐射光源、加速器光源与自由电子激光（侧重VUV/EUV/X射线波段）。 第6周： 先进光源III：电子束离子阱（EBIT）、束箔等实验室光源；VR体验与科研案例分析。 第7周： 分光器件——棱镜：原理、应用与实验演示；实验谱学的理论基础和实践。 第8周： 分光器件——光栅：原理、应用与光谱仪设计基础。 第9周： 模拟训练I：光栅光谱仪的仿真设计与操作。 第10周： 分光器件——晶体：原理及其在X射线光谱仪中的应用。 第11周： 模拟训练II：晶体光谱仪的仿真设计与操作。 第12周： 分光器件——干涉仪及其他新型分光技术导论；VR虚拟实验。 第13周： 光谱探测器I：光子放大器（如微通道板）与位敏探测器（如延迟线探测器）。 第14周： 光谱探测器II：动量谱仪；VR虚拟实验与数据分析。 第15周： 光谱探测器III：半导体探测器（CCD, SDD）、微热量计等原理与性能比较。 第16周： 前沿应用专题研讨与最终汇报指导。 第17-18周： 灵活安排，参观相关科研平台（如EBIT装置），课程总结与最终汇报 Course Schedule Week 1: Introduction to Spectroscopy and the Course; Unveiling the Microscopic World through Simple yet Profound Phenomena - Fluorescence. Week 2: The History of Spectroscopy: Milestone Events and Key Figures; Enlightenment from the Scientific Spirit. Week 3: Physical Foundations of Spectroscopy: Photon-Matter Interactions, Spectral Line Shapes, and Broadening Mechanisms. Week 4: Advanced Light Sources I: Principles of Plasma Light Sources in Tokamaks and Stellarators; VR Demonstration and Application Context in Fusion Energy. Week 5: Advanced Light Sources II: Synchrotron Radiation Sources, Accelerator-Based Sources, and Free-Electron Lasers (Focus on VUV/EUV/X-ray Bands). Week 6: Advanced Light Sources III: Laboratory Light Sources such as Electron Beam Ion Trap (EBIT) and Beam-Foil; VR Demonstration and Research Case Analysis. Week 7: Spectral Splitting Devices – Prisms: Principles, Applications, and Experimental Demonstration. Week 8: Spectral Splitting Devices – Gratings: Principles, Applications, and Fundamentals of Spectrometer Design. Week 9: Simulation Training I: Simulated Design and Operation of Grating Spectrometers. Week 10: Spectral Splitting Devices – Crystals: Principles and Their Application in X-ray Spectrometers. Week 11: Simulation Training II: Simulated Design and Operation of Crystal Spectrometers. Week 12: Spectral Splitting Devices – Introduction to Interferometers and Other Novel Splitting Technologies; VR Virtual Experiments. Week 13: Spectral Detectors I: Photon Amplifiers (e.g., Microchannel Plate - MCP) and Position-Sensitive Detectors (e.g., Delay Line Detector). Week 14: Spectral Detectors II: Momentum Spectrometers; VR Virtual Experiments and Data Analysis. Week 15: Spectral Detectors III: Principles and Performance Comparison of Semiconductor Detectors (CCD, SDD), Microcalorimeters, etc. Week 16: Frontier Application Seminars and Final Presentation Guidance. Weeks 17-18: Flexible Schedule, including visits to relevant research platforms (e.g., EBIT facility), Course Summary, and Final Presentations.

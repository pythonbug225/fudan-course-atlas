课程简介（中文）

本课程是以国际关系学科为重点的本科生写作技巧入门课程。课程旨在培养学生阅读和分析学术文献、寻找研究问题、构建论点、推理、使用证据支持论点、论文结构等技能。课程通过对国际关系领域的精选文章进行仔细分析，通过关于具体写作技巧的讲座，以及通过课堂练习和书面作业的互动发展来实现。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an introduction to undergraduate writing skills focused on the discipline of international relations. It seeks to develop skills such as reading and analyzing academic literature, finding research questions, constructing arguments, reasoning, using evidence to support arguments, essay structuring, and so on. It does so through a careful analysis of selected texts in the IR field, through lectures about specific writing skills, as well as through the interactive development of in-class exercises and written assignments.

教学方式

Weekly student presentation, lecturing, in-class discussion and exercise, writing assignments and feedback, mini-conference

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction

  • Course overview
  • Discussion: the value of writing
  • Discussion: How to write a lot?
  • To do: Think about the research you want to do for final paper and be prepared to take advise from instructors.

Week 2: Applied skills

  • Giving academic presentations
  • Writing applications for graduate schools

Week 3: Improving writing through reading

  • Bring a book or an article you read and like to the class.
  • How to read a book?
  • Reading analytically
  • Reverse-engineering others’ work

Week 4: How to write a research paper: An introduction

  • The value of writing a research paper
  • The mindset for analytical writing
  • The style of academic writing in political science
  • Key components of a research paper
  • Writing a research paper: steps and the schedule

Week 5: Finding a research question

  • How to find a topic?
  • Topics to avoid
  • From topic to a research question
  • The “so what?” question

Week 6: The literature

  • Why do we need a literature review section?
  • How to write a literature review?
  • Summarizing, Paraphrasing and Quoting
  • Avoiding plagiarism

Week 7: Thesis and evidence

  • In short, what do you want to say in your paper?
  • Research design
  • Finding information
  • Evaluating information

Week 8: Writing the main body

  • Using an outline to structure your paper
  • Writing about your findings
  • Writing clearly
  • Using graphics

 

Week 9: Revising your draft & proposal feedback

 

  • Basic revision skills
  • The importance of responding to feedback
  • How to respond to feedback

Week 10: Abstract, introduction, and conclusion

  • How to write an abstract?
  • Tell them what you want to tell.
  • Tell them what you have told them.

Week 11: Writing for the media

  • Writing news reports
  • Writing an opinion piece  

Week 12: Writing a policy report

  • The audience
  • The style
  • Key components of a policy brief/report
  • What are the problems? What are the solutions?

Week 13-15: Mini-conference

考核与成绩构成

项目比例评定说明
出勤Attendance10.0%Students are required to attend each session. Unexcused absences will lead to a 0 for that session.
汇报Presentation20.0%Students will be divided into groups. Starting from week 5, in each week, one group will deliver a 15-20-minute presentation about text(s) provided by the instructors (see below). In this presentation, the presenters will explain to the class: (1) how did the author(s) find the research question and why is the question important; (2) what does the existing literature says about the question; (3) what is the thesis and how the analytic framework was developed; (4) how did the text support the argument; (5) your comments to the article. The formal pre will be followed by a short Q&A session, in which presenters will address questions from the audience (this means that students not presenting should at least skim the text(s) to be discussed in that week).
作业/实验/实践Assignment20.0%Students will be required to write a long research paper by the end of the semester, which is an in-depth study of a topic of interest in the IR field. As a first step, students will be required to submit a 2-page proposal that includes one research question, a discussion of why it is important, an initial thesis statement that answers the question, and a plan for doing the research. The final paper can be on the same topic discussed in the short essay, but the question shall be framed as a research question (not an opinion) and the research paper must follow an academic style (no longer than 2 pages, double-spaced, Due Week 7).
课题研讨Topic Discussion5.0%This will take place as a mini-conference in weeks 13-15 of the class. Consider this as a chance to improve your presentation skills and a chance to get some feedback for your paper. This task is compulsory, failing to deliver the pre will cause the final paper to be marked down 2 scales.
课题论文Research Paper45.0%Build on the proposal developed in “assignment 4” and write an 8-10-page (around 2000-2500 words, can be longer if deemed necessary) (including in-text citations but excluding abstract, title, and bibliography) research paper. Students will need to find additional readings and supportive materials on their own. The paper will be graded based on the quality of the research question (including clearly identifying its relationship with the literature), the clarity of the arguments, the usage of evidence, the logic flow, the style, and grammar. (8-10 pages, 12 fonts, double spaced, can be longer, Due Week 16). A late paper will be marked down one scale (e.g., from B+ to B) for each day late.

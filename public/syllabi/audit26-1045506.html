课程简介（中文）

课程分为行政文书写作、外交文书写作两大模块。行政文书写作模块包括概述（行政文书写作的内涵、主题与材料、适用语言与修辞、行文规则与流程）、写作（会议纪要、函、通知、请示与批复）两个部分；外交文书写作模块也包括概述（外交文书写作的含义和种类、特点和作用、撰写步骤和要求）、写作（备忘录、邀请函、祝酒词、授权书）两个部分。

课程简介（英文）

The course is divided into two major modules: Administrative Document Writing and Diplomatic Document Writing. The Administrative Document Writing module consists of two parts: an Overview (the connotation of administrative document writing, themes and materials, appropriate language and rhetoric, drafting rules and procedures) and Writing (meeting minutes, letters, notices, requests for instructions, and official replies). The Diplomatic Document Writing module also comprises two parts: an Overview (the meaning and categories of diplomatic document writing, its characteristics and functions, writing procedures and requirements) and Writing (memorandums, letters of invitation, toast speeches, and letters of authorization).

教学目标

拟通过对行政与外交文书写作的理论知识和行政与外交文书写作的实践活动的讲解，帮助学生基本掌握行政与外交文书写作的技巧。让学生比较系统地了解行政与外交文书写作的规范和方法，基本掌握行政与外交文书写作的技巧。

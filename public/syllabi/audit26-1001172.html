课程简介（中文）

本课程是环境水文学入门课程，主要包含二部分：（1）介绍流域水文过程的基本理论以及峰值流量估算、频率分析等常用水文分析方法;(2)基于一开源流域建模软件SWAT介绍流域水文建模的基本过程。学生将基于现实流域的数据，学习从数据处理到模型构建以及模拟结果分析的SWAT建模全过程。作为水文学研究的重要工具，GIS及其在该领域中的应用也进行了介绍。

课程简介（英文）

This is an introductory course to Environmental Hydrology. It consists of two main parts. The first part is devoted to introducing the basic theories on hydrological processes such as precipitation, interception, infiltration, evaporation and transpiration, as well as some commonly used hydrological analysis methods such as water balance calculation, peak flow estimation, and frequency analysis. The second part is devoted to introducing the basic processes of watershed modeling with the aid of a popular open source watershed modeling software - SWAT (Soil and Water Assessment Tool). The fundamental concepts and steps of SWAT modeling are introduced through an example dataset. For term project, students are expected to finish a SWAT case study using the provided watershed datasets. As an important tool to hydrology studies, Geographic Information System (GIS) and its applications in the field are also introduced. 

教学目标

This course aims to introduce the fundamentals of environmental hydrology as well as some basic hydrological analysis and simulation methods. As an important tool for hydrologists, Geographic Information System (GIS) and its applications in hydrology are also introduced. Specifically, this course contains three modules: (1) introduction to the fundamental theories on hydrologic processes; (2) introduction to some commonly used hydrological analysis methods; and (3) introduction to using SWAT (Soil and Water Assessment Tool) for hydrological process simulations. By using a real-world watershed as an example, the course provides students with a comprehensive case introduction covering the entire process from data processing to SWAT model construction and result analysis. This enables students to gain a direct understanding of the watershed modeling process and enhances their abilities in comprehensive case analysis and scientific writing.

教学方式

  • Lectures will cover fundamental theories related to hydrologic processes and hydrological analysis methods.
  • Classroom discussions are organized to explore course materials.
  • In-class exercises are incorporated to reinforce understanding of the presented theories and methods.
  • A comprehensive SWAT case study will guide students through the complete workflow, including data processing, SWAT model development, and discussion of simulation results.

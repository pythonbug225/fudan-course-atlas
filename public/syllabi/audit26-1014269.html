课程简介（中文）

《配位化学》是无机化学中极为重要的分支学科,在化学基本理论研究及实际应用方面有着越来越重要的地位,它已渗透到生物、分离分析、医药、催化冶金、材料科学、环境科学等领域，与各学科有着日益广泛的联系。本课程分为两部分: 第一部分包括配位化合物的基本知识和基本理论、配合物的合成及表征方法等。第二部分包括现代配位化学的发展趋势，涵盖纳米配位化学、超分子与自组装、单原子催化剂等。文献阅读及读书报告汇报是考核的重要组成部分。 作为高年级选修课,通过本课程学习，使学生对配位化学的基本知识、基本理论、研究方法、应用及发展有较全面的认识,了解配位化学的研究方法、应用及其发展趋势。

课程简介（英文）

"Coordination Chemistry" is an extremely important branch of inorganic chemistry, playing an increasingly significant role in both fundamental chemical research and practical applications. It has permeated fields such as biology, separation and analysis, medicine, catalytic metallurgy, materials science, and environmental science, establishing ever-broadening connections with various disciplines. This course is divided into two parts: The first part covers the fundamental knowledge and basic theories of coordination compounds, as well as the synthesis and characterization methods of complexes. The second part addresses the development trends of modern coordination chemistry, including nano-coordination chemistry, supramolecular chemistry and self-assembly, single-atom catalysts, and more. Literature reading and book report presentations are important components of the assessment. As an advanced elective course, this course aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the basic knowledge, theories, research methods, applications, and developments in coordination chemistry, as well as insights into its research methodologies, applications, and future trends.

教学目标

通过学习了解配位化学的基本知识、理论体系、研究方法及研究前沿，了解配位化学与材料科学、医药、催化、生命科学等领域的交叉；通过阅读文献、读书报告的形式，着重培养同学们的文献阅读和综合能力：熟练运用所学知识的读懂文献的能力、收集和提炼文献中信息的能力、发现-分析-解决问题的能力、团队协作精神、语言表达能力、应对提问交流的能力等。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是面向已完成初级汉语（上） 或具备HSK 1级水平学习者的进阶基础汉语课程。课程在巩固语音、基础词汇和语法的同时，重点扩展实用表达场景，系统提升学习者在日常生活情境中的听、说、读、写综合交际能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an elementary Chinese course designed for learners who have completed Elementary Chinese (Part I) or possess HSK Level 1 proficiency. While reinforcing pronunciation, basic vocabulary, and grammar, the course focuses on expanding practical communication scenarios and systematically enhancing learners’ overall listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills in everyday life contexts.

教学目标

The course helps learners acquire approximately 150–200 new vocabulary words and key grammatical structures (such as result complements, directional complements, basic comparative sentences, and introductory “ba” sentences), enabling them to:  Understand and participate in short conversations on everyday topics (such as shopping, asking for directions, food and drink, making plans, etc.);  Use basic and coherent sentences to describe experiences, express needs, or offer suggestions;  Gain a preliminary understanding of relevant Chinese cultural customs, facilitating a transition from basic survival communication to handling everyday life situations.

教学方式

Scenario-based instruction: Centered around high-frequency daily life topics such as transportation, shopping, food and drink, health, and leisure. Balanced skill development: Enhances language application skills through task-based activities. Cultural integration: Incorporates Chinese social etiquette and cultural knowledge into language learning. Aligned with HSK Level 2: Lays a solid foundation for the Chinese Proficiency Test (HSK Level 2).

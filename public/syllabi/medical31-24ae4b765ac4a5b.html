1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程系统介绍神经病学的基本理论、核心知识和临床技能。内容包括神经系统常见疾病的临床表现、诊断、治疗和预防，重点涉及脑卒中、癫痫、运动障碍等。课程还鼓励学生积极参加小组汇报与讨论，提升学习效果与交流能力。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The course provides a systematic overview of the fundamental theories, essential knowledge, and core skills in neurology. We will cover the clinical features, diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of neurological diseases, with a focus on common conditions such as stroke, seizures, and movement disorders. Students will also be encouraged to participate in active group presentations to enhance learning and communication.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

This course aims to introduce: the basic principles of functional neuroanatomy that are essential for clinical localization; systematic approaches to evaluate and establish differential diagnoses in patients presenting with common neurological symptoms; the epidemiology, etiology, clinical features, imaging findings, pathology, pathophysiology, and treatments of common neurological conditions. Our goal is to equip graduates with a solid foundation for distinguished careers in clinical practice. We strive to create a learning environment that prepares students for a future where scientific knowledge, societal expectations, and human needs continue to evolve. We hope that, by participating in this course, students will cultivate an enthusiasm for lifelong learning.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

In-class lectures: PPT and Group presentation. Practice at wards: cases presentation and demonstration.

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

Lectures Week 1&2: Introduction to Clinical Neurology (4 academic hours) Week 3&4: Cerebrovascular Diseases (4 academic hours) Week 5: Movement Disorders (2 academic hours) Week 6: Epilepsy (2 academic hours) Week 7: CNS Demyelinating Diseases (2 academic hours) Week 8: Headache (2 academic hours) Week 9: Myelopathy (2 academic hours) Week 10: Peripheral Neuropathy (2 academic hours) Week 11: Muscle Disease (2 academic hours) Week 12: Dementia & Cognitive dysfunction (2 academic hours) Practice at wards, Neurology Department, Huashan Hospital & Zhongshan Hospital Week 4: Neurologic History & Examination (3 academic hours) Week 5: Cerebrovascular Diseases & Movement Disorders (3 academic hours) Week 6: Headache & Epilepsy (3 academic hours) Week 7: Myopathy & Demyelinating Disease (3 academic hours) Week 8: Neurological Diseases Review (3 academic hours) Week 1 Overview, Neurologic History inquiry Have a whole point of view of nervous system’s anatomy including central nervous system and peripheral nervous system. Review the basic function of nervous system, the developing history of Neurology and the role of nervous system in coordinating the human body and maintaining the homeostasis. Know the critical challenges that emerging neurological diseases bring to the public health. Week 2 Neurological Examination & Investigative Studies An understanding of the anatomical structure, physiological functions, and clinical significance of the various tissues of the nervous system, including the embryology, neurological examination and specific laboratory tests. Know how to perform neurological examination. Know how to select the optimistic investigative studies for patients with suspected neurological diseases. Week 3 Stroke-I Understand the etiology, clinical manifestations, and treatment of stroke Know the anatomical structure of cerebral vessels and adjacent structures. Be familiar with common causes of stroke. Know the common brainstem syndromes caused by infarction and how to figure out the causal vessel by interpreting the clinical symptoms. Week 4 Stroke-II Group presentation of different brainstem syndromes. Know the clinical manifestations of stroke. Know the diagnostic strategy of acute stroke. A basic understanding of brain imaging of stroke. Know the therapeutic strategy of stroke. Week 5 Movement Disorders Know the structural abnormality of movement disorders. The etiology and biochemical abnormalities in movement disorders. Clinical features and classifications of movement disorders. The current therapeutic strategy of movement disorders. Future perspectives and advances in precise therapy for patients with movement disorders. Week 6 Seizures The epidemiology and etiology of seizures The definition of seizures and refractory epilepsy A brief understanding of the diagnostic strategy of epilepsy Therapeutic strategy for treating patients with epilepsy, particularly for those with refractory epilepsy. Week 7 Demyelinating Diseases The epidemiology, etiology of demyelinating diseases, particularly in central nervous sytem. The clinical classification and presentations of demyelinating diseases. Manage the diagnostic strategy of demyelinating diseases. Know the current treatment and prognosis of demyelinating diseases. Week 8 Headache The epidemiology and etiology of Headache. Clinical classifications and according presentations of Headache. Diagnostic strategy and common laboratory test for Headache. Differential diagnosis of Headache, especially for ruling out the patients with acute or severe complications. An understanding of managing principle of different types of Headache. Week 9 Myelopathy The anatomical structure of the spine and the blood supply. The common causes of Myelopathy and according clinical manifestations. The diagnostic strategy and specific laboratory tests for myelopathy. An understanding of current therapeutic methods for treating patients with myelopathy. Week 10 Muscle Disease The anatomical structure of neuromuscular junction and skeletal muscles. Know the common clinical manifestations and diagnostic strategy of myasthenia gravis. Know the common clinical manifestations and diagnostic strategy of muscular dystrophies and myopathies, particularly Duchenne/Becker muscular dystrophy. The therapeutic strategy for patients with myasthenia gravis. Week 11 Peripheral Neuropathy Learn the epidemiology and etiology of peripheral neuropathy. Learn the anatomical structure of peripheral nervous system and the connection with central nervous system. Know the clinical classification of peripheral neuropathy and according clinical manifestations. Know the diagnostic strategy and current treatment for peripheral neuropathy. Week 12 Dementia and Cognitive Dysfunction Know the epidemiology, etiology and classification of dementia. Learn the clinical manifestations and diagnostic criteria of common types of dementia. Understand the current management strategies of dementia including prevention and treatment.

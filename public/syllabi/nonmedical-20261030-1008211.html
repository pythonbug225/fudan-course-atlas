课程简介（中文）

本课程提供公共经济学的核心理论框架，探讨市场经济中政府干预的经济学原理。课程将系统分析公共支出的理论依据，并深入研究以各类税收工具为核心的政府资源动员机制。本课程高度重视微观经济学理论的应用，在讲授国际前沿理论的同时，兼顾制度与实证层面，并特别关注中国在相关领域的实践与挑战。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a foundational understanding of the economic rationale for public sector intervention in a market economy. It is structured in two parts: the first examines the logic behind government expenditures, while the second concentrates on resource mobilization mechanisms, particularly taxation. While emphasizing conceptual and theoretical foundations, the course also integrates institutional and empirical analysis. A comparative perspective will be adopted, covering standard analyses in developed countries and paying special attention to the role of government in developing economies, especially China.

教学目标

In line with Fudan University's mission to cultivate high-level IPP talent with both a global perspective and local commitment, this course aims to equip graduate students with the core analytical tools of public economics to deeply understand real-world economic policies. The specific objectives are: To foster a nuanced understanding of the government's role in a market economy, encouraging students to apply rigorous economic logic to dialectically analyze the trade-offs between efficiency and equity, and to appreciate the vital role of public policy in advancing modern national governance. To ensure students systematically master core theories, including market failure, public goods, externalities, public choice, and tax incidence and efficiency. Students will also become familiar with the institutional applications of these theories and the frontiers of empirical research in both developed countries and developing nations, with a particular focus on China. To enhance students' ability to apply advanced microeconomic theory and mathematical models to analyze public expenditure and taxation policies. The course will develop their capacity for critical reading of academic literature and for conducting independent normative and positive policy analysis.

教学方式

Theoretical Lectures & Model Derivation: Primarily through rigorous in-class lectures, this course will systematically explain the core theories and analytical models of public economics. A dedicated session at the beginning of the course will review relevant topics from advanced microeconomics to ensure all students possess the necessary analytical foundation. Academic Literature Reading: Classic literature and cutting-edge research papers will be assigned as required reading. Students will be guided to lead in-class presentations and participate in in-depth discussions to track the latest developments in the field and cultivate their critical academic skills. Final Research Paper: Students will be required to choose a topic of interest in public economics and independently write a literature review or research paper that adheres to academic standards. This will serve as a comprehensive assessment of their theoretical mastery and independent research capabilities.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在根据《中国英语能力等级量表》4-6级的要求，培养学生的听力能力与听力策略。课程将融入丰富多样的练习，通过教材、媒体及语言学习网站中的真实视听材料，针对性训练学生语音理解、细节捕捉、篇章结构分析等核心技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to cultivate students’ listening abilities and strategies as required by the Levels 4-6 of China’s Standards of English Language Ability. The course will incorporate a rich variety of exercises designed to address key skills in understanding pronunciation, specific details, and overall structure by using authentic audiovisual materials from the textbook and media and language learning websites. Students are expected to learn the phonetic characteristics of fluent speech and listening strategies to identify, analyze and summarize information.

教学目标

Upon completion of this course, students are expected to understand authentic English conversations in social and campus scenarios, as well as English lectures, speeches, discussions, news reports, etc. They should grasp the central ideas, understand key points, and capture important details. students should achieve the following learning objectives: Comprehend listening materials at a speech rate of 100-140 words per minute（wpm）, which specifically includes: 1) oral instructions and authentic conversations, such as announcements in public places (e.g. train stations and sports arenas), multi-step instructions related to study, work, and daily life, as well as everyday conversations, academic dialogues, and business negotiations. Students should identify the viewpoints and attitudes of the communicators, and understand the cultural connotations within the discourse. 2) Oral narration. Students are expected to grasp the general idea of news or television programs presented at 100-140 wpm; and understand complex stories and their underlying morals or philosophies. 3) Oral argumentation. Students should be able to understand lectures and conference speeches on general topics at 100-140 wpm, capturing key points and specific details. Apply various listening strategies: such as guessing unfamiliar words based on context; predicting the information in listening materials; adjusting or confirming predictions during listening; and taking notes using codes and abbreviations to record key points and details of listening materials.

教学方式

The course is lecture-based, with the teacher providing in-class instruction with multi-media assistance and guiding activities in a task-based approach, supplemented by out-of-class exercises. Students are expected to actively engage in classroom activities, complete both in-class and after-class listening assignments，and fully utilize supplementary materials.

教学内容与进度安排 Course Content & Schedule Week 1 Orientation Get an overview of the course Week 2 & 3 Education: East & West Understand and use the language related to education, schooling; Learn about different educational systems, teaching and learning; Look into some cultural differences and ways to deal with them; Distinguish confusable sounds/words in a sentence or a paragraph. Week 4 & 5 Career Development Understand and use the language related to career development; Learn about the process of job seeking and the current job market; Prepare themselves for career change if necessary; Improve the comprehension of numbers and number-related words. After-class Practice (1) Week 6 & 7 Trade & Business Understand and use the language related to business and trade; Learn about issues concerning business and trade; Catch a glimpse of current affairs in the business world; Practice the listening skill of tackling new words. After-class Practice (2) After-class Practice (3) Week 8 & 9 Cutting-edge Science &Technology Understand and use the language related to cutting-edge science and technology; Get a general picture of some of the most promising science and technology; Look into some possible breathtaking changes the advanced technologies would bring to our lives; Practice the listening skill of identifying main idea. After-class Practice (4) After-class Practice (5) Week 10 Public Health Understand and use the language related to public health and infectious diseases; Learn about the healthy diet and personal hygiene; Look into the relationship of antibiotics and superbugs; Practice the listening skill of identifying the main points. After-class Practice (6) Week 11 Social Value Understand and use the language related to social values in life; Learn about social values in different forms around the world; Look into roles social values play in people's daily life; Practice the listening skill of identifying structure. After-class Practice (7) Week 12 Animals & Nature Understand and use the language related to animals; Learn about animal conservation, animal welfare and animal communication; Explore the relationship between animals and human beings; Practice the listening skill of identifying supporting details. After-class Practice (8) Week 13 Energy & Environment Understand and use the language related to energy and environment; Learn about the renewable energy sources and their development after fossil fuels; Look into some environmental problems and explore ways to solve them; Practice the listening skill of identifying linking words and making predictions. After-class Practice (9) Week 14 Gender Equality Understand and use the language related to gender equality; Look into the current issues about gender equality; Learn about the progress made in gender equality; Study and practice the listening skill of taking notes. After-class Practice (10) Week 15 Love & Marriage Understand and use the language related to love and marriage; Learn about the traditional marriage and the trend of marriage development; Look into the factors in happy and harmonious loving relations and marriages; Practice in an integral way the listening skills learned previously. Week 16 Q&A Clarify the difficult and unclear points; Get a clear picture of final exam format; Get advice on how to further enhance listening skills.

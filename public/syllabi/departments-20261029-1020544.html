课程简介（中文）

市场营销管理是一门创造有价值且可持续交换的艺术与科学。本课程不仅展现市场营销如何通过有效满足客户需求来推动组织成功，更将其作为战略决策与个人职业发展的重要框架。 课程将为学生奠定市场营销核心职能的全面基础，重点掌握包括情境分析（5C模型）、市场细分与目标定位（STP理论）以及营销组合策略（4P理论）在内的核心框架，并辅以市场研究方法论支撑。本课程将市场营销定位为通用管理的基石，以及商业分析的关键视角。

课程简介（英文）

Marketing Management is the art and science of creating valuable, enjoyable, and sustainable exchanges. This course demonstrates how marketing not only drives organizational success by profitably satisfying customers but also serves as a vital framework for strategic decision-making and personal professional development. Students will gain a comprehensive foundation in core marketing functions, mastering essential frameworks including situational analysis (5Cs), segmentation, targeting, and positioning (STP), and the marketing mix (4Ps), supported by marketing research methodologies. The course positions marketing as the cornerstone of general management and a critical lens for business analysis.

教学目标

The course is designed with the following goals in mind. Students should be able to achieve the following goals: Comprehend and describe the ro le of marketing and basic marketing principles(4Ps, STP, 5Cs); Analyze existing business marketing strategy using these frameworks; Propose the recommendations and plausible solutions in order to address thechallenges business face; Cultivate a “test” mindset by designing rigorous market research methods to answer questions when trying to design and/or position a business idea.

教学方式

A combination of lectures, class discussions, case analysis, individual homework, action learning project, recitations and code (in R language) sharing.

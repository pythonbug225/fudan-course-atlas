权力的艺术与科学

2025秋 · MBA60058.01 · 陈硕

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在如何理解权⼒，涉及到的组织包括家庭、家族、公司及政府。主讲人将结合权⼒的⼀般理论和组织中权⼒的应⽤实践探讨这个议题。主讲人将结合中国历史语境围绕两个与权力相关的议题展开: 承诺和继承。承诺与继承是权力运作的重要维度，也为理解历史上众多权⼒⽃争和演进提供了新视⾓。承诺议题从四个方面展开: 中央和地⽅间的承诺、政治精英间的承诺、政治精英和社会精英间的承诺、以及承诺产⽣的制度性根源；继承议题从三个方面展开：权力运作的特征、继承制度设置、权力交接步骤。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the relevant theories and knowledge of enterprise crisis management and crisis communication, with three main characteristics: firstly, the integration of theory with practice. Emphasize the application of basic theories of risk management, crisis management, and communication in the changing media ecology and public opinion environment; Secondly, emphasize practical orientation. By analyzing a series of influential and representative cases of enterprise crisis management and communication, interactive communication and group discussions are conducted with students to stimulate their participation and thinking; Thirdly, rooted in complex situations. The course emphasizes professionalism, complexity, and transcendence, focusing on specific contexts of politics, economy, society, and culture for more efficient crisis management and communication. This course aims to understand power, involving organizations such as families, clans, corporations, and governments. The speaker will explore this topic by combining the general theory of power and the practical application of power in organizations. The speaker will discuss two issues related to power in the context of Chinese history: commitment and inheritance. Commitment and inheritance are important dimensions of power operation, and provide a new perspective for understanding the numerous power struggles and evolutions throughout history. The commitment issue is discussed from four aspects: commitments between the central and local governments, commitments between political elites, commitments between political elites and social elites, and the institutional roots of commitments; The issue of inheritance is discussed from three aspects: the characteristics of power operation, the establishment of inheritance system, and the steps of power transfer.

教学目标

通过学习，预期达到以下三个目标： 第一，了解和掌握关于权力中承诺和继承的一般性理论。这些理论不涉及数理推导，侧重于分析框架与观点； 第二，知晓课程中所讲案例中的重要细节与时间线； 第三，通过一般理论和权力运作的实践理解权力的艺术性和科学性。

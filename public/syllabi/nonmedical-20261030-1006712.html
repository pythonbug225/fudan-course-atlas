课程简介（中文）

本课程连接计量经济学与数据科学，涵盖基础理论（最小二乘法、假设检验、模型设定）和面向大数据的现代方法（正则化、非参数方法、高维面板数据）。通过真实数据案例和R/Python编程实践，学生将处理序列相关、异方差和多重共线性等问题，同时学习适用于大规模数据的分析工具。课程兼顾理论与应用，为学术研究、政策制定或行业工作培养数据驱动的经济学分析能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course bridges econometrics and data science, combining foundational concepts (least squares, hypothesis testing, model specification) with modern techniques for big data (regularization, nonparametric methods, high-dimensional panels). Emphasis is placed on practical implementation using real-world datasets and coding labs in R/Python. Students will address key challenges like serial correlation, heteroscedasticity, and multicollinearity while learning scalable tools for large datasets. Designed for research, policy, or industry applications, the curriculum equips learners with both theoretical understanding and hands-on skills for data-driven economic analysis.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students will have a solid understanding of both classical and modern econometric techniques. They will be able to explain the properties and underlying assumptions of ordinary least squares (OLS) estimation and competently interpret and evaluate inferential procedures in multiple linear regression models. Students will also learn how to appropriately incorporate data scaling, functional form choices, and dummy variables into their regression analysis, recognizing their implications for interpretation and model performance. They will develop the skills to identify and address common econometric issues such as heteroscedasticity, including adjusting standard errors and implementing feasible generalized least squares (GLS) when necessary. In addition, students will be introduced to the challenges of working with time-series data, such as autocorrelation and non-stationarity, and will learn how to apply basic solutions. Finally, they will gain insight into modelling high-dimensional panel data, equipping them with the tools to engage with large-scale, complex datasets in empirical economics.

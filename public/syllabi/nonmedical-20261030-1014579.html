课程简介（英文）

The purpose of this course is not to reprise what has already been adequately addressed. Rather, it is a style introduction to a fundamentally new, seamlessly unified, and fully statistical approach to information fusion. This seamless unification includes the following: A unified theory of measurements, both single-target and multitarget; Unified mathematical representation of uncertainty, including randomness, imprecision, vagueness, ambiguity, and contingency; A unified single-target and multitarget modeling methodology based on generalized likelihood functions; A unification of much of expert systems theory, including fuzzy, Bayes, Dempster-Shafer, and rule-based techniques; Unified and optimal single-target and multitarget detection and estimation; Unified and optimal fusion of disparate information; A systematic multitarget calculus for devising new approximations.

教学目标

In a modern system, the diversity of information representations, information capacity and processing speed, and other requirements have greatly exceeded the ability of traditional information processing methods. Therefore, a new comprehensive information processing method -- data fusion technology emerged. The purpose of this course is to introduce the principle, method and application of data fusion technology, and discuss the existing problems of data fusion domain so that students can have a certain understanding of this technology.

Structured Finance Products: Innovation and Design

2025秋 · MF70013.01 · 陈剑

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This elective course is situated in the first module of the fall semester for the 2nd year EMF students at Fanhai International School of Finance. The students will learn and actively participate in topics related to structured products. After this course, students will be able to understand the distinct features of various structured finance products; will be able to analyze risk and return in financial markets; will be able to innovate and design structured finance products catering to Chinese financial market.

教学目标

After completing the course, students should be able to: Develop a solid understanding of structured finance and Understand the concepts and methods that are used in practice, for example, SPV, ABS, CDOs, CLNs, and Understand the role of structured finance and securitization in the 2007-08 financial crisis.

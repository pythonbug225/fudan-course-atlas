课程简介（中文）

地球系统数值模式能够反映大气、水（海洋与水文）、生物各圈层之间的相互作用，一方面提高数值预报能力，另一方面可以满足人类海上活动、环境保护、社会可持续发展等不断增加的不同需求，地球系统科学不断开创新的历史。本课程将介绍地球系统数值模式发展前沿知识。

课程简介（英文）

Atmosphere-Ocean Numerical Prediction is considered one of the greatest scientific and technological achievements in human history. A key factor in this success is the development of modern numerical models, as well as simulation and prediction techniques. With further advances in computational power, component models such as atmospheric models, oceanic models, and atmospheric-ocean biogeochemical models have been coupled, leading to a rapid transition toward Earth system numerical models. Earth system numerical models can represent the interactions among different spheres—including the atmosphere, hydrosphere (oceans and hydrology), and biosphere. These models not only enhance numerical prediction capabilities but also help meet the growing and diverse demands of maritime activities, environmental protection, and sustainable development. This course will cover the major advances during the past decade in Earth system modeling and prediction, focusing on the development of Earth system numerical models.

教学目标

大气海洋数值预报被认为是人类历史上最伟大科技成就之一。取得这一成就关键之一是现代数值模式、模拟和预测方法的发展。课程将拓展学生以大气和海洋科学为主的地球系统模拟和预测领域的知识面、认识前沿研究进展和发展方向，把握数值模式在天气/气候预报和预估中的应用领域与发展趋势，为将来从事科研、教学和管理领导工作拓宽视野，培养前瞻意识。

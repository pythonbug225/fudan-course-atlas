中国政府与治理

2025秋 · GESP40010.01 · 敬乂嘉、范梓腾

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在加深选课学生对中国公共管理实践经验的历史、背景及当前核心议题的理解，并重点关注自1970年代末改革开放启动以来的发展历程。课程将探讨中国公共治理的核心议题，比如，整体改革路径、行政体制改革、政府间关系、公共财政、公共服务、政策制定模式、政府与社会关系、公务员制度、协同治理和社会组织发展等。除了帮助学生掌握中国公共治理实践的基本知识以外，课程还期望学生能够培养出系统性分析中国公共治理实践和改革创新的基本能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to enhance the students' understanding of the history, context, and current issues of public administration in China, with a focus on the development since the initiation of economic reform in the late 1970s. Core issues of China's public sector, such as reform path, administrative reforms since 1978, intergovernmental relations, public finance, public services, government-society relation, policy making, civil service reform, and social organization development, will be discussed. Besides obtaining knowledge of China's public sector, students are expected to develop capacities to analytically write and debate China's governance issues.

教学目标

Upon successful completion of this subject, students are expected to: Understand in a holistic way the historical path as well as contemporary challenges in China's public governance; Understand the major issues, problems and reforms in specific areas of public governance and develop capacities to appreciate their internal connections; Learn to apply theoretical perspectives to interpret and explain China's governance issues; Learn to taking into consideration China's contextual factors, either local, national, regional, and global, to analyze governance issues and inform decision-makers.

教学方式

The course is primarily delivered through in-class lectures by the instructors. The lecture content covers fundamental and classic theories of public administration and governance, as well as case analyses of Chinese public administration practices. In addition, for each topic, the course will recommend relevant reading materials. Students are expected to read these materials and write summaries. Students will also work in groups to give presentations on their understanding of the assigned readings. Lastly, the course emphasizes classroom interactions between instructors and students, and encourages students to actively participate in class discussions by engaging with the course content.

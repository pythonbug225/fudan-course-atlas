语言心理学

2025秋 · FORE60007.01 · 陶衍

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

主要包括以下经典的语言加工领域以及相关的前沿讨论研究：1. 单词加工 2. 语义3. 句法加工4. 二语现象等 通过对以上议题的知识教授与探讨，课程将突出调动学生的本身积极性，强调对其思维模式的学术训练，同时也将涉及论文撰写等知识。

课程简介（英文）

Main discussions include understanding the fundamentals as well as frontier discovery in fields as follows: 1. word recognition 2. semantic representation 3. syntactic processing 4. bilingualism The course features a strong emphasis on student's self-driven learning, a high standard of academic training (especially regarding reasoning and questioning in the field) and a demanding requirement of essay writing.

教学目标

在内容教授上，本课程旨在让学生建立对心理语言学这一学科领域的全面基础，了解领域内的研究议题和主要的理论。在能力培养上，本课程的教学目的主要突出以下三点：1、 锻炼学生的高容量学术阅读能力；2. 锤炼学生的严谨思维能力；3. 提高学生针对特定学术议题的论文写作能力。

教学方式

正常授课，同时设置论文讨论反馈课

教学内容与进度安排

1 General introduction / course briefing 2 Word: spoken word recognition / topic understanding 3 Word: visual word recognition / topic understanding 4 Word recognition models & class discussion on Word / topic understanding 5 Connectionism + Lexical Access 1 (Assignment 1 due) / topic understanding 6 Lexical access II / topic understanding 7 Bilingualism I: syntactic processing / topic understanding 8 Bilingualism II: semantic processing / topic understanding 9 Feedback on Assignment 1; leftovers / Writing Skills 10 Meaning: semantic representation (Assignment 2 due) / topic understanding 11 Meaning: lexical ambiguity & selective meaning retrieval / topic understanding 12 Embodied cognition / topic understanding 13 Leftovers; Feedback on Assignment 2 / Writing Skills 14 Syntactic parsing 1 (Assignment 3 due) / topic understanding 15 Syntactic Parsing 2/ topic understanding 16 Feedback on Assignment 3; General Revision / Course revision

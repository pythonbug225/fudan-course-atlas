课程简介（中文）

法国哲学在西方哲学中占据着无可替代的重要位置。17 世纪笛卡尔的“我 思故我在”开创了近代西方主体哲学；18 世纪法国唯物主义和启蒙运动给哲学 带来了新气象；19 世纪唯灵论执着于研究理性的他者（知觉、意志、自觉、习 惯等）而对理性传统进行批判；20 世纪法国的实证主义、生命哲学、科学哲学、 存在主义、结构主义、解构主义、解释学、后现代主义等对近代哲学作了不同 程度的超越和克服。如果不了解法国哲学，很难想象如何去理解其他国家的哲 学。德法哲学与英美哲学一直处于互动关系之中。 此外，法国哲学与法国文化始终相互交织在一起。哲学与文学、绘画、音 乐艺术等的密切联系是法国哲学最主要的特征。

课程简介（英文）

French philosophy holds an irreplaceable position in Western philosophy. In the 17th century, Descartes' “I think, therefore I am” pioneered modern Western subject philosophy; in the 18th century, French materialism and the Enlightenment brought new vitality to philosophy; In the 19th century, spiritualism focused on studying the rational other (perception, will, self-awareness, habits, etc.) and criticized the rationalist tradition; in the 20th century, French positivism, philosophy of life, philosophy of science, existentialism, structuralism, deconstructionism, hermeneutics, postmodernism, and other schools of thought transcended and overcame modern philosophy to varying degrees. Without an understanding of French philosophy, it is difficult to imagine how to comprehend the philosophy of other countries. German and French philosophy have always been in an interactive relationship with Anglo-American philosophy. Additionally, French philosophy and French culture have always been intertwined. The close connection between philosophy and literature, painting, music, and other arts is the most distinctive feature of French philosophy. Translated with DeepL.com (free version)

教学目标

作为世界哲学的重要组成部分，法国哲学对我们有着极大的吸引力和思想影响。本课程旨在使同 学们对现当代法国哲学主要思想内容有较为全面和深入的了解，对某些重要的问题有自己的见解，在 进一步夯实西方哲学基础的同时，加深和拓展对西方哲学的理解，全面把握法国哲学与德国哲学、英 美哲学的互动关系。

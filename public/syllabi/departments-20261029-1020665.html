职业责任与领导力

课程简介（中文）

本课程通过讲授与案例分析相结合的方式，系统介绍领导力与职业责任的核心理论与实践工具。课程涵盖责任伦理、领导判断、影响力、跨文化沟通与冲突管理等主题，结合真实组织案例，培养学生在复杂情境中进行有效判断与负责任领导的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course explores the relationship between professional responsibility and leadership in modern organizations. Professional responsibility represents the baseline of professional conduct, guiding individuals to act with integrity, accountability, and awareness of the broader consequences of their actions. Leadership represents the ceiling of professional impact, where individuals influence teams, organizations, and society.

教学目标

By the end of the course, students will be able to understand the concept of professional responsibility and its role in organizations, analyze ethical dilemmas across individual, professional, and organizational levels, and recognize how leadership expands the impact of responsibility. The course also develops students’ ability to apply key leadership concepts, including decision making, influence, negotiation, teamwork, and organizational culture. Through case analysis, discussion, and experiential learning, students will strengthen core human capacities essential for responsible leadership, such as critical thinking, communication, and ethical judgment. The course ultimately aims to cultivate students’ sense of responsibility, enhance their leadership capabilities, and prepare them to lead ethically and effectively in a complex and rapidly changing world.

教学方式

This course combines multiple teaching approaches, including conceptual lectures, case discussions, class debates, experiential exercises, team decision-making activities, role plays, team presentations, and guest speakers from industry. These methods are designed to bridge theory and practice, encourage active learning, and develop students’ analytical and interpersonal skills. Students are expected to actively participate in class discussions and exercises, engage thoughtfully with course materials, and contribute to a collaborative learning environment.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 – Course Introduction & Responsibility Introduction to course themes. Definition of responsibility. Week 2 – Professional Responsibility Concepts of individual, professional, and organizational responsibility. Discussion different role responsibilities in professional lives through real-world cases Week 3 – Concept of Leadership I Foundations of leadership. Leaders as actors. Week 4 – Concept of Leadership II Foundations of leadership. Leaders as architects. Week 5 – Concept of Leadership III & Introduction to Decision Making Leadership exercises. Introduction to decision-making frameworks. Week 6 – Decision Making I Decision-making exercises. Week 7 – Decision Making II Cognitive biases in decision-making. Week 8 – Influence I Persuasion principles. Week 9 – Influence II Rules of Rhetoric. Week 10 – Negotiations I Negotiation fundamentals and preparation. Week 11 – Negotiations II Negotiation exercises Week 12 – Teamwork I Team dynamics and collaboration. Week 13 – Teamwork II Building trust, coordination, and effective team performance. Week 14 – Culture I Organizational culture and its influence on behavior. Week 15 – Culture II Leaders as architects of organizational culture. Week 16 – Course Review & PRL in Real Organizations Guest speakers share experiences applying professional responsibility and leadership in practice.

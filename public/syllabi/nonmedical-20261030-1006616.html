Principles and practices of social media communication in China

2025秋 · JCM70034.01 · 潘霁

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程引导学生探索中国文化中社交媒体传播的独特实践与原则。通过这一过程，学生将深入理解社交媒体在中国引发的持续变革。课程将讨论大量案例，聚焦社交媒体实践与中国文化中特有的社会网络与人际关系之间的交汇点。

课程简介（英文）

This course leads students to explore distinctive practices and principles of social media communication in Chinese culture. By so doing, Students will develop an in-depth understanding of the on-going revolutions that social media bring forth in China. A large range of cases at the intersection between social media practices and social networks/relations typical in Chinese culture will be discussed.

教学目标

By combining lectures, classroom discussions about readings and presentations, we aim to stimulate continued reflections/reading/analyses/discussions on how social media technologies and emerging media practices reshape the possibilities for Chinese society and change the everyday life of ordinary Chinese people against the backdrop of globalization.

教学方式

1-Lecture by instructor 2-Invited guest lectures 3-Focused seminar/discussion 4-Student presentation 5-Field trips (optional)

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Course Overview & Introduction (Assignment: Reading for next week) Week 2-4 Mediation: The overarching theoretical perspective (Assignment: Systematic readings about network society theory, about media practice theory, etc.) Week 5-7 Social media and marketing relationships in China (trust, branding, interactivity)(Assignment: Related readings and a small essay assginment) Week 8-10 Social media and transformed intimate relationship development (Assignment: Readings on social media dating, and uncertainty reduction theory) Week 11-13 Social media and enconters between strangers in Chinese cities (Assignment: Assigned readings and presentation) Week 14-16 Social media and public participation in China (Assignment: Readings and a reflection essay) Week 17-18 Review and QA sessions (Assignment: Final project)

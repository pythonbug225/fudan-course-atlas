课程简介（中文）

（1）生物合成药物的生物合成途径与代谢调控；（2）现代生物技术在药物生物合成中的应用；（3）微生物转化在化合物合成与中药现代化中的应用。

课程简介（英文）

Course Introduction:
(1) Biosynthetic pathways and metabolic regulation of biosynthetic drugs; (2) Application of modern biotechnology in drug biosynthesis; (3) Microbial transformation in compound synthesis and modernization of traditional Chinese medicine.

教学目标

坚持价值引领，培养学生的科研伦理意识与创新报国情怀，树立严谨求实的科学态度。注重知识传授，系统讲解现代生物技术与药物研发的核心理论与关键技术，构建从基础到前沿的知识体系。强化能力培养，通过实验设计与学术研讨，提升学生的科研实践能力、批判性思维与跨学科解决复杂问题的能力，为培养具有国际视野与创新能力的生物医药高层次人才奠定基础。掌握生物合成药物的研究方法、途径调控与发酵控制；熟悉其在临床应用与耐药机制；了解最新生物技术在药物合成中的应用；培养独立研究与解决问题的能力。

Upholding value guidance, the course aims to cultivate students' awareness of research ethics and a sense of innovation for national service, fostering a rigorous and truth-seeking scientific attitude. It emphasizes the systematic impartation of core theories and key technologies in modern biotechnology and drug research and development, building a comprehensive knowledge system from fundamentals to cutting-edge advances. The course strengthens ability cultivation by engaging students in experimental design and academic discussions, enhancing their practical research skills, critical thinking, and capacity to solve complex interdisciplinary problems. This foundation supports the cultivation of high-level talents in biomedicine with international perspectives and innovative capabilities.

Master research methods, pathway regulation, and fermentation control of biosynthetic drugs; familiar with clinical applications and drug resistance mechanisms; understand the application of latest biotechnologies in drug synthesis; cultivate independent research and problem-solving abilities.

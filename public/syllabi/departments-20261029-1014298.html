投资银行与资产估值

2025秋 · MF70034.01 · 张新

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将介绍投资银行的组织机构及基本业务构成，并重点介绍核心投资银行业务中广泛使用的各种估价模型，以及这些估价方法在实际业务中，比如首次上市和兼并收购等的应用。通过这门课的学习，学生们将建立起对投行开展业务的全面了解, 同时也能学习到扎实且贴近实务的估价方法与技巧。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the main businesses and organizational structure of investment banks, with a focus on investment banking and relevant valuation methods widely applied by finance professionals in practical cases such as IPOs and M&As. The course helps students build comprehensive grasps and solid understanding of the whole spectrum of investment banking businesses, and hardcore skills of investment analysis and valuation.

教学目标

Course Objectives: 1）Understand the business of investment banking 2）Gain familiarity with valuation models 3）Learn to apply valuation models to estimate IPO price, and to make M&A and other financial decisions

教学方式

NA

教学内容与进度安排

Subject 1 Overview of investment banking 1-2 Valuation 2-5 Capital Raising (IPO) 5-7 M&A

课程简介（中文）

通过本课程，学生可系统学习微流控技术在药物研发领域的创新应用，内容涵盖药物合成、药物筛选、药理、药物递送、药物分析等新药研发各环节。通过融入该领域最新科研成果和产业案例，使学生掌握药物研发微流控技术的原理、仪器和研究方法，提升学生在药物研发微流控技术领域的创新能力和实践技能，为培养具有交叉学科背景的新药研发人才奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

Through this course, students will systematically learn innovative applications of microfluidic technology in pharmaceutical research and development, covering all critical stages including drug synthesis, screening, pharmacology, drug delivery, and analysis. By incorporating cutting-edge scientific achievements and industrial case studies in the field, the course enables students to master the principles, instrumentation, and research methodologies of microfluidic technology for drug development. It aims to enhance students' innovative capabilities and practical skills in pharmaceutical microfluidics, laying a solid foundation for cultivating interdisciplinary talents in new drug research and development.

教学目标

本课程立足复旦大学“培养具有国家意识、人文情怀、科学精神、专业素养、国际视野”的人才培养定位，面向药学及相关专业学生开设。作为全英文课程，将结合药物研发前沿需求，使学生掌握药物研发相关微流控技术的基本原理、仪器和研究方法，了解该领域的发展史和最新进展，学习微流控技术在药物发现、药理、药物递送与分析中的应用，夯实学科交叉知识基础。通过实验课程与案例研讨，提升学生微流控系统设计、数据分析及解决复杂药物研发问题的实践能力，培养跨学科创新思维。

This course is designed for students majoring in pharmaceutical sciences and related disciplines, aligning with Fudan University's educational mission to cultivate talents with "national consciousness, humanistic sentiment, scientific spirit, professional competence, and global perspective." As a fully English-taught program, it integrates cutting-edge demands in drug development to equip students with fundamental principles, instrumentation, and research methodologies of microfluidic technologies relevant to pharmaceutical research and developmemnt. Participants will explore the historical evolution and latest advancements in the field, examine applications of microfluidics in drug discovery, pharmacology, drug delivery, and analysis, and strengthen their interdisciplinary knowledge base. Through hands-on laboratory sessions and case studies, the course aims to enhance students' practical skills in microfluidic system design, data analysis, and solving complex drug development challenges, while fostering innovative interdisciplinary thinking.

教学方式

为实现课程教学目标，本课程采用多元化教学方法，注重理论与实践结合、学科交叉融合及创新能力培养，具体策略和方法如下：

1. 理论讲授与前沿案例结合

采用模块化教学，系统讲解微流控技术基础理论（如流体力学、芯片设计原理）及其在药物筛选、递送与检测中的应用。还将结合国内外最新研究案例（如器官芯片、单细胞分析）和产业动态（如微流控即时检测），邀请行业专家介绍药物研发微流控技术案例和产业化现状，增强学生对技术前沿的认知。

2. 问题导向学习与小组研讨

设计真实药物研发问题（如高通量药物筛选优化、靶向递送系统开发），学生分组研讨解决方案，培养批判性思维与团队协作能力。引入学术文献研读与汇报，聚焦微流控技术的突破性研究，提升学术素养与科学表达能力。

3. 实验模拟与虚拟仿真

利用计算机辅助设计软件进行微流控芯片设计、建模与流体仿真，降低实操成本，强化设计能力。

还将通过虚拟实验平台模拟复杂实验流程（如液滴生成、细胞培养），弥补传统实验条件限制。

4. 实验驱动式实践

学生分组完成微流控芯片设计、加工、测试和微流体控制等实验，提升工程实践和动手能力。

并通过纳米载药颗粒微流控合成和分析实验学习仪器操作和锻炼实操技能。

5. 价值引领贯穿教学

在案例中融入科学家精神教育（如微流控技术奠基人的创新历程），结合我国生物医药“卡脖子”技术攻关实例，激发科技报国使命感。并通过伦理辩论（如微流控技术在动物实验替代中的伦理争议），培养社会责任意识。

组织形式：采用“大班授课+小班研讨+实验室轮转”模式，依托复旦大学的相关科研设施，保障教学与科研深度融合。此外，将借助基于 AI智能体的超星泛雅在线智慧课程平台开展“以学为中心的”线上线下混合式教学，将课件、参考资料等上传教学平台，实现AI赋能的创新教学。

To achieve the course objectives, this course employs diversified teaching methods, emphasizing the integration of theory and practice, interdisciplinary convergence, and the cultivation of innovative capabilities. The specific strategies and approaches are as follows:

1. Theory Lectures Combined with Cutting-Edge Case Studies

Adopting a modular teaching approach, the course systematically covers fundamental theories of microfluidic technology (e.g., fluid dynamics, chip design principles) and its applications in drug screening, delivery, and detection. It incorporates the latest domestic and international research cases (e.g., organ-on-a-chip, single-cell analysis) and industry trends (e.g., microfluidic point-of-care testing) to enhance students' awareness of technological advancements.

2. Problem-Based Learning and Group Discussions

Students engage in group discussions to solve real-world drug R&D challenges (e.g., optimizing high-throughput drug screening, developing targeted delivery systems), fostering critical thinking and teamwork. Academic literature review and presentations on breakthrough microfluidic research are introduced to strengthen scholarly competence and scientific communication skills.

3. Experimental Simulation and Virtual Modeling

Computer-aided design (CAD) software is used for microfluidic chip design, modeling, and fluid simulation, reducing hands-on costs while reinforcing design proficiency.
Virtual lab platforms simulate complex experimental procedures (e.g., droplet generation, cell culture), overcoming limitations of traditional lab setups.

4. Experiment-Driven Hands-On Training

Students work in groups to complete experiments in microfluidic chip design, fabrication, testing, and fluid control, enhancing engineering and practical skills.
Students gain hands-on experience in instrument operation by conducting microfluidic synthesis and analysis experiments of drug-loaded nanoparticles.

5. Value-Oriented Education Throughout Teaching

Case studies incorporate the spirit of scientific pioneers (e.g., the innovative journey of microfluidics founders) and China’s efforts to overcome "bottleneck" biopharmaceutical technologies, inspiring a sense of mission to contribute to national scientific progress. Ethical debates (e.g., controversies over microfluidics replacing animal testing) cultivate social responsibility awareness.

Organizational Structure

The course adopts a "large-class lectures + small-group seminars + lab rotations" model, leveraging Fudan University’s research facilities to ensure deep integration of teaching and research. Additionally, an AI-enhanced Superstar FanYa smart learning platform enables student-centered blended learning, combining online and offline instruction. Course materials and references are uploaded to the platform to facilitate innovative AI-powered education.

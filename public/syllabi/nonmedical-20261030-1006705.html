课程简介（中文）

本课程是热力学和统计物理的入门课程。前四周包括热力学入门。第二个四周涵盖了热力学量的集合理论和统计推导的介绍。课程的其余部分是将统计力学方法应用于各种系统，如多原子气体、固体、热辐射和自旋系统。最后几节课将重点讨论一维和二维Ising模型的相变和精确解。

课程简介（英文）

This course is introductory course to Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics. The first four weeks cover an introduction to thermodynamics. The second four weeks cover an introduction to ensemble theory and statistical derivation of thermodynamic quantities. The rest of the course is about applying statistical mechanics methods to various systems, such as multi-atomic gasses, solid bodies, thermal radiation, and spin systems. The last few lectures will focus on the phase transitions and the exact solutions for 1D and 2D Ising models.

教学目标

Upon completion of this course, you will get a basic understanding of thermodynamics: the notions of temperature, heat, integral energy, and entropy. You will learn the basics of ensemble theory: the notion of classical and quantum microstates and macrostates, methods of evaluating different partition functions, and deriving the thermodynamic properties. You will learn several methods to solve the lattice models.

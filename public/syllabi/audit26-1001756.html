课程简介（中文）

本课程以生药为对象，从生药的分类与本草沿革、生药的化学成分、生药鉴定的意义与方法、影响生药质量的因素、生药资源现状与合理利用、生药学研究的新技术与新方法、常用生药的介绍等几个方面展开讲授，让学生充分了解和掌握生药鉴定和中药资源等研究所必需的理论、方法和技术，并核心理解生药质量和生药资源对于中药现代化研究的重要意义。

课程简介（英文）

This course takes crude drugs as the object of study and covers several key aspects, including the classification of crude drugs and the evolution of herbal medicine, the chemical constituents of crude drugs, the significance and methods of crude drug identification, factors affecting the quality of crude drugs, the current status and rational utilization of crude drug resources, new technologies and methods in the study of crude drug science, and an introduction to commonly used crude drugs. The aim is to enable students to fully understand and master the theories, methods, and techniques essential for the identification of crude drugs and the study of Chinese medicinal resources. Moreover, the course emphasizes the core importance of crude drug quality and resources in the modernization of traditional Chinese medicine research.

教学目标

通过本课程的系统学习，要求学生掌握如何有效控制中药材及其制剂的质量（包括定性鉴别和定量测定）以及如何合理利用我国生药资源所必需的基础理论知识和实验技能，为今后从事生药鉴定和品质评价、中药资源利用等相关研究奠定基础。

教学方式

以教师课堂教学、以及实验教学为主，辅以学生ppt专题讨论。

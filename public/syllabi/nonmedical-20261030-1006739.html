政策与项目评估

2025秋 · MANA60017.01 · 涂文燕

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程聚焦项目评估理论、评估设计以及用于评估公共项目和政策的各种方法。课程涵盖项目评估理论的五个领域：需求评估、逻辑模型、过程评估、结果评估和经济评估。同时，本课程将系统介绍整个政策周期的评估内容。修读该课程的学生将学习如何区分高质量和低质量的政策评估，以及如何开展和设计评估工作。他们还将学习运用定量和定性方法，判断项目和政策的必要性，并评估其是否达成预期目标。本课程重点聚焦于基础项目理论、评估研究设计以及具体的分析工具（包括定量和定性工具）。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides students with an introduction to theories of program evaluation, evaluation design, and a variety of methods used to assess public programs and policies. The course covers five domains of program evaluation theories: needs evaluation, logic models, process evaluation, impact evaluation, and economic evaluation. A systematic introduction to evaluation throughout the whole policy cycle will be conducted. Students in the course will learn how to distinguish high-quality evaluations from low-quality ones, as well as how to conduct and design evaluations. They will also learn how to employ quantitative and qualitative methods to determine whether programs and policies are necessary and assess whether they have achieved their intended objectives. Much emphasis of this course is on basic program theories, designing evaluative research, and specific analytical tools, both quantitative and qualitative.

教学目标

Demonstrate a good understanding of theoreticalknowledge about program evaluation, including its concepts, purposes, models, and practices. Develop quantitative and qualitative research skills to evaluate the performance of programs and policies. Capable of applying academic knowledge and analytical skills to evaluate real-world policies and programs.

教学方式

lecture

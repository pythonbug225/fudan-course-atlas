课程简介（中文）

本课程以SDA Smart Data Analytics为主线，面向经济学、金融学与金融科技研究，介绍复杂数据、文本数据、网络数据和高维数据的分析方法。内容包括数据管理、网络信息抓取、主题模型、词嵌入、聚类、降维、网络中心性、金融风险计量、机器学习和数字金融案例。课程材料将使用Q2生态系统（Quantlet与Quantinar）以及教师指定的iCloud共享资源，并强调R/Python实践与可复现研究。

课程简介（英文）

This graduate course follows the SDA Smart Data Analytics materials and introduces methods for complex, text, network and high-dimensional data in economics, finance and FinTech. Topics include data management, web scraping, topic models, word embeddings, clustering, dimensionality reduction, network centrality, financial risk measurement, machine learning and selected digital-finance cases. Course materials will use the Q2 ecosystem (Quantlet and Quantinar) and the instructor-designated iCloud resources. The course emphasizes R/Python practice and reproducible research.

教学目标

English: By the end of the course, students should be able to formulate economic and financial questions as data-analytic problems, understand basic methods for text, network and high-dimensional data, replicate examples with R/Python, Quantlet and Quantinar, and present data, methods and results in a short project. Students should also become familiar with the Q2 ecosystem and the iCloud-based shared course materials. The course also stresses open science, data ethics and academic standards.

中文：学生应能够将经济金融问题转化为数据分析问题，理解文本、网络和高维数据的基本方法，使用R/Python、Quantlet和Quantinar复现示例，并在项目中规范呈现数据、方法和结果。学生还应熟悉课程所需的Q2生态系统和iCloud共享材料。课程同时强调开放科学、数据伦理和学术规范。

教学方式

English: The course combines lectures, case demonstrations, code replication, reading discussion and project presentation. The instructor will teach the core SDA materials and demonstrate R/Python examples from the Q2 ecosystem. The Fudan teaching team will support readings, discussion, computer-lab exercises and project supervision. Course materials and code will be accessed through the Q2 ecosystem (Quantlet: https://www.quantlet.com; Quantinar: https://www.quantinar.com) and the instructor-designated iCloud account. Bringing a laptop to every class is mandatory.

中文：课程采用专题讲授、案例演示、代码复现、文献讨论和项目汇报相结合的方式。主讲教师讲授SDA核心内容并展示Q2生态系统中的R/Python示例；复旦教学团队协助阅读组织、课堂讨论、上机练习和项目辅导。课程材料与代码将通过Q2生态系统（Quantlet: https://www.quantlet.com；Quantinar: https://www.quantinar.com）以及教师指定的iCloud账户共享。学生每次课必须携带笔记本电脑。

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture 1 / 第1讲（3 hours / 3学时）：Introduction to SDA, Data Management and Digital Society / SDA导论、数据管理与数字社会。Topics: SMART Data Analytics, data workflows, reproducibility, Q2 ecosystem setup (Quantlet and Quantinar) and iCloud material access. Task: laptop, R/Python, Q2 and iCloud setup.

Lecture 2 / 第2讲（3 hours / 3学时）：Web Data, Topic Models and Word Embeddings / 网络信息抓取、主题模型与词嵌入。Topics: web scraping, LDA, Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT. Assignment: short text-analysis exercise.

Lecture 3 / 第3讲（3 hours / 3学时）：Clustering, Dimensionality Reduction and Visualization / 聚类、降维与可视化。Topics: scagnostics, spectral and hierarchical clustering, MST, t-SNE, UMAP, LLE. Lab: replicate one Quantlet example.

Lecture 4 / 第4讲（3 hours / 3学时）：Network Analytics and Financial Risk Measurement / 网络分析与金融风险计量。Topics: network centrality, Kalman filtering, smoothing, FRM, HMM, SVM. Discussion: financial-risk monitoring.

Lecture 5 / 第5讲（3 hours / 3学时）：Machine Learning and Digital Assets / 机器学习与数字资产。Topics: random forests, ICA, neural networks, LSTM, CRIX, crypto option pricing, NFTs. Task: digital-finance case discussion.

Lecture 6 / 第6讲（3 hours / 3学时）：Advanced SDA and Project Presentations / 高级SDA与项目汇报。Topics: smart contracts, Elastic Net, Shapley values, GAN, boosting, LLM-related methods, model choice and GMM. Assessment: group presentation and short research memo.

* English: The course has 18 total contact hours, including 6 practice hours and 6 lab/computer-session hours. Practice components include readings/case discussion, group-project design, presentation and research-memorandum writing. Lab components include R/Python setup, access to the Q2 ecosystem (Quantlet/Quantinar), access to iCloud-based course materials, Quantlet replication, text analysis, clustering/dimension reduction, network analytics and financial-risk measurement exercises. Students must bring a laptop to every class.

* 中文：本课程共18学时，其中实践学时6学时、实验（含上机）学时6学时。实践环节包括文献/案例讨论、小组项目设计、项目汇报和研究备忘录写作；实验环节包括R/Python环境配置、Q2生态系统（Quantlet/Quantinar）访问、iCloud材料访问、Quantlet代码复现、文本分析、聚类/降维、网络分析和金融风险计量练习。学生每次课必须携带笔记本电脑。

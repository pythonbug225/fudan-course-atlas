课程简介（中文）

随着生产型经济的高度成熟和IT技术飞速发展、制造业和服务业相互融合和渗透，有效的物流管理可以成为企业竞争优势的重要源泉和价值增长点。在新时期，伴随着疫情影响和国际环境的变化，物流管理的价值和重要性不断提升。

本课程讲首先从企业的角度，围绕顾客价值、运输、库存和选址四个维度去探讨物流管理的基础理论和分析方法；其次，拓展到供应链和产业链视角，介绍物流管理给产业链供应链实现降本增效的具体方法、方案和工具；最后，从创新视角，探讨物流管理在物流金融、低碳经济、数字化运营等方面的最新实践和发展。

另外，应用AI工具来实现物流管理的设计及解决方案。

课程简介（英文）

As the production-oriented economy reaches a high level of maturity and information technology advances rapidly, with the manufacturing and service sectors deeply integrating and interpenetrating, effective logistics management can serve as a vital source of competitive advantage and a key driver of value creation for enterprises. In this new era, amidst the ongoing impacts of the pandemic and the evolving international landscape, the value and significance of logistics management continue to rise. This course will first explore the fundamental theories and analytical methods of logistics management from an enterprise perspective, focusing on four dimensions: customer value, transportation, inventory, and facility location. Second, it will expand to the perspectives of the supply chain and the industrial chain, introducing specific methods, solutions, and tools through which logistics management can achieve cost reduction and efficiency enhancement across industrial and supply chains. Finally, from an innovative perspective, it will examine the latest practices and developments in logistics management concerning logistics finance, the low-carbon economy, and digital operations.

Additionally, the course will cover the application of AI tools in designing and implementing solutions for logistics management.

教学目标

（1）   了解物流管理的基础理论、方法及最新的发展

（2）   结合实践问题，能够应用物流管理的技术、方法来实现企业及供应链的降本增效

（3）   了解物流和供应链管理实践的最新发展

（4）   学会用AI工具设计与实施现代物流管理的解决方案

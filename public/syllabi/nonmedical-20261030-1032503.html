课程简介（中文）

本课程满足中文专业学生的英语语言要求, 旨在帮助学生适应英语环境，并提升他们在学术场合使用英语的自信心和技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course fulfills the English language requirement for Chinese majors. It introduces the Chinese majors to the Chinese literary study in the English-speaking world in relation to the development of modern literary and cultural theories. This course accustoms students to an English-speaking environment and helps them to acquire confidence and skills in using English in an academic setting.

教学目标

This course accustoms students to an English-speaking environment and helps them to acquire confidence and skills in using English in an academic setting.

教学方式

讲座与讨论。

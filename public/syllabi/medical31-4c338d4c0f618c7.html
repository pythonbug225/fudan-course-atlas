1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 该课程融合组织胚胎学、生理学、病理学、药理学及中西医结合基础学科知识，以理论授课、实验及案例讨论的形式，融入内分泌和生殖系统的正常结构和生理功能，并以此为基础讨论主要相关疾病的病理变化、发生发展规律及常见药物治疗，内容以常见病、多发病的发病机制为重点，着重形态与功能之间的联系，强调局部和整体的关系。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | This course integrates fundamental knowledge from histology, embryology, physiology, pathology, and pharmacology with the principles of integrated Chinese and Western medicine. Through a combination of didactic lectures, laboratory sessions, and case-based discussions, it systematically explores the normal structure and physiological functions of the endocrine and reproductive systems, while examining the pathological changes, disease progression mechanisms, and common therapeutic interventions for major related disorders. With an emphasis on high-prevalence diseases, the curriculum highlights the correlation between morphological alterations and functional impairments, underscores the interplay between localized pathologies and systemic effects, and prioritizes mechanistic understanding of disease pathogenesis as a core learning objective.

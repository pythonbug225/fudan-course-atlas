课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在引导学生接触文化研究领域的核心争议议题，重点探讨拉丁美洲与西班牙的文化、民族认同及思想话语相关问题。通过将文学文本与其他文化载体结合分析，本课程帮助学生更全面地理解塑造西班牙语世界的历史、社会及象征语境。课程摒弃将文化视为固定、同质化实体的视角，转而强调其动态发展、由历史建构而成的本质属性。课程特别关注文学与文化创作在参与民族认同、现代性、记忆及文化差异相关广泛论争时所采用的多种方式。从这一意义而言，本课程致力于强化学生的分析与解读能力，同时引导他们更具批判性地认识文化意义在不同语境下的生成、协商与转译过程。最终，课程旨在为学生提供必备的理论工具，使其能够以反思性态度应对文化差异，并成长为翻译及跨文化传播领域中具备深厚学识的跨文化沟通者。

课程简介（英文）

The aim of this course is to introduce students to major debates in Cultural Studies, with particular attention to questions of culture, national identity, and intellectual discourse in Latin America and Spain. By examining literary texts alongside other cultural materials, the course offers students a broader understanding of the historical, social, and symbolic contexts that have shaped the Spanish-speaking world. Rather than approaching culture as a fixed or homogeneous entity, the course emphasizes its dynamic, and historically constructed character. Special attention is given to the ways in which literature and cultural production have participated in broader debates on national identity, modernity, memory, and cultural difference. In this sense, the course seeks to strengthen students’ analytical and interpretive skills while encouraging a more critical awareness of how cultural meanings are produced, negotiated, and translated across different contexts. Ultimately, it aims to provide students with the intellectual tools needed to engage with cultural difference in a reflective way and to develop as informed intercultural mediators in the fields of translation and cross-cultural communication.

教学目标

This compulsory two-credit course offers an introduction to key debates and approaches in Cultural Studies, with a focus on Latin America and Spain from an intercultural perspective. It provides a broad overview of cultural production in the Spanish-speaking world while examining the historical, political, and intellectual contexts that have shaped it. Through the study of literary texts and other cultural materials, the course explores major questions related to national identity, cultural discourse, and the formation of social imaginaries. Rather than presenting culture as a fixed body of knowledge, the course emphasizes its contested and dynamic character, paying particular attention to the ways in which texts and cultural practices reflect broader social debates. The course also encourages students to develop interpretive skills that are fundamental to advanced translation practice.

教学方式

The teaching method combines lectures, classroom discussion, and independent assignments. Each session is divided into two main parts: the first introduces key scholarly debates, previous research, and methodological approaches in Cultural Studies; the second applies these theoretical tools to the analysis of a specific literary text or other cultural expression. Active student participation is encouraged throughout the course in order to strengthen their interpretive skills. In addition, the course promotes the thoughtful use of technological tools from a humanistic perspective, encouraging students to reflect on how digital resources can support research, interpretation, and intercultural understanding without replacing critical judgment.

教学内容与进度安排

 

  1. Presentation of the subject. Multidimensional approach to “culture”. Definition and problems of the concept.
  2. Norbert Elias and the historical genesis of concept “culture”. Intellectual history of “Civilization” and “Culture” in Latin America.
  3. Race and Nation in Esteban Echeverría, and Domingo Faustino Sarmiento to José Vasconcellos and José Ingenieros.
  4. The role played by intelectuals in the development of Latin American countries. Writers, and politicians in the XIX Century.
  5. Orientalism and perception of the landscape in Latin American XIX Century. Edward Said and the case of Latin America.
  6. From the landscape to identity. Nation-State, intelectuals and the role of culture in Latin American early XX
  7. Modernity and new culture in Latin American early XX century. Avant garde and popular culture.
  8. Raymond Williams’ approach to the Cultural Studies. “Selective traditions” in the Hispanic writers from Domingo Faustino Sarmiento to Jorge Luis Borges.
  9. Development of the “cultural field”: universities, publications and circulations of intelectuals and other cultural actors.
  10. Midterm Exam.
  11. “Peripheral modernities” in Latin American biggest cities.
  12. Representation of the city in Xul Solar, Jorge Luis Borges, and Adolfo Bioy Casares. Urban culture, modernization, and mass-media in the continent. The
  13. The definition of “cultural field” by Pierre Bourdieu, and its application to Latin America.
  14. Relationship between writers, intellectuals, and politics during the fifties and sixties. The case of La Casa de las Americas.
  15. Cinema, mass-media, and youth during the sixties. The relation between popular culture and arts.
  16. Current Debates: gender, and transculturation. Michael Foucault and biopolitics of Latin America.
  17. Globalization and culture: Latin American arts in front of the XXI Century.
  18. Final Exam.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在通过创新与创业管理的视角，系统理解不同国家微观层面的商业环境。课程内容围绕全球背景下新价值的创造、创新管理过程以及新兴企业的运营展开，系统介绍美国、欧洲、澳大利亚、中国及其他新兴市场国家在创新与创业战略方面的主要特点与实践。 学生将学习全球创新与创业的基本原理、理论及实践，掌握在不同国家运营所需的关键工具、技能与思维方式。课程强调案例分析与实际应用，通过课堂讨论、小组项目、演示及分析性论文等多种教学方式，培养学生分析与评估各国创新与创业管理方法的能力。

课程简介（英文）

The subject is systematically organized around the creation of new value, processes of innovation management, and operation of new and emerging ventures in global environments. The subject covers major aspects of innovation and entrepreneurship strategy in countries such as U.S., Europe, Australia, China, and other emerging countries.

教学目标

The overall aim of this subject is to understand the micro business environment of multiple countries through the lens of managing innovation and entrepreneurship. Students will acquire a broad overview of principles, theories, and practice of innovation and entrepreneurship in global environment, together with an understanding of key tools, skills, and attitudes required to operate in different countries. On successful completion of this subject students should be able to: Explain the range, scope and complexity of the business environment related to the strategic management of technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship Analyze and evaluate various approaches to managing innovation and entrepreneurship in different countries Apply major theories and management tools to the analysis of case studies

教学方式

Teaching methods include lectures, case studies, classroom discussions, group projects, presentations, and analytical essays.

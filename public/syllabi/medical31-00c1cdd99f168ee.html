1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 医学心理学研究健康与疾病相互转化过程中心理与行为因素的规律，有助于医学生更深入地理解疾病，全面提升准确把握患者心理需求、给予人文关怀的能力。 本课程重点构建身心相关概念体系。通过系统学习医学心理学基础知识，掌握心理评估与心理治疗基本技能，使学生熟悉常见心理异常现象，培养运用身心相关概念诊断与治疗疾病的能力。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Medical psychology studies the laws of psychological and behavioral factors in the process of mutual transformation between health and disease, which helps medical students to understand diseases more deeply, and to improve the ability of accurately understanding patients' psychological needs and giving humanistic concern in an all-round way. The course of medical psychology focuses on the construction of psychosomatic related concepts. Through the study of basic knowledge of medical psychology, we can master the basic skills of psychological assessment and psychotherapy, familiarize students with common psychological abnormalities, and cultivate the ability to diagnose and treat diseases with psychosomatic related concepts.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

The meaning of health, the main characteristics of bio-psycho-social medical model; the concept, basic process and response of stress; the concept, scope and pathogenesis of psychophysiological diseases, the principles of prevention and treatment for common psychosomatic diseases; the concepts of patient role, doctor-patient relationship and disease experience; the basic skills of medical interview The stages and rhythms of sleep; the diagnosis and treatment of insomnia; the diagnosis and treatment of anorexia nervosa and bulimia nervosa; the concepts of crisis and crisis intervention; the principles of psychological measurement; the definition, classification and general principles of psychotherapy; the basic concepts and principles of behavioral therapy and cognitive therapy；the process of psychological counseling.

Theoretical class Panel discussion Panel presentation Case report analysis

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

Week 1 Introduction Section 1: Master the concepts of psychology, health and mental health; Be familiar with the importance of medical psychology and its related theories and branches; Section 2: Understand the research methods and development history of medical psychology. Week 2 Development Psychology and Mental Health, Abnormal psychology Section 1: Master the concept of Development Psychology and Mental Health, the content of good mental health. Section 2: Be familiar with the psychological development and hygienic characteristics of people of different ages. Week 3 Need Motivation and Frustration Section 1: Master the basic psychological process, basic needs, primitive and derivative motivations, Section 2: Be familiar with coping with frustration, the psychological defense mechanism. Week 4 Doctor-patient communication and doctor-patient relationship Section 1: Master the definition and the nature of doctor-patient relationship, the factors influencing DPR, the three models of doctor-patient relationship, and be familiar with the patient rights and responsibilities. Section 2: Be familiar with communication skills and the Balint group. Understand the changes in DPR and the future of DPR Week 5 Stress and Stress-related Disorder Section 1: Master the basic definitions of stress, stressor and stress reactions, general adaptation syndrome, be familiar with the characteristics of individual response to stress, effects of stress and stress management. Section 2: Master the clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles of PTSD, and to understand the treatment of PTSD. Week 6 Common psychological problems in general hospital and p sychosomatic Disease Section 1: Be familiar with common psychological problems in general hospitals and the seriousness of their impact. Section 2: Be familiar with the clinical feature and treatment of delirium. Understand the clinical manifestations and treatments of other common psychological problems. Section 1: Master the definition of psychosomatic diseases, be familiar with the pathogenesis and treatment principles of psychosomatic diseases. Section 2: Understand common psychosomatic diseases. Week 7 Chronic Pain Section 1: Master the definition of pain and related concepts, as well as the psychosocial factors affecting pain. Master the psychological and psychiatric problems related to pain. Section 2: Be familiar with the characteristics and classification of chronic pain. Familiar with adverse effects caused by chronic pain, clinical assessment and clinical psychological management for chronic pain. To understand the nomenclature of chronic pain and various pain mechanisms. Understand the definition and assessment of quality of life. Week 8 Eating Disorders Section 1: Master the definition of eating disorders, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles of anorexia nervosa. Be familiar with the epidemiological data, etiology and prognosis of anorexia nervosa. Section 2: Be familiar with diagnosis and differential diagnosis, and treatment principles of bulimia nervosa. To understand the clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles of bulimia. Week 9: Sleep Disorders Section 1: Master the phase and structure of normal sleep. Be familiar with the causes, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment principles of insomnia and sleep apnea syndrome. Section 2: Understand the physiological significance and mechanism of sleep. Understand the clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles of narcolepsy and other sleep disorders. Week 10 Suicide and Crisis Intervention Section 1: Master the definition of suicide, epidemiological investigation; related factors affecting suicide; suicide risk assessment. Understand the way forward for suicide prevention, Section 2: Master the concept of crisis, understand the development of crisis theory, master the application and steps of crisis intervention. Week 11 Clinical Psychological Assessment Section 1: Master the definition of assessment, basic principles of psychological assessment and the types of psychological measurement. Section 2: Understand the commonly used psychological scales, mental status exam, intelligence testing, mental assessment rating scales. Week 12 Psychological Therapies Section 1: Master the definition, classification and general principles of psychotherapy, and be familiar with the indications and contraindications of psychotherapy. Section 2: Understand the brief history of psychotherapy and the theory and method of psychodynamic therapy, and the evaluation of the efficacy of psychotherapy. Week 13 Group Therapy and Family Therapy Section 1: Master the definition of group therapy, family, family therapy, be familiar with the types of group therapy and family therapy. Section 2: Understand the features and techniques of group therapy and family therapy. Week 14 Behavior Therapy Section 1: Master the basic concepts and theories of behavioral therapy and biofeedback therapy. Section 2: Be familiar with common behavioral techniques, methods and indications, and clinical application of behavior therapy. Week 15 Cognitive Therapy Section 1: Master the basic concepts and theories of cognitive therapy. Section 2: Be familiar with common techniques, methods and indications, and clinical application of cognitive therapy. Week 16 Psychological Counseling Section 1: Understand the concept, significance and scope of psychological counseling; Section 2: Understand the difference between psychological counseling and psychotherapy

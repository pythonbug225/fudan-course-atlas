课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助学生全面理解公司金融的核心原理，重点涵盖以下内容：现值概念、净现值法则、内部收益率准则、资本预算决策、项目分析方法、风险与收益关系、资本资产定价模型（CAPM）、资本结构与资本成本，以及公司金融领域的其他关键议题。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles of corporate finance. It focuses on Present Value, NPV rule, IRR rule, Capital Budgeting, Project Analysis, Risk and Return, CAPM, Capital Structure and Cost of Capital, and other important issues in corporate finance.

教学目标

This course is aiming to introduce the fundamental financial theory, emphasize on its applications to corporate financial management, and provide insights necessary to understand the financial industry.

教学方式

Mainly lecturing.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction to Corporate Finance Week 2: Present Value Week 3: Present Value Continued and Valuation of Bonds Week 4: Public Holiday Week 5: Valuation of Bonds Week 6: Valuation of Common Stocks Week 7: Valuation of Common Stocks Continued Week 8: NPV and Alternatives to NPV Rules Week 9: Midterm and Cash Flow Calculations Week 10: Cash Flow Calculations Continued and Sensitivity Analysis Week 11: Introduction to Risk and Return Week 12: CAPM Week 13: Cost of Capital Week 14: Overview of Corporate Financing Week 15: Miller-Modigliani Proposition Week 16: Trade-off Theory and Pecking-Order Theory Week 18: Final Exam

教学参考资料

Principles of Corporate Finance 14th edition, Breadley, Myers, Allen and Edmans, ISBN-10: 1265074151, McGraw-Hill Education, Jan 2022.

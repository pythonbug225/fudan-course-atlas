课程简介（中文）

企业正越来越多地将部分营销工作转向数字技术，如在线付费广告、搜索引擎、社交媒体和移动终端。本课程聚焦于数字营销的理论与应用两大层面。 课程首先从对数字革命的理论认知切入，这一部分会介绍在多媒体、多设备、多渠道环境下消费者行为的新特征，以及如何将消费者分析转化为用于客户关系管理和品牌建设的营销策略。 接下来，课程会深入讲解各类数字营销工具的管理应用知识，包括在线广告、AIGC 营销和社交媒体营销等。与此同时，学生还将学习营销分析的相关应用，涵盖内容分析、网红达人识别、搜索引擎优化、A/B 测试及其他相关数据分析方法。

课程简介（英文）

Companies are increasingly shifting some of the marketing efforts to digital technologies such as online paid advertising, search engines, social media and mobile. This course focuses on both the theory and application of digital marketing. The course starts with the theoretical understanding of the digital revolution. This part introduces the new characteristics of customer behavior in a multi-media, multi-device and multi-channel environment, and how the customer analysis can be translated into marketing strategies for customer relationship management and brand building. Next, the course delves into managerial understanding of various digital marketing tools, including online advertising, AIGC marketing, and social media marketing. In the meanwhile, students learn applications of marketing analytics including content analysis, social influencer identification, search engine optimization, A/B testing and other related data analytics.

教学目标

The course is designed with the following goals in mind. Students should be able to achieve the following goals: Have in-depth knowledge of key concepts, theories, and issues in digital marketing. Make independent assessment of digital marketing strategies of companies. Understand digital marketing analytics and master some data analysis skills. Develop sustainable learning habits to learn the emerging digital marketing tools.

教学方式

授课方式: In-person weekly lectures: Students should expect to learn the basic concepts of digital marketing through the weekly in-person lectures. Individual assignment: Students should be able to test and enrich their understandings via case analyses. Team-based business simulation: Students can simulate the role of company interns and gain practical learning experience in a real business environment. Team-based project: All of these serves to benefit and prepare the students for the final team-based project presentation and report preparation.

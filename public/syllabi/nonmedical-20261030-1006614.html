课程简介（中文）

新兴媒体（如社交媒体、游戏、AI、AR、元宇宙、ChatGPT、Sora）正重塑从个人到全球的沟通。本课程旨在深化对改变人类生活与社会运作的媒介技术的理解。课程引导学生探索新媒体的本质与功能，其在新闻、健康、社会公正和伦理中的作用，以及数字进程与社会、文化、历史的交织。核心问题包括：数字化对人类存在与交流意味着什么？新兴媒体具有什么特征？它们如何推动正负变化？这些变化对研究、实践和未来有何影响？通过讲座、讨论和作业，学生将获得洞见，掌握词汇，培养批判思维并激发创造力。

课程简介（英文）

Emerging media—including social media, digital games, AI, AR, metaverse, ChatGPT, and Sora—are reshaping communication across interpersonal, organizational, societal, and global levels. This course deepens understanding of media technologies that transform human life and social operation. It guides students to explore the nature and affordances of new media, their roles in news, health, social justice, and ethics, and the interplay between digital processes and social, cultural, and historical contexts. Core questions include: What does digitalization mean for human existence and communication? What features define emerging media? How do they drive positive or negative change, and what implications arise for research, practice, and the future? Through lectures, discussions, and assignments, students will gain scholarly insight, develop critical thinking, acquire new vocabulary, and cultivate creativity in applying communication technologies.

教学目标

Introducing students to frontier theories and concepts about communication and emerging media Improving students’ ability to critically reflect and analyze diverse and representative communication phenomena in an emerging media context Enhancing students’ capability to innovate in applying emerging media within and across cultures

教学方式

Through instructional lectures, in-class discussion/activities, and mini-assignments, students will be exposed to the frontier of academic scholarship, learn the vocabulary about emerging media, develop critical thinking about new communication, and explore creative ideas in harnessing technologies.

教学参考资料

Course Readings Before each class, readings will be assigned. Readings are selected from cutting-edge research and professional journals. To benefit more from the class, students are required to keep up with the readings. You are encouraged to follow the recommended media channels or podcasts to keep yourself posted on what is happening about media. Besides, it is necessary to gain some hands-on experiences of using emerging media--such as ChatGPT, TikTok, WhatsAThe Red, Instagram, Bilibili, among others—on a regular basis throughout the semester.

考核构成

  • 课堂表现Participation 10.0% Please keep in mind, participation means more than presence in the classroom. For this course, an evaluation of your participation will be based on your attendance and contribution to the learning environment.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 30.0% Communication mini-assignments: For example: Conduct an affordance analysis of a select emerging media; describe and analyze a communication or cultural phenomenon occurring on emerging media; introduce a theoretical concept (which could come from a different discipline) and explain how it may help broaden our thinking about emerging media. Oral presentations, along with a written summary, are required for the assignments.
  • 课题论文Research Paper 60.0% Applied research proposal: Select an issue/problem/concern of interest to you, and of relevance to the course. The topic should be related to emerging media in a global and local context. After justifying its significance for emerging media and communication, you should identify high-quality literature and design your study. I expect your project to demonstrate logical conceptualization, solid literature, interesting research questions, and sound design. Moreover, consider how your research may inform communication practice and real-life changes. Some exploratory data collection and analysis are expected, too. An oral presentation of the proposal, around 15 - 20 minutes, is scheduled toward the end of the semester. You are required to present your research and exploratory findings to the class and respond to audiences’ questions.

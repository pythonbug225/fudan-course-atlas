课程简介（中文）

《法哲学原理》是黑格尔生前出版的5本哲学著作之一，是其成为柏林大学教授之后出版的首部也是最后一部著作，它在整个世界的学术影响力甚至超过其《精神现象学》或《逻辑学》。因为黑格尔意义上的“法哲学”，既不是法学院所理解的法理学，也不是法学院所理解的权利哲学，按照黑格尔自己的课程名称，它是“自然法与国家学”，其实就是考察个人权利、社会道德和国家伦理生活的规范机制的自然法根据和社会制度形成的机制，因而既包含了现在我们学科制中的法哲学、政治哲学、道德哲学、伦理哲学、社会哲学、历史哲学等等重要内容，我们可以笼统地以黑格尔实践哲学名之。因而，通过研读黑格尔的《法哲学》我们要学习的，不仅是以黑格尔所代表的传统形而上学思辨方法如何做哲学，而且更是探究西方形而上学完成阶段“实践的形而上学”如何对现代世界的精神行使“立法功能”，从而通过黑格尔而学习在哲学地建构世界中理解世界和把握世界的能力。

课程简介（英文）

Elements of the Philosophy of Right ranks among the five major philosophical works published during Hegel’s lifetime, and it holds a unique place as both his first and final scholarly output after assuming the role of professor at the University of Berlin. Globally, its academic impact even outweighs that of Hegel’s other seminal works, such as The Phenomenology of Spirit and The Science of Logic. Hegel’s "philosophy of right"—in its strict, original sense—defies simple categorization. It is not equivalent to the legal theory taught in law schools, nor is it merely a doctrinal philosophy of rights focused solely on abstract principles. As evidenced by Hegel’s own course title, this work is best understood as "Natural Law and Political Science": it explores the normative mechanisms governing individual rights, social morality, and the ethicality of the state, with its foundations rooted in natural law and its realization manifested through social institutions. In modern disciplinary terms, this scope transcends traditional boundaries, encompassing areas now divided into legal philosophy, political philosophy, moral philosophy, social philosophy, and the philosophy of history. For this reason, we can collectively characterize it as Hegel’s "practical philosophy." Studying Hegel’s Philosophy of Right offers dual insights. On one hand, it teaches us how to engage in philosophical practice using the speculative method of traditional metaphysics—with Hegel as its paradigmatic exponent. On the other hand, it reveals how the "metaphysics of the practical"—the culminating stage of Western metaphysical thought—exerts a "legislative function" that shapes the spirit of the modern world. In essence, Hegel equips us with a philosophical framework to construct, comprehend, and ultimately grasp the complexities of the world around us.

教学目标

（1）通过黑格尔学习传统哲学的形上思辨方法；（2）传统形而上学通过康德的实践转向，变成“行而上学”(Metaphysik des Praktischen)之后，在黑格尔“法哲学”中的体现与发展；（3）通过黑格尔法哲学的研读，确立现代文明的基本价值理念（个体性、主体性、自由权利，社会正义、国家道义和国际正义），探究现代规范秩序的构成、运行及其存在论结构，思考现代法治主义的基本架构和结构性问题。（4）思考全球化困境下黑格尔版本的全球正义推进方案。

教学方式

本课程采取原著精读、课程讲解和众多讨论相结合的方式，突出围绕经典哲学问题与原作者（黑格尔）和各个黑格尔诠释者进行学术对话以提供更好的思想论证的哲学方法。

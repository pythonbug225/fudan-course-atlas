国际金融

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在为现代国际金融理论奠定基础。课程运用以代数和图解为主的分析工具，在阐释关键理论模型的同时，结合当代实证依据展开探讨。教学内容涵盖评估实证依据、检验理论表现所需的计量经济学与定量方法。课程核心聚焦于分析各经济体如何及为何通过商品、服务和资产（包括货币）的全球市场产生联动，并重点研究外汇市场、不同汇率制度与金融市场体系，以及外汇动态与国际金融危机的发生机制。 课程首先回顾近年来关于"全球化"的实证研究，重点关注近几十年来国际贸易与跨境资本流动的显著扩张。通过梳理其历史背景、探讨衡量方法、总结主流理论解释，为后续分析提供基础。随后，课程将深入探讨国际货币市场的运行机制与相关理论，包括汇率决定机制、货币衍生品以及支撑这些市场的商品与资产交易。通过案例研究，课程还将剖析不同货币制度（包括单一货币区），并探究国际金融危机的成因与当代理论解释。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers a foundation in the modern theory of international finance. Employing analytical tools, primarily algebraic and diagrammatic techniques, it examines key theoretical models while presenting contemporary empirical evidence. The curriculum also addresses the econometric and quantitative methods used to evaluate this evidence and assess the performance of the theories. A central focus is the analysis of how and why aggregate economies interact through global markets in goods, services, and assets, including currencies. Special emphasis is placed on foreign exchange markets, alternative exchange rate and financial market regimes, as well as the dynamics of foreign exchange and international financial crises. The course begins by reviewing recent evidence on “globalization,” notably the dramatic expansion in trade and cross-border capital flows over recent decades. It provides historical context for this growth, discusses its measurement, and summarizes leading theoretical explanations. From there, the analysis moves to the institutional and theoretical workings of international currency markets, exchange rate determination, currency derivatives, and the trade in goods and assets that underpin these markets. Through case studies, the course explores alternative currency regimes, including single currency areas, and investigates the causes and modern theories of international financial crises.

教学目标

This course focuses on the macroeconomic and monetary dimensions of the global economy. Upon successful completion, students will be able to: Master Core Theoretical Frameworks Analyze the fundamental workings of international financial markets and the determinants of exchange rates. Explain the components, causes, and implications of a nation’s balance of trade and balance of payments. Develop and apply macroeconomic models to analyze open economies. Evaluate Macroeconomic Policy in a Global Context Assess the effectiveness of fiscal and monetary policy under different exchange rate regimes. Investigate the nature, causes, and evolution of currency and international debt crises. Apply theoretical models to contemporary international policy issues and debates. Apply Principles to International Financial Management Appraise the structure, role, and practices of foreign exchange markets. Evaluate the international working capital, investment, and financing decisions faced by multinational corporations. Discuss and compare alternative techniques, including financial derivatives, for hedging currency risk.

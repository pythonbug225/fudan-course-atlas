课程简介（中文）

本课程是营销学专业博士生（包含硕士生）的专业必修课，主要通过文献研讨的形式，对消费者研究的各个细分领域的理论进展和方法应用进行全面学习。目的是为了从事消费者行为研究的博士生具有理论基础和方法积累。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a compulsory subject for doctoral students (including master's students) majoring in marketing. It primarily employs a literature seminar format to provide a comprehensive study of theoretical advancements and methodological applications across various specialized areas of consumer research. The objective is to equip doctoral students engaged in consumer behavior research with a solid theoretical foundation and methodological expertise.

教学目标

Gain familiarity with social psychology and consumer research literature Master the essential skills for conducting behavioral research Develop high-quality research ideas and projects that meet publication standards

教学方式

教师课程讲授；学生文献阅读与汇报；研究计划研讨。

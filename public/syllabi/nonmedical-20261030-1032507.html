课程简介（中文）

本课程为中国现当代文学专业的研究生学位基础课，结合教师讲授、学生报告、专题讲座等形式，意在帮助学生了解中国现当代文学史研究领域中的重要研究论题、理论框架与论述策略，掌握史料搜集与运用的基本方法，并学习学术论文的写作。在每一次课上，我们都会针对一个具体的文学时段或关键论题，选取相关的研究论著，以此为基础展开讨论。这些阅读与讨论将帮助学生掌握中国现当代文学史研究领域中的核心概念和必要知识。同时，本课程意在将引导学生了解学术写作的基本规范，学习阅读文献、打磨问题、建构有效论述的方法，并且帮助学生围绕各自的研究兴趣和课题，练习学术综述的写作。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a foundational course for graduate students majoring in modern and contemporary Chinese literature. Combining lectures, student presentations, and invited talks, it aims to help students understand important research topics, theoretical frameworks, and argumentative strategies in the field of modern and contemporary Chinese literary studies. Students will master basic methods of archival works, and learn academic writing. In each class, we will select relevant research works based on a specific literary period or key topic, and conduct discussions accordingly. These readings and discussions will help students grasp the core concepts and essential knowledge in the field of modern and contemporary Chinese literary studies. Simultaneously, this course aims to guide students to understand the basic norms of academic writing, learn methods for reading literature, refining questions, and constructing effective arguments, and help students practice writing scholarship reviews based on their individual research interests and topics.

教学目标

1）精读本学科的核心著作，理解不同学者的基本研究框架、方法、和结论，掌握本领域的必要知识。 2）了解中国现当代中国文学史研究中的史料与文献搜集、使用的基本方法。 3）学会撰写文献综述，能够根据自身研究议题，撰写合格的文献综述。

教学方式

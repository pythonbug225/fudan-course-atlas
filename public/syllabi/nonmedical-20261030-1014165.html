论文写作

2025秋 · FORE40023.03 · 郑咏滟

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本门课程旨在培养语言学和应用语言学方向低年级研究生的学术论文写作能力。通过剖析论文的各部分结构、讲解论文写作的过程、体例、以及基本研究方法范式，本门课程帮助学生学会寻找相关文献、评价学术资源、批评性回顾、撰写文献回顾等基本论文技巧。 本门课程属于一门本研融通课程，旨在培养高阶的英语学术论文写作技能、思辨性阅读与分析写作技能，并培训初步的科研技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to develop the academic writing skills of first-year graduate students in the fields of linguistics and applied linguistics. By analyzing the structure of each part of a thesis, explaining the process of thesis writing, the format, and basic research methodology paradigms, this course helps students learn basic thesis skills such as finding relevant literature, evaluating academic resources, critically reviewing, and writing literature reviews. This course is an integrated undergraduate and graduate course, which aims to develop advanced English academic writing skills, critical reading and analytical writing skills, and basic research skills.

教学目标

1. Identify the requirements of a research paper and the structure of a B.A. thesis in English studies. —Remember 明确研究型论文的要求，知晓英语本科毕业论文的结构。 2. Distinguish the characteristics of the academic genre from other genres; Recognize and reproduce the linguistic features of academic thesis writing, and the mechanics of academic writing (e.g. citation styles, signposting, etc.). Label and identify the rhetorical features of the composing parts of an academic thesis, and able to locate and evaluate the rhetorical moves in academic essays.—Remember and Understand 区分学术体裁有别于其他文体的特征；认出并能自主使用学术论文的语言特征、学术写作的细节特征（包括如何引用、如何组织行文）。说出、辨别学术论文不同部分的修辞特征，并能够找出、评估学术论文的修辞语步。 3. Identify necessary steps of writing a thesis and the structure of a thesis; Distinguish the purposes of different parts of a thesis; —Remember and Understand 说出学术论文写作的必要步骤以及论文的结构组成；辨别论文不同部分的写作目的。 4. Find and select appropriate sources, evaluate and incorporate them into a critical review of past research on a selected topic with appropriate citation. —Apply and Evaluate 找到、选取合适的资源，评估、整合资源，并能使用适切的引用，针对预选的话题写出分析性文献回顾。 5. Analyze materials and writing samples in view of the content,tone, lexico-grammar, organization, rhetorical moves, etc.; Assess the effective ness of peer students’ academic writing, comment on peer writing from the lexico-grammatical, rhetorical, organizational, aspects. —Analyze and Evaluate 从内容、语气、词汇句法、行文组织、修辞语步等方面分析课程提供的写作样本；评估同伴写作的有效性；能从词汇句法、修辞论证、行文结构等方面对同伴写作进行评价。 6. Formulate feasible research questions; Propose a feasible research plan and write up a research proposal with a clear structure, organization and conforms to the academic writing style.—Create 构想出可行的研究问题；提出可行的研究计划；写出一篇结构清晰、篇章合理、符合学术写作风格的研究计划书。

教学方式

本课程采用混合式学习方式。利用复旦大学的超星慕课平台、学习通客户端，使用Camtasia8.0软件录制教学视频，课程内容完全实施信息化、网络化，作业、讨论、师生问答均在网络平台上完成。 在每次观看教学视频时，学生需要即时完成在线小测验。观看完课程视频后，也需要按照教学计划安排按时完成课后测验。

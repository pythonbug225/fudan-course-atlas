课程简介（中文）

本课程在现代金融理论框架下介绍商业银行的决策与管理。课程采用案例教学法，深入探讨商业银行管理中的一系列核心议题，内容包括银行业体系、资产负债管理、风险管理、商业银行估值以及金融科技应用等。通过系统学习，学生将掌握扎实的银行管理理论知识，并获得理解快速变化的金融行业所需的洞察力与实践视角。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces decision-making and management of commercial banks within modern finance theory framework. This course utilizes case study approach to examine a broad range of relevant issues in commercial bank management. We will cover topics on banking industry, assets and liability management, risk management, valuation of commercial banks, and fintech applications. The course intends to provide students with solid theoretical knowledge of bank management, as well as insights necessary to understand and work for this rapidly changing financial industry.

教学目标

To provide students with solid theoretical knowledge of bank management issues. To prepare students insights necessary to understand and work for this rapidly changing banking industry. To encourage active discussions on contemporary banking issues.

教学方式

Lectures: Weekly lectures introducing basic concepts and theories. Case study: Cases and related articles are distributed in class and students are encouraged to express their opinions based on the knowledge acquired from lectures. Group project: Groups of students collaborate on related topics. Students should learn to collect data from various sources and summarize their work. In-class discussions: Discussions and critical thinking are encouraged in class on relevant topics.

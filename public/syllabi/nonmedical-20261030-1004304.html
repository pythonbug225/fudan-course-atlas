课程简介（中文）

课程致力于全面提升学生的英语综合运用能力，内容涵盖高阶阅读与词汇训练、听力与口语交际训练、学术写作指导。通过丰富的语言实践，学生将深入接触不同文体与语境，锻炼精准表达与批判性思维。同时，课程融入中外文化对比，提升学生跨文化思辨能力，助力学生在多元文化语境中自信交流，为未来学术与职业发展筑牢语言根基。

课程简介（英文）

This Advanced English course focuses on integrated language skills training. It includes intensive reading and vocabulary building with authentic materials, listening and speaking practice through interactive activities, and academic writing guidance to enhance clarity and coherence. Students will engage in discussions, presentations, and writing tasks that expose them to various linguistic contexts. The course also incorporates cross-cultural communication scenarios to broaden students' global perspectives. Through these dynamic components, students will develop the proficiency needed for effective communication in both academic and professional settings.

教学目标

本课程以培养学生的语言能力、文化意识、思维品质与学习能力为核心，将英语学习视为塑造全面发展人才的重要途径。教学中注重提升学生的英语综合应用能力的同时，培养思辨能力、国际沟通能力和跨文化能力，使学生具备国际视野与社会责任感，以满足国家对国际化人才的战略需求和个人的多元发展愿景。

教学方式

本课程采用线下互动式教学模式，注重学生的积极参与和课堂互动。教学方法包括： 小组讨论：通过小组合作，促进学生之间的思想碰撞和观点交流。 课堂演讲与展示：锻炼学生的口头表达能力和自信心。 互动式讲解：教师结合多媒体教学材料，指导学生进行语言知识、思维知识和文化知识的建构，并随时与学生互动答疑。 角色扮演与情景模拟：通过模拟真实场景，提高学生的实际应用能力。 课堂练习与即时反馈：通过课堂练习及时巩固知识，教师即时反馈帮助学生纠正错误。

教学内容与进度安排

Week Module Teaching Goals 1 Orientation and Course Overview Learners are expected to: 1. understand the course objectives and overall semester arrangement; 2. familiarize themselves with learning tools that will facilitate their English language studies. 2 U1 Text A: Take This Fish and Look at it (Global analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. grasp the main idea (Professor Agassiz’s teaching method is a legacy left to the author) and structure of the text (narration in chronological order); 2. master the key language points and usages. 3 U1 Text A: Take This Fish and Look at it (Cultural study of the text) U1 Writing strategy: Sentence Variety Learners are expected to: 1. learn to use a combination of methods in developing an essay (chronological order along with comparison and contrast in this text); 2. conduct a series of reading, listening, speaking and writing activities related to the theme of the unit. 4 U2 Text A: Why Life in the Fast Lane Fails to Fulfill Us (Global analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of the pace of life and learn how to use it in context. 5 U2 Text A: Why Life in the Fast Lane Fails to Fulfill Us (Cultural study of the text) U2 Writing strategy: Argumentative Essay Learners are expected to: 1. learn to express themselves more freely about the fast pace of life; 2. know the basic structure of an argumentative essay. 6 U3 Text A: Is He Living or Is He Dead? (Global analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of human weaknesses and imperfections. 7 U3 Text A: Is He Living or Is He Dead? (Cultural study of the text) U3 Writing strategy: Writing a Coherent Account Learners are expected to: 1. learn to offer their ideas on the topic of human weaknesses more appropriately; 2. practice writing a coherent account of a person or an event. 8 Mid-term Examination 9 U4 Text A: A Vision for Agriculture (Global analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of farming and environmental changes. 10 U4 Text A: A Vision for Agriculture (Cultural study of the text) U4 Writing strategy: Proposing a Solution to a Problem Learners are expected to: 1. learn to appreciate how different farming methods reflect different visions of agriculture; 2. develop critical thinking abilities with regard to the relationship between agricultural development and environmental protection by placing the problem in the Chinese context and looking at it from a Chinese perspective; 3. know the basics of proposal writing. 11 U5 Text A: Law in the Age of Artificial Intelligence (Global analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of AI and its application. 12 U5 Text A: Law in the Age of Artificial Intelligence (Cultural study of the text) U5 Writing strategy: Presenting Arguments Cohesively Learners are expected to: 1. develop critical thinking abilities about AI and its application; 2. develop writing strategies for presenting arguments cohesively. 13 U6 Text A: A Wilderness Experience (Global analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of nature and man and learn how to use it in context. 14 U6 Text A: A Wilderness Experience (Cultural study of the text) U6 Writing strategy: Writing a Research Paper Course Wrap-up Learners are expected to: 1. learn to express themselves more freely on environmental protection and the relationship between nature and man; 2. know the basic structure of a research paper; 3. develop critical thinking abilities in the Chinese context. 15 Q&A 16 Final Oral Test (In-class test) 17 Final Written Test 18 Submission of Final Grades

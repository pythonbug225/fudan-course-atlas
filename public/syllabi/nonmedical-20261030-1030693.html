课程简介（中文）

《学术写作导论（II）》为英语语言文学及相关专业必修课，亦向具备扎实英语能力与学术写作兴趣的非专业学生开放。本课程系统介绍大学阶段学术写作规范，旨在帮助学生掌握必要知识与技能，以适应学术共同体的语言与语篇要求，并为后续深造奠定基础。最终，本课程致力于培养学生撰写达到出版水准的学术论文的能力。

课程简介（英文）

Introduction to Academic Writing (II) is a compulsory course for students majoring in English language and literature or related areas. Students from other departments should demonstrate solid English language skills and interest in academic writing. This course offers a continued introduction to academic writing at the university level. The course aims to prepare students with the essential knowledge and skills necessary to meet the linguistic and discursive expectations of the academic community and form solid basis for further study. Furthermore, it is my hope that students taking this course will gain the ability to write publishable quality papers.

教学目标

Build on our writing knowledge to reach an acceptable level of skill for upper-level college students.

教学方式

Lecture, Discussion, In-class writing, Peer review, Writing Assignments

创业企业金融与会计

2025秋 · GEIN10029.01 · 曹利

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要介绍创立和运营一家公司时遇到的主要金融与会计问题。课程通过分析创业企业的商业模式，分析收入和盈利模式，编制财务商业计划书等内容的讲解，帮助学生理解创业企业的估值逻辑和商业逻辑。本课程在教学中，将理论讲解、案例分析、学生实践等方式相结合，增强学生创新精神和创业意识，培养学生具备创业人才的基本专业金融和会计知识。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides the students with the knowledge of major finance and accounting issues they may encounter when setting up and operating a new business. The course is designed to help the students •    gain a better understanding of the different business models that start-up firms may choose from •    learn the various source of financing available to start-up firms at different stages of business •    and develop the competence to value startup firms using various financial models and valuation approaches At the end of the course, the students will have the opportunity to apply the knowledge and skills they learn throughout the course to develop a complete business plan for their own venture. The course will combine different teaching approaches including lecturing, case analyses, and hands-on learning to encourage innovation and entrepreneurship among students, and to equip them with professional finance and accounting knowledge necessary to succeed in their new business.

教学目标

本课程主要结合企业战略和商业模式，学习如何处理创立和运营一家公司时遇到的主要金融与会计问题。 学生通过课程学习，应完成如下学习目标： （1）能够描述创业企业的商业模式 （2）能够描述创业企业的收入和盈利模式 （3）能够编制创业企业财务计划； （4）运用多种估值方法对创业企业进行估值； （5）能够撰写创业项目商业计划书。

教学方式

教师理论授课+案例分析+小组讨论交流+小组作业

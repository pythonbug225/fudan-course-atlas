金融计量学

2025秋 · STAT70007.01 · 侯燕曦

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程面向数据科学学院对计量经济学与金融数据分析基础知识感兴趣、但背景相对薄弱的新入学研究生开设。然而，学生仍需具备初等概率论、随机过程与数理统计的基本知识。课程内容涵盖金融工程的基础理论、金融计量经济模型，以及针对金融市场数据的统计方法。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a preparation for new graduate students in School of Data Science, who are interested in basic knowledge of econometrics and financial data analysis but without strong background. However, some knowledge in elementary probability，stochastic processes and mathematical statistics are necessary. This course covers fundamental theory of financial engineering, financial econometric models and statistical methods for financial market data.

教学目标

- Study the basic theory of financial engineering and stochastic calculus. - Identify empirical features and characteristics of many types of financial data. - Learn about various financial econometric and statistical models that can capture these features. - Develop your own programs to implement these models and analyze the data.

教学内容与进度安排

模块1：资产定价理论（12学时） 资产组合理论 (Portfolio Theory) 资本资产定价模型（CAPM） 模块2：随机过程与随机分析基础（20学时） 随机过程基础 (Stochastic Processes) 鞅理论基础 (Martingale Theory) 伊藤随机积分基础 (Itô Stochastic Integration) 伊藤引理 (Itô's Lemma) 随机微分方程基础 (Stochastic Differential Equations) 模块3：衍生品定价与数值方法（16学时） 股票、债券、期权模型 (Models for Stocks, Bonds, and Options) Black-Scholes公式 蒙特卡洛模拟基础 (Monte Carlo Simulation Basics) 数值方法基础 (Numerical Methods Fundamentals) 期权定价公式与蒙特卡洛模拟方法 (Option Pricing Formulas & Monte Carlo Methods)

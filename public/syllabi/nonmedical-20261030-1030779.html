领导力课程系列一

2026春 · MF10001.01 · 韦祎、范英

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助每位学生在课程结束后能够： 基本了解金融领域各行业的职业概况与发展路径，并评估自身的职业价值观、兴趣与技能，判断是否适合相关职业发展方向。 设计具有针对性的个人简历与求职信。 建立专业的社交网络。

课程简介（英文）

It is intended that at the completion of this course, each student will be able to: Basic understanding the career overviews and development in various industries in the financial field, and assess their personal career values, interests and skills to assess whether they are suitable for a career path. Design a targeted resume and cover letters. Build a professional social network.

教学目标

This course ains to expose students to a wide range of different areas of the financial industry, to help them with career exploration and planning. This course will also serve as a precursor to the introduction and guidance of professional placements. The format of the course includes career talks, guest sharing, group discussions, etc. Students are expected to actively share their questions, ideas, and experiences in class.

教学方式

Lectures, Field Trips, Workshops etc.

教学内容与进度安排

Session 1 (Class 1&2): Overview and interpretation of career development in the primary market (mainly VC/PE) industry; Session 2 (Class 3&4): Overview and interpretation of career development in the secondary market (Buy-side/Sell-side) industry; Session 3 (Class 5&6): Overview and interpretation of career development in the derivatives industry; Session 4 (Class 7&8): Overview and interpretation of career development in the quantitative investment industry; Session 5 (Class 9&10): Business Writing I: resume & cover letter; Session 6 (Class 11&12): Overview and interpretation of career development in the financial regulators, trading and settlement institutions; Session 7 (Class 13&14): Overview and interpretation of career development in the investment banking; Session 8 (Class 15&16): Overview and interpretation of career development in the commercial banks, Internet financial enterprises; Session 9 (Class 17&18): Final presentation

考核构成

  • 出勤Attendance 100.0% 1. Grades in this course will be assigned as Attendance and Participation (100%) 2. Students need to complete at least 10 sessions of lectures, workshops, or field trips to earn the credit.

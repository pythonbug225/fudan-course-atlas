1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 细胞培养技术、转染技术与细胞工程；蛋白工程与蛋白药物修饰技术；抗体工程与新药研究；现代生物技术行业新进展。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Course Content: Cell culture technology, transfection technology and cell engineering; protein engineering and protein drug modification technology; antibody engineering and new drug research; latest advances in modern biotechnology industry.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

坚持价值引领，培养学生的科研伦理意识与创新报国情怀，树立严谨求实的科学态度。注重知识传授，系统讲解现代生物技术与药物研发的核心理论与关键技术，构建从基础到前沿的知识体系。强化能力培养，通过实验设计与学术研讨，提升学生的科研实践能力、批判性思维与跨学科解决复杂问题的能力，为培养具有国际视野与创新能力的生物医药高层次人才奠定基础。 Upholding value guidance, the course aims to cultivate students' awareness of research ethics and a sense of innovation for national service, fostering a rigorous and truth-seeking scientific attitude. It emphasizes the systematic impartation of core theories and key technologies in modern biotechnology and drug research and development, building a comprehensive knowledge system from fundamentals to cutting-edge advances. The course strengthens ability cultivation by engaging students in experimental design and academic discussions, enhancing their practical research skills, critical thinking, and capacity to solve complex interdisciplinary problems. This foundation supports the cultivation of high-level talents in biomedicine with international perspectives and innovative capabilities.

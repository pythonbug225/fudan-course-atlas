课程简介（中文）

适用对象： 具备约500-600汉语词汇量、学习约100学时或通过HSK三级的学习者。 课程目标与内容： 旨在全面提升语言综合运用与交际能力。通过日常生活实用主题，学习者掌握核心句型与词汇，结合结构化训练增强日常交际自信；融入文化专题以促进对中国社会的理解及在华适应能力。 教学特色： 创新融合人工智能技术。定制AI代理基于学习者水平与进度，智能生成适配材料并提供个性化任务与对话练习，构建以学生为中心、数据驱动的学习体验。

课程简介（英文）

This intermediate Chinese course is for learners who know about 500–600 Chinese words, have studied for around 100 hours, or have passed HSK Level 3. The course focuses on improving overall language skills and communication ability. Students will learn useful sentence patterns and vocabulary through practical topics and take part in activities like drills, role plays, and interactive exercises to build confidence in daily communication. Cultural topics are also included to help students better understand Chinese society and adapt to real-life situations in China. This course also incorporates artificial intelligence. A customized AI agent helps generate tailored learning materials and offers personalized task and conversation practice based on each learner’s language level and progress, creating a student-centered, data-driven learning experience.

教学目标

This course aims to further strengthen learners’ overall Chinese language skills, including reading, listening, speaking, and character writing. Grounded in the communicative approach, it provides diverse materials and practice opportunities to help students apply what they’ve learned. By the end of the course, learners are expected to: Recognize 200–250 new words, expanding their vocabulary to around 800 words in total Master 40–50 essential grammar structures commonly used in daily communication Confidently express themselves across 11 practical and relevant topics, such as food, travel, health, and work Improve reading pace, comprehension, and overall literacy in Chinese Build foundational speaking skills for everyday real-life interactions Develop a deeper understanding of Chinese customs, values, and social practices through integrated cultural content

教学方式

教学方式：本课程以交际型语言教学法（Communicative Language Teaching, CLT）为核心，强调“以学为本”的教学理念，融合任务型教学、情境教学、文化导入、产出导向式教学、AI辅助教学等多种方式，全面提升学生的语言运用能力与文化适应力

课程简介（中文）

本课程通过介绍当代中国政治制度的发展以及围绕当代中国政治的学术争论来增强学生对当代中国政治特征和发展的全面了解，增进对中国传统文化和政治制度的认同。

课程简介（英文）

In this course, you will advance your knowledge of some of the major questions in domestic Chinese politics and major scholarly research findings, methods, and debates organized around these questions. Theories on contemporary Chinese political development will be evaluated against empirical evidence and practices observed on the ground.

教学目标

Gain a more comprehensive and deeper understanding and knowledge of major events in contemporary Chinese political development and scholarly debates regarding Chinese political development.

教学方式

lecture, discussion on required readings, on-site visit and extracuricculum activity

教学内容与进度安排

week 1 Introduction to the Course, Introduction to Studies of Chinese politics

week 2 Fall of Imperial China, National Disintegration, Communist Revolution and Victory

week 3 Politics after the Foundation of P.R.C.

week 4 Reform and Opening: China’s Economic Transformation

week 5 Political Institutions and the Communist Party Rule

week 6 People’s Congress 

week 7 Policy Processes

week 8 Political Economy: Decentralization and Development

week 9 Elections and Representation

week 10 Law and Politics

week 11 State and Society

week 12 Information Management, Nationalism, and Legitimacy 

week 13 final presentation

教学参考资料

Naughton Barry. 1996. Growing Out of the Plan: Chinese Economic Reform, Cambridge University Press.

required readings (3-4 articles) on e-learning website.

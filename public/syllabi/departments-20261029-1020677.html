课程简介（中文）

毕业实习是本专业本科培养体系中的关键实践环节，旨在打通“课堂—企业—社会”的最后一公里。本课程要求学生在真实商业环境中，将所学的市场调研、消费者行为、企业管理、数字化建设等理论知识转化为解决实际问题的能力。实习单位涵盖快消、互联网、金融、咨询、新零售、文化创意及新兴科技企业等多个行业，鼓励学生深入一线业务场景，参与市场策划、用户运营、数据分析、企业信息系统建设、AI推广等核心工作。 作为指导教师，我将全程协同企业导师，通过定期线上/线下指导、阶段性报告审阅、中期检查与终期核查等方式，确保实习过程兼具专业深度与职业素养培养。本课程不仅是一次岗位体验，更是学生从“校园人”向“职场人”转型的重要过渡，为其未来就业或深造奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

The graduation internship is a critical experiential component of the undergraduate curriculum in this major, designed to bridge the "last mile" between classroom learning, enterprises, and society. This course requires students to apply theoretical knowledge—acquired in areas such as market research, consumer behavior, business management, and digital transformation—to solve real-world business challenges within authentic commercial environments. Internship placements span multiple industries, including fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), internet and technology, finance, consulting, new retail, cultural and creative sectors, and emerging tech enterprises. Students are encouraged to immerse themselves in frontline business operations and participate in core activities such as marketing planning, user operations, data analysis, enterprise information systems development, and AI-driven initiatives. As the faculty supervisor, I will work closely with on-site company mentors throughout the internship period. Through regular online/offline guidance sessions, review of progress reports at key milestones, mid-term evaluations, and final assessments, I aim to ensure that the internship experience integrates both professional depth and the cultivation of workplace competencies. This course is far more than a job shadowing opportunity—it serves as a pivotal transition for students as they evolve from "campus learners" into "professional practitioners," laying a solid foundation for their future careers or graduate studies.

教学目标

通过为期8–12周的全职毕业实习，学生应达成以下目标： 理论联系实际：能够运用信息管理核心理论（如数据分析，信息系统战略分析，AI应用等）分析企业真实问题，并提出可行建议； 提升职业能力：掌握市场调研、竞品分析、内容策划、数据可视化、AI应用等职场通用技能，熟悉企业运作流程与团队协作机制； 强化数字素养：在数字化营销实践中，熟练使用主流工具（如Excel、SPSS、Tableau、社交媒体后台、CRM系统等），理解AI、大数据在企业决策中的应用； 培养职业伦理与责任感：遵守企业规章制度与商业保密原则，树立诚信、责任、合作的职业价值观； 明确职业发展方向：通过沉浸式岗位体验，反思个人兴趣、能力与行业需求的匹配度，形成清晰的职业规划意识。

课程简介（中文）

通过前沿专题讲授，深化学生对生理学核心领域（器官、神经、免疫、内分泌）的现代研究理解，培养跨学科思维，衔接基础研究与临床/药物研发应用。本课程是复旦大学为生理学专业设计的高阶前沿课程，以模块化专题形式，系统性解析器官功能、神经机制、免疫调控及激素生理等领域的最新科研进展，由多位资深教师联合授课，兼具深度与广度。 课程聚焦生理学前沿领域，分为四大主题模块： 器官生理学：涵盖下丘脑、脂肪/肌肉、肝脏、胰岛、心脏、骨、淋巴管、肠道、肺等器官系统的功能与调控机制。 神经生理学：包括自噬与神经退行性疾病、视觉系统机制、受体信号转导与药物成瘾、神经退行性疾病药物研发等前沿议题。 激素与内分泌生理学：重点解析雌激素的生理作用。 免疫生理学：探讨炎症反应与免疫治疗的生理学基础。

课程简介（英文）

Through cutting-edge topics, this course deepens students' understanding of modern research in core areas of physiology (organ systems, neuroscience, immunity, endocrinology), cultivates interdisciplinary perspectives, and bridges fundamental research with clinical/drug development applications. Designed as an advanced frontier course for Physiology majors at Fudan University, it employs a modular format to systematically analyze the latest research advances in organ function, neural mechanisms, immune regulation, and hormonal physiology. Co-taught by multiple senior faculty, the course offers both depth and breadth. Focusing on physiological frontiers, the course comprises four thematic modules: Organ Physiology: Covers functional and regulatory mechanisms of organ systems including hypothalamus, adipose/muscle tissue, liver, pancreas islets, heart, bone, lymphatic vessels, intestine, and lung. Neurophysiology: Explores autophagy & neurodegenerative diseases, visual system mechanisms, receptor signaling transduction & drug addiction, and drug development for neurodegenerative diseases. Hormonal & Endocrine Physiology: Focuses on the physiological roles of estrogen. Immunophysiology: Examines the physiological basis of inflammatory responses and immunotherapy.

教学目标

1.培养研究生关注生理学前沿知识理论的兴趣，夯实生理学核心理论知识，拓展生理学前沿知识的边界。 2.培养研究生提出生理学前沿科学问题的能力，结合主讲教授开展生理学前沿的进展和心得体会，激发研究生对生理学研究的热爱。

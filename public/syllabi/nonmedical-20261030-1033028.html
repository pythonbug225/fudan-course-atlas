课程简介（中文）

我们将研究不同维度拓扑理论的不同方法及其在AKSZ治疗中的统一

课程简介（英文）

We will study different approaches to topological theories in different dimensions and their unification in AKSZ treatment

教学目标

Students would know different topological theories in different dimensions obtained by different methods. They would also know how these approaches are unified in the marvelous AKSZ construction. They will also understand boundary conditions and appearance of complexes.

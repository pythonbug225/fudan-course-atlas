课程简介（中文）

本课程聚焦卫生技术评估，以循证思想为脉络，将“临床决策”、“公共卫生决策”与“政策决策”相结合，从微观和宏观两个层面，系统介绍理论方法、应用实践与决策转化，呈现卫生技术评估的过去、现在和将来。

本课程尝试探索卫生技术评估的理论体系，让学生更系统全面的掌握卫生技术评估的方法、应用及决策转化。

本课程将传统方法与新方法进行融合，及时跟进卫生技术评估领域的新方法新进展，对于深刻理解及开展卫生技术评估研究具有前瞻性的参考价值。

本课程坚持把理论方法和实践相结合，在深入的理论方法学习的基础上，通过实践和案例的介绍，不断加深对卫生技术评估全局性问题的认识，对于全面理解卫生技术评估及其相关研究的系统性、预见性具有指导和促进的作用。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on health technology assessment, integrating "clinical decision-making", "public health decision-making", and "policy decision-making" within the framework of evidence-based thinking. It systematically introduces theoretical methods, application practices, and decision translation from both micro and macro perspectives, presenting the past, present, and future of health technology assessment.
This course attempts to explore the theoretical system of health technology assessment, enabling students to more systematically and comprehensively grasp the methods, applications, and decision-making translation of health technology assessment.
This course integrates traditional methods with new ones, keeping abreast of the latest advancements in the field of health technology assessment. It provides forward-looking reference value for a deep understanding and conducting research in health technology assessment.
This course adheres to the principle of combining theoretical methods with practice. Based on in-depth learning of theoretical methods, it continuously deepens the understanding of the overall issues of health technology assessment through the introduction of practice and cases. It plays a guiding and promoting role in comprehensively understanding the systematicness and predictability of health technology assessment and related research.

教学目标

本课程的目的是提高学生对卫生技术评估的内容、基本理论和主要方法的认识，并能初步开展卫生技术评估实践的能力。
通过学习这门课程，要求学生能够：
1. 了解卫生技术与卫生技术评估评估概述；
2. 了解卫生技术的有效性、安全性、经济性和社会适应性的评价；
3. 了解卫生技术评估与卫生政策的关系与转化；
4. 基本掌握系统评价的方法。

教学方式

课前要求学习MOOC教程；课堂教学、讨论和课后联系相结合。

Principles of Finance and Valuation

课程简介（中文）

This course provides an introduction to the principles of finance and valuation. Specifically, we will discuss: as corporate managers, how to evaluate prospective projects; as investors, how to evaluate firms. The course has two main topics. The first is corporate finance. We will start with the time value of money, then discuss how various factors affect the required rate of return, cost of capital and how to make the proper investment decisions. The second topic focuses on firm valuation, including the estimation of cash flows, future growth, discount rate, terminal value, and relative valuation using comparable companies and multiples.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to understand the basic principles in finance and how to evaluate projects or companies from both conceptual and practical framework.

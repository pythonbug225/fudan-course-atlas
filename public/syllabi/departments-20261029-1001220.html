课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助学生全面理解公司金融的核心原理，主要为企业的投资和融资决策。课程涵盖折现、现值、净现值、内部收益率、资本成本等基本概念，以及项目分析、资本资产定价模型（CAPM）、资本结构、融资方式和公司金融领域的其他关键议题。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles of corporate finance, focusing on investment and financing decisions for corporations. It covers basic concepts in finance such as discounting and Present Value, NPV and IRR, as well as methods and models including Project Analysis, CAPM, Capital Structure and Cost of Capital, and other important issues in corporate finance.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to provide students with fundamental financial theories and basic techniques in understanding and making corporate financial decisions, esp. in the digital economy. The course is also a precedent of many advanced courses in the finance major, including Financial Markets and Institutions, Investments and Multinational Financial Management.

教学方式

Lecturing (80%) + Class discussion (10%) + Quiz and Practice (10%)

课程简介（中文）

医学微生物学是临床医学、基础医学、法医学专业必修的基础课程，主要包括五个方面的内容：基础细菌学，临床细菌学，基础病毒学，临床病毒学和病原性真菌。基础细菌学和病毒学主要论述细菌和病毒的形态结构、生长繁殖、遗传变异、致病与免疫、实验室诊断和特异性防治等；临床细菌学和病毒学着重描述与医学相关病原菌和病毒的生物学性状，致病性与免疫反应、微生物学检查和防治；病原性真菌主要论述真菌的主要特点及其与医学的关系。

课程简介（英文）

Medical Microbiology is a compulsory basic course for clinical medicine, stomatology, basic medicine, and forensic medicine majors. It mainly includes five aspects: basic bacteriology, clinical bacteriology, basic virology, clinical virology, and pathogenic fungi. Basic bacteriology and virology mainly discuss the morphological structure, growth and reproduction, genetic variation, pathogenicity and immunity, laboratory diagnosis, and specific prevention and treatment of bacteria and viruses; clinical bacteriology and virology focus on describing the biological characteristics, pathogenicity and immune response, microbiological examination, and prevention and treatment of pathogenic bacteria and viruses related to medicine; pathogenic fungi mainly discuss the main characteristics of fungi and their relationship with medicine.

教学目标

了解病原微生物的生物学特性与致病性；认识人体对病原微生物的免疫作用，感染与免疫的相互关系及其规律；了解感染性疾病的实验室诊断方法及预防原则。熟练掌握医学微生物学常用英文词汇，熟练阅读本学科相关英文文献。

课程简介（中文）

Equity issuance, debt issuance, project financing, and distressed debt investment are the key business for advanced corporate finance. This course will provide a comprehensive review of these process. The course will include lectures designed for specific skills and concepts in advanced corporate finance; it will also use cases extensively to facilitate the learning.

教学目标

Students will gain a comprehensive understanding of advanced corporate finance topics, including underwriting, IPO, seasoned equity offering, debt issuance, asset management, distressed debt investment, and project/infrastructure financing. We will focus on key concepts, institutional settings and valuation challenges. This course is suitable for students who are considering careers in corporate finance, investment banking and private equities.

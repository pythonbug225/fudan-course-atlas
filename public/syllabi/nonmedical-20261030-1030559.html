全球化背景下的社会语言学

2026春 · FORE70076.01 · 宋旸

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

全球化带来了语言与物质资源以及人类身体的跨国流动。本课程旨在从理论工具与多样化情境中的研究议题两个方面，系统介绍全球化社会语言学。具体教学与学习目标包括： 理解用于理论化全球化的核心概念； 掌握全球化社会语言学领域的理论知识与分析能力； 运用社会语言学的理论与方法，对全球化时代的语言相关问题进行批判性分析。

课程简介（英文）

Globalization has brought along transnational mobility of linguistic and material resources as well as human bodies. The purpose of the course is to give a comprehensive introduction to sociolinguistics of globalization in terms of both theoretical toolkits and research foci associated with varied contexts. More specific teaching/learning objectives include: To understand the concepts for theorizing globalization; To acquire theoretical knowledge and analytical skills within the field of sociolinguistics of globalization; To apply sociolinguistic theories and methods to critically analyze language-related issues in the era of globalization.

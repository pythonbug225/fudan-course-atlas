课程简介（中文）

课程教学的基本内容包含以下四个部分：

 

（一）人工智能深度神经网络算法及其DNN专用处理器架构：具体包括经典机器学习、神经网络和深度学习人工智能的基本概念和算法，并介绍典型的神经处理器（NPU）架构。

（二）类脑拟态神经网络算法及SNN专用处理器架构：具体包括神经元、神经突触、脉冲传递机制，及STDP学习机制的等基本和概念，并介绍典型的脉冲神经网络（NPU）处理器架构。

（三）量子计算范式及量子计算机架构：介绍量子计算的基本原理、量子比特、量子门和量子纠缠、通信等内容，讲解量子计算机与经典计算机的区别和应用前景。

（四）其他可能出现的新型计算范式：分布式计算、后量子加密、同态加密、联邦学习、区块链算法等，具体内容根据实际情况安排决定。

课程简介（英文）

The course covers the following four major topics:

(1) Artificial Intelligence, Deep Neural Network Algorithms, and DNN-Specific Processor Architectures:
This part covers the fundamental concepts and algorithms of classical machine learning, neural networks, and deep learning, and introduces representative neural processing unit (NPU) architectures.

(2) Neuromorphic Spiking Neural Network Algorithms and SNN-Specific Processor Architectures:
This part introduces the fundamental concepts of neuromorphic computing, including neurons, synapses, spike-based information transmission, and spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP) learning mechanisms. Representative spiking neural network (SNN) processor architectures are also presented.

(3) Quantum Computing Paradigms and Quantum Computer Architectures:
This part introduces the fundamental principles of quantum computing, including quantum bits (qubits), quantum gates, quantum entanglement, and quantum communication. It also explains the differences between quantum and classical computers and discusses the potential applications and future prospects of quantum computing.

(4) Other Emerging Computing Paradigms:
This part may cover emerging topics such as distributed computing, post-quantum cryptography, homomorphic encryption, federated learning, and blockchain algorithms. The specific topics will be determined based on the latest developments and practical considerations.

教学目标

新型计算范式是指计算机科学中新兴的计算模型和方法，它们在设计和实现计算系统时采用了与以冯诺伊曼为代表的传统计算模型具有显著的差别。本课程拟教授面向这一领域的基础知识，通过介绍几类广泛应用的新型算法，并结合计算机体系结构知识帮助学生了解新算法和新架构协同设计的重要性，培养学生的软硬件协同设计的素养。

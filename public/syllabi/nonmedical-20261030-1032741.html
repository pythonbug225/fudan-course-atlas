课程简介（中文）

北魏郦道元撰写的《水经注》，是我国古代以水道为纲记载区域地理信息最为著名的典籍。它以西汉王朝的版图为基础(若干地区兼及域外),对许多重要河流及其流域进行综合性的描述,所涉及的内容包括自然地理与人文地理两大部分。英国著名的中国科技史专家李约瑟（Joseph Needham）在其代表作《中国科技史》中称《水经注》是“地理学的广泛描述”。本课程是对《水经注》文本的具体研读，涉及版本介绍、文字校勘、史料来源及所记载内容的地理复原。

课程简介（英文）

The Commentary on the Water Classic, authored by Li Daoyuan of the Northern Wei Dynasty, is the most renowned ancient Chinese classic that records regional geographic information by taking river courses as its framework. Based primarily on the territory of the Western Han Dynasty (with some areas extending beyond its borders), it provides comprehensive descriptions of many important rivers and their basins. The content covers both physical geography and human geography. The renowned British historian of Chinese science and technology, Joseph Needham, referred to this work as a "broad description of geography" in his magnum opus, Science and Civilization in China. This course involves a detailed study of the text of the Commentary on the Water Classic, covering aspects such as an introduction to its versions, textual collation, historical sources, and the geographic reconstruction of the content it records.

教学目标

《水经注》是历史地理专业的基础读物，但此书因涉及历史地理问题，极为难读。本课程试图通过对《水经注》文本的具体研读，使学生对《水经注》所载的具体内容，尤其是其中的地理方面的内容，有一个比较深入而具体的了解，从而激发学生对《水经注》进一步探究的兴趣。

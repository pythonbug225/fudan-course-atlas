课程简介（中文）

本课程学习内容是中国民事法律制度概论，主要讨论《中国人民共和国民法典》的总则编以及物权编、合同编的总则编、侵权编。如果时间允许也会讨论家事法律。

课程简介（英文）

This is a seminar course designed to discuss the general legal framework of Chinese civil law system, focusing on the Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China. Specifically, discussions will be focused on the General Part of the Civil Code and the other Books, such as the Book on Real Rights, Contracts, and Torts. If time allows, family laws may also be discussed.

教学目标

Students are expected to be extensively involved in the class discussion and presentation. In addition to lectures, the course will include several seminars each focusing on a specific topic of the civil law. Students need to write, present, and discuss on a topic assigned or approved by the instructor. The objective is that the students will understand the Chinese civil law system in comparison with the common law system or the system of some other countries.

教学方式

Students are expected to prepare before class. After the instructor’s lecture, the students are expected to actively participate in class discussions and Q&As and presentations of cases or theories.

课程简介（中文）

本课程专注于通过R编程语言应用现代统计方法，旨在为统计学、数据科学、数学和计算机科学专业的学生提供解决复杂统计问题的计算技术。课程内容包括蒙特卡洛方法、贝叶斯分析、马尔科夫链蒙特卡洛（MCMC）、以及Bootstrap和Jackknife等重抽样技术。学生还将学习EM算法、变分推断等高级计算统计方法，并了解如数据同化等前沿技术。通过实践操作，学生将掌握在R中实现这些计算方法的技能，并将其应用于统计建模与推断的实际问题中。

课程简介（英文）

This undergraduate course in computational statistics focuses on applying modern statistical methods through the programming language R. Designed for students in statistics, data science, mathematics, and computer science, the course covers essential techniques for solving complex statistical problems. Topics include Monte Carlo methods, Bayesian analysis, Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), and resampling techniques such as the bootstrap and jackknife. Students will also explore the EM algorithm, variational inference, and more advanced methods in computational statistics like data assimilation. Through hands-on experience, students will gain the skills necessary to implement these computational methods in R and apply them to real-world problems in statistical modeling and inference.

教学目标

This course aims to align with the university's educational philosophy in the age of AI, fostering a solid foundation in statistical computing and the ability to apply innovative methods. By exploring modern statistical techniques such as Monte Carlo methods, Bayesian analysis, and Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), students will develop the skills necessary to tackle complex statistical problems. The course not only focuses on knowledge delivery but also emphasizes hands-on practice and case analysis, enhancing students' problem-solving abilities and their competence in data processing and statistical modeling. Furthermore, the course encourages an integrated approach, guiding students to understand statistical methods from a multidisciplinary perspective, strengthening their capacity for innovation and practical application in data science. This prepares them for advanced research or high-level data analysis roles in the future.

教学方式

To achieve the course objectives, a variety of teaching methods will be employed, combining traditional lectures, hands-on programming sessions, and project-based learning. Lectures will provide the theoretical foundation for core statistical techniques, while coding exercises and assignments will enable students to directly apply these methods using R. Interactive discussions and case studies will foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills, allowing students to explore real-world statistical challenges. Projects will encourage collaboration, enabling students to tackle complex problems together and refine their ability to communicate statistical findings. Additionally, students will engage in self-directed learning through online resources and practical exercises that reinforce their understanding of computational methods. This blend of methods ensures that students not only gain knowledge but also develop the computational, analytical, and collaborative skills necessary for success in the field of computational statistics.

多种维数中的超对称规范场论与膜

2026春 · PHYS60032.01 · Tomoki Nosaka

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

超对称规范理论具有各种良好的性质，在这些理论中，各种观测值的非摄动精确评价是可能的。超对称规范理论还将超弦理论和M理论中被称为膜的物体描述为它们的世界体积理论，这意味着我们可以通过对超对称规范理论的分析来加深对超弦理论和M理论的理解，反之亦然。在这门课程中，我将首先回顾各种维超对称的一般方面，然后介绍3、4、5和6维超对称规范理论的各种例子，特别关注M理论中与m2膜和m5膜相关的理论。我们还解释了这些理论中的各种非摄动分析以及不同理论之间的非平凡关系。

课程简介（英文）

The supersymmetric gauge theories enjoy various nice properties and in such theories the non-perturbatively exact evaluation of various observables can be possible. Supersymmetric gauge theories also describe the objects called branes in the super string theories and M theory as their worldvolume theory, which implies that we can deepen our understanding of superstring theory and M theory through the analysis of the supersymmetric gauge theories and vice versa. In this course I would like to first review the general aspects of supersymmetry in various dimensions, and then introduce various examples of the supersymmetric gauge theories in 3,4,5 and 6 dimensions with particular focus on the theories related to M2-branes and M5-branes in M theory. We also explain various non-perturbative analysis in these theories as well as the non-trivial relations among different theories.

教学目标

The aim of this course is to explain: (i) supersymmetry in various dimensions and their relation; (ii) BPS states, the BPS solutions and their relation; (iii) analysis of 4d N=2 supersymmetric gauge theory by Seiberg and Witten; (iv) the basic aspects of M2-branes and M5-branes in M theory and the supersymmetric gauge theories describing multiple M2-/M5-branes; (v) the methods for exact calculations in supersymmetric gauge theories The topics listed under "Course Content & Schedule" may be adjusted according to the interests of the participants.

课程简介（中文）

学生能了解和识别乌迦利特语的书写及基本语法形式，具备对于简单例句的当场阅读和翻译能力，并能利用工具书阅读与翻译一些乌迦利特语的文本段落，进而对于闪米特语系特别是乌迦利特所属的西北闪米特语支的基本特点有所领会。在此基础上，能对于乌迦利特语与旧约希伯来语进行更具深度的语文学探索和比较研究。

课程简介（英文）

Students will be able to understand and recognize the written form and basic grammatical forms of Ugaritic, acquire the ability to read and translate simple examples on the spot, and use reference books to read and translate some Ugaritic text passages. This will allow them to gain a deeper understanding of the basic characteristics of Semitic languages, particularly the Northwest Semitic branch to which Ugaritic belongs. On this basis, they will be able to conduct more in-depth philological exploration and comparative study of Ugaritic and Old Testament Hebrew.

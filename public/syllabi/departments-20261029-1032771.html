课程简介（中文）

本课程为研究生提供学术写作理论、方法与技巧的入门介绍。课程重点强调科研过程及学术写作的作用、学术文本的基本结构，以及规范的文献引用。学生需分析若干指定的学术论文，撰写一篇小论文，并进行展示。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an introduction to the theory, methods, and techniques of academic writing for graduate students. It emphasizes the research process and the role of academic writing, the fundamental structures of academic texts, and proper literature citation. Students will be required to analyze specific academic papers, write a small paper, and present it.

教学目标

This course aims to help students develop a solid foundation in the theory, methods, and skills of academic writing. Key objectives include understanding the research process and the role of academic writing, analyzing the structure and content of academic papers, mastering the style and techniques of scholarly writing, and gaining the practical ability to analyze and compose academic papers for management journals. Specifically, the course covers: Understanding the academic research process and the significance of academic writing. Analyzing the structure and relevant content of academic papers. Mastering the stylistic conventions and techniques of academic writing. Developing the fundamental skills necessary to analyze and write management papers.

教学方式

The course combines lectures, interactive exercises, and peer feedback sessions. Lectures introduce the principles and conventions of academic writing, covering topics such as paper structure, paragraph development, citation practices, and stylistic clarity. Interactive exercises allow students to apply these concepts through in-class writing activities, analysis of published research papers, and collaborative worksheets. Peer feedback sessions provide opportunities for students to review and critique each other's work, fostering critical evaluation skills and exposing participants to diverse writing approaches. The course culminates in individual presentations where students pitch their research topics and defend their methodological choices.

课程简介（中文）

本课程以英美科幻文学为主要研究对象，系统梳理其历史发展、文学特征与文化影响。课程从科幻文学的早期源流入手，结合不同历史时期的代表性作家与作品，通过专题讲授、文本细读和课堂讨论，考察科幻文学从类型萌芽、形成到不断演变的历史过程。课程将结合经典科幻文本与相关理论研究，引导学生理解科幻文学与科技发展、社会变迁、政治文化之间的复杂联系，分析英美科幻文学如何参与全球科幻传统的形成与发展，以及跨文化交流和全球化如何塑造科幻文学的创作与传播。通过对不同历史时期和文学思潮的比较分析，课程旨在培养学生的文学文本分析与批判性思考能力，拓展其对现代文学与文化发展的认识，并进一步理解科幻文学所具有的独特想象力及其介入现实、思考未来的可能性。

课程简介（英文）

This module offers an in-depth exploration of British and American science fiction, tracing its historical development, thematic concerns, and cultural impact. Through a series of thematic discussions and close readings of seminal texts, students will examine the genre’s evolution from its origins to contemporary manifestations. Each week will focus on a specific thematic or historical period, covering topics such as “Science Fiction before the Genre,” “Pulp SF and its Others,” “After the War,” “The New Wave Revolution,” and “New Paradigms, After 2001,” among others. Through critical engagement with primary texts and secondary sources, students will gain insights into how Anglo-American science fiction intersects with and influences global science fiction traditions, as well as how it reflects cultural exchange and globalization. This module is designed to enhance students’ analytical skills, broaden their understanding of literary and cultural movements, and foster a deeper appreciation for the imaginative possibilities of science fiction literature.

教学目标

By the end of this module, student will:

  • understand ideas about genre and the cultural distinctions and hierarchies between “literary” and “genre” texts.
  • be familiar with recurring tropes such as robots, space exploration, genetic engineering, dystopian futures and post-apocalyptic scenarios.
  • have explored ways that SF has acted as a social commentary on contemporary society.
  • have considered how SF has explored ideas including parallel worlds, the implications of technology, and different models of time and reality.
  • have gained knowledge of diverse critical approaches to SF, including those from Marxism, structuralism, gender studies, postcolonial theory, cultural history, adaptation studies, ecocriticism, and utopianism.

教学方式

Lecture and Group discussion.

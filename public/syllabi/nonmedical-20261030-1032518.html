课程简介（中文）

汉字是单音节词，故骈偶化是书面语言对口语的修饰的基本方式。早期的书面语多有骈偶化特点。东汉后，经六朝世族社会积累，骈偶文取代了散体文的文正宗地位，成为书面语的唯一正宗形式。完成脱离了口语，这一现象在南朝尤盛，亦产生种弊端，改革之风时起。改革者以散体为古文，以骈偶文为时文，骈散之别也成为古今之争，涉及内容有三：1，南北文化问题：骈盛于南，古散多存于北，语体之别是隋唐文化对立的一种形态，文体磨合也是南北文化汇融的一种方式；2，文化观念问题：儒经以散体为主，倡古文散体者，强调师法儒家经籍，重传承儒学精神；学时文者以《文选》等南朝文为教材，重叙个人才情。倡古文多与复古道，振儒学相联系，多能取得意识形态的制高点。3，世庶文化问题；骈偶文的繁细化，完全脱离口语，成为非专门学习则不可掌握的特殊语言，在贵族社会里，接受书面训练是贵族特权之一，这种书面语言也成为贵族身份标志，也成为传情达志的障碍，势必为接近口语散体文取代。但也是吸取骈文语言艺术基础上形成的合古今骈散于一体新型书面语。韩柳是较早且较成功的探索者，从不同方面为此后书面语指明的方向，树立了范式。因此，探索韩柳文最初的努力与用心，可对中古近世书面汉语表达方式与功能有更深入的认识。

课程简介（英文）

Chinese characters are monosyllabic; therefore, parallelism is the fundamental method by which written language refines spoken language. Early written Chinese was largely characterized by parallelism. After the Eastern Han Dynasty, through the accumulation of the aristocratic society of the Six Dynasties, parallel prose displaced prose from its orthodox position and became the sole legitimate form of written language, completely breaking away from spoken language. This phenomenon was especially prevalent in the Southern Dynasties, yet it also gave rise to various drawbacks, and calls for reform arose from time to time. Reformers regarded prose as “ancient writing” and parallel prose as “contemporary writing”; the distinction between parallel and free prose thus became a debate between the ancient and the modern, involving three major issues: Northern and Southern cultural issues: Parallel prose flourished in the South, while ancient free prose survived mainly in the North. The stylistic difference was one form of cultural opposition between the Sui and Tang dynasties, and the reconciliation of literary styles was also a means of cultural integration between the North and the South. Cultural ideological issues: Confucian classics were mostly written in free prose. Advocates of ancient free prose emphasized modeling themselves on Confucian scriptures and prioritized inheriting the spirit of Confucianism. Those who studied contemporary writing took literary works of the Southern Dynasties such as Wen Xuan (Selections of Literature) as textbooks and focused on expressing personal talent and refinement. Advocating ancient writing was often associated with restoring the Dao and reviving Confucianism, allowing its proponents to seize the ideological high ground. Aristocratic and common cultural issues: The over-elaboration of parallel prose, which completely severed ties with spoken language, turned it into a special language unmasterable without specialized study. In aristocratic society, formal written training was one privilege of the nobility; this kind of written language thus became a marker of aristocratic status, yet also an obstacle to genuine communication. It was inevitably replaced by prose closer to spoken language. Nevertheless, the new written language that emerged integrated ancient and modern styles, merging parallel and free prose, and was formed on the basis of absorbing the linguistic art of parallel prose. Han Yu and Liu Zongyuan were early and relatively successful explorers. They pointed out the direction and established the paradigm for later written Chinese in different respects. Therefore, exploring Han Liuwen's original efforts and intentions can provide a deeper understanding of the expressions and functions of written Chinese in the Middle and Early Ages.

教学目标

介绍唐代文史研究成果，构建韩柳成长背景，系统了解唐代政治文化与韩柳生成发展之关系，认识中古书面语言转型与定制的深层文化元素； 通过对具体事件的分析，了解韩柳古文创作的互动关系，深度了度韩柳对中唐古文运动的领导作用； 通过对具体作品的比较深解，认识古文运动与韩柳文在古代文学史以及古典书面语言史上的意义。 ntroduce the research results of Tang Dynasty literature and history, construct the background of Han Liu's growth, systematically understand the relationship between the political culture of the Tang Dynasty and the generation and development of Han Liu, and understand the deep cultural elements of medieval written language transformation and customization. Through the analysis of specific events, we can understand the interactive relationship between Han Liu's ancient text creation and deepen the leading role of Du Hanliu in the ancient literature movement of the Middle and Tang Dynasties. Through the comparative and in-depth understanding of specific works, we can understand the significance of the ancient literature movement and Han Liuwen in the history of ancient literature and classical written language.

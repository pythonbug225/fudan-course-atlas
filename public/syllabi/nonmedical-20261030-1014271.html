分子生物学

2025秋 · BIOL30014.04 · 服部素之

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程的主要章节包括：DNA复制、DNA损伤修复、RNA转录与加工、蛋白质生物合成、基因表达调控、表观遗传调控、基因工程与基因编辑、转录组学及其他分子生物学相关技术。系统讲授分子生物学的基础知识、基本理论和研究方法，并介绍分子生物学的新成果、新进展以及未来发展趋势与挑战。

课程简介（英文）

The main chapters of this course are: DNA replication, DNA damage repair, RNA transcription and processing, protein biosynthesis, gene expression regulation, epigenetic regulation, genetic engineering and gene editing, and transcriptomics and other related technologies in molecular biology. This course systematically teaches the basic knowledge, fundamental theories and research methods of molecular biology, and introduces the new achievements, new progress, future development trends and challenges of molecular biology.

教学目标

Through the teaching of this course, we help students to understand and master the process of replication, transcription and translation of genetic information molecules in living organisms; to understand and master the characteristics and rules of the regulation of gene expression; to have agreater improvement in their ability and quality, and to enter into the study of the subsequent postgraduate courses with a better overall quality, higher interest in learning, a more solid knowledge base and a good learning state.

教学方式

The teaching will be provided with distinct gradation and accurate expression, and highlight the key points, integrate theory with practice, and will be vivid and interesting, and inspire thinking. Adaptation of multimedia and other modern educational technology to assist teaching will be employed to enrich classroom teaching and to improve teaching level and quality. Attention should be paid to the reform of each teaching link and the organic connection between each teaching link during the teaching process.

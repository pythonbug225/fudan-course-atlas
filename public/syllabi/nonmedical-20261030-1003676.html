课程简介（中文）

西班牙文学及作品导读课程是面向西班牙语专业本科生开设的必修课程，主要授课对象为大学三年级以及四年级的本科生。 本课程所涉及的文学作品的时间涵盖为：从中世纪开始至西班牙内战爆发（1936-1939）期间的文学作品（包括诗歌、戏剧、小说等多种体裁）, 通过对每个时期的代表作品的阅读、讨论、分析和评价，厘清西班牙文学的发展脉络，建立基本的文学批评的概念，同时通过本课程的学习， 将语言学习和历史、文化相关联，从宏观上建构文学发展图景，从微观上提升语言感知力和人文学科所要求的批判性思维能力、查阅资料和文献整合能力，以及跨文化理解和多样性意识等。

课程简介（英文）

Introduction to Spanish Literature and Works This required course for Spanish majors (typically taken in the third or fourth year) surveys Spanish literary works from the Middle Ages through the Spanish Civil War (1936-1939), encompassing poetry, drama, and novels. Through close reading, discussion, and analysis of representative texts, the course: Traces the historical development of Spanish literature Introduces fundamental concepts of literary criticism Integrates language study with historical and cultural contexts Learning Outcomes: Develops a comprehensive understanding of Spain's literary landscape Enhances language proficiency through textual analysis Strengthens critical thinking and research skills Fosters cross-cultural awareness and appreciation of diversity Key improvements: More concise structure with clear sections Stronger academic phrasing ("surveys" instead of "covers") Parallel structure in bullet points Removal of redundant phrases ("on a macro/micro level") More active verb choices ("strengthens," "fosters") Proper flow from course description to outcomes

教学目标

1.了解西语文学，提高西语文化素养； 2.了解掌握西班牙文学从中世纪开始至当下的几个重要历史节点； 3.熟悉各时期的代表作家、代表作品及当时所处历史文化语境的主要特点； 4.提高学生对于文学作品的阅读、鉴赏和分析能力。

课程简介（中文）

整个课程分为三个部分，第一部分为学术规范，即对科研诚信与学术规范的教育；第二部 分为范文赏析，即对范文进行阅读、分析、评价、欣赏，范文选自国际一流社会科学杂 志，如《美国社会学杂志》，《美国经济学评论》，和《美国政治学评论》等。通过分析 范文，总结分析社会科学经验研究的规范结构的八个组成要素，即：问题、理论、假设、 测量、数据、方法、分析、结论。第三部分为研究实践，要求课程参与者呈交并介绍自己 的研究课题（如硕士论文），通过课堂分析讨论，参照范文的规范结构修改提高。

课程简介（英文）

The entire course is divided into three parts. The first part is about academic norms, which includes education on research integrity and academic standards; THE SECOND PART Divided into sample essay appreciation, which involves reading, analyzing, evaluating, and appreciating sample essays. The sample essays are selected from top international social science disciplines Zhi, such as the American Journal of Sociology, the American Economic Review, and the American Political Science Review. Through analysis Sample essay, summarizing and analyzing the eight components of the normative structure of empirical research in social sciences, namely: problem, theory, hypothesis Measurement, data, methods, analysis, and conclusions. The third part is research practice, requiring course participants to submit and introduce themselves The research topic (such as a master's thesis) will be improved through classroom analysis and discussion, and modified according to the standardized structure of the sample essay.

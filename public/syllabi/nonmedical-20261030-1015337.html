言语产生和感知的解剖生理机制

2025秋 · CHIN70003.01 · 陈忠敏

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程讲解语言发声和感知的基本解剖学知识和生理特点，测试语言发声和感知的各种实验手段和方法，语言发声和感知的各种理论，以及与语言发声、感知相关的各种言语障碍及各种治疗手段，还可能包括语音田野调查及诊所里的治疗，发音动作的合成技术以及发音与感知的关系等内容。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an introduction to a range of topics in speech and hearing science of interest to students in linguistic phonetics and speech sciences. The course will introduce basic speech anatomy, physiology and function, current methods and measurement tools, and "hot" theoretical issues in speech production and perception relating to language cognition. Side topics may include: field and clinical applications, articulatory speech synthesis, and the interaction of articulation with perception.

教学目标

通过本课程的教学，同学们掌握语言的发声和感知的基本特点，为将来从事语言教学、语言本体研究、语言翻译、病理语言学等专业的深造打下坚实的基础。 This course provides an introduction to a range of topics in speech and hearing science of interest to students in linguistic phonetics, speech sciences and communication disorders (such as speech pathology and audiology).

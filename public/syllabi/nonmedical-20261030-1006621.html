博物馆学的理论与实践前沿

2025秋 · ARCH70016.01 · 陆建松

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要讲授博物馆学理论与实践前沿，包括博物馆学科建设、人才培养及职业资格、文物活起来理论与实践、博物馆使命与博物馆运营、博物馆传播利用体系建设、博物馆建设问题及思考、博物馆展示理论与实践、博物馆展览设计与规划、博物馆展览工程管理实践与探索等。

课程简介（英文）

This course primarily covers the forefront of museology theory and practice, including the development of the museum discipline, talent cultivation and professional qualifications, the theory and practice of revitalizing cultural relics, the mission and operation of museums, the construction of museum communication and utilization systems, issues and reflections on museum development, the theory and practice of museum exhibitions, the design and planning of museum exhibitions, as well as the practice and exploration of museum exhibition project management.

教学目标

通过本课程教学，旨在让学生了解中国博物馆学理论与实践的前沿问题，中国博物馆事业的发展及其发展趋势，从而激发学生对这些问题及发展趋势的进一步关注和思考。 The teaching of this course aims to familiarize students with cutting-edge issues in the theory and practice of museology in China, as well as the development and trends of China's museum sector. This will inspire students to further engage with and reflect on these issues and emerging trends.

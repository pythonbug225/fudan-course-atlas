课程简介（中文）

作为商业语言，会计在企业管理中扮演着至关重要的角色。会计技能在金融、银行、审计、税务筹划和咨询等诸多行业都备受重视。扎实的会计基础能为职业发展打开广阔空间。因此，通过学习会计基础知识、理解核心概念与原则、掌握底层逻辑并进行实操训练，对各位而言至关重要。 本课程将为您构建商业知识体系（尤其是会计领域）奠定基础。我们将首先探讨财务会计的基本框架：其本质、目标与职能，以及所处的运营环境和背景。课程将系统讲解财务会计的核心概念，深入剖析财务信息的记录、汇总与呈现流程，并重点培养对会计信息的解读能力。

课程简介（英文）

As the language of business, accounting plays a very important role in managing business organizations. Accounting skills are highly valued in many industries, including finance, banking, auditing, tax preparation, and consulting. Having a solid foundation in accounting can open up a wide range of career opportunities. Therefore, it is very important that you learn accounting by getting a good understanding of its basic concepts, principles and underlying logic as well as by actually doing problems. This course serves as a base on which you can develop your business and especially accounting knowledge. We will start with a discussion of the framework of financial accounting: its nature, intents and purposes, and the context and environment in which it operates. The course unpacks various core financial accounting concepts and further looks into the processes used to record, summarize, and present financial accounting information as well as, crucially, its interpretation.

教学目标

To introduce students to the concepts and principles of accounting. To learn how to record business transactions and prepare basic financial statements. 3. To develop critical thinking skills necessary to analyze financial data and make informed decisions.

教学方式

In-person classes with teaching assistant tutoring sessions

考核构成

  • 课堂表现Participation 10.0% Students are required to attend the lectures. Active participation in class is encouraged. A discretionary score up to 10% of final grade will be assigned by the instructor.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 15.0% You are required to turn in homework problems before the beginning of each class in the following week. You may do the problems in groups, but you should write down your answers independently. Late homework will not be accepted.
  • 汇报Presentation 15.0% The group project is to analyze and compare three companies’ financial statements. The requirements include a written report and a presentation. Students will be assigned to groups. This will be your “formal group.” You will learn to work with this group, just like what you will do when you go to work. 60% of the scores will be based on the content of the research papers you produce and the rest 40% on the presentations. To prevent free-rider problem, the scores will be adjusted based on peer evaluation within each group.
  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 15.0% During the course of the semester, there will be a total of three quizzes. No make-up quizzes.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 45.0% This course will have a closed book final exam. No make-up exams will be given except for medical reasons supported by proper documentation provided by doctors.

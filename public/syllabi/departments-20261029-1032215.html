课程简介（中文）

本课程是专为非税收专业的外国留学生开设的硕士研究生课程。课程是为了帮助学生了解掌握国际商务和国际投资中重要和常见的税务问题、税务优化、和相关税收规则，建立经商和管理跨国企业所必要的基本税收观念和风险意识，从而提高学生在未来国际商务实践中参与跨国企业的涉税经营决策和风险管理的能力。课程内容将包括跨国销售、跨国投资、跨国产业链、收购兼并和重组、有形资产、无形资产和融资安排中涉及到的税收征管、转让定价、税务风险管控、税务优化、反避税监管等方面的知识。课程将通过鲜活的实务案例以深入浅出的方式去解析深奥复杂的国际税收问题，教师与学生将一起探寻国际商务中的税收之道。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed specifically for non-tax-major foreign graduate students for the purposes of equipping them with necessary tax knowledge and skills in dealing with tax opportunities, challenges, and risks in international business operation. The course will go through major tax issues and relevant tax rules involved in the operation course of international business and finance, such as tax in cross-border sales and services, supply-chain management, cross-border investment, mergers & acquisition, internal re-organization, and intra-group resources sharing (e.g., financing, IP, HR, and tangible assets sharing, etc.). In the class, both MNCs’ maneuvers in tax optimization and tax authorities’ anti-avoidance measures will be discussed in an interactive manner. The ultimate goal of the course is to build up a comprehensive understanding and balanced view on the tax matters of international business.

教学目标

通过学习和研究跨国经营中的典型税务问题, 帮助学生学会把控战略决策和经营管理中的税务风险和税务成本，实现资本和企业的全球化经营最优化目标与法律遵从和社会责任之间的平衡。 By studying and researching typical tax issues in multinational operations, this course helps students learn to manage tax risks and costs in strategic decision-making and business management, achieving the balance between optimization of capital and corporate globalization and the duty of social contribution and legal compliance.

教学方式

理论授课+案例分析+实战

文化革命研究

2026春 · SOCI50039.01 · 王威海

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

文化大革命是史无前例的历史事件，在中国当代社会变迁的过程中产生了无可替代的影响。本课程的目标是让教师和学生一起从学术的角度对这段历史进行科学而理智的探讨，通过对文化大革命的起因、过程、性质，以及关键性的历史人物、历史事件、历史文献的理论分析，探讨在文化大革命中的国民经济、政治体制、文化教育、对外交往、党和人民的“抗争”等各方面的现实及原因，使我们能够对文革有更为科学和清晰的认识，为文革研究探讨出更为体系的理论与方法。

课程简介（英文）

The Cultural Revolution was an unprecedented historical event that exerted an irreplaceable influence on the social transformation of contemporary China. This course aims to enable teachers and students to jointly explore this period of history from an academic perspective, through scientific and rational analysis. By examining the causes, processes, and nature of the Cultural Revolution, as well as key historical figures, events, and documents, we will investigate the realities and underlying reasons of the national economy, political system, cultural education, foreign relations, and the "struggle" of the Party and the people during this era. This will allow us to develop a more scientific and clear understanding of the Cultural Revolution and to establish more systematic theories and methodologies for its study.

荷兰研究

2025秋 · ECON50007.01 · 丁纯

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程第一部分将探讨荷兰在欧洲一体化及当前中欧关系中的角色。其目标是审视自1992年《马斯特里赫特条约》至2009年《里斯本条约》期间荷兰欧盟政策的架构与成效。荷兰一直被视为欧洲超国家一体化最坚定的支持者之一。这一认知在多大程度上符合现实？其支持是否主要源于精英阶层的主导，而公众对此漠不关心？我们该如何解释荷兰自21世纪初以来日益高涨的疑欧情绪？从更宏观的角度来看：移民/难民问题、气候议题以及俄罗斯在乌克兰的军事行动产生了哪些影响？此外，本课程还将从政治和经济双重视角，探讨荷兰与中国关系的最新发展。荷兰企业在华运营及中国企业在荷发展的策略与成果如何？ 第二部分将介绍欧盟外交政策并分析当代中欧关系。课程首先会概述欧洲一体化进程及欧盟外交政策的形成，继而考察中欧关系的经济与政治维度，并探讨其在国际政治大背景下的意义。所有课程环节都将对欧盟外交政策及中欧关系的近期动态（如“一带一路”倡议、中欧投资协定、乌克兰冲突、“去风险”议题等）进行解读与讨论。

课程简介（英文）

The first part of the course will look at the role of the Netherlands in European integration and in current China-EU relations. It aims to scrutinize organization and performance of Dutch EU policy in the period since the Treaty of Maastricht (1992) and the Treaty of Lisbon (2009). The Netherlands has always been considered one of the most dedicated supporters of supranational integration in Europe. To what extent is this true, or was support largely due to elitist leadership and public indifference? How can we explain the rising euro-scepticism in the Netherlands since the early 2000s? And, in a more general sense: what is the impact of the migration/refugee problem, climate issues and the Russian military actions in Ukraine? In addition, the course will address current developments in Dutch relations with China, both from a political and economic perspective. What can be said about the strategies and results of Dutch companies operating in China and Chinese companies in the Netherlands? In a second step, the course introduces European Union foreign policies and analyses contemporary China-EU relations. It starts from a general presentation of European integration and the emergence of EU foreign policies. It will then look at China-EU ties their economic and political dimensions, and discuss their significance in the larger context of international politics. Recent developments in EU foreign policy and China-EU relations (like the Belt & Road Initiative, the China-EU investment agreement, the conflict in Ukraine, the question of de-risking, etc.) will be commented and discussed in all sessions.

教学目标

通过研究探讨荷兰在欧洲一体化和当前中欧关系中的作用、荷兰与中国关系的最新发展、荷兰公司在中国经营与中国企业在荷兰经营的战略和成果以及欧盟外交政策和中欧关系的最新发展（如英国脱欧、一带一路、经济和政治摩擦等），探讨中荷关系、中欧关系，激发学生对中欧研究的学术兴趣。

课程简介（中文）

本课程包括线性代数、空间几何、多元函数微分学、多元函数积分学、级数。

课程简介（英文）

This course includes linear algebra, geometry of space, differential calculus of multivariate functions, integral calculus of multivariate functions, and series.

教学目标

本课程是国际金融学院本科生的核心基础课程，是高等数学课程体系的第一部分。通过对多元函数微积分的学习，学生将掌握后续课程所必需的数学基础。此外，本课程旨在培养学生的抽象思维能力、逻辑推理能力、空间想象能力和科学计算能力，并提升其运用数学方法解决经济与金融问题的能力。 This course is a fundamental core course for undergraduate students at the School of International Finance and constitutes the first part of the higher mathematics curriculum. Through the study of calculus for Multivariate functions, students will acquire the essential mathematical foundations needed for subsequent courses. Additionally, the course aims to develop students' abilities in abstract thinking, logical reasoning, spatial imagination, and scientific computation, as well as their capability to apply mathematical techniques to solve problems in economics and finance.

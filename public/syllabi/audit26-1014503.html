课程简介（中文）

《中医骨伤科学概论》是高等医学院临床医学专业的专业选修课程，主要任务是传授中医骨伤科学的基础理论知识、培养学生对祖国传统文化的热爱、培养唯物主义辩证法思想。本课程内容包括：第一部分：中医骨伤科发展简史；第二部分：损伤的分类、病因病机；第三部分：辨证诊断、临床检查、治疗方法；第四部分：常见中医骨伤科疾病的发病和防治特点。

课程简介（英文）

Introduction to orthopedics and traumatology of traditional Chinese medicine is a professional elective course for clinical medicine majors in higher medical colleges. Its main tasks are to teach basic theoretical knowledge of orthopedics and traumatology of traditional Chinese medicine, cultivate students' love for the traditional culture of the motherland, and cultivate materialist dialectics. The contents of this course include: Part I: a brief history of the development of orthopedics and traumatology of traditional Chinese medicine; Part II: classification, etiology and pathogenesis of injury; The third part: syndrome differentiation diagnosis, clinical examination and treatment; The fourth part: the incidence and prevention characteristics of common orthopedic and traumatology diseases in traditional Chinese medicine.

教学目标

使医学生了解中医骨伤科发展简史以及学习损伤的分类，熟悉损伤的病因病机、辨证诊断、临床检查、治疗方法的基本概念，掌握常见中医骨伤科疾病的发病和防治特点，以丰富医学生的医学知识、拓展视野、改善知识结构，顺应临床对西医、中西医结合专业毕业生的要求，从而提高就业竞争力。

教学方式

教师授课，学生听课，固定教室。

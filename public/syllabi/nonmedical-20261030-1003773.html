课程简介（中文）

1776年《国富论》问世之时，重商主义仍主导着全球经济思想。亚当·斯密在著作中提出了一个极具说服力的观点：自由市场将带来繁荣，而政府干预仅在少数例外情况下必要。当时的英格兰作为全球贸易主力军，正经历着即将引爆工业革命的深刻变革——詹姆斯·瓦特完成蒸汽机重大改良，兰开夏郡工厂（毗邻全球贸易中心利物浦）开始采用珍妮纺纱机，这场即将在未来两百年彻底重塑人类历史并提升福祉的变革已现曙光。 转眼今日，历经四十年经济增长的中国，正站在跨越高收入国家门槛的临界点，并将在未来数年问鼎全球最大经济体。上海已成为世界第一大国际贸易港。经济学早已超越课堂教材的理论框架，它让我们得以解读人类历史，理解日常生活中万物运转的底层逻辑。 本课程立足大一微观经济学基础概念，聚焦现实经济现象与政策分析。我们将通过案例研究、实证观察与学术论文，让理论照进现实：探究稀缺资源配置机制背后的信息与激励结构，剖析承诺缺失、套牢问题、融资约束等市场缺陷的影响，并基于市场失灵理论解析产业政策、政府采购等政府行为。 课程强调学生主动参与和课堂讨论。学期结束时，学生应能熟练运用经济思维分析现实问题，并通过自主设计的专题研究项目展现理论应用能力。

课程简介（英文）

When the Wealth of Nations was published in 1776, mercantilism dominated global economic thought. In his book, Adam Smith presented a compelling argument that free markets would lead to prosperity, with government intervention necessary only in a few exceptions. At that time, England was a major force in global trade and was undergoing significant changes that would eventually lead to the Industrial Revolution. James Watt had made substantial improvements to the steam engine, and the spinning jenny was adopted in Lancashire factories, not far from Liverpool, which was then the global trade center. It was the dawn of a revolution that would profoundly reshape human history and improve welfare over the next two hundred years. Fast forward to today, after 40 years of economic growth, China is on the brink of joining the high-income country club and is set to become the world's largest economy in just a few years. Shanghai hosts the largest international trading port in the world. Economics goes beyond the textbook frameworks we have learned in classes; it enables us to understand human history and the underlying mechanisms shaping everything around us in our daily lives. This course builds on the foundational concepts of introductory microeconomics from your first year study and focuses on applying them to analyze real-world phenomena and policies. We will utilize case studies, anecdotal evidence, and research papers to bring theory to life. We will examine the information and incentive mechanisms that shape the institutions chosen to allocate scarce resources, while exploring the implications of imperfections such as commitment issues, holdup problems, and financial constraints. Additionally, we will analyze government policies, including industrial policies and government procurement practices, through the lens of market failures. The course highlights active student participation and in-class discussions. By the end of the semester, students should feel confident in applying economic thinking and using economics to analyze real-world phenomena. Students are expected to develop their own mini projects to demonstrate their ability to apply these concepts effectively.

教学目标

Course objective and features: Applications of microeconomics Real-world cases and examples In-class discussions Student mini research projects (team-based)

教学方式

This course combines theory with real-world applications through case studies, in-class discussions, and team-based mini-projects. Students will analyze economic policies and market behaviors using microeconomic principles, fostering critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

微观经济学理论

2025秋 · MF50007.01 · 古定威

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程为现代微观经济学理论入门课程，旨在满足经济学、金融学和管理学等研究生专业的学习需求。课程旨在介绍分析消费者行为的方法以及不完全竞争市场模型，揭示影响企业决策的基本因素。此外，课程还将探讨一些实际的商业策略及其市场结果，例如价格歧视、勾结和兼并。授课语言为英语。讲义和考试为英文。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an introduction to modern microeconomic theory， designed to meet the needs of graduate programs in the fields of economics, finance, and management. The course is designed to introduce methodologies for analyzing consumer behavior and models of imperfect market competition, which deliver the basic influencing factors that drive firms’ decisions. Moreover, the course will discuss some practical business strategies and their market outcomes, such as price discrimination, collusion, and merger. Lectures are presented in English. Lecture notes and exams are written in English.

教学目标

On completion of this course, students should be able to: 1）Understand the theory of consumer preferences and behavior 2）Understand firms’ pricing decisions under different forms of imperfect market competition 3）Use economic models to analyze business strategies and their potential market outcomes

教学方式

Lessons

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture Subject 1 Introduction; Preferences; Utility 2 Demand; Perfect Competition 3 Public Policies Evaluation 4 Imperfect Competition I: Monopoly 5 Price Strategies: price discrimination 6 Price Strategies: bundling and two-part tariff 7 Imperfect Competition II: Oligopoly

教学参考资料

[1] R. V. Vohra, 2020, Prices and Quantities - Fundamentals of Microeconomics. [2] Paul Belleflamme and Martin Peitz, 2015, Industrial Organization Markets and Strategies. [3] R. Gibbons, 1992, Game Theory for Applied Economists.

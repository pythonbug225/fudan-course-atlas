课程简介（中文）

学术英语(社科)选取与社会科学有关的材料作为学习内容，教授学生在社会科学领域中进行学术研究和活动所必备的英语技能，培养学生有效地、得体地使用英语进行初步的与社会科学相关的专业知识学习和学术交流的能力以及批判性思考能力（critical thinking），为学生今后使用英语进行专业学习和研究打下良好基础。教学内容包括一定的专业知识、基本的学术交流技能和英文学术写作规范，教学材料涉及经济学、政治学、社会学、和新闻学等学科。

课程简介（英文）

The main objective of the course is to help students learn English in participation; namely, master the language by using the language in academic activities related to social sciences. More specifically the course aims to improve students' ability to use academic English appropriately and effectively by providing practices in academic communication, especially reading and writing empirical research.

教学目标

Teaching Objectives: Academic English (Social Sciences) is a course designed to improve the ability to use academic English appropriately and effectively. Provide practice in seminar skills and academic discussion Improve expertise in academic writing Make the student a quicker and more effective reader Provide opportunities for lecture listening and note-taking practice More specifically Listening & note-taking skills Reading academic texts effectively Critical thinking skills Essay & report writing Discussion & presentation skills

教学方式

教师讲授结合小组活动、个人展示、和贯穿学期的研究项目

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Introduction: Academic English for Social Sciences -What is the course about? -Who is the course aimed for? -What will you do in the course? -Textbook, grading, and course requirements Week 2 -Presentation skills (Signpost language) -Types of scholarly articles -Choosing a feasible research topic -Why does a teacher give a lecture? -Academic Vocabulary: an introduction Week 3 -Being polite in speaking in the academic world -Writing introduction (background) -The structures of academic lectures Week 4 -Comparing formal and informal English -Writing an abstract: How does it differ from “introduction” -Finding relevant literature -What questions does a good listener ask? Week 5 -Paraphrase, summary, and synthesis skills -Writing literature review -How to read a research paper? Week 6 -Citation in social science papers in English -Collocation -Reporting research methods Week 7 -Voices in writing -Critical reading -Collecting data Week 8 -Formal English rules: do’s and don’ts -How to read and comprehend Scientific Research Week 9 -Describing tables and graphs -Reporting and analysing research results -Active academic reading Week 10 -Hedging -Tools for writing in academic English -Discussing research results -Deeply reading an academic text Week 11 -Transition, looping, and other issues with writing in English -Putting together the first draft -Academic reading skills Week 12 -Navigating academia -Writing further drafts -How to use the library Week 13 -Academic Word List -Marker phrases in a lecture -Seminars and tutorials Week 14 -A real case of economics writing -Listening: Cornell note-taking system Week 15 -What makes political science scientific? -Operationalizing a term in research Week 16 -Key features of Academic English Week 17 -Round-up of the course

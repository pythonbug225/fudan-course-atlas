课程简介（英文）

This course will provide an introduction of fundamental concepts, framework, main content of China ’s IP legal system and analytical tools in China ’s IP Law to international students at the graduate level. It will introduce, analyze and explore or expound the Chinese application of the rule of/by law as legal philosophy, with the case-analytical method, and try to reveal the acquisition, (management & exploitation, selective when time available) and protection of intellectual property rights (IPR in China), the implementation (enforcement) of IP System and their impacts upon doing better business in China. The course covers the following subjects: Copyright, Patent, Trade Mark, Trade Secret, and Anti-Unfair Competition as supplementary protection mechanism for IPRs.

教学目标

This course aims to help students systematically master the basic concepts, principles, and legal systems of Chinese intellectual property law with a better understanding of related Chinese legal culture as a part of environment for doing business, understand the core contents of China’s Patent Law, Trademark Law, Copyright Law and related supporting regulations, and grasp the application rules of intellectual property law in practice. It also intends to cultivate students’ ability to analyze and solve practical intellectual property disputes and establish a sense of innovation protection and intellectual property compliance. In addition, students will understand the role of IP laws in building a modern country under the rule of law and the market economy, as well as its status in China’s opening-up strategy. The students will be learning both from the legal texts and from discovering the unspoken rules of cultural-genetic application of laws and regulations in China under the current transition. Doctrine-hermeneutics and case-analysis will be applied in each part of the course.

教学方式

1. teaching in class 2. interactive communication 3. homework after class 4. final exam test/ paper writing and so on

教学内容与进度安排

1-2 Introduction to IP Law in China (including History of IP laws): Definition and scope of IP; Basic characteristics of IP (intangibility, exclusivity, territoriality, timeliness); theoretical basis and system function of IP system; status and role of IP law in China’s legal system Reading related materials. 3-6 Patent Law: the eligibility of patent application, the patent proceedings, grant, rights, the patent infringements, exemptions, and remedies, practice & dispute resolution. Reading related materials and Practicing case analysis. 7-10 Copyright Law: definition and characteristics of copyright; objects of copyright); works excluded from copyright protection; distinction between copyright and neighboring rights; basic knowledge of copyright and its relationship with industrial property; Subject matters, the ascription of copyright and neighboring right,content;term; copyright infringement and its remedies Reading related materials and Practicing case analysis; preparing for presentation 11-14 Trade Mark Law: Definitions and functions of TM; Classification of TM; Conditions and process for trademark registration; content of trademark right (exclusive right to use, right to license, right to transfer, etc); term, renewal, termination of trademark right; restrictions on trademark right (reasonable use, prior use); protection of well-known trademarks and geographical indication trademarks; trademark infringement & legal liability. Reading related materials and Practicing case analysis. Demonstrating Presentation 15-16 Law of Trade Secrets(TS) and Anti-Unfair Competition Law: definitions, functions ,features and rights of TS; conditions for a TS; types of unfair competition acts related to IP (counterfeiting, false propaganda, infringement of trade secrets, etc.); relationship between Anti-Unfair Competition Law and IP laws; The connection between technical standards and IP. Reading related materials and Practicing case analysis.

课程简介（中文）

课程以宇宙的热史为核心。它回顾了宇宙学中的大爆炸标准模型，包括大爆炸核合成、宇宙遗迹中微子以及重子不对称性的产生。我们将介绍暗物质与暗能量的概念，并回顾 ΛCDM 宇宙学模型。课程特别关注以下方面：i）宇宙微波背景辐射的现象学及其理论解释，包括暴胀情景与宇宙反弹情景；ii）通过引力波探测对一级相变的检验，以探索包括暗物质在内的新物理。

课程简介（英文）

The course hinges on the Thermal History of the Universe. It reviews the Big Bang Standard Model of Cosmology, including the Big Bang Nucleosynthesis, the Relic Neutrinos, the Generation of Baryon Asymmetry. We introduce the notions of Dark Matter and Dark Energy, and review the ΛCDM Cosmological Model. We focus in particular on: i) the phenomenology of the Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation and its theoretical explanations, including the Inflationary Scenario and Bounce Cosmology Scenario; ii) the tests of First Order Phase Transitions from the detection of Gravitational Waves, in order to probe new physics, including Dark Matter.

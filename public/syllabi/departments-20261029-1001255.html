课程简介（中文）

战略管理是日常商业活动不可或缺的核心部分。无论企业的规模大小、生产对象、行业或国别，有效的战略对企业都至关重要。在这一课程中，我们将集中学习战略管理的基础概念、理论和思考模式，这些工具可以帮助我们在企业（业务单元）层面和集团层面识别、分析和制定战略决策。 这门课将以全英文教学，请参考以下内容。

课程简介（英文）

Strategic management forms an integral part of everyday business life. It is important for all types of companies, whether they are large or small; or produces goods or services. In this course, we will focus on concepts, theories, and frameworks that help us to identify, analyze, and make strategic decisions at the firm level and the corporate level. This course offers a mixture of lectures and case discussion based on team presentations and individual participations. The lectures are designed to introduce the key theoretical concepts, frameworks, and empirical findings of strategic management. Examples of the topics discussed in the lectures include: Competitive advantage External environment analysis Internal resources and activity analysis Business-level strategy Corporate-level strategy The cases provide a further opportunity to clarify, integrate, and apply the theoretical concepts from the lectures and the book to a range of real-world business cases. Student engagement therefore is an essential component of this course.

教学目标

Through lectures and cases, students are expected to understand the essential strategic management concepts, theories, and frameworks. With these tools, they should be able to analyze and evaluate business strategies in various industries. This course prepares students for business analysis and strategic decision-making in the real world.

教学方式

This course will balance theoretical lectures, case discussion, and team work. Individual participation, especially in the case discussion, is essential to the class. Students are expected to think and contribute actively in class.

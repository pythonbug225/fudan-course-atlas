课程简介（中文）

作为金融专业方向的基础课程，围绕公司（corporation）和金融（Finance）这两个核心概念展开，前者以《公司法》为主线，包括公司的组织形式、市场准入、退出和经营流程为整体框架，辅以各环节的相关案例展开；后者从公司经营的现金流视角出发，针对公司融资、投资、资本结构、资本预算以及风险管理等核心概念，结合相关案例进行阐述，步步为营，逐一推进，帮助同学具备扎实的公司金融基础。

课程简介（英文）

Course Description Corporate Finance （MATH 50050）is the core and fundamental course for any study program of finance. This course consists of two basic concepts, corporation and finance. For the first, it focuses on the entering, operating and exiting the market of a (financial) corporation according to the PRC Company Law. For the second, it consists of a series of traditional topics of financing, investing, and budgeting and allocating, and risk management for a (financial) corporation. With the analysis of several real cases, this course helps students to establish necessary preparation to be financial professionals.

教学目标

以《公司法》为主线，了解公司的组织形式、业务流程及其核心金融环节，包括投融资方法、资本结构与资本杠杆等，以及相应环节的风险构成、计量方法和应对策略。帮助学生了解中国金融体系构成和金融机构的运作原理。 Corporate Finance （MATH 50050）is the core and fundamental course for any study program of finance.

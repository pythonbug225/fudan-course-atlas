课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养学生运用数学知识与方法分析和解决经济学问题的能力，涵盖理论研究与实际应用两个层面。通过系统的学习，学生将掌握将数学工具应用于经济问题的基本技能和思维方式。 课程内容 课程主要包括以下四个部分： 极限 导数及其应用 积分及其应用 常微分方程

课程简介（英文）

The course gives students’ skills of implementation of mathematical knowledge and practice to economic problems both theoretical and applied ones. There will be four parts in the course: 1) Limits; 2) Derivatives with applications; 3) Integrals with applications; 4) Ordinary differential equations.

教学目标

By the end of the autumn semester a student has to: Know about the principal concepts and results of single-variable calculus; Be able to apply calculus to solve problems; Know about the basic concepts and results of ordinary differential equations.

教学方式

Lecture, presentation, group discussion

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: sets, mappings, and functions Week 2: limit of sequences Week 3: limit of functions Week 4: continuity of functions Week 5: derivative of functions I Week 6: derivative of functions II Week 7: differential mean-value theorem Week 8: application of derivatives Week 9: indefinite integral I Week 10: indefinite integral II Week 11: definite integral Week 12: techniques of integration Week 13: application of integrals Week 14: improper integral Week 15: ordinary differential equations I Week 16: ordinary differential equations II

教学参考资料

1. Calculus, by James Stewart. 2. Thomas’ Calculus.

课程简介（中文）

众所周知，全纯映射是唯一的平面共形映射，并且它们的几何性质和解析性质彼此密切相关。然而，由于共形性是一个非常严格的条件，人们不禁要问——我们可以将其放宽到何种程度才能仍然得到有意义的结果？这正是我们在本课程中探讨的问题。我们将详细地展示，即使用角度上的“有界变形条件”（即拟共形性）代替共形性，仍然可以研究给定平面映射的几何性质和解析性质之间的联系。特别是，我们将证明如何运用这些思想来研究给定黎曼曲面上有多少种复结构。由于这些思想在数学内外都有许多应用，本课程将成为这些主题的入门课程。 先修课程要求：复分析、泛函分析以及对曲面的基本了解是必需的。建议了解黎曼曲面，但并非强制要求。

课程简介（英文）

It is well known that holomorphic maps are the only planar conformal maps, and that their geometric and analytic properties are strongly related to one another. However, since conformality is a very rigid condition, one could ask – how much can we relax it and still get meaningful results? It is precisely this question we consider in this course. In detail, we will show that be replacing conformality with a “bounded deformation condition” on the angle (i.e., quasiconformality), one can still study the connection between the geometric and analytic properties of a given planar map. In particular, we will prove how one can use these ideas to study how many complex structures are there on a given Riemann surface. As these ideas have many applications in and outside of mathematics, this course will be an introductory course to these topics. Prerequisites – Complex Analysis, Functional Analysis, and basic acquaintance with surfaces are a must. Knowledge of Riemannn surfaces is recommended, but not strictly mandatory.

教学目标

Basic acquaintance with Quasiconformal mappings and the basics of Teichmüller Theory. 基本了解拟共形映射和Teichmüller理论的基本知识。

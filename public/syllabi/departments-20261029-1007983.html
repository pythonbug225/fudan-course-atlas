离散事件系统

课程简介（中文）

现代科技催生了一种工程系统的新现实：从计算机网络、复杂制造设施到无线通信系统和基于网络的购物/拍卖中心，这些系统涉及对多种事件的监督与协调。这些主要由人为设计的系统不仅需要充当“事件协调者”，还需能够快速响应不可预测的事件、迅速适应变化的条件，并确保用户满意度。这类系统被称为离散事件系统（Discrete Event Systems, DES）。随机逼近（Stochastic Approximation, SA）是DES分析和机器学习中的一种常用方法。本课程旨在介绍该领域的最新进展，并探讨DES与SA的潜在研究方向。

课程简介（英文）

Modern technology has given rise to a new reality in engineering systems: from computer networks and complex manufacturing facilities to wireless communication systems and Web-based shopping/auction centers, these systems involve supervising and coordinating a multiple of events. Not only must these largely human-made systems act as “event coordinators”, but they are also expected to swiftly react to unpredictable events, to rapidly adapt to changing conditions, and to guarantee their users satisfactory. These systems have come to be known as Discrete Event Systems (DES). Stochastic approximation (SA) is a popular method in DES analyzing and machine learning. This course aims to introduce the most recent development and discuss possible research topics in the area of DES and SA.

教学目标

1. To learn basic concepts of DES and SA. 2. To learn applications of DES and SA in artificial intelligence, engineering, and management problems. 3. To learn recently developments and how to do research in this area

课程简介（中文）

本课程是国际政治英文授课博士项目学生的必修课程。本课程旨在为政治学领域的实证研究方法（包括定量与定性研究）提供系统性导引。课程设计旨在帮助学生掌握阅读学术论文及开展独立学术研究所需的知识与技能。

课程简介（英文）

This is a required course for students in the English-Taught Doctoral Program in International Politics. It offers an introduction to methods for empirical research, both quantitative and qualitative, in political science. This course is designed to equip students with knowledge and techniques for reading scholarly articles and conducting their own academic research.

教学目标

This course will be organized around four broad topics: formulating a research question, designing research, introducing different types of methods, and presenting findings. Introducing various methods is treated at greater length than the other topics. My aims are to introduce participants to a distinguished literature that considers and exemplifies these four topics and to engage participants in practical experience through rudimentary exercises. Participants will gain the knowledge and skills to begin to apply relevant methods to produce a high‐quality research product from an original research question.

教学方式

Lecture and discussion

教学内容与进度安排

week 1 Introduction to the Course, Introduction to Political Science Research week 2 What Is a Research Question? What Is Your Research Question? week 3 Causal Inference and Theory week 4 Concepts and Measurement week 5 Data Collection and Management week 6 Mid-term presentation and hypotheses refinement week 7 Case Study Method and Case Selection week 8 Historical Analysis week 9 Interviews, Focus Groups and Site-Intensive Methods week 10 Survey Research I week 11 Survey Research II week 12 Experimental Designs I week 13 Experimental Designs II week 14 Game Theory, Social Network Analysis, Mixed Methods week 15 Presentation of research proposal

1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程定位为《卫生技术评估》领域的 入门级通识选修课 ，结合选修课受众覆盖面广、基础差异较大、侧重实用认知与知识拓展的核心性质，兼顾零基础学习者的接受度与专业知识的严谨性，整体教学内容围绕“夯实基础、贴合实践、紧跟前沿”的核心思路设计，整体划分为两大核心板块，循序渐进搭建完整的课程知识体系，既避免过于晦涩的理论堆砌，又能帮助学生系统掌握学科核心逻辑，了解行业实际应用价值。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | This course is designed as an introductory general elective course in the field of Health Technology Assessment (HTA). Considering the core characteristics of elective courses — a wide audience coverage, diverse academic foundations among learners, and a focus on practical knowledge acquisition and academic expansion — the course strikes a balance between accessibility for beginners with no prior background and the rigor of professional expertise. The overall teaching content is structured around the core philosophy of "consolidating basic knowledge, integrating with practical applications, and keeping pace with cutting-edge developments", and is systematically divided into two core modules. By adopting a step-by-step approach, the course aims to build a complete knowledge system for students, avoiding overly obscure and redundant theoretical content, while helping them systematically grasp the core logic of the discipline and understand its practical application value in the healthcare.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

本课程设定明确的学习目标，要求学生扎实掌握卫生技术评估的核心基本理论，重点理解各章节贯穿的核心评估原则，逐步搭建起系统完整的卫生技术评估知识框架；深刻理解卫生技术评估在医疗卫生领域的核心意义与实践作用，明晰其学科价值；同时全面熟悉该技术在医药、器械、公共卫生等领域的实际应用场景，能够精准把握行业未来发展与变革趋势，真正适配选修课的学习定位与能力培养要求。

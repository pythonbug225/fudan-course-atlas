社会政策

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在向学生系统介绍社会政策的理论与实践，重点关注影响政策制定与实施的社会、经济与政治因素。课程分为三个部分。 第一部分将介绍社会政策的理论与意识形态基础，对社会政策的基本特征——包括核心概念、主要理论争论以及不同的意识形态取向——进行批判性回顾与讨论。第二部分聚焦政策评估与政策制定中的关键问题，旨在帮助学生掌握回答政策相关问题所需的一些基础性量化方法。第三部分将从比较视角考察不同社会情境下社会政策的发展，并通过具体的社会政策研究实例，来说明和深化第二部分中所涉及的核心思想与研究方法。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces students to the theory and practice of social policy, with a concentration on the social, economic, and political factors that influence policymaking and implementation. The course is divided into three sections. In the first part of the course, we will cover the theoretical and ideological foundations of social policy. The basic features of social policy-the key concepts, theoretical debates, and ideological approaches will be critically reviewed and discussed. The second part of the course will address key issues in policy evaluation and policymaking. The goal of this part is to provide students with a basic knowledge of how to perform some quantitative methods useful in answering policy related questions. In the last section of the course, we will examine the development of social policy in different social contexts from a comparative perspective, and examples from real social policy studies will be used to illustrate key ideas and methods covered in Section 2.

教学目标

After taking this course, students are expected to gain not only factual knowledge of social policy but also a critical assessment of current academic literature on social policy studies, based on which some students may develop their own research interests and research ideas.

教学方式

The class will meet once a week. The first three meetings will be lectures on the introduction of social policy studies. In the following two meetings, lectures on specific research methods will be given, with examples from real social policy studies published on decent journals in the field. The remaining five classes will be run in the form of seminars, with students expected to read the assigned materials before coming to class and to participate actively in class discussions.

教学内容与进度安排

Part 1: Foundational Concepts of Social Policy (Week 1-3) 1 Introduction: The Study of Social Policy 2 Social Policy Theories and Ideologies Part 2: Policy Evaluation Methods (Week 4-5) 1 Experiments and Quasi-experiments 2 Counterfactual Framework and Matching 3 Difference-in-difference 4 RD Design 5 Instrumental Variable and Others Part 3: Social Policies in the East and the West (Week 7-11) We will have thematic discussions focused on Chinese and international policy cases of interest to students (cases to be determined).

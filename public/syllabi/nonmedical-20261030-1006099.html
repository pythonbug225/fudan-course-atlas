课程简介（中文）

本课程的基本内容是基于对法律的理解来口头表达与书面翻译法律语言，包括对英美司法程序与判例中所涉主要法律概念和词语进行讨论，以及准确翻译法律文件。

课程简介（英文）

This course mainly covers the oral expression and written translation of legal languages based on the understanding of law. The judicial proceedings of the common law will be introduced first and the main legal concepts and terminologies are to be discussed. Then the course will be centered on accurate translation of legal documents.

教学目标

通过本课程，学生能掌握部分核心英文法律概念与词汇，用英文准确表达对法律的理解，并能准确翻译法律文本和文件。 Through this course, the students are expected to learn some core common law concepts and terminologies and be able to orally express their legal arguments in English, and accurately translate legal documents.

教学方式

学生课前预习，任课老师课堂讲授知识点，学生参与讨论，回答提问，并在课堂上以小组为单位进行案例分析讨论与展示，以及案例的理解、综述与翻译。 Students are expected to prepare before class. After the instructor’s lecture, the students are expected to actively participate in class discussions and Q&As, case-analysis presentations, as well as the comprehension and translation of cases.

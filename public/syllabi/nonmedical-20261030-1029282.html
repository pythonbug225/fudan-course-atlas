课程简介（中文）

课程描述： 本课程融合理论框架与强化训练，旨在提升学术英语应用能力。前半部分重点分析与练习摘要、研究计划、文献评注及评论文章的撰写，确保内容的清晰性、准确性与吸引力，并培养批判性思维技能。后半部分侧重于学术演示：指导学生开展跨学科研究，运用人工智能进行修辞分析，综合多元文献并构建叙述逻辑。课程重点涵盖研究论文写作流程、演示文稿设计与展示技巧。

课程简介（英文）

Course Description:​​ This course integrates theoretical frameworks with intensive practice to advance English for academic purposes proficiency. The first half focuses on analyzing and practicing abstracts, research proposals, annotations, and critique, ensuring clarity, accuracy, and interest, developing critical thinking skills. The second half emphasizes academic presentations: students conduct interdisciplinary research, utilize AI for rhetorical analysis, synthesize sources, and structure narratives. Emphasis is placed on research paper writing procedure and PPT design and presentation skills.

教学目标

Course Objectives:​​ Learn to analyze the IMRAD format and generic moves, linguistic features and communicative purposes for academic communities. Design, revise, and deliver PowerPoint presentations for international academic conferences. Design, draft and revise term papers for publication in international academic journals.

教学方式

Prerequisites:​​ Advanced English proficiency Clear pronunciation/intonation; ability to comprehend English lectures without subtitles Coherent English writing ​Teaching Methodology:​​ Theory Application: Genre-based Move Analysis and Rhetorical Devices Modeled Practice: Analyze sample journal articles Paper Revising: Draft/revise essays with feedback Academic Presentation: Research-based IMRAD talks

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule:​​ Week 1: Orientation: Effective and ethical use of AI in English research paper writing Week 2: Abstract analysis for an international academic conference Week 3: Sample article analysis: IMRAD format and annotation Week 4: Sample article analysis: Introduction Week 5: Sample article analysis: Literature Review and Methods Week 6: Academic Presentations: Effective PPT design Week 7: One-on-one conference Weeks 8-10: Midterm Assessment: 3-minute Thesis + Q&A Week 11: Manuscript Writing: Introduction and methods Week 12: Manuscript Writing: Results, discussion and conclusion Week 13: One-on-One Conferences Weeks 14-16: Term Project Presentations ​Class Preparation & Participation:​​ Pre-Class: Reading Assignments In-Class: Spoken/written Activities Post-Class: Revisions based on feedback

考核构成

  • 课题研讨Topic Discussion 20.0% 课堂讨论和作业
  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 30.0% 3MT和问答
  • 期末考试Final Examination：论文 50.0% 学术报告和期末论文

课程简介（中文）

本课程是代数1的延续。在回顾了群论的一些要素之后，我们将涵盖积分域、分解和不可约性。场，场扩展，伽罗瓦理论的一些元素和同调代数的一些元素。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a continuation of Algebra I. After a review of some elements of group theory, we will cover integral domains, factorization and irreducibility. Fields, field extensions, some elements of Galois theory and some elements of homological algebra.

教学目标

The main objective of this course is to deepen on concepts that were covered in Algebra I such as rings and modules. This course will cover advanced subjects such as aspects of integral domains, fields, field extensions, Galois theory and some linear algebra with a view towards applications to homological algebra. We will prove several important theorems such as Nullstellensatz, the fundamental theorem of Galois theory, and introduce important concepts in advanced linear algebra such as Ext and Tor functors. We will complement it with several enlightening exercises and examples.

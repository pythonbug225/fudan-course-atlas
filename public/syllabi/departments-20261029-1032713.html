课程简介（中文）

内容1（从空间外部性或集聚经济学视角）：本课程阐述《区域与城市经济学》的基本理论（国外更多地称为“城市经济学”），即经济活动的空间分布及其背后的机制。区别于其他课程的一条重要原则是：空间在经济活动分布的作用。涉及的几个重要概念：运输成本、空间垄断力量、竞租曲线、产业集聚等概念可以解释空间在经济活动的作用机理。 本课程与其他课程相联系的是（无空间的思维）：国际贸易、宏观经济、发展经济学等理论中的若干概念与机制可以挪用于“区域经济学。”如比较优势、资源禀赋理论等。 内容2（中国区域经济学的发展模式——基于制度转轨视角、政治经济学视角）：制度转轨、经济发展与中国城镇化、土地制度变革、收入分配不均（劳动力市场分割、城市贫困等话题） 内容3 ：人工智能对劳动力市场的影响。关注人工智能对劳动力市场影响的机理、最近实证研究的切入点，中国在此方面的进展等内容。 内容4: 任务型人力资本的形成与演变。任务型人力资本是传统人力资本理论的深化，本课程将考察全球化、技术进步对任务型人力资本调整的影响。同时，课程将任务视作基本的生产单位，以各种切入点来研究其对任务的影响。

课程简介（英文）

Content 1 (From the Perspective of Spatial Externalities or Agglomeration Economics): This course explains the fundamental theories of *Regional and Urban Economics* (more commonly referred to as "Urban Economics" internationally), focusing on the spatial distribution of economic activities and the underlying mechanisms. A key principle distinguishing this course from others is the role of space in the distribution of economic activities. Important concepts such as transportation costs, spatial monopoly power, bid-rent curves, and industrial agglomeration are introduced to elucidate the mechanisms through which space influences economic activities. This course also connects with other disciplines (non-spatial perspectives): concepts and mechanisms from theories such as international trade, macroeconomics, and development economics can be applied to "Regional Economics." Examples include comparative advantage and resource endowment theory. Content 2 (Development Models of Regional Economics in China—From the Perspective of Institutional Transition and Political Economics): Institutional transition, economic development, and urbanization in China, reforms in land systems, and income inequality (topics such as labor market segmentation and urban poverty). Content 3: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on the Labor Market. This section focuses on the mechanisms through which artificial intelligence affects the labor market, recent empirical research approaches, and China’s progress in this area. Content 4: The Formation and Evolution of Task-Based Human Capital. Task-based human capital represents an extension of traditional human capital theory. This course will examine the impact of globalization and technological advancements on the adjustment of task-based human capital. Additionally, tasks are treated as fundamental units of production, and various approaches are used to study their influence on tasks.

教学目标

结合导师自己的三个研究方向，与博士研究生的研究兴趣融合，确定可操作的、适合自己的研究方向。目前，就我本人的研究方向而言，无论是产业集聚、城市化模式（重点是当前地方债务的风险如何解决）、还是人工智能或其他自动化技术对劳动力市场的影响，都具有较大的研究空间。从方向上来看，随着数据的可得性增强，研究范围的拓展性变得越来越有可能。本课程的教学目标，帮助博士生找到研究方向，激发博士生的研究兴趣。

教学方式

教师主讲内容：约8次，内容为老师过往的研究内容与心得 学生互动：由学生讲解经典范文，并对自身的研究计划、研究进展进行互动交流

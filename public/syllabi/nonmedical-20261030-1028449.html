课程简介（中文）

本课程系外文学院韩国语专业基础课，适合该专业三年级学生修读。培养学生外语思维能力和使用能力，使学生达到能够熟练而正确地运用所学知识进行表达和交流。掌握高级阶段词汇及语法，促进语法知识转换为实际语言能力。主要讲授有代表性的韩国作品。主要涉及韩国社会、文化与生活的各个方面以及人们所关心的社会问题。对作品的思想内容，篇章结构、文体修辞和语言风格进行分析，以提高学生对韩国文学，经济，文化等方面的理解、分析及评述的能力，达到能够熟练而正确地运用所学知识，形象而具体地表达和交流，进一步提高语言运用能力。

课程简介（英文）

This foundational course is designed for third-year Korean majors in the School of Foreign Languages. It focuses on developing students’ ability to think in and use the language, aiming for proficient and accurate expression and communication. The course covers advanced vocabulary and grammar, with an emphasis on transforming grammatical knowledge into practical language skills. The main content includes representative Korean works, exploring various facets of Korean society, culture, life, and current social issues. By analyzing the content, structure, rhetoric, and style of these works, students will improve their comprehension, analytical, and critical commentary skills regarding Korean literature, economics, and culture. The objective is for students to achieve a higher level of language proficiency, enabling them to express themselves vividly and communicate effectively.

Global Environmental Governance

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

随着各国及其人民通过贸易、投资、文化交流等方式建立起前所未有的联系，一系列跨境环境问题也随之出现，对国际社会的可持续发展构成了严峻挑战。这类跨国环境问题包括荒漠化、臭氧层损耗、生物多样性丧失、气候变化等。在过去五十多年间，国际社会从全球到地方层面开展了各种跨国合作以应对这些挑战，其中一些努力取得了丰硕成果，但也有一些收效甚微。 为了在全球化时代更好地管理全球环境，未来的政策制定者、外交官、研究人员和从业者有必要深入了解全球环境治理的历史、现状和前沿领域。本课程重点探讨以下问题： 最具挑战性的全球环境问题有哪些？它们造成了哪些影响？又具有哪些特征？ 从理论和实践层面，我们应如何开展全球环境治理？ 国际社会是如何应对全球气候变化的？ 在全球气候政治的主要参与者中，中国、欧盟和美国之间是如何互动的？ 全球环境治理的新趋势是什么？各项改革举措的优势和不足又体现在哪里？

课程简介（英文）

As countries and their people have been unprecedentedly connected through trade, investment, cultural exchange, etc, a number of trans-boundary environmental problems have also emerged, posing serious challenges to the sustainable development of the international community. Such transnational environmental issues include desertification, ozone depletion, the loss of biodiversity, climate change, etc. In the past five decades, various transnational efforts from the global to the sub-national level have been made to address these challenges. Some of the efforts have proved to be fruitful; but some have not. To better manage the global environment in the globalization era, it is necessary for future policy-makers, diplomats, researchers, and practitioners to develop a good understanding of the history, current status, and the frontier of global environmental governance. The following are some examples of the key questions: What are the most challenging global environmental issues? What problems have they caused? What are their features? How should we approach global environmental governance, both theoretically and practically? How has the international community addressed global climate change? Among the major players in global climate politics, how have China, the EU and the US interacted with each other? What are the new trends in global environmental governance? What are the strengths and weaknesses of the reform initiatives?

教学目标

In this graduate-level course, classes will mainly be lecture-based. We will combine a comprehensive review of global environmental governance and an in-depth case study of the climate change issue. At the same time, we will also have in-class discussions and student presentations. With a focus on China, this course aims at providing some insight into the key issues in global environmental governance, the institutions and processes through which state and non-state actors respond to transnational environmental challenges, and the politics behind their interactions. In the first part (around 3 weeks), we start by introducing the transnational challenges that have affected the biosphere, the underlying causes of these problems, and the efforts that have been made to address them. Then we discuss the concept of and the major theoretical approaches to global environmental governance. In the second part (8-9 weeks), we first explore the climate change issue by discussing the climate strategies of China, the EU, and the US. Then we analyze Sino-EU interactions in global climate politics, and assess the effectiveness of global environmental governance. In the third part (around 3 weeks), we conclude and look into the future by discussing the pros and cons of the current global climate and trade regimes, representative reform initiatives, and China’s stances. The students are strongly encouraged to actively participate in discussions.

教学方式

The Professor will present on the important topic in each lecture first, followed by the presentation of one student based on the recommended literature, after which we will have semi-structured discussions. In the first week we will pass around a sign-up sheet so that students can choose their topics of interest. We hope that the discussions could be dynamic, and that every student could actively participate.

课程简介（中文）

药物不良反应(ADR)是伴随着新药创制与临床治疗而出现的一种必然风险。 本课程结合案例讲授药物不良反应（ADR）的概念、发生机理、监测体系、防治策略，帮助学生掌握常见系统的ADR表现，临床监测，因果关系评估，从而为新药开发、临床药物治疗提供风险评估和决策的科学方法与思路。在课堂讨论中结合具体案例进行ADR研究能力的培训。

课程简介（英文）

Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) is an inevitable risk associated with the process of new drug discovery and development as well as clinical treatment. This course teaches the concept, pathogenesis, monitoring system, and prevention and control strategies of ADR through case studies, helping students master the ADR manifestations in common organ systems, clinical monitoring methods, and causality assessment skills. In doing so, it provides scientific methods and approaches for risk assessment and decision-making in new drug development and clinical pharmacotherapy. During in-class discussions, targeted training on ADR research capabilities is delivered by analyzing specific clinical cases.

教学目标

通过本课程的教学帮助学生掌握药物不良反应（ADR）的基本概念、因果关系评估方法，常见系统的ADR表现，临床监测方法，从而为新药开发、临床药物治疗提供风险评估和决策的科学方法与思路。在课堂讨论中结合具体案例进行ADR研究能力的训练。

课程简介（中文）

课程通过理论讲解、案例分析、课题讨论、线上教学和线下教学相结合等方式，帮助学生更好理解卫生经济学的理论，掌握卫生经济学分析方法和思维模式，并通过案例教学，把理论和实践结合，培养学生规范应用卫生经济学理论，对医药卫生政策和医药卫生问题进行分析。

课程简介（英文）

By combining theoretical instruction, case analysis, group discussions, and both online and offline teaching, this course helps students grasp the theories, analytical methods, and mindset of health economics. Through case-based learning, it bridges theory and practice, equipping students to systematically apply health economics principles to the analysis of healthcare policies and problems

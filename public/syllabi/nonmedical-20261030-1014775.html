课程简介（中文）

本课程为旅游专业学生进行学术训练以及英文语言训练。学术训练的教学素材为学术论文以及学术专著；语言训练的教学素材以视频为主。2周一轮训练，总计6-7轮训练。每一轮训练都要求学生完成预习以及相应的作业。通过此课程的训练，学生将增加信心以应对即将面对的学术任务，同时提升语言能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course serves as a platform towards academic training via English as well as English language training for tourism and hospitality majors. Academic papers as well as chapters from both textbooks and academic books are collected for specific topics. At the same time, visual audio resources are availed for language training once evey two weeks. Students are expected to get familiar with relevant academic topics and groow proficient in English. There are altogether 6-7 rounds of training. For each round, students should be prepared by previews and assignments. Participation in class also matters to final grade.

教学目标

This course aims at both acadmic preparation and language competence. By the end of the course, students would be able to: 1. search for and find academic data for their research 2. read academic papers in English 3. get both intellectually and psychologcially prepared to lauch their MTA thesis 4. carry out full skill English training on their own

教学方式

Altogether six rounds of training will be prepared for students to develop both academic competence and English language skill step by step. Lecture, seminar, regular assignments and teamwork will be made availabe to serve specfic teaching targets.

课程简介（中文）

会计学作为一门古老而充满活力的学科，是经济与管理领域中一个至关重要的学科体系。会计技能在包括投资、银行、审计、税务、咨询等行业中均具有较高的应用价值。对于个人发展而言，掌握坚实的会计基础是开拓未来职业选择的必要条件。 作为会计学科中最基础的课程，本课程将从会计的基本职能和特点出发，介绍会计对于企业管理和经济决策的重要意义；系统介绍重要会计要素、会计科目、会计账户的相关理论；帮助学生掌握复式记账法、会计核算、编制基本会计报表等会计方法，并在此基础上进一步理解和分析财务报表信息。

课程简介（英文）

As the language of business, accounting plays a very important role in managing business organizations. Accounting skills are highly valued in many industries, including finance, banking, auditing, tax preparation, and consulting. Having a solid foundation in accounting can open up a wide range of career opportunities. Therefore, it is very important that you learn accounting by getting a good understanding of its basic concepts, principles and underlying logic as well as by actually doing problems. This course serves as a base on which you can develop your business and especially accounting knowledge. We will start with a discussion of the framework of financial accounting: its nature, intents and purposes, and the context and environment in which it operates. The course unpacks various core financial accounting concepts and further looks into the processes used to record, summarize, and present financial accounting information as well as, crucially, its interpretation.

教学目标

To introduce students to the concepts and principles of accounting. To learn how to record business transactions and prepare basic financial statements. To develop critical thinking skills necessary to analyze financial data and make informed decisions.

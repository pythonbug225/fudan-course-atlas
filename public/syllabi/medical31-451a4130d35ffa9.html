1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 2019年世界卫生组织资料显示，全球十大死亡原因中感染性疾病仍是重要原因，包括下呼吸道感染、腹泻和结核病，分别位于第3、第8和第9位。新冠病毒引发的疫情，让我们感受到科技发达的现代社会，感染性疾病对人类健康和经济发展的影响依然巨大。同时，随着人口老龄化的加深、癌症患者生存期延长，糖尿病、器官移植、皮质激素和免疫抑制剂的应用，导致免疫低下人群的大量积累，加之有创操作和手术的广泛应用，导致条件致病菌甚至非致病菌引起感染的比例显著增加。此外，便捷的旅行也让人类接触野生动物和原始生态环境而获得动物源性或罕见病原体感染的机会也明显增加。 越来越复杂的感染宿主、不断扩大的感染病原谱、持续加剧的微生物耐药问题，以及快速发展的非培养微生物检测技术，对临床医生识别感染性疾病过程的能力和抗菌药物管理的能力均提出了很高的要求。近年来，中山医院感染病科在细菌真菌感染和疑难复杂感染方面积累了大量的临床病例和经验，特别是在病原微生物宏基因二代测序mNGS和感染性疾病的影像学诊断方面确立了学科优势和特色。 本课程旨在通过对中山医院感染病科诊治的临床真实病例的学习，并对各类感染性疾病相关诊治指南及共识、国内外最新研究结果等加以总结讲授，提升医学生对感染性疾病的认识和兴趣，练就医学生正确的诊疗思维，培养集疑难复杂感染诊治、抗菌药物合理应用、医院感染防控等专业知识技能于一身的综合性医疗保健专业人员。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | According to the 2019 World Health Organization data, infectious diseases remain a significant cause among the top ten global mortality factors, including lower respiratory infections, diarrheal diseases, and tuberculosis, ranked 3rd, 8th, and 9th, respectively. The COVID-19 pandemic has demonstrated that even in our technologically advanced modern society, infectious diseases continue to exert a profound impact on human health and economic development. Meanwhile, with the deepening of population aging, extended survival rates of cancer patients, and the widespread use of diabetes treatments, organ transplants, corticosteroids, and immunosuppressants, the immunocompromised population has grown substantially. Additionally, the increasing application of invasive procedures and surgeries has led to a significant rise in infections caused by opportunistic pathogens or even non-pathogenic microorganisms. Furthermore, convenient travel has increased human exposure to wildlife and primitive ecological environments, elevating the risk of zoonotic or rare pathogen infections. The increasingly complex host factors, expanding spectrum of infectious pathogens, worsening antimicrobial resistance, and the rapid development of non-culture-based microbial detection technologies all place high demands on clinicians' ability to diagnose infectious diseases and manage antimicrobial therapy. In recent years, the Department of Infectious Diseases at Zhongshan Hospital has accumulated extensive clinical experience in bacterial, fungal, and complicated infections, establishing distinctive strengths and expertise in areas such as metagenomic next-generation sequencing (mNGS) for pathogen detection and imaging diagnosis of infectious diseases. This course aims to enhance medical students' understanding and interest in infectious diseases by studying real clinical cases from the Department of Infectious Diseases at Zhongshan Hospital. It also summarizes and teaches relevant guidelines, consensus documents, and the latest domestic and international research findings on infectious disease diagnosis and treatment. The goal is to cultivate correct clinical reasoning skills in medical students and train comprehensive healthcare professionals equipped with expertise in managing complex infections, rational antimicrobial use, and hospital infection prevention and control.

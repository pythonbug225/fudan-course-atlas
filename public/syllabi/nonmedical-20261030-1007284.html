课程简介（中文）

课程基本内容简介： 1）微分流形基本概念、例子； 2）切丛、余切丛、向量丛； 3）流形间的映照，切映照，秩定理及子流形的概念； 4）向量场、Frobenius 定理； 5）多重线性代数、张量场 6）微分形式、外微分、流形上的Stokes 定理。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an introduction to the fundamentals of smooth manifold theory and covers the following topics: 1) Basic concepts and examples of differential manifolds; 2) Tangent bundles, cotangent bundles, vector bundles; 3) Maps between smooth manifolds, the differential of a smooth map, the rank of a smooth map, submanifolds; 4) Vector fields, distributions, Frobenius theorem; 5) Multilinear algebra, tensors and tensor fields 6) Differential forms, exterior differential operator, Stokes’s Theorem

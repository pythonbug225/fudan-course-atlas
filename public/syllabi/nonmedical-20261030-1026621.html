微电子封装材料及工艺

2026春 · ICSE30013.02 · 刘盼

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程从典型封装中的材料、技术和工艺出发，引申到先进封装技术。重点介绍晶圆级封装技术、三维封装、系统级封装等涉及到的材料、技术和工艺。针对封装中常见的可靠性问题，介绍分析方法及产生的原因。最后，介绍新兴领域如射频、光电、功率等封装中涉及的新材料、新工艺和新技术。

课程简介（英文）

This course starts from traditional materials, technologies and processes for microelectronics packaging, followed by advanced packaging techniques. We focus on challenges on wafer level packaging, 3D packaging, system integration. Reliability will also be addressed for basis analysis methods. Finally, emerging fields of microelectronics packaging will be introduced such as RF, optoelectronics, power electronics, etc.

教学目标

1. 介绍传统及新兴领域（如射频、光电、功率等）中，微电子封装涉及的材料、工艺、技术。Introduction for traditional and emerging fields for microelectronics packaging, such as RF, optoelectronics, power electronics, etc. It also concludes materials, processes, and technologies for microelectronic packaging. 2. 掌握微电子封装技术的最新发展趋势 Follow the up-to-date development trends for microelectronics packaging. 3. 了解典型封装技术、先进封装技术，封装工艺流程及可靠性测试方法，为从事器件集成、系统封装等打下基础。Understand typical packaging technologies, advanced packaging technologies, packaging processes, and reliability testing, as a basis for future study on electronics system packaging and integration.

教学方式

讲授为主，工艺部分有相关视频辅助理解，学生报告和讨论为辅。The course is delivered mainly through lectures, with supplementary videos for the process segments and student presentations and discussions as secondary components.

教学内容与进度安排

第一周：微电子封装概论 课程要点：微电子封装定义，分类层级，发展趋势。 学习成效要求：掌握相关专有名词，明确电子封装分类层级，目前微电子封装发展的主要趋势。 Week 1: Introduction to Microelectronic Packaging Key points: definition of microelectronic packaging, hierarchy and classification, development trends. Learning outcomes: master the specialized terminology, identify the levels of electronic packaging, and describe the main trends in current microelectronic packaging. 第二周：传统封装工艺：晶圆切割、贴片、键合、塑封。 课程要点：晶圆切割、贴片、键合、塑封涉及到的具体材料及工艺要求、挑战。 学习成效要求：掌握激光切割vs机械划片的对比，芯片贴装常见的几类材料，芯片键合所需的材料和常用键合方法，传递模塑和压缩模塑的对比。 Week 2: Conventional Packaging Processes – Wafer Dicing, Die Attach, Wire Bonding, Molding Key points: specific materials, process requirements and challenges in dicing, attach, bonding and encapsulation. Learning outcomes: compare laser dicing vs. mechanical sawing; list common die-attach materials; identify materials and typical methods for wire bonding; contrast transfer vs. compression molding. 第三周：传统封装技术：金属、陶瓷和塑料封装。 课程要点：微电子封装在集成电路制造产业链中的位置，金属、陶瓷和塑料封装的优缺点，几类常见的气密封装。 学习成效要求：掌握金属、陶瓷和塑料封装的工艺流程，以及涉及到的常用材料。 Week 3: Conventional Packaging Technologies – Metal, Ceramic and Plastic Packages Key points: place of packaging in the IC manufacturing chain, advantages & drawbacks of metal, ceramic and plastic packages, common hermetic sealing approaches. Learning outcomes: describe the process flows and key materials used for metal, ceramic and plastic packages. 第四周：先进封装技术：倒装芯片 课程要点：倒装芯片技术，常见倒装工艺，倒装工艺的发展，倒装互连的等效电路计算。 学习成效要求：掌握倒装芯片技术的定义，常见倒装工艺所需材料及工艺流程，面临的失效挑战及原因；理解倒装互连的等效电路计算公式。 Week 4: Advanced Packaging – Flip-Chip Technology Key points: flip-chip concept, typical processes, technology evolution, equivalent-circuit calculation for flip-chip interconnects. Learning outcomes: define flip-chip technology; list required materials and process steps; explain major failure challenges and their root causes; perform basic equivalent-circuit calculations. 第五周：先进封装技术：晶圆级扇入型封装 课程要点：倒装芯片封装中RDL工艺的优势，晶圆级封装的优点，扇入封装与扇出封装的区别。 学习成效要求：掌握晶圆级封装与传统封装的工艺流程区别，晶圆级封装的优点，扇入封装常用的互连方案及涉及到的工艺和材料。 Week 5: Advanced Packaging – Fan-In Wafer-Level Packaging (WLP) Key points: benefits of RDL in flip-chip, advantages of WLP, distinction between fan-in and fan-out. Learning outcomes: contrast WLP and conventional process flows; state WLP advantages; describe common fan-in interconnect schemes and their associated processes/materials. 第六周：先进封装技术：晶圆级扇出型封装 课程要点：扇出封装的主要挑战，扇出封装的工艺流程，晶圆级封装的后道工艺挑战及可靠性。 学习成效要求：掌握扇出封装的优势及工艺流程，了解晶圆级封装的后道工艺挑战，了解相关可靠性失效，用前面所学的相关知识进行失效机理分析。 Week 6: Advanced Packaging – Fan-Out Wafer-Level Packaging Key points: key challenges of fan-out, process flow, back-end-of-line issues and reliability. Learning outcomes: explain fan-out benefits and process sequence; identify back-end challenges; recognize relevant reliability failures and analyze failure mechanisms using prior knowledge. 第七周：先进封装技术：先进材料和工艺 课程要点：晶圆级封装涉及的先进材料和传统封装有哪些不同，及相关工艺挑战。 学习成效要求：由于工艺改变带来的材料高性能需求，对于晶圆级封装的贴片、互连、塑封等工艺的影响。 Week 7: Advanced Materials & Processes for Wafer-Level Packaging Key points: differences between WLP and conventional materials, associated process challenges. Learning outcomes: explain how higher material performance—driven by process changes—affects die attach, interconnection and molding in WLP. 第八周：学生汇报：传统及先进封装中由于材料和工艺引发的可靠性问题讨论 课程要点：针对前期晶圆级封装及传统封装的相关材料和工艺，展开可靠性问题讨论。 学习成效要求：从微电子封装与材料的角度出发进行分析相关封装挑战，并进行文献调研，理解材料和工艺对于微电子可靠性的重要影响。 Week 8: Student Seminar – Reliability Issues Induced by Materials & Processes in Conventional & Advanced Packages Key points: reliability discussion based on materials and processes covered earlier. Learning outcomes: analyze packaging challenges from a materials viewpoint; conduct literature survey; understand the impact of materials/processes on microelectronic reliability. 第九周：封装可靠性测试方法 课程要点：可靠性定义，失效率定义，浴盆曲线，失效机理和失效模式的相关性，数理统计对于可靠性研究的重要性，加速因子对于电子封装可靠性的重要性。 学习成效要求：掌握可靠性与失效率的定义公式，理解为什么威布尔及拉格朗日分布在可靠性统计中常用，为什么通常文献中采用对数坐标来展现失效率变化，加速因子对于可靠性对比统计图的影响。 Week 9: Reliability Test Methods for Packages Key points: reliability & failure-rate definitions, bathtub curve, linkage between failure mechanisms & modes, role of statistics, importance of acceleration factors. Learning outcomes: write definitions/formulas for reliability and failure rate; explain why Weibull & log-normal distributions are common; justify log-scale plots of failure rate; describe how acceleration factors influence reliability comparison charts. 第十周：封装可靠性失效分析 课程要点：失效分析主要包括哪三个阶段，主要采用的实验方法（DSC-TGA、TMA、SEM-EDS、FIB、TEM、TOF-SIMS、X-ray、C-SAM） 学习成效要求：掌握常见失效分子中实验表征手段的基本原理。 Week 10: Failure Analysis of Packaged Devices Key points: three stages of failure analysis, major analytical techniques (DSC-TGA, TMA, SEM-EDS, FIB, TEM, TOF-SIMS, X-ray, C-SAM). Learning outcomes: state the basic principles of common failure-analysis characterization tools. 第十一周：光电封装技术 课程要点：LED传统封装及晶圆级封装的阐述。 学习成效要求：从功率密度角度对比LED传统封装及晶圆级封装，从工艺和材料角度分析晶圆级LED封装面临的挑战。 Week 11: Optoelectronic Packaging Key points: LED traditional vs. wafer-level packaging approaches. Learning outcomes: compare LED packages in terms of power density; analyze WLP LED challenges from materials and process perspectives. 第十二周：MEMS封装技术 课程要点：什么是MEMS器件，MEMS封装技术要求，MEMS常见应用领域及挑战。 学习成效要求：掌握MEMS器件的标准工艺，理解MEMS封装的难点，掌握目前MEMS封装的主流解决方案和应用场景。 Week 12: MEMS Packaging Technology Key points: what MEMS devices are, packaging requirements, application fields and challenges. Learning outcomes: outline standard MEMS fabrication flows; explain packaging difficulties; summarize mainstream MEMS packaging solutions and use cases. 第十三周：射频封装技术 课程要点：什么是射频器件，射频封装技术要求，通过案例分析，阐述射频封装常见应用领域及挑战。 学习成效要求：理解射频封装的发展趋势及难点，掌握目前射频封装的主流解决方案和应用场景。 Week 13: RF Packaging Technology Key points: definition of RF devices, RF packaging requirements, case-study review of applications and challenges. Learning outcomes: describe RF packaging trends and bottlenecks; identify current mainstream solutions and application scenarios. 第十四周：功率封装技术 课程要点：什么是功率器件，功率器件封装技术要求，通过案例分析，阐述功率器件封装常见应用领域及挑战。 学习成效要求：理解多物理场仿真计算对于功率封装的重要性。 Week 14: Power Device Packaging Technology Key points: definition of power devices, packaging requirements, case-study review of applications and challenges. Learning outcomes: appreciate the importance of multi-physics simulation for power packages. 第十五周：高温封装材料 课程要点：高温封装对于芯片的互连、塑封、贴装、基板等提出了更高的材料性能要求，目前高温封装的主要定义、常见材料性能对比。 学习成效要求：理解高温封装重要性，对于烧结贴装材料掌握其和焊接技术的原理区别、性能优势及可靠性优势。 Week 15: High-Temperature Packaging Materials Key points: higher material-performance demands on interconnect, molding, attach and substrate; current definitions and property comparisons for high-temperature materials. Learning outcomes: understand the significance of high-temperature packaging; contrast sintered attach materials with solder technology in terms of principle, performance and reliability advantages. 第十六周：微电子封装材料及工艺发展趋势 课程要点：微电子封装材料及工艺的发展趋势 学习成效要求：了解发布发展趋势分析的相关报告和组织，从传统封装到晶圆级封装对于材料和工艺带来的挑战，及进一步的更高功率密度、高工作温度、高性能需求的封装给材料和工艺提出的新需求。 Week 16: Trends in Microelectronic Packaging Materials & Processes Key points: future directions for packaging materials and processes. Learning outcomes: identify key reports and organizations forecasting trends; summarize challenges posed by the transition from conventional to warol-level packaging and by emerging demands for higher power density, operating temperature and performance. 第十七周：自学 Week 17: Self-Study 第十八周：上交结课论文 Week 18: Final term paper

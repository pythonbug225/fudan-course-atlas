金融中的机器学习和人工智能

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course is designed to provide students with an in-depth understanding of how machine learning and AI are revolutionizing the finance sector. Through lectures, case studies, and hands-on projects, students will learn to apply these technologies to enhance financial decision-making and strategy.

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to provide students with an in-depth understanding of how machine learning and AI are revolutionizing the finance sector. Through lectures, case studies, and hands-on projects, students will learn to apply these technologies to enhance financial decision-making and strategy.

教学目标

Understand the fundamentals of machine learning and artificial intelligence and their applications in finance. Learn how to leverage AI and machine learning models for financial analysis, prediction, and decision-making. Explore the ethical considerations and challenges of implementing AI in finance. Develop practical skills through case studies and projects involving real-world financial data.

教学方式

Lectures: Interactive lectures will introduce key concepts of ML and AI, specifically tailored to financial applications. Case Studies: Real-world examples will illustrate how established financial institutions leverage ML and AI for decision-making. Software Demonstrations: Hands-on demonstrations will showcase how ML algorithms are used for financial data analysis. Group Projects: Students will work collaboratively on mini-projects applying ML techniques to simulated financial scenarios. Class Discussions: Active participation in class discussions will foster critical thinking and debate on the ethical and practical implications of AI in finance.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Introduction to Machine Learning and AI in Finance Session 1 Overview of Machine Learning and AI Basics Contents Introduction to machine learning algorithms and AI concepts relevant to finance. Case Analysis of a company's transition to AI-driven financial decision-making. Reading AIF Chapter 1, 2, 3 Session 2 Data Handling and Preprocessing Contents Techniques for collecting, cleaning, and preparing financial data for analysis. Case Real-world example of data preprocessing in financial datasets. Reading AIF Chapter 4, 6 Week 2 Machine Learning Models in Finance Session 3 Supervised Learning for Financial Prediction Contents Application of regression and classification models in finance. Case Predicting stock prices using supervised learning models. Reading MLF Chapter 1, 2 Session 4 Unsupervised Learning and Anomaly Detection Contents Using clustering and anomaly detection techniques for fraud detection and portfolio management. Case Identifying fraudulent transactions using unsupervised learning. Reading MLF Chapter 6, 7. Week 3 Generative AI in Financial Strategy and Ethical Considerations Session 5 Large Language Model (LMM) and Applications in Finance Contents Exploration of AI in robo-advising, algorithmic trading, and personalized banking services. Case TBD Reading Industry reports on AIGC-driven services. Session 6 Ethical Considerations and Future of AI in Finance Contents Discussion on the ethical, legal, and social implications of AI in finance. Future trends and challenges. Case Ethical dilemma in the deployment of an AI system for credit scoring. Reading Ethical guidelines for AIGC in finance.

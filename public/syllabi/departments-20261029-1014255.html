风险管理

2025秋 · MF60035.01 · Valentin Haddad

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

Financial price volatility - such as volatility in exchange rates, volatility in prices of energy, and volatility in interest rates - can greatly affect fortunes of even well-run companies. For example, the value of the dollar has great impact in the profitability of Chinese companies exporting to the United States. Fortunately, a number of carefully designed hedging strategies using financial derivatives can help manage risk associated with financial price risk. Effective hedging strategies, however, require not only a sophisticated knowledge of implementation, but they also require a careful understanding of internal control and systems so that the use of derivatives is not abused. Recent turmoil in the financial markets in both the United States and China underscore this tension vividly. In this course we will study best practices in financial risk management. This class will be of interest to traders, financial institutions such commercial and investment banks, those involved in the treasury operations of corporations, hedge funds and those involved in investment management for high net-worth individuals. In addition, it may be useful to managers in information technology and consulting firms involved in designing support systems for the financial services industry. Risk management is also an effective tool employed by hedge funds who can realize superior returns for their investors using high leverage and by focusing on few key variables to enhance returns, and hedging the risks associated with other factors affecting return performance. The high leverage strategy is extremely risky and requires careful implementation of operational risk-management systems in place. This course will cover these topics rigorously showing both the promise of a well-designed risk management practice as well as the perils associated with poorly conceived strategies.

教学目标

1）Students will understand the appropriate role of risk management in both financial and non-financial firms and will be able to quantitatively assess a firm’s risk exposure. 2）Students will develop the technical expertise to evaluate and price risk management financial instruments and will be able to execute risk management strategies. 3）Students will recognize potential abuses and failures of risk management that may arise in practice. 4) Students will acquire the skills to understand the limits of risk management modelling, the role that these limits played in the 2008 and COVID-19 crises, and the potential role they may play in future crises.

教学方式

1）In-class participation 2）Case analyses 3）Final exam

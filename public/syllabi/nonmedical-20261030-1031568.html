中国医学史

2026春 · HIST70002.01 · 刘小朦

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

中国医学史课程以专题形式讲授中国历史上的医学、疾病与健康相关的论题，不仅关注医学的历史发展过程，同时也强调医学与历史上经济、社会、思想、文化的互动关系。一方面，中国医学史涵盖了历史上多种多样的知识和实践形式，这就要求我们以动态与多元的眼光来看待医学发展的历程；另一方面，医学也是形塑中国历史与文化不可或缺的部分，了解中国医学史可更深入理解中国历史中的知识体系、社会状况与文化脉络。 课程具体形式和要求如下： 1. 本课程包含讲授与讨论两部分，每次课程前90分钟由任课教师讲授中国医学史上的特定主题，后45分钟由师生一起根据每节课的阅读材料进行研读讨论。 2. 学生需在每次课程前完成阅读相应的阅读材料，准备好参与课堂讨论。每次课程的阅读材料共3篇，前2篇为必读，后1篇为选读。 3. 学生在期中（4月13日）、期末（6月8日）分别做一次报告。 4. 期末提交课程论文，研究论文与研究综述皆可。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces major themes and key sources in the history of medicine, disease, and health in China. We will explore the history of Chinese medicine in two ways. First, what we call “Chinese medicine” today incorporates diverse and heterogenous systems of knowledge and practice in history. This invites us to think the formation of “Chinese medicine” as a historical process. Second, medicine is indispensable to understand the broader trend in Chinese history and culture, and vice versa. Each class includes two sessions, the first session, about 90 min, will be a lecture about a specific theme in Chinese medical history; the second session, about 45 min, will be discussions based on the topic of assigned readings. Course Requirements： 1. The class meets once a week for lecture and discussions. Students should attend all classes, complete required readings before each class, and actively participate in discussions. 2. I will assign three articles/book chapters for each class. The first two are mandatory, the last one is optional. 3. Students will give two presentation respectively on April 13 and Jun 8. Students are welcome to present any topic that related to the main theme discussed in this course. 4. Complete a final essay. The final essay can be (1) a critical review of existing scholarships in an aspect of Chinese medical history; (2) an original research article using both primary and secondary resources. Final paper will be due on Jun. 15, 2022. Late submissions will not be accepted.

教学目标

1. 了解中国医学史的基本知识和前沿问题。Students will learn major themes, key sources, and up-to-date scholarships in the history of Chinese medicine. 2. 将中国医学放到中国社会、历史与文化中去理解。Students will learn to understand Chinese medicine within the context of Chinese history, society, and culture. 3. 能够针对中国医学史的某一主题进行前人研究的评述、并运用原始资料进行原创性的研究。Students will develop the ability to use primary and secondary source in the history of medicine critically in their own work.

教学方式

Class in each week will start with a lecture given by the instructor, which will last for around 90 min. Then a discussion session will follow for around 30 min.

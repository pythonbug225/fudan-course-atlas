课程简介（中文）

本课程将涵盖微分几何与拓扑学的基本概念及其在物理学中的应用。微分形式是理解麦克斯韦电磁理论的重要工具，而黎曼几何在爱因斯坦广义相对论中起着关键作用。诸如阿哈罗诺夫-玻姆效应、狄拉克磁单极子、贝里相位、量子霍尔效应、瞬子及反常等非微扰量子效应，均与向量丛及示性类密切相关。本课程将引导您掌握微分几何与拓扑学的基础知识，并带领大家从几何与拓扑的视角探索物理学的多个领域。课程全程采用英文授课。

课程简介（英文）

In this course, we will cover the fundamental concepts of differential geometry and topology and their applications in physics. Differential forms are an essential tool in understanding Maxwell's electro-magnetic theory, and Riemannian geometry plays a crucial role in Einstein's general relativity. Non-perturbative quantum effects such as the Aharonov-Bohm effect, Dirac monopoles, Berry phase, quantum Hall effects, instantons, and anomalies are all connected to vector bundles and characteristic classes. This course will introduce you to the basics of differential geometry and topology, and we will also explore various aspects of physics from the perspective of geometry and topology. The course will be conducted in English.

教学目标

We will learn the following subjects. 1.    differential manifolds, Riemannian manifold, complex manifolds 2.    theory of differential forms, harmonic forms 3.    vector bundles, connections, curvature, characteristic classes 4.    homology, cohomology, fundamental groups, homotopy groups 5.     relations to physics such as electromagnetism, general relativity, quantum physics,  condensed matter physics

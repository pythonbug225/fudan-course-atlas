课程简介（中文）

复旦大学研究生课程教学大纲 Fudan University Teaching Syllabus for EMA Course “Enterprise Internationalization: Theory and Practice” 1．课程概要/Course Summary 课程名称（中文） Course Title (Chinese) 企业国际化理论与实践 课程名称（英文） Course Title (English) Enterprise Internationalization: Theory and Practice 开课院系 School/Department Department of World Economy, SOE 开课学期 semester 2025-2026学年第一学期  Autumn 2025 课程代码 Course Code 任课教师Instructor(s) 程大中/ Dazhong Cheng 授课语言 Teaching Language English 适用学科专业 Discipline/Specialization International Business for EMA 学分数 Course Credit(s) 2 教学周数 Weeks 18 weeks 总学时 Teaching Hours in Total 46 hours 实验/实践学时 Hours for Experiments /Practice 8 hours 预修课程要求 Pre-requisite Course(s) Microeconomics, international trade, elementary econometrics 课程简介 Course Introduction This course consists of three sections that integrate basic theories, methodologies, and case studies of enterprise internationalization. In the first section, we analyze the background, facts, and general trend concerning the internationalization of enterprises. The second section is mainly on theories and methodologies, ranging from the theories on boundaries and governance of international firms (including Coase theory, contract theory, transaction cost theory, and principal-agent theory) to the theories on firms’ trade, international investment, and participation in global value chains (GVCs). The final section discusses the practices and experiences of firms in the process of internationalization, and the implications of internationalization for the firm itself and for the whole economy. 2. 教学目标/Course Objective This course aims to equip students with the basic theories and methods regarding the internationalization of enterprises, especially in the era of economic globalization and value chain division of labor as well as in the process of a country’s opening to the outside world. This course will be closely combined with China’s reality so that students can understand the background, process and impact of the internationalization of Chinese enterprises, and deepen their understanding of China's reform and opening up. 3．教学内容及进度安排/Course Content & Schedule 课次 No. 教学周 Week 教学内容及预期效果 Content & Expected Achievement 作业/实验/实践 Assignment 1 1 Background, facts and trends Pay attention to the basic international background and stylized facts of enterprise internationalization; understand the evolution of enterprise internationalization theory. Design the curriculum. Read the relevant literature 2 2 The boundaries of international enterprises: Starting from Coase Theory Understand Coase theory, theory of transaction cost, and the importance of these theories to analyzing the internationalization of enterprises Read the relevant literature; case studies 3 3 The governance of international enterprises: Based on theories of incentive, contract and property right Understand principal-agent theory, contract theory, property right theory, and the importance of these theories to analyzing the internationalization of enterprises Read the relevant literature; case studies 4 4 Firm-level international trade Understand firms’ behavior in the course of internationalization, such as how to make decisions on product mix, market, quality upgrading, R&D, resource reallocation, financial constraints, networks, and so on Read the relevant literature,  case studies 5 5 Firm-level international investment Understand firms’ outward foreign direct investment, and M&A. Read the relevant literature, case studies 6 6 Firms’ participation in global value chains Understand firms’ vertical and horizontal integrations, outsourcing, contracting; multinational corporations: parent and subsidiary firms; upstreamness and downstreamness, and so on. Read relevant literature, case studies, software programming and computation 7 7 Measurement of firm-level economic performances Understand firm-level financial performance, single factor productivity, TFP. Learn to compute firm-level TFP through software programming Read relevant literature, case studies, software programming and computation 8 8 Enterprise internationalization: Process, modes and portfolio Understand the different modes and processes of internationalization, as well as the mechanisms Read relevant literature, case studies 9 9 The relationship between enterprise internationalization and economic performances Understand the self-selection effect, premium effect, learning effect, and resource reallocation effect. Read relevant literature, case studies 10 10 Institutional and policy environment, macroeconomic factors, and enterprise internationalization Understand the institutional and policy environment, macroeconomic factors, international environment that affect the internationalization of enterprises Read relevant literature, case studies 11-- 11-- Students’ presentations (Case study) Course summary, Q&A, and test 4. 课程考核及成绩评定/Course Assessment & Grading 考核指标* Assessment Criteria 权重 Percentage 评定标准 Assessment Standard 出勤 Attendance 10% 课堂表现 Participation Include in the above 作业/实验 Assignment 15% Presentation 课程论文 Course Paper 30% Case study 期末考试 Term Exam 45% Written exam 其他 Other(s) * 各项考核指标可自由设置，总权重为100%。 5. 教材/Textbook(s) （如使用自编讲义，请在“名称”列中备注说明） 序号 No. 名称 Title 作者 Author(s) 标准书号 ISBN 出版机构 Publisher 出版日期 Publication Date 是否必读 Mandatory or Elective 1 Slides for this course (https://elearning.fudan.edu.cn/courses) Dazhong Cheng NA NA update Mandatory 6. 教学参考资料/Reading Materials and References 序号 No. 名称 Title 作者 Author(s) 标准号码 ISBN/DIO 出版机构 Publisher 出版日期 Publication Date 是否必读 Mandatory or Elective 1 Global Firms Andrew Bernard, Bradford Jensen, Stephen Redding, and Peter Schott Paper Journal of Economic Literature, 56 (2), pp. 565-619 2018 Mandatory 2 Global Production: Firms, Contracts, and Trade Structure Pol Antràs 0-691-16827-X Princeton University Press 2015 Mandatory 3 The Impact of Trade on Intra-Industry Reallocations and Aggregate Industry Productivity Marc Melitz Paper Econometrica, 71 (6), pp. 1695-1725 2003 Elective 4 The Coase Theorem at Sixty Steven G. The final section discusses the practices and experiences of firms in the process of internationalization, and the implications of internationalization for the firm itself and for the whole economy. 2. 教学目标/Course Objective This course aims to equip students with the basic theories and methods regarding the internationalization of enterprises, especially in the era of economic globalization and value chain division of labor as well as in the process of a country’s opening to the outside world. This course will be closely combined with China’s reality so that students can understand the background, process and impact of the internationalization of Chinese enterprises, and deepen their understanding of China's reform and opening up. 3．教学内容及进度安排/Course Content & Schedule 课次 No. 教学周 Week 教学内容及预期效果 Content & Expected Achievement 作业/实验/实践 Assignment 1 1 Background, facts and trends Pay attention to the basic international background and stylized facts of enterprise internationalization; understand the evolution of enterprise internationalization theory.

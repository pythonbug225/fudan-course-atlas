课程简介（中文）

专题讲授新时代中国特色社会主义理论和实践的重大问题，帮助学生进一步掌握中国特色社会主义理论体系，深化对习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想的认识，坚定对马克思主义的信仰、对中国特色社会主义的信念、对实现中华民族伟大复兴中国梦的信心

课程简介（英文）

This course centers on the theoretical exploration of adapting Marxism to the Chinese context and the needs of the times, with particular emphasis on its necessity and scientific connotations.  The course is designed to help students develop a theoretical understanding that the full advancement of the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation through Chinese modernization can only be achieved under the leadership of the Communist Party of China, with Marxism as the guiding ideology. It is essential to integrate the basic tenets of Marxism with China’s specific realities and with its fine traditional culture, remain firm in following the socialist path, and stay committed to building and developing socialism with Chinese characteristics.

课程简介（中文）

MTI口译研究方向硕士二年级学生学位专业选修课程之一。本课程定位为兼顾视译、交替传译等高年级口译课程模块的平行辅助配套专题练习课，每周两课时，供学生集中练习巩固所学针对各类商务会议会展环境下的口译技能和策略。课堂组织形式为学生练习讨论为主、教师讲解分析为辅。具体练习内容主要包括各类商务场景交际主题认知、视译及交传策略运用、语篇逻辑梳理、双语概述表达等板块，通过词汇讲解、长句示例分析、篇章小组项目等多种形式巩固所学。

课程简介（英文）

Course Title: Business Interpreting Practice (English-Chinese/Chinese-English) (One of the elective degree courses for second-year MTI students specializing in Interpreting Studies) Course Description: Positioned as a supplementary practical module complementing advanced interpreting courses such as Sight Translation and Consecutive Interpreting, this two-hour weekly course provides focused training for students to consolidate interpreting skills and strategies specific to diverse commercial conference and exhibition settings. The classroom format prioritizes student-driven practice and discussion, with instructor-led analysis serving in a supportive role. Core practice components include: topic familiarization for various business communication scenarios, application of sight translation and consecutive interpreting strategies, discourse logic structuring, and bilingual summarization. Learning is reinforced through vocabulary drills, complex sentence analysis, and collaborative group projects on extended texts.

教学目标

本课程旨在通过特设商务主题的集中学习讨论，有效提高学生商务语境把握、逻辑判断、分析重构以及双语地道表达能力，同时通过补充所涉领域相关背景信息，系统拓展学生知识面，激发商务相关学科学习兴趣，增强公开演讲自信心，在实际工作环境中磨砺译技，胜任各类商务场合专业需求。

制度与公共政策

2026春 · MANA60023.01 · 林曦

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在简要介绍制度与公共政策的核心要素。课程将研读公共政策研究各领域的文本，重点关注当代世界（特别是亚洲地区）公共政策制定、分析、改革与评估过程中的多元议题。同时将着眼于新技术（如算法、大数据、人工智能）在重塑政策格局中的兴起。课程鼓励学生以比较视角批判性审视各国政策分析模式的优劣。核心目标包括：理解政策制定流程的运作机制；比较并批判性评估各类政策分析方法；在当代实践中重新检验理论框架；评估技术革新如何重塑我们对政策制定实践的整体认知。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to introduce in a nutshell some key aspects of institutions and public policy. Texts from various areas in public policy research are examined, with special attention given to the diverse issues in the formulating, analysing, reforming and evaluating of public policy in contemporary world at large and in Asia in particular. Attention will also be paid to the emergence of new technology (e.g. algorithm, big data, AI) in the transforming of the policyscape. Students are encouraged to examine critically the (de)merits of different modes of policy analysis across countries in a comparative way. The course goals include a making sense of how the policymaking process(es) will work, a comparing and critical evaluating of various policy analyses, a re-examining of theories in contemporary practices, and an appraisal of how technology transforms our understanding of the policymaking practice in general.

教学目标

The objectives of the course align with our university's mission to cultivate innovative, globally oriented leaders in social sciences, emphasizing Asia's dynamic contexts amid our diverse student body of aspiring policymakers and analysts. It embodies value guidance by fostering ethical scrutiny of policy (de)merits and societal impacts, promoting critical thinking on equity and sustainability. Knowledge transmission focuses on core institutional theories, comparative policy analyses, and emerging technologies like AI and big data reshaping the policyscape. Ability cultivation develops skills in evaluating policymaking processes, re-examining theories in practice, and appraising technological transformations, fulfilling professional training requirements for competent, adaptive public policy professionals. 本课程的目标契合我校培养具有创新精神、全球视野的社会科学领袖人才的使命，在多元化的政策制定者与分析师生源中着重强调亚洲的动态语境。该课程通过培养对政策利弊及社会影响的伦理审视能力，促进对公平与可持续性的批判性思考，体现价值导向。知识传授聚焦核心制度理论、比较政策分析及重塑政策格局的新兴技术（如人工智能与大数据）；能力培养则侧重政策制定流程评估、实践中理论检验及技术变革评估，以满足培养胜任且适应性强的公共政策专业人才所需的专业训练要求。

教学方式

The teaching methods for the course are strategically designed to realize its objectives through a balanced mix of structured delivery and interactive engagement. Lectures form the core, systematically transmitting foundational knowledge on institutional theories, comparative policy analyses, and technology's role in policymaking, ensuring alignment with professional training requirements. Student presentations encourage practical re-examination of theories and appraisal of technological transformations, cultivating analytical abilities and innovative thinking. Extensive in-class discussions promote value-guided debates on policy (de)merits, equity, and sustainability, fostering critical evaluation skills among our diverse, globally oriented students. This approach enhances ethical awareness, collaborative learning, and adaptive expertise in public policy. 本课程的教学方法经过精心设计，通过结构化授课与互动参与的平衡组合实现教学目标。课堂讲授构成核心环节，系统传授制度理论、比较政策分析及技术在政策制定中的作用等基础知识，确保与专业培训要求保持一致。学生专题报告鼓励对理论进行实践性重审，并评估技术变革的影响，从而培养分析能力和创新思维。课堂深度讨论引导学生围绕政策利弊、公平性与可持续性展开价值导向辩论，在多元化的全球化视野学生群体中培育批判性评估能力。此教学模式有效提升公共政策领域的伦理意识、协作学习能力及适应性专业素养。

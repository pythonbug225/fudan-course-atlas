课程简介（中文）

本课程将向营养与食品卫生学相关专业的研究生介绍组学技术的基础知识、发展前沿及其应用于营养与食品科学研究中的最新研究成果，指导学生拓展研究思路和方法，学习将营养基因组学、转录组学、表观组学、蛋白质组学、代谢组学、脂质组学、金属组学、肠道微生物组学等技术与营养食品科学有机结合，从细胞生物学、生物化学、分子生物学、系统生物学等多学科的角度综合解释营养问题，更好的满足和促进营养学科的发展。

课程简介（英文）

This course will introduce postgraduate students majoring in nutrition and food hygiene to the basic knowledge and cutting-edge advances of omics technologies, as well as their latest research achievements in the field of nutrition and food science research. It aims to guide students to expand their research ideas and methodologies, learn to organically integrate technologies including nutrigenomics, transcriptomics, epigenomics, proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics, metallomics, and gut microbiomics with nutrition and food science, and comprehensively interpret nutritional issues from a multidisciplinary perspective involving cell biology, biochemistry, molecular biology, and systems biology, so as to better satisfy and promote the development of the nutrition discipline.

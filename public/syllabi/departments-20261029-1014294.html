课程简介（中文）

这是金融硕士项目（金融工程方向）研究生的一门核心课程。该课程聚焦于金融衍生证券，例如期权、期货和互换。课程涵盖的主题主要包括：各类衍生证券、衍生品市场的运作机制、衍生品定价模型，以及涉及衍生品的对冲技术和交易策略。

课程简介（英文）

This is a core course for graduate students of Master of Finance Program in Finacial Engineering. The course focuses on financial derivative securities, such as, options, futures and swaps. Topics included in this course are mainly: various types of derivative securities, mechanics of derivative markets, pricing models for derivatives, and hedging techniques and trading strategies involving derivatives.

教学目标

1）To understand basic concepts, tools and mechanics relating to financial derivatives. 2）To understand the principle of pricing financial derivative securities. 3）To develop capabilities for investment involving financial derivatives.

教学方式

1）Lectures 2）Case study 3）Homework

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture 1 Introduction Mechanics of futures markets Hedging strategies using futures Lecture 2 Interest rates Determination of forward and futures prices Interest rate futures Swaps Lecture 3 Mechanics of options markets Properties of stock options Trading strategies involving options Lecture 4 Binomial trees The Black-Scholes-Merton model Lecture 5 Options on stock indices and currencies Futures options Lecture 6 The Greek Letters Volatility Smiles Lecture 7 Credit risk Credit derivatives Homework: 2 to 3 questions will be assigned for each subject as homework.

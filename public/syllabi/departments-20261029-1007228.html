课程简介（中文）

《金融理论与政策》是金融专业硕士的专业基础课程。该课程通过梳理经典宏观金融理论与最新国内外学术研究成果，结合全球金融体系快速变迁中出现的重大新现象、新特征，聚焦中国宏观金融领域的重大问题与重要政策（尤其货币政策、汇率政策、宏观审慎管理等），试图梳理出基于全球视野解释中国重大事实特征与重大实践问题的分析框架。 课程内容安排共包括9大专题（不包含导论）和校外专家讲座（本学期预计共2场）。具体专题内容详见如下教学内容及进度安排。

课程简介（英文）

"Financial Theory and Policy" is a core foundational course for Master of Finance program. The course explores key issues and major policy developments in China's macro-financial landscape—particularly in fields such as monetary policy, exchange rate policy, and macroprudential regulation—through the lens of classical macro-finance theories and the latest academic research. It also incorporates analysis of emerging phenomena and new trends driven by the rapidly evolving global financial system. The course aims to construct an analytical framework grounded in a global perspective to better understand and interpret China’s distinctive macro-financial features and pressing practical challenges. The course comprises nine main modules (excluding the introduction) and includes lectures by guest experts from outside the university (two sessions are planned for this semester). For detailed topics and the teaching schedule, please refer to the syllabus below.

教学目标

本课程的教学目标是紧跟金融领域的实践与理论最新发展趋势，结合基本原理与实践，强调数据与案例分析、文献材料与依据，强调中国事实与问题，运用相关理论知识分析和理解现代金融现象和问题；课程强调思政育人和职业道德培养。

教学方式

（1）教师课堂讲授； （2）校外嘉宾讲座； （3）同学分组报告（某些专题作业）；

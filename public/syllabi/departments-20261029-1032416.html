课程简介（中文）

本课程致力于深入剖析支撑当代商业研究的高级统计技术与方法论。课程旨在通过传授必要的量化工具并构建严谨的分析思维，使学员具备在动态演进的商业与管理学科范畴内开展高水平研究的能力。借助理论讲授、实务演练与批判性研讨相结合的综合教学模式，学员将系统掌握统计方法在商业研究中的学理基础及其在实证中的具体应用。

课程简介（英文）

In this course, we will delve into advanced statistical techniques and methodologies that underpin contemporary business research. The course is designed to equip you with the quantitative tools and analytical mindset necessary to conduct high-quality research in the evolving landscape of business and management. Through a combination of lectures, hands-on exercises, and critical discussions, you will gain a comprehensive understanding of both the theoretical foundations and practical applications of statistical methods in business research.

教学目标

This course is designed to equip students with the quantitative tools and analytical mindset necessary to conduct high-quality research in the evolving landscape of business and management. Guided by the DEFI framework (Data, Exploration, Fit, Implication), the specific objectives are: Scholar Cultivation (Critical Independence): Students are encouraged to foster critical thinking that emphasizes discerning right from wrong based on evidence and logical reasoning. The goal is to empower students to make informed decisions without being unduly influenced by prevailing opinions, while knowing when to incorporate feedback. Methodological Proficiency: The course weaves together a variety of statistical research methods into a coherent narrative. Students will gain a solid foundational understanding of basic models (e.g., regression, machine learning) and examine common criticisms. The emphasis is on building a comprehensive "knowledge tree" to select and apply the most appropriate analytical tools. Practical Application: Beyond theory, students will engage in hands-on exercises to demystify complex analytical tasks. The course emphasizes essential academic skills, including effective presentation, articulate academic writing, and the ability to critique and revise. A thorough walkthrough of an empirical research project will serve as the foundation for future thesis work.

教学方式

The course utilizes a combination of theoretical instruction and practical application: Lectures & Critical Discussions: Delving into advanced statistical techniques and methodologies that underpin contemporary business research, discussing theoretical foundations and practical applications. Hands-on Exercises: Direct application of concepts using R for data cleaning, visualization, statistical tests, and regression (e.g., using the La Quinta Hotel dataset). Project-Based Learning: Students collect data relevant to their own field and build an empirical research project step-by-step throughout the course. Presentation & Peer Review: Dedicated sessions for students to present their research ("Showtime") and evaluate classmates' methodologies based on DEFI principles. One-on-One Mentoring: Personalized improvement sessions at the end of the course to polish research projects.

Agenda: Study statistical tests and situations; simple regression and variations; identify and address endogeneity. Activity: R tests and regression (La Quinta Hotel dataset). Pre: Complete Class 3 assignment. Post: Identify potential endogeneity issues in the key message from the previous assignment and consider solutions. 5 DEFI – Experiment and Quasi-experiment Goal: Establish causal relationships. Agenda: Present endogeneity issues; Experiment design (RCTs); Quasi-experimental designs (PSM, DID, RD). Activity: Brainstorm quasi-experiment designs in your field.  Pre: Complete Class 4 assignment. Post: Analyze two recent top journal papers using experimental methods; think how to infer causality in your own research. 6 DEFI – Practice Session Goal: Build the main analysis for your research. Post: Finish writing the Data, Methods, and Results sections of the final project. 7 DEFI – Forecasting and Machine Learning Goal: Explore forecasting and ML methods. Agenda: Time series forecasting; ML in forecasting; using ML to build constructs from text, images, and video. Pre: Complete Class 6 assignment. Post: Prepare a short proposal for a potential study involving forecasting or ML (question, methods, challenges). 8 DEFI – Rigor and Implication Goal: Critically evaluate and enhance study rigor. Agenda: Robustness checks and alternative-explanation analyses. Activity: Practice critical evaluation on partially developed projects. Pre: Complete Class 7 assignment. Agenda: Share research (30-min presentation) highlighting DEFI; Discuss a classmate's research (15-min) evaluating methodology and rigor. Pre: Complete Class 8 assignment. Post: Improve research based on in-class feedback. 10 DEFI – Revision Goal: Improve and polish the research project. Agenda: One-to-one customized improvement session. Pre: Collect more data if needed; conduct additional analyses. Post: Complete the research project write-up.

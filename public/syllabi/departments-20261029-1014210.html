职业核心能力-创造力与领导力的理论与商业实践

2025秋 · MIB70002.01 · 罗亦兵

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

在科技创新的时代背景下，创造力及领导力在职场中将成为个人发展的突出优势。在专业领域进行不断的创新突破，在团队合作中发挥更大的影响力以获取更大的资源支持，这两者离不开创造力和领导力的培养及提升。本课程将基于国内外的最新研究成果，结合具体的商业实践案例及校企互动，在学术与实践共创的环境下，为学生进行理论的输入及实践的指导，引导学生积极参与讨论及实践，为未来进入职场在创造力和领导力方面奠定基础

课程简介（英文）

Under the backdrop of technological innovation, creativity and leadership are emerging as distinctive advantages for personal career development in the workplace. Continuous breakthroughs in professional domains and exerting greater leadership influence in teamwork to secure enhanced resource support both rely on the cultivation and enhancement of creativity and leadership. This course, based on the latest research findings from both domestic and international sources, and integrating specific business practice cases, will provide cutting-edge knowledge and tools. Through collaborative academic and practical learning environments, students will be provided with theoretical frameworks and practical guidance, actively engaging with the real business world and laying a solid foundation for developing the creativity and leadership competencies essential for future career success.

教学目标

拓宽并加深对创造力、领导力、职业发展、组织创新、组织变革等相关理论知识的理解； 领悟个人的创造力、领导力在职业发展及组织变革中的推动影响作用； 提升学生的创造意识、创造能力及领导能力，使其适应多变复杂的商界环境； 促进跨学科、跨专业的团队合作，提高在实践中解决复杂问题的能力; 实现主动学习，参与商业实践; 为职业发展的前期做引导 Expand and deepen understanding of theoretical knowledge in creativity, leadership, career development, organisational innovation, and organisational change; Gain insights into the catalytic impact of personal creativity and leadership on career advancement and organisational change; Enhance students' creative cognition, innovative capabilities, and leadership skills to navigate dynamic and complex business environments; Foster cross-disciplinary and cross-functional teamwork, strengthening the ability to solve intricate challenges in practical business contexts； Engage in active learning in the business practice; Coach and mentor for career exploration and exposure.

教学方式

理论授课+企业家进课堂+案例分析+实战 Instruction and lectures on foundational theories Guest lectures by entrepreneurs Case study Practical application and real-world simulation

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在向学生介绍计量经济学，并将其应用于经济学和政策研究中的实证问题。虽然课程中会涉及一些数学推导，但重点在于帮助学生直观理解计量经济分析的基本原理。 课程内容包括以下部分： 1）导论； 2）简单回归模型； 3）多元回归模型——估计； 4）多元回归模型——推断； 5）渐近性质； 6）异方差性； 7）进一步的模型设定问题； 8）受限因变量模型； 9）面板数据分析； 10）工具变量（IV）与两阶段最小二乘法（2SLS）估计。

课程简介（英文）

The goal of this course is to give students an introduction to econometrics with applications to empirical problems in economics and policy research. While some mathematical derivations will be presented, the emphasis will be on gaining an intuitive understanding of the principles of econometrics analysis. The course aims to covers the following sections: 1) Introduction; 2) Simple regression model; 3) Multiple regression model – Estimation; 4) Multiple regression model – Inference; 5) Asymptotic properties; 6) Heteroskedasticity; 7) Further specification issues; 8) Limited dependent variable models; 9) Panel data analysis; and 10) IV and 2SLS estimation.

教学目标

After you finish the course, you are supposed to 1. Know basic methods for estimating casual effects or testing economic theories using nonexperimental data. 2. Be able to conduct empirical analysis.

教学方式

Lectures and small group discussion.

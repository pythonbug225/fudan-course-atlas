金融统计

2025秋 · MF60016.01 · 吴若沉

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程从理论和实践两方面介绍现代金融统计学的方法及其在现代金融数据分析中的应用。课程中覆盖的内容包括：金融数据的特征、概率论及数理统计基础、随机过程的平稳性、线性金融模型的估计、极大似然估计和随机波动模型等。本课程将帮助学生获得系统性分析金融数据的方法和技巧。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces modern financial statistical methods and their applications. It focuses on the methods from both the theoretical perspective and how they can be used in modern financial analyses. The contents of the course include the features of financial data, stationarity of stochastic processes, linear financial models, maximum likelihood estimator and stochastic volatility models. It will help the students to develop the skills required to analytically explore financial data.

教学目标

The objective of the course is to help the students understand that financial statistics are the basis of modern financial analyses. Through this course, the students should have a more consolidate comprehension of relevant concepts in probability theories and statistics. They will also establish a systematic understanding of the basic models and methods in financial statistics, which will be helpful when they take more advanced courses in financial statistics, and assist them in making financial analyses.

教学方式

Lectures and assignments.

教学内容与进度安排

1.      Introduction 1 Overall description of the methods and their development. 2.      Probability theories and statistics 1 A review of relevant knowledges in probability theories and statistics. E.g., univariate and multivariate random variables and their distributions. 3.      Features of financial data and methods 2 Introduce the features of financial data, and descriptive methods to analyze them. E.g., histograms, kernel densities, empirical CDF, empirical quantiles. Assignment 1 4.      Stationarity and co-integration 2 Introduce the vital role of stationarity in financial statistics and cointegration. E.g., weak and strict stationarity, the consequence of non-stationary series, D-K test, cointegration and testing. 5.      Linear Model 2 Introduce linear models used in financial statistics. E.g., OLS, heteroskedasticity and serial correlation, WLS, GLS. Assignment 2 6.      Generalized Method of Moments 2 Introduce the application of GMM in financial statistics. E.g., the principle of GMM, identification, some examples of estimators as GMM, optimal weighting matrix. Assignment 3 7.      Maximum Likelihood Estimator 2 Introduce the MLE in financial statistics. E.g., the principle of MLE, score vector, Hessian, Fisher Information, marginal and conditional likelihood. Assignment 4 8.      Stochastic volatility models 2 Introduce models for stochastic volatilities. E.g., ARCH and GARCH and their inferences, as well as their applications in financial analyses. Assignment 5

课程简介（中文）

    通过本课程学习，我们将和研究生共同探索外源化学物质或内源有毒代谢物对人类健康的影响。我们将探索毒理学、分子细胞学、系统科学的最新进展，从以下多个视角来阐释。

课程讲解这些毒物如何影响人类、组织和细胞的“生”、“老”、“病”、“死”，及其分子机制，重点在于如何拓展相关的研究思路和熟悉最新的研究方法。

  1. “生”：细胞生存必需的重要作用机制，包括氧化应激、线粒体、自噬和溶酶体。接触毒物或药物后，人体内稳态一旦失平衡后，这些作用机制会反过来导致严重的功能丧失、疾病、甚至死亡。
  2. “老”：社会老龄化大背景下，机体和细胞衰老的重要特征和研究思路。
  3. “病”：重要系统的疾病，包括神经系统、生殖和发育、以及肿瘤的发病和进展。
  4. “死”：多种细胞死亡的分子机制和研究思路。

    

课程简介（英文）

Through this course, we will explore the impact of exogenous chemicals or endogenous toxic metabolites on human health alongside graduate students. We will delve into the latest advancements in toxicology, molecular and cellular biology, and systems biology, examining these toxins from multiple perspectives. The course will explain how these toxins affect the "life," "aging," "disease," and "death" of human bodies, tissues, and cells, focusing on their molecular mechanisms. The emphasis will be on expanding related research ideas and familiarizing students with the latest research methods.

1. "Life" -- Essential mechanisms for cell survival, including oxidative stress, mitochondria, autophagy, and lysosomes. When the internal balance of the human body is disrupted by exposure to toxins or drugs, these mechanisms can lead to severe functional loss, disease, or even death.

2. "Aging" -- Important characteristics and research approaches to organism and cellular aging in the context of societal aging.

3. "Disease" -- Diseases of critical systems, including the nervous system, reproductive and developmental systems, and the onset and progression of tumors.

4. "Death" -- Molecular mechanisms and research approaches to various forms of cell death.

 

教学目标

这门课程既要求学生掌握毒理学学科重要的科学进展，更重要培养研究生的科研思维和方法学能力。

This course not only request the graduate students to grasp significant scientific advancements in the field of toxicology but also aims to cultivate the research thinking and methodological skills of graduate students.

教学方式

本课程以讲授式为主，不定时穿插讨论，最后进行课堂讨论和期末汇报。

This course is primarily lecture-based, with intermittent discussions interspersed throughout. It will culminate in a classroom discussion and a final presentation.

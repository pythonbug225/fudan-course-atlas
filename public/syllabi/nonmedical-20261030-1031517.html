课程简介（中文）

Mathematical fluid mechanics is one of the most important and interesting areas of the modern science. For example, the famous Clay Institute in USA published a list of the so-called "Millennium Problems" for mathematics of the 21st century. The fourth task on this list is the question of the existence of solutions to the Navier - Stokes differential equations that describe the motion of gas and liquid. Problem solving in this area is of great importance not only for mathematics, but also for physics and for applications (ship and submarine movements, aircraft movement, etc.). In our lectures, we consider how, using modern analysis, to study fundamental problems associated with a stationary Navier-Stokes system.

课程简介（中文）

本課程主要介绍关于中国地方社会与宗教关系的研究著作及最近的研究趋势。本課程讲述这种研究主要的题目，如家庭生活与礼仪；民间信仰与农村社会组织的关系；庙会与朝圣的社会功能；道教、佛教与地方社会；明清时期宗教文化与民众习俗、俗文学的关系；城市宗教活动；民间宗教的转变过程等。探讨的时间为唐、宋、元、明、清时期为主。本课程的内容以西方研究成果为主，以便解释西方学者在这研究领域里的贡献；但是大部分课程阅读资料是西方著作的中文译文。

课程简介（英文）

The aim of the course is to introduce major studies and modern tendencies in research of Chinese society and religion. It introduces the studies of major Chinese religions from the perspective of sociology, ethnology, anthropology, folklore and other initially Western disciplines. Many Western and Chinese scholars treated religion primarily as a social institution, so they analyzed the role of religious beliefs and practices in the state organization and local society. The course covers the time period from antiquity (2nd-1st millenniums BCE) till the Republican period (1912-1949), but the emphasis is on the late imperial period: Song, Yuan, Ming and Qing dynasties, though the course also deals with some recent developments in the social life. The emphasis is on Western works. The course is taught in English, but most materials for reading also areavailable in Chinese translation.

教学目标

The course objective is to give students understanding of the current state in the Western studies of traditional Chinese religious culture, major topics and problems in this field. The contents of this course include such topics as the definition of Chinese “state” and “popular religion”, the family life and organization of rural society; roles of Daoism and Buddhism in Chinese history and in the life of local society; religious culture and customs of the late imperial period; the role of temple festivals and pilgrimages; the links of ritual and theatre, religion and literature; city life; and the problems of transformation of popular religion.

教学方式

This course uses the mixed seminar-lecture mode, there are prescribed readings for each class, that will be discussed in class.

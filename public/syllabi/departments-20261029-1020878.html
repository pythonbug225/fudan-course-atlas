课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在全面介绍公司金融的核心原则与实践，以“一价定律”和作为“公司理财第一法则”的净现值（NPV）法则为理论基石。课程内容涵盖货币时间价值、证券估值、资本预算、风险与收益、资本结构及股利政策等关键议题。在传统理论的基础上，本课程紧跟数字经济时代步伐，特别融入了人工智能（AI）在金融领域的应用探讨，例如利用机器学习辅助现金流预测及理解算法交易对市场有效性的影响等。通过将严谨的理论框架与现代案例分析相结合，本课程致力于培养学生在快速变化的全球金融市场中进行科学、前瞻性财务决策的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the principles and practices of corporate finance, centered on the "Law of One Price" and the Net Present Value (NPV) rule as the "First Law of Corporate Finance." Students will explore essential topics including the time value of money, security valuation, capital budgeting, risk and return, capital structure, and payout policy. Beyond traditional theory, this course recognizes the transformative impact of the digital economy. We will integrate discussions on how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning are reshaping financial decision-making—from automated cash flow forecasting to algorithmic market efficiency. By combining rigorous theoretical frameworks with real-world case studies and modern AI-enhanced analytical perspectives, students will develop the skills necessary to make informed financial decisions in a rapidly evolving global market.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students will be able to: Master Core Valuation Principles: Apply the time value of money, the Law of One Price, and the Net Present Value (NPV) rule to evaluate complex investment opportunities and value financial assets including bonds and common stocks. Optimize Corporate Financial Strategy: Analyze and recommend optimal decisions regarding capital structure (debt vs. equity), payout policy (dividends vs. repurchases), and working capital management, while understanding the impact of taxes, agency costs, and financial distress on firm value. Assess Risk and Return: Utilize the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) and modern portfolio theory to estimate the cost of capital, distinguishing between systematic and idiosyncratic risk in financial decision-making. Integrate AI into Financial Analysis: Understand the emerging role of AI in corporate finance. Students will explore how Large Language Models (LLMs) can assist in qualitative financial statement analysis and how data-driven algorithms are enhancing the accuracy of earnings forecasts and risk modeling. Apply Theory to Practice: Develop practical skills in financial modeling (Excel) and critical thinking through comprehensive group projects and case studies, bridging the gap between academic theory and real-world corporate challenges.

教学方式

Lectures: Presentation of core theories and frameworks. Case Studies: Analysis of real-world scenarios to apply theoretical concepts to complex business problems. AI-Integrated Demonstrations: "Live" sessions showing how to use AI tools for summarizing earnings calls, extracting data from financial statements, or running basic sensitivity analyses. Group Projects: Collaborative valuation of a public company, requiring a written report and oral presentation. Problem-Based Learning: Quantitative assignments to ensure mastery of financial calculations.

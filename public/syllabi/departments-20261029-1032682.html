课程简介（中文）

供应链管理与物流管理已成为企业获取竞争优势的两大关键领域。然而，在当前产品种类不断扩展、产品生命周期缩短、外包日益增多以及业务全球化的商业趋势下，管理供应链是一项复杂且具有挑战性的任务。此外，包括互联网在内的信息技术的巨大进步，既推动了对更智能的供应链管理与物流管理的需求增长，也为其带来了更多发展机遇。

课程简介（英文）

Supply chain management and logistics management have emerged as two major areas where companies can gain a competitive edge. However, managing supply chains is a complex and challenging task, given the current business trends of expanding product variety, short product life cycles, increasing outsourcing, and globalization of business. In addition, the enormous progress in information technology, including the Internet, has contributed to both the increasing needs and opportunities for improved intelligent supply chain management and logistics management.

教学目标

The course will introduce you to the concepts and techniques of modern-day supply chain management. More specifically, the course attempts to: - Improve your knowledge of intelligent supply chain management and logistics management, as well as your confidence in these areas. - Provide a conceptual, strategic view of supply chain design and operations, allowing you to see the role of supply chain management in the overall strategy and performance of the firm. - Offer quantitative and qualitative tools for identifying, analyzing, and managing supply chain operations. At the end of the course, you should have gained an improved understanding of: - How supply chain strategy can be aligned with business strategy. - The dynamic challenges of supply chain management in the digital era. - The competitive potential of superior supply chain management. and acquired the skills to: - Analyze intelligent supply chain management/logistics management to identify problems and improvement opportunities. - Recommend and initiate actions and projects to improve the supply chain. - Enhance your teamwork and project management skills.

教学方式

The course is a mix of lectures, case studies, and games. The lectures are meant to explain operational models and concepts, making use of both qualitative and quantitative methods. Qualitative methods involve theoretical frameworks, conceptual analysis, and the evaluation of solution strategies, whereas quantitative methods involve mathematical analysis, probability theory, and the basic concepts of microeconomics. Case studies depict a business scenario at a certain existing company in which complex supply chain management decisions need to be made. The situation is often partly described from the perspective of one of the key players in the decision-making process; furthermore, a certain degree of urgency is present as well.

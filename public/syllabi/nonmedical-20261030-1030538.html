课程简介（中文）

本课程以翻译学导论开始，依次讲授翻译的目的、矛盾、标准、本质、方法等环环相扣的核心理论范畴，对于每一个理论范畴的讲授皆是在纵观国内外已有研究的基础上力图突破，守正创新。同时，兼顾传授基本的科研方法与规律，鱼渔双授。课程主体部分是跨学科翻译研究，包括概论课和翻译与思维、翻译与认知、翻译与心理、翻译与修辞、翻译与语篇和翻译与文化等几个专题。每一个专题都会梳理相关跨学科翻译研究的发展历程及研究进展，并前瞻其未来趋势，使学生对跨学科翻译研究有较为系统和深入的认识，激发科研兴趣，培养科研能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course begins with an introduction to translation studies, systematically addressing core theoretical domains including translation purposes, contradictions, standards, essence, and methodologies. Each theoretical domain is explored by reviewing existing domestic and international research while striving for innovative breakthroughs, balancing tradition with innovation. It concurrently imparts fundamental research methods and principles, teaching both “fish” (knowledge) and “fishing skills” (methodologies) . The core component focuses on interdisciplinary translation research, comprising an introductory module and specialized topics such as: Translation and Cognition Translation and Psychology Translation and Rhetoric Translation and Discourse Translation and Culture Each specialization systematically examines the historical development and research progress of cross-disciplinary translation studies while anticipating future trends. This structure aims to cultivate students’ systematic understanding of interdisciplinary approaches, stimulate research interest, and develop academic capabilities through comprehensive exploration of these emerging fields .

教学目标

本课包括知识、能力、素养三个层次的教学目标，三个层次的教学目标层层推进、有机结合。 知识层面，使学生了解翻译学基本理论范畴，跨学科翻译研究的历史、现状和趋势。 能力层面，树立学生正确的翻译观，提高翻译实践能力，了解基本的科研方法，形成初步的科研能力。 素养层面，培养学生的辩证思维、批判思维、开放思维和创新思维，培养学生勇于创新、攻坚克难的科研素养。

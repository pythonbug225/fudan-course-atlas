公司金融

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

《公司金融》博士课程是一门专注于公司金融领域实证研究的高级课程，旨在培养学生运用现代经济学和金融学理论，结合实证方法，深入分析公司金融相关问题的能力。课程要求学生阅读并汇报经典和前沿的学术文献，熟练掌握如何从数据中识别因果关系，并设计严谨的实证研究。学生还需完成独立的研究项目，运用所学方法解决公司金融领域的实际问题，培养独立研究能力。通过学习该课程，学生不仅能深入理解公司金融的理论与实证研究范式，还能为未来从事学术研究、政策分析或金融实践奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

The course aims to cover the most important materials in Panel Data analysis, with emphases on their applications to empirical corporate finance. The course will deliver a comprehensive list of empirical methods that allow researchers to interpret correlations and to identify causal relationships in data. Such tools are essential for graduate students who aspire to conduct careful, state-of-art empirical research. In addition, the course will provide general guidance on formulating and executing (empirical) research ideas.

教学目标

本公司金融博士课程（2025 春）教学目标聚焦三方面： 一是夯实公司金融前言的实证方法，助力学生掌握解读数据相关性、识别因果关系的前沿工具，适配实证公司金融研究需求； 二是培养学术研究能力，通过审稿训练、复现项目、汇报前言论文等方法，引导学生熟练运用所学方法批判分析论文、独立开展实证研究，掌握研究设计与执行要点； 三是强化学术规范与交流能力，提升英文汇报、学术讨论及拓展研究方向的综合素养，为开展高水平实证研究奠定基础。

教学方式

本课程采用 “理论讲授 + 实践应用 + 研讨互动” 三位一体教学方法。 第一部分以专题授课形式，系统讲解线性回归、工具变量等核心实证方法，结合经典文献深化理解； 第二部分采用研讨会模式，学生分组英文汇报指定论文，聚焦研究目标、方法与贡献并开展批判性讨论。通过撰写评审报告、用 Stata 完成数据复现项目、提交研究提案并进行 40 分钟汇报等实践任务，强化理论应用能力。同时设置 office 答疑时段，要求学生课前研读文献、课中积极互动，以多元形式助力学生掌握实证研究技能与学术交流能力。

Journal of Financial Economics 130 (2), 237-264 Part II: Special Topics In the second part of this course, students are expected to read a set of papers covering several important topics in corporate finance. Classes will be conducted in a seminar format. Students will present assigned papers and participate in the discussions of the papers. The time length of each presentation and the number of students presenting each assigned paper will depend on the class size. Presentations should focus on the following issues: The paper’s main objective, key findings, and contributions. Over time you will find that this is actually the most important part of a research paper, which is generally contained in the Introduction section of the paper. When you submit a paper to a journal, the journal editor will typically have only a limited amount of time looking at your paper, and coming up with a quick initial assessment of the paper’s potential. Therefore, you need to convince the editor, within that limited amount of time, that the problems you study are sufficiently interesting, and your findings substantially differ from prior research, hence making an important contribution to the literature. The paper’s main hypotheses and empirical methodology This is the part where you are expected to apply the stuff you learned in the first half of the course to provide a critical review of the paper’s methodology. By a critical review, I mean student presenters should not only describe the methodology employed in the paper, but also provide an assessment on the strength and weakness of the methodology.

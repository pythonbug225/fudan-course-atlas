英语公众演说

2025秋 · GEEF30012.01 · Patrick Lorcan Thame

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

一门面向高水平英语使用者的英语公开演讲入门课程

课程简介（英文）

An introductory course in English public speaking aimed at high level English speakers

教学目标

1) To build your self-confidence in English public speaking and communication; 2) To develop your ability to write effective English public speeches; 3) To develop your ability to effectively deliver English public speeches; 4) To develop your responsibility as a public speaker; 5) To develop your listening responsibility and skills; 6) To develop your research skills.

教学方式

Instructor’s lectures, student led group discussions and in-class practice

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 - Course overview - Reading assignment, APS chapters 1, 2 & 3 Week 2 - Managing speech anxiety - Ethics in public speaking - Reading, APS chapters 4 & 5 Week 3 - Topic selection for the informative speech; audience analysis - Giving your first speech (instructions) Week 4 (Speech Day) - Students deliver introductory speeches (10%) - Reading, APS chapter 10 Week 5 - Using language correctly; using language devices - Information gap activity - Read APS, 11 Week 6 - Speech delivery - Read APS 6, 7 & 8 Week 7 - Using evidence; organizing the speech (introduction, body & conclusion) Week 8 (Speech Day) - Students give the recitation speech (10%) - Read APS, 9, 12 & 13 Week 9 - Outlining the speech; using visual aids; speaking to inform - Instructions on the informative speech Week 10 (Speech Day) - Students deliver informative speech (20%) - Read APS, 15 Week 11 - Students finish delivering informative speeches - Speaking on special occasions Week 12 (Speech Day) - Students deliver ceremonial speech (10%) - Read APS, 14 Weeks 13 and 14 - Speaking to persuade - Instructions on persuasive speech Week 15 (Speech Day) - Students deliver persuasive speech (30%) Week 16 (Speech Day) - Students deliver persuasive speech

教学参考资料

1) Instructor’s Handouts. Lucas, Stephen E. The Art of Public Speaking (“APS”) 13th ed. FLTRP. 2020.

1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 颅颈交界区外科学是研究发生在斜坡、枕骨、枕骨大孔、寰枢椎及延髓和高位颈髓，以手术为主多学科综合治疗的交叉学科，常见病变包括先天畸形、创伤、炎症、肿瘤、血管病等，本课程通过理论讲述、手术录像等多种授课方式，初步展示相关常见疾病、现代医学技术与多学科联合的诊治手段。同时注重培养学生的医学人文素养，加强人文医学教育，培养创新型卓越医学人才。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Craniocervical junction surgery is an interdisciplinary field that focuses on the surgical and multidisciplinary comprehensive treatment of pathologies occurring in the clivus, occipital bone, foramen magnum, atlantoaxial vertebrae, as well as the medulla oblongata and upper cervical spinal cord. Common lesions include congenital malformations, trauma, inflammation, tumors, vascular diseases, etc. Through various teaching methods such as theoretical lectures and surgical videos, this course provides an initial overview of common diseases in craniocervical junction surgery, modern medical technologies, and multidisciplinary diagnostic and treatment approaches. At the same time, it emphasizes the cultivation of students' medical humanistic qualities, strengthens humanistic medical education and education on medical ethics and professional conduct, promotes the "truth", "goodness", and "beauty" of the medical spirit, and aims to cultivate innovative and outstanding medical talents who maintain their original aspirations, strengthen their beliefs, enhance their humanistic qualities, and possess great love.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

通过对颅颈交界区解剖、生物力学、常见疾病诊断和治疗等方面的学习，了解神经外科颅颈交界区常见的病变及其诊断和治疗，展示现代医学科技给颅颈交界区域外科治疗带来的机遇以及面临的挑战，提高对颅颈交界疾病诊断、治疗的的认识，同时激发学生对医学新技术的热情，建立对疾病多学科全方位的临床思维方式，拓展视野。教学过程中同时培养学生的医学人文素养，加强人文医学教育和医德医风教育，弘扬医者精神的“真”“善”“美”，培养守初心、铸信念、强人文、有大爱的创新型卓越医学人才。

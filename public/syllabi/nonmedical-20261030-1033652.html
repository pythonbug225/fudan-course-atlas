课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助学生了解中国文学史的概貌，由此对中国古典文学及文化形成较为清晰的认识。课程介绍中国文学的不同文体、其传统及其对当代中国的影响，涵盖先秦两汉诗歌、唐诗、宋词、元曲、民间文学、小说、电影等内容，横跨古典、现代与当代中国文学。通过系统学习，学生将一起研读部分中国文学经典作品，形成对中国文学的整体印象与理解。课程活动包括诗词诵读与创作，中国戏曲与小说赏析，电影片段观赏及博物馆参访等。学生需对译文作品进行文本细读，参与课堂讨论并完成期末任务。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the different genres and traditions of Chinese literature and its influences on today’s China, including sections like Classical Narratives, Shi, Song Lyric, Yuan Vernacular Song, Folklore, Novels, Films, etc., covering classical, modern and contemporary Chinese literature. Students will be guided to read some works in An Anthology of Chinese Literature (ed. and trans. by Stephen Owen, New York: W.W. Norton, 1996), to get a general impression and understanding of Chinese literature. Some activities such as reading poems, writing lyrics, watching parts of Chinese operas and films, and going to the museum will be included. Students will read translated pieces and are expected to do the readings and participate in class discussions, work on final projects.

教学目标

To help foreign students to understand the overall development of ancient Chinese literature history, have a basic idea of the characteristics of ancient prose, poetry, lyric, vernacular song, novel, as well as the representative writers and works of each genre, so as to have a preliminary understanding of the tradition of Chinese literature and culture.

教学方式

This course is mainly conducted through lectures, and in addition, a variety of classroom activities will also be organized.

课程简介（中文）

工业产品检验检测服务在当前市场经济中扮演着非常重要的角色但又似乎并没有走到幕前。买卖双方如何确认交易对象的品级、数重量、材质等经济属性，个体消费者如何维护购买货物质量的权力，国家战略物资在进出口环节中如何控制本国利益等需求不断出现，公平公正的检测服务工作既可以有效平衡市场，也可以服务政治需要。 本课程主要分三个板块： 一、检测及检测机构：通过学习，让学生了解检测及检测机构的发展历史，掌握检测在经济活动中的作用，在国际市场中的地位，在国民经济中的角色，以及国家战略发展中技术性贸易壁垒的需求。了解目前国际知名检测机构在我国的发展情况，及其主要业务和技术特点。 二、市场中热门产品的质量管理与检测的状况：外贸中的纠纷案例、以消费品行业在中国的发展及质量管理现状、电商（跨境电商）的发展与检测技术的关系。 三、各种商品的质量现状和测试方法（纺织品、玩具、食品接触产品、固体可再生原料、再制造产品等）

课程简介（英文）

‌Industrial Product Inspection and Testing Services in the Current Market Economy: Roles and Practices‌ While playing a crucial role in the current market economy, industrial product inspection and testing services often operate behind the scenes. Demands continually arise: buyers and sellers need to confirm the grade, quantity, weight, material composition, and other economic attributes of traded goods; individual consumers seek to safeguard their rights regarding purchased goods' quality; and nations require mechanisms to protect their interests during the import/export of strategic materials. Impartial and independent inspection and testing services can effectively balance market forces and serve political objectives. This course is divided into three main sections: ‌I. Inspection & Testing and Its Institutions‌ Through this module, students will learn about the historical development of inspection, testing, and the institutions that provide these services. They will master the understanding of inspection/testing's role in economic activities, its position in the international marketplace, its function within the national economy, and the need for technical barriers to trade in national strategic development. Students will also learn about the development status of internationally renowned testing institutions within China, along with their primary business areas and technical characteristics. 12 ‌II. Quality Management & Inspection Status of Popular Market Products‌ This section examines real-world cases of disputes in foreign trade, the development and current state of quality management within China's consumer goods industry, and the relationship between the growth of e-commerce (including cross-border e-commerce) and inspection/testing technologies. 24 ‌III. Quality Status and Testing Methodologies for Various Goods‌ This module focuses on the current quality landscape and specific testing methods for key product categories, including: Textiles, Toys, Food Contact Materials, Solid Renewable Raw Materials, and Remanufactured Products.

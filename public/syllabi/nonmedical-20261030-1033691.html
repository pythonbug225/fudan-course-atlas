报道中国与中国报道

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

晚清以降，外国驻华记者的中国报道是国际社会对华信息与认知的主要来源，“报道中国”也逐渐成为一种相对独立的新闻生产形态。本课程选取十余位晚清以来以中国报道成名的外国记者，通过阅读分析他们的代表作，追索“报道中国”的历史嬗变。这组知名记者的个案分为两类：一、乔治·莫里循、艾德加.斯诺、马克.吕布（摄影记者）、杰克. 贝尔登、帝奇亚诺·坦尚尼等驻华记者。二、奥丽亚娜.法拉奇、麦克. 华莱士等特派记者以及伊文思、安东尼奥尼等纪录片导演。本课程是一门研究型讨论课：以作品文本、文献与影像阅读为基础，还原记者的中国现场与历史场景，观察西方新闻理念与中国民族主义的互动与冲撞。学生将发现，“报道中国”在不同历史时期是如何建构和运作的，记者的个体经历、价值观以及所在媒体的“机构规训“ 如何孕育了“报道中国”的复杂生态。

课程简介（英文）

The history of foreign correspondents and their reporting from China are not merely a record of journalistic endeavors. It’s a chronicle of the Western geopolitical imagination, observation and perception about the “Middle Kingdom”. For nearly two centuries, the foreign correspondent have served as the primary intermediary between China and the rest of world, feeding both fascination and prejudice about one of the oldest civilizations in the world, resulting a narrative that has oscillated violently between romanticism and cynicism, especially in contemporary time when China had undergone tempertrous changes amidst wars and political uncertainties. This new course, the first of its kind in China, taught in English, traced the historical evolution of foreign correspondents in China, or the “China Watchers”, by focusing on the case studies of 13 prominent western journalists from mid-19th century to the early 21st century, against the tectonic shifts in China’s global status, from periods of the so-called “Yellow Peril” 、the “Cold War Bamboo Curtain” to the “Open-door” reform, and finally its systematic rise as we witness today. The course wants to reconstruct the trajectory of the “China Reporting” and key factors contributing to the making of “Reporting China”: texts, methodology and nuances.

教学目标

A/ 提高英文讨论/辩论专业问题的能力，强化逻辑思维与跨文化表达； B/ 尊重新闻文本，还原新闻现场，提升对信息的分析与解读能力； C/ 作为“闯入者”的中国报道，为理解中国制度生态提供独特视角； D/ 培育研究中西问题的比较、换位意识，增强全球视野与理解力。 This new course is particularly designed for the research students of the newly launched PhD program at the J-school, and postgraduate students majoring in international journalism students. The case studies are primarily drown from two distinctive groups: those who had served in China as a resident correspondent such as George Morrison、Edgar Snow and Tiziano Terzani, and those who were “parachuted” into China for exclusive reporting assignments such as Oriana Fallaci and Mike Wallace. The list is also extended, rightly, to prominent photojournalists and documentary filmmakers such as Marc Riboud、Joris Ivens and Michelangelo Antonioni. Drawing on both textual and visual materials, students are expected to learn how has “China” contextualized through the eyes of foreign reporters/observers, and the intricacies between western-style journalism and its complex psycho towards China. The course will be conducted in the style of research seminars. Each week students will be assigned with reading material in advance, plus suggested theme or research questions for, hopefully, vigorous classroom discussion. At mid-term a one-on-one tutorial will be organized for every student. A panel discussion with Shanghai-based foreign correspondents will be part of the curriculum. A postgraduate teaching assistant will be available to help between classes.

教学方式

全英语。教师选定课前阅读材料，研讨型授课，并设计小型研究计划。 This is an English - intructed course. The instructor selects pre-class reading materials, conducts seminar-style teaching, and designs small-scale research projects.

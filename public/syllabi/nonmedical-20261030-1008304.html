课程简介（中文）

本课程拟以二十大精神为指导，以跨文化传播学理论为基础，在对来华留学研究生调查、访谈的基础上，深入挖掘学习需求，根据有助于构建正面的国家形象和人民形象、有助于消除外国人对中国的偏见及刻板印象等标准，精选出中国故事、中国元素，特别是精选具有世界意义的中国故事、中国元素。主要介绍：自然山水 画意人间——苏州园林、泰宗秀维岳，崔崒刺云天——中华文化名山之泰山、感悟中国智慧、中西习俗文化比较、智慧之光 文明之舟 和平之旅——郑和下西洋、清香话茗品茶俗、人间巧艺夺天工——中国瓷器、衣冠五千年——中国服饰、舌尖上的中国——中华饮食文化、五气化万姿，阴阳万象生——拳上的智慧 、中国经济解读、一带一路——共同奏响丝路新合唱、走向复兴的故事——中国道路等内容。

课程简介（英文）

Guided by the spirit of the 20th CPC National Congress and grounded in theories of intercultural communication, this course will conduct in-depth research into learning needs through surveys and interviews with international graduate students in China. Based on criteria such as contributing to a positive national image, showcasing the Chinese people, and dispelling foreign misconceptions and stereotypes about China, we will meticulously select Chinese stories and cultural elements—particularly those of global significance—for exploration. Key Topics Include: Nature’s Canvas, Poetic Dwelling: Suzhou Gardens Lofty as Sacred Peak, Piercing the Clouds: Mount Tai, Icon of Chinese Culture Discovering Chinese Philosophical Wisdom Comparing Chinese and Western Cultural Customs Wisdom, Civilization, Peace: Zheng He’s Maritime Expeditions Fragrance in a Cup: Tea Culture and Traditions Divine Craftsmanship: The Art of Chinese Ceramics 5,000 Years of Adornment: Evolution of Chinese Attire China on the Tip of the Tongue: Culinary Heritage Qi Flows, Yin-Yang Unfolds: Wisdom in Chinese Martial Arts Decoding China’s Economic Landscape Belt and Road: Reimagining the Silk Road Symphony The Road to Revival: China’s Development Journey

教学目标

本课程拟以跨文化传播学理论为基础，调研教学对象，精选教学专题。通过本课程的学习，丰富留学生关于中国的知识，使之比较了解中国情况，助其汉语学习和专业学习，增强其跨文化交际能力，提高对中国的好感度和文化认同感。

教学方式

讲授与讨论、活动相结合。

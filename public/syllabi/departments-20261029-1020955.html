教学目标

Course Objectives This course aims to equip graduate students and senior undergraduates with a systematic understanding of China’s taxation system and fiscal policy framework. Students will command core facts about the evolution and operational mechanisms of key taxes (e.g., VAT, CIT) and Chinese fiscal tools, and evaluate policy impacts via empirical evidence. By comparing China’s practices with international systems and exploring pressing issues like local government debt and digital economy taxation, students will develop critical thinking and practical analytical skills to interpret and assess fiscal and taxation reforms in China.

教学方式

Teaching Methods This course adopts a diversified, interactive teaching approach integrating case study, literature reading, in-class discussion, group presentation, and final exam.

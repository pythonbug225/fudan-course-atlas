课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍社会科学研究方法的基本原理与进阶应用，涵盖定量与定性研究方法、数据收集技术、研究设计逻辑以及分析工具的综合运用。课程紧密结合当前国内外社会科学研究的热点议题，尤其关注研究生论文写作中常见的研究方法挑战，旨在帮助学生掌握规范严谨的社会科学研究范式，并提升其独立开展实证研究的能力。 在内容安排上，课程注重社会研究方法的逻辑结构和应用流程，既关注抽象理论层面的方法理解，也强调实践中资料获取、分析与解释的能力训练。通过本课程的学习，学生将建立起社会科学研究的基本框架，理解不同方法之间的适用情境与互补关系，逐步具备将科学方法应用于现实问题分析与学术论文写作的综合能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a systematic and in-depth introduction to research methodologies in the social sciences, with an emphasis on both theoretical foundations and practical applications. It covers a wide range of approaches, including quantitative and qualitative methods, data collection techniques, research design principles, and analytical tools. The course is designed to support students—particularly for graduate students aiming to address methodological challenges frequently encountered in scholarly research and the process of writing theses or dissertations. Rather than focusing solely on a specific disciplinary domain, the course encourages interdisciplinary thinking and promotes methodological literacy across a broad spectrum of social science research topics. Through a combination of structured learning, critical discussion, and hands-on research design, students will develop the ability to apply rigorous methods to complex empirical questions and enhance their capacity for independent scholarly inquiry. Course Structure: The course is divided into three modules: Module I: Theoretical Foundations and Methodological Tools (Self-directed Study) Students will engage in guided self-learning with key readings and supplementary materials provided by the instructor. Topics include research design, concept and variable construction, causal inference, fieldwork strategies, and textual analysis. This module aims to build a foundational understanding of core methods and their theoretical underpinnings. Module II: Research Workshops and Thematic Seminars This module adopts a workshop format, facilitating focused discussions on methodological debates and cutting-edge research topics in the social sciences. Students will be encouraged to engage in critical dialogue, examine diverse methodological perspectives, and explore real-world applications of research tools in selected case studies. Module III: Research Design and Academic Writing Practicum In this practice-oriented module, students will develop their own research projects based on selected topics. With close guidance from the instructor, they will formulate research questions, design data collection and analysis plans, and produce a scholarly paper. This stage is intended to support students through the full research process, from conceptualization to execution and academic communication.

教学目标

课程以价值引领为导向，强调研究伦理与学术规范，帮助学生树立严谨求实的学术精神；以知识传授为基础，全面覆盖定量与定性方法、数据采集与分析技术；以能力培养为核心，结合论文写作实践，提升学生独立发现问题、设计研究、开展分析与表达成果的综合能力，为未来从事高水平科研与政策分析工作打下坚实基础。

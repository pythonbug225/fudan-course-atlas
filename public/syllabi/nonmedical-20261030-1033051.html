量子信息科学的物理原理

2026春 · PHYS60033.01 · Dawei Ding

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

量子信息科学领域正处于一个重要阶段：量子硬件的快速进展使得之前纯粹理论的方案现在有物理实现的机会。然而，仍然存在一些关键的理论问题需要解决，而且这些问题是该领域发展的重要瓶颈。我将通过解释量子计算和量子通信硬件实现背后的相关物理原理来介绍这些关键问题。实验内容将涵盖量子计算的不同物理平台，如超导量子比特、中性原子、受限离子。量子通信的平台会主要讲量子光学。理论问题包括量子编译、容错量子计算，量子基准测评，和量子分布式计算。我也会简要提及违背贝尔不等式在现实问题中的潜在应用。

课程简介（英文）

The field of quantum information science is at an important stage where rapid progress in quantum hardware has brought theoretical ideas into experimental reality. However, there are still key theoretical problems that need to be tackled which are major bottlenecks in the field. I will motivate these problems by explaining the physics behind the hardware realization of quantum computing and quantum communication. Experimental topics will cover superconducting qubits, neutral atoms, trapped ions for quantum computing, as well as quantum optics for quantum communication. Theoretical questions include quantum compilation, fault-tolerant quantum computing, quantum benchmarking, and distributed quantum computing. I will also briefly mention potential applications of Bell inequality violation to real-life problems.

教学目标

Teach the relevant physics of quantum information science, including how quantum technologies are realized physically and important experimental knowledge that aspiring quantum information theorists should know to tackle key problems in the era of rapidly developing quantum hardware. 教量子信息科学的相关物理知识，包括量子技术如何在物理平台上如何实现，以及在量子硬件快速发展的时代，未来的量子科学理论学家需要了解的重要实验知识，以应对现领域里的关键问题。

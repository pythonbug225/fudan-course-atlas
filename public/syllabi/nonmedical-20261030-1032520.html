中古佛教文献研究

2026春 · CHIN50014.01 · 李猛

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

佛教《大藏经》五千余卷，加上敦煌吐鲁番等文献，中国佛教文献数量庞大、内容非常丰富，其中尤以中古时期（魏晋南北朝隋唐、宋元）的佛教文献最为丰富，研究价值最高。中古佛教文献已经是中古文学、中古史乃至哲学研究学者都不能绕开的重要文献。与传统的经史子集等文献不同，中古佛教文献，在编撰、生成、传播等方面都有其内在的逻辑与特点，相应的也存在一些问题和陷阱，因此如何处理与利用这批文献就显得非常有必要。本课程既总体讲述中古佛教文献的编撰、生成背景、版本流传、研究价值、研究现状、研究方法等；也以时间为经、以相应典籍为维，详细讲解重要的佛教文献。

课程简介（英文）

The Buddhist Canon (Da Zang Jing) comprises over 5,000 volumes, and when combined with the Dunhuang and Turfan manuscripts, the corpus of Chinese Buddhist literature is vast and extraordinarily rich in content. Among these, the Buddhist texts from the medieval period (Wei, Jin, Northern and Southern Dynasties, Sui, Tang, Song, and Yuan dynasties) are particularly abundant and hold the highest research value. Medieval Buddhist literature has become an essential resource for scholars in the fields of medieval literature, history, and even philosophy.Unlike traditional Confucian classics, historical records, philosophical works, and literary collections, medieval Buddhist texts follow their own inherent logic and characteristics in terms of compilation, creation, and transmission. Consequently, they also present certain challenges and pitfalls. Thus, knowing how to properly handle and utilize this body of literature is of great importance.This course will provide a comprehensive overview of medieval Buddhist literature, covering its compilation, historical background, textual transmission, research value, current scholarship, and methodologies. Additionally, it will examine key Buddhist texts in detail, using a chronological framework to explore significant works along the way.

教学目标

1.了解中古佛教文献的产生、编撰； 2.掌握中古佛教文献的流传与存佚等情况； 3.初步掌握中古佛教文献的研究价值； 4.从多种视角或者立场认识中古佛教文献与佛教文化； 5.熟悉并掌握查找、使用乃至研究中古佛教文献的基本方法。

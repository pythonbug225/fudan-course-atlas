课程简介（中文）

对生药学研究任务及生药学研究涉及的本草考证、DNA 分子标记、生药分析新技术、中药指纹图谱、药典标准研究与起草、安全性评价、民族药、新产品开发、生药专业英文、科研论文写作等专题进行讲授与案例讨论，并介绍生药学研究用提取分析设备的操作与维护，指导学生设计并完成生药质量评价相关的综合性实验。

课程简介（英文）

This course covers topics such as the research tasks in pharmacognosy, including herbal authentication, DNA molecular markers, new technologies in pharmacognostic analysis, traditional Chinese medicine fingerprinting, research and drafting of pharmacopoeial standards, safety evaluation, ethnic medicine, new product development, professional English in pharmacognosy, and scientific paper writing.  It also includes lectures and case studies on these topics, as well as instruction on the operation and maintenance of extraction and analytical equipment used in pharmacognosy research. Students will be guided in designing and completing comprehensive experiments related to the quality evaluation of crude drugs.

教学目标

学生掌握生药学研究涉及的本草考证、常用鉴定方法、资源现状与可持续利用、功效相关质量控制方法等基本知识与理念，了解生药学研究新技术、新方法的适用场景，学会生药学课题研究相关实验设计，培养生药学研究科研思维与中医药传承创新理念。

教学方式

课堂讲授与专题讨论

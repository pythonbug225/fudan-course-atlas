课程简介（中文）

这是一门参与环太平洋大学联盟（APRU）高校的联合课程，由南加州大学主导，多个联盟高校参与，知名全球健康专家在线讲座、不同高校学生分组线上讨论及远程小组交流等形式结合的课程。

 

课程简介（英文）

The course introduces students to the practice of leadership in global health from those working in the field. Students will learn how leaders have overcome challenges faced in the operationalization of complex health interventions, foreign policy, and working with key stakeholders and organizations in this context. They will be exposed to real-world cases in global health leadership. Students will learn from the book ‘Global Health Leadership: Case Studies from the Asia-Pacific’, and interact with, world-renowned leaders and peers working in the field from various countries through a series of ten guest lectures delivered via real-time videoconferencing (using Zoom).

The course covers topics on the various models and practice of leadership and the challenges in global health leadership from multiple perspectives, illustrating theoretical and conceptual ideas of leadership through case studies of successful global health interventions in the field of global health from the Pacific Rim region. Such as global health governance, global health & gender, the SDGs, global health workforce and etc.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students should be able to:

  • Identify key trends and issues in management of global health agencies and organizations;
  • explain the importance of cooperation in global health and explain the ways that diverse players in government, the private sector, non-governmental organizations and multi-lateral organizations can work together or individually to impact the health of a community;
  • Describe the current and historical role of the World Health Organization in global health governance and the various strategies that have been suggested for how to improve it;
  • Describe the Sustainable Development Goals and how they relate to global health;
  • Explain how cultural competency impacts policies, programs, and services; identify key challenges in developing and implementing health programs in resource-constrained settings;
  • Discuss the critical shortage of health care workers in developing country settings, including the cause and impact;
  • Identify critical traits that contribute to successful leadership and past and current leaders that exhibit these qualities;
  • Describe the various components of a health system and their overall functions;
  • Discuss examples of key successful global health case studies.

教学方式

理论讲授、案例研讨、小组项目、嘉宾讲座

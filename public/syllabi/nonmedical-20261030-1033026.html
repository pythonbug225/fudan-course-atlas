课程简介（中文）

分数量子霍尔效应是现代理论物理学中最有趣、最具挑战性的开放性问题之一。三十多年来，它激发了开创性的新思想和概念。除了其基本意义之外，通过使用拓扑保护的任意态，它还具有令人兴奋的量子计算潜力。在这些讲座中，我们将探索一系列强大的理论工具来描述分数量子霍尔效应-从陈-西蒙斯理论和共形场论到矩阵模型。

课程简介（英文）

The fractional Quantum Hall Effect stands as one of the most intriguing and challenging open problem in modern theoretical physics. For over thirty years, it has inspired groundbreaking new ideas and concepts. Beyond its fundamental significance, it also holds exciting potential for quantum computing through the use of topologically protected anyon states. In these lectures, we will explore a range of powerful theoretical tools developed to describe the fractional Quantum Hall Effect - from Chern-Simons theory and Conformal Field Theory to Matrix Models. The course will primarily follow David Tong's lecture notes, complemented by selected research articles, offering students both a solid conceptual overview and direct exposure to current developments in the field.

教学目标

The following theoretical methods will be studied - Topological effects and Berry phase connection - Laughlin's effective description - Fractional statistics, anyon and braiding properties - Chern-Simons quantum field theory, particle-vortex duality - Conformal field theory and conformal blocks, bulk-boundary duality - Polychronakos' Chern-Simons matrix model

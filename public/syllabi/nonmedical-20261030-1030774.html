课程简介（中文）

截面数据回归分析，内容包括：简单回归、多元回归分析（估计与推断）、OLS 渐近性质等。

课程简介（英文）

Regression analysis with cross-sectional data (simple regression, multiple regression analysis: estimation and inference, OLS asymptotics etc.)

教学目标

Econometrics uses quantitative methods to analyse economic and financial data to answer questions encountered in economics and finance. Econometrics is extremely influential and widely applied in areas of academia, policy and industry. This course serves to equip students with the basic principles and methods and teach them how to apply the knowledge in empirical studies. We shall use software Stata.

教学方式

Off-line teaching

教学内容与进度安排

A review on mathematics and statistics (week 1), the nature of econometrics and economic data (week 2), the simple regression model (week 3-4), multiple regression analysis: estimation (week 5-6), multiple regression analysis: inference (week 7-8), mid-term examination (week 9), OLS asymptotics (week 10-11), further issues (week 12-13), quantitative information (week 14-15), heteroskedasticity (week 16), revision (week 17), exam (week 18). Note that this is just a rough schedule. We will go through additional topics if time permits. On the other hand, we will only go through a subset of topics if time is tight.

应激与代谢

课程简介（中文）

细胞应激反应与细胞代谢调控有着非常密切的关系。细胞要通过能量感应系统监测其能量水平状态，在不同的物质和能量状态下不断地通过细胞内的代谢调控途径来调节其代谢水平以达到一种稳态。同时，细胞在面对内外界一些不良因素时也会做出相应的代谢变化，这些应激反应对细胞正常的生长和功能是极其重要的。如果这些应激反应失调，就会使细胞代谢发生异变，导致肥胖、脂肪肝、糖尿病和癌症等多种人类重大疾病的发生。最新研究表明，主动激活特定的应激反应，对生物体的健康与长寿都有积极的作用。学生通过课程学习提高科学素养，为其今后从事代谢生物学学习和科学研究奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

The cellular stress response is intricately linked to the regulation of cellular metabolism. Cells must continuously monitor their energy status via energy-sensing systems and adjust their metabolic levels through intracellular regulatory pathways under varying conditions of nutrient and energy availability to maintain homeostasis. Meanwhile, when confronted with adverse internal or external factors, cells undergo corresponding metabolic adaptations. These stress responses are crucial for normal cellular growth and function. Dysregulation of such responses can lead to aberrant cellular metabolism, contributing to the development of various major human diseases such as obesity, fatty liver disease, diabetes, and cancer. Recent studies suggest that proactively activating specific stress responses can positively impact organismal health and longevity. Through this course, students will enhance their scientific literacy and lay a solid foundation for future studies and scientific research in the field of metabolic biology.

教学目标

掌握应激和代谢领域的基本原理和研究方法； 学习应激与代谢领域的最新研究进展和技术手段； 了解胁迫应激与代谢直接的相互调控分子机制； 探讨应激稳态失衡导致相关疾病的发生与发展的机理； 探究主动激活特定应激反应对生物体健康的积极作用。

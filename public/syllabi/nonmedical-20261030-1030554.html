课程简介（中文）

《翻译教学理论与实践》是英语语言文学专业的核心课程，共 2 学分 32 学时，授课周期 16 周，以英语为主要授课语言，无预修课程要求。本课程立足国内外翻译教育发展现状，系统讲解翻译教学的核心概念、教师素养、课程设计、教材开发、教学方法与技术、测试评估等关键内容，同时融入 AI 在口笔译课堂的应用、优秀翻译教师培养等前沿与实践议题。课程通过理论讲授、专题研讨、课前研读等多元形式，引导学习者探究翻译教学的基本问题，掌握行动研究等翻译教学研究方法，明晰翻译教学各环节的设计与实施要点，最终帮助学习者理解翻译教学的本质与规律，提升翻译教学的理论认知与实践能力，感受翻译教学的专业魅力。

课程简介（英文）

Translation pedagogy: Theory and Practice is a core course for the major of English Language and Literature, with 2 credits and 32 class hours in a 16-week teaching cycle. The course is mainly delivered in English and has no prerequisite requirements. Based on the development status of translator education at home and abroad, this course systematically expounds the key contents of translation teaching, including core concepts, teachers' professional qualities, curriculum design, textbook development, teaching principles and methods, translation technology teaching, as well as translation and interpreting testing and evaluation. It also integrates cutting-edge and practical topics such as the application of AI in translation and interpreting classrooms and the cultivation of excellent translation teachers. Through diverse forms such as theoretical lectures, thematic discussions and pre-class reading, the course guides learners to explore the basic issues of translation teaching, master research methods such as action research in this field, and clarify the design and implementation points of each link of translation teaching. Ultimately, it helps learners understand the essence and laws of translation teaching, improve their theoretical cognition and practical ability of translation teaching, and appreciate the professional charm of translation teaching.

教学目标

 To know something about translator education at home and abroad To study the current translation teaching models in international community  To discuss basic issues: curriculum, textbooks, testing, and evaluation  To get research insights into translation teaching: action research  To enjoy the art of translation teachin

教学方式

Theoretical Lecturing Systematic delivery of core knowledge and theories of translation pedagogy, including key concepts, teaching principles, curriculum design, testing and evaluation systems, etc. Thematic Discussion In-depth discussion on cutting-edge and practical topics such as the application of AI in translation and interpreting classrooms, and the cultivation of excellent translation and interpreting teachers. Pre-class Reading & Preparatory Learning Guided pre-class reading of designated academic works and chapters (e.g., On Translation Teaching, Translation Teaching from Research to the Classroom), requiring students to complete targeted preparatory tasks. Group Thematic Presentation Organizing students to conduct group-based thematic classroom presentations, as an important part of classroom performance assessment. Research-oriented Learning Introducing action research and other research methods of translation teaching, guiding students to explore research insights into translation teaching practice. Classroom Interaction & Q&A Encouraging students to actively ask questions and participate in classroom interactions to deepen understanding of teaching content. Comprehensive Review & Consolidation Conducting overall review of the whole course content in the final stage to integrate and consolidate the knowledge and skills learned.

翻译理论与策略

2025秋 · FORE60003.01 · 陶友兰

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要是针对本科生四年级学生开设的，适当引介国内外代表性应用性翻译理论和代表人物，讲解基本的理论内容和观点，帮助学生更多了解中外译论，感悟翻译的真谛。培养学生对翻译理论的初步兴趣，构建自己的翻译观，从一个更长的历史与一个更深的层面来认识翻译的本质，扩大翻译认知视野；同时，通过对大量针对性的英汉、汉英句段的试译、思索、讨论，总结出一些行之有效的针对不同类型文本（如法律、新闻、科技、旅游等）的翻译策略，指导同类文本的翻译实践。

课程简介（英文）

This course is primarily designed for fourth-year undergraduate students. It introduces representative applied translation theories and key figures from both domestic and international contexts, explaining fundamental theoretical concepts and perspectives to enhance students' understanding of Chinese and foreign translation theories and help them appreciate the essence of translation. The course aims to cultivate students' initial interest in translation theory, guiding them to develop their own perspectives on translation and gain a deeper, more historical understanding of its nature, thereby broadening their cognitive horizons in the field. Additionally, through extensive practice in translating targeted English-Chinese and Chinese-English sentences and passages, as well as reflection and discussion, students will summarize effective translation strategies for different text types (such as legal, journalistic, scientific, and tourism texts) to improve their practical translation skills. The course combines theoretical exploration with hands-on practice, fostering both critical thinking and applied competence in translation.

教学目标

1. 理论认知目标 / Theoretical Understanding 介绍中外代表性应用翻译理论及核心学者，帮助学生理解翻译理论的核心理念。 / Introduce key applied translation theories and scholars from both Chinese and international contexts to enhance students' understanding of core theoretical concepts. 2. 思辨与翻译观培养目标 / Critical Thinking & Perspective Development 激发学生对翻译理论的兴趣，引导其形成个人翻译观。 / Cultivate students' interest in translation theory and guide them in developing their own perspectives on translation. 从历史与理论层面深化对翻译本质的认识。 / Deepen students' understanding of the nature of translation through historical and theoretical exploration. 3. 实践技能培养目标 / Practical Translation Skills 通过针对性练习，提升法律、新闻、科技、旅游等文本的翻译能力。 / Improve students' ability to translate various text types (e.g., legal, journalistic, scientific, tourism) through targeted exercises. 通过讨论与总结，提炼不同文本类型的有效翻译策略。 / Guide students in summarizing effective translation strategies through practice and discussion. 4. 认知拓展目标 / Cognitive Expansion 拓宽学生对翻译学科的认知，了解不同理论及实践方法。 / Broaden students' understanding of translation as a discipline by exposing them to diverse theoretical and practical approaches. 增强对翻译在不同专业与文化领域作用的理解。 / Foster awareness of translation’s role across various professional and cultural contexts. 5. 理论与实践结合目标 / Integration of Theory and Practice 通过理论分析与翻译实践，提升学生的应用能力。 / Strengthen students' applied skills by combining theoretical analysis with translation practice. 培养解决实际翻译问题的策略与思维。 / Equip students with problem-solving strategies for real-world translation c

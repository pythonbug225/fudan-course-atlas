政治人类学

2026春 · SOCI50054.01 · Horace Michel Ortiz (何瑞雪)

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

1) 政治人类学者普遍关注的议题如比较政治制度、地方政治以及权力和合法性的象征意义； 2) 国家和权力的本质、社会记忆的政治性和社区建构实践； 3) 强调田野研究为获取有关政治实践的地方性知识的主要手段。

课程简介（英文）

1) topics of general interest to political anthropologists such as comparative political systems, local politics, and the symbolic significance of power and legitimacy; 2) the nature of the state and power, the politics of social memory, and the practice of community construction; and 3) an emphasis on field research as the primary means of acquiring local knowledge about political practices

教学目标

1)以人类学理论和方法论对不同政治制度和政治组织实践的比较研究为重点，培养学生对权力、威权、政体、统治在日常生活中表现形式的认识和分析能力，摆脱“西方中心论”的束缚，从而深化对日常生活权力复杂关系的认识。 2) 基本掌握政治人类学的主要内容，对相关文献有所了解，并对特定主题有较深入研究 1) Focusing on the comparative study of political systems and practices of political organizations in different contexts through anthropological theories and methodologies, students will be able to understand and analyze the manifestations of power, authoritarianism, polity and governance in daily life, and to break away from the constraints of "Western-centrism," thus deepening their understanding of the intricate power relations in everyday life. (2) A basic understanding of the main elements of political anthropology, a knowledge of the relevant literature, and in-depth study of specific topics.

教学方式

研讨会阅读任务讨论及学生研究项目研讨 Seminar discussion of reading assignments and students' research projects

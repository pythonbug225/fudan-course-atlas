课程简介（中文）

《普通化学实验I》（Experiments in General Chemistry I）采用全英文授课，作为医科平台临床医学留学生英文班的基础课程，涵盖了无机化学、有机化学、分析化学、物理化学等各个二级学科的知识，面向临床医学专业的留学生开设。 

课程简介（英文）

“General Chemistry Experiments I” is one of the fundamental courses for university freshmen of MBBS students. Based on the idea of “Essential Techniques are the Central Part of Chemical Laboratory Education”, the “General Chemistry Experiments I”, as the entry course of experimental techniques, has fused together the contents of several secondary level subjects such as Inorganic Chemistry, Organic Chemistry, Analytical Chemistry, and Physical Chemistry, etc.  

教学目标

The objectives of this course are to introduce the basic aspects and characteristics of chemical science especially that of chemical experiments, elicit students’ desire and interest in chemistry, and provide training in scientific thinking and methods.

教学方式

In each class, students will finish one experiment. First of all, instructors will introduce the principles and methods of the experiment, show demonstrations of basic operations. After that, students are asked to do the experiment independently under the instruction of teachers.   

教学内容与进度安排

  1. Safety Education. Learn chemistry laboratory rules, be familiar with the surroundings of lab room, check the inventory of the glassware.
  2. Preparation of Alum Using Waste Aluminum Cans. Key points: basic operations of weighing, heating, dissolution, filtration, crystallization.
  3. Synthesis of Aspirin: a Fever-reducing Analgesic. Key points: (1) the preparation methods of simple organic compounds, (2) heating in water bath, (3) phenol color reaction.
  4. Determination of Iron by Absorption Photometry – Application of Spectrophotometric Technique. Key points: (1) Beer’s law, (2) the use of spectrophotometer, (3) use of volumetric pipet and graduated pipet, (4) data processing.
  5. Determination of Reaction Rate and Rate Constant. Key points: (1) reaction rate, (2) reaction rate constant, (3) reaction order, (4) activation energy, (5) data processing, (6) list tables and draw graphs of experimental data.
  6. The Properties of Buffer Solutions. Key points: (1) the use of pH meter, (2) buffer solutions, (3) buffer capacity, (4) calibration of pH meter using standard buffer solutions.
  7. Determination of the Molar Volume of a Gas. Key points: (1) ideal gas equation, (2) Dalton‘s Law of Partial Pressure.
  8. Synthesis of luminescent Europium (Eu) and Terbium (Tb) Coordination Complexes. Key points: (1) rare earth elements, (2) the luminescence process of rare earth complex.

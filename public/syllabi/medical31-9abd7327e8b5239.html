1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程摒弃编年史叙事，以“认识方式、研究方法、因果理论”三条脉络为主线，系统梳理流行病学思想史的关键转折点。融合中西经典案例（张举“猪灰”、伍连德东北鼠疫、赤脚医生等）与现代因果推断方法（目标试验模拟、孟德尔随机化等），注重课程思政，培养批判性思维与科学精神。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | This course moves away from a chronological narrative and instead follows three main threads—‘modes of cognition, research methods, and causal theories’—to systematically trace the key turning points in the history of epidemiological thought. By integrating classic case studies from both Eastern and Western contexts (such as Zhang Ju’s ‘pig ash’, Wu Lien-teh’s Northeast plague outbreak, and the Barefoot Doctors) with modern causal inference methods (such as target trial simulation and Mendelian randomisation), the course emphasises the integration of ideology and education, fostering critical thinking and a scientific spirit.

系统掌握流行病学思想史的三条脉络（认识方式、研究方法、因果理论）及其关键历史转折；深刻理解中西方在疾病观察、对照实验、因果推断上的智慧贡献与殊途同归；能够运用历史视角与现代方法批判性分析流行病学案例。

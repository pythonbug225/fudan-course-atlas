课程简介（中文）

传统线性代数课程主要讲解非奇异方阵的高斯消元法和基于特征多项式的特征值计算，但实际问题常涉及奇异矩阵或非方阵，理论方法无法提供有效解。数值线性代数侧重计算机算法的效率、内存优化及误差控制，例如采用部分主元消元法和高精度特征值算法，避免克拉默法则或多项式求根等低效方法。

课程简介（英文）

Standard linear algebra courses focus on Gaussian elimination for nonsingular square systems and eigenvalue computation via characteristic polynomials. However, real-world problems often involve singular or rectangular matrices, where theoretical methods fail to yield practical solutions. Numerical linear algebra emphasizes computationally efficient algorithms with controlled error propagation, avoiding impractical methods like Cramer’s rule or polynomial root-finding. Key techniques include partial pivoting in Gaussian elimination and robust eigenvalue algorithms, prioritizing numerical stability and precision for computer implementation.

教学目标

This course will cover the fundamental concepts in numerical linear algebra: matrix decompositions:LU, QR, Eigen, SVD; how compute them, and how to use them to solve linear systems, eigenvalue,least squares problems. Stability and conditioning of those solutions will be discussed. At the end of the course the students will know how to solve such problems using the MATLAB software.

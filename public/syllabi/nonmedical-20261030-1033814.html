课程简介（中文）

神经电子是理、工、医相结合的交叉学科，其将现代工程技术、近代物理学、生物学、医学和人工智能等结合起来，形成了对生命科学、现代医学具有极其重要意义的新兴交叉学科。其属于技术科学范畴，是以生命的人为对象，用工程学原理，研究开发防病、治病、人体辅助功能等为医学应用服务的人工装置和系统，医疗仪器、医疗器械即是其最广泛的应用。 本课程简要介绍神经电子学科的发展史，学科内涵和研究领域，以及未来展望，重点概括介绍神经电子基本原理和方法，包括解剖学、生理学、房室模型、生物医学信号的检测、处理和识别、生物医学电子、生物材料、生化反应、生物医学传感器、医学仪器与设备等方面。

课程简介（英文）

Neuroelectronics is an interdisciplinary field that combines science, engineering, and medicine. It combines modern engineering technology, modern physics, biology, medicine, and artificial intelligence to form an emerging interdisciplinary field of great significance for life sciences and modern medicine. It belongs to the field of technical science and is an artificial device and system that uses engineering principles to research and develop disease prevention, treatment, and human auxiliary functions for medical applications, targeting human beings. Medical instruments and medical devices are its most widely used applications. This course provides a brief introduction to the development history, connotation, research fields, and future prospects of the discipline of neuroelectronics. It focuses on summarizing the basic principles and methods of neuroelectronics, including anatomy, physiology, atrioventricular models, detection, processing, and recognition of biomedical signals, biomedical electronics, biomaterials, biochemical reactions, biomedical sensors, medical instruments and equipment, and more.

教学目标

《神经电子学》是突出神经电子这一交叉学科充满创新的特点，主要介绍神经电子的基本原理、典型应用、最新成果及未来发展。通过本课程的学习，要求学生掌握有关神经电子的基本原理及技术，为从事本专业的工作和研究奠定坚实的基础。

教学方式

本课程以课堂教学为主要教学方式。

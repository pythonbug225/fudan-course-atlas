课程简介（中文）

本课程的主要目的是帮助留学生掌握金融学领域的一些基本知识和概念，包括国际收支与平衡、外汇与汇率、国际货币体系、股票与股票市场等，并在此基础上提高留学生的汉语水平。从而为其深入学习有关金融投资领域的知识和技能奠定基础，也为留学生毕业后从事相关工作提供帮助。

课程简介（英文）

The main objective of this course is to assist international students in mastering some fundamental knowledge and concepts in the field of finance, including balance of payments, foreign exchange and exchange rates, the international monetary system, stocks and stock markets, among others. Additionally, the course aims to enhance the students' Chinese language proficiency. This foundation will prepare them for further study of knowledge and skills related to financial investment and provide support for their future careers in relevant fields after graduation.

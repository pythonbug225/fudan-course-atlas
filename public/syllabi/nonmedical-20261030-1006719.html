课程简介（中文）

深度生成模型是一类机器学习模型，旨在学习数据的底层分布，以便从该分布中生成新的、真实的样本。这些模型通过生成图像、文本和声音形式的高质量数据，彻底改变了计算机视觉、自然语言处理和音频合成等领域。一些最有影响力的深度生成模型包括变分自编码器（VAE）、生成对抗网络（GAN）、归一化流和扩散模型。这些模型中的每一个都为生成数据提供了独特的方法和优势，它们继续处于深度学习和人工智能研究的前沿。

课程简介（英文）

Deep generative models are a class of machine learning models that aim to learn the underlying distribution of data in order to generate new, realistic samples from that distribution. These models have revolutionized fields such as computer vision, natural language processing, and audio synthesis by enabling the generation of high-quality data in the form of images, text, and sound. Some of the most influential deep generative models include Variational Autoencoders (VAE), Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN), Normalizing Flows, and Diffusion Models. Each of these models provides unique approaches and advantages for generating data, and they continue to be at the forefront of research in deep learning and artificial intelligence.

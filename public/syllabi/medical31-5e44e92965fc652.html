1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程为基于基础、临床、人文医学各学科整合式病例进行的研讨型课程，学生8-9人一个小组进行学习。每个病例通过三次讨论课（6学时）、一次线上文献学习（2学时）和一次总结课（2学时）来完成，每个学期学习3个病例。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | PBLII mainly involves diseases of different systems, which are composed of 3 cases. Each case is arranged for three tutorials and one case wrap-up. Each case is composed of discussion and self-learning for 9 academic hours and case wrap-up for 2 academic hours. Meanwhile, the students are provided adequate time to search materials, deduce and prepare for discussion. Small group discussion (8-9 students) is adopted.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Objectives of the course is to foster student-centered learning, upholds lifelong learning, prominence on comprehension, in-depth learning and constructivist approach, arguments self-learning, better understanding and adeptness, reinforce interpersonal skills and teamwork and self-motivated attitude.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

PBLII mainly involves diseases of different systems, which are composed of 3 cases. Small group discussion (8-9 students) is adopted. The course is student-centered learning, which allows for the development of students’ skills and attributes.

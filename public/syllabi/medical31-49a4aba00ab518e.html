1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 外科学具体内容包括：骨科常见疾病（运动系统检查、骨折、关节感染、手外伤等）；泌尿外科常见疾病（泌尿系统损伤、感染、泌尿系结石及生殖系统肿瘤等）；胸外科常见疾病（胸部外伤、肺癌、食管癌等），以及外科基本操作。外科学的教学过程包括课堂教学和临床示教。授课内容以常见病、多发病为重点，同时介绍国内外医学领域的一些新进展，以帮助了解现代医学的发展新趋势。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The specific contents of surgery include: Orthopedic common diseases (movement system checks, fracture, joint infection, hand trauma, etc.), uropoiesis surgical department common diseases (urinary system injury, infection, urinary stone disease, reproductive system tumor, etc.), thoracic surgery common diseases (chest trauma, lung cancer, esophageal cancer, mediastinum tumor, etc.) and surgical operative surgery. The teaching process of surgery includes classroom teaching and clinical demonstration teaching. The content of the lecture focuses on common and frequently-occurring diseases, and also introduces some new advances in medicine at home and abroad, so as to understand the new trends of modern medicine.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

This course is designed to improve students’understanding of general surgery. The carefully chosen material is intended to sensitize students to anatomy, physiology, pathophysiology, and medical principle of relevant treatments of general surgery.

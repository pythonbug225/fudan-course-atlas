课程简介（中文）

市场营销管理是一门帮助人们以愉快、可持续的方式实现互惠交换的学科。它不仅能通过满足顾客需求来推动组织的发展，也能让学习者在认识并运用自己的优点和不足中获得个人成长。 本课程将带领学生了解市场营销的基本概念和工具，包括 5Cs、STP、4Ps 等常用框架，以及 市场调研的基本方法。课程也会帮助学生认识市场营销在企业管理中的重要性，并学习如何运用这些框架来进行商业分析和决策。

课程简介（英文）

Marketing management is the art and science of facilitating beneficial exchanges in an enjoyable and sustainable way. It does not only benefit organizational objectives by effectively satisfying customers but also help with personal development by taking advantages of all your strengths and weaknesses. This course provides students with an overview of the marketing function, which includes 5Cs, STP, 4Ps frameworks as well as marketing research methodology. The course also promotes an understanding of marketing as the basis for general management decision-making and a framework of business analysis.

教学目标

The course is designed with the following goals in mind. Students should be able to achieve the following goals:     Comprehend and describe the role of marketing and basic marketing principles (4Ps, STP, 5Cs frameworks);     Analyze existing business marketing strategy using these frameworks;     Propose the recommendations and plausible solutions in order to address the challenges business face;     Cultivate a “test” mindset by designing rigorous market research methods to answer questions when trying to design and/or position a business idea.

教学方式

A combination of lectures, class discussions, case analysis, individual homework, action learning project, recitations and code (in R language) sharing.

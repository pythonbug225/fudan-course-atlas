课程简介（英文）

Course Description (in English) This course examines the diaries of modern prominent figures from the perspectives of bibliography, history, and literature. It covers the manuscript, transcript, and printed versions of diary monographs as well as the strengths and weaknesses of collated editions. The course also explores the historical documentary value of diary content and potential thematic research, along with the stylistic features, literary connotations, and affiliation with biographical literature of diary works.

教学目标

通过近代日记经典的讲授，使学生从首尾完整的专著角度了解日记这一传统文体，以及近代日记的名家代表作。避免碎片化抽取日记片段，仅作为专题研究的资料库，或以猎奇的心理视之为掌故谈资，能从学术前沿视角严肃解读一部近代日记作品。

视听媒介产业与节目研究

2026春 · JCM70007.01 · 赵民、陆晔

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将对视听媒体产业的政策、机构及其规划进行简要介绍。 在全球化以及商业化的大背景之下，尤其是面对网络传播技术与生态对视听传媒产生的重大影响，本课的主题围绕中国视听传媒产业的改革及其成果展开。我们将提出两个主要问题：1）政治、经济、科技及文化因素是如何影响视听传媒产业的规划的？2）国家及地方视听传播机构（以广电机构为主）如何与国际性的视听集团（或媒体集团）竞争？在政治经济和文化全球化的关键框架之中，中国视听产业的案例势必成为分析和讨论的中心，从而对视听传播产业的现状有最直接的了解。 背景：全球化、新技术、市场化；重点：媒介制度、媒介结构、节目理念和形态变化、新技术的影响。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the policies, institutions, and planning of the audio-visual media industry. Against the backdrop of globalization and commercialization, especially in the face of the significant impact of network communication technologies and ecosystems on audio-visual media, the theme of this course focuses on the reform and achievements of China's audio-visual media industry. We will raise two main questions: 1) How do political, economic, technological, and cultural factors influence the planning of the audio-visual media industry? 2) How do national and local audio-visual communication institutions (mainly radio and television institutions) compete with international audio-visual groups (or media groups)? Within the key framework of political, economic, and cultural globalization, case studies of China's audio-visual industry are bound to be the focus of analysis and discussion, thereby providing the most direct understanding of the current status of the audio-visual communication industry.

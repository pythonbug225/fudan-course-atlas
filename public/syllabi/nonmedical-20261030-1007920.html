课程简介（中文）

The microeconomic course is an introduction to microeconomic theory at graduate level. We will go through the economic approach to the consumer’s behavior – how individuals make choices under constraints to maximize their utilities. This course also guides students through the firm’s managerial decision-making process – pricing and production decisions to maximize profits. Moreover, the students will gain exposure to general equilibrium theory, which attempts to explain the behavior of supply, demand, the prices in economic markets. Specifically, households’ decision-making process in financial markets will be discussed and examples of AI technologies in the field of microeconomics will be illustrated.

教学目标

On successful completion of this subject, students should be able to: Explain the core microeconomic theories of the consumer and the firm Acknowledge and identify the limitations of these theories, including how their underlying assumptions may differ from real-world problems Apply economic tools to analyse and solve problems facing consumers and firms Apply theories to economic problems faced by agents under different market structures

教学方式

Use economic reasoning in making decisions Use microeconomic principles to understand and explain economic events and other social phenomena Use economic reasoning to explain the strategic choices of individuals and organizations Be able to perform theoretical reasoning and think analytically

教学内容与进度安排

Class/Week 1 Topic: Consumer Theory Note: Introduction, Budget Constraint, Preference Background Reading: Chapters 2, 3 Class/Week 2 Topic: Consumer Theory Note: Utility, Choice, Demand Background Reading: Chapters 4, 5, 6 Class/Week 3 Topic: Producer Theory Note: Technology, Profit Maximization, Cost Minimization Background Reading: Chapters 19, 20, 21 Class/Week 4 Topic: Producer Theory Note: Cost Curves, Firm Supply, Industry Supply Background Reading: Chapters 22, 23, 24 Class/Week 5 Topic: Producer Theory Note: Monopoly, Monopoly Behavior, Oligopoly Background Reading: Chapters 25, 26, 28 Class/Week 6 Topic: Financial Market Decision Making, and Machine Learning Applications Note: Financial Portfolio Choice and Machine Learning Applications Background Reading: Course Slides

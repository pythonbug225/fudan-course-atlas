课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍亚太地区的历史演变以及该地区当前经济和政治舞台上的主要参与者。课程将理论探索与各类议题的实证研究相结合。通过修读本课程，学生可以了解该地区的主要政治和经济议题，并熟悉在区域和国家层面研究政治和经济的有效社会科学方法。本课程探讨的议题包括亚太地区安全架构、民主化、经济模式、亚洲经济崛起以及中美战略竞争等。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the historical evolution of the region of the Asia-Pacific and the major actors in the present economic and political arenas in the region. It combines theoretical exploration with empirical study of various issues. By taking this course, students can survey the major political and economic issues in the region and get familiar with effective social scientific approaches to politics and economy at both regional and state levels. The issues surveyed in this course include the Asia-Pacific regional security architecture, democratization, economic models, the economic rise of Asia, and US-China strategic competition, etc.

教学目标

This course is designed to introduce to students a series of in-depth studies of the politics and economy in the Asia-Pacific region from the theoretical perspectives of political science and international relations.By taking this course, students can get familiar with the region of the Asia-Pacific and learn to approach to a series of issues with social scientific methods. 

教学方式

Lectures by professor, and discussion in class.

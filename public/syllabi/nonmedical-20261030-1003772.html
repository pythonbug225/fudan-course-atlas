课程简介（中文）

本课程分为两个阶段。第一阶段讲授计算、网络和算法的基本原理；第二阶段采用项目式学习模式，通过应用开发、人工智能等专题教程，要求学生基于所学知识设计并实现一个完整的软件系统。

课程简介（英文）

The course is divided into two phases. The first phase introduces the fundamental principles of computation, networks, and algorithms. The second phase adopts a project-based learning approach, featuring tutorials on application development, artificial intelligence, and other topics. Students are expected to design and implement a complete software system based on the knowledge acquired in the tutorials.

教学目标

（Including value, knowledge and ability objectives） This course aims to equip students with a foundation in modern computing principles and techniques. By the end of the course, students will be able to understand and apply these techniques to solve practical problems.

教学方式

This course emphasizes both computational thinking and engineering capabilities through a combination of lectures and hands-on projects. It adopts a progressive learning model that transitions from foundational concepts to project-based applications. Students are encouraged to work collaboratively in teams to design and implement real-world software systems.

课程简介（中文）

在本课程中，我们将涵盖交换环理论的基本概念：素理想、局部化、扩展环、平坦度，以及交换环理论的更高级部分，包括：量纲理论、正则序列、深度、Cohen-Macaulay环、正则环。

课程简介（英文）

In this course we cover the basic concepts of commutative ring theory: prime ideals, localization, extension rings, flatness, and also the more advanced parts of the theory, including: dimension theory, regular sequences, depth, Cohen-Macaulay rings, regular rings.

教学目标

Understand the basic concepts of commutative ring theory: prime ideals, localization, extension rings, flatness, and the more advanced parts of the theory, including: dimension theory, regular sequences, depth, Cohen-Macaulay rings, regular rings.

课程简介（中文）

论文研讨独立课程，帮助博士生进一步完善选题思路和具体内容

课程简介（英文）

Independent course for thesis seminars

教学目标

A further examination of Varadaraja's work, Tarkikaraksa, on the Pramanya Theory. By consulting materials, reviewing translations, and summarizing philosophical ideas helps a PhD student complete a comprehensive literature review and the preliminary structure and content of their dissertation.

教学方式

Further examine the translated Sanskrit texts, meticulously proofread the English translations, and finally extract the philosophical ideas.

教学内容与进度安排

The specific arrangements will be made based on the actual progress of the doctoral student's dissertation.

教学参考资料

Tarkikaraksa Varadaraja Pandit Reprint 1903

课程简介（中文）

本课程将对语言类型学领域进行概述。课程计划涵盖以下主题：世界语言；词序类型学；词类系统；中心语标记和依存标记语言；世界语言数据库；以及语义地图。我们将主要研究来自多种语言的数据中的句法和形态学特征。

课程简介（英文）

This course will provide an overview of the field of linguistic typology. The course intends to cover the following topics: languages of the world; word order typology; word class systems; Head-marking and dependent-marking languages; Database of the world’s languages; and semantic maps. We will examine primarily syntactic and morphological features in data from a wide range of languages.

教学目标

At the end of the term, students will be able to address the following questions:

  1. How can we identify different cross-linguistic patterns? Which patterns are universal? Which patterns are rare?
  2. What are the motivations behind cross-linguistic patterns?
  3. What are the resources for doing typological research?
  4. What are the most important databases for typological studies? How to use them?
  5. How do we interpret semantic map and conceptual space?

教学方式

This course will adopt a student-centered teaching method, combining lectures, discussions, presentations by students and quizzes.

教学内容与进度安排

 

  1. Introduction（Week 1，Introduce the basic requirements and the contents of this course.）

-Further Reading: Comrie 1989 (chapter 1) or Croft 2003 (chapter 1); The Leipzig Glossing Rules

  1. Language typology（Week 2，Discuss the diversity and universality of the languages in the world.）

- Further Reading: Comrie 1989 (chapter 2-3) or Croft 2003 (chapter 2)

  1. Word order typology（Week 3-5，Introduce the basics of word order universals and ask the students to read some relevant papers.）

- Further Reading: Greenberg 1963; Comrie 1989 (chapter4); WALS (Chapter 81-91); Shopen 2007 (chapter 2)

  1. Presentations and discussion sessions（Week 6）
  2. Possessive constructions in the world’s languages（Week 7-8, topics surrounding the locus of marking in the languages of the world）

- Further Reading: Nichols 1986

  1. Presentations and discussion sessions（Week 9）
  2. Causative constructions in the world’s languages (Week 10-11)

- Further Reading: Comrie 1989 (Chapter 8)

  1. Database of the world’s languages（Week 12, Introduce the major cross-linguistic databases and teach the students how to uses them.）

- Further Reading: Forkel et al. 2018

  1. Presentations and discussion sessions（Week 13）
  2. Semantic maps: theory and methodology（Week 14-16，Introduce the basic theories surrounding semantic maps with classic case studies. ）

- Further Reading: Haspelmath 2003, Croft 2003 (chapter 5); Croft & Poole 2008

  1. Exam weeks (Week 17-18)

课程简介（中文）

课程分为理论部分和实验部分，理论课主要讲授细胞生物学的基本概念、细胞的结构特征、细胞的功能及其分子机制。实验过程将训练细胞生物学的基本实验技能。

课程简介（英文）

Cell biology is a basic course of medical science. We will discuss structures and functions of eukaryotic cells, including the nucleus, cytoplasmic organelles, the cytoskeleton, and the cell membrane. Also, cell regulation, including cell signaling, chemical material transport, cell motility, and cell cycle, are concerned.This course includes laboratory experiments，for training students basic cell biology techniques&skills.

教学目标

Learning Purpose：Students are required to master the basic structure and function of cells and their molecular mechanisms, and have a systematic knowledge network of the relationship between cell structure, functions, molecular mechanisms and the occurrence or development of clinical diseases.

Capability Purpose：Proficient in the using of cell biology knowledge to explain the changes in cells during the occurrence of diseases and possible methods that can be taken, and can establish scientific research ideas to explore the pathogenesis, prognosis and intervention methods of diseases from the cellular level.

Ideological and political Purpose：to cultivate medical humanistic qualities and the scientific research spirit of courage to explore and make breakthroughs.

教学方式

Lectures, combined with laboratory teaching, self-test exercises, problem discussions, after-class exercises, homework or reports.

教学内容与进度安排

Lectures 
Week 1            Cell Membranes  (2 classes) （teacher：Guo feng）
Master the structures of cell membrane, including the concept of amphipathic molecule, integral protein, peripheral protein, fluid mosaic model, lipid raft model, asymmetry, fluidity and impermeability of cell membrane structure. Master the functions of cell membrane, including the concept of simple diffusion, ligand gated channel diffusion, voltage gated channel diffusion, active transport, secondary active transport, 
Familiar with cases of chemical material transportation across cell membrane. 

Week 2                Cytoskeleton   (2 classes) （teacher：Guo feng）
Master the features of cytoskeleton, including the concept and structures of microtubule, microfilament, MTOC, the polymerization of cell microfilament and microtubule. Master the functions of cytoskeleton, including concept of motor proteins, the mechanism of muscle contraction, nonmuscle movements, flagella/cilia movement, and spindle fibers forming. Familiar with cases of cell movement.
Quiz1

Week 3 and 4   Structure and Function of Eukaryotic Organelles (4 classes)  (2 classes) （teacher：Guo feng）
Master the features of eukaryotic organelles, including the concept and structures of ER, GC, lysosome, mitochondria. Master the functions of eukaryotic organelles, including concept of SRP, signal hypothesis, protein glycosylation, clathrin, small GTP-binding protein, constitutive pathway, regulated secretory pathway, the mechanism of secretory/lysosomal proteins translocation, vesicles forming/trafficking,. Familiar with protein glycosylation process.
Quiz2

Week 5    and 6    Cell Signal Transduction  (4 classes)（teacher：Yang yunlong）
Master the features of signal transduction, including the concept of ligand, receptor, intracellular Receptors, RTK, GPCR, G protein, Second messenger, signal cascade, the mechanism of signal transfer pathways, including RTK-MAPK, cAMP-PKA, IP3-DAG-PKC, cGMP-PKG. Familiar with the angina case treated by nitric oxide.   

Week 7        Cell Nucleus  (2 classes)  （teacher：Zhu shun）
Master the features of nucleus, including the concept of nuclear pore complex, nuclear lamina, nucleosome, scaffold radial loop structure model, euchromatin, heterochromatin, kinetochore, telomere. the mechanism of nuclear proteins translocation, foming of chromosome.
Familiar with the karyotype.

Week 8    Cell Cycle  (2 classes) （teacher：Zhu shun）
Master the features of cell cycle, including the concept of cyclin. CDK, MPF, restriction point, start gene, S phase activator, cell cycle checkpoint, the mechanism of cyclin-CDK regulation.
Familiar with the CKI

Lab experiments
Week 6
Lab experiment    Cell Culture and Cell Counting (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Master the techniques of cell culture; learn to counting cells by hemacytometer.
Week 7
 Lab experiment     Cell Proliferation Assays  (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Master the techniques of measuring cell viability by MTT; learn to evaluate the culturing cell viability from morphological features. 
Week 8
Lab experiment   Cell Apoptosis assay  (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Master the techniques of measuring cell viability by Giemsa and Hoechst 33258 staining. evaluate the culturing cell viability by fluorescence microscope 
Week 9     
Lab experiment   Cell damage and protection  (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Establish a cell damage model and a cell protection model. Evaluate both effect by   immunocytochemistry techniques.

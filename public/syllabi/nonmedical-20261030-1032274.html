Urban Communication in China: A media perspective

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

城市化与数字化的深度融合，已深刻重塑人类文明。如今城市已覆盖全球逾六成区域，城市居民的日常体验模式、空间形态与感知结构均发生了根本性变革。 在此背景下，本课程以媒介技术为核心切入点，重点阐释网络化公共空间与媒介实践的发展、数字城市中日常生活的转型、智能技术与智慧城市之间矛盾且充满争议的关系；同时剖析全球传播中的数字化城市意象，以及中国城市的都市文化变迁。总体而言，本课程倡导以媒介视角理解中国城市正在发生的变革，进而为传播学研究探索新的研究方法与理论建构路径。

课程简介（英文）

The convergence of urbanization and digitization has significantly changed human civilization. Within cities, which have made over 60% of the world, the mode of everyday experience, the spatial configuration, as well as the experiential structure of dwellers have been profoundly transformed. Within the context, this course focuses upon media technologies to explicate the development of networked public space/media practices, the transformation of everyday life in digital cities, the ambivalent/controversial relationship between intelligent technologies and smart city; digitized city images in global communication and the changes of urban culture in Chinese cities. Overall, this course advocates for a media-based approach to understand ongoing changes in Chinese cities, so that new methodologies and theory-building for communication studies could be experimented with.

教学目标

This course aims to achieve the following objectives: Stimulate students to reflect critically on classical urban communication theories vis-a-vis the emerging digital media practices in Chinese cities; Use first-hand case studies and insights from digital urban communication professionals to push students to identify emerging issues and research agenda in urban communication; Based on the nexus between theoretical training, new empirical input and methodological reflections, this course sets out to enhance students’ analytical abilities to combine theory, digital urban communication experience and methods. An independent analysis and multiple sessions of discussions/presentation will be conducted as part of the course.

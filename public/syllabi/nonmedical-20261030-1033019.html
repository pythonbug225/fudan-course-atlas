课程简介（中文）

The course provides an introduction to random matrix theory, including an overview of the most fundamental ensembles and their associated quantities, such as partition functions, density of states, correlation functions, and spectral form factors. Additionally, the course will address some of the mathematical challenges that arise in the development of quantum technologies, with a focus on superconducting qubits. Finally, the course will examine the applications of random matrix theory in quantum information, specifically the role of random matrix ensembles in modelling quantum channels.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to gain a comprehensive understanding of a range of random matrix ensembles and to develop the ability to analytically compute the most relevant mathematical quantities associated with them. The objective is to gain an understanding of the fundamental mathematical issues that arise in the development of critical quantum technologies in the areas of quantum information and quantum computation. The course will equip students with the ability to apply random matrix theory to the study of quantum information problems involving quantum channels. For the most advanced students, this final section may entail the investigation of actual open research problems.

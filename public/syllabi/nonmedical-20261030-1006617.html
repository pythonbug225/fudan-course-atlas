国际市场营销的原则与创新

2025秋 · JCM60013.01 · 沈玢

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在向你介绍国际营销领域广泛的研究、理论与实践内容。我的教学理念以理论和方法为核心。我将理论视为通往知识的路径，将研究方法视为理解世界的地图，旨在带领你走过多条这样的路径。

课程简介（英文）

The purpose of this course is to introduce you to the breadth of research, theory, and practice on international marketing. My teaching philosophy is theory and method centered. Viewing theories as paths to knowledge, and viewing research methods as maps to understanding, I aim to take you down many such paths.

教学目标

Upon successful completion of the course, students should: Be able to understand much of theory literature in international marketing communication; Understand the methods and perspectives employed in the area; Develop more rigor in their thinking about theories and practices in international marketing; Comprehend the opportunities and challenges for marketing in a global context; Be able to utilize the knowledge to develop a marketing plan and presentation

教学方式

lecture + group presentations + case analysis

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Course Overview & Introduction Week 2-3 Discussions about what is research and what are research methods Week 4 National Day of China (We'll take a break.) Week 5-14 Two Rounds of Theory Learning and Campaign Presentations Week 15 Final Wrap-up

考核构成

  • 课堂表现Participation 20.0% Reading response per week
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 55.0% An advertising plan (25%) + A theory-learning session (30%)
  • 课题论文Research Paper 25.0% Individual research proposal

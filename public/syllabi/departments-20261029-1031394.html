课程简介（英文）

Overview: There's a pronounced disparity in income levels between affluent and impoverished nations. To illustrate, in 2019, Singapore reported an average income of approximately 65,000 USD, nearly 100 times greater than that of the Democratic Republic of Congo. What factors contribute to these vast income disparities across countries? Echoing the sentiment of a famous line from Anna Karenina, while wealthy countries may share similarities, each impoverished nation faces poverty due to unique circumstances. This course doesn't aim to catalog every possible reason for economic disparity. Instead, our focus will be on identifying the overarching and common factors that hinder income growth. We will explore a variety of developing nations, examining their economic growth trajectories, population trends, and investment yields. Furthermore, we will consider both informal and formal institutions as prevalent contributors to underdevelopment. By the course's conclusion, students will have developed a comprehensive framework for analyzing economic development and identifying the institutional drivers behind economic progress. 教学目标

This course aims to equip students with a systematic understanding of the core theories and empirical evidence underlying global economic development disparities. Students will master analytical frameworks to identify key drivers of cross-country income gaps, including formal institutions, informal institutions, state capacity, structural transformation, and resource allocation efficiency. Through case studies, literature analysis, and group research, students will develop the ability to evaluate the impacts of factors like elite dynamics, corruption, and industrial policies on development outcomes. By the end of the course, students will be able to independently design research topics related to economic development, conduct critical analysis of real-world development phenomena, and propose evidence-based interpretations of the institutional and structural roots of poverty and inequality in developing countries.

教学方式

It's a combination of literature reading and in-class discussion.

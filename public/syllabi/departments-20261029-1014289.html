课程简介（中文）

本课程探讨固定收益的理论、市场实证发现、及投资策略。我们将首先介绍国内及国际固定收益市场，并介绍各类固定收益证券。随后，我们将探讨利率与货币政策之间的关系、利率期限结构理论、和市场实证发现。此外，我们将探讨利率风险和信用风险的度量与管理，碧如介绍DV01,久期，KMV模型，讨论中国市场债券违约现象，并研究如何对信用风险建模。最后，我们将探讨如何在债券市场上进行投资，并讨论各种交易策略。 中国固定收益市场发展迅速，当前债券规模已达170万亿元，远超2024年国内生产总值（GDP），且规模远大于股票市场。可交易的固定收益产品正成为中国金融市场的主要金融工具，包括政府债券、金融机构债券、信用债券、抵押贷款支持证券（MBS）等；回购协议、掉期合约和债券期货等衍生品在市场上广受欢迎。随着利率市场化的推进，中国债券市场有望实现更快速的增长。从机构投资者的角度来看，商业银行、证券公司、基金和保险公司都在积极增加在中国债券市场的投资。例如，商业银行资产中约30%为债券；共同基金规模已达31万亿元，其中固定收益型共同基金占约80%；银行理财产品规模约为29万亿元；保险和信托资产管理产品持续增长。 在金融学中，有几个领域是理论引领行业实践的，固定收益就是其中之一。你会发现，课堂上学到的知识与实际操作非常契合。

课程简介（英文）

This course delves into the concepts, theories, evidence, and investment strategies of fixed incomes. We begin by introducing both domestic and international fixed income markets and discuss various fixed income securities. We explore the relationship between interest rates and monetary policy, as well as the theories to understand the term structure of interest rates. We address the measurement and management of interest rate risk. We introduce default risk and credit ratings, discuss history of default events, and study how to model credit risk. Lastly, we examine how to invest in the market and discuss various trading strategies. China's fixed income market is growing rapidly. The outstanding bonds has reached 170 trillion yuan in face value, significantly over the GDP in 2023 and way larger than size of the stock market. Tradable fixed income is becoming the dominant financial instrument in China's financial markets, attracting more and more students seeking to work in this field. Government bonds, financial institution bonds, corporate bonds, mortgage-backed securities (MBS), and derivatives, such as repos, swaps, and bond futures, are widely popular in the market. With the advancement of interest rate liberalization, China's bond market is expected to achieve even faster growth. From the perspective of institutional investors, commercial banks, securities firms, funds, and insurance companies are all actively increasing their investments in China's bond market. For instance, about 30% of commercial banks' assets are in bonds; the scale of mutual funds has reached 31 trillion yuan, with fixed income mutual funds accounting for approximately 80%; the scale of bank wealth management products is about 29 trillion yuan; insurance and trust asset management products continue to grow. In the financial field, there is a few areas where theories lead industry practices, and fixed income is one of them. You will find that the knowledge learned in the classroom aligns very well with practical operations.

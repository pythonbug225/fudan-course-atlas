课程简介（中文）

本课程主要介绍数据科学中所涉及的常用数学概念，相应的理论及方法。学生需先修读数学分析或高等数学、线性代数，并同时修读概率论。

课程简介（英文）

This course covers the mathematical concepts, theory, and algorithms most frequently encountered in data science.  Students are asked to complete Calculus and Linear Algebra prior to taking this course, and to take Probability concurrently.

教学目标

This course aims to provide students with the basic mathematical knowledge and tools necessary for data science.  Students should be able to apply what they have learned to solve related mathematical problems.

Lectures

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Linear Algebra

Systems of linear equations, solving systems of linear equations, vector spaces, linear independence, basis and rank, linear mappings, affine spaces.

 

Week 2: Analytic Geometry

Norms, inner products, lengths and distances, angles and orthogonality, orthonormal basis, orthogonal complement, inner product of functions, orthogonal projections, rotations. 

 

Weeks 3-4: Matrix Decompositions

Determinant and trace, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, Cholesky decomposition, eigendecomposition and diagonalization, singular value decomposition, matrix approximation, matrix phylogeny. 

 

Weeks 5-6: Vector Calculus

Differentiation of univariate functions, partial differentiation and gradients, gradients of vector‑valued functions, gradients of matrices, useful identities for computing gradients, backpropagation and automatic differentiation, higher‑order derivatives, linearization and multivariate Taylor series. 

 

Weeks 7-8: Probability and Distributions

Construction of a probability space, discrete and continuous probabilities, sum rule, product rule, and Bayes’ theorem, summary statistics and independence, Gaussian distribution, conjugacy and the exponential family, change of variables/inverse transform. 

 

Weeks 9-10: Continuous Optimization

Optimization using gradient descent, constrained optimization and Lagrange multipliers, convex optimization. 

 

Week 11: When Models Meet Data

Empirical risk minimization, parameter estimation, probabilistic modeling and inference, directed graphical models, model selection. 

 

Week 12: Linear Regression

Problem formulation for linear regression, parameter estimation, Bayesian linear regression, maximum likelihood as orthogonal projection. 

 

Weeks 13-14: Dimensionality Reduction with Principal Component Analysis

Problem setting of PCA, maximum variance perspective, projection perspective, eigenvector computation and low‑rank approximations, PCA in high dimensions, key steps of PCA in practice, latent variable perspective. 

 

Week 15: Density Estimation with Gaussian Mixture Models

Gaussian mixture model, parameter learning via maximum likelihood, EM algorithm, latent‑variable perspective. 

 

Week 16: Classification with Support Vector Machines

Separating hyperplanes, primal support vector machine, dual support vector machine, kernels, numerical solution.

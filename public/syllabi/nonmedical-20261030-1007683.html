Research Design in Political Science

2025秋 · POLI60025.01 · 张雪滢

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在向博士研究生介绍在政治学领域开展实证研究所需的基本原则和工具。课程期间，我们将讨论博士研究项目开发中的一般性与实践性问题，包括研究课题的选择、研究假设的提出、研究方法的选取以及实证数据的收集。 课程分为三个部分。第一部分将引导学生识别研究问题、提出假设并界定关键概念。第二部分将概述多种实证分析方法，使学生能够全面了解不同的分析路径，并根据自身的研究问题选择最合适的方法。最后一部分将聚焦于数据收集的技术环节，包括访谈、问卷调查和档案研究。 本课程力求在介绍研究设计的一般原则与鼓励学生在实践中应用这些原则之间取得平衡。课程要求学生积极参与讨论，简要汇报指定阅读材料，并参与个人和小组活动。此外，学生需进行至少两次课堂展示，提交一篇简短的文献综述，并撰写一份完整的研究设计。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to introduce PhD students to the fundamental principles and tools necessary for conducting empirical research in political science. Throughout the course, we will address both general and practical aspects of developing a PhD project, including selecting a research topic, formulating causal claims, choosing appropriate methods, and collecting empirical data. The course is divided into three sections. In the first section, students will learn to identify research questions, generate hypotheses, and define key concepts. The second section will provide an overview of various methods of empirical analysis, enabling students to develop a broad understanding of different analytical approaches and select the most suitable method for their research questions. The final section will focus on the technical aspects of data collection, including interviews, surveys, and archival research. This course aims to strike a balance between introducing general principles of research design and encouraging students to apply these principles in a practical context. Active participation is expected, with students engaging in discussions, presenting brief summaries of required readings, and participating in individual and group activities. Additionally, students will be required to deliver two presentations, submit a short literature review, and develop a comprehensive research design for their own projects at various stages throughout the course.

教学目标

本课程旨在使博士研究生全面掌握在政治学领域开展实证研究所需的基本原则和实践工具。课程结束后，学生将能够自主设计并完成一个严谨的研究项目，涵盖从识别研究问题、提出假设，到选择合适的实证方法以及有效收集和分析数据的全过程。通过积极参与，课程还将培养学生的批判性思维与实践能力，使其能够形成结构合理的研究设计，并自信地展示研究成果。 The objective of this course is to equip PhD students with a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles and practical tools necessary for conducting empirical research in political science. By the end of the course, students will be able to design and execute a robust research project, from identifying research questions and generating hypotheses to selecting appropriate empirical methods and effectively collecting and analyzing data. The course will also foster critical thinking and practical skills through active participation, enabling students to develop a well-structured research design and confidently present their findings.

教学方式

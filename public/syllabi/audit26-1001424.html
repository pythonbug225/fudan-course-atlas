课程简介（中文）

计算化学是一个计算机科学和化学的交叉学科。计算化学利用计算机程序解量子化学方程或采用计算机图论等数学模型来计算物质的性质、模拟化学反应过程并解释和预测一些具体的化学问题。

随着计算机的快速发展，计算化学在过去的30年中功能越来越强大。从最初的简单少量的实验数据处理、小分子波函数的计算和显示逐步过渡为化合物性质以及化学反应机理研究的重要工具，并且研究体系也逐步增大，目前已经涵盖了大多数的化学学科领域。从小分子的有机合成到大规模生物分子模拟以及复杂的固体材料模拟。

近年来，随着人工智能技术的发展（ANN，GNN, Diffusion, Transformer, GPT），计算化学和人工智能技术相结合在化学领域中快速进步。使得计算化学在化学和生物领域的应用呈现高速增长和高度智能的新特征。

 

本课程着重于应用领域，要求学生掌握基本的计算化学软件人工智能软件的使用方法，切实能够完成计算化学理论在实际体系中的应用并探讨核心机理。

本课程的目录如下：（1）计算化学的发展历程；（2）计算化学的发展目标；（3）从头算理论：一个方程控制的整个世界；（4）Gaussian软件的介绍；（5）分子性质的计算；（6）化学反应势能面和反应路径的计算；（7）分子反应动力学；（8）人工智能领域的发展；（9）人工智能在对计算化学中的应用；

 

课程简介（英文）

Computational chemistry is an interdisciplinary field that combines computer science and chemistry. It uses computer programs to solve quantum chemical equations or employs mathematical models such as computer graph theory to calculate the properties of substances, simulate chemical reaction processes, and interpret and predict specific chemical problems.

With the rapid advancement of computer technology, computational chemistry has become increasingly powerful over the past 30 years. Initially limited to processing simple, small-scale experimental data and calculating and displaying the wave functions of small molecules, it has evolved into a crucial tool for studying compound properties and chemical reaction mechanisms. The scope of research has also expanded significantly, now encompassing most areas of chemistry—from small-molecule organic synthesis to large-scale biomolecular simulations and complex solid-state material modeling.

In recent years, with the development of artificial intelligence technologies (ANN, GNN, Diffusion, Transformer, GPT), the integration of computational chemistry and AI has led to rapid progress in the field of chemistry. This has given rise to new features of high-speed growth and heightened intelligence in the applications of computational chemistry within chemical and biological domains.

This course focuses on practical applications, requiring students to master the use of basic computational chemistry and AI software. The goal is to enable them to effectively apply computational chemistry theories to real-world systems and explore core mechanisms.

The course outline is as follows:
(1) The development history of computational chemistry;
(2) The objectives of computational chemistry;
(3) Ab initio theory: One equation governing the entire world;
(4) Introduction to Gaussian software;
(5) Calculation of molecular properties;
(6) Computation of chemical reaction potential energy surfaces and reaction pathways;
(7) Molecular reaction dynamics;
(8) Advances in the field of artificial intelligence;
(9) Applications of artificial intelligence in computational chemistry.

教学目标

计算化学以量子化学和化学信息学为基础，针对本科学生，主要介绍计算化学的基本内容，以及计算化学、人工智能方面的最新进展。本课程着重于应用领域，要求学生掌握基本的计算化学软件、人工智能软件的使用方法，切实能够完成计算化学理论在实际体系中的应用并探讨核心机理。

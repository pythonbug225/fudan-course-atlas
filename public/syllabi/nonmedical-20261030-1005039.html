笔译理论与技巧(一)

2025秋 · FORE30035.01 · 杨雪燕

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

《翻译理论与技巧（一）》在介绍翻译理论与技巧的同时，将重点置于帮助同学们将理论和技巧应用于英译汉实践。课程从帮助同学们养成良好的翻译习惯开始，之后每周根据课堂上讲解和讨论的理论点或特定技巧，布置一篇课后翻译练习作业。预期经过一个学期的训练，同学们可以在一小时内将难度适当的短文（300-350英文单词）翻译成汉语。

课程简介（英文）

This course combines theories and techniques of translation with English-Chinese translation practice. Students are encouraged to form a good translating habit from the very beginning and are required to complete translation exercises on a weekly basis. By the end of the semester, students are expected to translate a text at the speed of 300-350 (English) words per hour.

教学目标

This course aims to help students to develop (a) their ability to translate texts of various genres and registers from English into Chinese, and (b) their understanding of both linguistic and cultural differences between English and Chinese.

教学方式

The course is conducted through lectures, seminars, group and class discussions, weekly translation exercises, and oral presentations.

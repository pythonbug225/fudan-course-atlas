1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程是微积分的入门课程，旨在系统介绍微积分学的核心概念，包括 函数、极限、微分和积分 。微积分作为一门研究变化的动态学科，是现代科学、工程、经济学等众多领域不可或缺的基础工具。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Calculus is fundamentally different from the mathematics that students have previously studied: it is less static and more dynamic. This course provides an introduction to calculus, covering key concepts such as functions, limits, differentiation, and integration . The objective is to assist students in understanding calculus for both its technical competence and its practical utility.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Calculus is widely used in many areas of science, such as physics, biology, and economics. In this foundational course, we will learn the core ideas of calculus through both theory and real-world applications: Functions : How to relate quantities to each other and understand their dependence on variables. Limits : How quantities converge to fixed values over time or space. Differentiation : How to calculate rates of change and slopes of curves. Integration : How to calculate the area under and between curves. Through this course, students will gain a deep understanding of calculus concepts and develop the skills to think logically and rigorously about continuous and instantaneous change. Overall, students will learn calculus both for its theoretical fundamentals and as a powerful tool for modeling real-world systems and solving complex problems. 本课程的目标不仅在于帮助学生掌握微积分的技术性计算能力，更在于理解其深刻的理论内涵与广泛的实用价值。通过本课程的学习，学生将： 深入理解微积分的基本概念及定理。 培养严谨的逻辑思维与抽象推理能力。 学习如何应用微积分工具对真实世界的问题进行数学建模并求解。 学生将通过理论讲解、习题训练和课堂讨论相结合的方式，掌握分析连续和瞬时变化的核心思想，为后续的专业学习和科研探索奠定坚实的数学基础。

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

The course will be delivered through a combination of the following methods: Lecture and Homework : Core concepts will be introduced through lectures, and understanding will be reinforced through assigned homework. Q&A : Dedicated time for students to ask questions and receive answers to clarify concepts. Discussion : In-class discussions will be used to explore complex topics and theorems. 本课程将通过以下方式结合进行教学： 课堂讲授与课后作业 : 通过课堂讲授介绍核心概念，并利用课后作业巩固知识。 提问与解答 : 安排专门的问答环节，以便学生提问并获得解答，澄清疑点。 课堂讨论 : 通过课堂讨论来深入探究复杂的知识点和定理。

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

Week 1: Historic problems in Calculus; introduction to calculus (differentiation, integration); Chapter 1 (Sections 1.1-1.6): Functions. Week 2: Review of Chapter 1; Chapter 2 (Section 2.1). Week 3: Chapter 2 (Sections 2.2-2.5): Limit of a function; calculating limits using the limit laws; precise definition of a limit; Continuity. Week 4: National Holiday. Week 5: Chapter 2 (Sections 2.6-2.8): Limits at infinity; derivatives and rates of change; the derivative as a function. Week 6: Review of Chapter 2; Chapter 3 (Sections 3.1-3.3): Product and quotient rules; derivatives of trigonometric functions. Week 7: Chapter 3 (Sections 3.4-3.5): The Chain Rule; derivatives of logarithmic functions. Week 8: Chapter 3 (Sections 3.6-3.9): Implicit differentiation; related rates. Week 9: Chapter 3 (Section 3.10): Linear approximations and differentials; Mid-term Exam . Week 10: Chapter 4 (Sections 4.1-4.2): Maximum and minimum values; the Mean Value Theorem. Week 11: Chapter 4 (Sections 4.3-4.4): How derivatives affect the shape of a graph; L’Hôpital's Rule. Week 12: Chapter 4 (Sections 4.5, 4.7): Curve sketching; optimization problems. Week 13: Chapter 4 (Sections 4.8-4.9): Newton’s method; antiderivatives. Week 14: Review of Chapter 4; Chapter 5 (Sections 5.1-5.2): The area and distance problem; the definite integral. Week 15: Chapter 5 (Sections 5.3-5.4): The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus; indefinite integrals. Week 16: Substitution Rule; review of Chapter 5; final-exam review. Week 17: Answering questions; administering the Final Exam . Week 18: Grading exams and submitting scores. 第一周 : 微积分的历史问题；微积分简介（微分、积分）；第一章 (1.1-1.6节): 函数。 第二周 : 复习第一章；第二章 (2.1节)。 第三周 : 第二章 (2.2-2.5节): 函数的极限；使用极限法则计算极限；极限的精确定义；连续性。 第四周 : 国庆假期。 第五周 : 第二章 (2.6-2.8节): 无穷远处的极限；导数与变化率；作为函数的导数。 第六周 : 复习第二章；第三章 (3.1-3.3节): 乘法与除法法则；三角函数的导数。 第七周 : 第三章 (3.4-3.5节): 链式法则；对数函数的导数。 第八周 : 第三章 (3.6-3.9节): 隐函数微分；相关变率。 第九周 : 第三章 (3.10节): 线性近似与微分； 期中考试 。 第十周 : 第四章 (4.1-4.2节): 最大值与最小值；中值定理。 第十一周 : 第四章 (4.3-4.4节): 导数如何影响函数图像；洛必达法则。 第十二周 : 第四章 (4.5, 4.7节): 函数图像的绘制；最优化问题。 第十三周 : 第四章 (4.8-4.9节): 牛顿法；反导数。 第十四周 : 复习第四章；第五章 (5.1-5.2节): 面积与距离问题；定积分。 第十五周 : 第五章 (5.3-5.4节): 微积分基本定理；不定积分。 第十六周 : 换元积分法；复习第五章；期末总复习。 第十七周 : 答疑与 期末考试 。 第十八周 : 批改试卷与提交成绩。

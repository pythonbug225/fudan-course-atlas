核电材料老化与管理

2025秋 · MASE60015.01 · 龚嶷

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

介绍核电厂常用的结构材料与功能材料的基本分类、服役环境、老化机理和管理大纲等。讲解核能应用与核电发展历史，以及当前国内外核电发展现状和趋势；概要讲述常见核电材料的种类与特点，包括锆合金、镍基合金、不锈钢、低合金钢、无机非金属材料、聚合物材料等，以及这些材料在热、氧、辐照、机械载荷、化学介质等典型服役工况下的老化效应，如腐蚀、磨损、断裂等；以非金属材料热氧与辐照老化为例，展示材料、服役环境、老化机理、老化效应、寿命评估、管理大纲间的相互关系与研究方法；阐述美国、国际原子能机构、中国分别建立的核电厂老化与寿命管理监管体系，及其对申请“运行许可证有效期限延续”，即俗称“延寿”的指导作用。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces fundamental classifications, service environments, degradation mechanisms, and management frameworks for structural and functional materials commonly used in nuclear power plants (NPPs). It covers the history of nuclear energy applications and nuclear power development, along with the current status and trends of nuclear power both domestically and internationally. The course outlines the types and characteristics of prevalent nuclear power materials, including zirconium alloys, nickel-based alloys, stainless steels, low-alloy steels, inorganic non-metallic materials, and polymeric materials. It examines the aging effects of these materials under typical service conditions—such as heat, oxygen, radiation, mechanical loads, and chemical media—including corrosion, wear, and fracture. Using the thermal-oxidative and radiation-induced degradation of non-metallic materials as a case study, the course demonstrates the interrelationships and research methodologies connecting materials, service environments, degradation mechanisms, aging effects, lifetime assessment, and management frameworks. Furthermore, the course elaborates on the regulatory frameworks for NPP aging and lifetime management established by the United States, the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), and China. It explains the guiding role of these frameworks in the application for "Operating License Renewal" (commonly known as "Life Extension").

教学目标

以理论联系实际为最终目标。在讲授核电厂的结构与设备、材料与老化、运维与管理等基本概念的基础上，介绍工程中遇到的实际案例。从而由浅入深、图文并茂地展示工程需求如何指导科学研究、科研成果如何服务实际工程，让学生直观感受到知行合一、理论联系实际的学习理念在核电领域的应用。

教学方式

讲授为主。并结合混合式教学，即组织学生小组讨论或观看教学视频，以及翻转课堂，即邀请学生上台进行专题汇报演讲。

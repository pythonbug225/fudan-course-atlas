来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将教授中级微观经济学的基本理论，模型及应用。本课程所涉及的理论模型以初级微观经济学为基础。通过本课程的学习，学生将懂得作为经济学家如何思考和认识世界。我们将学习个体的消费决策，企业的生产决策，市场失灵以及政府在改进社会福利中的作用等。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to use rigorous quantitative tools to re-examine the concepts and laws that you have learnt in “principle of microeconomics”. This will be the first course that you start learning how to analyze real life economic phenomenon as an economist. What you have learnt in principle of microeconomics is the way that economists think about the world, however, in this course, you will be learning economics models that are used to understand how individuals (firms) make decisions, how market works and fails, the role of government in improving social welfare and etc.

教学目标

Successful students will (1) memorize and understand models learnt in class (2) understand and use models leant in class to solve problems (3) understand the limitations of models learnt in class (4) use models learnt in class to analyze real life economics phenomenon

教学方式

In Class Lectures and TA lab sessions.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: chapter 1- 4 (preferences, budget constraint) Week 2-3: chapter 4- 8 (consumer theory) Week 4- 5: chapter 8-10 (demand curve and Slutsky equation) Week 6: chapter 10-12 (comparative statics and intertemporal choice) Week 7: chapter 13-16 (consumer welfare analysis) Week 8: midterm (chapter 2-16) Week 9: chapter21-22 (firm behavior) Week 10 chapter 22-24 (cost curves) Week 11 chapter 24-26 (monopolist) Week 12 chapter26-28(factor market, cournot model) Week 13 chapter 28-30 (game theory) Week 14 chapter 31-33 (general equilibrium) Week 15 review Week 16 review

教学参考资料

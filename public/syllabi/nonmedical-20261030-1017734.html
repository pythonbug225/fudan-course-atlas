大气化学

2026春 · ESE50060.01 · 王笑非、王琳

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将涵盖大气化学的基本概念、反应机制及部分最新研究进展。课程从大气化学基础入手，介绍大气物理的基本知识，包括模型与输送、地球物理循环以及温室效应，以帮助学生更全面深入地理解大气化学。课程主体内容包括化学动力学、平流层化学、对流层化学、气溶胶物理与化学、空气污染与酸雨，以及大气化学最新研究进展。

课程简介（英文）

This course will cover concepts, mechanisms, and some of the latest progresses in atmospheric chemistry. It starts with fundamentals of atmospheric chemistry. Basic ideas in atmospheric physics, including models and transport, geophysical cycles, and greenhouse effects, will be presented to assist a full and better understanding of atmospheric chemistry. The main body of this course will cover chemical kinetics, stratospheric chemistry, tropospheric chemistry, aerosol physics and chemistry, and air pollution and acid rain. In a special circumstance, a selected aspect of atmospheric chemistry with latest progresses will be discussed.

教学目标

This course is an elective for undergraduate students in the Environmental Science major. The fundamental aspects of atmospheric chemistry, including stratospheric chemistry, tropospheric chemistry, and aerosol physics and chemistry, will be presented during the lectures. The goal of this course is to help students develop a comprehensive and quantitative understanding of atmospheric chemistry, and build accuracy and fluency in scientific English. The course also prepares students for an advanced atmospheric chemistry course at the postgraduate level.

教学方式

Lecture

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Introduction Key points: Air Pollution and Atmospheric Chemistry; Ongoing Atmospheric Chemistry Research at Fudan Week 2 Fundamentals of Atmospheric Chemistry Key points: Mixing Ratio; Number Density; Partial Pressure; Vertical Profiles of Pressure and Temperature; Barometric Law; Sea Breeze Week 3 Models and Atmospheric Transport Key points: One-box Model; Multibox Models; Puff Models; Geostrophic Flow; the General Circulation; Vertical Transport; Week 4 Geochemical Cycles Key points: Geochemical Cycling of Elements; The Nitrogen Cycle; The Oxygen Cycle; The Carbon Cycle; Week 5 Greenhouse Effect Key points: Radiation; Effective Temperature of the Earth; Absorption of Radiation by the Atmosphere; Radioactive Forcing Week 6 Chemical Kinetics Key points: Gas-phase Reactions; Reverse Reactions; Photolysis; Radical-assisted Reaction Chains; Week 7 Stratospheric Chemistry I Key points: Chapman Mechanisms; Catalytic Loss Cycles Week 8 Stratospheric Chemistry II Key points: Polar Ozone Loss; The Aerosol Effect Week 9 Midterm (3 academic hours) Week 10 Tropospheric Chemistry I Key points: The Hydroxyl Radical; Global Budgets of CO and Methane Week 11 Tropospheric Chemistry II Key points: Cycling of HOx and Production of Ozone Week 12 Tropospheric Chemistry III Key points: Global Budget of Nitrogen Oxides; Global Budget of Tropospheric Ozone; Anthropogenic Influence on Ozone and OH Week 13 Aerosol Physics & Chemistry I Key points: Sources; Size distributions; Loss Processes; Direct and Indirect Effects; Week 14 Aerosol Physics & Chemistry II Key Points: Atmospheric Nucleation; Secondary Organic Aerosols; Heterogeneous Reactions; Week 15 Air Pollution & Acid Rain Key points: Air Pollution and Ozone; Ozone Formation and Control Strategies Ozone Production Efficiency; Sources of Acids; Effects of Acid Rain; Week 16 Final Examination

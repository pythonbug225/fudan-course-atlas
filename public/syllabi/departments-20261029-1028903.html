课程简介（中文）

整数规划是离散优化的基础和核心部分，广泛应用于管理科学、经济学、金融工程、工业工程与管理等多个领域。自20世纪50年代Dantzig、Fulkerson和Johnson的开创性工作以来，整数规划已成为解决现实优化模型的重要建模工具和方法论。本课程将涵盖整数规划的基础理论与求解方法，讨论分支定界、分支割平面、Benders分解、拉格朗日松弛和Dantzig-Wolfe分解等方法在整数规划求解中的应用。

课程简介（英文）

Integer programming involves mathematical problems where some or all decision variables are integers. It is a fundamental part of discrete optimization with broad applications in management science, economics, financial engineering, and industrial engineering. Since the pioneering work of Dantzig, Fulkerson, and Johnson in the 1950s, integer programming has become a key tool for modeling and solving real-world optimization problems. Various algorithms such as branch-and-bound, cutting planes, and relaxation techniques have been developed, leading to successful commercial solvers like CPLEX, widely used in both academia and industry. Recent advances in conic optimization, including polynomial-time algorithms for semidefinite and second-order cone programs, provide new approaches to tackle NP-hard integer programming problems. This course covers both the theory and solution methods of integer programming, including branch and bound, branch and cut, Benders decomposition, Lagrangian relaxation, and Dantzig-Wolfe decomposition, as well as practical use of commercial software and modeling frameworks.

教学目标

This course aims to introduce students to the use of integer variables as a fundamental tool for formulating complex mathematical models across various fields such as management science, industrial engineering, and transportation science. Students will gain hands-on experience with a range of software packages designed to solve integer programming models, enabling them to apply theoretical knowledge to practical problem-solving scenarios. The curriculum also focuses on familiarizing students with essential methodologies for solving integer programming problems, covering both the theoretical foundations and practical implementation of these techniques. Furthermore, the course will delve into advanced decomposition-based methods, equipping students with the skills to effectively tackle large-scale integer programming challenges that arise in real-world applications. Through this comprehensive approach, students will develop a deep understanding of integer programming and be well-prepared to apply these methods in academic research and industry practice.

教学方式

Lecture: the teacher imparts knowledge to students through narration and explanation. Flipped Classroom: Students independently acquire foundational knowledge prior to class through instructional videos, reading materials, and other resources. During class sessions, they participate in discussions, question-and-answer exchanges, and hands-on activities to deepen their understanding.

Module 1: Literature studies     We have meticulously curated a selection of scholarly articles published in esteemed journals, including Management Science, Production and Operations Management, Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, Operations Research, and the INFORMS Journal on Computing. This module is designed to deepen students' understanding of the latest research trends and advancements in integer programming and related disciplines. Engaging with this high-quality literature will offer valuable insights and foster meaningful intellectual discourse among peers.

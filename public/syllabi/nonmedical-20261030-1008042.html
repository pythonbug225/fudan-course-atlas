课程简介（中文）

课程内容：强化学习数学基础，包括贝尔曼方程、贝尔曼最优方程、价值迭代、策略迭代方法、随机近似方法；经典强化学习算法，如蒙特卡洛方法、时间差分法、函数逼近、策略梯度、演员-评论家等。课程将布置编程作业。不要求学生具备强化学习知识，但应熟悉概率论、微积分和线代等，并熟悉至少一项编程语言如Python、R、Matlab。

课程简介（英文）

This course will give a systematic introduction to the basic concepts, ideas, and algorithms in reinforcement learning (RL). The contents covered in this course include two aspects. The first is about the mathematical foundation of RL, which includes the Bellman equation, Bellman optimality equation, value iteration and policy iteration methods, and stochastic approximation. The second is about classic RL algorithms, which include Monte Carlo methods, temporal-difference methods, function approximation, policy gradient, and actor-critic methods. In addition to lecturing, this course will assign each students multiple programming assignments, through which the students can master the knowledge taught in the class. This course does not require the students to have any prior knowledge of RL. However, the students should be familiar with probability theory, calculus, and linear algebra, and also be familiar with at least one programming language such as Python, R, or Matlab.

教学目标

通过本课程，学生将掌握强化学习的基本概念和算法。他们将了解强化学习的数学基础，包括贝尔曼方程、贝尔曼最优方程和价值/策略迭代方法。他们将掌握强化学习中的经典算法，包括蒙特卡洛、时间差分、函数逼近、策略梯度、演员-评论家等算法。他们也将能够通过编程实现这些算法并将其应用于解决具体的强化学习任务。

教学方式

课程简介（中文）

Schur函数是数学中的一个基本研究对象，它最初来源于代数组合中对称函数的一组典范基，后来被发现在其他数学对象中也有体现，包括但不限于置换群不可约表示的特征标，复Grassmannian中的Schubert簇的对偶基，以及代数几何中ample向量丛上数值正多项式的基底等。本课程计划讲述上述相关基础知识和必要的背景知识。

课程简介（英文）

Schur functions are a fundamental object of study in mathematics. They originally arose as a canonical basis for symmetric functions in algebraic combinatorics, and were subsequently found to manifest in various other mathematical contexts. These include, but are not limited to, the characters of irreducible representations of the symmetric group, the dual bases of Schubert varieties in the complex Grassmannian, and the bases of numerically positive polynomials on ample vector bundles in algebraic geometry. This course is designed to cover these fundamental concepts along with the necessary background knowledge.

教学目标

让学生掌握对称函数基本事实，尤其与Schur函数有关事实；掌握置换群不可约表示的特征标表达式；掌握复Grassmannian流形上的Schubert胞腔剖分和其上同调的乘积公式；掌握ample向量丛上数值正向量丛的基本事实和相关应用。

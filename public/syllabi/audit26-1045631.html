课程简介（中文）

妇产科学一门研究女性各个时期生殖系统病理、生理及妊娠相关的生理和的学科，本课程的主要阐述妇产科学基础、女性生殖系统疾病等相关内容。深入浅出、简明扼要的讲述妇产科常见病及多发病，并注意结合临床，通过大课及示教使学生掌握妇产科学的基本理论、基本知识和基本技能，掌握妇产科常见病、多发病的发生发展规律、诊治方法及妇女保健、计划生育措施，另外，积极介绍妇产科学临床与科研的相关前沿及进展。传播妇产科医患沟通、生育保育、家庭关系、身体隐私、生殖伦理等医德医风和人文教学内容，提升人文精神、人类全生命周期等学习兴趣。建立正确的职业价值观和较高的综合素质，做患者信任的好医生。根据教学计划，妇产科学五年制课程教学周数16周，理论课46学时，见习示教课30学时。

课程简介（英文）

Obstetrics and Gynecology is the medical discipline dedicated to the study of the female reproductive system, encompassing its physiology and pathology throughout all stages of life, as well as the physiology of pregnancy and childbirth. This course primarily covers the fundamentals of the field and diseases of the female reproductive system.

 

The course is designed to provide a clear and concise overview of common and frequent diseases in obstetrics and gynecology, with a strong emphasis on clinical relevance. Through a combination of large-group lectures and clinical demonstrations, students will acquire a solid grasp of the fundamental theories, knowledge, and clinical skills. They will learn the progression, diagnostic methods, and treatment protocols for common gynecological and obstetrical conditions, as well as women’s healthcare and family planning measures. Furthermore, the course will actively introduce students to the latest advancements and cutting-edge research in both the clinical and scientific fields of obstetrics and gynecology.

 

An integral component of the course is the integration of medical ethics and humanities. It addresses key topics such as doctor-patient communication, childbirth and childcare, family dynamics, bodily privacy, and reproductive ethics. This content aims to cultivate students’ appreciation for humanistic principles and a holistic understanding of the human life cycle. The ultimate objective is to foster sound professional values and well-rounded competencies, preparing students to become compassionate and trustworthy physicians.

 

As per the curriculum, the Obstetrics and Gynecology course is conducted over a 16-week period, comprising 46 hours of lectures and 30 hours of clinical clerkship and demonstrations.

教学目标

通过对大纲中妇产科学基础及临床的课堂讲授及临床示教，掌握有关妇产科常见病、多发病的病因、临床特点及诊治原则的基础知识、基本理论；初步了解和掌握妇产科临床基本操作技能，了解妇产科学的相关新进展。为医学生今后从事临床妇产科和其他临床学科的工作奠定理论基础和实践基础。加强妇产科人文医学教育和医德医风教育，弘扬医者精神的“真”“善”“美”，助力培养守初心、铸信念、强人文、有大爱的创新型卓越医学人才。

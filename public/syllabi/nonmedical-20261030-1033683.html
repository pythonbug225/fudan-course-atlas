中华经典与美典

课程简介（中文）

经典与美典既各有偏重，又多互通互融，共同成为中国特色的文学典范。该课程旨在通过解读中华文学典籍文本之美及其所蕴含的传统文化内核，从中汲取智慧力量、接受美感熏陶。在具体实施中，注重以点带面，即通过代表性文本选讲，进而领会这些文学典籍蕴涵的天地共生、物我一体、感应会通、道法自然等审美体验和美学观念。《诗经》中的“琴瑟友之”、《庄子》中的“庄生梦蝶”，《楚辞》中的“香草美人”、《史记》中的“桃李不言”、《洛神赋》中的“惊鸿一瞥”、《陶渊明集》中的“桃源仙境”、《兰亭序》中的“畅叙幽怀”、《春江花月夜》中的“花月情根”、《杜工部集》中的“秋日登高”、《东坡乐府》中的“一蓑烟雨”、《稼轩词》中的“青山妩媚”、《西厢记》中的“长亭别恨”、《三国演义》中的“三顾茅庐”、《水浒传》中的“风雪山神庙”、《西游记》中的“真假美猴王”、《牡丹亭》中“游园惊梦”、《聊斋志异》中的“花妖狐媚”、《红楼梦》中“宝黛共读”。讲授中注重以小见大，引领学生进入名著原典胜境，饱览美感情境，以期学习中乐此不疲，流连忘返。

课程简介（英文）

Classics and esthetic classics have their own emphasis, but they are also interconnected and integrated, jointly becoming a literary model with Chinese characteristics.This course aims to draw wisdom and aesthetic influence from interpreting the beauty of Chinese literary classics and the traditional cultural essence they contain. In specific implementation, emphasis is placed on using points to guide the surface, that is, selecting representative texts to explain, and then understanding the aesthetic experiences and concepts contained in these literary classics, such as the coexistence of heaven and earth, the unity of things and ourselves, the integration of sensing and communication, and the natural Dao. In the Book of Songs, there is the concept of "friendship between qin and se"; in Zhuangzi, there is the concept of "Zhuangzi dreaming of butterflies"; in Chu Ci, there is the concept of "fragrant grass beauty"; in Records of the Grand Historian, there is the concept of "peach and plum blossoms speak for themselves"; in the poem "Ode to the Luo God", there is the concept of "a glimpse of a startled red eye"; in Tao Yuanming's collection, there is the concept of "a fairyland in the Peach Garden"; in Preface to the Orchid Pavilion, there is the concept of "leisurely reminiscence"; in Spring River, there is the concept of "flower and moon love roots"; in Du Gongbu's collection, there is the concept of "climbing high in autumn"; in Dongpo's Yuefu, there is the concept of "a misty rain"; in Jiaxuan The phrase 'Farewell to Changting' in 'Records of the Three Kingdoms',' Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage 'in' Romance of the Three Kingdoms', 'Temple of Snowy Mountain' in 'Water Margin', 'True and False Monkey King' in 'Journey to the West', 'Dream in the Garden' in 'Peony Pavilion', 'Flower Demon Fox Charm' in 'Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio', and 'Dream of the Red Chamber' Read together with Baodai. In teaching, emphasis is placed on seeing the big picture from the small, leading students into the realm of classic literature, enjoying the beauty of emotions, in order to never tire of it and linger on.

教学目标

该课程旨在通过解读中华文学典籍文本之美及其所蕴含的传统文化内核，从中汲取智慧力量、接受美感熏陶。 具体说，就是通过文学经典之美的讲解，一方面让学生获取知识，提高文学素养和鉴赏能力，另一方面，净化心灵，陶冶情操，感悟人生哲理，提升人生境界。

教学方式

讲授为主，讨论为辅助

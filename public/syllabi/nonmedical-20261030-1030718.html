教学目标

This course is primarily designed for J-school’s undergraduate students but equally beneficial for postgraduates specializing in journalism or communication studies. It serves as a foundation course for reading and critique of English Journalism (including printing, broadcasting and digital journalism). It aims to empower students with essential skills in reading English journalism, as well as basic knowledge on journalism genres and their institutional characters. Unlike similar courses it focuses not only how to read the texts of English journalism, more importantly, the institutional context that governs or underpins content production.

教学方式

Lectures、reading and seminars/discussions

课程简介（中文）

本课程将由四轮训练组成，每轮训练都由作业、讲课、讨论课三个部分组成。共4本原版书或者期刊上的章节/文章入选本课程。三轮训练之后，学生将自行挑选最符合自己智力模式的书本，全文阅读并写一份书评。

课程简介（英文）

This course will cover 4 texts, either from an English book or journal. Focus will be laid upon analyzing the structure and writing style of different types of tourism literature. Regular assignments and corresponding seminars, with 1 assignment and 1 seminar for each text, are designed to accompany regular lectures. Towards the end of the term, students will be encouraged to focus upon his/ her favorite book and write a reading review.

教学目标

This course aims at exposing students to a great variety of tourism literature which were written in different styles, with different purposes and orienting at different target readers. How to grasp the overall structure, how to follow the underlying logic and how to appreciate the writer’s strength are the major objectives of this English reading course. Vocabulary building and sentence structure will be treated as supplementary objectives if necessary.

教学方式

Pre-view assignments will precede each round of training. Lecture and seminar will be main teaching methods.

课程简介（中文）

本课程为旅游科学硕士的学术训练课程。讲课配套作业，循序渐进，以打好扎实的学术训练基础。旅游学术顶级期刊的英文论文是本课程的主要教学素材，泛读以及精读为主要的训练方式。

课程简介（英文）

This course serves as academic training of tourism graduates. A step by step approach will be applied. Regular assignments are measurements of each step of training. English papers from top academic journals will be read both extensively and intensively.

教学目标

This course aims at academic training via English. Reading is the top priority of this course. Students are required to do quite a few reading tasks by themselves before finishing each assignment. By the end of this training course, students would be able to: search for and find academic data for their research read academic papers in English do academic discussion & presentation in English

教学方式

Lecture and student presentation are main teaching methods.

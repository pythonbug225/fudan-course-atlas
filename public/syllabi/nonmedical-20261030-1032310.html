课程简介（中文）

【授课语言：英文】 本课程侧重于研究技术变革、创新、研发（R&D）及其对经济发展和公共政策的影响。本课程面向硕士及博士研究生，采用研讨会（Seminar）模式，并包含深度的文献综述。通过本课程的学习，学生将理解技术创新的决定因素及其产生的后果，并将其与企业战略及政府政策相结合。学生将学习从国家政府的角度分析创新议题，并培养分析与制定相关公共政策的专业技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the economics of technological change, innovation, research and development, and its implications for economic development and public policy. Aimed at both Master and PhD students, this will be a seminar-style course with intensive literature review. Through this course, students will understand the determinants and consequences of technological innovation, and link it to firm strategy and government policy. Students will learn to analyze issues of innovation from the vantage point of a national government and develop skills for analyzing and formulating related public policy.

教学目标

The primary objective of this course is to provide students with a rigorous theoretical and empirical foundation in the economics of innovation. By the end of the semester, students will be able to: Synthesize complex literature regarding the determinants and impacts of technological change on economic development. Critically analyze the interplay between firm-level R&D strategies and macroeconomic growth. Evaluate and formulate innovation policies from a national government perspective, utilizing evidence-based frameworks. Develop advanced research skills through intensive seminar discussions and literature reviews, preparing them for high-level academic research or professional policy analysis.

教学方式

Seminars. Students will be required to actively participate in every aspect of this course. This includes regular (and on-time) attendance of the lectures, reading assigned readings before each lecture, and active participation in class discussions.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Introduction Week 2 Innovation Process Week 3 Supply and Demand for Innovation Week 4 Appropriation Mechanisms -Intro to IP Week 5 Innovation and Competition Week 6 Diffusion Week 7 Innovation Strategy Week 8 Innovation Policy Week 9 Patent and Economic Development Week 10 Strategy and Policy Alternatives to IP Week 11 Strategic (Mis)use of IP Week 12 Student Presentations

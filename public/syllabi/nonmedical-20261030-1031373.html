课程简介（中文）

反常层是一类层的复形，其在奇异空间的研究中扮演了重要的角色，且在代数几何，算术几何，几何表示论中均有重大的应用。本课程中我们将介绍复代数簇上反常层的基础理论，包括可构造层的导出范畴和六函子，反常层的概念和基本性质，相交上同调复形，分解定理，以及nearby/vanishing cycles。我们还将简要介绍其在表示论中的应用，例如Springer理论，Kazhdan-Lusztig理论，和几何Satake等价。

课程简介（英文）

Perverse sheaves are certain family of complexes of sheaves that play important role in the study of singular spaces. They have also found significant applications in algebraic geometry, arithmetic geometry, and geometric representation theory. In this course, we introduce the basic theory of perverse sheaves on complex algebraic varieties. We will set up derived categories and six functor formalisms for constructible sheaves, introduce the notion of perverse sheaves and their basic properties, intersection cohomology complexes, decomposition theorem, and nearby/vanishing cycles. We will also briefly introduce some applications in representation theory such as Springer theory, Kazhdan-Lusztig theory, and Geometric Satake equivalence.

教学目标

本课程向学习过基础代数几何的学生介绍反常层的理论，为其进入算术几何、几何表示论等科研领域打好基础。通过本课程的学习，学生将熟悉局部系统和可构造层的定义与例子，能够使用六函子理论，熟悉反常层的定义、例子、性质和判别方法，并了解反常层在几何表示论中的部分应用，能够计算基本的例子。

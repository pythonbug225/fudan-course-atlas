金融衍生工具

2025秋 · MF30009.01 · 童国士

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

系统介绍金融衍生工具，包括相关市场的运作机制，衍生工具的价格决定和交易策略，也包含其在风险管理中的应用。讲授的工具包括远期，期货，互换和期权等。其中期货和互换市场的基本功能和运作机制，以及期权的定价和交易策略是本课程的重点内容。 本课程希望为学生日后从事金融领域的实务工作和学习衍生品高阶课程打下基础。

课程简介（英文）

We discuss a wide range of derivative instruments including forward and future contracts, swaps and options. We emphasize intuition as well as practical consideration in applying derivatives securities in speculation and risk management, as well as reading market consensus from derivatives prices.

教学目标

The main objective of this course is to help students gain the intuition and to provide the necessary skills for pricing and hedging of derivative securities, and for using them for investment, risk management, and prediction purposes.

教学方式

Lectures, Lab practices, Guest speakers and tours to China Financial Future Exchange

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture 1: Introduction to derivatives market Lecture 2: Forward and future contracts Lecture 3: Futures market and central clearing Lecture 4: Heding strategies using future contracts Lecture 5: Determination of forward and future prices Lecture 6: Swaps Lecture 7: Guided tours to China Financial Futures Exchange Lecture 8: Midterm quiz (In-class) Lecture 9: Mechanics of options markets Lecture 10: Properties of stock options and trading stratgies Lecture 11: Black Scholes Merton pricing model Lecture 12: Options on stock indices and currencies Lecture 13: Futures options Lecture 14: The Greek letters Lecture 15: Financial lab: mock trading Lecture 16: Final quiz

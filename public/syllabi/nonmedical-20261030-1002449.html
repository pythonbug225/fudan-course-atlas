课程简介（中文）

本课程是理论学习与实践展示的综合性音乐课程，在课程中将学习中国音乐记谱法体系中工尺谱等音乐知识，通过讨论、展演、编创等多种形式，从音乐、舞蹈、美术、文学、历史等多维度地去理解、体验、展示中国诗词之美，提高音乐审美能力与综合艺术素养。在浸润式、全方位的学习中，丰富学生的精神世界，塑造美好人格，增进学生的民族文化觉醒与自信，一起参与到传承、传播中华传统文化。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a comprehensive art course that combines theoretical study with practical demonstrations. Throughout the course, students will learn about Chinese ancient musical notation systems, such as Gongche notation, and explore the beauty of Chinese poetry through various forms including discussions, performances, and creative compositions. By integrating music, dance, fine arts, literature, and history, students will gain a multidimensional understanding and appreciation of Chinese poetry, enhancing their musical aesthetics and overall artistic literacy. Through immersive and holistic learning experiences, this course aims to enrich students' spiritual world, nurture their character, and foster a deeper cultural awareness and confidence in their national heritage. Together, students will participate in the preservation and dissemination of traditional Chinese culture.

教学目标

本课程是理论学习与实践展示的综合性音乐课程，在课程中将学习中国音乐记谱法体系中工尺谱等音乐知识，通过讨论、演唱、舞蹈等多种形式，更好理解表达中国诗词歌曲，提高音乐审美能力与综合艺术素养。课程将传唱诗词与传承中华传统相结合，坚持文学、史学、音乐、美术相结合，具有中国诗歌教育特色的课程。

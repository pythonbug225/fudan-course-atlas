课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍政策分析与项目评估的基本概念、理论框架与实践方法，重点培养学生在现实政策情境中理解、评估和分析公共政策与项目的能力。课程围绕评估理论基础、核心分析工具以及真实政策案例的应用展开，帮助学生掌握当代政策评估所需的关键知识与方法。通过阅读、案例分析与实践训练，学生将学习如何将评估理论与方法应用于实际问题，为从事公共管理、政策分析及相关职业奠定扎实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the basic concepts, theories, and practices of policy analysis and program evaluation. It focuses on developing students’ ability to understand, assess, and analyze public policies and programs in real-world contexts. The course integrates theoretical foundations, core evaluation tools, and applications to practical policy problems. Through readings, case studies, and applied exercises, students will learn how to use evaluation theories and methods to inform policy decisions and professional practice in public affairs and related fields.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students will be able to understand the core concepts and theoretical foundations of policy analysis and program evaluation, apply key evaluation methods and analytical tools, design and assess evaluation strategies in concrete policy contexts, and interpret evaluation results to inform policy judgment and professional decision-making.

教学方式

The course combines lectures with class discussion, centered on assigned readings. It emphasizes the integration of theory and methods through case discussions, empirical examples, and student presentations. Students are expected to complete readings before class and actively participate in discussions to strengthen their understanding and application of policy analysis and program evaluation.

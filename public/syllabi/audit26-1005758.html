课程简介（中文）

本课程是讲授现代前沿寄生虫研究知识的一门医学课程。课程由研究生主讲。旨在讨论人体寄生虫病的病原体本质，促进寄生虫病的诊断、防治等的研究，提高研究生文献阅读能力和锻炼思维逻辑能力，是医学研究生的专业选修课。

课程简介（英文）

The course is a medical professional course which is taught by graduate students. The purpose of this course is to discuss the pathogenesis of human parasitic diseases, promote the research of diagnosis and prevention of parasitic diseases, and improve the ability of graduate students to read papers and exercise their logical thinking ability. It is a professional degree course for medical graduate students.

教学目标

本课程主要由研究生讨论最新寄生虫的致病、免疫、分子诊断与防治等文献，着重于最新的寄生虫研究热点，最新的寄生虫研究技术等。通过对上述前沿文献展开讨论学习，培养研究生的创新思维能力。

The course mainly discusses the latest literatures on pathogenicity, immunity, molecular diagnosis and control of parasites, focusing on the latest research hotspots and the latest research techniques of parasites. Through studying the frontier literature mentioned above, so as to cultivate graduate students' innovative thinking ability.

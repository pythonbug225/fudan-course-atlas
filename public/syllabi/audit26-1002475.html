课程简介（中文）

医学遗传学是医学科学的基础课程。课程将讨论单基因疾病、多基因疾病、染色体疾病和线粒体疾病。内容还包括遗传病的诊断、治疗和会诊。同时，学生将会通过相关实验得到基本遗传学实验技术/技能的训练。

课程简介（英文）

Medical genetics is a basic course of medical science. We will discuss monogenic diseases, polygenic diseases, chromosomal disease and mitochondrial diseases. The contents also include the diagnosis, therapy and consultation of genetic diseases. Meanwhile，the training of basic genetics techniques/skills by laboratory experiments is also concerned.

教学目标

Learning Purpose：Students are required to master the phenotype, the pathology, and the genetic discipline. Treat genetic diseases with the genetic theory and the method of human genetics. The important content includes monogenic diseases, polygenic diseases, chromosomal disease and mitochondrial diseases.

Capability Purpose：Proficient in the using of human genetics knowledge to explain the occurrence of inheritance diseases and possible methods that can be taken, and can establish scientific research ideas to explore the pathogenesis, prognosis and intervention methods.

Ideological and political Purpose：to cultivate medical humanistic qualities and the scientific research spirit of courage to explore and make breakthroughs.

教学方式

Lectures, combined with laboratory teaching, self-test exercises, problem discussions, after-class exercises, homework or reports.

教学内容与进度安排

Lectures 
Week 9     Introduction of medical genetics / Gene mutation    (2 classes) （teacher：Gao shenglan）
1、Master the concept of point mutation, frame-shift mutation, dynamic mutation, the mechanism of mutation repair. 
2、Familiar with the causes of mutation.

Week10     Chromosome disorder  (2 classes) （teacher：Gao shenglan）
1、Master the concept of chromosome number aberrations, chromosome rearrangements, the mechanism of aberration chromosome forming.
2、Familiar with the clinic cases of chromosome aberrations

Week11         Single gene disorder   (2 classes) （teacher：Gao shenglan）
1、Master the concept of AD, AR, XD, XR, and Y-linked inheritance. the features of AD, AR, XD, XR, and Y-linked inheritance
2、Familiar with the clinic cases of single gene disorders.

Week12     Polygene disorder  (2 classes) （teacher：Gao shenglan）
1、Master the concept of susceptibility, threshold, liability, heritability. the features of Polygene disorders
2、Familiar with the clinic cases of Polygene disorders.

Week13     Mitochondrial disease (2 classes) （teacher：Gao shenglan）
1、Master the concept of maternal inheritance, genetic bottleneck effect, the features of Mitochondrial diseases. 
2、Familiar with the clinic cases of Mitochondrial diseases.
         
Week14     Genetics and cancer  (2 classes) （teacher：Wang yongbo）
1、Master the concept of oncogene, proto-oncogene, anti-oncogene, two–hit hypothesis, 
2、Familiar with the relationship between genetics and cancer

Week15     Birth defect  (2 classes) （teacher：Zhu shun）

1、Master the classification of birth defect, the common techniques of birth defect diagnosis. 
2、Familiar with the causes of birth defect

Week16     Student presentation  (2 classes) 
Master the skill of references searching, ppt making and presentation.

Lab experiments
Week11      Lab  Preparation of chromosome samples  (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Master the methods of isolating chromosome samples by hypotonic solution & fixing chromosome samples by methanol- acetic acid mixture. 

Week12   Lab  G banding chromosome staining   (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Master the methods of Giemsa staining. 
Observe the morphological features of G-banding chromosome.

Week 13    Lab     DNA extraction & PCR  (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Master the methods of DNA extraction. 
Learn how to run PCR.

Week 14   Lab     Analysis of gene mutation  (4 classes) （teacher：Yang ling）
Master the methods of examining PCR results by restriction enzyme analysis. 
Master the methods of examining PCR results by agarose gel electrophoresis.

课程简介（中文）

课程涵盖流行病学的基本原理，探讨重大公共卫生问题的决定因素，包括传染病、伤害、环境健康以及新兴威胁。学生将接受实际流行病学能力的培训，通过现场调查和公共卫生监测等研究设计，运用所学知识和技能解决现实中的公共卫生问题。课程还将讲授偏倚与混杂的原理，使学生能够全面掌握所有关键的流行病学概念。

课程简介（英文）

The course covers the basic principles of epidemiology, examining the determinants of major public health problem including infectious diseases, injuries, environmental health, and emerging threats. Students are trained to develop applied epidemiology competencies in field investigation and public health surveillance by using study designs where they apply their knowledge and skills to solve real life public health problems. Student will also address the principles of bias and confounding, thereby enabling them to familiarize themselves with all key epidemiological concepts.

教学目标

By completion of this course, students should be able to:

1.Identify major landmarks in the history of the discipline and understand its definition and broad scope；

2.Calculate and interpret measures of disease frequency such as prevalence and incidence, mortality, morbidity and understand their inter-relationship；

3.Identify the major types of study designs within observational (e.g. ecological, cross-sectional, cohort, case-control) and experimental (e.g. randomized controlled trials, cross-over trials) epidemiological studies, and compare their strengths and limitations；

4.Calculate and interpret various measures of association such as relative risk (risk ratio, rate ratio, odds ratio), attributable risk (risk difference, rate difference), attributable risk percent and population attributable risk;

5.Identify the major sources of bias in epidemiological studies and describe their potential effects on measures of association;

6.Understand the concepts of confounding, effect modification and mediation, and be able to identify and describe their potential effects on measures of association;

7.Distinguish association from causation;

8.Critically appraise published individual epidemiological studies using a logical framework to ascertain their internal and external validity;

9.Demonstrate understanding of the inter-relationships between host, agent, and environment in infectious disease epidemiology;

10.Demonstrate understanding of the epidemiologic rationale and recognize the relative health benefits of the main strategies for prevention (‘high-risk’ vs. ‘mass’);

11. Identify the requirements that a screening program must fulfil before it can be considered for possible public health application;

12.Recognize the future directions and current challenges in epidemiology.

教学方式

This course included various lectures, in-class discussion activities, exercises, case studies and homework assignment to help the student apply what they have learned from the course in class.

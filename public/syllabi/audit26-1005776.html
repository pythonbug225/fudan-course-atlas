课程简介（中文）

组织透明化及三维成像技术通过对大型、复杂、异质的医学或生物样品进行主动或被动透明，借助内源性或外源性特异敏感的荧光信号，快速、高通量、高清的光片显微镜获取整体组织器官、或全身系统的三维图像，突破二维切片对神经、血管、淋巴等线性结构的观察难点，并可对长程神经纤维投射、突触连接、蛋白/mRNA/DNA 的空间分布和不同物质间的相互作用等进行多维观察和大数据计算。

本课程涉及医学、化学、光学、计算机硬件、软件、艺术加工制作等学科综合应用和多学科交叉，为医学生进行形态学研究提高世界前沿技术手段，推动精准医疗研究和研发。

课程简介（英文）

Tissue clearing and 3D imaging is a novel technology, which obtains 3D images of the entire tissue organ or systemic system by actively or passively transparency in large, complex, and heterogeneous medical or biological samples. And endogenous or exogenous specific sensitive fluorescence signals, as well as the fast, high-throughput, high-definition light microscopy are uesed to  break through the observation difficulties of linear structures such as nerves, blood vessels, and lymph nodes in 2D slices, and enabling multidimensional observation and big data calculation,especially for  nerve fiber projections, synaptic connections, spatial distribution of proteins/mRNA/DNA, and interactions between different substances.

This course involves the comprehensive application and interdisciplinary intersection of medicine, chemistry, optics, computer, softwares, art processing and video production, etc. It aims to improve the world's cutting-edge technological means for medical students to conduct morphological research and promote precision medicine research and development.

教学目标

了解和掌握世界前沿的三维和四维形态学技术发展；从系统宏观的角度了解复杂医学组织或器官的结构、病理变化和相互关系；在此基础上，从全新视角发掘功能上的潜在联系，最终达到提高本科生、研究生科研应用水平的目的。

教学方式

以课堂理论授课和实验操作相结合。

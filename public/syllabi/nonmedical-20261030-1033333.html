算法治理

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程以算法治理（Algorithmic Governance）与智能治理（AI-enabled Governance）为核心分析框架，系统解读以大模型、算法决策系统、平台化治理和算力基础设施为代表的新一轮数字与智能技术革命，如何重塑国家能力、公共权力运行方式、社会结构、经济组织形态与规范秩序，并深入探讨算法嵌入治理过程所引发的权力再分配、制度张力与治理范式转型问题。 课程紧密结合全球最前沿的真实案例与政策实验，涵盖算法执法、智能城市、平台监管、自动化决策、生成式AI公共应用等关键议题，通过跨国比较与制度分析，对数字化转型中的“技术乐观主义”与“技术恐慌论”进行冷静反思，帮助学生在理解算法与模型基本原理的基础上，系统识别其治理潜能、结构性风险与长期制度后果。 在理论与实践互动中，课程将对比分析欧美算法治理与AI监管趋势（如风险分级治理、算法问责、可解释性制度、算力与数据主权）与中国在数字治理与智能治理中的独特实践路径，探讨不同政治制度和治理传统下算法嵌入公共治理的差异化逻辑，推动对“算法向善”“负责任AI”与“数字善治”的系统性理解与本土化理论建构。 课程采用高度互动与可视化的教学方式，配备前沿、审美与信息密度并重的高质量教学PPT，融合政策文本、数据可视化、真实系统界面与情景化讨论，力求做到好看、好玩、有料，同时引导学生形成面向未来治理挑战的批判性思维与战略性判断能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course utilizes Algorithmic Governance and AI-enabled Governance as its core analytical frameworks to systematically interpret the new wave of digital and intelligent technological revolution—represented by Large Language Models (LLMs), algorithmic decision-making systems, platform governance, and computing infrastructure. It explores how these technologies are reshaping state capacity, the exercise of public power, social structures, economic organization, and normative orders. Furthermore, the course delves into the redistribution of power, institutional tensions, and the transformation of governance paradigms triggered by the embedding of algorithms into the governance process. Integrating cutting-edge global case studies and policy experiments, the curriculum covers critical issues such as algorithmic law enforcement, smart cities, platform regulation, automated decision-making, and public applications of Generative AI. Through cross-national comparisons and institutional analysis, the course offers a balanced reflection on “technological optimism” and “technico-panic” within the digital transition. It aims to help students understand the fundamental principles of algorithms and models while systematically identifying their governance potential, structural risks, and long-term institutional consequences. Through the interaction of theory and practice, the course provides a comparative analysis of Western (EU/US) algorithmic governance and AI regulatory trends—including risk-based governance, algorithmic accountability, explainability frameworks, and data/computing sovereignty—against China’s unique practical path in digital and intelligent governance. It examines the differentiated logics of algorithmic integration within various political systems and traditions, fostering a systemic understanding and localized theoretical construction of “AI for Good,” “Responsible AI,” and “Digital Good Governance.” The course employs a highly interactive and visual teaching approach, featuring high-quality lecture materials that prioritize aesthetics, information density, and cutting-edge insights. By merging policy texts, data visualization, real system interfaces, and situational discussions, the course strives to be engaging, immersive, and substantive (Good-looking, Fun, and Content-rich), ultimately guiding students to develop critical thinking and strategic judgment for future governance challenges.

教学目标

帮助学生认识AI、算法、算法治理，提升数字治理素养； 引导学生结合案例讨论和自身业务领域，重新思考数字化转型； 提升学生对数字化转型风险和困境的认知，提供思考和分析框架； 探寻新技术环境下的政治社会善治之道； 将数字业务实践、学术研究与论文写作相结合； 让您比身边人多思考一步、看得更远一程。

教学方式

1. 课堂老师主讲：理论+前沿+技术+场景+趋势 2. 新技术分析与新案例探讨 3. 小组研究和课题展示 4. 课题辩论赛（例如，是否存在AI泡沫）

教学内容与进度安排

1． 数字、算法与AI技术前沿 2． 数字、算法与AI国家战略 3． 数字、算法与AI与政府转型 4． 数字、算法、AI与分配政治 5． 数字、算法、AI与监管政治 6． 数字、算法、AI与选举政治 7． 数字、算法、AI与信息政治 8． 数字、算法、AI与算力发展 9． 数字、算法、AI与国家能力 10. 数字、算法、AI与国际政治 11. 数字、算法、AI与政治未来

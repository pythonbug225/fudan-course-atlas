课程简介（中文）

本课程是管理科学领域的一门选修课。博弈论提供了一个数学框架，用于分析多个决策者之间的策略互动，其中任何个体的结果不仅取决于其自身的选择，也取决于他人的选择。博弈论的应用遍及政治学、经济学、生物学和计算机科学等多个领域，为广泛的实际问题提供了宝贵的见解。本课程旨在全面介绍博弈论。学生将学习使用博弈论的正式语言来描述策略情境，识别关键的策略考量，并应用核心博弈论概念来预测行为。课程结束时，学生应能分析和解决具有不同复杂程度的博弈。课程内容围绕四大类博弈展开：完全信息静态博弈、完全信息动态博弈、不完全信息静态博弈以及不完全信息动态博弈。与这四类博弈相对应的则是四个均衡概念：纳什均衡、子博弈完美纳什均衡、贝叶斯纳什均衡和完美贝叶斯均衡。在整个课程中，我们将借助大量与平台经济相关的例子来阐释和应用这些基础理论。

课程简介（英文）

This subject is an elective course within the field of management science. Game theory provides a mathematical framework for analyzing strategic interactions among multiple decision-makers, where the outcome for any individual depends not only on their own choices but also on the choices made by others. With applications spanning politics, economics, biology, and computer science, game theory offers valuable insights into a wide range of real-world situations. This course aims to provide a comprehensive introduction to game theory. Students will learn to formally describe strategic situations using the language of game theory, identify key strategic considerations, and predict behavior by applying core game-theoretic concepts. By the end of the course, students are expected to be capable of analyzing and solving games of varying complexity. The curriculum is organized around four main classes of games: static games of complete information, dynamic games of complete information, static games of incomplete information, and dynamic games of incomplete information. Corresponding to these are four equilibrium concepts: Nash equilibrium, subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium, Bayesian Nash equilibrium, and perfect Bayesian equilibrium. Throughout the course, we will draw on numerous examples related to the platform economy to illustrate and apply these foundational ideas.

教学目标

Upon completing this course, students will be able to: Formally describe strategic situations using the precise language of game theory. Identify key strategic considerations and accurately predict behavior in games by applying core game-theoretic concepts. Analyze and solve complex games by synthesizing and applying the principles learned throughout the course.

教学方式

Lectures:Instructors will deliver core theories and illustrate their applications through real-world examples and case studies. In-class Discussions:Students will engage in guided discussions on thought-provoking topics. This interactive format encourages active participation, peer learning, and deeper exploration of complex issues. Homework Assignments:Regular assignments will require students to apply classroom techniques to practical problems, providing valuable hands-on experience and reinforcing conceptual understanding.

2025秋 · LAW30001.01 · 高凌云

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程教学的基本内容主要包括对英美合同法的基本理论和案例的学习以及对有关英美普通法系的主要法律制度简介。教学围绕英文原版案例的理解、学习和掌握及英文听说读写的训练，使学生得以系统学习普通法系的主要法律理论、判例与审判实践等。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to cover the basic theories and cases in the area of common law of contracts through providing the students opportunities of analyzing cases, in-class discussions, and/or mock trial practices, and facilitation for the students to learn the main legal theories of litigation and trial practices of the common law system.

教学目标

通过学习本课程，学生能系统了解英美普通法中的合同法律制度，掌握英文法律词汇，提高阅读英文法律原著的能力及用英文听说读写的能力。本课程通过案例分析及课堂讨论，培养学生用英文理解法律以及分析问题和解决问题的能力。 Through taking this course, the students are expected to learn legal terminologies, enhance the ability of comprehending legal English, and understand the common law of contracts in a systematic way. The students will be trained to use English as a tool to understand the law and to be capable of analyzing and resolving legal problems.

教学方式

学生课前预习，任课老师课堂讲授知识点，学生参与讨论，回答提问，并在课堂上以小组为单位进行案例分析讨论与展示。课堂上严禁学生使用任何设备进行录音、录像、拍摄等活动。

教学内容与进度安排

教学内容安排 (共计18周，含考试周；建议具体到每周或每节课教学内容): Topic 1（第1周） 1. Introduction to common law system; US Court system 2. Legal Proceedings Topic 2（第2-4周） 1. Contracts/Classification of Contracts 2. Elements of Offer; Invitation to Offer; Termination of Offer 3. Multiple opinions（第2周） Topic 3 （第5-7周） 1. Acceptance 2. Consideration 3. Review: Formation of contract Topic 4（第8-10周） 1. Defenses: Misrepresentation 2. Defenses: Duress 3. Defenses: Undue influence 4. Defenses: Mistake 5. Defenses: Capacity / Illegality 6. Defenses: Statute of Frauds / Parol Evidence Rule Topic 5（第11-12周） 1. Review: Case Brief 2. Rights of TP; Assignment; Performance and Breach; Remedies [每周四节课集中授课，具体根据学生接受程度进行调整]

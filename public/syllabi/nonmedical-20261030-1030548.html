课程简介（中文）

第二语言习得是应用语言学领域发展迅猛的研究方向。本课程旨在帮助学生全面把握学界第二语言习得研究的当前范畴与动态。课程不仅涵盖该领域已趋成熟的研究方向，还将深入探讨第二语言习得的前沿的另类的研究路径—— 此处的 “另类” 是指这些路径与该领域主流的认知范式形成对照，或对其构成补充。同时，本课程还将培养学生批判性研读已发表研究论文、梳理相关文献，以及掌握研究设计方法的能力，这些能力对于学生未来开展硕士阶段的研究课题至关重要。

课程简介（英文）

Second Language Acquisition is a fast booming direction in applied linguistics. This course aims to provide the students with the scope of current SLA research in the academic field. The course will not only cover established directions in SLA research, but also will delve into alternative approaches to second language acquisition—“alternative” in the sense that they contrast with and/or complement the prevalent cognitive paradigm in the field. Students will also be trained to critically read published research papers, to summarize relevant literature, and to grasp the research design techniques that are crucial to the prospective M.A. research project.

教学目标

At the end of the course, students are expected to: Identify different research traditions in SLA research Locate and find sources from the database Critically read published research papers and write effective summaries Distinguish different research methods, and develop critical awareness of research design Produce critical review of relevant literature

教学方式

Combination of lecture and seminar.

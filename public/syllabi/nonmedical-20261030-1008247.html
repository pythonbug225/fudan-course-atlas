发展过程中的风险管理

2025秋 · POLI70028.01 · 李寅

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是复旦大学、日本神户大学、韩国高丽大学联合开设的国际研讨课程，旨在探讨中日韩三国在发展进程中风险管理的理论与实践。课程采用跨学科视角，内容涵盖经济危机、社会风险、科技治理等多元议题。学生将通过与三国顶尖教授的共同学习以及参与联合学生会议，掌握前沿理论与政策分析技能，拓展国际视野。

课程简介（英文）

This international seminar course, jointly offered by Fudan University, Kobe University (Japan), and Korea University (Korea), explores the theory and practice of risk management within the development process of East Asia. It provides students with the theoretical frameworks and practical skills to analyze policies and manage organizations concerning a wide range of developmental risks. The curriculum is co-taught by faculty from all three universities, covering diverse topics such as economic crisis, social risk, and innovation governance, and culminates in a joint student conference.

教学目标

In line with Fudan University's commitment to fostering talent with a global perspective and cross-cultural competence, this course aims to prepare students to be future leaders capable of navigating complex risks in a globalized world. To cultivate a comparative and collaborative mindset by critically examining the diverse approaches to risk management in China, Japan, and Korea. This fosters an appreciation for cultural context and the importance of international cooperation in addressing shared regional and global challenges. To provide a multi-disciplinary understanding of key risks in the development process, including their political, economic, social, and technological dimensions. Students will grasp relevant theories and empirical realities within the comparative context of the three East Asian nations. To develop advanced skills in policy analysis, enabling students to critically evaluate risk management strategies. The course will enhance cross-cultural communication and academic presentation abilities through direct engagement with international faculty and participation in a joint student conference.

教学方式

To achieve its unique educational goals, the course is designed as an interactive seminar that emphasizes collaboration and active participation. The primary teaching methods include: Expert-led Seminars: Each topic will be introduced through interactive seminars led by faculty members from Fudan University, Kobe University, or Korea University, allowing students to learn directly from leading experts in their respective fields. Comparative Case Studies: The course will heavily utilize case studies from China, Japan, and Korea to analyze real-world events, such as financial crises, natural disasters, or public health emergencies. This method facilitates a deep, comparative understanding of policy responses. Student-led Discussions and Presentations: As a seminar course, students are expected to actively contribute to discussions, critically engage with reading materials, and present their analyses to the class, fostering a dynamic learning environment. International Guest Lectures: The course structure incorporates dedicated sessions taught by faculty from Kobe University and Korea University, providing students with direct access to diverse international academic perspectives. Joint Student Conference: The course culminates in a joint student conference involving participants from all three universities. This capstone event serves as a platform for students to present their research, receive feedback from an international panel of faculty, and practice formal academic exchange.

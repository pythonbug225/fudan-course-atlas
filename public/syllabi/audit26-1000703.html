课程简介（中文）

本课程聚焦跨文化传播与国际交流的复杂性，采用跨学科研究方法，系统探究大众传播的发展演变、功能转型、社会目的及其经济社会影响。学生将学会批判性分析技术因素（重点关注人工智能与现代信息技术）、文化认同、权力结构如何塑造传播实践及其社会效应。课程特别鼓励学生融合中国视角与全球南方国家的经验，从而形成对世界各国政治传播规范与实践的批判性认知。

通过理论框架与案例研究的有机结合，学生将掌握有效应对文化差异、开展跨群体传播的专业技能。课程还将深入探讨跨文化与国际政治传播领域的现实议题，包括全球媒体技术发展、语言障碍问题以及跨文化冲突等挑战。

课程简介（英文）

This course explores the complexities of communication across cultures and international borders. It will take an interdisciplinary approach to investigate the development, transformation, purposes, and socio-economic impacts of mass communication. Students will learn to critically analyze the role of technology (with a particular focus on AI and modern information technology), culture, identity, and power in shaping communication practices and their impacts on the general public. It will also encourage students to engage with perspectives from China and the wider global south to develop a critical and contextualized understanding of existing norms in and practices of political communication around the world. Through a mix of theoretical frameworks and case studies, students will develop skills to effectively navigate cultural differences and communicate with diverse audiences. The course will also examine current issues and challenges in intercultural and international political communication, including global media and technology, language barriers, and cross-cultural conflict. 

教学目标

  1. To critically examine the role of communication in shaping cultural values, identities, and power dynamics in both Western and non-Western contexts.
  2. To explore the challenges and opportunities of intercultural communication, especially in the era of globalization and rising global tensions.
  3. To develop an understanding of different communication models, theories, and practices from a Marxist perspective.
  4. To cultivate critical thinking skills and encourage students to think deeply about the intersections of culture, communication, and power.
  5. To improve communication skills, such as storytelling, public speech, and PR crisis management of students.

教学方式

Lecture (instructor-led) and seminar (student-led);

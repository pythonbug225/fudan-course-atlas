课程简介（中文）

本课程着重介绍几何群论的一个重要分支：CAT(0) cube complex. CAT(0) cube complex 近期在Ian Agol, Daniel Wise 等人的推动下，逐渐发展为几何群论的一个重要研究对象。他们两位也因为利用CAT(0) cube complex理论，解决了三维流形中的virtual Haken猜想，因此获得维布伦奖，并在世界数学家大会做报告。本课程将从CAT(0)几何的基础开始讲起，逐渐深入，介绍科研的最前沿动态。

课程简介（英文）

This course is going to focus on an important topic in geometric group theory: CAT(0) cube complex. By the work of Ian Agol, Daniel Wise and many other Mathematicians, CAT(0) cube complex has become an important research subject in the past decades. Agol and Wise used the theory of CAT(0) cube complexes to solve the virtual Haken Conjecture in 3-manifold topology. Due to this work, they won the Veblen prize and were invited by the ICM to give talks. This course with start with the basics of CAT(0) geometry, then we will develop the fundamentals of the theory and potentially the frontiers of current reserach.

教学目标

Understand the basics of CAT(0) geometry, CAT(0) cube complex. Learn the basic skills on how to cubulate a group and determine whether the resulting cube complex is CAT(0) and special.

教学方式

线下教学，课堂授课。

教学内容与进度安排

第一周：CAT(0) cube complex 简介 第二周：度量空间基础 第三--四周：CAT(0) space基础 第五--七周，Gromov's link condition 及cube complexes 第八--十周，超平面及半空间。 第十一--十三周，Cubulating Coxeter 群 第十四--十六，前沿课题如：CAT(0) cube 群的Tits alternative, special cube complex 等。

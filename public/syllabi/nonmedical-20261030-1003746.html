行为金融学

2025秋 · MF30006.01 · 鲁小萌

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

传统金融学建立在“理性人”假设与市场有效性理论之上，形成了一个在过去几十年中广泛应用的分析框架。然而，日益增长的实证证据和理论挑战显示，该范式在解释现实金融市场行为方面存在局限。 行为金融学正是在这一背景下发展起来的。它结合心理学、行为经济学与金融学的理论基础，探索投资者行为、资产定价偏差和公司金融决策中的非理性因素。本课程将系统介绍行为金融学的主要研究成果，包括认知偏差、过度自信、有限理性等概念，以及它们对市场波动、价格偏离和金融决策的影响。 本课程的设置目的在于介绍行为金融学的最新进展，并拓宽学生对现代金融学的理解。行为金融学将引入心理学和更符合现实的假设条件，用以指导和发展金融市场的理论。我们将研究行为金融学的视角如何对传统研究范式进行补充，并介绍行为金融背景下投资者的交易模式、资产价格行为以及公司金融行为。

课程简介（英文）

Traditional finance is built upon the assumptions of the “rational individual” and market efficiency, forming an analytical framework that has been widely applied over the past few decades. However, a growing body of empirical evidence and theoretical challenges has revealed the limitations of this paradigm in explaining real-world financial market behavior. Behavioral finance has emerged in this context. It integrates theories from psychology, behavioral economics, and finance to explore the irrational factors underlying investor behavior, asset pricing anomalies, and corporate financial decisions. This course will provide a systematic introduction to the major findings in behavioral finance, including concepts such as cognitive biases, overconfidence, and bounded rationality, as well as their effects on market volatility, price deviations, and financial decision-making. The course is designed to introduce students to the latest developments in behavioral finance and to broaden their understanding of modern finance. Behavioral finance introduces psychological insights and more realistic assumptions to guide and advance the theory of financial markets. We will examine how behavioral finance complements traditional paradigms and study trading behavior, asset price dynamics, and corporate financial decisions in a behavioral context.

教学目标

To broaden students’ knowledge about modern finance and recent progresses in the behavioral finance literature and practice. The students will learn an alternative perspective into the financial markets and many facts and techniques that they may use in future investment and corporate finance decisions.

教学方式

Primarily lecture-based, supplemented by interactive group discussions.

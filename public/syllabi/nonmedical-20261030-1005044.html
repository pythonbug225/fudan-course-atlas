流行文化的批判性阅读

2025秋 · FORE50069.01 · 宋旸

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

流行文化通俗易懂而广受欢迎，但其所带来的社会影响也备受争议。本课程结合文化研究与话语分析的理论思路与方法，对不同媒介和体裁的流行文化文本，包括微电影、电影、电视剧、真人秀，新闻，微纪录片等，进行批评性分析，从而帮助学生思考并阐释流行文化如何反映和塑造大众价值观，并提高对自我日常文化消费活动的反思。

课程简介（英文）

Popular culture is accessible and widely appealing, yet its social impacts remain highly contested. This course, drawing on theoretical perspectives and methods from cultural studies and discourse analysis, provides critical analyses of popular culture texts across diverse media and genres—including mini-movies, films, TV dramas, reality shows, news, and mini-documentaries. Through these analyses, students will be encouraged to reflect on and articulate how popular culture both reflects and shapes collective values, while also cultivating a critical awareness of their own everyday cultural consumption practices.

教学目标

本课程旨在帮助学生： 1.理解文化研究的基本思路和方法 2.理解并阐释流行文化文本的基本类型和特征及其对个人价值观的可能影响 3.理解并应用相关理论对流行文化文本进行基于社会语境、文本体裁和表意方法等层面的批评分析 This course is designed to help students: Understand the basic ideas and methods of cultural studies. Comprehend and interpret the main types and characteristics of popular culture texts, as well as their possible influence on individual values. Apply relevant theories to critically analyze popular culture texts at the levels of social context, textual genre, and modes of representation.

教学方式

课堂教学、课堂小组讨论、课堂一对一作业反馈和小组课题咨询、线上E-learning讨论版作业等 Classroom lectures, group discussions, one-on-one assignment feedback and group project consultation, as well as online E-learning discussion board assignments.

高级宏观经济学（II）

2025秋 · ECON60015.01 · 奚锡灿

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将引导学生关注和理解宏观经济学的核心问题，并掌握前沿研究所使用的重要工具，为学生独立开展研究打下基础。具体而言，本课程将要讲授的议题包括：资源配置与经济发展；企业竞争与动态理论；贸易与经济增长；异质性家庭与不完全市场；宏观视角下的金融；劳动市场上的搜索与匹配；世代交叠理论等。

课程简介（英文）

This course will guide students to focus on and understand the core issues of macroeconomics, and to master the key tools used in cutting-edge research. This will lay the groundwork for students to conduct their own independent research. Specifically, the topics covered in this course will include: resource allocation and economic development; firm competition and dynamic theory; trade and economic growth; heterogeneous households and incomplete markets; macro finance; search and matching in the labor market; overlapping generations theory, and more.

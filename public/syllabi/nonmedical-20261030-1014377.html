科学哲学原著精读

2025秋 · PHIL60074.01 · Chuang LIU(刘闯)

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程引导学生对科学哲学史上的核心文献进行精读与研讨，着重训练学生高效理解原始文献，并培养其运用英语进行学术讨论的能力。课程旨在使学生掌握阅读专业研究文献的方法，为其未来学术生涯的开展奠定基础。 本次阅读课以"作为知识机器的科学"为主题，精读篇目涵盖亨普尔、波普尔、库恩、夏平、拉图尔及斯特雷文斯等学者的代表性著作。

课程简介（英文）

This course leads students through the close reading and discussion of key texts in the history of philosophy of science. It teaches students to read materials in its original language with efficiency and understanding, as well as discussing the materials in English. It aims at enabling students to read research literature for their own research projects in their future career. The theme of this reading course is “Science as the Knowledge Machine.” The works to read include selected works from Hempel, Popper, Kuhn, Shapin, Latour, and Strevens.

教学方式

This course will be conducted in a seminar format, where students are required to make oral presentations of the reading materials, to continue discussing the materials in depth, and to write ideally about the question of dissusion.

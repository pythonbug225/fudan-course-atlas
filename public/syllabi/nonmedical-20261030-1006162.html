课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在简要介绍中国法律文化的一些核心要素。课程将分析中国古典时期主要思想学派的经典文本，特别关注这些思想学派在历史上对塑造、形成和转变中国法律实践所发挥的作用。同时，课程还将探讨传统法律文化对当今中国乃至整个世界具有的现实意义。鼓励学生以比较视角，批判性地分析不同文化背景下法律思想学派的优缺点。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to introduce in a nutshell some key aspects of the Chinese legal culture. Texts from the major schools of thought in classical China are examined, with special attention given to the role played by the diverse schools of thought in instructing, forming and transforming Chinese legal practices in history. Attention will also be paid to the contemporary relevance of traditional legal culture to China today in particular and the world at large. Students are encouraged to examine critically the (de)merits of different schools of legal thoughts across cultures in a comparative way.

教学目标

This course covers the different areas of Chinese legal culture, at the end of which students will be able to gain a basic understanding of the various areas in legal traditions in imperial China, such as qingli, filial piety, the Confucianisation of the Law, the coalescence of morality and law, the adaptations of law to communist practices, the Ma Xiwu Trial Mode, and some contemporary practices, such as the Intellectual Property Rights protection in China today.

教学方式

It will be mainly lectures and in-class discussions.

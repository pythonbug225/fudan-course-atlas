课程简介（中文）

本课程严谨介绍辛几何及其前沿应用。内容涵盖辛流形、哈密顿向量场、动量映射、达布定理、拉格朗日子流形、泊松几何、辛叶层、辛约化等核心理论。课程后半部分深入探讨伪全纯曲线与辛不变量，并研究其在枚举几何与镜像对称的模空间构造中的应用。时间允许将讨论量子化问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a rigorous introduction to symplectic geometry, culminating in advanced applications. Foundational Structures: symplectic manifolds, Hamiltonian vector fields, and exploring their deep connections to classical mechanics. The course progresses through key topics including Darboux’s theorem, symplectomorphisms, and the theory of generating functions. Lagrangian submanifolds. Poisson geometry: Poisson structures and the Weinstein theorem, symplectic foliations, generalizing the symplectic framework and providing tools for understanding constrained systems. Symplectic reduction: Hamiltonian group actions and moment maps. Pseudoholomorphic curves: Compatible almost-complex structures, Kahler manifolds, definition of pseudoholomorphic curves. Applications: The final part of the course explores the role of pseudoholomorphic curves in defining symplectic invariants, culminating in an exploration of how these techniques apply to the construction and study of moduli spaces arising in enumerative geometry and mirror symmetry. Time permitting, we will also discuss applications to quantization problems.

教学目标

课程立足本校办学定位，坚持价值引领与知识传授相统一，注重培养学生扎实的几何直观与计算能力。教学内容从具体计算出发，系统建立辛几何理论框架，逐步引导学生掌握模空间、镜像对称与计数几何等前沿领域的研究基础，最终使学生具备开展相关创新研究的能力。

考核构成

  • 出勤Attendance 10.0% Attendance is warmly encouraged. Attending 6 lectures before the midterm evaluation and 4 lectures after the midterm evaluation will ensure maximal score in attendance. Then, 1% score decreases for each lecture not attended (for instance, only 8 lectures attended throughout the course corresponds to a score of 8%).
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 30.0% There will be between three and four homework assignments, with and individual score for each homework assignment. The average score of all the homework assignments will be the final assignment score.
  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 30.0% There will be an open-book mid-term examination. The grade of the midterm will count 30% of the final score if it is sufficient, and will not count toward the final score if the student fails the midterm examination.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：其他 30.0% There will be an open-book type final exam in the last week of the course. It will consist of problems covering all the material of the course. It will count 30% of the final grade, or 60% if the student failed the midterm examination.

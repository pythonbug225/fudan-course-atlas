来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本《专业外语》课程面向科学硕士研究生同学开放，通过课堂授课、课堂互动、课堂讨论等形式传授如何进行心理学论文阅读与写作、英语口头学术报告等内容。

课程简介（英文）

This course is open to master's students in Psychology. Through classroom lectures, interactive activities, and discussions, it teaches students how to read and write psychology papers, as well as deliver oral academic presentations in English.

教学目标

本《研究生专业外语》课程旨在讲授前沿的专业外语听、说、读、写基本规范与技巧，使得学生在专业外语应用上拥有前沿的国际视野。通过本课程的学习，学生应能熟练阅读和理解本专业的英文文献资料；能熟练用英语展开学术汇报，与国际同行交流；能按照美国心理协会（American Psychological Association)所要求的格式撰写专业研究论文。

教学方式

全英文授课、学生课堂讨论、课堂Presentation展示

教学内容与进度安排

课次 教学周 教学内容及预期效果 作业/实验 1 Introduction & Reading literature 2 Group discussion & How to give an oral presentation 3 Group Presentation 10 min for RP; 15 min for paper 4 Academic writing Write an outline for Introduction 5 How to write a research proposal Write an introduction based on revised outline 6 How to write a research paper Write method for the research proposal/or method/results for research paper 7 How to write a research paper 8 How to write a review Write a review or discussion for research paper

因果推断

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将系统介绍因果推断。首先介绍它的统计基础：因果效应的统计学定义。接着介绍针对随机实验的因果推断方法，并衍生到观测型研究下的因果推断方法。本课程会介绍因果推断里面常遇到的问题，例如，混杂性、交互性、选择性偏差等。这其中，本课程会重点介绍基于各种模型的因果推断。 由于因果推断不仅是统计和机器学习的重要领域，在生物医学、经济学、社会学等学科中也是重要的研究方法，所以欢迎其他各个学科的学生选修该课程。

课程简介（英文）

This course systematically covers the fundamentals of causal inference. It begins by introducing the statistical basis, focusing on the statistical definition of causal effects. Subsequently, the course delves into the causal inference method for randomized experiments, extending to the method's application in observational research. Common challenges encountered in causal inference, including confounding, interaction, and selection bias, will be discussed. Emphasis will be placed on causal inference utilizing various models. Given that causal inference plays a crucial role not only in statistics and machine learning but also in biomedicine, economics, sociology, and other disciplines, students from diverse academic backgrounds are encouraged to enroll in this course.

教学目标

（1）理解因果推断的定义和应用场景； （2）掌握常用的因果推断方法； （3）可以利用因果推断解决实际遇到的科研问题； （4）如果有可能，为后续研究这个领域提供基础。 (1) Grasp the definition and application scenarios of causal inference; (2) Acquire proficiency in common causal inference methods; (3) Acquire the skill to utilize causal inference to address real scientific research problems; (4) If possible, lay the groundwork for subsequent research in this field.

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程教学内容包括：演讲的选题，听众分析，演讲稿的撰写，演讲实践以及演讲的评估。演讲的训练涵盖：自我介绍演讲，说解型演讲，说服型演讲以及特殊场合演讲。

课程简介（英文）

NA

教学目标

本课程专注于锻炼一年级学生的英语会话演说能力。训练手段包复述，个人演讲，小组报告等；训练素材涉及文化，科技和政治等议题。通过本课程的学习，学生能够打开国际视野，了解英语的各种口音，提升英语口语水平、并能有效地用英语表达对某个议题的看法。 Understand linguistic, logic and communication concepts that serve as a basis for effective speaking, and apply this conceptual knowledge in practice to give effective speeches Be a more analytical listener & speaker, and develop the ability to analyze and evaluate presentations and deliver presentations relevant to the audience Learn to organize your ideas by outlining Learn how to ask good questions and provide meaningful answers to questions Gain experience in delivering professional and personal speeches and enjoy speaking publicly in a variety of situations

教学方式

课程融合各类授课方式，包括讲座、小组讨论、演讲实践以及同伴演讲互评等

教学内容与进度安排

1 Course introduction; Speaking in public 2 Speaking confidently and ethically/managing speech anxiety; Giving your first speech 3 Selecting a topic and a purpose; Analyzing the audience 4 In-class delivery of introductory speeches 5 Using language 6 Feedbacks on introductory speeches; Delivering the speech; 7 Supporting your Ideas; Organizing the body of your speech; Beginning and ending the speech 8 In-class delivery of recitation 9 Outlining the speech; Using visual aids; Speaking to inform 10 In-class delivery of informative speeches 11 Speaking on special occasions 12 In-class delivery of special occasion speeches 13 Speaking to persuade (I) 14 Speaking to persuade (II) 15 In-class delivery of persuasive speeches & Impromptu speeches (I) 16 In-class delivery of persuasive speeches & Impromptu speeches (II)

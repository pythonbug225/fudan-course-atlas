机械振动

2025秋 · IST40007.01 · 方虹斌

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程全面介绍离散和连续系统的线性振动理论。第 1 单元介绍单自由度系统的分析方法，为后面的单元奠定基础。第 2 单元介绍了拉格朗日方程和汉密尔顿原理（分析动力学），目的在于建立离散系统（第 3 单元）和连续系统（第 4 单元）的运动方程。第 3 单元介绍离散系统的振动理论，包括相关特征值问题的分析和特性。将考虑无阻尼和有阻尼系统。第 4 单元介绍连续系统的振动理论，重点是线性微分算子理论和基本特征值问题的特性。 响应问题的求解方法将以特征函数展开为基础。第5单元将初步介绍机械振动的一些计算方法。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a thorough development of linear vibration theory for discrete and continuous systems. Unit 1 introduces analysis methods for single degree-of-freedom systems, which forms the basis for later units. Unit 2 presents Lagrange’s equations and Hamilton’s principle (Analytical Dynamics) sufficient for developing the equations of motion for discrete (Unit 3) and continuous (Unit 4) systems. Unit 3 presents vibration theory for discrete systems including the analysis and properties of the associated eigenvalue problem. Both undamped and damped systems will be considered. Unit 4 presents vibration theory for continuous systems with a focus on linear differential operator theory and properties of the underlying eigenvalue problem. Solutions to response problems will be cast in terms of eigenfunction expansions. Unit 5 will introduce numerical techniques for mechanical vibration problems.

教学目标

通过本课程，学生将了解机械振动的基本理论和概念，以及它们在机器人工程中的应用。基于所学的力学原理和数学工具，学生还需要将机器人工程中的机械振动问题抽象为动态模型和运动方程。学生应能够分析 SDOF 系统、MDOF 系统和连续系统在外部激励下的瞬态动力学和稳态动力学。本课程将为今后在振动分析、机器人结构设计和机器人动力学领域的工作或研究奠定坚实的基础。 Through this course, the students are expected to understand the fundamental theories and concepts of mechanical vibrations, as well as their applications in robotic engineering. Based on the learnt mechanics principles and mathematical tools, the students are also expected to abstract the mechanical vibration problems of robotic engineering into dynamic models and the equations of motions. The students should be able to analyze the transient dynamics and steady-state dynamics of SDOF systems, MDOF systems, and continuous systems under external excitations. This course will lay a solid foundation for future work or research in the fields of vibration analysis, robot structure design, and robot dynamics.

教学方式

本课程以课堂教学为主，在原本3学分的机械振动教学内容的基础上，添加针对具体工程问题的讨论课和实验课。课堂教学中，坚持板书教学为主，多媒体教学辅助的教学方式。 This course is based on classroom teaching, and on the basis of the original 3-credit teaching content of Mechanical Vibration, it adds discussion classes and experimental classes for specific engineering problems. In the classroom teaching, we insist on handwritten scripts as the main teaching method, supplemented by multimedia teaching, and endeavor to improve students' ability of mathematical reasoning and independent thinking.

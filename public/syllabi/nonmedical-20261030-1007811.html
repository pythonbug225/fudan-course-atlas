课程简介（中文）

专题讲授新时代中国特色社会主义理论和实践的重大问题，帮助学生进一步掌握中国特色社会主义理论体系，深化对习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想的认识，坚定对马克思主义的信仰、对中国特色社会主义的信念、对实现中华民族伟大复兴中国梦的信心。

课程简介（英文）

Teaching major issues related to the theory and practice of socialism with Chinese characteristics in the new era, helping students further master the theoretical system of socialism with Chinese characteristics, deepen their understanding of Xi Jinping's Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, strengthen their faith in Marxism, Chinese socialism with Chinese characteristics, and their confidence in realizing the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation and the Chinese Dream.

教学目标

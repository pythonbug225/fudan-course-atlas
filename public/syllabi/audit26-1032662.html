课程简介（中文）

生物无机化学是一门前沿交叉学科，应用无机化学、配位化学、生物化学、化学生物学的理论概念研究生物体系中无机金属离子的行为和规律；探讨无机元素及其化合物与生物体的相互作用； 研究金属离子与各种生物活性配体的作用规律及这些规律的应用；以无机金属离子为探针来了解纷繁的生物过程和基本途径。阐明金属离子与生物大分子形成配合物的结构与功能的关系，揭示金属离子在生命过程中的奥妙，为生理、病理和相关药物研究提供理论基础。本课程综述了解生物无机化学近年来的研究进展，认识金属离子及其金属蛋白、金属酶在生理与病理中的作用机制及其研究进展，掌握关键金属蛋白、金属酶作为疾病标志物和药物靶标的作用及其研究进展。

 

课程简介（英文）

Bioinorganic chemistry is a cutting-edge interdisciplinary subject, applying the theoretical concepts of inorganic chemistry, coordination chemistry, biochemistry, and chemical biology to study the behavior and laws of inorganic metal ions in biological systems; exploring the interaction between inorganic elements and their compounds and organisms Role; studying the rules of metal ions and various biologically active ligands and the application of these laws; using inorganic metal ions as probes to understand the numerous biological processes and basic approaches. It is to clarify the relationship between the structure and function of the complex formed by metal ions and biological macromolecules, to reveal the mystery of metal ions in the life process, and to provide a theoretical basis for physiological, pathological and related drug research. This course summarizes the research progress of bioinorganic chemistry in recent years, understands the mechanism and research progress of metal ions and their metal proteins and metal enzymes in physiology and pathology, and masters key metal proteins and metal enzymes as disease markers and drug targets Role and research progress.

 

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，使学生综述了解生物无机化学近年来的研究进展，认识金属离子及其金属蛋白、金属酶在生理与病理中的作用机制及其研究进展，掌握关键金属蛋白、金属酶作为疾病标志物和药物靶标的作用及其研究进展。

 

Through the study of this course, students can understand the research progress of bioinorganic chemistry in recent years, understand the mechanism and research progress of metal ions and their metal proteins and metal enzymes in physiology and pathology, and master key metal proteins and metal enzymes as The role and research progress of disease markers and drug targets.

教学方式

本课程按照系列专题进展学习，以老师主讲、学生分组讨论方式进行。学生按照分组后选择某一个专题，通过查找资料、编写综述论文、在班上向其他组同学讲述和分享各组所查的主要内容，老师点评，同学提问讨论。在老师讲解和同学参与讨论的方式，让同学们对生物无机化学前沿领域有较深刻理解，掌握前沿领域研究进展。

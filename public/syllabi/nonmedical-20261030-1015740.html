课程简介（中文）

国际传播能力已成为新时代高校人才培养工作的重要一环。一流学术人才必须能用国际通用语英语在国际期刊上发表成果、在国际学术研讨会上熟练交流。本课程聚焦学术英语口语及写作中的核心、可迁移技能以及学术人文素养，帮助学生提升专业领域的国际交往能力和国际竞争力。

课程简介（英文）

International communication competence has become a crucial component of talent cultivation in higher education institutions in the new era. Scholars must demonstrate the ability to publish their research in international journals and communicate fluently at global academic conferences using English as the lingua franca. This course focuses on core, transferable skills in academic English speaking and writing, as well as academic literacy. It aims to help students enhance their international engagement and competitiveness in their respective fields.

教学目标

This course focuses on core, transferable skills in academic English speaking and writing, as well as academic literacy and integrity. It aims to help students engage in their professional studies and future work in English, thereby enhancing their international communication skills and competitiveness in their respective fields.

教学方式

Lecture: The instructor explains key concepts, providing theoretical frameworks and background knowledge. Pair work/ Group discussion: Students will work in pairs or groups to share viewpoints and insights. Presentation: Students are guided to give presentations related to their major field of study.

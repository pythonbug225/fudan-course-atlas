课程简介（中文）

Students enrolling in this course are expected to serve as RAs for SOE's professors. It is for the UIPE degree students only, who will submit the evaluation form and report after completing an RA's job.

课程简介（英文）

Students enrolling in this course are expected to serve as RAs for SOE's professors. It is for the UIPE degree students only, who will submit the evaluation form and report after completing an RA's job.

教学目标

此课程旨在鼓励本科生作为RA参加教师发起的科研活动，致力于提升学生的独立研究能力，包括提出问题、实验设计、数据分析和报告撰写。通过实践活动和研讨交流，学生将锻炼批判性思维，为未来学术或职业道路打下基础。课程结束时，学生需提交研究报告，接受导师评估。

教学方式

Serve as RA for a professor of SOE

教学内容与进度安排

As arranged by the professor

考核方式

作业/实验/实践Assignment 100.0%（Complete the RA job according to the professor's requirements）

考核构成

  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 100.0% Complete the RA job according to the professor's requirements

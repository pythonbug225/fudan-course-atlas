比较文化研究

2026春 · FORE50075.01 · 郑咏滟

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本门课程从中西文明比较的视角出发，让学生了解熟悉中西方文化在关键议题上的区别，能够发展出文化审辨性思维，并且生发出文化自觉、文化自信、文化敏感性作为一门基础性的文化类课程，本门课程为以后课程中高阶的文化类课程以及跨文化课程奠定了基础。作为基础课，本门课程另一目的是锻炼学生的基础学术阅读和学术写作能力。

课程简介（英文）

From the perspective of comparing Chinese and Western civilizations, this course enables students to understand and familiarize themselves with the differences between Chinese and Western cultures on key issues, develop cultural critical thinking, and foster cultural awareness, cultural confidence, and cultural sensitivity. As a foundational cultural studies course, it lays a solid groundwork for advanced cultural courses and cross-cultural courses in subsequent study stages. Another objective of this basic course is to cultivate students’ fundamental abilities in academic reading and academic writing

教学目标

知识目标：在学习观、家庭观、人与环境、人的本质、世界观、全球与人类未来等六大议题上能够理解中西方文明的基本差异；了解比较文化研究路径 技能目标：能够就重要的比较文化议题采用多模态的方式进行数字表述；能够阅读并分析相关的跨文化研究学术论文 素养目标：培养文化审辨性思维，并且生发出文化自觉、文化自信、文化敏感性

教学方式

本门课程以面授为主。课堂上教师讲授，学生参与讨论。课上使用ppt，并在课后发放给学生，学生根据ppt进行复习。

教学内容与进度安排

Session 1 Introduction (reading after the first lecture) Session 2 Encounter 1.1: Traditional Ideas of Learning Session 3 Encounter 1.2: Modern Ideas of Learning Session 4 Encounter 2.1: Traditional Families Session 5 Encounter 2.2: Modern Families Session 6 Classroom project: The role of women in yesterday and today’s families Session 7 Encounter 4.1: Traditional Views of Humans and their Surroundings Session 8 Encounter 4.2: Modern Views of Humans and their Surroundings Mid-term Review Session 9 Encounter 3.1: Traditional Ideas of Being Human Session 10 Encounter 3.2: Modern Ideas of Being Human Session 11 Encounter 6.1: Traditional Values and Worldviews Session 12 Encounter 6.2: Modern Values and Worldviews Session 13 Overview I: Today: Global Perspectives Session 14 Overview II: Tomorrow: Humanity at Risk Session 15 Final review Session 16 Final exam

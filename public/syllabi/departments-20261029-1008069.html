课程简介（中文）

本课程涵盖了当代统计学中两大重要的应用统计方法。具体来讲，它包括稀疏统计学习方 法专题、Bootstrap方法专题等。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers the following state-of-the-art topics in modern applied statistic topics in Statistical Learning with Sparsity, Bootstrap et al.

教学目标

在现代数理统计的基础上，本课程旨在使学生能够对当代统计学中最重要的一些前沿理论与方法有相当程度的熟悉与理 解。在此课程结束时，学生应能在对基本原理深刻理解的基础上，运用这些前沿方法进行初步的、尝试性的研究。 Building on Advanced Statistics, this graduate level course introduces students to a selection of most important advanced theories and methods in modern statistics. By the end of the course, students should acquire a solid comprehension of the topics discussed in the lectures. Moreover, students should be able to use these advanced methods to conduct research in a reasonably professional manner.

教学方式

教授讲授和学生报告相结合的形式进行教学。

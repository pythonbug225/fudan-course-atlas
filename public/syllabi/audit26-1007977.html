课程简介（中文）

本课程在学生具备《有机化学》、《药物化学》、《药物设计学》《和《药理学》等知识基础上，进一步深入学习药物化学发展历史、药物化学研究内容和任务、先导化合物的发现途径和结构优化方法、新药研究与开发概况等知识，同时结合药物化学的多个研究方向，系统性介绍针对治疗某领域疾病的相关药物的研究起源、研究思路和最新进展以及相关案例分析。

课程简介（英文）

Based on the students’ knowledge of “Organic Chemistry”, “Medicinal Chemistry”, “Drug Design” and “Pharmacology”, this course takes further in-depth study of the medicinal chemistry development history, medicinal chemistry research content and tasks, the cutting-edge strategies for the discovery of lead compounds and further optimization, and the overview of developing new drug modalities. This course also systematically and comprehensively introduces the detailed case-study of the exemplified drugs for the treatment of different diseases.

教学目标

《药物研发新策略》是一门面向药学研究生开设的全面介绍药物化学各种原理、研究方法和技术、以及反映当代药物化学学科最新发展水平的专业选修课程。在《药物化学》的基础上，阐述了药物化学各个领域的发展历史与前沿研究热点，学生能够更深入系统地掌握创新药物设计策略、成药性特征和优化原理、以及生物学效应等基础知识，增进对现代药物化学发展现状与趋势的宏观认识，开阔药物研发新视野，逐步培养创新思维和独立分析、解决问题的能力，为培养兼备系统专业知识和能够进行创新药物研发的高级药学人才夯实基础。

“New strategies for drug discovery” is a specialized elective course for pharmacy graduate students. This elective course comprehensively introduces various principles, research methods and technologies of medicinal chemistry, and reflects the latest development level of contemporary medicinal chemistry disciplines. On the basis of “Medicinal Chemistry”, it thoroughly explains the development history and cutting-edge hotspots in various research fields of medicinal chemistry. Students gain deep and systematical understanding of the basic knowledge of innovative drug design strategies, druggable characteristics, optimization principles, and biological effects. This course also enhances the students’ macroscopic understanding of the development status and trends of modern medicinal chemistry, broadens their vision of drug discovery, and gradually cultivates their innovative thinking and independent problem-solving abilities. Overall, this course paves a solid foundation for cultivating senior pharmaceutical talents with comprehensive professional knowledge and the ability to conduct innovative drug discovery research.

教学方式

针对"药物研发新策略"的全英文教学，可采用理论与实践深度融合的创新模式。在理论环节，按照教学大纲设置模块化专题课程，重点讲授基于AI的虚拟筛选、DEL技术（DNA编码化合物库）、分子动力学模拟以及靶向蛋白降解等前沿技术，结合辉瑞、诺华等企业的经典案例进行剖析。实践教学方面，可搭建计算机辅助药物设计（CADD）虚拟仿真平台，让学生亲自操作分子对接、药效团建模等流程；此外，引入"项目驱动式"教学，将学生分组模拟创新药研发团队，从靶点发现、先导化合物优化到临床前研究进行全流程演练，期间安排企业导师指导。定期举办学术沙龙，邀请工业界专家分享PROTAC、分子胶等新技术应用，并组织学生进行文献汇报和方案答辩，培养解决实际问题的能力。通过这种多元化的教学形式，帮助学生掌握小分子药物研发的核心技术和创新思维。

1. 绪论/Introduction

1

内容：药物化学发展史、当代药物化学发展新趋势以及药物发现新技术概况。

目的:系统熟悉课程的基本内容以及学习要求，能够描述药物化学研究的发展概况。

Content：Introduction of the medicinal chemistry development history，the current trend of the medicinal chemistry development, and the overview of the novel strategies for drug discovery.

Object: Be systematically familiar with the basic contents of this course and the learning requirements. The students should be able to describe the development profile of the medicinal chemistry research.

课堂小测试，不计入总成绩。

Class quiz, that is not included in the final score.

2. 高通量筛选/High throughput screening (HTS)

1

内容：介绍基于表型的高通量筛选技术和基于结构的虚拟高通量筛选技术，以及相关案例分析。

目标：能够掌握高通量筛选的流程，并能够基于已知致病蛋白结构进行化合物库的虚拟筛选。

Content: Introduce the classic phenotype-based high-throughput screening and structure-based virtual high-throughput screening with detail discussion of relevant case studies.

Object: Students are able to know the regular process of the high-throughput screening and conduct simple virtual high-throughput screening of compound libraries based on the known structure of the disease-caused proteins.

选择一个已知蛋白结构的靶标，进行分子对接和实施简单的虚拟筛选。

Select a target with a known protein structure to perform molecular docking and a simple virtual screening.

3. 基于结构的药物设计/Structure-based drug design (SBDD)

1

内容：介绍基于结构的药物设计的基本原理，并结合案例分析如何利用靶蛋白的三维结构包括酶底物结合的活性位点，受体与配体之间的相互作用等，根据其构效关系直接设计和优化药物。

目的：能构掌握基于蛋白与配体的结构信息来设计和优化苗头化合物得到先导化合物。

Content: Introduce the basic principles of the structure-based drug design, and how to use the three-dimensional structure information, including the active site for the enzyme substate binding and the interactions between the receptor and the ligand, etc., to design and optimize the hit compound.

Object: Students are able to use this structure-based drug design strategy to design and optimize the hit compound based on the structural information of the target proteins and the ligands.

选择一苗头化合物，根据已知的晶体结构信息，提出设计和优化思路。

Based on the crystal structure of the hit compound in complex with the target protein, propose the promising strategies to design and optimize the hit compound to get the lead compound.

4. 基于片段的药物设计/Fragment-based drug design (FBDD)

1

内容：介绍基于片段的药物设计的基本原理，以及如何挑选有利的片段组合或延伸以获得新的药物分子。

目的：熟悉筛选片段化合物的技术手段。

Content: Introduce the basic principles of the fragment-based drug design, and how to use the promising fragments to re-design and construct novel drug candidate.

Object: Be familiar with the technical methods for screening fragment compounds.

简述NMR光谱法筛选片段的技术优势。

Briefly describe the advantages of the NMR spectroscopy for screening effective fragments.

 

5. 计算机辅助药物设计/Computer-aided drug design (CADD)

1

内容：介绍计算机辅助药物设计的基本原理，结合成功案例，通过计算机模拟、计算和预测药物与受体之间的关系，设计和优化先导化合物。

目标：熟悉掌握分子模拟、分子对接、全新设计、药效基团模型以及QSAR等基本操作。

Content: Introduce the basic principles of computer-aided drug design. Select an example to demonstrate the basic strategy to design and optimize lead compound through computer simulation, calculation and prediction of the relationship between drugs and receptors.

Object: Be familiar with basic operations such as molecular simulation, molecular docking, new design, pharmacophore models and QSAR.

查阅文献，讲述一个利用计算机辅助药物设计策略的药物研发项目，从最早的靶点分析、骨架跃迁到后期先导化合物优化，贯穿各种模拟方法。

Review the literature and describe a drug development project using computer-aided drug design strategies, from the earliest target analysis, skeleton transition to late-stage lead compound optimization.

6. 人工智能辅助药物设计/Artificial intelligence-aided drug design (AIDD)

1

内容：介绍人工智能辅助药物设计的基本原理和深度学习在药物研发中的应用。

目的：了解分子表示、特征提取、药物属性预测、分子生成与优化、靶点预测与蛋白结构预测，药物相似性计算和虚拟筛选等关键技术。

Content: Introduce the basic principles of AI drug design and the application of deep learning in drug discovery.

Object: Understand key technologies such as molecular representation, feature extraction, drug attribute prediction, molecule generation and optimization, target prediction and protein structure prediction, drug similarity calculation and virtual screening.

分析和比较计算机辅助药物设计和人工智能辅助药物设计的相似和不同之处。

Analyze and compare the similarities and differences between computer-aided drug design and AI drug design strategies.

7. DNA编码化合物库技术/ DNA-encoded chemical library (DEL)

1

内容：介绍DNA编码化合物库的构建、DEL技术的筛选方法和基本原理，以及成功案例分析。

目的：了解如何使用DEL库筛选与目标靶蛋白具有亲和力的DNA编码的先导化合物，并通过PCR扩增和二代基因测序，解析出潜在的与目的蛋白具有高亲和力的化学小分子。

Content: Introduce the design and construct of DNA-encoded chemical libraries，the screening methods and basic principles of DEL technology, as well as analysis of the successful cases.

Object: Students are able to know the basic knowledge of using DEL library to screen DNA-encoded lead compounds with affinity to the target protein, and how to identify the final chemical molecules with potential high affinity to the target protein through PCR amplification and second-generation gene sequencing.

简述DEL技术如何拓展成药空间。

Make a presentation to demonstrate how DEL technology expands the pharmaceutical space.

 

8. 共价策略/Covalent bonding strategy

2

内容：介绍共价药物的简要历史，以及可逆共价和非可逆共价弹头在药物发现中的应用。

目的：能够比较可逆共价和非可逆共价的区别。

Content: Introduce the development of covalent drugs and the applications of reversible and non-reversible covalent warheads in drug discovery.

Object: Be able to compare the difference between reversible and non-reversible covalent inhibitors.

结合ABPP技术平台简述共价策略在促进药物发现中的作用。

Combined with the ABPP technology platform，briefly describe the role of covalent strategies in promoting drug discovery。

9. 靶向蛋白降解/Target protein degradation

2

内容：介绍多类靶向蛋白降解技术的发展历程和优缺点，分析进入临床研究的PROTAC和分子胶药物进展。

目标：能够描述和阐述各类靶向蛋白降解技术的作用机制，分析和比较出各类降解剂与小分子药物的优缺点。

Content: Introduce the development history, advantages and disadvantages of multiple types of the target protein degradation technologies, and analyzes the progress of PROTAC and molecular glue that have entered clinical research.

Object: Students are able to describe and elaborate the mechanism of action, analyze and compare the advantages and disadvantages of different types of targeted protein degradation technologies.

选取进入临床或在临床前的PROTAC或分子胶药物进行案例分析和讨论。

Select one PROTAC or molecular glue that have entered clinical stages for case analysis and discussion.

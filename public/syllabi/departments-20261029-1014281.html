课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍与会计与财务报表分析相关概念。会计是商业世界通用语言，财务报表被各类决策者广泛运用，使用者包括银行家、金融分析师、专业投资者及其他。因此，金融专业的学生必须能够理解财务报表的基本概念，并从中挖掘出有用的信息。本课程的核心目标是理解财务报表的内涵，将其作为决策工具，而非仅仅是记账工具。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces concepts related to accounting and financial statements analysis. Accounting is a business language around the world. Financial reporting is widely used by various decision makers including bankers, financial analysts, sophisticated investors and other stakeholders. Thus, it is essential for the students majoring in finance to be able to understand the fundamental concepts of financial statements and explore useful information out of them. The primary objective of this course is to comprehend the fundamental nature of financial statements, viewing them as a decision-making tool rather than a bookkeeping tool.

教学目标

1) To further students’ abilities to speak in the language of accounting. 2) To deepen their understanding of the basics of financial reports and statement preparation. 3) To advance their abilities to navigate annual reports and the financial disclosure. 4) To build critical-thinking skills related to analyzing and evaluating financial statements.

教学方式

Lectures: concepts and knowledge will be presented in the class. In-class discussions: Students are encouraged to discuss and share opinions. Case studies: discuss and solve business problemsbased on the concepts and data analysis.

教学内容与进度安排

Introduction: Course objectives 1, 2, 3 Balance sheet 1: Course objectives 1, 2, 3 Balance sheet 2: Course objectives 1, 2, 3 Income statement and others 1: Course objectives 1, 2, 3 Income statement and others 2: Course objectives 1, 2, 3 Financial statement analysis: Ratios: Course objectives 3, 4; Case analysis Financial statement analysis: Forecasting & valuation: Course objectives 3, 4

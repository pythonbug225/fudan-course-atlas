课程简介（中文）

作为《李群和李代数I&II》系列的第一部分，本课程将从首先介绍有限群及其表示，再初步介绍矩阵李群和矩阵李代数。

课程简介（英文）

As the first part of the "Lie Groups and Lie Algebras I&II" series, this course will start by introducing finite groups and their representations, and then provide an initial introduction to matrix Lie groups and matrix Lie algebras.

教学目标

本课程主要包括有限群（尤其是置换群的表示）以及矩阵李群李代数的相关内容。主要是从简单例子出发，期望学生能对基本的有限群、李群李代数及表示论有一些基本了解。 This course mainly covers the relevant contents of finite groups (especially the representation of permutation groups) and Lie algebras of matrix Lie groups. Mainly starting from simple examples, it is expected that students can have some basic understanding of finite groups, Lie groups, Lie algebras and representation theory.

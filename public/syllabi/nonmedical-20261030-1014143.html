交替传译（上）

2025秋 · TS50003.01 · 管玉华

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

MTI口译研究方向硕士一年级学生学位专业必修课程之一。本课程是在学生完成本科阶段英汉互译技巧、基础口译和中级口译等课程的基础上，系统讲授会议口译体系中英汉汉英交替传译模式下的各类翻译策略和技巧，通过课堂和线下操练，使学生交传技能使用能力融会贯通且偏重实际运用的口译高层次基础课程。包含交替传译绪论、传译技巧策略分类介绍、记忆能力提升、分主题实践指导、录像观摩讨论精讲和译员基本素质等几大模块。

课程简介（英文）

Course Title: Consecutive Interpreting (English-Chinese/Chinese-English) (One of the compulsory degree courses for first-year MTI students specializing in Interpreting Studies) Course Description: Building upon students' undergraduate foundation in translation techniques, basic interpreting, and intermediate interpreting, this course systematically explores the diverse strategies and techniques within the conference interpreting framework, specifically focusing on the consecutive interpreting mode between English and Chinese. Through intensive classroom instruction and practical offline exercises, it is designed as a higher-level foundational course that emphasizes practical application, enabling students to develop comprehensive and proficient consecutive interpreting skills. Key Modules Include: Introduction to Consecutive Interpreting Classification and Application of Interpreting Strategies & Techniques Memory Enhancement Training Theme-Based Practical Sessions & Guidance Video Demonstration, Analysis & Discussion Professional Ethics and Essential Qualities of Interpreters

教学目标

本课程着重系统介绍英汉、汉英交传的基本特点、规律表现、笔记方法以及实操战术与策略,使学生通过大量课堂和线下自我及配对训练，在第一学期系统掌握各类交传应用的基本技能，并能学以致用，初步胜任通用主题、普适难度的会议交际交替传译（大会开闭幕、主旨发言和新闻访谈、发布会等）辅助工作，同时为第二学期更高层次融合训练打好基础。

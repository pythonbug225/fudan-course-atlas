课程简介（中文）

本课程内容包括语言学的性质、语言的性质和功能、语音学、音系学、形态学、句法学、语义学、语用学、语言习得、语言的心理与神经机制、语言的变异与演变和社会语言学等。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the foundational principles of linguistics, covering: the nature of linguistics as a discipline; essential properties and functions of human language; core subfields including phonetics, phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, and pragmatics; as well as interdisciplinary domains such as language acquisition, psychological and neural mechanisms of language processing, language variation and change, and sociolinguistics. Through systematic exploration of these areas, students will gain an integrated understanding of linguistic theory and empirical methodology, with illustrative examples drawn from Chinese and cross-linguistic contexts to reinforce theoretical concepts.

教学目标

本课程为语言学入门课，中国语言文学系学生必修。课程介绍语言学基本概念和基本原理。修完本课程后，学生应：

  1. 了解语言学及其各次领域的基本研究范围、内容与现状；
  2. 掌握各次领域中的核心概念与术语；
  3. 熟悉语言学的各类分析与研究方法，能初步解决较简单的语言学问题。

教学方式

以老师讲授为主，辅以作业。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养非英语专业硕士研究生的学术英语能力。 课程设计基于以下三个理念： 1）中国高等教育英语教学从通用英语向学术英语的范式转型，要求在课程体系中纳入学术英语课程； 2）学术英语日益增长的跨学科导向，鼓励学生在混合学科小组中探讨学科内容，并通过对比学科差异培养学科身份认同； 3）研究生作为初阶研究者，需要参与更为复杂的学术交流活动，因此针对研究生的学术英语课程需涵盖广泛的专业知识与学术研究技能。 基于此，本课程将为学生提供接触多学科领域材料的机会，培养其在学术语境中所需的语言应用能力，为其在以英语为交流媒介的特定学科研究生阶段学习奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to develop non-English major graduate students’ academic English language proficiency. The course design is based on the following three rationales: 1) The paradigm shift from English for General Purposes (EGP) to English for Academic Purposes (EAP) in English language education at the tertiary level in China necessitates the inclusion of EAP courses in the curriculum; 2)The growing interdisciplinary focus in EAP encourages students to address disciplinary content within mixed disciplinary groups and develop their disciplinary identities through contrasting disciplinary differences; 3) Graduate students, as novice researchers, are increasingly engaged in more sophisticated academic communications and thus EAP for graduate students entails a vast array of subject knowledge and academic skills. This course, therefore, will provide students with opportunities to expose themselves to materials across subject areas and develop their language proficiency as needed for communication in academic contexts, thus preparing them for graduate study in a specific discipline where English is used as the medium of communication.

教学目标

At the end of the course, students are expected to understand the key features of academic English and develop their listening, speaking, reading and writing skills in academic contexts when English is used as the medium of communication. Specifically, students will be able to: build their academic vocabulary, phrases and sentence patterns; listen to academic lectures or interviews using skills such as how to identify main points and details, and how to take notes; prepare and deliver academic presentations, lead and participate in academic discussions.

教学方式

1. Interdisciplinary Project-Based Learning At the beginning of the semester, students form interdisciplinary teams based on their academic backgrounds. Each team collaborates on a research report exploring intersections of their fields (e.g., AI and medical ethics, sustainable development and urban planning). Throughout the project, students present disciplinary knowledge in English and engage in cross-disciplinary discussions to understand diverse academic conventions and develop their disciplinary identity. 2. Multimodal Input and Corpus-Assisted Learning The course replaces traditional general English textbooks with authentic academic materials, including journal articles, lectures, TED Talks, and conference videos. By engaging with multimodal academic content, students become familiar with discipline-specific language features and learn to use corpus tools for independent exploration—shifting from "learning" to "learning how to learn." 3. Academic Communication-Oriented Output Tasks Instruction emphasizes contextual appropriateness and communicative effectiveness in academic settings. Writing tasks involve composing academic texts and participating in peer review; speaking tasks include seminars, simulated conference presentations, and academic Q&A sessions. All tasks are designed to develop logical argumentation, critical evaluation, and synthesis of ideas. 4. Flipped Classroom with Process-Oriented Assessment Students acquire foundational knowledge through pre-class micro-lectures and readings, allowing class time to focus on project discussions, skill practice, and interaction. Assessment consists of process-oriented evaluation (40%)—including group presentations, reading notes, peer review, and participation—and a final evaluation (60%), ensuring continuous tracking of academic development.

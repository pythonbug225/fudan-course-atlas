AdS/CFT 对应关系

2026春 · MATH60114.01 · Georgios Linardopoulos

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

介绍AdS/CFT对应关系，强调在相关函数的计算中使用威腾图。

课程简介（英文）

An introduction to the AdS/CFT correspondence which emphasizes the use of Witten’s diagrams in the computation of correlation functions.

教学目标

Introduction to holography and the AdS/CFT correspondence Introduction to Witten’s method for computing correlation functions Introduction to modern applications of the AdS/CFT correspondence

课程简介（中文）

课程简介​ 本课程聚焦国际期刊论文写作规范，通过： (1) 解析期刊论文的语步结构、体裁特征与学术语言风格 (2) 专项训练标题设计、摘要撰写与关键词提取 (3) 分模块演练选题确立、引言撰写、文献综述、方法论（含研究设计/数据收集表述）、结论写作 培养学生构建符合国际标准的学术文本能力，最终完成选题论文初稿

课程简介（英文）

Course Description This course prepares graduate students for international research publication by: Analyzing rhetorical moves, genre conventions, and academic style in peer-reviewed journal articles Developing specialized skills for crafting titles, abstracts, and keywords Practicing structured research writing: • Topic identification & problem framing • Critical literature synthesis • Methodology description (research design/data collection) • Data interpretation & conclusion drafting Through scaffolded writing practice and GenAI-assisted refinement, students will produce a submission-ready paper draft.

教学目标

Learning Objectives​ Upon completion, students will: Deconstruct disciplinary writing conventions through genre analysis Apply rhetorical strategies for each IMRAD section Utilize GenAI ethically for language enhancement Produce a draft-ready English research paper Deliver conference-style presentations with effective visual communication

教学方式

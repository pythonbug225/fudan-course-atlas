课程简介（中文）

本课程提供微分拓扑的基本介绍，强调直觉。我们将解释流形上的横向、浸入、相交和积分等。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an elementary introduction to differential topology, emphasizing intuitions. We will explain transversality, submersions, intersections, and integration on manifolds etc.

教学目标

We start by defining manifolds and smooth mappings. We talk about Sard’s theorem and it applications, deducing the existence of Morse functions and Whitney’s embedding theorem. We then talk about the transversality theorem, define the intersection numbers, classification of one-manifolds. We construct intersection theory with orientations. The Euler characteristic and primitive Lefschetz fixed point theorem is discussed. We also deduce Poincare-Hopf index theorem. We close by integration on manifolds. The goal is to let students see the pictures and intuitions for several classical and abstract major theorems.

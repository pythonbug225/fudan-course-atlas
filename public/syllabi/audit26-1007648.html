课程简介（中文）

本课程以复旦护理学科发展重点领域为主线，邀请各专科领域专家，结合案例教学法，遵循“现象-问题-理论-方法-创新意识”的教学逻辑，介绍AI赋能护理科研与健康照护实践的最新理论、技术方法、应用场景与前沿进展，旨在人工智能快速发展的时代趋势下，拓宽学生以AI解决护理问题的学科交叉思维，助力高水平数智护理人才培养。课程教学内容设置遵循AI助力护理健康评估与诊断-AI赋能循证护理知识更新-AI提升健康照护实践的逻辑主线进行串联，共分为五个专题，包括概述、AI赋能健康评估、AI赋能循证应用、AI赋能护理实践、AI赋能健康传播。

课程简介（英文）

This course is structured around the key development areas of Fudan University's nursing discipline, inviting experts from various specialized fields to employ case-based teaching methods. Following the pedagogical logic of "phenomenon-problem-theory-method-innovation awareness," it introduces the latest theories, technical approaches, application scenarios, and cutting-edge advancements in AI-powered nursing research and healthcare practice.

Against the backdrop of rapid AI development, the course aims to broaden students' interdisciplinary thinking in leveraging AI to address nursing challenges and cultivate high-level digital-intelligent nursing talent. The curriculum is organized along a logical progression: AI-assisted health assessment and diagnosis → AI-enhanced evidence-based nursing knowledge updates → AI-driven healthcare practice improvement. It is divided into five thematic modules:

Overview

1. AI-Powered Health Assessment

2. AI-Enhanced Evidence-Based Applications

3. AI-Driven Nursing Practice

4. AI-Facilitated Health Communication

教学目标

知识目标：掌握AI的最新理论、相关技术方法；熟悉AI应用的场景；了解AI的前沿进展；
能力目标：掌握AI常用的技术；熟悉AI 技术在护理评估、诊断、实践、健康传播以及循证中的应用；
思政目标：拓展护理学研究生以 AI解决护理问题的学科交叉思维，提高学生的信息素养和创新能力。

教学方式

本课程以基于案例教学的课堂讲授为主，同步结合线上专题讨论、小组讨论、课程实践、作业汇报等形式。

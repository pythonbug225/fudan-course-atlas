课程简介（中文）

本课程涵盖国际宏观经济学与国际金融，重点在于理解国家之间宏观经济与金融联系的运行机制。主要内容包括国民收入核算、国际收支平衡、汇率制度的运作、国际资本市场以及国际货币体系。课程还将探讨汇率、利率、经常账户余额、产出和价格水平如何在货币政策与财政政策冲击下进行调整的内在机制。此外，课程将分析货币危机、主权债务违约，以及国际货币体系的历史演变与未来发展等问题。课程还将结合中国经济的实际情况，讨论经常账户失衡、跨境资本流动以及人民币汇率等前沿议题。

课程简介（英文）

This course covers international macroeconomics and international finance, with a focus on understanding the macroeconomic and financial linkages between countries. Key topics include national income accounting, the balance of payments, the operation of exchange rate systems, the international capital market, and the global monetary system. We will also explore the mechanisms through which exchange rates, interest rates, current account balances, output, and price levels adjust in response to monetary and fiscal policies. Additionally, we will examine issues such as currency crises, sovereign debt defaults, and the history and future of the international monetary system. Advanced topics related to the Chinese economy, including current account imbalances, capital flows, and the RMB exchange rate, will also be covered.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the key concepts in international macroeconomics and finance. Students will gain insight into the economic and financial linkages between countries, focusing on topics such as exchange rate systems, international capital markets, and the global monetary system. The course will also explore the effects of monetary and fiscal policies on macroeconomic variables and examine issues like currency crises and sovereign debt defaults. Special attention will be given to advanced topics related to the Chinese economy, including current account imbalances, capital flows, and the RMB exchange rate. Through this course, students will develop the analytical skills needed to evaluate global economic trends and policy decisions.

教学方式

Lectures, case studies, group discussions, and assessments (quizzes, assignments and exams).

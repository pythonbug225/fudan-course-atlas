《庄子》精读

2026春 · CHIN70010.01 · 吴冠文

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

概述《庄子》成书过程和先秦道家文学，综合介绍《庄子》相关海内外译介、研究情况，精读辨析《庄子》内、外、杂篇代表作品或作品片段，尤其是内篇《逍遥游》、《齐物论》、《养生主》、《人间世》、《大宗师》，外篇《秋水》和杂篇《天下》的文本和历代注解论述，细读这些篇章的文本时，结合《庄子》其他存有类似主旨或论述的篇章，尝试对《庄子》一书各篇的成书时间和撰者等文献问题、其“三言”等文学表现形式，以及其沾溉后人无限的哲学思想等，作初步研讨。

课程简介（英文）

Provides an overview of the writing process of the Zhuangzi and pre-Qin Daoist literature; comprehensively introduces the translation and research of the Zhuangzi, both domestically and internationally; and closely analyzes representative works or excerpts from the inner, outer, and miscellaneous chapters of the Zhuangzi. It particularly examines the texts and commentaries of the inner chapters "Free and Easy Wandering," "Discussion on Making All Things Equal," "The Secret of Caring for Life," "In the World of Men," and "The Great and Venerable Teacher," the outer chapter "Autumn Floods," and the miscellaneous chapter "The World." While carefully reading these chapters, it also considers other chapters in the Zhuangzi that contain similar themes or arguments, and attempts a preliminary discussion of the chapter's composition time and authorship, the literary devices used throughout the book, and its profound philosophical and aesthetic influence on later generations.

教学目标

通过《庄子》经典篇目及历代重要注释的精读和讨论，结合先秦两汉其他子书，如《老子》、《列子》、《淮南子》对相关主题的文学表现，对《庄子》文本的语言特色和表现方式有切实的把握，能够体会《庄子》的基本哲学思想，对《庄子》一书在中国文学史、哲学思想史上的地位有具体而较深入的了解。

教学方式

精细研读，讨论交流

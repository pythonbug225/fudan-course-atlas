课程简介（中文）

行为干预是疾病预防与控制的主要手段。国内外大量研究与实践都已证实：开展以健康教育、健康促进为主要手段的综合干预是公共卫生最有力手段之一，是提升人群健康水平的首要策略。健康促进也是实现健康中国战略目标的重要途径。因此，应用行为科学相关理论开展健康教育和健康促进项目是公共卫生和预防医学专业科学学位研究生必须掌握的专业技能。

本课程以健康生态学模型为框架，从个体、家庭、社区、社会等多个层次关注健康的影响因素，介绍个体、人际、组织、社区等不同水平的行为改变理论，还将介绍行为改变技术、行为经济学、实施科学等前沿知识，并应用上述理论开展健康促进项目的设计、实施与评价。

课程简介（英文）

Behavioral interventions are a primary means for disease prevention and control. Extensive research and practice, both domestically and internationally, have demonstrated that implementing comprehensive interventions centered on health education and health promotion stands as one of the most powerful tools in public health and is the foremost strategy for enhancing population health. Health promotion is also a vital pathway for achieving the strategic goals of the Healthy China Initiative. Consequently, applying behavioral science theories to conduct health education and health promotion projects is an essential professional skill that graduate students in scientific degree programs within public health and preventive medicine must master.

This course employs the health ecology model as its framework. It examines health determinants across multiple levels—individual, family, community, and societal—and introduces behavior change theories applicable at various levels (individual, interpersonal, organizational, community). It will also cover cutting-edge knowledge such as behavior change techniques, behavioral economics, and implementation science. Furthermore, the course will apply these theories to the design, implementation, and evaluation of health promotion programs.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，能够掌握健康相关行为的理论并深刻理解健康、健康决定因素以及健康促进的主要策略，具备独立设计、实施和评价健康促进项目的基本能力。既能应用健康相关行为的理论从不同层面来解释个体和群体行为的发生和发展，分析个体和群体行为改变的途径或机制，着力探讨“原因背后的原因”；也能在不同领域公共卫生的实践中，采用综合性的干预措施开展包括家庭、社区、学校、医院等不同场所的健康促进，评价健康促进项目的效果。

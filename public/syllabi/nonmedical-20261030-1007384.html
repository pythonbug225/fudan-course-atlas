课程简介（中文）

本课程是关于国际事务中的一门全面性、综合性课程，与“区域国别研究Ⅱ”一起构成国际事务专业硕士课程区域国别教学的主要内容。有别于传统概述型的区域国别课程，本课程将“全球北方”置于国际关系的视野下进行探究。本课程探讨的核心问题是：“全球北方”在国际事务中扮演了何种角色？为此，课程聚焦全球北方重点区域和国家，深入分析重点区域与国家的政治、经济与外交，加深学生对相关区域与国家在国际事务中的角色的理解。本课程分三大模块。第一个模块是北美，重点分析美国与加拿大；第二个模块是欧亚，聚焦欧盟、俄罗斯与中亚；第三个模块是东北亚，重点介绍日本与韩国（朝鲜半岛）。各个模块相对独立，又交叉为一体，以政治、经济和外交议题为核心，将相关区域国别研究理论与方法贯穿其中，构成对“全球北方”与国际关系的整体理解。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a comprehensive and integrated course on international affairs. Together with "Regional Country Studies II", it constitutes the main content of regional country teaching in the master's program of international affairs. Different from the traditional overview-type regional country courses, this course explores the "Global North" from the perspective of international relations. The core question explored in this course is: What role does the "Global North" play in international affairs? To this end, the course focuses on key regions and countries in the Global North, deeply analyzes the politics, economy and diplomacy of key regions and countries, and deepens students' understanding of the role of relevant regions and countries in international affairs. This course is divided into three modules. The first module is North America, focusing on the United States and Canada; the second module is Eurasia, focusing on the European Union, Russia and Central Asia; the third module is Northeast Asia, focusing on Japan and South Korea (Korean Peninsula). Each module is relatively independent and intertwined. With political, economic and diplomatic issues as the core, the relevant regional country research theories and methods are integrated throughout, forming an overall understanding of the "Global North" and international relations.

教学目标

-认识“全球北方”在国际事务的角色，包括共性与差异； -熟悉“全球北方”中的重点区域与国别，包括北美、欧亚与东北亚等区域与国别的政治、经济与外交议题上的主要特 征； -提升学生在相关区域国别问题上的知识水平，掌握区域国别研究的相关理论与方法。

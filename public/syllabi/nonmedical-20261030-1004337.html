课程简介（中文）

课程致力于全面提升学生的英语综合运用能力，内容涵盖高阶阅读与词汇训练、听力与口语交际训练、学术写作指导。通过丰富的语言实践，学生将深入接触不同文体与语境，锻炼精准表达与批判性思维。同时，课程融入中外文化对比，提升学生跨文化思辨能力，助力学生在多元文化语境中自信交流，为未来学术与职业发展筑牢语言根基。

课程简介（英文）

This Advanced English course focuses on integrated language skills training. It includes intensive reading and vocabulary building with authentic materials, listening and speaking practice through interactive activities, and academic writing guidance to enhance clarity and coherence. Students will engage in discussions, presentations, and writing tasks that expose them to various linguistic contexts. The course also incorporates cross-cultural communication scenarios to broaden students' global perspectives. Through these dynamic components, students will develop the proficiency needed for effective communication in both academic and professional settings.

教学目标

本课程以培养学生的语言能力、文化意识、思维品质与学习能力为核心，将英语学习视为塑造全面发展人才的重要途径。教学中注重提升学生的英语综合应用能力的同时，培养思辨能力、国际沟通能力和跨文化能力，使学生具备国际视野与社会责任感，以满足国家对国际化人才的战略需求和个人的多元发展愿景。

教学方式

本课程采用线下互动式教学模式，注重学生的积极参与和课堂互动。教学方法包括： 小组讨论：通过小组合作，促进学生之间的思想碰撞和观点交流。 课堂演讲与展示：锻炼学生的口头表达能力和自信心。 互动式讲解：教师结合多媒体教学材料，指导学生进行语言知识、思维知识和文化知识的建构，并随时与学生互动答疑。 课堂练习与即时反馈：通过课堂练习及时巩固知识，教师即时反馈帮助学生纠正错误。

教学内容与进度安排

Week Module Teaching Goals 1 Orientation and Course Overview Learners are expected to: 1. understand the objectives, content,schedule, grading policy and the overall semester arrangement of the course; 2. familiarize themselves with online and AI resources that will facilitate their English language studies. 2 U1 Text A: Is True Friendship Dying Away (Text analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. grasp the main idea (social media is making true friendship dying away) and the structure of the text (thesis-support-conclusion); 2. master the key language points and usages. 3 U1 Text B: Friends.com (presenting contradictory views) U1 Writing strategy: Making a comparison and contrast U1 Speaking strategy: Conducting a debate Learners are expected to: 1.learn about two major ways of making a comparison and contrast (the block method and the point-by-point method); Have a mock debate 2. conduct a series of reading, listening, speaking and writing activities related to the theme of the unit (friendship in the age of social media). 4 U2 Text A: Can Emotional Intelligence Be Learned (text analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary for the discussion of how to enhance EQ 5 U2 Text B: Charisma---The Mysterious Personality of Charm (Cultural study of the text) U2 Writing strategy: Writing an Argumentative Essay Learners are expected to: 1. express themselves more freely about the character traits related to EQ and Charisma ; 2. know the structure of a standard argumentative essay. 6 U6 Text A: The Way (Text analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of the concept of the Way in Chinese philosophy. 7 U6 Text B: The Chinese Writing System (Cultural study of the text) U3 Writing strategy: Poster Presentation Learners are expected to: 1. develop a critical understanding of the Chinese writing system as opposed to other major writing systems; 2. Design a poster on a Chinse craft and make a presentation. 8 Mid-term Examination 9 U5 Text A: The Planetary Killer (Text analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of endangered species and nature conservation. 10 U5 Text B: How Ignoring climate change could sink the US economy (Cultural study of the text) U4 Writing strategy: Writing a report Learners are expected to: 1. expand their vocabulary about global warming; 2. complete a report on an environmental issue entitled “The Three Cs (causes, consequences and control) of…” . 11 U3 Text A: Characteristics of Science (Text analysis and Language study) Learners are expected to: 1. achieve a thorough understanding of Text A contextually and linguistically; 2. build up an active vocabulary around the theme of science in life. 12 U3 Text B: Coffee Stains (Cultural study of the text) U5 Writing strategy: Presenting Arguments Cohesively Learners are expected to: 1. develop critical thinking abilities and ways to explain a natural phenomenon in a scientific way. 2. develop writing strategies for presenting arguments cohesively. 13 Presentation 1 Learners are required to give a presentation related to the topics in the textbook. 14 Presentation 2 Learners are required to give a presentation related to the topics in the textbook. 15 Q&A 16 Final Oral Test (In-class test) 17 Final Written Test 18 Submission of Final Grades

课程简介（中文）

本讨论班是为二年级研究生研究算子理论和算子代数开设的，它以研究生的在一年级的基础出发循序渐进地让学生进入到能开始课题研究的地步。内容有顺从代数与核代数，顺从群与Weyl环和Weyl环的自同态与同构的关系。

课程简介（英文）

This seminar is intended to provide some studying area including operator theory and operator algebras. It is based on the bases of the graduates freshmen and leads the graduate students to the reserach topic. It include amenable algebras and nuclear algebras, amenable groups as well as Weyl rings and its endmorphisms and automorphisms.

教学目标

It is intended to let the participants merging into the research areas, studying the problems they pick up.

教学方式

Graduate students give talks in each week.

教学内容与进度安排

1. Ameable groups 2. Nuclear aglebras 3. Weyl rings 4. Automorphism

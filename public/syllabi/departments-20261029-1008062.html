课程简介（中文）

本课程将根据会计理论框架体系，结合理论界的研究进展，主要讲授如下内容： 1、会计理论框架体系：阐释会计理论研究的主流脉络以及会计研究主要领域的内容； 2、财务报告的决策基础：结合市场有效理论，研究财务会计报告的决策相关性； 3、会计政策选择的经济后果：分析会计政策选择的经济后果的同时，研究经济后果对会计准则的影响； 4、管理者激励补偿与公司价值：研究管理者激励补偿制定依据以及激励补偿策略与公司价值的关系； 5、盈余管理：研究盈余管理的动机、方式及其正、负面效应； 6、会计准则制定的经济和政治问题：分析不同利益主体的利益冲突与协调，管制理论对准则的影响。

课程简介（英文）

This course, structured around the framework of accounting theory and incorporating the latest research advancements, primarily covers the following topics: Accounting Theory Framework: Explains the mainstream approaches in accounting theory research and the content of major accounting research areas. Decision Basis of Financial Reporting: Investigates the decision relevance of financial accounting reports in conjunction with the Efficient Market Hypothesis. Economic Consequences of Accounting Policy Choice: Analyzes the economic consequences of accounting policy choices while examining how these consequences influence accounting standards. Managerial Incentive Compensation and Firm Value: Studies the basis for formulating managerial incentive compensation and the relationship between incentive compensation strategies and firm value. Earnings Management: Explores the motivations for earnings management, its methods, and its positive and negative effects. Economic and Political Issues in Accounting Standard Setting: Analyzes conflicts and coordination among different stakeholders, and the impact of regulatory theories on accounting standards.

教学目标

会计理论专题(Accounting Theory)将根据会计理论框架体系，结合理论界的研究进展，实现以下教学目标： 第一、使学生系统掌握会计理论研究框架及研究进程 第二、培养学生运用规范的研究方法 第三、使学生能够在我国的制度背景下提出、分析并解决我国企业的实际问题

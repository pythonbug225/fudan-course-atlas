领导力课程系列二

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程以案例分析、互动小组合作、项目式学习以及为我院本科生定制的两次实地考察为主要特色。四个板块的核心内容如下： （1）有效沟通：旨在提升学生的英语学术写作与实际应用写作（如邮件、感谢信等）能力，同时加强英语口语表达、高效团队协作及跨文化沟通技巧； （2）项目管理：介绍项目管理的基本概念与实践方法，通过真实商业场景帮助学生锻炼战略沟通、分析思维、问题解决和利益相关者管理能力； （3）数字化社交网络：探讨如何运用电子邮件、领英、推特等社交平台，建立良好的个人品牌与专业人脉网络，介绍当前最佳实践； （4）撰写有力的申请文书：延伸前三个板块所学技能与理念，重点提升学生在国际商业竞赛及研究生申请中所需的思维整合、协作能力、文书撰写与口头陈述能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course features case analysis, interactive group works, project-based learning, as well as 2 fieldtrips tailor-made for our FISF’s undergraduate cohort. The main contents of the 4 sections are as below: Effective Communication: Aims to improve students’ English academic writing and practical writing (such as emails, thank-you letters, etc.) skills, as well as English speaking, effective teamwork, and cross-cultural communication skills; Project Management: Introduces the fundamental concepts and practices of project management and facilitates students to practice strategic communication, analytical thinking, problem-solving, and stakeholder management skills through real-world business scenarios; Digital Networking: Discusses the contemporary best practices of building a strong personal brand and professional network by utilizing email, LinkedIn, Twitter and other social media platforms; Writing Powerful Application Essays: Extends skills and concepts learned in the previous three sections, focusing on accelerating students’ thinking, collaborating, writing, and oral presenting abilities that could be applied in both international business competitions and graduate applications.

教学目标

This course aims to develop students’ communication and leadership skills that are crucial for their later studies as well as career journey, encouraging the young learners to become brave explorers, effective team-players, and future charismatic leaders. The training and assignments focus specifically on improving students’ ability to write, speak, work in a team, and communicate across cultures in their roles as future business leaders or finance experts. To meet the objectives, the curriculum outlines carefully selected contents in four sections, which are (1) effective communication, (2) project management, (3) digital networking, and (4) writing powerful application essays. Furthermore, as this course is delivered fully in English, the diverse types of class activities would improve students’ English proficiency, enabling them to transition smoothly into the subsequent professional courses taught in English. * This Leadership Series could be set as a prerequisite for the Intellection through Action course.

教学方式

Group discussions; group projects; individual assignments; reading assignments.

教学内容与进度安排

Introductions: Guiding students through the syllabus, course requirements, policies, assignments, and fieldtrip arrangements; Effective Communication: Defining effective communication; Achieving effective communication in an English-speaking context by using culturally appropriate expressions and speaking styles; Learning business etiquette through professional scenario practices; Understanding the keys of effective communication in formal English writing; Writing professional English emails; Introducing practical online tools for improving efficiency; Exploring logical expression, personal influence, and empathy in relation to effective communication. Project Management: Defining and describing project management roles, life cycles, and basic methodologies, and discovering the transferrable mindset of a successful project manager; Introducing useful collaboration tools for managing tasks, such as Excel, Google Sheet, Asana, Slack, and Trello; Setting project goals wisely and initiating your project; Mapping out your project in the planning phase, identifying best practices and resources, and understanding potential risks; Executing the project, managing team dynamics, and enabling continuous improvement; Closing and presenting your project effectively and collecting feedback from stakeholders; Understanding Agile project management approach and its applications in the finance industry. Digital Networking: Building your resume on LinkedIn; Using social media to build and sustain a global professional network; learning to engage with professionals and scholars on Meta/Facebook and LinkedIn; Examining the pros and cons of online networking and exploring effective techniques; Mastering online meetings and video interviews in an English-speaking context; Scenario Practice I； Scenario Practice II； Writing Powerful Application Essays: Introducing different types of academic essays and graduate application writings, including personal statement, research proposal, and statement of interests; Exploring potential methods to demonstrate leadership in your application essays; Brainstorming and writing practice workshop; Introducing top-tier international business competitions and analyzing the requirements, learning outcomes, and impacts on personal development; Adopting the necessary researching, analytical, and interpreting skills that help you to achieve in business competitions; Writing effectively to make persuasive arguments in business competitions; Showcasing different types of leadership skills in business competitions; Studying and discussing business cases, grouping, and explaining the thesis for final mock business competition. Final 1: Group Project Presentation; Final 2: Mock Business Competition.

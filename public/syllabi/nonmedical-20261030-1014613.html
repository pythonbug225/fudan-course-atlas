课程简介（中文）

本课程采用英语教学，主要用来提高研究生的专业英语听说读写的能力。课程全部采用英文教学，必要的时候采用少量中文讲解。以同学阅读专业和非专业文献，然后课堂作报告，老师点评为主要课堂形式，课后会布置相应写作任务。希望通过该课程，让中高年级研究生能够轻松阅读英语文献，并能够熟练用英文作报告和撰写论文。

课程简介（英文）

This course will be taught in English, primarily aims to improve graduate students' professional English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. The course is taught entirely in English, with limited Chinese instruction where necessary. The primary format is for students to read both specialized and non-specialized literature, then present in class with teacher commentary. Following class, students will be assigned a writing assignment. The goal is to enable mid- to advanced-level graduate students to readily read English literature and become proficient in presenting and writing papers in English.

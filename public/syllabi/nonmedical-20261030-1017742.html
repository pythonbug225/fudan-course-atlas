土壤环境化学

2026春 · ESE50070.01 · 潘泽真、王梓萌

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

水体及界面化学过程在微量金属和营养元素的生物地球化学循环，以及污染物在土壤和地下水等环境中的迁移与归趋中发挥着重要作用。本课程围绕自然与工程水环境体系中相关的化学反应过程展开讲授。课程内容主要聚焦于土壤与地下环境体系中的化学过程，同时也将系统介绍水化学无机部分的一般概念与研究方法。课程强调定量分析方法，重点训练学生利用化学平衡与动力学理论解决实际环境化学问题的能力，并对土壤介质中重要的环境化学行为进行讲解。课程主要内容包括：化学平衡与反应动力学、酸碱平衡与碱度、土壤矿物的溶解与沉淀过程、氧化还原反应、微团聚体（microaggregates）概念、碳循环、磷循环、有机污染物的环境归趋与迁移，以及土壤生态系统相关过程。课程在讲授土壤化学基本原理的同时，将结合典型案例研究，对相关过程进行深入分析。

课程简介（英文）

Aquatic and interfacial chemical processes govern aspects of the biogeochemical cycling of trace metals and nutrients, contaminant fate and transport in various environments including soil and groundwater. This course examines chemical reactions relevant to natural and engineered aquatic systems. While the topics of the course will be primarily about soil and subsurface systems, general concepts and approaches of inorganic aspects of aquatic chemistry will be compressively discussed. A quantitative approach emphasized the solution of chemical equilibrium and kinetics problems. Topics covered include chemical equilibrium and kinetics, acid-base equilibrium and alkalinity, dissolution and precipitation of soil minerals, oxidation-reduction processes, concepts of microaggregates, carbon cycle, phosphate cycle, the fate and transport of organic pollutants and soil ecosystems. Course lectures on soil chemistry principles are supplemented by detailed examinations of case studies.

教学目标

After completing this course, students should be able to: Explain the relevance and importance of significant reactions in soil environmental systems. Formulate and solve chemical equilibrium problems for complex soil interfacial systems. Critically evaluate soil environmental chemistry processes such as the mineral-carbon interactions. Apply a software application to assist in solving chemical equilibrium problems. Communicate and explain soil chemistry principles in multiple formats.

教学方式

This course is primarily delivered through lectures, complemented by case studies and classroom discussions. The lectures systematically introduce the fundamental theories and analytical approaches related to aquatic and soil interfacial chemical processes, including chemical equilibrium, reaction kinetics, and relevant quantitative problem-solving methods. Building on these theoretical foundations, representative environmental case studies are incorporated to help students connect conceptual principles with real environmental processes. A quantitative and problem-oriented approach is emphasized throughout the course. Worked examples and in-class exercises are used to train students to apply thermodynamic and kinetic principles to analyze and solve environmental chemistry problems. Selected topics will also be linked to current research themes in soil and environmental chemistry, such as mineral–organic matter interactions, carbon and phosphorus cycling, and contaminant fate and transport in soil–groundwater systems, encouraging analytical thinking and scientific discussion. In addition, students will be encouraged to consult relevant scientific literature and participate in topic-based discussions. Through the analysis of representative research cases, the course aims to strengthen students’ ability to interpret environmental geochemical processes and to apply theoretical knowledge to complex environmental systems.

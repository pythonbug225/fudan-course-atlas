课程简介（中文）

本课程系统讲授蛋白质纯化与鉴定的靶向及非靶向策略，深入解析生物质谱的基本原理及其在生命科学与生物技术领域中的应用。教学内容涵盖质谱分析基础、生物分子的软电离技术、各类质量分析器的工作原理，以及液相色谱-质谱联用技术在定量蛋白质组学中的实践，包括无标记定量、SILAC和TMT标记等方法。此外，课程还将介绍离子迁移谱、深度学习等前沿技术在质谱分析领域的最新进展，帮助学员掌握现代蛋白质组学的核心技术与发展趋势。

课程简介（英文）

This course systematically introduces targeted and untargeted strategies for protein purification and identification, and provides an in-depth analysis of the fundamental principles of biological mass spectrometry and its applications in life sciences and biotechnology. Topics covered include the basics of mass spectrometry, soft ionization techniques for biomolecules, the working principles of various mass analyzers, and the application of liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) in quantitative proteomics, including label‑free quantification, SILAC, and TMT labeling. In addition, the course will present recent advances in cutting‑edge technologies such as ion mobility spectrometry and deep learning in mass spectrometry analysis, helping students master the core technologies and development trends in modern proteomics.

教学目标

本课程面向化学、生命科学及医学相关专业的本科生与研究生，旨在帮助学生理解质谱技术在生命科学与生物技术中的核心作用，拓展跨学科研究视野，并掌握将质谱方法整合应用于生物与临床研究领域的能力。学生将系统学习质谱的基本原理，包括电离技术、质量分析器及数据采集方法，深入掌握定量蛋白质组学的常用策略。课程同时注重培养学生运用靶向与非靶向蛋白质组学方法进行实验设计的能力，使其能够有效分析和解读质谱数据，从而应对生物研究中的复杂挑战。

教学方式

本课程采用“理论—技术—应用”三维融合的教学模式，突出前沿性、系统性与实践性的有机结合。具体安排如下：
1. 理论讲授：系统讲解质谱技术的理论基础、仪器原理及前沿进展。
2. 案例研讨：围绕心血管疾病、肿瘤、神经疾病等真实研究案例，引导学生掌握质谱技术在生物医学中的具体应用。
3. 课堂小组讨论：以“生物样本—组学数据—机制验证—转化探索”为主线，组织跨环节专题研讨，引导学生构建从基础研究到临床转化的系统性科学思维。
通过多元教学方式的融合，本课程致力于构建开放互动的学习平台，助力学生扎实掌握蛋白质组学技术，为未来开展跨学科研究奠定坚实基础。

教学内容与进度安排

1. Introduction

Topic: Why do we need protein and proteome analysis in life sciences and biotechnology?

Expected Achievement: Understand the significance of proteins and proteomics in advancing research and its role in solving biological and clinical challenges.

2. Classical targeted protein analysis

Topic: Functional assays, protein purification, and identification.

Expected Achievement: Gain familiarity with traditional protein analysis methods and their applications.

3. Liquid chromatography of proteins

Topic: principles, strategies and chromatographic modes.

Expected Achievement: Understand the role and methods of chromatographic techniques in protein separation.

4. Basic principles of proteome analysis

Topic: Bottom-up and top-down proteomics.

Expected Achievement: Learn the fundamental approaches for comprehensive proteome studies.

5. Mass spectrometry basics

Topic: Principles, definitions, and results.

Expected Achievement: Develop a basic understanding of how mass spectrometry works and its core principles.

6. Mass Spectrometry – Soft Ionization Methods

Topic: Techniques for analyzing biomolecules and their applications.

Expected Achievement: Grasp the techniques like ESI and MALDI and their importance in proteomics.

7. Mass Spectrometry – Mass Analyzer

Topic: Principles of different types of mass analyzers.

Expected Achievement: Comprehend the working principles of mass analyzers and their application in proteomics.

8. Mass Spectrometry – Hybrid Instruments

Topic: Applications in protein analysis and proteomics.

Expected Achievement: Understand advanced hybrid mass spectrometry technologies for protein studies.

9. Protein Identification via Mass Spectrometry

Topic: Methods for identifying proteins in complex samples.

Expected Achievement: Learn workflows for protein identification and data interpretation.

10. Quantification of Proteins: Label-Free Approaches (Untargeted)

Topic: Using untargeted LC-MS approach for biomarker identification.

Expected Achievement: Master untargeted strategies for discovering biomarkers.

11. Quantification of Proteins: Label-Free Approaches (Targeted)

Topic: Using targeted LC-MS approach for biomarker validation.

Expected Achievement: Understand targeted quantification for biomarker validation.

12. Quantification of Proteins: Label-Based Approaches

Topic: SILAC, TMT, and other labeling techniques.

Expected Achievement: Gain expertise in label-based techniques for accurate protein quantification.

13. Bioinformatics for Proteomics

Topic: Tools and software for analyzing proteomic data.

Expected Achievement: Learn to process and interpret complex proteomic datasets using bioinformatics tools.

14. Differential Quantitative Proteomics

Topic: Deciphering molecular mechanisms underlined cardiovascular diseases and cancers.

Expected Achievement: Apply quantitative methods to uncover biological mechanisms.

15. Proteoform Analysis

Topic: The next level of proteomics.

Expected Achievement: Develop knowledge of proteoform diversity and its biological implications.

16. Databases, Knowledgebases, and Atlases

Topic: Resources for proteomics.

Expected Achievement: Utilize available proteomics resources for research and data validation.

17. Discussion and Q&A

Topic: Course review, discussions, and paper assignments.

Expected Achievement: Clarify concepts, address questions, and plan for course paper writing.

18. Course Paper Writing

Topic: Guidance on writing course papers.

Expected Achievement: Demonstrate understanding by applying course knowledge to a well-structured scientific paper.

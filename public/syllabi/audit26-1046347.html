课程简介（中文）

《临床皮肤学基础》介绍了皮肤学的基本理论，包括皮肤的解剖结构和功能、皮肤疾病的症状和治疗、常见病和多发病的诊断、鉴别诊断以及预防原则。

课程简介（英文）

The Basics of Clinical Dermatology is an introduction of the basic theory of dermatology, including the anatomy and function of the skin, the symptoms and treatment of skin diseases, diagnosis of common and frequently-occurring diseases, differential diagnosis and prevention principles.

教学目标

Dermatology is a subject in which morphological teaching plays an important role. We hope that each student will grasp the basic characteristics and principles of the dermatology and venereology through the study of this course. Through the teacher's informative presentations, the classroom PPT, books and reference materials, our students will have a basic understanding of dermatology and venereology, and will have a preliminary perception of common diseases. This curriculum will provide the student with clinical internship in both outpatient clinic and wards, which will establish a solid foundation for post-undergraduate and possible future further developments in dermatology. Last but no least, we hope our curriculum will inspire our students’ interest in dermatology.

教学方式

Method of lecture and trainee teaching.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Structure and function of human skin, introduction of skin structure, the histology of the skin and function of the skin.

Master：the histological and anatomical features of the skin epidermis and the important physiological functions of the skin. Includes keratinocyte layers (stratum corneum to basale), melanocytes, barrier functions, UV protection, and vitamin D synthesis.

Be familiar with： the distribution, histological and anatomical features, and functions of skin appendages. Covers hair follicles (mechanical protection), sebaceous glands (sebum secretion), sweat glands (thermoregulation), and nails (protective structures).

Understand ：the dendritic cells within the epidermis and their key physiological roles. Focus on Langerhans cells (antigen presentation, immune surveillance) and other dendritic cell types in epidermal immunity.

 

Week 2 Diagnosis of Skin Disease, general medical history and special medical history, skin lesions ，physical examination and check-up of laboratory.

Master：the basic lesions of various dermatological diseases and differentiate their distinguishing features. Focus on recognizing morphological variations (e.g., macules, papules, vesicles) and contrasting their clinical presentations.

Be familiar with：primary and secondary lesions through clinical case studies. Primary lesions: Macules, papules, nodules, vesicles, pustules. Secondary lesions: Scales, crusts, erosions, ulcers, scars. Practice identifying these lesions in context (e.g., differentiating psoriasis scales vs. eczema crusts).

Understand ：general diagnostic methods，physical examination and check-up of laboratory.

 

Week 3 Drug Eruptions, the definition, etiology, laboratory tests, clinical classification, presentation and treatment of drug eruption.

Master：the clinical manifestations and treatment of various types of drug eruptions, especially the management of severe drug eruptions.

Be familiarize with： the diagnosis and differential diagnosis of drug eruptions.

Understand： the pathogenesis of drug eruptions.

 

Week 4 Psoriasis，the definition, clinical classification, presentation and treatment of psoriasis.

Master: the clinical manifestations, diagnostic criteria, and treatment principles of poriasis Vulgaris.

Be miliar with：the clinical manifestations and treatment principles of poriasis athropathica、psoriasis pustulosa and psoriasis erythrodermica.

Understand：recent advances in psoriasis treatment.

 

Week 5 Therapy of Skin Disease, drugs in dermatology, topical therapy, physical therapy and dermatologic surgery.

Master：the mechanism of action and adverse effects of antihistamines, as well as the pharmacodynamics, formulations, therapeutic principles, and precautions for both topical and systemic glucocorticoids.

Be familiar with：the indications and primary adverse reactions of other systemic medications.

Understand：common dermatological treatments, including systemic/topical medications, physical therapies, and surgical interventions, along with indications and recent advances in physical/surgical therapies.

 

Week 6 Viral Infections, varicella and herpes zoster, herpes simplex and warts, hand-foot-mouth disease and HIV.

Master：the etiology, pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, and treatment of herpes zoster, herpes simplex, varicella (chickenpox), and verruca vulgaris (common warts).

Be familiar with：the etiology, pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, and treatment of molluscum contagiosum and verruca plana (flat warts).

Understand：the etiology, pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, and treatment of hand-foot-mouth disease (HFMD) and HIV.

 

Week 7 Acne, the classification of acne severity and the clinical presentation, the etiology and pathophysiology, the treatment of acne.

Master: the treatments for acne.

Be familiar with: the clinical manifestations of acne.

Understand: the pathogenesis of acne.

 

Week 8 Vitiligo, the epidemiology and etiology, symptoms and signs, diagnosis and treatment.

Master: the treatments for vitiligo.

Be familiar with: the clinical manifestations of vitiligo.

Understand: the pathogenesis of vitiligo.

 

Week 9 Atopic Eczema and Contact Dermatitis, the definition, clinical presentation and treatment of atopic eczema and contact dermatitis.

Master: the symptoms, diagnosis, and management of allergic diseases such as atopic eczema and contact dermatitis.

Be familiar with: the pathogenic mechanisms of allergic diseases including atopic eczema and contact dermatitis.

Understand: recent research advances in h allergic diseases.

 

Week 10 Lupus Erythematosus, definition, classification, etiology, clinical manifestations, histopathology, laboratory findings, diagnosis and managements.

Master: the clinical manifestations, laboratory investigations, and diagnostic criteria of systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE).

Be familiar with: the etiology, classification, prevention, and management of lupus erythematosus.

Understand: the pathogenesis and recent advances in lupus erythematosus.

 

Week 11 Urticaria and angioedema, the definition, clinical classification, presentation and treatment of urticaria and Angioedema.

Master: the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and treatment of urticaria and angioedema.

Be familiar with: the etiology and classification of urticaria and angioedema.

Understand: the pathogenesis of urticaria and angioedema.

 

Week 12 Sexually Transmitted Diseases(1), introduction, gonorrhea, nongonococcal urethritis and condyloma acuminatum.

Master: the etiology, clinical manifestations, laboratory investigations, diagnosis, and management of gonorrhea and condyloma acuminatum (genital warts).

Be familiar with： the etiology, clinical manifestations, laboratory investigations, diagnosis, and management of non-gonococcal urethritis (NGU).

Understand: the definition of sexually transmitted diseases (STDs).

 

Week 13 Sexually Transmitted Diseases(2), syphilis, chancroid and genital herpes.

Master：the etiology, clinical manifestations, and treatment of syphilis and genital herpes.

Be familiar with：the epidemiology, laboratory tests, and diagnosis of syphilis and genital herpes.

Understand：the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and treatment of chancroid.

 

Week 14 Fungal Infections, the definition, clinical presentation and treatment of dermatophytosis and candidiasis infection and pityrosporum infection.

Master：the incidence, pathogens, modes of infection, clinical manifestations, laboratory examinations, diagnosis, and prevention of common fungal diseases.

Be familiarize with： the clinical manifestations and treatment of candidiasis and tinea versicolor.

Understand：the latest research progress on dermatophytosis and candidiasis.

 

Week 15 Blistering disorders, the pathogenesis, clinical manifestation, histological findings and treatment of pemphigoid and pemphigus.

Master： the clinical manifestations and treatment of pemphigus and pemphigoid.

Be familiarize with: the pathogenesis of pemphigus and pemphigoid.

Understand: the classification of autoimmune blistering diseases.

 

Week 16 Q&A

There were five clinical internships, two dermatology outpatient trainees, and three dermatology ward trainees:

Week6 Dermatology outpatient trainee, the overview of dermatology, the structure and function of human skin, the diagnosis of skin disease and the therapy of skin disease.

Week8 Dermatology ward trainee, the overview of ward.

Week10 Dermatology ward trainee, the common severe dermatology diseases(1).

Week 12 Dermatology ward trainee, the common severe dermatology diseases(2).

Week 14 Dermatology outpatient trainee, the common diseases in outpatient.

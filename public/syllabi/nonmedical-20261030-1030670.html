语言与社会

2026春 · FORE50066.01 · 宋旸

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在从社会语言学角度介绍语言与社会的关系。从语言角度出发，课程将介绍语言特征（语音、词汇、语法、结构等）在社会和历史因素（如地区、社会阶层、性别、种族和民族，以及不同历史时期等）影响下的流变。从社会角度出发，课程也将介绍中西语境下语言使用在社会进程（包括全球化、城市化、科技变革等）中的作用。

课程简介（英文）

This course is meant to introduce the relationship between language and society from the perspective of sociolinguistics. We focus on how language aspects (pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar, structure) change and vary in response to social factors (region, social class, gender, race and ethnicity) and historical periods. We also focus on how language use plays a role in social processes in different social contexts around the world, such as processes of institutionalization, globalization, and education as well as the construction of cosmopolitan and virtual identities. The course integrates theoretical and methodological issues by discussing classic studies in sociolinguistics.

教学目标

通过修读本课程，学生预期能够： （1）理解语言与社会的关系 （2）解释并反思语言使用如何完成社会活动和建构社会身份 （3）运用社会语言学理论与方法分析社会语境下的语言活动 After taking this course, students are expected to be able to： 1. Understand the relationship between language and society; 2. Analyze and reflect on how social processes have been partly realized by and reflected in language use; 3. Analyze and reflect on language use in social contexts with sociolinguistic theories and approaches.

课程简介（英文）

The course Theory and Practice of Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era is built on the idea that it is not only a mandatory ideological and political course but also a liberal arts course in the humanities and social sciences. Guided by the concept of "global perspective, local action," this course comprehensively and systematically explains and analyzes the theoretical qualities, historical significance, structural framework, and core content of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era. The course aims to cultivate a group of high-caliber talents with profound humanities and social sciences literacy, contributing to the reform and opening-up in the new era. We encourage all students to thoroughly study the textbook and extensively read books in the humanities and social sciences. Through systematic and professional learning, we expect students to develop strong humanistic qualities, master scientific research methods, acquire professional knowledge, and become qualified citizens.

教学目标

培养学生独立理解和分析中国、西方和马克思主义传统中的基础理论脉络、基本范畴、基本范式、基本制度的能力 培养研究生的理论凝练能力和运用核心理论解释现实问题的能力 培养学生运用马克思主义中国化的基本理论对政治、经济、社会、文化、生态和国际问题进行阐释和分析的能力 培养学生运用比较方法进行论文写作和批判研究的能力

教学方式

中国、西方、马克思主义基本元典阐释学 原理+案例式讲解和分析方法 比较研究和批判的方法

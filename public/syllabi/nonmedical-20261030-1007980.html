Application of Blockchain in Finance

2025秋 · MF70011.02 · 唐敦哲

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

Blockchain is an emerging and highly disruptive technology. This course introduces the fundamental mechanism behind this technology and introduces its applications in Finance. The applications include cryptocurrency like Bitcoin, cryptocurrency with smart contract functions like Ethereum, advanced blockchain applications like DeFi, NFT, and others. The course also discusses current blockchain related policy of different countries around the world. In this course students will learn what blockchain is and how it can create value by tokenization in cryptocurrencies and in many other practical applications.

教学目标

The goals of the course are to: (i) provide an advanced understanding of the various blockchain technologies; (ii) determine the specific business situations where blockchain technology can be deployed to solve important problems; (iii) select the specific blockchain technology that has the best chance of success for a particular problem; and (iv) detail the risks presented by this new technology.

教学方式

Students are encouraged to participate in in-class discussion and are required to collaborate with each other to finish the group project.

课程简介（中文）

本课程探讨当今企业所面临的商业和技术伦理挑战，以及个人和公司如何应对这些挑战。本课程旨在探讨能够帮助学生在个人、组织和社会等不同层面识别、分析以及做出伦理决策的概念、理论和框架。课程讲授内容涵盖的主题包括：伦理推理、科技伦理原则以及商业中广泛存在的伦理道理问题，以及解决这些伦理问题的规范。

课程简介（英文）

This course explores the ethical challenges faced by businesses in the era of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence, big data, and digital platforms. It examines how individuals and organizations make ethical decisions and respond to complex dilemmas. The course introduces key concepts, theories, and frameworks that help students identify, analyze, and evaluate ethical issues at individual, organizational, and societal levels. Topics include moral reasoning, ethical principles in business and technology, and analyses of real-world ethical challenges.

教学目标

The course aims to develop students’ ethical awareness, theoretical understanding, and practical decision-making ability. Students will learn to recognize ethical issues in business and technology contexts, understand their importance, and analyze them using structured frameworks. They will also gain the ability to evaluate ethical implications of technological applications and propose responsible solutions. In addition, the course emphasizes cultivating a sense of responsibility and critical thinking in organizational and societal contexts.

教学方式

The course combines lectures, case discussions, and interactive exercises to facilitate learning. Students will engage in discussions of real-world cases, participate in in-class exercises, and complete team-based assignments. The teaching approach emphasizes critical thinking, application of theory to practice, and active participation.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 (3/2): Introduction to Business and Technology Ethics: course overview, ethical challenges in modern business. Week 2 (3/9): Introduction continued: ethical issues in AI, data, and digital platforms. Week 3 (3/16): Moral Reasoning I: Introducing the basic process of ethical decision-making Week 4 (3/23): Moral Reasoning II: Moral development Week 5 (3/30): Moral Reasoning III: Impediments of moral behaviors, with a with a specific focus on cognitive biases and moral disengagement. Week 6 (4/6): Holiday – No Class. Week 7 (4/13): Ethical Principles I:  Major ethical theories and frameworks in business such as Utilitarianism and Rights Theory. Week 8 (4/20): Ethical Principles II: Major ethical theories and frameworks in business such as Justice Theory and fairness. Week 9 (4/27): Ethical Principles III: Major ethical theories and frameworks in business such as Care Ethics and corporate ethical codes. Week 10 (5/4): Holiday – No Class. Week 11 (5/11): Corporate Social Responsibility I: CSR theories. Week 12 (5/18): Corporate Social Responsibility II: CSR in real-world business cases. Week 13 (5/25): Ethical Issues – Environment: Discussion of environmental challenges and corporate environmental responsibilities, including sustainability and the environmental impact of emerging technologies. Short presentations will be given by student teams. Week 14 (6/1): Ethical Issues – Consumers: Discussion of ethical issues affecting consumer rights, including data privacy, data protection, and algorithmic influence. Short presentations will be given by student teams. Week 15 (6/8): Ethical Issues –Discussion of ethical issues affecting employees’ rights, including AI in hiring, workplace monitoring, and diversity and inclusion. Short presentations will be given by student teams. Week 16 (6/15): Final team presentations on selected research papers related to technology. Week 17–18: Final exam period.

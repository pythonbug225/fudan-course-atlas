话语分析

课程简介（中文）

本课程对人文和社科领域常用的话语分析方法进行介绍，包括会话分析、内容分析、扎根理论、体裁分析、批评话语分析、多模态话语分析、基于语料库的话语分析等。课程不仅追溯各种话语分析方法产生的背景、挖掘其背后的理论框架，而且让学生亲身体验话语分析的操作过程，帮助学生在实践中领悟不同话语分析方法的异同，以便在自己的研究中能够选择并运用恰当的话语分析方法。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces various approaches to discourse analysis that are commonly used in humanities and social sciences, including conversation analysis, content analysis, grounded theory, genre analysis, critical discourse analysis, multi-modal discourse analysis, corpus-based discourse analysis, and so on.The course not only explores the origin(s) and the theoretical framework(s) behind each approach, but also give students opportunites to experience the process of doing discourse analysis, with a view to letting students find out for themselves both differences and similarities among the various approaches to discourse analysis, so that they would be able to choose and use an appropriate approach in their own academic research.

教学目标

This course aims to help students to a) gain familiarity with concepts, methods, current trends and directions of discourse analysis; b) gain insights into, and experience of, various approaches to discourse analysis, so as find an approach suitable for their own research needs; c) develop their ability to conduct research using methods of discourse analysis to solve academic or practical problems.

教学方式

The course is conducted through lectures, workshops, actual analyses of discourse, and other kinds of hands-on research activities.

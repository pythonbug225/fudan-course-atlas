课程简介（中文）

本课程提供了统计推断理论的严格基础，为统计及相关领域的高级学习和研究奠定了基础。主题包括点估计，区间估计和假设检验的理论，重点是推导和评估使用数理统计原理的统计程序。 所涉及的关键概念包括估计量的性质（如无偏性、一致性、充分性和效率）、最大似然方法、矩方法、cram<s:1> - rao下界、Rao-Blackwell定理和lehman - scheff<s:1>定理，以及用于假设检验的Neyman-Pearson框架。其他主题可能包括似然比测试，统一最强大的测试，并介绍贝叶斯推理。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a rigorous foundation in the theory of statistical inference, laying the groundwork for advanced study and research in statistics and related fields. Topics include the theory of point estimation, interval estimation, and hypothesis testing, with a focus on deriving and evaluating statistical procedures using principles of mathematical statistics. Key concepts covered include properties of estimators (such as unbiasedness, consistency, sufficiency, and efficiency), the method of maximum likelihood, the method of moments, the Cramér-Rao lower bound, the Rao-Blackwell and Lehmann–Scheffé theorems, and the Neyman–Pearson framework for hypothesis testing. Additional topics may include likelihood ratio tests, uniformly most powerful tests, and an introduction to Bayesian inference.

教学目标

By the end of the course, students will gain a deep theoretical understanding of fundamental inferential methods and develop the analytical skills necessary to derive and assess statistical procedures in a wide range of applications.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在使学生全面了解基督教哲学，并为其未来在该领域的学习和研究奠定坚实的基础。 本课程内容分为五个基本部分，将通过直接考察塑造基督教历史理解的原始文献来进行教学。 宗教哲学方法论导论 上帝论 三位一体论 道成肉身教义 赎罪教义

课程简介（英文）

This course will give students a broad knowledge of Christian philosophy and serve as a strong foundation for future education and research in the subject.The content of this course is divided into five basic sections: Introduction to the methodology of philosophy of religion: How is this methodology different from that of philosophy in general? In what order should we discuss Christian teachings? The Doctrine of God: How should Christians talk about God? How does Christianity understand God as related to creation? The Doctrine of the Trinity: What does it mean to talk about the Christian God as “Trinity”? What are the relations between the three “Persons” of the Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit)? The Doctrine of the Incarnation: How should we understand the claim that God became human? What do Christians believe this event accomplished? The Doctrine of Atonement: What do Christians believe the crucifixion of Jesus Christ accomplished for humanity? How have the mechanics of this event been variously understood?

教学目标

The course introduces the most important teachings (doctrines) that make Christianity distinctive: the Doctrine of God, the Doctrine of the Trinity, the Doctrine of the Incarnation, and the Doctrine of Atonement. It also discusses and analyzes several of the most influential and universally read scholars from across the history of Christianity. By examining the different explanations that each of these scholars give for the doctrines under discussion, the course demonstrates the diversity of Christian philosophy. It additionally gives students an opportunity to develop their own analytical skills to assess which explanations they find most convincing. The language of teaching for this course is English, giving students an supportive environment to practice reading philosophical texts in English, become familiar with key philosophical terms in English, and express their own academic ideas in oral and written English. The course content serves as an introduction to the analytical and research methodologies expected for international publication in philosophy. It will therefore be of particular benefit for students who intend to progress to graduate-level study or wish to continue their studies abroad.

教学方式

This course will be taught via weekly lectures that describe and analyze key sources in the field of Christian philosophy. Each lecture will end with a discussion about the text(s) covered, giving students the opportunity to raise any questions and develop critical thinking skills in a controlled environment.

教学内容与进度安排

Introduction to the methodology of philosophy of religion 1: Outline the structure of the course. Define philosophy of religion and its functional differences from philosophy generally considered. Doctrine of God 1: Explain what it means to talk about God through an examination of Thomas Aquinas’ ontological arguments. Doctrine of God 2: Discuss the Thomas Aquinas’ use of “divine simplicity” as a structuring principle for discussion of God. Doctrine of God 3: Discuss Karl Barth’s contrasting approach to the doctrine of God in terms of divine act. Doctrine of God 4: Examine Barth’s description of God as “the one who loves in freedom”. Doctrine of the Trinity 1: Explain why the doctrine of the Trinity does not propose the existence of three Gods. Doctrine of the Trinity 2: Discuss Augustine’s illustration of the triune relations in terms of the human mind. Doctrine of the Trinity 3: Examine an Sarah Coakley’s alternative approach to the Trinity in terms of contemplation and desire. Doctrine of the Incarnation 1: Outline the basic arguments regarding the incarnation as stated in the Nicaean and Chalcedonian definitions. Doctrine of the Incarnation 2: Explain the traditional understanding of the incarnation in terms of “non-competitive relation”. Doctrine of the Incarnation 3: Explore an alternative “kenotic” approach to the Incarnation proposed by Hegel. Doctrine of Atonement 1: Discuss the earliest ransom/cosmic theory of atonement as presented by Gustaf Aulen. Doctrine of Atonement 2: Explain the satisfaction theory of atonement presented by Anselm. Doctrine of Atonement 3: Outline Julian of Norwich’s rejection of the satisfaction and ransom theories of atonement in favour of universal salvation. Introduction to the methodology of philosophy of religion 2: Outline and discuss various approaches to the order in which we should discuss Christian teachings and their relative advantages. Recap and Discussion of course syllabus: Brief summary of the content of the course. Classroom debates regarding the most convincing approaches to each of the core doctrines covered.

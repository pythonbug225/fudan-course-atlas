时间序列分析的方法

2025秋 · ECON40004.02 · 王晓虎

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

大量的经济和金融变量拥有时间序列观察数据。本门课程将介绍时间序列数据的特点和基本的建模和预测方法。时间序列数据一般含有三个组成成分，即trends + seasonality + cycles. 本门课程将依次介绍这三个部分的建模和分析方法。重点将会放在对cycles 部分的分析，介绍内容包括平稳及非平稳时间序列的定义，基本特征，建模和预测方法，非平稳性检验，协整关系及其检验方法，以及对波动率数据的分析方法。     通过对本门课程的学习，学生将会较深入的了解时间序列数据的基本特点和基本的建模及预测方法。面对现实数据，学生应会自己构建模型，做出分析，并生成对数据未来走势的预测。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed as a sequel to Introduction to Econometrics and focuses on forecasting. Forecasting is essential because forecasts are constantly made in business, economics, government, finance, and many other fields, and much depends on them. This course introduces basic econometric models and techniques that are commonly used in generating forecasts of time series in economics and finance. A wide range of topics is covered,including basic concepts of forecasting, forecasting trends and seasonality, ARIMA processes, the combination of forecasts, evaluation of forecasts, and volatility forecasting. Upon successful completion of this course, students are expected to comprehend basic properties of modern forecasting models. Accordingly, students can skillfully select appropriate forecasting models to fit real data sets and to generate their forecasts.

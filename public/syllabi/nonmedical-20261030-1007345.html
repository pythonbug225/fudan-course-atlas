当代中国行政专题研究

2025秋 · MANA60060.01 · 扶松茂

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要探讨当代中国政治制度与行政制度的关系；当代中国行政制度的发展变革历程；当代中国行政制度的治理效应；中国行政体制的内容和特点；中国各级政府的职能配置、结构和运行机制等。主要内容包括：现代政府的观念、价值与功能；坚持与完善党对政府的全面领导；坚持与完善人大行使对政府的法定职权；国家元首制度；国务院的职权、机构与管理体制；政府间关系与区域发展战略；中国行政区划发展与变革；政府的组织系统与机构改革；政府的人员编制管理；行政立法制度；公共预算制度；行政监督制度；当代中国行政制度改革与国家治理能力、治理体系现代化。

课程简介（英文）

This course mainly explores the relationship between the political system and administrative system of contemporary China; the development and reform process of the administrative system of contemporary China; the governance effect of the administrative system of contemporary China; the content and characteristics of China's administrative system; the functional configuration, structure and operation mechanism of governments at all levels in China, etc. The main contents include: the concepts, values and functions of modern government; adhering to and improving the Party's overall leadership over the government; adhering to and improving the National People's Congress (NPC) in exercising its legal powers over the government; the system of state 元首 (head of state); the powers, institutions and management system of the State Council; intergovernmental relations and regional development strategies; the development and reform of China's administrative divisions; the government's organizational system and institutional reforms; the government's personnel establishment management; the administrative legislation system; the public budget system; the administrative supervision system; the reform of the administrative system of contemporary China and the modernization of the national governance capacity and governance system.

教学目标

通过本课程学习，了解中国政治制度与行政制度的关系；当代中国行政制度的发展变革历程；当代中国行政制度的治理效应；中国行政体制的内容和特点；中国各级政府的职能配置、结构和运行机制等。能运用中国行政制度的理论和实践知识，分析中国各级政府的职能转变、机构改革、运行机制等现代化变革面临的实际问题与完善路径。

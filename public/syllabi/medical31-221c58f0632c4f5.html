1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 人类易受约1400种不同微生物病原体的感染。其中约60%是具有动物宿主的微生物，而这些微生物中绝大多数是RNA病毒。过去15年来，新发病毒感染的发生率至少为每年一种。因此，在环境和社会变化的驱动下，新发病毒性疾病已成为21世纪严峻的公共卫生挑战。全球亟需确保做好充分准备，以应对新出现的、此前未被识别的疾病以及那些出乎意料地适应不断变化的环境条件的疾病。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Humans are susceptible to infection by approximately 1,400 diverse microbial pathogens. Around 60% of these are microbes with animal reservoirs, and among these the majority are RNA viruses. New emerging virus infections are occurring at the rate of at least one per year for the past 15 years. Emerging virus diseases thus represent a serious public health challenge in the 21st Century, driven by environmental and societal change. There is a global need to ensure adequate preparedness for combating both new, hitherto unrecognized diseases and those that unexpectedly adapt to changing environmental conditions.

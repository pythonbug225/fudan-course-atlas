课程简介（中文）

通过本课程学习，我们将带领学生探索日常生活中隐藏的毒物危机，揭示它们对我们健康的潜在影响。从生活住家到工作场所，再到社会环境，我们将一起揭开毒物的神秘面纱。在这个课堂上，我们将探索有趣的案例，如城市中人人接触的空气污染、食品中的不时接触的毒素，生活场所中的潜在毒物，工作场所的特殊危险物质，临床中经常遇到的药物中毒，真实事件和小说中的中毒事件等。除了深入了解毒物的知识外，我们还将探讨相关的监管标准和法规，以及毒物防治的最佳实践。我们将通过案例分析和互动讨论，培养学生的问题解决能力和批判思维，使他们能够在面对现实生活中的毒物挑战时游刃有余。

课程简介（英文）

Through this course, we will lead students to explore the hidden toxic hazards in daily life, revealing their potential impact on our health. From homes to workplaces and broader social environments, we will uncover the mysteries of toxins together. In this course, we will explore fascinating cases, such as air pollution that affects everyone in urban areas, toxins occasionally encountered in food, hidden hazardous substances in living spaces, dangerous chemicals in workplaces, frequently encountered drug poisonings in clinical settings, as well as real-life and fictional poisoning incidents. Beyond gaining in-depth knowledge of toxic substances, we will also examine relevant regulatory standards and laws, along with best practices for toxin prevention and control. Through case studies and interactive discussions, we aim to develop students' problem-solving skills and critical thinking, empowering them to confidently navigate real-world toxicological challenges.

教学目标

这门课程不仅仅是关于毒物的知识，更是关于如何保护自己和我们所珍视的环境。我们将穿插社会热点事件，以毒理学的角度对毒物中毒和环境污染进行深入解读。通过了解这些案例，学生将能够更好地认识保护因素和有害因素，培养环保意识和可持续发展观念，培养出对社会热点问题的敏感度和解决问题的能力，以及提升自我保护意识。

This course is not just about knowledge of toxins but also about how to protect ourselves and the environment we cherish. We will incorporate current social hot topics, providing in-depth interpretations of poisonings and environmental pollution from a toxicological perspective. By studying these cases, students will gain a better understanding of protective and harmful factors, cultivate environmental awareness and a mindset for sustainable development, develop sensitivity and problem-solving skills for social hot topics, and enhance their self-protection awareness.

教学方式

本课程将主要采用讲授式教学，期间不定期穿插互动对话与讨论。课程最终将以翻转课堂和期末成果汇报作为总结，让学生能够主动参与并展示对课程内容的理解与掌握。

The course will primarily adopt a lecture-based format, with periodic interactive dialogues and discussions interspersed throughout. The learning experience will culminate in a flipped classroom session and a final presentation, allowing students to actively engage with and demonstrate their understanding of the course material.

1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 免疫学是面向临床医学专业国际学生开设的六年制医学培养方案中的一门核心课程。本课程以课堂讲授为主，并辅以实验教学和小组讨论。课程内容首先介绍先天免疫系统，以及先天免疫如何引导和激活适应性免疫；系统讲解适应性免疫中的关键效应细胞——T 淋巴细胞和 B 淋巴细胞的发生、活化过程及其在细胞免疫和体液免疫中的作用机制。随后，课程进一步拓展至多种免疫病理状态，包括过敏反应、自身免疫性疾病、超敏反应及免疫缺陷等。课程还涵盖转化免疫学相关内容，如移植免疫学和肿瘤免疫学。实验教学部分主要介绍免疫学中的经典实验方法及基础实验操作技能。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Immunology is one of the core courses in the six-year medical program for international students who major in Clinical Medicine. It is a lecture-based course and is supplemented with laboratory sessions and group discussion. The course begins with introducing the innate immune system and how innate immunity leads to the activation of adaptive immunity; how T and B lymphocytes, the key players of adaptive immunity, are generated, activated and finally mediate cellular or humoral immunity. Then the course transitions to different immunopathological conditions including allergy, autoimmunity, hypersensitivities and immunodeficiency. The topics of translational immunology including transplantation immunology and tumor immunology will also be covered. The laboratory sessions introduce classic experiments and basic lab skills in immunology.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

To provide an overview of basic and clinical immunology, to help students master the essential immunology concepts and principles, and develop basic experimental skills and techniques in immunology. Through the course, students will be able to explain the pathophysiology of the clinical problems, which is beneficial for passing USMLE Step I.

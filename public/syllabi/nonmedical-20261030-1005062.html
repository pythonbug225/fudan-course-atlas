课程简介（中文）

学术写作入门（I）是英语语言文学或相关专业学生的必修课。其他院系的学生应具备扎实的英语语言技能并对学术写作感兴趣。本课程提供大学水平的学术写作入门课程。课程旨在帮助学生掌握必要的知识和技能，以满足学术界对语言和论述的期望，并为进一步学习奠定坚实的基础。此外，我希望学生能够具备撰写高质量论文的能力，能够发表高质量的论文。

课程简介（英文）

Introduction to Academic Writing (I) is a compulsory course for students majoring in English language and literature or related areas. Students from other departments should demonstrate solid English language skills and interest in academic writing. This course offers an introduction to academic writing at the university level. The course aims to prepare students with the essential knowledge and skills necessary to meet the linguistic and discursive expectations of the academic community and form solid basis for further study. Furthermore, it is my hope that students taking this course will gain the ability to write publishable quality papers.

教学目标

The course aims to prepare students with the essential knowledge and skills necessary to meet the linguistic and discursive expectations of the academic community and form solid basis for further study. Furthermore, it is my hope that students taking this course will gain the ability to write publishable quality papers. Students should be able to read and analyze academic writing, understand what separates good writing from bad writing, and develop their own writing styles in a way that is appropriate for English language academics. Argumentation, Logic, Structure, and correct methods for building scientific hypotheses will all play a part in this.

教学方式

Lectures, Reading, Discussion, In-class writing, Peer review, Writing assignments

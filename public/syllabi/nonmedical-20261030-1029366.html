西班牙语语法

2026春 · FORE30049.01 · 鹿秀川

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要系统讲授西语句法，包括句子的主要成分、句子成分的一致性、动词时态变位的意义、句子分类、简单句的基本句型及复合句的基本句型、间接引语、替代、省略、倒装等。

课程简介（英文）

The aim of this course is to help students further systematize the Spanish grammar knowledge and skills they have already acquired, while also gaining a preliminary understanding of theoretical approaches to Spanish grammar. Through structured and in-depth study, students will strengthen their awareness of grammatical patterns and rules, develop greater accuracy and flexibility in language use, and enhance their ability to express themselves clearly and appropriately in Spanish. At the same time, the course seeks to cultivate analytical thinking about language, enabling students to recognize, interpret, and solve grammatical problems in real communicative contexts. Ultimately, it is designed to consolidate students’ linguistic foundation and prepare them solidly for advanced-level Spanish study in the future.

教学目标

本课程旨在使学生在已有西语语法知识和技能的基础上进一步加以梳理和系统化，帮助学生对西语语法体系形成整体认识，同时初步了解西语语法的基本理论与分析方法。通过课堂讲授与练习训练，提升学生对语法规则的理解能力和运用能力，增强语言表达的准确性与规范性，培养发现和解决语法问题的意识与能力，从而全面提高西语综合运用水平，为后续高级阶段的西班牙语学习奠定扎实基础。

教学方式

混合式课程

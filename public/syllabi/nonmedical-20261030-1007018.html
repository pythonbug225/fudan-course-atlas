2025秋 · MANA60013.01 · Marie Kieran Waxman

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

People are central to both the causes of unsustainable development, and the creation of a sustainable future. In order to tackle the challenges of sustainable development, we must pay attention to the social processes underlying both problems and solutions. Social research provides a means for examining these processes, and any sustainable development researcher will be well served by having an appreciation of techniques of social enquiry. This course is designed to provide an introduction to the practice of social research. The emphasis of the lectures and workshops will be principles of qualitative research design and research methods (no quantitative research techniques will be coverd in this course). The teaching approach will use small practical exercises in class to embed the learning for the students. The goal of this course will be to introduce a wide array of qualitative techniques so students can make informed decisions about methodological approaches. We will work through examples in class to ensure participants gain not only theoretical appreciation of methods, but also practical skills they can use to develop their own research projects.

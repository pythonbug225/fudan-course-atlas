课程简介（中文）

How to mobilize the financial sectors to achieve a better environment? Answers to this question involve the data, metrics, targets and scenario in measuring the environmental risks, the potentiality of financial mechanism in supporting the transition to a sustainable world, the re-framing of financial institutions including the regulations and various actors, as well as practical insights into green finance investment across equity, bonds, insurance, corporate asset, etc.

教学目标

On completing all the course activities, students should be able to demonstrate: An ability to define key concepts, main facets and some other basic information regarding the definition, institutions and main products of green finance. A knowledge of environmental science (emission control, climate change mitigation and adaption, biodiversity etc.) that relates to the financial system. An understanding of how environmental protection relates to investing, the different investment approaches that can be used to incorporate environment and climate considerations into the analysis and valuation of assets and investment instruments, and in portfolio management and construction. An analytical competence to identify the strengths and weakness of the basic theories of green finance. An application of the key concepts of sustainable finance and critical assessment of the incentives and actions of firms, investors, and other stakeholders regarding green finance, as well as their outcomes. An ability to design innovative finance tools and instruments fitting with a broader sustainable strategy.

课程简介（中文）

本课程面向硕士及博士研究生，旨在系统提升高阶英语公共演讲能力。课程融合理论与密集训练，前半段重点培养信息型与劝说型演讲的核心技巧，包括内容组织、受众分析和临场表达；后半段专注于学术演讲，指导学生运用AI工具进行修辞分析，并按照IMRAD结构整合研究、设计可视化数据及完成跨文化学术汇报。评估包含出勤参与、期中考核（3分钟论文演讲与即兴演讲）及期末研究汇报（7分钟学术演讲）。课程强调批判性思维、伦理化使用AI辅助及原创性讲稿撰写。

课程简介（英文）

Please translate this into English Course Overview This course is designed for Master's and PhD candidates, aiming to systematically enhance advanced English public speaking proficiency. It integrates theoretical frameworks with intensive practical training. The first half focuses on developing core skills for informative and persuasive speeches, including content organization, audience analysis, and impromptu delivery. The second half emphasizes academic presentations, guiding students in using AI tools for rhetorical analysis, synthesizing research following the IMRaD structure, designing data visualizations, and delivering cross-cultural academic reports. Assessment is based on attendance/participation, a midterm assessment (a 3-minute thesis presentation and an impromptu speech), and a final research presentation (a 7-minute academic talk). The course emphasizes critical thinking, the ethical use of AI assistance, and the creation of original speech content.

教学目标

Course Objectives: Demonstrating confidence and effectiveness in English public speaking across diverse contexts. Developing audience-centered delivery techniques to engage listeners while refining self-expression. Designing, adapting, and delivering tailored speeches (both prepared and impromptu) for specific rhetorical situations.

教学方式

Course Description: This course integrates theoretical frameworks with intensive practice to advance English public speaking proficiency. The first half focuses on analyzing and practicing informative and persuasive speeches, ensuring clarity, accuracy, and audience engagement. It develops critical thinking skills and techniques for both prepared and impromptu speaking. The second half emphasizes academic presentations: students will conduct interdisciplinary research, utilize AI tools for rhetorical analysis, synthesize sources, and structure compelling narratives. Emphasis is placed on the research process, cross-cultural communication, and persuasive storytelling with global perspectives. Prerequisites: Advanced English proficiency. Clear pronunciation and intonation; ability to comprehend English-language lectures without subtitles. Coherent English writing ability. Teaching Methodology: Theory Application:​ Genre-based move analysis, rhetorical devices, and linguistic features. Modeled Practice:​ Analysis of exemplary speeches. Speech Crafting:​ Drafting and revising scripts with feedback. Performance Training:​ Rehearsing prepared and impromptu speeches. Academic Presentation:​ Delivering research-based talks following the IMRaD structure.

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule: Week 1:​ Effective and Ethical Use of AI for Impactful Speaking Week 2:​ Orientation: Five Fundamental Public Speaking Skills Week 3:​ Text Analysis: "Why We Speak to Inform" + Quiz Week 4:​ Informative Speech Analysis + Rehearsal Week 5:​ Text Analysis: "How We Speak to Persuade" + Quiz Week 6:​ Monroe’s Motivated Sequence + Persuasive Speech Rehearsal Week 7:​ One-on-One Conference Weeks 8-10:​ Midterm Assessment: Three-Minute Thesis + Impromptu Speech Week 11:​ Research Structure: The IMRaD Format Week 12:​ Academic Presentations: Effective PPT Design Week 13:​ One-on-One Conference Weeks 14-16:​ Term Project Presentations Class Preparation & Participation: Pre-Class:​ Listening/Reading Assignments In-Class:​ Spoken/Written Activities Post-Class:​ Revisions and Reflections

课程简介（中文）

免疫工程是一门将免疫学的前沿进展与最新工程学手段相结合，研究免疫分子、细胞的结构与功能信息，加工改造并使其获得新功能的课程。主要内容包括抗原识别的结构信息、免疫分子的基因工程表达与纯化、疫苗的分子设计、基因工程抗体的制备与应用、基于DC或Car T细胞的免疫治疗等。通过本课程的学习，有利于熟悉免疫学的前沿知识、最新理论和研究进展及相应的工程技术交叉内容，为今后的临床和科研工作打下良好的基础。

课程简介（英文）

Immunology Engineering is a course that combines cutting-edge advancements in immunology with the latest engineering techniques to study the structural and functional information of immune molecules and cells, process and modify them, and endow them with new functions. Key topics include structural information on antigen recognition, genetic engineering expression and purification of immune molecules, molecular design of vaccines, preparation and application of genetically engineered antibodies, and immunotherapy based on dendritic cells (DCs) or CAR T cells. By taking this course, students will gain familiarity with cutting-edge immunological knowledge, the latest theories and research advancements, as well as the corresponding interdisciplinary engineering content, laying a solid foundation for future clinical and research work.

教学目标

在介绍免疫生物学基本理论知识的前提下，进一步就免疫学的前沿知识、最新理论和研究进展及相应的生物工程技术等与免疫学知识有机融合、传授给学生，使他们能对免疫学知识能有更深入的理解并将其实际应用到临床与科研工作中。

机器学习与量化投资

2025秋 · AIS631007.01 · 李隽业

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

在大数据时代，人工智能及机器学习提供了可以解决高维问题的有效工具。这些工具在量化投资领域有着巨大的应用潜力。然而，在金融市场中，由于金融数据的信号噪音率非常低、数据产生过程经常存在着结构性变化、及数据分析同时要考虑协方差，我们无法在投资分析中简单套用这些现成的方法。因此，机器学习方法需要更紧密地与先验信息及经济理论结合起来，才能更有效的在量化投资中展开应用。         本课程分为两大部分，第一部分介绍资产定价和证券组合基本理论；第二部分讨论机器学习方法在量化投资应用中面临的主要问题，然后有针对性地讨论如何结合先验信息与资产定价理论使得机器学习在量化投资应用中更加有效。 本课程将理论与实证结合起来，不仅介绍可用于量化投资和资产配置的主流机器学习和人工智能方法，而且还会讨论学术界最新开发的可改善投资效率的机器学习工具。该课程面向应用，将在课堂内外进行大量的编程及数据分析工作。

课程简介（英文）

In the era of big data, artificial intelligence and machine learning provide effective tools for solving high-dimensional problems. These tools hold enormous application potential in the field of quantitative investment. However, in financial markets, we cannot simply apply these methods off-the-shelf to investment due to the low signal-to-noise ratio of financial data, the frequent structural changes in the data-generating process, and the need to simultaneously consider covariances in data analysis. Therefore, machine learning methods need to be more closely integrated with prior knowledge and economic theory to be deployed more effectively in quantitative investment applications. This course comprises two main parts: the first part introduces the fundamental theories of asset pricing and portfolio management, and the second part discusses the major challenges faced when applying machine learning methods in quantitative investment, followed by detailed discussions on how to integrate prior knowledge with asset pricing theory to enhance the effectiveness of machine learning in quantitative investment applications. Combining theory with empirical practice, this course not only introduces mainstream machine learning and artificial intelligence methods applicable to quantitative investment and asset allocation, but also discusses the latest machine learning tools developed in academia to improve investment efficiency. Practical and application-oriented, the course will involve substantial programming and data analysis work both in and out of class.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，将达到如下几个目标： 帮助学生掌握资产定价和证券组合的基本理论； 帮助学生掌握主流金融机器学习方法及其在量化投资应用中面临的问题； 帮助学生利用金融机器学习方法和应用思想开发基本的量化投资策略； 把资产定价与投资理论和实践操作关联起来，帮助学生为进入金融机构工作或进一步学习深造打下坚实的基础。

人工智能与公共政策

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程面向公共政策与公共管理类学生，系统探讨人工智能与公共政策/公共治理领域的双向互动关系。课程从Policy of AI, AI in Policy 和 AI for Policy三个方面出发，深入探讨AI的发展过程、AI治理规制、智能公共治理、政策分析工具应用等前沿核心议题，其中融合全球各国的治理范式比较。课程中将涵盖系统的学术训练，包括比较政策分析框架、质性研究方法、政策文本分析等核心技能，为有志于攻读公共政策、科技政策等方向博士学位的学员奠定研究基础。此外，本课程培养的“AI+政策”复合能力可广泛服务于泛业界需求。相比单一背景人才，本课程拟培养学生在技术创新与政策环境之间建立连接，成为组织内部推动AI可持续发展的关键力量。

课程简介（英文）

Government, universities, international organizations, and consulting firms now need people who understand AI — not just as engineers, but as policy professionals who can analyze its risks, regulate its use, and deploy it strategically. This course gives you that capability. We organize the course around three questions that map directly to the roles you will occupy: Policy OF AI — How do states promote and regulate AI? (Relevant to: regulatory affairs, government, trade policy) AI IN Public Policy — How is AI transforming governance and politics? (Relevant to: digital government, international organizations, think tanks) AI FOR Public Policy — How can you use AI as a tool in your own policy work? (Relevant to: any analytical role) Note: Given the rapid pace of AI development, specific readings, case studies, and policy examples may be updated during the semester to reflect the latest developments. The core analytical framework and course structure will remain stable.

教学目标

本课程深度融入复旦新文科建设战略，依托全球公共政策研究院的全球视野与学术积淀，聚焦AI与公共政策交叉领域。在价值引领上，强化国家治理与全球治理责任意识，引导学生深入探讨技术治理的要点、难点、伦理与实践；在知识传授上，系统掌握AI治理范式及其内在逻辑，明晰通用技术与专业领域知识的互动关系；在能力培养上，通过前沿理论阐释与案例及操作教学，提升政策研判、跨学科协作与AI工具应用能力。本课程旨在培养兼具政策素养、国际视野与技术理解力的复合型人才，为国家数字治理现代化与全球AI治理贡献复旦智慧。 This course is deeply integrated into Fudan University’s Education and Teaching Reform, drawing on the global perspective and academic strengths of the Institute for Global Public Policy to address the intersection of AI and public policy. In terms of value formation, the course strengthens students' sense of responsibility toward national and global governance, guiding them to critically examine the key challenges, ethical dimensions, and practical implications of technology governance. In terms of knowledge exploration, students systematically develop an understanding of AI governance paradigms and their underlying logic, with attention to the relationship between general-purpose technologies and domain-specific expertise. In terms of capability building, through engagement with cutting-edge theory alongside case-based and applied instruction, students build skills in policy analysis, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the use of AI tools. The course aims to cultivate well-rounded professionals who combine policy literacy, international perspective, and technological fluency, which contributes to Fudan's intellectual resources to global AI governance.

教学方式

本课程以课堂讲授为主,辅以专题讨论。每周围绕核心议题布置阅读材料，课堂以教师讲授分析框架为基础，结合学生参与讨论，培养批判性思维与政策分析能力。学生须完成独立研究论文，经历选题、初稿、答辩、修改的完整训练周期。教师每周设置固定答疑时间，并要求每位学生学期内至少完成一次一对一面谈。 The course is primarily lecture-based and is supplemented by in-session seminar discussions. Weekly readings provide the foundation for each session, with lectures introducing core analytical frameworks and structured discussion encouraging critical engagement with the material. Students complete an independent research paper through a full cycle of topic proposal, drafting, defense and revision. The instructor holds weekly office hours and requires each student to meet individually at least once during the semester.

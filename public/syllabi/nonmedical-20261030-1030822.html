课程简介（中文）

本课程包含三个环节，分别为：（1）项目介绍会，（2）进展会议，以及（3）最终汇报。 1）在项目介绍会中，担任课程主讲教师的行业专家将介绍所选项目的核心概念、方法论及任务要求。每个项目最多分配给2组学生。 2）进展会议旨在让教师跟踪各小组进度并收集学生反馈。项目难度可根据实际进展进行调整，学生也可借此机会提出关键问题或展示阶段成果。 3）最终汇报用于展示各团队在八周内的成果，是项目评估的核心依据。汇报期间所有成员均需参与，行业导师将担任评委进行提问、反馈与评分。学生需在最终汇报后提交项目报告

课程简介（英文）

This course consists of three components, which are (1) project introduction sessions, (2) progress meetings, and (3) final presentation. At Introduction Sessions, industry experts, serving as the main instructors for the course, would introduce the key concepts, methodologies, and assignment requirements of the chosen projects. Each project will be assigned to no more than 2 groups of students. Progress meetings are arranged for the instructors to track each team’s progress and collect student feedback. Project difficulty may be readjusted based on the actual progress, and students may also seize the opportunity to ask important questions or present milestones. Demonstrating the teams’ efforts throughout the 8-week period, the final presentation serves as the core reference for the projects’ evaluation. During the presentation session, every team member should participate, and the industry mentors would be the judge to ask questions, provide feedback, and give grades. Students are required to submit a project report after the final presentation.

教学目标

Intellection through Action is central to our mission of delivering both rigor and relevance in our curriculum. Highlighting project-based learning and group work, this Intellection through Action course aims to incorporate a wide range of practical skills and academic knowledge to provide an informative learning experience that enhances students’ teamworking, planning, researching, strategic thinking, and presenting competencies.

教学方式

Lectures, discussion sessions, and group presentations.

教学内容与进度安排

Duration: 9 weeks with 54 credit hours The general time arrangement and content framework are listed below: Section 1: Data Visualization Lecture Section 2: Introductions (18 credit hours) - Introducing projects and assignments; Section 3: Progress Meeting 1 - Tracking the progress: conducting preliminary analysis, Section 4: Progress Meeting 2 - Tracking the progress: collecting and analyzing data; Section 5: Progress Meeting 3- Tracking the progress: drawing preliminary conclusions; Section 6: Final Presentation - Presenting the final project, reflecting and evaluating learning outcomes.

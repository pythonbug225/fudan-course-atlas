课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在探讨推动中国崛起的关键因素、如何把握其日益扩大的市场机遇、中国崛起对世界格局的影响，以及威胁其可持续发展的挑战。课程内容涵盖经济、商业、文化与政治等多个视角，力求为学习者提供全面深入的理解。课程首先回顾中国的历史遗产，并分析其对当代经济发展和社会结构的深远影响。接着，课程聚焦于经济体系中的关键参与者（包括政府、多样化的企业以及金融市场），并探讨它们之间的互动关系。此外，课程还将深入研究人力资本发展、劳动力市场与婚姻市场等领域，视其为未来经济增长的重要基础。第四部分将识别制约中国经济表现的主要挑战，特别强调收入不平等问题。最后，课程将分析中国与世界其他国家的互动关系，并对中国的未来发展前景进行展望。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to address the factors driving China's rise, how to capitalize on its growing market, the impact of its rise on the world, and the challenges that jeopardize its sustainable development. It offers insights from various perspectives such as economy, business, culture, and politics. The course begins by examining the historical legacy and its influence on current economic development and society. It then focuses on studying the key economic players (governments, diverse firms, and the financial market) and their interactions. Additionally, it explores human capital development, labor and marriage market, seen as vital for future growth. The fourth part identifies challenges that hinder economic performance, with emphasis on the income inequality. Finally, the course analyzes China's interactions with the rest of the world and her future development.

教学目标

After you finish the course, you are supposed to Understand the economic, political, social and cultural characteristics of China Tell the differences in economic development strategies adopted by China and other transitional/developing economies Be able to analyze the business culture of China Be aware of the obstacles and difficulties besetting the economic growth and their roots Be aware of the international environments faced by China during its development Be able to develop research topics related to this course for your final project

教学方式

Lecture including invited lectures， Discussion， Essay and Video Presentation

教学内容与进度安排

The course comprises six comprehensive topics that address crucial aspects of understanding the Chinese economy. Each module will span between two to seven sessions, with each session consisting of a two credit-hour lecture time (45 mins per credit hour). Throughout the course, you will be assigned two homework assignments, one midterm essay, and one final video project. The modules are outlined as follows: Module 1: The past + Orientation (4 sessions) Module 2: The Government and other economic players (7 sessions including one invited lecture) Module 3: Finance and real estate (3 sessions) Module 4: Population, education, labor and marriage (4 sessions) Module 5: Inequality and poverty reduction (3 session) Module 6: China, the world and the Future (2 sessions)

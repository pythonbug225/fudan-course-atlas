课程简介（中文）

本课程是一门零起点汉语普通话基础教程，学生将学习汉语基础知识，包括从基础会话技能到购物、使用公共交通、看病、租房等各种场景使用的词汇和句子。学生完成本课程后，能够在日常生活和工作中使用汉语与母语者进行交流。

课程简介（英文）

This is a beginning Mandarin Chinese course in which the students will learn the basics of Chinese language, including from  basic converstional  skills to being able to shop, use the public transport, see a doctor, rent an apartment, etc. Once the students finish the course , they can use Chinese to communicate with native speakers in daily life and at work.

教学目标

This course aims to understand basic pronunciation and grammar rules of Chinese Mandarin language; to learn and pronounce standard Chinese words; to read all of those words and construct correct sentences and convey them orally in broadcasting standard quality; to become conversationally conversant with Mandarin speaking Chinese people based on the information obtained from the textbook; to provide students with the understanding of Chinese culture with respect to classical Chinese literature and history. As a result, by the end of a semester of learning, students should have an elementary command of basic Chinese language structure, and all the students should be able to achieve HSK level 1.

教学方式

Lectures, in-class exercises, after-class assignments, discussions, presentation, etc.

社会科学数据挖掘

2026春 · STAT50021.01 · 周阳

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程聚焦运用数据科学工具解决社会科学问题，系统介绍社会科学理论与分析框架、数据科学的方法与模型及其融合路径，为跨学科研究奠定基础。课程涵盖因果推断、金融与经济学基础，以及夜光数据、文本数据等社会科学应用专题，旨在帮助学生理解数据科学在社会科学中的研究潜力。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on using data science tools to solve questions in social science. It would equip students with the theories and analytical tools in social science topics, methods, and models in data science, how they would merge, etc. It serves as a foundation for scholarly research of interdisciplinary studies and aims to let students see the potential of data science in other areas. This course would cover the introduction of contemporary research and empirical methods in causal inference, as the most popular thought and research method in social science, and how it would be improved by data science; the basic theory and models in finance and economics, as the start points of social science topics; several special topics in data application on social science studies, such as nightlight data, textual data, and other related terms.

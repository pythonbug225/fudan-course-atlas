课程简介（中文）

这门课程阅读自美国早期印第安文学到18世纪末美国文学中的作品，使学生熟悉这一时期美国文学的发展脉络，了解其内容，并以此掌握美国18世纪文化中的重要问题，知晓其社会之主要关切。

课程简介（英文）

 This course reads the major works of American literature from early Native American literature to the end of the 18th century. Students are expected to familiarize themselves, through reading these works, with the major trends of literary development of this period, the cultural traits of the 18th century and the social changes that North America went through in the long 18th century.  

教学目标

这门课程的目的是使学生通过阅读主要作品熟悉18世纪及其之前的美国文学的发展脉络，了解其内容，18世纪是美国文学的萌芽期，也是与印第安传统文化交融、冲突的主要时刻，在文化和文学表征上具有鲜明的杂糅性特点，课程将介绍两个不同文化思潮及其文学表征，并以此掌握美国18世纪文化中的重要问题，知晓其社会之主要关切。

教学方式

以教师课堂讲授配合学生的课堂汇报为主要形式。

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: course intro; early American history and culture;

Week 2: Native American oral tradition (I)

Week 3: Native American oral tradition (II)

Week 4: Early Native American literary writings(I)

Week 5: Early Native American literary writings(II)

Week 6:Colonial period: American Puritanism

Week 7: Literature of early settlement: John Smith

Week 8: William Bradford

Week 9: Anne Bradstreet

Week 10: Thomas Paine

Week 11: Philip Freneau

Week 12: Benjamin Franklin

Week 13: Thomas Jefferson

Week 14: Interaction between Native and White American literatures

Week 15: Discussion and paper

Week 16: final exam

教学参考资料

Norton Anthology of American Literature, 9th ed.

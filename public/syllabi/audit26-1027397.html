课程简介（中文）

本课程由三部分组成。第一部分涵盖了欧盟的基础知识，包括欧洲一体化的主要理论，其核心机构，决策程序和法律秩序。在第二部分，本课程探讨欧盟与第三方之间的主要双边关系。最后一部分聚焦于对欧盟内部稳定和外部姿态至关重要的特定政策领域。

课程简介（英文）

This course consists of three parts. The first part covers the basics of the EU, including major theories of European integration, its core institutions, decision-making procedures, and legal order. In the second part, this course explores the major bilateral relations between the EU and third parties. The last part focuses on selected policy areas critical to the EU’s internal stability and external posture.

教学目标

 

This course aims to help students acquire the basic knowledge of the development and functioning of the EU. Additionally, students are expected to analyze the EU’s role in various sectoral policies and its relationships with selected partners.

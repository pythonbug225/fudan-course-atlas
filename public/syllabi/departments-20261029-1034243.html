课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在研究国际商务与战略管理领域的热点议题，聚焦近几十年来成为重要学术方向的跨国管理、新兴市场跨国公司及数字跨国公司。由于中国市场数十年来一直是发达市场跨国公司的主要竞技场，同时中国企业在近几十年迅速跃入全球市场，课程采用比较视角与方法，以促进学生深入理解跨国公司在华经营的经验与趋势，以及中国企业全球化的动因与绩效。课程内容涵盖以下四个模块：1）跨国管理理论；2）发达市场跨国公司在新兴市场；3）新兴市场跨国公司的比较研究；4）数字全球化与天生全球化企业。

课程简介（英文）

The course is designed to study the hot issues of international business and strategic management fields focusing on transnational management，emerging markets-based multinationals, and digital multinationals, which have become attractive academic streams in recent decades. As Chinese market has been one of the main playing grounds of advanced market-based multinationals for decades and Chinese firms have jumped rapidly into the global market in recent decades, comparative perspective and method are utilized in the course in order to promote students’ deep understanding on the experiences and trends of foreign multinationals in China, and determinants and performance of Chinese firms’ globalization as well. The content covers four sections as follows: 1）Theories of transnational management; 2）Advanced market-based multinationals in emerging markets; 3) Comparisons of emerging market-based multinationals; 4) Digital globalization and Born-globals.

教学目标

The purpose of this course is to improve students’ knowledge and research capabilities in the field of international business through advanced textbook learning and top journal articles reading. The following key questions will be answered: 1) What are challenges and solutions for multinational enterprises to formulate, implement and transform their transnational strategies in the dynamic and diversified context of global market? 2) How special and attractive are emerging markets for advanced market-based multinational enterprises? 3) How special and competitive are emerging market-based multinational enterprises in recent decades, especially those ones from BRICS? 4) How to understand the institutional context, diving forces, expansion strategies, performance and trends of Chinese firms in the global market, especially in the context of Belt & Road Initiative? 5)How to understand the new trends of digital multinationals and born-globals?

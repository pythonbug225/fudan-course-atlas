课程简介（中文）

在第一部分，本课程重点讲解系综理论、量子统计的表述、简单气体理论、理想玻色系统以及理想费米系统。在第二部分，课程转向相互作用系统的统计力学、相变理论以及临界现象。将采用精确模型和重整化群方法来说明基本概念。

课程简介（英文）

In the first part, this course focuses on ensemble theory, formulation of quantum statistics, the theory of simple gases, ideal Bose systems, and ideal Fermi systems. In the second part, this course turns to the statistical mechanics of interacting systems, the theory of phase transition, and criticality. Both exact models and the renormalization group approach will be employed to illustrate the basic ideas.

教学目标

Master ensemble theory and formulation of quantum statistics, including applications to simple gases, ideal Bose systems, and ideal Fermi systems. Master the theory of phase transition and criticality, including applications to some concrete models. Understand the basic idea for dealing with interacting systems. Understand the basic idea of the renormalization group approach.

教学方式

Classroom teaching plus Q&A

1     The Statistical Basis of Thermodynamics 2     Elements of Ensemble Theory, the microcanonical ensemble 3     The Canonical Ensemble 4     The Grand Canonical Ensemble 5     Formulation of Quantum Statistics 6     The Theory of Simple Gases 7     Ideal Bose Systems 8     Mid-term Exam 9     Ideal Fermi Systems 10   Statistical Mechanics of Interacting Systems: Cluster Expansions 11    Phase Transitions 12   Criticality, Universality, and Scaling 13   Phase Transitions: Exact Results for Various Models 14   Phase Transitions: The Renormalization Group Approach 15   Fluctuations and Nonequilibrium Statistical Mechanics (optional) 16   Q&A 17   Final exam

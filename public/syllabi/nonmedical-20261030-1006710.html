课程简介（中文）

Kahn-Markovic使用测地线流来解决表面子群猜想。最近，由Calegari-Marques-Neves提出了用齐次动力学研究负弯曲流形表面的新进展。我们的目标是讨论三个不同领域之间的相互作用：表面子群、均匀动力学和负弯曲流形。

课程简介（英文）

Kahn-Markovic uses geodesic flows to resolve the surface subgroup conjecture. There is a recent development on using homogeneous dynamics to study the surfaces in negatively curved manifolds, initiated by Calegari-Marques-Neves. Our goal is to talk about the interactions between the three different areas: surface subgroups, homogeneous dynamics, and negatively curved manifolds.

教学目标

This is a reading course based on the following papers. [Kahn-Markovic, '09] Immersing almost geodesic surfaces in a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold. [Calegari-Marques-Neves, 20] COUNTING MINIMAL SURFACES IN NEGATIVELY 3-manifolds [Kahn-Markovic-SMILGA, 23] Geometrically and topologically random surfaces in a closed hyperbolic three manifold [Shah, '90] CLOSURES OF TOTALLY GEODESIC IMMERSIONS IN MANIFOLDS OF CONSTANT NEGATIVE CURVATURE There are no exams or homeworks. The grading is based on students’ presentations on a topic related to the course materials.

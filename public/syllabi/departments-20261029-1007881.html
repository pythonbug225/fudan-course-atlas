课程简介（中文）

课程研究企业理论，包括现代微观经济学中的公司组织及其理论与设计。课程特别关注公司的内部组织（奖励机制、内部分工、内部沟通、权力分配等）和边界（合同、所有权关系、联盟等）。课程将研究理解公司内部和外部组织所必需的关键概念，如信息不对称、效率、激励冲突、产权、特定资产、组织结构和组织决策。

课程简介（英文）

The course studies the theories of the firm, including the modern microeconomics of firm organization and the theory and design of organizations. The particular focus is on the internal organization (rewards, the internal division of labor, internal communication, the allocation of authority, etc.) and the boundaries (contracts, ownership positions, alliances, etc.) of firms. The course studies the key ideas that are essential for understanding the internal and external organization of firms, such as asymmetric information, efficiency, incentive conflicts, property rights, specific assets, organizational structures and organizational decision-making. Students will study classical papers and models in the firm theory. By doing so, students will acquire tools that can be used to assess, for example, whether an input should be produced internally (in-house) or purchased from the market (buy); how strong incentives should be inside firms; how organizational structures and systems interact and impact organizational performance; how knowledge can be effectively governed within and between firms; etc.

教学目标

学生将学习公司理论中的经典论文和模型。通过这些学习，学生将获得评估工具，例如，判断某一投入是否应在内部生产（自制）或从市场购买（外购）；公司内部应设定多强的激励；组织结构和系统如何相互作用并影响组织绩效；知识如何在公司内部及公司之间有效管理等。

教学内容与进度安排

This course in organizational economics prepares doctoral students for further study in the field. The course introduces the classic papers and some recent research. The material is organized into the following modules: Module 1: Boundaries of the firm Module 2: Internal hierarchy of the firm Module 3: Decision-making in firms Module 4: Structures and processes in firms Each class session covers a few classical and leading papers.

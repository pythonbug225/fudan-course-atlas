课程简介（中文）

中国在过去三十年中已成为重要的经济体和区域强国，在此期间经历了重大的法律变革与外资涌入的增长。本课程通过考察法律体系根植的社会文化背景，对中国法律体系进行全景式介绍。课程要求学生系统学习中国法律制度研究，通过将法律问题置于具体本土语境中进行解析，深入探究中国法律与社会的独特之处。课程重点将关注比较法学理论、法社会学研究以及研究方法论领域的内容。

课程简介（英文）

China has been considered as an economic and regional power which has been accompanied by both major legal change and increased foreign investment in the past three decades. This course provides an overview of the Chinese law by examining the social-cultural context in which this system is embedded. This course requires students to familiarize themselves with the study of Chinese legal system and to explore the uniqueness of Chinese law and society by putting the legal issues into the specific local context in which they are operated and developed. Attention is paid to theories of comparative law, social-legal studies as well as research methodology.

教学目标

Understand the historical, social, political and culturalcontext of the development of law in China; Develop an understanding of Chinese legal institutions and of current issues and problems in the Chinese legal system; Understand the connections and tensions between law and society; Have an understanding of the theoretical and practical challenges to the study of Chineselegal systems, from different methodological perspectives.

教学方式

Lecture and discussion.

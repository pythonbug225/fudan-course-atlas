课程简介（中文）

本课程对当代中国的外交政策作全面介绍，既包含理论探讨，又重视实证分析，既聚焦历史，又关注当前实务。本课程还探讨中国与各大国、区域、邻国的关系以及其对国际组织的参与。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to provide a comprehensive introduction to contemporary China’s diplomacy and its foreign policy, emphasizing both theoretical and empirical studies and both history and current affairs. This course will also investigate China’s bilateral relations with the major powers, regions and neighboring countries as well as its participation in international organizations. 

教学目标

The objective of the course is that, upon completion of the course, students should be able to grasp a big picture of the evolution of Chinese diplomacy and foreign policy, have a deep understanding of its ideational principles and major issues, and develop expertise to analyze China’s foreign policy.

教学方式

Lectures, presentations, and discussions.

课程简介（中文）

This course covers econometric and statistical methods as applied to finance. It introduces commonly used econometrics and statistical methods that answer important questions arising in financial theory and applications. The course covers both the time series and cross-sectional aspects of the returns of financial assets. From a time series aspect, this course covers all important time series models, such as ARIMA models, GARCH models, and stochastic volatility models. From cross-sectional aspects, the course covers portfolio theory, multifactor pricing models, intertemporal equilibrium models, and present value models. In addition, various statistical techniques such as testing the efficient market hypothesis, maximum likelihood principles, delta methods, validating financial models and pricing theory, uncertainty quantifications, estimating large volatilities, principal component analysis, and factor models are introduced.

教学目标

1）Learning financial econometrics theory and methods 2）Mastering implementation skills of these theory and methods 3）Learning financial economics, asset pricing, and portfolio theory and their practical implementations.

教学方式

1）On-site lectures 2）Questions and answers during the class 3）Homework practice problems and their solutions

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture Subject Supplement/Reference Homework 1 Asset returns and efficient markets 2 Linear time series and dynamics of returns 3 Discrete time volatility models of returns 4 Multivariate time series and volatility* 5 Efficient portfolios and CAPM 6 Multifactor pricing models 7 Portfolio allocation and risk assessment 8 Consumption-based CAPM 9 Present value models

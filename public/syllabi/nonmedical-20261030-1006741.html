中国公共政策

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程将公共政策定位为社会科学的重要分支领域，以中国特色治理体系为核心，引导学生探究政策制定的决定因素、核心理念与理论依据，剖析影响政策成效的制度性约束与价值取向制约，并深入掌握政策全周期各环节的干预策略。课程将结合现实政策议题展开案例分析。 顺利完成本课程后，学生将能够运用政策制定与分析领域的现有理论、方法及模型，系统解析中国及国际社会当下的政策议题并提出解决方案。本课程旨在为学生奠定扎实的学术基础，为其后续修读政策、行政与管理等专业方向课程做好知识储备。

课程简介（英文）

The recent Chinese reforms highlight many systemic transformations that significantly impact regulatory and redistributive public policies. This course will position public policy as an important subfield of social science. Focusing on governance with Chinese characteristics, students will learn the policy determinants, the underlying ideologies and rationales, discover many of the institutional and values-based constraints that affect policy outcomes, and become more familiar with strategies to influence all phases of the policy process. Throughout the semester we will draw on relevant policy topics to illustrate these points. Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to draw from existing theories, approaches, and models in policymaking and policy analysis to dissect contemporary policy issues in China and beyond and generate policy solutions. This course is intended to provide students with the intellectual foundation necessary to pursue more specialized courses in policy, administration, and management.

教学目标

This course will provide knowledge and analytical skills related to policy investigation and study, including: Distinguish and re-examine the major influences on policymaking and the dominant perspectives of Chinese public policy. Identify the key actors: individuals, groups, parties, and coalitions involved in the public policy-making process, and detect how some ideas/issues succeed to reach the policy agenda while others fail to do so. Generate new perspectives by applying various threads of theoretical knowledge to analyze real-life situations and Chinese public policies. Reflect critically on the notion of rationality as applied to policy-making, and analyze the complexities of the policy-making process. Develop capacity to evaluate and recommend a policy response to a specific policy problem using criteria of effectiveness, efficiency, and political feasibility. Evaluate policy effectiveness and assess the expected and actual impacts of public policy choices.

教学方式

This is a “hybrid” course with both lectures and seminar-style discussions.

教学内容与进度安排

1 Introduction: Chinese Public Policy and Governance System 2 Evolution of Public Policy in Contemporary China 3 Key Actors and Stakeholders 4 Policy Entrepreneurship in China 5 Agenda Setting and Decision Making 6 Central-local Relations and Policy Process 7 Policy Experiments in China 8 Policy Innovation and Diffusion 9 Policy Implementation and Capacity Building 10 Policy Implementation and Collaboration 11 Policy Feedback and Policy Change 12 Individual Presentation I 13 Individual Presentation II 14 Globalization and Chinese Public Policy 15 Policy Analysis I: Population Control Policy in China 16 Policy Analysis II: Poverty Alleviation Policy in China

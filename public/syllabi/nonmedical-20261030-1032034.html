课程简介（中文）

数字技术已成为公共治理中不可忽视的变量，被广泛引入公共治理的各个领域，包括政府运作，公共服务、城市治理、公众参与等各个方面。数字治理不仅仅是技术问题，更是治理问题，为何要利用数字技术赋能治理？背后的驱动力和推动者有哪些？数字技术实际上是如何在治理过程中被使用的？数字治理是否真正提升了治理绩效？给公共治理带来了哪些方面的实际影响？存在有哪些风险和“副作用”？数字治理受到哪些因素的影响？本课程围绕数字技术与治理的关系从不同维度展开研讨，对既有研究文献和实践案例展开深入评析和研讨，以提升学生在该领域开展有价值的、规范的学术研究的能力。

课程简介（英文）

Digital technology has become an indispensable variable in public governance, being widely applied across various domains including government operations, public services, urban governance, and citizen engagement. Digital governance is not merely a technological issue, but fundamentally a governance challenge. Why should digital technologies be leveraged to empower governance? What are the underlying drivers and key proponents behind this trend? How are digital technologies actually utilized in governance processes? Has digital governance genuinely enhanced governance performance? What tangible impacts has it brought to public governance? What potential risks and "side effects" exist? And what factors influence the outcomes of digital governance? This course explores the relationship between digital technology and governance from multiple dimensions, conducting in-depth analysis and discussions on existing research literature and practical case studies. The aim is to enhance students' ability to conduct valuable and rigorous academic research in this field.

教学目标

要求学生通过文献学习、理论研究和主题研讨，熟悉数字治理领域的基本理论、关键问题及主要变量间的相互关系，掌握相关的研究方法和分析工具，能独立完成研究设计，并对数字治理领域的相关实践与研究展开批判性思考和讨论。

教学方式

文献学习、理论研究、主题研讨

课程简介（中文）

本课程将阐释传统金融科技的核心知识及其潜在颠覆性影响，涵盖人工智能（AI）、区块链、云计算、元宇宙、大数据等技术，以及数字货币、Web 3.0、元宇宙等新兴数字金融/数字经济议题。这些技术之间存在强烈的协同效应，能够产生广泛的颠覆性影响。课程将涵盖这些技术的基础原理，并重点探讨由此引发的商业模式创新。课程还将深入探讨金融科技在金融与数字经济产业中的产品服务应用场景。课程采用互动式教学模式，围绕各类商业应用案例展开讨论。课程强烈鼓励学生以创新开放的思维运用金融科技，并通过期末项目检验概念理解能力及实际应用能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course will explain core knowledge about the traditional Fintech and its potential disruption, including artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain, cloud computing, metaverse, big data, and the emerging digital finance/digital economy topics including digital currency, Web 3.0, Metaverse. There are strong synergies among these technologies, enabling a broad spectrum of disruptive impact. The course will cover the fundamentals of these technologies and focus on resulting business model innovation. The class will also delve into the applications and use cases of Fintech for products and services in the finance and digital economy industries. The course is designed to be interactive with discussions involving various business use cases. The class highly encourages students to adopt an innovative and open-minded attitude to leverage technology in finance. The class will involve a final project that will test understanding of concepts and the student’s ability to apply these in the real-world.

教学目标

By successfully finishing this course, students should be able to understand the basics of Fintech, its applications in modern finance, and its potential impact on the finance industry and economy.

教学方式

This course requires students to carry out case studies and actively participate in class discussions and practical activities. The group project requires student groups to review and share their progress.

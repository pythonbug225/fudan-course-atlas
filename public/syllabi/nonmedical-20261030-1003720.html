课程简介（中文）

这门 2 学分的必修课向学生介绍西班牙语写作的基本原则。课程和相关活动旨在帮助学生提升西班牙语书面表达的语法、词汇水平，并增强对文体的认知。通过指导性的课堂练习、家庭作业和短篇阅读材料，学生将学习如何构建简单句、将想法组织成段落，以及运用基本的连接词使表达清晰且连贯。该课程为学生的进一步学习奠定基础，并帮助他们做好准备，以便在日常书面语境中进行有效的沟通。

课程简介（英文）

The aim of this course is to introduce students to the fundamentals of writing in Spanish at the upper-elementary to intermediate level. Through guided exercises and selected readings, students will build their vocabulary, improve grammatical accuracy, and become familiar with basic principles of Spanish writing conventions. The course will emphasize sentence construction, paragraph organization, and the use of appropriate connectors and expressions. By the end of the semester, students will be able to understand and apply the main functions of writing in Spanish for everyday contexts across Spanish-speaking countries.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to develop student’s writing skills for Academic purposes in Spanish. The classes are designed to increase grammar and vocabulary for professional contexts, introducing principles of style, uses of citation, and basic knowledge of scientific research. As result, students will be able to present a research plan and write an academic paper.

教学方式

The course combines the professor’s explanations, interactive classroom activities, and homework assignments. Each lesson is divided into two parts: the first introduces writing techniques, basic to intermediate grammar, and examples of short prose; the second is devoted to guided student exercises and the revision of their own compositions. The professor embraces a collaborative learning approach, where active participation, regular writing, and continuous practice are viewed as essential to student progress.

教学内容与进度安排

Course Introduction – Presentation of the subject; review of basic grammar and sentence structure; self-introduction in writing. Describing People and Places – Vocabulary for appearance, location, and characteristics; guided writing practice. Spelling and Accent Marks – Rules for stress, punctuation basics; practice exercises. Describing Daily Routines – Use of present tense; writing short paragraphs about everyday life. Past Events – Introduction to past tenses (pretérito perfecto, indefinido); writing about past experiences. Future Plans – Introduction to future tense; short writing on plans and intentions. Giving Opinions – Vocabulary and structures for expressing opinion; opinion paragraph writing. Connectors and Cohesion – Introduction to linking words (porque, pero, aunque, primero, después, etc.); guided practice. Midterm Exam. Narrating a Story – Combining past tenses; writing a short narrative from a prompt. Formal vs. Informal Register – Comparing styles in emails, letters, and messages; practice writing in both. Describing Processes – Sequencing and imperatives; writing basic instructions (recipes, directions). Reading for Writing – Using short adapted texts as models; identifying useful vocabulary and structures. Writing Summaries – Techniques for summarizing short texts; guided exercises. Expressing Cause and Consequence – Structures with porque, por eso, ya que, etc.; short writing tasks. Basic Persuasive Writing – Structuring a simple argument; writing a short opinion piece. Revision and Editing – Checking grammar, vocabulary, and organization; peer review exercises. Final Exam.

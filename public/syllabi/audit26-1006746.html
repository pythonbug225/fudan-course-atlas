课程简介（中文）

本课程将从神经生物学的基本概念出发，介绍神经系统各个组成部分的结构，神经系统基本功能单位，神经元突触结构与功能，神经系统信号传递和编码。重点讲授神经系统的发生发育，感觉，运动，情感，思维，行为，学习记忆等神经活动规律以及分子，细胞和整体水平的机制。除了系统的讲课，还将结合文献阅读和讨论，介绍神经科学常用的研究方法，训练学生运用科学的思维和科学研究方法分析问题、解决问题，并掌握查阅文献、综合分析问题的方法与技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course will begin with fundamental concepts in neurobiology, introducing the structure of various components of the nervous system, the basic functional unit of the nervous system (the neuron), synaptic structure and function, as well as signal transmission and encoding in the nervous system. Emphasis will be placed on the developmental processes of the nervous system, as well as the principles and mechanisms—spanning molecular, cellular, and systemic levels—underlying neural activities such as sensation, movement, emotion, cognition, behavior, learning, and memory.

In addition to systematic lectures, this course will incorporate paper reading and discussions to introduce commonly used research methods in neuroscience. It aims to train students in applying scientific thinking and methodologies to analyze and solve problems, while equipping them with the skills to search for literature, synthesize information, and critically evaluate scientific questions.

教学目标

《神经生物学》课程主要介绍神经生物学的基础理论，研究方法、技术手段和前沿进展。通过本课程的教学，希望能使学生对神经生物学有全面系统的认识，帮助学生构建出神经生物学的基本知识体系，了解并掌握神经生物学的基本研究方法和技术手段。

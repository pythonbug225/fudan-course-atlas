生物心理学

2025秋 · BIOL70037.01 · Nashat Abumaria、李薇

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

用心理学的视角和生物学的方法来解释行为心理学。教育学生从进化，遗传，环境，经验和其交互作用等多方面因素来研究行为，了解构成这些行为变化的神经生物学基础。介绍与人类行为有关的动物模型/测试，修正某些错误的概念和教导学生在科学文献中理解和正确使用多个重要概念，例如：认知（cognition）vs 意识（consciousness），威胁反馈（threat response）vs恐惧反馈（fear response），抑郁（depression） vs 抑郁样（depression-like）表型。本课程最重要的目的是帮助学生开拓新的解决科学问题的思维方法和提高处理和解决相应科学问题的能力。这门课程不仅能有利于丰富和发展学生关于生物心理学相关的理论知识，更可以促进他们的科学批判性思维方式和创新性思维方式。

课程简介（英文）

This course explains behavioral psychology from a psychological perspective with biological methods, educating students to study behaviors from multiple factors such as evolution, genetics, environment, experience, and their interactions, and understand the neurobiological basis of these behavioral changes. It introduces animal models/tests related to human behaviors, corrects certain misconceptions, and teaches students to understand and properly use several key concepts in scientific literature, such as: cognition vs consciousness, threat response vs fear response, depression vs depression-like phenotypes. The most important goal of this course is to help students develop new thinking methods for solving scientific problems and improve their ability to process and address corresponding scientific issues. This course can not only help enrich and develop students' theoretical knowledge related to biopsychology, but also promote their scientific critical and innovative thinking modes.

教学方式

心理学与生活导论（3 学时）：心理学的核心议题与分支、认知、意识、心智的关系；遗传与行为；实验动物的应用。 中枢神经系统导论：从突触到解剖结构（3 学时）。 感觉系统（6 学时）： a. 视觉系统：神经基础，以及视觉系统如何在心理学中作为意识体验的典型案例。 b. 听觉系统与化学感觉：神经基础，不同感官的物理与心理维度，以及个体差异如何导致认知世界的方式差异。 学习与记忆及认知功能（15 学时）： a. 记忆的类型；从突触层面（赫布理论）到系统整合的记忆表征与存储机制；海马体与记忆 / 遗忘症；记忆障碍研究带来的启示。 b. 学习与记忆的动物模型 / 实验。 c. 认知功能：有意识认知体验与无意识认知体验；意识与注意力。 d. 语言作为高级认知功能：语言的进化历程；语言的脑机制；语言如何影响智力表现（例如：汉语在数学与计算机编程中的优势）。 e. 动物认知行为与人类认知行为：避免过度解读或夸大结论。 情绪行为（12 学时）： a. 人类为何需要情绪？ b. 情绪相关的脑区（人类与动物研究）。 c. 自主神经系统与唤醒的生物学过程（人类与动物研究）。 d. 情绪的进化理论：从达尔文理论到当代学者（如约瑟夫・勒杜）的研究。 e. 动物情绪模型与人类情绪：避免过度解读或夸大结论。 心境障碍、焦虑与精神分裂症（意外发现之旅）（6 学时）： a. 病因学：化学失衡、神经病理改变、遗传与生活事件、理查德・本托尔的心理学理论（《疯狂的解释》）、进化相关理论。 b. 治疗方法（药物治疗与认知疗法）。 c. 焦虑、抑郁与精神分裂症的动物模型。 开放性辩论环节：求异思维…… 或未必？（9 学时）： a. 精神疾病的迷人侧面：罹患精神疾病的名人及其艺术作品。 b. 这些现象是否属于人类行为范畴的延伸？是否是防御机制？是否属于需要药物干预的真实疾病？ 最后环节（第 7 部分）设置为辩论环节，学生可选择支持上述任意观点。各组需检索文献、提供真实案例及 / 或设计（人类或动物）实验，以支持己方观点或反驳对方观点。

教学内容与进度安排

1. Introduction to psychology and life (3 academic hours): major issues and divisions of psychology, cognition, consciousness, mind relationships. Genetics and behavior. Use of experimental animals. 2. Introduction to central nervous system from synapse to anatomy (3 academic hours). 3. Sensory systems (6 academic hours): a. Visual system: Neural basis, how visual system represents an example of conscious experience in psychology. b. Auditory system and chemical senses: Neural basis, physical and psychological dimensions of how different senses and individual differences lead to different ways of knowing the world. 4. Learning and memory and cognitive functions (15 academic hours): a. Types of memories. Representation of memory and its storage starting from synapse (Hebb theory) up to system consolidation. Hippocampus and memory/amnesia. What memory disorders have taught us? b. Animal models/testing for learning and memory. c. Cognitive functions: conscious cognitive experiences vs non-conscious cognitive experiences. Consciousness and attention. d. Language as a high cognitive function: How language evolved? Brain mechanisms of language. How can our language determine our intellectual performance (e.g. Chinese Language as a superior language for math and computer programming). e. Animal cognitive behavior vs human cognitive behavior: avoiding overstatements and/or over-interpretations. 5. Emotional behaviors (12 academic hours) a. Why do we need emotions? b. Brain areas involved in emotions (human and animal studies) c. Biological processes of autonomic nervous system and arousal (human and animal studies). d. Evolution theories on emotions: from Darwanian theories up to contemporary scientists like Joseph LeDuox. e. Animal models vs human emotions: avoiding overstatements and/or over-interpretations. 6. Mood disorders, anxiety and schizophrenia (a Journey of serendipity) (6 academic hours) a. Etiology: chemical imbalance, neuropathological changes, Genetic and life events, Richard Bentall psychological theory (madness explained). Evolution related theories. b. Treatments (pharmacological and cognitive therapy) c. Animal models of anxiety, depression and schizophrenia 7. Open debate section: Think different… or may be not? (9 academic hours) a. The fascinating side of psychiatric disorders: Famous names with psychiatric disorders. Famous artistic works. b. Are they an extension within the range of human behavior? Are they defensive mechanisms? Are they actual diseases that require pharmacological interference? The final part (section 7) will be set as a debate section during which students can choose to support any of the above mentioned views. Thereafter, each group should search literature, provide real life examples and/or design experiments (in human or in animal) to support their view or argue against the other views.

教学参考资料

Some Aspects related to emotions, cognition and consciousness, psychiatric disorders will be taken from: Anxious, By Joseph LeDuox, 2015, first edition. Madness explained: psychosis and human nature, by Richard Bentall 2003. For the exam: The contents of the main book (Biological psychology) and my PPT should cover all required knowledge. The students are not obliged to read the other books. The contents of the debate section will not be included in the exam as this represents individuals’ personal views.

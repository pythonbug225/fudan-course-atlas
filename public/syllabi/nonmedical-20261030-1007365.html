课程简介（中文）

本课为国际政治相关专业研究生开设的具有国际法与国际关系跨学科研究专业课。课程内容包括：国际法历史；国际法基本原理；中国与国际法的关系。本课特点包括：（1）通过案例介绍国际法一般原理和知识；（2）立足中国与世界关系讲国际法，突出国际法背后的国际政治；（3）突出中国参与国际法以及处理国家间关系的国际法经验和特点；（4）重视中国处理与世界的关系的国际法体系。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for graduate students in international politics and related fields as an interdisciplinary study program focusing on international law and international relations. The course content includes: the history of international law; the fundamental principles of international law; and the relationship between China and international law. The features of this course include: (1) introducing the general principles and knowledge of international law through case studies; (2) discussing international law from the perspective of China’s relationship with the world, highlighting the international politics behind international law; (3) emphasizing China’s participation in international law and its unique experiences and characteristics in handling interstate relations through international law; (4) focusing on the international legal system through which China manages its relationship with the world.

教学目标

通过一个学期的学习和课堂研讨，希望学生能：（1）掌握国际法基本精神与原理；（2）思考中国在国际法各个领域的参与和实践特点；（3）在中国崛起的背景下思考国际法和国际体系建设问题；（4）将学习国际法与学习国际关系结合起来；（5）掌握国际法与国际关系跨学科研究方法。

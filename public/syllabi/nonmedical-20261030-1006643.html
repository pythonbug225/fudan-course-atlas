Comparing China's Financial System

2025秋 · MBA70035.01 · 钱军

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

Through the comparison with other developed and emerging economies, this course aims to analyze the structure of China’s financial system and its major components, including the banking sector and capital markets. It also covers how financial innovations reshape the traditional financial system, and how to prevent various types of financial crises.

课程简介（英文）

Through the comparison with other developed and emerging economies, this course aims to analyze the structure of China’s financial system and its major components, including the banking sector and capital markets. It also covers how financial innovations reshape the traditional financial system, and how to prevent various types of financial crises.

教学目标

Understand the structure of the financial system and its relationship with economic growth in China and other large developed and emerging economies; Through the comparisons with other economies, we want to better understand the current status, risks, and further development of the major segments of the Chinese financial system.

教学方式

线下面授

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Topic: Overview of financial systems (of China and other large economies); the Chinese stock market; introduction of the banking sector Contents: • Financial markets, part 1: structural problems of the A share market； • Further reforming the stock market: registration system, corporate governance and investor structure • Banking sector: history, structure and current status Case: Introduction of Case #1 (and valuation methods of financial institutions) Readings: Week 2 Topic: The banking sector and “shadow banking” sector; introduction of the bond market Contents: • Reforming the banking sector: IPO and ownership structure; • Shadow banking, part 1: WMPs issued by banks—its growth and risks; • Shadow banking, part 2: implicit guarantees—how we can “break” it (the trust products sector); • Financial markets, part 2: overview of the bond market and major segments Case: Case #1: Q&As and class discussion Reading: Week 3 Topic: Real estate market and restructuring debt; latest development in Fintech Contents: • Financial markets, part 3: Overview of real estate markets • Bubbles in real estate markets and financial crises; debt restructuring and crisis prevention; • Fintech applications 1：Collateral-free loans based on big data • Fintech applications 2: An evolving, two-tier structure of (global) digital currencies: CBDCs (e.g., digital RMB) and “de-fi” platforms and systems Case: Presentations of Term Project (preliminary draft) Reading:

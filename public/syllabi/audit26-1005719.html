课程简介（中文）

本课程是教授现代前沿寄生虫研究知识的一门医学专业课程。本课程旨在阐明人体寄生虫病的病原体本质，促进寄生虫病的诊断、防治等的研究，是医学硕士生的学位专业课。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a medical professional course which teaches frontier parasite research knowledge. This course aims to clarify the pathogenicity of human parasitic diseases and promote the diagnosis and prevention of parasitic diseases. It is a master's degree course for medical students.

教学目标

本课程是教授现代前沿寄生虫研究知识的一门医学专业课程。本课程旨在阐明人体寄生虫病的病原体本质，促进寄生虫病的诊断、防治等的研究，是医学硕士生的学位专业课。

The course mainly teaches the pathogenicity, immunity, molecular diagnosis and control of important parasites, focusing on the latest research hotspots of parasites, modern research techniques of parasites, etc. Through the above research hotspots, study will be carried out to combine the theoretical knowledge and practical application, so as to cultivate the innovative thinking ability of master students.

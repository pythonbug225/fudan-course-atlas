课程简介（中文）

本课程面向研究生，深入探讨控制土壤和地下水系统中微量金属、养分及新兴污染物生物地球化学循环的水-土界面环境化学过程。在已有土壤和水化学基础知识的基础上，课程将讲解复杂反应机制、矿物-有机质相互作用、氧化还原转化路径及分子尺度的表征技术。重点关注复杂环境介质中物理化学过程如何调控污染物和元素循环等行为，主要针对物质的生物地球化学过程进行阐述。

在巩固化学平衡、酸碱化学、氧化还原反应及吸附等核心化学反应的基础上，课程将引入前沿研究领域，如金属污染物的地球化学过程、微塑料与土壤相互作用、非均质介质中的反应-运移过程以及气候变化压力下的土壤界面行为。

课程内容将通过前沿文献的分析与案例研究加以补充，学生需独立完成案例分析与引领讨论。本课程旨在为学生从事环境地球化学和土壤科学等相关科研与专业工作提供理论基础与实践能力。

课程简介（英文）

This graduate-level course provides an in-depth examination of interfacial environmental chemistry processes that govern the biogeochemical cycling of trace metals, nutrients, and emerging contaminants in soil and groundwater systems. Building on foundational knowledge in soil and aqueous chemistry, the course focuses on complex reaction mechanisms, mineral–organic matter interactions, redox transformation pathways, and molecular-scale characterization techniques. Emphasis is placed on how physicochemical processes in complex environmental media regulate contaminant behavior and elemental cycling, with a central focus on biogeochemical processes.

In addition to reinforcing fundamental chemical reactions such as equilibrium, acid-base reactions, redox transformations, and sorption, the course incorporates cutting-edge topics including the geochemistry of metal pollutants, microplastic-soil interactions, reactive transport in heterogeneous subsurface environments, and the impact of climate stress on soil interfacial processes.

Course content is enriched through critical analysis of recent literature and case studies. Students are expected to independently conduct case-based analyses and lead discussions. This course aims to equip students with both theoretical grounding and practical skills for advanced research and professional work in environmental geochemistry and soil science.

教学目标

  1. Develop a mechanistic understanding of biogeochemical processes at soil–water interfaces, including trace metal cycling, redox transformations, and mineral–organic matter interactions.

  2. Apply principles of chemical equilibrium, sorption, and redox kinetics to discuss contaminant fate and nutrient dynamics in soil and subsurface systems.

  3. Critically evaluate current research on soil environmental chemistry, with emphasis on emerging pollutants, microplastic interactions, and interfacial reactivity under environmental stressors.

  4. Interpret and integrate advanced characterization and modeling approaches to assess complex chemical behaviors in heterogeneous environmental media.

  5. Conduct case-based analyses and lead discussions that synthesize theory, literature, and real-world environmental challenges.

教学方式

Classroom lectures, case discussions, and student-led literature seminars.

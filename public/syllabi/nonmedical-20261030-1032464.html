智能哲学概论

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

智能（intelligence）是什么？它等于感知、思考、认知等能力的总和吗？人工智能与自然智能（人和动物的智能）有什么不同，又有什么相同之处？自然智能是怎么产生的？人工智能的基本原理存在于逻辑思维和计算机科学中吗？智能哲学是一门探索智能根本结构、深层含义及其深远影响的进阶人工智能哲学课程。为弄清智能的根源与本质，本课程将触及演化博弈论和神经科学领域相关内容的学习。为了认识自然和人工智能的基本性质和原理之间的异同，我们还将学习不同科学解释范式的区别，分清贝叶斯机（动物大脑）和玻尔兹曼机（AI 大模型）在基本原理和范式上的不同。 本课程的准确名称应该是：智能科学的自然哲学基础（The Natural Philosophy of the Science of Intelligence）。 本课程希望通过阅读、讨论、课堂报告和课程论文等形式，促进学生深入思考人工智能的本质和未来发展方向。我们将采用多学科综合，多哲学视角，结合认知科学、神经科学和计算机科学等领域的经验研究成果，通过哲学的分析与综合，引导学生反思与智能相关的根本概念、理论及实践。这门课程旨在拓展学生的思维视野，挑战传统哲学关于智能的观念，培养批判性思考能力。

课程简介（英文）

Intelligence is the sum of abilities such as perception, thinking, and cognition. What are the differences and similarities between artificial intelligence and natural intelligence (human and animal intelligence)? How does natural intelligence arise? Do the fundamental principles of artificial intelligence lie in logical reasoning and computer science? The Philosophy of Intelligence is an advanced course in the philosophy of artificial intelligence that explores the fundamental structure, deeper meanings, and far-reaching implications of intelligence. To understand the origins and essence of intelligence, this course will delve into related content in evolutionary game theory and neuroscience. To recognize the similarities and differences between the fundamental nature and principles of natural and artificial intelligence, we will also study the distinctions among different scientific explanatory paradigms, clarifying the differences in basic principles and paradigms between Bayesian machines (animal brains) and Boltzmann machines (AI large models). The precise title of this course should be: The Natural Philosophy of the Science of Intelligence. This course aims to promote students' in-depth reflection on the nature and future direction of artificial intelligence through activities such as reading, discussions, class presentations, and term papers. Adopting a multidisciplinary and multi-philosophical perspective, the course will integrate empirical research from fields such as cognitive science, neuroscience, and computer science. Through philosophical analysis and synthesis, it will guide students to reflect on fundamental concepts, theories, and practices related to intelligence. This course seeks to broaden students' intellectual horizons, challenge traditional philosophical notions of intelligence, and cultivate critical thinking skills.

教学目标

在本课程结束时，学生将： 能够阐明自然与人工智能背后的根源、基本原理、假设和设计思路，可以比较分析智能发展的趋势，全面把握自然和人工智能的现状、局限和未来趋势，为质询关于智能的理论做好准备。能够独立思考人工智能发展的意义，对人类社会福祉的贡献与危害，判断层出不穷的 AI 模型之间的优劣，对人机共存的未来社会有独立思考判断的能力，最终更有信心地成为知识社区的一分子，并乐在其中。

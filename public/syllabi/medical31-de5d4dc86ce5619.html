1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 生物统计学主要涉及对生物学数据的收集、分析、解释和展示，其具体应用领域包括流行病学、临床试验以及公共卫生。本课程内容涵盖描述性统计与基本概率论，并介绍推断性生物统计学中的基础主题，如回归分析、置信区间与假设检验。课程的核心重点在于统计方法的实际应用与结果解读。 本课程是针对留学生的课程，所以其他材料全部用英文撰写。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Biostatistics concerns the collection, analysis, interpretation and presentation of biological data. Specific applications include epidemiology, clinical trials and public health. This module covers descriptive statistics and elementary probability, and introduces basic topics in inferential biostatistics, including regression, confidence intervals and hypothesis tests. The primary focus of the course is on the practical use and interpretation of statistical methods.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

After this course, students will be able to: 1. Understand study designs; 2. Present data using appropriate tabular and graphical formats; 3. Define and calculate standard measures of location and dispersion of data; 4. Define probability and recognize common probability distributions including the Binomial and Normal distributions; 5. Estimate and interpret confidence intervals for means and proportions based on random samples from a population; 6. Calculate and interpret p-values for simple hypothesis tests; 7. Interpret parameter estimates and confidence intervals from simple linear regression model; 8. Use R software to do simple statistical analysis

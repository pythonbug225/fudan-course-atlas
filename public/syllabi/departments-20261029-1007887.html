高等数理统计（一）

2025秋 · STAT50037.01 · 徐勤丰

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是概率统计专业与统计学专业研究生的理论基础课，也是统计学专业本科学术兴趣班学生的选修课。 本课程在测度论基础上介绍数理统计的经典理论，包括基本概念、典型的问题与方法、构建与评价统计方法的原理，目的是为学生进一步学习数理统计专业课程、从事科研奠定基础。课程以介绍基于有限样本及参数模型的统计理论与方法为主，内容覆盖：统计模型，统计量的抽样分布、充分性及完备性、统计决策概论、点估计、假设检验、区间估计等。 本课程要求修读的学生具有良好的数学分析、高等代数基础，并已学习过本科阶段的概率论、数理统计等课程。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a theoretical foundation course for graduate students majoring in probability and statistics, and is also an elective course for undergraduate students majoring in statistics. This course introduces the classical theory of mathematical statistics based on measure theory, including basic concepts, typical problems and methods, and the principles for constructing and evaluating statistical methods, aiming to lay a foundation for students to further study mathematical statistics courses and engage in research. It mainly introduces statistical theories and methods based on finite samples and parameter models. The course content covers: statistical models, sampling distribution of statistics, sufficiency and completeness, an introduction to statistical decision theory, point estimation, hypothesis testing, interval estimation, etc. This course requires students to have a good foundation in mathematical analysis and advanced algebra, and to have studied undergraduate courses in probability theory and mathematical statistics.

教学目标

让学生熟悉数理统计的经典理论，包括基本概念、典型的问题与方法、构建与评价统计方法的原理。 让学生熟悉重要的统计思想、常用的统计方法及其原理。 为学生进一步学习其他统计学课程、理解统计学的进展、从事科研奠定基础。

教学方式

课堂讲授，布置课外作业。

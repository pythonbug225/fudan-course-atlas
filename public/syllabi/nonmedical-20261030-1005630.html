课程简介（中文）

本课程拟结合中国金融实践，介绍《货币银行学》、《中央银行学》、《金融市场学》、《新金融》和《金融产品与风险管理》等基础知识。主要包括：中央银行制度与货币政策实践，银行体系和非银体系（包括证券公司、信托投资公司、）的组织、结构、业务和功能，重要金融市场（股票市场、债券市场）、金融产品及其风险管理、金融组织及其监管的相关实践，以及银行体系和资本市场的主要创新状况。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to introduce fundamental knowledge of Money and Banking, Central Banking, Financial Markets, New Finance, and Financial Products and Risk Management in conjunction with China's financial practices. Key contents include: The institutional framework of central banks and practical applications of monetary policies; The organizational structures, operational models, and functional roles of the banking system and non-bank financial institutions (including securities companies and trust investment firms); Practices related to major financial markets (e.g., stock markets, bond markets), financial products and their risk management mechanisms, as well as the supervision of financial institutions; The latest innovation trends in both the banking system and capital markets.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，学生将了解金融机构与组织、金融市场、金融产品（特别是债券和理财产品）、金融监管等相关的基础知识和中国实践，有助于增强对金融实践问题的认知能力和敏感性，提高对金融资料的检索、分析和处理能力。

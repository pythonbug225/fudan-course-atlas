课程简介（中文）

本课程主要介绍中观经济学理论体系、框架与应用。中观经济学是以区域政府作为研究主体，讨论其在经济中的行为逻辑与重要作用，从而解释市场现实对经典微观、宏观经济学理论提出的挑战。本课程将介绍区域政府的属性特点、其在区域发展中资源生成与资源配置的作用、区域政府竞争理论、有效市场与有为政府的组合模式等，并结合国内外实践，尤其是中国特色的经济市场制度讨论中观经济学的应用。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an overview of the theoretical foundations, framework, and applications of Meso-economics. Focusing on regional governments as the primary subject of study, Meso-economics examines their behavioral logic and pivotal role in the economy, thereby addressing the challenges that real-world market conditions pose to classical microeconomic and macroeconomic theories. The course will cover the attributes of regional governments, their roles in resource generation and allocation within regional development, the theory of regional government competition, and the combined model of an "efficient market" and a "proactive government." Furthermore, it explores the practical applications of Meso-economics by drawing on domestic and international practices, with a particular focus on China's unique market economic system.

教学目标

1. 理解中观经济学的主要理论框架与内涵。 2. 掌握区域政府的属性特点与行为逻辑，理解区域政府竞争与超前引领理论。 3. 理解区域资源配置的逻辑。 4. 理解有效市场与有为政府的内涵与标准。 5. 掌握使用中观经济学理论框架理解中国经济现象。

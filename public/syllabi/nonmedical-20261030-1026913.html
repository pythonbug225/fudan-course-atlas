人类进化遗传学

2026春 · BIOL50017.01 · 徐书华、陈璐、王轲、陆艳、樊少华、邓恋

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

进化遗传学建立在这样一个基本原则之上：生命的遗传记录蕴藏于现存物种的基因组之中，它揭示了从所有物种的最后共同祖先一直延续至今的进化过程与亲缘关系。要了解这一祖先生物，我们必须比较生命之树上亲缘关系最为遥远的分支。而对亲缘关系更为接近的个体（例如同一物种内个体）进行比较，则能够提供关于更近期进化过程的证据。近年来，我们解读这一遗传记录的能力取得了巨大进展，尽管我们早已认识到有关过去的信息确实存在于可遗传物质之中。遗传学证据主要来源于两个方面： • 现存个体的基因组，这些基因组必然是从祖先一代代传递下来的； • 来自保存良好的有机遗骸中的古DNA，这些DNA可能在当今的存活后代中存在，也可能已经不再被代表。 人类进化遗传学研究的是不同人类基因组拷贝之间的差异，以及它们与我们最接近的亲缘物种——其他灵长类动物基因组之间的差异。基因组之间的差异也是人类学、医学遗传学和法医学遗传学的基础。这些领域都因两项重要进展而经历着飞速发展。首先，人类和非人类基因组序列的公开获取，并且这些序列已被注释出重要的功能元件（如基因）以及遗传变异位点。其次，能够在全基因组范围内分析大多数遗传变异的技术不断发展：最初是基于杂交的微阵列（或芯片）技术，随后发展为快速且低成本的全基因组测序方法（即所谓的下一代测序）。 这一信息的爆炸式增长推动了前所未有的创新，使我们拥有了分析规模空前、质量卓越的大型数据集的工具。丰富的数据本身也在催生新的解释方法的发展。此外，随着其他现存灵长类基因组序列的发表，以及来自已灭绝的人属成员基因组数据的获得，我们如今能够系统而全面地编目人类与其最近亲缘物种之间的遗传差异。正如本课程将要展示的，这些令人瞩目的进展正使我们能够回答关于人类起源的一些最为根本的问题。对人类进化遗传学而言，这无疑是一个令人振奋的时代。 要从任何一个特定的历史时期或事件中提取信息，都需要进行谨慎的解释，以将其与之前和之后的过程区分开来。这些信息不仅可以揭示人群的起源和人口历史，还能通过自然选择对遗传变异的影响，反映这些人群所面临的环境挑战。 常言道，“过去是现在的源头”，这一点在人类进化遗传学领域同样适用。这一充满活力的学科之所以达到今天的地位，得益于过去170年来遗传学、古生物学、考古学、人类学以及语言学等领域的发展与争论。在本课程中，我们并未对这段历史进行系统梳理，而是采取一种二十一世纪的视角加以阐述。

课程简介（英文）

Evolutionary genetics is founded on the principle that the genetic record of life is contained in the genomes of living species and it reveals evolutionary processes and relationships all the way back to the last universal common ancestor of all species. To find out about this ancestral organism we have to compare and contrast the most distantly related branches on the tree of life. Comparisons among much more closely related individuals, such as those from the same species, provide evidence on much more recent evolutionary processes. Our ability to read this genetic record has developed enormously in the last few years, although our confidence that information on our past exists within our heritable material is somewhat older. Genetic evidence comes from two main sources: The genomes of living individuals that must have been passed down from ancestors Ancient DNA from well-preserved organic remains, which may or may not be represented now in living descendants Human evolutionary genetics involves the study of how different copies of the human genome differ from one another, and from those of our closest relatives, other primates. Differences between genomes also form the basis of anthropological, medical, and forensic genetics. All of these fields are experiencing massive advances as a result of two developments. First, the public availability of human and nonhuman genome sequences, annotated with important functional elements such as genes, and with sites of genetic variation. Second, technology allowing analysis of most of this genetic variation across the genome, initially with the development of hybridization microarrays (or chips), followed by the development of methods to sequence whole genomes rapidly and cheaply (so-called next-generation sequencing). This explosion of information is driving an unprecedented period of innovation that gives us the tools to analyze huge datasets of unparalleled quality and quantity. This wealth of data is itself catalyzing the development of new interpretative methods. In addition, with the publication of genome sequences from other extant primates, as well as data from the genomes of extinct members of the genus Homo, we can now comprehensively catalog the genetic differences between humans and our closest relatives. As we shall see in this course, these impressive developments are allowing us to answer some of the most fundamental questions regarding human origins. These are indeed exciting times for human evolutionary genetics. Extracting information on any one past period or event requires careful interpretation to isolate it from previous and subsequent processes. This information can tell us not only about the demographic history and origins of populations but also something about the environmental challenges faced by those populations, through the influence of natural selection on genetic variation. It is said that “the past is the source of the present” and this is true in the academic field of human evolutionary genetics as much as elsewhere. This exciting subject owes its current status to developments and debates over the last 170 years in genetics, paleontology, archaeology, anthropology, and linguistics. In this course we have avoided cataloging this history, instead taking a twenty-first-century perspective.

教学目标

"Human Evolutionary Genetics" is one of the most ideal courses that can naturally integrate the theoretical methods of natural science and humanistic ideas into classroom teaching. Through the systematic description of the genetic laws in the origin and evolution of human beings, discuss the theories, principles and methods, keep up with the frontiers and trends of cutting-edge technology development, and combine the major life and health issues facing human beings, combine the grand narrative with Micro-mechanism analysis is organically combined to cultivate students' critical thinking ability, cognitive structure, and humanistic feelings.

教学方式

Class teaching is accompanied with intensive Q&A interactions, and classical & latest literature reading, as well as presentation and practice involved all of the students.

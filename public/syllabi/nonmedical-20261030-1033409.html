课程简介（中文）

课程简介：专业英语为研究生学术英语听说读写的水平提升提供基础，其核心目标是帮助学生掌握英文文献阅读、学术写作、国际交流等关键能力，从而服务于科研与学术发展需求。 课程教授英文文献阅读，英文学术词汇含义，学术文章结构，英文期刊写作，英文报告汇报。同时，课程分解教授文章的题目摘要到正文结论等规范。

课程简介（英文）

课程简介（英文）： Professional English serves as a cornerstone for graduate students seeking to enhance their academic proficiency in English, encompassing listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills. The course's primary objective is to equip students with essential competencies, including the ability to read and interpret English literature, engage in academic writing, and participate in international academic discourse, all of which are crucial for their research and academic advancement. The curriculum comprehensively covers a range of topics, such as English literature analysis, the precise meanings of academic vocabulary, the structural components of academic texts, writing for English-language journals, and the preparation and delivery of English presentations. Additionally, the course provides a systematic breakdown of academic paper conventions, guiding students through the intricacies of title formulation, abstract composition, main body development, and conclusion construction.

教学目标

教学目标：研究生掌握专业英语的基本能力，独立完成英文学术论文写作，快速阅读英文专业文献，国际学术交流等关键能力。 课程教授英文文献阅读，英文学术词汇含义，学术文章结构，英文期刊写作，英文报告汇报。课程分解教授文章的题目摘要到正文结论等规范。

教学方式

教学方式：采用课堂讲解，1学分，9周时间。

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction and Publication Rule: Define a Topic Week 2: Literature Review: Getting Information and Reading Week 3: Writing Paper 1 Week 4: Writing Paper 2 Week 5: Revision for Research Paper Week 6: Revision for Literature Review Paper Week 7: Give Presenetation, Communcation, Response to Revierwers Week 8: Project Presentation

教学参考资料

教学参考资料：1. The Elements of Style， William Strunk， 2006； 2. How to write and publish a Scientific Paper. Robert Day, 北京电子工业出版社，2010

课程简介（中文）

《物理化学》是药学专业本科生必修课，系统讲授化学热力学、化学动力学、电化学、表面化学及胶体分散体系等核心内容，并探讨物理化学原理在药学领域的应用。通过理论教学与实验相结合，培养学生运用物理化学方法分析和解决药物研发、生产及质量控制中的实际问题，为后续药剂学、药物分析等专业课程奠定理论基础。

课程简介（英文）

Physical Chemistry is a compulsory course for undergraduate students majoring in Pharmacy. It systematically covers core topics such as chemical thermodynamics, chemical kinetics, electrochemistry, surface chemistry, and colloidal dispersion systems, while exploring the application of physicochemical principles in pharmaceutical sciences. Through a combination of theoretical instruction and laboratory experiments, the course aims to equip students with the ability to analyze and solve practical problems in drug development, production, and quality control using physicochemical methods. It also lays a theoretical foundation for subsequent specialized courses such as Pharmaceutics and Pharmaceutical Analysis.

教学目标

  1. 知识目标：使学生掌握化学热力学、动力学、电化学、表面与胶体化学等物理化学核心理论，理解其在药物溶解度、稳定性、制剂设计及药物吸收等药学领域的应用机制。

  2. 能力目标：培养学生运用物理化学原理与方法分析和解决药物研发、生产及质量控制中的实际问题，提升实验设计与数据处理能力。

  3. 素养目标：强化科学思维与创新意识，为后续药剂学、药物分析等专业课程奠定理论基础，并培养严谨求实的科研态度。

Knowledge Objectives: To enable students to master the core theories of physical chemistry （including chemical thermodynamics, kinetics, electrochemistry, and surface and colloid chemistry） and to understand their application mechanisms in pharmaceutical fields such as drug solubility, stability, formulation design, and drug absorption.

Skill Objectives: To cultivate students' ability to apply physicochemical principles and methods to analyze and solve practical problems in drug development, production, and quality control, while enhancing experimental design and data processing skills.

Competency Objectives: To strengthen scientific thinking and innovative awareness, lay a theoretical foundation for subsequent specialized courses such as pharmaceutics and pharmaceutical analysis, and foster a rigorous and truth-seeking research attitude.

教学方式

  1. 理论讲授 系统讲解物理化学核心理论，结合药学应用案例（如药物溶解度预测、制剂稳定性分析），采用启发式教学，通过课堂提问与讨论提升学生参与度。

  2. 问题导向学习（PBL）分组研讨药学实际问题（如药物降解动力学、药物提取），引导学生运用物理化学原理提出解决方案，培养问题分析与团队协作能力。

  3. 实验教学 开设基础与综合实验，强化仪器操作与数据处理的规范化训练。

  4. 数字化工具辅助 利用elearning等数字化教学辅助平台分享课件和学习资料，并与学生互动和答疑。

  5. 过程性评价 通过随堂测试、实验报告及可课外作业等多维度考核，动态反馈学习成效，针对性调整教学策略。

Lectures
Systematically explain core theories of physical chemistry, integrated with pharmaceutical application cases (e.g., drug solubility prediction, formulation stability analysis). Employ heuristic teaching methods to enhance student engagement through in-class questioning and discussions.

Problem-Based Learning (PBL)
Organize group discussions on practical pharmaceutical issues (e.g., drug degradation kinetics, drug extraction). Guide students to propose solutions using physicochemical principles, fostering problem-solving skills and teamwork.

Laboratory Training
Conduct fundamental and comprehensive experiments to strengthen standardized training in instrument operation and data processing.

Digital Tool Support
Utilize elearning platforms and other digital teaching aids to share course materials and learning resources, while facilitating interaction and Q&A with students.

Process-Based Assessment
Implement multi-dimensional evaluations, including pop quizzes, lab reports, and extracurricular assignments, to provide dynamic feedback on learning outcomes and enable targeted teaching adjustments.

教学内容与进度安排

第1周

绪论

学习物理化学的任务和内容、物理化学的发展及其与药学的关系和物理化学的学习方法。

第一章 热力学第一定律

学习热力学概论、热力学的一些基本概念、体积功和可逆过程、热力学第一定律概述、焓和热容。

第2周

第一章 热力学第一定律

学习热力学第一定律的应用、热化学和几种热效应。

第二章、热力学第二定律

学习热力学第二定律概述、卡诺循环和卡诺定理、熵函数及过程方向性的判据、熵变的计算、热力学第三定律概述。

第3周

第二章、热力学第二定律

学习亥姆霍兹和吉布斯自由能、亥姆霍兹自由能变和吉布斯自由能变的计算、热力学函数间的关系、非平衡态热力学简介

第三章、多组分系统热力学

学习多组分系统和组成表示法、偏摩尔量、化学势、稀溶液的两个经验定律

第4周

第三章、多组分系统热力学

学习气体混合物中各组分的化学势、液体混合物、稀溶液、真实溶液中组分的化学势、稀溶液的依数性和分配定律。

第5周

第四章 化学平衡

学习化学反应的吉布斯自由能变化和平衡条件、化学反应等温方程式与标准平衡常数、平衡常数表示法、平衡常数测定和平衡转化率的计算、标准反应吉布斯能变化及化合物的标准生成吉布斯能、温度对平衡常数的影响、其他因素对平衡的影响、反应的耦合。

第6周

第五章 相平衡

学习相律及其基本概念、单组分系统、完全互溶双液系统、部分完全互溶双液系统和完全不部分完全互溶双液系统。

第7周

第五章 相平衡

学习两组分固-液系统、三组分系统。

第六章、电化学

学习法拉第电解定律和离子的电迁移、电解质溶液电导和测定、强电解质溶液的活度和活度系数、可逆电池和可逆电池热力学。

第8周

第六章 电化学

学习标准氢电极和电极电势、浓差电池、电池电动势的应用、电解与电极的极化和生物电化学。

第9周

第七章 化学动力学

学习化学反应速率和化学反应速率方程、具有简单级数反应、温度对反应速率的影响几种、几种典型的复杂反应

第10周

第七章 化学动力学

学习链反应及其速率方程、反应速率理论、溶液中的反应、光化学和催化反应

第十章 大分子溶液

学习大分子及大分子溶液的基本特性、大分子溶液的流变性、大分子溶液的超离心沉降和凝胶。

第11周

学习表面吉布斯能、表面张力以及表面的热力学关系式、弯曲表面的性质、溶液的表面吸附、铺展与浸润、不溶性表面膜、表面活性剂、固体在溶液中的吸附。

第12周

第九章 胶体分散系统

学习溶胶分散系统及其制备、溶胶的动力学性质、溶胶的光学性质、溶胶的电学性质、溶胶的溶胶的稳定性和聚沉、乳状液及微乳状液。

第17周

考试。

第18周

阅卷和评分。

Week 1
Introduction
Tasks and content of physical chemistry, its development, relationship with pharmacy, and learning methodologies.

Chapter 1: The First Law of Thermodynamics
Overview of thermodynamics, basic concepts, volume work and reversible processes, the first law of thermodynamics, enthalpy and heat capacity.

Week 2
Chapter 1: The First Law of Thermodynamics (Continued)
Applications of the first law, thermochemistry, and various thermal effects.

Chapter 2: The Second Law of Thermodynamics
Overview of the second law, Carnot cycle and theorem, entropy function and criteria for process directionality, entropy change calculations, overview of the third law of thermodynamics.

Week 3
Chapter 2: The Second Law of Thermodynamics (Continued)
Helmholtz and Gibbs free energy, calculations of Helmholtz and Gibbs free energy changes, relationships between thermodynamic functions, introduction to non-equilibrium thermodynamics.

Chapter 3: Thermodynamics of Multicomponent Systems
Multicomponent systems and composition representation, partial molar quantities, chemical potential, two empirical laws of dilute solutions.

Week 4
Chapter 3: Thermodynamics of Multicomponent Systems (Continued)
Chemical potential of components in gas mixtures, liquid mixtures, dilute solutions, chemical potential of components in real solutions, colligative properties of dilute solutions, and distribution law.

Week 5
Chapter 4: Chemical Equilibrium
Gibbs free energy change and equilibrium conditions for chemical reactions, reaction isotherm and standard equilibrium constant, representations of equilibrium constants, determination of equilibrium constants and calculation of equilibrium conversion rates, standard Gibbs free energy change and standard formation Gibbs free energy of compounds, effect of temperature on equilibrium constants, influence of other factors on equilibrium, coupled reactions.

Week 6
Chapter 5: Phase Equilibrium
Phase rule and basic concepts, single-component systems, completely miscible binary liquid systems, partially miscible and completely immiscible binary liquid systems.

Week 7
Chapter 5: Phase Equilibrium (Continued)
Binary solid-liquid systems, ternary systems.

Chapter 6: Electrochemistry
Faraday's laws of electrolysis and ion migration, conductivity of electrolyte solutions and its measurement, activity and activity coefficients of strong electrolyte solutions, reversible cells and thermodynamics of reversible cells.

Week 8
Chapter 6: Electrochemistry (Continued)
Standard hydrogen electrode and electrode potential, concentration cells, applications of cell electromotive force, electrolysis and electrode polarization, bioelectrochemistry.

Week 9
Chapter 7: Chemical Kinetics
Reaction rates and rate equations, reactions with simple orders, effect of temperature on reaction rates, several typical complex reactions.

Week 10
Chapter 7: Chemical Kinetics (Continued)
Chain reactions and their rate equations, reaction rate theories, reactions in solutions, photochemistry and catalytic reactions.

Chapter 10: Macromolecular Solutions
Basic characteristics of macromolecules and macromolecular solutions, rheology of macromolecular solutions, ultracentrifugal sedimentation of macromolecular solutions, and gels.

Week 11
Surface Gibbs energy, surface tension and thermodynamic relationships of surfaces, properties of curved surfaces, surface adsorption of solutions, spreading and wetting, insoluble surface films, surfactants, adsorption of solids in solutions.

Week 12
Chapter 9: Colloidal Dispersion Systems
Preparation of colloidal dispersion systems, kinetic properties of colloids, optical properties of colloids, electrical properties of colloids, stability and coagulation of colloids, emulsions and microemulsions.

Week 17
Examinations.

Week 18
Grading and evaluation.

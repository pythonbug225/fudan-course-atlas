课程简介（中文）

本课程以中国古代经典文本的精读为基础，引导学生探索先秦至汉代主要思想传统中的灵性经验与内在世界。课程涵盖道家、儒家、法家及早期佛教传统，通过选读核心文献，带领学生体察文本背后的生命体验与精神历程，比较各学派在灵修层面的不同取径，并培养跨文化比较研究的基本能力。

课程简介（英文）

Grounded in close reading of Chinese classical texts, this course guides students through the spiritual experiences and inner worlds embedded in China's major intellectual traditions from the Pre-Qin period to the Han dynasty. Spanning Taoist, Confucian, Legist, and early Buddhist traditions, the course invites students to uncover the lived experience behind canonical writings, compare the distinct spiritual orientations of each school, and develop foundational skills in cross-cultural comparative study.

教学目标

Based on an attentive reading of excerpts of Chinese Classics, this course is designed to introduce students to the main currents of Chinese ancient spiritual traditions (until the Han period, with focus on the Pre-Qin period), tracing into literary and philosophical texts the inner experience that they carry and interpret. Pondering over a selection of relevant excerpts, the students will navigate throughout Taoist, Confucian, Legist and early Buddhist traditions. Students will be able to understand and compare the dynamic of spiritual experience as suggested by various schools, mystics and authors, and to approach the journeys that have been undertaken. The goal of the course is also to provide students with skills in comparative textural and cultural studies, by drawing their attention to the lived experience that lies behind major texts of Chinese culture.

教学方式

The student will be expected to read carefully the material assigned before each session. Active participation through questions and discussions is also expected form them. Course requirements include active class attendance, attentive and critical reading, an oral presentation of the final paper’s topic, and the writing of a final paper.

课程简介（中文）

现代西方哲学大致可以分成两块：一块是英美哲学，另一块是欧洲大陆哲学。这一学期集中讲解和讨论现代西方的欧陆哲学。十九世纪中叶以来的大陆哲学也呈现出一种连贯性。唯意志主义-生命哲学-存在主义都注重人的生命的统一性，突出人的意志、情感、目的追求和价值取向在人生中的重大意义，在方法论上以现象学和解释学为主，强调内在体验和对生命意义的解释的不可替代作用。相对而言，它们与人文科学关系密切，往往以散文、小说、文艺评论、诗歌诠释的方式阐发哲理。叔本华和尼采的散文、狄尔泰的文艺评论、萨特的小说、海德格尔对诗的诠释，都堪称一绝。他们的哲学活动不束在校园内，经常走出课堂，对公众的影响较大，成为形形色色的欧洲社会运动的助推剂。 现代西方的欧陆哲学包括“意志主义-生命哲学”思潮、“现象学-存在主义”思潮、“后现代主义”思潮和“社会批判理论”思潮。

课程简介（英文）

Modern Western philosophy is broadly divided into two main branches: Anglo-American philosophy and Continental philosophy. This semester, we will focus specifically on Continental philosophy. Since the mid-19th century, Continental philosophy has shown a remarkable continuity. Voluntarism, the philosophy of life, and existentialism all emphasize the unity of human life. They highlight the profound significance of human will, emotion, purposeful pursuits, and value orientations in our existence. Methodologically, they are primarily grounded in phenomenology and hermeneutics, stressing the irreplaceable role of inner experience and the interpretation of life's meaning. Relatively speaking, these traditions are closely tied to the humanities. Philosophical ideas are often articulated through essays, novels, literary criticism, and poetic interpretations. The essays of Schopenhauer and Nietzsche, Dilthey's literary criticism, Sartre's novels, and Heidegger's interpretations of poetry are all masterpieces in their own right. Their philosophical activities were never confined to the ivory tower; they frequently stepped beyond the classroom to exert a significant public influence, serving as catalysts for various European social movements. Broadly speaking, modern Western Continental philosophy encompasses four major currents: Voluntarism and the philosophy of life, Phenomenology and existentialism, Postmodernism, and Critical theory.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在介绍计量经济学基础理论和实践，将通过学习相关工具掌握运用经济数据进行实证研究的能力。课程重点探讨检验经济理论和商业应用所涉及的统计方法，涵盖计量模型构建、参数估计及统计推断等核心方法，其中着重讲解单方程估计及其可能存在的估计问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an introduction to Econometrics where you will learn the tools that will enable you to conduct empirical research using economic data. The course examines the statistical techniques used in testing economic theory and business applications. The course covers leading methods for formulating econometric models, estimating their parameters, and performing statistical inference. The emphasis is placed on estimating a single equation and the problems associated with such estimation. (Course Requirements: No prerequisites, though students are expected to be familiar with basic calculus and matrix algebra. Basic knowledge of statistics is additionally preferred.)

教学目标

1. Understand the fundamentals of estimation and inference using the ordinary least squares model, as well as its benefits and drawbacks. 2. Comprehend the features of economic or financial data, either cross-sectional, time-series, or panel data, and build suitable econometric models on them. 3. Gain proficiency in the use of econometric software (such as STATA) for quantitative and statistical analysis. 4. Develop research and analytical abilities such as problem framing, building, estimating and testing econometric models for the analysis of economic and business problems in a variety of contexts. 5. Equip students with solid skills for potential careers in business or economics, whether in the public or commercial sector, or for further study in economics or related areas.

Hedge Fund

2026春 · MBA60068.01 · 孙林

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course introduces the alternative investment strategies, which behave differently from traditional investment strategies. Firstly, participants will be provided with a comprehensive introduction to the fundamentals of alternative investment strategies including their risk-return relationship, role in portfolio diversification, and performance evaluation. Investments in hedge funds, fund of funds, commodities, and financial derivatives will also be discussed. Secondly, latest academic research results will be presented to help students to build a solid theoretical background. Thirdly, case studies will give participants a comprehensive look at the real stories of successful alternative asset managers. Finally, the course project with industry mentors requires participants to solve real-life problems by applying what they learn from the course.

教学目标

Through the course, participants will be able to: Develop a broad view of alternative investment Discuss the global trend of the alternative investment Demonstrate the risk and return relationship of various investment Evaluate the performance of alternative investment managers. Construct an alternative investment portfolio using quantitative skills

教学方式

This course will start with an overview of hedge funds industry. Through the hedge funds investment strategies, participants will understand how various hedge fund strategies work. Together with the real-life case studies, participants are able to apply the theoretical knowledge from the course to solve real life problems. The course also discusses the latest academic research works. The discussion covers several topics including: performance evaluation, fund characteristics, and managerial incentives. Latest market trend and updates will also be presented and discussed. Industry projects with industry mentors will further enhance the participants’ expertise in the area.

教学内容与进度安排

Class 1 Topic: Introduction to alternative investment strategies Note: hedge fund history, industry outlook, role of various participants, and various asset classes. Background Reading: When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Class 2 Topic: Investment strategies part 1 Note: equity long/short, market neutral, distressed securities, event-type strategies, convertible arbitrage, and commodity trading advisors. Background Reading: When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: Alpha Architect: Is Momentum Losing Steam? by Richard B. Evans and Aldo Sesia. Class 3 Topic: Investment strategies part 2 Note: equity long/short, market neutral, distressed securities, event-type strategies, convertible arbitrage, and commodity trading advisors. Background Reading: When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: McDonald’s, Wendy’s, and Hedge Funds: The Impact of Hedge Fund Activism on Corporate Governance by David P. Stowell. Class 4 Topic: Risk and return Note: arbitrage, factor model, market risk, operational risk, liquidity risk. Background Reading: When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: Openinvest by Shawn Cole, Boris Vallee, and Nicole Tempest Keller Class 5 Topic: Characteristics of various hedge funds Note: fees, leverage, compensation structures, strategies, regulations, and differences from mutual funds. Background Reading: When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: ProShares Hedge Replication ETF by Pedro Matos and Anil Demir. Class 6 Topic: Fund of funds, Chinese hedge fund market and other related topics. Note: fund selections, performance evaluation. Background Reading: When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: AQR’s DELTA Strategy by Daniel Bergstresser, Lauren Cohen, Randolph Cohen, and Christopher Malloy.

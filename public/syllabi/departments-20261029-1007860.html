医学影像分析与应用

2025秋 · STAT70013.01 · 庄吓海

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

该课程针对有意深入医学影像和医学图像处理领域研究的硕士和博士研究生。课程的内容主要是由图像处理领域的高阶知识和当前医学影像分析领域的前沿文章的研究部成果组成。选修本课程的学生需要主动学习相关的论文知识，并针对某个主题深入理解其代表性算法和代码实现其功能。该课程内容主要关注医学图像计算、分析的基本算法，会涉及少量医学物理和临床医学影像技术等多个学科，具有跨学科交叉领域研究的特点。 课程主要内容包括医学图像介绍、图像分割、图像配准、图像联合计算、图像统计建模应用。教学形式以授课老师引导介绍和学生的研讨为主。通过参与本课程的学习，学生将了解医学影像分析的基础知识和当前该领域的研究方向；学生将掌握进入该领域研究的基本方法和技能，并具有使用跨学科知识和编程技术解决医学影像临床应用的实际问题的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for master's and doctoral students who intend to delve into the fields of medical imaging and medical image processing. The course content primarily consists of advanced knowledge in image processing and cutting-edge research findings from current studies in medical image analysis. Students enrolled in this course are expected to proactively study related academic papers and gain an in-depth understanding of representative algorithms and their code implementations for specific topics. The course focuses on fundamental algorithms for medical image computation and analysis, touching upon a range of disciplines including medical physics and clinical imaging techniques, thus embodying the characteristics of interdisciplinary research. The main topics covered include an introduction to medical imaging, image segmentation, image registration, joint image computation, and applications of image statistical modeling. The teaching format emphasizes instructor-led introductions and student-led discussions. By participating in this course, students will gain an understanding of the foundational knowledge and current research directions in medical image analysis. They will acquire the essential methods and skills needed to enter this field of research and develop the ability to solve practical problems in clinical medical imaging using interdisciplinary knowledge and programming techniques.

教学目标

通过本授课老师课程介绍，学科研究最新方向的引导，以及学生对课程的学习和研讨，使学生能够深刻掌握图像计算，医学影像，医学影像分析研究相关的研究的算法细节和实现细节，包括内容： 1 医学图像计算基础； 2 医学图像分割； 3 医学图像配准； 4 医学图像统计建模 5 医学图像联合计算。

教学方式

教师授课与学生研讨并行。

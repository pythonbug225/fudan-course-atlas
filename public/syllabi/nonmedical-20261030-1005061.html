课程简介（中文）

本课程涵盖用英语学习外语和文学的学生所需的学术写作技巧的基本要素。课程主要侧重于学术论文和写作技巧，包括用语法正确的句子构成的结构化段落进行写作，了解论文的不同部分，并开始用学术语体进行写作。写作是一个过程，学生将在规划、审查、编辑和修改作品等阶段得到指导。

课程简介（英文）

This course covers the fundamental elements of academic writing skill needed by those studying Foreign Languages and Literature in English. The course focuses primarily on the academic essay and the skills needed for writing it, which includes writing in structured paragraph that comprise grammatically correct sentences, understand the different parts of essays, and beginning to write in an academic register. Writing is presented as a process and students are guided through the stages of planning, reviewing, editing and revising their work.

教学目标

This course offers an introduction to academic writing in English for Freshman students. The course aims to prepare students with the essential knowledge and skills necessary to meet the linguistic and discursive expectations of the academic community and form solid basis for further study. It encourages the development of writing skills in stages by scaffolding essay writing over multiple planning, review, and revision sessions. AI and other digital tools are used throughout the course, encouraging students to use AI responsibly and purposefully. Fundamental grammar issues are dealt with in each class through a designated “Grammar Focus” slot. The course utilizes multiple feedback forms, including teach feedback, self- and peer review, and AI feedback.

教学方式

The course is designed to scaffold the writing process, allowing students to develop. Each fundamental skill is practices multiple times throughout the course in-class and in homework assignments. The course also accommodates limited experience in writing in English and aims to develop confidence and competence in using English in students. The classes are dynamic, involving students continually in the process of their own learning and utilizing both traditional and digital modes of teaching, learning and assessment. The course comprises lectures, discussions, in-class exercises, homework, and AI exercises.

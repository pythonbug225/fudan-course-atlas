宗教与政治

2025秋 · POLI60050.01 · 涂怡超

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课通过政治学、社会学、宗教学、国际关系学的理论和方法，有系统的介绍东西方主要宗教的政治观点及其在政治格局中的地位与作用；中国的政教关系及宗教在政治格局中的地位和作用；同时阐释宗教与现代社会诸多议题，如安全、民族主义、人权、女权、婚姻、生死选择和环保等的相互关系。

课程简介（英文）

Politics and Religion covers a broad range of issues and concerns of interest to the student of Comparative Politics and International Relations: political theology, institutional formation and change, state power and authority, legitimacy and resistance, nationalism, as well as the shifting and productive boundaries between the sacred and the profane, the religious and the secular. The course will introduce students to a variety of different analytic approaches to religion, exploring the tensions between them. These include, but are not limited to, positivist approaches that posit religion as a causal factor, public policy-orientated approaches that view religion as a challenge to and an instrument for governance, and interpretivist and poststructuralist approaches that seek to question religion as a social scientific concept. The course will address these issues through a comparative exploration of religion and politics in a variety of contexts.

教学目标

学生应掌握东西方主要宗教的政治观点及其在政治格局中的地位与作用；中国的政教关系及宗教在政治格局中的地位和作用；宗教与现代社会诸多议题，如民主、人权、女权、婚姻、生死选择和环保等的相互关系；通过政治学、宗教学、社会学和国际关系学的基本理论和方法，对宗教与政治的全局与议题进行批判性思考，培养对相应问题的理解和讨论能力。

教学方式

以教师课堂讲授为主，辅之以PPT、讨论、答疑和辅导等。

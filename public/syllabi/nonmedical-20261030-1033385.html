智能控制理论

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

智能控制理论涉及复杂、不确定、非线性大型系统智能控制器的设计，涉及模糊控制系统、专家系统、神经网络控制器等。当前业界对于智能控制技术人才需求很大，本学院博士通过对该学位课的学习获得专业知识，可有效拓展他们的专业水准。

课程简介（英文）

n recent years, the theory of intelligent control has been rapidly advanced under the development of artificial intelligence theory, and has intersected with various disciplines to become a research frontier in each field. Meanwhile, the application of intelligent control theory has gradually begun to influence our lives. It is a new technical science that studies, develops theories, methods, techniques and application systems for simulating, extending and expanding human intelligent control. The research in this field includes the design of intelligent controllers for complex, uncertain and nonlinear large-scale systems, involving fuzzy control systems, expert systems, neural network controllers, etc. The various specialties in the field of electronic information aim to cultivate high-level compound talents with a good mathematical and physical foundation, initial research ability, certain practical working ability to solve engineering and technical problems, and a forward-thinking mindset and preliminary knowledge of the frontier. The opening of this course is based on the current technological hotspots, aiming to explore the frontier research directions of intelligent control systems based on artificial intelligence and stimulate students' interest in the current technological hotspots research. It can effectively supplement the lack of frontier courses in the current training program. In addition, the current industry has a great demand for talents in intelligent control technology. Through the study of this degree course, doctoral students in this college can effectively expand their professional level.

教学目标

通过本课程学习，了解智能控制理论的技术进展，掌握智能控制、智能算法 相关理论基础，学会智能控制器主流工具箱的使用和典型应用的开发，探索认知智能控制理论、人工智能等前沿研究方向，激发智能控制理论研究兴趣。使学生了解智能控制在控制科学与工程中的地位和作用，培养正确的解决工程控制问题的正确方法。

教学方式

讲授与讨论的策略、小任务的实验等

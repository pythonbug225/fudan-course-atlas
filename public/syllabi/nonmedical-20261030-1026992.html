数据科学及物理问题中的算法导论

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍算法的基础概念，以及机器学习、状态估计/推断和现代数据科学中若干广泛应用的方法，旨在帮助学生深入理解机器学习算法的核心原理与理论基础。课程内容涵盖：基础概率与统计技术、模拟方法、优化策略，以及高维问题与重构算法的核心思想。为紧跟大数据与现代分析技术的快速发展，课程还将探讨一系列针对大规模数据处理和高维统计分析的前沿算法。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers an introduction to the fundamental concepts of algorithms and several widely used methods in machine learning, state estimation/inference, and modern data science. A key objective is to help students understand the underlying principles and theoretical foundations of machine learning algorithms. Topics covered include basic probability and statistical techniques, simulation methods, optimization strategies, and essential ideas from high-dimensional problems and reconstruction algorithms. To keep pace with the rapid evolution of big data and modern analytics, the course also explores a selection of contemporary algorithms developed for large-scale data processing and high-dimensional statistical analysis.

教学目标

本课程面向对机器学习与现代数据分析的基础原理及实际应用感兴趣的高年级本科生和研究生，专业包括物理学、计算机科学、数据科学或相关领域。同时也适合希望加强在状态估计、优化、计算机视觉及物理中的算法方法理解的人员。建议具备基本的微积分、线性代数和概率论背景，课程中也将根据需要对关键概念进行回顾。热烈欢迎大家参加。 (This course is intended for advanced undergraduate and graduate students in physics, computer science, data science, or related fields who are interested in the foundations and practical applications of machine learning and modern data analysis. It is also suitable for researchers and professionals seeking to strengthen their understanding of algorithmic methods in state estimation, optimization, computer vision and physics. A basic background in calculus, linear algebra, and probability is recommended, though key concepts will be reviewed as needed throughout the course. You are all very welcome to attend the course!)

教学方式

每周安排1次课程，每次135分钟。课程作业分为两类：解析类问题（如估算或推导）和编程类任务。解析类问题相对直接，而编程任务则需要更深入的设计与实现思考。每周将布置适当的练习题，以加深对课堂核心概念的理解。这些练习紧密结合实际应用，涵盖物理、状态估计、不确定性下的几何推理与优化等领域中的常用技术。 (One 135-minute class is held each week. There will be two types of exercises: analytical problems (such as estimations or derivations) and programming assignments. The analytical problems are relatively straightforward, while the programming tasks require more thoughtful design and implementation. Each week, suitable exercises will be assigned to reinforce the key concepts covered in class. These exercises are designed to closely reflect practical techniques in areas such as physics, state estimation, geometric reasoning under uncertainty, and optimization.)

课程简介（中文）

本课程提供了爱因斯坦广义相对论的严格介绍，涵盖其数学基础和物理含义。主题包括狭义相对论、张量微积分、时空曲率、爱因斯坦场方程、黑洞、引力波和宇宙学。重点将放在概念理解和解决问题的技巧，为学生进一步学习理论物理和引力研究做准备。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a rigorous introduction to Einstein’s theory of General Relativity, covering both its mathematical foundations and physical implications. Topics include special relativity, tensor calculus, spacetime curvature, Einstein’s field equations, black holes, gravitational waves, and cosmology. Emphasis will be placed on both conceptual understanding and problem-solving techniques, preparing students for further study in theoretical physics and gravitational research.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to equip students with a deep understanding of the mathematical structures and physical principles underlying General Relativity. By the end of the course, students will be able to derive and analyze Einstein’s field equations, explore the properties of black holes and gravitational waves, and apply relativistic principles to cosmological models. The course aims to develop both theoretical insight and computational proficiency necessary for advanced research in gravitational physics.

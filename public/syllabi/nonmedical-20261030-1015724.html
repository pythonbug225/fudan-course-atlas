课程简介（中文）

本课程致力于通过系统而深入的语言教学，全面提升学生的英语语言运用能力。课程内容涵盖听力、口语、阅读、写作和翻译等关键语言技能，并通过一系列课堂活动、作业和线上辅助练习，激发学生的学习热情。学生将在深入探索语言知识的同时，深化对于相关社会文化因素的理解，洞察语言表达的微妙之处和有效沟通的影响力。

课程简介（英文）

The course is designed to enhance students’ overall language proficiency through a comprehensive and systematic approach. It focuses on developing listening, speaking, reading, writing, and translation skills through a variety of engaging classroom activities, homework assignments, and online supplementary exercises. Students will be exposed to new linguistic phenomena, fostering a deeper understanding of the socio-cultural dimensions inherent in language learning. They will also gain insights into the subtleties of language expression and the impact of effective communication.

教学目标

Integrated College English, as a comprehensive course, not only aims to equip students with the necessary language skills to meet the Levels 4-5 of China’s Standards of English Language Ability but also seeks to enrich their educational experience in the following several aspects: Language Proficiency and Effective Communication: The course will hone students’ abilities in listening, speaking, reading, writing, and translation, with a special emphasis on both written and oral communication. Cultural Awareness and Appreciation: The course will incorporate elements of cultural studies, encouraging students to appreciate the diversity of English-speaking cultures and to develop a global perspective. Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: Students will be guided to think critically about the information they encounter in English texts, fostering the ability to question, analyze, and synthesize information. Digital Literacy: Given the course’s emphasis on computer-assisted learning, students will also develop digital literacy skills. They will learn how to effectively use technology for language learning and communication, preparing them for the digital age. Lifelong Learning and Self-improvement: The course will instill a passion for lifelong learning and self-improvement, encouraging students to continue developing their English skills beyond the classroom and to seek out opportunities for personal and professional growth.

教学方式

Lecture-based, with the teacher providing in-depth or general explanations of texts, vocabulary, and various language knowledge and skills, supplemented by group discussions, group presentations, or other forms of classroom activities.

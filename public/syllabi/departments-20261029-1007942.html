管理学经典理论研究

2025秋 · BA70016.01 · 郑琴琴、马胜辉

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将系统地回顾经典管理理论及其对中国企业管理和组织实践的启示。教学过程中会涉及论文汇报、小组讨论、课堂展示和书面作业等。为了更好地参与课堂讨论，学生应在课前阅读所有指定的阅读材料。

课程简介（英文）

In this course, we will systematically review classic management theories and their implications for management and organization practices in Chinese enterprises. For teaching we will utilize a variety of techniques including lectures, group discussions, class presentations and written assignments, etc. To actively participate in class discussion, students are expected to read all the assigned readings before class.

教学目标

To succeed in this course, you are expected to be able to do the following: Understand the central notions of each theory being discussed Comment on various arguments in these theories Relate these theories to the unique traits of Chinese companies Improve the skills in applying these theories to your specific research questions

教学方式

授课方式包括：课程讲授、课堂讨论、小组作业等。

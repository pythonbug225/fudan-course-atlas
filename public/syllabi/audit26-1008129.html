课程简介（中文）

《微流控芯片技术及其应用》这门课程是研究生专业选修课，主要介绍微流控芯片的发展历史、芯片的设计和加工、芯片驱动和微流控制、样品引入、芯片毛细管电泳、微流控芯片技术在各领域的应用和新进展等内容，适合药学、医学、化学和微电子学等学科研究生选修。通过学科交叉视角，培养学生跨学科创新能力和微纳系统研发思维，为前沿科研提供关键技术支撑。

课程简介（英文）

"Microfluidic Chip Technology and Its Applications" is a graduate-level elective course. It provides a comprehensive introduction to microfluidic chip technology, covering its historical development, chip design and fabrication techniques, fluidic actuation and control mechanisms, sample introduction methods, and chip-based capillary electrophoresis. The course also explores cutting-edge applications and recent advancements of microfluidic technology across multiple disciplines. Designed for graduate students in pharmacy, medicine, chemistry, and microelectronics, the curriculum adopts an interdisciplinary approach to cultivate students' cross-disciplinary innovation capabilities and develop their research thinking in micro/nano-system development, thereby supporting advanced scientific research with key technological foundations.

教学目标

使学生掌握药物毛细管电泳分析的基本原理、分类、仪器系统和相关分析技术，了解该领域的发展史和最新进展，学习毛细管电泳分析技术在药物分析中的应用，并通过具体药物分析案例的课程实验学习仪器操作和获得实操技能。

This course is designed to equip students with comprehensive knowledge and practical skills in pharmaceutical analysis using capillary electrophoresis. Through systematic instruction, students will master the fundamental principles, classification methods, and instrumental systems of capillary electrophoresis technology. The curriculum covers the historical evolution and contemporary advancements in this field, with particular emphasis on drug analysis applications. Students will gain hands-on experience by conducting laboratory experiments focused on real-world drug analysis cases, enabling them to develop essential operational competencies. The course integrates theoretical foundations with practical training, adopting a case-based teaching methodology to bridge the gap between classroom learning and professional application.

教学方式

本课程采用多元化的教学方法，主要包括：

  1. 理论讲授

    1）通过多媒体课件与互动讨论系统讲解核心理论；

    2）结合学科发展史梳理技术演进脉络。

  2. 案例教学

    1）精选药物分析典型案例（如抗生素纯度检测、手性药物分离等）；

    2）采用PBL（问题导向学习）模式开展小组研讨。

  3. 实验实训

    设置1-2个单元的实验模块，通过实验实操加深对课程相关知识的理解和认识。

  4. 文献研讨

    1）重点解读5-8篇标志性文献；

    2）组织前沿进展学术沙龙。

教学特色：
1）实行"课前在线预习-课堂重点突破-课后拓展训练"的混合式教学；
2）实验设计体现"基础→综合→创新"的能力进阶；
3）引入相关行业项目作为教学案例；
4）采用过程性评价与成果评价相结合的方式。

Teaching Methods:

This course adopts diversified teaching approaches, including:

Lectures

  1. Systematic explanation of core theories through multimedia presentations and interactive discussions;

  2. Tracing the technological evolution by incorporating disciplinary development history.

Case-Based Learning

  1. Selected classic pharmaceutical analysis cases (e.g., antibiotic purity testing, chiral drug separation);

  2. Group discussions using the PBL (Problem-Based Learning) model.

Laboratory Training

1-2 experimental modules are designed to enhance understanding through hands-on practice.

Literature Seminars

  1. In-depth analysis of 5-8 landmark research papers;

  2. Organization of academic salons on cutting-edge advancements.

Teaching Features:

  1. Blended learning model: "Pre-class online learning → In-class intensive learning → Post-class extended training";

  2. Progressive experimental design: "Basic → Comprehensive → Innovative" skill development;

  3. Integration of real-world industry projects as teaching cases;

  4. Combined assessment of process evaluation and outcome evaluation.

Course Schedule

Session Week Teaching Content & Expected Outcomes Assignment/Experiment
1 2 Introduction: Master the brief history, classification, and development trends of microfluidic chips. Read supplementary materials and write a review on the development history of microfluidic chips.
2 3 Microfluidic Chip Fabrication Techniques: Understand the structure, materials, and fabrication technologies of microfluidic chips. Search and submit literature on microfluidic chip fabrication techniques.
3 4 Microfluidic Driving Techniques: Learn about various microfluidic pumps and their applications in microfluidics control. Retrieve, read, and summarize relevant literature.
4 5 Microfluidic Control Techniques: Study various microvalve control technologies and their applications in microfluidics. Search for literature on microfluidic control techniques.
5 6 Conventional Capillary Electrophoresis: Grasp the principles, injection methods, and separation modes of conventional capillary electrophoresis. Read specialized literature on conventional capillary electrophoresis.
6 7 Chip Capillary Electrophoresis: Master the principles, injection methods, and separation modes of chip capillary electrophoresis. Write a review on chip capillary electrophoresis.
7 8 Sample Introduction Techniques in Microfluidic Chips: Learn about sample introduction methods in microfluidic chips. Search for literature on microfluidic chip sample introduction techniques.
8 9 Sample Pretreatment Techniques in Microfluidic Chips: Master liquid-liquid microextraction, dialysis separation, filtration, solid-phase extraction, PCR, and immunoassays in microfluidic chips. Read literature on microfluidic chip sample pretreatment techniques.
9 10 Microfluidic Chip Bioreactors: Study enzyme reactors, organ-on-a-chip, and other biochemical reactor technologies. Read literature on microfluidic chip bioreactors.
10 11 Microfluidic Chip Modification Techniques: Learn various microfluidic chip modification methods. Conduct an extracurricular discussion on microfluidic chip modification techniques.
11 12 Microfluidic Chip Detection Techniques: Understand the principles and applications of fluorescence, chemiluminescence, electrochemical, and mass spectrometry detection in microfluidic chips. Write a review on microfluidic chip detection techniques.
12 13 Applications in Drug Analysis and Food Safety: Master the use of microfluidic chip analysis in drug testing and food safety supervision. Conduct research on microfluidic chip applications in drug analysis and food safety.
13 14 Applications in Biomedical Research: Learn the application techniques of microfluidic chips in biomedical research. Search for literature on microfluidic chip applications in biomedical research.
14 15 Applications in Proteomics: Master the use of microfluidic chip analysis in proteomics. Write a review on microfluidic chip applications in proteomics.
15 16 Applications in Clinical Diagnostics: Study the application of microfluidic chip analysis in clinical diagnostics. Search for literature on microfluidic chip applications in clinical diagnostics.

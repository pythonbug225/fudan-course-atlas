课程简介（中文）

课程从MOSFET器件的基本物理特性开始，由浅入深地介绍模拟集成电路的基本设计单元，包括单级放大电路、差分放大电路、电流镜、偏置电路、运算放大器与基准参考源电路等。课程重点介绍运算放大器的不同结构和性能，结合小信号模型分析电路的频率响应以及稳定性。理论分析与实际设计案例相结合，引导学生比较不同电路结构的优缺点，从实际应用出发，选择最优的电路结构、设计方法和版图结构。

课程简介（英文）

The aim of the course is to advance understanding of the principles of operation of analog CMOS circuits and their design procedures. Students will learn basic circuit elements in analog IC design and basic building blocks for realizing amplifier circuits. Upon completion of the course, students will be able to perform circuit analysis using small-signal equivalent circuits, and frequency analysis using Bode plots. The course combines theoretical analysis with practical design examples, helping students understand trade-offs among different circuit architectures.

教学目标

On completion of this course, students should be able to:

  • Understand basic single-transistor amplifier configurations, i.e., CS, CG and source follower amplifiers
  • Use small-signal equivalent circuits in analysing circuit performance
  • Analyse the low-frequency response of discrete-circuit amplifiers and estimate the corner frequency
  • Analyse the high-frequency response of discrete and integrated amplifiers using Miller theorem and open-circuit time constant methods
  • Sketch the magnitude and phase response of an amplifier in a Bode plot
  • Understand filter basics
  • Use positive or negative feedbacks in realizing analog circuit blocks
  • Design differential and multi-stage amplifiers down to transistor level
  • Perform noise analysis on CMOS circuits

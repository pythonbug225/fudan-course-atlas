中国证券法

2025秋 · LAW70015.01 · 陈立

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要探讨中国证券监管体系，重点关注资本市场准入和企业融资问题。学生将系统了解中国证券市场的法律框架，包括首次公开发行、股票发行、公司治理机制和股东权益保护等内容。课程还将深入探讨公司并购、资本市场监管以及公司法与证券法的相互关系。这些主题将置于中国改革时期的法律体系、商业环境和监管政策的大背景下进行分析。课程特别关注中国资本市场的实践挑战与机遇，使学生既能掌握理论原理，又能了解企业融资和证券交易的实际应用。

课程简介（英文）

This course examines securities regulation in China, with a focus on capital market access and corporate financing. Students will develop a comprehensive understanding of the legal framework governing securities markets in China, including initial public offerings, stock issuance, corporate governance mechanisms, and shareholders' rights protection. The course explores corporate mergers and acquisitions, regulatory oversight of capital markets, and the interplay between corporate law and securities regulation. Throughout the course, these topics will be analyzed within the broader context of China's evolving legal system, business environment, and regulatory policies during the reform era. Special attention will be given to practical challenges and opportunities in Chinese capital markets, enabling students to understand both theoretical principles and their real-world applications in corporate financing and securities transactions.

教学目标

This course aims to develop students' comprehensive understanding of securities regulation and corporate financing in China's capital markets. Students will master the fundamental principles and legal framework of securities regulation while developing analytical skills to address complex corporate governance issues and regulatory challenges. Through case studies and practical problem-solving exercises, students will learn to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world business scenarios and evaluate the legal and policy implications of capital market transactions. The integration of interactive learning methods and engagement with industry practitioners will equip students with both theoretical foundations and practical insights essential for legal practice in securities and corporate finance.

教学方式

This course employs diverse teaching methods, combining traditional lectures with interactive case studies, group discussions, and Socratic questioning. Students are required to complete assigned readings before class and participate actively in critical analysis and discussions. To bridge theory with practice, guest speakers from the legal and business communities will be invited to share their professional experiences and insights, offering students practical perspectives on commercial transactions and current market developments. This multi-faceted approach aims to develop students' analytical skills, critical thinking abilities, and practical understanding of real-world commercial legal issues.

教学内容与进度安排

Module 1: Introduction to Chinese Financial Markets Week 1: Understanding China's Financial Markets - Overview of structure, development, and characteristics Week 2: Financial Regulatory Framework - Regulatory bodies, policies, and supervision systems Module 2: Securities Market Operations Week 3: Securities Offerings - IPO process, public offerings, and private placements Week 4: Securities Listings - Listing requirements, procedures, and regulatory compliance Week 5: Post-Listing Issues - Continuous disclosure, corporate governance, and compliance obligations Week 6: Market Misconduct - Securities fraud, insider trading, and market manipulation Module 3: Corporate Control and M&A Week 7-9: Takeovers of Listed Companies (Three Parts) Legal framework for acquisitions Takeover procedures and regulations Case studies and practical considerations Module 4: Private Markets Week 10-11: Private Equity (Two Parts) Private equity investments Fund formation and operation Exit strategies and regulations Week 12: Course Review

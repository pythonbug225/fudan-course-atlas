课程简介（中文）

本课程分为7个模块：

1.外交的定义与功能

2.影响外交的基本因素

3.经济外交概述

4.经济外交的分类

5.经济外交研究的基础理论

6.经济外交的理论分析框架

7.经济外交的效应评估

课程简介（英文）

The course is divided into 7 modules:1. Definition and Functions of Diplomacy, 2. Basic Factors Influencing Diplomacy, 3. Overview of Economic Diplomacy,4. Classification of Economic Diplomacy, 5.Basic Theories of Economic Diplomacy Research, 6.Theoretical Analytical Framework of Economic Diplomacy, 7.Effect Assessment of Economic Diplomacy

教学目标

本课程通过对经济外交的理论、政策与实践的讲解，帮助学生基本了解和初步掌握经济外交的基本内涵、表现形式、运行机制，经济外交研究的基础理论框架，经济外交的实践手段，经济外交的分析方法与经济外交政策的效应评估。
This course will help students gain a basic understanding and initial grasp of the fundamental connotations, forms of expression, and operational mechanisms of economic diplomacy, as well as the basic theoretical framework for economic diplomacy research, practical means of economic diplomacy, analytical methods of economic diplomacy, and the evaluation of the effects of economic diplomacy policies through explanations of the theories, policies, and practices of economic diplomacy.

教学方式

以教师讲解为主，辅以案例解析。
The course primarily consists of teacher-led lectures, supplemented by case studies.

Students are required to identify the types of economic diplomacy activities, explain the underlying theories and methods used in their analysis, illustrate the positive and negative impacts with specific case studies, propose measures to address both positive and negative impacts, and provide suggestions for choices to be made by their country or region. The paper should be no more than 3,000 words. The grading criteria are as follows: 1. Type of economic diplomacy activity: 10 points 2. Explanation of the underlying theories used in the analysis: 15 points 3. Explanation of the methods used in the effect assessment: 15 points 4. Illustration of positive and negative impacts with specific case studies: 25 points 5. Proposed measures to address both positive and negative impacts: 25 points 6. Citations and footnotes: 5 points 7. Word count within 3,000 words: 5 points

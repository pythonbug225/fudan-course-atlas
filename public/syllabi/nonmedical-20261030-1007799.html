采购与仓储管理

2025秋 · MEM40001.01 · 吉阿兵

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

随着互联网技术的不断发展和国际地缘政治的不断演变，全球供应链面临巨大重构压力，供应链间的竞争更为激烈、更为复杂，如何保持企业供应链的稳定性和高性价比成为企业经营必须解决的难题。采购是企业供应链管理的最重要环节之一， 企业最终的成品质量和价格高度依赖于采购的原物料和服务的质量和价格，如何高效地完成采购活动是企业运营管理的最重要策略。仓储管理是企业原材料和产成品的蓄水池，高效的仓储管理水平不仅有利于提高企业运作效率，而且能有效降低资金和人员成本。本课程主要讲述采购管理过程中所涉及的基本概念、方法、流程，仓储管理的基本方法和优化措施，采购和仓储管理中常用的决策方法。

课程简介（英文）

With the continuous development of Internet technology and the constant evolution of international geopolitics, the global supply chain is facing enormous pressure for restructuring. Competition among supply chains has become more intense and complex, and how to maintain the stability and high cost-effectiveness of an enterprise's supply chain has become a crucial problem that must be solved in business operations. Procurement is one of the most important links in an enterprise's supply chain management. The quality and price of an enterprise's final products are highly dependent on the quality and price of the raw materials and services purchased. Therefore, how to efficiently carry out procurement activities is the most important strategy in enterprise operation management. Warehouse management serves as a "reservoir" for an enterprise's raw materials and finished products. A high level of efficient warehouse management not only helps improve the operational efficiency of the enterprise but also effectively reduces capital and personnel costs. This course mainly covers the basic concepts, methods, and processes involved in procurement management, the basic methods and optimization measures for warehouse management, as well as the commonly used decision-making methods in procurement and warehouse management.

教学目标

系统了解和掌握采购管理的基本概念、方法，熟练掌握采购操作流程，了解战略采购的地位、角色、流程和供应商评估选择办法。对在全球化供应链管理背景下的采购绩效评估、AI对采购方式的意义等有较为清楚的了解。掌握仓储管理的基本方法和常见优化措施。掌握采购和仓储管理中的决策方法。

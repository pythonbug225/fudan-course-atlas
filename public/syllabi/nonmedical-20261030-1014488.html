课程简介（中文）

本课程是一门面向学习过30～34学时，掌握150个左右汉语词的学习者进阶基础汉语课程。课程在巩固语音、基础词汇和语法的同时，重点扩展实用表达场景，系统提升学习者在日常生活情境中的听、说、读、写综合交际能力。学生完成本课程后，能够使用所学知识在日常生活和工作中使用汉语与母语者进行简单交流。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an elementary Chinese course designed for learners who have completed 30–34 hours of study and mastered approximately 150 Chinese words. While reinforcing pronunciation, basic vocabulary, and grammar, the course focuses on expanding practical communication scenarios and systematically enhancing learners’ overall listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills in everyday life contexts.Once the students finish the course , they can use Chinese to communicate with native speakers in daily life and at work.

教学目标

Course Learning Objectives: This course enables learners to: Acquire Foundational Language Elements: Master approximately 150-200 new vocabulary items. Master key grammatical structures (including expressions for summarization, verb reduplication, resultative complements, the "比" sentence pattern, etc.). Develop Core Communication Skills: Comprehend and participate in short conversations on everyday topics (e.g., travel, shopping, dining, transportation, making plans). Use basic yet coherent sentences to describe experiences, express needs, or make suggestions. Gain Initial Cultural Awareness: Develop a preliminary understanding of relevant Chinese cultural customs and practices. Achieve Functional Progression: Transition from basic survival communication to effectively handling common daily life situations.

教学方式

Scenario-Based Instruction: Focuses on high-frequency daily life contexts, including travel，transportation, shopping, food and drink, health, and leisure activities. Integrated Skill Development: Enhances practical language application through task-based learning activities. Cultural Integration: Seamlessly incorporates essential Chinese cultural knowledge and social etiquette within the language curriculum. HSK Level 2 Alignment: Explicitly aligns with the Chinese Proficiency Test (HSK) Level 2 standards, establishing a solid foundation for certification.

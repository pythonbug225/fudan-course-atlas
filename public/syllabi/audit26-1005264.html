课程简介（中文）

课程主要讲授全球卫生研究的基础理论知识和实践研究方法，包括科学研究与项目实施两个方面。同时，结合新现再现传染病、艾滋病、精神卫生、卫生筹资与公平性等全球卫生主要研究领域，重点讲授当前全球卫生研究的机遇与挑战。

课程简介（英文）

The past decade has witnessed remarkable advances in global health in China. However, global health education remains uncommon and slow for Chinese medical students majoring in public health, medicine, and nursing. This course provides an introduction to both principles and practice that include strategies for global health implementation, research methods and tools, and also major challenges in emerging and re-emerging infectious diseases, HIV/AIDS, mental health, health financing and equity, and so on, through experiential case studies from global health leaders.

教学目标

面向医学与其他相关专业研究生，介绍全球卫生研究的理论与方法，并结合实例分析，使学生掌握全球卫生研究的基本知识与技能，熟悉全球卫生研究的主要领域、进展及面临的挑战，为我国积极参与全球卫生研究及决策提供知识储备。

This course introduces the principles of global health; presents strategies for using scientific methods and tools to understand global health; and uses case studies to contextualize implementation successes and failures. At the end of this course, students should be able to identify major challenges in global health, develop professionally the ability to conduct global health implementation, and prepare well for further global health engagement.

教学方式

1. 理论讲授：讲授全球卫生研究的定义、意义、常用理论和方法（包括实施研究），使学生熟悉全球卫生研究的主要领域和面临挑战。Theoretical teaching: To give the lectures of definition, significance, principles and methods (including implementation science) of global health study, and facilitate students being able to understand the main fields and challenges in global health study. 

2. 案例分析：选取全球卫生研究的主要领域和典型案例，使学生初步掌握全球卫生研究的常用方法和技能。Case study: To select the main fields and typical cases of global health study, and enable students to preliminarily understand the common methods and skills of global health study. 

3. 课堂讨论：在上述理论讲授和案例分析过程中，增加师生互动，使学生更加深刻理解全球卫生和全球卫生研究。Class discussion: In the process of theoretical teaching and case study, to increase teacher-student interaction to enable students to have a deeper understanding of global health as well as global health study. 

4. 课后作业训练：安排两次课后作业，训练学生从全球卫生角度理解在不同背景下如何开展全球卫生研究、如何组织和评价实施研究。Homework training: To arrange two homework assignments and train students to understand how to conduct global health study, organize and evaluate implementation science research in diverse settings and contexts from a global health perspective. 

5. 分组汇报：按照选课人数进行分组，在课程最后进行分组汇报，评估学生对全球卫生的理解、全球卫生研究设计的可行性和科学性。Group presentation: To classify students to multiple groups, and conduct a group presentation at the end of the course to evaluate their understanding of global health, scientific merit and feasibility of global health study design.

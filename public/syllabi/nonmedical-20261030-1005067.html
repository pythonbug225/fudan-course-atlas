北欧电影

2025秋 · GEEC10146.01 · 朱建新

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养学生从政治与美学两个层面深入分析北欧电影的能力，理解其如何表达社会与文化价值及历史责任。课程以电影作为承载历史与社会意义的重要媒介为出发点，聚焦1945年至今的北欧代表性影片，探讨北欧电影人如何再现并重构时代变迁中的社会文化景观。 学生将结合每个历史阶段的重要政治事件与社会文化趋势，分析其对电影创作的深远影响。为实现课程目标，将重点围绕以下三个方向展开系统探讨： a）国家在电影制作与传播中的管控与扶持机制； b）北欧电影的文化与美学特征，既包括区域性共性，也涵盖各国独有的艺术表达； c）在跨国流动与全球化语境中，北欧电影的演变与身份重构。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to equip students with the analytical tools to evaluate how Nordic films—both politically and aesthetically—articulate their social and cultural values and commitments. Grounded in the understanding of film as a medium of historical and social significance, the course focuses on cinematic works from 1945 to the present, exploring how Nordic filmmakers have represented and imagined the shifting social and cultural landscapes of their time. Students will also engage with key political events and sociocultural trends that shaped each decade and left a discernible imprint on the region’s cinematic output. To achieve these aims, the course will critically examine three core areas: a) the role of state control and public support in the production and distribution of films; b) the cultural and aesthetic characteristics of Nordic cinema, including both shared regional traits and nationally specific expressions; c) the evolution of Nordic cinema in the context of transnationalism and globalization.

教学目标

Through the course, students will be able to: --demonstrate knowledge of the major political, social, and cultural changes in the Nordic countries, especially since 1945; --identify key characteristics of Nordic cinema; --contextualize films in terms of particular auteurships, recent film history and/or contemporary social and political issues.

教学方式

This course adopts online and offline hybrid teaching mode. Students meet in classroom every other week for lectures and discussions. During the week when the class does not meet, students are required to watch the recorded PPTs and the course film and finish the assigned exercises online.

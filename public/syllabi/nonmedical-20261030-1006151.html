中国环境资源法

2025秋 · LAW70063.01 · 沈灏

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是面向中国商法（LLM）法学硕士学生的全英文选修课程。全面介绍环境法的基本内容，涵盖其基础原理、历史背景与发展脉络。学生将深入学习环境法的基本特征、核心概念、基本原则、制度框架以及法律责任体系，并探讨环境法与其他法律部门之间的联系。 课程结构分为三个主要模块：“环境法的理论与原则”、“比较环境法”以及“国际环境法与气候变化”。课程涵盖从传统的地方性环境问题到跨境乃至全球性挑战（包括气候变化）在内的广泛议题，深入探讨立法、行政、司法、社会组织与个人在应对环境问题中的作用。 尽管课程名称为“中国环境法”，但教学中将采用比较法视角，引入包括美国在内的其他国家的环境法案例，以拓展讨论深度，丰富学生的理解。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a comprehensive overview of environmental law, covering its foundational principles, historical background, and development. Students will explore the defining features, concepts, principles, core frameworks, and legal responsibilities within environmental law, as well as its connections with other legal fields. The course is organized into three main sections: [Theories and Principles of Environmental Law], [Environmental Law from a Comparative Perspective], and [International Environmental Law and Climate Change]. It addresses a wide range of environmental issues, from traditional local concerns to cross-border and global challenges, including climate change. The roles of the legislature, executive, judiciary, civil society, and individuals in addressing environmental issues are examined in depth. Although titled Chinese Environmental Law, the course adopts a comparative perspective, analyzing environmental law cases from other jurisdictions, including the United States, to enrich discussions and broaden understanding.

教学目标

This course aims to equip students with a solid theoretical and practical foundation in environmental law by systematically exploring its principles, structures, and enforcement mechanisms. Through a combination of Chinese legal frameworks and comparative perspectives, the course enables students to critically analyze key environmental challenges and legal responses at the local, national, and global levels. It also fosters an understanding of the roles of various actors—legislatures, courts, administrative agencies, civil society, and individuals—in shaping and implementing environmental law.

教学方式

The course adopts a combination of lectures, case studies, class discussions, and student presentations. Lectures provide foundational knowledge of environmental law principles and institutions. Case studies—drawn from both Chinese and international contexts—are used to illustrate how environmental laws are applied in practice. Class discussions encourage critical thinking and active engagement with contemporary environmental issues.

[Theories and Principles] Chapter 1 Environment and Environmental Problems (No readings assigned) Aim: Introduce students to fundamental concepts of the environment and key environmental challenges, laying the conceptual groundwork for environmental law. Challenges to Environmental Governance in China, Journal of Environmental Law, 2016, 28, 325–335. Aim: Analyze the theoretical foundations and principles of environmental legislation, with emphasis on the role of public participation in lawmaking.

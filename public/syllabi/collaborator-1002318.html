来源：教务系统教学大纲；2025秋 · CHIN40067.01。以下为考核与教材字段，非大纲全文。

考核构成

  • 课堂表现Participation 20.0% The assessment criteria for participation in class include active engagement in discussions, demonstrating understanding of the material, contributing thoughtful insights, asking relevant questions, collaborating effectively with peers, and showing respect for diverse viewpoints.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 20.0% The assessment criteria for an assignment encompass content accuracy and completeness, originality and creativity, clarity and coherence of expression, adherence to instructions and formatting guidelines, and proper citation of sources.
  • 汇报Presentation 20.0% The assessment criteria for a group project presentation include the clarity and organization of the presentation, the accuracy and depth of the content, the effectiveness of visual aids, the engagement and interaction with the audience, the equitable participation of all group members, and the overall professionalism and delivery.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：论文 40.0% The assessment criteria for a term paper include content relevance and accuracy, depth and breadth of analysis, logical structure with a clear introduction, well-organized body, and concise conclusion, proper citation and referencing, and adherence to formatting guidelines.

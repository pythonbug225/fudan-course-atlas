课程简介（中文）

本硕士课程通过跨学科方法探讨拉丁美洲的重要文化与社会问题，结合文学分析、文化理论与历史反思。借鉴罗兰·巴特将文化理解为一切象征性表达的观点，本课程不仅将文学视为一种艺术形式，更将其视为理解更广泛文化进程与社会意义的独特路径。课程涉及的议题包括知识分子网络、性别与作者身份、大众文化、迁移，以及文化生产的全球流通等。文学文本作为核心材料，引导学生分析小说、随笔与文化叙事如何回应社会变迁。课程特别关注拉丁美洲在全球文化动态中的位置，包括跨国流通、翻译以及区域间新兴交流。通过细读文本、课堂讨论与研究实践，学生将掌握分析工具，从而在当代与历史语境中更深入地理解西语世界及其文化发展动态。

课程简介（英文）

This course examines key cultural and social questions in Latin America through an interdisciplinary approach that combines literary analysis, cultural theory, and historical reflection. Following Roland Barthes’ understanding of culture as any symbolic expression, the course considers literature not simply as an artistic form but as a privileged way of approaching broader cultural processes and social meanings. The course addresses issues such as intellectual networks, gender and authorship, popular culture, migration, and the global circulation of cultural production. Literary texts serve as primary materials through which students explore how fiction, essays, and cultural narratives engage with social transformations. Special attention is given to Latin America’s position within global cultural dynamics, including transnational circulation, translation, and emerging exchanges across regions. Through close reading, discussion, and research activities, students will develop analytical tools that foster a broader understanding of the Spanish-speaking world and its cultural dynamics in contemporary and historical contexts.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to provide students with advanced analytical tools to examine key cultural and social issues in Latin America. The course aims to develop critical approaches to literature as a privileged site for exploring broader cultural dynamics, including intellectual networks, gender roles, popular culture, migration, and global cultural circulation. By engaging with literary texts alongside theoretical perspectives, students will strengthen their ability to interpret cultural production within its historical and social contexts while recognizing Latin America’s position in contemporary global exchanges. The course also seeks to foster independent research skills, critical discussion, and academic writing in Spanish, enabling students to analyze cultural phenomena from interdisciplinary and comparative perspectives.

教学方式

This course is organized as a seminar combining close reading of literary texts, guided discussion, and critical reflection on cultural and social issues in Latin America. Classes emphasize active student participation, collaborative analysis, and the development of independent research perspectives. the course encourages dialogue, textual analysis, and problem-oriented discussion. Literary works are approached as key materials for exploring broader cultural processes, while theoretical perspectives from Cultural Studies provide analytical frameworks for interpretation. Students will progressively develop research skills through discussion, short written assignments, and the preparation of a final research paper in Spanish.

考核构成

  • 出勤Attendance 10.0% Students are required to attend at least 75% of the classes. Absences must be properly justified according to university regulations.
  • 课堂表现Participation 10.0% Active and informed participation in class discussions is essential. Students are expected to complete the required readings in advance and contribute regularly to debates, demonstrating critical engagement with both literary texts and theoretical materials.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 10.0% Short analytical responses and research-oriented exercises submitted throughout the semester. These assignments are designed to support the development of the final research paper.
  • 课题论文Research Paper 70.0% The final assignment consists of a research paper (in Spanish) addressing a cultural and social problem related to Latin America. The paper must demonstrate theoretical grounding, close textual analysis, and independent critical argumentation.

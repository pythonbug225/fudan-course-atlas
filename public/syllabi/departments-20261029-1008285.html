金融市场和机构

2025秋 · BA70035.01 · 蒋肖虹、战昕彤

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养博士生从事金融市场与机构研究的能力。通过研讨会形式，我们将建立一个综合框架，重点分析股票市场、交易结构、机构交易以及机构投资者在价格发现与公司决策中的作用，以产出可发表于高质量学术期刊的研究成果。

课程简介（英文）

The objective of this course is to prepare doctoral students for research in financial markets and institutions. In this seminar, we will develop an integrated framework for understanding the issues in financial markets with emphasis on equity markets, market trading structures, institutional trading and the role of institutional investors in price discovery and corporate decisions, for the purpose of producing research that is publishable in high quality refereed academic journals.

教学目标

Develop an Analytical Framework: Establish an integrated approach to studying financial markets, focusing on equity markets, trading mechanisms, and institutional roles. Analyze Market Structure: Examine how trading architectures (e.g., order books, liquidity provision, high-frequency trading) impact price formation and market efficiency. Study Institutional Trading: Investigate the strategies and behaviors of institutional investors and their influence on market dynamics. Understand Price Discovery: Explore the role of institutional investors in asset pricing, information incorporation, and market efficiency. Link to Corporate Finance: Assess how institutional ownership affects corporate governance, shareholder activism, and firm decision-making. Produce Publishable Research: Guide students in developing theoretical or empirical papers meeting the standards of top finance journals (e.g., JF, RFS, JFE).

教学方式

Interactive Seminar Discussions – Students will critically analyze assigned readings, fostering debate on financial market theories and empirical findings. Research Presentations – Regular student presentations will develop communication skills and provide feedback on work-in-progress Leadership in Discussions – Active participation  will be encouraged through student-led topic discussions and Q&A engagement.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction to Finance Research Topic: Course Overview & Foundations of Financial Markets Research Objectives: Understand key research paradigms in finance Learn how to critically evaluate academic papers Develop a framework for the semester-long research project Assignment: Read & Prepare: Fama (1970, 1991), Grossman & Stiglitz (1980) Discussion Questions: What are the key differences between Fama’s (1970) and Grossman & Stiglitz’s (1980) views on market efficiency? How would you test market efficiency empirically? Week 2: Institutions and Market Efficiency Topic: Institutional Investors' Impact on Price Discovery Objectives: Analyze how institutions affect market efficiency Understand institutional trading patterns Assignment: Read & Present: Boehmer & Kelley (2009), Edelen et al. (2016), Cao et al. (2017) Discussion Prep: Identify the main hypothesis and empirical strategy of each paper Compare the findings—do they support or contradict each other? Week 3: Short Selling Topic: Limits to Arbitrage and Short-Selling Constraints Objectives: Examine short-selling mechanisms and risks Understand arbitrageurs' role in price correction Assignment: Read & Present: Ljungqvist & Qian (2016), Engelberg et al. (2018), Chen et al. (2019) Discussion Prep: What are the key frictions preventing arbitrage? How do short-selling constraints affect market efficiency? Week 4: Corporate Bond Markets Topic: Institutional Behavior in Fixed Income Markets Objectives: Analyze fire sales and liquidity spirals Understand bond fund fragility Assignment: Read & Present: Ellul et al. (2011), Goldstein et al. (2017), Jiang et al. (2022) Discussion Prep: How do institutional flows impact bond market stability? Compare liquidity dynamics in bonds vs. equities. Week 5: ESG Investing Topic: Institutional ESG Preferences Objectives: Evaluate ESG’s financial materiality Understand investor motivations for ESG Assignment: Read & Present: Hartzmark & Sussman (2019), Cao et al. (2020), Riedl & Smeets (2017) Discussion Prep: Do investors truly value ESG, or is it window dressing? How might ESG preferences distort asset prices? Week 6: Market-Corporate Finance Interface Topic: Institutional Ownership and Corporate Outcomes Objectives: Analyze institutional impact on governance Understand foreign institutional effects Assignment: Read & Present: Gaspar et al. (2005), Kacperczyk et al. (2021), Massa & Zhang (2021) Discussion Prep: How do different types of institutions influence firms differently? What are the limitations in measuring institutional impact? Weeks 7-8: Market Efficiency Foundations Topic: Evolution of Market Efficiency Theories Objectives: Trace empirical tests of market efficiency Understand the joint hypothesis problem Assignment: Read & Present: Fama & MacBeth (1973), Cowles (1933), Easley et al. (2002) Discussion Prep: How has the definition of market efficiency evolved? What are the biggest challenges in testing efficiency? Weeks 9-10: Institutional Trading Performance Topic: Trading Strategies and Execution Costs Objectives: Evaluate institutional trading skill Analyze persistence in trading costs Assignment: Read & Present: Hu et al. (2014), Anand et al. (2012), Puckett & Yan (2011) Discussion Prep: Do institutions have superior information, or are they just liquidity providers? How should we measure "trading skill"? Weeks 11-12: Liquidity Dynamics Topic: Commonality and Asset Pricing Objectives: Understand liquidity risk factors Examine liquidity provision mechanisms Assignment: Read & Present: Chordia et al. (2000), Amihud & Mendelson (1986), Pastor & Stambaugh (2003) Discussion Prep: Is liquidity risk priced? If so, why? How does commonality in liquidity arise? Weeks 13-14: Market Microstructure Topic: Block Trading and Price Impact Objectives: Analyze upstairs/downstairs markets Measure permanent vs. temporary impact Assignment: Read & Present: Madhavan & Cheng (1997), Conrad et al. (2003), Chiyachantana et al. (2017) Discussion Prep: Why do block trades have asymmetric price impacts? How do trading venues affect execution costs? Week 15: Investor Sentiment Topic: Noise Trader Models Objectives: Understand sentiment indicators Analyze limits to arbitrage Assignment: Read & Present: DeLong et al. (1990), Baker & Wurgler (2006), Lee et al. (1991) Discussion Prep: Can sentiment be measured reliably? How do noise traders affect market efficiency? Week 16: ETFs and Market Structure Topic: ETF Effects on Underlying Securities Objectives: Evaluate ETF informational role Analyze liquidity spillovers Assignment: Read & Present: Huang et al. (2021), Ben-David et al. (2018), Agarwal et al. (2017) Discussion Prep: Do ETFs improve or harm price discovery? How do ETFs affect volatility and liquidity? Week 17: Emerging Topics Topic: Common Ownership Networks Objectives: Examine anti-competitive concerns Analyze network effects Assignment: Read & Present: Azar et al. (2018), Koch et al. (2021), He et al. (2019) Discussion Prep: Does common ownership reduce competition? How should regulators respond? Week 18: Research Presentations Topic: Final Paper Workshop Objectives: Present original research Incorporate peer feedback Assignment: Prepare: 20-minute presentation of final paper

课程简介（英文）

Advanced Russian (II) is a compulsory course for third-year undergraduate students majoring in Russian. Its instruction is based on the premise that students have completed Basic Russian (I and II), Intermediate Russian (Parts I and II), and Advanced Russian (I). Since 2015, the College of Foreign Languages and Literature has implemented extensive reforms in undergraduate foreign language education in accordance with the requirements of Fudan University. The fundamental goal of these reforms is to shift the focus from purely language skill training in the upper grades to texts with intellectual depth and cultural significance, emphasizing the cultivation of students' critical thinking and logical expression abilities. The Russian Department actively aligned with the college's teaching reforms by making significant adjustments to the undergraduate curriculum and class hours. This textbook was compiled by Li Xinmei, Associate Professor in the Russian Department of the College of Foreign Languages and Literature at Fudan University, along with two Russian foreign teachers from the department, N.V. Andryushchenko and L.G. Yun, for the second semester of the third undergraduate year. Designed for a weekly schedule of four class hours, the textbook selects eight Russian classic short and medium-length novels as the focus of intensive reading and detailed analysis. These literary masterpieces span various historical periods, cover diverse themes, and exhibit distinct linguistic features of their respective eras as well as the unique stylistic characteristics of the authors. Through the intensive study of these literary works, students can not only enhance their language proficiency but also gain a deep appreciation of different authors' writing styles and acquire a thorough understanding of Russian cultural and national knowledge from ancient to modern times. This semester consists of 14 teaching weeks, during which six literary works in their original language are selected for study. Each literary work is covered over two weeks (eight class hours). The teaching adopts a blended online and offline approach, with each unit focusing on the in-depth study of one literary masterpiece while incorporating extensive training in fundamental language skills. Each unit is planned to include six hours of offline instruction and two hours of online instruction.

教学目标

1.继续转换学生的学习方式，培养学生高度的自学能力 通过前半学年的引导和训练，学生已经具备了较好的自学能力。课前能够按照教师要求进行预习，课堂上能掌握大部分教师讲解的内容，课后能够及时复习并归纳总结课堂上讲过的重点和难点等。学生也善于自己借助于词典、网络等辅助学习手段获得自己需要的知识。下半学年，教师将继续引导学生转变学习方式，继续培养学生的自学能力和独立工作能力。 2.强化训练学生的理解力和思辨能力 本学期使用教师从文学经典作品中精选的原著文本作为精读课材料，旨在促使学生阅读文本、深入理解文本，并针对文学文本发表自己的看法和观点，同时用正确的俄语表达自己的思想。 3．继续培养学生自由熟练运用现代俄语的能力 1）通过对文学原著的仔细研读和学习，掌握并熟练运用新旧词汇和言语表达模式，广泛深入了解俄罗斯语言、文化、文学、国情等知识； 2）通过课堂上对重点语言现象的精讲精练，以及课后的口头和笔头作业，巩固并灵活运用新旧词汇和各种言语表达手段； 3）通过课堂上组织学生围绕原著内容进行口头讨论，训练学生的思辨能力和俄语口头表达能力。思想和观点的能力。

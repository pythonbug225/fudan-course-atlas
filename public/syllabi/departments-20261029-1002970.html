课程简介（中文）

本课程为本科劳动经济学选修课。本课程主要教授学生基础的劳动经济学理论，包括劳动供给和需求，人口流动理论，歧视理论等等。在此基础上，本课程还将通过介绍领域内前沿的研究论文来培养学生的研究兴趣。

课程简介（英文）

Labor economists study labor supply, labor demand, minimum wages, taxes and transfers, immigration, human capital, education production, inequality, discrimination, unions and strikes, and unemployment. We will focus on applying applied microeconomics theory to the empirical data analysis.

教学目标

This is an introductory level of labor economics. Our objectives include: 1. Learn the basic applied microeconomics theory in labor economics; 2. Learn how to read academic papers; 3. Learn how to present academic papers.

教学方式

In class lectures

教学内容与进度安排

The schedule is tentative and subject to change. Introduction Labor Supply Labor Demand Labor Market Equilibrium and Wages Compensation Education Home production Labor Mobility Discrimination Unemployment

教学参考资料

Borjas, George J., and Jan C. Van Ours. Labor economics. Boston: McGraw-Hill/Irwin, 2010.

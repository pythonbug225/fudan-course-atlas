课程简介（中文）

本课程是为基础医学、临床医学、预防医学和药学各专业研究生开设的公共基础课。由于分子生物学理论与技术的发展十分迅速，新技术新方法层出不穷，本课程将根据学科的发展，介绍新的知识与技术。课程主要内容包括分子生物学基本理论的简要概述，分子生物学常用技术的原理及其应用，以及各类常用分子生物学实验。通过课堂的讲授和实验的实际操作，使学生在掌握常用分子生物学技术在医学领域的应用的同时，熟悉掌握实验设计、实验方法和实验结果分析，培养学生发现问题和解决问题的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a fundamental course for graduate students majoring in basic medicine, clinical medicine, preventive medicine, and pharmacy. Due to the rapid development of molecular biology theories and technologies, with new techniques and methods constantly emerging, this course will introduce updated knowledge and technologies in line with the advancement of the discipline. The main content of the course includes a concise overview of the fundamental theories of molecular biology, the principles and applications of commonly used molecular biology techniques, and various common molecular biology experiments. Through classroom lectures and hands-on experimental practice, students will not only master the application of commonly used molecular biology techniques in the medical field but also become proficient in experimental design, methodologies, and result analysis, thereby cultivating their ability to identify and solve problems.

教学目标

主要目标是让学生了解分子生物学基本理论与进展，掌握分子生物学常用技术及部分前沿技术的原理及应用，熟练掌握常用分子生物学实验的操作和结果分析，通过理解和掌握分子生物学技术操作，学会在医学生物学研究中设计技术路线和实验方案，培养学生的科研能力。

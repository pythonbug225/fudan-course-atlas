课程简介（中文）

《法医生物学》研究生课程主要讲授法医遗传学的研究基础及前沿进展，通过讲授DNA测序技术基础及新方法新技术、DNA鉴定技术、案例现场分析、法医物证研究前沿进展等内容，帮助学生掌握法医遗传学目前的研究方法、了解法医遗传学目前的研究热点和研究难点，以及法医物证学实践中常遇到的问题，为学生进入相关研究领域奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

The postgraduate course Forensic Biology mainly covers the fundamental theories and cutting-edge advances in forensic genetics. By delivering lectures on topics including the fundamentals, novel methodologies and emerging technologies of DNA sequencing, DNA identification techniques, crime scene case analysis, and frontier research progress in forensic physical evidence, the course helps postgraduate students master the current research methods of forensic genetics, understand its prevailing research hotspots and challenges, as well as the common problems encountered in the practice of forensic physical evidence. This lays a solid foundation for students to embark on relevant research fields.

教学目标

通过本课程学习，让学生掌握DNA测序技术基础及新方法新技术、DNA鉴定技术、案例现场分析方法、法医物证研究前沿进展，了解法医物证的发展方向，为学生进入科学研究奠定基础。同时通过课程教授，融入思政元素教学，让学生了解法医物证学的发展历史。

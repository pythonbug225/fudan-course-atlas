课程简介（中文）

本课程探讨契约理论的核心议题，包括：合谋、官僚与俘获；分权与集权背景下的合谋研究；无转移支付的机制设计与委托模型；主观评价；关系合约与执行；组织内的竞争与合作；专业知识；以及对多边契约理论的探索。

课程简介（英文）

This course explores key topics in the theory of contracts, including collusion, bureaucracy and capture; collusion and decentralization versus centralization; mechanism design without transfers and delegation models; subjective evaluation; relational contracts and enforcement; competition versus cooperation in organizations; expertise; and toward a theory of multilateral contracting.

教学目标

How to design institutions in order to provide good incentives for economic agents is a central question of economics today. This course explores key topics in the theory of contracts, including collusion, bureaucracy and capture; collusion and decentralization versus centralization; mechanism design without transfers/delegation models; subjective evaluation; relational contracts/enforcement; competition versus cooperation in organizations; expertise; and toward a theory of multilateral contracting. This course aims to provide the methodological tools needed to design such institutions and contracts, helping students, researchers, and others who might find themselves pondering what contracts—and the incentives they embody—are really all about.

课程简介（中文）

本课程以“考试”为透镜，深入探讨考试制度与中国社会结构、文化传统、政治治理及现代转型之间的复杂互动。课程将融合考试学基本理论、历史比较、社会学分析与政治经济学视角，重点解析科举制度的历史遗产、高考改革的当代争议，以及考试在形塑社会流动、教育公平与国家认同中的作用。通过跨学科研讨，学生将理解考试不仅是教育测量工具，更是中国政治合法性塑造、文化统合、价值塑造、知识普及、经济效益增殖的关键机制。（本学期将延迟开始，具体联系高教所教务）

课程简介（英文）

This course uses "examinations" as a lens to delve into the intricate interplay between the examination system and China's social structure, cultural traditions, political governance, and modern transformation. Integrating fundamental theories of examinology, historical comparisons, sociological analysis, and political-economic perspectives, the course focuses on analyzing the historical legacy of the imperial examination system, contemporary controversies surrounding gaokao reform, and the role of examinations in shaping social mobility, educational equity, and national identity. Through interdisciplinary seminars, students will come to understand that examinations are not merely tools for educational assessment but also a critical mechanism for shaping political legitimacy, cultural integration, value formation, knowledge dissemination, and economic benefit amplification in China. 教学目标

掌握考试研究的基本理论与方法，能够批判性分析考试制度的社会功能。 梳理中国考试制度从科举到现代高考的大体脉络，理解其制度变迁的社会动力。 剖析考试改革背后的政治逻辑与社会争议。 运用社会学视角分析考试与政治、经济、社会的关系，以及未来面临的挑战。

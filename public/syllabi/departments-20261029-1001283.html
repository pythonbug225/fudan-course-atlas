房地产金融

2025秋 · BA30056.01 · 战昕彤

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍商业、工业及住宅地产市场，涵盖抵押贷款类型（固定/浮动利率）、估值方法及融资渠道分析。学生将学习二级市场运作、证券化结构及杠杆效应，并通过案例研究掌握房地产投资评估与决策技能。课程注重培养市场分析、投资建议及团队协作能力，结合实践项目强化逻辑思维与专业演示技巧，为房地产金融与投资领域的职业发展奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to real estate markets, covering commercial, industrial, and residential properties. Students will analyze mortgage types (FRM, ARM) and financing options, apply valuation techniques, and evaluate investment opportunities. Key topics include secondary market dynamics, securitization, and the role of leverage. Through case studies and projects, learners will develop analytical, decision-making, and presentation skills while gaining practical experience in real estate investment analysis. Emphasis is placed on critical thinking, teamwork, and communication to prepare students for careers in real estate finance and investment.

教学目标

Value Differentiate between commercial, industrial, and residential real estate Identify mortgage types (e.g., FRM, ARM) and explain their key features Apply standard investment valuation methods to real estate Knowledge Calculate payments for FRMs/ARMs and evaluate mortgage choices Analyze major secondary market players and their functions Explain securitization’s structure and role in real estate markets Compare commercial financing sources and assess leverage impact Ability Conduct real estate investment analysis and provide recommendations Develop critical skills: Creative problem-solving Self-directed learning Leadership and teamwork Analytical and communication skills Professional presentation techniques

教学方式

Instruction will be the main teaching method of the course. Student will also contribute to the class and larn by doing case analysis and group presentation.

教学内容与进度安排

1: Introduction to Real Estate Finance 2: Mathematics of Real Estate Finance and Mortgage Instruments: Fixed Rate Mortgages (part 1) 3: Mortgage Instruments: Fixed Rate Mortgages (part 2) 4: Mortgage Instruments: Adjustable-Rate Mortgages (part 1) Individual assignment 5: Mortgage Instruments: Adjustable-Rate Mortgages (part 2) 6: Incremental Borrowing Cost, Loan Refinancing 7: Mid-term exam (1.5 hours, closed-book) 8: Residential Mortgage Banking, Mortgage Origination and Underwriting 9: Real Estate Market and the Global Financial System 10: Introduction to Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) 11: Introduction to Commercial Real Estate Development 12: CRE Valuation and Investment Analysis at the Micro-Level 13: Property Cash Flows Proforma 14: Real Estate Proforma Modeling (Monthly Cash Flow) Scenario Analysis 15: Portfolio Theory and Asset Price Theory 16: Group Presentation 17-18: Final exam (2 hours, closed-book)

课程简介（中文）

This is a graduate level course. It is assumed that the students know Numerical Linear Algebra at a graduate level. 在科学与工程建模中，常需数值求解线性方程组Ax=b。此类问题广泛存在于航空航天、机器学习等领域，且多为包含大量零元素的大型稀疏方程组。传统直接法（如LU分解）虽能获得精确解，但会产生新的非零元导致存储困难。迭代法通过近似求解避免此问题，具有更高存储效率，已成为MATLAB等科学计算软件的核心工具，能有效解决关键工程问题。

课程简介（英文）

This is a graduate level course. It is assumed that the students know Numerical Linear Algebra at a graduate level. Numerical solutions for linear systems Ax=b are essential in science and engineering. Large, sparse systems frequently arise in fields like aerospace, machine learning, and signal processing. While direct methods (e.g., LU decomposition) provide exact solutions, they often require significant memory by creating new non-zero entries. Iterative methods offer an efficient alternative by computing approximate solutions without generating new non-zero elements. These memory-efficient techniques are widely adopted in scientific computing and are embedded in software like MATLAB, proving crucial for solving large-scale problems.

教学目标

The focus of this course is on modern iterative solvers for large linear systems of equations using MATLAB. This will allow students to immediately test and understand the basic constructs of iterative solvers and apply them to solve important linear systems arising in science and engineering problems modelling. At the end of the course the students will know how to solve such problems using the MATLAB software.

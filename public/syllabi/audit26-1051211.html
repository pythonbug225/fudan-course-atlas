课程简介（中文）

自改革开放以来，随着中国经济的高速发展与中国金融市场的不断发展与完善，中国金融系统正在世界经济中扮演越来越重要的角色。因此，外界了解和理解中国金融体系的运作变得愈发重要。本课程将概述中国金融体系整体框架，详解公司治理、银行与影子银行、货币、汇率、债市、股市、房地产及金融科技，并展望未来发展方向，全面呈现中国金融体系的历史、现状与未来。

课程简介（英文）

Over the past four decades, along with rapid economic growth and the continuous sound development of its financial markets, China has been playing an increasingly important role in the global economy. It is therefore more important than ever for the outside world to understand how China’s financial system operates. This course provides an overview of the overall framework of China’s financial system, explains in detail corporate governance, banking and shadow banking, currency, exchange rate, bond market, stock market, real estate and fintech, and looks into its future development, offering a comprehensive picture of its history, current status and prospects.

教学目标

The course objective is to understand the role and mechanism design provided by the financial system in China’s economic development recently, as well as the investment opportunities and risk presented by the system to the outside world. Finally, it will provide us a future trend on the direction of its financial development by overviewing and analyzing the history and current situation of Chinese Finance System，and find out the research areas by research proposal related to China’s Financial Markets.

教学方式

This course adopts a student-centered, interactive, and research-oriented teaching model to achieve its learning objectives. The main teaching methods include:

（1）Lectures with Case Studies：In-class lectures systematically explain the framework, institutions, policies, and mechanisms of China’s financial system, combined with real‑world cases, data, and recent developments to enhance understanding.

（2）Interactive Q&A and Classroom Discussions：Regular in-class quizzes, open questions, and topic discussions encourage active participation, critical thinking, and in-depth analysis of financial issues.

（3）Group Work & Presentations：Students conduct small-group research on selected topics related to China’s finance, prepare presentations, and share findings in class to improve communication and teamwork.

（4）Research Proposal Guidance：Instructor provides step-by-step guidance on academic writing, literature review, and research design to support students in completing their final research proposal.

（5）Reading & Material Analysis：Students read assigned chapters, research papers, and policy documents before class, then analyze and reflect in class to strengthen independent learning.

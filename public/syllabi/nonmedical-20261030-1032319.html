课程简介（中文）

半导体光电子学是研究半导体中光子和电子相互作用、光和电相互转换的一门科学，涉及量子物理、固体物理、半导体物理、半导体材料、半导体器件等多门内容，是一个综合性的交叉学科，在信息和能源领域有着广泛应用，同时在学术界也是一个前沿交叉的热门研究领域。本课程是一个导论性课程，主要学习半导体材料、半导体器件中的光子和电子相互作用、光和电的相互转换，以及半导体光电子器件在光电集成中的应用以及相关科学前沿。

课程简介（英文）

Semiconductor Optoelectronics is a discipline that studies the interactions between photons and electrons, as well as the mutual conversion between light and electricity in semiconductors. It covers a wide range of fields including quantum physics, solid-state physics, semiconductor physics, semiconductor materials and semiconductor devices, making it a comprehensive interdisciplinary subject. It is widely applied in the information and energy sectors, and also represents a cutting-edge and popular interdisciplinary research area in academia. This course serves as an introductory one, which mainly focuses on the interactions between photons and electrons and the mutual conversion between light and electricity in semiconductor materials and devices, the applications of semiconductor optoelectronic devices in optoelectronic integration, and relevant cutting-edge scientific research.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，使学生了解半导体光电子学的研究内容和基本特征，以及在集成电路中的应用方向，激发学生对半导体光电子学的兴趣，主动课后深入学习掌握相关知识，培养解决问题的能力，对未来工作和学习能够有所帮助。

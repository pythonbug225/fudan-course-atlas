1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 法医学是应用医学、生物学及其他自然科学的理论和技术，研究并解决法律实践中有关医学问题的一门科学，其可为刑事侦查提供线索，为司法公正提供科学依据。本课程聚焦与临床医学紧密相关的法医学知识，涵盖法医病理学、法医遗传学、法医毒理学及法医临床学。教学形式包括专题讲座、案例教学及专题研讨等。成绩评定依据课堂表现与期末考试综合评定。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Forensic medicine is an interdisciplinary course between medicine and forensic science, which applies theories and technologies of medicine, biology and other natural sciences to resolve forensic problems related with injuries. It can provide clues to criminal investigation, scientific evidence for justice and civil meditation. The course concentrates on the contents related tightly with clinical medicine, including forensic pathology, forensic genetics, forensic toxicology, and clinical forensic medicine. The forms of teaching include cases-based teaching, demonstrations, lectures and panel discussions. The score is based on the classroom performance and a final theory examination.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

The objective of this curriculum is to help students to understand basic theory and technique of forensic medicine (e.g., forensic pathology, forensic genetics, forensic toxicology), to let students to get familiar with activities that may be involved in medico-legal investigation process, and, if necessary, acquire the ability to help the departments concerned to solve the forensic issues in the judicial proceedings as a physician expert. Moreover, this curriculum can help students to prevent medical errors and malpractice, so as to raise the level of medical technology and medical service quality in future health-care work.

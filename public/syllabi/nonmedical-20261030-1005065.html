英国文学史(上)

2025秋 · FORE20003.01 · 苏耕欣

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

这门课程阅读从中世纪到18世纪末的主要英国作家的主要文学作品，考查其在相关历史、政治、哲学和宗教背景下的意义，并使学生在阅读过程中体验英国历史进程中发生的思想冲突与观点对抗。这门课程并不要求机械性的记忆和术语背诵，而主要关注文学作品所蕴含的思想。

课程简介（英文）

This course reads the major literary works by major English authors from the Middle Ages to the end of the 18th century in their respective historical, political, philosophical and religious contexts, with the purpose that students will uncover and hopefully experience the clash of ideas and confrontation of opinions in the course of English history. Time constraints limit our close reading to a few excerpts of the works. Successful completion of this course does not entail mechanical memorizing of names and terms or a synopsis of any particular work, but a grip on the ideas that underlie the literary works.

教学目标

这门课程旨在阅读从中世纪晚期到18世纪末的英国文学主要作品之节选，讲解这一时期的重要历史、宗教、哲学和政治事件与发展，以此帮助学生在阅读过程中体验英国历史进程中发生的思想冲突与观点对抗，使学生了解文学发展历史，熟悉文学史涉及的重要思想、流派和主要问题。

教学方式

堂授

教学内容与进度安排

第1周: Overview of English history and literature, introduction to the Middle Ages Read Norton pp.3-18, 238-242 第2周: Canterbury Tales—General Prologue (part), The Wife of Bath’s Tale Prologue Read Norton pp.243-248 (for general info), 282-300 (more closely) 第3周: English Reformation and Elizabeth I’s cult of love Read Norton pp. 531-562, 677-684, 749-750, 754-756, 763-766 第4周: Spenser: Faerie Queene Read Norton pp. 766-777, 781-795 (Canto I), 795-800 (but as many verses of Canto II as you can read) 第5周: Fall Break, no class 第6周: Sidney: The Defense of Poesy; Astrophil and Stella Read Norton pp. 1037-1038, 1044-1045, 1048-1060; 1084-1096 (only printed verses) 第7周：Shakespeare: Hamlet Read Norton pp.1066-1071; Hamlet Iv., II.i.555-571, III.i, III.iii 第8周：17th century: intro, civil war, Metaphysical poets, Donne; Cavalier poets, Crashaw, Herrick and Lovelace Read Norton pp.1341-1364, 1370-1373, 1385, 1414, 1740-41, 1752-1755, 1756, 1762, 1779 第9周: Milton: Paradise Lost: I, III Read Norton pp. 1364-1367; 1897-1950, 1987-1995. If you have time, continue to read for next 第周. 第10周：Paradise Lost IV, IX Read Norton pp. 2003-2014, 2091-2099, 2103-2116, Puritan Hostility to the Theatre 第11周: Restoration: neo-classicism, Dryden: Mac Flecknoe and Hobbes: Leviathan Read Norton pp. 1855-1867, 2177--2199, 2208-2242 第12周：Locke: Treatise on Government; and Bunyan: Pilgrim’s Progress Read Norton pp. 2269-2278, 3014-3018 第13周：Glorious Revolution & Whig domination; Swift Read Norton pp.2464-2466, 2487-2488, 2505-2512, 2573-2581 第14周：Age of Pope: “Essay on Man” Read Norton pp. 2665-2688, 2714-2721 第15周：Rise of the middle class and the novel; Pamela Read Norton pp. 2199-2201, Pamela: a selection; Ian Watt selection 第16周: Age of Johnson: Rasselas；“Preface to Shakespeare” Read Norton pp. 2201-2205, 2841-2843, 2856, 2870-2872, 2881-2882, 2913-2914, 2936-2945. Read Rasselas for ideas, not for plot.

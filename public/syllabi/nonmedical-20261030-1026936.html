课程简介（英文）

During the process from embryonic development to birth, growth, aging, and even death, organisms form a set of traits including morphological characteristics, functions, behaviors, and patterns of molecular composition. These encompass physical phenotypes such as external morphology and organ size, chemical phenotypes such as proteins and metabolites in vivo, as well as biological phenotypes such as cardiopulmonary function and psychological behavior. Comprehensive and precise measurement of various phenotypes is the foundation for the systematic and refined dissection of phenotypes, the construction of phenotypic networks, and the clarification of inter-phenotypic relationships. This course introduces the principles, measurements, and analytical methods specifically for constitutional (or physiological) phenotypes, imaging phenotypes, functional phenotypes, cellular phenotypes, immunophenotypes, and molecular phenotypes.

教学目标

人类表型组研究是人类基因组计划完成后新的战略制高点。基因和环境相互作用决定人体特征，人类全部特征的集合即是表型组。开展人类表型组研究，系统解析表型组与基因组的关联，发现人类健康和疾病等表型特征形成的内在规律和生物标志物，将有助于实现疾病预防，提出针对性的健康维护方案，推进生命科学和医学的进步。本课程的教学目的是通过向学生系统和深入介绍人类表型组的各方面，使得学生认识人类各种表型及其表型间的内在联系，从而更好地帮助理解我们自身。

教学方式

讲解为主，结合课堂讨论和人类表型组研究院现场参观。

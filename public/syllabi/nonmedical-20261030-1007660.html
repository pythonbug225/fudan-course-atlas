Studies of China’s Foreign and Security Policies

2025秋 · POLI70018.01 · 吴心伯

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在探讨中国外交与安全政策的理论依据、发展动态及主要特征。其目标是帮助学生掌握中国国家安全战略的知识与分析方法。本课程分为三个部分：第一部分探讨中国不断演变的世界观与安全观念、国家安全利益、大战略以及决策过程；第二部分讨论中国对台湾问题的处理方式、与美国的关系、区域合作、全球治理及软实力；第三部分则深入分析中国的安全政策。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to examine the rationale, dynamics, and main features of China’s foreign and security policies. Its objective is to help students obtain the knowledge of and master the approach to China’s national security strategy. The course has three parts: part one deals with China’s evolving world views and security concepts, its national security interests, grand strategy, as well as the decision-making process; part two discusses China’s approaches to the Taiwan issue, relations with the United States, regional cooperation, global governance, and soft power; part three explores China’s security policy.

教学目标

This course is designed to examine the rationale, dynamics, and main features of China’s foreign and security policies. Its objective is to help students obtain the knowledge of and master the approach to China’s national security strategy. Students are trained to understand the formation of China’s foreign and security policies, to learn what resources to access in deciphering the official positions, and to develop a framework to analyze China’s policies and behaviors.

教学方式

This is mainly a lecture-based course. Instructor will give lecture for about one hour for each class, while students are encouraged to make presentations on related topics for about 15 -20 minutes, leaving the rest of time for class discission. Toward the end of the semester, instructor will engage students in roundtable discussion about their final papers.

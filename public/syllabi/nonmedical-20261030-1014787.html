课程简介（中文）

本课程采用互动体验式的学习方式，4天的课程教学中将使用8个丰富的模拟现实的谈判练习，从简单的双方单事项谈判到团队多事项的谈判，从理性谈判到心理层面影响力的应用，覆盖了索取价值、创造价值、冲突管理等内容。从而帮助EMBA学员真正掌握所学的谈判、沟通、信息分享、创造价值等技巧和艺术并能在以后工作中加以熟练运用。

课程简介（英文）

This course adopts an interactive experiential learning approach. During the 4-day course, 8 rich simulated reality negotiation exercises will be used, covering topics such as simple single item negotiations between both parties, team multi item negotiations, rational negotiations, and the application of psychological influence. The exercises will cover topics such as value seeking, value creation, and conflict management. So as to help EMBA students truly master the skills and arts of negotiation, communication, information sharing, value creation, etc. they have learned, and be able to proficiently apply them in their future work.

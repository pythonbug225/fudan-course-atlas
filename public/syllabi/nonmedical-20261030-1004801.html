课程简介（中文）

《艺术哲学与审美问题》是一门注重引导学生形成“问题意识”与“学术体验”的通识教育核心课程，并努力融“理论性”与“实践性”，“讨论性”与“体验性”为一体。 通过围绕“艺术在人类生活中的位置”、“艺术作品之存在”、“艺术与审美经验”和“艺术批评”等四大方面问题的讲解，并通过指导学生初步阅读中西方著名艺术哲学、美学经典著作片断，让学生在理论上掌握东西方艺术哲学不同特点、不同看法和不同观点的同时，学会独立思考“艺术哲学与审美问题”和独立鉴赏“艺术和艺术之美”的方法，包括立场和步骤等等。

课程简介（英文）

Philosophy of Art and Aesthetic Issues is a core liberal arts course designed to cultivate students' "awareness of philosophical problems" and "academic engagement." It strives to integrate "theoretical depth" with "practical application," and "discursive inquiry" with "experiential learning." The course centers on four key thematic areas: the role of art in human life, the mode of being of artworks, the relationship between art and aesthetic experience, and art criticism. Through guided introductory readings of selected passages from renowned Eastern and Western philosophical and aesthetic classics, students will acquire theoretical understanding of the distinctive characteristics, perspectives, and viewpoints within different traditions of art philosophy. Simultaneously, they will develop methods—including specific stances and procedures—for independent critical thinking about philosophical and aesthetic issues, as well as for the autonomous appreciation of art and artistic beauty.

教学目标

本课程试图在与学生进行平等的、开放的和互动的对话中，通过梳理一系列关于艺术哲学和审美问题的基本立场和不同观点，在增进学生艺术哲学理论知识和涵养学生艺术鉴赏素养的同时，一是培养起学生独立思考“艺术哲学与审美问题”的思想能力，二是培养起学生独立鉴赏“艺术和艺术之美”的实践能力。

教学方式

教师讲授。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是面向汉语水平达到hsk 5、6级的外国学生开设的一门选修课。本课程旨在提高学生的语言技能，包括听、说、读、写，以及词汇扩展、复杂句子和语法。 本课程适合世界各地的长期汉语学习者使用。每本教科书分为十课。通过对每篇课文的关键词和语言点的学习，理解课文的中心思想和段落的大意，然后通过一系列的综合练习巩固学习。旨在培养学习者听、说、读、写的语言技能，培养汉语语言能力和汉语交际能力。

课程简介（英文）

The content of this course includes, newspaper materials, magazines and contemporary articles in the past 2 decades. Common language phenomena in life, including allegorical sayings, idioms, etc. Some intermediate and advanced grammars will be involved in the content.

教学目标

This course is an optional Comprehensive course aimed at foreign students whose Mandarin proficiency is about HSK-5 or 6. This course is designed to improve students’ language skills including listening, speaking, reading and writing, along with vocabulary extension, complicated sentences and grammars. This course can be used by long-term Chinese learners from all over the world. Each textbook is divided into ten lessons. Through the study of the key words and language points of each text, understand the central idea of the text and the general idea of the paragraphs, and then consolidate learning through a series of comprehensive exercises. It aims at developing learners' language skills in listening, speaking, reading and writing, and cultivating Chinese language ability and Chinese communication ability.

教学方式

1.Using pictures to introduce key words 2.Listen to the dialogue and understand the meaning of new words 3.Using new words to make sentences 4.Read and retell the text 5.Practice new words and write short essays

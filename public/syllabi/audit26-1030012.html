课程简介（中文）

Pharmacology is a discipline that studies the interaction of drugs with organisms (including pathogens). It consists of pharmacodynamics (exploring the mechanisms of drug actions on organisms) and pharmacokinetics (studying on the changes of drugs under the influence of organism). The main objectives of pharmacology include: elucidating the effect and mechanism of drug, providing theoretical basis for rational use of drugs and preventing adverse reactions of drugs in clinic, research and develop new drugs, providing important scientific basis and research methods for other life science research.

教学目标

By studying the interaction and the law of action between drugs and body, the students can master the basic action, mechanism and adverse reaction of common drugs in clinic, so as to provide theoretical basis for the rational use of drugs in clinic, the best effect of drugs and the prevention and treatment of adverse reactions. What is more important is to develop studentsundefined ability of active learning, problem finding, problem analysis and problem solving. It lays a good foundation for its cultivation as a qualified doctor.

课程简介（中文）

本课程为环境工程专业研究生必选课程，旨在帮助学生深入理解实验和工程研究过程中的现象和数据，强化和提升学生对实际研究过程的理论认识。本课程基于环境水处理工程、大气污染过程、固体废弃物处理工程、污染与生态修复工程等涉及的技术和理论原理，结合环境工程全周期的实际需求，讲授环境工程原理基础（重点阐述环境工程学的基本概念和理论。物料与能量守恒原理、流体动力学和质量传递过程等）、分离过程原理（沉淀、过滤、吸收、吸附、离子交换、膜分离的基本原理）和反应工程原理（化学和生物反应动力学、各类反应器的过程解析与基本设计理论等），对环境工程过程的基本原理进行深入剖析，并为环境工程实践中工艺选择、反应器及工程设计提供理论依据。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a compulsory course for postgraduate students majoring in environmental engineering. It aims to help students deeply understand the phenomena and data in the experimental and engineering research processes, and strengthen their theoretical understanding of the actual research process. This course is based on the technologies and theoretical principles involved in environmental water treatment engineering, air pollution processes, solid waste treatment engineering, pollution and ecological restoration engineering, etc. It combines the actual needs of the entire cycle of environmental engineering. This course teaches the fundamentals of environmental engineering principles (with a focus on elaborating the basic concepts and theories of environmental engineering), principles of materials and energy conservation (basic principles of sedimentation, filtration, absorption, adsorption, ion exchange and membrane separation, principles of separation processes (basic principles of sedimentation, filtration, absorption, adsorption, ion exchange, and membrane separation), and principles of reaction engineering (chemical and biological reaction kinetics, process analysis and basic design theories of various reactors, etc.). This course conducts an in-depth analysis of the fundamental principles in environmental engineering processes and provides theoretical basis for process selection, reactors and engineering design in environmental engineering practice.

教学目标

将基本科学原理与实际工程应用相互结合，系统、深入地学习环境净化与污染控制工程的基本技术原理、工程设计计算的基本理论以及分析问题和解决问题的方法论，为后续的专业课程学习和解决实际工程问题打下良好的基础。

课程简介（中文）

本课程为来华留学生（研究生阶段）开设，旨在帮助留学生系统了解中国的自然环境，历史发展，经济建设，思想信仰，文化传统等知识。增强学生对中国的正确认识，提升跨文化理解能力，树立正确的中国观。

课程简介（英文）

In order to let international students studying in China better understand China， this course gives a comprehensive, systematic and objective introduction to the basic content and knowledge of China and Chinese culture. The guide principle for the course is to reflect comprehensively “Chinese thought”, ”Chinese experience” and the great achievements in contemporary China’s economic and social development. The purpose of our course is to help students systematically and comprehensively understand the history and current situation of China's geography, history, culture, economy and other aspects.

教学目标

This course aims to help foreign students to have thorough background knowledge about China in history, philosophy, economy, education, social life and other aspects so as to enable them improve their ability to adapt to China and their communication skill.

教学方式

Thematic lectures, classroom discussion. and online learning materials

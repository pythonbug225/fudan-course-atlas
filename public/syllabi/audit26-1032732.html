课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍健康保险的基本理论和应用实践。通过课程学习，聚焦有关健康保险的体系、保险筹资、支付、监管和评价，以及国内外健康保险体系概况，系统掌握健康保险学有关的基本理论和研究方法，在此基础上围绕我国近期深化医药卫生体制改革中有关重点领域进行专题研讨，深化对有关理论方法的应用。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the fundamental theories and practical applications of health insurance. Through the course, students will focus on the health insurance system, insurance financing, payment, regulation, and evaluation, as well as an overview of domestic and international health insurance systems. They will systematically master the basic theories and research methods related to health insurance. Based on this, special topic discussions will be conducted on key areas related to China's recent efforts to deepen the reform of the medical and health system, aiming to deepen the application of relevant theoretical methods.

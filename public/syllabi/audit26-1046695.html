课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在介绍话语分析的基本概念和研究方法，帮助学生理解语言在社会和文化背景中的作用。课程涵盖主要理论框架，如结构主义和社会语言学，探讨文本与语境之间的关系，以及定性和定量分析方法的应用。通过案例研究和小组讨论，学生将提升批判性思维与分析能力，为其在教育、政治和媒体等领域的应用奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the basic concepts and research methods of discourse analysis, helping students understand how language functions within social and cultural contexts. It covers major theoretical frameworks, including structuralism and sociolinguistics, and examines the relationship between text and context, as well as the application of qualitative and quantitative analytical methods. Through case studies and group discussions, students will develop their critical thinking and analytical skills, laying a solid foundation for applying discourse analysis in fields such as education, politics, and the media.

教学目标

•    provide a comprehensive overview of the major approaches to and methodological tools used in discourse analysis;
•    introduce both traditional perspectives on the analysis of texts and talk as well as more recent approaches that address technologically mediated and multimodal discourse;
•    incorporate practical examples using real data;
•    include articles from key authors in the field, including Jan Blommaert, William Labov, Paul Baker, Ron Scollon and Suzie Wong Scollon.

教学方式

教师授课、课堂讨论和书面提交作业、课堂小组报告

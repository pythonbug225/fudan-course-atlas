课程简介（英文）

Students are encouraged to apply the foundational skills acquired during “Introduction to English Writing” in Year-1, including description, narration, argumentation, and more. This will enable them to delve into more sophisticated styles and genres of writing. “Multi-genre Writing I” is structured into three main sections: review, travel writing, and features. Each of these sections can be further subdivided into various sub-genres.

教学目标

The primary goal of this module is to acquaint Year-2 students with the fundamental aspects of multiple genres, encompassing their structure, authorship, readership, and requisites. Students will be guided towards acquiring essential skills for effectively articulating their thoughts, observations, and arguments using appropriate forms of writing.

教学方式

Lecture, group discussion, presentation, after-class writing.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Module introduction - Genres. Roundtable conversation: What do you want from me (and from the module)? Week 2: Book reviews I – structure, ideas, close reading, critique. Week 3: Book reviews II Week 4: Film reviews – director’s message, performance, scripts, visual/audial presentation, market reception Week 5: Game reviews – what can we read from games, and how can we write about it? Worldbuilding, utopia, imagining alternatives. Task – write a review of any kind. (30%) Week 6: Peer-reviewing Day I Week 7: Travel writing – landscape What can we learn from place and space? Week 8: Travel writing – seascape Isolation and mobility Week 9: Travel writing – society Politics of space and roadmap. Week 10: Travel writing – urban locality Observing Shanghai – multiculturalism, gentrification, migrant workers Task: complete a piece of travel writing (30%) Week 11: Peer reviewing Day II Week 12: News writing – from news to features. Week 13: Interviews – questions, structures, biases Week 14: Feature writing I Week 15: Feature writing II Task: Complete a piece of feature writing (30%) Week 16: Peer-reviewing Day III

课程简介（中文）

本课程基于“方法+应用”的理念，教授大数据商业分析中关键的数据分析方法及实际应用。课程首先介绍大数据的核心概念与关键考量因素。在商业智能分析的背景下，课程探讨运用关键商业分析技术（如机器学习、深度学习和大模型）解决核心管理问题，并穿插指标构建、指标选择和模型选择等内容。课程还涵盖文本分析和大型语言模型等高级内容的导论。

课程简介（英文）

This course teaches the key data analysis methods and practical applications used in Big Data business analytics, using a 'Method + Application' approach. The course begins with an introduction to the concepts and key considerations of big data. In the context of business intelligence analytics, the course discusses the use of key business analytics techniques (e.g. machine learning, deep learning and big models) to address key management issues, interspersed with basic techniques for metric construction, selection and model selection. The course also provides an introduction to higher-level content such as text analytics and LLMs.

教学目标

Students will learn to: Describe relevant concepts of big data business analysis (including data science, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, classification, predictive model, etc.) Apply data visualization methods to present and analyze business data Utilize classic application algorithms of big data for analysis in various industries and business domains (such as decision trees, random forests, predictive analytics, etc.) Utilize deep learning and large language models (LLMs) approaches for analysis in various industries and business domains Identify management issues and management practices applied in business analysis

教学方式

Overall organization: complete teaching (offline) Design of learning activities based on comprehensive active learning: Classroom sessions Lecture: To provide concepts and knowledge of the course content. Class Discussion: Encourage classroom interaction and discussion between students and instructor. Group discussion: Through the analysis and discussion of actual enterprise cases, master the mode and method of enterprise big data analysis. Case discussion: Through the discussion and learning of actual enterprise cases, students deeply understand the business analysis of big data and the corresponding management rules and methods. Video Presentation: Through some relevant video materials, students will be able to arouse their enthusiasm and understanding of the course. After class session Homework: Students are required to complete the assigned homework after class to consolidate what they have learned.

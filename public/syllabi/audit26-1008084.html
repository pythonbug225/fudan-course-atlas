课程简介（中文）

光催化作为一种十分独特的催化反应体系被公认为廉价、节能且清洁的催化反应，在环境治理（如大气污染物治理、水体污染处置、生活场景中的有害气体和VOCs降解等）、有害微生物消杀、自清洁涂涂料、能源转化（氢能、CO2转化）等领域的应用受到广泛关注。作为高效、廉价的光催化剂，二氧化钛纳米材料无论是产业界还是基础研究领域都被广泛研究。因此，本课程从光催化研究意义出发，先讲述什么是光催化反应，并介绍光催化的特点、优势以及挑战。然后以纳米二氧化钛作为例子，详细介绍该材料的组成、结构和物化特性；进一步介绍纳米二氧化钛合成方法、表征手段、组成-结构-理化性质关系（骨架掺杂、晶面控制、颗粒大小和形貌、表面改性、与其它纳米材料复合），深入介绍各种典型的二氧化钛基光催化纳米材料的具体光催化应用实例，阐明材料的构-效关系。

课程简介（英文）

Photocatalysis, as a highly unigue catalytic reaction system, is widely recognized as aninexpensive, energy-saving and clean catalytic reaction. Its applications in environmentalgovernance (such as the treatment of air pollutants, water pollution disposal, and the degradation of harmful gases and VOCs in daily life scenarios), the elimination of harmfulmicroorganisms, self-cleaning coatings, and energy conversion (hydrogen production, CO2 conversion) have received extensive attention, As an efficient and inexpensive photocatalyst.titanium dioxide nanomaterials have been widely studied both in the industrial sector and inbasic research. Therefore, starting from the significance of photocatalysis research, this coursefirst explains what a photocatalytic reaction is, and introduces the characteristics, advantagesand challenges of photocatalysis. Then, using nano-titanium dioxide as an example, thecomposition, structure and physicochemical properties of the material are described in detail;.Furthermore, we will introduce the synthesis method, characterization means, composition - structure -physicochemical property relationship (framework doping, crystal plane control, particle sizeand morphology, surface modification, and combination with other nanomaterials) of nano-titanium dioxide, elaborate on the specific photocatalytic application examples of varioustypical titanium dioxide-based photocatalytic nanomaterials, and clarify the structure-activityrelationship of the materials.

教学目标

通过本课程，使学生较系统地掌握光催化基本理论、概念、发展历程、研究/产业现状以及未来发展趋势。本课程使学生了解和基本掌握无机光催化剂的合成方法、合成原理以及所得到光催化材料的相关表征手段及其原理。通过本课程学习，学生们不仅对于光催化相关研究有了较深刻的认识，而且对于太阳能的利用、环境治理与保护、光催化有机合成、光催化能源转化等等有了实际深刻认识。

课程简介（中文）

《神经生物学实验技术和方法》是神经科学领域的核心专业实验技术课程。本课程旨在系统培养学生的科研思维，使其熟练掌握从分子、细胞到整体动物水平的多维度神经科学研究方法，提升独立开展前沿课题的实验设计与操作能力。

本课程以“在体-离体”双轨并进为教学主线，全面覆盖现代神经生物学研究的核心技术：

  • 在体神经科学研究模块：系统讲授脑立体定位注射及切片技术，结合光遗传学在神经科学中的应用，实现对特定脑区神经环路的功能操控；通过膜片钳电生理分析技术精准记录神经元电活动，并利用动物行为学检测对脑功能进行分析，建立从细胞功能到整体行为的完整研究链条。同时，辅以免疫组织化学和免疫荧光染色技术进行形态学解析，运用Real-time qPCR方法、常用蛋白质生化检测方法及HPLC分析技术，深入探讨脑内基因表达、蛋白变化及神经递质系统的动态特征。此外，课程还引入流式细胞仪技术在医学生物学中的应用，支持脑内不同类型细胞的精准分选与后续研究。

  • 离体模型与前沿技术模块：课程全面升级了传统细胞培养内容，重点聚焦人脑类器官的培养技术，使学生掌握构建复杂三维神经发育与疾病模型的前沿方法。同时，课程引入秀丽线虫模式生物在神经科学中的应用，帮助学生理解如何利用简单模式生物解析复杂的神经机制。

在教学模式上，本课程坚持“理论-实践-示教”三位一体，将技术原理的深度讲解与学生动手实操、精密仪器（如膜片钳、流式细胞仪等）的现场示教紧密结合。通过这一多维度的课程体系，学生不仅能建立起扎实的技术功底，更能紧跟神经科学领域的最新研究范式，为其未来的科研探索与学术创新奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

Neurobiology Experimental Techniques and Methods​ is a core, specialized laboratory course in the field of neuroscience. Designed to systematically cultivate students’ scientific research mindset, this course enables learners to master multi-dimensional research methodologies spanning molecular, cellular, and whole-animal levels, while enhancing their capacity for independent experimental design and execution in cutting-edge research projects.

The curriculum follows a dual-track teaching framework integrating in vivo​ and in vitro​ approaches, comprehensively covering the core techniques of modern neurobiological research:

In Vivo Research Module:​ This module systematically covers stereotaxic injection and brain slicing techniques. It incorporates optogenetics to achieve functional manipulation of specific neural circuits within targeted brain regions. Precise neuronal activity recording is conducted via patch-clamp electrophysiology, complemented by behavioral assays to analyze brain function, thereby establishing a complete research pipeline from cellular function to integrated behavior. Furthermore, morphological analysis is supported by immunohistochemistry and immunofluorescence staining. Real-time qPCR, standard protein biochemical assays, and HPLC are employed to investigate gene expression, protein dynamics, and neurotransmitter system alterations in the brain. Additionally, the application of flow cytometry in biomedical research is introduced to facilitate the precise sorting and subsequent analysis of distinct neural cell types.

In Vitro Modeling and Frontier Techniques Module:​ Moving beyond traditional cell culture, this module focuses on advanced human brain organoid culture techniques, equipping students with the skills to construct sophisticated three-dimensional models for studying neural development and disease pathogenesis. The module also introduces the application of the model organism Caenorhabditis elegans(C. elegans), helping students understand how simple model systems can be utilized to dissect complex neurological mechanisms.

Regarding pedagogy, the course adopts an integrated "Theory-Practice-Demonstration" model. This approach combines in-depth lectures on technical principles with hands-on student practice and live demonstrations of sophisticated instrumentation (e.g., patch-clamp setups, flow cytometers). Through this multidimensional curriculum, students not only build a solid foundation in essential techniques but also stay abreast of the latest research paradigms in neuroscience, laying a robust groundwork for future scientific exploration and academic innovation.

教学目标

本课程专为缺乏神经科学实验背景的学生设计，致力于通过系统的原理讲解与技能训练，使学生全面了解并掌握现代神经科学研究的主流技术体系。课程内容注重技术与科学的结合，不仅要求学生掌握脑立体定位、电生理、分子生化及人脑类器官培养等关键技术操作，更强调引导学生理解各项技术在解析神经科学问题中的合理应用与局限性，从而为其后续开展高水平的论文研究提供扎实的方法学支撑。

教学方式

本课程采用理论讲授、精密仪器示教与分组动手操作相结合的多元化教学模式，系统介绍神经生物学关键实验技术原理与应用。学生将实践脑立体定位注射、Westernblot、RNA抽提、脑片免疫组织化学染色、形态学观察等常用实验技术，并利用这些学习到的实验技术设计实验，通过这一全流程训练，全面提升科研实操能力与科学思维。

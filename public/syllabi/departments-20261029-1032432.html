智能仿真

2026春 · MSE70018.01 · 吕甲梦

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍智能仿真与在线学习的基本概念、理论方法及其在智能决策中的应用。涵盖蒙特卡洛方法、方差缩减、排序择优、多臂赌博机、上下文赌博机、样本平均近似、梯度估计、随机优化等关键技术，帮助学生掌握该领域重要内容与前沿的研究方法。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a systematic introduction to the fundamental concepts, theoretical methods, and applications of intelligent simulation and online learning in artificial intelligence. It covers key techniques such as Monte Carlo methods, variance reduction, ranking and selection, multi-armed bandits, contextual bandits, sample average approximation, gradient estimation, and stochastic optimization, equipping students with essential knowledge and cutting-edge research methodologies in this field.

教学目标

知识维度： 系统掌握智能仿真、在线学习与优化的基本概念、核心算法架构及其在典型场景中的应用逻辑。 方法维度： 能够熟练运用概率论与随机过程工具，对智能仿真与在线优化中的不确定性问题进行数学建模与推导。 技能维度： 具备基本的算法实现能力，能够利用主流编程语言（如 Python）复现仿真与在线学习的经典算法，并完成性能调试。 前沿维度： 紧跟领域学术前沿，能够独立检索、阅读并评述相关高水平论文，具备识别研究热点与潜在研究方向的素养。

教学方式

上课讲课为主，通过课后理论作业与实践作业巩固课上内容，并通过前沿论文汇报的方式了解最新研究动向。

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction & Motivation of Simulation and Online Learning Week 2: Probability Foundations and Concentration Inequalities Week 3: Monte Carlo Methods Week 4: Variance Reduction Techniques Week 5: Ranking and Selection (R&S) and Sample Average Approximation (SAA) Week 6: Stochastic Approximation (SA) and Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) Week 7: Multi-Armed Bandits I: Greedy, $\epsilon$-Greedy, and ETC Week 8: Multi-Armed Bandits II: Elimination-based Algorithms, UCB Week 9: Multi-Armed Bandits III: Thompson Sampling Week 10:  Multi-Armed Bandits IV: Adversarial Bandits Week 11: Linear Bandits: LinUCB Week 12: MNL Bandits Week 13: Non-stationary Bandits: Sliding Window and Restarting Week 14: Non-stationary Simulation Optimization and Adaptive SAA Week 15: Presentations I Week 16: Presentations II

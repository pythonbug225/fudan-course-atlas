课程简介（中文）

通过《毒理学方法》这门课程，讲者与研究生将共同探讨分子生物学、组学、系统毒理学、替代毒理学（如类器官、模式生物）以及人群毒理学等领域的最新理念与技术革新。

如何有效地利用技术更新确实是一门艺术，值得深入学习和探索。技术的飞速发展为我们提供了前所未有的工具和方法，但关键在于如何将这些技术与研究目标无缝结合，以最大化其价值。这不仅需要对技术本身有深刻的理解，还需要具备清晰的科学思维和创新能力，能够根据具体问题灵活选择、优化和整合技术手段。

课程简介（英文）

Through the course "Toxicological Methods," professors and graduate students will jointly explore the latest concepts and technological innovations in molecular biology, omics, systems toxicology, alternative toxicology (such as organoids and model organisms), and population toxicology.

How to effectively utilize technological updates is indeed an art, worthy of in-depth learning and exploration. The rapid advancement of technology provides us with unprecedented tools and methods, but the key lies in how to seamlessly integrate these technologies with research objectives to maximize their value. This requires not only a profound understanding of the technology itself but also clear scientific thinking and innovative capabilities to flexibly select, optimize, and integrate technological approaches based on specific problems.

教学目标

课程旨在帮助研究生把握毒理学科学前沿，掌握如何合理且高效地运用各类先进技术，从多维度、多层次、逻辑严谨的角度解决科学问题，从而培养其独立科研能力与创新思维。

The course aims to help students grasp the forefront of toxicological science and master the rational and effective use of various advanced technologies to address scientific problems from multiple dimensions, hierarchical levels, and a logically rigorous perspective, thereby cultivating their independent research capabilities and innovative thinking.

教学方式

本课程以讲授式为主，不定时穿插讨论，最后进行课堂讨论和期末汇报。

This course is primarily lecture-based, with intermittent discussions interspersed throughout. It will culminate in a classroom discussion and a final presentation.

1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 临床肿瘤免疫学课程引入了对治疗癌症的各个领域的先进技术，这些课程能够紧密地将基础科学与临床实践联系起来。本课程以细胞与分子免疫学为基础， 以免疫与 肿瘤 的关系为主线 。 其目的是通过临床教学，为学生提供理论与实际相结合的平台，使学生掌握 肿瘤发生的机制与机理，对肿瘤的预防、发生、诊断以及治疗有更深刻地了解，锻炼学生的临床与科研思维。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The implementation of basic immunology of clinical oncology has introduced the advanced of technology in various areas in fighting cancer which such classes are capable of tightly bridging basic sciences to clinical practices. This course is based on cellular and molecular immunology and focuses on the relationship between immunity and tumor. Its purpose is to provide a platform for students to combine theory with practice through clinical teaching, so that students can master the mechanism and mechanism of tumor occurrence, have a deeper understanding of the prevention, occurrence, diagnosis and treatment of tumor, and exercise students' clinical and scientific thinking .

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

(1)价值引领 ：引导学生深刻理解肿瘤免疫学在“健康中国”战略中的重要意义，树立投身科研、攻克疾病的责任感与使命感，在案例探讨中培育医学伦理与人文关怀精神。 (2) 知识传授：系统传授免疫系统识别、应答及调控肿瘤的核心理论与前沿进展，使学生扎实掌握免疫监视、免疫编辑、免疫治疗（如检查点抑制剂、CAR-T等）的细胞与分子机制。 (3) 能力培养：着力培养学生批判性评估科研文献、解析复杂临床案例的能力，并激发其将基础免疫学知识转化为临床应用思维的创新潜力，为未来从事前沿生物医学研究或临床工作奠定坚实基础。 (1) Value Cultivation: Inspire students to appreciate the significance of tumor immunology in advancing national health initiatives, fostering a sense of duty and ethical responsibility in scientific research and clinical practice. (2) Knowledge Delivery: Provide a comprehensive understanding of fundamental principles and cutting-edge advances in how the immune system recognizes and eliminates tumors, including mechanisms of immune surveillance, evasion, and modern immunotherapies. (3) Capacity Building: Develop students' abilities to critically analyze scientific literature and complex clinical data, and to bridge the gap between basic immunology and translational medicine, thereby preparing them for future leadership in biomedical research or clinical oncology.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

（1）第1- 11周引入PBL和CBL教学方法，鼓励和引导学生主动学习。 （2）第 12 ~ 1 3 周，带领学生开展临床实习学习，为晚期肿瘤免疫治疗患者进行会诊和体格检查，关注其诊疗过程和实验室结果。 第13周会 参观复旦大学附属浦东医院“肿瘤治疗性疫苗转化研究中心”和“肿瘤 特色 治疗中心”，了解自身免疫细胞的采集、培养、冷冻保存和复苏的全过程，以及实验室中免疫治疗的检测方法。 1.The first 11 weeks , PBL and CBL teaching methods are introduced to encourage and guide students to take the initiative in learning. 2.12 to 13 weeks , leading the students to carry out clinical practice learning, conducted consultation and physical examination for patients with advanced tumor immunotherapy, and paid attention to their diagnosis and treatment process and laboratory results. In the week 13 leading the students to visit the "Translational Research Center of Tumor Therapeutic Vaccine" and "Tumor Special Treatment Center" of Pudong Hospital affiliated to Fudan University to understand the whole process of collection, culture, freezing storage and resuscitation of autoimmune cells, as well as the detection methods of immunotherapy in the laboratory.

社会分层与流动

2026春 · SOCI30026.01 · 杜世超、李煜

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程以对社会分层和流动的基本概念和理论为基础，对分层流动的实质性过程和重要后果进行系统性的介绍和分析，帮助学生对分层流动的过程和后果以及当前中国社会分层状况、原因及其发展趋势有所理解。 课程分四个部分，第一部分以社会分层的成因和机制为核心，介绍分层与流动的理论框架、测量和中国分层与流程的进程与状况。第二部分侧重从分层过程的动态视角，介绍家庭养育、教育获得、劳动力市场等不平等的再生产模式。第三部分关于分层不平等后果的专题研究，包括婚姻匹配、阶层认同、生活方式等内容。最后对社会分层格局的未来趋势进行讨论。

课程简介（英文）

This course is based on the basic concepts and theories of social stratification and mobility, systematically introducing and analyzing the substantive process and significant consequences of stratification and mobility, helping students understand the process and consequences of stratification and mobility, as well as the current situation, causes and development trends of social stratification in China. The course is divided into four parts. The first part focuses on the causes and mechanisms of social stratification, introducing the theoretical framework of stratification and mobility, measurement, and the progress and status of stratification and processes in China. The second part focuses on introducing unequal reproduction models such as family upbringing, access to education, and the labor market from a dynamic perspective of the stratification process. The third part is a special study on the consequences of stratified inequality, including marriage matching, class identity, lifestyle and other contents. Finally, the future trends of the social stratification pattern are discussed.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习 （１）掌握有关社会分层和流动的基本概念、分析框架及重要理论； （２）熟悉社会分层流动研究中的基本分析方法和技术； （3）了解社会分层的基本过程和后果的重要成果和结论 （4）了解有关转型期中国社会分层和流动的研究进展

教学方式

课堂讲授为主，学⽣参与（pre）

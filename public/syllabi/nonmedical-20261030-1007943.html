Portfolio Theory and Behavioral Investing

2025秋 · MF60021.01 · 杨春柳

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course covers the basic concepts and models of modern portfolio theory, such as utility functions, asset allocation, the Capital Asset Pricing Model and multi-factor pricing models. It also reviews the key propositions of behavioral finance to help students understand behavioral biases and irrational behavior in financial markets. The focus of the course is on the applications of portfolio theory and behavioral finance for real-world portfolio management. Topics on the current practice of the mutual fund industry is also covered.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students are expected to: Understand the concepts and definitions in portfolio theory and behavioral finance; Demonstrate skills required to apply theories to issues of real-world portfolio management.

教学方式

Lectures, homework, and exam.

教学内容与进度安排

课程简介（中文）

全球治理课程分为理论和实证两部分，倡导“理论导向的实证研究”（Theory-Guided Empirical Studies）。在理论部分，课程将讨论全球治理研究的主要中层理论；在实证部分，课程将以研讨会的形式来讨论全球治理中层理论在实证研究中的应用，努力做到“学以致用”。

课程简介（英文）

The Global Governance course has two parts, namely “Theoretical part + Empirical part”.

 

Theoretically, We will introduce three new institutionalisms in Global Governance, including Rational Choice Institutionalism (RCI), Sociological Institutionalism (SI) and Historical Institutionalism (HI). The three new institutionalisms represent three different ways of thinking of international institutions and global governance, namely economics, sociology and political science. In the class, we will discuss many related middle-level theories, including transaction cost theory, rational design theory, socialization theory, social network theory, critical juncture theory and gradual institutional change theory.

 

Empirically, We will try to use these middle-level theories to explain the variations in the global security, economic, environmental and social governance, which is consisted of joint lectures provided by the professors from three top Asian Universities, namely Keio University in Japan, Yonsei University in South Korea and Fudan University in China.

教学目标

Generally, the objective of the course is to get students as interested and even excited about studying the global governance as I am. Global Governance is a fascinating and extremely important inter-disciplinary research area, and I hope to help students understand its significance and complexities.

Specially, the course is to expose students to a broad range of theories and how to apply these theories in global governance:  (1) try to help students understand the theories of global governance; (2) try to apply these theories to do the theory-guided empirical studies.

教学方式

Professor Lecture+ Students Group Discussion+Students Global Governance workshop

质性研究方法

课程简介（中文）

本课程将介绍质性研究在社会科学研究中的运用。本课程首先从认知论、研究伦理和研究设计使学生了解质性研究方法的基本要点。再结合质性数据收集方式 (interviews, participant observation, focus groups)，通过课堂教授、课堂讨论和实操使学生熟悉质性数据的收集方法的使用，操作过程的细节，以及初步质性数据分析的技巧。

课程简介（英文）

Scholars engaged in qualitative research face a number of choices – from the epistemological assumptions they take with them, to the kinds of justification they deploy to the larger discipline, among others. But even a cursory look at recent work shows that there is not a correspondence between those identified as “qualitative scholars” and those choices, not to mention there are modes of research that are difficult to qualify as “quantitative” or “qualitative.” So rather than try to define and codify “the qualitative” (and perhaps then offer a toolbox that goes with it), this course is designed as an admittedly incomplete field guide to the choices involved in the craft of qualitative research, or, a course in Qualitative Methodology (as opposed to a course in “qualitative methods”). The course is organized around four stages, or four moments in the process of research when we face choices: Epistemology (How do we know what we know?), Design (How do structure the inquiry to know what we want to know), Methods (What do we actually do to get to know what we want to know?), and Justification (How do we present and justify what we know as valid?). The course will be oriented around the specificity of those questions to qualitative modes of research but the themes are generally applicable to a wide range of research modes.

教学目标

通过学习本课程，学生能够： 了解研究设计中的认识论 区分、辨别质性研究和量性研究的设计原理 审视质性研究中的伦理问题 提出符合质性研究的研究问题 熟悉质性研究过程的具体操作 熟悉质性数据的收集过程 了解并实践质性数据的分析方法

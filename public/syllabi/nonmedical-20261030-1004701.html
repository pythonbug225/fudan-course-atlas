广告与社会

2025秋 · JCM50091.01 · 谢静、楚亚杰

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程从广告与社会关系角度，分析广告实践及其影响。 本课程分为四大板块： 一、广告图像分析：介绍图像分析的主要理论和方法，分析广告的视觉说服技术及影响。 二、广告中的文化：介绍戈夫曼、威廉斯等有关广告问题的代表性研究，理解广告与社会文化的意义互嵌。 三、广告中的美学：介绍广告当中的重要审美范畴（魅力、可爱、有趣、噱头等），从审美心理角度理解广告传播中常用的传播策略。 四、商业传播生态：讨论知识经济视角下广告行业的兴起与变动、广告服务的竞争与共存、广告与数据增值等问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course analyzes advertising practice and its impact from the perspective of the relationship between advertising and society. This course is divided into four modules: 1、 Advertising image analysis: introduce the main theories and methods of image analysis, and analyze the visual persuasion technology and influence of advertising. 2、 Culture in Advertising: introduce representative studies on advertising issues such as Goffman and Williams, and understand the embedded meaning of advertising and social culture. 3、 Aesthetics in Advertising: introduce the important aesthetic categories in advertising (charm, cute, interesting, gimmick, etc.), and understand the commonly used communication strategies in advertising communication from the perspective of aesthetic psychology. 4、 Business communication ecology: discuss the rise and change of the advertising industry, the competition and coexistence of advertising services, and the value-added of advertising and data from the perspective of knowledge economy.

教学目标

本课程旨在介绍与“广告与社会”相关的理论知识， 培养批判性理解广告传播影响社会的能力， 提升批判性分析广告传播实践的技能与素质， 激发学生在实践中反思、在反思中实践， 体现复旦新闻学院培养“兼具理论深度与实践能力的领军者”的目标。

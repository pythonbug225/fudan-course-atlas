1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程内容以“常见肿瘤为核心、少见类型为补充、前沿进展为延伸、临床实践为支撑”展开，具体分为理论教学与临床实践两大模块： （一）理论教学 按“总论-各论-交叉领域”逻辑划分，覆盖妇科肿瘤全谱系，结合“基础要点+研究进展”双维度讲解。总论章节介绍妇科恶性肿瘤研究进展概述，梳理领域发展脉络、核心诊疗技术演进、当前研究热点与挑战。各论包含宫颈癌专题，涵盖宫颈癌的研究进展、保育治疗、少见恶性肿瘤、复发宫颈癌的诊疗与预后管理。子宫肿瘤专题包括子宫内膜癌的研究进展、保育治疗、子宫肉瘤的病理分类、诊疗难点与进展。卵巢肿瘤专题覆盖卵巢上皮性恶性肿瘤、性索间质肿瘤、生殖细胞肿瘤、复发卵巢癌的治疗策略。其他妇科肿瘤专题介绍外阴癌与阴道癌、滋养细胞肿瘤、高危型妊娠相关滋养细胞肿瘤。此外还涉及妇科肿瘤的交叉与前沿领域包括介绍妇科肿瘤遗传学、子宫内膜异位症和子宫腺肌症恶变的相关内容。 （二）临床实践 以“多学科协作（MDT）”为核心形式，帮助学生衔接理论与临床。通过观摩妇科肿瘤MDT团队（妇科、病理科、影像科、肿瘤科等）对复杂病例（如晚期卵巢癌、复发宫颈癌）的诊疗讨论，学习病例汇报逻辑、多学科意见整合方法、临床决策制定过程；课后可结合示教病例回顾理论知识，深化理解。 教学周数16周，理论课32学时，见习4学时。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The content of this course is developed around the core of "common tumors, supplemented by rare types, extended with cutting-edge advances, and supported by clinical practice", and is specifically divided into two major modules: theoretical teaching and clinical practice. (I) Theoretical Teaching It is structured according to the logic of "General Theory - Specific Topics - Interdisciplinary Fields", covering the entire spectrum of gynecological tumors, with explanations integrating the dual dimensions of "basic key points + research progress". The General Theory chapter introduces an overview of research advances in gynecological malignancies, sorting out the development context of the field, the evolution of core diagnosis and treatment technologies, as well as current research hotspots and challenges. The Specific Topics include a special section on cervical cancer, covering research progress, fertility-preserving treatment, rare malignant tumors, and diagnosis, treatment and prognosis management of recurrent cervical cancer. The special section on uterine tumors includes research progress and fertility-preserving treatment of endometrial cancer, pathological classification of uterine sarcomas, and key and difficult issues as well as advances in diagnosis and treatment. The special section on ovarian tumors covers epithelial ovarian malignancies, sex cord-stromal tumors, germ cell tumors, and treatment strategies for recurrent ovarian cancer. The special section on other gynecological tumors introduces vulvar and vaginal cancer, gestational trophoblastic neoplasms, and high-risk pregnancy-related gestational trophoblastic neoplasms. In addition, it involves interdisciplinary and cutting-edge fields of gynecological tumors, including gynecological tumor genetics, and content related to the malignant transformation of endometriosis and adenomyosis. (II) Clinical Practice Centered on the core form of "Multidisciplinary Team (MDT) collaboration", it helps students connect theory with clinical practice. By observing the diagnosis and treatment discussions of complex cases (such as advanced ovarian cancer and recurrent cervical cancer) by the gynecological oncology MDT team (including gynecology, pathology, radiology, oncology, etc.), students will learn the logic of case reporting, methods for integrating multidisciplinary opinions, and the process of clinical decision-making. After class, students can review theoretical knowledge in combination with demonstration cases to deepen their understanding. The course lasts for 16 teaching weeks, including 32 class hours of theoretical courses and 4 class hours of clinical internships.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

本课程聚焦妇科肿瘤领域的临床核心要点与前沿研究进展，旨在帮助学生构建系统的妇科肿瘤知识体系，实现“理论认知-临床应用-前沿视野”的三维能力提升。通过系统知识的介绍，使学生全面了解宫颈癌、子宫内膜癌、卵巢肿瘤、外阴阴道癌、滋养细胞肿瘤等常见及少见妇科肿瘤的发病机制、临床诊断标准、治疗原则（含保育治疗）、复发管理策略，以及妇科肿瘤遗传学、子宫内膜异位症恶变等交叉领域的核心知识，熟悉国际通用的FIGO分期标准与 NCCN诊疗指南。通过理论学习理解诊疗逻辑，培养学生将理论知识与临床实践结合的能力。参加临床示教（MDT 会议、病例分析）可以熟悉妇科肿瘤病例的分析思路，初步具备识别典型妇科肿瘤临床特征、判断治疗方向的基础能力，为后续临床实习与专科学习奠定基础。本课程将引导学生关注妇科肿瘤领域的研究动态与未解决问题，培养科学思维与创新意识；通过临床实践中对患者需求、多学科协作（MDT）模式的接触，树立以患者为中心的诊疗理念，强化医学人文素养与团队协作意识。

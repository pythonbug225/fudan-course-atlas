人工智能

2025秋 · ICE60004.01 · 徐丰

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

近年来，人工智能技术在深度学习理论的发展下得到快速推动，与各个学科产生交叉并成为各领域研究前沿，同时人工智能技术产生的应用已逐渐开始影响我们的生活，它是研究、开发用于模拟、延伸和扩展人的智能的理论、方法、技术及应用系统的一门新的技术科学。该领域的研究包括机器视觉、语言识别、自然语言处理和专家系统等。电子信息类各专业旨在培养具有良好的数理基础，具有初步的研究能力和一定的解决工程技术问题的实际工作能力，具有前沿思维和初步前沿知识的高水平复合型人才。本课程的开设结合当前科技热点，探索认知以深度学习为基础的人工智能前沿研究方向和激发学生对当前科技热点研究兴趣为目的，能有效补充目前培养方案中前沿课程缺乏的问题。此外，当前业界对于人工智能技术人才需求很大，通过对该选修课的学习获得初步知识，可有效拓展的就业面。

课程简介（英文）

In recent years, driven by advancements in deep learning theory, artificial intelligence (AI) technology has rapidly progressed. It has intersected with various disciplines and become a research frontier across multiple fields. Simultaneously, applications stemming from AI technology have gradually begun to impact our lives. AI is a new technological science that studies, develops, and applies theories, methods, technologies, and systems to simulate, extend, and expand human intelligence. Research in this field includes machine vision, speech recognition, natural language processing, and expert systems. Programs in electronic information disciplines aim to cultivate high-level, versatile talent with a strong foundation in mathematics and physics, preliminary research capabilities, practical skills to solve engineering problems, forward-thinking perspectives, and basic knowledge of cutting-edge developments. The establishment of this course combines current technological trends to explore frontier AI research directions based on deep learning and stimulate students' interest in these hot topics. It effectively addresses the lack of advanced courses in current curricula. Furthermore, as industry demand for AI technical talent is substantial, gaining foundational knowledge through this elective course can significantly broaden employment opportunities.

教学目标

通过本课程学习，了解深度学习等最新人工智能技术进展，掌握机器学习、神经网络、深度学习、人工智能相关理论基础，学会深度神经网络主流工具箱的使用和典型应用的开发，探索认知人工智能等前沿研究方向，激发人工智能研究兴趣。

教学方式

理论讲授、作业题、实验课题等。

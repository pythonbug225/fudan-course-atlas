课程简介（英文）

The course aims to give students an overview of the financial markets and an understanding of the theory and practice of monetary policy. It takes both an empirical and historical perspective to analyze the development of money and the financial system, as well as the past practices of relevant policies. Such discussions will also be supported by graphic demonstrations of simple models without too much mathematics involved. In the first part of the course, we start by an overall discussion of the role played by the financial system as a vital part in global economies, and why it is under close regulation of the government. We then move on to discuss different financial instruments and intermediaries, and how they have been developed for smoother functioning of the financial system as a hole. The second part of the course focuses on money, interest rates and policies. We shall first see the history of different forms of money, and the role of money in modern economies, with discussions regarding different theories regarding the demand for money. This will be followed by discussions regarding interest rates, which are the price of funding, including the relationship between interest rates and asset prices as well as the risk and term structures of interest rates. Then we will discuss the generation of money supply as a cooperation between central banks and commercial banks, as well as monetary/fiscal policies and their potential influences on the economy. Finally, the discussions of the policies will be set in the framework of the IS-LM and AS-AD models, where a more structural analysis will be performed on the economy as a whole. After taking this course, students are expected to have a basic understanding of how the financial system helps to make the flow of funds in an economy more fluent. In addition, they will also gain the ability to analyze the potential outcomes of monetary/fiscal policies in a systematic manner.

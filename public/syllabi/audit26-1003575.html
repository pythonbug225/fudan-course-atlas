课程简介（中文）

本课程为基于基础、临床、人文医学各学科整合式病例进行的研讨型课程，学生8-9人一个小组进行学习。每个病例通过三次讨论课（6学时）、一次线上文献学习（3学时）和一次总结课（2学时）来完成，每个学期学习3个病例。

课程简介（英文）

PBLII mainly involves diseases of different systems, which are composed of 3 cases. Each case is arranged for three tutorials and one case wrap-up. Each case is composed of discussion and self-learning for 9 academic hours and case wrap-up for 2 academic hours. Meanwhile, the students are provided adequate time to search materials, deduce and prepare for discussion. Small group discussion (8-9 students) is adopted.

教学目标

Objectives of the course is to foster student-centered learning, upholds lifelong learning, prominence on comprehension, in-depth learning and constructivist approach, arguments self-learning, better understanding and adeptness, reinforce interpersonal skills and teamwork and self-motivated attitude.

教学方式

PBLII mainly involves diseases of different systems, which are composed of 3 cases. Small group discussion (8-9 students) is adopted. The course is student-centered learning, which allows for the development of students’ skills and attributes.

Quantitative Research Methods

2025秋 · MANA40003.01 · 张平

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

这是一门面向硕士层次公共管理/公共政策专业的定量研究方法入门课程（需要时会增加一些高级材料）。本课程为学生提供了发展可应用于公共管理和政策研究问题的定量分析技能的机会。我们将讨论用于收集定量证据以及分析定量数据的方法。课程主题将包括抽样、描述性统计、概率分布、置信区间、假设检验、均值比较、普通最小二乘法、处理多元回归基本假设违反问题以及逻辑回归模型。课程将主要学习和实践用于统计分析的Stata软件。

课程简介（英文）

This is an introductory course (will add some advanced materials when needed) on quantitative research methods for public administration/public policy at the master level. This course provides students an opportunity to develop quantitative analysis skills that can be applied to public management and policy research questions. We will discuss the methods used to collect quantitative evidence and to analyze quantitative data. Topics will include sampling, descriptive statistics, probability distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, comparison of means, ordinary least squares, dealing with violations of the underlying assumptions of multiple regression, and the logit model. I will also provide an introduction to Stata software for statistical analysis.

教学目标

This course will help students to identify and propose questions of analysis that are pertinent to the contemporary study of public policy and public administration, to identify the most appropriate methodological techniques for analysis given a research question and available data, as well as to identify and offer resolution to various problems encountered during quantitative analysis. We also expect students to conduct data analyses using the methodologies covered in the course and manage data and conduct analyses using Stata.

教学方式

Lectures & Stata Practice.

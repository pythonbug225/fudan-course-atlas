课程简介（中文）

本课程是面向护理学专业学位研究生的专业选修课，同时也可以面向护理学全日制本科学生的本研融通课。本课程结合国内外康复护理学最新进展，从我国康复护理专科临床能力提升需求出发，融合复旦大学附属华山医院康复科优势的专业课程。主要内容包括康复护理学概述、康复护理评定、康复护理技术、神经系统疾病康复护理、运动系统疾病康复护理、呼吸系统疾病康复护理、其他疾病康复护理等。通过本课程的学习，旨在培养康复护理专科人才，提升临床护理人员康复护理专科知识和技能，解决临床常见康复护理问题能力，为我国老龄化社会形势、大健康理念下培养一批高水平专科康复护理人才打下基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course is specialized for graduate students pursuing a professional degree in Nursing, while also serving as an integrated undergraduate-graduate course for full-time undergraduate nursing students. Combining the latest advancements in rehabilitation nursing both domestically and internationally, the curriculum is designed to address the growing demand for enhanced clinical competencies in rehabilitation nursing in China. It integrates the academic strengths of the Rehabilitation Department, Huashan Hospital, Fudan University. Key content includes: Overview of Rehabilitation Nursing；Rehabilitation Nursing Assessment；Rehabilitation Nursing Techniques；Rehabilitation Nursing for Neurological Disorders；Rehabilitation Nursing for Musculoskeletal Disorders；Rehabilitation Nursing for Respiratory Disorders；Rehabilitation Nursing for Other Medical Conditions. Through this course, we aim to cultivate specialized professionals in rehabilitation nursing, enhance clinical nurses' expertise and practical skills in rehabilitation care, and develop their ability to address common clinical rehabilitation challenges. The program seeks to lay a foundation for training high-level rehabilitation nursing specialists, aligning with China's aging population trends and the national "Healthy China" strategic vision.

教学目标

通过本课程的教学，使学生理解和掌握康复护理相关理论、方法和技术，拓展学生的康复护理前沿知识，如神经重症康复进展、间歇经口管饲技术等，提升学生的康复护理知识水平和康复护理技能，以及应用所学知识解决患者常见疾病康复问题的能力。

 

教学方式

该课程在教学过程中采用课堂讲授、CBL、实践教学相结合的教学方式。

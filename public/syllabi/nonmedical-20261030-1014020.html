课程简介（中文）

本课程是中级综合课，专为汉语水平应该已经达到HSK3级水平的外国留学生开设。本课程学习汉语词汇、对话、短文、语法、中国文化等，通过多样的学习活动帮助学生提高他们使用汉语的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an intermediate comprehensive course specifically designed for international students who have already reached the HSK 3 level in Chinese proficiency. It aims to help students expand their Chinese vocabulary and sentence patterns, and improve their ability to use Chinese.

教学目标

本课程旨在提高学生的语言技能，包括听、说、读、写，以及词汇扩展和语法学习，中国文化也包括在内。通过一个学期的学习，学生需要掌握220个词汇，包括“哪壶不开提哪壶”等俗语，能理解并运用汉语基本关联词语等其他语法知识，能阅读并理解10篇长对话、10篇短文、10篇拓展文章，能围绕所给话题写中文短文，能就抽烟、做客、休闲生活、照骗、支付方式等话题用中文表达自己的观点。

教学方式

词汇教学：PowerPoint辅助讲解与练习，近义词辨析，写故事，“你来比划我来猜”等游戏。 句子教学：学生看图说句子，改错句等。 语法教学：创设生活、学习等现实情景，帮助学生理解语法知识，然后运用该语法知识完成实际的语言任务。 对话教学：角色扮演。 课文教学：朗读课文，复述课文，表演课文，讨论与发表观点等。 阅读教学：阅读拓展课文，回答问题，引导学生围绕一些话题发表自己的观点。 写作教学：课堂上规定时间写一段话，课后每个作业中都有一篇150-200字的写作练习。 Vocabulary teaching: PowerPoint-assisted explanation and practice, analysis of near-synonyms, story writing, games like "You Act and I Guess", etc. Sentence teaching: Students describe sentences based on pictures, correct wrong sentences, etc. Grammar teaching: Create real-life scenarios (such as daily life and study) to help students understand grammatical knowledge, and then use this knowledge to complete practical language tasks. Dialogue teaching: Role-playing. Text teaching: Reading texts aloud, retelling texts, performing texts, discussing and expressing opinions, etc. Reading teaching: Reading extended texts, answering questions, and guiding students to express their opinions on relevant topics. Writing teaching: Writing a paragraph within a specified time in class, and each after-class assignment includes a 150-200-word writing exercise.

考核构成

  • 出勤Attendance 15.0% 缺勤1/3及以上的学生不可以参加考试，“病假”“事假”都算作“缺勤”，“迟到”三次等于一次“缺勤”。Students who are absent for 1/3 or more of the classes are not allowed to take the exam. Both "sick leave" and "personal leave" are considered "absences", and three "late arrivals" are equivalent to one "absence".
  • 课堂表现Participation 15.0% 学生应认真上课，积极参加课堂活动。Students should attend classes carefully and actively participate in classroom activities.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 15.0% 学生应按时提交作业。Students should submit their assignments on time.
  • 课题研讨Topic Discussion 15.0% 每个学生都要围绕所给话题，用中文做展示。Each student is required to make a presentation in Chinese on the given topic.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 40.0% 口语考试，读写考试Oral test, Reading and Writing test

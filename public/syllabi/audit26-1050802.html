课程简介（中文）

本课程系统讲授公共管理学完整理论与分析工具，涵盖公共管理基础理论、公共组织体系、市场与政府失灵、公共部门领导、战略规划、公共人力资源、公共预算财务、多元公共服务供给与绩效评估、数字治理创新、公共危机管理、国家治理现代化等核心模块；课程搭建 “治理基础 — 组织运行 — 政策过程 — 治理成效 — 现代转型” 递进分析框架，突出从传统管理逻辑转向现代治理逻辑的思维转变，每讲配套公共卫生真实案例教学，引导学生运用各类公共管理理论工具剖析国内医疗卫生政策、基层卫生治理困境、突发公卫事件等现实问题，培育医防管复合型卫生治理分析能力。

课程简介（英文）

The course systematically teaches complete theories and analytical tools of public administration, covering core modules including basic theories of public management, public organization system, market and government failures, public sector leadership, strategic planning, public human resources, public budget and finance, diversified public service supply and performance evaluation, digital governance innovation, public crisis management, and modernization of national governance. It builds a progressive analytical framework of "governance foundation — organizational operation — policy process — governance effect — modern transformation", emphasizing the shift from traditional management logic to modern governance logic. Real public health cases are arranged in each lecture to guide students to apply various public management theories to analyze practical issues such as domestic medical and health policies, grassroots health governance dilemmas and public health emergencies, so as to cultivate compound health governance analytical capabilities integrating medicine, disease prevention and administration.

教学目标

本课程旨在通过对公共管理学中的基本概念、理论和方法的讲解，培养学生从公共管理的视角去发现、分析和解决当前卫生健康领域关键问题的能力，对卫生健康领域有更宏观、更理性的认识，具备更专业的“管理”意识与分析问题的能力，为培养一流的卫生管理者奠定基础。

教学方式

课堂讲授为主，同时辅以课堂教学实践活动（分组讨论、案例研讨），并在最后做专题案例分析。

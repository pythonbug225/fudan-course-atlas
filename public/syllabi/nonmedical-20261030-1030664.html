应用语言学导论

2026春 · FORE20024.01 · 秦文娟

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将系统介绍普通语言发展、第二语言习得、语言政策等领域的重要理论与实证研究成果。同时，学生还将熟悉应用语言学研究中的多种研究方法，包括基于语料库的语言分析方法，以及定量与定性的数据分析路径。

课程简介（英文）

This course will cover critical theories and empirical research in general language development, second language acquisition, language policy, etc. In addition, students will also get familiar with a variety of methodology in AL research, including corpus-based linguistic analysis, quantitative and qualitative approaches of data analysis.

教学目标

This course aims to offer students fundamental knowledge and skills in the field of Applied Linguistics (AL). By the end of the course, students should be able to acquire both theoretical and practical knowledge to scaffold their advancement in future academic studies and professional work in modern language teaching and researching.

教学方式

Lecture, group projects, research methods workshops, etc.

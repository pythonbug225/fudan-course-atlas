Derivatives and Risk Management

2025秋 · MF60022.01 · 童国士

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

Derivatives have become a popular hedging and investment tool over the last three decades, and their concepts are required for almost every advanced finance topic. This course provides students an introduction of derivatives (forwards, futures, options). Students will first learn the basic concepts of derivatives, how to price them, and some general principles on how to use them for hedging, speculation and arbitrage.

教学目标

Understand the mechanics of forwards, futures and options. Understand the general features of derivative markets Get familiar with derivative products in China, including those from DCE. Learn how to determine forward/futures prices as well as values of forward/futures contracts. Learn the determinants and bounds for option prices. Understand the no arbitrage principle and call put parity. Understand the Greek letters for risk management. Learn basic option trading strategies. This course is also part of a joint collaborative project with Dalian Commodity Exchange (DCE). Therefore, we will also have industry experts appointed by DCE to share with us with some real-life examples of trading derivatives in both equity and commodity markets.

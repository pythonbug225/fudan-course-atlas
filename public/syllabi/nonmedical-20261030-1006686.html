硅酸盐类文物的科技研究

2025秋 · CULT60010.01 · 王荣

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

文物具有物质性和社会性，物质性需要自然科学的帮助，提取其背后的潜在信息。在文物学上，发掘文物的科技价值，更好地体现其社会性，即历史价值、艺术价值等；在考古学上，更好地理解文物资源的获取和利用（生产和使用），理解文物在古代社会中所起的作用，透物见社会，更好地复原古代社会面貌；在文物保护上，有助于更好的评估文物的保存状态，分析其劣化机制，在此基础上探求抢救性的加固保护方法，以及预防性的保护环境。 硅酸盐文物包含玉石器、陶瓷器、玻璃器等，是无机质文物的重要类别。本课程拟从多学科角度对硅酸盐质文物研究的理论、方法和内容，以及科学保护等内容进行探讨。

课程简介（英文）

Cultural relics are material and social, and materiality requires the help of natural science to extract the potential information behind them. In cultural relics, the scientific and technological value of cultural relics is explored to better reflect their sociality, that is, historical value, artistic value, etc.; in archaeology, it is better to understand the acquisition and utilization (production and use) of cultural relics resources, understand the role of cultural relics in ancient society, see society through objects, and better restore the appearance of ancient society; in cultural relics protection, it helps to better evaluate the preservation status of cultural relics, analyze their deterioration mechanism, and on this basis explore rescue reinforcement protection methods, as well as preventive environmental protection. Silicate cultural relics include jade, ceramics, glassware, etc., which are an important category of inorganic cultural relics. This course intends to explore the theory, methods and content of silicate cultural relics research, as well as scientific protection, from a multidisciplinary perspective.

教学目标

通过本课程的讲授，学生能够掌握以下基本内容： 1）掌握古代玉器科技研究的理论、方法与实践 2）掌握古代陶瓷器科技研究的理论、方法与实践 3）掌握古代玻璃器科技研究的理论、方法与实践 进而能将科技考古与文物保护两个学科融合，从理论、方法和实践层面理解硅酸盐文物的一般研究范式。

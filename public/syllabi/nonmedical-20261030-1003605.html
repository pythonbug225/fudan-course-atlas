课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在充分调动学生的学术积极性，帮助其发掘并明确自身的学术写作兴趣，培养持续探索和自主创作的动力。通过鼓励学生积极分享学术写作体验与心得，促进课堂交流与相互启发。课程内容涵盖学术论文写作的各个关键环节，包括学术论文的基本知识与写作规范、选题原则与技巧、引言的构思与撰写、文献综述的整理与批判、方法论部分的结构与表达、讨论与结论的逻辑安排、参考文献的引用规范，以及摘要的撰写方法等，旨在帮助学生系统掌握学术论文的写作流程与技巧，提升科研表达与学术交流能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to fully stimulate students’ academic initiative, helping them to explore and define their interests in academic writing while cultivating sustained motivation for inquiry and independent creation. By encouraging students to actively share their experiences and insights in academic writing, it fosters classroom interaction and mutual inspiration. The course content covers all key aspects of academic paper writing, including fundamental knowledge and conventions, principles and techniques for topic selection, structuring and composing introductions, organizing and critically reviewing literature, constructing and presenting the methodology section, arranging the logic of discussions and conclusions, applying proper citation formats for references, and writing abstracts. It aims to equip students with a systematic mastery of the academic writing process and techniques, thereby enhancing their skills in scholarly expression and academic communication.

教学目标

知晓学术论文的要求及其框架结构； 认出并能自主使用学术论文的文体特征、语言特征； 说出学术论文的组成部分、写作步骤； 搜索、选择相关文献、撰写文献综述； 对比、分析、评估同伴写作； 提出研究问题、撰写研究计划； 撰写出一篇符合规范的英语学术论文。

教学方式

讲授为主。在大量课外阅读的基础上，课堂上将采用测验、讨论、案例分析、同伴互评等方式，对学生的阅读进行检测、评估、分享、引导和深化。教师会进行介绍、引导和总结。

教学内容与进度安排

第一周：The basics of academic paper writing and publication 第二周：Areas to explore; topic selection; adjusting a topic 第三周：Topic collection; examples and evaluation 第四周：The introduction section 第五周：Case study and comparative analysis 第六周：Literature collection; the literature review section 第七周：Case study and comparative analysis 第八周：The theoretical framework section 第九周：Methodologies in translation studies 第十周：The methodology section 第十一周：The results section 第十二周：The discussion section 第十三周：Case study and comparative analysis 第十四周：The conclusion, limitation & reference sections 第十五周：Final presentation 第十六周：Term paper submission 第十七周：Revision 第十八周：Revision

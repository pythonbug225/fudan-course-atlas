课程简介（中文）

国际旅游服务贸易是国际服务贸易的一种，也是全球服务业中发展最快的产业之一，在国际服务贸易领域中有重要的地位。近几年，随着我国国际地位不断提升，我国旅游服务贸易规模不断扩大，尤其以出入境旅游为例，全球市场繁荣态势显著。国际旅游服务贸易不仅促进了我国与其他国家（地区）之间的商品贸易、资本流动、信息交流和人员往来，而且带动了中国文化走向世界，对塑造国家形象、提升我国的国际地位和国际形象具有重要的作用。 本课程内容覆盖旅游服务贸易的理论、政策、规则、发展趋势和变化特点、产业基础等内容，旨在培养学生成为具有国际服务贸易领域专业知识与技能的高层次人才。通过课程学习，学生可以更加深入了解和学习关于国际旅游服务贸易的相关理论与专业知识，掌握国际旅游服务贸易的最新发展动态以及最新的统计数据，了解国际旅游服务贸易领域学术前沿研究专题。

课程简介（英文）

International trade in tourism services is a key component of international trade in services and one of the fastest-growing sectors in the global service industry, holding significant importance within the field. In recent years, as China’s international standing has steadily risen, the scale of China’s tourism services trade has expanded substantially—particularly evident in the robust growth of inbound and outbound tourism, reflecting a thriving presence in the global market. This sector not only fosters goods trade, capital flows, information exchange, and people-to-people exchanges between China and other countries or regions, but also serves as a vital channel for promoting Chinese culture globally, thereby playing a crucial role in shaping China’s national image and enhancing its international status and reputation. This course covers core topics including theories, policies, regulatory frameworks, development trends, evolving characteristics, and industrial foundations of trade in tourism services. It aims to cultivate high-caliber professionals equipped with specialized knowledge and skills in international trade in services. Through this course, students will gain deeper insights into relevant theories and professional knowledge, stay abreast of the latest developments and statistical data in international tourism services trade, and become familiar with cutting-edge research topics in the field.

教学目标

了解国际旅游服务贸易的基本概念、特征、形式、发展历程； 掌握国际旅游服务贸易的基本理论、贸易政策和规则体系； 了解世界各国、各地区旅游产业发展与贸易现状及趋势； 掌握国际旅游服务贸易的实务流程，能够熟练查阅并分析国际旅游服务贸易的最新发展动态以及最新的统计数据； 了解并把握国际旅游服务贸易领域的前沿研究问题，并有自己的学术主张。

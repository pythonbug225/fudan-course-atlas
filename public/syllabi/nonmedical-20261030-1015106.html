东亚国际关系

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在较为全面和系统地阐述东亚国际关系。将首先分析全球政治经济，特别是其演变和趋势，识别其对东亚国际关系的影响。之后，课程将探讨区域层面的主要问题，即安全合作与冲突、经济一体化、区域认同建构等。第三部分将重点关注主要大国及其关系，特别是美国在该地区扮演的角色、中国的崛起、中日关系、朝核问题与南北关系、东盟+进程等。

课程简介（英文）

This optional course is designed to introduce and analyze the international relations of East Asia. For the purposes of this course, East Asia is defined as the region encompassing the Russian Far East, China, Koreas, Japan and Southeast Asia. In today’s global political economy, East Asia is one of the most dynamic and consequential regions. The international relations of East Asia are influenced by global powers and global forces while having its reversal impacts on global political economy. The course will start with the analysis of global political economy, in particular its evolution and trends, and try to identify its impacts on international relations of East Asia. The course will then move to explore the main issues at the regional level, namely security cooperation and conflicts, economic regionalization, and regional identity-building. The third part will then focus on the major powers and their relationships. We will look into the role played by the United States in the region, the issue of China’s rise, Sino-Japanese relations, Korean nuclear issue and North-South relationship, and the ASEAN+ process, to name just a few.

教学目标

Students will be expected to acquire knowledge and understanding of: Major theories in explaining the international relations in East Asia The evolvement of East Asian regionalism and its limits The dynamics of major power relationships in the region The implications of China’s rise for the region The teaching methods should facilitate the development of cognitive and presentational skills including: The delivery of structured oral presentations Participation in group discussions and research works Individual research at postgraduate level

教学方式

The course is a part of a joint distant learning program with two top universities in East Asia, namely Keio University in Tokyo and Yonsei University in Seoul. The Course thus includes Fudan-only classes in September and December, with seven joint classes in-between. Each class will be a mixture of lecture, discussion, occasional video and other media presentations, and group activities. Fudan-Keio-Yonsei joint classes start at 8:30am and end at 10:00am, Beijing time.

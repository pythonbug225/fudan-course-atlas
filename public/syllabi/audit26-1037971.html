课程简介（中文）

本课程选取中国古代经济史、主要是明清经济史研究的若干重要领域，对近年在学术界研究较多、影响较大的课题的学术积累和前沿进展作深入的介绍与探讨。在使学生了解经济史研究的理念、方法、模式及发展动向的同时，加深对于史学研究的基本性质、史学与社会科学交叉研究的可能性与问题的理解。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on several key areas of ancient Chinese economic history, particularly the economic history of the Ming and Qing dynasties. It provides an in-depth introduction to and discussion of the academic body of work and cutting-edge developments surrounding topics that have received significant attention and exerted considerable influence in academic circles in recent years. While helping students understand the concepts, methods, models, and trends in economic history research, the course also deepens their understanding of the fundamental nature of historical research, as well as the possibilities and challenges of interdisciplinary research between history and the social sciences.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，使学生了解传统中国社会经济生活的基本情形，懂得以科学、理性和实证的观点分析历史时期的社会经济现象，训练经济史基本文献的解读与分析能力，相较本科基础课程，对中国经济史学科的研究现状及其前沿进展有相对进阶的了解。

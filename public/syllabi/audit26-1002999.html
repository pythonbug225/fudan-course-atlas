课程简介（中文）

《精神病学》是每个医学生临床医学的主要课程之一。精神疾病在各个临床科室的日常生活中会经常出现，学习《精神病学》这门课程后，对每个学生今后的临床实践和科研工作都会有所帮助。

课程简介（英文）

Psychiatry is one of the main classes in clinical medicine for each medical student. Mental disorders will be often seen in daily life of each clinical department, it will be helped for each student to do your works in future clinical practices and researching works after learning psychiatry.

教学目标

The contents of psychiatry include followings: basic ideas of psychiatry,  Sings and Symptoms, Dementia, delirium and Amnestic Disorders, Schizophrenic and Related Psychoses,  Depressive disorders, Bipolar disorders, Anxiety and  Stress-Related Disorders, Personality, Child’s psychiatry, Therapy of mental disorders.

Through the study of this course, students are required to master the basic theories, basic knowledge and basic skills of psychiatry, not only to cultivate the clinical ability of medical students, but also to cultivate students' self-learning ability, applied knowledge ability and expression ability, and to inspire students' innovative thinking and exploration. The consciousness of research pays attention to the cultivation of students' professional ethics, pays attention to bilingual teaching, and improves students' professional foreign language ability.
 
The theoretical course requires at least 3 attendance checks per semester.

教学方式

Theoretical class combined with trainee and practical class. Lectures in the class and practice in the Shanghai Mental Health Center.

课程简介（中文）

从经典和量子主方程出发，发展奇辛流形、位移辛结构和导出临界轨迹的几何语言，解释这些思想如何统一辛几何、变形理论和同调代数的概念。 在此基础上，本课程将引入因式分解代数作为量子场论中局部可观测代数的严格数学模型。 重点放在明确的例子-如陈-西蒙斯理论，泊松西格玛模型和AKSZ结构-说明BV量化和分解代数如何在拓扑和微扰场理论中相互作用。

课程简介（英文）

Starting from the classical and quantum master equations, the course develops the geometric language of odd symplectic manifolds, shifted symplectic structures, and derived critical loci, explaining how these ideas unify concepts from symplectic geometry, deformation theory, and homological algebra. the course introduces factorization algebras as rigorous mathematical models for the algebra of local observables in quantum field theory. Following the approach of Costello and Gwilliam, we explore how local-to-global principles, renormalization, and topological field theory can all be expressed in the language of higher algebra and cosheaf theory. emphasis is placed on explicit examples—such as Chern–Simons theory, the Poisson sigma model, and the AKSZ construction—illustrating how BV quantization and factorization algebras interact in both topological and perturbative field theories. The course is suitable for graduate students with backgrounds in differential geometry, homological algebra, or mathematical physics.

教学目标

.By the end of this course, students should be able to: Understand the Batalin–Vilkovisky (BV) formalism as a homological approach to gauge theory and quantization. Understand the use of homological algebra, L∞-algebras, and shifted symplectic geometry in the formulation of classical and quantum field theories. Learn how factorization algebras provide a rigorous mathematical framework for local quantum field theory. Connect the BV formalism with derived geometry, deformation quantization, and topological field theory. ．在本课程结束时，学生应该能够： 将Batalin-Vilkovisky （BV）形式主义理解为规范理论和量子化的同调方法。 了解使用同调代数，L∞-代数，并在经典和量子场论的表述辛几何移位。 学习因式分解代数如何为局部量子场论提供严格的数学框架。 将BV形式化与派生几何、变形量化和拓扑场论联系起来。

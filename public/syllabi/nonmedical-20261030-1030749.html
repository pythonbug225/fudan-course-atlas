课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助学生为撰写和发表医学英语研究论文做好准备，并提升其在国际会议上的有效沟通能力。课程设立的核心理由包括：1) 发表医学英语研究论文对我校医学类博士生至关重要；2) 医学英语研究论文写作是学生学术能力发展的核心体现；3) 医学生正获得越来越多在国际会议交流展示研究成果的机会。因此，本课程通过构建自主、协作与互动的学习框架，最终培养学生掌握医学英语研究论文的写作技能，并具备在国际会议上用英语进行学术汇报与交流的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed and offered to help the students to prepare themselves for writing and publishing medical papers in English and to effectively communicate at international conferences. The rationales this course is based on include 1) publishing medical papers in English is of paramount importance for Ph.D. candidates in Fudan University; 2) collaboration in conducting research and writing medical paper is an integral part of academia; and 3) medical students and professionals are provided with more and more opportunities to communicate and present their research findings at the international conferences. This course, therefore, is framed to enable students to explore autonomous, collaborative and interactive learning opportunities with the ultimate goal of developing skills in writing medical papers and communicating and delivering presentations in English at the international conferences.

教学目标

At the end of the course, the students are expected to be familiar with the process of submission of medical papers in English; have a good knowledge of the format requirements of medical papers in English; get familiar with the components of a typical medical journal paper and their characteristics; know the principles to abide by while writing a medical paper in English; build up a stock of English language for writing medical papers; coauthor a medical paper in English; be able to prepare and deliver a poster presentation of the English medical paper at a simulated conference; be skilled in communicating at an international academic conference.

教学方式

In order to improve the teaching effects, the classes will have a trial of small private online courses (SPOC) with specific focus on online autonomous learning and real-class discussion.

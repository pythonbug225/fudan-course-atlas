课程简介（中文）

光电功能高分子材料是一类具有电学、光学、光电转换功能的高分子材料，在信息、通讯、能源、医疗等领域有广泛的应用，是目前的科学研究前沿方向。该课程将介绍导电高分子基本概念及其基础理论知识以及在晶体管、发光二极管、太阳能电池、传感器等领域的应用，使学生了解电子工业常用高分子的种类、基本特点和加工工艺，拓宽学生的专业知识和视野。

课程简介（英文）

Photoelectric functional polymers are a class of high-performance polymers with electrical, optical, and photoelectric conversion properties, and are widely applied in fields such as information technology, communication, energy, and healthcare, representing a cutting-edge research frontier. This course will introduce the fundamental concepts and theoretical knowledge of conducting polymers, along with their applications in transistors, light-emitting diodes, solar cells, sensors, and other areas. It aims to familiarize students with the types, basic characteristics, and processing techniques of commonly used polymers in the electronics industry, thereby broadening their professional expertise and perspectives.

教学目标

高分子材料是电子信息产业中具有重要应用前景的一类材料，也是未来电子材料的发展方向之一。本课程将介绍光电功能高分子材料的基础理论知识及最新的研究进展，系统讲授这类材料在晶体管、发光二极管、太阳能电池等领域的应用。

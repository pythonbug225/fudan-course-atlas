课程简介（中文）

从知觉出发介绍人的认知特点；分析有效沟通应考虑的三大原则；在讨论对人对己看法，得出 ‘We are OK !’是可以持续、学习的生活态度的基础上；介绍三大沟通技能，分别是：你/我信息（换位思考式沟通）；自信表达和积极倾听。课程还将讨论人际冲突产生的原因、说服的理论和技能、谈判中的价值创造和索取策略，倾听言外之意、动作表情识别、演讲、有效与自己对话等内容。通过学习，达到系统认识和掌握管理沟通的理论和技能。

课程简介（英文）

Starting from perception, this course introduces the cognitive characteristics of individuals; analyzes the three major principles to consider in effective communication; and, based on discussing perceptions of oneself and others, establishes that "We are OK!" is a sustainable and learnable life attitude. It then presents three key communication skills: "You/I messages" (empathy-based communication), assertive expression, and active listening. The course will also explore the causes of interpersonal conflicts, theories and skills of persuasion, value creation and claiming strategies in negotiation, recognizing implied meanings, identifying facial expressions and body language, public speaking, and effective self-talk. Through this learning process, students will achieve a systematic understanding and mastery of management communication theories and skills.

教学目标

沟通是人类社会的基本生活方式，是人们通过共同的符号系统进行意见或思想交流的过程。沟通是管理过程的重要组成部分。杰克·韦尔奇（GE前CEO）曾说过:“管理就是沟通，沟通，再沟通。”本课程介绍管理沟通策略的原理和运用方式。

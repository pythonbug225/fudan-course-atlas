组织行为学专题研究

2025秋 · MSE70007.01 · 徐笑君、彭贺

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是为博士生开设的组织行为专题研讨课程。课程会设四个板块：第一板块以组织中的个体层面研究为主要内容，讨论个体心理和行为特征的差异；第二板块是群体板块，分析个体进入群体以后的变化，群体如何影响个体，以及群体自身的行为特征，关注群体有效性；第三板块是系统梳理组织行为研究中的主要因变量，从员工管理角度看组织有效性；第四板块主要关注研究方法，尤其关注研究选题与研究方法的结合，特别关注跨文化议题，考察组织行为学原理的普适性和特殊性，介绍跨文化研究的工具和本土研究的议题，这对于我们在中国情景下从事管理研究非常重要。我们每个版块是相互联系，层层递进点。 为实现以上目标，本课程会运用讲授、学生汇报、讨论、书面作业等多种方式相结合。一般来说，每周三课时的安排方式如下：第一位主讲学生根据要求的模板准备好要讲解论文的PPT，每篇文章讲解30分钟，老师和其他同学就此论文一起讨论30分钟；然后第二位主讲学生讲第二篇论文，每周我们会讨论两篇论文；最后，就当日的主题内容的系统框架进行介绍，并讨论该研究议题下未来有价值的研究问题，就理论构建、学术写作方法和技能等进行相关讨论。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a specialized seminar on organizational behavior designed for doctoral students. It consists of four main sections: the first section focuses on individual-level research within organizations, discussing the differences in individual psychological and behavioral characteristics; the second section addresses group dynamics, analyzing the changes individuals undergo upon entering a group, the influence of the group on the individual, and the behavioral characteristics of the group itself, with an emphasis on group effectiveness; the third section systematically reviews the primary dependent variables in organizational behavior research from the perspective of organizational effectiveness in employee management; the fourth section concentrates on research methodologies, particularly the integration of research topics with methods, with a special emphasis on cross-cultural issues, examining the universality and specificity of organizational behavior principles, and introducing tools for cross-cultural research and topics relevant to local studies, which is crucial for conducting management research within the Chinese context. Each section is interconnected, building upon one another progressively. To achieve these objectives, the course will employ a combination of lectures, student presentations, discussions, and written assignments. Generally, the weekly structure consists of three class sessions as follows: the first presenting student will prepare a PowerPoint presentation based on the required template for their chosen paper, delivering a 30-minute presentation, followed by a 30-minute discussion with the instructor and fellow students regarding the paper; the second presenting student will then present the second paper, with two papers being discussed each week; finally, the session will conclude with an introduction to the systematic framework of the day’s thematic content, followed by a discussion of future valuable research questions related to the topic, encompassing theoretical construction, academic writing methods, and skills.

教学目标

在这门课里，我们希望通过对于组织行为领域的理论和经典实证研究的系统学习，培养学员的研究兴趣，并尝试撰写研究计划书。学习目标是： 理解课堂讨论的相应理论及其关键变量 对于这些理论及其关键变量的诸多分支进行评价 建立这些经典理论和变量与中国管理实践的联系 提升理论思考和探索能力, 同时学习写作学术论文的基本方法 尝试将这些理论和本领运用到自己的研究计划中。根据学到的知识和理论，结合中国管理实践，设计研究项目，进行学术写作。

教学方式

每周教学分三个环节进行：学生报告论文；集体讨论；老师讲解本领域相关研究进展与动态。

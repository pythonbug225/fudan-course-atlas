Alternative Investment Strategies

2026春 · MF60030.01 · 孙林

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course introduces the alternative investment strategies, which behave differently from traditional investment strategies. Firstly, participants will be provided with a comprehensive introduction to the fundamentals of alternative investment strategies including their risk-return relationship, role in portfolio diversification, and performance evaluation. Investments in hedge funds, fund of funds, commodities, and financial derivatives will also be discussed. Secondly, latest academic research results will be presented to help students to build a solid theoretical background. Thirdly, case studies will give participants a comprehensive look at the real stories of successful alternative asset managers. Finally, the course project with industry mentors requires participants to solve real-life problems by applying what they learn from the course.

教学目标

Through the course, participants will be able to: Develop a broad view of alternative investment strategies. Discuss the global trend of the alternative investment industry. Demonstrate the risk and return relationship of various investment strategies. Evaluate the performance of alternative investment managers. Construct an alternative investment portfolio using quantitative skills.

教学内容与进度安排

Class 1 Topic: Introduction to alternative investment strategies Note: history, industry outlook, role of various participants, and various asset classes. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Class 2 Topic: Investment strategies part 1 Note: equity long/short, market neutral, distressed securities, event-type strategies, convertible arbitrage, and commodity trading advisors. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: Hedge Fund Due Diligence at Leman Alternative Asset Management Company by Pedro Matos. Class 3 Topic: Investment strategies part 2 Note: equity long/short, market neutral, distressed securities, event-type strategies, convertible arbitrage, and commodity trading advisors. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: McDonald’s, Wendy’s, and Hedge Funds: The Impact of Hedge Fund Activism on Corporate Governance by David P. Stowell. Class 4 Topic: Risk and return Note: arbitrage, factor model, market risk, operational risk, and liquidity risk. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: JUST Capital by Pedro Matos, Mary Margaret Frank, Aaron Fernstrom. Class 5 Topic: Characteristics of various hedge funds Note: fees, leverage, compensation structures, strategies, regulations, and differences from mutual funds. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: ProShares Hedge Replication ETF by Pedro Matos and Anil Demir. Class 6 Topic: Fund of funds, Chinese hedge fund market and other related topics. Note: fund selections, performance evaluation, growth of the China’s alternative investment industry, and global hedge funds trend. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: AQR’s DELTA Strategy by Daniel Bergstresser, Lauren Cohen, Randolph Cohen, and Christopher Malloy.

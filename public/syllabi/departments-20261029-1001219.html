财务管理

2025秋 · BA20009.01 · 方曙红

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程聚焦公司财务问题引入财务金融的基础理论，其主要内容有现值及其计算, 净现值法则及其应用, 项目价值分析, 风险与收益的权衡, 资本结构与资本成本等公司金融问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the fundamental financial theory and emphasizes on its applications to corporate financial management. It focuses on Present Value, NPV rules, Capital Budgeting, Project Analysis, Risk and Return, Capital Structure and Cost of Capital, and other important issues in corporate finance.

教学目标

Provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles of corporate finance. Equip students with  knowledge and capability for real-case applications

教学方式

Class teaching and discussion; After-class discussion in the office and on the Wechat.

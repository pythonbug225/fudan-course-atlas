课程简介（中文）

宏观经济学是经济学的核⼼学科。理解宏观经济学对于理解经济⾏为和实施经济政策⾄关重要。本课程涵盖了基 本理论，如产出、失业、通货膨胀、利率和汇率的决定因素，增⻓理论和商业周期理论。课程应⽤这些理论来解 释当前的宏观经济事件，并分析宏观经济政策的影响。

课程简介（英文）

Macroeconomics is a core subject of economics. Understanding macroeconomics is fundamental to understanding the behavior of the economy and conducting economic policy. The course covers basic theories such as the determinants of output, unemployment, inflation, interest rates, and exchange rates, growth theory and business cycle theory. The course applies these theories to interpret current macroeconomic events and to analyze the eﬀects of macroeconomic policies.

教学目标

The main goal of this course is to introduce students to the two most important issues: how the economy functions (positive view); whether and how we can do anything to improve the economic performance (normative view). Upon successful completion of the course, students should be familiar with the key theories of macroeconomics and be able to think critically about macroeconomic problems and evaluate the effects of macroeconomic policy.

教学方式

Classroom instruction includes theoretical lectures and case-based discussions, complemented by scheduled office hours after class. Students are required to maintain class notes and complete assignments on time.

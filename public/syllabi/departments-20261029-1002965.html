课程简介（中文）

本课程将探讨如何撰写研究论文，在此过程中我们还将涵盖经济学研究中的若干主题。授课教师将为每个主题提供一般性指导与具体文献范例，学生将共同分析文献以及他们感兴趣的研究课题。

课程简介（英文）

This course will discuss how to write research papers, during which we will also cover several topics in economic research. The instructors will provide general guidance and specific examples for each subject, and students will collaboratively work on particular examples and their topics of interest. The course will address topics and questions such as: What is a good topic? How do you choose one? How to find and read relevant literature? How to write a paper in the field of Economics? How to present research papers effectively? How to publish and revise a paper?

教学目标

This course aims to explore the components of dissertations and academic papers in economics, guiding students in transitioning from coursework to independent paper writing. Through lectures and discussions, we will cover the basic requirements and examine specific examples of research papers to understand the general rules and best practices for writing. Active participation in on-site discussions is strongly encouraged. Students are encouraged to identify topics of interest and draft several chapters or paragraphs for their potential dissertations.

教学方式

Lecture, presentation, group discussion

Thin Film Optics

2026春 · OPTI70003.01 · 张千鹏

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

介绍薄膜光学的基础理论。薄膜光学作为现代光学和材料科学的重要分支，涵盖光在薄膜材料中的传播、反射和透射等基本现象。学习薄膜材料的基本物理和化学特性，理解这些特性如何影响薄膜的光学性能。探讨薄膜的设计与制造技术。重点讲解如何评估和优化薄膜的性能。讨论实际应用中可能遇到的问题，提出改进薄膜设计的策略，以满足更严格的性能指标。

课程简介（英文）

Introduce the fundamental theories of thin-film optics. As an important branch of modern optics and materials science, thin-film optics covers basic phenomena such as the propagation, reflection, and transmission of light in thin-film materials. Study the fundamental physical and chemical properties of thin-film materials and understand how these properties influence their optical performance. Explore the design and fabrication technologies of thin films, with a focus on evaluating and optimizing their performance. Discuss potential challenges in practical applications and propose strategies to improve thin-film designs to meet stricter performance requirements.

教学目标

探究薄膜光学的基础理论及核心技术。分析多层薄膜系统的光学机制，阐述光在不同薄膜材料内的传播、反射与透射行为。解读干涉现象，并细致探讨其在多层薄膜设计里的运用。 了解光学薄膜设计的相关知识。能够对多层薄膜在实际运用中的光学性能进行评估，并提出针对性的优化策略，以达成更为严苛的性能指标。 培养学生将薄膜技术应用于实际问题的能力，强化团队精神与合作能力。通过小组合作完成相关项目，并展示项目成果，锻炼团队协作能力和表达能力。 Explore the fundamental theories and core technologies of thin-film optics. Analyze the optical mechanisms of multilayer thin-film systems, explaining the behavior of light propagation, reflection, and transmission in different thin-film materials. Interpret the phenomenon of interference and thoroughly discuss its application in the design of multilayer thin films. Acquire knowledge related to optical thin-film design. Gain the ability to evaluate the optical performance of multilayer thin films in practical applications and propose targeted optimization strategies to achieve stricter performance standards. Cultivate students' ability to apply thin-film technology to real-world problems while strengthening teamwork and collaboration skills. Complete relevant projects through group collaboration, present project outcomes, and develop team cooperation and communication abilities.

教学方式

教师讲授为主，学生完成课程课题研究，撰写论文，并进行汇报。 The course is primarily teacher-led, with students completing course-related research projects, writing papers, and presenting their findings.

教学内容与进度安排

第1-4周，薄膜光学基础 - 薄膜光学的历史与应用 - 光与薄膜的相互作用基础 - 薄膜的定义、分类以及基本特性 - 光波的反射、折射 - 多层膜的结构和光波在不同界面上的行为 - 薄膜系统的厚度、相位差的概念 - 薄膜光学的光学阻抗、阻抗匹配原理 第5-8 周，波动光学理论 - 光的波动理论简介、波的类型 - 光波的波动方程、平面波和球面波的解 - 光在不同介质中的传播、折射率的概念，斯涅尔定律和临界角 - 干涉现象，干涉的条件，经典干涉实验的介绍，干涉在长度测量和光谱分析中的应用 - 衍射的基本类型、衍射光栅 - 偏振的理论与应用，非线性光学的简介 第9-10周，薄膜的制备与特性 - 常用的材料沉积方法 - 薄膜的光学与物理特性测试方法 第11-12周，薄膜设计 - 薄膜设计的基本原理 - 光学矩阵计算，利用菲涅尔方程、传输矩阵法等进行理论计算 - 使用设计软件进行薄膜设计 第13周，薄膜在各领域的应用 - 在光学仪器中的应用 - 在光通信系统中的应用 - 在太阳能电池中的应用 第14-15周，薄膜光学在科学研究领域的最新进展与课程回顾 - 新型光学材料应用（如太阳能电池、光子晶体与超材料等）中的光学薄膜，结合最新的科学文献进行分析 - 课程回顾 第16-17周 进行小组项目报告 第18周 提交课题论文 [1] Weeks 1-2: Fundamentals of Thin-Film Optics and Wave Optics Theory (2 weeks) Basics of the interaction between light and thin films. Definition, classification, and basic properties of thin films. Structure of multilayer films and the behavior of light waves at different interfaces. Concepts of film thickness and phase difference. Introduction to wave optics theory and types of waves. Light propagation in different media, refractive index concepts, Snell's law, and critical angles. Interference phenomena: conditions for interference, introduction to classical interference experiments, and applications in length measurement and spectral analysis. Basics of diffraction types and diffraction gratings. Theory and applications of polarization, introduction to nonlinear optics. [2] Weeks 3-4: Thin-Film Fabrication and Properties (2 weeks) Early techniques for optical coating deposition. Ion-assisted deposition, ion plating, sputtering. Cathodic arc evaporation, pulsed laser deposition, chemical vapor deposition, atomic layer deposition, sol-gel processes, etching, and other techniques. Methods for testing the optical and physical properties of thin films. High-precision optical coating production strategies: deterministic production strategies, optical broadband monitoring, hybrid thickness monitoring concepts, virtual deposition systems, real-time correction tools, design stability during production, and deposition control for continuous refractive index variation coating systems. [3] Weeks 5-6: Thin-Film Design (2 weeks) Basic principles of thin-film design. Modern numerical analysis techniques for thin films: Fourier transform methods, non-uniform refinement, needle methods, and global optimization. Manufacturability issues. Hybrid designs: waveguide substrate coatings, structured coatings, solar cell applications. Optical matrix calculations: theoretical computations using Fresnel equations and transfer matrix methods. Thin-film design using specialized software. [4] Weeks 7-8: Optical Monitoring Strategies for Coating Manufacturing (2 weeks) Classification of optical monitoring strategies. Turning-point optical monitoring and error self-compensation effects. Level monitoring: passive and active monochromatic monitoring strategies. Monitoring through swing values. Direct broadband optical monitoring. Indirect optical monitoring strategies. [5] Weeks 9-11: Applications, New Thin-Film Materials, and Their Properties (3 weeks) Applications in optical instruments, optical communication systems, and solar cells. Complex materials with subwavelength inclusions for optical thin-film applications: Fundamental physics of new materials with subwavelength inclusions. Ceramic matrices embedded with nanostructures. Alternative materials to resonant inclusions. Characterization of new subwavelength inclusion materials. Scattering properties of random structures in thin films: Numerical solutions of simplified Rayleigh equations for light scattering in films with 1D rough surfaces. Scattering and transmission of P-polarized and S-polarized light in films with two 1D rough surfaces using simplified Rayleigh equations. Optical characteristics of thin-film materials at short wavelengths: Material behavior across spectral ranges. Reflection and transmission in absorbing materials. Optical constants of materials at short wavelengths. Relationship between n and k values using Kramers-Kronig analysis. Experimental determination of optical constants. [6] Weeks 12-13: Latest Advances in Thin-Film Optics in Scientific Research and Course Review (2 weeks) Analysis of optical thin films in cutting-edge applications of novel optical materials (e.g., solar cells, photonic crystals, and metamaterials), based on the latest scientific literature. More topics include: Control of surface thermal radiation. Optical coating colors. Organic optical coatings. Surface Doppler effects in films with periodic inhomogeneities. Quantum dot optical thin films. Optical coatings for displays and lighting. Course review. [7] Weeks 14-15: Group Project Presentations (2 weeks) [8] Week 16: Submission of Final Research Paper

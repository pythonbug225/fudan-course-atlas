课程简介（中文）

本课程为全英文课程，其基本内容包括对普通法制度以及英美侵权法的理论与判例的介绍。

课程简介（英文）

This is a 2-credit seminar course taught in English and designed to introduce the common law system and the theories and cases of the common law of torts.

教学目标

Students are expected to learn more core legal English concept and terminologies, improve the ability of listening, speaking, reading, and writing of legal English, and be able to analyze and write on any given case facts. The objective is to train the students to think, speak, and write as a lawyer.

教学方式

Students are expected to prepare before class. After the instructor’s lecture, the students are expected to actively participate in class discussions and Q&As, case-analysis presentations, as well as the comprehension and translation of cases.

教学内容与进度安排

Week Content & Expected Achievement Assignment 1-2 Common Law in General Reading Assignments 3-7 Intentional Torts to the Person and Property Case Presentations 8-12 Defamation and Privacy Case Presentations 13-16 Negligence Torts Presentation/Submission of Course Report

  • 期末考试Final Examination：论文 50.0% 提交高质量论文

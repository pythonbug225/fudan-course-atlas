课程简介（中文）

本课程为财政学专业学生开设的必修课程。课程目的是要让学生了解国家预算的普适性流程，即预算的编制、审批、执行、调整、决算、审计等流程，并探讨国别预算，以及国别预算的更高级形态——国际组织预算。课程分为概述、流程、年度、周期、国别预算（当然，首先是中国国家预算，其次，是亚洲其他国家与欧美非洲代表性国家预算等）、国际组织预算（如欧盟预算、非盟预算、东盟预算、上合组织预算、联合国预算）等问题。

课程简介（英文）

This is a compulsory course designed for students majoring in Public Finance. The primary objective of the course is to equip students with an understanding of the universal process of national budgeting, namely the procedures of budget formulation, examination and approval, implementation, adjustment, final account compilation, and auditing. It also explores national budgets as well as their more advanced form—budgets of international organizations. The course is structured around the following core modules: an overview of national budgeting, budget procedures, annual budgeting, budget cycles, national budgets (with a primary focus on China’s national budget, followed by those of representative countries in other parts of Asia, Europe, America, and Africa), and budgets of international organizations (such as the budgets of the European Union, the African Union, the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, and the United Nations).

教学目标

了解国家预算“编制-审批-执行-调整-决算-审计”普适流程。理解各国别预算流程的宪法等法规规定、历史变迁、预算运行机制、发展与改革问题。掌握编制的基础性、审批与审计的约束性、调整的必要性。理解国家预算与其更高级形态——国际组织预算的递进性。深刻理解中国在国际组织预算中的交互作用。

教学方式

熟练利用AI大模型辅助搜集、整理与分析各流程数据。随堂提出问题、分析问题、解决问题。

课程简介（中文）

本课程设计为旅游学情境下的英语训练平台。课程将以主题形式展开，通过一轮轮的训练以培养学生的英语技能。每轮训练要求学生进行预习并参与课堂讨论。期中测试以小组合作为形式，期末测试以闭卷考为形式。出席率、课堂表现、作业都将作为最终考核成绩的组成部分。

课程简介（英文）

This course serves as a platform towards academic tourism training via English as well as English language training. Academic papers as well as chapters from both textbooks and academic books are collected for specific topics. At the same time, visual audio resources are availed for language training once every two weeks. Students are expected to get familiar with relevant academic topics and grow proficient in English. There are altogether 6 rounds of academic plus language training. For each round, students should be prepared by reading relevant texts and papers before each lecture and do homework after each language training class. Participation in class is also counted towards final grade.

教学目标

Students are expected to get familiar with step-by-step language training. Training habits are as important as language performance. In other words, students are expected to grow confident both in training zone and in performance zone. Towards the end of the term, students are expected to be proficient with English input and output in tourism scenarios.

教学方式

Lecture,seminar and teamwork are major teaching methods.

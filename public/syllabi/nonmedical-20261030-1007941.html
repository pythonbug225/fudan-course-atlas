课程简介（中文）

This course focuses on certain aspects of human behaviors, at both the individual and group level, which are important determinants of people’s lives in the workplace. In particular, it covers issues such as self-management, motivation, managing up, leading people and change management.

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on certain aspects of human behaviors, at both the individual and group level, which are important determinants of people’s lives in the workplace. In particular, it covers issues such as self-management, motivation, managing up, leading people and change management.

教学目标

Increase participants’ sensitivity towards the potential impact of human issues in the workplace; Help participants explore their own behavior and the impact that it can have on others; Provide participants with some tools to analyze organizational situations and design actions.

教学方式

The course is mainly based on cases, articles and lectures about specific issues, some role playing, a few self-evaluation tools and a lot of in-class dialog. In order to fully take advantage of each session it is extremely important that you are prepared to engage in a rich exchange of ideas during the classes. So prior to each class, read the assigned readings carefully and critically. Related teaching and learning activities Interactive Lectures with Discussions The instructor will explain and illustrate the key concepts and theories of organizational behavior by providing interactive presentations with Powerpoint slides and whiteboard writing. The instructor will also explain how to apply these concepts and theories by small case discussions. The instructor will invite and encourage the students to share their views and experiences in class discussions. In-class Exercises and Assignments Based on the content of specific topics, the instructor will ask the students to participate in some individual or group exercises (e.g., personality tests, decision- making tests, practical problem-solving exercises) to deepen students’ understanding of specific concepts and theories and to develop their application capability on specific topics. Students will be asked to share their exercise outcomes and thoughts, thereby enhancing their expression skills, listening skills, and multi-perspective thinking and creative thinking skills. Case Analysis and Discussion The instructor will distribute case-study assignments corresponding to specific topics to students one week before the class. Each student is required to prepare for the class by completing pre-class case reading, case analysis and group case discussion following the assigned questions. In class, each group is expected to actively participate in and contribute to the class discussions. The instructor will provide comments and summaries. Case analysis and discussion are great ways to integrate and apply knowledge and experiences. It is also a process in which different viewpoints and ideas collide and intermingle, thereby helping students to train their judgment, decisiveness, and logical thinking and creative thinking skills. Group Project The students will be allowed to form groups freely. It requires each group to present a case on the hot issue in organizational behavior (e.g., the maintenance and breach of psychological contract, conflict management, leadership style, team experience, employee motivation, organizational change), and appropriately apply the organizational behavior theories to analyze the case. This group project will facilitate the students’ active learning, and enhance their ability to integrate and apply business knowledge, to diagnose problems with multiple perspectives, and to solve problems creatively. It will also promote team coordination, team decision-making, team leadership, and conflict management. This group assignment will also help students to better understand behaviors in organizations. Self-Study Students are required to read the corresponding chapters in the recommended textbook and other readings, following the course schedule.

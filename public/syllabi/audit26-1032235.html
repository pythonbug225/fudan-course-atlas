课程简介（中文）

课程的教学目是，讲授较为系统的外交政策分析的理论与方法，旨在让学生掌握外交政策分析的基本技能，熟悉外交调研报告（如内参）的写作，培养学生对实际外交政策的理解、解释与预测。

课程简介（英文）

Despite the attempts by IR theorists to simplify the reality around us,international politics is an extremely complex issue. Why countries behave in a certain way not in others cannot be understood treating them like billiard balls pushed and pulled by mysterious forces. We can make sense of what we read in the news only if we understand the process behind the policies adopted by each country’s decisionmakers. Hence, the course explores how decisionmakers in different countries formulate and implement their foreign policies. It aims to provide the students with a crucially important set of notions and analytical tools that will increase their capability to study and explain international affairs. This will be done through lectures and in-class discussion of case studies.

教学目标

(1) By the end of the course students will be able to interpret foreign policy events and to compare, contrast and evaluate different interpretations and explanations;

(2) Detect the underlying assumptions behind foreign policy arguments;

(3) Recognize the interpretative possibilities in any given foreign policy event;

(4) Critically reflect upon and evaluate their own standpoints and those of others.

教学方式

The teaching method was conducted in the form of a combination of teacher lectures and student presentations.

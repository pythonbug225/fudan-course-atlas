课程简介（中文）

《组织学与影像结合的科研与临床》主要包括两大主题内容：人体组织学和医学影像学。前者是研究正常人体的显微结构及其相关功能的学科；而后者则是借助某种介质(如X射线、电磁场、超声波等)与人体的相互作用，把人体内部组织器官的结构、密度、功能等以影像方式表现出来，供医师根据影像提供的信息进行诊断和治疗，从而对人体健康状况进行评价的一门学科。本课程不是上述两块内容的简单叠加，而是将人体组织学与医学影像学进行多学科的理实一体的融合，这主要表现在：第一，结合组织学和影像学的最新研究进展，实现课程教学中专业知识与科研创新、临床诊疗的有效结合；第二，从价值引领上坚定学生爱国爱校的家国情怀和科学家精神，开拓学生的国际视野，培育学生的协作创新能力。

课程简介（英文）

Histology and Imaging in Research and Clinical Practice consists of two main topics: human histology and medical imaging. The former is the study of the structure of the normal human body under the microscope and its related functions; while the latter is a discipline that evaluates the health status of the human body by means of the interaction of a certain medium (e.g., X-rays, electromagnetic fields, ultrasound, etc.) with the human body, which expresses the structures, densities, and functions of the tissues and organs of the human body in the form of images for the physician to make a diagnosis and treatments based on information provided by the images. This course is not a simple superposition of the above two contents, but a multidisciplinary integration of human histology and medical imaging, which is mainly manifested in the following ways. Firstly, combining the latest research progress of histology and imaging, this course aims to realize the effective combination of professional knowledge, scientific research and innovation, clinical diagnosis and treatment in the course teaching, empowerec through AI. Secondly, it aims to firm up students' patriotic and university-loving national sentiment and the spirit of scientists in the value guidance, open up students' international vision, cultivating and developing students' international vision. Finally, it will strengthen students' patriotism, nationalism and scientists' spirit in terms of value guidance, develop students' international vision, and cultivate their collaborative and innovative ability.

教学目标

本课程的教学目标是将人体结构的组织学基础与临床影像手段的检测与评估相结合，旨在促进基础专业的研究生在课题设计上明确未来的临床应用、临床专业的研究生在课题设计中夯实组织学基础，结合组织学和影像学的最新进展，通过AI赋能促进学科交叉并最终提高研究生的科研能力和水平。同时，通过本课程的教学与讨论，培养和提升研究生在撰写研究综述方面的能力。

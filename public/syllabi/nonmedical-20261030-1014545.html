课程简介（中文）

该课程包含矩阵论、概率论、参数估计与优化方法三部分内容，其中线性代数介绍线性空间与线性变换，正交投影，矩阵计算，矩阵分解，线性方程组求解等内容；概率论包括随机变量、多维随机变量、交叉熵、KL散度、参数的最大似然估计和假设检验等内容；参数估计与优化算法介绍凸集、凸函数等基本概念，线性规划的基本性质、单纯形法、无约束与有约束条件下非线性优化问题的最优解的必要条件和充分条件，最速下降法、牛顿法等搜索算法，最小二乘法、拉格朗日乘数法等。

课程简介（英文）

The course consists of three parts: matrix theory, probability theory, parameter estimation and optimization methods, including linear algebra to introduce linear space and linear transformation, orthogonal projection, matrix calculation, matrix factorization, and linear equation system solving. Probability theory includes random variables, multidimensional random variables, cross-entropy, KL divergence, maximum likelihood estimation of parameters, and hypothesis testing. Parameter estimation and optimization algorithms introduce the basic concepts of convex sets and convex functions, the basic properties of linear programming, the simplex method, the necessary and sufficient conditions for the optimal solution of nonlinear optimization problems under unconstrained and constrained conditions, the search algorithms such as the fastest descent method and Newton's method, the least squares method, the Lagrange multiplier method, etc.

教学目标

通过本课程学习，学生将深入掌握线性代数、概率论、最优化理论的基础知识，奠定学习人工智能算法所需的数学基础，培养学生利用数学工具分析问题的能力；学生将通过完成相应的课程习题与项目作业，提高解决工程问题的能力，为在人工智能相关领域从事研究与开发工作打下坚实基础。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在介绍数据科学中的核心算法与计算方法。学生将学习数据预处理、降维、预测建模、聚类分析、优化与表示学习等关键任务的基本原理和实现方法。课程涵盖经典机器学习算法与现代深度学习方法，注重数学原理、算法思想与计算实践的结合，为进一步学习和应用数据科学与人工智能方法奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces core algorithms and computational methods in data science. Students will learn the fundamental principles and implementations of key tasks, including data preprocessing, dimensionality reduction, predictive modeling, clustering, optimization, and representation learning. The course covers both classical machine learning algorithms and modern deep learning methods, with an emphasis on connecting mathematical foundations, algorithmic thinking, and computational practice.

教学目标

  1. Understand the basic principles and mathematical foundations of commonly used algorithms in data science, including statistical analysis, model fitting, optimization, and computational complexity, and be able to analyze and compare algorithms from both statistical and computational perspectives.
  2. Be familiar with classical and modern data science algorithms, including data preprocessing, dimensionality reduction, regression, classification, clustering, probabilistic inference, optimization, and deep learning.
  3. Develop the ability to implement, evaluate, and apply data science algorithms using Python, and to select appropriate methods based on the characteristics of data, computational requirements, and practical problems.

教学方式

1. Combine classroom lectures with discussions.
2. Assign programming homework to allow students to implement and apply algorithms hands-on.
3. Arrange group projects to allow students to solve real-world data science problems.

教学内容与进度安排

  1. Course introduction, review of mathematical and Python coding foundations
  2. Data preprocessing
  3. Dimensionality reduction algorithms
  4. Linear models and model fitting
  5. Regularized linear models
  6. Classification algorithms
  7. Optimization and classical learning algorithms
  8. Midterm
  9. Clustering and mixture models
  10. Variational inference
  11. Optimal transport algorithms
  12. Neural networks
  13. Training deep neural networks
  14. Advanced neural network architectures
  15. Representation and self-supervised learning
  16. Deep generative models

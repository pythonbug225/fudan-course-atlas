1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 生物医药是落实《健康中国行动（2019—2030年）》和推动“中国创造”的重要支撑。药物设计与药物发现是新药创制的核心前沿学科。本课程作为国家级一流本科课程“药物设计学”的研究生进阶课程，由具有丰富实践经验的教师授课，系统介绍药物设计的基本理念、方法学及最新进展，注重科研思维与分子设计能力的培养。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Biomedicine is a critical pillar for advancing the Healthy China Initiative (2019–2030) and promoting the transition from “Made in China” to “Created in China.” Drug design and drug discovery constitute the core frontier disciplines of innovative drug development. As an advanced graduate-level course building upon the nationally recognized first-class undergraduate course Drug Design , this course is taught by faculty with extensive practical experience and provides a systematic introduction to the fundamental principles, methodologies, and recent advances in drug design, with an emphasis on developing scientific thinking and molecular design capabilities.

掌握药物的筛选途径、药物靶点、类药性、成药性、基于结构等药物设计的基本原理、方法论及思想；熟悉代表性药物的发现历史、经验教训及学科的发展方向，药物设计及药物发现的新技术、新方法、新策略；了解药物设计及药物发现的最新进展及趋势。

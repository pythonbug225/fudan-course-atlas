论文写作

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是外文学院英文系系列写作课程的有机组成部分。先修课程包括一年级到三年级的必修课《写作入门》、《多文体写作》、《学术写作》。在先修课程基础上，本课程主要讲授高阶的学术论文写作。由于学科特色和差异，本课程分为文学班与语言学班，此教学大纲适用于文学班。 本门课程旨在培养英语专业本科生毕业论文的写作能力。通过剖析论文的各部分结构、讲解论文写作的过程、体例、以及基本研究方法范式，本门课程帮助学生学会寻找相关文献、评价学术资源、批评性回顾、撰写文献回顾等基本论文技巧。 专业定位：本门课程属于高年级英语专业必修课，旨在培养高阶的英语学术论文写作技能、思辨性阅读与分析写作技能，并培训初步的科研技能，更好服务于本科学位论文写作。此外，作为大学四年最后一门英语专业必修课，本课程定位为衔接了英语专业本科教育与更高一级的研究生学习的“出口课程”。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an integral part of the series of writing courses in the Department of English at the School of Foreign Languages. Prerequisite courses include required classes from the first to the third year: 'Introduction to Writing,' 'Multi-Genre Writing,' and 'Academic Writing.' Building on these foundational courses, this course primarily teaches advanced academic paper writing. Due to the distinct characteristics and differences in disciplines, this course is divided into a Literature class and a Linguistics class, and this syllabus is applicable to the Literature class.The aim of this course is to develop the writing skills necessary for undergraduate students majoring in English to complete their graduation theses. By analyzing the structure of various parts of a paper, explaining the writing process, formatting, and basic research methodologies, this course assists students in learning to find relevant literature, evaluate academic resources, conduct critical reviews, and write literature reviews, among other essential paper-writing skills.Professional Orientation: This course is a required course for advanced English majors, aimed at cultivating high-level English academic writing skills, critical reading and analytical writing skills, and preliminary research skills, thereby better serving the writing of undergraduate dissertations. Additionally, as the last compulsory English course in the four-year university program, this course is designed as a 'capstone course' that bridges undergraduate education in English studies and higher-level graduate study.

教学目标

Identify the requirements of a research paper and the structure of a B.A. thesis in English studies. 明确研究型论文的要求，知晓英语本科毕业论文的结构。 Distinguish the characteristics of the academic genre from other genres; Recognize and reproduce the linguistic features of academic thesis writing, and the mechanics of academic writing (e.g. citation styles, signposting, etc.). Label and identify the rhetorical features of the composing parts of an academic thesis, and able to locate and evaluate the rhetorical moves in academic essays. 区分学术体裁有别于其他文体的特征；认出并能自主使用学术论文的语言特征、学术写作的细节特征（包括如何引用、如何组织行文）。说出、辨别学术论文不同部分的修辞特征，并能够找出、评估学术论文的修辞语步。 Identify necessary steps of write a thesis and the structure of a thesis; Distinguish the purposes of different parts of a thesis; 说出学术论文写作的必要步骤以及论文的结构组成；辨别论文不同部分的写作目的。 Find and select appropriate sources, evaluate and incorporate them into a critical review of past research on a selected topic with appropriate citation. 找到、选取合适的资源，评估、整合资源，并能使用适切的引用，针对预选的话题写出分析性文献回顾。 Analyze materials and writing samples in view of the content, tone, lexico-grammar, organization, rhetorical moves, etc.; Assess the effective ness of peer students’ academic writing, comment on peer writing from the lexico-grammatical, rhetorical, organizational, aspects. 从内容、语气、词汇句法、行文组织、修辞语步等方面分析课程提供的写作样本；评估同伴写作的有效性；能从词汇句法、修辞论证、行文结构等方面对同伴写作进行评价。 Formulate feasible research questions; Propose a feasible research plan and writeup a research proposal with a clear structure, organization and conforms to the academic writing style. 构想出可行的研究问题；提出可行的研究计划；写出一篇结构清晰、篇章合理、符合学术写作风格的研究计划书。

教学方式

鉴于本门课程的混合式教学特色，实行单周面授（平行班），双周自行观看视频的形式。 为了进一步满足同学们撰写毕业论文的需求，本门课程开设了平行班。郑老师带的班级主要涵盖语言学话题，寿老师带的班级主要涵盖文学话题。单周面授不同方向的同学进入不同老师的班级，双周观看视频则是大班统一进度。

互联网与社会研究

2026春 · SOCI30033.01 · 桂勇、黄荣贵

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

通过研究实践型教学，本课程的修课者将了解“互联网与社会”这一研究领域的重要议题与前沿的社会学大数据分析方法，初步具备利用互联网资料进行社会研究的能力。 本课程的学习目的包括： 学生能够掌握python编程语言的基本知识，熟悉常见的数据结构、控制结构、函数和类对象的编写，了解计算机语言和社会科学研究术语之间的逻辑对应关系；能在大模型的辅助下完成可运行的编程任务。 培养学生的学习能力：使他们学会解决编程过程中所遇到的问题、学会搜索拓展学习的资源，尤其是发现、跟踪和学习计算社会学新工具的能力（以避免仅熟悉课堂所讲授的知识点）；能更加关心与这门课程相关的内容，如对大数据分析、人工智能和大语言模型感兴趣。 培养学生利用新兴的计算工具服务社会学实证研究的能力，学会从社会学研究的目标和需要出发，通过编程语言、大模型或其他新工具对非结构化数据清洗，形成结构化数据。 鼓励学生思考计算方法和传统研究范式的关系，形成自身关于计算社会学范式的理解；学生能够从社会学研究设计的视角出发，思考计算方法和研究设计之间的互强关系与紧张关系，以及两者整合的可能性和方向。 学会如何成为更好的学生，能够负责任地组织授课内容、讲义和延伸学习资料，完成自己的课后作业；更加关注计算社会学科的前沿发展方向，以及数智技术发展对社会发展以及研究范式所带来的冲击和影响。 培养学生跨学科沟通能力，使得他们具备同时从计算机科学和社会科学的视角描述社会问题和解决方案的能力，为他们适应当代社会的数智化转型提供启蒙。 本课程期望修读者： 对大数据分析技术、互联网与社会议题、大模型应用等具有兴趣。 能积极主动参与课堂和课后的讨论与互动。 根据教学进度的安排，准时提交各阶段布置的作业并提交eLearning课程站点。 学期结束时提交一份基于互联网数据的完整的研究报告。 曾修读Python语言相关课程或基本掌握Python编程语言。

课程简介（英文）

By completing this practice-oriented course, students will understand the important sociological issues and cutting-edge big data analysis methods in the field of "Internet and Society", and acquire the ability to conduct social research using Internet data or digital footprint data. The course requires students to have a certain knowledge in the Python programming language. The learning objectives of include: Students can master the foundamental knowledge of the Python programming language, be familiar with common data types, control structures, function and class object, and understand the logical correspondence between computer languages and social science research terms; can complete executable programming tasks with the assistance of large language models. Cultivate students' learning ability: enabling them to solve problems encountered in programming, learn to search for further learning resources, especially the ability to discover, track and learn new tools in computational sociology (to avoid being limited to only the knowledge points taught in this class); cultivate interests in the topics related to this course, such as big data analysis, artificial intelligence and large language models. Cultivate students' ability to use emerging computational tools to serve sociological empirical research, and learn to clean unstructured data into structured data through programming languages, large language models or other new tools, depending on the goals and needs of sociological research. Encourage students to think about the relationship between computational methods and traditional research paradigms, and form their own understanding of the computational sociology paradigm; students can critically understand the mutual reinforcement and tension between computational methods and research design from the perspective of sociological research design, as well as the possibility and direction of their integration. Learn how to be better students, be able to responsibly organize course content, handouts and extended learning materials, and complete their own homework; pay attention to the cutting-edge development of computational sociology and the impact and influence of digital intelligence technology development on social development and research paradigms. Cultivate students' interdisciplinary communication skills, enabling them to describe social problems and solutions from both computer science and social science perspectives, providing enlightenment for them to adapt to the digital transformation of contemporary society. This course expects students to: Have an interest in big data analysis technology, the Internet and social issues, and the application of large language models. Be able to actively participate in discussions and interactions in class. Submit the assignments in a timely manner according to the teaching schedule and submit them on the eLearning course site. Submit a complete research report based on digital data at the end of the semester. Have taken courses related to the Python programming language or have a basic grasp of Python programming.

教学目标

修读者了解“互联网与社会”研究领域的重要议题 修读者了解前沿的社会学大数据分析方法 修读者初步了解大模型辅助的社会研究方法和路径 修读者初步掌握Python语言及在大数据分析中的应用，能借助大模型实现高效编程 修读者具备利用互联网资料进行社会研究的基本能力 修读者对大模型所带来的新议题进行初步的批判性分析

教学方式

教学同时兼顾课堂讲解、小组讨论、论文写作等内容。其中，论文写作是在授课教师的密切指导下进行的实践型研究。 如配备助教，助教的职责包括： 辅助授课老师准备和完善教学资料 解答编程过程中遇到的问题 课程相关知识点的答疑

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在向学生介绍经济增长与发展前沿研究所探讨的问题及使用的工具，并帮助他们为未来在该领域开展独立研究（无论是政策导向还是学术导向）做好准备。本课程涵盖了经济增长与发展领域的重要议题，并聚焦于两个相互关联的核心问题： 为何人均收入在不同时期和不同国家之间存在如此巨大的差异？ 为何经济活动在不同部门之间的分布，会在不同时期和不同国家之间存在差异？

课程简介（英文）

This course covers important topics in economic growth and development. It focuses on two distinct but related questions: why does income per capita differ so much over time and across countries? And why does the allocation of economic activity across broad sectors change over time and across countries? The goal of the course is to introduce the students to the questions asked and the tools used in frontier research of economic growth and development, and prepare them to pursue their own research agenda in the field, whether it’s policy or academically oriented.

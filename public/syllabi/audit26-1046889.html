课程简介（中文）

专业实习为学生提供了宝贵的亲身体验，让他们切实感受真实的工作状态。它起到了连接大学正规教育与职场生活的桥梁作用，促进学科知识的整合与综合运用，并推动人际交往与沟通能力的发展。

本科劳动教育融入专业实习之中，使学生能够通过社会实践和专业实践，锤炼意志品质，强化吃苦耐劳的精神。通过专业实习，学生掌握必要的劳动技能，做到手脑并用、知行合一，具备团队协作能力，并提升创造性劳动技能。

课程简介（英文）

An internship provides students with an invaluable hands-on experience of what an actual job would be like. It serves to bridge formal university education and work life, facilitate the integration and synthesis of subject knowledge, and the development of interpersonal and communication skills.

Undergraduate labor work education is infused into professional internship, and students are able to strengthen their will and the quality of hard work through social practice and professional practice. Through the professional internship, students master the necessary labor skills, use hands and brains, unite knowledge and action, and have the ability to work in teams and enhance creative labor skills.

教学目标

Through the internship, students can

  • Obtain valuable job-search skills such as CV writing and interviewing skills 
  • Relate general business knowledge in a coherent manner and apply methods and theories learnt in classes
  • Gain work experience and transferable skills
  • Learn one’s strengths and weaknesses by receiving feedback from supervisors and colleagues

 Develop one’s overall maturity by strengthening self-discipline and sense of responsibility

教学方式

On-site training, one-on-one consultation

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 – week 16 Internship

Week 17-Week 18 Final report submission

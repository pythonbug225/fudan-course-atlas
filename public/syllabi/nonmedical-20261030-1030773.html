投资学

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

学生将以投资组合管理人的角色来学习投资学相关的理论和实证知识。第一，课程将详细介绍资本市场主要的投资产品，市场上不同参与者所扮演的角色，以及风险和收益衡量方法。第二，课程将详细讲解资产定价相关的理论。如：投资组合理论（Portfolio Theory），资本资产定价模型（CAPM），套利定价模型（APT）和有效市场假说（EMH）。第三，学生将学习固定收益产品，权益产品和衍生品的定价模型。第四，教师将展示市场主要参与者公募基金，私募证券基金和ETF基金所扮演的角色和相应的评价模型。最后，学生将通过课程项目设计运用所学的理论和实证知识构建自己的投资组合。

课程简介（英文）

This course will equip students with both theoretical knowledge and empirical skills needed to become portfolio managers. Firstly, this course starts with a comprehensive introduction to the basics of investments including various investment products, roles of different market players, and measures for risk and return. Secondly, students will learn the various asset pricing theories including Portfolio Theory, Capital Asset Pricing Model, Arbitrage Pricing Theory, and Efficient Market Hypothesis. Thirdly, instructor will demonstrate the valuation methods for bonds, equities, and derivatives. Fourthly, the instructor will introduce the role of mutual funds, hedge funds, and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) as well as the relevant evaluation models. Finally, students will apply the theoretical and empirical skills to construct their own investment portfolios through group project.

教学目标

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to: Understand the function of various investment products, role of major market players, and measure for risk and return. Explain major asset pricing theories including Portfolio Theory, Capital Asset Pricing Model, Arbitrage Pricing Theory, and Efficient Market Hypothesis. Apply the valuation method to calculate the fair value for bonds, equities, and various derivative products. Evaluate performance of mutual funds, hedge funds, and exchange-traded funds (ETFs). • Construct their own investment portfolios and evaluate the performance.

教学方式

Instructors give lectures in class. Students participate in discussions and work on assignments.

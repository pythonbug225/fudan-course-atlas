课程简介（中文）

本课程将涵盖博弈论中的四种基本情形： 1）完全信息下的同时行动博弈； 2）完全信息下的序贯行动博弈； 3）不完全信息下的同时行动博弈； 4）不完全信息下的序贯行动博弈。 课程重点将主要放在应用方面，不过也会讨论一些一般性原则，以便更严谨地处理战略情形。 课程将通过日常生活和商业领域的不同实例来展示所讨论概念的重要性和实用性。

课程简介（英文）

This course will cover four fundamental scenarios in game theory: simultaneous-move game under complete information; sequential-move game under complete information; simultaneous-move game under incomplete information; sequential-move game under incomplete information. The focus of the course will mainly be applied, although some general principles will be discussed in order to address the strategic situations more rigorously. Different examples, ranging from daily life and business fields, will show the importance and usefulness of the concepts discussed in class.

教学目标

The main goal of this course is to develop your skills to employ game theory to examine interactive decision problems, i.e., situations whereby every agent’s actions have payoff consequences on other agents. In particular, by the end of this course, you will be able to: analyze the available strategies for each decision-maker; solve related equilibria; predict and describe how a game is going to be played by the players (i.e., their equilibrium behavior); construct the structure of interactive decision problems; model economics and business topics covered in other courses using game theory.

教学方式

Multimedia teaching is adopted. Students are required to preview before class, take notes in class, and do exercises after class. During the teaching process, real cases will be discussed to help students better grasp the basic concepts and logic of the course.

课程简介（英文）

C.A.R.E. stands for Culture, Art, Responsibilities and Ethics, which will extend beyond trainings of common skill set and to cover a wild range of subject areas, it strives to open students’ minds, inspire and motivate them, and to support in their transition to become a responsible, creative individual in the finance world.

教学目标

By the end of this semester, students are expected to: Experience hands-on urban ecological restoration, and gain a deeper understanding of how micro-interventions contribute to macro-ecological resilience and sustainable urban systems. Show an increasing awareness of active, creative and healthy lifestyles. Experience the trendy sports, and gain a deeper understanding of how body mechanics contribute to the execution of complex movements and strategies in both sports.

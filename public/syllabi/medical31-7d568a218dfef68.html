1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程基本内容包括常用药物中文名称、人体解剖结构的中文名称、八大系统相关器官的中文名称，常见病的中文名称、一些常见病主要症状及其体征的汉语表达、日常会话交流、中文语法、日常词汇用法等。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The basic content of this course includes Chinese names of commonly‑used medicines, Chinese terms for human anatomical structures, Chinese designations for organs of the eight major body systems, Chinese names of common diseases, Chinese expressions for major symptoms and signs of certain common illnesses, daily conversational communication, Chinese grammar, and usage of everyday vocabulary.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Medical Chinese I is a compulsory course designed for MBBS students in preparation for their future clinical observation and internship in hospitals. The course focuses on medical Chinese and practical everyday Chinese communication skills. Its primary aim is to lay a solid foundation in Chinese for MBBS students before they begin clinical training and internships in Chinese hospitals, enabling them to communicate basically and effectively in Chinese with healthcare professionals, patients, and their family members.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

Teaching Methods and Classroom Activities 1. Pre-class Preparation Students preview the assigned learning materials before class. 2. Warm-up Introduction and instruction of medical Chinese vocabulary, including new words and expressions, followed by explanations of dialogues or texts related to medical contexts. 3. Pair Work Students practice dialogues and communication with each other. 4. Role-play Students participate in role-playing activities based on different medical scenarios. 5. Group Discussion Students discuss assigned topics in small groups and practice using medical Chinese in communication. 6. In-class Quiz Short quizzes are conducted to assess students’ understanding and learning progress. 7. In-class Practice Students complete various classroom exercises to reinforce the vocabulary, expressions, and communication skills covered in the lesson. 8. Homework Students complete assigned exercises and learning tasks after class. 9. Guided Extracurricular Reading Students read short articles on medical science and health education outside of class. Key medical terminology is explained, together with relevant medical knowledge, to enhance students’ understanding of medical Chinese and their ability to comprehend medical-related materials.

课程简介（中文）

通过对数篇英文文献的引介，旨在让学生了解历史地理领域常用专业术语、常用英文工具书、期刊；通过引导学生对文本的阅读理解和讨论，培养学生独立思考的能力。专业英语重在实践，由论文研读、研讨以及翻译实践三个环节组成，阅读材料主要为论文，除了两篇必读，其余论文每学期作相应的调整。

课程简介（英文）

The course is designed to help students familiarize with professional terminology and commonly used English-language reference tools and academic journals in the field of historical geography. Through guided reading and analysis of selected papers, combined with discussion and translation practice, the course aims to develop students’ critical thinking and practical application skills. It consists of three main components: research paper reading and analysis, topic-based discussion, and translation exercises. The main reading materials are drawn from published academic articles; in addition to two mandatory readings, the remaining selections may be adjusted according to the specific focus or context of the course.

教学目标

引导学生阅读有关历史地理专业方面的一些英文文献，在课堂上讲授文中疑难问题，引导学生就相关问题展开讨论。通过本门课的讲授和讨论，拟让学生掌握一定的专业词汇和英文论文写作的一些规范，开展翻译实践的初步训练，培养学生阅读英文文献的兴趣。

教学方式

讲授+讨论

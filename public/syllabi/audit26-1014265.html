课程简介（中文）

本课程讲授内容分为通论、分论两个部分。

“通论”部分主要探讨工程伦理的基本概念、基本理论以及工程实践过程中面对的共性问题。第1章和第2章分析工程和伦理的概念、工程实践中的伦理问题以及处理工程伦理问题的基本原则。第3章至第5章分别从责任伦理与伦理责任、利益分配与公正、环境伦理与环境正义三个方面探讨所有工程实践都可能面对的一些共性问题。第6章重点探讨了工程师的职业伦理。

“分论”部分主要有针对性地分析不同的工程领域面对的特殊问题，分析不同工程领域的工程伦理规范。分论分别涉及信息工程、化学工程、环境工程、生物医药工程和纳米技术等具体的工程领域。

课程简介（英文）

The content of this course is divided into two main parts: General Principles and Specific Applications.

 

The General Principles section primarily explores the fundamental concepts and theories of engineering ethics, as well as the common issues encountered in engineering practice. Chapters 1 and 2 analyze the concepts of engineering and ethics, ethical issues arising in engineering practice, and the basic principles for addressing engineering ethics problems. Chapters 3 to 5 respectively explore common issues encountered in all engineering practices from three perspectives: Responsibility Ethics and Ethical Responsibilities, Benefit Distribution and Justice, and Environmental Ethics and Environmental Justice. Chapter 6 focuses specifically on Professional Ethics for Engineers.

 

The Specific Applications section provides targeted analysis of the unique issues faced by different engineering fields and examines the specific engineering ethics codes relevant to those fields. This section covers specific engineering domains such as Information Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, and Nanotechnology, etc.

教学目标

1.深入理解工程伦理相关的概念和理论，培养化学等学科相关从业者的工程伦理意识。

2.系统把握工程伦理相关的基本规范，掌握具体工程领域的伦理规范要求。

3.全面提高工程伦理的决策能力，提升解决工程实践中的复杂伦理问题的能力。

中国商事组织法

2025秋 · LAW60028.01 · 葛伟军

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍中国商事组织法律制度，聚焦三大核心模块：（1）商法基础，解析中国商事法律体系与营商环境框架；（2）合伙企业法，涵盖普通/有限合伙设立、运营及责任分配；（3）公司法，深入剖析有限责任公司与股份有限公司的治理结构、股东权利及董事责任。通过案例分析与比较法视角，帮助学生掌握中国商事组织的法律运作机制，为投资创业奠定实务基础。课程以英文授课，适配国际学生知识背景。

课程简介（英文）

This course systematically introduces the legal system of commercial organizations in China, focusing on three core modules: (1) Fundamentals of Commercial Law, analyzing the framework of China's commercial legal system and business environment; (2) Partnership Enterprise Law, covering the establishment, operation and liability allocation of general and limited partnerships; (3) Company Law, providing an in-depth analysis of the governance structure, shareholders' rights and directors' responsibilities of limited liability companies and joint stock limited companies. Through case analysis and a comparative law perspective, help students master the legal operation mechanism of Chinese commercial organizations and lay a practical foundation for investment and entrepreneurship. The courses are taught in English, which is suitable for the knowledge background of international students.

教学目标

本课程旨在帮助国际学生：（1）系统构建中国商法核心框架，理解营商环境与法律体系互动关系；（2）精准掌握合伙企业设立、内部治理及无限/有限责任划分规则；（3）深度解析公司法资本制度及公司法人治理结构（含股东会、董事会、监事会）；（4）提升实务能力，通过中外制度比较与典型案例，培养中国商事组织设立、风险识别及法律纠纷预防的实操技能，为跨境投资与创业提供法律决策支持。 This course aims to assist international students in: (1) systematically constructing the core framework of Chinese commercial law and understanding the interactive relationship between the business environment and the legal system; (2) accurately mastering the rules for the establishment of partnership enterprises, internal governance, and the division of unlimited and limited liability; (3) in-depth analysis of the capital system of the Company Law and the corporate governance structure (including the shareholders’ meeting, the board of directors, and the board of supervisors); (4) enhancing practical capabilities, through comparisons of Chinese and foreign systems and typical cases, by cultivating practical skills in the establishment of Chinese commercial organizations, risk identification, and prevention of legal disputes, and providing legal decision support for cross-border investment and entrepreneurship.

教学方式

课堂授课，学生发表 Classroom teaching, student presentation

教学内容与进度安排

教学内容主要包括三块内容。第一，商法基础，涉及中国商事立法体系、市场主体类型及商事登记、商业名称和商行为等。第二，合伙企业法，涉及普通合伙和有限合伙的设立程序、财产分配规则、合伙人责任边界等。第三，公司法，涉及公司类型、资本制度、公司治理、公司社会责任、解散和清算、公司并购等。 The teaching content mainly consists of three parts. First, the foundation of commercial law, which involves China's commercial legislative system, types of market entities and commercial registration, commercial names and commercial activities, etc. Second, the Partnership Enterprise Law involves the establishment procedures of general partnerships and limited partnerships, the rules for property distribution, and the boundaries of partners' responsibilities, etc. Third, Company Law, which covers company types, capital systems, corporate governance, corporate social responsibility, dissolution and liquidation, and corporate mergers and

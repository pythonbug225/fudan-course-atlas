中国新闻的历史、理论和实践

2025秋 · JCM60003.01 · 洪兵

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本研究生课程以媒介社会学为框架，介绍中国新闻事业的历史、理论与实践。课程围绕三个主要主题展开：首先，理解爱国主义、启蒙精神与党媒传统作为中国新闻业的重要支柱；其次，深入探讨规范中国媒体机构与社交媒体的相关政策与法律；最后，研究中国新闻实践的多种模式、叙事方式以及新闻伦理。课程将批判性地分析中国新闻业的发展与面临的挑战，课程形式包括讲座、研讨、学生展示与课堂讨论。

课程简介（英文）

This graduate-level course introduces the history, theory, and practice of Chinese journalism within the framework of media sociology. The course explores three main themes: Firstly, to understand patriotism, enlightenment, and party journalism as significant pillars of Chinese journalism. Then it delves into policies and laws regulating Chinese media organizations and social media. Lastly it studies various patterns of media practice, narratives, and journalistic ethics in China. The course critically analyzes the developments and challenges in Chinese journalism, examining the interplay between commanding journalism, market-driven journalism, and journalism for public interest in contemporary China. The course includes lectures, seminars, student presentations, and discussions.

教学目标

Course Objectives: Provide students with insights into key issues and trends in the contemporary Chinese media industry. Instruct students to analyze Chinese media cases from a theoretical perspective within the context of China.

教学方式

Lectures, seminars, student presentations, and discussions.

教学内容与进度安排

Topic I – The Rise of Chinese Journalism and Party Journalism: Patriotism, Enlightenment, and Mass Propaganda Weeks 1 to 3: Introduction: Traditions as a Profound Legacy Weeks 4 to 6: Topic II – Media Laws and Media Policies Weeks 7 to 11: Topic III – Media Practice&Media Ethics Week 12 to Week 16: Workshop – Covering China: from the Perspective of an International Media Company In-class presentation Field Trip to Sixth Tone and other media companies

教学参考资料

因为新闻传播业态与传播技术的迅速发展，教学参考材料包括新闻报道、相关研究报告及论文等，在学期中也将不断更新，然后提前提交选课学生。

课程简介（中文）

本阅读课程帮助英语专业的一年级学生为未来在该领域的学习奠定必要的基础。

课程简介（英文）

This reading course helps 1-st year students of the English programme lay the necessary foundation for future studies in this field.

教学目标

First, by having students read articles or chapters from books that discuss important ideas or key issues of major cultural periods of the West, it helps them acquire a basic understanding of Western thought and its development. Second, in reading these pieces, which are often written in the best style of English prose, students will be able to improve their own skills in reading and writing in the English language.

教学方式

课堂讲解小组讨论相结合 Assignments will be posted and submitted at eLearning. The exams will test you on both language skills (correct use of words, comprehension of sentences and passages, etc.) and the understanding of important ideas and concepts.

教学内容与进度安排

根据自编教材课文顺序，要求学生掌握课文相关背景知识、核心论点及逻辑、较难的语言用法。 Week 1-2 The Idea of a University Week 3-4 The Way of the Greeks Week 6-7 The Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire Week 8-9 The Decline and Fall of Literature Week 9 Mid-term Exam Week 10-11 Courtly Love Week 12-13 What is Humanism Week 14 Salt Week 15-16 Progress

考核构成

  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 30.0% 根据试卷要求用英文回答
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 40.0% 根据试卷要求用英文回答
  • 汇报Presentation 25.0% 每人两次，一次关于课文词汇，一次关于课程内容
  • 出勤Attendance 5.0%

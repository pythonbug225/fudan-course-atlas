课程简介（中文）

The Overseas Module explores the intersection between the financial and production economies in investigating how the finance industry impacts entrepreneurship and innovation. By studying early stage investing, venture capital, and the entrepreneurial process in its different phases, this OM provides a hands-on experience in one of the most vibrant entrepreneurial regions in the world. Formal lectures will address both theory and practice surrounding angel/venture capital from the perspectives of both investors and entrepreneurs. Company visits will show students the best practices and environments that have fostered some of the most innovative firms in the world. This is augmented with discussions and reflection as well as cultural immersion.

教学目标

1.Practical Application: Students will be able to apply their specialist knowledge and skills to realworld problems, with a focus on China-specific applications of global best practices. 2.Global Perspective: Students will attain a global perspective on the finance industry working with faculty and industry leaders from around the world, both in the classroom and through study abroad. 3.Professionalism: Students will consistently demonstrate professional and ethical conduct as well as high-level communication, teamwork, leadership and problem-solving skills. 4.Responsibility: Students will work and conduct themselves in accordance with the highest sense of professional and social responsibility, aiming to have a positive impact on the industry and community.

教学方式

Lectures, Reading, Discussions, Exercises and Examination.

教学内容与进度安排

1 Students will sit for oneseminar and discussion.Then, they will split into groups to: prepare bio information for key lectures and do due diligence and prepare Q&A for lectures and company visits. 2 Active participation is expected throughout, including attendance, preparation for meetings, and engagement in breakout discussions

课程简介（中文）

本课程面向已完成初级阶段学习的国际学生，系统提升综合汉语能力。课程以真实生活、校园与基础商务情境为核心，融合词汇与语法拓展、语篇理解与情境表达训练，强化实际交际与跨文化沟通能力。采用以学生为中心的任务互动式方法，融入文化要素与AI 智能辅助，提供个性化学习支持，构建以学生为中心的数据驱动学习体验。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for learners who have acquired approximately 500–600 Chinese words, completed around 100 hours of Chinese study, or passed HSK Level 3. It aims to enhance learners’ overall Chinese language proficiency and daily communication skills. The course content is organized around practical topics from everyday life, helping students systematically develop commonly used vocabulary and core sentence patterns, and build confidence in real communicative situations. Cultural components are integrated to deepen learners’ understanding of Chinese society and support their adaptation to study and life in China. In addition, intelligent learning support is incorporated to create a student-centered, data-driven learning experience tailored to individual progress.

教学目标

This course aims to further strengthen learners’ overall Chinese language skills, including reading, listening, speaking, and character writing. Grounded in the communicative approach, it provides diverse materials and practice opportunities to help students apply what they’ve learned. By the end of the course, learners are expected to: Recognize 200–250 new words, expanding their vocabulary to around 800 words in total Master 40–50 essential grammar structures commonly used in daily communication Confidently express themselves across 11 practical and relevant topics, such as food, travel, health, and work Improve reading pace, comprehension, and overall literacy in Chinese Build foundational speaking skills for everyday real-life interactions Develop a deeper understanding of Chinese customs, values, and social practices through integrated cultural content

教学方式

教学方式：本课程以交际型语言教学法（CLT）为核心，强调“以学为本”的教学理念，融合任务型教学、情境教学、文化导入、产出导向式教学以及AI辅助教学等多种方式，系统提升学生的语言运用能力与文化适应力。

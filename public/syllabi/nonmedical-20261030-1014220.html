课程简介（中文）

This course is intended to help international students in the Fanhai International School of Finance develop communicative competence through systematic, sustained, and all-around training of four skills (speaking, listening, reading and writing) via vocabulary collection, grammar acquirement, texts comprehension, discussions, presentations, activities and assignments. Organised topics around the themes of study, work, social and travel, the course build confidence through activities based on everyday situations. Learning tips throughout the course help students develop the skills they need to understand and be understood. Students are encouraged to get them interacting with native speakers.

教学目标

Through this course, students can have a basic understanding of Chinese language and are capable to hold simple conversations of daily life such as exchanging personal information, making appointments, ordering food, shopping, talking about one’s health, hobbies, plans, the seasons and the weather, etc. Moreover, this course will help students to raise their awareness of developing effective learning habits, when working on a language as exotic as Chinese. After the completion, students are expected to reach the following specific goals: • Students will learn about 200 new words and 40 language points. • Listening: Students will be able to grasp the main idea and important details of basic conversations and reports. • Speaking: Students will be able to give a 2-3minutes talk/presentation with moderate fluency on a given topic. • Reading: Students will be able to read pinyin fluently as well as recognize over 100 characters, comprehending or interpreting texts with an accuracy of 75%. • Writing: Students will be able to make a dialogue of at least about 60 words within 30-60 minutes. The dialogue should be grammatically correct, well-developed and coherent.

教学方式

Face to face teaching

教学内容与进度安排

Class Schedule Week 1&2 Phonetics refreshment and summary Lesson1 How have you been lately Daily greetings, exchanging names Week 2&3 Lesson2 Which country do you come from Introducing others, identifying items, asking about one’s likes Week 3&4 Lesson3 How many people are there in your family Introducing one’s family, talking about one’s age and profession, entertaining guests Week 5&6 Lesson4 What time do you have class tomorrow Talking about studying and major, making an appointment, asking about time Week 6&7 Lesson5 Happy birthday to you Talking about the date, expressing regret, congratulating someone on his/her birthday Week 8&9 Lesson6 The library is to the north of the cafeteria Describing locations, asking for directions, consoling someone, asking about new words, expressing not hearing clearly or understanding Week 10&11 Lesson7 How much is half a kilo of apples Asking about the price, making a request and giving permission, settling a bill, praise and response Week 12&13 Lesson8 I am not feeling well at all Urging someone to do something, asking/talking about someone’s health, expressing a need, expressing a possibility Week 14&15 Lesson9 It’s getting cool Talking about the seasons and the weather, talking about one’s plan, suggesting an activity, talking about transportation Week 16&17 Lesson10 Merry Christmas Talking about something that has happened, talking about someone who has changed, holiday greetings, passing on someone’s regards Week 18 Examination Speaking Test including repeating and answering the questions Listening Comprehension including characters recognition Reading Comprehension including vocabulary and grammar tests

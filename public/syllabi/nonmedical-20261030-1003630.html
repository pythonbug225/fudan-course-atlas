课程简介（中文）

《大问题：阅读与辩论》（Big Questions: Readings and Debate，课程代码 FORE110131）是一门英文授课、2 学分、每周 2 学时的通识选修课。课程围绕政治哲学六大核心议题——“正义、秩序、繁荣、自由、政体、文明”——展开阅读、研讨与辩论，旨在帮助学生掌握经典理论脉络，培养批判思维、论证策略与英文学术表达能力。 教学形式采用“理论讲授 ↔ 研讨／辩论”交替模式：鲍刚胜教授阐释各议题的历史脉络与分析框架；何晶副教授训练论证构建、证据运用与修辞策略。学生须预习指定原典（如 Sandel《Justice》、Fukuyama《Political Order》、Mill《On Liberty》、Huntington《Clash of Civilizations》），课堂以小组陈述、跨组辩论和问答深化理解。 考核比例：出勤 10 ％；课堂参与与短讲 20 ％；小组展示 30 ％；期末政治哲学演说 40 ％。完成课程后，学生应能：①精准阐释各理论流派的核心主张；②就公共议题构建基于证据、逻辑严谨的论证；③用英语进行高质量的批判性演讲与辩论。

课程简介（英文）

Big Questions: Readings and Debate (course code FORE110131) is a 2-credit, English-medium general-education elective meeting two hours per week. It integrates lecture and debate practice around six political-philosophy modules—Justice, Order, Prosperity, Liberty, Polity, and Civilization—so that students acquire a structured map of major traditions and controversies while sharpening critical thinking and argumentative English. Delivery alternates between theory lectures by Prof. Gangsheng Bao, who situates each module historically and analytically, and seminars/debates led by Assoc. Prof. Jing He, who drills evidence-based reasoning and rhetoric. Selected chapters from classical readings (e.g., Sandel’s *Justice*, Fukuyama’s *Political Order*, Mill’s *On Liberty*, Huntington’s *Clash of Civilizations*) are to be completed beforehand and serve as the basis for group presentations and formal debates. By semester’s end, students should be able to (1) explain competing theories in each module; (2) craft evidence-driven, logically coherent arguments on contested issues; and (3) deliver fluent, critically informed speeches and debates in English, preparing them for cross-disciplinary research and civic engagement.

教学目标

To learn about classical theories and the development of political philosophy. To cultivate critical thinking, argumentative strategies and academic presentation ability.

教学方式

Alternation of lecture and seminar. (Lecture on theoretical framework for one session, seminars on key issues, and debate and argumentative analysis for another session)

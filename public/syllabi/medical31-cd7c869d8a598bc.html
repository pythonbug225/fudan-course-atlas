1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 肥胖已成为严重威胁我国居民健康的重要危险因素，中国肥胖人数居全球第一，采取积极的应对措施对肥胖及所合并的代谢性疾病进行预防与治疗对于提高国人身体健康水平与生活质量具有重要的意义。虽然包括减重与代谢外科手术在内的肥胖医学在西方发达国家得到普遍开展和推广，但将其作为一个新兴学科引入我国并获得广泛推广应用仅十余年的时间，对其相关的基础理论与临床应用的认知度还存在不足。本课程旨在通过介绍肥胖的基本概念、发病机制与病理生理改变、诊断与评估方法、对机体各系统器官的危害以及治疗方法等在内的内容，使医学生对肥胖的基础理论与临床实践有正确的认识和理解，进而达到更好地促进肥胖医学在我国的发展，造福我国广大肥胖人群的目的。本课程紧密结合我国当前肥胖医学发展的现状，从病因、发病机制、病理生理改变、肥胖对机体心血管、呼吸、消化、内分泌等各系统器官的影响、其诊治现状，特别是减重代谢外科技术的应用等多学科领域系统阐述了当代肥胖医学所取得的成就与进展。通过本课程的学习，同学们将能够正确理解并掌握肥胖医学的相关知识，认识肥胖的危害，正确掌握包括减重与代谢外科手术在内的肥胖防治手段，进而有力促进肥胖防治知识的普及与临床应用，实现提高国人健康水平的目的。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Obesity has become a significant risk factor seriously threatening the health of our population, with China having the highest number of obese individuals globally. Taking proactive measures to prevent and treat obesity and its associated metabolic diseases is of great importance for improving the physical health and quality of life of our citizens. Although obesity medicine, including bariatric and metabolic surgery, has been widely developed and promoted in Western developed countries, it was introduced as a new discipline in our country and has only been widely applied for over a decade, with insufficient awareness of its related basic theories and clinical applications. This course aims to provide medical students with a correct understanding and comprehension of the basic theories and clinical practices of obesity by introducing its fundamental concepts, pathogenesis, pathophysiological changes, diagnostic and assessment methods, its harm to various organ systems, and treatment approaches. The ultimate goal is to better promote the development of obesity medicine in our country and benefit the vast population suffering from obesity. Closely aligned with the current status of obesity medicine development in China, this course systematically elaborates on the achievements and progress in contemporary obesity medicine from multidisciplinary perspectives, including etiology, pathogenesis, pathophysiological changes, the impact of obesity on cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, endocrine, and other organ systems, its current diagnosis and treatment landscape, with a particular focus on the application of bariatric metabolic surgery techniques. Through this course, students will be able to correctly understand and master the knowledge of obesity medicine, recognize its hazards, and accurately grasp prevention and treatment methods, including bariatric and metabolic surgery. This will effectively promote the popularization of obesity prevention and treatment knowledge and its clinical application, ultimately achieving the goal of improving the health of our population.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

本课程的教学目标旨在引导学生系统了解肥胖医学作为新兴亚专科的学科发展历程与前沿趋势，全面掌握肥胖症的病因学、病理生理机制、诊断标准及规范化治疗原则等核心知识体系，重点培养多学科协作诊疗（MDT）思维，特别是深入理解减重代谢外科在肥胖综合治疗中的关键作用与临床决策逻辑，为未来从事肥胖相关疾病的预防、诊断与治疗奠定扎实的理论与实践基础。

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

课堂讲解。 在课程的中后期，遴选若干名学生前往华东医院减重代谢外科中心见习，加深印象，进一步熟悉与掌握减重代谢外科在肥胖治疗中的重要作用，时长约半天。

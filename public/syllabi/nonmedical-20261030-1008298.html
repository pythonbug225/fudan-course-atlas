课程简介（中文）

本课程为针对生科院研究生开设的专业选修课，通过本课程学习，使学生掌握该领域内的 基本概念， 熟悉转基因动物制备的各种途径和方法，熟悉转基因动物的常用遗传修饰策略及包括 ZF N/TALE/CAS9 等基因编辑技术最新进展，熟悉转基因动物在生物、医学及畜牧业上应用，熟悉转基因动 物技术目前 存在问题及对策，了解转基因动物及其产品的生物安全问题，最终使学生对转基因动物技 术有更深的、 全面的了解，从而为学生以后利用转基因动物技术在生物和医学上的研究或应用奠定基础 。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a professional elective designed for graduate students in the School of Life Sciences. Through this course, students will master the basic concepts in the field, become familiar with various approaches and methods for the production of transgenic animals, become familiar with common genetic modification strategies for transgenic animals, including the latest developments in gene-editing technologies such as ZFN/TALE/CAS9, become familiar with the applications of transgenic animals in biology, medicine, and animal husbandry, become familiar with the current issues and countermeasures in transgenic animal technology, and understand the biosafety issues related to transgenic animals and their products. Ultimately, this course aims to provide students with a deeper and more comprehensive understanding of transgenic animal technology, thereby laying a solid foundation for their future research or application of transgenic animal technology in biology and medicine.

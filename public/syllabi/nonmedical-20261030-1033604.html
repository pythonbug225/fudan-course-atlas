课程简介（中文）

本课程将系统聚焦中国语境下的白领犯罪，涵盖其发展历史、类型划分及实施手段；同时剖析中国刑事司法体系中，针对白领犯罪的预防、执法与惩治等核心议题。课程将选取腐败犯罪、环境犯罪、网络犯罪、金融诈骗罪等典型白领犯罪类型展开深度解析，结合相关罪名构成与抗辩事由进行详细探讨。

课程简介（英文）

White-Collar Crimes focuses on a wide range of criminal activity that is committed by people who hold some sort of privilege in an economic and/or political position. This course will examine white collar crimes in China, including its history, types, and methods of perpetrating. It also reviews salient issues regarding the prevention, enforcement, and sanctioning of white-collar crimes in the Chinese criminal justice system. A selection of white-collar crimes, including official corruption, consumer crime, environmental crime, computer crime, and crime of financial fraud, will be examined in greater detail, taking into account the legal offences and defenses. While the focus of this course will predominantly be on the legal/regulatory aspects of white collar crimes, part of the discussion will include examining the ways to how compliance programs can prevent these types of white-collar crimes in practice and manage them in the event of occurrence.

教学目标

This course is designed to provide students with an overview of the white-collar crime, its characteristics, its complexities and its prosecutions in the Chinese criminal justice system. Upon completion of this course students are expected to have solid knowledge on the legal aspects of white collar crime in China and be able to analyze the schemes behind each type of crime that are covered in this course.

教学方式

Taught mainly through engaging lectures, this course also features lively case discussions and presentations.

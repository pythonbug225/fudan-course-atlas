1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程涵盖口腔颌面解剖生理学、牙体牙髓病学、牙周病学、口腔黏膜病学、口腔颌面创伤、感染与肿瘤、颞下颌关节病及口腔保健等部分。 本学科通过精心设计的理论授课与临床教学，不仅全面的传授口腔医学知识，同时，通过把握口腔科学的知识，旨在培养学生对于临床医学的大局观，通过整合资源进行学科交叉，进一步加强学生的社会责任感与勇于探索的求知精神。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The course includes practical subdisciplines such as oral and maxillofacial anatomical physiology, cariology and endodontology, periodontal diseases, oral mucosal diseases, oral and maxillofacial trauma, infections and tumors, temporomandibular joint diseases and oral health care. Through carefully selected lectures and clinical teaching, the department not only provides comprehensive dental knowledge in a wide array of public health topics, but serves as a role model for future doctors in order to bring quality services and preventive measures in need of dental care.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

This course is designed to introduce students the scope and basic principles of a variety of dental disorders and their clinical application in order to diagnose and treat some popular diseases involved oral cavity on a biological basis, with confidence, and with a high percentage of success

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

Course Schedule (Please supply the details about each lesson) : Week 1 Oral and maxillofacial anatomical physiology ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) To know the main structure of maxillofacial region To master the tooth structure and its surrounding tissues. To understand the bone, muscle, nerve and vascular supply in the maxillofacial region. Week 2 Periodontal diseases ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) To master various gingival and periodontaldiseases in terms of definition, etiology, symptom diagnosis and treatment principles. To know theclassification of periodontal diseases of 1999 edition To understand the relationship between periodontal diseases and general diseases. Week 3 Oral mucosal diseases ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) To master vsrious oral muconsal diseases in terms of etiology, clinicalmanifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles. To understand the inclusion of precancerous lesion. To know the oral manifestation of sexually transmitted diseases. Week 4 Cariology and Endodontology(Part I) ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) To master the etiology, symptoms, clinicalclassification and treatment measures of different caries. To master thedevelopment from caries to pulpal and periapical diseases. To understand the bacterias and mechanismas for causing caries. Week 5 Cariology and Endodontology(Part II) ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) To master the etiology, symptoms, clinicalclassification and treatment measures of various pulpal and periapical diseases. To master the relationship and difference between pupal nad periapical diseases. To master the etiology, symptoms, clinicalclassification and treatment measures of various non-caries diseases. To understand the procedure of endodontic treatment. Week 6 Oral health care ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) 1) To master the definition of three levels of oral health care 2) To master main methods for promoting oral health 3) To master the visual symptoms of potential mouth cancer 4) To understand special measures of oral health care designed for particular group populations. Week 7 Oral and maxillofacial trauma, infections; temporomandibular joint diseases ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) To master the diagnosis and treatment principles for oral and maxillofacial traumas. To master the clinical manifestation and management of medical emergencies related to oral and maxillofacial region. To understand Temporpmandibular Joint Disease (TMJ)’s anatomy, etiology, clinical symptoms and treatment measures. To master the facial space infection of maxillofacial region. To know the osteomyelitis of the jaws. Week 8 Oral and maxillofacial tumors ( 2 teaching hours, 90 minutes) To understand different oral and maxillofacial cysts in terms of their clinical manifestation and treatment principles. To master the benign tumors in maxillofacial region in terms of their clinical manifestation and treatment principles. To master the malignant tumors in maxillofacial region in terms of their clinical manifestation and treatment principles.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助学生全面理解公司金融的核心原理，重点涵盖以下内容：现值概念、净现值法则、内部收益率准则、资本预算决策、项目分析方法、风险与收益关系、资本资产定价模型（CAPM）、资本结构与资本成本，以及公司金融领域的其他关键议题。为深化与拓展荣誉项目学生对金融领域的理解，课程增设了专题研讨环节，涵盖相关延伸议题及文献研读内容。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles of corporate finance. It focuses on Present Value, NPV rule, IRR rule, Capital Budgeting, Project Analysis, Risk and Return, CAPM, Capital Structure and Cost of Capital, and other important issues in corporate finance. This course is intended for honor students who would pursue graduate degrees in the future. In order to deepen and widen the understanding of finance in the honors program, there are additional discussions, including relevant extended topics, and reading literature.

教学目标

This course is aiming to introduce the fundamental financial theory, emphasize on its applications to corporate financial management, understand how to conduct basic academic research in Finance, and provide insights necessary to understand and work for this rapidly changing financial industry.

教学方式

Mainly lecturing and in class discussion.

Week 16: Regular: Trade-off Theory and Pecking-Order Theory Week 18: Final Exam

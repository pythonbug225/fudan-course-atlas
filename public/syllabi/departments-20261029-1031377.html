课程简介（中文）

本课程是国际商务专业硕士的专业必修课程。课程全面涵盖国际商法的核心内容，以及相关商务环境领域的基础知识，主要包括国际分工、国际贸易、国际投资、国际金融市场、新兴市场增长战略以及世界经济区域化与全球化等重要议题。

课程简介（英文）

This course is one of the core courses required by EMA in International Business.  It comprehensively covers a range of topics in international business law and its relevant environments, including international division of labor, international trade, international investment, international financial market, growth strategies in emerging markets, regionalization and globalization of the world economy.

教学目标

The purpose of this course is to introduce the foundational knowledge of international business law systems and global business environments.  International law is the part of law that regulates various affairs of international business transactions and international commercial organizations. Moreover, the tools of international business law requires context as well, and this is made up of its environments and relationships. Therefore, the main aim of this course and its learning outcomes are to provide a clear, logical explanation of international business law and global business environments, through an examination from a number of angles: the historical, legal and economic in particular. 本课程旨在介绍国际商法体系与全球商业环境的基础知识。国际商法作为法律体系的重要组成部分，其功能在于规范跨国商业交易与国际商事组织的各类事务。同时，国际商法的运用也离不开具体情境，这种情境由其外部环境与关联关系共同构成。因此，本课程的核心目标与学习成果在于通过多维度——尤其是历史、法律与经济视角——的考察，对国际商法及全球商业环境作出清晰、系统的阐释。

教学方式

The Students' acquisition of core knowledge is achieved primarily through course lectures and independent study, supported by eleven 3 - teaching hour sessions. Each session, in turn, comprises two elements. Generally, the first part of each session will take the form of a formal lecture. The second part, will mainly be seminar-based, which leaves considerable room for interaction. Most seminars are student-led and will be assigned at the beginning of the course. The course lecturer will perform a primarily facilitative role in the seminars ensuring that learning outcomes are met.

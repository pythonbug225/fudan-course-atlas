课程简介（中文）

梵文文献阅读课。针对已修习梵文1和梵文2的学生，主要阅读印度哲学相关的梵文文献。

课程简介（英文）

This course is for students who have taken Sanskrit 1 and 2 and focuses on reading Sanskrit texts related to Indian philosophy.

教学目标

We will start reading the first chapter (on mithyatva) in Madhusudana Sarasvati’s Advaitasiddhi. Apart from the obvious goal of a Sanskrit reading class, viz. to enhance the students' knowledge of Sanskrit, another course objective is to provide insights into the philosophical ideas of the original sources.

教学方式

Students who choose the course take turns translating Sanskrit literature.

教学内容与进度安排

1 Brief introduction 2-17 Consecutive study of the contents of the Advaitasiddhi: Roundtable presentation of the students' weekly preparation 18 Oral exam

教学参考资料

Advaitasiddhi Madhusudana Sarasvati Pandurang Jawaji, ‘Nirjaya-sagar’ Press 1937

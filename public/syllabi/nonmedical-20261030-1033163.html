国际商事调解

课程简介（中文）

调解是解决冲突、实现有意义、创造性和持久解决方案且能够保留原有商业关系的最有效过程之一。 调解的特点是当事人自主决定。调解员不做决策，而是引导各方讨论观点，提出新选项并制定有效解决方案。 调解通常在个人设定环境中保密进行。公正的调解员通常以团队形式工作，经历一系列的不同阶段引导争端当事人（个人或团体），帮助他们找到自己的解决方案。 该课程旨在教育和培训个人掌握相关技能和调解国际商务交易相关各方争议所需的技术;为学生提供协助各方解决在国际商业交易背景下产生的争议所需的知识，技能、工具和技术;让学生了解调解中伦理以及管理此类争议的法律和监管框架。课程还将围绕体验模块及引入不同调解环境的国际商业调解实践教育学生法律框架及最佳知识。

课程简介（英文）

Mediation is one of the most effective processes for addressing conflicts and achieving meaningful, creative and lasting solutions that preserve relationships. Mediation is characterized by self-determination of the parties; mediators do not make decisions but rather facilitate the parties to discuss their viewpoints, generate new options and create effective solutions. Mediations are usually conducted confidentially in private settings. Impartial mediators, often working in teams, guide individuals and groups through a series of stages so they can find their own solutions. The course will be designed to educate and train individuals on the skills and techniques needed to mediate disputes between parties involved in international business transactions; to provide students with the knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques required to assist parties in resolving disputes that arise in the context of international business transactions; for students to learn about the ethics as well as the legal and regulatory frameworks that govern such disputes. The course would also educate students on the legal framework and best practices for international commercial mediation around experiential modules and the introduction of different mediation environments.

教学目标

Overall, the objective of the course would be to produce skilled and knowledgeable mediators who can help parties resolve disputes efficiently and effectively in the complex and diverse world of international business. ● To understand the role of mediation and its different processes. ● To apply mediation as an international commercial dispute resolution tool to a variety of fact scenarios and analyze the utility and efficacy of different techniques applied as part of the process. ● To understand how cultural differences impact the mediation. ● To compare mediation with other dispute resolution processes. ● To understand the role of different participants to a mediation.

教学方式

Teaching method will be combination of lecturing, observation of mediation via vedio and mock mediation practice.

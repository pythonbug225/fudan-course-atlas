课程简介（中文）

The course analyzes macroeconomic phenomena in an open economy. Through the teaching of this course, students will be assisted in mastering important theories and cutting-edge tools of open economy macroeconomics and understanding relevant economic realities. The course consists of four parts. Part I includes a chapter that will introduce the current state of the global macroeconomy. Part II includes three chapters that will focus on exchange rates and discuss how they are determined. Part III includes three chapters that will concentrate on the balance of payments and investigate how it is adjusted. Part IV includes four chapters that will turn to policy issues and the latest topics around open economy macroeconomics

教学目标

This course focuses on applying theoretical and empirical techniques used in open economy macroeconomics to current academic and policy issues. The course is geared toward sharpening the skills of students who wish to proceed from the master's program to economic analyst employment in either the private or public sectors and provides additional experience for students wishing to pursue a PhD in economics or finance. After the course, you should have: Become knowledgeable about current issues in open economy macroeconomics; Acquired training that is befitting of an economics professional; Developed a facility with economic data.

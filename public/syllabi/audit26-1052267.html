课程简介（中文）

本课程围绕智能感知技术在信息电子、生物、材料和人工智能等多学科的交叉研究，从生物感官启迪和物理响应机制出发，系统讲授各类传感器原理、结构设计制备、信号和性能评估、系统集成和功能展示，旨在从基础概念到前沿应用研究层面让学生全面理解和掌握智能感知技术，形成跨学科交叉的知识储备和技术素养。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on interdisciplinary research in intelligent sensing technologies spanning information electronics, biology, materials science, and artificial intelligence. Starting from biological sensory inspirations and underlying physical response mechanisms, it systematically covers the operating principles of various sensors, structural design and fabrication, signal processing and performance evaluation, system integration, and functional demonstrations. The course aims to help students develop a comprehensive understanding and mastery of intelligent sensing technologies, from fundamental concepts to cutting-edge applications, and to build a strong interdisciplinary knowledge base.

教学目标

本课程面向包括但不限于电子科学与技术专业的理工类本研学生，围绕智能感知技术与信息、生物、人工智能、材料的交叉融合，系统讲授各类传感原理、传感器设计构筑、信号处理与智能算法及系统集成应用。通过课程学习，使学生掌握多模态感知核心技术原理、前沿研究和应用，具备设计、分析与实现简易智能感知系统的基本能力，形成跨学科思维与严谨务实的科研素养，能够在前沿信息系统与智能感知相关场景中开展技术应用与初步创新。

This course is intended for undergraduate and graduate students in science and engineering, including but not limited to those majoring in Electronic Science and Technology. Focusing on the interdisciplinary integration of intelligent sensing with information science, biology, artificial intelligence, and materials science, it systematically introduces sensing principles, sensor design and fabrication, signal processing and intelligent algorithms, as well as system integration and applications. Through this course, students will gain a solid understanding of the core principles, frontier advances, and applications of multimodal sensing technologies; develop the basic ability to design, analyze, and implement simple intelligent sensing systems; cultivate interdisciplinary thinking and rigorous, practical research skills; and become prepared to pursue technological applications and preliminary innovation in scenarios related to advanced information systems and intelligent sensing.

教学方式

采用PPT与板书相结合的方式讲授课程，在讲授课程的同时，配合课程提问和课程小测的方式强化学生对课程内容的掌握。

The course is delivered through a combination of PowerPoint presentations and blackboard teaching. During instruction, in-class questioning and short quizzes are incorporated to reinforce students’ understanding and mastery of the course content.

教学内容与进度安排

第一周：绪论Ⅰ

（讲授各类生物感官的发展、智能感知和传感的技术原理基础）

Week 1: Introduction I
(Overview of the development of biological sensory systems and the fundamental principles of intelligent sensing and sensor technologies)

第二周：绪论Ⅱ

（讲授智能感知技术的发展历程和应用现状）

Week 2: Introduction II
(Introduction to the development history and current applications of intelligent sensing technologies)

第三周：智能视觉感知技术Ⅰ

（讲授视觉传感技术的生物学基础、光电和图像传感器概念和工作原理）

Week 3: Intelligent Visual Sensing I
(Biological foundations of visual sensing, as well as the concepts and operating principles of optoelectronic and image sensors)

第四周：智能视觉感知技术Ⅱ

（讲授视觉感知技术前沿研究，覆盖视觉器件设计制备、信号处理、系统级集成和应用，包括视网膜、视觉假体、电子眼、机器视觉等，根据课程进度布置一次作业或随堂小测）

Week 4: Intelligent Visual Sensing II
(Frontier research in visual sensing, covering device design and fabrication, signal processing, system-level integration, and applications, including retinas, visual prostheses, electronic eyes, and machine vision; one assignment or in-class quiz may be arranged depending on course progress)

第五周：智能听觉感知技术Ⅰ

（讲授听觉传感技术的生物学基础、振动和声学传感器概念和工作原理）

Week 5: Intelligent Auditory Sensing I
(Biological foundations of auditory sensing, as well as the concepts and operating principles of vibration and acoustic sensors)

第六周：智能听觉感知技术Ⅱ

（讲授听觉感知技术前沿研究，覆盖听觉器件设计制备、信号处理、系统级集成和应用，包括人工耳蜗、语音助手等）

Week 6: Intelligent Auditory Sensing II
(Frontier research in auditory sensing, covering device design and fabrication, signal processing, system-level integration, and applications, including cochlear implants and voice assistants)

第七周：智能触觉感知技术Ⅰ

（讲授触觉传感技术的生物学基础、基于力学、热学或者摩擦学的触觉传感器概念和工作原理）

Week 7: Intelligent Tactile Sensing I
(Biological foundations of tactile sensing, as well as the concepts and operating principles of tactile sensors based on mechanics, thermotics, or tribology)

第八周：智能触觉感知技术Ⅱ

（讲授触觉感知技术前沿研究，覆盖触觉器件设计制备、信号处理、系统级集成和应用，包括电子皮肤、灵巧手、智能假肢等，根据课程进度布置一次作业或随堂小测）

Week 8: Intelligent Tactile Sensing II
(Frontier research in tactile sensing, covering device design and fabrication, signal processing, system-level integration, and applications, including electronic skin, dexterous hands, and intelligent prosthetics; one assignment or in-class quiz may be arranged depending on course progress)

第九周：答疑、讲解和考试

（进行前期作业小测讲解、疑问回答和课程的期中考试）

Week 9: Q&A, Review, and Examination
(Explanation of previous assignments and quizzes, question answering, and the midterm examination)

第十周：智能嗅觉感知技术Ⅰ

（讲授嗅觉传感技术的生物学基础、各类气体传感器的概念和工作原理）

Week 10: Intelligent Olfactory Sensing I
(Biological foundations of olfactory sensing, as well as the concepts and operating principles of various gas sensors)

第十一周：智能嗅觉感知技术Ⅱ

（讲授嗅觉感知技术前沿研究，覆盖嗅觉器件设计制备、信号处理、系统级集成和应用，包括电子鼻和各类气味感知设备等，根据课程进度布置一次作业或随堂小测）

Week 11: Intelligent Olfactory Sensing II
(Frontier research in olfactory sensing, covering device design and fabrication, signal processing, system-level integration, and applications, including electronic noses and various odor-sensing devices; one assignment or in-class quiz may be arranged depending on course progress)

第十二周：智能味觉感知技术Ⅰ

（讲授味觉传感技术的生物学基础、与味觉相关的生物传感器概念和工作原理）

Week 12: Intelligent Gustatory Sensing I
(Biological foundations of gustatory sensing, as well as the concepts and operating principles of biosensors related to taste sensing)

第十三周：智能味觉感知技术Ⅱ

（讲授味觉感知技术前沿研究，覆盖味觉器件设计制备、信号处理、系统级集成和应用，包括电子舌和味觉电生理感知设备等）

Week 13: Intelligent Gustatory Sensing II
(Frontier research in gustatory sensing, covering device design and fabrication, signal processing, system-level integration, and applications, including electronic tongues and electrophysiological taste-sensing devices)

第十四周：面向未来智能感知展望Ⅰ

（讲授视、听、触、嗅、味觉的未来发展展望，包括各类感知技术本身的瓶颈和发展趋势，以及技术间的交叉融合-多模态感知等）

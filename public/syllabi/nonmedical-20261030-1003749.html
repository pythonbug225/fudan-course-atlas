教学目标

The goals of the course are: (i) to help students to understand the basic knowledge in international trade (ii) to introduce students to current topics of interest in international trade (iii) to help students develop skills for conducting research, including evaluating existing research, presenting their work, working collaboratively, and defining and organizing a research project.

教学方式

Primarily lecture-based

教学内容与进度安排

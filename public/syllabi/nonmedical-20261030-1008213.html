Chinese Public Administration

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在深化学生对中国公共行政管理历史脉络、发展背景及现实议题的理解，重点关注20世纪70年代末改革开放以来的演进历程。除系统掌握中国行政体制及其改革实践外，课程着力培养学生对中国公共行政管理各维度的分析思考与学术写作能力。 课程的核心目标包含三个层面： 理论认知：拓展学生关于中国公共行政基础理论的系统知识，涵盖政治体制、公务员制度、行政改革、府际关系等制度架构，以及政策制定、政策执行、政策扩散与学习、公民社会、协同治理、环境治理等治理过程； 分析能力：通过中国公共政策案例分析，帮助学生建立对地方治理议题的深度理解； 方法论训练：提升运用实证方法解析当代中国行政现象的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to enhance a student’s understanding of the history, context, and current issues of public administration in China, with a focus on the development since the initiation of economic reform in the late 1970s. In addition to achieving knowledge of China’s administrative systems and their reforms, the course is designed to sharpen student’s ability to think and write analytically about the various aspects of China’s public administration. The ultimate goal of this course is to enlarge students’ knowledge of basic theories of Chinese public administration, including political system, civil service, administrative reform, intergovernmental relations, policy making, policy implementation, policy diffusion and learning, civil society, collaborative governance and environmental governance. The course will also help students to generate further understanding of local policy issues through analyzing Chinese public policy issues.

教学目标

1. Knowledge Objectives • Acquire systematic understanding of China's public administration system, including its political structure, civil service, and intergovernmental relations • Comprehend key theoretical frameworks of Chinese administrative reforms since 1978 economic reform • Master specialized knowledge in policy processes (formulation, implementation, diffusion) and governance models (collaborative governance, environmental governance) 2. Skill Objectives • Develop analytical capabilities to critically examine China's public policy issues using empirical evidence • Enhance academic writing skills for articulating research findings on Chinese governance • Apply comparative perspectives to evaluate China's administrative innovations and challenges • Conduct evidence-based policy analysis through case study methodology 3. Value Objectives • Cultivate objective understanding of China's governance system beyond Western-centric paradigms • Appreciate the historical and cultural contexts shaping Chinese public administration • Develop professional ethics for studying administrative systems with cultural sensitivity • Foster evidence-based attitudes toward policy analysis in transitional societie

教学方式

This course employs a blended pedagogical approach to foster both theoretical understanding and practical analytical skills. The teaching methods include: 1. Lectures by Instructor Systematic delivery of core theories and frameworks Emphasis on historical evolution and contemporary reforms of China's public administration Integration of multimedia resources (government reports, policy documents, and archival materials) 2. Student-led Literature Discussions Critical reading and presentation of selected academic papers Guided debates on key themes Comparative analysis of Eastern and Western scholarly perspectives 3. Case Study Analysis Examination of landmark policy cases Group-based problem-solving exercises simulating real administrative scenarios Application of theoretical frameworks to analyze policy successes/failures

课程简介（中文）

本课程专为提高学生高级法语水平而设计，旨在通过对当代法国媒体和新闻的批判性研究，使学生的语言能力达到专业C1标准。课程依托真实的新闻报刊文本，全面培养学生的语言、分析与文体适应能力。首先，学生将解码媒体结构、新闻标题及含蓄修辞，随后通过文学与电影评论探讨文化新闻学，以掌握主观评估的表达技巧。接下来，课程将聚焦于美食、文化遗产和旅游新闻学中的描述性语言。最后，学生将分析现代社会辩论话题，并掌握交叉比对不同媒体冲突观点的能力。本课程采用行动导向教学法，通过沉浸式语言模拟、创意媒体工作坊以及互动式同伴互评进行授课。课程将赋予学生严谨的论证工具、文本灵活性和媒介素养，为学术研究或专业职场环境奠定坚实基础。具体的教学内容将根据学生的学习进度和理解能力进行动态调整。

课程简介（英文）

This advanced course is designed for elevating students’ French language proficiency to a professional C1 standard through the critical study of contemporary French media and journalism. The course relies on authentic press articles to refine students' linguistic, analytical, and stylistic agility. First, students decode media architecture, headlines, and implicit rhetoric and then, explore cultural journalism through literary and film reviews to master subjective evaluation. Next, the course focuses on descriptive language within gastronomy, heritage, and travel journalism. Finally, students analyze modern societal debates while mastering cross-referencing conflicting media perspectives. Adopting an action-oriented pedagogical approach, classes are conducted via immersive language simulations, creative media workshops, and interactive peer-review sessions. This course equips students with the sophisticated argumentative tools, textual flexibility, and media literacy necessary for academic research or professional environments. The detailed educational content may be supplemented or adapted based on the students’ pace and understanding.

教学目标

  • Master advanced French journalistic registers and rhetorical conventions.
  • Develop extensive and critical reading skills to understand implicit meanings and stylistic figures in contemporary media.
  • Distinguish clearly between informative, argumentative, and subjective writing styles.
  • Acquire the specialized lexical fields of French cultural criticism (literature, cinema, gastronomy, and territory promotion).
  • Synthesize multiple conflicting sources and diverse points of view.
  • Sharpen self-correction and editorial skills through collaborative peer-review workshops.

教学方式

  • Full French language immersion
  • Interactive workshops and group projects.
  • Action-oriented simulations engaging students in professional or cultural tasks.
  • Collaborative peer-reviewing and remediation to develop critical skills and proofreading habits.

中美关系与美国政治

2025秋 · POLI70021.01 · 信强、韦宗友

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程包括对美国政治思想、政治制度、政治文化和政治过程的介绍，以及中美关系在历史上的演变和新时代战略竞争中中美关系变化的逻辑；同时，还将指导学生了解美国外交政策是如何制定的，其战略目标以及实现这些目标的方式。

课程简介（英文）

It includes the introduction of American political thoughts, political institution, political culture and political processes, as well as the evolution of Sino-US relations in history and the logic of change in Sino-US relations in the new era of strategic competition; meanwhile, it will also tell students how American foreign policies are made, their strategic goals and ways to achieve these goals.

教学目标

This course aims to help students to get some deeper understandings about China-US relations and American politics. In sum, this course will deepen students’understanding of America and its relations with the outside world especially China, thus broadening their international horizon.

教学方式

A basic knowledge of political science and international relations is expected. The study of this course involves attending lectures, doing presentations, discussions and Q & A. There will be no homework. Students are also encouraged to read popular press widely and observe related phenomenon in the real world and use what they learn in class to analyze it.

教学内容与进度安排

Course introduction Interaction between US domestic politics and foreign policy Executive power in American foreign policy making process Legislative power in American foreign policy making process Judiciary power in American foreign policy making process Partisan politics in American foreign policy making process Interest groups politics in American foreign policy making process Ideology in American foreign policy making process History of China-US relations before 1949 Development of China-US relations: from 1949 to 1979 Development of China-US relations: from 1979 to 1991 Development of China-US relations: from 1991 to 2016 Development of China-US relations from 2016 and the prospect Perception, strategy and policy of the US towards China Perception, strategy and policy of China towards the US Course summary and Q & A

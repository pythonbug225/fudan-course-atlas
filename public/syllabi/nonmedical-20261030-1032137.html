课程简介（中文）

自二十世纪末国际学界“图像转向”以来，视觉文化研究层出不穷，本课程以探讨明清至近现代中国文学与视觉现代性为主题，每周讨论专题包括明清小说戏曲与插图、西方视觉器具与文学、照相与石印画报与电影等。结合中外理论与研究成果对文学与视觉文本进行研读，旨在全球视野中建立中国问题意识、掌握专业知识与拓展跨文化研究方法，并提高独立思考与科研能力。

课程简介（英文）

Since the "pictorial turn" in international academia at the end of the twentieth century, visual culture studies have proliferated. This course explores the relationship between Chinese literature and visual modernity from the Ming-Qing period to the modern era. Weekly topics include: illustrations in Ming-Qing novels and drama, Western visual technologies and literature, photography and lithographic pictorials, and cinema. Through critical engagement with both Chinese and Western theoretical frameworks and research, the course aims to: (1) develop a China-centered problem consciousness within a global perspective; (2) acquire specialized knowledge; (3) expand interdisciplinary and cross-cultural research methodologies; and (4) enhance independent critical thinking and scholarly research capabilities.

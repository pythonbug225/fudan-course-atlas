国际关系基础理论

课程简介（中文）

本课程采用国际法与国际关系的跨学科视角，揭示既有跨学科研究如何深化法学生对国际法的制定、解释与实施的认知，以及我们对国际关系中法律化路径的成因、局限与后果的理解。本课程将通过围绕具体议题的讨论，系统阐释国际关系的核心理论，包括现实主义、机构主义、自由主义及建构主义。任课教师将通过国际贸易法、国际投资法、国际刑法、国际环境法、国际海洋法以及国际人权法的案例研究来阐释相关核心概念。 本课程将探讨以下具体问题：国际事务中的权力与法律如何互相影响？哪些因素会增强或削弱国际法的遵守程度？国际事务日益法律化的后果是什么？如何解构国内的决策过程，尤其是考虑到这些决策过程决定了国家利益的构成，并在特定议题上对同时存在的国家利益进行排序？国家为何通过国际组织行事？国际机构的设计如何影响其成果与有效性？条约、国际组织、国际法院、仲裁委员会及审查机构的激增，究竟是增强还是削弱了国际法律体系的协调性？国际司法与准司法机构及国内法院在国际事务中扮演何种角色？它们如何影响国内法律与政治？为何某些国际协议采取非正式形式？国际组织如何履行立法职能？此类立法行为是否合法？如何在国际法中嵌入灵活性机制？国际法在解决全球公共产品短缺问题中扮演何种角色？

课程简介（英文）

This course draws on interdisciplinary perspectives on international law and international relations to reveal how the existing interdisciplinary scholarship has contributed to our knowledge about the making, interpretation, and enforcement of international law and our understandings of the causes, limits, and consequences of legalized approaches in international relations. Fundamental theories of international relations, including realism, institutionalism, liberalism, and constructivism, will be covered in concrete issue-specific discussions over the semester. The course instructor will illustrate key concepts with case studies in international trade law, foreign investment law, international criminal law, international environmental law, the law of the sea, and international human rights law. Specifically, this course discusses the following questions: How do power and law influence each other in international affairs? What factors enhance or weaken compliance with international law? What are the consequences of the increased legalization of international affairs? How to decompose the domestic decision-making processes that determine what constitutes state interests and prioritize a country’s concurrent national interests on a given issue? Why do states act through international organizations? How does the design of international institutions influence their outcomes and effectiveness? Does the proliferation of treaties, international organizations, international courts, arbitral committees, and review bodies enhance or undermine the coherence of the international legal system? What roles do international judicial and quasi-judicial bodies and domestic courts play in international affairs and influence domestic law and politics? Why are some international agreements informal? How do international organizations function as lawmakers, and is it legitimate for them to do so? How to embed flexibilities in international law? What is the role of international law in solving the shortage of global public goods?

教学目标

This course concentrates on the interactions between international law and international relations with a legal practice orientation. Its primary objective is to expose students to the fundamental theories and concepts of international relations, helping them understand why and when states and non-state actors turn to certain international rules and institutions and how this influences domestic law and politics in turn. Second, it seeks to help students develop a toolkit in spotting, framing, and linking issues that transcend regimes, in making strategic uses of various sources of international law and institutions, and in shopping from available dispute resolution mechanisms and law-making forums, state and non-state actors, and legalized or non-legal approaches. This course applies the covered theories to specific case studies in a given setting, providing students with an immersive experience as an attorney working for a government, a lawyer recruited by a transnational corporation confronted with discriminative treatments or compliance difficulties in a foreign country or business limitations in its home state, or a public interest lawyer representing an injured individual. This class is designed to be very participatory, with mandatory commenting of students two times.

教学方式

Primarily lecturing, supplemented by in-class interaction.

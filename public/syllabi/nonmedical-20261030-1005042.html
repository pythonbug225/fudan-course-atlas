跨文化交际

2025秋 · FORE20028.02 · 宋旸

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍跨文化交际这一学科。课程首先追溯文化与交际的概念，以及跨文化交际作为对更为传统的跨文化比较研究学科的批判性出发点。 在介绍该领域的关键理论概念与研究方法之后，后续课程将聚焦于跨文化交际的多样化语境，例如教育、商务、家庭与恋爱关系、代际差异等。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a systematic introduction to the discipline of intercultural communication. It starts by tracing the conceptions of culture and communication together with the genealogy of intercultural communication as a critical departure from the more conventionalized discipline of cross-cultural communication. After introducing the key theoretical notions and research methods in the field, the following sessions will devote to diverse contexts of intercultural communication, such as education, business, family and love relationship, generational differences, etc.

教学目标

本课程旨在为学生提供对跨文化交际学科的全面而系统的介绍。完成本课程后，学生应能够达到以下学习成果： 理解文化的多种概念，并认识这些概念如何影响我们将跨文化交际作为一个研究领域的切入方式； 理解跨文化交际与日常生活的相关性，并掌握该领域的系统训练如何帮助应对多样化情境，尤其是在跨文化交际对社会平等和个人/群体利益具有关键意义时； 能够运用理论概念和研究方法，分析媒体或现实生活中的跨文化交际场景； 培养跨文化交际能力，将相关知识、技能与批判性意识整合为工具，以应对全球化时代可能出现的交际挑战。 This course aims to provide students with a thorough and systematic introduction to the discipline of intercultural communication. After taking this course, students are expected to achieve the following learning outcomes: To understand the diverse conceptions of culture and how they impact the way we approach intercultural communication as a field of inquiry To understand how intercultural communication can be relevant to everyday life and how systematic training in this field can help cope with diverse situations when intercultural communication become critical for social equality and personal/group interest To apply the theoretical concepts and methods to analyze media/real-life intercultural communication scenarios To develop intercultural communication competence as an integrative toolkit of relevant knowledge, skills and critical awareness to face potential communicative challenges in the era of globalization.

教学方式

课堂教学、课堂小组讨论、课堂一对一作业反馈和小组课题咨询、线上E-learning讨论版作业等 Classroom lectures, group discussions, one-on-one assignment feedback and group project consultation, as well as online E-learning discussion board assignments.

two in-class case studies using prior concepts. 9 IC in international education Relevance to international education; “critical thinking” as institutional discourse & student practice. 10 IC in the AI era (I): AI as discourse & ideology How AI is framed in culture/communication; ideological discourses of AI in IC settings. 11 IC in the AI era (II): AI as mediator AI as a potential mediator (education/work/daily life); opportunities & limits of AI-enabled communication. 12 IC in the AI era (III): Ethics, bias & cultural adaptation Ethics/politics in AI for IC; bias, fairness, and cultural sensitivity in AI interactions. 13 IC in transnational families Socialization; analyze IC cases in transnational family contexts. 14 IC at work & for sale (combined) (1) Workplace discourse & professional communication; analyze selfies/vlogs as identity work. 17 Becoming an intercultural mediator Cultivate IC competence as lifelong goal; explore personal potential as mediator. 18 Final W & A Course wrap-up & assessment (W & A).

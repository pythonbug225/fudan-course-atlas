课程简介（中文）

本课程分为两个部分。理论部分包括线性规划、整数规划、网络优化、无约束优化、二次规划和有约束优化等主题。实践部分则探讨在物流、制造、运输、大数据、项目管理和金融等领域的应用。参加此课程的学生将理解优化建模的基础方法、理论和算法，掌握解决实际问题所需的建模技能，并熟练运用常见的优化算法和软件。

课程简介（英文）

This course is divided into two parts. The theoretical segment covers topics such as linear programming, integer programming, network optimization, unconstrained optimization, quadratic programming, and constrained optimization. The practical component explores applications in logistics, manufacturing, transportation, big data, project management, and finance. Students enrolled in this course will gain an understanding of the foundational methods, theories, and algorithms of optimization modeling. They will acquire the skills necessary to construct models that address practical problems, and will develop proficiency in using common optimization algorithms and software.

教学目标

This course is designed to systematically introduce the theory, algorithms, and applications of optimization methods within the fields of economics and management sciences. Students will learn the fundamental techniques of optimization modeling and algorithm design.

教学方式

Teaching Methods: 1)       Lectures: Fundamental theories and applications will be presented during class sessions. 2)       In-class Discussions: The lecturer will occasionally pose discussion questions. Students are encouraged to actively participate and exchange opinions with peers. These discussions foster deeper engagement with debatable topics. 3)       Homework Assignments: Students will complete tasks applying the techniques discussed in class, allowing them to gain practical experience with these methods.

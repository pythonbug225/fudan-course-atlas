医疗大数据统计学

2026春 · STAT50020.01 · 傅博

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要包括3个部分。第一部分介绍生存分析理论和方法以及如何应用统计软件分析时间事件数据。第二部分介绍统计因果推断方法及其在健康医疗大数据研究中的应用。第三部分讨论医学诊断检查和筛查中的统计方法应用。

课程简介（英文）

This course mainly consists of three modules. The first module introduces the theory and methods of survival analysis and how to use statistical software to analyze time-to-event data. The second module introduces statistical causal inference methods and their applications in health and medical big data research. The third module discusses the application of statistical methods in medical diagnostic tests and screening.

教学目标

教学目标 Course Objectives To understand the concepts and principles of biostatistics and study various biostatistical topics including survival analysis, causal inference and diagnosit test and screening. To apply these methods in real data analysis with the aid of statistical software To interpret the results from a real data analysis

教学方式

本课程以主讲教师讲授为主，加上一部分统计软件应用和上机实践课、习题练习课、和案例分析课等

教学内容与进度安排

教学内容与进度安排 Course Content & Schedule Health data analysis examples Introduction to time-to-event data: survival function, Hazard function, Mean life time (life expectancy), Mean life residual time Parametric failure time models (e.g. exponential, weibull, log-normal) Estimate the survival function: 1) Kaplan-Meier product-limit method; 2) life table estimate; 3) Nelson Aalen estimate. Cox proportional hazards (PH) model Accelerated failure time (AFT) model Model selection, model diagnostics. analysis of data where proportional hazards assumption violated Analysis of correlated censored data, competing risks and recurrent event data Introduction to causal inference, estimating causal effects of treatment or exposure from observational data 10 Estimating causal effects of treatment in randomised controlled trials with non-compliance 11 Deal with observed confounders: matching, stratification, propensity score 12 Deal with unobserved confounders (instrumental variable models, Mendelian randomisation) Introduction to diagnostic test and screening Sensitivity and specificity, positive and negative predicted values,likelihood ratio 15 ROC curve, tests based on logistic regression, comparison between diagnostic studies 16 Design of diagnostic studies, bias in diagnostic tests

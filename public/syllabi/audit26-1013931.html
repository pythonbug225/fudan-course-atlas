课程简介（中文）

模型引导的药物开发（MIDD）是国内外监管部门力推的新型药物开发范式。药动学/药效学（PK/PD）是MIDD的重要技术基础。本课程旨在通过对PK/PD基本概念、理论基础和应用案例的介绍，使学生了解这一新兴技术在新药临床开发中的关键作用，培养学生在临床研究和药物开发中应用PK/PD分析方法的能力。

课程简介（英文）

Model-informed drug development (MIDD) is a new paradigm of drug development promoted by regulatory authorities both domestically and internationally. Pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics (PK/PD) is an important technical skill for MIDD. The purpose of this course is to introduce the basic concepts, theories, and application cases of PK/PD, so that students can understand the key role of this emerging technology in the clinical development of new drugs. Through the study of PK/PD modeling and simulation, students can better understand the PK/PD behaviors of drugs in the population, as well as understand the sources of inter-individual variation, thereby promoting the optimization of clinical dosage selection. In addition, this course will also improve students' hands-on experience in clinical research and drug development, enabling them to effectively apply their learned knowledge in future study and contribute to the optimization design of clinical trials.

教学目标

使学生掌握药动学/药效学（PK/PD）的基本概念、理论和应用。通过本课程，学生将理解个体差异对药物反应的影响，学习如何通过统计方法和模型分析药物在群体中的行为，进而提高药物治疗的个体化水平。此外，本课程还将培养学生在临床研究和药物开发中的实践能力，使其能够在实际工作中有效应用所学知识，为提高患者的用药安全性和有效性做出贡献。

教学方式

课堂上，理论授课结合业界经典案例讲解；课后，有整合性的课程项目供学生进行实际的实操演练；最后通过小组汇报讨论的形式进行交叉评议，加深学习效果

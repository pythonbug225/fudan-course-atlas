课程简介（英文）

The Chinese arbitration practice began from the 1950 th , when CIETAC and CMAC were established to resolve international commercial disputes at vacancy of arbitration laws, the first Chinese Arbitration Law was made 1994 and effective in 1995 and need to be revised due to the fast development of commercial arbitration practice. The main contents of this course include: A general introduction to arbitration; Determination of the validity of arbitration agreement under Chinese Arbitration Law; Arbitral tribunal and its jurisdiction; main procedural issues under Chinese Arbitration law; Arbitral awards; Determine governing law of substantive issues; Judicial review of the People’Court to arbitral award; Recognition and enforcement of different kinds of arbitral awards in China. From the above, some interesting and special issues will be addressed e.g. whether foreign arbitration institution is allowed to conduct arbitration seated in China? And whether awards made outside China without foreign elements can be recognized and enforced according to the 1958 New York Convention.

教学目标

The aim of this course is to help students understand systematically the progress of Chinese Arbitration Laws, find out the way to resolve commercial disputes, either domestic or international through arbitration according to the current Chinese arbitration laws

1. 课堂授课 (teaching in class ) 2. 互动讨论 (mutual discussing ) 3. 作业提交展现所学(homework ) 4. 论文撰写、批改与指导 (paper writing \directing \correcting )

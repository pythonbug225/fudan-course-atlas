Qualitative Research Methods

2025秋 · MANA40004.01 · 李海默

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程侧重于社会科学中小规模 N 或单一案例研究的定性方法。它涉及定性研究中因果推理的理论问题，并将其与定量研究中的因果推理进行比较。每周的指定读物包括有关方法的论文和在实证研究中使用特定方法的实例。主题包括定量研究和定性研究之间的关系、小型 N 个案例研究和一般理论、混合方法、关键节点、社会科学中的自然实验、过程追踪和/或路径依赖、分析叙述、人种学研究和比较历史分析、基于访谈的研究中的方法问题等。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on qualitative methodology in small N or single case studies in social sciences. It addresses the theoretical issue of causal reasoning in qualitative research and compares it with that embodied in quantitative studies. The assigned readings for each week consist of both papers on methods and examples of using that particular method in empirical research. Topics include the relationship between quantitative and qualitative research, small N case studies and general theory, mixed methods, critical junctures, natural experiments in social sciences, process-tracing and/or path dependence, analytical narratives, ethnography research, and comparative historical analysis, methodological issues in interview-based research.

教学目标

This course aims to expose the students to the recent progress in qualitative methodology and help them develop design their respective research projects. At the end of the course, the students will be able to conduct data collection, analyze qualitative data and finish a qualitative research paper.

教学方式

lectures prepared by the teacher and presentations [based on required reading materials] by the students who participate in the class.

教学内容与进度安排

week 1 Introduction: paradigms in scientific research week 2 Methodology and ontology in social scientific research week 3 The quantitative and qualitative debate week 4 Research question and literature review week 5 Research design, data collection and measurement week 6 Small N case studies and general theory week 7 Selection bias in qualitative research week 8 Negative cases or paired comparison week 9 Mixed methods week 10 Controlled comparison and conceptual clarity week 11 Natural experiments in social science week 12 Critical junctures week 13 Interpretative methods and social scientific research week 14 Participants or observers in fieldwork week 15 Qualitative data analysis week 16 Presentation: research proposal

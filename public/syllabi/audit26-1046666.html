课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在介绍社会科学研究中的现代数据挖掘与计算方法。课程首先从数据基础入手，系统介绍社会科学研究中常见的数据结构、数据类型与数据来源，并重点讨论社交媒体数据以及其他社科大数据在当代社会科学研究中的重要性及其应用。同时，课程将介绍人工智能（Artificial Intelligence, AI）和大语言模型（Large Language Models, LLMs）的基本概念，并结合社会科学及相关领域的实际应用，帮助学生理解人工智能技术对社会科学研究范式和研究方法带来的新变化。

在此基础上，课程进一步培养学生的数据处理与文本分析实践能力，主要内容包括数据清洗、文本数据预处理、分词与标记化（Tokenisation）以及自然语言处理等。课程还将涵盖情感分析、主题模型、机器学习和模型评估与可解释性，以及词嵌入、语义相似度和Transformer模型等重要方法与技术。此外， 课程还将介绍生成式人工智能（Generative AI）、提示词工程（Prompt Engineering）和人工智能智能体（AI Agents）等前沿内容，重点讨论这些新兴工具如何应用于数据分析，并辅助社会科学研究中的资料处理、分析、解释以及研究流程优化。通过本课程的学习，学生将能够建立对现代计算社会科学方法的系统认识，并初步掌握运用数据分析、机器学习和生成式人工智能工具开展社会科学研究的基本能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces contemporary data mining and computational methods for social science research. It begins with core data foundations, including common data structures, types, and sources, and examines the growing importance of social media and other forms of social big data. The course also introduces key concepts in artificial intelligence (AI) and large language models (LLMs), with applications across social science and related fields.

The course then develops practical skills in data cleaning, textual data preprocessing, tokenisation, and natural language processing. Topics include sentiment analysis, topic modelling, machine learning and model evaluation & interpretability, as well as word embeddings, semantic similarity, and transformer models. Finally, the course introduces modern generative AI, prompt engineering, and AI agents, highlighting how these tools can support data analysis and social science research workflows.

教学目标

On successful completion of this subject students should be able to:

  • Understand the basics of data foundations in social science
  • Understand the basics of large language models and generative AI in social science.
  • Clean, transform and prepare text for textual analysis
  • Understand the fundamental natural language processing methods
  • Understand the fundamentals of machine learning models
  • Apply Generative AI tools in social science research

教学方式

The majority of the content will be delivered via lectures. Students are expected to complete practice exercises and group projects to aid their understanding of class material.

课程简介（中文）

韩国近现代史涉及的资料非常广泛，从语言上来讲包括中文、近代韩文、日文、西文等，这些不同语言的史料又可分成不同的类型。本课程主要对这些庞杂的韩国近代史资料进行详细梳理，介绍这些资料的类型、收藏情况、蒐集和利用方法等，同时选取不同类型的资料与学生进行研读讨论，使学生对韩国近现代史资料有一个总体的把握，能够准确地判读和利用不同类型的资料进行韩国史的相关研究。

课程简介（英文）

The materials related to modern and contemporary Korean history are extensive, spanning multiple languages, including Chinese, modern Korean, Japanese, and Western languages. These historical materials in different languages can be further categorized into various types. This course provides a detailed overview of these diverse materials on modern Korean history, covering their types, collection status, methods of gathering and utilization, and more. Additionally, the course includes reading and discussion sessions on different types of materials, enabling students to develop a comprehensive understanding of modern and contemporary Korean historical sources. The goal is to equip students with the ability to accurately interpret and utilize different types of materials for relevant research on Korean history.

教学目标

通过教学，使学生能比较全面地了解韩国近现代史料的类型、收藏情况等，掌握蒐集自己研究中所需要的韩国近现代史相关史料的基本方法，学会如何整理、辨别和判读所搜集的史料，为撰写学位论文和今后的科研工作打下坚实的基础。

教学方式

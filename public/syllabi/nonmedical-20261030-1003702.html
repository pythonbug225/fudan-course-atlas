课程简介（中文）

本课程教授西班牙语写作的基本原则，课程内容包括如何陈述与展开一篇文章的中心思想、按逻辑组织段落以及将他人观点融入作者自身分析。综合课堂练习、家庭作业和其他阅读材料，旨在帮助学生掌握进行学术陈述所需的写作表达和技能，并达到熟练水平。此外，本课程还向学生介绍批判性阅读与写作，以提升他们在西班牙语国家大学的专业环境中的写作能力。

课程简介（英文）

This compulsory course focuses on the principles of writing composition in Spanish. The course covers how to state and develop a central idea of an essay, organize paragraphs into logical sequence, and integrate the ideas of others into a paper that expresses the writer's own analysis. Classroom exercises, homework, and other readings are customized to help the students to achieve a proficient level in writing expressions and skills necessary for giving an oral academic presentation. The course also introduces students to critical reading and writing to promote the students’ ability to navigate professional context in Spanish-speaking universities.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to develop student’s writing skills for Academic purposes in Spanish. The classes are designed to increase grammar and vocabulary for professional contexts, introducing principles of style, uses of citation, and basic knowledge of scientific research. As result, students will be able to present a research plan and write an academic paper.

教学方式

Teaching method combined professor’s explanations, interaction in the classroom, and homework. Each lesson will be divided into two sections: the first dedicated to analyzing prose techniques and complex grammar; and the second to student’s exercises and revision of their own composition.

教学内容与进度安排

Presentation of the subject. Basic concepts about writing for academic propose. Describing a topic. Difference between academic, and other professional written styles. Narrowing a research topic. The importance of the question. The specific objectives and general objectives in a research project. Summarizing the main ideas of an author. Citation styles. Direct and Indirect speech. Writing a book review for scientific journals. The Literary Review in a dissertation. Mid-term Exam Theoretical Framework. Quantitative and qualitative research methods. Writing a research proposal. Feasibility and time plan. Academic genres. Difference between essay, article, and thesis. Diving a central idea into chapters. Writing the abstracts and the table of contents. Organization of the logical sequence in a dissertation. Final Exam

考核构成

  • 课堂表现Participation 10.0% This course encourages students to participate in class’ discussion and practice several written techniques in Spanish. Several activities and homework will be provided by the professor with the propose of improving writing skills and critical reading.
  • 课题研讨Topic Discussion 10.0% As the compulsory reading of this subject, How to Write a Thesis by Umberto Eco introduce the students about the main challenges of an academic writer. Students are expected to sustain discussion about specific chapters of the book.
  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 10.0% The mid-term exam consists in the presentation of personal statement with the research topic that the student plans to develop in the future. During the classes, students will need to present their result in an oral presentation (10 to 15 minutes).
  • 期末考试Final Examination：论文 70.0% The final exam consists of a research plan, written in Spanish. Students need to offer a proposal with the research question, literary review and basic methodology that could become their final essay taking into consideration requirements established by Fudan University.

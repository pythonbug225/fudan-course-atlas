课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍大型稀疏线性方程组和特征值问题的迭代解法，包括稀疏矩阵、基本迭代法、投影算法框架、正交投影 Krylov 子空间方法、斜投影 Krylov 子空间方法、预条件迭代法、预条件技巧、并行实现、多重网格方法、区域分解方法、隐式重启动、Krylov-Schur方法等。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces iterative methods for solving large sparse linear systems and eigenvalue problems. Topics include sparse matrices, basic iterative methods, projection algorithm frameworks, orthogonal and oblique projection Krylov subspace methods, preconditioned iterative methods, preconditioning techniques, parallel implementations, multigrid methods, domain decomposition methods, implicit restarting, the Krylov–Schur method, the Jacobi–Davidson method, and more.

教学目标

使学生了解并熟悉现代迭代法的基本思想、投影空间选取、投影算法格式设计及收敛性分析、预条件格式、预条件子构造、重启动技巧等内容，初步具备求解实际大型科学和工程问题的能力。 This course introduces iterative methods for solving large sparse linear systems and eigenvalue problems. Topics include sparse matrices, basic iterative methods, projection algorithm frameworks, orthogonal and oblique projection Krylov subspace methods, preconditioned iterative methods, preconditioning techniques, parallel implementations, multigrid methods, domain decomposition methods, implicit restarting, the Krylov–Schur method, the Jacobi–Davidson method, and more.

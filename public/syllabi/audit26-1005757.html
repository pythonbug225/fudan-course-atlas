课程简介（中文）

本课程是教授现代前沿寄生虫研究知识的一门医学专业课程。课程由博士生主讲。旨在阐明人体寄生虫病的病原体本质，促进寄生虫病的诊断、防治等的研究，提高博士生文献阅读能力和锻炼思维逻辑能力，是医学博士生的学位专业课。

课程简介（英文）

The course is a medical professional course which is taught by doctoral students. The purpose of this course is to discuss the pathogenesis of human parasitic diseases, promote the research of diagnosis and prevention of parasitic diseases, and improve the ability of doctoral students to read papers and exercise their logical thinking ability. It is a professional degree course for medical doctoral students.

教学目标

本课程主要由博士生讨论重要寄生虫的致病、免疫、分子诊断与防治等，着重论述最新的寄生虫研究热点，现代寄生虫研究技术等。通过上述研究热点展开讨论学习，把所学的理论知识与实际应用相结合，培养博士生的创新思维能力。

The course mainly discusses the pathogenicity, immunity, molecular diagnosis and control of important parasites, focusing on the latest research hotspots of parasites and modern research techniques of parasites. Through the above research hotspots to discuss and study, the theoretical knowledge and practical application are combined to cultivate the innovative thinking ability of doctoral students.

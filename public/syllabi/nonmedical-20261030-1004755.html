气候变化与极端天气

2025秋 · GEEC10017.01 · 高聿超

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

在“气候变化与极端天气”课程中，学生们即将踏上一场充满探索的旅程，深入了解气候变化的科学原理，以及为何全球各地的极端天气事件越来越频繁。这门为各学科背景的学生设计的入门本科课程，不仅涵盖了基础的大气科学原理，还将探讨维系地球气候的动态系统。通过课堂讲解、实地考察和亲自动手的数据分析项目，学生们将深入探索气候变化的内在机制、极端天气事件（如台风、飓风、干旱、洪水、热浪、野火等）背后的物理过程，以及科学家如何使用模型来预测这些现象。在培养批判性思维和解决问题的能力的同时，学生还将研究人类活动对气候变化的影响以及减缓与适应的策略。通过本课程的学习，学生将掌握面对我们这个时代最紧迫挑战的知识和技巧，采取积极有效的方式来应对气候变化和极端天气。

课程简介（英文）

In "Climate Change and Extreme Weather," students embark on an investigative journey to demystify the science behind climate change and the increasing occurrence of extreme weather events around the world. Aimed at students from a variety of academic backgrounds, this introductory undergraduate course integrates fundamental atmospheric science principles with an examination of the dynamic systems that govern Earth’s climate. Through a blend of lectures, field study, and hands-on data analysis projects, students will delve into the mechanisms of climate change, the physical processes driving extreme weather events (such as typhoons/hurricanes, droughts, floods, heatwaves, wildfires, etc.), and the methodologies scientists use to model and predict these phenomena. Emphasizing critical thinking and problem-solving, students will also investigate the role of human activity in climate change and the strategies for mitigation and adaptation. By the end of the course, students will be empowered with the knowledge and skills to navigate the complex issues surrounding climate change and extreme weather, promoting a proactive and informed approach to one of the most pressing challenges of our time.

教学目标

Upon completing this course, students will: Climate Systems: Gain a foundational understanding of the components and processes that make up the Earth's climate system. Weather vs. Climate: Differentiate between weather and climate, understanding the timescales and dynamics that distinguish short-term weather events from long-term climate patterns. Climate Variability: Learn about the natural and anthropogenic factors that cause climate variability and change, including greenhouse gases, solar radiation, volcanic activity, and oceanic circulations. Extreme Weather: Examine the science behind extreme weather events such as hurricanes, tornadoes, droughts, floods, and heatwaves, focusing on their cause, development, and impacts. Climate Modeling and Prediction: Explore the principles of climate modeling and the use of statistical and dynamic models to predict climate change and extreme weather events. Scientific Inquiry and Data Analysis: Develop skills in scientific inquiry and data analysis, using real climate and weather data to conduct basic research projects. Communicating Climate Science: Enhance abilities to communicate complex climate science concepts effectively to a non-specialist audience, fostering greater public understanding and awareness.

教学方式

Teaching Methods: Lectures and Presentations: Key concepts, theories, and scientific principles will be introduced through lectures and multimedia presentations. These sessions will cover the fundamental science of climate change, climate modeling techniques, the mechanics of extreme weather events, and the latest research findings. Case Studies: Analysis of case studies will be a core component of the curriculum, enabling students to examine the impacts of extreme weather events and climate change on various regions and communities. This approach fosters critical thinking and application of theoretical knowledge to real-world scenarios. Group Project: Students will collaborate on projects that include hands-on climate modeling using simplified interfaces and online simulation tools, as well as analysis of historical climate data and extreme weather events. These activities are designed to develop practical skills in data interpretation and climate prediction, deepen understanding of course concepts, and encourage teamwork, research, and analytical thinking. Field Study: Cloud observation exercises on campus will be organized to offer experiential learning opportunities. Students will analyze different cloud types and their implications for weather patterns, enhancing their understanding of atmospheric processes through direct observation. Student Presentations: Research-based presentations on recent extreme weather events will allow students to explore causes, impacts, and response measures. This encourages application of theoretical knowledge to current real-world scenarios while developing presentation and research skills. Discussion and Debate: Class discussions and formal debate sessions will be held to encourage dialogue on the ethical, social, and economic implications of climate change and extreme weather. This will help students develop their communication skills and form well-rounded viewpoints on complex issues. These teaching methods are designed to engage students actively with the material, enhance their understanding of complex scientific concepts, and equip them with the skills and knowledge to address the challenges of climate change and extreme weather effectively.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction to Climate Change and Extreme Weather Overview of course objectives and key concepts in climate science. Observations, data, and evidence for climate change. Week 2: Atmospheric Composition Greenhouse gases, the greenhouse effect, and human contributions. Aerosols: sources, types, and impacts on climate and weather. Week 3: Climate Feedback Mechanisms Positive and negative climate feedback loops. The roles of water vapor, ice-albedo feedback, and clouds in the climate system. Week 4: Ocean-Atmosphere Interactions Ocean circulation patterns and their effects on climate. Ocean's role in heat storage and redistribution, as well as sea-level rise. Week 5: Cloud Observations Week 6 - 7: Climate Models and Projections Introduction to climate modeling, types of models, and prediction methods. Hands-on activities with basic climate model output. Week 8: Extreme Weather Phenomena I: Hurricanes and Tropical Storms Formation and intensification processes of tropical cyclones. The impact of sea surface temperatures and atmospheric conditions. Week 9: Extreme Weather Phenomena II: Droughts, Heatwaves, Wildfires Causes and effects of prolonged droughts, heatwaves, and wildfires. The role of atmospheric conditions in extreme heat events. Week 10: Extreme Weather Phenomena III: Floods and Heavy Precipitation Mechanisms leading to heavy rainfall and flooding. Influence of atmospheric patterns on precipitation events. Week 11: Extreme Weather Phenomena IV: Extreme Cold and Snow Storms Formation of snow storms and extreme cold weather. Impact of polar vortex on winter extreme weather. Week 12: Current Extreme Weather Events Presentation Week 13: Policy and International Response to Climate Change Overview of global climate agreements, policies, and negotiations. Role of technology and policy in climate change mitigation. Review of key concepts, discussion on integrating knowledge into action. Week 14: Climate Change Debate Student groups debate on climate change topics, such as the pros and cons of geoengineering. Critical evaluation of opposing viewpoints and arguments. Week 15: Future of Climate Research Highlights of emerging research topics in climate science. Carbon neutrality and other emerging topics: Emission reduction, carbon capture, and alternative energy solutions. Week 16: Final Exam

课程简介（中文）

本课程将会介绍Maulik-Okounkov的stable envelope理论及其进展，以及在几何表示论、计数几何中的应用

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces Maulik-Okounkov’s stable envelope theory, its recent progress, and applications to geometric representation theory and enumerative geometry.

教学目标

Upon completion of this course, the student * is able to give definition of stable envelope and state the existence result * knows the R-matrix and Yang-Baxter equation * knows the construction of quantum groups from Nakajima quiver varieties * is able to describe the quantum multiplication by divisors on Nakajima quiver varieties using quantum groups * is able to produce the main ideas in the proofs of the most important results connected to the notions above.

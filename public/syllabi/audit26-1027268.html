课程简介（中文）

语篇是说话者在一次言语交际中说出/写出的连续的语句组成的整体，或由单个语句独立构成的完整交际单位。换言之，语篇是具有语篇性的语言交际单位，其构成符合一定语言社区的规约性模式和认知心理。语篇现象及其规律不能由语句内的语言学（主流的语言学包括语法学）揭示；而从语篇的角度，可以更合理地分析包括句式、时体、语气与情态等语言学中的经典问题。

本课程旨在帮助学生初步掌握语篇语言学的基本概念和方法，树立起语篇的观念，学会从语篇的角度更全面、准确地观察和解释语言现象。

课程简介（英文）

Discourse (or ‘Text’) is defined as a whole composed of continuous sentences spoken/written by a speaker in one verbal communication act, or a complete communicative unit composed of one single sentence independently. In other words, discourse is a language communication unit with texture, and its composition conforms to the conventional patterns and cognitive psychology of a certain language community. The phenomena and patterns of discourse cannot be revealed by linguistics-within-sentences (mainstream linguistics, including grammar); From the perspective of discourse, classic linguistic issues such as sentence structure, tense and aspect, mood and modality, can be analyzed more reasonably.

This course aims to help students gain a preliminary understanding of the basic concepts and methods of discourse linguistics, establish a concept of discourse, and learn to observe and explain language phenomena more comprehensively and accurately from the perspective of discourse.

教学目标

引导学生掌握语篇的概念，学会在合理分类的基础上从语篇的角度更加全面、合理地分析语言现象包括主流语言学一直未能给出公认解释的现象和问题，如时体、情态与语气、信息结构、语篇中的管界与衔接连贯、元话语与话语标记等，掌握语篇中的话语模式的分析方法。

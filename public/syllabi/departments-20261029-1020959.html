课程简介（中文）

本课程讲授公司金融学基本理论。通过本课程学习，学生将掌握公司金融学涵盖的所有知识要点，并将学习运用课程中学到的理论来具体分析企业投融资决策，公司治理结构，企业并购重组等相关案例。

课程简介（英文）

This undergraduate course provides a comprehensive introduction to the principles and practices of corporate finance. Students will explore the key concepts and tools used in financial decision-making within a corporation. The course covers essential topics such as time value of money, risk and return, stock valuation, financial statement analysis, capital budgeting, capital structure, payout policy and working capital management.  Emphasis is placed on the application of theoretical concepts to real-world scenarios, equipping students with the analytical skills necessary for effective financial management.

教学目标

The purpose of this course is to help the students develop deep insights on important topics in the area of corporate finance. By the end of the course, students will be able to evaluate investment opportunities, make informed financial decisions, and understand the impact of financial policies on a firm's value. This course is ideal for students pursuing careers in finance, accounting, consulting, or any field that requires a solid foundation in corporate financial principles.

教学方式

Lectures and discussions Case studies and real-world applications Group projects and presentations

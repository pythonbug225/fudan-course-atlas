课程简介（中文）

This course is designed to provide students with a solid understanding of modern macroeconomics theory, focusing on its application to the Chinese economy. We will cover three key topics: economic growth, structural transformation, and business cycles.

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to provide students with a solid understanding of modern macroeconomics theory, focusing on its application to the Chinese economy. We will cover three key topics: economic growth, structural transformation, and business cycles.

教学目标

Our aim is to equip students with the tools of modern macroeconomics so they can comprehend the essential features of both the global economy and China's economy. By the end of the course, students will have a grasp of the factors driving China's growth miracle, the main challenges facing its economy, and be able to suggest policy changes for future reforms.

教学方式

Face to face teaching, assignment and final paper

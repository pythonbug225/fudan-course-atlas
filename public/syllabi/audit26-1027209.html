课程简介（中文）

内容包括：（1）认知语言学概说；（2）汉语中的隐喻和转喻；（3）原型范畴/家族相似性和汉语研究；（4）凸显和汉语中的凸显现象；（5）有界和无界和汉语研究；（6）象似性和汉语研究；（7）认知图景理论和汉语研究，主要介绍相关概念及其在语法、语义理解方面的运用。以丰富的事实说明汉语中的哪些现象与认知有关。

课程简介（英文）

The course of “Cognitive Linguistic Approach to Chinese” mainly includes: (1) an outline of cognitive linguistics. cognitive linguistics are roughly classified into two disciplines: cognitive grammar and cognitive semantics; figure-ground theory; trajectory-landmark; frame. (2) metaphors and metonymies in modern Chinese; (3) prototypes or family resemblance and their application to Chinese; (4) prominence and the relevant phenomena in Chinese; (5) boundedness or unboundedness and the research of relevant phenomena; (6) iconicity and the research of relevant phenomena; (7) the theory of cognitive scene and its application to the Chinese Language, introducing some key terms and their application to the grammar or semantic understanding in Chinese. Various Chinese facts are used to demonstrate the relevant theories.

教学目标

汉语认知语言学这门课为选修课，认知语言学在世界语言学界日益受到重视。本课程旨在通过对相关内容的讲授，使学生了解认知语言学的核心理念、基本概念，了解汉语中的哪些现象存在认知基础，以便用这种理论方法进行研究。

教学方式

讲授和讨论相结合。

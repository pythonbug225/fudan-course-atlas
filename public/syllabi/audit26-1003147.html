课程简介（中文）

外科学的具体内容包括：骨科常见疾病（运动系统检查、骨折、骨关节结核、骨关节感染、非化脓性关节炎、骨肿瘤、手外伤等）、泌尿外科常见疾病（泌尿系统损伤、感染、结核、尿石症、尿路梗阻、生殖系统肿瘤等）、胸外科常见疾病（胸部外伤、肺癌、食管癌、纵膈肿瘤等）和外科手术学。外科学的教学过程，包括课堂讲授和临床示教两方面。讲授内容以常见病、多发病为重点，也要介绍一些国内外医学的新成就，以了解现代医学的新动态。授课时注重基础与临床结合、理论与实际结合，充分利用病例示范讨论、大体标本及影像资料、以及录像或电影等电化教具，使授课既生动、具体，又能突出重点。除授课内容外，教材中的其他内容则作为学生自学之用。示教内容以临床常见病为重点，给同学展示疾病典型的临床表现、实验室检查和辅助检查，以及疾病的常规治疗原则。示教中应强调和患者的交流以及人文关怀。

课程简介（英文）

The specific content of surgery includes common orthopedic diseases (such as motor system examination, fractures, bone and joint tuberculosis, bone and joint infections, non suppurative arthritis, bone tumors, hand injuries, etc.), common urological diseases (such as urinary system injuries, infections, tuberculosis, urolithiasis, urinary tract obstruction, reproductive system tumors, etc.), common thoracic diseases (such as chest trauma, lung cancer, esophageal cancer, mediastinal tumors, etc.), and surgical procedures. The teaching process of surgery includes both classroom lectures and clinical demonstrations. The teaching content focuses on common and frequently occurring diseases, and also introduces some new achievements in domestic and foreign medicine to understand the latest developments in modern medicine. During teaching, attention should be paid to the combination of basic and clinical knowledge, theory and practice. Case demonstration discussions, gross specimens and imaging materials, as well as audio-visual teaching aids such as videos or movies, should be fully utilized to make the teaching vivid, specific, and highlight key points. Except for the teaching content, other contents in the textbook are used for students to self-study. The teaching content focuses on common clinical diseases, showing students typical clinical manifestations, laboratory and auxiliary examinations, as well as the principles of routine treatment for diseases. In teaching demonstrations, emphasis should be placed on communication with patients and humanistic care.

教学目标

本课程旨在提高学生对普通外科基本知识和基础疾病的理解和认识。通过外科学总论和普通外科各论的讲解，从解剖学、生理学、病理生理学、诊断学和外科治疗原则等多方面讲授各个疾病的诊疗原则。课程讲授过程中需加强人文医学教育和医德医风教育，弘扬医者精神的“真”“善”“美”，培养守初心、铸信念、强人文、有大爱的创新型卓越医学人才。

要求掌握和熟悉包括：骨科常见疾病、泌尿外科常见疾病、心胸外科常见疾病的病理生理、临床表现、诊断与鉴别诊断要点、防治原则和手术适应证等，了解骨科、泌尿外科和心胸外科常见疾病的病因。

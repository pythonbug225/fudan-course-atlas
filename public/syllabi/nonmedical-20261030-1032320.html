智能传播

课程简介（中文）

本课程为复旦大学新闻学院双学位课程，全英文授课。 本课程将主要从媒介心理学与认知科学的视角，帮助你深入理解人工智能应用对传播方式、个体生活以及社会所产生的影响。 课程将首先深入探讨这些新兴技术如何改变我们的沟通方式、思维方式与生活方式，以及它们对文化与社会结构所带来的影响，以及这些影响如何改变现有的传播学理论与研究。鉴于本课程的出发点从媒介心理学与认知科学，本课程将更加侧重于人类—人工智能/机器传播，而非其他类型的人工智能相关传播形式。 考虑到该领域的快速发展与变化，课程将通过充分的课堂讨论，与大家共同探索和思考这些议题。

课程简介（英文）

This course will provide you with in-depth understanding of artificial intelligence applications’ impact on communication, individual life, and the society, primarily from communication science and psychology perspectives. We will start with the history and fundamental knowledge of AI technology, and then delve into the impact of these emerging technologies on ways we communicate, think, and live, as well as their influence on our culture and society. As AI has been deeply integrated into our lives, this course will emphasize more on human-AI/machine communication than other forms of AI-related communication. Given the fast-changing nature of this area, we will engage in discussions to explore all these ideas together.

教学目标

By the end of this course, I expect to be able to: Describe simplified processes underlying artificial intelligence techniques. Evaluate the impact of artificial intelligence on society and individual lives. Understand and analyze ethical problems of artificial intelligence. Demonstrate the ability to associate communication theories with AI-mediated communication, and to reconceptualize these theories in AI contexts. In order to achieve those objectives, you will also need to develop/improve the following skills throughout this course: To enhance academic abilities in information seeking and critical reading. To develop critical thinking skills on the topic of communication, technology and social change. To develop skills to collaborate with and learn from others.

教学方式

This course wil be seminar based.

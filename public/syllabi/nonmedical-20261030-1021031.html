课程简介（中文）

本课程涵盖核科学基础知识、核科学基本工具与方法、核能、核武器、辐射的生物学效应与防护，以及核技术在生命科学、材料、工业、农业等领域的应用。课程将帮助学生思考如何把所学的核物理基础知识应用到具体的研究工作中。我们期望通过本课程学习，使学生深入掌握核技术知识，融会贯通，并拓展至其他学科领域。

课程简介（英文）

The course covers the basic knowledge of nuclear science, the basic tools and means of nuclear science，nuclear energy, nuclear weapons, the biological effects of radiation and protection and the applications of nuclear technology in life science, materials, industrial, agricultural and other scientific fields. Interdisciplinary applications and researches will be emphasized. We hope, through this course, students will come to understand the knowledge of nuclear technology, and expand their knowledge to other disciplines as well.

教学目标

Nuclear technology is technology that involves the reactions of atomic nuclei. It has played, and continues to play, a significant role in various disciplines after the discovery of radioactivity and the development of nuclear science. Therefore, it is necessary that the essence and application of nuclear technology should be comprehensively introduced. We hope, through this course, students will come to understand the knowledge of nuclear technology, expand their knowledge to other disciplines and enhance the ability of scientific research and exploration.

教学方式

Introduction to Nuclear Technology Related Knowledge. Discussion of skill developing questions. Oral presentation of recent development.

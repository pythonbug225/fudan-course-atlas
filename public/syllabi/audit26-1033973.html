课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在系统性地探讨药物递送系统的分类、前沿技术及研究进展。 课程将涵盖纳米颗粒、脂质体、RNA-LNP、CAR-X等主流及新兴递送体系，深入解析每类系统的设计逻辑、理化特性与应用场景。通过本课程的学习，学生将掌握药物递送的核心原理，敏锐捕捉领域前沿动态，并重点关注相关技术在基础科研与临床转化中的最新突破。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to systematically explore the classification, cutting-edge technologies, and research advances in drug delivery systems (DDS). The curriculum covers mainstream and emerging delivery platforms, including nanoparticles, liposomes, RNA-LNP, and CAR-X, providing an in-depth analysis of the design logic, physicochemical properties, and application scenarios of each system. Through this course, students will master the core principles of drug delivery, gain insights into the latest field dynamics, and focus on the most recent breakthroughs in basic research and clinical translation.

教学目标

  1. Fundamental Knowledge: Comprehend the design principles of DDS and monitor cutting-edge research trends worldwide.
  2. Clinical & Industrial Insight: Assess the progress of DDS in drug development and clinical settings to identify future directions in therapeutics.
  3. Innovation & Critical Thinking: Evaluate current technologies to propose optimizations, fostering the ability to innovate and think critically.
  4. Ethics & Responsibility: Strengthen the understanding of research ethics, professional conduct, and the social impact of scientific work.

教学方式

The course employs a multifaceted pedagogical approach designed to foster innovation and active engagement:

  1. Multimedia-Based Frontier Lectures: High-quality instructional videos and curated presentations (PPTs) are utilized to systematically analyze the latest global breakthroughs and dynamic applications in drug delivery systems.
  2. In-depth Face-to-Face Seminars: Extensive in-person discussions are organized to facilitate profound academic dialogue, moving beyond traditional lecture formats to create an immersive learning environment.
  3. Interactive & Student-Led Sessions: Embracing the "flipped classroom" philosophy, the course features interactive segments where both faculty and students take turns leading the discussion. This bidirectional exchange encourages students to present insights from literature reviews, fostering critical thinking and intellectual synergy through high-frequency interaction.

1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 内科学（上）的基本内容包括： （1）内科学绪论、呼吸系统疾病、心血管系统疾病、消化系统疾病，教授以上各系统中常见疾病的病因、病理、临床表现、诊断与鉴别诊断要点、防治原则等。 （2）教授临床诊疗的基本思路，培养学生的临床思维能力，在床旁教学的过程中，指导学生如何接触患者，如何与患者建立基本关系并获取有效的病史信息，以及在体格检查等过程中的医学人文关怀等。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The basic content of Internal Medicine (Part I) includes: (1) Introduction to Internal Medicine, respiratory system diseases, cardiovascular system diseases, and digestive system diseases. It teaches etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, key points of diagnosis and differential diagnosis, and principles of prevention and treatment for common diseases in these systems. (2) It imparts the basic thinking for clinical diagnosis and treatment and cultivates students' clinical thinking ability. During bedside teaching, students are guided on how to approach patients, how to establish basic relationships with patients and obtain effective medical history information, and the importance of medical humanistic care in physical examinations and other processes.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

(1) To master the etiology, pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles of common and key diseases of various systems. (2) Combined with the significance of symptoms, signs and laboratory tests learnt during the diagnosis course, deepen the knowledge and understanding of the important features of the diseases, as well as the main points of differential diagnosis among the diseases. (3) Further proficiency in general physical examination and history writing. Ability Objectives To master the learning method of internal medicine, to learn the basic theories and fundamental knowledge of internal medicine as well as its clinical application by combining multidisciplinary knowledge, and to cultivate the ability of clinical thinking. To achieve the ability to obtain history information, symptoms and signs through contact with patients. Literacy Objectives (1) Correctly understand the relationship between disease and patient. To develop the concept of following an evidence-based perspective and evidence-based decision-making. (2) Throughout the teaching and learning process of internal medicine, students will understand the importance of developing a rigorous professional attitude to clinical work. (3) To develop students' ability to care for the humanities, so that they can demonstrate their deep care and great love for patients in medical practice.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

The theory course adopts multimedia teaching format, combined with clinical bedside teaching of key diseases and common diseases after the class. Bedside teaching adopts group teaching and case analysis and discussion based on cases. Presentation homework supplied by the students for every system. Practice for clinical thinking by CBL in clinical ward: 1)&nbspGastrointestinal and Hepatic System: 4 times, 2 hours each time, main diseases including Peptic Ulcer, Liver Cirrhosis, Acute Pancreatitis and Gastrointestinal bleeding 2)&nbspCardiovascular system: 4 times, 2 hours each time, main diseases including CHD, Essential Hypertension, Arrhythmias and Heart Failure 3)&nbspRespiratory system: 4 times, 2 hours each time, main diseases including Respiratory Failure, COPD, Pulmonary Infection and Lung cancer

To be familiar with the pathogenesis of hepatic encephalopathy, and to make a relationship with the therapy. To understand the etiology of hepatic encephalopathy and the diagnostic methods of subclinical hepatic encephalopathy. 11- Acute Pancreatitis To master clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and therapy of both kinds of acute pancreatitis. To be familiar with the differential diagnosis, complications, and prognostic factors of this disease. To be familiar with the epidemiology, pathophysiology, and pathogenesis of asthma. To be familiar with patient education and management of asthma. 8- Lung cancer To master the classifications, TNM staging, clinical manifestations and therapy of lung cancer. To be familiar with the epidemiology, pathophysiology and prevention of acute PE. To be familiar with the manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of chronic PE.

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍量子化学的基本知识、基本理论、基本方法及程序应用，使得学生掌握从基础量化理论到理论化学计算模拟的当代理论化学框架。本课程包括薛定谔方程基本理论和解法（含经典势阱体系，算符理论等），氢原子和角动量，量子力学基本假定，近似求解方法（变分和微扰），Hartree-Fock方程和自洽场方法，Hartree-Fock-Roothaan方程，半经验方法，密度泛函理论，常见计算化学软件，计算模拟建模和数值模拟常见算法。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the basic knowledge, theories, methods and program applications of quantum chemistry, enabling students to master the contemporary theoretical chemistry framework from fundamental quantitative theories to theoretical chemistry computational simulation. This course covers the basic theory and solution methods of the Schrodinger equation (including the classical potential well system, operator theory, etc.), hydrogen atoms and angular momentum, the basic assumptions of quantum mechanics, approximate solution methods (variational and perturbation), the Hartree-Fock equation and the self-consistent field method, the Hartree-Fock-Roothaan equation, and semi-empirical methods. Density functional theory, common computational chemistry software, common algorithms for computational simulation modeling and numerical simulation.

教学目标

 

学生按大纲具体要求，掌握课程的基本知识、基本理论、基本方法，掌握量子化学的基本原理和软件应用基础；了解量子化学求解物质体系中使用的各种理论计算方法的特点、应用范围及局限性，初步具有根据实际问题选择合适量子化学计算的能力。

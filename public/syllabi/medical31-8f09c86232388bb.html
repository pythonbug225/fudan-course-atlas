1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程针对八年制临床医学专业三年级学生开设，着重提升专业英语能力与医学人文素养融合。课程设置为7个教学单元（每单元2周），在涵盖诊断学、治疗学、心血管系统等临床主题基础上，有机融入精准医学、医疗人工智能等新兴医学范式，同步开展中医药文化传播、临终关怀伦理等课程思政教育。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | This course targets third-year clinical medicine students, focusing on professional English proficiency and medical-humanities integration. Structured into 7 units (2 weeks per unit), it covers clinical topics such as Diagnosis, Therapeutics, and Cardiovascular System, while embedding new medical paradigms (precision medicine, AI in healthcare) and ideological-political education (e.g., TCM promotion, end-of-life care ethics). Each unit employs a six-part framework: 1. Viewing to Warm Up : Short videos with interactive exercises (e.g., oral reports, summaries). 2. Medical Terminology : Root-word analysis and crossword puzzles for retention. 3. Reading for the Basics : Subject-specific texts with vocabulary guides and content-based exercises. 4. Expanded Reading : Multidimensional materials (medical humanities, socio-medical issues) with critical discussion prompts. 5. Language Skill Development : Grammar, translation, and vocabulary drills. 6. AI-Empowered Exploration : Independent research using AI platforms, fostering innovation and analytical rigor.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

By the end of this course, students will be able to: master core medical terminology (e.g., integumentary, cardiovascular, oncology) and apply it in clinical and academic contexts. analyze professional medical texts , integrating content-based learning with critical thinking. demonstrate advanced language skills through translation, academic writing, and oral presentations aligned with clinical practice. engage with medical humanities (e.g., ethical dilemmas, low-cost vs. high-tech treatments) to foster empathy and ethical decision-making. utilize AI tools for research and problem-solving, critically evaluating AI-generated content for accuracy and relevance. apply multimodal resources (videos, crosswords, digital platforms) to enhance learning efficiency and adaptability in a technology-driven medical environment.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

Flipped Classroom : Pre-class video analysis and AI exploration followed by interactive discussion (e.g., AI in Oncology Diagnosis). Case-Based Learning : Simulated clinical scenarios (e.g., emergency case discussions) to apply terminology and ethical reasoning. Multimodal Integration : QR code-linked audio/video resources, digital workbooks, and Chaoxing platform for collaborative assignments. Student-Centered Activities : Role-plays addressing socio-medical issues (e.g., Advocating for Low-Cost Treatments). Group discussion and debates on the issues covered.

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍经济物理在金融数据分析与市场建模中的研究进展和成果，涵盖价格波动特征、市场结构分析与复杂系统建模等内容。通过理论与案例结合，使学生理解物理学理论和方法在社会经济系统研究中的作用，掌握跨学科分析与建模能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course presents research progress and achievements in econophysics, focusing on financial data analysis and market modeling. Topics include price fluctuation characteristics, market structure analysis, and complex system modeling. By integrating theory with case studies, the course enables students to understand the role of physical theories and methods in the study of socio-economic systems, and to develop interdisciplinary analysis and modeling skills.

教学目标

Through learning the application of physics theories and methodologies to financial markets, the course aims to inspire students to deepen their understanding of emergent phenomena in complex systems and to foster their engagement in future multidisciplinary research.

教学内容与进度安排

1.  1st week: On the discrete microscopic modeling of complex systems General approaches to complex physical, socio-economic and biological systems. 2.  2nd week: Complex system and financial markets A general discussion on the characteristics of complex system to demonstrate financial markets as typical systems with a complex nature. 3.  3rd week: Introduction to the mathematics of finance Introduction of the stochastic processes for price and the no-arbitrage principle in financial markets. 4.  4th week: Discussion on the standard finance theory Introduction of the efficient market hypotheses and the derivation of Langevin equation, Fokker-Plank equation of price returns, and the derivation of Black-Scholes equation. 5.  5th week: Fluctuations, correlations in fluctuations of financial markets I Introduction of the power law distribution of price return, the slow decay of autocorrelation of financial time series, the scaling law and fractality of distribution functions. 6.  6th week: Fluctuations, correlations in fluctuations of financial markets II Introduction of the random matrix theory for price correlations and their applications in the portfolio optimizations 7.  7th week: Fluctuations, correlations in fluctuations of financial markets III Introduction of the order book statistics and the application of the fluctuation-dissipation theorem. 8.  8th week: Financial market model based on local information I Introduction of the percolation model and Ising spin model for financial markets. 9.  9th week: Financial market model based on local information II Extended percolation model with the prospect theory for risk aversion 10. 10th week: Financial market model based on global information I The art for designing bottom-up type models of financial markets 11. 11th week: Financial market model based on global information II Minority Game, Grand Canonical Minority Game and the Market Game models including basic structures, adaptive modeling, pay-off function, and the price formation. 12. 12th week: Financial market model based on global information III Theory for the spontaneous cooperation in Minority Game including the reduced strategy space, the interaction of correlated and anti-correlated group and the effective size of herd. 13. 13th week: Option pricing in econophysics I Properties of option, the core discipline of financial engineering including European and American options, the put-call parity and the risk aversion with options. 14. 14th week: Option pricing in econophysics II The derivation of data-driven option price formula with the application of no-arbitrage principle including the calculation of the pay-off, the no-biased price assumption and the numerical integration of conditional price distributions. 15. 15th week: Option pricing in econophysics III The derivation of data-driven formulae for the optimized hedging strategies including the calculation of risk and its minimization and the numerical integrations. 16. 16th week: Machine learning in finance I Review of the recent studies on the application of machine learning to finance 17. 17th week: Machine learning in finance II A special topic on modeling the price fluctuations with the generative adversarial networks 18. 18th week: Assignments and Q&A

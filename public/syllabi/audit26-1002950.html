课程简介（中文）

本课程教学对象为MBBS的本科生。《儿科学》是一门研究儿童生长发育、卫生保健及疾病防治的医学科学，是医学生的必修课。自胎儿至青少年是一个体格、心理、智力不断成熟的过程，机体在解剖、生理、病理、疾病的发生发展以及防治方面与成人相比有明显不同，且在不同年龄阶段各有其特点。通过课程内容，讲授儿科学基础知识、儿科常见病和多发病的理论知识。

课程简介（英文）

Pediatrics involves pulmonology, cardiology, gastroenterology, nephrology, rheumatology, hematology, neurology, endocrinology and metabolic diseases, as well as children’s health care, growth and development, neonatal diseases, critical diseases, pediatric acute abdomen, congenital malformations, immunodeficiency, mental health, adolescent medicine, etc.

教学目标

This course is designed to let students to have an intimate knowledge of law of development, diagnosis, management and prevention of diseases during infants, children and adolescent and the differences to adults. Based on professional knowledge, integrate ideological education factors, strengthen emotional education, and promote interactive feedback.

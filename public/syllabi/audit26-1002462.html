课程简介（中文）

医学微生物学》是我国高等医学院校学生必修的一门基础医学课程，主要研究与医学有关的病原微生物的生物学性状、致病性、免疫性、微生物学检查法和防治原则的科学。 通过本门课程的教学，使学生掌握与医学相关的微生物（细菌、病毒、真菌等）的基本生物学性状、感染与抗感染免疫的机理、感染性疾病的诊断、预防与治疗原则，为学习相关基础医学和临床医学课程奠定坚实的基础。

课程简介（英文）

Medical Microbiology introduces basic principles and then applies clinical relevance in the academic preparation for physicians. This rigorous course includes many etiological agents responsible for global infectious diseases. It covers the biology of bacteriaand viruses as well as other pathogens and the related infectious diseases in humans. In order to foster a student’s ability to solve problems in their future clinical career, the course provides the conceptual basis for understanding pathogenic microorganisms and focuses on their mechanisms of pathogenicity. It also provides opportunities to develop informatic and diagnostic skills in microbiology, including the practical application and interpretation of laboratory tests for the diagnosis of infectious diseases.

教学目标

Knowledge

  • Define principles of microbial taxonomy, structure, physiology, genetics，immunology and pathogenesis;
  • Develop a knowledge of microbial organisms and the infectious diseases they caused;
  • Understand the principles of prevention and treatment of pathogenic microorganisms infection in humans.

Skills

  • Demonstrate use of the laboratory to diagnose infections, including appropriate specimen collection and selection of tests;
  • Develop the ability to manipulate the laboratory tests to identify pathogenic microorganisms;

Understand the principles underlying the laboratory diagnostic tests used to identify  pathogenic microorganisms

教学方式

Our Departments of Microbiology endeavor to prepare candidates for the Doctor of Medicine degree with the following professional objectives for their successful career. A strong desire to practice these objectives in Medical Microbiology (and in other aspects of undergraduate medical education) will substantially enhance a student’s acquistion of knowledge, humility, and technical skills for a successful and rewarding career.

Demonstrate respect for others

Students are asked to attend all class meetings, particularly small group exercises.

Students respond in dignified manner to faculty, staff, and student colleagues, exemplifying their maturity and empathy.

Students agree to abide by appropriate biosafety practices when in BSL-2 exercises.

Exhibit honesty and integrity

Students abide by the Honor Code of Fudan University in all exams, quizzes, and graded assignments.

Students discuss honestly any grading oversights or computer/technology errors with Course faculty as soon as any discrepancies arise.

Practice concern through communication, respect, and understanding

Students grasp opportunities to “rehearse” professional etiquette when dealing with faculty in situations of uncertainty and/or ignorance.

Students appreciate the enormous benefits of faculty variation in education and/or background which facilitatesunderstanding of diverse populations.

Students encourage their classmates to heightened levels of understanding by promoting questions and through supportingshared personal experiences.

Perform assignments responsibly and with accountability

Students complete the assignments of each lecture, according to the Section schedule.

Students arrive promptly at classes as well as laboratory activities.

Students work effectively as team members when assigned, realizing they gain more when fully engaged in teamwork.

Students seize the opportunity to learn from a diverse group of faculty instructors in small group settings, focused on basic relevance of clinical cases.

Seek excellence and scholarship

Students strive to excel at all four areas of Medical Microbiology, including immunology, bacteriology, mycology, and virology.

Students read to supplement the foundational scaffold of information presented in the lectures.

Students seek to develop treatment behaviors that will reduce the incidence of antimicrobial resistance in the world.

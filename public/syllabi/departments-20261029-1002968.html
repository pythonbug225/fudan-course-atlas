微观经济学原理

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

1776 年《国富论》出版时，重商主义仍是全球经济思想的主流。亚当・斯密在书中提出了极具说服力的观点：自由市场将推动繁荣，政府干预仅在少数特殊情况下有必要。彼时的英国已是全球贸易领域的重要力量，且正经历重大变革，这些变革最终催生了工业革命。詹姆斯・瓦特对蒸汽机进行了重大改良，珍妮纺纱机也在兰开夏郡的工厂中得到应用 —— 该地区距离当时的全球贸易中心利物浦并不遥远。一场将深刻重塑人类历史、并在未来两百年间提升人类福祉的革命，就此拉开序幕。 时光快进到今天，经过 40 年的经济增长，中国已濒临加入高收入国家行列，并有望在未来数十年内成为全球最大经济体。上海拥有全球规模最大的国际贸易港口。从积极层面看，人工智能等新技术发展迅猛，甚至可能日新月异；但从消极层面而言，作为新型重商主义的保护主义正不断抬头。而我们十分幸运，能在这样一个时代，在充满活力的上海这座城市学习经济学。 在本课程中，我们旨在将古典经济理论与现代经济现象相联结，并培养运用经济学视角看待世界的能力。我们将探讨一系列核心概念，包括均衡、贸易收益、市场效率、市场失灵以及政府干预。课程结束时，学生应能理解经济资源如何通过市场与非市场两种机制进行配置，以及这两种配置方式所涉及的效率问题。同时，我们也期望学生能逐步养成观察经济现象、独立进行批判性思考的能力。

课程简介（英文）

When the Wealth of Nations was published in 1776, mercantilism dominated global economic thought. In his book, Adam Smith presented a compelling argument that free markets would lead to prosperity, with government intervention necessary only in a few exceptions. At that time, England was a major force in global trade and was undergoing significant changes that would eventually lead to the Industrial Revolution. James Watt had made substantial improvements to the steam engine, and the spinning jenny was adopted in Lancashire factories, not far from Liverpool, which was then the global trade center. It was the dawn of a revolution that would profoundly reshape human history and improve welfare over the next two hundred years. Fast forward to today, after 40 years of economic growth, China is on the brink of joining the high-income country club and is set to become the world's largest economy in the coming decades. Shanghai hosts the largest international trading port in the world. On the positive side, new technologies, such as artificial intelligence, are evolving rapidly, possibly on a daily basis. On the downside, protectionism, a new form of mercantilism, is on the rise. We are fortunate to study economics at this time, in the vibrant city of Shanghai. In this course, we aim to connect classical economic theories with modern economic phenomena, and we will practice viewing the world through an economic lens. We will explore concepts such as equilibrium, gains from trade, market efficiency, market failure, and government intervention. By the end of the course, students should be able to understand how economic resources are allocated through both market and non-market mechanisms as well as their efficiency implications. Students are also expected to develop the ability to observe economic phenomena and think critically on their own.

Learn basic principles of microeconomics Command the basic tools in economic analysis Understand the price mechanism and how a market allocates resources Understand the circumstances under which the market fails to work efficiently Learn policy tools to correct market inefficiency Train critical thinking skill

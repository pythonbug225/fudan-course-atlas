Art Investment

2025秋 · MBA70053.02 · 黄文叡

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course covers five major topics: 1. Art History in the market - designed for the students without a background in art. Based on the performance of the art market; 2. Introduction to the art market - let students understand the different trading platforms of artworks and the different pricing standards of artworks on each platform; 3. Planning for art collecting - teach students how to use accurate and effective methods to construct art collections, and explore the ways used by the world's major collectors for their art collections; 4. Practice and management of art investment; 5. Laws and regulations of art trade and related taxation - discuss legal disputes and solutions in art trade from the past cases, and discuss how to use art as a tool for tax deduction.

教学目标

This course combining theory and practice aim to explore the development and mechanism of Chinese and western art market, and then set up a professional and feasible methodology for art collecting and investment in art, in order to improve students' ability of art appreciation, and provide students engaged in art business as their career options.

教学内容与进度安排

Class/Week 1 Topic: Art History in the Market Content: 1. Chinese Art–Paintings, Ceramic Works of Art, Furniture and Collectibles 2. Western Art–From Impressionist Art to Contemporary Art 3. Case Studies on Major Artists Reading: Class/Week 2 Topic: Introduction to Art Market Content:： 1. The Segments of Art Market– a. Primary Market、Secondary Market and Private Dealing b. Global Market vs. Regional Market/Buyer’s Market vs. Seller’s Market c. The Relationship of Artists, Dealers and Collectors d. The Mechanism of Art Market in the East and West 2. The Value of Art– a. Aesthetic Value vs. Market Value b. Pricing in the Primary Market vs. Secondary Market c. Pricing in the Regional Market vs. Global Market d. Pricing for the Living Artists vs. Deceased Artists 3. Speculation a. Nominal Price and Market Bubble b. The Threat of Stardom in the Art Market c. 9 Ways to Drop an Emerging Artist Dead 4. The introduction and Features of the World's Major Art Fairs Reading: Class/Week 3 Topic: Planning for Art Collecting Content: 1. How to Build a Collection a. The Methods and Attitude for Art Collecting b. 4-ty for Art Collecting: Authenticity, Quality, Availability, Liquidity c. Case Studies on the Top 5 Art Collectors in the World 2. The Acquisition of Artworks and the Methodology a. Where to Acquire Artworks b. How to Evaluate the Emerging Artists c. Understanding a Work of Art: provenance, literature, exhibition d. Art Appraisal and Valuation 3. Art Shipping, Handling, Maintenance, Storage and Insurance Reading: Class/Week 4 Topic: Practice and Management of Art Investment Content: 1. Art as An Alternative Investment a. Art Fund b. NFT Art c. Art as A Vehicle for Asset Allocation d. Art Loan 2. How to Build an Investment Portfolio a. The Market Trend and Art Index b. The Timing and Methods for Choosing Target Works c. The Methods to Select the Long- and Short-Term Targets d. Fund Size, Lock-Up Period and ROI 3. Risk Management a. The Risk of Art Investment b. How to Predict Investment Risk Effectively c. How to Control Investment Risk Effectively Reading: Class/Week 5 Topic: Laws, Regulations of Art Trade and Related Taxation Content: 1. Art Law a. Contracting for Art Transactions b. Common Disputes over Buying and Selling (Case Studies) c. The Traps and Precautionary Measurements of Art Transactions 2. Related Taxation a. Payment and Due Diligence b. Taxation of Cross-border Art Transactions c. The Feasibility of the Involvement of Artworks in Family Trusts Reading: Class/Week 6 Topic: How to Use Art as a Tool for Tax Deduction Content: 1. Tax Deduction a. Art as a Gift b. Art as a Corporate Asset c. Non-for-Profit Art Organizations 2. Professionals and Specialties Needed for Art Transactions a. Lawyers and Services Provided b. Accountants and Services Provided c. Art Consultants and Services Provided Group Presentations

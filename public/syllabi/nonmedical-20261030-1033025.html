课程简介（中文）

本课程是热带镜像对称的介绍。前四周将涵盖热带几何的基础知识，并解释热带枚举Gromov-Witten （GW）不变量，它涉及在环面基上计数图。接下来的五周将涵盖拓扑量子力学在图上的基础知识，展示其振幅描述热带GW不变量，并描述镜像图。接下来的三周将涵盖Landau-Ginzburg-Saito （LGS）理论的基础知识，并制定热带GW不变量与LGS相关函数之间的热带镜像关系。最后四周将涵盖GW和LGS理论中的引力后裔，并证明它们之间的镜像关系。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an introduction to Tropical Mirror Symmetry. The first four weeks will cover the basics of tropical geometry and explain the tropical enumerative Gromov-Witten (GW) invariants, which involve counting graphs on a toric base. The following five weeks will cover the basics of topological quantum mechanics on graphs, show that its amplitudes describe the tropical GW invariants, and describe the mirror map. The next three weeks will cover the basics of the Landau-Ginzburg-Saito (LGS) theory and formulate the tropical mirror relation between the tropical GW invariants and LGS correlation functions. The last four weeks will cover the gravitational descendants in GW and LGS theories and prove the mirror relation for them.

教学目标

Upon completion of this course, students will gain a basic understanding of tropical geometry, enumerative GW invariants in the tropical setting, topological quantum mechanics, and Landau-Ginzburg-Saito theory. The course will expose students to one of the very powerful ideas in modern mathematical physics, the mirror symmetry conjecture, which connects various aspects of symplectic geometry, complex geometry, and quantum field theory.

课程简介（中文）

本课程授课内容涵盖俄罗斯宪政制度、政党政治、经济转型、外交政策调整，以及俄罗斯的大国外交、地区外交。课程采用课堂讲授、案例分析与学术研讨相结合的方式，帮助学生理解俄罗斯内政外交的联动性及其对全球政治经济、全球治理的影响。本课程适合国际关系、政治学及相关专业研究生选修，有助于培养对欧亚地缘政治的分析洞察能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course covers Russia's constitutional system, party politics, economic transformation, foreign policy adjustments, as well as its major-power diplomacy and regional diplomacy. Through a combination of lectures, case studies, and academic seminars, the course helps students understand the interplay between Russia’s domestic and foreign policies and their impact on global political economy and governance. Designed for graduate students in international relations, political science, and related disciplines, it aims to cultivate analytical and insightful perspectives on Eurasian geopolitics.  

教学目标

This course is grounded in Fudan University's educational orientation of being rooted in China while embracing a global perspective. Aligned with the talent development objectives of the International Politics and Global Governance program,aims to cultivate high-level foreign affairs professionals with both a strong sense of national identity and a broad international outlook.  

Value Guidance:  
Students will gain an in-depth understanding of Russia's political system and foreign policy, fostering their recognition of China's major-country diplomacy with distinctive characteristics. The course also seeks to instill a sense of global community and commitment to serving national strategic needs.  

Knowledge Impartation:  
The course systematically covers Russia's constitutional system, party politics, economic transformation, and the evolution of its foreign policies. It analyzes their impact on global governance and the geopolitical landscape of Eurasia, strengthening students' theoretical grasp of international politics.  

Skill Development:  
Through interactive teaching methods such as case studies and policy simulations, the course enhances students' critical thinking, cross-cultural analytical skills, and international negotiation competencies, equipping them with practical abilities to engage in global governance.  

With an interdisciplinary approach integrating political science, international relations, and area studies, the course contributes to the cultivation of versatile talents, thereby bolstering China's voice in global governance.

教学方式

this course employs a teaching approach that combines instructor-led lectures, case analysis, and academic seminars, aiming to help students gain an in-depth understanding of Russia's political system, foreign policy, and global role. The course cultivates students' critical thinking and analytical skills by examining the historical context, political structure, and theoretical and practical aspects of Russia's foreign policy.  

During instruction, professors integrate classical literature, the latest research findings, and real-world case studies to guide students in conducting comprehensive analyses of Russia's domestic politics and external relations. Through group discussions and academic seminars, students not only deepen their understanding of the background of each political event but also enhance their communication and debate skills in interactive settings. Additionally, the course will organize thematic workshops, inviting experts from relevant fields to deliver guest lectures, providing a combination of practical perspectives and theoretical insights.  

Through this interactive and case-driven teaching methodology, students can better grasp key concepts in Russian politics and international relations, laying a solid foundation for future research or policy analysis in related fields.

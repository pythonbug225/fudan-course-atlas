课程简介（中文）

本课程系统讲解分子影像学基础及无创影像技术在生物药物开发中的应用。学生将学习PET/SPECT/MRI/光学成像等多模态技术原理，掌握影像探针在靶向药物筛选、生物药物体内分布监测、实时治疗效果评价中的关键作用。课程涵盖三大核心模块：1. 影像指导的靶向药物设计与优化；2. 无创定量评估药物代谢动力学；3. 影像生物标志物替代传统活检的临床转化策略。通过学习，学生将理解影像技术如何加速精准药物研发进程，掌握诊疗一体化探针、人工智能影像分析等前沿动态，并通过全英文文献研讨、学术汇报及案例解析（如FDA影像指导药物获批实例）显著提升专业英语听说写能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course explores the integration of molecular imaging and biopharmaceutical R&D, focusing on how non-invasive imaging (PET/SPECT/MRI/optical) revolutionizes drug development. Key topics include: 1. Imaging-assisted target validation and lead compound screening using targeted probes; 2. Real-time monitoring of drug biodistribution and pharmacokinetics; 3. Quantitative imaging biomarkers for therapeutic efficacy evaluation, replacing invasive biopsies. Students will acquire cross-disciplinary knowledge, analyze cutting-edge trends (e.g., theranostics, AI-based image analysis), and enhance professional English skills through critical literature review (e.g., EMA/FDA case studies) and simulated international academic presentations.

教学目标

通过本课程学习，使学生了解分子影像学和无创影像指导下生物技术药物开发等方面的基础知识；认识不同类型影像技术和探针在靶向性药物筛选、生物药物开发、实时治疗效果评价等方面的重要作用；最终目标是使学生掌握分子影像学和药物开发这一前沿交叉领域的研究动态，并提高学生的专业英文听，说，写水平。

教学方式

课堂教学。

注重课堂交流能力（10%）；期中考试以15分钟英文ppt演讲形式进行（30%）。

期末考试开卷考试。在查阅英文资料后，撰写一篇影像与药物开发方面的专业论文（3000-5000 words，60%）。

教学内容与进度安排

Chapter 1: General Information of Molecular Imaging (2 courses)

Content:

  1. The definition of molecular imaging
  2. The relationship between the conventional imaging and molecular imaging
  3. The history,development and future of molecular imaging

Aims:

  Learning the basic information of the molecular imaging, the relationship between the conventional medical imaging and molecular imaging, the advantages and potential applications of molecular imaging in drug discovery.  

 

Chapter 2: Basic principle of imaging modalities (8 courses)

Content:

  1. X-rayimage
  2. Computer tomography (CT)
  3. Ultrasonic imaging (UI)
  4. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI）

   How MRI works?

   T1-weighted MRI

   T2-weighted MRI

   Magnetic resonance spectroscopic imaging

   Functional MR

   Chemical exchange saturated transfer (CEST) imaging

  1. Positron emission tomography (PET)
  2. Single photon-emission computed tomography (SPECT)
  3. Optical imaging

   Bioluminescence imaging (BLI)

Fluorescence-mediated tomography (FMT)

Photoacoustic imaging (PAT)

Brain lesion detection, Hemodynamics monitoring, breast cancer diagnosis

Aims:

  Learning the basic theory, imaging features and application ranges of different imaging modalities used in clinical diagnosis, comparing the advantages and disadvantages of different imaging modalities, predicting their potential development in molecular imaging.

Content:

The application of SPECT radiotracers in clinic

   Near-infrared fluorescence probe

   Tumor vasculature targeting fluorescence probe

   Tumor microenvironment targeting optical imaging probe

  1. Contrast media used in ultrasonic imaging
  2. SERS probes and their mechanism
  3. Photoacoustic imaging probes
  4. Multimodalimaging media

   MRI/PET contrast media

   MRI/optical imaging contrast media

  1. The applications of the multimodal imaging probes used in clinic

Aims:

  Learning the basic theory of contrast media mediated signal enhancement, the role of contrast media played in diagnosis of major human diseases, how to increase the targeting specificity of the contrast media, how to prepare the novel contrast media with high sensitivity, low toxicity and good biocompatibility.   

 

Chapter 4: The applications of image-guided therapy (6 courses)

Content:

  1. Image-guided drug delivery
  2. Image-guided surgery
  3. Image-guided diagnosiswith assistance of endoscope
  4. The technologies and probes used in image-guided therapy

Aims:

  Learning the role of imaging played in evaluating the disease development stages before the treatment, dynamically monitoring the bio-distribution of the drug in vivo during the treatment and real-timely evaluating the therapeutic efficacy after the treatment.   

 

 

Chapter 5: The application of molecular imaging in drug discovery (6 courses)

Content:

  1. The application of imaging technology in pharmacokinetic study;
  2. The application of imaging technology in Clinical 0 phase;
  3. The application of imaging technologyin monitoring therapeutic efficacy.  

Aims:

  Learning the role of image played from the initial targeting domain selection to the tracking the drug dynamic distribution in animal models to the finally real-timely evaluating the therapeutic responses of the testing drug.   

 

 Chapter 6: The development and clinical practice for radiomics (2 courses)

Content:

  1. The definition of radiomics;
  2. Origin and development of process for radiomics;
  3. Common research methods of radiomics;
  4. The clinical application for radiomics with existing operating procedures.

Aims:

  Combining with the current status of imaging, learning the concept of radiomics, and the relationship between them. Understand the content and role of radiomics in tumor diagnosis and efficacy evaluation, and make a prospect for its development.

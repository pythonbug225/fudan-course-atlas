课程简介（中文）

神经精神疾病II课程将是基础医学的选修课程，将利用细胞/遗传学原理来解释精神疾病的临床表现和发病机制。尤其是神经退行性疾病和神经系统发育异常

课程简介（英文）

Neuropsychiatric Disorders Ⅱ uses the cellular / genetic principles to explain the clinical appearance and pathogenesis of psychiatric disorders. especially neurodegenerative diseases and developmental anomaly of nervous system

教学目标

Learning Purpose：students obtain a basic understanding of several of the most serious and common psychiatric disorders

Capability Purpose：gain experience in reading, presenting, and discussing important scientific papers, and obtain new insights that will help you in your own field of research.

Ideological and political Purpose：to cultivate medical humanistic qualities and the scientific research spirit of courage to explore and make breakthroughs

教学方式

Lectures, combined with topic discussion，students presentation， homework or reports

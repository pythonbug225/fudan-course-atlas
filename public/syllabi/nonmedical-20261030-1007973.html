Art Investment

2025秋 · MBA70053.01 · 黄文叡

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course covers five major topics: 1. Art History in the market - designed for the students without a background in art. Based on the performance of the art market, especially the top 15 sales of each artist, the students can easily understand what kinds of art made in what certain periods of time by what types of artists would take an important place and play a crucial role in the history of art as well as in the art market; 2. Introduction to the art market - let students understand the different trading platforms of artworks and the different pricing standards of artworks on each platform; 3. Planning for art collecting - teach students how to use accurate and effective methods to construct art collections, and explore the ways used by the world's major collectors for their art collections; 4. Practice and management of art investment - lead students to construct investment portfolio, understand the methods used to build an investment portfolio from practical cases, and then discuss the risks and risk management that investment managers should face; 5. Laws and regulations of art trade and related taxation - discuss legal disputes and solutions in art trade from the past cases, and discuss how to use art as a tool for tax deduction.

教学目标

This course combining theory and practice aim to explore the development and mechanism of Chinese and western art market, and then set up a professional and feasible methodology for art collecting and investment in art, in order to improve students' ability of art appreciation, and provide students engaged in art business as their career options.

教学方式

The students are obliged to participate in the class and engaged in the group discussion apart from fulfilling their assignments. Besides, some designated field trips to visit art galleries, museums, auction houses, or art fairs will be made possible as part of this class requirement to have the students experience a variety of art activities in the art world.

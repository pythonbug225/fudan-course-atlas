课程简介（中文）

英语学术论文写作是一门旨在教授学生学术研究论文的结构及如何独立完成论文撰写的课程。本课程将系统讲解学术论文的组成部分、写作流程以及优秀学术写作所需的核心技能。课程采用"写作工作坊"的教学模式，使学生能够将所学知识付诸实践。同时，所有课程作业的设计也都遵循这一理念，让学生通过实践操作来深化课堂所学的理论知识。 本课程采用全英语授课。

课程简介（英文）

English Academic Paper Writing is a course intended to teach students about the structure of an academic research paper and how to write one of their own. This course will teach students about the different parts of an academic paper, the process of writing a paper, and the important skills needed for good academic writing. This course will incorporate the use of the writer’s workshop format so that students are able to apply what they learn in practice. The assignments in this course are also designed, with the same purpose, so that students can gain more practical experience of the theoretical knowledge learned in class. The teaching language of this course is English.

教学目标

At the completion of the course students will be able to identify and define the characteristics of an academic research paper. Students will also be able to explain the structure of a research paper and produce different parts of the paper as needed. Finally, students will develop the skills needed to write and construct their own research papers.

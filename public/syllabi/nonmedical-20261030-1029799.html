课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提高德语专业学生在中级阶段的写作能力，帮助学生将所学德语知识运用到与日常生活、高校学习和跨文化交流相关的写作实践中。本课程引领学生自主掌握各类应用文体、数据分析和观点陈述等方面的写作交际技能，并激发学生在写作上的创造力和表达力。同时也积极引导学生将文化差异、中国故事和文化国际传播的内容纳入自己的写作交际中，树立学生在中德文化交流中的主体意识，推动他们发挥应有的能动作用。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to systematically enhance the writing proficiency of students majored in German Studies at the intermediate level. It focuses on the practical application of acquired language skills in rich, contextual writing scenarios related to everyday life, university studies, and intercultural exchange. Content and Methodology: Participants will autonomously develop the competence to compose various text types (including exposés, essays, reports, and argumentative commentaries). The course provides specific techniques for presenting data, structuring arguments coherently, and persuasively presenting viewpoints. It is designed to actively foster written creativity and individual expressive power. A special emphasis is placed on integrating intercultural perspectives. Students are guided to thoughtfully incorporate experiences of cultural difference, elements of "Chinese Stories," and questions of international cultural transmission into their texts. This process serves to build a distinct personal standpoint within the Sino-German dialogue and empowers them to assume an active, constructive role in intercultural communication.

教学目标

按照学校十五五规划，从加强国际传播能力和培养外语复合型人才的目标出发，提高学生在中级阶段的德语写作能力，推动学生充分把握德语的基本知识、写作技巧与写作规范，能用流畅、熟练、地道的德语进行日常交际，跨文化沟通，讲好中国故事。

教学方式

1）分主题进行写作方法的指导 2）循序渐进地进行课堂写作训练 3）以自评、互评和教师点评的方式改进写作质量 4）推动学生结合自己的文化背景和跨文化经验，进行有创造性的模拟现实的写作工作坊

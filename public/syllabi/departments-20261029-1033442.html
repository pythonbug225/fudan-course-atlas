课程简介（中文）

本课程拟讲授常见的金融产品或衍生金融工具的风险管理及其控制策略。按照产品分类，主要讲授期货（包括商品期货、股指期货、国债期货）、债券及利率衍生品（利率远期、利率互换、货币互换）、期权及结构化理财产品（包括简单数值期权、特殊的复杂奇异期权）的风险管理，特别是套期保值、风险对冲的策略设计、运用及评价。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to impart knowledge and practical insights into the risk management and control strategies for common financial products and derivative financial instruments. Structured by product category, it primarily covers the risk management of futures (including commodity futures, stock index futures, and treasury bond futures), bonds and interest rate derivatives (including interest rate forwards, interest rate swaps, and currency swaps), as well as options and structured financial products (including simple binary options, and complex exotic options). Special emphasis will be placed on the design, application, and evaluation of hedging and risk offsetting strategies.

教学目标

了解常见的金融产品或衍生金融工具的风险管理及其控制策略，学会期货、债券及利率衍生品、期权及结构化理财产品的套期保值或风险对冲的策略设计、运用与评价，为从事金融产品设计、投研和风险管理打下良好的知识基础。

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍数字经济的核心微观经济学原理，帮助学生理解科技如何重塑市场结构、企业战略与竞争格局。课程内容涵盖需求与定价、市场势力、战略互动与福利分析等主题。课程运用博弈论与产业经济学分析企业合作、进入与平台竞争。通过案例学习与课堂讨论，学生将探讨网络效应、算法定价及市场监管等现实问题。课程旨在为学生建立分析数字市场商业模式与政策挑战的经济学框架，使其能够运用经济学思维理解并评估数字时代的经济现象。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the microeconomic principles and foundation that underlie today’s digital economy. It equips students with basic economic tools to understand how technology transforms markets, firm strategy, and competition. Core topics include demand and pricing, market power, strategic interaction, and welfare analysis, all reframed for digital contexts such as online platforms, data-driven pricing, and two-sided markets. Game theory and industrial organization are used to study cooperation, entry, and platform competition. Real-world cases—covering digital platforms, auctions, and market design—illustrate how firms leverage network effects and strategic data use. Through lectures, discussions, and applied exercises, students will develop an economic framework for analyzing issues like platform dominance, algorithmic pricing, and market regulation in the digital age. By the end of the course, participants will be able to apply microeconomic reasoning to evaluate business models and policy challenges in digital markets.

教学目标

This course aims to build students’ understanding of the fundamental economic principles that shape the digital economy. It provides a rigorous analytical framework grounded in microeconomic theory, enabling students to interpret how digital technologies—such as platforms, data analytics, and algorithms—affect market structure, competition, and consumer welfare. The course emphasizes the integration of theory and practice, guiding students to apply tools from game theory, industrial organization, and information economics to real-world digital contexts. Through lectures, case studies, and group projects, students will develop critical thinking, data literacy, and strategic reasoning skills essential for navigating and leading in the digital age. Beyond technical competence, the course fosters ethical awareness and social responsibility, encouraging students to evaluate the broader implications of digital innovation for fairness, efficiency, and sustainability. By the end of the course, students will be equipped to analyze, design, and assess strategies and policies in complex digital market environments.

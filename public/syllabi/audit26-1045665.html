课程简介（中文）

生物学哲学已经成为当代科学哲学中最具活力的研究领域之一。一方面，生物学自身的迅速发展，在本体论、认识论和方法论层面产生了一系列新的重要哲学问题，回应和解决这些问题迫切地需要哲学家的深度参与；另一方面，生物学领域的成果也为哲学家思考和解决传统的哲学问题提供了新的资源和启示。

本课程是关于生物学哲学的导论性课程，主要面向两类学生：对生物学中的哲学问题感兴趣的哲学学院学生和对于哲学问题感兴趣的生命科学大类的学生。当然也欢迎其他对生物学哲学感兴趣的同学选课。

本课程将带领学生了解关于生命科学的经典哲学议题，包括生物学中是否有定律？模型和机制在生物学中扮演着怎样的角色？生物学中的解释是否可以还原为物理和化学中的解释？什么是自然选择？什么是基因？自然选择的作用对象是有机体、基因还是群体？演化论的成果对于伦理学有哪些可能的影响？如果所有的生物个体在本质上是自私的，那么利他行为又是如何而来的？什么是演化博弈论？它有可能解释社会行为的起源吗？等等。

在对这些问题进行学习和探讨的过程中，我们也将逐步了解和掌握开展科学哲学研究的基本方法。

课程简介（英文）

Philosophy of biology has become one of the most dynamic research fields in contemporary philosophy of science. On the one hand, the rapid development of biology itself has given rise to a series of new important philosophical issues at the ontological, epistemological and methodological levels. Responding to and solving these issues urgently requires the in-depth participation of philosophers; on the other hand, achievements in the field of biology have also provided new resources and inspiration for philosophers to think about and solve traditional philosophical issues.


This course is an introductory course on the philosophy of biology. It is mainly intended for two types of students: students in the School of Philosophy who are interested in philosophical issues in biology and students in the life sciences who are interested in philosophical issues. Of course, other students interested in philosophy of biology are also welcome to take courses.


This course will lead students to understand classic philosophical issues about life sciences, including whether there are laws in biology? What role do models and mechanisms play in biology? Are explanations in biology reducible to explanations in physics and chemistry? What is natural selection? What are genes? Does natural selection act on organisms, genes, or populations? What are the possible implications of the results of evolutionary theory for ethics? If all living things are inherently selfish, how does altruistic behavior come about? What is evolutionary game theory? Could it potentially explain the origins of social behavior? etc.


In the process of studying and discussing these issues, we will also gradually understand and master the basic methods of conducting research on the philosophy of science.

教学目标

（1）了解生物学哲学研究的基本问题； 

（2）掌握开展生物学哲学研究的基本方法； 

（3）对于生物学哲学中的经典争论，能够把握辩论双方的主要观点和论证策略；

（4）提高阅读和理解专业文献的能力、学术沟通与表达的能力以及学术写作的能力；

（5）培养批判性思维，能够将生物学哲学应用到实际科学事件的思考中去。

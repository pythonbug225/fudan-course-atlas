1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程是复旦大学医学本科生全英文专业选修课程，聚焦临床研究全流程实践。学生通过案例分析、结构化工具训练与模拟设计，熟悉队列研究、随机对照试验及大数据研究等核心方法。课程强调“做中学”，以中期答辩和终稿修订为驱动，指导学生完成临床研究计划书，培养批判性思维与独立科研能力，助力未来参与创新性医学研究。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | This course is a fully English-taught elective for medical undergraduates at Fudan University, focusing on practical training in the entire clinical research process. Through case analysis, structured tool training, and simulated study design, students gain proficiency in core methodologies such as cohort studies, randomized controlled trials, and big data research. Emphasizing "learning by doing," the course guides students in developing a clinical research proposal via midterm presentations and final revisions. It cultivates critical thinking and independent research skills, preparing them to engage in innovative medical research.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Students explain the core characteristics and appropriate contexts of case reports, cohort studies, randomized controlled trials, meta-analysis, and real-world research. Students articulate key design components of clinical research, including scientific question, study design, data collection, statistical analysis, and report methods. Students critically review published studies using standardized tools, identify methodological limitations, and propose improvements. Students draft a clinical research protocol under guidance, utilizing literature search tools and structured templates to outline the research design framework. Students maintain a lifelong learning attitude through academic practice, integrating evidence-based updates in medical knowledge.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

Case-Based Lecture Strategy: Deconstructs real-world research papers to demonstrate design logic and reporting standards, reinforced by interactive Q&A sessions. Organization: Lectures interspersed with mini-discussions, supported by standardized checklists as analytical tools. Group Workshop Strategy: Simulates real-world research scenarios through collaborative tasks to train design, evaluation, and teamwork skills. Organization: In-class small-group activities. Staged Protocol Development and Dynamic Feedback Loop Strategy: Enhances iterative design skills through a structured "draft-peer review-revision" cycle. Organization: Multi-week assignments with phased submissions, anonymous peer feedback, and instructor evaluations to drive continuous improvement.

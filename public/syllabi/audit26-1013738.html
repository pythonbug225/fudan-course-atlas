课程简介（中文）

《临床药理学概论》是一门面向医学与药学等临床相关专业研究生的英文课程，围绕药物在体内的作用机制、体内过程（ADME）、药效评价及临床应用等核心内容展开，涵盖新药研发、个体差异、不良反应与临床试验等模块。课程注重理论与实践结合，通过案例分析、小组讨论与专题研讨，提升学生对药物治疗科学问题的理解与解决能力，为后续从事临床药物研究、药物评价与转化应用奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

"Introduction to Clinical Pharmacology" is an English-taught graduate course designed for students in clinical-related disciplines such as medicine and pharmacy. Centered on the mechanisms of drug action, pharmacokinetic processes (ADME), efficacy evaluation, and clinical applications, the curriculum spans critical modules including drug development, individual variability, adverse reactions, and clinical trials. Emphasizing the integration of theoretical knowledge with practical applications, the course employs case studies, group discussions, and seminars to enhance students' analytical and problem-solving skills in pharmacotherapy. It aims to equip learners with a robust foundation for future endeavors in clinical drug research, pharmacological evaluation, and translational medicine.

 

教学目标

本课程面向药学、临床医学等专业的硕士及博士研究生，旨在从临床问题出发，系统解析药物在人体内的作用机制与治疗路径，强调药理数据在新药研发、临床试验设计及治疗策略优化中的核心作用。课程采用文献导读+案例解析的教学方式，引导学生在多源数据背景下锻炼批判性思维，提升将药理学知识转化为临床应用与科研设计的能力。

教学方式

本课程共计18周、36学时，面向临床相关专业（医学、药学）研究生，采用全英文授课。课程内容以临床药理学核心理论为主干，结合药物研发实例、真实世界用药问题与前沿研究热点，系统讲解药物在人体内的动态过程、效应机制及其在不同疾病治疗中的临床应用策略。教学强调跨学科整合能力与临床转化思维的培养，配合小组案例分析、文献导读、课堂讨论和结课展示，全面提升学生的学术表达能力、批判性思维和临床药理实践素养。

教学内容与进度安排

课程主要模块与课时分配如下：

1. 绪论与基础理论（第1–4周，8学时） 本模块介绍临床药理学的发展脉络、研究范式与核心任务，结合经典药物案例讲解药物靶点识别策略、毒理机制解析及ADME过程在人体中的动态演变。通过机制梳理与模型入门训练，帮助学生构建“药物-人体-疾病”之间的功能逻辑框架。 目标：理解临床药理学的知识边界，掌握药物从分子机制到人体暴露路径的基础理论。

Introduction and Foundational Theories (Weeks 1–4, 8 hours)
This module outlines the historical evolution, research paradigms, and core missions of clinical pharmacology. Classic drug case studies illustrate strategies for target identification, toxicological mechanisms, and dynamic ADME processes in humans. Mechanism-based training and model simulations help students build a functional framework linking "drug-body-disease" interactions.

Objectives: Understand the scope of clinical pharmacology and master foundational theories from molecular mechanisms to human drug exposure pathways.

2. 临床应用与评价策略（第5–8周，8学时） 聚焦于药效动力学建模、药物相互作用机制、不良反应预测及个体化治疗策略。通过真实用药数据与治疗指南解读，引导学生建立“暴露-效应-安全性”之间的综合判断能力，理解特殊人群（如儿童、老年、肝肾功能异常）药物治疗中的关键考量。 目标：能够分析不同人群中的药效变化，理解合理剂量设定与治疗优化的科学逻辑。

Clinical Applications and Evaluation Strategies (Weeks 5–8, 8 hours)
Focuses on pharmacodynamic modeling, drug-drug interaction mechanisms, adverse reaction prediction, and personalized therapy. Real-world data and treatment guidelines guide students to analyze "exposure-effect-safety" relationships and address challenges in special populations (e.g., pediatric, geriatric, hepatic/renal impairment).

Objectives: Analyze variations in drug efficacy across populations and rationalize dosing strategies for therapeutic optimization.

3. 新药转化与监管科学（第9–11周，6学时） 介绍新药从IND申请到NDA审评的全过程，结合FDA/国家药监局审评报告拆解其中的临床药理模块，讲授如何撰写药代参数、DDI建议和特殊人群说明。引入模型辅助审评（如虚拟生物等效性、剂量调整模拟）等最新监管趋势。 目标：掌握监管科学中临床药理证据的生成、呈现与评价方式。

Drug Translation and Regulatory Science (Weeks 9–11, 6 hours)
Covers the drug development lifecycle from IND to NDA, dissecting clinical pharmacology components in FDA/NMPA review documents. Topics include PK parameter reporting, DDI recommendations, and special population labeling. Introduces emerging regulatory trends like model-informed drug development (MIDD) and virtual bioequivalence simulations.

Objectives: Master the generation, presentation, and evaluation of clinical pharmacology evidence for regulatory decision-making.

4. 系统疾病药理案例专题（第12–13周，4学时） 以系统疾病（如心血管、神经系统、代谢疾病、肿瘤和感染）为切入点，分析相关代表性药物的PK/PD特征、治疗挑战与临床应用路径。每节课聚焦一个系统疾病，配合文献导读与案例拆解进行深入剖析。 目标：训练学生将理论知识与临床决策相结合，提升临床药理知识的综合应用能力。

System-Specific Pharmacology Case Studies (Weeks 12–13, 4 hours)
Explores PK/PD characteristics, therapeutic challenges, and clinical pathways of representative drugs in systemic diseases (e.g., cardiovascular, neurological, metabolic, oncology, infectious). Each session pairs disease-focused lectures with literature reviews and case analyses.

Objectives: Integrate theoretical knowledge into clinical decision-making and enhance holistic application of pharmacology principles.

5. 模型导引与前沿认知（第14-16周，6学时） 介绍临床药理中的量化研究工具（如PBPK模型、QSP模拟、虚拟试验平台等）的原理与实际案例，理解模型在药物暴露预测、人群差异量化、路径转化分析中的作用与价值。 目标：建立对数字化药物评价手段的基本理解，为未来研究方法打下基础。

Modeling Tools and Advanced Concepts (Week 14-16, 6 hours)
Introduces quantitative tools (e.g., PBPK modeling, QSP simulations, virtual trial platforms) and their roles in exposure prediction, population variability analysis, and translational research. Real-world case studies highlight digital drug evaluation methodologies.

Objectives: Build foundational knowledge of computational pharmacology tools for future research.

6. 课程总结与汇报（第17–18周，4学时） 课程最后两周为学生团队展示环节，围绕一个新药/代表药物开展完整的临床药理分析，内容覆盖药物机制、PK/PD特征、临床研究路径与个体用药建议，要求英文汇报并现场答辩。 目标：提升科研沟通能力、整合表达能力与批判性思维，完成对全课程内容的综合性掌握与应用。

Course Summary and Final Presentations (Weeks 17–18, 4 hours)
Student teams conduct comprehensive clinical pharmacology analyses of a novel or representative drug, covering mechanisms, PK/PD profiles, clinical development pathways, and personalized therapy recommendations. Final deliverables include English presentations and live Q&A sessions.

Objectives: Strengthen scientific communication, integrative thinking, and critical analysis while synthesizing course content.

教学参考资料

该课程目前无全国统一的规划教材，拟采用《Drug Discovery and Evaluation: Methods in Clinical Pharmacology》（编著者Hans Gerhard Vogel.ISBN: 3540898905, Springer出版社，2011年）作为理论部分的参考，学术水平较高，同时符合我国政治把关标准。此外，课程大部分内容将以上市药物信息为例子进行授课分析，选用的参考资料是审评中心所公开的药物说明书。

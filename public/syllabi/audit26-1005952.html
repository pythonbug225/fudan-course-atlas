课程简介（中文）

职业卫生与职业医学进展主要是在本科职业卫生与职业医学课程的基础上，介绍该学科在主要领域的发展以及面临的问题，让同学充分认识到职业卫生与职业医学领域永远存在需要研究、解决的问题。 课程重点围绕几大职业危害因素与健康的关系、职业危害的控制策略、职业卫生的法律法规以及监督管理、 不同国家的职业卫生管理对策等主题进行介绍和讨论。本门课程可供预防医学或与人群健康相关专业的博士研究生、学术型硕士研究生和专业型硕士研究生修选修。

课程简介（英文）

Advanced Occupational Health introduces the developments and challenges in key areas of the discipline based upon the undergraduate course of Occupational Health and Medicine. It aims to help students fully recognize that there will always be issues in the field of occupational health and occupational medicine that require further research and resolution. The course focuses on introducing and discussing topics such as the relationship between major occupational hazards and health, strategies for controlling occupational hazards, occupational health laws and regulations, supervision and management, as well as occupational health management approaches in different countries. This course is available as an elective for doctoral students, academic master's students, and professional master's students specializing in preventive medicine or population health-related fields.

教学目标

1.掌握职业卫生与职业医学的工作理念，熟悉常见职业危害因素及其对健康影响的特点；

2.熟悉职业危害因素的控制原则及常用方法；

3.了解国家对职业卫生监管的思路及其他国家的相关发展；

4.了解健康促进在工作场所实施的优势及特色。

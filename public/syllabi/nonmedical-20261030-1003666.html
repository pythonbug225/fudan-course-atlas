课程简介（中文）

这门 2 学分的必修课概述了拉丁美洲文化研究的经典理论及最新发展。课程旨在全面呈现该大陆的文化成果，同时从历史、经济及政治层面探讨其对于思想史与艺术运动的影响。课程鼓励学生将研究方法付诸实践，并对西班牙裔文化的丰富传统展开研究。

课程简介（英文）

This two-credits compulsory course offers an overview on classical theories and recent developments of Cultural Studies in Latin America. The classes are disegned to cast a panoramic view on the cultural production of the continent, while examing the historical, economic and political dimension that have affected the history of ideas and artistic movements. The classes encourage the students to put into practice research methods and conduct research on the rich traditions of the Hispanic Culture.

教学目标

The objective of this course that students get a general knowledge of current debates and research methods of Cultural Studies, as a way to understand the cultural diversity in Latin American. Through literature, films, music and other expressions, the classes offer a multidimensional approach on the Spanish speking countries’ cultures, based on how the social structure have inetlectuals and artists have shaped the identity of continent.

教学方式

The teaching method combined professor’s explanations, interaction in the classroom, and homeworks. Each lesson will be divided into two sections: the first dedicated to analyze previous research and methodological tools of relevant scholars on Cultural Studies; and the second to apply the theoretical tools on a given artistic expression.

教学内容与进度安排

Presentation of the subject. Multidimensional approach to “culture”. Definition and problems of the concept. Norbert Elias and the historical genesis of concept “culture”. Intellectual history of “Civilization” and “Culture” in Latin America. Race and Nation in Esteban Echeverría, and Domingo Faustino Sarmiento to José Vasconcellos and José Ingenieros. The role played by intelectuals in the development of Latin American countries. Writers, and politicians in the XIX Century. Orientalism and perception of the landscape in Latin American XIX Century. Edward Said and the case of Latin America. From the landscape to identity. Nation-State, intelectuals and the role of culture in Latin American early XX Modernity and new culture in Latin American early XX century. Avant garde and popular culture. Raymond Williams’ approach to the Cultural Studies. “Selective traditions” in the Hispanic writers from Domingo Faustino Sarmiento to Jorge Luis Borges. Development of the “cultural field”: universities, publications and circulations of intelectuals and other cultural actors. Midterm Exam. “Peripheral modernities” in Latin American biggest cities. Representation of the city in Xul Solar, Jorge Luis Borges, and Adolfo Bioy Casares. Urban culture, modernization, and mass-media in the continent. The The definition of “cultural field” by Pierre Bourdieu, and its application to Latin America. Relationship between writers, intellectuals, and politics during the fifties and sixties. The case of La Casa de las Americas. Cinema, mass-media, and youth during the sixties. The relation between popular culture and arts. Current Debates: gender, and transculturation. Michael Foucault and biopolitics of Latin America. Globalization and culture: Latin American arts in front of the XXI Century. Final Exam.

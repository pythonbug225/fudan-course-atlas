健康与社会政策

2025秋 · MANA60021.01 · 朱剑峰

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程以人类学的视角来探讨健康与社会政策，核心问题是：生命如何通过健康被治理？我们并不将政策视为抽象的法律或自上而下的指令，而是通过阅读民族志，去理解健康政策如何在日常生活中成形，以及它们如何在不同人群之间分配照护、风险与责任。课程将借助一些基础的理论工具——生命政治、治理术与人口治理——展开，继而进入来自全球不同地区的一系列民族志案例。

课程简介（英文）

Course Description This course takes an anthropological approach to health and social policy by asking: how is life governed through health? Rather than treating policy as abstract law or top–down directive, we will read ethnographies that show how health policies take shape in everyday life, and how they distribute care, risk, and responsibility across different populations. Drawing on foundational theoretical tools—biopolitics, governmentality, and population governance—the course then moves through a series of ethnographic case studies from around the world. Each week focuses on a different dimension of policy: how populations are made and managed how data and evidence become instruments of governance, how addiction and mental health expose the limits of policy, how maternal health and gendered responsibility are framed in global health programs, how war and humanitarianism redefine health infrastructures , how pandemics regime govern risk, and how biological citizenship and therapeutic sovereignty create new forms of belonging and exclusion. By moving across these ethnographic sites, students will learn to see health policy not simply as official regulation but as lived practice, contested terrain, and moral horizon. The course emphasizes comparison across regions—Cuba, China, Iraq, West Africa, the U.S., and beyond—while also engaging with the global politics of health and medicine. Students will develop conceptual and empirical tools to critically analyze contemporary health policies and to imagine alternative ways of governing life.

教学目标

Understand anthropological approaches to studying health and social policy. Apply theoretical concepts such as biopolitics, governmentality, citizenship, sovereignty, and improvisation to concrete cases. Critically analyze how health policies shape everyday experiences of care, inequality, and responsibility. Compare ethnographic cases across different regions and policy regimes. Produce original analysis that connects anthropological insights to contemporary policy debates.

教学方式

It will be run as a seminar. We discuss the reading materials in class.

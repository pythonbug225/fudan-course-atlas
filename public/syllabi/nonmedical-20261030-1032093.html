Fixed Income

课程简介（中文）

The goal of this course is to learn the basics of Fixed Income and Credit Risk. There are three main parts, with the first two on interest rate risk and related derivatives and the last one on corporate bonds and credit default swaps. The course is an intensive 6-week course and I expect that it will be one of the most quantitative courses. I will try to focus largely on the tools, applications, and concepts that go into Fixed Income and Credit Risk. You should expect to apply formulas and do calculations. In some cases, you will need to use Excel or Matlab for calculations. In addition to the basic quantitative exercises, topics related to fixed income, as you will see, appears in our daily life everywhere. Importantly, it is tightly linked to macroeconomics and policymaking. Therefore, I will comment on latest news on fixed income periodically and lead in-class discussions.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students should be able to explain and communicate the nature of fixed income in an academic or a business forum, master the basic computation tools to make financial decisions related to fixed income and credit risk.

教学内容与进度安排

Core Lectures: Lecture 1: Introduction to Fixed Income Markets Reading: Veronesi 1 Lecture 2: Bond Valuation Reading: Veronesi 2 Lecture 3: Duration and Convexity Reading: Veronesi 3&4 Lecture 4: Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities Reading: Veronesi 7.4 Lecture 5: Spot and Forward Rates Reading: Veronesi 5 Lecture 6: Interest Rates Swaps and Floating Rates Bonds Reading: Veronesi 2.5, 5.4 Lecture 7: Interest Rate Options Reading: Veronesi 9, 11.1, 12.1 Lecture 8: Intro to Corporate Bond Market & Ratings Reading: Sundaresan 10 Lecture 9: The Merton Model and Corporate Bond Valuation Reading: Sundaresan 10 Lecture 10: Credit Default Swaps Reading: Sundaresan 18 Auxiliary Lectures (optional): Lecture 1: Monetary Policy Lecture 2: Chinese Bond Market Case Discussion Lectures (optional): Case 1: Deutsche Bank: Finding Relative-value Trades Case 2: Interest Rate Derivatives Case 3: First American Bank: Credit Default Swaps

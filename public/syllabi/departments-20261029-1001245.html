服务营销

2025秋 · BA30022.01 · 裘理瑾、范秀成

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程为复旦大学营销专业本科学生提供一个学习服务情境下高阶营销理论和商业实践的机会。 课程包含如下4项核心内容: 服务营销基本概念; 战略服务营销; 服务运营和服务系统管理; 服务营销与管理的高阶议题.

课程简介（英文）

This course offers a chance of learning advanced marketing knowledge in the service era from both theory and cases and transferring these ideas to individual own situation of Fudan undergraduate students majored in marketing. The course is composed of the following 4 parts of the content: Fundamental concept of service marketing; Strategic service marketing; Service operation and service system management; Advanced issues of service marketing and management.

教学目标

The course is committed to reach the following goals gradually and ultimately with the common efforts of the students: Introducing the students to key service marketing concepts and facilitating them to have overviews with a framework of theoretical knowledge. Developing students’ abilities of studying fast-moving marketing situations, in particular, China as an immature market and an emerging market in service industry as well. Enhancing students’ skills in thinking and decision-making with service marketing perspectives and insights in the complex service environment.

教学方式

The course will be designed as a hybrid course with the integration of online lecture and offline class study. The course will be organized by introducing the following teaching methods into the course framework, which are the lectures, the individual assignments, the group case studies and the group term project studies. For the term project study, the following steps of project development are recommended: The topic of the term project is New Product Development in Service Context. Carefully choose the research object. It could be a totally new-to-the-world service product which you want to introduce into Chinese market, or it is an incremental new product for a real and live brand/company in China. Draft a research plan for the project, describing the topic, research objectives, questions, procedure, approach, allotment of work, and possible difficulties. The plan is set up by the 3rd Carry out the plan, continuously discussing and reviewing every process of the project in the group. Referring to the instructor is welcomed. Draft and revise the report, using complementary and in-depth research. Finalize the report and prepare the group presentation of the project.

教学内容与进度安排

Prerequisite Course: This course is only opened to students who are majored in marketing or the students who are majored in business administration and have taken the Marketing Management course or Fundamental of Marketing course as the prerequisite course. Section 1:  Service Nature and its Implication (Week 1&2, by QIU) Nature of service industry Definition of service and service marketing Core concepts in service marketing Business implication of service marketing Section 2:  Service System (Week 3&4, by QIU) Customer experience Service quality Expectation management Service system Implication of service journal Implication of service blueprinting Group Case I presentation Section 3:  Service Marketing Communication (Week 5&6, by FAN) The role of communication in service marketing Communication Challenges and opportunities in service Branding of Communication Service Guarantee Group Case II report submission Section 4:  Demand Management and Revenue Management (Week 7&8, by FAN) Pricing policy in service marketing Time is a critical issue in service Demand management Capacity management Revenue and service productivity. Group Case III presentation Section 5:  Advanced Issues of Service Marketing (Week 9&10, by QIU&FAN) Relationship marketing and CRM Customer involvement and value co-creation Section 6:    Group Term Project Presentation (Week 11, by QIU&FAN) Section 7:    Final Exam (Week 12, Date and Time will be arranged by school) * Note: 3 credit hours per week

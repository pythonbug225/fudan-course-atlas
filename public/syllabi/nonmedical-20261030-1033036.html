课程简介（中文）

反常层源于代数簇拓扑研究，结构精妙，在表示论、数论及代数几何中有广泛应用。本课程将介绍其基础，并通过实例展示其威力。

课程简介（英文）

Perverse sheaves emerge naturally in the study of the topology of algebraic varieties and exhibit rich, subtle structures. They have found broad applications across representation theory, number theory, and algebraic geometry. This course will cover the foundations of perverse sheaves and demonstrate the efficacy of this framework through selected applications.

教学目标

介绍偏屈层的基本知识与技巧，帮助学生掌握研究代数簇拓扑学的基本工具。 Introduce the basic knowledge and skills of partial curvature layers to help students master the basic tools for studying algebraic cluster topology.

高等大气飞行动力学

2025秋 · AAST50027.01 · 艾剑良

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

高等大气飞行动力学是飞行器设计博士研究生专业基础课程之一。本课程主要内容包括： 飞机刚体运动动力学，飞行稳定性和操纵性分析，飞行品质与设计规 范，古典飞行控制 设计方法，现代飞行控制设计方法，典型飞行控制系统， 现代飞行控制技术等。 通过该 课程的学习，使得学生熟悉飞行控制的基本概念，能够运用 飞行控制知识对基本动力学 现象进行解译，熟悉飞行控制的基本设计理论 和方法，并用之进行飞行控制律的设计， 为今后从事飞行器控制系统设计、 制造以及研究工作打下基础。

课程简介（英文）

Advanced Atmospheric Flight Dynamics is one of the fundamental specialized courses for Ph.D. students in Aircraft Design. The main contents of this course include: - Rigid-body flight dynamics of aircraft - Flight stability and controllability analysis - Flying qualities and design specifications - Classical flight control design methods - Modern flight control design methods - Typical flight control systems - Modern flight control technologies Through this course, students will: - Become familiar with the fundamental concepts of flight control - Gain the ability to interpret basic dynamic phenomena using flight control knowledge - Master the essential design theories and methods of flight control - Apply these principles to flight control law design - Lay a solid foundation for future work in aircraft control system design, manufacturing, and research

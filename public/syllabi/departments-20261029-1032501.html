来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将结合近年营销实践中发生的重大变化，集中讨论营销新理论、新方法、新技术。主要内容包括四个部分：（1）价值共创。重点探讨顾客在价值创造和创新中扮演的角色，探讨顾客介入设计、生产与提供而导致的价值创造新过程与新方式，研究顾客与企业协同与配合的新理论与新方法，分析顾客参与价值创造过程中的激励机制和行为规范。（2）新技术与营销。重点分析大数据、移动通讯、人工智能等技术的发展和应用带来的营销机遇与挑战。（3）消费者幸福感和福祉。介绍幸福感相关理论，分析影响消费者幸福感的因素和渠道，探讨消费情景中提高幸福感的方法。（4）特殊专题。涵盖消费者跨文化研究、具身认知、及神经营销学。

课程简介（英文）

This course will focus on the significant changes that have occurred in marketing practice in recent years, concentrating on new theories, methods, and technologies in marketing. The main content is divided into four parts: (1) Value Co-Creation. This section emphasizes the role of customers in value creation and innovation, exploring the new processes and approaches to value creation resulting from customer involvement in design, production, and delivery. It examines new theories and methods of collaboration and coordination between customers and firms, as well as analyzing incentive mechanisms and behavioral norms governing customer participation in the value creation process. (2) New Technologies and Marketing. This part focuses on analyzing the marketing opportunities and challenges brought about by the development and application of technologies such as big data, mobile communications, and artificial intelligence. (3) Consumer Well-being and Happiness. This segment introduces theories related to happiness, analyzes the factors and channels influencing consumer well-being, and explores methods to enhance happiness within consumption contexts. (4) Special Topics. This section covers cross-cultural consumer research, embodied cognition, and neuromarketing.

教学目标

（1） 通过阅读大量的最新相关文献，让学生熟悉营销学科的前沿研究动向及其进展； （2） 提高学生的专业文献阅读能力； （3） 培养学生从纷繁复杂的实践和研究文献中识别和确定科学研究问题的能力； （4） 培养学生独立开展科研的能力； （5） 培养学生面向实践和服务社会的意识。

中国外商投资法

2026春 · LAW40001.01 · 唐应茂

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助学生了解中国外商投资法律框架及实务，涵盖规范外商来华投资和中国对外投资的法律、法规和规章，以及中国缔结的国际投资条约。 本课程探讨在现行法律制度下，境内外投资的不同架构方式，分析不同投资项目所涉问题，并总结这些投资项目的经验与教训。 本课程剖析不同投资项目的理论基础与动因，帮助学生理解影响外商对华投资及中国对外投资增减的各种驱动因素。 此外，本课程还涉及旨在培养学生在外商投资项目中进行法律尽职调查和法律文书起草实务技能的相关内容。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to assist students in understanding the legal framework and practices of Chinese foreign investment law, including laws, regulations and rules governing foreign inbound investments and Chinese outbound investments as well as international investment treaties entered to by China. This course discusses different methods/ways that inbound or outbound investments are structured under the existing legal regime, analyze issues arising from different investment projects and review experiences of, and lessons learned from, those investment projects. This course analyzes rationales and theories behind different investment projects to assist students in understanding the different forces driving the increase or decrease in foreign investments into or outside China. This course also includes topics that are intended to train students practice skills in relation to legal due diligence and legal document drafting in foreign investment projects.

教学目标

本课程旨在协助学生： (1) 学习中国外商投资法律框架； (2) 通过案例研究，理解不同的方式（即直接投资与另类投资）或在中国架构外商投资项目的不同途径； (3) 提升在识别问题、分析法律影响以及起草外商投资项目相关法律文书方面的法律技能；以及 (4) 理解不同外商投资法律和投资项目背后的基本原理与理论。 This course is designed to assist students in: learning the legal framework of Chinese foreign investment law; understanding different methods (i.e. direct investment vs. alternative investment) or different ways of structuring foreign investment projects in China through case studies; improving legal skills in identifying issues, analyzing legal impact and drafting legal documentation in relation to foreign investment projects; and understanding rationales and theories behind different foreign investment laws and investment projects.

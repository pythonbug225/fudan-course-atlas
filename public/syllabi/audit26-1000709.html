课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在将外交作为一个实践的过程，帮助学生了解实现外交政策的机制与机构、过程与手段、规范与技能，了解外交部、驻外使领馆、地方外事、对外经济、对外宣传等涉外机构的实务，并结合外交热点进行案例模拟，以帮助学生掌握外交实践的政策工具和业务技能。课程采取主题讲授与专家主题讲座相结合的方式，邀请在外交和外事管理一线工作的专家和相关专业学者授课，并在教师指导下进行专业模拟。

课程简介（英文）

To treats diplomacy as a practical process, this course helps students understand the mechanisms and institutions, processes and means, norms and skills for implementing foreign policies, and the practice of foreign institutions such as the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, embassies and consulates abroad, local foreign affairs office, foreign economic communication institutions. It also conducts case simulations to help students master the policy tools and skills of diplomatic practice. The course will invite experts in the front line of diplomacy and foreign affairs to give lectures, and conducts professional simulations under the guidance of teachers.

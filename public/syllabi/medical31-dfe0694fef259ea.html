1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 生物技术的迅猛发展促进了生物技术药物的大量生产。生物技术药物（又称生物药，Biological Drugs or Biologics）是指以细胞及其组成分子为原始材料，利用生物系统、活生物或其衍生物生产出来的用于预防、治疗和诊断的生物活性物质，包括多肽、蛋白质、酶、核酸和细胞生长因子等类药品。该类药品因生产技术上的特殊性使其与传统意义上的一般化学药品有着许多不同之处。 生物技术药物给药系统是指肽类、蛋白质和核酸等药物以一定的给药形式经一定的给药途径较为精确地应用于人体的技术系统，亦即药物从制剂制备到临床应用整个过程的研制体系。以蛋白肽类药物为主的生物技术药物在结构、功能和作用上与其他药物的不同决定了它们在临床使用中的复杂性和困难性，单一的注射剂远远不能满足日益增长的生物技术药物的临床应用需求。因此，研究开发生物技术药物给药技术与给药系统并制备成高质量的新制剂既是现代药剂学面临的重大而艰巨的任务，也是现代药剂学研究的一个热点领域。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The rapid advancement of biotechnology has facilitated the large-scale production of biotechnological drugs. Biotechnological drugs (also known as biological drugs or biologics) refer to bioactive substances used for prevention, treatment, and diagnosis. They are produced using biological systems, living organisms, or their derivatives, with cells and their constituent molecules as raw materials. These drugs include peptides, proteins, enzymes, nucleic acids, and cell growth factors, among other categories. Due to the uniqueness of their production technology, such drugs differ in many aspects from conventional chemical drugs in the traditional sense. The biotechnological drug delivery system is a technical system that enables drugs such as peptides, proteins, and nucleic acids to be administered to the human body with relative precision in a specific dosage form and via a specific route of administration. In other words, it is a research and development system covering the entire process from the preparation of pharmaceutical formulations to their clinical application. Biotechnological drugs, predominantly protein and peptide-based, differ from other drugs in terms of structure, function, and mechanism of action. This uniqueness determines the complexity and challenges associated with their clinical use. A single type of injectable formulation is far from meeting the growing clinical needs for biotechnological drugs. Therefore, researching and developing drug delivery technologies and systems for biotechnological drugs, as well as preparing high-quality new formulations, is not only a significant and arduous task faced by modern pharmaceutics but also a hot research area in the field of modern pharmaceutics.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

本课程的教学目的旨在围绕生物技术药物理化特性和生物学性质的特点，紧密结合《药剂学》和《生物药剂学和药物动力学》的理论知识，系统传授生物技术药物制剂给药系统的基本理论、处方设计、制备工艺、质量控制和合理使用等药剂学相关理论和应用技术。本课程是药剂学知识体系的补充和完善，帮助学生建立较为系统的药物制剂体内外研究基本理论和方法，并了解该领域的最新进展。

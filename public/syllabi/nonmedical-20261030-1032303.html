Party Politics in the Developing World

2026春 · POLI70024.01 · 曾庆捷

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

关注发展中国家福祉的学者，大多将研究重点置于实现经济繁荣与财富公平分配的方法之上。然而，若仅聚焦经济领域的学术路径，将很快遭遇瓶颈，并不得不承认政治在塑造公民生活中的关键作用。政治——被理解为人际共同体中的权力关系及其对稀缺资源配置的影响——不仅通过国家与公民的直接互动发挥作用，也通过其对经济发展的深远影响显现其重要性。 本课程将向学生介绍关于发展中国家政治生态的一些核心理论视角与实证研究成果。课程涵盖的主题包括：国家形成与国家建构、民主化与民主衰退、非竞争政体的多样形态、族群冲突、殖民主义的历史遗产，以及国际力量对国内政治的影响。

课程简介（英文）

Scholars concerned with the well-being of the developing world have devoted most of their attention to the methods of achieving economic prosperity and equitable distribution of wealth. An intellectual approach that focuses exclusively on the economic sphere, however, will soon reach its bottleneck and have to recognize the critical role of politics in shaping the lives of citizens. Politics, understood as the power relations in a human community and their impact on the allocation of scarce resources, matters both through the direct interaction between the state and citizens and through its effects on economic development. This course introduces the students to some of the most important theoretical approaches and empirical findings regarding the politics of the developing world. Topics covered in this class include state formation and state building, democratization and democratic backsliding, variety of authoritarianism, ethnic conflict, the legacy of colonialism, and the impact of international forces on domestic politics.

教学目标

The class will be conducted as a seminar in which the instructor and students discuss various questions raised and answered by the readings. The instructor will lecture for the first half of each class to introduce the basic concepts and research questions. The second half of the class will involve student presentations and deliberative exploration of the readings. Each student should select two weeks to present an assigned article. The presentation should last for no more than 15 minutes, summarize the article and offer some discussion questions. After the first meeting on February 19, please send me your three most preferred weeks (For example: first choice: week 4, second choice: week 10, third choice: week 3). The instructor will try to accommodate your preferences when finalizing the assignment. As a course requirement, each student must write a research paper (at least 4000 words) to explore a question related to this class. You are encouraged to discuss your paper topics with me as early as possible and required to send me a research proposal (about 1000 words) by Week 7. The research paper will be due on Week 12. In addition, each student will present the final research paper towards the end of the semester.

教学方式

Participation and Attendance: All students are expected to contribute to our discussion each class. Participation is a key component of your grade (20%)—I unfortunately will have to give students who never speak in class a zero for participation. You will get full credits for participation if and only if you participate at a substantial level and in a meaningful way every week. Note, therefore, if you never talk in class the highest score you can earn for the course overall is 80%! Please come talk to me if you are uncomfortable with the idea of speaking in class. Similarly, you cannot participate in discussion if you are not physically present in class—frequent absences will harm your participation grade. I will take attendance at the beginning of each class. Students who come to class late will also be counted as absent. Evaluation Scheme Class attendance and participation: 30% Research paper proposal: 10% Final presentation: 20% Research paper: 40%

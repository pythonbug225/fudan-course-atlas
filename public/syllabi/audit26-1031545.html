课程简介（中文）

本课程在法医病理学理论基础上，升格为理论联系实践、理论前沿创新的阶段。主要讲授法医病理实践中鉴定内容（复杂死因分析、死亡时间推断等）及研究热点的进展（数智化法医病理技术、谱学技术等），具体形式上将实践场景与案例讨论相结合，使学生主动参与，主动思考，了解法医病理学实践内容及主要研究前沿，并基本具备法医病理司法鉴定与研究技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course builds upon the theoretical foundation of forensic pathology and elevates it to a stage that integrates theory with practice and fosters theoretical innovation. It primarily covers the identification content in forensic pathology practice (such as complex cause-of-death analysis and time-of-death estimation) and advancements in research hotspots (including digital and intelligent forensic pathology techniques, spectroscopy technologies, etc.). In terms of specific formats, it combines practical scenarios with case discussions, encouraging students to actively participate, think critically, understand the practical aspects of forensic pathology and its leading research frontiers, and acquire fundamental skills in forensic pathological judicial identification and research.

教学目标

通过教学，使学生了解法医病理学工作内容、实践难点及其研究进展。

通过案例讨论及法医病理司法鉴定实践，使学生对法医病理学基本框架、基本知识更深入了解，掌握法医病理鉴定的技能、程序，能协助有关部门初步解决司法诉讼中的法医学问题，为加强有中国特色的社会主义法制建设服务。

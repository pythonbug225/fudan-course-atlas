国际商务管理

2025秋 · BA30018.03 · 吴冬媛

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将涵盖国际商务的背景和最新发展、国际商务环境（政治与法律环境、经济环境、文化环境等）的分析、国际商务的重要理论、国际市场的选择与进入模式、跨国经营的战略选择与组织管理、国际战略联盟与战略合作管理、跨国公司在中国的发展与本土企业的生存战略、中国企业的国际化等内容。

课程简介（英文）

This course will cover the background and latest developments of international business, the international business environment (political and legal environment, economic environment, cultural environment, etc.) analysis, important theories of international business, the choice and entry mode of international market, the strategic choice and organization management of transnational operation, international strategic alliance and strategic cooperation management, the development of multinational companies in China and the survival strategies of local companies, the internationalization of Chinese companies, etc.

教学目标

This course aims to provide students with an international perspective on business management in the digital age. Through lectures, case analyses, class discussions, and group projects and presentations, students will learn: The evolution and latest developments of economic globalization; Basic knowledge and principles in international business; General rules and principles in international business and strategies to deal with environmental challenges of international business; The motivations, basic methods, entry decisions for international operation, as well as strategy and organization of multinational companies; Establishment and management of international strategic alliances; Global human resource management. This course will help establish a solid knowledge foundation for students’ future practices in international business activities and transnational operations in the digital age. In addition, this course also incorporates analyses of the development history of multinational companies in China as well as the challenges faced by Chinese companies in their international development.

教学方式

In addition to the case analyses and individual assignment, this course also includes a group project that requires students working in groups to conduct research and interview with companies. In the end of the semester, each group is expected to submit a formal report and give a presentation in class.

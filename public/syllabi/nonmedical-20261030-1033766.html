课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在加深学生对中国数字政府发展的理解。具体而言，课程将围绕信息公开、数字公共服务、电子参与及数据治理等政策议题展开探讨。课程首先会介绍可用于研究数字政府的不同理论视角，这些视角源自公共管理、政治学和组织学领域的经典理论，并阐明其对中国数字政府研究场景的适用性。随后，课程将结合中国数字政府的政策实践案例进行分析。最后，课程将深入考察与数字政府相关的各类政策问题，涵盖政府网站、一站式政务服务平台、社交媒体和人工智能等新兴技术的多个领域。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to deepen a student’s understanding of digital government development in China. Specifically, we will explore the policy issues surrounding information disclosure, e-service provision, e-participation and data management. We will first give insight into different types of theoretical perspectives one can use to study digital government, taken from existing theories in public administration and management, and show their relevance for studying digital government in China. Then, there will be given examples from policy practices of digital government in China. Finally, we will seek to explore various relevant policy issues in government websites, online one-stop shops, social media platforms, emerging technologies (e.g. AI) and more.

教学目标

After completing this course, students should be familiar with key issues in digital government in China and be able to analyze policy in the field of digital government by using one or more of the theoretical perspectives from class.

教学方式

Class Attendance: Students are expected to be prepared and on time to attend all discussion sessions. If uncontrollable circumstances require your absence, please contact the TA or the instructor by email in advance of class. Unexcused absences may result in a reduction of 5 points in the overall grade. The maximum deduction is 10 points. Three absence leads to a grade of F. Academic Discipline: Students are expected to follow the University’s standards of academic integrity. Plagiarism, cheating or other violations are strictly forbidden. Evaluation and Grading: Coursework is weighted as follows in calculating the overall grade. Term paper: The final assignment is an essay/paper on a relevant issue of digital government in China by applying theories or perspectives learned in this class. The essays/papers do not have to be a finished research article, but must have as a minimum one or more research questions, theoretical perspectives and analytical parts and conclusions, written with Times New Roman 12 in a MS-WORD format. The essay/paper should be at least 3000 words, and no more than 4000 words (excluding references). The paper should have an academically acceptable format, and use the APA reference style. Final paper should be submitted to the TA at the end of the semester.

教学内容与进度安排

Topic / Theme Topic 1 Course Overview and Introduction to Digital Government Objectives: understand the course goals, schedule, requirements - as well as the basic knowledge about digital government. Due by class: be prepared to make a brief introduction to yourself and reasons you take this class; talk about your experiences of e-services. Topic 2 Effects of Digital Technology on Government and Governance Objectives: understand the potential effects of implementing digital technology on the quality of governance (e.g. transparency, efficiency, accountability). Topic 3 Facilitators and Barriers to Digital Technology Implementation in Government Objectives: understand organizational, environmental and individual factors that may affect the process of government digital transformation with a focus on China. Topic 4 Performance Evaluation of Digital Government Projects Objectives: introduce the basic framework for performance evaluation of digital government and give an overview of the policy & practice in China. Topic 5 Dynamics of Digital Government Strategies in China Objectives: explore what characterizes the evolution of government digital transformation over the past 30 years and discuss what could be some major forces driving this evolution. Topic 6 Governing Systems of Digital Government in China Objectives: explore the roles of government agencies (e.g. government data management agencies at the local level), technology enterprises, social organizations and individual citizens in the policy process of government digital transformation in China. Topic 7 Government Websites and Information Disclosure Objectives: introduce the practice of building government websites in China; explore the antecedents and consequences of building government websites. Topic 8 Online One-stop Shops and Public Service Delivery Objectives: introduce the practice of building online one-stop shops in China; explore how online one-stop shops may affect the quality of public service delivery. Topic 9 Government on the Social Media Objectives: obtain knowledge about the ways government agencies and officials use social media to change their management styles and promote citizen-state interaction. Topic 10 Class Presentation (Term Paper Proposal) Description: Student should make a presentation on the proposal or short plan of their term essay/paper for 15 minutes, and take questions from the “audience”. Topic11 Government Data Management: Data-Sharing and Open Government Data (OGD) Objectives: obtain knowledge about the Chinese government’s attempts to facilitate the quality of data-sharing and OGD; discuss the hidden obstacles to data-sharing and OGD. Topic 12 Data Management: Government Data Security and Information Privacy Objectives: understand emerging concerns about data security and information privacy in digital government implementation. Topic 13 E-participation and Digital Divide Objectives: introduce digital empowerment as a process for enhancing citizen-state interaction; understand emerging concerns about fairness and equity in digital technology implementation (e.g. digital divide). Topic 14 The Future of Digital Government Objectives: understand the current debate in the use of emerging digital technologies such as AI and algithorims in government and governance. Topic 15 Class Presentation Description: Student should make a presentation on the term essay/paper for 15 minutes, and improve the quality of manuscript based on comments and suggestions by audience.

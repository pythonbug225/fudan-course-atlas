顶点代数及其形变

课程简介（中文）

顶点代数 (Vertex Algebras, VAs)” 的概念由 Richard Borcherds 于 1986 年引入。其最初的动机纯粹是为了无限维李代数的一个代数构造，以及他随后对**“怪兽月光”猜想 (Monstrous Moonshine conjecture)** 的证明。 它们的一个子类——顶点算子代数 (Vertex Operator Algebras, VOAs)，后来被 Frenkel, Lepowsky 和 Meurman 确认为二维共形场论 (Conformal Field Theory, CFT) 的代数框架。 一个 VA 是一个复向量空间，拥有一个特定的真空向量和满足某些公理的顶点算子，这些公理可以用 CFT 的语言来理解。 在本课程中，我们将从学习顶点代数的基本理论及其表示开始。随后，我们将探讨几何构造的实例，以及由此产生的一些应用。 这种几何视角自然地引出了 VA 的推广，即它们的形变 (deformations) 形式。我们将学习两种不同类型的形变，它们的例子，以及它们在几何学中的作用。

课程简介（英文）

The concept of vertex algebras (VAs) was introduced by Richard Borcherds in 1986, motivated purely by an algebraic construction of infinite-dimensional Lie algebras and his subsequent proof of the Monstrous Moonshine conjecture. Their subclass of vertex operator algebras was later recognized by Frenkel, Lepowsky, and Meurman as the algebraic framework for two-dimensional conformal field theory (CFT). A VA is a complex vector space with a distinguished vacuum vector and vertex operators satisfying axioms that can be understood using CFT. In the course, we will begin by studying the basic theory of vertex algebras and their representations. We will then discuss examples of their constructions from geometry and some consequent applications. This geometric perspective naturally motivates generalizations of VAs in the form of their deformations. We will learn about two different types of deformations, their examples, and their roles in geometry.

教学目标

Learn the basic theory of vertex algebras (VAs)and their representations. Understand the meaning of conformal elementsleading to vertex operator algebras (VOAs). Study examplesof VOAs and their historical applications. Learn modern methods for constructing VOAs from geometry. Study applications of such geometric VOAs. Axiomatize two different types of refinements of VAs/VOAs. Learn how to constructthem using K-theory or equivariant cohomology. Study the immediate applications of these constructions.

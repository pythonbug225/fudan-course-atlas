课程简介（中文）

本课程专为希望在全球化商业环境中提升英语应用能力的学生设计。课程的核心目标不仅在于培养扎实的语言技能，更致力于建立在各类专业场合中自信、高效使用英语的专业素养。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for students aiming to enhance their command of the English language for the global business environment. The primary objective is to build both linguistic competence and the confidence to use English effectively and professionally.

教学目标

Course Objectives: This course is designed to sharpen students' analytical capabilities in contemporary economics and finance. A key objective is to foster a deeper interest and proficiency in engaging with English-language academic and media sources. Through a critical examination of pivotal global trends, students will be equipped to analyze complex issues at the intersection of finance, technology, and international business. Core Topics Include: Global Economic & Financial Dynamics: An analysis of market developments and macroeconomic trends in major world economies, with a special focus on China's role in the international business landscape. The Anatomy of Financial Crises: Investigating the causes and consequences of major financial crises and their cascading effects on global trade and corporate strategy. The FinTech & AI Revolution: Exploring the disruptive impact of Financial Technology (FinTech) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) on financial services, cross-border transactions, and business models. Strategy in International Business: Examining key challenges and strategies in international trade, foreign direct investment, and managing operations in a complex geopolitical environment.

教学方式

This course will utilize a variety of teaching methods, including lectures, group presentations, role-playing exercises, and active in-class discussions to encourage student participation and critical thinking.

教学内容与进度安排

Module Week Content 1 1-2 The Big Picture: Global Economic Growth and Structural Shifts 2 3-4 Global Financial Markets: Instruments, Institutions, and Capital Flows 3 5-6 Anatomy of Financial Crises: From 2008 to Modern Systemic Risks 4 7-8 Sovereign Credit and Local Government Debt Risk 5 9-10 The Future of Finance: AI, FinTech, and Digital Assets 6 11-12 International Trade and Geopolitical Risk 7 13-14 Digital Silk Road: Opportunities and Strategies in Cross-Border E-commerce 8 15-16 Review & Final Exam

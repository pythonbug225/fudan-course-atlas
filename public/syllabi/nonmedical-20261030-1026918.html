生物热力学

2026春 · BIOL50023.01 · 吴荻

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

教授热力学原理及其在生物学中的应用。重点介绍热力学第一定律、热力学第二定律，以及热力学的相关概念。同时介绍一些动力学相关知识。在介绍理论的同时还将介绍如何应用理论解决生物学中的相关问题，包括介绍热力学和动力学知识在药物研发领域的应用。

课程简介（英文）

Course lectures include introductions of the basic thermodynamic concepts and their applications in biology. We focus on introducing the First Law and the Second Law of Thermodynamics, and the related principles including chemical equilibria, phase equilibria, binding equilibria, etc. In addition, the basic concepts of kinetics will be introduced along with their applications in biology. Towards the end of the course, there will be a brief discussion of the computational drug design project.

教学目标

Thermodynamic principles are widely applied in biological research work, especially in studying the structures and functions of biomolecules. Although these are physical theories, they are fundamental to understand the macromolecule properties and hence are important to the students studying life sciences. The aim of this course is to help students understand the basic thermodynamic and kinetics principles and apply them in biological research work, e.g., the drug design project. A special feature of this course is that the theories will be introduced along with their biological applications, so that the students know where to use them and how to apply these principles.

教学方式

All lectures will be given offline, taught in English. Students need participate in the Discussion Section in two of these lectures, 45 minutes each.

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture 1: Introduction to Biological Thermodynamics Descriptions of the lectures, and overview of the basic knowledge of biological thermodynamics and kinetics. Lecture 2: Introduction of the Laws of Thermodynamics Using Biological Examples Specific biological examples are selected and will be studied in detail, where the thermodynamic principles are used to explain theses processes. The students should know how to use these theories to study such processes. Lecture 3: Heat, Work, Energy, and Biofuels Fundamental thermodynamic concepts will be introduced, along with their applications in biological problems. Biofuels will be introduced as a special topic, so that the students know their practical usage at the current time. Lecture 4: Enthalpy, Heat Capacity, and Their applications in Biology Enthalpy and heat capacity are fundamental concepts to study biological reactions, and will be introduced in this lecture. The students should know how to calculate these quantities and how to measure them using the experimental methods. Lecture 5: Entropy, and Its Applications in Biology Entropy and the related thermodynamic principles will be introduced. Entropy has been used in various biological research projects. In this lecture, some of these applications will be studied. The students should have some idea of how to apply the entropy principles in the relevant biological problems. Lecture 6: The Second Law of Thermodynamics, and Its Applications in Biology The Second Law of Thermodynamics is frequently encountered in biological research work, and will be discussed in detail in this lecture. The students should understand the theories and know how to use them to study specific problems. Lecture 7: Free Energy, and Its Applications in Biology Free-energy principles are useful to study biological processes and biomolecule properties. The students should understand these theories and know how to use them to study biological problems. Lecture 8: Phase Equilibria Phase equilibria describe the physical properties of biosystems. The students should understand the processes of phase separation and phase equilibria, and understand the fundamental theories describing these processes. Lecture 9: Holiday Break Lecture 10: Stability of Protein Structures Utilizing thermodynamic theories, we will discuss the protein structural stability and study the protein folding problem. In the second half of this lecture, we will discuss this topic: Discussion (45 minutes): Protein folding and structural stability explained by the thermodynamic principles. Lecture 11: Fudan University Sports Day, Class Canceled Lecture 12: Introduction to Kinetics Fundamental concepts of kinetics will be introduced, along with their applications in biological problems. The students should understand these concepts, and know how to use them. Lecture 13: Ligand Binding More kinetics theories related to the binding process will be introduced. And specific theories of ligand binding will be studied in detail, along with biological applications. The students should understand these theories and be able to choose different theories to study different biological problems. Lecture 14: Thermodynamics and Kinetics of Drug Binding and a Brief Introduction to Computational Drug Design Drug design procedures and the related theories will be introduced in this lecture. We know that drug design can be studied by the thermodynamic and kinetics theories. In this lecture, we enable the students to discuss drug design using these theories, especially using the ones that they have learned in the previous lectures. In the second half of this lecture, we will discuss this topic: Discussion (45 minutes): The drug design methods using the thermodynamic and kinetics theories. Lecture 15: Presentations by the Students Lecture 16: Holiday Break

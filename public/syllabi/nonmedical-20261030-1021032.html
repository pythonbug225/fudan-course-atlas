课程简介（中文）

在国际权威期刊发表论文的数量与质量，是衡量研究人员、科研机构乃至整个国家学术水平与成就的重要标尺。尽管本科生已学习英语多年，但在科技英语论文写作方面仍存在诸多问题。本课程系统讲解科技论文的结构构建、选题策略、摘要精炼、背景阐述、研究结果写作规范及结论提炼等关键环节。通过本课程的学习，本科生将掌握撰写优质科技论文的方法要领。

课程简介（英文）

The quality and quantity of papers published in international authoritative journals is a measure of the academic level and achievements of researchers, research institutions, and even the whole country. Although undergraduate students have been studying English for many years, there are still many problems in the writing of scientific and technological English papers. This course introduces the construction of the structure of the scientific and technological papers, the selection of the topics of the papers, the refinement of the abstracts, the introduction of the background, the writing standard of the research results, and the extraction of the conclusions. With the help of this course, the undergraduate students should have a better idea on how to write a good scientific paper.

教学目标

The purpose of this course is to develop undergraduate students' practical ability to use English for academic writing, to guide undergraduate students to master the elements of using English to write abstracts, introductions, materials and methods, discussions, conclusions, etc. We also go through the techniques and skills required to give a proper (good) oral presentation. Finally we discuss things like CV and formal letter writing. All skills are needed in later life.

教学方式

Lectures

教学内容与进度安排

Weeks 1,2 Introduction to the course, Key points in Science and Technology writing, introduction to writing ideas and processes Week 3 Structure and outline of scientific and technological papers, setting and specification of title of papers Week 4 Introduction and abstract writing of scientific papers Week 5 How to write the background introduction of scientific papers and the conclusion Week 6 Preparation of papers, research results, and literature Week 7 How to correctly expound and express one's research achievements in scientific and technological papers Week 8 The standardization and beauty of drawing in the paper and the standardization of tables Week 9 Midterm examination Week 10 How to quote references correctly Week 11 Summary of common mistakes and problems in the writing of scientific papers Week 12 Common English grammatical errors and misnomer in scientific papers Week 13 Journal writing standards and format of the American Society of Physics Week 14 Other issues needing attention in the process of writing scientific papers Week 15 After the paper is finished, how to polish further Week 16 Go through the techniques and skills required to give a proper (good) oral presentation. Week 17 Finally we discuss things like CV and formal letter writing. Week 18 Final Exam

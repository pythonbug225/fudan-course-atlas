课程简介（中文）

本课程为中国语言与文化课程（下），专为来自不同国家的母语非中文的学习者设计。经过一学期的学习，学习者目前的语言水平为HSK2级左右。课程定位为综合课程，以实用汉语为核心，整合听、说、读、写各项技能，融合语言学习与文化学习，围绕日常生活、学习场景展开教学。通过丰富多样的教学活动，激发学生学习兴趣，为进一步学习中文或在华学习生活奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

Introduction to Chinese language and culture （II）, specifically designed for non-native Chinese learners from various countries. After a semester of study, learners currently have a language proficiency level of around HSK Level 2. Positioned as a comprehensive course, it centers on practical Chinese, integrating listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, as well as blending language learning with cultural learning. The teaching is conducted around daily life and study scenarios. Through a variety of engaging teaching activities, it aims to stimulate students' interest in learning and lay a foundation for their further Chinese language studies or their study and life in China.

教学目标

This course aims to enable language leraners who are at around HSK Level 2, be capable of recognizing Pinyin to master basic Chinese vocabulary and grammar, possess the ability to engage in simple daily communication, understand daily conversations, read simple short passages, and write simple sentences. Through the comparison between Chinese and Western cultures, it helps students grasp the characteristics of Chinese culture, enhances their cultural sensitivity and cross-cultural communication awareness, cultivates their autonomous learning and cooperative learning abilities, and provides linguistic and cultural support for their subsequent Chinese language learning and study and life in China.

教学方式

A variety of teaching methods are employed. The classroom teaching primarily focuses on interactive approaches. Through activities like scenario simulations and role-plays, students can enhance their listening and speaking skills in practical situations. Multimedia resources, such as images and videos, are utilized to assist teaching and enhance its visual appeal. Group discussions are organized to encourage students to share their viewpoints, fostering their cooperative and expressive abilities. Meanwhile, appropriate reading and writing tasks are assigned. Combined with cultural comparison explanations, these tasks guide students to conduct independent exploration, achieving the synchronous improvement of language skills and cultural literacy.

考核构成

  • 出勤Attendance 5.0% If students need to take leave, they are required to send an email to the instructor one week in advance to explain the situation; otherwise, it will be recorded as an absence. Students with more than one-third of classes absent will not be permitted to take the final exam. If the accumulated number of leave hours reaches 20 class hours, the attendance score will be recorded as zero.
  • 课堂表现Participation 10.0% Students are encouraged to show their enthusiasm and commitment through active participation and good behavior in class.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 15.0% Dictations.
  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 25.0% The oral exam will be conducted on a one - on - one basis and is divided into two parts. In the first part, students will be required to answer questions. The second part involves delivering a speech on a given topic for no less than 3 minutes. The topic range will be provided 1 - 2 weeks in advance. As for the mid - term exam, it is tentatively scheduled for either Week 8 or Week 9.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 45.0% The exam will be conducted in a closed-book format. The questions will refer to the exercises in the textbook, and there will be some identical questions appearing. Additionally, students are required to write a composition of about 100 words in Chinese.

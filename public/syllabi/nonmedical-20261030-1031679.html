课程简介（中文）

《新媒体研究》为传播学专业硕士研究生课程，目的在于通过课堂面授以及互动研讨，引导学生系统总结新媒体研究的主要特点，深入理解新媒体传播的重要规律，准确把握新媒体发展趋势，进而增强对新媒体传播现象进行理论分析及实证研究的能力。为此，要求学生将新媒体研究与相关专业课程的内容（如传播学基本理论与方法、人际传播理论、传媒与社会，以及社会学、政治学、语言文学、心理学等学科的相关课程）连接起来，做到融会贯通、整合运用；把现实问题与理论研究结合起来，做到活学活用。本课程还将着力于培养学生自主研究的能力，促进同学间的相互学习、共同提高。

课程简介（英文）

New Media Research is a core course for master’s students majoring in Communication Studies. Through in-person lectures and interactive seminars, the course aims to guide students to systematically examine the key theories of new media research, gain an in-depth understanding of the fundamental dynamics of new media communication, and accurately grasp trends in new media development. In doing so, it seeks to strengthen students’ ability to conduct both theoretical analysis and empirical research on new media communication phenomena. To achieve these goals, students are expected to connect new media research with content from related disciplinary courses—such as foundational communication theories and methods, interpersonal communication theory, media and society, as well as relevant courses in sociology, political science, linguistics and literature, and psychology—so as to integrate and apply knowledge holistically. Students are also encouraged to link real-world issues with theoretical inquiry, applying theory flexibly to practice. In addition, the course places strong emphasis on cultivating students’ capacity for independent research and promoting peer learning and collective academic growth.

教学目标

通过学习，学生将： 了解新媒体研究所涉及的研究范式、主要理论及研究方法 掌握用所学理论及方法研究各类与新媒体相关的传播问题 引导学生进行以理论创新和实践指导为目标的新媒体研究，培养并提升学生的学术研究兴趣和能力

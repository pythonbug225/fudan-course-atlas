先秦儒家与法家

2025秋 · GEEC10352.01 · 白彤东

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将涵盖"古典"（先秦）儒家与法家思想。这是理解中国哲学的一个良好起点，因为先秦哲学是中国哲学的根源所在——中国哲学家在此阶段直接回应人类社会的根本问题，而非借助前代哲人的术语体系进行间接探讨。因此，理解先秦哲学既能带来直接的思想碰撞，又能为把握后续发展奠定基础。我们将着力探究：先秦儒家与法家哲学家试图解决何种问题？他们提出了哪些解答？这些解答在单一文本内部是否自洽？不同流派的主张如何相互关联？通过研读，我们有望发现这些哲学典籍确实以哲学本体形态探讨了超越民族、时代与地域的永恒命题。进而通过对比这些思想家与其他哲学传统（包括中国本土及域外思想）的解决方案，我们既能洞察其理论得失，这种理解又将反过来帮助我们更深入地把握那些至今仍与我们息息相关的哲学命题。

课程简介（英文）

This course will cover “classical” (pre-Qin) Confucianism and Legalism. It is a good beginning to understand Chinese philosophy because pre-Qin Chinese philosophy is the root of Chinese philosophy, in which Chinese philosophers address the human problems directly, instead of addressing them indirectly through jargons of previous philosophers’. Thus, to understand the pre-Qin Chinese philosophy offers us immediacy and also a foundation to understand later developments. We will try to understand what problems the pre-Qin Confucian and Legalist philosophers try to answer, what their answers are, whether the answers are coherent within one text and how these answers are related to each other, etc. Hopefully, we will discover that these philosophical texts, as philosophical texts, do address philosophical problems, that is, problems that transcend a particular people, a particular time, and a particular locale. Then, by comparing these philosophers’ answers to those by other philosophers (Chinese or non-Chinese), we can see the merits and problems of these answers, and this understanding will in turn help us to understand better the related philosophical problems, the problems that are still relevant to us today.

教学目标

Training of how to use comparative methods, to understand the original texts, and to deal with some specific issues about Pre-Qin Confucianism and Legalism.

教学方式

Lectures and discussions

教学内容与进度安排

Meetings 1-2: Introduction. Meetings 3-10: Selections of the “Four Books” (The Analects, The Mencius, Great Learning, The Doctrine of the Mean). Meetings 11-14: The Han Fei Tzu (read Sections 5-8, 49, and 50 in H; and SUP #1-8).

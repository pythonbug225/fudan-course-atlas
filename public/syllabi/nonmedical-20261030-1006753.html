课程简介（英文）

Chinese Society and Social Policy Instructor: SHEN Ke, [联系方式已隐去] Credits: 3 credits Course Description China has been undergoing exceedingly rapid transformations in the past half a century: a demographic transition with dramatic decrease in fertility and mortality, a social transformation from a traditional society to a modernized society, and an economic transition from a planned economy to a market economy. The compressed demographic transition has made China a country with ultra-low population growth and accelerated population aging, and the unprecedented socioeconomic reforms have lifted China to the ranks of middle-income countries with rising social inequality. These historical transformations are not independent of each, but have been closely intertwined with each other and deeply shaped by China’s social policies. This course will explore the causes, processes and consequences of sociodemographic and economic transformations with a particular focus on social policies. Requirements and Evaluation Course evaluation will be based on class participation, presentation, and a term-paper. Students are expected to read the assigned materials before class. Since the third week, two students are expected to present the assignments marked with * each week. For the term-paper, students need to select a topic related to this course in the context of their home countries. Students will have an opportunity to present a progress report of their term-paper, and a final paper is due at the end of the semester. The paper should have a length of at least 3,000 English words. This term-paper is expected to follow the style of an academic research paper, consisting of introduction, literature review, findings and conclusion. The course grading is comprised of: Class attendance and presentation (30%), presentation of final paper (30%), Term paper (40%).

教学目标

This course aims to enable students to master key demographic concepts and theories, as well as fundamental demographic methods; understand the history, influencing factors, and future trends of China's sociodemograpic transition, as well as the background, objectives, and impacts of relevant population policies; recognize the close connections between China's demographic transition and socio-economic issues such as marriage markets, urbanization, and economic growth, while grasping key focuses in domestic and international research on population issues; explore students' interests in the field of population and development in China, and conduct preliminary research by applying knowledge gained in the course.

教学方式

Lectures, Group discussion and presentation

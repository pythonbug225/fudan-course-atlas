课程简介（中文）

本课程专为汉语零基础学习者量身打造，系统涵盖初级汉语核心知识：从拼音的准确发音、声调的细微变化，到日常高频词汇的含义与用法，到基础句型结构、虚词搭配等实用语法。学习过程中，还会融入与在华生活紧密相关的文化内容，比如日常问候礼仪、购物交流技巧、公共场合行为规范等，快速掌握生存必备的语言技能与文化常识，为融入中文环境打下扎实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course is for the beginners in Chinese language study. Students are going to learn phonetic, writing, vocabulary and grammars of elementary Chinese. In the meantime, students will also get some knowledge of Chinese cultures related to the survival skills in China.

教学目标

This course is designed for beginners. Through one semester of systematic study, three key objectives will be achieved: 1.Students will systematically master the Chinese phonetic system, including accurate pronunciation of pinyin and rules of tone changes. They will proficiently recognize and write nearly 200 basic Chinese characters, understanding their structural logic, and grasp the meanings, collocations, and usage of 300 high-frequency vocabularies, laying a solid foundation for further Chinese learning. 2.Building on vocabulary learning, students will simultaneously understand basic sentence patterns and grammatical rules of elementary Chinese. They will be able to use simple sentence structures to express meanings, achieving a transition from vocabulary accumulation to sentence construction. 3.Through simulated scenario training, students will learn to communicate in Chinese in daily situations such as asking for directions, shopping, and ordering food. They will acquire basic language skills for living in China, realizing the transformation from "learning" to "applying."

教学方式

This course primarily adopts a combination of classroom lectures and dialogue simulations to systematically introduce Chinese phonetics, vocabulary, grammar, and conversational applications, etc. Through classroom lectures, students will receive a systematic introduction to knowledge such as Chinese phonetics, vocabulary, and grammar, with explanations integrated with the content of the course textbooks. Through dialogue practices, students will learn to communicate in Chinese in daily scenarios, helping them master common conversations used in real-life situations.

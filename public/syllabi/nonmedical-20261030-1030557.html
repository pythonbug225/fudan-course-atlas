叙事理论与短篇小说阐释

2026春 · FORE50168.01 · 段枫

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

帮助学生了解叙事作品的基本形式特色和基本分析方法，对经典和后经典叙事理论的主要研究方法和 工具进行有侧重点的全面介绍，并辅以相关具针对性的短篇小说文本作为案例解读，从理论研究工具和文本细读两个方面帮助学生掌握叙事作品阅读、分析的基本路径，了解文学研究的内部和外部研究途径。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to help students grasp the fundamental formal characteristics and basic analytical methods of narrative works. It offers a focused and comprehensive introduction to the major research methods and tools of classical and post-classical narratology, supplemented by targeted interpretations of short story texts as case studies. Through the dual perspectives of theoretical research tools and close textual reading, students will master the basic approaches to reading and analyzing narrative works, and gain an understanding of the intrinsic and extrinsic approaches to literary research.

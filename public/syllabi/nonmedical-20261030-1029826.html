课程简介（英文）

Representative writers of German Romanticism Representative works of German Romanticism Representative composers of German Romanticism and their works Representative painters of German Romanticism and their works Reflections on and critiques of German Romanticism A comparative study of German Romanticism, Romanticism in other European countries, and the Chinese “Generation of Romanticism” writers

教学目标

帮助学生了解对于德语及世界文化来说都至关重要的浪漫主义，逐一探讨浪漫主义的重要代表人物，从文学和思想角度究其根源，考察它对德国近现代历史及思想的影响，梳理对浪漫主义的研究与批判。在课上设置讨论环节，并辅以文本的阅读和片段翻译。

教学方式

课堂讲解，学生报告，讨论

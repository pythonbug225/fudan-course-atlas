课程简介（中文）

工程心理学关注的是如何基于人的信息加工能力和限度，设计和制造设备和产品，以提高工作、学习效率，创造安全、舒适环境，防止事故发生。本课程系统介绍工程心理学的学科特点和研究方法，在深入讲解人的信息加工过程（感知觉、注意、记忆、决策与反应）的基础上，全面展示人机系统、人机交互、人工智能交互、神经人因学等工程心理学的核心内容及前沿发展。课程旨在帮助学生深入理解工程心理学，课程旨在帮助学生深入了解工程心理学，理解人类局限性背后的信息加工理论原理，培养学生运用心理学理论解决人机系统设计和制造等工程实践问题的能力。

课程简介（英文）

Engineering psychology focuses on how to design and manufacture equipment and products based on human information-processing capacities and limitations, in order to improve efficiency in work and learning, create safe and comfortable environments, and prevent accidents. This course provides a systematic introduction to the disciplinary characteristics and research methods of engineering psychology. Building on an in-depth discussion of human information-processing mechanisms (perception, attention, memory, decision-making, and response), it offers a comprehensive overview of core topics and cutting-edge developments in engineering psychology, including human–machine systems, human–computer interaction, human–AI interaction, and neuroergonomics. The course is designed to help students gain a deep understanding of engineering psychology, to grasp the information-processing theories underlying human limitations, and to develop the ability to apply psychological theories to solve engineering practice problems in the design and development of human–machine systems.

教学目标

本课程旨在使学生掌握工程心理学的基本框架和研究方法，深入理解人类信息加工过程（感知觉、注意、记忆、决策与反应）的特点与局限性。通过系统学习人机系统、人机交互、人工智能交互等核心内容，培养学生运用工程心理学理论分析和解决实际问题的能力。

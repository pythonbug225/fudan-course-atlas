课程简介（中文）

考虑两个平面域之间的全纯映射-这种映射具有非常刚性的性质，这是由于这种映射是保角的。在本课程中，我们也将学习全纯映射-但不是平面域，我们将考虑它们的泛化：即曲面。在这个过程中，我们将看到如何使用分析、代数和拓扑工具的混合来研究这种映射的独特性质。

课程简介（英文）

Consider a holomorphic map between two planar domains – such maps have very rigid properties, which arise due to such maps being conformal. In this course we will also study holomorphic maps – but instead of planar domains, we will consider their generalization: i.e., surfaces. In the process, we will see how one can use a mixture of analytic, algebraic, and topological tools to study the unique properties of such maps.

教学目标

Basic acquaintance with Riemann Surfaces, and related topics from Complex Analysis. The grade will be based on several exercises given along the course, plus a final project that you will need to present in front of the class. Some theorems which will not be proven during the semester could serve as a final project. 黎曼曲面的基本知识，以及复数分析的相关主题。 分数将基于课程中给出的一些练习，以及你需要在全班面前提交的期末项目。一些本学期不会被证明的定理可以作为期末作业。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提供英语学术写作方面的指导，内容包括：学术英语的特点、研究论文的结构、文本连贯性与衔接策略，以及如何撰写摘要、引言、文献综述、方法部分、讨论与结论。每个主题或知识点均配有来自已发表论文的示例，并辅以专项练习，帮助学生培养特定技能。在教师的指导下，学生将接受英语研究论文写作的实践训练，并期望能够掌握如何撰写和修改研究论文，以及运用课程中学到的语言技巧。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides assistance in English academic writing, its contents including: characteristics of academic English, organization of a research paper, strategies for textual cohesion and coherence, techniques for writing the abstract, introduction, literature review, methods section, discussion and conclusion. Each topic or knowledge point is accompanied with illustrative examples that come from published papers, and supplemented with exercises that help cultivate a particular skill. The students will receive hands-on training in English research paper writing, under the teacher’s instructions, and are expected to know how to write and revise a research paper, applying all the linguistic tricks learned over this course.

教学目标

This course aims to improve the students’ knowledge and skill in academic English writing, and prepare them for tasks such as research paper publishing and international academic conferences. By the end of this course, the students, hopefully, will have developed a sharpened awareness of the characteristics of academic English and the organization of a research paper, acquired the ability to deploy specific linguistic strategies in research paper writing, and become cognizant of what to do in every phase of paper writing and publishing. This course also attempts to instill in the students a sense of academic integrity which underpins every plagiarism-avoiding technique.

教学方式

Genre pedagogy with multiple guided genre analysis tasks

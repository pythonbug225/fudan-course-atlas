课程简介（中文）

本课程通过讨论形式，探讨会计学前沿的理论与实证文献，旨在系统构建学生的知识体系，并培养其批判性与创造性思维。课程鼓励学生基于个人兴趣，自主识别并发展研究课题。鉴于课程关注新兴学术动态，教学内容将定期更新。本学期拟涵盖的主题包括：创新、网络、ESG、人工智能、大数据以及供应链。

课程简介（英文）

This course examines frontier research in Accounting through seminar-style discussions of cutting-edge theoretical and empirical literature. It aims to systematically build students’ knowledge while fostering critical and creative thinking. Participants are encouraged to independently identify and develop research ideas based on their interests. Given its focus on emerging scholarship, course content is updated regularly. Topics proposed to be covered this semester include innovation, network, ESG, AI, big data, and supply chain.

教学目标

Value Guidance: Cultivate a rigorous academic ethic and a sense of social responsibility by critically examining the ethical implications and societal impacts of frontier accounting research, particularly in areas such as ESG and AI. Knowledge Transmission: Systematically master the core theories, seminal findings, and evolving debates within key frontier areas of accounting, including innovation, networks, ESG, AI, and supply chain. Ability Development: Develop advanced capabilities to independently evaluate research methodologies, synthesize academic literature, and formulate original, feasible research proposals that address contemporary issues in the field.

教学方式

This course employs a dynamic, participatory seminar format to achieve its learning objectives. The instruction is organized around three core interactive methods: Faculty-Guided Lectures & Discussions: The instructor will introduce key theoretical frameworks and methodological approaches for each frontier topic, using seminal and contemporary papers as springboards. Student-Led Presentations & Peer Review: Students will lead in-depth analyses of assigned literature. This role requires synthesizing research, evaluating methodological choices, and presenting critiques. A structured peer-feedback session following each presentation fosters collaborative learning and sharpens analytical judgment. Research Proposal Workshops: The course culminates in the development of an original research proposal. Progress will be scaffolded through iterative workshops where students present their evolving ideas—including research questions, literature review, and proposed design—for collective brainstorming and constructive critique from both peers and the instructor, simulating a professional research environment. This integrated approach ensures a continuous cycle of knowledge input, critical application, and creative synthesis, directly supporting the course's goals in value shaping, knowledge acquisition, and research skill development.

教学参考资料

The primary learning materials are the most recent and influential scholarly articles published in top-tier English-language academic journals across key accounting research frontiers. This approach ensures that course content is at the cutting edge of the discipline, reflecting ongoing debates, novel methodologies, and emerging findings. Students will engage directly with the primary sources of knowledge creation, which is essential for developing critical reading skills, evaluating research design, and identifying gaps for their own scholarly work. Access to these articles will be provided through the university's library databases.

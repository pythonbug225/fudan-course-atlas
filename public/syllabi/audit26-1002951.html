课程简介（中文）

This course is designed to improve medical students better understanding the principle and strategy for Obstetric and Gynecologic Diagnosis and Treatment.

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to improve medical students better understanding the principle and strategy for Obstetric and Gynecologic Diagnosis and Treatment.

教学目标

The course targets the enhancement of the following skills:

1)Understanding the fundamentals of Obstetrics and Gynecology;

2)Relating this to the requirements of professional education for clinical competence;

3)Developing practical, generic skills in teaching and assessment, underpinned by an understanding of educational theory;

4)Promoting reflective practice in education and clinical practice;

5)Developing professional skills and attitudes;

The ultimate goal is to mastering the principle and strategy for Obstetric and Gynecologic Diagnosis and Treatment.

教学方式

Theory teaching & clinical teaching

The design of class discussion or exercise, practice, experience and so on:

Theory teaching

1. Female Reproductive Physiology

1. To master the structure and function of the organs of the female reproductive system.

2. To master the steps of oogenesis.

3. Differentiate among the phases of the ovarian cycle.

4 master the process of the uterine (menstrual) cycle.

5. aster the functions of progesterone and estrogen.

6. master the e hormonal regulation of the female reproductive cycle (H-P-O axis).

 

2. Pregnancy Physiology

1. To master the development of embryo and fetal.

2. To master the function of attachment of the fetal.

3. To master maternal changes during pregnancy.

 

3. Pregnancy & Prenatal Diagnosis

3.1 Pregnancy Diagnosis

1. To master common symptom, sign and examination of early pregnancy.

2. To be familiar with common symptom, sign and examination of middle and late pregnancy, and also the definition of fetal lie, fetal presentation and fetal position.

3. To Know differential diagnosis of pregnancy.

3.2 Prenatal Diagnosis

1. To Master common techniques of prenatal diagnosis and Down syndrome screening strategy.

2. To be familiar with the definition and goal of prenatal diagnosis, and neural defects screening strategy.

3. To Know genetic disorders diagnosis strategies and preimplantation diagnosis.

 

4. Normal Delivery

1. To Master the clinical course and management of delivery.

2. To Master the mechanism of labor in occiput presentation.

3. To be familiar with the 4 factors deciding labor and delivery.

 

5. Ectopic Pregnancy and Abortion

5.1 Ectopic Pregnancy

1. To master the definition of ectopic pregnancy.

2. To master the diagnostic process of tubal pregnancy.

3. To master the principles of treatment for tubal pregnancy.

4. To understand the risk factors causing ectopic pregnancy.

5. Some basic concepts. What do STD, ART, hCG, TVS and A-S reaction stand for

5.2 Abortion

1. To master the definition and types of abortion.

2. To master the differential diagnosis of abortion.

 

6. Abnormal Delivery

1. To Master the presentation and diagnosis of abnormalities of expulsive force, birth canal and fetal position.

2. To Master the principle of management for dystocia.

3. To Master the complications of dystocia.

 

7. Preterm Labor and Premature Rupture of Fetal Membranes

1. To Master the definition and causes of preterm labor.

2. To Master the diagnosis and to learn about the treatment of preterm labor.

3. To Master the definition and causes of premature rupture of membranes (PROM).

4. To Master the diagnosis and to learn about the treatment of PROM.

 

8. Multiple Pregnancy and Disorders of Amnionic Fluid Volume

8.1. Multiple Pregnancy

1. To be familiar with what does the multiple pregnancy mean to mother and fetus, risk and complications.

2. To Master the methods of determination between monozygotic and dizygotic.

3. To Master the mechanism and diagnosis of TTTS.

8.2 Disorders of Amnionic Fluid Volume

1. To Master the role of amnionic fluid and its circulation.

2. To be familiar with the methods to evaluate volume of amnionic fluid.

3. To be familiar with what is polyhydramnios or oligohydramnios, the associated conditions, diagnosis, and complications respectively.

 

9. Bleeding in Late Pregnancy （placenta previa & placenta abruption）

1. To master the definition of placenta previa & placenta abruption

2. To master the classification of placenta previa & placenta abruption

3. To master the clinical management of placenta previa & placenta abruption

 

10. Gestational Diabetes Mellitus

1. To master screening practices and diagnostic criteria of GDM and PGDM.

2. To master the maternal and fetal complications of GDM.

3. To master diagnosis and treatment of GDM.

 

11. Postpartum Hemorrhage and Amniotic Fluid Embolism

11.1 Postpartum Hemorrhage

1. To be familiar with the causes of postpartum hemorrhage.

2. To Master the procedure to evaluate and treat the postpartum hemorrhage .

3. To be familiar with the prevention of postpartum hemorrhage.

11.2 Amniotic Fluid Embolism

1. To know the risk factors of amniotic fluid embolism

2. To be familiar with the symptoms of amniotic fluid embolism

3. To master the treatment of amniotic fluid embolism

 

12. Hypertension in Pregnancy & Intrahepatic Cholestasis Pregnancy

12.1 Hypertension in Pregnancy

1. To master hypertension of pregnancy of classification and definitions.

2. To master hypertension of pregnancy of pathology.

3. To master evaluation and management of preeclampsia.

12.2 Intrahepatic Cholestasis Pregnancy

1. To master the clinical manifestation of ICP.

2. To master the diagnosis and differential diagnosis of ICP.

3. To master the management of ICP.

 

13. Anatomic Disorders of the Female Reproductive System

1. To know the development of the female reproductive system.

2. To be familiar with the manifestation, diagnosis and treatment of common anatomic disorders of the female reproductive system.

 

14. Gynecologic Infection

1. To master the definition of cervicitis, vaginitis, vulvitis, and PID

2. To master the clinical signs, diagnosis and principle of treatment.

3. To understand the complications of PID.

4. To master differential diagnosis between female inflammation and tumor.

 

15. Abnornormal uterine bleeding with ovulatory dysfunction

1. To be familiar with the definition and PALM-COEIN classification system of abnormal uterine bleeding (AUB)

2. To understand the etiology and pathophysiological mechanism of AUB with ovulatory dysfunction (AUB-O).

3. To master different pathological changes in endometrium and their clinical significance.

4. To be familiar with the clinical manifestations of AUB-O.

5. To master diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment strategy of AUB-O.

6. To be familiar with medication regimen for AUB-O.

 

16. Amenorrhea

1. To know H-P-O axis and the establishment of menstruation

2. To master the primary cause of amenorrhea

3. To master diagnosis of amenorrhea

4. To be familiar with the treatment principles of amenorrhea

 

17. Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome

1. To know the causes of PCOS.

2. To master the definition and diagnosis of PCOS.

3. To master the treatment methods of PCOS.

 

18. Cervical Cancer

1. To be familiar with the etiology and tumor genesis of cervical cancer.

2. To be familiar with the prognosis, prevention and follow up of cervical cancer.

3.To master the clinical stage and spread of cervical cancer.

4. To master the diagnosis and differential diagnosis of cervical cancer.

5. To master examination and treatment of cervical cancer.

 

19. Uterine leiomyoma and sarcoma

1. To master the four type of degenerations，classification and clinical features of uterine Leiomyoma.

2. To master the effect of myoma on pregnancy.

3. To master the principle and indications of medication treatment and surgery.

4. To be familiar with the causes and diagnosis of uterine leiomyoma.

5. To be familiar with the surgery procedures for myoma.

6. To be familiar with classification of uterine sarcoma and clinical features, diagnosis and prognosis of three common types of uterine sarcoma.

 

20. Gestational Trophoblastic Disease (GTD)

1. To master the definition of GTD and gestational trophoblastic neoplasia (GTN) and to know the differences between the two.

2. To master the symptoms, diagnosis, treatment and follow-up protocol for hydatidiform mole, invasive mole and choriocarcinoma.

3. To know the pathological features of hydatidiform mole, invasive mole and choriocarcinoma.

4. To know the clinical significance of human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) in GTD.

5. To know the indications of methotrexate (MTX)-based chemotherapy for patients with choriocarcinoma.

 

21. Endometrial Cancer

1. To understand the screening practices and diagnostic criteria of cancer of the endometrium.

2. To understand the the histological classification of endometrial cancer.

3. To master the clinical signs, diagnosis and principle of treatment.

4. To master the differential diagnosis between benign and malignant ovarian tumor.

 

22. Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia and Vulvar Cancer

22.1 Vulvar Cancer

1. To master the principle of treatment of vulvar cancer.

2. To know how to prevent the occurrence of vulvar caner.

3. To know how to make a diagnosis of vulvar cancer promptly and correctly.

22.2 Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia

1. To be familiar with the definition and risk factors of CIN.

2. To master the diagnostic procedure for CIN and early stage of cervical cancer.

3. To master the classification of CIN and SIL, familiar with the basic rule of treatment of CIN.

4. To know the exact meanings of the following abbreviations: LSIL, HSIL, AIS, CIS and CIN

 

23. Endometriosis and adenomyosis

At the end of the 2-class hour course, participants should have a good understanding of:

1. The definition, signs and symptoms of endometriosis

2. Different phenotypes of endometriosis

3. The ASRM staging of endometriosis.

4. Multi-disciplinary approach for management of severe endometriosis

5. The definition and imaging features of adenomyosis

6. Principles of treatment for adenomyosis.

 

24. Ovarian Tumor

1. To be familiar with the histological classification of ovarian tumor.

2. To be familiar with the complications of ovarian tumor.

3. To Master the clinical signs, diagnosis and principle of treatment.

4. To Master differential diagnosis between benign and malignant ovarian tumor.

 

25. Pelvic floor dysfunction

1. To master the definition of pelvic organ prolapse (POP),

2. To be familiar with the manifestation, the POP quantification and staging system of pelvic organ prolapse.

2. To be familiar with the definition, etiology and diagnosis of pelvic organ prolapse.

3.To master the definition of stress urinary incontinence.

4. To be familiar with the physical examination and tests of stress urinary incontinence.

 

26. Infertility

1. To be familiar with the frequently used assisted reproductive technology.

2. To be familiar with the complications of assisted reproductive technology.

3. To Master the causes, diagnosis and treatment of infertility.

 

27. Family planning

1. To know the mechanism of hormonal contraception

2. To be familiar with the type of non-hormonal methods of contraception，especially IUD

3. To understand the contraindications of hormonal contraception

4. To understand the methods of early pregnancy termination.

Chinese Stock Market and Market Microstructure

课程简介（中文）

This elective course is situated in the first module of the fall semester for the 2nd year EMF students at Fanhai International School of Finance. In the coming decade, market microstructure has become one of the fastest growing fields of financial economics. The competition among exchanges, development of trading algorithms, design of robust market systems all require combining the theory of market microstructure with an understanding of institutional detail of how financial markets work in practice. Liquidity, transaction costs, trading strategies, insider traders, short sellers, high- frequency trading, crashes, market fragmentation, circuit breakers, market design are topics of great interest to finance professors, market participants, policy makers, and sometimes even to the general public.

教学目标

Introduce the basic understanding of structured finance Understand topics in structured finance, such as shadow banking, tradecredit, project finance, government debt etc. Compare the structured finance between S. and China and discuss the recent development.

课程简介（中文）

本课程以英语授课。本课程将商务谈判研究与国际经济学相结合，为驾驭全球商业互动提供了一个全面的框架。学生将探索谈判心理学的核心概念，包括决策理论、冲突解决策略和人际动态，同时将这些策略建立在贸易政策、市场力量和跨境金融体系等经济原则的基础之上。

课程简介（英文）

This course is taught in English. This course integrates the study of business negotiation with international economics, providing a comprehensive framework for navigating global commercial interactions. Students will explore core concepts in the psychology of bargaining—including decision-making theories, conflict resolution tactics, and interpersonal dynamics—while simultaneously grounding these strategies in economic principles such as trade policies, market forces, and cross-border financial systems.

教学目标

By the end of the semester, students will develop a profound understanding of 1) the dynamics of interpersonal and inter-group conflicts and their resolution 2) navigating negotiation in the globalization landscape 3) the mechanisms of international economics.

教学方式

70% Lecture,  10% Group Discussion and  20% Group Presentation

课程简介（中文）

基于领域内重要文献，本课程对当代中国国际关系进行全面审视。本课程旨在全面探讨中国作为一个复杂行为体如何与日益复杂的国际社会互动。课程将涵盖重要的理论路径和议题领域，这些路径和领域贯穿于中国与世界各区域和国家的关系之中。课程还将深入研究中国与世界不同地区关系的演变和动态，阐明关键行为体、核心机制、新兴问题以及发展趋势。

课程简介（英文）

Building on a survey of important literatures in the field, this course offers a comprehensive examination of contemporary China’s international relations. The course seeks to present balanced arguments about how China as a complex actor itself engages an increasingly complex world. The course begins with important theoretical approaches and issue areas that cut across China’s relations with regions and countries around the world. It then delves into the evolution and dynamics of the relations between China and different parts of the world, shedding light on the key actors, core mechanisms, emerging issues, and future trajectories.

教学目标

By engaging with theoretical frameworks, empirical analyses, and critical discussions, this course aims to equip students with the analytical tools and knowledge necessary to navigate the complexities of China’s international relations. Students will also develop general capabilities to critically engage in scholarly discussions, analyze cases and dynamics, and write papers with academic rigor.

教学方式

Lectures by professor; presentations and informed participation by students

教学内容与进度安排

Week Content 1 Course intro + Unpacking China’s international relations 2 Domestic institutions and China’s international relations 3 China and international institutions 4 China and international norms 5 China as an international economic actor (I) 6 China as an international economic actor (II) 7 China as an international security actor 8 China and the United States 9 China and Europe (Russia) 10 China in Asia 11 China and the Global South 12 The future of China’s international relations

课程简介（中文）

本课程面向公共管理研究领域的博士生，旨在帮助学生系统掌握该学科的主要专题领域。课程将向学生介绍相关领域的经典文献与前沿发展，要求学生深入理解这些领域的基本分析框架与理论观点，并能够进行批判性评价与整合，进而以恰当方式将这些理论视角应用于经验研究。通过本课程的学习，学生将培养批判与发展公共管理研究与理论的核心能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is for starting level Ph.D. students to acquire a solid understanding of major thematic areas of public management research. The course introduces to students classic readings and frontier development in these areas of study. Students are expected to know in-depth the basic analytical structures and arguments of these areas, critically assess and integrate them, and apply these theories and perspectives in their own studies in appropriate ways. The course aims to help students develop fundamental abilities to criticize and advance public management research and theory.

教学目标

Get familiar with major topical areas of public management, and important literature and scholars; Understand major theoretical developments, debates, and frontier issues; Learn to critically appraise, analyze, extend and integrate existing theories; Learn to apply, test, and develop theories in empirical studies.

教学方式

Students read the required and elective readings first, and submit reading memos each week before the class. Then in class the professor will guide students for in-depth discussions and debates.

考核构成

  • 课堂表现Participation 10.0% Class attendance. Students are expected to attend all classes. One absence without asking for a leave in advance leads to a deduction of 5 points. The maximum deduction is 10 points. Three absence leads to a grade of F. Being late for 15 minutes is equal to an absence. Class participation: Students are expected to actively participate in class discussions and contribute to the discussion quality. In-class discussions accounts for about 50% of the class time.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 10.0% Memos: Students shall do a 1-2 page memo (12 font size, single-spaced) for each class, summarizing your critical thinking of the readings and the topic. This shall be sent to the teaching assistant 1 day (at least 24 hours) before the class.
  • 汇报Presentation 10.0% Class presentation: Every student is required to do two presentations during the semester at the beginning of one class. The purpose is to share the student’s understanding of the TOPIC of that week. The maximum time of presentation is 10 minutes. Topics are assigned to the students during the class of Week 1. Notes: Simply summarizing the readings in the memos and presentations is not sufficient. 1
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 70.0% The final exam tests students’ understanding of the theories studied in class. Student who receives an F grade in the class must retake this course in the next year.

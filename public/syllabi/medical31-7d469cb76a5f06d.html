1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 眼科学教学是紧密眼科学基础和临床诊治技术更新发展的教学过程，并且需要有光学、心理学、神经科学等学科基础，与临床诸多学科密切相关。采用多媒体教学手段，以眼球及其附属器的解剖生理为基础，结合眼科特别检测、治疗技术手段，对常见眼病尤其是致盲眼病的流行病学、疾病的发生、发展、临床表现、诊断及鉴别诊断、治疗原则和预后转归进行课堂教学和临床教学。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Ophthalmology teaching is a teaching process that closely follows the foundation of ophthalmology and the development of clinical diagnosis and treatment technology, and needs the basis of optics, psychology, neuroscience, etc., and is closely related to many clinical disciplines. Using multimedia teaching methods, based on the anatomy and physiology of the eyeball and its appendages, combined with ophthalmology special detection and treatment techniques, the epidemiology, disease occurrence, development, clinical manifestation, diagnosis and diagnosis of common eye diseases, especially blind eye diseases. Differential diagnosis, treatment principles and prognosis outcomes for classroom teaching and clinical teaching, and always paying attention to human care and communications.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

We aim to cultivate high-level professionals in clinical medicine with broad foundations, solid professional skills, strong innovation capabilities and great development potential. Internship and clerkship teaching as a supplement to theoretical teaching is a process of combining theory, practice and human care Through the course, we help our students to master the main clinical manifestations and treatment principles of common diseases in ophthalmology, familiar with the common examination methods of ophthalmology, and understand the purpose of new progress in the discipline.

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

一、 Theoretical Course Teaching Plan Week 1 Anatomy, Ophthalmologic examination 1.To know the basic concept of the eye and vision function, the importance of eye disease to health and development of ophthalmology and visual science. 2.To master the anatomical structure, physiological functions and clinical significance of the various tissues of the eye, understand the embryonic development of the eye, basic examination of the ophthalmology and special administration methods. Week 2 Lids, Lacrimal Apparatus & Tears, Conjunctiva 1.Understand the etiology, clinical manifestations and treatment of neonatal dacryocystitis 2.Familiar with common causes of epiphora. 3.Master the tear production and discharge 4.Master diagnosis and treatment of epiphora . 5.Master the clinical manifestations and treatment of the acute and chronic dacryocystitis 6.Understand the clinical manifestations of phlyctenular keratoconjunctivitis, keratoconjunctivitis sicca. 7.Familiar with the diagnosis and treatment of vernal conjunctivitis, gonorrheal conjunctivitis and pterygium. 8.Master the clinical manifestations and treatment of common acute conjunctivitis (bacterial or viral). 9.Master the clinical and pathological characteristics, complications , prevention and treatment of trachoma Week 3 Cornea To comprehend the underlined diseases, including the etiology, clinical features and treatments. 1)keratodermatomalacia; 2) neurotrophic keratitis; 3) exposure keratitis and 4) Mooren’s ulcer. The knowledge need advanced comprehension are 1) the pathophysiologic process of keratitis; 2) the diagnosis and differential diagnosis of bacterial/viral/fungi keratitis, and the corresponding treatments ; 3) the clinical features and therapy of corneal ulcer caused by pseudomonas aeruginosa. Week 4 Lens 1.Understand lens disease, the major type of cataract 2.Understand the management of aphakia 3.Know the pathogenesis and classification of cataract 4.Know the treatment and the surgical indication of cataract 5.Master the stages and clinical features of cataract 6.Master the preoperative management Week 5 Uveal , Sclera 1.learn the characteristics of scleritis 2.learn the relationship of disease with anatomic and tissue structure of sclera 3.Know the clinical characteristics and treatment. 4.learn the characteristics of Vogt-Koyanagi- Harada Syndrome 5.know the clinical characteristics and prevention protocol of sympathetic Ophthalmia 6.master the clinical characteristics ,complications, sequelae, differential diagnosis and treatment of uveitis Week 6 Vitreous, Retina 1.Master the meanings of common fundus lesion signs: retinal hemorrhage, edema, exudation. 2.Master the clinical manifestations and treatment principles of age-related macular degeneration, rhegmatogenous retinal detachment, and diabetic retinopathy. 3.Familiarize with the fundus characteristics of hypertensive arteriosclerosis and central retinal artery/vein occlusion. 4.Understand the fundus characteristics and clinical significance of nephritis, pregnancy-induced hypertension, and leukemia. Week 7 Glaucoma 1.To know the concept and classification of glaucoma, research progress in the diagnosis and treatment of glaucoma. 2.To understand glaucoma related anatomical pathophysiology, normal value of intraocular pressure, aqueous humor production and circulation. 3.To understand the common causes of secondary glaucoma. 4.To understand the diagnostic evaluation of congenital glaucoma. 5.To master the clinical signs and treatment principle of Primary Angle Closure Glaucoma. 6.To master the diagnostic methods for Primary Open Angle Glaucoma. 7.To master the pharmacological actions, indications, and administration of antiglaucoma drugs. 8.To konwthe principles and indications and new developments of anti-glaucoma surgery Week 8 Optics & Refraction 1.master the symptoms, examination and treatment of myopia, hyperopia, presbyopia and astigmatism. Master the prevention of juvenile myopia 2.know the fundus’ change and common complications of high myopia, the relationship between accommodation and presbyopia, know the subjective refraction and prescription, know the accommodative myopia 3.learn the anisometropia, contact lenses, causes and treatment of amblyopia Week 9 Strabismus 1.Master the clinical tests of strabismus, the therapy’s principles of strabismus and the difference between comitant and incomitant strabismus. 2.Know the anatomical physiology of extraocular muscle and common cause of strabismus. 3.Learn binocular vision and examination of diplopia. Week 10 Ocular & orbital trauma 1.Understand the clinical manifestations, prevention, and treatment of ocular thermal burns and radiation-induced eye injuries. 2.Familiarize with the clinical manifestations, prevention, treatment, and complications of chemical eye injuries. 3.Familiarize with the prevention and treatment of exogenous endophthalmitis. 4.Master the clinical manifestations and treatment of corneal foreign bodies, penetrating ocular injuries, ocular blunt trauma, and intraocular foreign bodies. Week 11 Neuro-ophthalmology 1.Mastering the diagnosis, differential diagnosis and principal of the treatment of optic neuritis, papilledema and optic nerve atrophy. 2.Be familiar with physiology of visual pathway and the differential diagnosis of acute visual loss. 3.Understanding the visual field defect on visual pathway. Week 12 Orbit 1.diagnosis and management of ocular tumors—eyelid tumors, intraocular tumors and some orbital diseases. 2.As a common eyelid malignancy, sebaceous gland carcinoma should be differentiated from chalazion in clinical practice. 3.No doubt every medical student should be familiar with concept and advances of retinoblastoma . 4.The main signs and symptoms of orbital diseases should also be acknowledged. 二、Demonstration Course Teaching Plan Demonstration Lesson1: Anatomy and Examination of the Eye Demonstration Lesson2: Multimedia Teaching of Common Eye Diseases Demonstration Lesson3: Teaching Ward Round (Glaucoma, Cataract) Demonstration Lesson4: Teaching Ward Round (Ocular Tumors, Fundus) Demonstration Lesson5: Teaching Rounds (Cornea, Strabismus and Amblyopia, Optometry)

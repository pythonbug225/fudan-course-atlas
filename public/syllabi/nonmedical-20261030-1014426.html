发展与教育心理学专题

2025秋 · PSYC60004.01 · 王燕

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本门课程的教学内容涵盖三方面。一是围绕依恋理论，介绍发展与教育心理学领域的主要理论体系构成及其应用；二是围绕母婴关系，从根源探讨早期环境同个体发展间的关系模式；三是通过阅读发展与教育心理学的最新文献，了解该领域的发展新趋势和研究新动态。 通过该门课程学习，学生需要掌握以下内容和技能。一是能够从整体上把握发展心理学、教育心理学领域的基本理论构成，尤其是对核心的重要理论，从理论提出、发展及应用不同侧面，有较为深入的理解。二是能够从早期微观及宏观环境的角度，深入了解个体心理形成和发展轨迹，能够深层剖析个体不同行为模式的成因及影响因素。

课程简介（英文）

This course covers three topics. Firstly, on the basis of attachment theory, it introduces the main theoretical systems and their applications in the fields of developmental and educational psychology. Secondly, focusing on the mother - infant relationship, it explores the relationship patterns between the raising environment and individual’s development from the early years in life. Thirdly, by reporting the latest literature in developmental and educational psychology, students can understand the new development trends and hot topics in the related field. Through the study of this course, students are required to master the following knowledge and skills. Firstly, they should be able to comprehensively grasp the basic theoretical composition in the fields of developmental and educational psychology, especially have a relatively in - depth understanding of the core theories from different aspects such as theory formulation, development, and application. Secondly, from the perspectives of the early micro - and macro - environments, they should be able to deeply understand the formation and development trajectory of the individual’s development, and be able to deeply analyze the causes and influencing factors of individuals’discrimitive behavior patterns.

教学目标

以专题讲座的形式，结合本领域的研究新进展，介绍近年来发展心理学及教育心理学领域具有一定影响力的文献，帮助学生了解该领域的研究动态，掌握该领域的研究范式，批判性地阅读该领域的研究报告，培养学生从事研究的基础和综合能力。

教学方式

老师讲授，同学阅读分享文献，课程讨论。

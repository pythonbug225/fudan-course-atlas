英语笔译

2025秋 · GEEF20007.01 · 孙东云

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在通过混合式教学，帮助学生了解英语笔译的基本性质，掌握在英汉互译过程中常用的翻译技巧和策略；通过翻译微项目小组活动，鼓励学生探索和掌握基本的翻译理论，完成翻译任务，培养学生独立的思考和科研能力，将所学的翻译理论运用到具体的翻译作品分析中去。

课程简介（英文）

This course utilizes a blended learning approach to enhance students' comprehension of fundamental English translation principles and their mastery of common techniques and strategies for both Chinese-English and English-Chinese translation. Through translation micro-project, students are encouraged to delve into essential translation theories and fulfill translation assignments while cultivating independent critical thinking and research skills. Additionally, the course empowers students to apply these translation theories to the analysis of specific translation works.

教学目标

本课程致力于培养学生的跨文化翻译能力。学生能够在技术工具的辅助下，独立完成英汉互译任务，并进一步夯实英语语言基本功。同时，在翻译微项目活动中，学生通过资料检索和互动合作，有效提升团队合作意识和批判性思维能力。

教学方式

本课程采用线上线下混合式教学模式，注重学生的学习能力培养。教学方法包括： 线上录播课学习：学生通过超星学习通观看翻译教学视频，积极思考视频中提出的各种问题。 线上自主学习：学生自主学习平台上共享的翻译学习资源并完成自选翻译练习。 互动式讲解：教师结合经典、时新的语料，指导学生进行翻译技巧与策略、文化知识和思维知识的建构，并随时与学生互动答疑。 翻译微项目：学生围绕选定的主题，分小组完成口头汇报和书面报告。 课堂学生互评与师评：学生听众与汇报小组就汇报内容当堂进行口头互动，听众完成书面评价；教师就小组汇报当堂进行点评。 课堂练习与即时反馈：通过课堂练习及时巩固知识，教师即时反馈帮助学生纠正错误。

教学内容与进度安排

Week Module Teaching Goals 1 Orientation and course overview Learners are expected to: 1. understand the course objectives and overall semester arrangement; 2. familiarize themselves with learning tools that will facilitate their English language studies. 2 U1 Introduction to translation Learners are expected to: 1. understand some of the basic concepts in translation, such as the nature, process, and product of translation; 2. understand some of the fundamental considerations in translation practice, such as the purpose, audience, and context; 3. understand competence of translators, resources and tools for translators, and the role of translation in ideological guidance. 3 U2 The two languages in focus Learners are expected to understand the fundamental differences between the two languages from the flowing perspectives: 1) Synthetic vs. analytic 2) Hypotactic vs. paratactic 3) Complex vs. simplex 4 U2 The two languages in focus (Online learning) Learners are expected to understand the fundamental differences between the two languages from the flowing perspectives: 1) Impersonal vs. personal 2) Static vs. dynamic 3) Substitutive vs. repetitive 5 U3 Translation skills Learners are expected to learn and practice translation skills at the word level. 6 U3 Translation skills (Online learning) Learners are expected to learn and practice translation skills at the sentence level. 7 U3 Translation skills Learners are expected to learn and practice translation skills at the text level. 8 U4 Approaches to translation (Online learning) Learners are expected to learn and practice literal translation vs. free translation. 9 U4 Approaches to translation Learners are expected to know more about translation approaches. 10 U4 Approaches to translation (Online learning) Learners are expected to learn and practice domestication vs. foreignization. 11 U5 Applied translation Learners are expected to: 1. understand the general view of translation skills for applied fields; 2. watch and assess the presentation from Group 1 and Group 2. 12 U5 Applied translation (Online learning) Learners are expected to learn and practice translation skills for specific applied fields, including science and technology, public signage, and Chinese political discourse. 13 U6 Literary translation Learners are expected to: 1. understand the general view of translation skills for literary works; 2. watch and assess the presentation from Group 3 and Group 4. 14 U6 Literary translation (Online learning) Learners are expected to learn and practice translation skills for literary works. 15 Quiz Learners are expected to: 1. watch and assess the presentation from Group 5 and Group 6; 2. fulfill a Chinese-English translation quiz. 16 Q&A 17 Final Written Test 18 Submission of Final Grades

课程简介（中文）

本学期语法知识词法部分包括集合数词、双部句中主要成分表示法、定语表示法、补语表示法、原因状语、条件状语、地点状语、呼语、插入语、同位语、大数表示法、分数表示法等；句法部分包括说明从句、定语从句、条件从句、时间从句、程度度量从句、比较从句、让步从句、结果从句、接续从句、句子的独立次要成分、插入句、并列复合句、无连词复合句等。语法练习注重针对性、实用性和交际性等特点。 本学期充分考虑俄罗斯语言、文化等国情特点，着重培养学生的语言运用和跨文化交际能力。通过城市、太空、旅游、环保、教育、文化等12个主题，扩大学生的国情背景视野，丰富文化知识。积极学习相关领域词汇，培养学生运用外语讲述中国故事的交际能力。

课程简介（英文）

This semester, the lexical part of grammar knowledge includes collective numerals, ways to express the main components in two-part sentences, expression of attributes, expression of complements, adverbials of cause, condition, and place, forms of address, parenthetical expressions, appositives, expression of large numbers, fractions, etc. The syntactic part covers subordinate clauses such as explanatory clauses, attributive clauses, conditional clauses, temporal clauses, clauses of degree and measure, comparative clauses, concessive clauses, result clauses, consecutive clauses, as well as independent secondary parts of sentences, inserted sentences, compound sentences, and asyndetic compound sentences. Grammar exercises emphasize features such as targeted practice, practicality, and communicative effectiveness. This semester fully considers the characteristics of Russian language, culture, and other national conditions, focusing on cultivating students' language application and cross-cultural communication skills. Through 12 themes including cities, space, tourism, environmental protection, education, and culture, students' background knowledge of national conditions is expanded, and their cultural literacy is enriched. Students actively learn vocabulary in related fields, fostering their communicative ability to use a foreign language to tell China's stories.

教学目标

本课程旨在培养学生掌握基础语法知识和运用语法知识的技能。积极扩大学生语言使用范围，增加词汇量，提高运用积极词语的能力；加强连贯口笔语训练，提高叙述和表达思想的能力。密切集合课文教学和语法教学培养逻辑思维和独立使用语言的能力。

教学方式

小班教学，为学生积极参与课堂活动提供保证；要求学生课前预习，课上练习，课后复习；授课语言以俄语为主，辅以必要的汉语作为解释。

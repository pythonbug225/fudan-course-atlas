国际法

课程简介（中文）

本课程涵盖的基本内容包括国际法的主要渊源、行为主体和相关机构；国内法院对国际法的适用；国际司法机构的裁决；国内法的域外适用；以及国际犯罪、国际贸易和使用武力等国际法的具体领域。

课程简介（英文）

This course will cover topics including the key sources, actors and institutions of international law; the application of international law by domestic courts; adjudication by international judicial bodies; extraterritorial application of domestic law; and specific issue areas such as international crimes, international trade, and the use of force.

教学目标

This course offers a broad introduction to international law and provides a foundation for specialized courses in international law. Additionally, this course also aims at guiding students in building legal capacities to solve daily issues of international law and understanding the values of international law.

教学方式

Lecture, combined with in class class studies and discussions. Class 10 will be distributed to review the mock mid-term exam so that students will be able to get better prepared for the final exam.

教学内容与进度安排

Class 1: Introduction to International Law Objective: Understand the differences in the actors, major issues, applicable laws and solutions between the foreign office model and the global governance model Class 2: Treaties I: Conclusion and Invalidation of Treaties Objective: Understand and apply the substantive and procedural requirements of treaty conclusion and invalidation Class 3: Treaties II: Interpretation and Termination of Treaties Objective: Understanding and apply the VCLT rules on treaty interpretation and termination Class 4: Treaties III: Reservation to Treaties Objective: Understand the requirements, procedures, and effects of treaty reservation Class 5: Customary International Law Objective: Understand the criteria of establishing a rule of customary international law Class 6: Customary International Law II and Soft Law Objective: Know how and where to find supportive evidence of an emerging rule of customary international law, whether and when an emerging rule of customary international law will prevail over a pre-existing one, and understand the relationship between customary international law, treaty, and soft law Class 7: General Principles of Law Objective: Understand different approaches in ascertaining a general principle of law and the major controversies over them Class 8: What is a State Objective: Understand the criteria of statehood in theory and in practice, the different standards in establishing and maintaining statehood, and the effects of recognition Class 9: International Organizations Objective: Understand the role of international organizations as an actor of international law Class 10: Practice on a Mock Mid-term Exam (not graded) Objective: Build the primary capacity in issue-spotting, identifying, interpreting, and applying relevant rules of international law, structuring the answer Class 11: Jurisdiction to Prescribe: The Extraterritoriality Objective: Understand the fundamental bases of jurisdiction to prescribe and how to solve conflicts of concurrent jurisdictions Class 12: Extraterritorial Jurisdiction to Adjudicate/Enforce Objective: Understand the limits of extra territorial jurisdiction to adjudicate and enforce Class 13: The Prohibition Against Torture Objective: Understand the nature, scope and meaning of the prohibition against torture by focusing on the case-study of foreign detainees of the US captured in its “Global War on Terror” Class 14: WTO Dispute Settlement: Balancing Trade and Non-trade Values Objective: Understand the balancing approach adopted by the WTO Dispute Settlement Body in weighing non-trade values against trade liberalization Class 15: The Use of Force: The Gulf War and the Iraq War Objective: Understand the major controversies surrounding “self-defense” in law and in practice Class 16: The Relations between International Law and Domestic Law Objective: Understand how international law enters into the domestic legal system

I may lower your final exam score by five points (e.g., from 90 to 85) if you do not show up when being called in class and did not inform me of your absence in an email BEFORE class begins. I may raise your final exam score by five points (e.g., from 90 to 85) to reward sustained and high-quality contributions to class discussions during the course. If you feel unprepared for a particular class, I would appreciate if you would send me an email BEFORE class begins.

The final exam will consist of an issue-spotting essay question and/or a policy question. The exam will be an open book one. Students will have a better idea of final exam questions in preparing and reviewing the mock mid-term exam later in this semester.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提升学生在专业领域的英语应用与科技写作能力。涵盖专业文献查询、整理及阅读、学术论文撰写、科研成果展示等核心内容。通过系统学习，学生能够熟练掌握专业英语词汇与表达，了解科技写作规范与技巧，为未来的学术研究与国际交流打下坚实基础，助力科研成果的有效传播与分享。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to boost graduate students' English-language proficiency in their majors and science-and-technology-writing skills. The course focuses on key aspects like professional-literature reading, academic-paper writing, and research-outcome presentation. By studying this course, students can master professional English vocabulary and expressions, get familiar with science-and-technology-writing standards and techniques. This will equip them for future academic and international exchange work, promoting effective research-outcome communication and sharing.

教学目标

Integrating academic integrity education with cross-cultural communication to develop students' rigorous academic attitudes and global perspectives, and shape them into high-quality talents with international competitiveness. Focusing on specialized English vocabulary, terminology, and scientific writing standards, covering various text types such as academic papers and research reports, to strengthen students' theoretical foundation in specialized English and scientific writing. Emphasizing the integrated training of listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, particularly enhancing practical scientific writing abilities to meet the requirements of training well-rounded talents and boost students' future academic and career development. 融合学术诚信教育与跨文化交流，培养学生严谨治学态度和全球视野，塑造其成为具备国际竞争力的高素质人才。 聚焦专业英语词汇、术语及科技写作规范，涵盖学术论文、科研报告等多元文体知识讲解，夯实学生专业英语与科技写作的理论基础。 注重听说读写综合技能训练，尤其强化科技写作实践能力，满足复合型人才培养要求，为学生未来学术研究与职业发展助力。

教学方式

Primarily lecture-based teaching, combined with multimedia instruction and case analysis. Group discussions and project presentations to cultivate students' innovation and teamwork. 课堂讲授为主，结合多媒体教学与案例分析。 小组讨论与项目汇报，培养学生的创新能力和团队合作精神。

教学内容与进度安排

Teaching contents (12 teaching weeks, 36 class hours in total): Week 1: Course introduction and retrieval of English-language scientific literature Teaching contents: Course goals, contents, and assessment methods are introduced. The importance of professional English and scientific writing is emphasized. Types of literature, commonly-used databases for literature search, and main methods for retrieving English-language scientific literature are presented. After-class assignment #1: Students are required to consult 5-10 English and Chinese literature articles from different fields (including Chinese and English degree theses, patents, journal reviews, and research articles). They should familiarize themselves with the usage of common academic search databases. Week 2: Extensive reading of professional English literature (I) Teaching contents: Extensive-reading skills and methods for professional English literature are taught. The structure and writing features of literature are introduced. Students are guided to identify the innovation points, shortcomings of papers, and collect useful words and sentences. Week 3: Extensive reading of professional English literature (II) Teaching contents: Intensive-reading skills for professional English literature, methods for accumulating and memorizing professional English vocabulary, and analysis of sentence structures in professional literature. Students are taught techniques for understanding long and complex sentences. After-class assignment #2: Students are required to extensively read around 20 English articles in their field and write a 50-100-word Chinese summary for each. For 3-5 articles, they should take notes on vocabulary and analysis of long and complex sentences. Besides, they need to describe the strengths and weaknesses of these articles and share their reading experiences. Week 4: Academic writing standards Teaching contents: The overall structure of academic papers is introduced. Requirements and techniques for writing titles, authors, introductions, and acknowledgments are explained. Key points of writing paper abstracts (purpose, methods, results, and conclusions) are covered. Principles for selecting keywords and requirements and techniques for writing the experimental section and the results-and-discussion section are explained. Week 5: Grammar and expression in scientific English Teaching contents: Differences in word usage between scientific English and general English writing are taught. Common independent structures and other grammatical features and expressions in scientific English writing are introduced. After-class assignment #3: Students are to come up with a title, an abstract and keywords for an academic paper and write the introduction (200-300 words) and the acknowledgment section based on the title they created. Week 6: Creating and using charts and graphs in scientific writing Teaching contents: Common methods for creating charts and graphs (bar charts, line charts, pie charts, etc.) are taught. Standards for reasonably inserting and citing charts and graphs in scientific writing are explained. Week 7: Writing literature reviews Teaching contents: The writing process of literature reviews (literature collection, organization, analysis, and summary) is explained. Excellent literature-review cases are analyzed. After-class assignment #4: Students are required to write a literature review of 1,000-1,500 words. Weeks 8: Writing research reports Teaching contents: Types and formats of research reports are introduced. Key points of writing research reports (problem statement, method description, results analysis, etc.) are explained. Week 9: Creating English-language academic-presentation PPTs and communication Teaching contents: How to create English-language PPTs for academic presentations and how to give oral presentations in English are explained. Relevant techniques and methods are covered. After-class assignment #5: Students are to create a simple PPT for an academic presentation. Week 10: Academic paper submission and publication Teaching contents: The common types of academic papers are introduced. The submission process, requirements, and key considerations for academic papers are explained. The publication process and important points to note are outlined. Week 11: Discussion on AI-empowered professional English and scientific writing Teaching contents: Group discussions are held on how artificial intelligence can empower scientific English writing. After-class assignment #6: Groups are to submit their assignments and innovative suggestions. Week 12: Course review Teaching contents: Key course contents are reviewed. Course assessment-related contents (including scientific-writing practice and oral presentations) are introduced. 教学内容（12教学周，共36学时）： 第 1 周：课程简介及英文科技文献检索 教学内容：介绍课程目标、内容、考核方式；阐释专业英语与科技写作的重要性，介绍文献类型、文献查找常用数据库和英文科技文献检索的主要方法。 课后作业#1：查阅5-10篇不同类型专业领域中英文文献（中英文学位论文、中英文专利、期刊综述、期刊论文等），熟悉常见检索数据库的用法。 第 2 周：专业英语文献阅读基础（一） 教学内容：讲解专业英语文献泛读技巧与方法；介绍文献结构与写作特点，学会凝练论文创新点、不足及整理好词好句。 第 3 周：专业英语文献阅读基础（二） 教学内容：讲解专业英语文献精读技巧与专业英语词汇积累与记忆方法；分析专业文献句子结构，掌握长难句理解技巧。 课后作业#2：完成20篇左右专业领域英文文献泛读，每篇撰写50-100字的中文小结。精读 3-5 篇专业英文文献，整理词汇与长难句分析笔记，描述文章优缺点及阅读心得体会。 第 4 周：学术论文写作规范 教学内容：介绍学术论文整体结构；讲解标题、作者、引言、致谢撰写要求与技巧；阐述论文摘要写作要点（目的、方法、结果、结论）；讲解关键词选取原则；讲解实验部分及结果与讨论部分写作要求与技巧。 第 5 周：科技英语的文法与表达 教学内容：讲授科技英语与普通英语写作用词的区别；介绍科技英语写作常用独立结构等文法与表达。 课后作业#3：拟定 1 篇学术论文标题、摘要与关键词，撰写 200-300 字的引言部分及致谢部分。 第 6 周：科技写作中的图表制作与运用 教学内容：教授常用图表（柱状图、折线图、饼图等）制作方法；讲解图表在科技写作中的合理插入与引用规范。 第 7 周：文献综述写作 教学内容：讲解文献综述的写作流程（文献收集、整理、分析与总结）；分析优秀文献综述案例。 课后作业#4：撰写 1 篇 1000-1500 字的文献综述。 第 8 周：科研报告写作 教学内容：介绍科研报告类型与格式；讲解科研报告写作要点（问题陈述、方法描述、结果分析等）。 第 9 周：学术报告英文PPT制作及交流 教学内容：讲解如何制作学术报告英文PPT以及如何进行口头英文汇报，讲述相关技巧及方法。 课后作业#5：制作一份简单的学术报告PPT。 第 10 周：学术论文投稿与发表 教学内容：介绍学术论文的常见类型；讲解学术论文投稿流程、要求及注意事项；介绍学术论文发表的流程及注意事项。 第 11 周：AI赋能专业英语与科技写作研讨 教学内容：分组研讨人工智能如何赋能科技英语写作 课后作业#6：分组提交作业及创新建议。 第 12 周：课程总结 教学内容：回顾课程重点内容；进行课程考核相关内容（包括科技写作实践与口头汇报）。

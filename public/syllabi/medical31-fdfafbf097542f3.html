1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程作为药学本科生专业选修课，以全英文授课，以药理学知识为基础，通过案例研究更深入地讨论药物的作用机制及其临床应用。目的是通过教学加深学生对药物相关药理学知识的理解，并通过多种学习过程提高学生对药理学前沿与应用的能力与水平。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Problem based Pharmacology is a professional elective course designed for undergraduate students majoring in pharmacy. The course is entirely in English and is mainly based on the Pharmacology course. It uses English teaching methods to discuss the mechanism of action of drugs and their clinical applications in more depth through case studies. The aim is to deepen students' understanding of drug-related pharmacology knowledge through teaching, and to improve their ability to learn the frontiers of pharmacology and to practically use the knowledge.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

以 “问题驱动学习” 为核心，聚焦培养学生解决实际药理学问题的能力，包括： 1.建立问题导向的知识框架，以疾病为切入点，掌握药物作用机制与临床应用的逻辑关联；关联基础药理学与临床药理学知识，形成跨学科知识网络。 培养问题解决与实践创新能力——提炼关键药理学问题，基于药理学原理设计解决方案，评估不同治疗方案的利弊。 沟通能力——在角色扮演中具备模拟不同角色的能力，并能用通俗语言和专业语言自如切换和表达；对药物研究前沿有一定深入理解并能表达。 Focusing on "problem-driven learning", the course aims to cultivating students' ability to solve practical pharmacological problems, including: Establish a problem-oriented knowledge framework, taking diseases as the entry point, and master the logical connection between the mechanism of drug action and clinical application; Link the knowledge of basic pharmacology and clinical pharmacology to form an interdisciplinary knowledge network. Cultivate problem-solving and practical innovation capabilities - Distill key pharmacological issues, design solutions based on pharmacological principles, and evaluate the pros and cons of different treatment options. Communication skills - Possess the ability to simulate different roles in role-playing and be able to switch and express oneself freely between plain language and professional language. Have a certain in-depth understanding of the frontiers of drug research and be able to express it.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

1. 线上学习：通过学习上传在在线学习平台上的前沿文献资料，结合平台上的知识点短视频，在互动讨论区参与学习与讨论，并完成测试；按要求准备好线下交流内容。（突出课程基本知识点，引导学生有效查找资料） 2. 线下学习：对线上学习要求的内容开展“翻转课堂”式讨论；以小组为单位现场讨论分析病例并给出方案，组间交流；小组开展角色扮演教学活动并随机展示。（侧重学生创新意识与应用能力的培养，训练学生表达与交流能力） 1. Online study: Upload the updated literature materials on the online learning platform, combined with short videos of knowledge points on the platform, the students should learn and participate in the interactive discussion section online, and complete the test; They will be asked to prepare the offline discussion content as well. (These aim to highlight the basic knowledge points of the course and guide students to search for information efficiently) 2. On-site study: A "flipped classroom" style discussion will be conducted on the content required for online learning; Students will also discuss and analyze cases on-site as a group and provide solutions, and communicate between groups; The groups will conduct role-playing teaching activities and randomly display will be selected.

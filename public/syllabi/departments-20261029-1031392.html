中国金融体系

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

China is growing at an unprecedented speed in the past 48 years and it has been already the world’s second largest economy in terms of total GDP. Furthermore, China’s financial markets are developing rapidly as it is pushed forward its liberalization process and integration with the rest of the world. As the current trend continues, it has been increasingly important role for the outside world to learn and understand the workings of China’s financial systems. This course will introduce the overview of China Financial System as a whole framework, then Corporate Governance System in China, Banking System and Shadow Banking, Monetary System， Exchange Rate & RMB Internalization, Bond Markets, Stock Markets, Real Estate Markets, and Fintech in China in details with cases. Furthermore, this course explores the direction of its financial development in future as well. As a result, the course will present a whole map of the history, current and future on Chinese Finance System.

教学目标

The course objective is to understand the role and mechanism design provided by the financial system in China’s economic development recently, as well as the investment opportunities and risk presented by the system to the outside world. Finally, It will provide us a future trend on the direction of its financial development by overviewing and analyzing the history and current situation of Chinese Finance System，and find out the research areas by research proposal related to China’s Financial Markets.

教学方式

The teachers have a lecture on sites and also encourage the students discuss the current issues by groups or individuals.Each week the students shall submit the assginment and analyze the Cases-based on the Chinese Financial markets.

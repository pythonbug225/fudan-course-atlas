课程简介（中文）

实用断层解剖学是用断层的方法研究和表达人体正常器官结构的形态、位置、相互关系及其基本功能的科学。是随着CT、MRI和B超等医学影像诊断技术的发展而产生的一门新兴学科，是临床医学、生物医学工程和基础医学相关专业的重要基础课程。其任务是通过教学使学生掌握人体主要结构在连续解剖和影像断层中的形态、位置及其变化规律，为疾病的影像学分析和诊治打下坚实的基础。教学以精讲多练、辅以局解标本、模型、文献讨论等多种形式，同时介绍一些医学图像处理、人工智能技术在本课程中的应用，以提高课堂教学效果，达到理解基本理论，加深巩固基本知识的目的。

课程简介（英文）

Clinically oriented sectional anatomy is a science that studies and expresses the morphology, position, interrelationships, and basic functions of normal human organ structures using tomographic methods. This emerging discipline has emerged with the development of medical imaging diagnostic technologies such as CT, MRI, and B-ultrasound, serving as an essential foundational course for clinical medicine, biomedical engineering, and related fields in basic medical sciences. Its goal is to teach students to understand the morphology, position, and variation patterns of major human structures in both continuous anatomical views and tomographic images, laying a solid foundation for the imaging analysis and diagnosis and treatment of diseases.

The teaching approach emphasizes concise lectures and ample practice, with students observing tomographic specimens as the primary method, supplemented by sectional specimens, models, literature discussions, and other forms to enhance classroom effectiveness, aiming to deepen understanding of basic theories and reinforce fundamental knowledge.

教学目标

(一)基本理论知识

1．掌握人体主要结构（脑、肺、肝等）在连续断层中的形态、位置及其变化规律。

2．熟悉重要层面上结构之间的相互关系。

（二）基本技能

1．通过观察标本、组织讨论，培养学生的观察能力、空间想象力等能力。

2．通过观察断层标本，培养学生与CT、MRI和B超等医学影像学横向联系的能力和精确的形态学定位能力。

3. 一些医学图像处理、人工智能技术在本课程中的应用。

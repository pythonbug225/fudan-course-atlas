课程简介（中文）

课程的主要内容包括导论、公共部门的经济活动、外部效应、公共品理论、公共选择理论、公共支出理论、公共预算、公共收入、税收原则、税收分类、税收效应、税收转嫁等相关理论，以及公债、财政乘数和多级政府间财政等公共经济学问题。并根据理论知识结合相关实际案例，讲解其具体应用。课时分配在以上知识点为2-4课时。

课程简介（英文）

The main content of the course includes relevant theories such as Introduction, Economic Activities of the Public Sector, External Effects, Public Goods Theory, Public Choice Theory, Public Expenditure Theory, Public Budgeting, Public Revenue, Tax Principles, Tax Classification, Tax Effects, and Tax Shifting, as well as public economics issues such as public debt, fiscal multipliers, and intergovernmental finance across multiple levels. It combines theoretical knowledge with relevant practical cases to explain their specific applications. Each of the above knowledge points is allocated 2-4 class hours.

以教师讲授为主，鼓励学生参与课堂讨论并提出问题。

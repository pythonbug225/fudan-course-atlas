课程简介（中文）

中西医结合学科的一个重要方面，就是利用现代医学技术和方法去探究中医基础理论及治病机制。近年来，中西医结合基础研究蓬勃发展，本课程从中西医结合研究发展简史讲起，通俗易懂地介绍中医基础理论的现代理解、国际上补充和替代医学（Complementary and Alternative Medicine, CAM）的新动态、针刺疗法的科学基础、针刺在临床神经系统疾病中的应用、中西医结合疗法在镇痛、神经保护、内分泌功能调节方面的基础研究（缺血性卒中、老年性痴呆、抑郁症、多发性硬化等）、中西医结合改善代谢性疾病、治疗慢性疲劳综合症等；同时给予实践操作课程，让学生近距离接触和体验针灸、推拿等中医特色疗法、动物模型建立及动物针灸。

课程简介（英文）

An essential aspect of the discipline of integrative Chinese and Western medicine is the use of modern biomedical techniques and methodologies to explore the fundamental theories and therapeutic mechanisms of traditional Chinese medicine (TCM). In recent years, basic research in integrative medicine has grown rapidly. This course begins with a historical overview of the development of integrative medicine and provides a clear, accessible introduction to the modern interpretation of core TCM concepts. It also covers recent global trends in Complementary and Alternative Medicine (CAM), the scientific foundations of acupuncture, and its clinical applications in neurological disorders. In addition, the course examines the basic research underlying integrative approaches to pain relief, neuroprotection, and endocrine regulation—including conditions such as ischemic stroke, Alzheimer’s disease, depression, and multiple sclerosis—as well as their relevance to metabolic disorders and chronic fatigue syndrome. Practical sessions are also included, offering students hands-on experience with traditional therapies such as acupuncture and tuina, along with training in animal modeling and experimental acupuncture techniques.

教学目标

介绍我国独创的中西医结合医学的发展历程，着重介绍中医的基础理论，中医疗法优势病种的相关中医基础理论、相关病机及可选择的中西医治疗方法，以及中西医结合的基础研究相关手段及研究进展，为中西医结合基础及临床科研方向研究生开展今后的中西医结合相关的实验研究提供思路和借鉴。

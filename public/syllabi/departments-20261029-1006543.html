课程简介（中文）

本门课程是高级计量系列课程的第二门。主要讲授时间序列（ARMA, GARCH, 单位根，向量自回归，VECM），以及面板模型、分位数回归、非参数方法和机器学习（如有时间）。

课程简介（英文）

This course is the second course of the Advanced Econometrics series. It will cover time series models (ARMA, GARCH, Unit Root, VAR，VECM）, Panel models, Quantile Regression, Nonparametric Eocnometrics and Machine Learning (if time permits).

教学目标

This course aims to get students famaliar with advanced Time Series models, Panel Models, Quantile Regression, Nonparametric Econometrics and Machine Learning (if time permits).

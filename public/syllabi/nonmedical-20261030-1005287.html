课程简介（中文）

科技英语论文写作是非英语专业研究生在对外学术交流中必不可少的一项能力要求,借助这一手段才能将研究成果发表在国际性学术期刊,达到与国外学者交流的目的,同时论文在国际权威期刊的发表质量和数量也是研究者、研究机构乃至整个国家的学术水平和成果的一个衡量标准。尽管研究生学习英语已经很多年，但科技英语论文写作中仍然存在很多问题,如母语的影响,篇章结构的不合理,论文格式不符合规范等。本课程结合许多科技论文实例，介绍科技论文结构的构建，论文题目的选定，摘要的精炼，背景的介绍，研究成果的书写规范和以及结论的提炼，同时总结了非英语专业研究生在科技英语写作中突出存在的一些问题,以及解决问题的方式。借助于该课程的开设指导研究生更好的完成科技论文的写作。

课程简介（英文）

The ability to write scientific and technical English papers is an essential requirement for non-English major graduate students in international academic exchanges. Only through this means can they publish their research findings in international academic journals and achieve the goal of communicating with scholars abroad. Moreover, the quality and quantity of papers published in internationally authoritative journals serve as a measure of the academic level and achievements of individual researchers, institutions, and even the country as a whole. Although graduate students have studied English for many years, numerous issues persist in their scientific and technical English writing, such as the influence of their native language, illogical text structure, and non-compliant paper formatting. This course introduces the construction of scientific paper structures, the selection of paper titles, the refinement of abstracts, the presentation of background information, the standardized writing of research findings, and the distillation of conclusions, supported by numerous examples of scientific papers. Additionally, it summarizes the prominent problems encountered by non-English major graduate students in scientific English writing and provides solutions to address them. Through the offering of this course, graduate students will be better guided to complete their scientific paper writing more effectively.

教学目标

本课程旨在培养学生使用英语进行学术写作的实际能力，引导学生掌握使用英语撰写摘要、引言、材料和方法、讨论、结论等诸要素；强调学以致用，突出与学术论文写作相关的技巧和要求，强化学术写作的实用方法，从而在一定程度上帮助学生针对性了解英语学术论文写作的相关要点，有效提升英语写作能力。

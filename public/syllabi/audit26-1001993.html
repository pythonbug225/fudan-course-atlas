课程简介（中文）

本课程从三国魏曹植所写《洛神赋》入手，首先讲授魏晋南北朝文学写作的历史背景和政治环境。其次，从《洛神赋》在后世的传播、成为经典，讲授魏晋南北朝时期文学所依托的传播媒介以及相关的物质文化。最后说明魏晋南北朝文学是与其产生的社会有密切关系的。

课程简介（英文）

This course begins with Cao Zhi's "The Ode to the Luo River Goddess" from the Three Kingdoms period of Wei, first introducing the historical background and political environment of literary creation during the Wei, Jin, Southern and Northern Dynasties. Secondly, it discusses the transmission of "The Ode to the Luo River Goddess" in later generations and its becoming a classic, explaining the transmission media and related material culture on which the literature of the Wei, Jin, Southern and Northern Dynasties relied. Finally, it clarifies that the literature of the Wei, Jin, Southern and Northern Dynasties is closely related to the society in which it originated.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在为学生提供更为全面的多元文化视野，使学生得以接触全球各种不同的政治思潮，明晰其脉络，掌握其方法，汇通其主题。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to introduce students to a range of political theories beyond the framework of traditional Western political thought and political theory.

It will try to utilize new perspectives and methods that go beyond Western-centric theory to pinpoint problems, understand problems, analyze problems, interpret problems, and even solve problems preliminarily.

Comparative political theory, as the name implies, is to introduce more non-Western texts into the field of study of political theory/philosophy for observation and comparison.

教学目标

Students are expected to leave the course with:

  • Appreciation of major theoretical perspectives as well as analytical tools in political theories in general, and comparative political theories in particular;
  • knowledge of political cultures, their political roots and the long shadows they usually cast on national politics;
  • Exposure to some comparative strategies at theoretical as well as empirical level.

教学方式

lecture, student presentation & class discussion

课程简介（中文）

本课程是绿色化学课程的深化和提高。课程旨在通过最新前沿性科学和技术进展和大量实例的介绍，在拓展学生知识面的同时，揭示绿色化学与人类生活密不可分的关系，使学生在进一步了解绿色化学起源、理念、原则和发展趋势的基础上，逐步培养和树立绿色化学理念，增强环保意识和社会责任感，为经济和社会的可持续发展作贡献。

课程简介（英文）

This course is the deepening and expansion of green chemistry course, and it is a course that constantly integrates the progresses of cutting-edge science and technology. The course content includes green catalysis, green energy, green chemical industry, green process, etc. It aims to reveal the inseparable relationship between green chemistry and human life through the introduction of the latest scientific research progresses and a large number of applied practices, which will enable students to further understand the origin, concept, principle, development trend of green chemistry and the relationship between green chemistry and modern human life, and to be gradually cultivated and firmly established the concept of green chemistry, enhancing the awareness of environmental protection and social responsibility. This course will also make the students pay attention to the implementation of the principle of green chemistry in their future work, and contribute to the sustainable development of economy and society.

教学目标

本课程从目前人类面临的环境问题出发，通过最新科研进展和大量实例的介绍，揭示绿色化学与人类生活密不可分的关系，使学生在进一步了解绿色化学起源、理念、原则、发展趋势以及绿色化学与人类现代生活关系的基础上，拓展学生知识面，逐步培养和牢固树立学生的绿色化学理念，增强环境保护意识，使学生在今后工作中始终注意贯彻绿色化学的原则，为实现经济和社会的可持续发展作贡献。

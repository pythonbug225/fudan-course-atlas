课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养学生的学术交流能力。本课程将系统介绍海报汇报，专题汇报，文章撰写等多种学术交流方式。作为入门课程，本课程将首先解析科学研究流程及其在不同领域的差异，以帮助同学们加强对跨学科学术交流的理解。此外，本课程将通过口头报告、海报展示及摘要撰写等实践训练提升学生的科学表达能力。期末成绩将综合出勤、课堂参与度及作业质量进行评定。课程虽聚焦学术交流中的英文表达能力，但所学技能对学生未来任何职业发展皆具重要价值。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for students to learn how to communicate their science to fellow scientists in professional contexts. Namely, we introduce both oral communication modalities (e.g., presenting at a scientific conference) and written modalities (e.g., abstracts and papers). As this is an introductory course, we start with describing the scientific process and discuss how it differs across fields (which is particularly important for students who will be working in interdisciplinary research). Then we invite students to practice the skills of scientific communication through oral and poster presentations, as well as writing a sample abstract. Feedbacks will be given from the lectures and fellow students. The final grade will be based on their attendance, participation in the course, quality of assignments and paper works. Although the course is specifically focused on scientific communication, many of the skills learned will be valuable, independently of the later career choices of the students.

教学目标

The aims of this course are: 1) to introduce the process of science communication in different contexts (academic publications, scientific conferences, seminars), and help students be familiar with the real-world settings they will encounter in later research; 2) to improve students' practical skills of oral presentation and academic writing, emphasizing on the ability to pitch their work towards a variety of target audiences; 3) to encourage discussion and learning from each other via giving and receiving feedback about their work; 4) to encourage students to communicate their work with other people and build up their confidences in speaking English in public.

教学方式

This class will combine both theoretical instructions on various topics related to scientific communication as well as student-led presentations and group discussion.

中国周边安全与周边外交分析

2025秋 · POLI60044.01 · 赵卫华、祁怀高、薛松

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程主要讲授新时代（中共十八大以来）中国周边安全挑战与周边外交政策。主要教学内容包括：梳理新时代中国周边安全环境与周边外交特点；分析中国在东北亚、东南亚、南亚、中亚四个次区域（方向）面临的周边安全挑战，并讨论中国在上述四个次区域的周边外交政策；分析中国与美国、俄罗斯、印度、日本在亚洲的互动关系，以及美俄印日四个大国因素对中国周边安全和周边外交的影响；分析中美西太海权博弈、亚洲跨境非传统安全、共建“一带一路”、共同推进现代化进程、中国沿边地方政府角色等热点议题；探讨中国如何对未来5年至10年的周边外交战略进行顶层设计，以实现中国与周边国家的共同发展和繁荣。本课程将使用案例比较方法和量化统计方法，比较分析中国在周边四个次区域的安全关切与周边外交政策，量化分析中国在周边四个次区域的政治参与、经济参与、军事参与。

课程简介（英文）

The course primarily focuses on the security challenges and diplomacy of China’s neighboring regions since the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China (CPC). The main content includes: An overview of the security environment and characteristics of China's diplomacy in its neighboring regions during the new era. An analysis of the security challenges China faces in the four sub-regions of Northeast Asia, Southeast Asia, South Asia, and Central Asia, and a discussion of China’s neighboring diplomacy in these areas. An examination of China’s interactions with the U.S., Russia, India, and Japan in Asia, as well as the impact of these four major powers on China’s regional security and neighboring diplomacy. An analysis of the China-U.S. strategic competition in the Western Pacific, cross-border non-traditional security issues in Asia, the Belt and Road Initiative, the joint progress toward modernization, and the role of local governments along China’s borders, among other hot topics. A discussion on how China can design its neighboring diplomacy strategy for the next 5 to 10 years to achieve common development and prosperity with neighboring countries. This course will use case comparison methods and quantitative statistical analysis to compare and analyze China’s security concerns and foreign policies in the four sub-regions, as well as quantify China’s political, economic, and military involvement in these sub-regions.

教学目标

本课程将通过研讨式教学，在实践上帮助研究生客观认识中国在21世纪第三个十年所处的周边安全环境，在理论上帮助研究生理性探讨新时代中国如何开展周边外交，在方法上帮助研究生熟练掌握案例比较方法和量化统计方法。

教学方式

教师讲授、学生研讨、文献阅读、课外实践。

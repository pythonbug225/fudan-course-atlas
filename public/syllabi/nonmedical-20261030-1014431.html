2025秋 · PSYC60005.01 · 朱磊

课程简介（中文）

课程“心理学研究技术”拟向学生介绍心理学研究中常用的各种技术，包括相对较简单的行为实验技术，以及近年来新兴的虚拟现实、事件相关电位、核磁共振和生物反馈技术，以期通过指导学生进行借助于各种技术的实验设计，并编制相应的实验程序，帮助学生全面掌握如何将各种技术灵活应用到心理学研究中。

课程简介（英文）

The course "Psychological Research Techniques" aims to introduce students to various techniques commonly used in psychological research, including relatively simple behavioral experimental techniques, as well as emerging technologies such as virtual reality, event-related potentials, magnetic resonance imaging, and biofeedback. The goal is to guide students in designing experiments using these various techniques and developing corresponding experimental procedures, helping them fully grasp how to flexibly apply these techniques to psychological research.

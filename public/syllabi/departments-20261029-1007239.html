课程简介（中文）

课程基本内容包括三大部分：第一部分是债券估值及其风险管理，包括债券的债项特征及其结构设计、国内债券市场的发展与创新状况、利率债估值的基本原理、公司债和信用债的估值方法、债券风险管理、利率期限结构模型与估计。第二部分是利率衍生品，包括国债期货的报价与结算规则、国债期货的套保策略、国债期货的交易策略、国债期权的资产定价模型。第三部分是结构化产品，包括结构化产品的流行结构设计、简单结构化产品的估值与风险管理。

课程简介（英文）

Through this course, students will gain insights into the development and innovation of China's fixed income market. They will master the structural design features, asset pricing methodologies and risk management strategies for bond products (e.g., interest rate bonds, corporate bonds, credit bonds), partial interest rate derivatives (e.g., treasury bond futures, treasury bond options) and structured products. This knowledge will provide a solid foundation for careers in fixed income. The curriculum is divided into three core modules: Module 1: Bond Valuation and Risk Management • Characteristics and structural design of bonds； • Development and innovation in China's bond market； • Fundamental principles of interest rate bond valuation； • Valuation methods for corporate and credit bonds； • Bond risk management； • Interest rate term structure models and estimation techniques. Module 2: Interest Rate Derivatives • Quotation and settlement rules for treasury bond futures; • Hedging strategies using treasury bond futures; • Trading strategies for treasury bond futures; • Asset pricing models for treasury bond options. Module 3: Structured Products • Popular structural designs of structured products; • Valuation and risk management of basic structured products.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，学生将了解中国固定收益市场的发展与创新状况，掌握债券产品（如利率债、公司债、信用债）、部分利率衍生品（如国债期货、国债期权）、结构化产品的结构设计特征、资产定价方法及其风险管理策略，为从事固收工作奠定良好基础。

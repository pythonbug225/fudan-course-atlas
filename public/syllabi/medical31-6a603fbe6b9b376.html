课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Based on students' basic knowledge of "Organic Chemistry", "Medicinal Chemistry I" and "Pharmacology", this course further delves into the study of the development history of medicinal chemistry, the research content and tasks of medicinal chemistry, the search approaches and structural optimization methods of lead compounds, as well as the overview of new drug research and development. At the same time, based on the research accumulation of each research group in this department in different drug fields, systematically explain the origin, research ideas and the latest progress of related drug research for treating diseases in a certain field.

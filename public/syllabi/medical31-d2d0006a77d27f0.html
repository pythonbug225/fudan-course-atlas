1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 耳鼻喉科学是一门紧密学科基础和临床诊治技术更新发展，要求学生掌握耳鼻喉科学的基本理论、基本知识、基本技能，不仅培养医学生的临床能力，还培养学生的自学能力、应用知识能力和表达能力，启发学生创新性思维和探索研究的意识，注重学生职业道德素质的培养，重视双语教学，提高学生的专业外语能力的课程。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Otolaryngology is a discipline that closely integrates fundamental science with the continuous advancement of clinical diagnostic and therapeutic techniques. It requires students to master the basic theories, knowledge, and skills of otolaryngology. The curriculum not only cultivates medical students' clinical competence but also develops their self-directed learning abilities, practical application skills, and communication proficiency. It inspires innovative thinking and research awareness, emphasizes the cultivation of professional ethics, prioritizes bilingual instruction, and enhances students' specialized foreign language proficiency.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

The objective of this course is to train the students to get solid foundation of the knowledge and operational capacity, strong ability of innovation and great development potential. The internship program is the supplement of the theoretical courses.This course is designed to recognize the relationship between clinical anatomy, physiologic function and causation of diseases. Knowing the diagnosis and treatment of common otorhinolaryngology diseases.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

Through the study of this course, students are required to master the basic theories, basic knowledge and basic skills of otolaryngology, not only to cultivate the clinical ability of medical students, but also to cultivate students' self-learning ability, applied knowledge ability and expression ability, and to inspire students' innovative thinking and exploration. The teaching methods of this course includes theoretical lecture and internship.The attendance of students in theoretical courses need to be checked three times each semester.

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

一、Theoretical Course Teaching Plan 1 st Week Preface, clinical anatomy and physiology of ear, trauma of ear, disease of auricle Anatomy of the external, middle and internal ear. Physiology of hearing and equilibrium. Examination of the external ear and tympanic membrane. Investigation of Eustachian tube function. Tuning-fork test, pure tone audiometry, acoustic impedance, auditory brainstem response audiometry and vestibular function test. The clinical manifestations and management of injury of the tympanic membrane. The clinical manifestations and management of fracture of the temporal bone. 2 nd Week Acute otitis media, chronic otitis media, middle ear cholesteatoma The etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic otitis media and cholesteatoma. 3 rd Week Examination of hearing, otosclerosis, prevention and therapy of deafness The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment of otosclerosis. The etiology, clinical features, diagnosis, treatment and prevention of conductive, sensorineural and mixed deafness. 4 th Week Examination of vestibular function, Meniere disease, ear neoplasms The etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment of Ménière disease Clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of papilloma of the external auditory canal, cancer of the middle ear and tumors of lateral skull base and internal auditory canal. 5 th Week Otogenic complication, facial paralysis The diagnosis and management of otogenic complication. T he etiology, pathophysiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of peripheral facial paralysis. 6 th Week Clinical anatomy and physiology of the nose and paranasal sinuses, rhinitis Anatomical structures of external nose, nasal cavity and paranasal sinus. Distribution of blood vessels, nerves and lymph nodes in nasal cavity. Physiological functions of the nasal cavity. The diagnosis and management of rhinitis. 7 th Week Trauma of nose, epistaxis, disease of nasal septum, foreign body in the nose The diagnosis and management algorithm of the fracture of nasal bone and paranasal sinus. The clinical manifestations and treatment of deviation of the nasal septum. The etiology and treatment of epistaxis. The diagnosis and treatment of foreign bodies in nasal cavity and paranasal sinus. 8 th Week Rhinosinusitis, nasal and skull base neoplasms The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of the acute and chronic rhinosinusitis. The clinical manifestations and treatment of nasal papilloma. The clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of malignant tumors of nasal cavity and paranasal sinus. 9 th Week Clinical anatomy of pharynx, tonsillitis, adenoidal hypertrophy, periamygdalitis, pharyngeal abscess , OSAHS, pharyngeal neoplasms Composition of the pharynx. Space of the pharynx: retropharyngeal and parapharyngeal space. Lymph nodes, blood vessels and nerves of the pharynx. Physiological functions of the pharynx. The etiology, clinical manifestations, complications, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic tonsillitis. The indication and contraindication of tonsillectomy. The clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of adenoidal hypertrophy. The etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment of peritonsillar abscess. The etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of retropharyngeal abscess. The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of parapharyngeal abscess. The clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of OSAHS, nasopharyngeal fibroangioma and nasopharyngeal carcinoma. 10 th Week Clinical anatomy of the trachea, bronchi and esophagus, foreign bodies in the trachea and bronchi, foreign bodies in the esophagus Anatomical features and clinical significance of principal bronchi. The clinical significance of the four physiological strictures of the esophagus. The etiology, sorts, location, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of foreign bodies in trachea and bronchi. The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of foreign bodies of the esophagus. Complications of foreign bodies of the esophagus. 11 th Week Clinical anatomy and physiology of larynx, injuries of larynx, disease of laryngeal nerves, inflammation of larynx Cartilage framework of the larynx. Composition of laryngeal muscles. Structure of laryngeal cavity: supraglottic, glottic and infraglottic portion. Blood vessels, nerves and lymph nodes of the larynx. Anatomical features and clinical significance of the larynx in children. Physiological functions of the larynx: respiration, phonation, protection and holding breath. The etiology, clinical manifestations and treatment of contusion of the larynx. The etiology, clinical manifestations and treatment of cutting injury of the larynx. The etiology, clinical manifestations and treatment of scald, burning injury of the larynx. The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of stenosis of the larynx. The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of hysterical aphonia. The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of laryngeal paralysis. The etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of acute epiglottitis. The etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of laryngitis. The clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of acute laryngitis in children. The clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of vocal nodules. The clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of polyp of vocal cord. 12 th Week Laryngeal obstruction, tracheotomy, laryngeal neoplasms The etiology, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment of laryngeal obstruction. The clinical anatomy of trachea. Surgical indication and procedures. Postoperative care and complications. The etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations and treatment of laryngeal papilloma. The diagnosis and treatment of other benign tumors of the larynx (hemangioma, fibroma and neurofibroma). The etiology, pathology, staging, clinical manifestations, metastasis, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment of laryngeal carcinoma. 二、Demonstration Course Teaching Plan Lesson 1: Anatomy and Physiology of the Ear, Nose, and Throat Lesson 2: Otolaryngological Examination and Symptomatology Lesson 3: Otolaryngology Diseases part I (Otology) Lesson 4: Otolaryngology Diseases part II (Rhinology) Lesson 5: Otolaryngology Diseases part III (Throat + Head and Neck)

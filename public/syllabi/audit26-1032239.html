课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍中国对外经济政策与关系的形成过程、政策内容及理论基础。课程分为三个部分：首先，我们探讨中国对外经济政策的制定机制与制度框架；其次，分析贸易、投资、货币、援助和对外制裁等核心经济政策； 第三，聚焦中国与周边国家及主要大国的经济关系。课程核心问题包括：国家利益与国内政治如何界定及其演变轨迹？政治制度如何塑造中国对外经济政策？中国对外经济政策产生何种影响？

课程简介（英文）

This course offers a general introduction to the process, policies, and theories of China’s foreign economic policies and relations. The course is divided into three parts. First, we draw on the making process and institutions in China’s foreign economic policies. Second, we examine key economic policies, such as trade, investment, monetary, aid, sanctions and so on. Third, we focus on China’s economic relations with regional countries and major powers. Some of the questions we ask throughout the course are: how are national interest and domestic politics defined and how have they evolved? How do political institutions shape China’s foreign economic policies? What is impacts of China’s foreign economic policies?

教学目标

This course is to examine the policies of China’s foreign economic policies, including domestic institutions and foundations, foreign trade policy, financial policy, investment policy and currency policy, etc. The objective is to help students obtain main features and dynamics of China’s foreign economic policies, and understand China's economic relations with the rest of world.

教学方式

  1. Lecture-based learning: Professor will lead a lesson by presenting on, showing visuals of and modeling examples of a topic. While professor is presenting, students can listen, watch, take notes and copy the teacher's demonstrations.

  2.Group learning: While in their presentation teams, students can discuss subjects and learn about the perspectives of others. Teachers can assign group presentations so students can convey information to the rest of the class, ask and answer questions and interact with each other.

课程简介（中文）

本课程将理论训练与实际应用相结合，系统介绍公共管理学科的核心概念，并涵盖公共部门管理的主要方法。课程首先回顾从公共行政到公共管理的理论演进历程，引导学生探究公共管理者职责与技能的变迁。在此基础上，进一步解析公共管理关键职能领域的实用工具，培养学生批判性运用多种机制提升公共管理效能的实践能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a core course for the IMPA program aiming to familiarize students with the fundamental areas of public management and its global frontier practices. While globalization has created strong momentum to shape global reform trends and encourage diffusion of public management ideas and innovations, on-the-ground practices demonstrate much variations and context-driven adaptations. Understanding these global trends and local differences prepares students with critical analytical perspectives to evaluate real-world public sector activities and changes. This course selects some key topics of public management, provides related theoretical landscapes and debates, examines major trends of reforms and changes in global and comparative contexts, and engages students in concrete case scenarios and deliberations.

教学目标

Course Objectives Upon successful completion of this subject, students are expected to: Acquire theoretical perspectives on major issues of public management across countries; Understand the frontier reforms, changes, and developing trends of these issues from a global/comparative perspectives; Learn to critically analyze public management reforms/innovations/changes by doing case studies empowered by the study results; Learn to apply what’s learned in class to analyze real-life issues in students’ own agencies and countries.

教学方式

This course mainly includes thematic lectures, case analyses, group discussions, etc.

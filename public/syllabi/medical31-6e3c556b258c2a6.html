1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程是为期两个学期的微积分课程中的第二学期内容，主要涵盖第6至第15章，重点包括积分及其应用、微分方程、反常积分以及多元微积分。本课程将首先介绍不定积分和定积分，随后讲授微积分基本定理、换元积分法和分部积分法，并介绍数值积分与反常积分。最后，我们将学习多元微积分的相关内容。由于本课程面向非数学专业学生，因此教学重点不在于繁复的计算技巧，而在于如何运用微积分方法解决实际问题。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | This is the second-term course of calculus which covers two terms. In this course, we will mainly learn chapter 6-15 that mainly focused on integral calculus with its applications, differential equations, improper integrals, and calculus of several variables. We will first introduce indefinite integral and the definite integral. Then we will introduce the fundamental theorem of calculus, integrgration by u-substitution, and integration by Parts. We also introduce numerical integration and improper integrals. Finally, we introduce calculus of several variables.Since this course is oriented for students not majoring in mathematics, so, our focus is not put on calculation capability but on how to apply calculus to solve real problems.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Calculus is a branch of mathematics focused on limits, functions, derivatives, integrals, and infinite series. It has two major branches, differential calculus and integral calculus , which are related by the fundamental theorem of calculus. Calculus is the study of change and its applications in equation. So, calculus is a basic algebra skill that students should have to master in order to solve problems in various domains. In this course, we will introduce basic concepts and skills of calculus and play the emphasis on training students the capability of solving problems using calculus in fields such as business, economy and social science.

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

微积分课程分为上，下两个学期，本课程为微积分（下），是教材《Calculus》（James Steward）中的后半部分，分别是： Chapter5&6: INTEGRALS The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, The Substitution Rule, Areas between Curves, Volumes, Chapter 7: TECHNIQUES OF INTEGRATION Integration by Parts, Trigonometric Integrals, Trigonometric Substitution, Integration of Rational Functions by Partial Fractions, Strategy for Integration, Approximate Integration, Improper Integrals. Chapter 8: FURTHER APPLICATIONS OF INTEGRATION Arc Length, Area of a Surface of Revolution. Chapter 9: DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS Separable Equations, Linear Equations. Chapter 10: PARAMETRIC EQUATIONS AND POLAR COORDINATES Curves Defined by Parametric Equations, Calculus with Parametric Curves, Polar Coordinates. Chapter 11: INFINITE SEQUENCES AND SERIES Sequences, Series, The Comparison Tests Power Series Taylor and Maclaurin Series. Chapter 12: VECTORS AND THE GEOMETRY OF SPACE The Dot Product, The Cross Product, Equations of Lines and Planes. Chapter 13: VECTOR FUNCTIONS Derivatives and Integrals of Vector Functions, Arc Length and Curvature. Chapter 14: PARTIAL DERIVATIVES Partial Derivatives, The Chain Rule, Directional Derivatives and the Gradient Vector. Chapter 15: MULTIPLE INTEGRALS Double Integrals over Rectangles, Iterated Integrals, Double Integrals over General Regions.

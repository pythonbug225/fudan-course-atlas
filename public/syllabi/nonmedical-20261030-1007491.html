课程简介（中文）

本课程主要探讨几何分析领域若干重要研究对象，涉及调和函数、Laplace 算子特征函数、特征值， 热核的性质及其估计， 完备黎曼流形的几何拓扑等。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on several important objects of research in the field of geometric analysis, including harmonic functions, eigenfunctions and eigenvalues of the Laplace operator, properties and estimates of the heat kernel, and the geometric topology of complete Riemannian manifolds.

教学目标

掌握几何分析研究的基本方法和技巧。 This course focuses on several important objects of research in the field of geometric analysis, including harmonic functions, eigenfunctions and eigenvalues of the Laplace operator, properties and estimates of the heat kernel, and the geometric topology of complete Riemannian manifolds.

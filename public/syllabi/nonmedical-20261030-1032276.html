课程简介（中文）

该课程向外国学生介绍全球化背景下中国对外传播的理念和实践

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces students to the background of China’s political communication and provides overview of China’s international communication in a global context. By the end of the course, the students will have acquired a broad perspective of China’s international communication and gain familiarity with its political consequences in globalization. Four general topics will be explored: understanding China’s media system; China’s international communication system; Chinese nationalism in international conflicts; China’s public diplomacy and global image. For each topic, the course will be conducted with several lectures and seminars. The students are required to do presentations about book review. The final project is required to complete a term paper. Students can choose a topic from the content of the course to examine Chinese international communication.

教学目标

让外国学生了解中国对外传播的基本理念和实践，对当前世界上政治传播和国际传播的案例和现象能用课程观点进行分析，并且进行小组讨论，能够形成良好的氛围，团队协作，对对外传播中的一些重要议题进行深入的探讨，并且能够形成自己的观点

教学方式

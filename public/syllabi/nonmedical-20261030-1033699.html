课程简介（中文）

LIVE stands for “Learning through Integrated and Vicarious Experience” and is central to our mission of delivering both rigor and relevance in our curriculum. The goal of LIVE Learning is to help our students put classroom theory into practice through curriculum that links the classroom with the boardroom. Put simply, we believe that education today is not complete until it has been encapsulated in the real-world.

教学目标

This course fosters innovation through the application of modern investment tools in the start-up, venture capital, and private equity space. We engage investment firms with small groups of students for a 2-week project, during which our students provide investment analysis or consulting services. These may take the form of due-diligence, quantitative filtering and/or qualitative market assessment, or acceleration services aimed at helping a target investment mature more quickly.

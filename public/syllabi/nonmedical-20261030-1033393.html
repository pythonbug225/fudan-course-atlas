New Methods in Signal Processing

2026春 · EST70052.01 · 王斌

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

结合长期的研究活动，阐述并评论信息处理学科发展中的若干重大问题，分析和研讨自适应信号处理、混合信号盲分离、基于深度学习的信号处理新进展等前沿研究和进展。另外，介绍信号分解/合成理论、从不完整数据中估计原始信号的方法、几种盲信号恢复技术、线性多分辨率分析理论及其应用、非线性金字塔分析理论及其应用、信号的分形理论及其应用、信号的混沌理论及其潜在应用等。

课程简介（英文）

Combining long-term research activities, this paper elaborates on and comments on several major issues in the development of information processing, analyzing and discussing cutting-edge research and advances in adaptive signal processing, hybrid signal blind separation, and new developments in deep learning-based signal processing. Additionally, it introduces signal decomposition/synthesis theory, methods for estimating original signals from incomplete data, several blind signal recovery techniques, linear multi-resolution analysis theory and its applications, nonlinear pyramid analysis theory and its applications, fractal theory of signals and its applications, as well as chaotic theory of signals and its potential applications.

教学方式

First, the lead instructor will provide an overview of the course and a summary of recent developments in the research field; subsequently, the class will focus on seminar discussions, supplemented by the instructor's explanations and feedback.

教学内容与进度安排

主要内容及基本要求： 第一部分：自适应信号处理理论深入研究混合信号的盲分离，以及信道盲均衡的理论与应用；此外，探讨非线性混合情况下若干问题的解决方案。 第二部分：信号恢复理论研究信号恢复领域中的若干新兴研究热点。首先，研究不完备数据条件下的信号重构与恢复；其次，针对当前热点超分辨率重构问题进行研讨；最后，探讨盲信号恢复问题。 第三部分：多分辨率分析理论系统研究多尺度分析、多分辨率分析、线性与非线性金字塔理论，包括形态小波、形态金字塔、金字塔的条件及理想的恢复等；研讨这些理论在图像分析中的应用。 第四部分：非线性信号处理理论系统研究非线性信号处理中的若干热点问题。例如，研究分形分析的理论及其在信号处理中的应用，包括多分形分析；研究混沌理论及其在信号处理中的应用，利用混沌的基本理论、混沌的形成机制、混沌同步等，探讨混沌在脑电图（EEG）分析中的应用。 第五部分：基于深度学习的信号处理新方法重点调研和讨论基于深度学习的信号处理理论与方法的新发展，并结合各位同学的研究方向开展较为深入细致的研讨。 Main content and basic requirements: Part One: Adaptive Signal Processing Theory Conduct in-depth research on blind source separation of mixed signals and the theory and applications of blind equalization in communication channels. Additionally, explore solutions to several problems arising in nonlinear mixing scenarios. Part Two: Signal Recovery Theory Investigate emerging research topics in the field of signal recovery. First, study signal reconstruction and recovery under incomplete data conditions. Second, discuss current hot topics such as super-resolution reconstruction. Finally, examine blind signal recovery problems. Part Three: Multiresolution Analysis Theory Systematically study multiscalar analysis, multiresolution analysis, and linear and nonlinear pyramid theories—including morphological wavelets, morphological pyramids, conditions for pyramids, and ideal reconstruction. Explore the application of these theories in image analysis. Part Four: Nonlinear Signal Processing Theory Systematically investigate several key issues in nonlinear signal processing. For example, study the theory of fractal analysis and its applications in signal processing, including multifractal analysis; explore chaos theory and its applications in signal processing by utilizing fundamental principles of chaos, mechanisms of chaos generation, chaotic synchronization, etc., with a focus on applications in electroencephalogram (EEG) analysis. Part Five: New Approaches to Signal Processing Based on Deep Learning Focus on researching and discussing recent advancements in deep learning-based signal processing theories and methodologies, and conduct in-depth, detailed discussions tailored to each student's research focus.

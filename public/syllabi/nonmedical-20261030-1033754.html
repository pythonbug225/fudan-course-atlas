课程简介（中文）

政策分析是为知情决策做准备的活动。政策分析这一技艺始于对问题的理解，进而审视各种替代性解决方案，包括考虑支持或反对不同政策选项的证据。当一项政策被选定并实施后，我们运用项目评估的工具来衡量这些政策的有效性，而这一评估结果又会反馈到后续的政策分析环节中。 政策是关于政府提供公共产品与服务的选择。这种选择本身正是本课程的出发点。课程首先探讨政府干预市场的经济学原理，我们将讨论效率的不同概念及其来源，重点关注经济效率。政策分析者经常需要评估政策选择对公平性的影响，本课程将涵盖公平的多种概念，并探讨政策选择如何影响这些公平维度。课程还将深入探讨税收政策以及不同收入方案的评估分析方法。课程最后将对两个政策领域的研究证据进行综述。

课程简介（英文）

Policy Analysis is the activity of preparing for informed public decisions. The craft of policy analysis begins with understanding a problem, and proceeds with the review of alternative remedies, including the consideration of evidence for and against policy alternatives. After a policy has been selected and implemented we use the tools of program evaluation to assess the effectiveness of these policies, and this evaluation feeds back into subsequent rounds of policy analysis. Policies are choices concerning the government provision of public goods and services. That choice itself (to do something as opposed nothing) is the departure point for this course, which begins with the economic rationale for government intervention in markets. We will discuss different notions of efficiency and where they come from with primary attention on economic efficiency. Policy analysts are often tasked with the assessing the impacts of policy choices on fairness and the course will cover many of these notions and how they are affected by policy choices. The course includes an extended look at tax policy and how different revenue alternatives are analyzed. The course includes a review of the state of evidence in two policy domains (most likely one example from social welfare policy and one from trade policy).

教学目标

By the end of the semester, students should be able to: Explain rationales for government intervention in the private market and identify the various roles of the government in the market. Apply economic concepts to public policy issues. Understand how policy choices affect different notions of fairness. Demonstrate how to evaluate policy alternatives.

教学方式

Requirements: This is a doctoral seminar and thus problem sets and practice problems are not a graded part of class work. Course grades will be based upon an exam (30%) an article presentation (10%) scholarly research paper presentations (10%) and a scholarly research paper (50%). ARTICLE PRESENTATION ASSIGNMENT Students will select an article of policy analysis or program evaluation of the public policy issue that they have selected for their term paper assignment. Students will present key lessons from the article to the class, and stand for questions. SCHOLARLY RESEARCH PAPER ASSIGNMENT Students will select a policy topic of interest to them and review the existing evidence on that topic. The paper will include a problem statement, a research question, discussion of an optimal design to answer that research question and a critical literature review (identifying gaps and shortcomings) showing how the policy has been analyzed to date. Conclusions should be focused on what the state of knowledge is and what decision-makers can be confident about what we know, and do not know, about this policy domain. The class presentation of this paper is an essential part of the assignment, however, the final paper need not be complete at the time of the presentation. Requirements The paper will be the original work of the student. The paper will be between 3,000 and 5,000 words in length. For the evaluation portion of the paper you must rely upon peer reviewed journal articles for your evidence. The class presentation of this paper is an essential part of the assignment.

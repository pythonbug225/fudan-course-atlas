1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 循证医学核心思想是医务人员应该认真地、明智地、深思熟虑地运用在临床研究得到的最新、最有利的科学研究信息来诊治病人。它是一种理念，将最佳证据、医生的临床经验以及病人的需求和价值观三者结合起来。实践循证医学的步骤包括提出问题、检索证据、评价证据、应用证据和效果评价。主要教学内容分为10个单元：(1)绪论;(2)临床证据资料及检索方法;(3)疾病诊断证据的分析评价;(4)疾病治疗证据的分析评价;(5) 病因及危险因素的分析评价; (6)疾病预后证据的分析评价; (7) 生命质量的分析评价;(8) 药物不良反应的分析评价;(9) 系统综述与Meta分析; (10)临床实践指南。 在教学中，将循证医学的学科特点与科学方法论、医患共决策、学科发展趋势结合起来，围绕科学思辨与人文关怀这一思政主题，提高学生自主学习能力，培养医学生循证医学临床应用能力，形成“以病人为中心”的职业素养和“基于证据决策”的学术修养，最终提高临床医学岗位胜任力的核心能力。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The core of evidence-based medicine (EBM) is that medical professionals should diagnose and treat patients by conscientiously, judiciously, and thoughtfully applying the latest and most robust scientific evidence derived from clinical research. As a guiding principle, EBM integrates three essential elements: the best available evidence, the clinician’s clinical expertise, and the patient’s needs and values. The steps to practice EBM consist of five phases: formulating a clinical question, searching for evidence, critically appraising evidence, applying evidence in clinical practice, and evaluating the outcomes of application. The main teaching content is divided into 10 modules: Introduction to Evidence-Based Medicine Clinical evidence resources and literature retrieval methods Critical appraisal of evidence for disease diagnosis Critical appraisal of evidence for disease treatment Critical appraisal of evidence for etiology and risk factors Critical appraisal of evidence for disease prognosis Assessment of health-related quality of life Critical appraisal of evidence for adverse drug reactions Systematic review and Meta-analysis Clinical practice guidelines In the teaching process, the disciplinary characteristics of EBM will be integrated with scientific methodology, shared decision-making between physicians and patients, and disciplinary development trends. Centering on the ideological and political theme of scientific thinking and humanistic care, the course aims to enhance students’ autonomous learning abilities, cultivate their competence in the clinical application of EBM, foster their professional ethics of patient-centered care and academic attainments of evidence-based decision-making, and ultimately improve their core competencies required for clinical medical practice.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

循证医学极大改变了21世纪医疗实践模式。通过本课程学习，旨在让临床医学专业学生树立正确循证医学观念，熟练掌握其基本理论与方法，能精准检索、评价和综合不断更新的临床证据，切实提升循证医学临床应用能力，具备科学临床决策能力与扎实临床科研功底，为后续临床工作筑牢根基。同时，课程深度融入思政元素，强化人文医学教育与医德医风教育，引导学生将科学思辨巧妙融入人文关怀，使二者深度交融并内化为个人修养。在临床决策中，通过科学、个性化及医患共决策的方式，更好地救助病人，致力于培养一批守初心、铸信念、人文底蕴深厚、心怀大爱的创新型卓越医学人才。

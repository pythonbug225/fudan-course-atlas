课程简介（中文）

本课程向学生介绍社会创新和社会创业的策略与流程。它为学生提供机会，关注中国的社会问题，如扶贫与经济增长、环境可持续性以及教育等。课程为学生提供评估和分析社会创新与创业举措的工具，并让他们有机会讨论并开发自己在这个领域的潜在举措。在整个课程中，学生将通过案例研究、最佳实践分析和相关阅读来审视社会创新。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces students to the strategies and processes of social innovation and social entrepreneurship. It provides students the opportunity to focus on the social problems in China, such as poverty alleviation and economic growth, environmental sustainability, and education. The course provides students with tools to assess and analyze socially innovative and entrepreneurial initiatives, and gives them an opportunity to discuss and develop their own prospective initiatives within this field. Throughout the course students will examine social innovation through case studies, best practice analyses, and relevant readings.

教学目标

Course objectives: Develop a better understanding of the social problems in China and around the globe, such as poverty alleviation and economic growth, environmental sustainability, and education. Develop skills to analyze social problems and develop product or service solutions to solve the problems. Develop social innovation leadership

教学方式

Lectures, case study, project based learning.

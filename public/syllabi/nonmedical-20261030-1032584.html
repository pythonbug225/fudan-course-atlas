课程简介（中文）

本课程专为研究生设计，精选12篇涵盖诗歌、短篇小说、散文及思辨性文本的经典作品，旨在通过深度解读与跨文本分析，助力学员提升文学鉴赏能力与批判性思维，深入洞悉语言背后的社会、人性与文化内核。课程将结合成人学员的人生阅历与学习需求，采用文本细读、主题研讨等方式，引导学员挖掘作品的艺术特色与思想深度，在提升英语文学素养的同时，实现对自我、社会及文化的多维思考。

课程简介（英文）

This course is specifically designed for postgraduates. It carefully selects 12 classic works covering poetry, short stories, essays, and speculative texts. Aimed at enhancing students' literary appreciation ability and critical thinking through in-depth interpretation and cross-textual analysis, it helps them gain profound insights into the social, humanistic, and cultural cores behind the language. Integrating the life experience and learning needs of adult students, the course adopts methods such as close reading and thematic discussion to guide students in exploring the artistic characteristics and ideological depth of the works. While improving their English literary literacy, it enables them to achieve multi-dimensional thinking about themselves, society, and culture.

教学目标

立足MEM专业的办学定位与人才培养要求，结合学生认知特点，本课程教学目标如下：价值引领层面，引导学生通过文本解读树立正确文化观与价值观，增强文化自信与包容度；知识传授层面，助力学生系统掌握所选诗歌、小说等文本的核心主旨、艺术特色及背后的社会文化与人性议题；能力培养层面，重点提升学生文本细读、跨文本分析及批判性思维能力，强化英语文学鉴赏素养，为专业发展与终身学习奠定坚实基础。

教学方式

本课程以“文本为核心、互动为导向”，融合多种教学方法。核心采用文本细读，引导学员逐句剖析诗歌的韵律、小说的叙事技巧及散文的思辨逻辑；组织课堂主题研讨，围绕人性、社会、文化等核心议题，结合学员人生阅历展开深度交流。

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture 1: False Happiness and Social Myths Texts: Cinderella – Anne Sexton Miss Brill – Katherine Mansfield Core Focus: Happiness as performance Stories society tells us to survive Discussion Question: Is illusion a weakness—or a survival strategy? Lecture 2: Work, Loss, and Indifference Texts: Out, Out— – Robert Frost What Work Is – Philip Levine Core Focus: Work as necessity vs. meaning Society’s emotional distance from suffering Discussion Question: Does work give dignity, or does dignity come from somewhere else? Lecture 3: Busyness, Productivity, and Modern Traps Texts: The Busy Trap The Unknown Citizen – W. H. Auden Core Focus: Measurement replacing meaning Efficiency as a moral value Discussion Question: Can a life be successful and empty at the same time? Lecture 4: Ideals, Lies, and Collective Myths Texts: Let America Be America Again – Langston Hughes Dulce et Decorum Est – Wilfred Owen Core Focus: National ideals vs. lived reality Moral lies told to sustain systems Discussion Question: Why do societies need beautiful lies? Lecture 5: Authority, Waiting, and Self-Imprisonment Text: Before the Law – Franz Kafka Core Focus: Power without force Psychological obedience Discussion Question: Who is really responsible for the man’s failure—the gatekeeper or himself? Lecture 6: Communication, Silence, and Gender Texts: Hills Like White Elephants – Ernest Hemingway Language, Lies and Sex: Why It Is Hard for Men and Women to Talk to Each Other? Core Focus: What is not said Power in conversation Discussion Question: Is silence sometimes more violent than speech? Lecture 7: Breakdown, Conflict, and the Edge of Meaning Text: Popular Mechanics – Raymond Carver Core Focus: Emotional collapse Language stripped to the bone Final Discussion Question: What happens when language can no longer hold human relationships together?

教学参考资料

工程管理硕士（MEM）学员均具备丰富工作经验，其认知特点、学习习惯与其他类别人群存在显著差异。因此，讲义选编需紧密结合学员的行业实践背景、问题导向型学习需求及已有知识储备，避免照搬通用教学材料，唯有如此，才能更精准地匹配学员学习痛点，提升教学的针对性与实效性，最终达成理想的教学效果。所选材料都是经典文学读物，思想高雅，内容深刻。

《新闻传播经典著作选读》（上）

2025秋 · JCM60022.01 · 孙玮、谢静、潘霁、周葆华

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

这门课是在人文、社科的大背景下，通过经典文本的细读与分析，描画新闻传播学的学科理论体系的发展历史和知识谱系，为博士阶段的研究打下较为全面与扎实的基础。通过设置本学科的核心议题构成课程的各个板块，既呈现了新闻传播学的学科体系的基本样貌，也搭建了跨学科对话的桥梁，显示出新闻传播学研究的开放性。在此基础上，根据新闻传播实践和理论的演进，适当增加数字时代的前沿成果，以跟踪学科的最新发展。

课程简介（英文）

This course is conducted in the context of humanities and social sciences. Through reading and analysis of classic texts, it depicts the development history and knowledge spectrum of the theoretical system of journalism and communication, laying a comprehensive and solid foundation for doctoral research. By setting the core topics of this discipline to form the various sections of the curriculum, it not only presents the basic appearance of the disciplinary system of journalism and communication, but also builds a bridge for interdisciplinary dialogue, demonstrating the openness of research in journalism and communication. On this basis, according to the evolution of news communication practice and theory, it is appropriate to add cutting-edge achievements in the digital age to track the latest developments in the discipline.

教学方式

本课程由相关领域的资深教师领衔，分三个板块进行课程建设并负责开课讲授。分别由孙玮教授、谢静教授、潘霁教授和周葆华教授负责。

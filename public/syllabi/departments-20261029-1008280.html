实证资产定价

2025秋 · BA70003.01 · 李隽业

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程严谨、量化且以研究为导向，要求学生熟悉套利、随机贴现因子、市场完备性及资产估值等主题。课程内容涵盖投资组合理论、经典资产定价理论、线性因子模型检验、时间序列回报可预测性、预期回报横截面分析以及基于消费的资产定价模型。此外，课程还将探讨机器学习在资产定价领域的最新进展，以及宏观金融模型的估计与检验方法。学生需投入大量时间研读前沿学术论文并进行专题汇报。

课程简介（英文）

The course is rigorous, quantitative, and research-oriented and assumes that students are familiar with those topics on arbitrage, stochastic discount factors, market completeness, and valuation. The course covers the topics such as portfolio theory, classic asset pricing theory, testing linear factor models, time-series return predictability, the cross-section of expected returns, and consumption-based asset pricing models. Furthermore, it also introduces the latest developments on machine learning in asset pricing and estimating and testing macro-finance models. Students are expected to devote a considerable time to reading pioneering research papers and making presentations.

教学目标

The course is research-oriented and will introduce the key methods and frontier topics in empirical asset pricing. After this course, the students should understand the main econometric methods, the key research questions, and the current frontier topics in asset pricing, and be able to choose their own research fields and/or topics.

教学方式

以授课为主，辅以学生课堂报告及课后作业。

教学内容与进度安排

Topic 1 Portfolio Theory and Asset Allocation Topic 2 Stochastic Discount Factor and Equivalence Theorem Topic 3 Basic Consumption-based Asset Pricing Models and Equity Premium Puzzle Topic 4 Advanced Consumption-based Asset Pricing Model Topic 5 Expected Returns in Time Series Topic 6 GMM and MLE Topic 7 Econometrics of Linear Factor Models Topic 8 Expected Returns in Cross Section Topic 9 Machine Learning and Asset Pricing I Topic 10 Machine Learning and Asset Pricing II

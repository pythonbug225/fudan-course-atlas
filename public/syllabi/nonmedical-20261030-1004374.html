课程简介（中文）

本英语口译课程专为非英语专业学生设计，旨在提升其语言能力并掌握实用的口译技能。课程从基础概念入手，逐步深入到交替传译、接续传译及初级同声传译的强化训练，这些技能高度适用于广泛的学术与社会场景。课程将理论教学与沉浸式实践相结合，涵盖学术研讨会、文化交流活动、非正式商务会议等模拟场景。通过构架式小组练习、案例分析及个人实践，可培养学生不同语言间进行精准意义转换的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This English Interpretation course is tailored for non-English majors seeking to enhance their language proficiency and acquire practical interpretation skills. The curriculum commences with fundamental concepts, gradually progressing to in-depth training in alternative interpretation, consecutive interpretation and primary simultaneous interpretation that are highly applicable to a wide range of academic and social contexts. The course integrates theoretical instruction with immersive practical sessions, featuring simulated scenarios such as academic seminars, cultural exchange activities and informal business meetings. Through structured group exercises, case analyses and individual practice, students will develop the proficiency to convey meaning precisely between languages.

教学目标

Students will systematically master core techniques, including efficient note-taking methodologies, rapid semantic conversion strategies, and the ability to maintain linguistic accuracy under immense time pressure. Upon completion, learners will be well-equipped to navigate multilingual communication effectively, enhancing their competitiveness in both academic pursuits and future professional endeavors. 学生将系统掌握核心技巧，包括高效笔记法、快速语义转换策略，以及在高强度时间压力下保持语言准确性的能力。课程结束后，学习者将具备有效应对多语言沟通的能力，从而在学术追求与未来职业发展中提升竞争力。

教学方式

讲授为主；课堂学习以教师讲授为主，并辅以相关师生互动以及学生实操练习。

教学内容与进度安排

This is an optional course designed for a 2-period weekly class over a 16-week semester. Each week the interpreting teacher focuses on a specific theme related to etiquette, diplomacy, economy and trade, education, culture, environment, society and so on. The first week: An introduction to interpreting (consecutive interpreting and simultaneous interpreting) in order to enable students to understand interpreting. The second week: Learning etiquette and interpreting skills in order to make students learn the knowledge of etiquette and master interpreting skills of listening. The third week: Learning ceremonial speech and diplomacy /diplomatic relations, and interpreting practice in order to make students learn the knowledge of diplomatic relations and master interpreting skills of working memory. The fourth week: Learning economic terminologies and practicing consecutive interpreting in order to make students learn the knowledge of economy and master interpreting skills of long term memory and short term memory. The fifth week: Learning economic terminologies and practicing consecutive interpreting in order to make students learn the knowledge of economy and master interpreting skills of transferring information I. The sixth week: Learning economic terminologies and practicing consecutive interpreting in order to make students learn the knowledge of economy and master interpreting skills of transferring information II. The seventh week: Model negotiation interpreting practice. The eighth week: Learning educational terminologies and practicing education passages in order to make students learn the knowledge of education and master interpreting skills of note taking I. The ninth week: Learning educational terminologies and practicing education passages in order to make students learn the knowledge of education and master interpreting skills of note taking II. The tenth week: Learning cultural terminologies and practicing educational passages in order to make students learn the knowledge of culture and master interpreting skills of output I. The eleventh week: practicing some cultural passages in the way of consecutive interpreting. The twelfth week: Learning environmental and tourist terminologies and practicing environmental and tourist passages in order to make students learn the knowledge of environment and tourism and master interpreting skills of output II. The thirteenth week: Learning terminologies on society and practicing some passages in this filed in the way of consecutive interpreting in order to make students learn the knowledge of society and master interpreting skills of expression I. The fourteenth week: Learning political terminologies and practising some passages in this field in order to make students learn the knowledge of politics and master interpreting skills of expression II. The fifteenth week: Learning the terminologies of sports and games and practising some passages in this field in order to make students learn the knowledge of sports and games and master interpreting products. The sixteenth week: course review; Q & A

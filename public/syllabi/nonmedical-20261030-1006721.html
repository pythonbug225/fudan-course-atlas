课程简介（中文）

经典力学是描述从点粒子到行星等自然物体运动的学科。经典力学的基础是由牛顿奠定的。运动定律后来由欧拉、拉格朗日、泊松、汉密尔顿、雅可比等人在一个更优雅的几何框架中重新表述。用几何方法研究力学的一个概念上的优点是数学形式主义的明显普遍性。此外，它还提供了电动力学、广义相对论和量子力学蓬勃发展的基本语言。本课程针对数学专业的本科生，将介绍力学的基本概念，并以泊松几何的形式来表述。所涵盖的主题，如在续篇中详细介绍，包括：辛和泊松几何；变分原理;Hamilton-Jacobi方程和Euler-Lagrange方程；对称性和守恒定律。

课程简介（英文）

Classical mechanics is the discipline that describes the motion of natural objects, from point particles to planets. The foundation of classical mechanics was laid down by Newton. The laws of motion have later been reformulated in a more elegant geometric framework by Euler, Lagrange, Poisson, Hamilton, Jacobi, and others. A conceptual advantage of the geometric approach to mechanics is the manifest universality of the mathematical formalism. Moreover, it provides the basic language from which electrodynamics, general relativity and quantum mechanics flourished. This course, aimed at undergraduates in mathematics, will introduce the basic notions of mechanics, formulated in terms of Poisson geometry. The topics covered, as detailed in the continuation, include: symplectic and Poisson geometry; variational principles; Hamilton-Jacobi equations and Euler-Lagrange equations; symmetries and conservation laws.

教学目标

The course is oriented to provide practical skills to perform explicit computations. The objective is to understand Poisson geometry and its significance to classical mechanics and symmetries, and to gain the ability to derive and solve equations of motions in both Hamiltonian and Lagrangian formalism. Additionally, the students are expected to understand the concept of symmetries and conservation laws.

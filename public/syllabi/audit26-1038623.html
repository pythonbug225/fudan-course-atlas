课程简介（中文）

作为西方世界最具影响力的国家之一，美国的国内政治与对外政策决策深刻塑造着全球格局。本课程系统梳理美国政治的核心理论、制度演变与关键议题，并深入探讨国内政治动态如何塑造其对外政策。课程内容涵盖美国政治制度的基础架构、政党竞争与国家治理、政治极化的根源与影响、选举制度的运作逻辑，以及公众舆论在外交决策中的作用。同时，课程聚焦美国大战略的形成机制，并结合当代案例，分析中美关系的演变与挑战。

课程简介（英文）

The United States is one of the most important countries in the western world, and its domestic politics and foreign policy decisions have far-reaching global consequences. This course provides an overview of key scholarly works and major public debates on American domestic politics and examines how internal political dynamics influence U.S. foreign policymaking. Topics include foundational theories and the evolution of U.S. political institutions, party politics, governance, political polarization, electoral processes, American grand strategy, the role of public opinion in foreign policy, and U.S.–China relations.

教学目标

    This course is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the structure and function of American political institutions, the processes through which U.S. foreign policy is formulated and implemented, and the dynamics of the United States’ relationships with other countries and international organizations.

    In addition to acquiring factual knowledge, students will develop analytical skills by applying major theoretical frameworks from political science and international relations to evaluate the motivations, strategies, and outcomes of U.S. political behavior both at home and abroad. By the end of the course, students will be equipped to critically assess the internal logic of U.S. policymaking and understand the far-reaching consequences of American political decisions in a global context.

教学方式

Lectures, presentations, and group discussions

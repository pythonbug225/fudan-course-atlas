1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 组织胚胎学包括组织学和胚胎学两门既有联系又相互独立的科学。其中组织学是研究机体微细结构及其功能的科学；胚胎学则是研究个体发生及其机制的科学，主要探讨一个受精卵如何发育成一个新个体的过程，尤其专注于先天畸形的形成原因及过程。本课程的教学目的是使学生掌握人体各种细胞、组织及主要器官的微细结构及其相应功能；掌握人体胚胎的早期发生和主要器官的发生、发展规律。从而为学习其它基础医学课程（如生理、病理）与临床医学课程（如内科、外科、妇产科、儿科）奠定扎实基础。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Histology & Embryology is composed of both Histology and Embryology that are independent subjects but closely related to each other. Histology focuses on the study of micro- and ultra-structure of human body observed under light and electron microscope, and also on their relevant physiological functions. Embryology concentrates in the study of embryonic developmental process and relevant mechanisms by which birth defects occur.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

The aims of this curriculum are firstly to understand both micro- and ultra-structure of various kinds of cells and tissues in human body and their relevant physiological functions, and secondly to understand the early developmental process in human embryos and formation of main organs, as well as their developmental regulations. Thus this curriculum provides a solid basis for the pursuit of other basic medical curriculums (e.g., physiology and pathology) and clinical medical curriculums (e.g., internal medicine, surgery, obstetrics & gynaecology, and pediatrics).

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

It is small teaching course, with the combination of theoretical and practical contents. The practical course includes the observation of tissue sections under light microscope and following drawing of what you observed, and also supplements with the active response to raised questions, teamwork for the topic discussion and real-time quiz.

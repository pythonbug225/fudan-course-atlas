课程简介（中文）

课程描述：​​ 本课程将理论与实践紧密结合，旨在全面提升英语公共演讲能力。 ​前半学期重点：​​ 深入分析专家演讲（包括TED Talks及毕业致辞），强化批判性听力与阅读训练，系统掌握有稿演讲与即兴演讲的核心技巧。 ​后半学期重点：​​ 聚焦学术演讲。学生将开展跨学科研究，运用人工智能进行修辞分析，整合各类文献资料，并构建严谨的叙事结构。 ​课程核心培养目标：​​ 贯穿始终强调的是批判性思维、跨文化沟通能力，以及融入全球视角的说服性叙事技巧。

课程简介（英文）

Course Description:​​ This course integrates theoretical frameworks with intensive practice to advance English public speaking proficiency. The first half focuses on analyzing expert speeches (TED Talks, commencement addresses), critical listening/reading exercises, and mastering techniques for prepared/impromptu speaking. The second half emphasizes academic presentations: students conduct interdisciplinary research, utilize AI for rhetorical analysis, synthesize sources, and structure narratives. Emphasis is placed on critical thinking, cross-cultural communication, and persuasive storytelling with global perspectives.

教学目标

Course Objectives:​​ Demonstrate confidence and effectiveness in English public speaking across diverse contexts. Develop audience-centered delivery techniques to engage listeners while refining self-expression. Design, adapt, and deliver tailored speeches (prepared/impromptu) for specific rhetorical situations.

教学方式

Teaching Methodology:​​ Theory Application: Study rhetorical principles Modeled Practice: Deconstruct exemplar speeches Speech Crafting: Draft/revise scripts with feedback Performance Training: Rehearse prepared/impromptu speeches Academic Presentation: Research-based IMRAD talks

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule:​​ Week 1: Orientation: Essential tools for impactful speaking Week 2: Text analysis "Why we speak to inform" + quiz Week 3: Informative speech analysis + rehearsal lab Week 4: Text analysis "How we speak to persuade" + quiz Week 5: Monroe’s Motivated Sequence + persuasive speech rehearsal Week 6: Midterm Assessment 1: Prepared Speech Recital (5 mins) Week 7: Midterm Assessment 2: Impromptu Speech (3 mins) Week 8: Research Structure: IMRAD format + literature review Week 9: Academic Presentations: Effective PPT design Week 10: Script Development: Outline drafting/revision Week 11: One-on-One Conferences Weeks 12-16: Term Project development & presentations

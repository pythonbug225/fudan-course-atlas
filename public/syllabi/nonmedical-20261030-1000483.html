课程简介（中文）

材料力学实验课程是一门专业基础课。‌旨在通过实验环节加深学生对材料力学理论知识的理解，‌并培养其实验技能和分析能力，‌为后续的专业课程学习和职业发展奠定坚实的力学基础。课程涵盖以下内容：1. 材料力学性能的测试方法，‌包括材料的拉伸、‌压缩、‌剪切、‌扭转和弯曲等基本力学性能的测试。‌2. 实验应力分析的基本原理和技术，‌涉及应力状态的测定和分析方法。‌3. 组合变形下杆件的强度计算。4. 压杆的稳定性概念，‌轴向受压杆的临界力和临界应力的计算。5. 低碳钢和灰口铁等常见材料的基本力学性能及其测试方法。

课程简介（英文）

The material mechanics experimental course is a fundamental course in the field. The aim is to deepen students' understanding of material mechanics theory through experimental activities, and cultivate their experimental skills and analytical abilities, laying a solid foundation in mechanics for subsequent professional course learning and career development. The course covers the following content: 1 The testing methods for the mechanical properties of materials, including the testing of basic mechanical properties such as tension, compression, shear, torsion, and bending of materials. 2. The basic principles and techniques of experimental stress analysis, involving the determination and analysis methods of stress states. 3. Strength calculation of rods under combined deformation. 4. The concept of stability of compression bars, the calculation of critical force and critical stress of axial compression bars. 5. Basic mechanical properties and testing methods of common materials such as low carbon steel and gray iron.

教学目标

掌握机械测量、电阻应变测量和应力分析方法。 提高学生的动手能力，理论联系实际工程问题的能力。

英语新闻写作

2026春 · MJC70013.01 · 高永伟

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程面向具备基础英语能力的学习者，系统训练英语新闻写作技能。课程内容涵盖新闻体裁与结构（如倒金字塔）、标题与导语写作、信息筛选与事实核查、引用与改写、客观中立的语体、媒体偏见、常见新闻语法与用词规范。通过读写结合，分析真实英文媒体案例，完成消息、简讯、人物采访稿与特稿的写作练习，并获得逐稿反馈与修改指导。课程旨在帮助同学提升用英文进行报道、编辑与传播的能力，并培养学生的媒体素养。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for learners with a basic command of English and provides systematic training in English news writing. It covers news genres and structures (such as the inverted pyramid), headline and lead writing, information selection and fact-checking, quotation and paraphrasing, an objective and neutral register, media bias, and common grammar and word-choice conventions in journalism. Through an integrated approach to reading and writing, students analyze authentic English-language media examples, complete writing practice in news reports, briefs, interview-based profiles, and feature stories, and receive draft-by-draft feedback and revision guidance. The course aims to help students strengthen their ability to report, edit, and communicate in English, while also developing media literacy.

教学目标

This course is aimed at helping students get acquainted with various aspects of news writing in English, improve their language proficiency and ultimately hone their writing skills. Reading and writing will be two prongs of this course. Firstly, students are required to read in class or out of class different news stories, which will help them understand the basic structure of news reporting and master the use of words or expressions typically used in news. Secondly, students will be asked to write headlines, leads, news stories, critical reviews, etc.

教学方式

教师主讲，穿插学生陈述

教学内容与进度安排

可持续金融

2025秋 · MF50009.01 · 战昕彤

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助硕士生全面理解可持续金融的核心原理与实践，重点探讨金融与可持续发展的交叉领域。学生将学习如何从环境、社会和治理（ESG）视角评估金融决策，内容包括：可持续发展目标中金融的作用、ESG因素在投资中的整合，以及可持续投资的风险与收益分析。课程涵盖绿色债券、社会影响力债券等金融工具，并解析相关监管框架（如联合国负责任投资原则、欧盟可持续金融行动计划）。同时涉及企业社会责任（CSR）的战略整合、利益相关方参与及可持续性信息披露（如GRI、SASB标准）。通过案例研究和实践应用，学生将培养分析金融决策对可持续发展影响的批判性思维。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides master’s students with a comprehensive understanding of sustainable finance, focusing on the intersection of finance and sustainability. Students will learn to evaluate financial decisions through an environmental, social, and governance (ESG) lens, covering topics such as sustainable development goals, ESG integration in investments, and risk-return analysis in sustainable finance. The curriculum explores key instruments like green bonds, social impact bonds, and sustainable funds, alongside regulatory frameworks such as the UN Principles for Responsible Investment (PRI) and the EU Sustainable Finance Action Plan. Additionally, it examines corporate social responsibility (CSR), including stakeholder engagement, CSR integration into strategy, and sustainability reporting (e.g., GRI, SASB). Through case studies and practical applications, students will develop critical thinking skills to assess the real-world impact of financial decisions on sustainability.

教学目标

Analyze trade-offs between sustainability goals and financial performance using modern finance frameworks. Evaluate corporate philanthropy and CSR strategies, identifying key challenges in environmental/social performance. Examine linkages between non-financial corporate behaviors (e.g., ESG) and investment/financing decisions. Decipher ESG frameworks, assessing rating methodologies and key factors across agencies. Apply ESG-integrated valuation to asset classes and analyze capital markets’ role in driving sustainable development. Develop professional skills (critical analysis, teamwork, leadership) through case studies and group projects simulating real-world investment scenarios.

教学方式

Lectures: Instructor-led sessions covering core principles, frameworks, and emerging trends in sustainable finance (e.g., ESG integration, green bonds, climate risk pricing). In-Class Exercises: Applied problem-solving tasks, such as calculating carbon footprints of portfolios, analyzing ESG ratings, or modeling green bond pricing. Case Studies: Real-world examples (e.g., Tesla’s carbon credits, BlackRock’s ESG strategies) to critique sustainable investment decisions and regulatory impacts. Group Discussions: Structured debates on topics like "Shareholder vs. Stakeholder Capitalism" or "Transition Risks in Fossil Fuel Divestment." Final Project: Group-based ESG portfolio analysis or sustainable finance policy memo, presented to peers.

教学内容与进度安排

1.Introduction to Sustainable Finance 2.Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and ESG framework 3.Sustainable Financial Reporting 4.Sustainable Investing 5.Climate finance and green debt 6.Sustainable Asset Management 7.Sustainable Finance and the Future & Group Presentation

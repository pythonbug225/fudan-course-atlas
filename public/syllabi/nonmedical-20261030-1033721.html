课程简介（中文）

本课程是“美德伦理与儒家”课程的前半部分，集中讨论当代美德伦理学，先介绍、研读其两个主要流派的著作：以 Rosalind Hursthouse 为代表的新亚里士多德主义的理性主义和以Michael Slote为代表的休谟主义的情感主义，然后分析讨论当代美德伦理学所面临的一些主要挑战和批评，特别是来自义务论和后果论的批评，包括美德伦理必然会面临的两难问题、美德伦理具有自我中心主义的问题、和情景主义认为美德（因而恶德也）根本不存在的问题等。

课程简介（英文）

This course is Part I of the larger, two-semester course, "Virtue Ethics and Confucianism". In this part, we will focus on contemporary virtue ethics, first introducing the two most important schools of virtue ethics: neo-Aristotelian rationalism represented by Rosalind Hursthouse and neo-Humean sentimentalism represented by Michael Slote, and then discussing some most important objections to virtue ethics, especially those coming from deontologists and consequentialists, such as the inevitable dilemmas of virtue ethics, the self-centeredness objection to virtue ethics, and situationist criticisms of virtue ethics.

教学目标

Virtue ethics as a normative theory, with its ancient roots, was eclipsed by deontology and consequentialism in modern philosophy but has experienced an impressive revival since the second half of the 20th century and become a viable alternative to deontology and consequentialism. This course aims to help students get familiar with the main schools of virtue ethics as well as the main challenges it faces, and thus paving the way for the second part of the large course "Virtue Ethics and Confucianism", in which we will critical examine the various attempts to response to such criticisms by drawing on the resources from the Confucian tradition.

教学方式

The course will be conducted with a combination of lectures, classroom discussions, and student presentations, all aiming to understand the original text and critically exmaming its problems.

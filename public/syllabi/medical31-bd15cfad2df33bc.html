1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程的教学过程，包括课堂讲授和临床示教两方面。讲授内容以常见病、多发病为重点，也要介绍一些国内外医学的新成就，以了解现代医学的新动态。授课时注重基础与临床结合、理论与实际结合，充分利用病例示范讨论、大体标本及影像资料、以及录像或电影等电化教具，使授课既生动、具体，又能突出重点。除授课内容外，教材中的其他内容则作为学生自学之用。在课堂授课及临床示教中，通过优秀的案例分享和模拟演练，加强人文医学教育，树立榜样，弘扬真善美。培养和加强学生爱岗敬业的意识。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The teaching process of this course encompasses both classroom lectures and clinical demonstrations. The lecture content focuses on common and frequently occurring diseases, while also introducing selected new domestic and international medical achievements to keep students informed of the latest developments in modern medicine. Instruction emphasizes the integration of basic sciences with clinical practice and theory with practical application. Teaching aids such as case-based discussions, gross specimens, imaging materials, and audiovisual resources (including videos and films) are fully utilized to make the lectures vivid, concrete, and focused on key points. Beyond the content covered in lectures, the remaining material in the textbook is designated for students' self-study. Throughout both classroom teaching and clinical demonstrations, outstanding case studies and simulated exercises are used to strengthen humanistic medical education, set positive examples, and promote the values of truth, goodness, and beauty. Students' professional dedication and sense of responsibility are also cultivated and reinforced.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

本课程旨在通过课堂授课以及临床示教的方法，提高学生对外科学的理解。通过对外科常见疾病的学习，使学生能够掌握外科常见疾病的临床表现、诊断、治疗。并了解国内外对该疾病的研究进展。注重培养学生理论结合实际的能力，能将所学只是与临床实际想结合。加强人文医学教育和医德医风教育，弘扬医者精神的“真”“善”“美”，培养守初心、铸信念、强人文、有大爱的创新型卓越医学人才。更好为患者服务，为学科建设和发展打下基础。

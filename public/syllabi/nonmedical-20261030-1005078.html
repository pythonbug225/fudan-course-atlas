课程简介（中文）

本课程面向海外学生，旨在系统提升英语阅读能力。核心目标包括：培养批判性阅读思维、提高理解力、丰富词汇储备，并增强学生在解读和分析英语文本时的信心。考虑到非英语母语学习者的特殊需求，课程将提供针对性的支持，帮助学生提高阅读的流畅度和理解深度。教材涵盖多种体裁和不同难度，通过精心设计的训练，学生将掌握高效的阅读策略，如快速浏览、重点提取和深入精读等。同时，课程也注重文化意识的培养，帮助学生理解语言中的细微差异，更好地解读多样化的文本。完成本课程后，学生不仅能显著提升阅读速度和理解力，还能积极参与讨论，并能够撰写有深度的阅读反思。

课程简介（英文）

The primary objectives of this English Reading module for overseas students are to cultivate critical reading skills, enhance comprehension abilities, expand vocabulary knowledge, and foster confidence in interpreting and analyzing English texts. Recognizing the unique challenges faced by students whose first language is not English, the module will focus on providing tailored support to improve reading fluency and comprehension. Through a variety of reading materials spanning different genres and levels of complexity, students will develop strategies for effective reading, including skimming, scanning, and close reading techniques. Additionally, emphasis will be placed on building cultural awareness and understanding nuances in language usage to aid in the interpretation of diverse texts. By the end of the module, students should demonstrate improved reading fluency, increased comprehension of English texts, and the ability to engage in meaningful discussions and written reflections on the material covered.

教学目标

The essential content of this English Reading module encompasses a diverse range of texts carefully selected to cater to the needs and interests of overseas students at the year-1 level. The module will introduce students to various literary genres, providing exposure to different writing styles and perspectives. Key components of the module will include guided reading activities aimed at developing essential reading skills such as skimming, scanning, inference-making, and critical analysis. Students will engage with texts that reflect global themes, cultural diversity, and contemporary issues, allowing them to broaden their understanding of the English language while gaining insights into different cultures and worldviews. Furthermore, the module will incorporate vocabulary-building exercises and language comprehension tasks to support students in expanding their lexical repertoire and enhancing their comprehension abilities.

教学方式

Lecture, group discussion, after-class writing.

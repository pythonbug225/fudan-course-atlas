课程简介（英文）

This course is divided into three parts: theory, issues, and practice. The theoretical part starts from the research and teaching of China's international strategy in the new era, and combines classic international security theories to construct an analytical framework for China's strategic research. The topics section focus on major international strategic issues, including global strategic stability, regional conflicts and security, governance of emerging technologies such as global cyberspace, outer space, and artificial intelligence, as well as Global South and South-South cooperation. The practical part involves conducting research on relevant international organizations and think tanks, organizing simulated negotiations, and mastering general methods for policy analysis and evaluation.

口译理论与实践

2025秋 · FORE40024.02 · 陶衍

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

理论层面包括口译概念和涵义、英汉语言对比等内容，技巧训练包括视译练习、记忆练习、笔记练习、交替传译练习。

课程简介（英文）

The theoretical aspect covers content such as the concepts and implications of interpretation, as well as comparisons between English and Chinese languages. Skill training includes sight interpretation practice, memory practice, note-taking practice, and consecutive interpretation practice.

教学目标

《口译理论与实践》这一课程理论与实际结合，具有实操属性，旨在让学生理解口译的概念，理解基本的练习方法并进行相关训练，培养较强的英汉语语言技能，可靠的记忆能力，过硬的心理素质，从而掌握基本的口译技巧，可以进行基本的口译活动。

教学方式

正常上课，在语音实验室操作

教学内容与进度安排

1 General Introduction & Self-practice exercises 2 Foundational skills: short sight interpretation, Memorization & Note I (E-C) 3 Foundational skills: short sight interpretation, Memorization & Note II (C-E) 4 Theoretical aspects: E-C Comparison: words 5 Foundational skills: sight note taking & interpretation; Quiz 1 (unassessed) 6 Intermediate skills: Memorization and Note-taking Interpretation I (E-C) 7 Intermediate skills: Memorization and Note-taking Interpretation II (C-E) 8 Theoretical aspects: E-C Comparison: English sentences 9 Theoretical aspects: E-C Comparison: Chinese sentences 10 Revision & Comprehensive Quiz 2 (semi-assessed) 11 Long sight-interpretation and Notes & Themed CI practice (E-C) 12 Long sight-interpretation and Notes & Themed CI practice (C-E) 13 Intensive Themed CI practice I 14 Intensive Themed CI practice II 15 Mock-Exam & Course feedback 16 In-class exam

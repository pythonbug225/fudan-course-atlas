课程简介（中文）

本课程有助于学生理解开放经济条件下的经济活动规律，涉及国际贸易、跨国公司和国际投资、国际金融和国际宏观等内容。本课程基于中国经济对外开放事实以及经济全球化和国际经济学发展脉络，集中讲解国际贸易理论和政策、汇率决定和变动、国际收支调节方法、开放经济下宏观经济政策、国际货币体系等内容。通过课程学习，学生可以基于国际贸易理论、国际投资理论、汇率理论、国际收支理论、国际宏观经济理论等，运用现代经济学分析方法，结合宏观经济数据和微观经济数据，对国际经济现象进行解释，对国际经济运行态势进行研判，并能对国际经济重要问题开展学术研究。

课程简介（英文）

This course helps students understand the laws governing economic activities in an open economy, covering international trade, multinational corporations and international investment, international finance, and international macroeconomics. Based on the facts of China's economic opening-up, as well as the context of economic globalization and the development of international economics, the course focuses on international trade theory and policy, exchange rate determination and fluctuations, methods of balance of payments adjustment, macroeconomic policies in an open economy, the international monetary system, and related topics. Through this course, students will be able to use modern economic analysis methods, drawing on international trade theory, international investment theory, exchange rate theory, balance of payments theory, and international macroeconomic theory. They will also learn to combine macroeconomic and microeconomic data to explain international economic phenomena, analyze the trends of the international economy, and conduct academic research on key international economic issues.

教学目标

通过讲授国际贸易与国际金融领域的经典理论，引导学生夯实国际经济学理论基础，掌握应用国际经济学理论分析解释现实经济问题的能力。在理论教学外，还对学生提供关于学术入门、学术写作的初步指导。从理论和学术入门两个方面进行教学。

教学方式

教学方式是课堂讲授。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是中级汉语综合课，专为汉语水平达到HSK3级或以上水平（已掌握800个左右常用词）的外国留学生开设。本课程学习汉语词汇、对话、短文、语法、中国文化等，通过多样的学习活动帮助学生提高他们使用汉语的能力。通过一个学期的学习，学生需要掌握250个左右词汇，能理解并运用汉语常见副词等其他语法知识，能阅读并理解10篇长对话、10篇短文、10篇拓展文章，能围绕所给话题写中文短文，能就开学事宜、饮品、运动、生日、宠物、旅游等话题用中文表达自己的观点。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an intermediate comprehensive course specifically designed for international students who have already reached the HSK 3 level in Chinese proficiency. It aims to help students expand their Chinese vocabulary and sentence patterns, and improve their ability to use Chinese.

教学目标

This course aims to improve students' language skills, including listening, speaking, reading, and writing, as well as vocabulary expansion and grammar learning. Chinese culture is also included. Through one semester of study, students are required to master around 250 vocabulary words，understand and use basic Chinese adverbs and other grammatical knowledge, read and comprehend 10 long dialogues, 10 short essays, and 10 extended articles, write Chinese short essays on given topics, and express their opinions in Chinese on topics such as school enrollment arrangements, beverages, sports, birthdays, pets and travel.

教学方式

词汇教学：PowerPoint辅助讲解与练习，近义词辨析，写故事，“你来比划我来猜”等游戏。Vocabulary teaching: PowerPoint-assisted explanation and practice, analysis of near-synonyms, story writing, games like "You Act and I Guess", etc. 句子教学：学生看图说句子，改错句等。Sentence teaching: Students describe sentences based on pictures, correct wrong sentences, etc. 语法教学：创设生活、学习等现实情景，帮助学生理解语法知识，然后运用该语法知识完成实际的语言任务。Grammar teaching: Create real-life scenarios (such as daily life and study) to help students understand grammatical knowledge, and then use this knowledge to complete practical language tasks. 对话教学：角色扮演。Dialogue teaching: Role-playing. 课文教学：朗读课文，复述课文，表演课文，讨论与发表观点等。Text teaching: Reading texts aloud, retelling texts, performing texts, discussing and expressing opinions, etc. 阅读教学：阅读拓展课文，回答问题，引导学生围绕一些话题发表自己的观点。Reading teaching: Reading extended texts, answering questions, and guiding students to express their opinions on relevant topics. 写作教学：课堂上规定时间写一段话，课后每个作业中都有一篇150-200字的写作练习。Writing teaching: Writing a paragraph within a specified time in class, and each after-class assignment includes a 150-200-word writing exercise.

考核构成

  • 出勤Attendance 15.0% 缺勤1/3及以上的学生不可以参加考试，“病假”“事假”都算作“缺勤”，“迟到”三次等于一次“缺勤”。Students who are absent for 1/3 or more of the classes are not allowed to take the exam. Both "sick leave" and "personal leave" are considered "absences", and three "late arrivals" are equivalent to one "absence".
  • 课堂表现Participation 15.0% 学生应认真上课，积极参加课堂活动。Students should attend classes carefully and actively participate in classroom activities.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 15.0% 学生应按时提交作业。Students should submit their assignments on time.
  • 汇报Presentation 15.0% 每个学生都要围绕所给话题，用中文做展示。Each student is required to make a presentation in Chinese on the given topic.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 40.0% 读写考试Reading and Writing test

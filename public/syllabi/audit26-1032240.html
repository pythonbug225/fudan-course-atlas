课程简介（中文）

课程采用思想史、全球史方法，梳理19-20世纪亚非世界对帝国主义全球扩张、资本主义现代化、殖民发展观的认识与回应，以及亚非殖民地与半殖民地国家在谋求独立与解放过程中产生的对全球秩序的设想。本课程将重点讨论如下几个问题：亚非国际主义、第三世界主义的形成与发展，亚非国家去殖民解放的政治与困境，世界体系中的半殖民地问题，亚非的区域主义等。

课程简介（英文）

This module takes an Afro-Asian perspective in understanding the global imperialist expansion and capitalist modernization in the 19th and 20th centuries. Intellectual history and global history will be the main methodological approaches of this module. Students will engage with ideas, ideals, and practices of the colonial and semi-colonial people in the Afro-Asian world in their process of pursuing national independence and social liberation. The main issues include, Afro-Asian internationalism, Third Worldism, social liberation and its dilemma in the process of decolonization in Afro-Asian countries, semi-colony in the world system, and ideals of regionalism in Asia and Africa. 

教学目标

  1. Allowing students to understand the limits of mainstream IR theories and historical narratives through expanding their scopes of historical investigation. Providing students with an Afro-Asian perspective in theoretical thinking.
  2. Helping students to understand the underlining logic of Western-centrism and its forms of cultural domination. Encouraging students to acquire a global historical perspective in both empirical and theoretical studies.
  3. Supporting students to use English and Chinese theoretical and archival materials in their research. Introducing students to methods of interdisciplinary investigation.
  4. Enabling students to understand the means and ends of area studies through seminars and independent guided research projects.  

教学方式

Lectures offered by a team of 6 lecturers, focusing on general theory and area specific discussions.

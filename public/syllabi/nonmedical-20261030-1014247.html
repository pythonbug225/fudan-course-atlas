Econometric Analysis of Financial Data

2025秋 · MF60033.02 · 汤海涵、蒋亮

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

Part A of this course provides an introduction to the econometric methods on causality in economics/finance. Part B provides an introduction to financial econometrics. The whole course will provide students with a toolbox and working knowledge of cross-sectional, panel, and time series data empirical methods for the analysis of financial data. Such tools are essential for you to conduct empirical research in finance and to work with quantitative models in the financial industry.

教学目标

Part A: 1) Econometric evaluation of causality in economic / financial data 2) Implementation of econometrics in Stata Part B: 1) Financial econometric methods 2) Computational/algorithmic implementations of methods in Python Relevant applications in economics and finance

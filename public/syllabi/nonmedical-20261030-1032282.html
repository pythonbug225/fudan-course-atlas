课程简介（中文）

本课程以中国当代艺术为独特文本、在历史与全球语境下探讨当代中国相关议题。课程采用文化社会学路径并重点关注传播相关问题。本课程不是“艺术传播”课，而是“将艺术作为传播”。

课程简介（英文）

This course takes Chinese contemporary Arts as unique texts to understand and explore Contemporary China within historical and global contexts. The approach is cultural-social and the emphasis is on communication, not “the communication of arts” but “arts as communication”.

教学目标

This is NOT an atheistic course but a cultural and sociological one. By reading Chinese contemporary artists, works, events and movements with discussions of relevant theoretical literatures, this course offers and encourages students to find new approaches to i). further inquire and explore contemporary China and present it in your own manners, and ii). reflect upon key concepts of our major i.e. the “media”, the “message”, the “audience” and ultimately “communication”.

教学方式

in class teaching 60% in class students' participation 20% on site teaching/ seminar 20%

  • 汇报Presentation 40.0% Each student is required to do one 30 minute presentation, either analysing one Chinese contemporary artist and his / her works, or an event, an institute, a phenomena, an issue that is worth discussing socio-culturally or political economically.
  • 课题论文Research Paper 50.0% Written and revised version of the presentation. 3000 words. In basic academic format.

课程简介（中文）

本课程针对环境与健康领域，开展环境流行病学和环境毒理学的学习。课程负责人是国家级青年人才，课程团队是复旦大学公共学院学院环境流行病学和毒理学专业领域的核心骨干。讲课语言是英语。

课程曾获得了复旦大学全英文课程优秀课程奖，入选复旦大学线上线下混合课程建设项目，获得复旦大学第四届教师教学创新大赛新医科组二等奖。

课程简介（英文）

  • Teaching lanauge: English
  • Setting up pictures on climate change and air pollution, human environmental exposure and health, including pollutants’ chemical or biological components.
  • Understanding the ambient air pollution monitoring and exposure assessment in relation to human mortality and diseases, as well as advanced methodologies of exposure assessment to understand and even apply in exposure and health effects assessment.
  • Critical thinking on the methodology of environmental epidemiology and toxicology, their roles in supporting the standard- and policy- making.

教学目标

  • To build up the knowledge map and frame on environment, climate change and health.
  • To set up a thinking strategy to understand the relationship between environment and health, environmental epidemiology and environmental toxicology.
  • To inspire students’ deep interests and curiosity on our planetary health, not only human health, but animals, plants, microorganisms and even the viruses. We have ‘One Health’!

教学方式

Combination of in person and online. Oral presentation in person combined with students’ self-learning online

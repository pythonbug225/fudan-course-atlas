营销研究

2026春 · BA30020.01 · 庄雷

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程的主要目标是帮助学生掌握营销研究的核心概念、流程和方法，并学会如何将这些工具应用于解决现实中的商业问题。本课程更加侧重于定量研究方法，以帮助学生应对本领域的最新研究趋势。课程将通过课堂讲授系统介绍市场调研的技术层面，包括定性与定量研究方法，并结合各类文章和案例讨论其在现实商业中的应用。本课程的一个重要组成部分是市场调研项目，学生需要运用所学的市场调研方法，识别并解决一个真实的商业问题。

课程简介（英文）

The main objective of this course is to equip students with the key concepts, process, and methods of marketing research, and learn how to apply those tools to solve real-life business problems. This course puts more emphasis on quantitative research methods to prepare students for the newest development in the field. We will cover the technical aspects of marketing research (both qualitative and quantitative research methods) through lectures and discuss real life applications using various articles and cases. An important element of this course is a marketing research project where students identify and solve a real business problem using marketing research methods.

教学目标

To develop an appreciation for the potential contributions and limitations of marketing To learn about the steps in the marketing research process, which include problem definition, research design (exploratory, descriptive and causal), data collection methods, questionnaire design, sampling schemes, and data analysis. To operationalize several key marketing concepts such as customer preference, segmentation, targeting, positioning and new product

教学方式

course/discussion/report

教学内容与进度安排

Session 1 Introduction to Marketing Research Session 2 Topics and Methodology Session 3 Methods and Data Source Session 4 Marketing Measurements Session 5 Survey Design /Psychology of Survey Session 6 Mediation and Moderation Session 7 Sampling Session 8 Hypothesis Testing Session 9 T-test Session 10 ANOVA Session 11 Chi-Square Session 12 Linear Regression I Session 13 Linear Regression II

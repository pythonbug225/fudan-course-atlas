课程简介（中文）

The course combines interactive lectures, case analyses, and direct engagement with practitioners. The course draws on the rapidly growing body of research in entrepreneurship and finance. A central theme is that achieving competitive advantage requires startup founders to balance the process of experimentation and learning inherent to entrepreneurship with the choice and implementation of an entrepreneurial strategy. The cases and assignments are designed to help students integrate and apply the entrepreneurial strategy framework in real-world contexts. Active classroom participation—including engagement with case studies—is an essential component of the course.

教学目标

This course is structured around two interconnected themes. The first introduces students to an integrated strategic framework for guiding innovation-driven entrepreneurship. The second offers an overview of the venture capital industry and the investment practices involved in backing high-growth ventures. The course equips students with a strategic toolkit to navigate the core decisions faced by entrepreneurs and provides a framework for developing and executing entrepreneurial strategy in dynamic environments. It also builds students’ understanding of how to screen and advise startups from an investor’s perspective. The course is particularly valuable for students interested in launching or working at a startup, as well as those pursuing careers in venture capital or management consulting focused on high-growth firms or industries.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Topic: Foundations of entrepreneurial strategy & The role of venture capital Contents: What is the role of strategy in a new enterprise? What are the key strategic choices facing start- ups? Are entrepreneurs better off just improvising? What is role of venture capital in promoting high-growth entrepreneurship? Reading: EntStrat Chapters 1 & 4. Week 2 Topic: Choosing your customer Contents: Start-ups must choose between different customer segments when choosing to bring their product to market, as the value proposition received by one segment can be at odds with another. How should entrepreneurs choose their customers? What factors do you think most important in customer segment choice? Case: Lytro, mimeo, MIT Sloan School Reading: EntStrat Chapter 5 Week 3 Topic: Choosing your organization & Choosing your entrepreneurial strategy Contents: Effective entrepreneurial strategy depends on choosing a founding team and organization structure that allow you to establish the capabilities and resources to realize your purpose as founders. How can founders structure the organization to help them realize their overall entrepreneurial strategy? How do you choose an overall entrepreneurial strategy? While some firms choose to develop the entire value proposition and disrupt established firms in the product market, other firms choose strategic approaches that involve significant cooperation with established firms, from simple licensing deals, to collaborative partnerships, to the development of technology platforms. We will start from an overview of the key strategic trade-offs entrepreneurs face in choosing an entrepreneurial strategy, and how the process of choosing and executing an entrepreneurial strategy can help a firm establish and then sustain competitive advantage. Reading: EntStrat Chapters 7 & 8 Gans, J., E. Scott, and S. Stern (2018). “Strategy for Start-ups”. Harvard Business Review, May-June. Week 4 Topic: Putting entrepreneurial strategy to the test Contents: A central challenge for start-ups is selecting and implementing an effective entrepreneurial strategy. This session explores the range of strategic options available to entrepreneurs and introduces an integrated framework for systematically applying the entrepreneurial strategy process. A case study will be used to illustrate the key concepts in context. Reading: EntStrat Chapter 13 Week 5 Topic: Valuation of a start-up & Term sheet Content: The main methods for valuing entrepreneurial companies; how to select a valuation method that is most suitable for a company; how entrepreneurs and investors write term sheets that define their contractual rights and obligations. Reading: EntFinance Chapter 5 & 6 Week 6 Topic: Structuring a VC deal & Corporate governance Contents: The process of how entrepreneurs and investors come together and negotiate an investment deal; how investors can enhance the value of their portfolio companies by exercising corporate governance. Reading: EntFinance Chapter 7 & 8

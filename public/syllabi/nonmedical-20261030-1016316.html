强国之路：形势、政策与使命Ⅰ

课程简介（中文）

本课程结合物理学系学科特色优势，围绕“学会学习”，从不同角度进行探讨，引导学生初步确立成长目标，积极进行学业规划，合理安排大学四年生活。

课程简介（英文）

This course leverages the distinctive strengths of the Department of Physics, focusing on "learning how to learn" from various perspectives. It guides students in initially setting their growth goals, actively planning their academic paths, and rationally organizing their four-year university life.

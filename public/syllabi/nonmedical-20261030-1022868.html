课程简介（中文）

以分子结构-分子运动-性能为主线,高分子结构的空间尺度跨越从链段、串滴、链的平均尺寸、多链体系的特征关联长度到相结构尺寸.每个空间尺度拥有着特征弛豫时间尺度的运动模式， 构成了可预测线性粘弹力学性能的弛豫时间谱。并阐明各种凝聚态结构(如玻璃态、多相结构、界面、结晶和取向态等)对材料的性能的影响.

课程简介（英文）

The main line of this course reflects the relationship among structure, macromolecular motion and property of polymer. The dimensions of polymer structure scale from segment length, blob size, radius gyration, correlation length of multiple chain system to domain size of phase structure. Each spatial length dominates a mode of macromolecular motions, and possess a characteristic relaxation time scale. Both the characteristic spatial length and time scale contribute to the relaxation time spectrum with wide time range and hence the linear viscoelastic properties of polymer can be approached by this spectrum. In addition, this course needs to elucidate that various condensed structures (e.g. glass, multiphase, interface, crystalline and orientation states, etc.) have different degrees of effect on the mechanical properties of polymer materials. After completing this course, the students should be able to develop an understanding of structure­motion-property relationships in polymers, and improve their ability to analyze and solve practical problems by applying fundamental theories of polymer physics.

教学目标

通过本课程的教学，帮助学生掌握高分子的结构、性能以及分子运动三大方面的基本理论知识，提升运用高分子物理的基本理论分析和解决实际问题的能力。根据课程综合运用到其它学科如物理和数学中的理论方法的特点，强化学生的数理基础，培养学生的科学思维。

教学方式

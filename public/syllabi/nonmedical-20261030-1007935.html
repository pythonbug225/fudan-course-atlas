课程简介（中文）

This course is the first course in causal inference in econometrics/statistics. It will provide students with a toolbox and working knowledge of cross-sectional and panel data empirical methods for identifying casual relationships in economic / financial data. Such tools are essential for you to conduct empirical research in finance. Besides, more and more companies, especially tech companies, such as Amazon, are relying on these tools to make sound business decisions.

教学目标

1) Econometric evaluation of causality in economic / financial data 2) Implementation of econometrics in Stata 3) Relevant applications in economics and finance

教学方式

COURSE OUTLINE: Causality Panel Data Estimation (fixed effects) Instrumental Variables Difference-in-Difference COMPUTATION: The bulk of class will be devoted to the formal development of the material, albeit with empirical illustrations as well as ample discussions of the various practicalities of implementation. However, I do intend to reserve some class time for actual practice with computation. Computational examples will be done using Stata.

老龄化与社会

2025秋 · SWK30022.01 · 陈琳

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程从老龄化的社会背景、老龄化过程和相关的老年人的生理、心理、社会交往变化，以及对老人本身和他们的家庭成员所产生的影响出发，重点阐述社会工作在老年人的社会支持体系、社会功能、身心健康等相关范畴之内的作用。具体来说，本课程将首先介绍人口老龄化的现象、过程及其影响，然后介绍相关理论来理解人的老化过程，讨论人如何适应老化。其次，本课程将讨论老化对老年人和社会带来的影响与挑战，以及我们如何面对这样的挑战。第三，本课程将讨论如何从社会工作实务和政策来面对人口老龄化并帮助老年群体。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to familiarize students with theories, concepts, and phenomena of aging in terms of its implications for the society. Aging is a biological phenomenon, but also a social process for both individuals and society at large. We will discuss the economic, healthcare, intergenerational, and environmental, and cultural factors woven in the social processes of aging. Topics will include but not limited to aging and life course, demography, dementia, family, caregiving, community support, ageism, productive aging, and social policy. Aging is a life-long process—this course will guide students in developing an understanding of how aging shapes the lives of individuals, families, and society in a holistic sense.

教学目标

To describe the nature and characteristics of population aging, noting the culture of aging including attitudes toward older adults To understand various perspectives in understanding aging To understand how health, intergenerational relationships, families, communities, societies, cultures, and policies construct and reconstruct experiences of aging To sensitize students with aging population and understand this population’s unique needs and experiences To engage in critical thinking in order to gain a deeper and broader understanding of various aspects of aging—i.e., cultivate your gerontological imagination

教学方式

Lecture, discussion, group projects

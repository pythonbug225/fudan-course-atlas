课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在介绍经济学中广泛使用的数据分析方法，重点讲授因果推断方法，包括：随机分配、匹配与分层、线性回归、面板数据模型、工具变量回归、断点回归设计、双重差分估计及合成控制法。课堂上将演示相关STATA命令的操作应用。完成本课程后，学生应能规范评估实证研究，并掌握如何运用STATA在相关领域规范开展实证研究。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to introduce widely used data analysis techniques in Economics. We focus on causal inference methods, including random assignment, matching and subclassification, linear regression, panel data models, instrumental variables regression, regression discontinuity design, differences-in-differences estimation, and synthetic control. Relevant STATA commands will be illustrated in class. By the end of this course, students are expected to know how to properly assess empirical research and how to properly conduct empirical research using STATA in related fields.

教学目标

This course introduces key data analysis techniques in economics so that students can use them to conduct empirical research. We also discuss about all kinds of issues related to thesis writing over the semester, such as choosing a proper title, highlighting innovations, paper organizations, etc.

教学方式

The course will be mainly taught by the instructor. Applications of core techniques will be introduced. Active particiation of students are expected.

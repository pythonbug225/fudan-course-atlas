课程简介（中文）

宗教是人类文明的一部分，有人存在的地方就有宗教；随着全球化的深入，宗教更成为与全球政治、经济与文化想关联的现象。宗教社会学从社会学出现伊始就是社会学中非常重要的一个领域﹐涂尔干、马克斯·韦伯、卡尔·马克思三位古典社会学家无不对宗教社会学有着突出的贡献。宗教社会学的发展与现代社会的命运紧紧相关﹐随着社会的发展宗教社会学又出现了各种新的理论，包括宗教社会学的中国研究。本课程重点在介绍宗教社会学的经典理论，探讨宗教和宗教性在当代世界的经验；宗教在文化（东方和西方的）中扮演的角色，包括宗教与现代化的关系、宗教与道德的关系、宗教与宗教之间的互动关系、宗教与暴力的关系、宗教与全球化及人口流动的关系、宗教在未来世界的影响、如何认识儒家/教在当代的复兴与发展问题等，通过宗教社会学了解人类文明和社会变迁。

课程简介（英文）

Religion and Society: An Introduction to the Sociology of Religion Religion is an integral part of human civilization; wherever there are people, there is religion. With the deepening of globalization, religion has increasingly become a phenomenon intertwined with global politics, economy, and culture. From the very inception of sociology, the sociology of religion has been a vitally important field within the discipline. The three classical sociologists—Émile Durkheim, Max Weber, and Karl Marx—each made outstanding contributions to the sociology of religion. The development of the sociology of religion is closely bound up with the fate of modern society. As society evolves, new theories continue to emerge within the field, including the development of Chinese studies in the sociology of religion. This course focuses on introducing classical theories in the sociology of religion and exploring the empirical manifestations of religion and religiosity in the contemporary world. It examines the role religion plays in both Eastern and Western cultures, addressing such topics as: the relationship between religion and modernization; the relationship between religion and morality; the interactions between different religions; the relationship between religion and violence; the relationship between religion and globalization as well as population mobility; the influence of religion in the future world; and how to understand the contemporary revival and development of Confucianism as a religious phenomenon. Through the lens of the sociology of religion, this course seeks to enhance our understanding of human civilization and social change.

教学目标

本课程目标是通过对宗教社会学的基本概念、理论及与现代社会相关性的学习，来认识宗教的本质及其在社会结构中的互动，宗教与社会多方面的关系，包括政教关系、文明之间的交流与冲突、宗教在日常生活中的存在、宗教与族群的关系、宗教与世界格局的互动等。

课程简介（中文）

本课程为新闻传播学博士研究生专业基础必修课。总体课程由上、下两个部分构成，旨在为博士生描绘新闻传播学的知识地图，奠定进入独立研究之前的专业基础。课程注重新闻传播学经典著作的阅读与理解，兼顾新闻传播相关议题的跨学科思想与观点，以在当前新技术传播革命的背景下，拓展新闻传播学的研究视野。本学期课程为此门课程的下，由“传播政治经济学”“视觉与图像研究”“文化研究”三个板块组成，在阅读原典的基础上，兼顾新技术时代新闻传播的前沿话题，帮助博士研究生对上述三个板块的相关学术脉络、研究取向、核心概念、关键议题的加深理解。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a compulsory foundational course for doctoral students in Journalism and Communication Studies. It is divided into two parts, aiming to provide doctoral students with an intellectual map of the field and establish the professional foundation necessary for independent research. The course emphasizes the reading and comprehension of classic works in journalism and communication studies, while also incorporating interdisciplinary perspectives on related topics. Against the backdrop of the current technological revolution in communication, it seeks to broaden the research horizons of the discipline. This semester covers the second part of the course, which consists of three modules: "Communication Political Economy," "Visual and Image Studies," and "Cultural Studies." Grounded in the reading of original texts, the course also addresses cutting-edge topics in journalism and communication in the era of new technologies. It aims to deepen doctoral students' understanding of the academic lineage, research orientations, core concepts, and key issues within these three modules.

教学目标

本课程为新闻传播学博士研究生必修课。 通过三个板块“传播政治经济学”、“视觉与图像研究”、“文化研究”的经典文献阅读和讨论，兼顾新技术时代新闻传播的前沿话题，帮助博士研究生加深对新闻传播理论研究相关学术脉络、研究取向、核心概念、关键议题的理解，开阔理论视野，为后续博士研究生学习和展开独立研究夯实理论基础。

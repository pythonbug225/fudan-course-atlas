公司金融

2025秋 · ECON30122.04 · 沈红波

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

公司金融学这门课程主要介绍投资决策、融资决策和资产管理的基本原则、方法；介绍现值理论、资本结构理论、股利政策、公司治理、公司控制权理论等主要的公司金融理论，强调公司金融决策可能的理论解释。

课程简介（英文）

The course of Corporate Finance mainly introduces the basic principles and methods of investment decision-making, financing decision-making, and asset management; Introduce major corporate finance theories such as present value theory, capital structure theory, dividend policy, corporate governance, and corporate control theory, emphasizing possible theoretical explanations for corporate financial decision-making.

教学目标

The course objective of Corporate Finance is to enable students to master the basic principles and methods of investment decision-making, financing decision-making, and asset management, and to become familiar with the possible theoretical explanations for these practices. This course mainly introduces the basic principles and methods of investment decision-making, financing decision-making, and asset management; Introduce major corporate finance theories such as present value theory, capital structure theory, dividend policy, corporate governance, and corporate control theory, emphasizing possible theoretical explanations for corporate financial decision-making.

教学方式

理论--案例的教学模式。

译学理论研读

2025秋 · FORE60009.01 · 陶友兰

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

该课程教学主要内容为从不同视角研究国内外翻译理论论述和相关理念，采用lecutre+discussion+presentation形式授课。要求同学阅读原著，参与课堂讨论并进行思考，不定期撰写小型汇报，至少主讲一到两个翻译理论，进行理论方面的介绍、批评和应用，然后教师点评和补充。期末考查，提交自己对中外译论的洞见，并结合博士论文撰写理论基础部分。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers an in-depth exploration of translation theories and related concepts from both domestic and international perspectives. Adopting an interactive approach, the class combines lectures, discussions, and student-led presentations to foster critical engagement with foundational and contemporary translation studies. Students are expected to: Read and analyze primary theoretical texts. Actively participate in seminar-style discussions. Complete short reflective assignments at regular intervals. Deliver 1–2 presentations focusing on the introduction, critique, and practical application of selected translation theories, followed by instructor feedback and further elaboration. The final assessment requires a scholarly paper that synthesizes the student’s original insights into Chinese and Western translation theories, while also laying the theoretical groundwork for their doctoral dissertation.

教学目标

本课程通过批判性阅读国内外主流翻译理论，提高学生对翻译理论的认识（非知识），提升翻译理论批评能力，发展翻译理论建构能力，构建比较系统的理论体系，为毕业论文和今后的研究打下扎实的理论基础。通过本课程学习，学生的价值观和知识面得到拓展，并增强融通中外话语体系建构能力。

教学方式

课前预习，课堂讨论，课后反思；微信沟通

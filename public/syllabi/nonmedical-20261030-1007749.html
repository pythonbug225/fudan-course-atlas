Modern Chinese Popular Culture

2025秋 · CHIN60151.01 · 陈宜然

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

该课程主要介绍和分析21世纪以来中国流行文化，力图涵盖民众生活各方面并侧重休闲娱乐，将论及城乡、数字生活、体育、旅游、文学、音乐、消费等领域的热门文化现象，展示中国人所关注、喜爱和依赖的潮流、机制与事物。为了便于学生理解当代流行文化，课程也将涉及中国传统文化和现代文化史，作为必要的补充。

课程简介（英文）

This course primarily introduces and analyzes Chinese popular culture from the 21st century onwards, aiming to cover various dimensions of everyday life with a particular focus on leisure and entertainment. It will examine prominent cultural phenomena across domains such as urban and rural life, digital culture, sports, tourism, literature, music, and consumerism, thereby highlighting the trends, mechanisms, and objects that engage, captivate, and shape the preferences of the Chinese public. To facilitate students’ understanding of contemporary popular culture, the course will also address elements of traditional Chinese culture and modern cultural history as essential supplements.

教学目标

课程目标是引导外国学生从各种文化现象中以小见大地熟悉当代中国社会氛围和大众生活。即便学生对于中文的掌握尚浅，也能够了解当代中国大众的心态思想、趣味品位以及目前大众文化的大致成因和发展趋势。对于各种文化现象，除了为学生介绍其现状，也会详述其存在或运作的深层原理，令学生明白文化现象和产品是如何制造出来并维护更新的。由此可增强学生主动参与文化制造、体验和利用文化产品的能力，而非仅仅作中国流行文化的旁观者。

教学方式

1 课程参考面向外国人的中国文化主题书籍和研究著述，但避免照本宣科，而是基于教师本人的日常观察，以近年来国内热门文化现象为例子进行专题阐述。文化案例的展示和讲解将基于大量影音、图文形式的生动材料。 2 课堂以教师讲授为主，适当辅以讨论、鼓励学生发言。

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: 介绍从二十世纪至今的中国历史，使学生能够理解主要的历史脉络和社会变迁的大趋势，并认识中国如何发展到当下的状态。 Introduction to Chinese history from the twentieth century to the present, providing students with an understanding of major historical currents and the broader patterns of social change, as well as insights into how China has evolved into its current state. Week 2: 探讨数字化生活如何让中国普通民众的日常生活更加便捷，以及它如何激发自我表达、自我实现和经济收益的新机遇。 An exploration of the digital lifestyle that has made the daily lives of ordinary Chinese people more convenient, and how it has opened new avenues for self-expression, self-realization, and economic opportunities. Week 3: 探讨改革开放以来中国农村地区、社区和生活方式在近几十年的变化，以及农村居民如何在新的社会潮流与机遇中既感到困惑又获得力量。 An examination of how rural China—its communities and lifestyles—has transformed over recent decades following the Reform and Opening-Up policy, and how rural residents have been both challenged and empowered by emerging social trends and opportunities. Week 4: 探讨上海流行文化的发展，重点关注其核心价值、精神与文化认同，同时兼顾其草根层面与奢华面向。 An exploration of the evolution of Shanghai’s popular culture, with a particular focus on its core values, spirit, and cultural identity, highlighting both its grassroots dimensions and its luxurious facets. Week 5: 从观赛与参与的双重视角介绍流行体育文化，展示体育文化如何深度融合国家荣誉感、边缘活力与草根创造力。 An introduction to popular sports culture from the dual perspectives of spectatorship and participation, illustrating how sports culture intertwines with national pride, marginal vitality, and grassroots creativity. Week 6: 探讨中国女性如何自二十世纪以来借助社会变革与政策调整获得赋权，并实现自我赋权。 A discussion of how Chinese women have been empowered—and have empowered themselves—from the twentieth century to the present by leveraging social transformations and policy changes. Week 7: 借助文学与影视资料，探讨中国女性如何努力表达情感、建构自我叙事。 An inquiry into how Chinese women strive to articulate their emotions and construct their own narratives drawing on literary and cinematic materials. Week 8: 介绍网络文学的平台机制与热门类型及其影视改编，思考其中所反映的中国大众心态。 An introduction to the platform mechanisms and popular genres of online literature, along with their screen adaptations, in order to explore the popular mentality reflected in Chinese online literature. Week 9: 观察面向全球读者的热门中国网络文学的翻译与输出，以及近期中国短剧在市场上的主导趋势，以理解短剧的普遍吸引力及其在海外观众中走红的原因。 An observation of the translation and global dissemination of popular Chinese online literature, alongside the recent rise of Chinese internet short dramas dominating the market, aiming to understand the universal appeal of short videos and the reasons for their popularity among overseas audiences. Week 10: 探讨多种以语言为核心的流行娱乐形式，包括中国小品、脱口秀以及流行俗语，揭示其语言艺术的内在形成规律，以帮助外国学生更好地理解和掌握流行语言。 An exploration of several popular forms of language-based entertainment, including Chinese short skits (xiaopin), talk shows, and trendy vernacular expressions, with the aim of uncovering the underlying patterns of the linguistic artistry and making popular language more accessible to foreign students. Week 11: 展示当代中国流行文化中英雄形象所反映的文化理想，并探讨那些曾被世代推崇的传统英雄所体现的道德价值是否已经过时。 A demonstration of the cultural ideals reflected in the portrayals of heroes in contemporary Chinese popular culture, along with an exploration of whether the moral values embodied by traditional heroes—once revered for centuries—have become obsolete. Week 12: 讲解中国饮食文化中的礼仪、民俗与社会关系，以及近年来兴起的新型饮食文化。 An explanation of the etiquette, customs, and social relationships embedded in Chinese food culture, as well as the emerging trends in contemporary culinary culture. Week 13: 探讨中国旅行者如何在主动与被动、期待与失落之间游走，并分析社交媒体对旅行趋势与旅游产业的影响。 An exploration of how Chinese travelers navigate the tension between agency and passivity, as well as between expectations and discontents, accompanied by an analysis of the impact of social media on travel trends and the tourism industry. Week 14: 介绍电子游戏与潮流玩具如何通过情感消费吸引中国消费者。 An introduction to how video games and trendy toys engage Chinese consumers through emotional consumption. Week 15: 探讨中国传统戏曲在传承与复兴中的成就与困境，分析戏曲与传统音乐元素如何为流行音乐注入新鲜活力，以及普通民众如何积极参与音乐的创作与传播。 An examination of the achievements and challenges of traditional Chinese opera (xiqu) in its reinvigoration and inheritance, exploring how xiqu and traditional musical elements infuse new vitality into popular music, and how ordinary people actively participate in the creation and dissemination of music. Week 16: 从中国民俗与当代社会变迁的视角，探讨中式恐怖与中式“梦核”视觉文化的独特成因、情感内核与小众美学。 From the perspectives of Chinese folk customs and contemporary social transformations, this topic explores the unique origins, emotional core, and niche aesthetics embodied in the visual culture of Chinese horror and “dream-core”.

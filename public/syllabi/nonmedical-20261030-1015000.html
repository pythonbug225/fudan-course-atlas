课程简介（中文）

物理电子学是电子学、近代物理学、光电子学、量子电子学、超导电子学及相关技术的交叉学科，主要在电子工程和信息科学技术领域内进行基础和应用研究。近年来本学科发展特别迅速，不断涵盖新的学科领域，促进了电磁场与微波技术、微电子学与固体电子学、电路与系统等二级学科以及信息与通信系统、光学工程等相关一级学科的拓展，形成了若干新的科学技术增长点，如光波与光子技术、信息显示技术与器件、高速光纤通信与光纤网等，成为下一世纪信息科学与技术的重要基石之一。

课程简介（英文）

Physical Electronics is an interdisciplinary field that intersects electronics, modern physics, optoelectronics, quantum electronics, superconductive electronics, and related technologies. It primarily involves fundamental and applied research within the domains of electronic engineering and information science and technology. In recent years, this discipline has experienced particularly rapid development, continuously encompassing new fields and promoting the expansion of sub-disciplines such as electromagnetic fields and microwave technology, microelectronics and solid-state electronics, and circuits and systems, as well as related first-level disciplines like information and communication systems, and optical engineering. This has led to the formation of several new scientific and technological growth areas, such as lightwave and photonics technologies, information display technology and devices, and high-speed optical fiber communication and optical fiber networks, positioning it as one of the crucial cornerstones of information science and technology in the next century.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，了解当前物理电子学研究的相关前沿领域和研究进展。掌握相关的理论、常见的研究表征和测试方法。 本课程基本内容如下：1.前言；2.物理电子学研究领域和进展；3.太阳能电池研究；4.LED器件研究；5.有机光电器件；6.信息存储研究；7其他物理电子学研究内容。

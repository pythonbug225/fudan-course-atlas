功能语言学

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍系统功能语言学的理论。该理论基于语境解释词汇语法形式的功能，在话语分析领域得到广泛应用。课程内容围绕语言的三个元功（概念功能、人际功能和谋篇功能），对词—词组—小句—句群各不同词汇语法单位的功能加以描述和解释。教学方式除教师讲授之外，包括工作坊、小组讨论、课堂讨论、学生陈述以及话语分析实操。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers an introduction to Systemic Functional Linguistics, a linguistic theory that interprets lexico-grammatical forms based on their functions when used in contexts, thus a widely used tool in discourse analysis. Course topics are organized around the three metafunctions of language (ideational, interpersonal and textual), involving a description and interpretation of the functions of words, groups/phrases, clauses and clause complexes. The course is conducted through lectures as well as workshops, group or class discussions, student presentations, and hands-on discourse analyses.

教学目标

This course aims to enable students to a) develop an understanding of the nature of human language and how it works; b) gain familiarity with a way of thinking that relates lexico-grammatical forms to the construction of human experience, human relations, and a coherent text; and c) master an analytical framework that can be applied in studies of various kinds of language-related issues, for either academic or practical purposes.

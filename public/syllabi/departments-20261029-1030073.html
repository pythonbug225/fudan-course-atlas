课程简介（中文）

本课程的目标是通过课堂讲授以及鼓励学生自主学习，使学生了解政府财政的主要职能，能够运用学到的理论与知识分析常见的财政税收现象，从而为其未来具备理解政府经济行为、分析判断公共政策以及积极有效参与公共事务的能力打下扎实的基础。本课程的主要内容包括五大部分，一是财政学基本理论（导论、公共品和外部性），二是财政支出分析，三是税收理论与税收制度，四是政府预算和公共选择，五是政府间财政关系。

课程简介（英文）

This course , opened for students majoring in International Trade and Insurance, aims to enable the students to grasp and understand the basic rules of Finance, which refers to the principle of national revenue and expenditure, and the principle of macro financial policy. Through this course, the students can improve their abilities of understanding and analyzing the economic phenomena and policies relevant. This course consists of four parts. The first part includes two topics, one is about the basic concepts of public finance and the basic task of China during its transformation from the national finance to the public finance. The other introduces the market failure theory and public product theory. The second part mainly discusses the public finance expenditure. This part includes three topics. The first one analyses the principle, scale merit, classification and structure of the fiscal expenditure, while the second topic inquires the theoretical explanation on the expansion of fiscal expenditure and the differentiation between purchasing expenditure and transferring expenditure. The last topic focuses on the efficiency of fiscal expenditure, along with the analysis on the scale and structure of Chinese fiscal expenditure. The third part discusses the national revenue, under which five topics will be inquired. First comes the introduction on the concept, classification of fiscal revenue and the factors that affect the scale of fiscal revenue. The second topic focuses on the problem of tax principles, including both shifting and incidence of tax burden. The following topic concentrates on the tax system, under which factors on tax system and the main tax types will be discussed. The last two topics are mainly about government bonds and international taxes. The last part of this course concentrates on the financial control system. The first topic of this part is about the government budget, while the other three topics discusses the system of tax distribution, macroeconomic policies of the government and the problem of fiscal balance.

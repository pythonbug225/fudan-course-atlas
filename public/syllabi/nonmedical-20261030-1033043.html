课程简介（中文）

我们知道Jordan范式对于线性系统是多么有用。本课程发展了常微分方程的类似结构理论，首先在正式环境下，然后在p 进阶解析环境下，以收敛半径取代延拓。我们将涵盖超度量分析，微分模块，Fuchs和Turrittin定理，收敛半径和p进分解定理。

课程简介（英文）

We know how useful Jordan normal form is for linear systems. This course develops the analogous structure theory for ordinary differential equations, first in the formal setting and then in the p adic analytic setting where radii of convergence replace continuation. We will cover ultrametric analysis, differential modules, Fuchs and Turrittin theorems, radii of convergence and p-adic decomposition theorems.

教学目标

Understand the formal and p-adic analytic structure theory of linear differential equations: ultrametric analysis, differential modules, Fuchs and Turrittin theorems, radii of convergence and p-adic decomposition theorems.

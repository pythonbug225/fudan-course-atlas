课程简介（中文）

这是一门面向本科高年级学生的宏观经济理论课程。在本课程中，我们将探讨宏观经济和货币问题，并研究各种旨在帮助我们理解这些问题的模型。政策制定者在塑造宏观经济结果方面的作用将是我们关注的重点。在课程的最后部分，我们还将深入研究金融危机和大衰退。

课程简介（英文）

This is a course in macroeconomic theory at an advanced undergraduate level. In the course, we will be examining macroeconomic and monetary issues and studying various models designed to help us understand those issues. The role of policymakers in shaping macroeconomic outcomes will be our focus. In the final part of the course, we will also take a close look at the financial crisis and the Great Recession.

教学目标

Value Objectives: - Understand the importance of economic growth and its impact on society. - Appreciate the role of government policies in stabilizing the economy during business cycles. - Recognize the significance of central banks and their influence on interest rates and the broader economy. Knowledge Objectives: - Comprehend the factors that explain economic growth and the reasons behind varying economic performance across different countries and times. - Identify the causes of booms and recessions and the policies available to stabilize the economy. - Explain the phenomenon of unemployment and the policies that can help reduce it. - Understand how central banks affect interest rates, the implications of these rates on the economy, and the actions central banks should take during recessions and financial crises. Ability Objectives: - Analyze the effectiveness of different economic policies in promoting growth. - Evaluate the tools available to government authorities for managing business-cycle fluctuations. - Assess unemployment policies and their potential impact on reducing unemployment rates. - Critically analyze the actions of central banks during economic downturns and financial crises and their effects on the economy. By the end of this course, students should be well-versed in these areas, equipped with the knowledge and skills to analyze and understand complex macroeconomic issues.

教学方式

本课程采用“主讲+辅学+讨论”模式。教师主讲中级宏观经济学模型，辅以课上练习与每周作业；助教精讲习题答案并拓展知识；学生组队汇报论文，开展课程讨论，通过理论讲解、练习巩固、互动研讨实现教学目标。 This course adopts a "lecture + tutoring + practice" model. The instructor lectures on intermediate macroeconomic models, supplemented by in-class exercises and weekly assignments. Teaching assistants elaborate on exercise answers and expand knowledge. Students form teams to report papers and conduct course discussions, achieving teaching objectives through theoretical explanations, practice consolidation, and interactive seminars.

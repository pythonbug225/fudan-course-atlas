课程简介（中文）

《初级汉语》是一门面向汉语零基础的留学生开设的语言技能课程。课程旨在帮助学生全面掌握汉语的语音、基本词汇、核心语法结构和日常交际表达，逐步建立听、说、读、写四项基本语言技能，并为后续的汉语学习打下扎实基础。

课程简介（英文）

"Elementary Chinese" is a language skills course designed for international students with no prior knowledge of Chinese. The course aims to help students systematically master Chinese pronunciation, basic vocabulary, core grammatical structures, and everyday communicative expressions. It gradually builds the four fundamental language skills—listening, speaking, reading, and writing—and lays a solid foundation for further Chinese language study.

教学目标

1) Chinese Language Proficiency Objectives: Learners can spell Pinyin correctly and use it for self-learning; master certain vocabulary and grammar knowledge, and have preliminary listening, speaking and reading skills in Chinese; be able to use Chinese to communicate and meet the basic communicative needs of daily life, study and general social situations. 2) Cultural Competence Objectives: Learners will have an initial understanding of traditional and contemporary Chinese culture, and will be able to develop a basic understanding of cultural differences in intercultural communication and resolve conflicts arising from the cultural differences. 3)Value Objective:   Learner will develop independent reflection and collaborative interaction learning strategies. Learners will establish a correct view about China and expand their global perspective.

教学方式

The course follows a step-by-step approach, progressing from pronunciation to vocabulary, sentence patterns, and short texts. Language points are introduced systematically to ensure a solid foundation. Each new point is accompanied by targeted practice activities to help students reinforce and internalize the knowledge effectively.

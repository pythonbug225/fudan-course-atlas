课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养学生运用数学知识与方法，解决理论与应用经济学问题的实践技能。课程内容分为以下三个部分： 1）无穷级数、函数项级数、幂级数与傅里叶级数； 2）多元函数微分学及其应用； 3）多元函数积分学及其应用。

课程简介（英文）

The course gives students’ skills of implementation of mathematical knowledge and practice to economic problems both theoretical and applied ones. There will be three parts in the course: infinite series, function series, power series, Fourier series; 2) multivariable derivatives with applications; 3) multivariable integrals with applications.

教学目标

By the end of the semester a student has to: Know about the principal concepts and results of infinite series, function series, power series, Fourier series, multivariable-variable calculus; Be able to apply calculus to solve problems; Know about the basic concepts of real analysis.

教学方式

Lecture, presentation, group discussion

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule Week 1: infinite series Week 2: infinite series, function series Week 3: function series, power series Week 4: power series Week 5: Fourier series Week 6: limit and continuity of multivariable functions Week 7: derivative of multivariable functions I Week 8: derivative of multivariable functions II Week 9: inverse function theorem and applications Week 10: integral with parameters Week 11: line integral Week 12: double integral Week 13: triple integral Week 14: surface integral I Week 15: surface integral II Week 16: ordinary differential equations

教学参考资料

References: 1. Mathematical Analysis, by Vladimir Zorich. 2. 数学分析, by 陈纪修、於崇华、金路.

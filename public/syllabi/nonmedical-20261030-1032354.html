人口与治理专题

课程简介（中文）

人口是人类社会存在与发展最为重要的议题之一。国际社会日益强调“将人口问题充分纳入国家规划、政策制定和决策进程”以实现可持续发展等治理目标，我国政府相继提出“中国式现代化是人口规模巨大的现代化”“以人口高质量发展支撑中国式现代化”等重大命题。“人口”是“发展”的必要条件，锁定“人口-发展”关联往往需要“治理”去兑现。人口治理作为重要的治理主题愈受重视，其基本诉求是通过调节人口要素或顺应人口规律以协调人口与社会经济发展以及生态环境等的关系，反映出政府及相关治理共同体制定或执行政策与提供服务的形态及能力。作为一个人口大国，中国人口治理是国家与社会治理现代化的重要主题和核心领域之一，亟须强化其教学研究。

课程简介（英文）

Population is one of the most critical issues for the existence and development of human society. The international community increasingly emphasizes "fully integrating population issues into national planning, policy-making, and decision-making processes" to achieve governance goals such as sustainable development. The Chinese government has successively proposed major propositions like "Chinese modernization is modernization with a massive population scale" and "Supporting Chinese modernization through high-quality population development." Population is a necessary condition for development, and establishing the "population-development" link often requires governance to materialize. As an important governance theme, population governance has gained growing attention, with its fundamental objective being to coordinate the relationship between population and socio-economic development as well as ecological environment by regulating population factors or adapting to population dynamics. This reflects the forms and capabilities of policies formulated or implemented by governments and related governance systems, as well as their service provision. As a populous country, population governance in China is a key theme and core domain in the modernization of national and social governance, urgently requiring enhanced teaching and research.

教学目标

通过学习这门课程，学生应该能够做到： （1）初步了解人口学和公共管理领域的交叉研究，对人口治理研究的现状和发展有所了解； （2）掌握人口和治理交叉研究领域的基本概念和理论，能理解并实践其基本研究逻辑及方法； （3）培养在具体研究议题中应用人口治理研究的视角和相应理论知识、技术方法的能力。

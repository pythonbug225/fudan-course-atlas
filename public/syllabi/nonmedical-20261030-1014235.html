课程简介（中文）

Financial Accounting is a core course in the Accounting field. This course mainly focuses on applying international accounting standards to daily accounting entries and related adjustments. Meanwhile, students should understand and apply the latest fair value concept. It is also crucial to understand and hence analyze the commonly used financial statements.

课程简介（英文）

Financial Accounting is a core course in the Accounting field. This course mainly focuses on applying international accounting standards to daily accounting entries and related adjustments. Meanwhile, students should understand and apply the latest fair value concept. It is also crucial to understand and hence analyze the commonly used financial statements.

教学目标

Understand the role of accounting standards and related conceptual framework Skilled in making daily accounting entries and adjustments Know what the fair value adjustment means and how it matters Analyze the financial statements

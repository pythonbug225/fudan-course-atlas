企业发展

2025秋 · GEEC10237.01 · DAVID FERNANDO MARTINEZ NAVARRO

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（英文）

Students need to be prepared for a world that has an unprecedented rate of change, especially in countries such as China, with unparalleled levels of development, competition and digitalization. Traditional pedagogy is focused to create logical, sequential learning that is purposefully aimed at building competency or perspectives. Simulating experiences and a fully experiential entrepreneurship allows this course to complement those skills mentioned. This course applies design thinking, creative thinking, story telling and other entrepreneurial development creative theories.The goal of entrepreneurial development is to equip students with tools and methodologies that can help them identify and pursue business opportunities, while developing problem solving skills that will be useful in many aspects of their lives.

教学目标

Entrepreneurial Mindset: Understanding the entrepreneurial mindset and its importance. ● Innovation and Creativity: Understanding the relevant literature and methodologies that drive innovation, creativity, and disruption. ● Problem Solving and Opportunity Recognition: Identifying potential problems, formulating solutions, and recognizing how they can be transformed into business opportunities. ● Resource Management: Understanding that entrepreneurship is possible even with limited resources. ● Opportunity Identification and Empathy: Identifying opportunities with economic, social, or environmental impact, and empathizing with client and market needs. ● Creative Problem-Solving Skills: Developing skills for creative processes and approaching/solving problems from different perspectives. ● Customer Experience Mapping: Learning how to structure and create a customer experience map. ● Communication and Customer Engagement: Understanding how to effectively communicate ideas and connect with potential customers. ● Chinese Innovation Environment: Understanding China's innovation environment and the opportunities it presents for entrepreneurs. ● Chinese Economic Trends and Cross-Border Impact: Identifying growing trends in the Chinese economy and understanding their potential impact on cross-border economic development. ● Interaction with Chinese Entrepreneurs: Interacting and engaging with entrepreneurs who are conducting business in China.

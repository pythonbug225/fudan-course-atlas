课程简介（中文）

The microeconomics core course is an introduction to microeconomic theory and applications at the intermediate level, suitable for first-year graduate students. Using the microeconomic theory framework, we first investigate consumer’s behavior -- how rational agents make choices under constraints to maximize their utilities. We then explore the managerial decision-making on the producer’s side -- pricing and output decisions to maximize profits. Combining the two sides, we develop the general equilibrium theory, which attempts to explain the behavior of supply, demand, and pricing in an economic system. Examples from economic history and business industry will be used to complement the understanding of the theoretical frameworks.

教学目标

On successful completion of this subject students should be able to: Explain core microeconomic theories of consumers and producers Acknowledge and identify the limitations of theories, including how their underlying assumptions may differ from real-world problems Apply economic tools to analyze and solve problems facing consumers and producers Apply theories to economic problems faced by agents under different market structures

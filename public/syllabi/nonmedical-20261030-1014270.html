课程简介（中文）

本课程面向来华留学生，旨在系统介绍中国文化与当代社会。通过“农耕文明的价值观”“中西方文明差异”等核心议题，采用中西对比视角，解析文化差异及其深层逻辑，帮助学生理解中国人在处世方式、性格特征及价值判断上的独特性。教学内容立足中国文化本体，注重跨文化比较，培养学生在多元背景下认知中国社会文化特质的能力。

课程简介（英文）

Designed for international students in China, China Panorama systematically introduces Chinese culture and contemporary society. By examining core topics such as "values rooted in agrarian civilization" and "East-West civilizational contrasts," the course employs a Sino-Western comparative lens to analyze cultural differences and their underlying logic. This approach enables students to understand distinctive Chinese characteristics in interpersonal conduct, personality traits, and value judgments. Grounded in Chinese cultural frameworks while emphasizing cross-cultural comparisons, the course cultivates students' ability to discern Chinese sociocultural identity within diverse global contexts.

教学目标

（1）Value Guidance: Cultivate a nuanced, evidence-based understanding of China’s cultural identity and developmental path, aligning with Fudan’s mission to foster global citizens with cross-cultural literacy. （2）Knowledge Framework: Systematically explore core themes—such as agrarian civilizational values, Sino-Western comparative perspectives, and contemporary societal transformations—to provide a structured intellectual foundation. （3）Critical Competencies: Develop analytical skills to decode cultural codes (e.g., behavioral norms, value systems) through interdisciplinary lenses, empowering students to engage with China’s socio-cultural complexities in academic and professional contexts. （4）Dialogue Catalyst: Design discussion-driven modules that bridge theoretical knowledge with real-world applications, encouraging students to contextualize China’s global role within their respective research fields.

教学方式

Comparative Case Studies‌: Analyze parallel historical events (e.g., industrialization in China vs. Europe) using primary sources to reveal divergent value systems. Peer-Led Seminars‌: Small groups present interdisciplinary connections (e.g., how Daoist cosmology influences environmental governance). Role-Playing Simulations‌: Model cross-cultural negotiations (e.g., Sino-German business meetings) with debriefs on nonverbal communication pitfalls.

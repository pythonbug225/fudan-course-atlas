课程简介（中文）

本课程的主要目标是为学生奠定基础，使其能够理解并开展关于口语词汇识别的实验研究。课程重点探讨口语词汇识别的潜在机制，以及影响该过程的词汇和亚词汇因素。课程内容包括讲座、课堂讨论、口头报告，以及撰写专题和研究计划书。

课程简介（英文）

The primary goal of this course is to provide students with the foundation needed to understand and carry out experimental research on spoken word recognition. The course focuses on the mechanisms that have been proposed to underlie spoken word recognition, and the lexical and sublexical factors that influence it. The course will consist of lectures, in-class discussions, oral presentations, and written topic and research proposals.

教学目标

Throughout the course, students will: learn about the most important models of spoken word recognition; become familiar with the experimental methods used to investigate spoken word recognition; read studies that examine how various factors (frequency, phonotactic probability, neighborhood density, phonetic and phonological variation, etc.) affect spoken word recognition; discuss the findings of these studies in relation to models of spoken word recognition; develop a research proposal that investigates one aspect of spoken word recognition using the theoretical, methodological, and empirical knowledge gained from the assigned readings.

教学方式

Teaching methods will include instructor's lecturers and students' presentations. Class Participation (10%) Class participation includes attendance, punctuality, and contribution to class discussions. Class attendance is crucial. If you need to miss class due to a schedule conflict, please talk to the instructor to let her know about this conflict. Participation points will be deducted for missing a class without a valid schedule conflict or a medical emergency. Please be punctual and arrive in class on time. Late arrivals disrupt the class. Participation points will be deducted for arriving late in class. All students are expected to contribute voluntarily to the class (speak up, ask questions, etc.). Students should always come prepared for class. This entails reading the material to be covered in class BEFORE coming to class. Participation points will be deducted for not contributing to class discussions. Critical Review of Article (20%) Students will present ONE article on the assigned reading list to the rest of the class. One week before giving their presentation, students will write and submit a 5-to-7-page critical review of the article they have chosen for their presentation. The instructor will read and comment on the paper and return it to the students by Monday the following week, in time for students to incorporate the feedback in their presentation. Late assignment policy: The critical review paper is due by 1:00 PM exactly ONE WEEK before the article presentation is scheduled. The paper will lose 20% of its grade (i.e., 2 of 10%) for EACH DAY that it is late (0-24 hours late = –20%, 24-48 hours late = –40%, etc.). Papers that are more than 4 days late (>96 hours after the due day and time) will not receive any points. Oral Presentation of Article (10%) Students will present ONE article on the assigned reading list to the rest of the class using PPT (or other equivalent forms of visual presentation). The article presented in class should be the same article discussed in the critical review paper. The presentation should provide a critical review of the article. This oral presentation is intended to take the form of an open discussion, so students should expect to be interrupted during the presentation. Presentations should be no longer than 30 slides. Late assignment policy: Students should submit their PPT to the instructor before 1:00 PM on the day of the presentation. Oral presentations will lose 20% of the presentation grade (i.e., 2 of 10%) for EACH DAY that the PPT slides are late (0-24 hours late = –20%, 24-48 hours late = –40%, etc.), even if the presentation was made in class. Article presentations are due the day of the presentation and CANNOT be made up except in case of a medical emergency. Final Research Proposal Project (50%) Students will come up with a research proposal for a study investigating one aspect of spoken word recognition. This assignment will include three components: (i) a topic proposal (5%), an oral presentation (15%), and a final paper (30%). Topic proposal (5%) Students must receive approval from the instructor prior to selecting a project for this class. They should write and submit a 1-page topic proposal outlining the research questions that the study will answer and the experimental method that it will use to answer these questions. Late assignment policy: The topic proposal is due before 1 PM on Wednesday, October 29. The topic proposal will lose 20% of its grade (i.e., 1 of 5%) for EACH DAY that it is late (0-24 hours late = –20%, 24-48 hours late = –40%, etc.). Topic proposals that are more than 4 days late (>96 hours after the due day and time) will not receive any points (but must still be submitted for the project to be approved). Oral presentation (15%) Students will present their final research proposal project as part of a 30-minute oral presentation in which they will receive feedback from the instructor and from their peers. The oral presentation should include: a literature review including a summary of 2-3 previous studies, research question(s) and hypotheses, and the research method and predictions. Late assignment policy: The oral presentations will take place during the last two weeks of classes. PPT slides should be submitted to the instructor before 1 PM on the day of the presentation. Oral presentations will lose 20% of the presentation grade (i.e., 3.75 of 15%) for EACH DAY that the PPT slides are late (0-24 hours late = –20%, 24-48 hours late = –40%, etc.), even if the presentation was made in class. Presentations for which the PPT slides were submitted more than 4 days late (>96 hours after the due day and time) will not receive any points. Presentations not made on the day they are scheduled CANNOT be made up except in case of a medical emergency. Final research proposal paper (30%) Students will write a final research proposal paper presenting their proposed project. The paper should include: a literature review including a detailed summary of 2-3 previous studies, research question(s) and hypotheses, and the research method and predictions. Late assignment policy: The final project proposal paper is due by 11:59 PM on Wednesday, December 31. Papers will lose 20% of their grade (i.e., 7.5 of 30%) for EACH DAY that they are late (0-24 hours late = –20%, 24-48 hours late = –40%, etc.), and papers that are more than 4 days late (>96 hours after the due day and time) will NOT be accepted. Academic Misconduct Academic misconduct will not be tolerated. Strict procedures for reporting plagiarism in written assignments will be enforced. If plagiarism discovered, you will receive a zero for that portion of your final grade and the incident will be reported to University authorities. If such academic misconduct occurs a second time you will receive an F in the course and the incident will again be reported to University authorities. Note that a second incidence of plagiarism on record at the university may in some cases result in a transcript citation. Grading A(90-100), A-(85-89), B+(82-84), B(78-81), B-(75-77), C+(71-74), C(66-70), C-(62-65), D(60-61), F-(<60)

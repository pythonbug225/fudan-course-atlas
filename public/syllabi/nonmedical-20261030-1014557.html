Technologies of Coherent Communication

2025秋 · EST50036.01 · 周雯

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

随着宽带光电器件的发展和新DSP技术的出现，相干光通信系统DSP仍然是大容量系统的唯一解决方案。通过该课程，学生学习单载波/多载波OFDM调制的概念单载波相干光的格式和基本算法传输系统。此外，学生还将分析非线性从光纤和无线电传输系统中了解非线性补偿算法以及高效技术，包括概率整形、高级调制编码和无载波幅度相位调制（CAP）。此外，学生还将学习如何实现光纤接入中的强度调制/IQ调制直接检测系统。本课程也会介绍和演示一些先进的神经网络（NN）训练和KK检测算法。

课程简介（英文）

With the development of broadband optical and electrical device and the emergence of new DSP technologies, coherent optical communication systems with DSP is still the sole solution for large-capacity system. Through the course,students learn the concept of single-carrier/multi-carrier OFDM modulation formats, and the basic algorithm of single-carrier coherent optical transmission system. In addition, students will also analysis the nonlinearity from optical and radio-over-fiber transmission systems and know the nonlinear compensation algorithms, as well as high efficient technologies including probability shaping, advanced modulation coding and Carrier-less amplitude phase modulation (CAP). Furthermore, students will also learn how to realize intensity modulation/IQ modulation direct detection in optical fiber access system. Some advanced neural network (NN) training and KK detection algorithms will also be demonstrated in this course.

教学目标

Students learn new DSP technologies of coherent optical communication system, and students will be trained to program and do simulation for DSP in coherent communication.

教学方式

Teachers impart knowledge, skills, and experience to students through lectures, demonstrations, and demonstrations.

教学内容与进度安排

1）The 1st -2nd week：Single-carrier advanced modulation format and Optical OFDM principle. 2）The 3rd week： The basic algorithm of single-carrier coherent optical transmission system. 3）The 4th week： The exploration of the high spectral efficiency super-Nyquist wavelength division multiplexing system. 4）The 5th -6th week：Nonlinear compensation algorithm of fiber channel. 5）The 7th -8th week：Research of probability shaping technology, Ultra-high baud rate optical signal transmission technology and four-dimensional modulation. 6）The 9th-12th week：PAM4 signal modulation and detection techniques based on digital signal processing , Basic digital signal processing technology of direct detection OFDM , Intensity modulation and direct detection of the high-speed optical fiber access system and High-speed optical fiber access system based on I/Q modulation direct detection. 7）The 13th -14th week： Machine learning algorithm in optical communication system. 8）The 15th week： The principle of KK algorithm. 9）The 16th -18th week：Review the lessons, complete programming, simulation and final report.

课程简介（中文）

本课程将从宏观政策、节能技术、节能管理、综合管理和创新示范等多个领域介绍公共机构节能的理论基础及政策实践。课程旨在帮助公共机构各类节能从业人员熟悉公共机构节能相关政策、法规，树立高度的忧患意识和资源节约意识，提高思想觉悟和认识，增强从事节能工作的使命感和责任感；了解节能管理能力和技术，掌握公共机构节能项目管理与控制方法和策略，更好地在本机构推动开展节能项目并熟悉公共机构能源管理体系实施试点相关知识，有效构建本机构的能源管理体系等。

课程简介（英文）

This course will introduce the theoretical foundations and policy practices of energy conservation in public institutions across multiple areas, including macro-level policies, energy-saving technologies, energy management, integrated management, and innovative demonstration practices. The course aims to help various energy conservation practitioners in public institutions become familiar with relevant policies and regulations, develop a strong sense of crisis and resource conservation awareness, and raise their ideological understanding and awareness. It also seeks to enhance their sense of mission and responsibility in undertaking energy-saving work; to provide knowledge of energy management capabilities and technologies; and to equip them with methods and strategies for managing and controlling energy conservation projects in public institutions. Ultimately, the course will support participants in better promoting and implementing energy-saving projects within their organizations and in gaining familiarity with the implementation of pilot programs for energy management systems in public institutions, thereby effectively building an energy management system within their organizations.

教学目标

本课程旨在通过公共机构节能管理理论和现实案例相结合的方式，帮助学生： (1)从理论层面掌握公共机构节能的概念内涵和理论框架； (2)从实践层面掌握我国公共机构节能管理的制度规范及管理流程； (3)全面了解国内外公共节能管理的历史、现状、改革动向和学术前沿； (4)培养良好的资源节约理念和素养，使之具备较高的公共机构节能管理能力。

教学方式

本课程采用多元化的授课方式，包括课堂讲授、专题讨论与实地调研相结合。通过教师系统讲解相关理论与政策，组织同学围绕节能热点问题进行互动交流，增强理解与应用能力。

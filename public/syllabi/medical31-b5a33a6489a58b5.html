1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 在医学行为中，有效的问诊能够建立良好的医患关系，提高诊断准确性，也能优化医疗资源利用。本课程详细阐述11步循证问诊方法和模拟演练以患者为中心的问诊，其中包括 5个大步骤和21个子步骤，将指导医学生收集更多有效信息并与患者建立值得信赖的关系。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | In medical behavior, effective interviewing can establish a good doctor-patient relationship, improve the accuracy of diagnosis, and optimize the utilization of medical resources. This course elaborates on an 11-step evidence-based approach to questioning and a simulation of patient-centered questioning, which includes 5 steps and 21 sub-steps, which will guide medical students to gather more effective information and build trustworthy relationships with patients.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Presenting an integrated patient-centered and clinician-centered interviewing; Helping medical students master the new medical interview skills.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

Theories: basic knowledge on clinical work and patient-center interviewing skills. Immersion teaching strategies:clinical case observation and scene play creation. Role-playing:lots of practice in playing different roles of a patient or a doctor.

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在向学生介绍商业信息系统的基本概念，课程将探讨信息系统的定义、核心组件及其相互关系，并阐释企业如何通过信息系统实现卓越运营和战略优势。课程内容涵盖五大主题： 1）信息系统的组织与管理基础； 2）信息系统相关的基础设施与新兴技术； 3）信息技术驱动的产品、服务及商业模式创新； 4）信息技术支持下的业务流程革新； 5）信息系统的开发、实施与管理。 完成本课程后，学生将能够从个人、组织和社会层面深入理解信息系统的关键问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to introduce students to the fundamental concepts of business information systems. It examines what is meant by an information system, its basic components and their inter-relationships, and why and how such system is being used in businesses to achieve operational excellence and strategic advantage. There are five major themes covered in the course: 1) organizational and managerial foundations of information systems; 2) fundamental and emerging infrastructure related to information systems; 3) IT enabled product, service and business model innovation; 4) IT enabled business process innovation; and 5) developing, implementing, and managing information system. After taking the course, students should gain a good understanding of important issues pertaining to information systems at the individual, organizational, and societal levels.

教学目标

1) Develop understanding of relevant concepts of management information systems and their use in organizations. 2）Develop capabilities for identifying, assessing, and resolving the important issues related to management information systems. 3) Develop fundamental managerial competency and balanced outlook in implementing and employing management information systems. 4）Develop capabilities for analyzing and articulating cases of contemporary information management in organizations.

教学方式

1)  Lectures: delivery of fundamental concepts and issues of management information systems. 2）Participation and discussions: These discussions stimulate students’ reflection on the learning materials. Students are encouraged to participate in discussions and share their views and knowledge with peers. This also allows the instructor to gauge how well students understand the course concepts, and to promote mutual learning among the students. 3） Assignments: There are individual assignment and group assignment. Some assignments require the use the computer software. Through the assignments students get the opportunities to apply the knowledge learnt to solve the problems given to them. In the group assignment, students will learn to communicate and coordinate with other group members to ensure successful assignment completion. 4) Demonstration: live demonstrations of management information systems implemented in modern organizations to provide students a better understanding of related technologies in practice. Multimedia elements such as video clips will be used where appropriate for the demonstration purpose.

课程简介（中文）

本课程以新价值创造为核心，系统探讨创新管理流程与新兴企业运营，内容涵盖创意与设计思维、开放式创新与众包、精益创业战略、创业团队组建与融资，以及数字化转型与反向创新，帮助学生掌握创新创业的全流程实践能力。

课程简介（英文）

The course is systematically organized around the creation of new value, processes of innovation management, and operation of new and emerging ventures. The subject covers major aspects of innovation and entrepreneurship strategy, including creativity and design thinking, open innovation and crowd sourcing, lean startup strategy, founding team and fund raising, digital transformation and reverse innovation.

教学目标

The overall aim of this subject is to establish the foundations of innovation and entrepreneurship, and use tools such as design thinking to create values. Students will acquire a broad overview of principles, theories, and practice of innovation and entrepreneurship, together with an understanding of key tools, skills, and attitudes required to operate in an entrepreneurial environment. On successful completion of this subject students should be able to: Explain the range, scope and complexity of the issues and problems related to the strategic management of technology, innovation and entrepreneurship Analyze and evaluate various approaches to managing innovation and entrepreneurship, in both startup and mature organizations Apply major innovation theories and entrepreneurial tools (e.g., design thinking, lean startup strategy, open innovation) to the analysis of case studies Explain the innovative and entrepreneurial evolution and phenomena taking place today

教学方式

Teaching methods include lectures, case studies, classroom discussions, group projects, presentations, and analytical essays.

教学内容与进度安排

Week Topic 1 Introduction 2 Business model canvas and value proposition 3 Lean startup strategy and entrepreneurial opportunity 4 Business idea and hypotheses testing 5 Entrepreneur and founding team 6 Fund Raising and scaling-up 7 Open innovation and crowd sourcing 8 Innovation culture and entrepreneurship leadership 9 Creativity and design thinking 10 Digital transformation 11 Emerging economy and reverse innovation 12 Born global firms 13 Social entrepreneurship 14 Review + Presentations 15 Presentations The schedule might adapt based on teaching plans.

教学参考资料

There is no required textbook for the course. Slides are the main teaching materials. Students need to read certain cases before specific sessions, which will be indicated in class.

考核方式

出勤Attendance 10.0%（Students are required to attend classes. Voluntary before class participation for extra credits.）；作业/实验/实践Assignment 30.0%（Case analysis）；汇报Presentation 30.0%（Students are to form a group of no more than 4 students. no later than week 4. Students should present a business plan, based on the rubric indicated in class.）；课题论文Research Paper 30.0%（The term paper evaluates students’ ability to independently analyze an entrepreneurial case or issue. • Critical thinking, comprehensive analysis, and clear articulation of ideas are key assessment components.）

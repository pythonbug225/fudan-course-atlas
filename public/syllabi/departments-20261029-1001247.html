属性数据分析

2025秋 · STAT40015.01 · 黎德元

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程主要介绍分析属性数据分析的经典方法与现代方法，帮助学生理解属性数据分析的基本思想与原理，掌握属性数据分析的主要方法，培养学生分析实际数据的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course mainly introduce the classical and morden statistical methods to analyze categorical data. It covers contingency tables, generalized linear models, Logistic linear models, Logit models, loglinear models, models for matched pairs, and generalized linear mixed models. Students are required to use statistical software, such as SAS, R or C, to analyze real data.

教学目标

帮助学生理解属性数据分析的基本思想与原理，掌握属性数据分析的主要方法（列联表，广义线性模型，Logistic回归模型，Logit模型，对数线性模型，配对数据模型，广义线性混合模型等），培养学生分析实际数据的能力。

教学方式

课堂授课、文献阅读并报告、案例分析并报告、作业

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Distributions and Inference for Categorical Data (Ch. 1) Week 2: Describing Contingency Tables (Ch. 2) Week 3: Inference for Contingency Tables (Ch. 3) Week 4,5: Introduction to Generalized Linear Models (Ch. 4) Week 6: Logistic Regression (Ch. 5) Week 7: Building and Applying Logistic Regression Models (Ch. 6) Week 8: Midterm Exam（期中考试） Week 9: Logit Models for Multivariate Responses (Ch. 7) Week 10: Loglinear Models for Contingency Tables (Ch. 8) Week 11: Presentation 1（第一次小组报告） Week 12: Models for Matched Pairs (Ch. 10) Week 13: Analyzing Repeated Categorical Response Data (Ch. 11) Week 14: Random Effects: Generalized Linear Mixed Models (Ch. 12) Week 15-16: Presentation 2（第二次小组报告） Week 17: Final Exam（期末考试）

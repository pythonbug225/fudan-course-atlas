课程简介（中文）

医学微生物学是临床医学、基础医学、法医学专业必修的基础课程，主要包括五个方面的内容：基础细菌学，临床细菌学，基础病毒学，临床病毒学和病原性真菌。基础细菌学和病毒学主要论述细菌和病毒的形态结构、生长繁殖、遗传变异、致病与免疫、实验室诊断和特异性防治等；临床细菌学和病毒学着重描述与医学相关病原菌和病毒的生物学性状，致病性与免疫反应、微生物学检查和防治；病原性真菌主要论述真菌的主要特点及其与医学的关系。

在授课过程中，将微生物学前沿研究热点引入课程讲授，使学生能及时掌握新理论和新技术，熟悉常见微生物的分子生物学知识及防治对策，跟踪前沿技术理论及研究进展，为今后的研究工作打下必要的理论和实验基础。

课程简介（英文）

Medical Microbiology is a compulsory basic course for students majoring in clinical medicine, stomatology, basic medicine, and forensic medicine. It mainly includes five aspects: basic bacteriology, clinical bacteriology, basic virology, clinical virology, and pathogenic fungi. Basic bacteriology and virology mainly discuss the morphological structure, growth and reproduction, genetic variation, pathogenicity and immunity, laboratory diagnosis, and specific prevention and treatment of bacteria and viruses; clinical bacteriology and virology focus on describing the biological characteristics, pathogenicity and immune response, microbiological examination, and prevention and treatment of pathogenic bacteria and viruses related to medicine; pathogenic fungi mainly discuss the main characteristics of fungi and their relationship with medicine.

During the course, we incorporate cutting-edge research topics in microbiology into the teaching, enabling students to grasp new theories and technologies promptly, become familiar with molecular biology knowledge and prevention strategies for common microorganisms, and stay updated with the latest technological theories and research advancements. This lays a necessary theoretical and experimental foundation for their future research endeavors.

教学目标

本课程旨在使医学生在掌握医学微生物学的基本知识、基本理论和基本技术的基础上，拓展医学微生物学的现代诊断和治疗技术以及学科发展的前沿进展，为学习基础医学有关课程及从事微生物所致疾病的诊断和防治工作奠定基础的同时，增强学生解决实际问题的意识和能力，培养高水平医学及医学相关领域领军人才。

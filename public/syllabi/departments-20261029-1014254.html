课程简介（中文）

This class will give students a thorough introduction to quantitative investment strategies for asset management. The last couple of decades have seen tremendous growth in the use of quantitative methods in mutual funds, exchange traded funds (ETF), and hedge funds. The class will cover the most well-known strategies, focusing on the equity market, and provide students with tools to come up with, implement, and evaluate their own quantitative trading strategies. We will also discuss how optimal portfolio choice relates to the quantitative strategies we consider. The class requires willingness to understand and apply statistical methods, calculus and linear algebra. The students will work with actual market data and some coding experience (R, Python, or similar) will be beneficial.

教学目标

1） Introduction to the most prevalent quantitative trading strategies in equities, including value and momentum trading. 2）A thorough introduction to performance evaluation and risk-adjustment using state-of-the-art multifactor risk models. 3） Optimal portfolio choice as it relates to quantitative investment strategies.

教学方式

1）Lectures 2）Mini-cases 3）In-class discussion and attendance

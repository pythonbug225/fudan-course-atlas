博物馆学的相关理论与方法

2025秋 · MUSE50003.01 · 周婧景

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

尽管改革开放以来我国进入博物馆数量高速发展通道，同时各项业务质量也逐步提升。但博物馆学作为一个综合性很强的新兴学科，其针对研究问题的理论和方法探讨还较为薄弱。基于我系研究生在学术论文（如学位论文）等撰写中的理论依据和方法使用尚存较大的提升空间。因此，本课程致力于围绕“博物馆研究问题”，参考相关的人文社科类学科，探究其相关的理论与方法（理论和方法着力于那些已经在博物馆领域得到初步或全面使用的），以期为学生撰写学术论文提供理论和方法上的支撑和启发，以帮助我系研究生提升学术论文撰写的理论水平。

课程简介（英文）

Although since the reform and opening up, China has entered the channel of high-speed development of the number of museums, while the quality of all operations has been gradually improved. However, museology, as an emerging discipline with strong comprehensiveness, is still weak in its theoretical and methodological exploration of research problems. Based on the department's graduate students in the academic papers (such as dissertations) in the writing of the theoretical basis and methodology of the use of a greater room for improvement. Therefore, this course is committed to focusing on “museum research problems”, referring to relevant humanities and social sciences disciplines, exploring their related theories and methods (theories and methods focus on those that have been initially or comprehensively used in the field of museums), with a view to providing students with theoretical and methodological support and inspiration for the preparation of academic papers to help Graduate students in our department to enhance the theoretical level of academic paper writing.

教学目标

本课程希望围绕博物馆中的重要问题，尝试引入和分析人文社科相关的理论与方法，其中方法涉及实务层面的方法和研究层面的方法，以期给我系研究生撰写学术论文时，提供理论与方法的参考和启发，增强我系研究生专业水平。

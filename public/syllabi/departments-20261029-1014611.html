课程简介（中文）

本课程从政治经济学视角对中国发展相关的学术研究进行系统梳理，旨在激发研究生探索本领域内亟待深入研究问题的兴趣。课程从东西方大分流的争论切入，探讨中国为何踏上不同于欧洲的独特发展道路。随后，课程聚焦国家概念的出现及其作用、家庭结构及社会价值观演变等关键主题，帮助学生通过政治经济学的角度理解中国。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers an in-depth overview of literature on China’s trajectory from a political economy perspective. Designed to inspire graduate students, it encourages exploration of understudied areas in the field. The course begins with the Great Divergence debate, examining why China followed a distinct development path compared to Europe. It then focuses on key topics, including the role of the state, the structure of the family, and the evolution of societal values.

教学目标

This course aims to equip students with conceptual frameworks necessary to engage deeply with the historical political economy of China. Students will be trained to critically evaluate existing literature—identifying key questions, methodological choices, and theoretical contributions—while also developing the skills to formulate their own research ideas.

教学方式

阅读、授课、讨论

教学内容与进度安排

Dec 15 Lecture 1: The Great Divergence 1: Introduction to the topic, key debates, and major perspectives in the existing literature. Identify gaps and challenges. Dec 16 Lecture 2: The Great Divergence 2: Continuation of Lecture 1. Class discussion to generate potential research questions. Dec 17 Lecture 3: Institutions 1: Overview of the institutional literature with a focus on foundational institutions in Chinese history. Dec 17 Lecture 4: Institutions 2: In-depth examination of selected research on Chinese bureaucracy. Brainstorm potential research directions. Dec 18 Lecture 5: Culture: Survey of literature on culture and its economic/political implications, with an emphasis on the Chinese context. Dec 19 Lecture 6: Doing research on historical political economy: some suggestions.

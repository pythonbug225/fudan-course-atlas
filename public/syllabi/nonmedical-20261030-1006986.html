中国古代小说与小说理论研究

2025秋 · CHIN70066.01 · 罗书华

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本学期“中国古代小说与理论”课将以 “《红楼梦》脂评本研究”为主要教学对象与内容。梳理过去一百年的版本研究历史，还原《红楼梦》版本演化的真实谱系。以此为契机，培养学生：1）原始文本的细读习惯与能力；2）对知识生成性的认知；3）以及学术质疑的习惯与能力；4）摈除盲从他人及标准答案思维。顺便了解《红楼梦》的思想内容，中国小说与理论历史，以及脂评在中国小说理论史上的地位。

课程简介（英文）

This semester, the course "Ancient Chinese Fiction and Theory" will focus on "A Study of the Zhi Pingben (脂评本) in Dream of the Red Chamber" as its primary teaching subject and content. We will systematically examine the past century of textual research to reconstruct the authentic lineage of Dream of the Red Chamber's version evolution. Through this study, we aim to cultivate in students: The habit and ability to engage in close reading of primary texts; An awareness of the generative nature of knowledge; The habit and capacity for scholarly critique; The ability to reject blind conformity to others' views and the reliance on "standard answers." Additionally, we will explore the philosophical content of Dream of the Red Chamber, the history of Chinese fiction and theory, and the significance of the Zhi Pingben (脂评本) in the development of Chinese literary criticism.

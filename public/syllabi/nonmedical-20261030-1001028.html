卫星互联网

2025秋 · ICE50001.01 · 高跃、陈哲、邱堃

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍卫星互联网的设计原理，主要内容包括：链路预算与规划、调制/编码与多址技术、数字音视频广播、卫星个人通信系统及卫星组网技术。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the design principles of satellite Internet. The topics include link budgets and planning, modulation, coding and multiple access, digital audio/video broadcasting, satellite personal communication systems and satellite networking.

教学目标

Study of design principle of satellite Internet Study of phased array and link budgets Study of satellite-ground baseband Study of satellite network architecture

教学方式

Lectures to provide the fundamental knowledge Class discussion to encourage interaction and participation.

教学内容与进度安排

1. Introduction to Satellite Internet: State of the Art Today (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To understand the current status, key technologies, and development trends of satellite internet, fostering a comprehensive awareness of the field. Satellite Passes, Satellite Beamforming, and Minimum Elevation Angle (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To master the principles of satellite passes, understand beamforming technology, and how the minimum elevation angle affects communication quality. Satellite Link Design, Satellite Radio Channel and Propagation (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To learn satellite link budget and design methods, and gain an in-depth understanding of satellite channel characteristics and signal propagation mechanisms. Experiment-1: Link Budget Measurement (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To reinforce understanding of link budgeting through practical measurement, enhancing practical operational skills. Experiment-2: Beamforming Control (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To practice beamforming technology, learning how to control and optimize satellite antenna beams. Digital Modulation Techniques, Modulation Formats such as PSK, APSK (Dr. Zhe Chen) Course Objective: To understand common digital modulation techniques, mastering the principles and applications of modulation methods like PSK and APSK. Bit Error Rate in Digital Modulation (Dr. Zhe Chen) Course Objective: To comprehend the concept of bit error rate, analyzing the impact of different modulation methods on bit error rate. Satellite Multiple Access Methods: Review of FDMA, TDMA and CDMA in a Fixed Satellite Network and MF-TDMA (Dr. Zhe Chen) Course Objective: To study multiple access technologies in satellite communications, comparing the advantages, disadvantages, and application scenarios of various access methods. Experiment-3: Channel Gain Measurement (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To understand how channel characteristics affect communication performance by measuring channel gain. Experiment-4: Modulation/Channel Coding (Dr. Zhe Chen) Course Objective: To practice modulation and channel coding techniques, enhancing understanding of improving communication system reliability. Satellite Networks and Constellations (Dr. Kun Qiu) Course Objective: To learn about satellite network architectures and constellation design, studying their roles in global communications. Protocol Design and Challenges in Satellite Networking (Dr. Kun Qiu) Course Objective: To explore the design principles of satellite network protocols, understanding the technical challenges and solutions. Satellite Simulation Platforms and Routing Algorithm Design (Dr. Kun Qiu) Course Objective: To master the use of satellite network simulation tools, designing and evaluating routing algorithms. Experiment-5: Constellations Construction in Digital Twin (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To practice digital twin technology by constructing virtual satellite constellations and simulating their operation. Experiment-6: Satellite Network Routing (Dr. Kun Qiu) Course Objective: To practice satellite network routing protocols, understanding the impact of routing strategies on network performance. Future of Satellite Communications: Update on Current Innovations and New Systems Proposals (Prof. Yue Gao) Course Objective: To learn about the latest innovations and future development directions in satellite communications, fostering forward-thinking skills.

课程简介（中文）

工程伦理与研究方法是一门思政与价值塑造课程，培养研究生的创新思维、创造精神和精益求精的大国工匠精神，激发科技报国的家国情怀和使命担当，响应“四个面向”的号召主动服务国家。进行工程伦理、职业道德与学术规范方面的学习，注重学生创新研究的调研总结和提出问题的能力培养，工程开发技巧和经验传授，文本写作、汇报演讲与团队协作能力锻炼。

课程简介（英文）

Engineering Ethics and Research Methodology is an ideological and political course oriented toward value shaping. It aims to cultivate postgraduates' innovative thinking, creative spirit, and the craftsmanship spirit of excellence for a great country, inspire their patriotism and sense of mission to serve the nation through science and technology, and respond to the call of the "Four Orientations" to take the initiative in serving national development needs. The course covers the study of engineering ethics, professional ethics, and academic norms, emphasizes the cultivation of students' abilities to conduct research summaries, identify research problems in innovative studies, imparts skills and experience in engineering development, and hones their competencies in academic writing, presentation speeches, and team collaboration.

教学目标

通过课程学习，培养专业学位研究生的创新思维、创造精神和精益求精的大国工匠精神，激发科技报国的家国情怀和使命担当，响应“四个面向”的号召主动服务国家。培养创新研究的调研总结和提出问题的能力，强化文本写作、汇报演讲与团队协作能力锻炼。

教学方式

理论教学、案例讲解、实践汇报等

课程简介（英文）

This course represents the rst in a series of econometric classes required for the completion of your masters program. This course introduces you to a range of fundamental statistical methodology and econometric techniques widely used by nancial professionals in the empirical work that requires rigorous data analysis, systematic testing of theories, or identi cation of causal effects for economic forecasting. We will begin with the foundational concepts of probability  and statistics, before dedicating the majority of our time to classical simple and multivariate regression analysis, encompassing both quantitative and qualitative variables. Additionally, we will explore the practical implications of relaxing various classical assumptions. Time permitting, we will also address certain topics in time series analysis, which will provide insights that are pertinent to your subsequent, more advanced econometrics courses. As the inaugural econometrics course in the curriculum, this course is designed to establish a solid foundation in essential econometric theories and methods that will facilitate the future development of advanced techniques and more sophisticated applications for quantitatively analyzing nancial markets. Nevertheless, this course is intended to equip you with comprehensive econometric training that will enhance your pro ciency and professionalism in empirical and analytical tasks throughout your future career. To achieve this objective, you are required to become pro cient in at least one programming software capable of managing large-scale nancial data and developing customized computational algorithms.

教学目标

To acquire and internalize knowledge of statistical methods used by economists and financial professionals. To develop the ability to discern which method is most appropriate in a given situation, and understand the limitations of the chosen method. To acquire the skills to apply these methods in a variety of contexts and with a variety of instruments. To familiarize at least one programming software to implement various econometric tasks and numerical algorithms to the nancial data.

结构生物学

2026春 · BIOL50034.02 · 冯喆、服部素之

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

结构生物学是一门以生物大分子的空间结构及其与生物功能之间的关系为核心，揭示生命过程分子机制的科学。近年来，结构生物学发展迅速，已成为分子生物学乃至整个生命科学领域的前沿引领学科，对药物设计、疾病机制解析以及生物技术创新等方面产生了深远影响。本课程将系统讲授结构生物学的基础知识、核心理论与主要研究方法，介绍该领域的最新成果与前沿进展。同时，课程将探讨人工智能在结构生物学中的创新应用，展示AI驱动下该学科的发展方向与未来挑战。课程旨在帮助学生建立系统的知识框架，深刻理解分子结构与功能的内在联系，培养运用先进方法解决生命科学问题的综合能力。

课程简介（英文）

Structural biology is a science that reveals the molecular mechanisms of life processes by focusing on the three-dimensional structures of biological macromolecules and their relationships to biological functions. In recent years, structural biology has developed rapidly and has become a frontier and leading discipline in molecular biology and even across the life sciences, exerting profound impacts on drug design, elucidation of disease mechanisms, and biotechnological innovation. This course will systematically cover the fundamental knowledge, core theories, and major research methods of structural biology, and introduce the latest achievements and advances at the cutting edge of the field. In addition, the course will explore innovative applications of artificial intelligence in structural biology, highlighting emerging directions and future challenges driven by AI. The course aims to help students build a systematic knowledge framework, gain a deep understanding of the intrinsic links between molecular structure and function, and develop comprehensive abilities to address life-science problems using advanced approaches.

教学目标

Through the teaching which is integrated by imparting knowledge, cultivating ability and improving quality, students can not only learn the knowledge of structural biology, but also greatly improve their ability and potential. With better comprehensive potential, high learning interest, solid knowledge foundation and good learning state, they can then better prepare for the postgraduate courses.

教学方式

The teaching will be provided with distinct gradation and accurate expression, and highlight the key points, integrate theory with practice, and will be vivid and interesting, and inspire thinking. Adaptation of multimedia and other modern educational technology to assist teaching will be employed to enrich classroom teaching and to improve teaching level and quality. Attention should be paid to the reform of each teaching link and the organic connection between each teaching link during the teaching process.

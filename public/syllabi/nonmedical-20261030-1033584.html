中国对外贸易法

2026春 · LAW60076.01 · 梁咏

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程属于国际商法LLM班学生学习中的学位专业课，是一门实践性和时效性较高的课程。主要包括：对外贸易法概论和中国对外贸易法的法律渊源、中国对外贸易法重点问题和最新热点问题，以及双边和多边体制及其未来发展。本课程特别注意多角度、多层次思考，并将理论探讨、规则研读和案例研究有机结合，帮助学生了解中国对外贸易法理论与实践的特性，并为运用相关规则维护合法权益夯实基础。本课程的教学中将根据学生的特点，重点关注国际视野和国内视野的对接。从国际视角看，将特别关注WTO危机对国际贸易规则带来的影响、《美墨加协定》（USMCA）、《全面与进步的跨太平洋伙伴关系协定》（CPTPP）发展趋势以及中国主导的《区域贸易伙伴协定》（RCEP）、中欧投资协定（CAI）、“一带一路”倡议下国际贸易争端解决；从国内视角看，将特别关注贸易纠纷中的经典问题和热点问题，结合实例从合同设计、风险预防、争端解决等各方面进行思考和完善。

课程简介（英文）

The course is a compulsory course LLM students in International Commercial Law Program. It mainly includes the introduction to international trade law and legal sources of Chinese foreign trade law, key issues and latest host issues of Chinese foreign trade law, as well as bilateral and multilateral systems and their future developments. This course concentrates on diversified aspects, intentionally combines theoretical research, textual interpretation and case study to help students to acknowledge the characteristics of the theory and practice of China's foreign trade law, as well as lays a solid foundation for the use of relevant rules to safeguard legitimate rights and interests. The contents and teaching will focus on the connection between international vision and domestic vision according to the characteristics of students. From the international perspective, the course will focus on the crisis of the WTO’s influence on the international trade rules, the developments of the U.S.-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA), the Comprehensive and Progressive Trans-Pacific Partnership Agreement (CPTPP) and China’s promotion in the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP), China-EU Agreement on Investment (CAI) as well as the international trade dispute settlement under “B&R Initiative”. From the domestic perspective, the course will explore classical issues and hot issues in trade disputes, and will make consideration and improvements from the contract design, risk prevention, dispute settlement and other aspects.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，使学生了解中国对外贸易法的基本体系和主要制度，特别是中国近年来在对外贸易法领域缔结的国际条约、参与的国际谈判以及目前实践中面临的法律问题。尤其关注2018年以来美国对华采取的一系列贸易限制措施等可能产生的变化及其应对，关注国内法和国际法之间的互动，培养学生分析问题和解决问题的能力。 The course will be beneficial for students having a better understanding of the basic system and main provisions of Chinese foreign trade law, especially the international treaties concluded by China, the international negotiations that China has participated in and the legal problems in practice in the recent years. In particular, it pays great attention to the possible changes caused by the series of trade restraints adopted by U.S. against China since 2018 and China’s responses, concentrates on the interactions between domestic laws and international laws and cultivates student’s ability to analyze and solve problems.

教学方式

授课过程中将按照选课人数安排1~2次案例讨论（建议学生可以单独或者组队参加），通过学生自己讲演、讨论，推进学生自主学习和主动性思考，达到一定“翻转式课堂”教学目的，主讲教师进行简要点评，进行必要引导。 During the course, 1–2 case study sessions will be arranged based on the number of enrolled students (students are encouraged to participate individually or in teams). Through student-led presentations and discussions, this format aims to promote self-directed learning and active thinking, achieving a certain degree of "flipped classroom" teaching objectives. The instructor will provide brief comments and offer necessary guidance.

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍量子化，这是现代数学物理的一个基本概念。本课程结合代数技术与量子场论的见解，解释量子化的不同形式及它们之间的关系。虽然量子化需要额外的结构，并且通常难以系统地进行，但变形量子化是一个系统的过程。变形量子化理论已经导致了一些美丽的结果，比如孔采维奇的形式。在讨论了量化的各种主题之后，我们转向代数指标定理。众所周知，伪微分算子环可以看作是余切束上的函数的变形代数。将变形量化的迹表示为拓扑指标的代数指标定理可以看作是Atiyah-Singer指标定理的阿尔及利亚形式。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers an introduction to quantization, a fundamental concept in modern mathematical physics. Combining algebraic techniques with insights from quantum field theory, the course explains different formalisms of quantization and relations between them. While quantization requires additional structures and is hard to proceed systematically in general, deformation quantization is a systematic procedure. The theory of deformation quantization has led to beautiful results such as Kontsevich’s formality. After various topics in quantization, we move to the algebraic index theorem. It is well known that the ring of pseudifferential operators can be viewed as a deformed algebra of functions on the cotangent bundle. The algebraic index theorem, which expresses the trace of the deformation quantization as a topological index, can be seen as the algerbaic form of the Atiyah-Singer index theorem.

教学目标

Gain acquaintance with the basic notions of Hilbert spaces, geometric quantization, deformation quantization, and the algebraic index. Apply different formalisms to construct (deformation) quantizations and explicitly construct traces.

课程简介（中文）

本课程为一学期制国际经济学课程。上半学期，我们将深入研习经典贸易模型，剖析国家间跨境贸易的核心动因；继而聚焦贸易政策，探讨贸易壁垒、国际贸易协定等关键政策工具的内涵与应用。下半学期的教学重心将转向汇率动态：我们将系统探究汇率的决定机制，以及不同汇率制度的运行原理。同时，课程会搭建课堂理论模型与前沿研究之间的桥梁，激发同学们对国际经济学领域研究的热情。

课程简介（英文）

This is a one-semester course in International Economics. In the first half, we will delve into classic trade models to unpack the core rationale behind cross-border trade between economies. We will then introduce trade policies, such as trade barriers and international trade agreements. The second half of the course shifts focus to the dynamics of exchange rates: we will explore how exchange rates are determined, as well as the operational mechanisms of different exchange rate regimes. Additionally, we will bridge the gap between the theoretical models covered in class and cutting-edge research papers, aiming to ignite students’ enthusiasm for research in the field of International Economics.

教学目标

This course aims to help students have a deeper understanding of International Trade and International Macroeconomics, and prepare students for advanced study in International Economics. By the end of the semester students are expected to Understand the fundamental mechanism and classic models in International Trade and International Macroeconomics Apply what have been learned in class to analyze real-world issues and evaluate policies in international economics

教学方式

90% Lecture 10% Group presentation at the end of the semester

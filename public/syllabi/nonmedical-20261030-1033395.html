统计信号处理

课程简介（中文）

本课程研究统计模型下信号的参数估计、滤波及重构问题。最早从雷达、通信、声纳等电子信号处理系统中产生、发展起来，目前其应用已涉及到几乎所有科技领域。其研究方法是把所要处理的问题，归纳为一定的数学模型，再运用概率论、随机过程、数理统计等数学工具进行综合分析，完成基于给定假设下的估计、滤波及重构方案的选择，给出统计意义下估计及重构性能的评估，以及各种估计及重构性能界的优劣对比。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the parameter estimation, filtering, and reconstruction of signals under statistical models. It originated and developed from electronic signal processing systems such as radar, communication, and sonar. Currently, its applications have extended to almost all fields of science and technology. The research method involves summarizing the problem to be addressed into a certain mathematical model, then using mathematical tools such as probability theory, stochastic processes, and mathematical statistics for comprehensive analysis. This enables the selection of estimation, filtering, and reconstruction schemes based on given assumptions, providing evaluations of the estimation and reconstruction performance in statistical terms, as well as comparisons of the advantages and disadvantages of various estimation and reconstruction performance bounds.

教学目标

通过课程学习，使得学生能够对带参信号的估计、滤波及重构问题得到清晰、深入的理解，熟练掌握随机接收信号情形下典型估计问题的建模及求解，能够对不同估计方案的性能优劣完成统计意义下的分析评价，对工程中的估计问题能够进行简单的提炼和分析。

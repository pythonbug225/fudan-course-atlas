书报讨论（生物物理）

2025秋 · BIOL70013.01 · 付玉华

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

该课程主要讲授生物学研究前沿领域（自噬、多潜能干细胞、神经退行疾病、脂代谢和脑类器官及靶向降解技术等）的文献（包括研究论文和研究方法及评述），文献选取主要为近5年（个别经典文献为近10年）发表在主刊和大子刊水平论文。致力于培养学生关注和精读自己感兴趣文献的方法、培养学生吸收和学习关键文献的思路与策略、培养学生剖析和提炼及讲解分享文献的能力。总体目标：会选，会读，会用，会讲的四大目标，提升研究生的科研认知和专研水平。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the critical reading of cutting-edge literature in biological research (including autophagy, pluripotent stem cells, neurodegenerative diseases, lipid metabolism, brain organoids, targeted degradation technologies, etc.). The selected publications—comprising research papers, methodologies, and reviews—are primarily drawn from top-tier journals and their major sub-journals published within the last five years (with select seminal works from the last decade). It aims to cultivate students’ abilities to: Identify and intensively study literature aligned with their research interests; Digest and learn key research concepts and strategies from publications; Analyze, synthesize, and effectively present scientific literature. Overall Objectives: Achieve "Four Masteries" in scientific literature—selecting, reading, applying, and presenting—to enhance graduate students’ scientific literacy and research expertise.

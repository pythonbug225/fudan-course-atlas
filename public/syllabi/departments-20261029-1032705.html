课程简介（中文）

《货币银行学》主要内容包括：一是传统模型框架下的poole模型、Barro-Gordon模型等介绍，涉及货币政策目标的选择、通货膨胀的变化和时间的不一致性。二是在拉姆齐模型的理论框架中融入货币因素的分析，包括MIU模型、CIA模型、Shopping time模型等，考察宏观经济变量的变动和货币政策的效果。三是在实际经济周期模型的框架下，融入货币考察弹性价格下和粘性价格下的宏观经济变量在不同冲击下的变动轨迹，以及货币政策的效果，包括弹性价格的DSGE模型、粘性价格的DSGE模型。三是介绍开放经济下的DSGE模型，考察货币政策变动对汇率等因素的影响。同时该课程还将介绍dynare软件，包括一些简单代码的编写以及一些程序的运行操作。

课程简介（英文）

The main contents of this course include the following aspects: An introduction about relative models under the traditional framework, such as the Poole Model and the Barro-Gordon Model, involving the selection of monetary policy objectives, variations in inflation, and the issue of time inconsistency. An analysis that incorporates monetary factors into the theoretical framework of the Ramsey Model, including the Money-in-Utility (MIU) Model, the Cash-in-Advance (CIA) Model, and the Shopping Time Model, to examine the fluctuations of macroeconomic variables and the effects of monetary policy. An exploration that integrates monetary factors into the Real Business Cycle (RBC) Model framework, to study the dynamic trajectories of macroeconomic variables under flexible prices and sticky prices in response to various shocks, as well as the effects of monetary policy. This part covers the flexible-price Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium (DSGE) Model and the sticky-price DSGE Model. An introduction to the DSGE Model in an open economy context, to investigate the impact of monetary policy adjustments on factors such as exchange rates. In addition, the course will also cover the Dynare software, including the writing of simple codes and the operation procedures of related programs.

教学目标

通过该课程的学习，基本能够掌握poole模型、Barro-Gordon模型、MIU模型、CIA模型、Shopping time模型、实际经济周期模型、弹性价格下和粘性价格下的DSGE模型、开放经济下的DSGE模型等主要思想和内容，能够阅读和理解中英文期刊货币理论和政策领域的相关论文，能够掌握构建简单的货币经济学领域模型的方法，特别是一些简单的DSGE模型。逐步掌握dynare软件，能够编写和运行简单模型的程序，包括DSGE模型。能够运用所学的相关理论和方法分析国内外的一些现实问题，并进行数值模拟研究。

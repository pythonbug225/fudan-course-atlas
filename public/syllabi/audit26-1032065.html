课程简介（中文）

《护理领域量性研究的分析方法》是一门面向护理学硕士、博士研究生的研究生专业选修课，3 学分、共 54 学时，春季学期开设。课程聚焦护理研究中常见的问卷/量表与二次数据分析场景，系统训练从数据预处理到统计建模、再到结果解释与科研写作的完整分析链条，强调“可复现的分析思路 + 可落地的工具实操”。

课程目标包括：理解量性研究中数据预处理、统计描述与推断的关键逻辑；掌握回归、分类与降维、面板数据分析与预测模型等常用方法；并能结合护理研究中常见的潜变量、混杂因素与连续变量等数据特征，独立选择合适的数据来源与统计策略开展分析实践。建议修读者已完成《医学统计学》《护理研究》和/或《循证护理学》等课程。

课程内容覆盖：量表心理测量学指标分析、样本量计算与 PASS 应用、数据录入与清洗；回归/广义线性回归与多变量回归（混杂控制）；路径分析与结构方程模型；聚类/判别、探索性与验证性因子分析；潜在类别/潜在剖面模型；面板数据因果推断、重复测量分析、静态/动态面板与 GMM；以及临床预测模型、复杂网络分析、孟德尔随机化等专题，帮助学生形成“选题—数据—方法—解释—写作”的闭环能力。

教学采用理论讲授 + 案例解析 + 课堂工具实训 + 小组汇报的混合模式。考核由课堂出勤（20%）、小组讨论与汇报（30%）、课程论文及汇报（50%）构成，强调以指定或公开数据完成可解释、可复现的完整分析报告。

课程简介（英文）

Quantitative Data Analysis Methods & Techniques in Nursing Science is an elective graduate-level course designed for master’s and doctoral nursing students (3 credits; 54 contact hours), offered in the spring semester. The course focuses on common nursing research scenarios involving questionnaires/scales and secondary data analysis, providing an end-to-end analytic workflow from data preprocessing and model implementation to interpretation and scientific reporting, with an emphasis on reproducible thinking and hands-on skills.

Upon completion, students will be able to: (1) understand the core logic of data preprocessing, descriptive statistics, and statistical inference; (2) apply widely used methods such as regression, classification/dimension reduction, panel-data analysis, and predictive modeling; and (3) independently select suitable data sources and analytic strategies for nursing datasets characterized by latent constructs, confounding, and predominantly continuous variables. Prior coursework in Medical Statistics, Nursing Research, and/or Evidence-Based Nursing is recommended.

Key topics include psychometric analyses of measurement scales, sample size calculation (with PASS), and data entry/cleaning; linear and generalized linear models and multivariable regression (confounding control); path analysis and structural equation modeling; clustering/discriminant analysis, exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis; latent class/profile models; panel-data causal inference, repeated-measures analysis, static/dynamic panel models and GMM; and applied modules on clinical prediction modeling, complex network analysis, and Mendelian randomization. Multiple group-based secondary data analysis presentations and a final paper-plus-presentation help students build an integrated capability chain from study question and data to methods, interpretation, and writing.

Teaching is delivered through lectures, case-based discussions, in-class software/tool training, and group presentations. Assessment includes attendance (20%), group discussions/presentations (30%), and a group project paper plus presentation (50%), emphasizing a transparent, interpretable, and reproducible analysis report using designated or public datasets.

教学目标

知识目标——通过本课程学习，要求学生理解问卷与量表、二次数据的数据预处理、统计描述、统计推断中回归和分类降维、面板数据、预测模型等研究的分析思路和分析方法。

能力目标——学生通过本课程的学习，能掌握护理研究领域中潜变量多、混杂因素多、连续变量多的数据特征，能够对各类不同的研究问题，选择合适的数据来源和统计方法进行分析，并开展分析实践。

思政目标——通过本课程的学习，学生加深对护理领域中常用量性研究分析方法的理解，体会各类研究不同分析思路在提升护理领域科学决策中的作用，加强职业认同感，深化专业内涵理解，提升专业自信。

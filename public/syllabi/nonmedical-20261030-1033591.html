课程简介（中文）

Contract law is one of the basic Chinese commercial law subjects. The Contract Law is divided into two parts: general provisions and specific provisions. The general provisions are deemed as the core of the Contract Law, while the specific provisions provide detailed rules applied to each particular type of Contract. During the semester, we will focus on certain subjects of the law of contract, such as formation, effectiveness, defenses, performance, assignment, breach and remedies, as well as third parties.

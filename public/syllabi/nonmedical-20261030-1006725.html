适合大学高年级或研究生初学者的代数入门。

课程简介（英文）

An introduction to algebra suitable for upper-level undergraduate or beginning graduate courses。

教学目标

简单介绍集合论，范畴，群，环，模。通过比较现代的数学语言，先从范畴论的语言入手，建立范畴论视角下的代数学习基础。在简单介绍群环模的基础之后，重点证明一些群伦的基本定理，为学习高阶代数打下基础。 A brief introduction to set theory, categories, groups, rings, and modules. By comparing the modern mathematical language, we will start with the language of category theory and establish the foundations of algebraic learning from a category theory perspective. After a brief introduction to the foundations of groups, rings, and modules, the focus will be on proving some basic group and ring theorems to lay the foundation for learning higher-order algebra.

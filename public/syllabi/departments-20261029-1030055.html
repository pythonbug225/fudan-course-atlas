课程简介（中文）

本课程有助于学生理解开放经济条件下国际经济活动规律，涉及国际贸易、跨国公司和国际投资、国际金融和国际宏观经济政策协调等内容。本课程基于中国经济对外开放事实以及经济全球化和国际经济学发展脉络，集中讲解国际贸易理论和政策、汇率决定和变动、国际收支调节方法、开放经济下宏观经济政策、国际货币体系等内容。 通过本课程学习，学生可以基于国际贸易理论、国际投资理论、贸易政策理论、汇率决定与汇率制度理论、国际收支理论、国际宏观经济政策协调理论等，运用现代经济学分析方法，结合宏观和微观经济数据，对中国与世界经济中的贸易、跨国投资、中美贸易摩擦、国际收支失衡、汇率超调、货币区及人民币国际化等现象进行阐释，对国际经济运行态势进行研判，进而能够对中国与世界经济中的重要问题开展学术研究。

课程简介（英文）

This course helps students understand the laws governing international economic activities under the context of an open economy, covering areas such as international trade, transnational corporations and international investment, international finance, and the coordination of international macroeconomic policies. Based on the facts of China’s economic opening-up, as well as the context of economic globalization and the development of international economics, the course focuses on explaining theories and policies of international trade, the determination and fluctuation of exchange rates, methods for balancing international payments, macroeconomic policies in an open economy, and the international monetary system. Through studying this course, relying on theories including those of international trade, international investment, trade policies, exchange rate determination and exchange rate systems, international balance of payments, and coordination of international macroeconomic policies, students will be able to adopt modern economic analysis methods and integrate macroeconomic and microeconomic data. They can then explain phenomena in China’s and the global economy such as trade activities, cross-border investment, China-US trade frictions, international balance of payments imbalances, exchange rate overshooting, currency areas, and the internationalization of the RMB. Furthermore, students will be capable of assessing the operational trends of the international economy and thereby conducting academic research on key issues in both China’s and the global economy.

教学目标

本课程教学以习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，讲授国际经济学基本知识点和一般原理，结合中国具体事例进行国情世情教育，培养学生学习和掌握国际经济学基本知识点和一般原理，能够应用于分析和解决中国具体事例问题。

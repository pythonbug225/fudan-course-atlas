自守L函数导论

课程简介（中文）

首先，我们将回顾Tate的论文，该论文用阿德尔语言重新表述并证明了Dedekind L-函数的性质。我们将回顾必要的背景知识。 接着，我们将研究GL(2)的局部表示和自守表示。 我们将进一步定义并证明它们的L-函数的一些解析性质。 然后，我们将考虑上述情况的四元数情形，并观察GL(2)的自守表示与其四元数内形式之间的关系，这一关系称为Jacquet-Langlands对应。 最后，我们将阐述GL(2)的局部和全局Langlands猜想的一个版本，并讨论该问题的现状。

课程简介（英文）

First we will review Tate’s thesis, which reformulates and reproves properties of Dedekind L-functions in the adelic language. We will recall the necessary background. Then we will study local and automorphic representations of GL(2). We will further define and prove some analytic properties of their L-functions. We will then consider the quaternionic case of the above situation, and observe a relation between and automorphic representations of GL(2) and its quaternionic inner forms, called the Jacquet-Langlands correspondence. We will finally state a version of local and global Langlands conjectures for GL(2), and discuss where the problem stands.

教学目标

The purpose of this course is to become familiar with smooth representation theory of local and global reductive groups, and to see how such theories can be applied to solve problems of number theoretic nature.

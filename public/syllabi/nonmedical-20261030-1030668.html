美国文学史

2026春 · FORE20005.01 · 张琼

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

通过对美国文学史历时性的系列课堂讲座，自本土裔声音、殖民时期直到后现代主义文学，让学生们对各重要历史时期的文学发展，主要流派，代表作家和作品有概述性了解，从而总体了解美国文学的发生、发展脉络及前景。

课程简介（英文）

The course consists of a series of lectures on the history of American literature, ranging from the colonial American literature to the postwar literature.

教学目标

The course aims at introducing to the students the historical & cultural background of American literature, and laying a necessary contextual base for their further critical reading and research of American literature.

教学方式

lecture series and class discussion

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction to the course and early Native Voice Week 2: Colonial America and New Nationhood Week 3: American Transcendentalism (1) Week 4: American Transcendentalism (2) Week 5: The Age of Realism and Local Colorism Week 6: American Realism and Naturalism Week 7: American Imagism and Postwar Poetry (1) Week 8: American Imagism and Postwar Poetry (2) Week 9: Mid-term Q & A Week 10: Fitzgerald and Hemingway Week 11: American West and Mid-West Week 12: American Southern Writing Week 13: American Drama Week 14: Fiction: 1945 to the Present (1) Week 15: Fiction: 1945 to the Present (2) Week 16: Diversity and Multiethnic Writing Week 17: Course wind-up and review Week 18: Final Exam

教学参考资料

Richard Grayauth, A Brief History of American Literature. Wiley-Blackwell, 2011 Nina Baym, General Editor, The Norton Anthology of American Literature (2 volumes). W.W. Norton & Company, 1998 常耀信, 《美国文学简史》, 南开大学出版社, 2006

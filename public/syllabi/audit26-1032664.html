课程简介（英文）

This course primarily introduces the concepts and a brief history of single-molecule science, single-molecule measurement methods and their advantages, single-molecule reaction dynamics, single-molecule optical imaging and tracking, single-molecule mechanics and non-equilibrium thermodynamics, as well as the applications of single-molecule research in interdisciplinary frontiers.

课程简介（中文）

本课程是一门高阶方法类课程，面向希望深化定量研究技能的研究生开设，为学生接触当代社会科学研究中前沿的定量方法搭建了桥梁。课程涵盖广泛主题：首先训练常用统计模型，如广义线性模型（GLM）系列与双向固定效应模型；随后深入探讨因果推断的进阶技术，包括断点回归与广义合成控制法；此外，课程还涉及近年兴起的分析方法，如网络分析与调查实验。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an advanced methodological course designed for graduate students who wish to deepen their quantitative research skills, serving as a bridge for students to access cutting-edge quantitative methods in contemporary social science research. The course covers a wide range of topics: it first trains students in commonly used statistical models, such as the Generalized Linear Model (GLM) family and two-way fixed effects models; then delves into advanced techniques for causal inference, including regression discontinuity and generalized synthetic control methods; additionally, the course also addresses analytical approaches that have emerged in recent years, such as network analysis and survey experiments.

教学目标

By the conclusion of this course, students can expect to accomplish the following learning objectives: (1) grasp the fundamentals of statistical models commonly used in both social scientific research and various industries; (2) develop a critical eye for assessing the quality of quantitative analyses presented in academic journals and media outlets; (3) proficiently utilize R for data processing and quantitative analysis, (4) apply statistical models to address a diverse array of questions in their future research.

教学方式

This course uses a theory-practice integrated model to balance knowledge and skill building, while easing potential student anxiety around quantitative work. Theoretical content is delivered via lectures, starting with foundational statistical models (e.g., OLS, GLM) and advancing to causal inference (regression discontinuity, synthetic control) and emerging approaches (network analysis). Real social science research cases link abstract methods to practical scenarios. Early in the course, instructors normalize common challenges (e.g., R coding errors) and highlight the course’s value (e.g., enabling independent research) to boost confidence. Practical training centers on R software: lab tutorials teach data processing, modeling, and visualization. Weekly assignments (75% of the grade) combine conceptual questions (testing theory) and software tasks (applying methods). Later, students complete an independent quantitative research project (25% of the grade), using course methods for original or replicatory work to integrate theory into practice.

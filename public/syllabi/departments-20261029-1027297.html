课程简介（中文）

供应链管理已成为企业获取竞争优势的关键领域之一。在当前产品种类日益丰富、产品生命周期缩短、外包业务增加、商业全球化以及信息技术持续进步的商业趋势下，管理供应链是一项复杂且充满挑战的任务。此外，作为运营管理的自然延伸，供应链管理不仅是进一步优化运营的根本驱动力，也是增强企业竞争地位的核心要素。包括互联网在内的信息技术巨大进步，既催生了改进供应链管理的迫切需求，也为其创造了更多机遇。企业进军新市场或采用新技术时，必须确保其供应链能够应对新的商业挑战并把握发展机遇。

课程简介（英文）

Supply chain management has emerged as one of the major areas for companies to gain a competitive edge. Managing supply chains is a complex and challenging task, given the current business trends of expanding product variety, short product life cycles, increasing outsourcing, globalization of business, and continuous advances in information technology. Furthermore, supply chain management, as a natural successor to operations management, is a fundamental driver not only for further improving operations but also for strengthening the competitive position of a company. The enormous progress in information technology, including the Internet, has contributed to both the increasing needs and opportunities for improved supply chain management. Companies moving into new markets or new technologies must have their supply chains prepared for the new business challenges and opportunities.

教学目标

The course will introduce you to concepts and techniques related to the design, planning, control, and improvement of supply chains and supply chain operations. More specifically, the course attempts to: Make you conversant in the language of supply chain management and expand your confidence in these areas. Allow you to see the role of supply chain management in the overall strategy and performance of the firm by providing a conceptual, strategic view of supply chain design and operations. Analyze the underlying issues and trade-offs between the sourcing of raw materials, manufacturing, storage, transportation of products, and the services required to fulfill customer expectations and the metrics of the firm. Provide you with quantitative and qualitative tools to identify, analyze, and manage supply chain operations and issues. At the end of the course, you should have gained an improved understanding of: How supply chain strategy can be aligned with business strategy. The dynamic challenges in supply chain management. The competitive potential of superior supply chain management. and acquired the skills to: Analyze supply chains and supply chain operations to identify problems and improvement opportunities. Recommend and initiate supply chain improvement actions and projects. Enhance your team working and project management skills.

教学方式

The course is a mix of lectures, case studies, and games. The lectures are meant to explain supply chain models and concepts using both qualitative and quantitative methods. Qualitative methods involve theoretical frameworks, conceptual analysis, and evaluation of solution strategies, whereas quantitative methods involve mathematical analysis, probability theory, and basic concepts of microeconomics. Case studies depict a business scenario at a certain existing company, in which complex supply chain management decisions need to be made. The situation is often partly described from the perspective of one of the key players in the decision-making process; furthermore, a certain degree of urgency is present as well. A teaching case requires very thorough preparation by the participant.

教学内容与进度安排

This course will cover the following topics in sequence: Session 1.    Introduction to Supply Chain Management: Overview of fundamental concepts and importance of supply chain management. Session 2.    Supply Chain Forecasting: Techniques and methods for predicting future demand in supply chains. Session 3.    Planning & Managing Inventories: Strategies for effective inventory control and management. Session 4.    Global Transportation Networks: Examination of international logistics and transportation systems. Session 5.    Supply Chain Contracts and Coordination: Analysis of contracts and coordination mechanisms within supply chains. Session 6.    Strategic Alliance: Exploration of partnerships and alliances to enhance supply chain performance. Session 7.    Manufacturing Strategies: Discussion on various manufacturing strategies and their impact on supply chains. Session 8.    Facility Logistics Management: Principles of managing logistics within and between facilities. Session 9.    Procurement and Outsourcing: Strategies for sourcing and managing external suppliers and services. Session 10.    Supply Chain Risk & Finance: Identifying and managing financial risks in supply chains. Session 11.    Revenue Management and Smart Pricing: Approaches to optimizing revenue through strategic pricing. Session 12.    Supply Chain Game: Interactive session simulating supply chain dynamics and decision-making. Session 13.    Game Discussion and the Value of Information: Debriefing on the supply chain game and the importance of information flow. Session 14.    Supply Chain Management and IT Technologies: Role of information technology in enhancing supply chain operations. Session 15.    New Challenges in SCM: Discussion on emerging trends and challenges in supply chain management. Session 16.    Wrap-up Course: Summary and review of key concepts covered throughout the course.

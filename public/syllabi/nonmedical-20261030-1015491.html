东南亚政治与对外关系

2025秋 · POLI40004.01 · 贺嘉洁

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

东南亚是中国的近邻，也是连接太平洋与印度洋、亚洲与大洋洲的战略要地，它的发展以及与外部世界的关系既影响着中国的周边环境，决定了亚太地区的秩序与稳定。东南亚又是一个非常多样化的区域，域内国家在历史文化、宗教传统、政治制度以及经济发展各个方面呈现出极其不同的特点，但同时又发展出了一定程度的区域主义和规范，并塑造着区域内的国际关系。尽管东南亚在世界体系中并不处于中心位置，但是它的地理位置、与外界交往的历史和当代国际关系的实践为我们理解西方中心以外的国际关系提供了绝佳的范例，也是我们全面了解中国周边不可或缺的一环。 本课程主要介绍东南亚国际关系的历史与现实，外部势力的渗透和竞争对区域国家的冲击，全球化背景下区域国家所面临的挑战，以及东南亚自身的区域主义和国际关系实践对国际关系理论的贡献。本课程希望培养学生们对东南亚的兴趣，为他们今后进一步研究这一区域打下扎实的基础。

课程简介（英文）

Southeast Asia is China’s close neighbor and a strategic hub linking the Pacific and Indian Oceans as well as Asia and Oceania. Its development and relations with the outside world not only shape China’s immediate environment but also influence the order and stability of the broader Asia-Pacific region. At the same time, Southeast Asia is highly diverse: countries in the region differ significantly in their histories, cultures, religious traditions, political systems, and levels of economic development. Yet they have also cultivated a certain degree of regionalism and shared norms, which in turn shape regional international relations. Although Southeast Asia does not occupy a central place in the global system, its geographical position, its history of engagement with the wider world, and its contemporary international practices provide an excellent example for understanding international relations beyond a Western-centric framework. It is also an indispensable component in comprehensively grasping China’s surrounding environment. This course introduces the history and contemporary dynamics of international relations in Southeast Asia, examines the impact of external powers’ penetration and competition on regional states, explores the challenges faced by these states under globalization, and analyzes the contributions of Southeast Asian regionalism and international practices to the study of international relations theory. The course aims to cultivate students’ interest in Southeast Asia and to lay a solid foundation for their future research on the region.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，学生应该对东南亚国家间交往的历史、区域主义的发展过程以及与外部世界的互动有全面的了解，并能够把在其他课程中学到的国际关系理论灵活运用到对东南亚国际关系的分析与解释中去。

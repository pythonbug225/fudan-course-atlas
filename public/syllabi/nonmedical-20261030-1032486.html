课程简介（中文）

本课程通过对程颢、程颐、朱熹、王阳明四位新儒学思想家的经典著作进行重点研读，深入阐释宋明理学的核心要义。在研读原始文献、探究其形而上学体系、修身之道及人性论的基础上，课程始终将理论置于中西比较的视野中，以彰显宋明理学特有的思辨传统，并探讨这些思想资源对当代哲学论述可能产生的丰富与拓展。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers an in-depth introduction to Song-Ming Neo-Confucianism (Song-Ming lixue 宋明理學) through a focused examination of the works of four pivotal Neo-Confucian philosophers: the Cheng brothers (Cheng Hao 程顥 and Cheng Yi 程頤), Zhu Xi 朱熹, and Wang Yangming 王陽明. In addition to engaging with primary texts to explore their theories of metaphysics, self-cultivation, and human nature, the course will consistently situate these theories within a Chinese-Western comparative framework in order to highlight their theoretical uniqueness and examine how these unique perspectives may enrich contemporary philosophical discourse.

教学目标

To familiarize students with the foundational history, key figures, and major conceptual developments within the Song-Ming Neo-Confucian tradition. To enable students to engage critically with primary philosophical texts from core thinkers, specifically the Cheng brothers (Cheng Hao and Cheng Yi), Zhu Xi, and Wang Yangming. To guide students in analyzing and interpreting central Neo-Confucian theories concerning metaphysics, human nature, and practices of self-cultivation. To develop students' ability to situate and examine these Neo-Confucian ideas within a structured Chinese-Western comparative philosophical framework. To cultivate critical thinking by assessing the theoretical uniqueness of Neo-Confucian perspectives and their potential to contribute to contemporary philosophical debates. To enhance students' academic capabilities, including research skills, structured oral presentation, and the composition of substantial analytical writing in philosophy.

教学方式

This course will primarily adopt a lecture-based format, with students engaging in brief discussions based on questions raised by the instructor and classmates during class.

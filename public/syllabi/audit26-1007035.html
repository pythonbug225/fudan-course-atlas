课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在介绍水文分析和流域建模的基础知识以及最新发展。课程分为两个主要部分。第一部分主要介绍水文过程的基本理论，如降水、截留、入渗、蒸发和蒸腾，以及一些常用水文分析方法，如峰值流量估算和频率分析。第二部分主要通过一个开源流域建模软件—SWAT来介绍流域建模的基本过程。首先介绍SWAT建模的基本概念和步骤;其次基于对SWAT文献的讨论，进一步加深对SWAT建模过程的理解。鉴于人工智能的快速发展，本课程还将讨论基于SWAT模型与深度神经网络模型耦合以提高极端径流模拟的新思路。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to introduce the fundamentals as well as the latest developments in hydrological analysis and watershed modeling.It consists of two main parts. The first part is devoted to introducing the basic theories on hydrological processes such as precipitation, interception, infiltration, evaporation and transpiration, as well as some commonly used hydrological analysis methods such as peak flow estimation and frequency analysis. The second part is devoted to introducing the basic processes of watershed modeling with the aid of a popular open source watershed modeling software - SWAT. The fundamental concepts and steps of SWAT modeling are first introduced. A number of published studies are then discussed to further the understandings of SWAT modeling processes. Given the rapid development of artificial intelligence, this course will introduce the new concept of coupling a process-based SWAT model with a deep neural network model to improve the simulation of extreme streamflow. 

教学目标

This course aims to introduce the fundamentals as well as new development in the field of hydrological analysis and watershed modeling. As an important tool to hydrology studies, Geographic Information System (GIS) and its applications in the field are also introduced. Specifically, this course is designed to present an understanding of: (1) the movement of water through hydrologic cycle, such as precipitation, interception, infiltration, evaporation, transpiration, and soil water balance; (2) hydrological analysis methods; and (3) development of the process-based SWAT model and its coupling with a deep neural network (DNN) model. Both the status quo and future trend of the discipline will be discussed. At the end of the class, the students are expected to grasp the basic concepts of hydrological modeling and independently establish a hybrid SWAT-DNN model with real-world datasets.

教学方式

  • Lectures will cover fundamental theories related to hydrological processes and hydrological analysis methods.
  • Classroom discussions are organized to explore course materials.
  • In-class exercises are incorporated to reinforce understanding of the presented theories and methods.
  • Class presentations of published SWAT studies. 
  • A comprehensive SWAT case study will guide students through the complete workflow, including data processing, hybrid SWAT-DNN model development, and discussion of simulation results.

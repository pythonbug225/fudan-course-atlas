课程简介（中文）

 肥胖已成为严重威胁我国居民健康的重要危险因素，中国肥胖人数居全球第一，采取积极的应对措施对肥胖及所合并的代谢性疾病进行预防与治疗对于提高国人身体健康水平与生活质量具有重要的意义。虽然包括减重与代谢外科手术在内的肥胖医学在西方发达国家得到普遍开展和推广，但将其作为一个新兴学科引入我国并获得广泛推广应用仅十余年的时间，对其相关的基础理论与临床应用的认知度还存在不足。本课程旨在通过介绍肥胖的基本概念、发病机制与病理生理改变、诊断与评估方法、对机体各系统器官的危害以及治疗方法等在内的内容，使医学生对肥胖的基础理论与临床实践有正确的认识和理解，进而达到更好地促进肥胖医学在我国的发展，造福我国广大肥胖人群的目的。本课程紧密结合我国当前肥胖医学发展的现状，从病因、发病机制、病理生理改变、肥胖对机体心血管、呼吸、消化、内分泌等各系统器官的影响、其诊治现状，特别是减重代谢外科技术的应用等多学科领域系统阐述了当代肥胖医学所取得的成就与进展。通过本课程的学习，同学们将能够正确理解并掌握肥胖医学的相关知识，认识肥胖的危害，正确掌握包括减重与代谢外科手术在内的肥胖防治手段，进而有力促进肥胖防治知识的普及与临床应用，实现提高国人健康水平的目的。

课程简介（英文）

Obesity has become a critical health threat and major risk factor endangering the well-being of Chinese citizens. With China having the largest obese population globally, implementing proactive measures for the prevention and treatment of obesity and its associated metabolic diseases is of paramount importance for enhancing the health status and quality of life of the nation's people. Although obesity medicine, including bariatric and metabolic surgery, is widely practiced and promoted in developed Western countries, its introduction to China as an emerging field dates back just over a decade. Recognition and understanding of its fundamental theories and clinical applications remain insufficient domestically.

This course aims to cultivate accurate understanding among medical students regarding the fundamental theories and clinical practices of obesity medicine. It covers essential topics including basic concepts of obesity, pathogenesis and pathophysiological changes, diagnostic and assessment methods, its detrimental effects on various organ systems, and treatment approaches. The ultimate goal is to better promote the development of obesity medicine in China and benefit its vast population affected by obesity.

The course closely aligns with the current state of obesity medicine development in China. It systematically elaborates on contemporary achievements and advances in the field from a multidisciplinary perspective. This includes exploring etiology, pathogenesis, pathophysiological alterations, the impact of obesity on cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, endocrine, and other organ systems, the current landscape of diagnosis and treatment, and notably, the application of bariatric and metabolic surgical techniques.

Through this course, students will gain a correct understanding and mastery of obesity medicine knowledge, recognize the hazards of obesity, and accurately acquire prevention and treatment strategies, including bariatric and metabolic surgery. This will significantly contribute to the widespread dissemination of obesity prevention knowledge and its clinical application, thereby achieving the objective of improving the health level of the Chinese population.

教学目标

认识肥胖的危害，正确掌握包括减重与代谢外科手术在内的肥胖防治手段，进而有力促进肥胖防治知识的普及与临床应用；培养守初心、铸信念、强人文、有大爱的创新型卓越医学人才，提高国人健康水平，为推进健康中国建设贡献力量。了解肥胖症的基本概念、肥胖症的病理生理学、肥胖症的遗传学、如何使用道德以及非污名化的沟通方法来评估患者的肥胖症和相关疾病。了解使用临床批准的治疗方法治疗肥胖症的策略：行为学治疗、药物治疗和手术治疗，以及肥胖症的未来治疗。

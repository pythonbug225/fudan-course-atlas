衍生证券和固定收益

2025秋 · BA70032.01 · 范龙振

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程学习金融衍生工具与固定收益证券，聚焦衍生证券和固定收益市场的理论模型、实证分析结果，并结合中国市场讨论相关领域的研究问题。 课程的第一个部分学习金融衍生证券，展示多种金融衍生工具的交易策略、定价模型和市场实证结果。主要内容包括：期权、期货、资产证券化证券、互换的定价理论模型、市场实证结果，以及各种衍生工具在投资策略、风险管理中的应用等。 课程的第二部分聚焦固定收益市场，学习理论模型、分析市场实证表现，讨论研究进展。主要内容包括: 固定收益证券的风险和收益、利率期限结构理论、信用风险评估模型、利率债和信用债定价及市场实证等。 中国债券市场是世界第二大债券市场，2024年末债券存量超过170万亿元，远大于股票市场，回购和现券交易量是股市交易量的5倍以上；中国市场的互换、期货、期权、资产证券化资产越来越重要。 中国金融市场的经济和政策背景、金融市场结构不同于西方发达市场，本课程在学习西方前沿理论模型的基础上，结合中国市场，探讨研究课题。

课程简介（英文）

The course covers financial derivatives and fixed-income securities, focusing on theoretical models and empirical evidences in the derivatives and fixed-income markets, and discusses relevant research issues, especially in the Chinese market. The first part of the course covers financial derivative securities, such as options, futures, asset-backed securities, swaps, presenting trading strategies, pricing models, and market empirical results for various financial derivative instruments, as well as the application of various derivative instruments in investment strategies and risk management. The second part of the course focuses on the fixed-income market, covering theoretical models, analyzing market empirical performance, and discussing research developments. Key topics include: risk and return of fixed-income securities, interest rate term structure theory, credit risk assessment models, pricing of interest rate bonds and credit bonds, and market empirical results. China's bond market is the world's second-largest bond market, with a size of exceeding RMB 170 trillion by the end of 2024, far larger than the stock market. Trading volume of repos and  bonds  are more than five times that of the stock market; swaps, futures, options, and asset-backed securities are becoming increasingly important in the Chinese market. The economic and monetary policy background of China's financial markets, as well as structure of the financial market are quite different from those of Western developed markets, necessitate a course that combines the study of Western cutting-edge theoretical models with an exploration of research topics tailored to the Chinese market.

教学目标

1. 深入了解金融衍生工具和固定收益证券市场 2. 掌握衍生金融工具和固定收益证券的定价理论模型 3. 学习金融衍生工具和固定收益证券的交易策略和风险管理 4. 熟悉主要理论和实证进展。 5. 结合中国市场，学会提出研究课题。

教学方式

1.课堂讲解 2.学习材料阅读 3.完成一个规定的研究项目 4.完成两个自己选题的研究项目

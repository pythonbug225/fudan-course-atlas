课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在批判性分析“可持续发展”的相关概念、全球范围内的相关讨论及其在实践中的应用。课程将引导学生了解可持续发展文献中来自不同视角和学科领域的关键学者，并探讨为何不同国家和地区在可持续发展道路上呈现出差异性。课程将聚焦过去四十年间围绕可持续发展的辩论，特别是《2030年可持续发展议程》及可持续发展目标。

课程简介（英文）

The course aims to analyze critically the concepts concerning “sustainable development”, the global discussions about it and how sustainable development is applied to practice. The course will familiarize students with key authors in the sustainable development literature from different perspectives and disciplines, as well as discuss why countries and regions follow different paths in terms of sustainable development. The classes will focus on the debates around sustainable development in the last forty years, particularly the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

教学目标

The students will be able to understand the different meanings of sustainable development, its global implications and how those have evolved over time. They will be capable of identifying the main theories, ideologies and policies behind the debates on sustainable development in order to carry out simple political economy analyses of sustainable development situations in practice.

教学方式

The course is made of several seminars for discussion of different topics related to sustainable development globally. The students are required to read all assigned material and participate in the classes, as there will be debates about each topic and the students are expected to contribute to those debates. One or more students will be assigned to lead part of the class and brief the participants about the readings and topic. The lecturer will also ask other students to give comments on some of the articles.

课程简介（中文）

本课程专为高水平运动员设计，旨在通过“数字平台自主学习 + 线上互动研讨”的灵活模式，全面提升学生在国际竞技与文化交流中的英语实际运用能力。课程核心任务紧密结合运动员的职业特质，通过 We Learn 数字化教学平台，引导学生利用训练间隙异步完成涵盖听、说、读、写、译的系统性练习。课程的最终考核形式包括与体育主题相关的口试和写作。这种方式不仅能激发学生的学习热情，更能帮助他们在深化语言知识的同时，提升职业自信心，洞察跨文化沟通的影响力。

课程简介（英文）

Tailored specifically for elite athletes, this course adopts a flexible model of “asynchronous digital learning + interactive online seminars” to comprehensively enhance students' practical English proficiency in international competitions and cultural exchanges. The core curriculum is closely integrated with the professional characteristics of athletes. Through the We Learn digital platform, students are guided to utilize their training intervals to complete systematic exercises covering listening, speaking, reading, writing, and translation. The final assessment consists of an oral examination and a writing task, both centered on sports-related themes. This approach not only ignites students’ passion for learning but also helps them deepen their linguistic knowledge while boosting professional self-confidence and gaining insights into the impact of effective cross-cultural communication.

教学目标

Integrated College English, as a comprehensive course, not only aims to equip students with the necessary language skills to meet the Levels 4-5 of China’s Standards of English Language Ability but also seeks to enrich their educational experience in the following several aspects: 1. Language Proficiency and Effective Communication: The course will hone students’ abilities in listening, speaking, reading, writing, and translation, with a special emphasis on both written and oral communication. 2. Cultural Awareness and Appreciation: The course will incorporate elements of cultural studies, encouraging students to appreciate the diversity of English-speaking cultures and to develop a global perspective. 3. Critical Thinking and Problem Solving: Students will be guided to think critically about the information they encounter in English texts, fostering the ability to question, analyze, and synthesize information. 4. Digital Literacy: Given the course’s emphasis on computer-assisted learning, students will also develop digital literacy skills. They will learn how to effectively use technology for language learning and communication, preparing them for the digital age. 5. Lifelong Learning and Self-improvement: The course will instill a passion for lifelong learning and self-improvement, encouraging students to continue developing their English skills beyond the classroom and to seek out opportunities for personal and professional growth.

教学方式

Online Autonomous Learning: Utilizing the We Learn platform for asynchronous reading and linguistic exercises. Scaffolded Writing Instruction: Providing conceptual frameworks and AI-assisted drafting tools for the final essay. Communicative Language Teaching (CLT): Focus on functional communication during the final online meeting.

教学内容与进度安排

Sessions 1-6 Unit 1 完成 Text A & B 阅读；完成 We Learn 平台配套练习 (Task 1 & 2, Language Practice)。 Sessions 7-12 Unit 2 完成 Text A & B 阅读；We Learn 平台异步监控学习进度。 Sessions 13-18 Unit 3 完成 Text A & B 阅读；重点掌握体育与意志力相关词汇。 Sessions 19-24 Unit 4 完成 Text A & B 阅读；结合 App 进行自主听力与阅读训练。 Sessions 25-30 Unit 5 完成 Text A & B 阅读；整理常用写作句型。 Sessions 31-33 Unit 6 & Preparation Unit 6 重点内容概览；期末作文《My Sporting Idol》构思，指导利用 AI 辅助润色初稿。 Sessions 34-36 Final Assessment 期末考核：地点： 线上（腾讯会议）。 时间： Session 36（最后一次课），口试及作文。

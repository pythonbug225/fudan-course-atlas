PhD Program - International Politics and Theories

2025秋 · POLI60023.01 · 吴澄秋、殷之光

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程是国际政治专业英文博士项目的必修课。通过比较各种相互竞争的国际关系理论及其在国际政治各个方面的应用，本课程旨在帮助学生从理论和实践两个角度对国际政治建立基础性理解。 本课程分为三个部分。在第一部分，我们将探讨国际关系的不同研究方法及其历史演变。我们将简要探讨如何解答国际关系的基本问题，以及当代国际关系的历史背景。在第二部分中，我们将回顾国际关系理论的演变，介绍其分析层次，并比较各种相互竞争的理论视角。我们将详细讨论现实主义、自由主义、激进主义、建构主义和后现代主义等不同的理论范式。在第三部分中，我们将探讨一些中程国际关系理论。我们将重点关注国际安全与联盟理论、国际谈判与外交政策分析、国际政治经济学（IPE）以及全球治理。我们不仅会探讨理论理解，还会探讨与这些问题相关的国际现实。

课程简介（英文）

This is a required course for Master’s students of the Chinese Politics and Diplomacy Program. By comparing contending theories of international relations (IR) and their application to various aspects of international politics, this course is designed to provide students with a foundational understanding of international politics from both a theoretical and factual perspective. The course is organized in three sessions. In the first session, we will examine different approaches to and the historical evolution of IR. We will briefly consider how to develop the answers to foundational questions of IR and what is the historical context of contemporary IR. In the second session, we will review the evolution of IR theory, introduce the levels of analysis, and compare contending theoretical perspectives. Different theoretical paradigms such as realism, liberalism, radicalism, constructivism, and postmodernism will be discussed in detail. In the third session, we will look at some intermediate-range international relations theories. Particularly, we will focus on international security and alliance theory, international bargaining and foreign policy analysis, international political economy (IPE), and global governance. We will cover not only theoretical understandings, but also international realities with respect to these issues.

教学目标

The course is designed to provide students with a thorough understanding of international politics from both a theoretical and factual perspective. Since international politics as a subject is very complex, the curriculum is fashioned in a way to present students not with everything, but instead zooms in on fundamental aspects of the interaction between states and other actors in international politics. The focus is on comparing contending theories of international relations (IR) and their application in the study of international politics. Students are required to not only understand various IR theories but also to employ those theories to describe and explain international processes and events.

教学方式

The instructors give lectures, and students give presentations, followed by discussion.

教学内容与进度安排

Session I: Introduction: Theory and History Week 1: Course Overview: Approaches to International Relations Week 2: The Historical Evolution of International Relations Week 3: Theory and International Relations Theory Session II: Contending Theories of International Politics Week 4: Realism Week 5: Neorealism Week 6: Liberalism and Liberal Institutionalism Week 7: Constructivism Week 8: The English School Week 9: Marxism Week 10: Critical Theory Week 11: Third World International Relations Theories Week 12: Postmodernism Week 13: Chinese International Relations Theory (Review Essay due) Session III: Major Issues of International Politics Week 14: International Security and Alliance Theory Week 15: International Bargaining and Foreign Policy Analysis Week 16: Concluding Discussion (Final Research Paper due) Besides the above sessions, the following five more sessions are required for doctoral students. Session 1: International Political Economy and Global Governance Session 2: Historical Dialecticism and International Relations Theory Session 3: History of International Political Thoughts and International Relations Theory Session 4: Theory of International Political Economy and Psychological Approach to International Politics Session 5: Dissertation Topics and Theories

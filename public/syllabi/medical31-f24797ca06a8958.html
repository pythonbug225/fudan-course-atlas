1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 这门全英文课程中同学们将学习针对基于人群研究的某一医学研究领域的文献评阅，通过分析引用到的文献，最终完成一篇期末综述论文，体现对研究论文的评判性评价，对研究成果的总结，提供相应的结论，并将综述论文在课堂进行汇报展示。通过课程教学活动的设置和老师的引导，同学们将会获得解释研究结果、写作、以及口头表达的专业技能。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | In this course, conducted in English, students will learn to do a review of research literature about a topic in population-based medicine study. Based on a careful analysis of the cited literature students will construct a completely documented term paper in which they will critically evaluate the research literature, summarize the findings, and provide conclusions. The review will also be the basis for an oral presentation given to the class. From course activities and personal guidance, you will acquire skills at interpreting research results and at writing and speaking in a professional manner.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Professionals in the field of medicine must be able to review the published literature, evaluate and integrate that information, and communicate the findings both verbally and in writing. You will be required to do a review of research literature about a topic in population-based medicine. This review will be used to construct a completely documented term paper in which you will critically evaluate the research literature, summarize the findings, and provide conclusions based on a careful analysis of the cited literature. The review will also be the basis for an oral presentation given to the class. From course activities and personal guidance, you will acquire skills at interpreting research results and writing and speaking professionally. Upon completion of this course, you should be able to: Develop and implement a library literature search on a specific topic using online databases Review original research papers - critically evaluate the methods, results, and conclusions of these papers Prepare summaries of research articles Prepare an outline of your research paper that addresses a scientific issue Write a concise review paper on a topic relevant to population-based medicine based on primary scientific sources, and include appropriate documentation of literature sources Participate in journal clubs and give an oral PowerPoint presentation on one research paper

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

The primary teaching method is in-class teaching, with discussion, presentations, and group activities. The design of class discussion or exercise, practice, experience and so on: Two groups (of about eight students each) will be assigned at the beginning of the course. Thus, you will be in either Group 1 or Group 2 for the entire course. Each group will be subdivided into four teams (two students per team). You will have the same classmate on your team throughout the semester. You will meet with your teammate three times throughout the semester to discuss your assignments.

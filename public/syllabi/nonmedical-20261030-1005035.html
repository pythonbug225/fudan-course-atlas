英语短篇小说导读

2025秋 · GEEC10156.01 · 张琼

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程包含对大约30余则经典、典型、重要的英语短篇小说的解读、评析、讨论和探究，所涉作品和作家涵盖英国、美国、爱尔兰、加拿大、澳大利亚等国，采用课堂精读式导读和课后推荐式研读的方式。

课程简介（英文）

The course consists of a series of interpretive and critical readings of about 30 English short story masterpieces, by authors ranging from British, American (for the early stage of the course development), Irish, Canadian and Australian (to be added as replacements later on). The course aims at introducing to the students the art of short story writing and the cultural messages therein contained, as well as the way to interpret and write critically on short stories.

教学目标

课程主旨是向学生们概述短篇小说的艺术，文化信息，以及相关文化反思，并以英语文学中重要的代表性作家及其文学创作为契入，深入浅出地讲解具有典型性的文学艺术特征，在巩固和提升学生的英语语言基础的前提下，进一步提高文学艺术审美和批评能力，了解一定历史语境和文化特色中的代表作家及作品，以点及面地进行文学史和作品讲解，培养学生对文字、文学、文化的理解和鉴赏能力，提高人文素养。

教学方式

课堂讲授解读与课堂讨论结合 lecture series + class discussion

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction to English short stories Week 2: Lawrence: “Tickets, Please” Week 3: Joyce: “Araby” Week 4: Mark Twain: “My Platonic Sweetheart” Week 5: Hemingway: “The Killers” Week 6: Greene: “The Destructors” Week 7: Salinger: “A Perfect Day for Bananafish” Week 8: Chopin: “The Story of an Hour” & “Desiree’s Baby” Week 9: Wharton: “Roman Fever” (Alice Munro: “The Bear Came over the Mountain” or “Hateship, Friendship, Courtship, Loveship, Marriage”) Week 10: Jackson: “The Lottery” Week 11: Mansfield: “Miss Brill”; “The Singing Lesson” Week 12: Malamud: “Idiots First” Week 13: Porter: “The Jilting of Granny Weatherall” Week 14: Maugham: “The Verger” Week 15: Faulkner: “A Rose for Emily” Week 16: Course wind-up & Course exam: passage comments and a short critical essay (in class) Replacement Sessions (according to special need and circumstance): Target Reading (two or three fictions with relevance + follow-up in-class discussion) (2 sessions)

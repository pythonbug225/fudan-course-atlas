卓越服务运营

2026春 · MIB70018.01 · Jelmer Pier van der Gaast

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

服务运营是日常生活中不可或缺的一部分，涵盖医疗、交通、零售、娱乐和教育等多个领域。人们不断以消费者和服务提供者的身份与各类服务互动，但服务质量却常常令人失望，表现为等待时间过长、流程低效以及交付延迟。本课程探讨这些服务失效产生的原因，以及低质量服务与卓越服务之间的差异。服务质量的核心取决于组织如何设计、组织和管理其服务交付运营。服务不佳通常源于资源配置不当以及未能应用服务运营管理的基本原则。课程将系统介绍服务运营管理的本质、特征与挑战，讲解适用于各类服务组织的关键管理原则，并提供评估和改进运营绩效的实用工具与框架。通过真实案例和现代分析模型，课程旨在培养设计、控制和运营高效服务系统的能力，实现服务运营卓越。

课程简介（英文）

Service operations are central to everyday life, encompassing sectors such as healthcare, transportation, retail, entertainment, and education. People constantly engage with services as both consumers and providers, yet service quality is frequently disappointing, marked by long waits, inefficiencies, and delays. This course examines why such failures occur and what distinguishes poor service from excellent service. The core determinant of service quality lies in how effectively organizations design, organize, and manage their service delivery operations. Substandard service typically results from misallocated resources and the neglect of fundamental service operations management principles. The course explores the nature, characteristics, and challenges of managing service operations across diverse contexts. Students will learn key principles applicable to all service organizations and gain practical tools and frameworks to evaluate and improve operational performance. Through real-world cases and modern analytical models, the course aims to develop the capability to design, control, and operate service systems that achieve operational excellence.

教学目标

Understand how to measure and improve processes Understand how to improve the productivity of processes and how to consistently deliver products and services Understand how to manage service operations by anticipating customer demand Understand how to manage service operations by responding to customer demand

教学方式

The course is a mix of lectures, case studies, and interactive games. The lectures are designed to explain key service management models and concepts using both qualitative and quantitative approaches. Qualitative methods focus on theoretical frameworks, conceptual analysis, and the evaluation of service design and improvement strategies, while quantitative methods emphasize data analysis, probability concepts, and basic principles of microeconomics relevant to service systems. Case studies present real-world service scenarios from existing organizations, in which complex service management decisions, such as capacity planning, service quality, customer experience, and process design, must be addressed. These situations are often described from the perspective of a key decision-maker and typically involve time pressure and operational constraints. Each teaching case requires thorough preparation by participants in order to enable effective analysis and meaningful classroom discussion.

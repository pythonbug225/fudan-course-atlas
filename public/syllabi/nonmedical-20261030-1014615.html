课程简介（中文）

本课程主要介绍新型传感原理及其应用，重点在阻抗式传感器、化学传感器、生物传感器、光纤传感器、i等。结合最新发展趋势，介绍国内外传感器的发展趋势。掌握传感器基本原理、材料工艺、电路系统及实际应用的关系。结合实验室方向展开课程论文讨论。了解传感器网络及其应用，特别是在物联网应用。熟悉传感器相关文献检索的方法。

课程简介（英文）

This course primarily introduces the principles and applications of novel sensors, with a focus on impedance sensors, chemical sensors, biosensors, fiber optic sensors, and other types. It combines the latest development trends to introduce the trajectory of sensor development both domestically and internationally. Students will master the fundamental principles of sensors and understand the relationships between materials and processes, circuit systems, and practical applications. Course paper discussions will be conducted based on our laboratory's research focus. The course also covers sensor networks and their applications, particularly within the Internet of Things (IoT). Students will become familiar with methods for conducting literature searches related to sensors.

教学目标

掌握传感器的原理、材料、工艺和应用的关系，其基本关系：材料是基础，原理定量前提，工艺是制备关键，应用是最终目标 掌握各种新型传感器的结构原理及应用，掌握常见的几种传感器的原理和应用 熟悉传感器文献检索方法 结合课题组研究方向探讨可开展传感器的可行性

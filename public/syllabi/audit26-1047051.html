课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在系统深入梳理双语与多语研究领域的核心议题。在高度全球化的当今世界，双语现象与多语现象已是人类生活的重要组成部分。事实上，全球双语者、多语者的数量已经超过单语者。我们亟需从跨学科视角认识这一普遍现象，涵盖语言学理论、神经语言学、心理语言学、社会语言学、语言教育以及区域研究等多个领域。

课程简介（英文）

This course intends to provide an in-depth survey of key issues related to bilingualism and multilingualism. Bilingualism and multilingualism have constituted a major facet of life in the highly globalized world today. There are in fact more bilingual/multilingual speakers in the world than there are monolinguals. It’s imperative to understand such an extensive phenomenon from an interdisciplinary perspective including linguistic theory, neurolinguistics, psycholinguistics, sociolinguistics, language education, and area studies.

教学目标

This course takes seminars as the teaching method, and students are expected to contribute to classroom discussion.  

By the end of the course, students are expected to be able to:

  • Identify the key issues in the field of bilingualism and multilingualism
  • Distinguish research traditions of bilingualism and multilingualism, and familiarize with important researchers and their theories.
  • Critically read published research papers and write effective summaries
  • Familiarize with common research methods adopted in bilingualism and multilingualism research
  • Conceptualize an empirical research project in a chosen topic area

教学方式

本课程将采取讲授与讨论交替的方式。学生需要深入阅读大量双语和多语研究文献，并且在课堂上进行分享和讨论。

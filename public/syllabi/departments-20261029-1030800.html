课程简介（中文）

《国别与地区经济》课程是世界经济专业的专业选修课程。课程全面系统地考察了对世界经济以及我国经济影响较为突出的国家和地区的经济现状，发展模式以及未来趋势。

课程简介（英文）

The course "Country and Regional Economics" is an elective course for World Economics majors. The course comprehensively examines the economic systems, development models, and future trends of countries and regions that have a significant impact on the world economy and the Chinese economy. This course focuses on the development processes and models of major economies (the United States, Japan, Germany, Sweden, Russia, etc.) as well as the regional economies of Europe and Asia, analyzing and discussing the problems they face. The goal is to help students understand the mechanisms and patterns of economic development in different regions and countries from an international perspective and from the angle of international division of labor and regional development. Students will also master the basic analytical theories and tools of international economics and cultivate the ability to independently research regional and national economic issues.

教学目标

针对主要经济体（美国、日本、德国、瑞典、俄罗斯等）以及欧洲和亚洲区域经济的发展过程和发展模式，面临的问题展开分析和讨论。帮助学生在基于国际视野以及国际分工与区域发展的角度，理解不同地区，不同国家的经济发展的机制和规律，掌握国际经济学的基本分析理论和工具，培养独立研究地区和国别经济问题的能力。

教学方式

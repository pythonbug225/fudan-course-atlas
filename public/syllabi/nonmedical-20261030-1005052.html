课程简介（中文）

本课程分为两个部分：学期前半段主要以学术写作为重点，旨在提升学生在表达原创思想时的写作与口语能力，使其熟悉学术论文的撰写与投稿原则和流程，并为参与各类学术活动做好准备。学期后半段则转向创意写作，除阅读相关基础文章外，还将鼓励学生创作自己的趣味小短篇。

课程简介（英文）

The course is divided into two parts: lectures in the first half of the semester are mainly focused on academic writing. They are designed to enhance students’ writing and oral skills in delivering their original ideas, familiarize them with the principles and procedures of composing and submitting research papers, and prepare them for participating in various academic activities. In the second half of the semester, our focus shifts to creative writing. In addition to reading significant articles on the basics of creative writing, students will also be encouraged to create their own short stories.

教学目标

Committed students will: Learn about fundamental rules, skills, and strategies in constructing research papers; Familiarize themselves with typical processes, formats, and requirements of certain academic activities, such as attending academic conferences, publishing journal articles, and writing theses; Further enhance their writing and oral skills through practices and peer reviews.

教学方式

Lectures, in-class discussions and workshops, writing assignments, examinations. Students are strongly encouraged to contribute to the class actively. The ways of participation include but are not limited to participating in in-class discussions, and proposing thought-provoking questions in the Q&A sessions after each presentation. There will also be 5 written assignments throughout the semester (in addition to the final paper), taking successively 3%, 7%, 5%, 5%, and 5% of your overall score. The first one is a take-home assignment, whereas the other four will be accomplished in-class.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: An introduction to the course, and a brief discussion on academic format (Assignment #1: submit a short proposal of your course paper (approximately 500 words), with up to 5 keywords and a reading list of about 10 entries.) Week 2: Academic language Week 3: Academic language (cont.) Week 4: “Argument” (Assignment #2: polish your assignment #1 (research proposal and reading list) according to the rules and standards we have discussed in the past a few weeks.) Week 5: Abstract Week 6: Research/ personal/ teaching philosophy statement Week 7: Cover Letter Week 8: Midterm (in class) Week 9: Setting and “stage prompts” (Writing practice #3 (in-class, 5%)) Week 10: Setting and “stage prompts” (cont.) Week 11: Plot (Writing practice #4 (in-class, 5%)) Week 12: Plot (cont.) Week 13: Character (Writing practice #5 (in-class, 5%)) Week 14: Character (cont.) Week 15: Flash ficiton Week 16: Flash fiction (cont.)

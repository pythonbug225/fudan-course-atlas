课程简介（中文）

研读10篇已发表的英文学术论文，了解典型教育研究学术论文的专业性词汇、语法结构以及文章架构。特别注意以下几点：lead sentences（导语句）、define key terms(界定关键概念)、thorough and no repetition(一次讲清楚)、abstract（摘要）。

课程简介（英文）

Study 10 published English academic papers to understand the specialized vocabulary, grammatical structures, and organizational framework of typical educational research papers. Pay special attention to the following aspects: Lead sentences (introductory statements that guide the reader), Defining key terms (clearly establishing core concepts), Thorough and non-repetitive explanations (presenting ideas concisely and thoroughly), Abstracts (summarizing the study's purpose, methods, findings, and significance). The goal is to analyze how these elements contribute to the clarity, coherence, and academic rigor of research writing.

教学目标

通过学习研读已发表英文学术论文，掌握教育研究中常见英文专业性词汇、语法结构以及文章架构，从而能熟练精读和速读英文学术论文；同时学习在学术论文写作时有意识通过导语句组织文章架构，明白概念界定、一次讲透彻、摘要的要点这些学术写作要素。

教学方式

深度阅读英文文献，并且每周就论文的内容做课题汇报。

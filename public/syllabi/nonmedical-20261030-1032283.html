New Chinese Cinema

2026春 · JCM70039.01 · 朱建新

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在引导学生深入探讨当代中国政治、社会与文化领域的变迁，剖析现代化与全球化进程对其文化重构与身份认同产生的深刻影响。课程以中国第五代、第六代导演享誉国际的影片为主要文本，通过观摩分析精选作品，引导学生运用批判性思维，审视每部影片所承载的文化叙事与导演独特的美学表达。

课程简介（英文）

This course is intended to offer insights into the political, social and cultural changes in contemporary China and the impact of modernization and globalization on its cultural redefinition and identity reforming. Using primarily a selection of films directed by the internationally acclaimed Chinese fifth and sixth generation directors, the course will invite students to exercise their critical thinking skills to appraise the cultural narratives of each selected film, and the aesthetic presentation produced by each film director.

教学目标

This course is designed to offer graduate journalism students a thorough understanding of how Chinese cinema reflects and shapes the political, social, and cultural changes in contemporary China. It connects with the university's educational focus on value-driven learning, knowledge transmission, and the development of professional skills. Students will critically analyze films by influential fifth and sixth generation directors, exploring the cultural narratives and aesthetic choices that define China’s modernization and globalization. The course aims to strengthen students’ ability to assess media in a global context, improve their analytical and journalistic skills, and equip them to engage with contemporary debates on culture and identity.

教学方式

The course will adopt a blend of lectures, classroom discussions, and individual presentations to facilitate a dynamic learning environment. Lectures will provide foundational knowledge on Chinese cinema, its history, and its cultural significance, while classroom discussions will encourage active participation, critical thinking, and debate on the themes and techniques explored in the films. Students will also be tasked with delivering individual presentations, allowing them to delve deeper into specific topics, develop their analytical skills, and engage with their peers. This combination of teaching methods is designed to foster an interactive and engaging learning experience, enabling students to not only acquire knowledge but also develop their critical media analysis and communication skills.

城市社会与公共政策

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

我们现在生活在一个快速城市化的世界，全球超过一半人口生活在城市。中国是全球城市化最快、城市人口最多的国家，在全球范围内具有重要影响力。大规模快速城市化成为经济和社会发展的强大引擎，也带来一系列公共政策挑战。课程以当代城市社会基础理论与实践挑战为基础，广泛介绍全球城市经验，同时重点探讨中国城市化发展的特色理论与实践经验。

课程简介（英文）

We now live in a rapidly urbanizing world: half of the world’s population now lives in cities and all trends point to the rise of scores of multi-million “megacities” and a dramatic increase in urban populations across Asia, Latin America, and Africa. Cities highlight the best and worst of everything and also reveal the most remarkable contradictions of our time. This course analyzes the public policy challenges faced by urban society. The aim of the course is, first, to provide students with an understanding of global urbanisation and urban problems; second, to prepare students to design, analyze, and evaluate urban public policies. Throughout the course, we will take a comparative approach to the topics, not only drawing case studies drawn from different regions in the world but also paying particular attention to the ongoing urban transformation in China.

教学目标

1. Knowledge: Students will develop a comprehensive understanding of fundamental contemporary urbanisation and development theories. Specifically, they will learn about the processes, characteristics, driving forces, policy outcomes, and challenges associated with China’s urbanisation. 2. Skills: Through conducting a research project, students will become proficient in comparative urban studies methodologies. Students will learn to critically observe, analyse, and interpret urbanisation trends and policies in diverse national and regional contexts. 3. Competencies for lifelong learning: Collaborating on a research project improves students' organisational skills, communication abilities, and capacity to work effectively as part of a team. Additionally, participating in activities such as classroom discussions and field trips broadens their cross-cultural communication skills and deepens their intercultural understanding.

教学方式

The course is composed of three main segments. The first segment, entitled Global Urban Transformation offers students both conceptual and empirical orientation for understanding the ongoing global urbanization. The second segment, entitled Foundations of Urban Society, builds up theoretical foundations and introduces the key issues about contemporary urban society. The third segment, entitled Urban Governance and Policymaking, explores changing urban governance and public policy-making in response to urban problems. Two field trips to Lujiazui (A case of global-city landscape) and Songjiang New Town (a case of suburban development) are tentatively planned. In addition, a guest lecture may be arranged during the semester depending on the availability of speakers.

The lecture will first offer concepts of suburbanisation from a comparative perspective. It will then focus on suburban growth in China. Afterwards, the dynamics of recent suburban new town development are examined in detail. Readings Fishman, R. (1987). Week 13 Urban political economy: the case of land development This lecture will take a closer look at the processes and some of the principal groups of people involved in the production of the built environment. It then describes the evolution of the real estate industry and housing market cycles in China. Second, the lecture discusses the framework of “structures of building provision”, and recognizes the groups and institutions involved in the land development process in China. Readings Logan, J.

课程简介（中文）

This course provides an overview of the major principles of macroeconomics and their applications to socialistic economy with Chinese characteristics. We will study the macro economy piece by piece and then as a whole. The course will cover topics about the measurement of national income and the cost of living, unemployment, inflation, monetary system, interest rates, and etc. Main theories in macroeconomics such as Solow model, the IS-LM model, and the AD-AS model will be discussed. We will also talk about how macroeconomic policies (including Fiscal and Monetary policies) work. Both the short-term and long-term effects of macroeconomic policies will be analyzed.

教学目标

This course aims to guide students to have a deep understanding of the basic concepts and tools in macroeconomics. Students will learn how to use these tools to analyze the macroeconomy, especially Chinese economy. Upon completion of this course, students should: have a good understanding of key macroeconomic variables and their driving forces (e.g., GDP, inflation, interest rates, employment, and ); understand the framework of the macroeconomy, both generally and with Chinese characteristics; have a good understanding of Solow model, IS-LM model, AD-AS model, and know how to use them to analyze issues in the economy; know how to analyze the effects of monetary policy and fiscal policy; have a good understanding of theoretical and practical achievements of socialism with Chinese

课程简介（中文）

本课程针对当前老年临床医学的前沿方向、前沿临床诊疗技术与思想、老年学科整合医学、人工智能与脑科学等进行系统地阐述与讲解，内容包括老年整合医学与临床、老年综合评估与康复、老龄化与临床诊疗和照护、老年人快速康复外科、老年人围手术期临床、临床营养干预与抗衰老、肠道微生态干预与老年人疾病、老年人脑科学与临床、以及人工智能在老年临床医学中的应用与研究前瞻等。课程充分融入了老年医学临床诊疗的前沿理念与技术，以现代老年医学内涵为基础，避免“老调重弹”的问题，注重与时俱进和引领性，旨在加强临床专业学位研究生追踪前沿学科建设、加强人文精神、突出对老龄化疾病的系统思维、加强健康管理以及新时代智能背景下老年临床医学教育等新理念的教育和培养，提升专业学位研究生的职业素养和对老龄化疾病的整体诊疗水平，为其将来参加临床工作，构建和谐医患关系打下坚实的基础。

课程简介（英文）

 

This course systematically explores and explains the cutting-edge directions in geriatric clinical medicine, advanced clinical diagnostic and therapeutic techniques and concepts, integrated geriatric medicine, artificial intelligence, and brain science. The content covers geriatric integrated medicine and clinical practice, comprehensive geriatric assessment and rehabilitation, aging and clinical diagnosis and care, rapid rehabilitation surgery for the elderly, perioperative clinical care for the elderly, clinical nutritional intervention and anti-aging, gut microbiota intervention and diseases in the elderly, geriatric brain science and clinical practice, as well as the application and research prospects of artificial intelligence in geriatric clinical medicine. The course integrates the latest concepts and technologies in geriatric clinical diagnosis and treatment, grounded in modern geriatric medicine. It emphasizes staying current and leading, aiming to enhance the tracking of frontier disciplines by clinical professional degree graduate students, strengthen humanistic spirit, highlight systematic thinking about age-related diseases, improve health management, and promote new educational philosophies in geriatric clinical medicine under the new era of intelligent technology. This enhances the professional qualities and overall diagnostic and therapeutic capabilities of professional degree graduate students, laying a solid foundation for their future clinical careers and the establishment of harmonious doctor-patient relationships.

教学目标

 

本课程以培养热爱老年医疗卫生事业，具有良好的职业素养和专业素养的高层次临床医生为目标。旨在加强学生对当前老年临床医学的前沿方向、前沿临床诊疗技术与思想、老年学科整合医学、人工智能与脑科学等进行系统地阐述与讲解，提升专业学位研究生的职业素养和对老龄化疾病的整体诊疗水平，为其将来参加临床工作，构建和谐医患关系打下坚实的基础，满足临床医学专业学位研究生培养的需求，使之能在未来的临床时间和科研工作中能够独立、规范地承担老年常见疾病的诊治工作。

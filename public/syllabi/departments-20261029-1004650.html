当代中国经济

2025秋 · ECON30024.01 · 吴建峰、宋弘、张军

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程以系列研究论文和文献的研讨为载体，系统阐释 1949 年以降、尤以改革开放以来中国经济的发展范式、增长机理、结构嬗变、城市化进程、空间演进格局、人力资本形成及劳动力市场发育等重大命题。课程包括三个模块： “转型中国”模块，聚焦1950年代初实行中央计划经济时期的资源配置模式及1978年后实行改革开放的经济发展与崛起全景，从发展经济学和转型经济学视角讨论计划经济模式、农业改革、本土工业化、分权与政府间财政关系、经济增长和宏观经济的特征等。 “城市中国”模块，聚焦改革开放以来城市化进程，从空间视角和制度性障碍视角讨论城市增长和二元经济结构、户籍政策和城市规模、土地制度和土地财政以及地方性债务、城市高房价形成和不动产金融风险等。 “人口中国”模块，聚焦中国经济发展历程中的人力资本与劳动力市场相关问题，结合中国经济社会发展历程中的重要改革和政策，关注人口红利向人力资本红利转型过程中，人力资本流动和供给的决定性因素，探索增加高技能人才供给、提高人才配置的有效途径。从医疗健康和社会保障角度，探索如何通过医疗和社会保障提升健康与人力资本，从上述维度尝试更好的理解中国经济增长。

课程简介（英文）

This course uses a series of research literature discussions as a vehicle to systematically explore major themes in Chinese economic development since 1949, particularly following the reform and opening-up period. These themes include the development paradigm, growth mechanisms, structural evolution, urbanization processes, spatial development patterns, human capital formation, and labor market development. The course comprises three modules: The "Transforming China" module focuses on the resource allocation model during the central planning economy of the early 1950s and the panoramic view of economic development and rise after the 1978 reforms. From the perspectives of development economics and transition economics, it discusses the planned economy model, agricultural reforms, indigenous industrialization, decentralization and intergovernmental fiscal relations, and the characteristics of economic growth and macroeconomics. The "Urban China" module examines the urbanization process since the reform and opening-up, exploring urban growth and the dual economic structure, household registration (hukou) policies and city scale, land systems and land finance, local government debt, the formation of high urban housing prices, and real estate financial risks from spatial and institutional barrier perspectives. The "Population China" module focuses on issues related to human capital and the labor market in the context of Chinese economic development. It integrates major reforms and policies in China's socioeconomic development, examining the transition from demographic dividends to human capital dividends, the determinants of human capital mobility and supply, and pathways to increase the supply of high-skilled talent and improve talent allocation efficiency. From the perspectives of healthcare and social security, it explores how to enhance health and human capital through medical and social security systems, offering a deeper understanding of Chinese economic growth from these dimensions.

教学目标

本课程的宗旨是通过研讨系列研究论文系统讲授1949年后、尤其是改革开放以来中国经济的发展模式、经济增长、结构转变、城市化、空间发展、人力资本、劳动力市场等重要问题。课程设计的目的主要是启发学生利用经济学理论来思考中国经济发展问题。本课程还将利用分组项目讨论形式，考察同学分析社会现象的经济学思维和能力。

教学方式

本课程要求同学具备阅读英文文献的能力； 本课程要求同学具备参与小组合作和课堂讨论的精神和能力； 讲授+小组讨论和答辩

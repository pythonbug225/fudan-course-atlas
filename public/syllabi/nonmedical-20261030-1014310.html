Corporate Finance

2025秋 · MF60018.04 · 马晓崟

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course aims to cover the basic corporate finance principles, apply the analytical frameworks to examine important financial decisions faced by financial managers, and analyze how well-crafted decisions can generate lasting value impact. This course emphasizes the both the theoretical and applied side of corporate finance, and is structured around the most important financial decisions made by a firm. The topics we will cover include investment decisions, capital structure decision, payout policy, corporate governance, international corporate finance and so on.

教学目标

By successfully finishing this course, students should be able to explain and communicate the nature of corporate finance in an academic or a business forum and distinguish different types of business structure, identify the major corporate financial decisions and corporate objective.

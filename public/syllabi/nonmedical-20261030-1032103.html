Alternative Investment Strategies

2026春 · MF60030.02 · 孙林

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍另类投资策略，其运作模式有别于传统投资策略。首先，学员将系统学习另类投资策略的基础知识，包括风险收益关系、在投资组合多元化中的作用以及绩效评估方法。课程还将探讨对冲基金、基金中的基金、大宗商品及金融衍生品的投资策略。其次，课程将呈现最新学术研究成果，帮助学员建立扎实的理论基础。第三，通过案例研究，学员将全面了解成功另类资产管理者的真实实践。最后，学员需在行业导师指导下完成课程项目，运用所学知识解决实际问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the alternative investment strategies, which behave differently from traditional investment strategies. Firstly, participants will be provided with a comprehensive introduction to the fundamentals of alternative investment strategies including their risk-return relationship, role in portfolio diversification, and performance evaluation. Investments in hedge funds, fund of funds, commodities, and financial derivatives will also be discussed. Secondly, latest academic research results will be presented to help students to build a solid theoretical background. Thirdly, case studies will give participants a comprehensive look at the real stories of successful alternative asset managers. Finally, the course project with industry mentors requires participants to solve real-life problems by applying what they learn from the course.

教学目标

Through the course, participants will be able to: Develop a broad view of alternative investment strategies. Discuss the global trend of the alternative investment industry. Demonstrate the risk and return relationship of various investment strategies. Evaluate the performance of alternative investment managers. Construct an alternative investment portfolio using quantitative skills.

教学方式

Class Participation (individual) Homework Assignment (individual) Course Project (group)

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule: Class 1 Topic: Introduction to alternative investment strategies Note: history, industry outlook, role of various participants, and various asset classes. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Class 2 Topic: Investment strategies part 1 Note: equity long/short, market neutral, distressed securities, event-type strategies, convertible arbitrage, and commodity trading advisors. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: Alpha Architect: Is Momentum Losing Steam? by Richard B. Evans and Aldo Sesia. Class 3 Topic: Investment strategies part 2 Note: equity long/short, market neutral, distressed securities, event-type strategies, convertible arbitrage, and commodity trading advisors. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: McDonald’s, Wendy’s, and Hedge Funds: The Impact of Hedge Fund Activism on Corporate Governance by David P. Stowell. Class 4 Topic: Risk and return Note: arbitrage, factor model, market risk, operational risk, and liquidity risk. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: Openinvest by Shawn Cole, Boris Vallee, and Nicole Tempest Keller. Class 5 Topic: Characteristics of various hedge funds Note: fees, leverage, compensation structures, strategies, regulations, and differences from mutual funds. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: ProShares Hedge Replication ETF by Pedro Matos and Anil Demir. Class 6 Topic: Fund of funds, Chinese hedge fund market and other related topics. Note: fund selections, performance evaluation, growth of the China’s alternative investment industry, and global hedge funds trend. Reading: Handbook of Hedge Funds, When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management, relevant news article related to hedge funds. Case discussion: AQR’s DELTA Strategy by Daniel Bergstresser, Lauren Cohen, Randolph Cohen, and Christopher Malloy.

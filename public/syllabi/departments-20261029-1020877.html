课程简介（中文）

本课程为本科生设计的基础数学课程，系统讲授线性代数的核心概念、理论与方法，涵盖向量与矩阵、线性变换、线性方程组、正交性与投影、行列式、特征值与特征向量、二次型、奇异分解等内容。课程强调理论与实践结合，旨在培养学生的抽象思维和逻辑推理能力，为数学建模、数据分析、机器学习等后续课程奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a foundational mathematics course designed for undergraduates, systematically covering the core concepts, theories, and methods of linear algebra, including vectors and matrices, linear transformations, systems of linear equations, orthogonality and projections, determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, quadratic forms, etc. The course emphasizes the integration of theory and practice, aiming to develop students' abstract thinking and logical reasoning skills, laying a solid foundation for subsequent courses such as mathematical modeling, data analysis, and machine learning.

教学目标

This course aims to guide students in systematically mastering the fundamental theories and core computational methods of linear algebra, as well as developing a deep understanding of its wide applications in economics and related disciplines. Through diverse problem-solving exercises, the course will effectively enhance students' logical reasoning and abstract thinking abilities. As a foundational course in the UIPE program of the School of Economics, the curriculum is designed to emphasize seamless integration with subsequent courses (such as Probability and Statistics, Panel Data Analysis, and Time Series Analysis), laying a solid quantitative analysis foundation for students.

教学方式

The course will adopt a teaching approach that combines lectures with interactive learning, supplemented by applied analysis, problem-solving discussions, and computer lab sessions (using MATLAB).

教学内容与进度安排

Weeks 1-2: Vectors and Vector Spaces This module introduces fundamental concepts such as vectors, abstract vector spaces, bases, linear independence, dimension, and inner product spaces, laying the groundwork for understanding linear algebra. Assignment 1 Weeks 3-4: Matrices and Linear Transformations This section covers the relationship between linear transformations and matrices, basic matrix operations (addition, multiplication, transpose), and block matrix multiplication. Assignment 2 Week 5: Invertible Transformations Topics include invertible linear transformations and matrices, Gaussian elimination, elementary row (column) operations, Gauss-Jordan inversion, and techniques for determining matrix invertibility. Assignment 3 Weeks 6-7: Rank and Solving Linear Systems An in-depth exploration of rank and dimensionality reduction, the Rank-Nullity Theorem, the four fundamental subspaces (row space, column space, null space, left null space), and solving homogeneous/non-homogeneous linear systems. Assignment 4 Week 8: Orthogonality and Projection Covers orthogonality between vectors and subspaces, orthogonal complements, projection methods, projection matrices, and the Gram-Schmidt process (QR decomposition). Assignment 5 Weeks 9-10: Determinants Discusses the definition, properties, and geometric interpretation of determinants, various computation methods, and applications (e.g., Cramer’s Rule). Assignment 6 Weeks 11-12: Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors Introduces eigenvalues, eigenvectors, algebraic/geometric multiplicity, methods for solving eigenproblems, their geometric significance, and the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra. Assignment 7 Week 13: Similarity and Jordan Canonical Form Covers matrix similarity, diagonalization criteria, diagonalization of real symmetric matrices, and Jordan canonical form decomposition (including generalized eigenspaces). Assignment 7 (continued) Weeks 14-15: Quadratic Forms Focuses on completing the square, standard/canonical forms, the Inertia Theorem, Sylvester’s Criterion for positive definiteness, and applications. Assignment 8 Week 16: Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) Covers SVD for real matrices, its geometric interpretation, and connections to the four fundamental subspaces.

课程简介（中文）

本课程以各类学术及当下社会热点话题为切入点，紧密结合丰富的音视频素材，助力学生实践并掌握多种信息捕捉方法与技巧。同时，课程拓宽听力内容范畴，帮助学生就特定话题或现象展开相对深入的探讨。

课程简介（英文）

This course takes a wide range of academic and current social topics as entry points, closely integrating rich audio and video materials to enable students to practice and master various methods and techniques for capturing information. At the same time, it broadens the scope of listening content and helps students engage in relatively in-depth discussions on specific topics or phenomena.

教学目标

This course aims to broaden students' views and cultivate students’ listening comprehension and oral communication skills. Upon completion of the course, students are expected to be able to understand authentic English lectures, speeches, discussions etc and be able to grasp the central ideas, understand key points, and capture important details. Students are also expected to engage in relatively in-depth discussions on specific topics or phenomena, usually similar to the topics of listening practices.

教学方式

The course is lecture-based, with the teacher providing in-class instruction with multi-media assistance and guiding group and class discussions in a task-based approach, supplemented by out-of-class exercises. Students are expected to actively engage in classroom activities, complete both in-class and after-class listening and speaking assignments，and fully utilize supplementary materials.

课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍产业组织理论，重点分析企业行为、市场结构以及公共政策的作用。课程内容涵盖市场势力与定价、寡头竞争与战略互动、产品差异化、进入与退出、纵向关系、信息与激励机制以及竞争政策等主题。课程将理论模型与实证证据和现实案例相结合，旨在帮助学生掌握分析现代经济中竞争与监管问题的核心分析工具。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to industrial organization, focusing on the behavior of firms, the structure of markets, and the role of public policy. Topics include market power and pricing, oligopoly and strategic interaction, product differentiation, entry and exit, vertical relationships, information and incentives, and competition policy. The course combines theoretical models with empirical evidence and real-world applications to equip students with analytical tools for studying competition and regulation in modern economies.

教学目标

This course introduces students to the core theories and empirical tools of industrial organization. The objective is to develop a rigorous understanding of how firms’ strategic behavior, market structure, and public policy interact to shape market outcomes. By the end of the course, students will be able to analyze firm conduct using formal models, evaluate empirical evidence on competition and regulation, and apply IO frameworks to real-world market and policy issues.

教学方式

Lectures, case studies, group discussions, presentations and assessments (quizzes, assignments and exams).

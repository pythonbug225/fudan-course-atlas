1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 精神医学是临床医学的一个重要分支，通过学习使学生了解精神医学在今后临床工作的重要性，掌握常见精神障碍的临床表现、诊断与鉴别诊断、治疗原则和常用方法，熟悉精神障碍的患病率、病因、发病机制、预后等相关知识，了解我国精神医学发展，以便学生在毕业后的临床和研究工作中有所帮助。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Psychiatry is an important branch of clinical medicine. Through learning, students can understand the importance of psychiatry in future clinical work, master the clinical manifestations, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment principles and commonly used methods of common mental disorders, familiarize themselves with the incidence, etiology, pathogenesis, prognosis and other related knowledge of mental disorders, and understand the development of psychiatry in China. This will help students in their clinical and research work after graduation.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Psychiatry is one of the main classes in clinical medicine for each medical student. Mental disorders will be often seen in daily life of each clinical department, it will be helped for each student to do your works in future clinical practices and researching works after learning psychiatry

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

Theoretical class combined with trainee and practical class.

4.教学内容及进度安排 /Course Content & Schedule （ 按照教学周编排，应包含每节课教学内容及教学目标，如有作业及考核要求，请一并列明）

Course Schedule Week 1 (Sep 7): Introduction/Etiology Section 1: Master the concept of psychiatry and to be familiar with the research progress of psychiatry and related disciplines. Section 2: Understanding the development of psychiatry and its branches; Understanding the etiology of mental disorders. Week 2-3 (Sep 15 and Sep 22): Sings &Symptoms Section 1: Master the definition and clinical manifestations of common psychiatric symptoms such as thinking disorder, emotional disorders, motor and behavioral disorders, and insight. Section 2: Familiar with the definition and expression of memory disorder, mental disorder and consciousness disorder; Understanding the definition and expression of willpower disorder and attention disorder Week 4 (Sep 29): Dementia, Delirium & Amnestic Disorders Section 1: Master the clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles of delirium and dementia. Section 2: Understand the clinical manifestations and treatment principles of amnesia and other organic syndromes. Alzheimer disease Week 5: National Day：Schizophrenia and related disorders Section 1: Master the concept, clinical manifestation, classification, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, prognosis and outcome of schizophrenia. Section 2 ：Familiar with the principles of treatment and rehabilitation of schizophrenia, and understand the epidemiology, etiology and pathogenesis of schizophrenia. Week 6 (Oct 13): Depressive Disorders Section 1: Master the concept and clinical manifestations, classification, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment principles of depressive disorder； Section 2 ：Familiar with the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment principles of dysthymia, and understand the pathogenesis of dysthymia. Week 7 (Oct 20): Bipolar Disorder Section 1: Master the concept and clinical manifestations, classification, diagnosis and differential diagnosis of bipolar disorder； Section 2: Master the treatment principles of bipolar disorder. Week 8 (Oct 27): Anxiety and Stress-Related Disorders Section 1: Master the definition of anxiety disorder, the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment principles of panic disorder, phobia, generalized anxiety disorder. Section 2 ：Familiar with the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment principles of PTSD, acute stress disorder and adjustment disorders Week 9 (Nov 3): Personality Disorders and OCD Section 1: Master the concept and common characteristics of personality disorder and Obsessive-compulsive disorders (OCD) Section 2 ：Familiar with the classification, clinical manifestations, diagnostic criteria and treatment of personality disorders and OCD Week 10 (Nov 10): Child psychiatry Section 1: Master the clinical features, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and differential diagnosis of childhood autism and hyperactivity disorder, and to be familiar with the treatment of childhood autism and hyperactivity disorder. Section 2: Understanding the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, prevention and treatment of other mental disorders in children and adolescents. Week 11（Nov 17）：Drugs treatments Section 1: To master the pharmacological effects, classifications, indications, adverse reactions and treatment of antipsychotics. Section 2: To master the pharmacological effects, classification, indications, adverse reactions and treatment of antidepressants and mood stabilities drugs. Week 12 (Nov 24)： Introduction to Psychiatric Treatment (drugs) Section 1: Understand the clinical application of anti-anxiety drugs, drugs for dementia and other adjuvant therapies. Section 2; Familiar with physical therapy including modified electroconvulsive therapy (MECT), Rtms，DBS etc

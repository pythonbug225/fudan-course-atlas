课程简介（中文）

这是一门功能分析的入门课程。我们涵盖了Banach空间，Hilbert空间和局部凸向量空间的基本知识，以及这些空间的凸性和完备性的结果。我们进一步研究了线性空间之间的映射、弱拓扑和对偶的应用。研究了线性算子的谱及其在可交换C*代数中的应用。在整个课程中，将特别强调应用和与其他分析领域的联系。我们大致遵循Lax和eintredler - ward的文本。

课程简介（英文）

This is an introductory course in functional analysis. We cover the basics of Banach spaces, Hilbert spaces, and locally convex vector spaces and the consequences of convexity and completeness for these spaces. We further study maps between linear spaces, weak topologies, and the applications of duality. We also study of the spectrum of linear operators and the application to commutative C* algebras. Throughout the course, special emphasis will be given to applications and connections with other areas of analysis. We loosely follow the texts of Lax and Einsiedler-Ward.

教学目标

The course objective is to obtain a solid understanding of the Hahn-Banach theorem, the Banach-Steinhaus theorem and the Open-Mapping theorem and their applications to various areas of mathematics. Another objective is to obtain a solid understanding of the spectrum of an operator and the way in which it can be used. A third objective is to become familiar with a large class of function spaces, for example, the Lp spaces and various spaces of continuous or differentiable functions.

教学内容与进度安排

1 Vector spaces, normed linear spaces, Banach spaces and examples. Examples from function spaces, verifying completeness. 2 Inner products, Hilbert spaces, inequalities, examples. Closed subspaces and the algebra of projections. 3 Locally convex vector spaces, questions of metrizability, examples from function spaces. 4 Operators between above spaces, linear functionals. Hahn Banach separation theorems and applications. 5 Baire category and its consequences. Uniform boundedness principle, Open mapping theorem. 6 Non compactness of the unit ball, Banach Aloaglu theorem, applications of duality and weak convergence. 7 Vector valued integrals, Cauchy integral formula for functions valued in a complex Frechet space. 8 More on convex sets and weak topology. Krein Milman theorem, Krein Smulian theorem, Choquet’s theorem. 9 Continuous mappings on metric spaces, introduction to ergodic theory, existence of ergodic measures. 10 Small detour into Fourier analysis on Euclidean space, applications of weak topologies to existence theorems for solutions of differential equations 11 Introduction to the spectrum of operators on Banach spaces and Hilbert spaces. Spectral radius formula. 12 Functional calculus, projection-valued measures on the spectrum. 13 Applications to compact operators. 14 Back to ergodic theory, proof of mean ergodic theorem by use of spectrum. 15 More examples/review. 16 Final review or assessment 17 Final assessment 18 Grading work and result submission

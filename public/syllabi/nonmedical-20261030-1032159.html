课程简介（中文）

This course provides an intensive six-week introduction to the fundamentals of Fixed Income and Credit Risk. It is structured into three main parts: the first two focus on interest rate risk and related derivatives, while the final section covers corporate bonds and credit default swaps (CDS). As one of the more quantitatively rigorous courses, it emphasizes the tools, concepts, and practical applications essential to understanding fixed income markets and credit risk. Students should be prepared to apply formulas, perform calculations, and, in some cases, use Excel or MATLAB to complete problem sets and assignments. Beyond the quantitative focus, the course also explores the real-world relevance of fixed income instruments, which are deeply embedded in everyday financial decisions and closely tied to macroeconomics and policymaking. To highlight these connections, I will periodically comment on recent developments in fixed income markets and lead in-class discussions on current events.

教学目标

By the end of the course, students are expected to understand and communicate key concepts in fixed income and credit risk, both in academic and professional settings. They will also develop the analytical and computational skills needed to make informed financial decisions in these areas.

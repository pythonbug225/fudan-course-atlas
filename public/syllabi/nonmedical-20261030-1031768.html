世界传播学说史

2026春 · JCM80008.01 · 孙玮

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程为新闻传播专业博士选修课。 本课程通过重要经典文献阅读，从“传播”的不同理解，梳理、展示世界传播学术思想及其研究的历史脉络，重点结合“媒介”“技术”“城市”三个关键概念，对传播学术变迁的历史语境、动力因素、问题指向、不同路径等进行批判性分析和理解。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an elective for doctoral students in Journalism and Communication. Through the reading of key classic texts, this course traces and presents the historical development of global communication scholarship and its intellectual foundations from diverse perspectives on "communication." Focusing on three core concepts—"media," "technology," and "the city"—it offers a critical analysis and understanding of the historical contexts, driving forces, problematics, and varied approaches within the evolution of communication studies. On this basis, the course not only assesses the current state of the field but also explores alternative possibilities—what communication scholarship could or should be. It aims to inspire students to engage in reflective thinking, to ground their inquiry in China’s historical and contemporary communication realities, to raise new questions, and to open up new intellectual spaces, thereby contributing to the innovation of local communication research.

教学目标

本课程通过引导学生阅读重要经典文献，一方面对传播学术现状做出评价，另一方面，探讨传播学术研究本来或者应该存在的其他可能性，从而启发学生思考，并能立足于中国传播的历史和现实，提出新的研究问题，拓展新的空间，以推动本土传播研究的创新。

教学方式

讲授与讨论相结合。

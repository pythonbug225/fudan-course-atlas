金融市场实证分析

2025秋 · MF50010.01 · 耿哲

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

这门课程的主要内容包括： 理解金融市场与实体经济之间的联系，包括国债市场与央行货币政策、信贷市场与中国政府支持、股票市场与共同基金及对冲基金的表现等。 运用金融数据和计量经济学工具对金融模型进行实证检验，并在股票、国债、公司债券、外汇等主要金融市场中提取关键定价因素。理解不同市场之间的内在联系和相似性。 最后，我还会选择在课程期间发生的实际金融经济事件进行分析并得出结论，旨在帮助学生培养实践能力、分析能力和思维能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is about: Understand the link between financial markets and real economy, including the treasury market and the central bank's monetary policy, the credit market and the China’s government support, the stock market and the performance of mutual funds and hedge funds, etc. Use financial data and econometric tools to empirically test financial models and extract key pricing factors in each major financial market, including stocks, treasuries, corporate bonds, currencies. Understand the intrinsic connections and similarity across different markets. Finally, I will also choose actual events that occurred during the course lectures to conduct analysis and draw conclusions, aiming to help students develop practical, analytical and thinking abilities.

教学目标

1）Understand the pricing models and key risk factors of major financial markets and their similarity. 2）Understand the key role of central bank plays in the financial systems and how does central bank conduct monetary policy. 3）Understand the link between financial markets and the real economy. 4）Be able to quantify the impact of real financial events through quantitative financial data analysis.

教学方式

Class Discussion Group Presentation

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture Subject Homework 1 Introduction and Overview of China's Financial System 2 Treasury Market and Yield Curve 3 Central Banking and Monetary Policy HW1 4 Credit Market, Shadow Banking 5 Monetary Policy and Household Income 6 Equity Market HW2 7 Quantitative Investment

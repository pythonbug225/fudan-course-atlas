中国金融市场与金融机构

2025秋 · MF50014.01 · 耿哲

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程将探讨和研究主要金融市场及金融系统，并重点对比和分析中国与美国的区别。我们将聚焦金融系统与实体经济之间的关系，借助统计学工具，对金融理论模型与真实市场数据进行融合。通过系统化学习，学生不仅能够强化量化分析能力，更能培养对金融现象的经济学洞察力和直觉判断。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an in-depth exploration of key financial markets and system in general, followed by a discussion of its unique manifestation in the Chinese context. We shall focus on how the financial system is connected to the real economy. We will integrate finance models with financial data, with the help of statistical and econometric tools. Through this learning process, students are expected to gain strength not only in quantitative and analytical capabilities but also in economic insights and intuitions.

教学目标

1）Understand the key risk factors of the major financial markets including stock, treasury, corporate bond and currency. 2）Understand the key role of central bank plays in the financial systems and how does central bank conduct monetary policy. 3）Understand the role of banks and shadow banks. 4）Understand the connection between finance and real economy. 5）Analyze current domestic or global financial market news, and be able to conduct analysis and draw conclusions.

教学方式

Class Discussion Group Presentation

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture Subject Homework 1 Introduction and Overview of China's Financial System 2 Treasury Market and Yield Curve 3 Central Banking and Monetary Policy HW1 4 Credit Market, Shadow Banking 5 Monetary Policy and Household Income 6 Equity Market HW2 7 Quantitative Investment

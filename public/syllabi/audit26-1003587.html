课程简介（中文）

这门全英文课程中同学们将学习针对基于人群研究的某一医学研究领域的文献评阅，通过分析引用到的文献，最终完成一篇期末综述论文，体现对研究论文的评判性评价，对研究成果的总结，提供相应的结论，并将综述论文在课堂进行汇报展示。通过课程教学活动的设置和老师的引导，同学们将会获得解释研究结果、写作、以及口头表达的专业技能.

课程简介（英文）

In this course, conducted in English, students will learn to do a review of research literature about a topic in population-based medicine study. Based on a careful analysis of the cited literature students will construct a completely documented term paper in which they will critically evaluate the research literature, summarize the findings, and provide conclusions. The review will also be the basis for an oral presentation given to the class. From course activities and personal guidance, you will acquire skills at interpreting research results and at writing and speaking in a professional manner.

教学目标

Upon completion of this course, you should be able to:

  1. Develop and implement a library literature search on a specific topic using online databases
  2. Review original research papers - critically evaluate the methods, results, and conclusions of these papers
  3. Prepare summaries of research articles
  4. Prepare an outline of your research paper that addresses a scientific issue
  5. Write a concise review paper on a topic relevant to population-based medicine based on primary scientific sources, and include appropriate documentation of literature sources
  6. Participate in journal clubs and give an oral PowerPoint presentation on one research paper

教学方式

The primary teaching method is in-class teaching, with discussion, presentations, and group activities.

The design of class discussion or exercise, practice, experience and so on:

Two groups (of about eight students each) will be assigned at the beginning of the course. Thus, you will be in either Group 1 or Group 2 for the entire course. Each group will be subdivided into four teams (two students per team). You will have the same classmate on your team throughout the semester. You will meet with your teammate three times throughout the semester to discuss your assignments.  

Plagiarism quiz: Read the plagiarism tutorial, take the quiz, and provide a copy of the certificate of completion. You can access the tutorial and quiz at https://plagiarism.iu.edu/tutorials/index.html.To receive the certificate, 9 out of 10 questions must be answered correctly. 

Primary and secondary topic selection: Choose a primary and secondary population-based medicine topic of interest to you. The topic must lend itself to a scholarly investigation (i.e., there are enough papers in the peer-reviewed scientific literature to evaluate the evidence base on your chosen topic), and be of current interest. Controversial topics are acceptable, however, you must present both sides of the “debate” in your paper.

One original research paper and one review article summary: Select an original research paper and a review paper that you going to use for your paper, and summarize both papers (about 1 page or less for each paper).

PubMed search strategy and identify 10 research papers: Please list the keywords you used.

First three original research article summaries: Each summary should briefly describe the methods, results, and conclusions of the article along with the strengths and weaknesses of the study. Please use complete sentences and do not use abbreviations extensively, defining them when they are first used. The results should include numerical data and P-values of only the pertinent data. This summary should not be a copy of the abstract nor should it paraphrase the abstract.

Second seven original research article summaries: See above.

Paper Outline: You will use your research article summaries to develop an outline of your paper. Be ready to discuss your outline in the class “Preparing a Detailed Outline.” You likely will need to revise your outline based on the discussion in class. 

Draft of Paper: Although this can be called “preliminary,” this draft should be complete with all sections and all references included. It should be your best attempt at writing this paper. Drafts will be graded on your evaluation of the research quality, sophistication of data interpretation, and quality of writing. 

Final Paper: Papers will be graded on research quality, the sophistication of data interpretation, writing quality, and the practice application recommendations you make. All of the corrections and suggestions made must be incorporated into your final paper. The paper should be 8 pages and review 11 original research articles plus 1 review article. Your goal is to organize your review into a “story” that describes what you found, what it means, the implications of the research conducted, how the articles conflict or to agree with each other, and the practical application of the research summarized. Note that the title page, abstract, figures, and references are NOT included in the 8-page limit. If you want to include figures or tables, put them into an appendix at the end of the paper, and do NOT count these as part of your 8 pages. Please type your papers using 12 pt Times or Times New Roman. The paper should be double-spaced with one-inch margins. All pages must be numbered. The scientific papers cited in the draft are not to be handed in.

Oral presentation: Present your final paper in class with power points.

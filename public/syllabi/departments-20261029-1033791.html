课程简介（中文）

This course covers all key topics in corporate finance, including valuation, capital structure (debt vs. equity), and payout policy. The format consists of lectures, applied problem sets using Excel or your preferred programming language, and cases. We will begin with basic valuation concepts, including forecasting of free cash flows and appropriate discount rates. Next, we will conduct an in-depth analysis of the best uses of debt vs. equity financing, depending on firm and market characteristics. With these tools in hand, we will return to a more sophisticated application of valuation methods, including fundamental valuation, as well as valuation via comparables and multiples. Finally, we will study payout policy, the IPO decision, and an introduction to financial and real options.

教学目标

Understand Valuation Concepts: Students will learn to forecast free cash flows and compute appropriate discount rates to perform basic and advanced valuation analyses. Analyze Capital Structure Decisions: Students will evaluate the trade-offs between debt and equity financing, considering firm-specific and market characteristics. Apply Advanced Valuation Techniques: Students will master valuation methods, including fundamental analysis, comparables, and multiples, and apply these techniques to real-world cases. Evaluate Payout Policies: Students will analyze corporate payout strategies, including dividends and share repurchases, and assess their implications for firm value. Examine IPO Decisions: Students will explore the strategic and financial considerations behind initial public offerings, linking theory to practice. Understand Options in Corporate Finance: Students will learn the basics of financial options and how real options analysis applies to corporate investment decisions. Develop Practical Skills: Through applied problem sets and case studies, students will gain proficiency in using Excel or programming tools for financial analysis and decision-making. Enhance Critical Thinking: Students will learn to integrate quantitative and qualitative analysis to solve complex corporate finance problems and make informed recommendations.

教学方式

Lectures: Structured presentations introducing theoretical frameworks and analytical techniques, with interactive Q&A throughout. Case Studies: Three in-depth case analyses requiring students to apply course concepts to real company situations (Apple, HCA, Baidu). In-Class Exercises: Hands-on calculations and spreadsheet work to reinforce quantitative techniques. Group Work: Collaborative case assignments developing teamwork and presentation skills. Practice Problems: Applied problem sets using Excel or programming tools for financial analysis.

1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 《肿瘤分子生物学》课程提供了一种全新的视角，从癌症的分子和细胞生物学的基础科学出发，重点探讨癌症的机制、靶点及治疗方法。该课程通过展示对驱动癌症的缺陷机制的理解及如何促进新型靶向治疗药物的开发，深入解析癌症的治疗策略，尤其是每章都会向学生展示这些知识。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The course of the Molecular Biology of Cancer offers a fresh approach to the study of the basic science of the molecular and cellular biology of cancer and focuses on the mechanisms, targets, and therapeutics of cancer by showing how our understanding of the defective mechanisms that drive cancer is leading to the development of new targeted therapeutic agents. Uniquely, within each chapter, students are shown how this knowledge is being applied to develop new targeted therapeutic strategies.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

The course will emphasize new developments in the molecular biology of cancer. Opening with an overview of the key hallmarks of cancer as a disease state, the course will lead students through the principal processes that become altered in cancer cells and will explain how the failure of these processes can lead to the onset of cancer.

3 . 教学方式 /Teaching Methods （应体现实现教学目标所采用的具体策略、方法和组织形式）

This course is taught in a lecture format. The lecture is designed to provide students with maximum viewing access to audio-visual content, including PowerPoint presentations, videos, blackboard writing, etc. An audio-visual tool is a key element accompanying a traditional teaching method. Students will receive a thorough evaluation of their study via attendance, quizzes, assignments, discussions, presentations, and examinations. Free handouts are offered in each class so that students can focus on listening without missing important information. In addition, students can access online resources on campus or at home under the guidance of professors. Questions raised by students will be answered by the lecturer within the class and via email or WeChat communication by teachers and a TA. Questions regarding the Mid-term and Final Examinations will be answered by the course director.

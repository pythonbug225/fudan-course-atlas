房地产金融与投资

2025秋 · MF50008.01 · 战昕彤

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程专为对房地产这一重要另类投资领域感兴趣的硕士生设计，系统介绍房地产金融的核心概念、理论与行业实践。课程重点讲解房地产估值与评估方法，帮助学生掌握住宅及商业地产融资的分析技能，涵盖金融杠杆、抵押贷款产品与商业地产金融等主题。此外，课程还涉及房地产投资与开发的完整流程，从基础理论、投资决策分析工具，到实务中风险管理与收益评估的日常操作方法，旨在培养学生运用专业知识解决实际问题的综合能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for master students who are interested in an important alternative investment asset, i.e., real estate and exposes the students to concepts, theories, and industry practice in real estate finance. The course also introduces valuation and appraisal methods used for real estate finance and investment decision making. The main objective is to help students acquire the knowledge and develop the analytical skills of real estate finance, including the institutions and instruments used for finance residential and commercial real estate. The topics covered includes valuation methods, financial leverage, mortgage products, and commercial real estate finance. In addition, the This course provides all aspects of real estate investment and development, from basic theoretical concepts and analytical methods and tools useful for making investment and finance decisions regarding real estate assets, to the type of things practitioners deal with every day on analysis and management of investment risk and return.

教学目标

Master real estate valuation techniques and apply them effectively. Analyze fixed-rate (FRM) and adjustable-rate mortgages (ARM), compare payment structures, and evaluate financing choices. Assess refinancing decisions using the incremental cost of borrowing method. Examine key real estate market participants, market dynamics, and securitization’s role. Quantify risk-return trade-offs in property development and investment appraisals. Explore REITs’ evolution, industry structure, and growth drivers. Apply valuation methods to asset classes and project feasibility using market data for investment decisions. Develop analytical, teamwork, and presentation skills through case studies and group projects

教学方式

Lectures – Instructor-led sessions covering core theories, models, and industry practices with real-world examples. In-Class Exercises – Problem-solving activities to apply valuation techniques, mortgage calculations, and investment analysis tools during lectures. Group Discussions & Presentations – Structured debates and team-based analysis of case studies to evaluate financing strategies, market trends, and risk-return trade-offs. Case Studies – Hands-on examination of real estate investment scenarios, including REIT performance, refinancing decisions, and development feasibility. After-Class Assignments – Individual and group projects (e.g., property valuation reports, mortgage comparisons) to reinforce analytical skills. Peer Learning – Collaborative projects simulating real estate investment teams, emphasizing teamwork, leadership, and communication.

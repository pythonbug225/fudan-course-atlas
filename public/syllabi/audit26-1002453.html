课程简介（中文）

本课程主要介绍世界范围内重要的人体寄生虫，主要包括：总论、医学原虫学、医学蠕虫学、医学节肢动物学及寄生虫病原检查技术及抗寄生虫病药物。在教学内容上，以寄生虫的生活史、致病或传病作用及病原诊断为重点，联系流行因素及防治原则，并适当介绍本学科某些成熟的理论和技术，当前世界寄生虫学研究发展趋势。

课程简介（英文）

It is a branch of medical science dealing with parasites living in and on the body of humans and with aspects of this host-parasite relationships having medical significance. The content of rigorous course includes medical protozoa, medical helminthes and medical arthropods, many etiological agents responsible for global infectious diseases. It covers all biology, pathogenesis, diagnosis, epidemiology, treatment and prevention of parasites that related with infectious diseases in humans.

教学目标

The aim of Human Parasitology course is to introduce basic principles and application relevance of clinical disease for students who are in preparation for physicians and public health professionals.

教学方式

In order to foster a student’s ability to analyze and solve problems in their future clinical and public health career, the course will provide the conceptual basis for understanding pathogenic microorganisms and particularly address the fundamental mechanisms of their pathogenicity. It will also provide opportunities for a student to develop both informatic and diagnostic skills in parasitology, including the practical application and interpretation of laboratory tests for the diagnosis of infectious diseases.

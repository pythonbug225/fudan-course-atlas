课程简介（中文）

本课程简介中药药性基本理论及复方组方基本原理；有效成份、有效部位、复方和中药新剂型的选题与设计的基本原则；中药与天然药物的新药注册分类、国内外的发展状况与政府的管理要求；常用的中药炮制、提取、纯化、制剂成型新技术及其在新药开发中的应用；介绍中药新药质量标准和稳定性研究的技术和前沿发展，中药与天然药物新药药效、毒理研究的要求和程序，为今后进行中药和天然药物新药的开发奠定基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the basic theory of traditional Chinese medicine properties and the principles of compound prescriptions for selecting and designing effective ingredients, effective parts, compound formulas, and new dosage forms of traditional Chinese medicine; Classification of new drug registration for traditional Chinese medicine and natural medicine, development status in China and abroad. Common new technologies for processing, extracting, purifying, and formulating traditional Chinese medicine and their applications in new drug development will be introduced. Introduce the technology and cutting-edge development of quality standards and stability research for traditional Chinese medicine new drugs, as well as the requirements and procedures for the efficacy and toxicity research of traditional Chinese medicine and natural medicine new drugs, laying the foundation for the future development of traditional Chinese medicine and natural medicine new drugs.

教学目标

使学生了解中药新药研发的历史及前沿研究、认识中医药复方组方基本原理及其在中药新药开发中的重要指导意义、中药与天然药物的新药注册分类、国内外的发展状况与政府的管理要求、中药新药的研发要求与流程，中药炮制、提取、分离纯化、制剂成型的新技术与方法，中药质量控制与稳定性研究及其前沿发展、药效学与毒理学评价、专利保护的具体过程与要求。

教学方式

讲授为主，并辅以课堂讨论.

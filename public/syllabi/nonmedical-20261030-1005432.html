课程简介（中文）

英语学术语言特点（书面语语篇特征、名词化、被动结构、长句复杂句、时态、常见搭配，语篇衔接与连贯），研究选题、范围确定与原创性，文献回顾（文献检索、查阅、英语文献资料语言特征的分析和总结、文献回顾的写作技巧、引文格式和规范等），研究方法（如何在论文中表述研究思路、实验设计、数据采集等内容），导论与结论（论文导论的写法和常见误区，论文结论部分的写法（总结、进一步论述，等等）），论文摘要（摘要的基本类型、语言和语篇特点、论文标题、关键词）。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to enhance your academic English writing and research skills. You will learn the characteristics of academic English, including discourse features, nominalization, passive structures, complex sentences, tenses, common collocations, and coherence. The course covers selecting a research topic, defining scope, and ensuring originality. You'll master literature search and review, summarizing literature, writing a literature review, and proper citation practices. Additionally, you'll learn to describe research design, detail experimental setups, and discuss data collection methods and ethics. The course also includes writing effective introductions and conclusions, crafting abstracts, and selecting keywords. Through weekly modules, lectures, practical assignments, workshops, and peer reviews, you will develop the ability to write clear, coherent academic texts and present your findings effectively. Evaluation is based on ongoing assignments, a final research paper, and an oral presentations. Join us to elevate your academic writing and research skills.

教学目标

The course aims to enhance students’ academic English writing skills and research capabilities. By understanding the characteristics of academic English, the organization of research papers, discourse structure, and writing conventions, students will be equipped to produce high-quality research papers and participate in international academic exchanges effectively.

教学方式

本课程采用线下教学加线上补充模式，注重学生的课堂操练。教学方法包括： 小组讨论：通过小组合作，促进学生之间的观点交流和团队合作能力。 课堂演讲与展示：锻炼学生的口头表达能力、思辨能力和信息整合能力。 互动式讲解：教师结合多媒体教学材料，指导学生进行语言知识、思维知识和文化知识的建构，并随时与学生互动答疑。 线上课堂练习与作业提交：通过课堂练习以及提交课堂作业及时巩固知识。

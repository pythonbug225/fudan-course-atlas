课程简介（中文）

这门两学分的必修课程旨在向学生介绍西班牙语写作与作文的基本原理。课堂活动侧重于强化语法运用、扩展词汇量，并培养学生对西班牙语文体风格的意识。通过课堂中的引导性练习、课后作业以及简短阅读材料，学生将学习如何写出准确的句子、将思想组织成连贯的段落，并运用多种手段提高文本的衔接性与连贯性。本课程旨在提升学生在日常语境中的书面表达能力，并为今后在职业用途方面进一步学习西班牙语写作打下坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This two-credit compulsory course introduces students to the basic principles of Spanish writing and composition. Class activities are designed to strengthen grammatical use, expand vocabulary, and develop awareness of style in Spanish. Through guided practice in class, homework assignments, and short readings, students will learn to produce accurate sentences, organize ideas into paragraphs, and use several devices to improve cohesion and coherence. The course supports the development of effective written communication for everyday contexts and provides a solid basis for continued study in Spanish writing for professional purpose.

教学目标

The aim of this course is to develop students’ Spanish writing skills at the intermediate to upper-intermediate level. Through guided writing practice and selected readings, students will learn to produce longer, and coherent texts, with stronger control of vocabulary, grammar, and style. The course focuses on text structure, effective use of connectors and discourse markers. Special attention will be given to revising and editing strategies, as well as to adapting tone and register according to different communicative contexts. By the end of the semester, students will be able to write well-structured texts such as descriptions, narratives, opinion pieces, and short argumentative essays, showing improved grammatical accuracy, lexical variety, and communicative effectiveness across a range of everyday situations in the Spanish-speaking world

教学方式

The course combines instructor-led explanations, interactive classroom activities, and regular homework assignments. Each lesson is typically divided into two parts: the first introduces writing techniques, key grammar points from intermediate to upper-intermediate levels, and models drawn from short prose texts; the second focuses on guided practice, writing tasks, and the revision of students’ own compositions. The course adopts a collaborative learning approach in which active participation, consistent practice, and ongoing feedback are essential to student progress

教学内容与进度安排

Course Introduction – Overview of course objectives and assessment; review of cohesion and paragraph structure. Paragraph Development and Text Unity – Topic sentences, supporting ideas, and concluding sentences. Advanced Cohesion – Contrast, concession, emphasis, and reformulation. Narration with Style – Writing narratives with richer detail; revision of past tense usage. Description with Precision – Expanding descriptive vocabulary; contextual description in short texts. Expressing Opinion with Nuance – Argument vs. personal preference; hedging and emphasis; reflexive verbs in Spanish. Argumentative Paragraphs – Claim, reasons, examples; paragraph structure for persuasion. Text Organization and Planning – Outlines, drafting strategies, and logical structure. Midterm Exam. Formal Tone – Introductions and conclusions; neutral tone; avoiding overly informal expressions. Professional Writing – Requests, complaints, applications; politeness strategies and model analysis. Writing Summaries and Paraphrasing – Summarizing longer texts and paraphrasing. Reporting and Describing Information – Writing short reports; objective tone and source attribution. Cause, Consequence, and Concession – Advanced structures to atribution; indirect statement. Comparing and Contrasting Ideas – Structured comparison; connectors and rhetorical organization. Writing Short Essays– Introduction–body–conclusion; integrating examples; clarity in longer texts. Revision and Editing Workshop – Peer feedback; rewriting for clarity, cohesion, and style; common error patterns. Final Exam

课程简介（中文）

这是一门量子场论的入门课程，量子场论是一个结合量子力学、狭义相对论和场论来描述高能物理和凝聚态系统中的粒子的理论框架。本课程主要探讨四维时空及其在高能物理中的应用，如量子电动力学。

课程简介（英文）

This is an introductory course on Quantum Field Theory, a theoretical framework combining quantum mechanics, special relativity and field theory to describe particles in high energy physics and condensed matter systems. This course will mainly focus on four dimensional space-time and applications in high energy physics, such as Quantum Electrodynamics(QED)

教学目标

The main objectives of this course are: 1) Study the canonical quantization of Klein-Gordon and Dirac equations. 2) Introduce the notion of quantum fields for the theories of free scalars, spinors and vector fields (both massive and massless) using both Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formalism. 3) Introduce tools for studying quantum field theories with interacting particle: S-matrix, propagators and Feynman rules. 4) Apply these techniques to study explicit physical phenomena in QED, such as Moeller and Bhabha diffusion.

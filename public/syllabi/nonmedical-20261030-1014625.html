课程简介（中文）

可重构计算指的是在运行中使用现场可编程门阵列（FPGA）等可重构器件的系统。这使得高度灵活的系统得以实现，此类系统能够根据手头的问题调整并优化其计算架构。本课程将介绍可重构计算系统的核心概念、技术、方法及应用领域。

课程简介（英文）

Reconfigurable computing refers to systems that utilize reconfigurable devices such as field-programmable gate arrays (FPGA) in their operation. This enables implementation of highly flexible systems that are able to adapt and optimize their computing architectures to the problems at hand. The course introduces the key concepts, technologies, methods, and application areas of reconfigurable computing systems.

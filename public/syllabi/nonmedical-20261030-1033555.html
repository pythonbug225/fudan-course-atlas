课程简介（中文）

本课程的课程简介: 本课程分为两部分。一是通过阅读、讨论现象学、诠释学的一系列经典文本（海德格尔、伽达默尔、梅洛-庞蒂），了解现象学和诠释学美学的哲学谱系、方法论、重要概念和基本关切，尝试使用现象学和诠释学的视域解读艺术作品、探究其界限，深入思考人作为感知的存在如何与作品发生关联。二是结合受现象学、诠释学美学影响、使用其方法论进行艺术批评的现当代思想家（福柯、南希、迪迪-于贝尔曼、Ettinger），体会图像、感知（视觉、触觉、听觉、通感）和意义生成之间的互动。

课程简介（英文）

Course Description This course is divided into two parts: Part I: Foundational Lineage and Methodology By reading and discussing a series of classic texts in phenomenology and hermeneutics (Heidegger, Gadamer, Merleau-Ponty), students will explore the philosophical genealogy, methodology, key concepts, and fundamental concerns of these fields. We will attempt to use phenomenological and hermeneutic horizons to interpret artworks and investigate their boundaries, while reflecting deeply on how human beings, as perceptual subjects, relate to the work of art. Part II: Contemporary Applications and Artistic Criticism Building on the foundations of the first part, we will examine modern and contemporary thinkers influenced by these traditions (Foucault, Nancy, Didi-Huberman, Ettinger). This section focuses on the interaction between images, perception (visual, tactile, auditory, and synesthetic), and the generation of meaning.

教学目标

教学目的： 学生应对现象学、诠释学美学的经典文本、哲学谱系、方法论、基本概念和关切形成一定了解，能通过现象学、诠释学视域对相关艺术作品做出思考和批评，以作为存在者的诠释者为立场对作品、感知、意义生成的结构、形式、流变和当代性进行思考和言说。

课程简介（中文）

内科学（上）的基本内容包括：

（1）内科学绪论、呼吸系统疾病、心血管系统疾病、消化系统疾病，教授以上各系统中常见疾病的病因、病理、临床表现、诊断与鉴别诊断要点、防治原则等。

（2）教授临床诊疗的基本思路，培养学生的临床思维能力，在床旁教学的过程中，指导学生如何接触患者，如何与患者建立基本关系并获取有效的病史信息，以及在体格检查等过程中的医学人文关怀等。

课程简介（英文）

The basic content of Internal Medicine (Part I) includes: (1) Introduction to Internal Medicine, respiratory system diseases, cardiovascular system diseases, and digestive system diseases. It teaches etiology, pathology, clinical manifestations, key points of diagnosis and differential diagnosis, and principles of prevention and treatment for common diseases in these systems. (2) It imparts the basic thinking for clinical diagnosis and treatment and cultivates students' clinical thinking ability. During bedside teaching, students are guided on how to approach patients, how to establish basic relationships with patients and obtain effective medical history information, and the importance of medical humanistic care in physical examinations and other processes.

教学目标

(1) To master the etiology, pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, diagnosis and treatment principles of common and key diseases of various systems.

(2) Combined with the significance of symptoms, signs and laboratory tests learnt during the diagnosis course, deepen the knowledge and understanding of the important features of the diseases, as well as the main points of differential diagnosis among the diseases.

(3) Further proficiency in general physical examination and history writing.

Ability Objectives

To master the learning method of internal medicine, to learn the basic theories and fundamental knowledge of internal medicine as well as its clinical application by combining multidisciplinary knowledge, and to cultivate the ability of clinical thinking. To achieve the ability to obtain history information, symptoms and signs through contact with patients.  

Literacy Objectives

(1) Correctly understand the relationship between disease and patient. To develop the concept of following an evidence-based perspective and evidence-based decision-making.

(2) Throughout the teaching and learning process of internal medicine, students will understand the importance of developing a rigorous professional attitude to clinical work.

(3) To develop students' ability to care for the humanities, so that they can demonstrate their deep care and great love for patients in medical practice.

教学方式

The theory course adopts multimedia teaching format, combined with clinical bedside teaching of key diseases and common diseases after the class.

Bedside teaching adopts group teaching and case analysis and discussion based on cases.

Presentation homework supplied by the students for every system.

Practice for clinical thinking by CBL in clinical ward:

1) Gastrointestinal and Hepatic System: 4 times, 2 hours each time, main diseases including Peptic Ulcer, Liver Cirrhosis, Acute Pancreatitis and Gastrointestinal bleeding

2) Cardiovascular system: 4 times, 2 hours each time, main diseases including CHD, Essential Hypertension, Arrhythmias and Heart Failure

3) Respiratory system: 4 times, 2 hours each time, main diseases including Respiratory Failure, COPD, Pulmonary Infection and Lung cancer

Course Requirements:

Disorders of the Cardiovascular System

1-Introduction to Cardiovascular Disease

2-Heart Failure

To master the main causes and pathogenesis of heart failure.

To master the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and differential diagnosis of heart failure.

To master the principles of therapy in heart failure, especially appropriate application of blockade of rennin-angiotensin-aldosterone and adrenergic system, diuretics, and vasodilators; and to master the salvage of acute heart failure.

3-Arrhythmias

Mastering the causes, clinical features, diagnostic and therapeutic principles of common cardiac arrhythmia.

To be familiar with the electrocardiography features for the diagnosis of the common arrhythmia.

To understand the mechanism of arrhythmias.

4-Valvular Heart Disease

To master pathology, physical findings, and laboratory examination of mitral and aortic valve diseases.

To be familiar with the etiology, differential diagnosis, treatment of mitral and aortic valve diseases.

To know pathology, clinical manifestation, physical findings, and laboratory examination of other valvular diseases.

5-coronary heart disease  

To master the risk factors, mechanism, and prevention of atherosclerosis

To master the mechanism of myocardium ischemia in coronary heart diseases (CHD)

To master the main clinical types of coronary artery diseases: chronic stable CHD and acute coronary syndrome (ACS)

To master the characteristics of chest pain and therapeutic principle of stable angina pectoris

To master the mechanism and clinical manifestation of ACS

To master clinical manifestation, ECG, cardiac markers, diagnosis, differentiation, and management of acute myocardial infarction

To be familiar with the ECG changes, classification, and differentiation of stable angina pectoris

To be familiar with the complication of acute myocardial and approach of reperfusion therapy

To be familiar with the pathophysiology of the angina pectoris and myocardial infarction

To be familiar with the common drugs for the anti-ischemic and anti-thrombotic therapy

6-Essential Hypertension

To master the diagnosis and treatment of hypertension

To be familiar with secondary hypertension and malignant hypertension

7-Cardiomyopathies and myocarditis

To master

1.classification of cardiomyopathy.  

2.Dialated cardiomyopathy: symptoms, physical examination, laboratory examinations and treatment.  

3.hypertrophic cardiomyopathy: symptoms, physical examination, laboratory examinations and treatment.

To be familiar with

1.treatment of dilated cardiomyopathy and hypertrophic cardiomyopathy

  1. restrictive cardiomyopathy
  2. myocarditis

8-Infective endocarditis

To master the definition, clinical manifestations, laboratory examinations, diagnosis, and therapeutic principles of infective endocarditis.

To grasp the etiology, pathogenesis, and differential diagnosis of infective endocarditis.

To understand the prevention methods and prognosis.

Disorders of the Gastrointestinal System

1-Introduction to Gastrointestinal and Hepatic Diseases

2-Gastritis

To master the etiology and clinical manifestations of acute gastritis.

To master the classification, diagnosis, and differential diagnosis of chronic gastritis.

To be familiar with etiology, pathology, and therapeutic principles of chronic gastritis.

3-Peptic Ulcer

To master the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and therapy of this disease.

To be familiar with the etiology and pathogenesis of this disease.

To understand the complications of this disease.

4-Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease

To master the definition, classification, manifestation and diagnosis of GERD.

To be familiar with the pathogenesis, diagnostic measures, and therapy of GERD

5- Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD)

1.To understand the risk factors, diagnostic criteria, and treatment principles of MASLD

2.To be familiar with the pathology and prognosis of MASLD

3.To gain an understanding of the pathogenesis of MASLD

6-Irritable Bowel Syndrome

To master the definition, Rome III criteria, major clinical features, treatment of IBS.

To be familiar with the pathogenesis of IBS, the laboratory parameters.

7-Gastrointestinal bleeding

To master etiology of GI bleeding, high risk patients, endoscopic examination, therapy of GI bleeding.

To be familiar with manner of presentation, resuscitation of GI bleeding patients, GI bleeding of obscure origin.

8-Inflammatory Bowel Disease

To master clinical manifestations, diagnosis and differential diagnosis of this disease.

To be familiar with the therapeutic principles of this disease.

To understand the etiology and pathogenesis of this disease.

9-Liver Cirrhosis

To master the definition, etiology, major clinical features, treatment of liver cirrhosis and the management of its complications.

To be familiar with the pathogenesis of cirrhosis, the laboratory parameters which are associated with etiology and severity (compensate or decompensate cirrhosis).

10-Hepatic encephalopathy

To master clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, and therapeutic principles.

To be familiar with the pathogenesis of hepatic encephalopathy, and to make a relationship with the therapy.

To understand the etiology of hepatic encephalopathy and the diagnostic methods of subclinical hepatic encephalopathy.

11-Acute Pancreatitis

To master clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and therapy of both kinds of acute pancreatitis.

To be familiar with the differential diagnosis, complications, and prognostic factors of this disease.

To understand the common etiology, classification of pathology and prevention of this disease.

12-Hepatocellular Carcinoma

To master: diagnosis and treatment of HCC

To be familiar with epidemiology, etiology

 

Disorders of the Respiratory System

1-Introduction to Respiratory System

2-Respiratory Failure

To master the definition, diagnosis, principle of treatment of respiratory failure, understand report of blood gas analysis

To be familiar with pathophysiology, clinical presentation of respiratory failure, the principle of mechanical ventilation

3-acute respiratory distress syndrome

To master definition, diagnosis, and principle of treatment of ARDS

To be familiar with typical imaging of ARDS, pathophysiology of ARDS, oxygen therapy, and mechanical ventilation.

4-Pulmonary Infection (Pneumonia)

To master concept, major category, diagnosis, and principal treatment of pneumonia induced by gram positive and negative pneumonia.

To be familiar with properties of major bacteria species, the features of pneumonia induced by different bacteria, other pathogens induced lung infection.

5-Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease

To master the definition, clinical manifestation, diagnosis, staging, treatment, and prevention of COPD.

To be familiar with the epidemiology, pathophysiology, and pathogenesis of COPD.

To be familiar with GOLD guideline and China guidelines for COPD.

6-Pulmonary Tuberculosis

To master the clinical manifestation, diagnosis, and treatment of pulmonary tuberculosis.

To be familiar with the epidemiology, current challenges, and pathogenesis of pulmonary tuberculosis.

7-Bronchial asthma

To master the definition, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, classification according to levels of control, severity of exacerbation, and treatment of asthma.

To be familiar with the epidemiology, pathophysiology, and pathogenesis of asthma.

To be familiar with patient education and management of asthma.

8-Lung cancer

To master the classifications, TNM staging, clinical manifestations and therapy of lung cancer.

To be familiar with the etiology and pathogenesis.

9-Interstitial Lung Disease and Sarcoidosis

To master the common features of ILD and clinical feature, treatment of IPF.

To be familiar with some knowledge of mechanism, diagnosis of ILD

10-Disease of Pleura

To master the common etiology and differential diagnosis of pleural effusion.

To be familiar with the prevention and treatment of tuberculous pleurisy.

To master the classification and manifestation of pneumothorax.

11-Pulmonary Thromboembolism

To master the definition, risk factors, clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and treatment of acute PE.

To be familiar with the epidemiology, pathophysiology and prevention of acute PE.

To be familiar with the manifestations, diagnosis and treatment of chronic PE.

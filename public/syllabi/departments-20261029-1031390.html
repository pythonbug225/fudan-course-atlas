课程简介（中文）

本课程面向已完成中级1阶段学习的国际学生，系统提升综合汉语能力。课程以真实生活、校园与基础商务情境为核心，融合词汇与语法拓展、语篇理解与情境表达训练，强化实际交际与跨文化沟通能力。采用以学生为中心的任务互动式方法，融入文化要素与AI 智能辅助，提供个性化学习支持，构建以学生为中心的数据驱动学习体验。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for learners who have completed Intermediate Chinese I, possess a solid foundation in everyday Chinese, and are ready to advance to a higher level of proficiency. The course focuses on further developing integrated language skills, with particular emphasis on listening and speaking in extended and more complex communicative contexts. Students expand their vocabulary and grammatical knowledge through practical topics related to daily life, campus communication, and introductory business settings. Learning activities include drills, role plays, interactive tasks, and guided discussions to strengthen fluency and communicative confidence. Cultural elements are systematically incorporated to enhance learners’ intercultural awareness and ability to handle real-life situations in China. The course also integrates artificial intelligence support through a customized AI agent, which provides personalized learning materials, adaptive practice, and targeted feedback based on individual progress, creating a student-centered and data-driven learning experience.

教学目标

This course aims to develop further integrated Chinese language abilities（including reading, listening, speaking, and character writing skills). Based on the communicative approach, learners are provided with various learning materials to practice their learning. At the end of this course, the learners will reach the following proficiency: 1)Recognizing more than 550 new words and reaching with vocabulary level of 1200 in total 2)Grasping around 300 key Chinese grammar patterns 3)Developing expressions related to 22 practical topics 4)Improving reading pace and efficiency 5)Building essential oral Chinese ability for real-life situations 6)Developing greater insight into Chinese culture

教学方式

教学方式：本课程以交际型语言教学法（CLT）为核心，强调“以学为本”的教学理念，融合任务型教学、情境教学、文化导入、产出导向式教学以及AI辅助教学等多种方式，系统提升学生的语言运用能力与文化适应力。

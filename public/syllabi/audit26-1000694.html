课程简介（中文）

本课程分为6个模块：1.行政文书写作的内涵；2.行政文书的主题与材料；

3.行政文书的格式与结构；4.行政文书的适用语言与修辞；5.行政文书的行文规则与流程；6.各类行政文书的写作

课程简介（英文）

The course is divided into 6 modules: 1. The meaning of administrative document writing; 2. The theme and materials of administrative documents; 3. The format and structure of administrative documents; 4. Applicable language and rhetoric of administrative documents; 5. The writing rules and process of administrative documents; 6. The various types of administrative document writing.

教学目标

本课程通过对行政文书写作的理论知识和行政文书写作的实践活动讲解，帮助学生基本掌握行政文书写作的方法，培养学生在政府、国有企业中的就业技巧，从而提升复旦大学本科毕业生的综合就业能力。

The course will help students to master the basic methods of administrative document writing through the explanation of theoretical knowledge and practical activities of administrative document writing, to cultivate employment skills of students in the government and state-owned enterprises, thereby improving the comprehensive employment ability of undergraduate graduates of Fudan University .

教学方式

教师讲解与学生写作练习相结合。

Combining teacher explanation with student writing practice

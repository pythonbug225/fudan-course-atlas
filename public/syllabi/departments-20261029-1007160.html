医疗大数据高等统计方法

2025秋 · MAS70001.01 · 傅博

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

课程主要介绍分析健康医疗数据的相关统计方法，包括生存分析、因果推断、医学诊断与筛查统计方法等。

课程简介（英文）

In this course, we will introduce some statistical methods related to health data science, including topics such as survival data analysis, causal inference and statistical methods in diagnostic tests and screening.

教学目标

1 理解生物统计学基本概念与原理。学习多种生物统计方法，包括生存分析、因果推断以及医学诊断检验与筛查统计方法等。 2 借助统计软件，在实际数据分析中应用这些方法。 3 解释实际数据分析的结果。 To understand the concepts and principles of biostatistics and study various biostatistical topics including survival analysis, causal inference and diagnosit test and screening. To apply these methods in real data analysis with the aid of statistical software To interpret the results from a real data analysis

教学方式

课程以讲授为主，包括课程讨论、案例分析、小组分工的项目与报告展示。

教学内容与进度安排

1. Health data analysis examples 2. Introduction to time-to-event data: survival function, Hazard function, Mean life time (life  expectancy), Mean life residual time 3. Parametric failure time models (e.g. exponential, weibull, log-normal) 4. Estimate the survival function: 1) Kaplan-Meier product-limit method; 2) life table estimate; 3) Nelson Aalen estimate. 5. Cox proportional hazards (PH) model 6. Accelerated failure time (AFT) model 7. Model selection, model diagnostics. analysis of data where proportional hazards assumption violated 8. Analysis of correlated censored data, competing risks and recurrent event data 9. Introduction to causal inference, estimating causal effects of treatment or exposure from observational data 10 Estimating causal effects of treatment in randomised controlled trials with non-compliance 11 Deal with observed confounders: matching, stratification, propensity score 12 Deal with unobserved confounders (instrumental variable models, Mendelian randomisation) 13. Introduction to diagnostic test and screening 14. Sensitivity and specificity, positive and negative predicted values,likelihood ratio 15 ROC curve, tests based on logistic regression, comparison between diagnostic studies 16 Design of diagnostic studies, bias in diagnostic tests

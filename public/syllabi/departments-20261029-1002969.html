经济全球化与中国

2025秋 · ECON30152.01 · 刘宇

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本研讨会课程围绕全球化的影响展开探讨，重点关注中国视角下的相关议题。课程内容将涵盖课堂阅读、专题讨论与小型课题研究。我们将共同研读近年来的经济学文献，研究范畴从鸦片战争时期延伸至当下的贸易摩擦阶段。 本课程采用 “学术论文深度研读” 与 “学生主导的现实议题展示及讨论” 相结合的模式。其中，现实议题的素材来源广泛，包括学术研究论文、新闻报道、商业案例分析、实际案例见闻及个人观察所得。 课程要求学生每周在课堂上阅读 2 至 4 篇与当周 “全球化特定主题” 相关的文章。此外，学生需在课程初期启动一项小型课题研究，在学期中期获取相关反馈，并在学期末向全班展示研究成果。

课程简介（英文）

This seminar course explores the impacts of globalization, with a focus on China. The course will include in-class readings, discussions, and mini-projects. We will together read through the recent economics literature, ranging from the Opium War to the ensuing trade war. The course combines rigorous reading of academic research papers with students-led presentations and discussions of real-world issues, which will be sourced from research papers, news, business case studies, anecdotal evidence, and self-observations. Each week, students are expected to read 2-4 articles in class related to a particular theme of globalization. Students will begin working on a mini-project at the start of the course, receive feedback midway through, and present their findings to the class by the end of the semester.

教学目标

Number Course Objectives 1 Describe the ups and downs of globalization; 2 Learn the key events related to China and globalization and understand their impacts; 3 Learn state-of-the-art empirical methods; 4 Improve English reading skills; 5 Get some research experience.

教学方式

1. Lectures 2. Literature reading 3. Interactions & Discussions 4. Group presentation 5. Final exam

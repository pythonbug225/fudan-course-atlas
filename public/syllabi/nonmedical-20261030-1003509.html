课程简介（中文）

这门课程旨在培养学生在统计推理和数据分析方面的基础技能，重点关注参数估计和模型解释。在整个学期中，学生将学习如何估计并解释描述单一变量以及变量之间关系的关键统计参数。主要分析工具是普通最小二乘回归（OLS 回归），同时也可能介绍其他统计模型和研究设计。课程目标是使学生具备运用统计技术、以清晰易懂的语言解释分析结果，并从数据中做出有根据的推断的能力。 在本学期中，你将学习如何计算以下内容，并用通俗语言进行解释： 单变量统计量（描述一个变量的特征） 双变量统计量（描述两个变量之间的关系） 多变量统计量（描述三个或更多变量之间的关系） 对于变量之间关系的分析，将关注以下几个方面： 方向性（例如：相比年轻人，40岁以上的人是否更有可能认为在择业时“工作稳定性”最为重要？） 强度（例如：40岁以上的人比年轻人更有可能认为工作稳定性最重要的程度有多大？） 可推广性（例如：基于这个样本，我们能否推断在总体中，40岁以上的人比年轻人更有可能认为工作稳定性最重要？如果可以，这种差异的具体程度是多少？）

课程简介（英文）

This course develops foundational skills in statistical reasoning and data analysis, with an emphasis on parameter estimation and model interpretation. Throughout the semester, students will learn how to estimate and interpret key statistical parameters that describe both individual variables and relationships between variables. The primary analytical tool will be Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression, though other statistical models and research designs may be introduced as the course progresses. The goal is to equip students with the ability to apply statistical techniques, interpret results in clear and accessible language, and make informed inferences from data. Over the course of this semester, you will learn how to calculate the following and to interpret them in clear language: Univariate statistics (describing one variable at a time) Bivariate statistics (describing a relationship between two variables) Multivariate statistics (describing relationships among three or more variables) The analysis of relationships between variables looks at their: Direction (e.g., are people over 40 are more or less likely than younger adults to say that job stability is most important when choosing a job?) Strength (e.g., how much more likely are people over 40 than younger adults to say that job stability is most important when choosing a job?), and Generalizability (e.g., based on this sample, can we conclude that people over 40 are more likely than younger adults to say that job stability is most important when choosing a job in the population? If so, by how much?)

教学目标

This course aims to achieve the following objectives: Cultivating a scientific and critical thinking for undergraduate students who are interested in public policy analysis with quantitative methods (“价值引领”); Teaching conceptual and theoretical basics for understanding public policy process and examining policy choices (“知识传授”); Filling the capability gap of students with various disciplinary backgrounds to prepare for a more focused study in policy science (“能力培养”) Equipping students with practical, state-of-the-art econometric techniques to establish a solid foundation for their future research in quantitative analysis (“技能训练”)

教学方式

Teaching Methods: Lectures as the main component (讲授为主) The instructor will deliver the course content primarily by lectures. Class discussion and student presentations will be used as the complementary components of the course.

全球公共政策

2026春 · MANA60015.01 · 叶欣

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程围绕全球公共政策核心主题展开，涵盖全球化与治理转型、关键行为体互动、全球公共产品供给等内容，聚焦气候变化、公共卫生、移民难民、技术治理等全球议题。通过理论讲解、案例分析与实践探讨，剖析从威斯特伐利亚体系到多利益相关方框架的演变，对比不同治理模式的成效与局限。课程兼顾历史脉络与当代挑战，引导学生理解全球政策的制定逻辑、权力动态及实施障碍，培养分析全球复杂问题的能力，最终通过研究论文与展示深化对全球治理实践的认知。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on core themes of global public policy, covering globalization and governance transformation, interactions among key actors, and the provision of global public goods. It addresses pressing global issues such as climate change, public health, migration and refugees, and technology governance. Through theoretical lectures, case analyses, and practical discussions, the course examines the evolution from the Westphalian system to multi-stakeholder frameworks, contrasting the effectiveness and limitations of different governance models. Integrating historical contexts with contemporary challenges, it guides students to understand the formulation logic, power dynamics, and implementation barriers of global policies, fostering their ability to analyze complex global issues. Students will deepen their understanding of global governance practices through research papers and presentations.

教学目标

本课程以价值引领为核心，秉持公平正义理念，引导学生认识全球治理的多元性与包容性，树立人类命运共同体意识。在知识传授上，系统讲解全球化、全球公共产品等核心概念，梳理全球治理历史演进脉络，涵盖气候、健康、技术等关键议题的政策框架与实践案例。在能力培养方面，通过案例分析、实证研究等形式，提升学生解构复杂全球问题的能力、跨主体互动分析能力与政策评估素养，锻炼学术研究与口头表达能力，最终助力学生形成兼具国际视野与现实关怀的思维方式，为参与全球治理相关研究与实践奠定基础。 Centered on value guidance, this course upholds the concept of fairness and justice, guiding students to recognize the diversity and inclusiveness of global governance and foster a sense of a community with a shared future for mankind. In terms of knowledge impartment, it systematically explains core concepts such as globalization and global public goods, sorts out the historical evolution of global governance, and covers policy frameworks and practical cases of key issues including climate, health, and technology. For competence development, through case analysis, empirical research, and other forms, it enhances students’ abilities to deconstruct complex global problems, analyze interactions across multiple actors, and develop policy evaluation literacy, while refining their academic research and oral expression skills. Ultimately, it helps students form a way of thinking that combines an international perspective with practical concern, laying a foundation for their participation in global governance-related research and practice.

教学方式

本课程采用理论讲授、实践探究、学术产出三位一体的教学模式，通过多元策略助力教学目标实现。在理论传授上，以专题讲座为核心，系统拆解核心概念与政策框架，结合案例对比（如成功与失败的多边合作案例）深化理解；配套指定经典文献与前沿研究，引导学生夯实理论基础。实践层面融入互动式教学，包括条约谈判模拟、政策议题结构化辩论等，锻炼学生的协作与思辨能力；借助数据可视化分析、案例解构等方式，强化对现实问题的解读能力。组织形式上，兼顾集体授课与分组研讨，后期通过15分钟PPT展示+5 分钟问答的学术汇报环节，搭配3000字实证研究论文撰写，推动学生将理论转化为学术实践，全面提升研究、表达与政策分析素养。 This course adopts a trinity teaching model integrating theoretical instruction, practical inquiry, and academic output, employing diverse strategies to facilitate the achievement of teaching objectives. In terms of theoretical delivery, it centers on thematic lectures to systematically dissect core concepts and policy frameworks, with case comparisons (e.g., successful vs. failed multilateral cooperation cases) to deepen understanding. Complementary assigned classic literature and cutting-edge research guide students to consolidate their theoretical foundation. For practical learning, interactive teaching methods are incorporated, including treaty negotiation simulations and structured debates on policy issues, to foster students’ collaborative and critical thinking skills. Data visualization analysis and case deconstruction are utilized to enhance students’ ability to interpret real-world problems. In terms of organizational form, the course balances collective teaching with group discussions. In the later stage, through an academic presentation session consisting of a 15-minute PPT presentation plus 5-minute Q&A, combined with the writing of a 3,000-word empirical research paper, students are encouraged to translate theory into academic practice, comprehensively improving their research, expression, and policy analysis competencies.

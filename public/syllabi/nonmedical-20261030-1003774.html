金融中的数学方法

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程重点讲授买方与卖方机构在数理金融领域常用的数学理论与方法。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the mathematical theories and techniques that are frequently used in the buy-side and sell-side mathematical and quantitative finance areas.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to review the basic concepts of university level mathematics such as mathematical analysis, advanced algebra, real and functional analysis, ordinary differential equations and partial differential equations, and to equip students with the theories and techniques that will be frequently applied in buy-side and sell-side mathematical and quantitative finance areas. The theories and techniques include, but are not limited to probability theory, mathematical statistics, discrete time econometrics, stochastic process, stochastic calculus and stochastic differential equations, Malliavin Calculus, dynamic programming (HJB Equations and Martingale methods). Towards the end of the class, asset pricing theory and machine learning mathematics will be discussed.

教学方式

Lectures Group Discussions and Presentations Homework Assignments Projects and Final Exam

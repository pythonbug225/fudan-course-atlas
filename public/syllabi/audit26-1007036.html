课程简介（中文）

本课程将针对目前中国在发展过程中面临的气候变化和环境污染领域两大问题，讲授由于化石能源的消耗，特别是煤炭的使用带来的温室气体排放和大气污染物之间的相互作用和关联的协同效应问题 。通过本课程学习，学生将掌握基本温室气体减排与大气污染控制协同效益理论体系，并通过掌握的研究方法（定性和定量分析方法）和知识点能达对温室气体减排与大气污染控制协同作用机制和机理进行认识和分析并解决问题。这一课程可以培养学生结合理论解决实际问题的能力，为学生将来从事相关环 境管理工作和研究奠定基础。

 

课程简介（英文）

This course addresses two major challenges China faces during its development—climate change and environmental pollution. It focuses on the synergistic effects and interconnections between greenhouse gas emissions and atmospheric pollutants resulting from fossil energy consumption, particularly coal use.

Through this course, students will acquire a fundamental theoretical framework for understanding the co-benefits of greenhouse gas mitigation and air pollution control. By applying qualitative and quantitative analytical methods, they will learn to analyze the mechanisms and principles of these synergistic effects and develop problem-solving skills. The course aims to cultivate students' ability to integrate theory with practical applications, laying a solid foundation for future careers in environmental management and research.

教学目标

This course is designed for students majoring in Environmental Science and Engineering, Public Administration, and related fields. Focusing on the coordinated governance of climate change and air pollution, it systematically covers theories of synergistic control of greenhouse gases and pollutants (knowledge acquisition) while incorporating practical training in quantitative analysis and policy evaluation (skill development). The course guides students to gain an in-depth understanding of the scientific principles and social responsibilities underlying environmental governance. The teaching approach emphasizes: (1)Strengthening the concept of ecological civilization; (2)Cultivating innovative problem-solving abilities using interdisciplinary methods to address complex environmental challenges; (3)Enhancing theoretical and practical knowledge through case studies from China and international comparisons.

教学方式

This course adopts a tripartite "Learning-Practice-Discussion" teaching methodology:

(1)Theoretical Instruction - Conducted through combined study of classical literature and cutting-edge research papers;

(2)Problem-Oriented Practical Training - Utilizing real-world cases to develop analytical and critical literature evaluation skills;

(3)Academic Discussion Skills Development - Featuring periodic research presentation sessions employing "presentation + Q&A" format to enhance academic discourse abilities. Through literature analysis and academic writing exercises, the course systematically improves students' capabilities in literature review, problem analysis, and scholarly communication.

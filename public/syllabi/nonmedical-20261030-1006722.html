课程简介（中文）

本课程将介绍Floquet量子力学问题的主题，Floquet量子力学问题是指具有显式时间依赖性的量子系统，通常是由于外部驱动。本课程将阐明这种设置在量子计算发展中的重要性，因为它描述了量子计算机最相关架构的量子模型，包括超导量子量子比特和离子阱模型的使用。本课程将广泛关注具有外部驱动器的两能级量子系统，从量子光学和量子物质相互作用的角度开始，到量子计算中的当前问题结束。本课程也将讨论其他量子电路。

课程简介（英文）

This course will introduce the subject of Floquet quantum mechanical problems, which refers to quantum systems with an explicit time dependence, normally due to an external drive. The course will elucidate the significance of this setting in the development of quantum computation, as it delineates the quantum models underlying the most pertinent architectures for quantum computers, including the use of superconducting quantum qubits and ion trap models. The course will pay extensive attention to two-level quantum systems with external drives, starting with the point of view of quantum optics and quantum-matter interactions and ending with current problems in quantum computation. The course will also address other quantum circuits.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to facilitate the attainment of a comprehensive understanding of Floquet systems and their contemporary relevance in the domain of quantum computation. Emphasis will be placed on the comprehension of theoretical models of quantum qubits in the form of quantum two-level systems with external drives. Additionally, the course will provide exposure to contemporary advancements in other quantum circuits.

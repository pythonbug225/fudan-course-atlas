课程简介（中文）

本课程主要围绕2019年新颁布的《药品管理法》，讲授药品管理立法，药品注册、生产、经营、使用以及特殊管理的药品等药品全生命周期的监督与管理。目的是使学生掌握药事管理相关的法律法规及药师的职责与行为准则，熟悉药品研制、生产、流通、使用等各环节的质量保证和控制，明确药品的安全性、有效性和质量稳定性等特性与药品管理的关系，并了解药品相关的法规基本知识及经济全球化形势下医药行业的发展趋势。

本课程涉及内容广泛，应用性强，通过本课程的教学，学生应获得以下知识和能力：①药事管理学的基本理论、基本知识和研究方法；②药品质量监督管理的知识；③药事组织与管理体制及其职能；④我国医药相关的主要法律法规；⑤药品研制、生产、经营、使用管理的知识，以及特殊管理药品的管理知识。

本课程以课堂教学为主，案例讨论为辅。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the Drug Administration Law newly promulgated in 2019. It systematically introduces drug regulatory legislation and the supervision and management of the entire drug life cycle, including drug registration, production, distribution, use, and the management of special categories of drugs. The purpose of this course is to enable students to master relevant laws and regulations in pharmaceutical administration, understand the responsibilities and professional codes of conduct of pharmacists, and become familiar with quality assurance and control at each stage of drug research and development, production, circulation, and use. Students will clarify the relationship between drug safety, efficacy, quality stability, and drug regulation, and understand the fundamental regulatory framework and development trends of the pharmaceutical industry in the context of economic globalization.

The course covers a wide range of topics and has strong practical relevance. Upon completion, students are expected to acquire the following knowledge and abilities:

  • Basic theories, knowledge, and research methods of pharmaceutical administration;
  • Knowledge of drug quality supervision and management;
  • Understanding of pharmaceutical organizations, management systems, and their functions;
  • Familiarity with major pharmaceutical laws and regulations in China;
  • Knowledge of the supervision and management of drug research, production, distribution, use, and special drug categories.

Teaching is primarily lecture-based, supplemented by case discussions.

教学目标

This course aims to cultivate pharmacy students’ awareness of law, responsibility, self-discipline, and service. It seeks to transform the traditional single-dimensional knowledge and skill structure of pharmacy students, enabling them to distinguish between lawful and unlawful conduct and to comprehensively apply regulatory knowledge in pharmaceutical practice to meet the needs of the rapidly developing pharmaceutical industry in China. Specifically:

  1. Master fundamental knowledge of drug-related laws and regulations;
  2. Understand the basic status and development trends of China’s pharmaceutical industry;
  3. Apply laws and regulations to distinguish lawful from unlawful practices in the pharmaceutical industry;
  4. Develop a strong awareness of drug quality and a value system oriented toward serving human health.

第一章 药品管理法立法（4学时）

1.掌握：《药品管理法》的立法宗旨，药品生产、经营及医疗机构的药剂管理规定，药品管理的规定，假劣药的认定与禁止性规定。

2. 熟悉：药品包装和价格、广告管理规定，进口药品的管理规定，违反《药品管理法》及其实施条例应承担的法律责任。

3. 了解：《药品管理法》的适用范围、药品监督方面的规定。

Chapter I Legislative Foundations of the Drug Administration Law (4 class hours)

1. Master: Legislative purpose of the Drug Administration Law; regulations on drug production, distribution, and pharmaceutical administration in medical institutions; drug management provisions; identification and prohibition of counterfeit and substandard drugs.

2. Familiarize: Regulations on drug packaging, pricing, advertising; management of imported drugs; legal liabilities for violations.

3. Understand: Scope of application; regulatory provisions.

第二章 药品注册管理 （4学时）

1.掌握：药品注册的概念和类型，药品注册的管理机构，新药、仿制药的概念，药品注册申报要求，药物临床试验的基本内容，药品上市后的再评价与监测管理。

2.熟悉：药品注册检验、注册标准的概念和要求，药品注册的审批程序

3.了解：药品注册时限、复审、药品注册管理的必要性。

Chapter II Drug Registration Administration (4 class hours)

1. Master: Concepts and types of drug registration; regulatory authorities; definitions of new drugs and generics; application requirements; fundamentals of clinical trials; post-marketing reevaluation and monitoring.

2. Familiarize: Registration testing and standards; approval procedures.

3. Understand: Time limits; review procedures; necessity of registration management.

第三章：药品生产监督管理（4学时）

1. 掌握：药品生产及药品生产管理的特点，质量管理的概念、原则，药品生产监督管理的主要内容，《药品生产质量管理规范》的主要内容。

2. 熟悉：药品委托生产的管理，药品召回管理。

3.了解：国内外药品生产管理的概况，开办药品生产企业的审批规定及药品生产许可证管理。

Chapter III Supervision and Administration of Drug Production (4 class hours)

1. Master: Characteristics of drug production and management; quality management concepts and principles; main contents of supervision; Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP).

2. Familiarize: Entrusted production; drug recall management.

3. Understand: Overview of domestic and international production management; approval requirements for establishing drug manufacturing enterprises; licensing management.

第四章：药品经营监督管理（4学时）

1.掌握：药品流通的主要概念，药品流通管理的主要规定，《药品经营质量管理规范》的主要内容。

2.熟悉：药品经营企业的经营方式和经营范围，互联网药品交易服务企业应具备的条件和应遵守的行为规范。

3.了解：《药品经营许可证》的申请程序，药品批发零售企业的含义，电子商务的含义及交易模式。

Chapter IV Supervision and Administration of Drug Distribution (4 class hours)

1. Master: Key concepts in drug circulation; main regulatory provisions; Good Supply Practice (GSP).

2. Familiarize: Business models and scopes; requirements and codes of conduct for online drug trading enterprises.

3. Understand: Application procedures for Drug Distribution Licenses; wholesale and retail definitions; e-commerce models.

1. 掌握：麻醉药品、精神药品、医疗用毒性药品的概念及其生产、经营、使用的管理要点。

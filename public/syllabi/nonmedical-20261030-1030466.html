课程简介（中文）

侵权法课程深入探讨法律框架内侵权法的原则、理论和应用。本课程专为希望理解侵权行为的复杂性及针对个人所受损害的法律救济方式的法律专业学生设计。 课程贯穿侵权法的历史发展、分类体系及塑造侵权责任的基础政策等多个主题。教学内容涵盖过失侵权、故意侵权、严格责任与产品责任等核心领域，以及诽谤和隐私权等当代侵权法前沿议题。 课程设置为15周，每周聚焦侵权法的特定维度，从基础概念延伸至进阶议题。学生将学习分析判例法、将法律原则应用于假设情境，并理解侵权法在全球化与数字化世界中的影响。 课程核心组成部分包括： 历史脉络：理解侵权法从令状制度到现代改革的演进过程 法律框架：熟悉规制侵权诉讼的成文法与判例法 批判分析：培养批判性评估侵权法案例及运用法律推理的能力 实践应用：通过小组专题报告与研究论文写作展现对学科内容的掌握 修完本课程后，学生将全面理解侵权法及其社会意义，并获得处理侵权行为相关法律挑战所需的专业技能。课程致力于为学生奠定成功的法律职业基础，使其掌握在不同法律情境中维护正义与问责所需的知识体系与分析能力。

课程简介（英文）

The Tort Law course provides an in-depth exploration of the principles, theories, and applications of tort law within the legal framework. This course is designed for law students who seek to understand the complexities of tortious conduct and the legal remedies available for wrongs committed against individuals. Throughout the course, students will engage with a variety of topics, including the historical development of tort law, the classification of torts, and the underlying policies that shape tortious liability. The curriculum covers essential areas such as negligence, intentional torts, strict liability, and products liability, as well as contemporary issues in tort law, including defamation and privacy rights. The course is structured into 15 weeks, each focusing on a specific aspect of tort law, from foundational concepts to advanced topics. Students will learn to analyze case law, apply legal principles to hypothetical scenarios, and understand the implications of tort law in a globalized and digitized world. Key components of the course include: Historical Context: Understanding the evolution of tort law from the writ system to modern reforms. Legal Framework: Familiarization with statutory and case law governing tort actions. Critical Analysis: Developing the ability to critically assess tort law cases and apply legal reasoning. Practical Application: Engaging in group presentations and writing research papers to demonstrate mastery of the subject matter. By the end of the course, students will have a comprehensive understanding of tort law, its societal implications, and the skills necessary to navigate legal challenges related to tortious conduct. This course aims to prepare students for successful careers in law, equipping them with the knowledge and analytical abilities required to advocate for justice and accountability in various legal contexts.

教学目标

The objectives of the Tort Law course are designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles, theories, and applications of tort law. The course aims to develop students’ knowledge, skills, and values essential for their future legal careers. The objectives are categorized into three main areas: value objectives, knowledge objectives, and ability objectives. Value Objectives Ethical Understanding: Foster an appreciation for the ethical considerations in tort law, including the balance between individual rights and societal interests. Social Responsibility: Encourage students to recognize the role of tort law in promoting justice and accountability within society. Commitment to Justice: Instill a commitment to advocating for the rights of individuals harmed by wrongful acts and understanding the importance of legal remedies. Knowledge Objectives Foundational Knowledge: Equip students with a solid understanding of the fundamental concepts and principles of tort law, including negligence, intentional torts, and strict liability. Legal Framework: Familiarize students with the statutory and case law that governs tort actions, including landmark cases and contemporary issues in tort law. Critical Analysis: Develop the ability to critically analyze tort law cases and apply legal principles to various factual scenarios. Ability Objectives Legal Research Skills: Enhance students' ability to conduct thorough legal research using primary and secondary sources relevant to tort law. Analytical Thinking: Cultivate strong analytical skills to evaluate legal arguments, assess evidence, and formulate sound legal conclusions. Effective Communication: Improve students' written and oral communication skills, enabling them to articulate legal concepts clearly and persuasively in both presentations and research papers. Practical Application: Prepare students to apply tort law principles in real-world contexts, including the ability to draft legal documents and engage in negotiations related to tort claims. By achieving these objectives, students will be well-prepared to navigate the complexities of tort law and contribute meaningfully to the legal profession.

教学方式

The Tort Law course employs a variety of teaching methods designed to enhance student engagement, facilitate deep understanding of the material, and develop practical skills essential for legal practice. The following methods will be utilized throughout the course: Lectures Structured Learning: Lectures will provide a foundational understanding of tort law principles, historical context, and key concepts. Instructors will present core material, supplemented by real-world examples and case studies. Interactive Elements: Lectures will include opportunities for student questions and discussions to encourage active participation and clarify complex topics. Case Studies Practical Application: Students will analyze landmark tort law cases to understand the application of legal principles in real-world scenarios. This method will help students connect theoretical knowledge with practical implications. Group Discussions: Case studies will be discussed in small groups, allowing students to collaborate, share perspectives, and develop critical thinking skills. Group Presentations Collaborative Learning: Students will work in groups to research and present on specific tort law topics or cases. This method fosters teamwork, communication skills, and the ability to synthesize information. Peer Feedback: Presentations will include opportunities for peer evaluation, encouraging constructive feedback and enhancing learning through collaboration. Research Assignments Independent Inquiry: The research paper assignment will require students to conduct independent legal research, promoting self-directed learning and critical analysis of tort law issues. Writing Workshops: Workshops may be held to guide students in legal writing, citation practices, and structuring their research papers effectively. Class Discussions and Socratic Method Engaged Learning: Class discussions will encourage students to articulate their thoughts, challenge assumptions, and engage with their peers. The Socratic method will be employed to stimulate critical thinking and deeper understanding of legal concepts. Real-Time Feedback: Instructors will provide immediate feedback during discussions, helping students refine their arguments and legal reasoning. Online Resources Supplementary Materials: The course will utilize online resources, including articles and legal databases, to enhance learning and provide diverse perspectives on tort law. By employing these diverse teaching methods, the Tort Law course aims to create a dynamic and interactive learning experience that prepares students for successful careers in the legal field.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction to Tort Law Objectives: Understand the definition of torts and tort law. Discuss the significance of tort law in the legal system. Explore the political and social implications of tort law. Week 2: Historical Development of Tort Law Objectives: Examine the evolution of tort law from the writ system to modern practices. Identify key historical milestones in the development of tort law. Discuss the impact of historical context on current tort law principles. Week 3: Understanding Wrongs and Recourse Objectives: Define the concept of wrongs in tort law. Explore the various forms of recourse available to victims. Analyze the trial-centered account of recourse. Week 4: Basics of Negligence Objectives: Introduce the elements of negligence: duty, breach, causation, and damages. Discuss the standard of care and its application in negligence cases. Explore the concept of fault-based liability. Week 5: Advanced Negligence Topics Objectives: Delve into limited-duty rules and affirmative duties. Discuss the implications of pure economic loss and emotional distress. Analyze causation issues and defenses in negligence claims. Week 6: Battery and Related Torts Objectives: Define battery and its elements: act, intent, and touching. Discuss defenses available in battery cases. Explore related torts such as assault and false imprisonment. Week 7: Property Torts Objectives: Understand the structure and elements of property torts. Discuss trespass to land, nuisance, and conversion. Analyze defenses applicable to property torts. Week 8: Strict Liability Objectives: Define strict liability and its application to abnormally dangerous activities. Discuss the rationale behind strict liability. Explore case studies illustrating strict liability principles. Week 9: Products Liability Objectives: Examine the history and evolution of products liability law. Discuss the types of product defects: manufacturing, design, and failure to warn. Analyze the legal standards and rationales for products liability claims. Week 10: Defamation and Privacy Torts Objectives: Define defamation and its elements: libel and slander. Discuss contemporary issues in defamation law, including the actual malice standard. Explore privacy torts and their implications for individual rights. Week 11: Damages in Tort Law Objectives: Understand the types of damages available in tort cases: compensatory and punitive. Discuss the principles of make-whole compensation and the eggshell skull rule. Analyze the apportionment of liability among multiple defendants. Week 12: Tort Reform and Contemporary Issues Objectives: Explore the current trends in tort reform and their implications. Discuss the impact of globalization and digitization on tort law. Analyze case studies reflecting contemporary tort issues. Week 13: Tort Law and Human Rights Objectives: Examine the intersection of tort law and human rights. Discuss the role of tort law in protecting individual rights in a global context. Analyze case studies involving tort claims related to human rights violations. Week 14: The Role of Insurance in Tort Law Objectives: Understand the relationship between tort law and liability insurance. Discuss the implications of insurance on tort claims and defenses. Analyze the role of insurance in promoting accountability. Week 15: Emerging Trends in Tort Law Objectives: Explore emerging trends and challenges in tort law. Discuss the impact of technology and social media on tort claims. Analyze potential future developments in tort law. This course schedule provides a comprehensive overview of tort law, ensuring students gain a solid understanding of both foundational concepts and contemporary issues in the field.

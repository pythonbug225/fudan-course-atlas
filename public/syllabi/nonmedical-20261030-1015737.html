课程简介（中文）

《学术英语视听说》课程适用于《中国英语能力等级量表》中提高阶段（五至六级）的学习者。课程旨在培养学生的听力理解能力和口头表达能力。通过本课程的修读，学生应能接近《中国英语能力等级量表》中听力和口语表达的七级水平。

课程简介（英文）

This course takes a wide range of academic and current social topics as entry points, closely integrating rich audio and video materials to enable students to practice and master various methods and techniques for capturing information. At the same time, it broadens the scope of listening content and helps students engage in relatively in-depth discussions on specific topics or phenomena.

教学目标

The course Academic English: Listening and Speaking is designed for learners at the Developing Stage (Levels 5–6) of the China’s Standards of English Language Ability (CSE). It aims to cultivate students’ listening comprehension and oral communication skills. Upon completion of the course, students are expected to approach Level 7 of the CSE in listening and speaking.”

教学方式

Multimedia-based instruction Group and class discussions

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Course introduction and orientation Introduce the course structure, expectations, and key learning outcomes to ensure that students are well prepared for the upcoming semester’s activities. Week 2 Economic Life Help students master the language skills needed to conduct interviews in English, including how to ask questions, introduce topics, listen actively, and conclude the interview. Week 3-4 Psychology Apply basic psychological terminology to analyze and explain human behavior. Week 5 Technology Enable students to understand the latest technological breakthroughs and their impact on society. Week 6 Sports Familiarize students with the scientific principles underlying sports training. Week 7 Health Introduce some presentation techniques that help them use PowerPoint to clearly and confidently express their ideas around a single theme. Objective: Week 8 Gender Equality Understand gender issues and related discussions across social, cultural, and economic dimensions. Week 9 Mid-term Exam Week 10 Education Grasp the role of education in personal growth and social progress, and understand the challenges faced by Western societies in the field of education. Week 11 Group Presentation Week 12 Forces of Nature Understand the various forces and manifestations of nature. Week 13 Zoology Understand the academic study of animal behavior. Week 14-15 Urban Planning Become familiar with the basic principles of urban planning, and understand how design and infrastructure can improve quality of life and support urban development. Week 16 Mock Test; Q&A Week 17 Final Written Test

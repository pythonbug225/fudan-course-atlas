课程简介（中文）

本课程的主要内容包括一元函数的极限与连续性、一元函数的微分学、一元函数的积分学以及常微分方程。

课程简介（英文）

Limits and continuity of single-variable functions, differential calculus of single-variable functions, integral calculus of single-variable functions, and ordinary differential equations.

教学目标

This course is a fundamental core course for undergraduate students at the School of International Finance and constitutes the first part of the higher mathematics curriculum. Through the study of calculus for single-variable functions, students will acquire the essential mathematical foundations needed for subsequent courses. Additionally, the course aims to develop students' abilities in abstract thinking, logical reasoning, spatial imagination, and scientific computation, as well as their capability to apply mathematical techniques to solve problems in economics and finance.

教学方式

Primarily lecture-based, supplemented with exercise sessions.

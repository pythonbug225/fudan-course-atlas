课程简介（中文）

这门两学分的必修课程向学生介绍拉丁美洲和西班牙文化研究领域中的当代重要学术论争与新兴研究议题。课程探讨文学及其他文化生产形式如何回应和介入重大社会与历史问题，包括记忆与历史再现、环境保护以及近几十年来的社会变迁。课程特别关注当代文学文本，围绕移民、全球化与西语世界的文化多样性等主题展开讨论。同时，学生还将考察文学与电影、视觉艺术等其他媒介之间的关系，从而理解这些议题如何塑造当代西语社会的文化实践。

课程简介（英文）

This two-credit compulsory course introduces students to key contemporary debates and emerging research topics in cultural studies in Latin America and Spain. The course examines how literature and other forms of cultural production engage with major social and historical questions, including memory and representations of the past, environmental protection, and social change in recent decades. Particular attention is given to contemporary literary texts, with thematic discussions on migration, globalization, and cultural diversity in the Spanish-speaking world. Students will also explore the relationship between literature and other media (such as film and visual arts) in order to understand how these debates shape cultural practices in contemporary Spanish-speaking societies

教学目标

This course aims to introduce students to major contemporary debates and emerging research topics related to culture and society in the Spanish-speaking world. Through guided discussion, selected readings, and the analysis of diverse forms of cultural production, students will learn to identify key social issues and examine how they are presented in academic and public contexts. By the end of the semester, students will not only deepen their academic interests, but also enhance their ability to navigate the cultural complexity of the Spanish-speaking societies

教学方式

The course combines instructor-led lectures, guided seminars, and in-class discussions. Each class is typically organized into two parts: the first introduces key concepts, historical context, and relevant academic perspectives related to the weekly topic; the second focuses on the close analysis of selected materials (such as literary texts, film excerpts, visual arts, and other cultural expressions). Throughout the semester, students will complete short writing assignments and oral presentations in order to develop their ability to formulate arguments, interpret cultural texts, and engage with current academic debates in Spanish

教学内容与进度安排

Course Introduction – Overview of course objectives and assessment; key concepts in cultural studies; defining “contemporary debates” in Latin America and Spain. Culture is Changing – Culture as a social field; representation, discourse, and cultural production making in the Spanish-speaking world. Culture is Changing II – Cultural texts and interpretation: how narratives, images, and media dialogue with the current public debates. History and Narrative I – Literature and history: narrative forms of interpreting the past in Latin America and Spain. History and Narrative II – Cultural memory and historical imagination. Globalization and Modernization I – Global cultural flows; modernization and dialy life; changing cultural practices in the Spanish-speaking world. Globalization and Modernization II – Cultural industries, and urban experience; new lifestyles and social change. Midterm Assessment – Written task and discussion-based evaluation using selected cultural case studies. Mobility and Cultural Diversity I – Mobility and cultural exchange; narratives of belonging, community, and cultural encounter. Mobility and Cultural Diversity II – Transnational connections in the Spanish-speaking world; multicultural environments and cultural hybridity. Cultural Identities and Subjectivities I – Identity in contemporary culture; self-representation and social imaginaries. Cultural Identities and Subjectivities II – The body, emotions, and everyday life represented in cultural production. New Cultural Voices I – New authors and cultural expressions in contemporary Spain and Latin America. New Cultural Voices II – New writers and renewed cultural agendas; contemporary debates in cultural production. Ecology and Environmental Narratives I – Nature, landscape, and environmental protection in cultural texts; new imaginaries of sustainability. Ecology and Environmental Narratives II – Ecological themes across literature and film. Intermediality and Digital Culture – Literature, film, visual arts, and the digital sphere; circulation of cultural meanings across media. Final Project– Research question development, and final submission

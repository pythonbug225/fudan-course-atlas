公司金融

2025秋 · BA70011.01 · 孔爱国、王克敏

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

The course content and style is targeted at doctoral students in Finance. We will primarily be using academic articles. However, we also recommend some textbooks. The course outline describes the main topics discussed in class covering the fundamentals of corporate finance and development of the modeling tools. In the course outline: (i) articles followed by asterisk (*) are the ones to be studied in more detail; (ii) book chapters are referred using the underlined abbreviations above and, for each topic, we indicate the relevant book chapter readings. The goal of this class is to stimulate research ideas that may turn into journal publications. In order to encourage you get started on a research topic one of the main course requirements is to submit a short research paper or a research proposal, which will be due approximately one month after the end of the course. In your paper or proposal, you should briefly review the literature and state what your original contribution will be. If you are doing theoretical work, you should present the structure of model and begin to develop its implications. If you are doing empirical work, you may want to gather some of data and provide some summary statistics and preliminary results.

教学目标

The course aims to provide a thorough, doctoral level survey of both theory and empirical work in modern Corporate Finance. The primary objective of the course is to prepare students for a career as a scholar in finance. We will cover a wide range of topics.

教学方式

教师授课、课堂讨论、学术讲评

教学内容与进度安排

1.Introduction: A Survey of Corporate Finance; 2.Capital Structure Choice: M&M, and the trade-off Theory;3.Agency Problems;4.Asymmetric Information; 5.Initial Public Offerings(IPOs);6.Private vs. Public Choice;7.Payout Policy;8.Corporate Governance;9.Compensation Contracts and Stock Options;10.Monitoring, Corporate Control and Takeovers;11.Mergers and Acquisitions;12.Corporate Cash Holdings;13.Corporate Investment;14.Financing Constraints;15.Corporate Innovation;16.Uncertainty and Corporate finance.

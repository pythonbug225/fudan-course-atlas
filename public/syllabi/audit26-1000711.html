课程简介（中文）

本课程是面向国际关系专业本科生的英文写作技能入门课，旨在培养学生的阅读理解、观点建构、推理与证据运用、论文结构安排以及批判性思维等能力。课程通过对精选的国际关系文本和学术辩论的深入分析以及书面作业的互动式写作训练，帮助学生逐步掌握这些写作技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an introduction to undergraduate writing skills focused on the discipline of international relations. It seeks to develop skills such as how to read arguments, how to construct arguments, forms of reasoning and evidence, structuring an essay, and critical thinking. It does so through a careful analysis of selected international relations texts and debates, as well as through the interactive development of written assignments.

 

教学目标

By the end of this course, students will be able to effectively communicate complex ideas and analyses in the field of International Relations through well-structured, coherent, and persuasive writing. They will demonstrate proficiency in crafting research papers and policy briefs, enabling them to participate in informed and impactful discourse on international affairs.

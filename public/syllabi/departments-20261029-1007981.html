组合优化

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提供关于组合优化问题、计算复杂性及近似算法的基础理论。第一部分对NP完全性理论进行了深入探讨，注重证明各种组合问题NP完全性的概念和方法。第二部分聚焦于若干组合问题的近似算法，包括背包问题、装箱问题以及最小完工时间调度等。第三部分则专注于排序问题及近似算法，探讨涉及单台机器、并行机器和多工厂的多种调度场景。

课程简介（英文）

The course offers a comprehensive introduction to the fundamental techniques associated with combinatorial optimization. It is organized into three distinct parts. The first part provides a thorough examination of NP-completeness theory, highlighting the concepts and methodologies for demonstrating the NP-completeness of various combinatorial problems. The second part focuses on approximation algorithms for several significant problems, including the knapsack problem, the bin packing problem, and minimum makespan scheduling, among others. It will introduce various approximation schemes, such as Polynomial Time Approximation Schemes (PTAS), Fully Polynomial Time Approximation Schemes (FPTAS), and Asymptotic Polynomial Time Approximation Schemes (APTAS). The third part concentrates on scheduling problems, exploring a range of scheduling scenarios involving single machines, parallel machines, and multiple shops. Additionally, pertinent approximation algorithms for these problems will be discussed. Overall, the course aims to provide a foundational understanding of the concepts underlying combinatorial optimization problems, computational complexity, and techniques for developing approximation algorithms.

教学目标

1. To enable students to comprehend the foundational theories and concepts related to combinatorial optimization. 2. To equip students with preliminary methods and techniques for proving NP-completeness of combinatorial problems. 3. To familiarize students with fundamental approaches to the design of approximation algorithms and approximation schemes. 4. To provide students with an introductory understanding of scheduling theory and applications.

教学方式

The teaching content is presented during class sessions. Following class, students are required to complete their assignments. At the end of the term, students submit their project reports.

Traveling salesman problem,set cover problem and Steiner tree problem Week 11. Single machine scheduling problem to minimize the total weighted completion time and the maximum lateness Week 12. Single machine scheduling problem to minimize the number of tardy jobs and the total tarndiness Week 13. Parallel machine scheduling problem to minimize the makespan Week 14. Introduction to flow shop and job shop scheduling problems Week 15. Shift scheduling and multimodularity Week 16. Course overview

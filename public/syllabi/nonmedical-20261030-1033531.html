课程简介（英文）

This course introduces students to selected issues relating to the law and practice of international commercial transactions in the Asian context, focusing primarily on the representative common law systems of Hong Kong and Singapore, with English law as an occasional comparator. Students will be exposed to debates and developments in respect of sale of goods, international money transfers, commercial dispute resolution, and sustainability and technological challenges.

教学目标

Upon completing the course, it is expected that students will have: Enhanced their skills in case-reading, legal analysis, critical thinking, and oral expression. Gained knowledge of contemporary legal issues affecting international commercial transactions. Been sensitized to the role of the law in facilitating commerce, having had the opportunity to observe, evaluate, and critique developments in the law against a broader commercial background.

教学方式

1. 课堂授课 2. 互动讨论 3. 作业提交展现所学 4. 期末考核、论文撰写、批改与指导

课程简介（中文）

“学术英语（理工）"课程是为理工科研究生开设的选修课。本课程的目标是通过让学生参与听、说、读、写活动，显著提高学生的学术英语语言技能，重点是增强学生在学术环境中的交流技能。该课程还旨在为学生打下学术英语基础，学生可以通过其他大学课程或个人学习进一步提高学术英语水平。经过本课程的系统学习，学生不仅能掌握学术英语，还能培养对学术研究的兴趣，为学术写作和今后的学习与研究打下坚实的基础。

课程简介（英文）

The course “Academic English for Science and Engineering” is an elective course designed for graduate students in science and engineering disciplines. The goal of this course is to measurably improve students’ academic English language skills by engaging them in listening, speaking, reading, and writing activities, with a focus on communication skills in academic settings. It is also designed to provide a foundation in academic English that students can further extend through other university courses or individual study. After a systematic study in this program, students will not only master academic English but also develop an interest in academic research and a strong foundation for academic writing and future study and research.

教学目标

The primary objective of this course is to assist students in enhancing their academic English skills, enabling them to communicate more effectively in academic settings. After taking this course, students should: Have an increased awareness of the key features of academic English for science and engineering; Acquire more academic vocabulary; Be able to listen to academic lectures and take effective notes; Be able to read and understand academic texts on a variety of topics, especially those in the students' field of study, and to objectively examine and evaluate the significance and main idea of the texts; Be able to communicate and discuss in depth on topics related to the students' major, and to effectively describe, interpret, argue, and comment, and to express themselves in a standard and appropriate manner.

教学方式

Lecture: The instructor will introduce and discuss the essential qualities of academic English specific to science and engineering. Each week, students will analyze a well-written paper from their research field, examining its structure, language, and rhetorical strategies to deepen their understanding of effective academic writing in their discipline. Discussion: The instructor will facilitate class discussions focusing on the structure and academic language of scientific and engineering papers. These discussions will highlight core features of academic English, with attention to how ideas are organized, how arguments are developed, and how language is used to convey complex concepts clearly and precisely. Peer Workshop: On the day assignments are submitted, we will hold peer workshops where students share their research assignments with classmates. These workshops will provide an opportunity for collaborative feedback on project plans, as students discuss strengths and areas for improvement, helping one another refine their ideas and approaches for future assignments.

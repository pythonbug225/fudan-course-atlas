课程简介（中文）

线性模型中的估计与推断；实对称矩阵与多元正态分布的复习；回归模型的诊断与问题解决；多重共线性及其应对方法；模型选择。

课程简介（英文）

Estimation and inference in linear models; Review of real symmetric matrices and multivariate normal distributions; Diagnostics and solutions to issues in regression models; Multicollinearity and solutions; Model selection.

教学目标

This course aims to equip students with a solid understanding of fundamental statistical concepts and core methodologies in regression analysis, including both simple and multiple linear regression models. Emphasis is placed not only on the application of regression techniques, but also on the mathematical reasoning and theoretical foundations that support them. Students will learn how to translate real-world problems into appropriate regression frameworks and gain hands-on experience in implementing these models using the R programming language. In addition, the course will foster the development of practical skills for regression diagnostics, such as identifying influential observations, detecting multicollinearity, and assessing model assumptions. These skills are essential for ensuring the reliability and validity of statistical inferences. The course also introduces students to modern developments and theoretical advancements in regression analysis, providing a broader perspective on its evolving role in data science and applied research. By the end, students will be well-prepared to analyze complex data using rigorous regression-based tools.

教学方式

Lecture-based.

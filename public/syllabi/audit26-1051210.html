课程简介（中文）

通过审视超过150年的经济变迁，本课程描绘了中国在千禧年之初崛起为全球经济超级大国的历程，并深入剖析了几个关键的转折点，例如19世纪中叶中国的开放、1911年清朝的覆灭、20世纪50年代新中国的成立，以及20世纪70年代末至今的改革开放时代。本课程通过聚焦上海作为全球城市的辉煌崛起，彰显了长期与全球视角的重要性。

课程简介（英文）

Through a survey of over 150 years of economic change, this course charts the rise of China as a global economic superpower at the beginning of the new millennium, with in-depth coverage of crucial turning points such as the opening of China in mid-19th century, the collapse of Qing in 1911, the rise of New China in the 1950s and the current reform era since the late 1970s.This course demonstrates the significance of long-term and global perspective by highlighting the spectacular rise of Shanghai as a global city. 

教学目标

The aim of the course is to offer both a global and local perspective of the rise of Chinese economy to largely third and fourth year students at Fudan University. By demonstrating the importance of historical context, this course showcases the value of a long-term perspective and multi-disciplinary approach. The course also aims to combine academic readings with importance case studies and personal observations of Chinese economy and the development trajectory of Shanghai.

教学方式

The course will be taught with a combination with academic lectures, reading-based student presentations, field trips to sites and enterprises with historical and economic significance.

教学内容与进度安排

  1. An Introduction to Chinese Economy;
  2. When did China fall behind: measuring long-term living
  3. Why Industrial Revolution did not happen in China: resource endowments and technology.
  4. Culture and Institutions in traditional Chinese economy
  5. Money and Finance in traditional China;
  6. State Capacity in a comparative view.
  7. Modern Chinese Economy during 1850-1950;
  8. The Rise of Shanghai as a global financial city;
  9. Economies of New China 1950 -1978 (1);
  10. Economies of New China 1950 -1978 (2);
  11. Reform and Opening-up (1);
  12. Reform and Opening-up (2);
  13. Reform and Opening-up (3);
  14. The Emerge of China as an economic superpower;
  15. The Future of Chinese Economy.
  16. Final Exam

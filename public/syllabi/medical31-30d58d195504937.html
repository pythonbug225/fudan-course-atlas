1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 通过在医学生（完成通识教育之后）中开设《生殖医学概论》课程，介绍生殖医学的基本知识，阐述疾病、社会和环境与生殖的关系及结局，以及生育相关问题，向医学生传播生殖健康的理念，加强医学生对生殖医学的认知，提高其对生命质量研究的兴趣，加强生殖医学在医学教学体系的重要性，提升医学生即未来医生对生殖医学的认知度，进而提升生殖医学在医学界乃至大众中普及度。本课程将继续为培养适应社会主义现代化建设和面向21世纪社会经济发展需要的“基础扎实、能力强、素质高”将来能从事生殖医学工作的高级实用型医学人才打下坚实的基础，为医学生今后从事生殖医学和其他临床学科的工作奠定理论基础和实践基础。加强生殖医学的人文医学教育和医德医风教育，弘扬医者精神的“真”“善”“美”，助力培养守初心、铸信念、强人文、有大爱的创新型卓越医学人才。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | By introducing the course “Introduction to Reproductive Medicine” to medical students (after completing their general education), we aim to impart foundational knowledge in reproductive medicine. This includes elucidating the relationship between diseases, society, and the environment with reproductive health and its outcomes, as well as addressing fertility-related issues. This initiative seeks to disseminate the concept of reproductive health among medical students, enhance their understanding of reproductive medicine, and stimulate their interest in research on quality of life. enhance the significance of reproductive medicine within the medical education system, elevate future physicians' awareness of this field, and thereby increase its prevalence within the medical community and among the general public. This course will continue to lay a solid foundation for cultivating advanced, practical medical professionals with “strong fundamentals, robust capabilities, and high quality” who can engage in reproductive medicine work, meeting the needs of socialist modernization and 21st-century socioeconomic development. It will establish the theoretical and practical groundwork for medical students pursuing careers in reproductive medicine and other clinical disciplines. It will strengthen humanistic medical education and medical ethics training in reproductive medicine, promoting the “truth,” “goodness,” and “beauty” of the physician's spirit. This will help cultivate innovative, outstanding medical professionals who remain true to their original aspirations, forge strong convictions, possess deep humanistic qualities, and embody great compassion.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

本课程通过对生殖内分泌、生殖生理、生殖技术、妊娠分娩、医学伦理等重要知识和技术的讲授，结合临床实例，通过大课及示教等教学方式，介绍生殖医学的基本知识点、技术及相关领域。本课程相关医学领域重视医学伦理、医学心理等内容的建设，课程开设相应内容传播生殖医学的人文伦理精神的传播、医患沟通、生育保育、家庭关系、身体隐私等医德医风和人文教学内容，提升医学生对人文精神、人类全生命周期等学习兴趣和认知。建立正确的职业价值观和较高的综合素质，做患者信任的好医生。 教学周数16周，理论课32学时，见习4学时。

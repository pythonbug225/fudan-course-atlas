Ancient Chinese Culture and Tradition

2025秋 · CHIN60150.01 · 汪习波

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程简要概述了中国古代文化和传统，探讨了那些常被塑造和夸大，被视为中国文化特征和传统的内外叙述。作为对中国文化身份的各种认知，课程会研读一些古代文献，对从古至今的一些正统观点和细微细节提出质疑；同时，也会对周边国家和西方世界的一些观点进行讨论，并在合理的评价体系中给予一定尊重。

课程简介（英文）

The course gives a brief survey of ancient Chinese culture and traditions, including the influence of the natural and human environment, political history, religious beliefs, literature, and idea systems. It also gives some discussions on both external and internal narratives which were often shaped and overstated as Chinese cultural characters and traditions. As kinds of recognitions of Chinese cultural identity, some ancient texts are perused, and some orthodox perspectives and subtle details from beginning through recent time are questioned; in the meantime, some other’s views from neighboring people and western world are argued and treated with some respect in the reasonable evaluation system.

教学目标

本课程期于介绍中国古代文化和传统，包括自然与人文环境、政治历史、宗教信仰、文学和思想体系的影响等诸层面。学生应具有比较的视野和平衡的判断，带着平静和理性的眼光了解中国。对于课程讲授中特别关注的经典表述及其思想系统，能够做到入乎其内，出乎其外，理性、客观、公平地把握其内涵及其当代回音。 This course aims to introduce ancient Chinese culture and traditions, covering various aspects such as the natural and humanistic environment, political history, religious beliefs, and the influence of literature and ideological systems. Students should have a comparative perspective and balanced judgment, and understand China with a calm and rational outlook. Regarding the classic expressions and their ideological systems that receive special attention in the course lectures, students should be able to immerse themselves in them and also stand outside of them, so as to rationally, objectively, and fairly grasp their connotations and contemporary echoes.

教学方式

Most of the class time is used for topic teaching.

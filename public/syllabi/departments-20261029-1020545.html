运营管理

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

运营管理（OM）规划并协调产品（商品和服务）生产和交付过程中的所有活动。有效的运营管理是大多数行业取得成功的关键要素。实现运营卓越是提高效率和获得竞争优势的最重要策略之一。本课程将涵盖定性和定量方法的结合，为在运营中做出明智决策提供必要的工具。

课程简介（英文）

Operations Management (OM) plans and coordinates all activities in the process of producing and delivering products (goods and services). Effective operations management is a key ingredient of success in most industries. Achieving operations excellence is one of the most essential strategies to improve efficiency and to gain a competitive advantage. This course will cover a mix of qualitative and quantitative methods that provide the necessary tools to make intelligent decisions in operations.

教学目标

The goal of this course is to introduce students to the fundamental concepts, problems, and strategies in the operations function of a firm. After this course, the students will be able to apply operations management tools in solving practical problems in manufacturing and service industries.

教学方式

The instructor will adopt a variety of approaches, including lectures, multimedia materials, case studies, games, and experiments.

教学内容与进度安排

This course will cover the following topics in sequence, with each topic taking 1-2 weeks. Course Overview and Introduction Quality Management:  Usecookie experiment to learn how to plot control charts to control the quality of cookies. Also introduce six-sigma philosophy in the debrief Process Fundamentals Constraint Management Lean Systems:  Show a video which illustrates the Toyota production system Waiting Line Models: An introduction to queueing theory via real-world examples Inventory Management: Using mini cases to show the needed business analytics Forecasting Newsvendor Model:  “LL Bean” case Revenue Management:  Using airline industry and hotel industry as examples Supply Chain Strategies: Ask students to play Beer game to understand the information distortion along the supply chain

教学参考资料

Textbook: Operations Management – Processes and Supply Chains. L.J. Krajewski, M. Malhotra, and L.P. Ritzman (Pearson). As expected, the book does not provide a thorough coverage of all OM-related subjects. Hence, additional references will be provided to enrich the students’ knowledge of particular course subjects.

课程简介（中文）

飞行力学与飞行控制是飞行器设计与工程专业的专业基础课。本课程以有人驾驶固定翼飞机为研究对象，建立其六自由度非线性运动方程，在此基础上首先研究飞机质心的运动规律，并讲授飞机的基本飞行性能、续航性能、机动性能、起降性能等的计算方法。然后利用线化理论研究飞机的稳定性和操纵性——着重分析存在外界扰动或操纵作用下飞机的运动特性，包括如何实现各种平衡飞行、平衡飞行状态受外界扰动后所呈现的运动稳定性及飞机对于操纵的响应等。最后简要介绍现代智能飞行控制系统的基本原理和方法。

课程简介（英文）

Flight Mechanics and Flight Control is a foundational professional course in the Aircraft Design and Engineering program. This course focuses on manned fixed-wing aircraft, establishing their six-degree-of-freedom nonlinear equations of motion. Based on this foundation, it first explores the laws governing the motion of the aircraft's center of mass and teaches the calculation methods for fundamental flight performance, endurance performance, maneuverability, and takeoff and landing performance. Subsequently, linearization theory is employed to study aircraft stability and controllability, with an emphasis on analyzing the motion characteristics of the aircraft under external disturbances or control inputs. This includes how to achieve various equilibrium flight conditions, the motion stability exhibited after equilibrium flight states are perturbed by external disturbances, and the aircraft's response to control inputs. Finally, the course provides a brief introduction to the basic principles and methods of modern intelligent flight control systems.

教学目标

1、掌握有人驾驶固定翼飞机六自由度非线性运动方程的建立方法 2、掌握基本飞行性能、续航性能、机动性能、起降性能等的计算方法 3、了解如何实现各种平衡飞行、平衡飞行状态受外界扰动后所呈现的运动稳定性及飞机对于操纵的响应等 4、了解现代智能飞行控制系统的基本原理和方法。

This course is designed to extend the knowledge of the basic microeconomic principles that will provide the economics foundation for the senior undergraduate students. 教学目标

The objective of the Intermediate Economics course, designed for EMBA students in the School of Economics at Fudan University, is to deepen understanding of core economic theories and analytical tools, bridging introductory concepts and advanced applications. Specifically, the course aims to: Equip students with a robust understanding of microeconomic principles, including consumer behavior, firm decision-making, market structures, and general equlibrium. Develop proficiency in applying mathematical and graphical models to analyze economic problems and predict outcomes. Enhance critical thinking skills to evaluate economic policies, market dynamics, and their implications for resource allocation and welfare. Prepare students for advanced coursework by fostering the ability to interpret real-world economic phenomena through theoretical frameworks. Encourage data-driven analysis using empirical tools and case studies to connect theory with practical economic issues.

教学方式

Lectures: Structured sessions deliver core microeconomic theories, using mathematical models, graphical analysis, and real-world examples to clarify complex concepts. Problem-Based Learning: Students engage in problem sets  to apply theoretical frameworks to practical scenarios. Interactive Discussions: Class discussions encourage critical analysis of economic issues, with students debating policy implications and theoretical applications to real-world events.

教学内容与进度安排

1st-2nd Week: Introduction & Mathematics 3rd-4th Week:Preferences and Utility Functions 5rd-6th Week:Optimization, Indirect Utility and Expenditure Function 7th Week:Topics in Consumer Theory 8th-10th Week: Production Theory 11th Week: General equlibrium & Market Structure 12th Week: Final Examianation

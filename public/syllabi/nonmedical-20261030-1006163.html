中国国际法实践

2025秋 · LAW70066.01 · 龚柏华

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程专为中国商法法学硕士项目的外国学生设计，旨在帮助他们了解中国在国际法方面的理论与实践。本课程涵盖但不限于对适用于中国的重要条约的介绍，以及涉及中国政府或中国国有企业的重要案例。

课程简介（英文）

This course is specially designed for the foreign students of Chinese Business Law LL.M. program to understand the Chinese theory and practice on international law. The course includes but not limited to the introduction of the important treaties which applied to China, the important cases which involved with the Chinese government or Chinese State-owned enterprises.

教学目标

1 to know the basic information about the China's practice on international law 2 to master the basic skill to analysis the cases which involved the Chinses government(investor) or foreign government (investor) 3 to familiar with the Chinese law or regulation related to international law

教学方式

lecture and case study

教学内容与进度安排

1. China and International law: history and present 2. Chinese theory on international law 3. Research tool and skill to study the China's practice on international law 4. The relation between international law and Chinese law 5. Chinese practice on international treaty 6. The International Organizations which China joined 7. International Dispute Settlement and China’s Practice 8. WTO cases which China is involved and and Chinese proposals on WTO reform 9. Sovereign Immunity and the cases which Chinese government be sued in foreign court 10. The relevant Chinese law or regulation to Law of Seas 11. The nationality law of PRC and case study 12. The immigration law of PRC and case study 13. Investor-State Arbitration: Chinese government as the defendant 14. Investor-State Arbitration: foreign government as the defendant 15. Optional 16. Exam (open book)

教学参考资料

read the assigned materials in accordance with designed subjects before seminar and prepare the discussion.

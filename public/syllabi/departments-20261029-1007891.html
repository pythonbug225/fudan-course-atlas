来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程培养学生将数学知识与实践应用于经济问题的能力，涵盖理论与应用两个方面。课程将分为四个部分： 1）极限； 2）导数及其应用； 3）积分及其应用； 4）常微分方程。

课程简介（英文）

The course gives students’ skills of implementation of mathematical knowledge and practice to economic problems both theoretical and applied ones. There will be four parts in the course: 1）limits; 2) derivatives with applications; 3) integrals with applications. 4) ordinary differential equations

教学目标

By the end of the autumn semester a student has to: Know about the principal concepts and results of single-variable calculus; Be able to apply calculus to solve problems; Know about the basic concepts and results of ordinary differential equations.

教学方式

Lecture, presentation, group discussion

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule Week 1: sets, mappings, and functions Week 2: limit of sequences Week 3: limit of functions Week 4: continuity of functions Week 5: derivative of functions I Week 6: derivative of functions II Week 7: differential mean-value theorem Week 8: application of derivatives Week 9: indefinite integral I Week 10: indefinite integral II Week 11: definite integral Week 12: techniques of integration Week 13: application of integrals Week 14: improper integral Week 15: ordinary differential equations I Week 16: ordinary differential equations II

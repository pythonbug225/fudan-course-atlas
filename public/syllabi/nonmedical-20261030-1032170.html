课程简介（中文）

本研究生课程旨在融合医学科学的严谨及医疗护理的伦理、文化和沟通纬度。课程聚焦当代医疗核心议题——“医学的科学与艺术双重属性”、“遗传学伦理”、“临终关怀”、“医患关系与沟通”等，通过研究型视角引导学生深入探索医学人文。通过剖析经典生命伦理学案例，探究中国悠久医德传统与全球医学伦理的契合点。课程采用探究驱动、学生主导、协作与互动融合的学习模式。

课程简介（英文）

This graduate-level course is designed to bridge the scientific rigor of medicine with the ethical, cultural, and communicative dimensions of health care. Centered on themes critical to contemporary health care—such as “Medicine as Both a Science and Art,” “Ethics in Genetics,” “End-of-Life Issues,” and “Doctor-Patient Relationship and Communication,”—the curriculum immerse students in rigorous explorations of medical humanities through a research-oriented lens. By dissecting critical bioethics cases, students also examine how China’s time-honored medical ethos aligns with global medical ethics. The course highlights inquiry-driven, student-centered, collaborative and interactive learning to shape students into reflective practitioners.

教学目标

By the completion of this course, students will be able to: Critically analyze ethical dilemmas in medicine (e.g., end-of-life care, genetic engineering) using frameworks from global bioethics and China’s humanistic medical traditions. Articulate culturally sensitive perspectives on doctor-patient dynamics, health equity, and professional integrity in formal and academic English. Evaluate historical and contemporary cases to discern intersections between technological progress and humanitarian values. Design patient communication strategies that balance evidence-based practice with empathy. Synthesize China’s philosophical contributions to medical humanities. Enlarge discipline-specific vocabulary by accurately employing academic and medical terminology in discussions, presentations, and written analyses of topics related to medical humanities. Strengthen oral communication skills through structured debates and individual/group presentations, articulating ethical arguments with clarity and cultural sensitivity in professional English. Enhance academic writing proficiency by drafting reflective essays and case study critiques, showcasing critical thinking and advanced medical English proficiency.

教学方式

Medical literature analysis: Critical analysis of peer-reviewed international journal articles in bioethics and narrative medicine. Case-based debates: Analysis of real-world clinical ethics dilemmas (e.g., CRISPR applications, medical decision-making) through structured debates. Role-plays: Simulated cross-cultural doctor-patient communication scenarios (e.g., end-of-life care discussion, genetic counseling). Written reflections: Guided reflective essays on topics like “the concept of a ‘good death’”.

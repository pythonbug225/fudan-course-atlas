课程简介（中文）

梵文文献阅读课。针对已修习梵文1和梵文2的学生，主要阅读印度哲学相关的梵文文献。

课程简介（英文）

This course is for students who have taken Sanskrit 1 and 2 and focuses on reading Sanskrit texts related to Indian philosophy.

教学目标

In the present course we will discuss the contents of excerpts of 2 Sanskrit works which we have studied in the past 3 semesters, namely the pramanya-section in Varadaraja’s Tarkikaraksa and the passage on manasapratyaksa in Durveka Misra’s Dharmottarapradipa. Moreover, we will start reading the first chapter (on mithyatva) in Madhusudana Sarasvati’s Advaitasiddhi. Apart from the obvious goal of a Sanskrit reading class, viz. to enhance the students' knowledge of Sanskrit, another course objective is to provide insights into the philosophical ideas of the original sources.

教学方式

Students who choose the course take turns translating Sanskrit literature.

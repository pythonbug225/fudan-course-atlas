课程简介（中文）

自清教徒在北美建立殖民地，宗教就开始在北美社会政治生活中发挥重要作用。在当今世界发达民主国家中，美国因其高度宗教性而独树一帜，宗教依凭美国政治体系、公民社会和文化传统对美国社会和政治均产生深远影响。本课程主要讲授美国宗教历史、宗教与当代美国政治、社会、经济、法律、文化之间的关系。

课程简介（英文）

The key issue of this course is the place of religion in American life. The United States is a unique mixture of sacred and secular, "one nation under God" which insists on separating church and state. It's a nation founded with a sense of Christian mission which has had to accommodate millions of immigrants who have brought competing religious views and expectations. The course examines the history of religious encounters and the formation of diverse religious identities in the American Society from the colonial time to now. How the relationships between church, state and the individual have changed over the years and how religious values (or the struggle against them) have been at the heart of many of the most important developments in American life will be introduced. The course will then address the question of what different religions are and what role they play in the American Society in the new millennium.

教学目标

本课程深入客观介绍宗教因素在美国社会、政治、经济、文化等方面的地位和作用，旨在提高学生对宗教及相关问题的分析能力和对美国社会的全面了解，从而培养和提高学生的理论分析能力和实践把握能力。要求学生在学习过程中深入研读教材、注重跟进美国宗教与社会的实时互动，积极参与课堂讨论，掌握文献检索能力和学术论文写作能力。

教学方式

以教师课堂讲授为主，辅之以课堂讨论、答疑和个别指导等

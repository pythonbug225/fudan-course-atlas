课程简介（中文）

（一）教学内容。本课程涉及国际贸易、跨国公司和国际直接投资、国际金融市场和国际宏观经济等内容。本课程基于中国经济对外开放事实以及经济全球化和国际经济学发展脉络，集中讲解国际贸易理论和政策、国际金融市场、汇率决定和变动、国际收支调节方法、开放经济下宏观经济政策、国际货币体系等方面的前沿理论和现代经济学分析方法。（二）基本要求。通过课程学习，学生可以基于国际贸易理论、跨国公司和国际直接投资理论、汇率决定理论、国际收支调节理论、国际宏观经济理论等，运用现代经济学分析方法，对国际经济现象进行解释，对国际经济运行态势进行研判，并能对国际经济重要问题开展学术研究，特别是能够对中国开放经济实践进行理论总结，对国际经济学前沿问题，开展并引领前瞻性研究。

课程简介（英文）

(1) Course Content. This course covers international trade, multinational corporations, international direct investment, international financial markets, and international macroeconomics. Grounded in the facts of China’s economic opening-up as well as the evolution of economic globalization and international economics, it focuses on cutting-edge theories and modern analytical methods in areas such as international trade theory and policy, international financial markets, exchange rate determination and fluctuations, balance of payments adjustment, macroeconomic policies in open economies, and the international monetary system. (2) Basic requirements. Through this course, students will be able to employ modern economic analysis methods and apply theories of international trade, multinational corporations and international direct investment, exchange rate determination, balance of payments adjustment, and international macroeconomics. They will be equipped to interpret international economic phenomena, assess trends in the global economy, and conduct academic research on key international economic issues. In particular, they will be capable of providing theoretical insights into China’s open-economy practices and undertaking as well as leading prospective research on frontier topics in international economics.

教学目标

本课程有助于学生理解开放经济条件下的经济活动规律。通过课程学习，学生可以运用现代经济学分析方法，结合宏观经济数据和微观经济数据，对国际经济现象进行解释，对国际经济运行态势进行研判，并能对国际经济前沿问题开展学术研究。

教学方式

教师讲授专题；学生课堂展示。

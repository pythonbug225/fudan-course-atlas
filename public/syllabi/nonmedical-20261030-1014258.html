Fintech

2025秋 · MF60024.01 · Richard Jen-hwa Wang

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course will explain core knowledge about the traditional Fintech and its potential disruption, including artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain, cloud computing, big data, and emerging digital economy/digital finance topics including central bank digital currency, Web 3.0, and Metaverse. There are strong synergies among these technologies, enabling a broad spectrum of disruptive impact. The course will cover the fundamentals of these technologies and focus on resulting business model innovation. The class will also delve into the applications and use cases of Fintech for products and services in the finance and digital economy industries. The course is designed to be interactive with discussions involving various business use cases. The class highly encourages students to adopt an innovative and open-minded attitude to leverage technology in finance. The class will involve a final project that will test understanding of concepts and the student’s ability to apply these in the real-world.

教学目标

To help students understand the basics of Fintech, its applications in modern finance, and its potential impact on the finance industry and economy.

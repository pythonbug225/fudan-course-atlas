课程简介（中文）

在当今的商业世界中，公司金融作为企业管理的核心领域，发挥着至关重要的作用。它涵盖了公司治理、融资、项目评估、财务会计以及营运资本管理等多个方面，旨在帮助企业实现资金的有效配置、优化风险管理和提升经营效率。随着全球化的加速和市场竞争的加剧，掌握公司金融的知识和技能对于企业的成功至关重要。     本课程作为英国精算师IFoA认证课程的一部分，旨在为学生提供深入的公司金融知识，特别是关注保险行业的特殊性和应用。通过本课程的学习，学生将能够全面了解公司治理和组织形式的基本原理，掌握公司融资的基本方法和策略，熟悉项目评估的流程和技巧，理解财务会计的基本规范和报告要求，以及掌握营运资本管理的有效方法。     此外，本课程还将特别关注保险公司在公司金融方面的应用。通过案例分析和实践操作，学生将能够深入了解保险产品的金融特性、保险公司的风险管理策略、投资组合管理以及财务报告与监管要求等方面的知识。这将有助于学生在未来的职业生涯中更好地适应保险行业的需求和挑战。

课程简介（英文）

In today's business world, corporate finance plays a vital role as a core area of enterprise management. It encompasses various aspects such as corporate governance, financing, project evaluation, financial accounting, and working capital management, aiming to help companies achieve effective fund allocation, optimize risk management, and enhance operational efficiency. With the acceleration of globalization and intensifying market competition, possessing knowledge and skills in corporate finance is crucial for the success of businesses. This course is part of the certification program by the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) in the UK, designed to offer students in-depth knowledge of corporate finance. Through this course, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles of corporate governance and organizational structures, master basic methods and strategies of corporate financing, become familiar with the processes and techniques of project evaluation, understand the basic norms of financial accounting and reporting requirements, and grasp effective methods of working capital management. Furthermore, this course will specifically highlight the application of corporate finance in insurance companies. Through case studies and practical exercises, students will delve into knowledge about the financial characteristics of insurance products, risk management strategies of insurance companies, investment portfolio management, financial reporting, and regulatory requirements. This will assist students in better adapting to the needs and challenges of the insurance industry in their future careers. The objectives of this course not only involve imparting knowledge but also focus on nurturing students' critical thinking and practical skills. Through course studies, students will develop the ability to think independently and solve problems, master basic methods of analyzing and evaluating corporate finance issues, thus laying a solid foundation for their future professional development.

教学目标

以价值引领塑造学生正确的金融价值观与职业操守；全面传授公司金融知识，涵盖公司治理、融资、项目评估等核心领域；着重强化能力培养，借助案例研讨与实践项目，提升学生资金有效配置、风险精准管控及财务决策分析等实操能力，培育其独立思考与解决复杂金融问题的思维，为未来在金融领域稳健发展筑牢根基。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在介绍佛教哲学，重点关注其中国传统。课程将涵盖中国佛教哲学传统从公元2世纪左右的开端，直至9世纪中叶主要哲学学派确立的历程。 课程主题包括： 印度佛教基本思想；佛教传入中国；中国早期佛教；《肇论》；《大乘起信论》；中国中观学派；中国唯识学派；三论宗；天台宗；华严宗；禅宗。

课程简介（英文）

The present course serves as an introduction to Buddhist philosophy with special reference to its Chinese tradition. It will cover the Chinese tradition from the beginning around 2nd century CE to the establishment of the main philosophical schools of Chinese Buddhism around the mid 9th century. Topics include: The basic ideas of Indian Buddhism; The introduction of Buddhism to China; Early Chinese Buddhism; Zhaolun 肇論; Dacheng qixin lun 大乘起信論; Chinese Madhyamaka; Chinese Yogācāra; Sanlun School; Tiantai School; Huayan School; Chan Buddhism.

教学目标

This course will introduce the origin and development of Chinese Buddhism, discussing the important questions in its history, sketching its major schools and lives and thoughts of important thinkers. It will be helpful for students to grasp the main thread and core thoughts of Chinese Buddhism.

教学方式

Lecture

教学内容与进度安排

1 The basic ideas of Indian Buddhism 2 The introduction of Buddhism to China 3-4 The theories of emptiness in early Chinese Buddhism 5-6 Early Chinese Madhyamaka and Zhaolun 肇論 7 The first introduction of Yogācāra in China 8 Dacheng qixin lun 大乘起信論 9 Doxography and theory of Buddha-nature 10-11 The establishment of the Tiantai School 12 The establishment of the Sanlun School 13-14 Xuanzang and the Faxiang School 15 The establishment of the Huayan School 16 The establishment of Chan Buddhism 17 Q&A 18 Essay writing

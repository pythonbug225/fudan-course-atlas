课程简介（中文）

本课程与后继的数学分析AII是一个整体，主要内容围绕极限、微分与积分展开。本部分的主要内容有： 集合与映射，序列极限,函数极限，连续函数，导数与微分,微分中值定理,Taylor 展式与应用, 不定积分, 定积分,反常积分

课程简介（英文）

This course together with its successive course Mathematical Analysis AII is mainly focused on limit and calculus. In its contents we have: Set and Mapping, The limit of sequences, The limit of functions, Continuous function, Derivatives and Differentiation, The mean value theorem, The Taylor Expansions and their applications, Indefinite integrals, Definite integrals, Improper integrals.

课程简介（中文）

在当今互联互通的世界中，学生、创业者以及企业高管们理解源于中国并正在向全球传播的创新浪潮至关重要。在这过程中、其所面临的限制条件及其独特特征，对于把握由此带来的机遇具有关键意义。 本商业课程将深入剖析中国的商业环境，提供宝贵见解。课程将探讨促使中国采取开放政策、发展出口导向型经济及其后续发展的基础，并分析中国近年来向以创新为核心的高科技经济转型的趋势及其内在挑战。 课程还将聚焦中国的科技行业、充满活力的互联网格局以及人工智能生态系统，探索中国企业家如何改变交通、绿色产业、汽车、最后一公里配送、微出行、零售、金融、娱乐、机器人、能源和人工智能等传统行业。 此外，课程将梳理中国的现代化政策，包括“中国制造2025”、“中国互联网”以及最新五年规划，分析这些政策对中国发展的意义，以及它们对商业环境和社会的影响。课程内容还涉及人工智能及其对中国社会、经济与文化的影响。 最后，本课程将探讨中国的“一带一路”倡议及其在国际舞台上的关键作用。 总体而言，本课程旨在帮助学生建立起对中国细致而深入的理解——中国不仅是商品供应国，更是一个具备巨大国际化潜力的商业创新型经济体。

课程简介（英文）

In today's interconnected world, it is crucial for students, entrepreneurs, and executives to comprehend the wave of innovation originating from China and spreading globally. Understanding this process, its constraints, and its distinct characteristics is vital to capitalizing on these opportunities. This business course offers valuable insights into the Chinese business landscape. It examines the foundations that led China to adopt open policies, fostering an export-based economy and its subsequent development, and the recent shift towards an innovation based high-tech economy, along with its inherent challenges. The course also delves into China's technology sector, its dynamic internet landscape and artificial intelligence ecosystem, exploring how Chinese entrepreneurs are transforming traditional industries such as transport, green industries, automotive, last-mile delivery, micro-mobility, retail, finance, entertainment, robotics, energy and artificial intelligence, among others. Additionally, it reviews China's modernization policies, including China 2025, China Internet, and the latest Five-Year Plan, analyzing their implications for China's development and their impact on the business environment and society. The course also covers Artificial Intelligence and its influence on Chinese society, economy, and culture. Finally, the course examines China's Belt and Road Initiative and its pivotal role in the international arena. Overall, this course provides students with a nuanced understanding of China, not merely as a supplier of goods but as a business innovative economy with significant potential for internationalization.

教学目标

Understanding the shift from factor accumulation (investment-led growth) to Total Factor Productivity (TFP) as the primary engine for China's future growth. Evaluating the "Government + Market" Nodal Economy model: how state strategic investments create spillover effects that private markets capitalize on. Analyzing firm-level efficiency in China by contrasting "Big" companies (revenue-driven) with "Great" companies (driven by Return on Invested Capital - ROIC). Understanding the shift of the Chinese economy from export and infrastructure investment to a service-centered, consumption-based, and innovation-driven model, and its effects on business. Examining the factors that drove China's manufacturing dominance and how new social, cultural, and economic factors are transforming the country. Understanding the policies and processes that have made China a leader in green energy. Understanding China's modernization policies (e.g. China 2025, China Internet, Five-Year Plan) and their implications for development, business, and society. Differentiating between market-oriented and state-planned economies. Challenging the idea that China is only a producer of commodities and a copier, not an innovator. Critically evaluating the drivers of major trends in China and their domestic and international effects. Understanding how Chinese entrepreneurs are changing traditional industries (e.g. green industries, automotive, last-mile delivery, micro-mobility, retail, finance, entertainment, AI). Understanding China's growing technology sector and competitive internet environment. Understanding AI developments in China. Understanding China's international foreign investment landscape and reasoning. Understanding how innovation has changed in China, how a state-run innovation system is being developed, and which industries will benefit from this. Understanding China as a new business model. Understanding China's current technological landscape and its effects. Understanding China's complex and competitive business environment. Understanding the characteristics that make Chinese entrepreneurs world-class innovators. Understanding China's innovative business environment; from sponges to innovators. Understanding the efficiency of Chinese business logistics. Understanding the future of supply chains and business, using China as a case study. Understanding how to conduct business in China. Understanding China's OBOR initiative and its impact. Understanding theoretical supply chain concepts. Application and use of supply chains in modern business: studying real supply chain structures in existing corporations.

课程简介（中文）

作为数字信息处理与传输实验室的内部讨论班，通过每周的学生汇报，帮助学生熟悉信号处理与传输的技术进展及前沿，引导学生通过阅读论文和课程讨论，掌握低时延高可靠传输技术的关键技术和相关理论，完成基本的建模及算法性能分析；在信号处理理论的框架下，掌握图信号处理的基本方法，学习图傅立叶变换、图滤波器、图信号的采样、估计、检测等典型问题，并将其运用至传感器网络、社交网络、脑网络等分布在不规则拓扑上的信息处理。

课程简介（英文）

As an internal seminar of the Digital Information Processing and Transmission Laboratory, the weekly student presentations aim to help students stay updated with the latest technological advancements and cutting-edge developments in signal processing and transmission. Through reading academic papers and participating in course discussions, students are guided to master the key technologies and relevant theories of low-latency, high-reliability transmission. They will also learn to complete basic modeling and algorithm performance analysis. Within the framework of signal processing theory, the seminar covers fundamental methods in graph signal processing. Students will study topics such as Graph Fourier Transform, graph filters, sampling, estimation, and detection of graph signals. These methods will be applied to information processing in irregularly structured systems, such as sensor networks, social networks, and brain networks.

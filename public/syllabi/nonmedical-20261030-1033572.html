课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍儒家经典的基础知识，内容涉及四书（《大学》《中庸》《论语》《孟子》）与五经（《周易》《尚书》《诗经》《礼记》《春秋》）。 除经典文本外，课程还将探讨儒家思想在秦、汉、宋、明、清及近现代的发展脉络。 本课程旨在培养学生的文献阅读与自主学习能力。

课程简介（英文）

Introducing the basic knowledge of Confucian classics, this course will involve in Four books (Great Learning, The Doctrine of Mean, The Confucian Analects, Work of Mencius) and Five Classis ( Book of Changes, History, Songs, Rites, Spring and Autumn Annals). Beyond of classics, the development of school of Confucianism in Qin, Han, Song, Ming, Qing Dynasties, and modern era will be discussed. Reading and self learning ability will be cultivated in this class.

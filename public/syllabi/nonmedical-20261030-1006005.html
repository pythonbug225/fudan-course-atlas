西方法理学研究

2025秋 · LAW60034.01 · 侯健

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课是面向法学专业特别是法学理论专业研究生的选修课程。本课的主要内容是述评第二次世界大战之后当代西方法理学的理论和流派，研究当代西方法理学研究的学术动态、前沿问题、最新成果和发展趋势，分析主要流派的社会和学术背景、代表人物和代表性著作的思想观点以及流派之间的讨论和争辩。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an elective designed for postgraduate students specializing in jurisprudence, particularly those in legal theory programs. The primary focus is on reviewing and critiquing contemporary Western jurisprudential theories and schools of thought emerging after World War II. It examines academic developments, cutting-edge issues, latest achievements, and evolving trends in contemporary Western jurisprudence research. The course also analyzes: (1) the social and intellectual contexts of major theoretical traditions; (2) the ideological perspectives of their representative figures and seminal works; and (3) the debates, dialogues, and intellectual exchanges between competing schools. Key components include critical evaluation of theoretical frameworks, contextualization of academic genealogies, and interdisciplinary analysis of methodological interactions within the field of jurisprudence.

教学目标

本课程教学目的主要在于开拓学生的理论视野，培养学生的理论素质，提高学生对学术文献的阅读、理解和概括能力，初步掌握对学术观点、学术争论和理论问题的分析能力。本课强调文本研读与社会分析相结合，理论追问与实践关怀相结合，透视西方与反观中国相结合。

教学方式

教师讲授与学生讨论相结合。学生带着问题，阅读文本，撰写读书笔记，参加课堂讨论。

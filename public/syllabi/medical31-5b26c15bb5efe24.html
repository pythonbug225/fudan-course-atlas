1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 妇产科学是临床医学中涉及面较广、整体性较强，专门研究妇女特有的生理、病理的一门学科。包括妇科和产科两大部分。本课程的主要内容为妇产科学基础、女性生殖系统疾病等相关内容。此外，传播妇产科医患沟通、生育保育、家庭关系、身体隐私、生殖伦理等医德医风和人文教学内容，提升人文精神、人类全生命周期等学习兴趣。建立正确的职业价值观和较高的综合素质，做患者信任的好医生。 理论课程注重培养学生的自学能力，使其掌握妇产科学的基本理论、基本知识和基本技能，掌握妇产科常见病、多发病的发生发展规律、诊治方法及妇女保健、计划生育措施，积极介绍妇产科学临床与科研的相关最新进展。根据教学计划，妇产科学八年制课程教学周数6教学周，44个学时。 示教PBL床旁课程包含思维课程训练（妇/产科临床思维与诊疗规范概述）、妇产科案例分析（异位妊娠、妊娠期高血压疾病、妊娠期糖尿病、前置胎盘、胎盘早剥、产后出血、异常分娩、宫体肿瘤、卵巢肿瘤、子宫颈癌及宫颈上皮内病变等）及微创技术在妇产科的临床应用实训启发学生独立思考的能力，并培养学生分析问题和解决问题的能力。根据教学计划，本学科教学时数共12周，36学时。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | Obstetrics and Gynecology is a broad and comprehensive field within clinical medicine that specializes in the study of the unique physiological and pathological characteristics of women. It comprises two main branches: gynecology and obstetrics. The primary content of this course includes the fundamentals of obstetrics and gynecology, as well as diseases of the female reproductive system. Additionally, the course covers medical ethics and humanities-related topics such as doctor-patient communication in obstetrics and gynecology, reproductive health, family relationships, bodily privacy, and reproductive ethics, aiming to enhance students’ interest in humanistic values and the full human life cycle. The course aims to develop correct professional values and high overall quality, enabling students to become doctors trusted by patients. Theoretical courses emphasize cultivating students’ self-learning abilities, enabling them to master the basic theories, knowledge, and skills of obstetrics and gynecology, understand the development patterns, diagnostic and treatment methods of common and prevalent diseases in obstetrics and gynecology, as well as women’s health care and family planning measures. The courses also incorporate the latest advancements in clinical practice and research in obstetrics and gynecology. According to the curriculum plan, the eight-year obstetrics and gynecology program consists of 6 teaching weeks and 44 academic hours. The PBL bedside demonstration courses include clinical thinking training (including an overview of clinical reasoning and diagnostic/therapeutic standards in obstetrics and gynecology), case analysis in obstetrics and gynecology (ectopic pregnancy, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy, gestational diabetes, placenta previa, placental abruption, postpartum hemorrhage, abnormal labor, uterine tumors, ovarian tumors, cervical cancer, and cervical intraepithelial neoplasia), and clinical application training of minimally invasive techniques in obstetrics and gynecology to enhance students’ ability for independent thinking and cultivate their skills in analyzing and solving problems. According to the curriculum, the total teaching hours for this discipline are 12 weeks and 36 academic hours.

课程简介（中文）

本课程主要介绍利用分子自组装技术设计制备的在原子/分子层次上具有规则结构和分子取向的分子聚集体材料和超薄膜。课程分三个部分内容。第一部分主要介绍气液界面单层膜的基本性质及其LB膜的制备，以及固体表面的分子自组装和层层组装技术及其应用。第二部分主要介绍分子聚集体（纳米）材料和超薄膜的表征技术的原理、特点和应用；包括光谱技术、能谱技术和显微镜技术等方法。最后一部分介绍分子聚集体材料及其组装技术在研制低维纳米材料、分子器件和清洁能源的生产和转换等方面的应用。

课程简介（英文）

Molecular assembling techniques include the Langmuir-Blodgett (LB) films, self-assembled monolayers and layer-by-layer multilayers. Based on these techniques, well-defined, organized ultrathin films and molecular aggregates can be designed and constructed at the interfaces, with the potential applications in the development of advanced materials and nanoscale or molecular devices. This course is composed of three sections. The first one is the fundamental concepts and knowledge of these techniques and their history, particularly their principles, features and potential applications. The second part is mainly on the characterization techniques for these aggregates and thin films, particularly on the spectroscopy, X-ray energy spectrum related technology, and microscopic technique. Finally, this course will introduce the potential applications using these techniques for the development of nanomaterials, molecular devices, and clean energy conversion devices.

教学目标

（1）掌握自下而上的分子自组装技术的基本原理、制备方法及其在新型材料和器件制备方面的应用。

（2）了解纳米材料、分子聚集体材料和超薄膜的常用的表征技术的基本原理、特点、应用范围及其优缺点。

（3）了解分子自组装技术在当前材料科学前沿领域中应用。

Strategic Management

2026春 · MBA60067.01 · 王琴

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

In this course students will learn the basic strategy management theory and can use strategy analysis tools to realize current business. Special emphasis is placed on strategic management process including environment analysis, strategy choice, business model and strategy implementation. Detailed information on business level strategy, corporate level strategy and its application.

课程简介（英文）

In this course students will learn the basic strategy management theory and can use strategy analysis tools to realize current business. Special emphasis is placed on strategic management process including environment analysis, strategy choice, business model and strategy implementation. Detailed information on business level strategy, corporate level strategy and its application.

教学目标

CLO1.Understand the basic strategy management theories and related analysis methods. CLO2. Analyze the external environment and internal environment of the company. CLO3. Understand corporate-level strategy and business-level strategy. CLO4. Grasp related theories of competitive advantages, globalization, M&A, cooperation strategy and strategy governance, organization. CLO5. Analyze the company’s strategy management and give dialectical comments.

教学方式

TLA 1. Lecture The teacher will impart the knowledge about the fundamental principles, tools, and strategies of strategy management in class. TLA 2. In-class Case Discussion To enhance the students’ impression about some interesting theories and phenomenon, the teacher may organize some class discussion. Everyone has the responsibility to join in the discussion. TLA 3. Group Discussion There will be teamwork in the process The student will collaborate with other students in the same group, discuss the questions raised by the teacher, and provide your ideas and solution. TLA 4. Project Presentation To examine the groups’ ability to find out, analyze, and solve the problems, discussion and exporting will be arranged before the end of the course. Evaluation will be made on both oral presentation and written project report. TLA 5. Individual Assignment Every student has to participant in the group work, in-class discussion, other homework like project presentation.

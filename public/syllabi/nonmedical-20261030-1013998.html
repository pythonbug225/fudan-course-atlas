课程简介（中文）

本课程的核心内容聚焦于一元函数微积分，这是一个在多个学科领域中都起着基础性作用的数学基础课程。具体涵盖的知识点包括一元函数的极限与连续性、一元函数微分学、一元函数积分学，以及数项级数和函数项级数及其收敛判别定理。这些内容不仅构成了微积分学的基本框架，也为后续的高级数学课程以及许多应用学科提供了坚实的理论基础。 在教授这些内容时，本课程特别引入了凸函数的概念。凸函数作为一种特殊的函数类型，既可以作为连续函数和单边可导的直观实例，帮助学生更深入地理解函数的连续性和可导性；同时，它也在优化问题中扮演着重要角色。通过凸函数的讨论，学生可以掌握优化问题的基本知识，这有助于他们处理日后在科研工作中遇到的优化问题。 此外，本课程还将详细介绍非线性方程求解的牛顿迭代法。这一方法不仅是微积分在实际应用中的一个重要体现，也展示了求导运算和数列极限的巧妙结合和强大应用。通过学习牛顿迭代法，学生可以感受到微积分学的魅力，并学会如何运用这一方法去解决实际问题。 综上，本课程不仅涵盖了一元函数微积分的基本内容，还通过引入凸函数和优化问题、介绍非线性方程求解的牛顿迭代法等方式，使学生能够在掌握微积分学知识的同时，提高他们的问题解决能力和科研素养。

课程简介（英文）

The core content of this course focuses on univariate calculus, a fundamental mathematics course that plays a foundational role in multiple academic disciplines. Specifically, the knowledge points covered include limits and continuity of univariate functions, differential calculus of univariate functions, integral calculus of univariate functions, as well as numerical series, function series, and their convergence criteria theorems. These contents not only form the basic framework of calculus but also provide a solid theoretical foundation for subsequent advanced mathematics courses and numerous applied disciplines. In teaching these contents, this course particularly introduces the concept of convex functions. As a special type of function, convex functions serve as both intuitive examples of continuous functions and unilaterally differentiable functions, helping students gain a deeper understanding of function continuity and differentiability. Meanwhile, they also play a significant role in optimization problems. Through discussions on convex functions, students can master the basic knowledge of optimization problems, which will assist them in addressing optimization issues encountered in their future scientific research work. Additionally, this course will elaborate on Newton's iterative method for solving nonlinear equations. This method is not only an important manifestation of calculus in practical applications but also demonstrates the ingenious combination and powerful application of differentiation operations and sequence limits. By studying Newton's iterative method, students can appreciate the charm of calculus and learn how to apply this method to solve real-world problems. In summary, this course not only covers the basic content of univariate calculus but also enhances students' problem-solving abilities and scientific research literacy by introducing concepts such as convex functions and optimization problems, as well as presenting Newton's iterative method for solving nonlinear equations.

教学目标

数学是医学领域中一门重要的基础学科。通过学习一元函数微积分及常微分方程等数学知识，学生不仅能够打下坚实的数学基础，还能为后续的医学课程提供有力的支撑。这些数学知识是培养学生抽象思维、逻辑推理和科学计算能力的关键。 一元函数微积分的学习，使学生能够深入理解函数的变化规律，掌握求导和积分的技巧。在医学中，许多现象和过程都可以通过数学模型进行描述，如药物在体内的代谢过程、生物信号的传递等。通过微积分的学习，学生可以将这些现象转化为数学问题，进而利用数学方法进行分析和预测。 常微分方程的学习则为学生提供了解决动态问题的工具。在医学中，许多疾病的发展过程都是动态的，如疾病的传播、病情的恶化等。通过常微分方程的学习，学生可以建立这些动态过程的数学模型，并利用数学方法进行求解，从而为疾病的预防和治疗提供科学依据。 通过本课程的学习，旨在培养学生运用数学技术分析和解决实际问题的能力。医学是一个实践性很强的学科，学生需要将所学的数学知识应用到实际问题中去。通过解决医学中的实际问题，学生可以锻炼自己的抽象思维、逻辑推理和科学计算能力，提高自己的综合素质。 通过学习一元函数微积分及常微分方程等数学知识，学生不仅能够掌握学习后续医学课程所需要的数学基础，还能够培养自己的抽象思维、逻辑推理、科学计算的能力，以及运用数学技术分析和解决实际问题的能力。这些能力对于医学专业的学生来说至关重要，将为他们未来的学习和工作奠定坚实的基础。

教学方式

本课程采用中英文双语教学模式。充分利用教材与教学参考书上的习题，以及精心设计的补充习题，来引导学生进行深入的讨论和练习。这些习题不仅涵盖了基础知识的巩固，还包括了拓展应用的内容，旨在帮助学生将理论知识与实际问题相结合。同时，本课程还注重引导学生将所学知识应用到实际情境中，以锻炼他们的实践能力和解决问题的能力。

教学内容与进度安排

第一周：函数的定义与性质：幂函数、多项式函数、有理函数、代数函数、三角函数、指数函数、对数函数、双曲函数等；复合函数；反函数；一对一函数。函数的极限；函数极限的运算法则。函数极限的比较定理；夹逼定理；函数的无穷极限；自变量无穷大时的函数极限。 第二周：函数在单点处的连续性；连续函数；连续函数的复合定理；函数在单点处的连续性延拓。 第三周：凸函数及其李氏连续性。 第四周：数列的定义；数列的极限；数列极限的夹逼定理。单调收敛定理；一些重要的数列极限及引理。 第五周：导数的定义；切线；导数的记号；可导函数；高阶导数。 第六周：微分运算法则：常数函数、幂函数、指数函数；导数的乘积法则；导数的除法法则。三角函数的导数；双曲函数的导数；链式法则；隐式求导；对数函数的导数。对数求导法；一般幂函数的导数；三角反函数的导数；双曲反函数的导数。 第七周：函数的线性化逼近；连续函数的最值，最值定理。Fermat定理、驻点、闭区间法；罗尔定理；拉格朗日中值定理；柯西中值定理；中值定理的应用。洛必达法则：有限点处零比零型。有限点处无穷比无穷型、无穷远处零比零型和无穷比无穷型，其他不定型。 第八周：牛顿迭代法。凸函数的一阶导数刻画、二阶导数刻画。不带约束的凸优化、带约束的凸优化；均值不等式、琴生不等式及其应用。 第九周：定积分：定义、记号、几何意义；可积函数；使用右端点的定积分；定积分的性质。微积分基本原理：第一部分和第二部分，不定积分的概念。不定积分公式表、不定积分与定积分的变量替换法则、对称函数的定积分。 第十周：积分的应用：曲线包围的面积、体积、积分中值定理。分部积分及其应用：双曲函数的几何意义。 第十一周：第二换元法：万能替换，有理替换。三角替换。 第十二周：反常积分及其应用。级数的定义、发散判别法、积分判别法。 第十三周：直接比较判别法、极限比较判别法、交错数列及其判别法、绝对收敛级数、交错调和级数的计算。级数的重新排列、比值判别法、斐波那契数列及其相应级数。 第十四周：求根判别法、级数收敛的判别策略。幂级数、收敛半径、收敛区间、函数的几何级数表达方法。逐项求导和积分定理、泰勒级数、麦克劳林级数、欧拉公式、拓展的二项式公式。 第十五周：常微分方程：可分离情况和线性情况。常微分方程解的存在行：皮卡迭代算法。常微分方程解的唯一性：Gronwall不等式。 第十六周：复习、考试。 1st week: Concepts and properties of functions: polynomials, rational functions, algebraic functions, trigonometric functions, exponential functions, logarithmic functions, hyperbolic functions; composite functions; inverse functions; one-to-one functions. Limit of functions; limit laws for functions. Comparing theorem for limits; Sandwich theorem; infinite limits of functions; limits as x approaches to infinity. 2nd week: Continuity at a point; continuous functions; composition of continuous functions; continuous extension to a point. 3rd week: Convex function and its Lipschitz continuity. 4rd week: Definition of sequences; limits of sequences; Sandwich theorem for sequences; Monotonic sequence theorem. Some important limits and lemmas for sequences. 5rd week: Definition of derivatives; tangent lines; notations of derivatives; differentiable functions; higher derivatives. 6th week: Differential rules: derivatives of constant functions, derivatives of power functions, derivatives of exponential functions, product rules for derivatives, quotient rules for derivatives. Derivatives of the trigonometric functions derivatives for hyperbolic functions; the chain rule; implicit differential; derivatives of logarithmic functions. Logarithmic differential; derivatives of general power functions; derivatives of inverse trigonometric functions; derivatives for inverse hyperbolic functions;. 7th week: Linearization and approximation; Maximum and minimum values of the continuous functions, the extreme value theorem. Fermat’s theorem, critical point, the closed interval method; Rolle’s theorem; the Lagrange mean value theorem; Cauchy’s mean value theorem; applications of the mean value theorems. L’Hospital’s rule: zero over zero case at the finite points. Infinity over infinity case at the finite points, zero over zero case and infinity over infinity case at the infinite point, other indeterminate forms. 8th week: Newton’s Method. Convex function and its one-side derivatives, first-order characterization, second-order characterization. Unconstrained convex optimization, constrained convex optimization; Inequality of arithmetic and geometric mean, Jensen’s inequality and its application. 9th week: Definite Integral: definition, notation, geometric interpretation, integrable functions, integral using right endpoints, properties of definite integral. The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: Part I and Part II, the definition of Indefinite Integral. Table of indefinite integrals, the substitution rule for indefinite integrals and definite integrals, definite integrals of symmetric functions. 10th week: The application of integrals: areas between curves, volumes, the mean value theorem for integrals. Integration by parts and its application: the geometric meaning of the hyperbolic functions. 11th week: The reverse substitution rule for integrals: the universal substitution; rational substitution. Trigonometric substitution. 12th week: Uniqueness theorem for ODEs: Gronwall’s inequality. Improper integral and its application. Definition of series, test for convergence, integral test. 13th week: Direct comparison test, limit comparison test, alternating series and its test, absolutely convergent series, evaluation for alternating harmonic series. Rearrangement of series, ratio test, Fibonacci sequence and its corresponding series. Term-by-term differential and integration theorem, Taylor series and Maclaurin series, Euler formula, extended binomial formula. 14th week: Root test, strategy for testing series. Power series, radius of convergence, interval of convergence, representation of functions using geometric series. 15th week: Ordinary differential equations (ODEs): separable case, linear case. Existence theorem for ODEs: Picard iteration. 16th week: Review and Final Test.

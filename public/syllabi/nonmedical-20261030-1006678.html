博物馆学理论与实践

2025秋 · MUSE50002.01 · 陆建松

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

讲授当代中国博物馆事业发展的概况以及中国博物馆学研究的主要领域——中国博物馆事业发展现状与趋势、博物馆专业人才队伍建设、博物馆展览理论与实践、博物馆教育理论与实践、博物馆运行管理、博物馆建设实践、博物馆展示策划与设计、博物馆展览工程等的理论与实践发展动态。

课程简介（英文）

This course delivers an overview of contemporary museum sector development in China and examines key domains within Chinese museum studies, with a focus on theoretical and practical developments in: Current status and trends of China's museum sector development Professional workforce development in museums Theory and practice of museum exhibitions Museum education theory and practice Museum operational management Museum construction practices Museum exhibition planning and design 8. Museum exhibition engineering management

教学目标

通过当代中国博物馆事业发展实践以及中国博物馆学研究主要领域的讲授，旨在让学生从宏观上了解中国博物馆事业发展和运营现状、中国博物馆学研究的发展动态和主要理论，拓宽学生的视野，激发学生的关注与思考，帮助他们开展博物馆学理论研究和掌握博物馆工作主要实务。 Through instruction on both the practical development of China's contemporary museum sector and the main research domains of Chinese museum studies, this course aims to: Provide students with a macro-level understanding of China's museum development landscape and operational realities Familiarize students with evolving theories and key academic trends** in Chinese museological research Broaden perspectives and stimulate deeper engagement with museum-related issues Equip students to conduct theoretical research in museology while **mastering core operational practices

教学方式

讲授+讨论

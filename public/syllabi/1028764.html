广义相对论 · 2026春 · PHYS60019.01

教师：Cosimo Bambi · 来源：复旦教务系统教学班大纲

考核与成绩构成

  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 50.0%：Mid term test and final term test, each of them counts for 50% in the final mark. The mid term test will be in the 9th week and will cover the topics discussed in the weeks 1-8 (Special Relativity), the final term test will be in the 18th week and will cover the topics studied in the weeks 10-17 (General Relativity). Each test has three problems and the students can choose two problems. Each problem can give 0 to 50 scores, so two correct problems give 100 scores.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 50.0%：Mid term test and final term test, each of them counts for 50% in the final mark. The mid term test will be in the 9th week and will cover the topics discussed in the weeks 1-8 (Special Relativity), the final term test will be in the 18th week and will cover the topics studied in the weeks 10-17 (General Relativity). Each test has three problems and the students can choose two problems. Each problem can give 0 to 50 scores, so two correct problems give 100 scores.

课程简介（中文）

本课程将从一些介绍性材料开始：标量、向量和张量的概念、最小作用原理、测地线方程以及伽利略相对论和麦克斯韦方程之间的不一致性。本课程的第一部分将致力于狭义相对论的研究：洛伦兹变换、相对论力学、协变形式的电动力学。本课程的第二部分，也是主要部分，将致力于广义相对论的学习：黎曼几何、爱因斯坦引力、史瓦西时空、GR的经典测试、黑洞、GR中的宇宙学模型、引力波。

课程简介（英文）

The course will start with some introductory material: concepts of scalars, vectors, and tensors, Principle of Least Action, geodesics equations, and inconsistency between Galilean Relativity and Maxwell's Equations. The first part of the course will be devoted to the study of Special Relativity: Lorentz Transformations, relativistic mechanics, electrodynamics in covariant form. The second and main part of the course will be devoted to the study of General Relativity: Riemannian geometry, Einstein's gravity, Schwarzschild spacetime, classical tests of GR, black holes, cosmological models in GR, gravitational waves.

教学目标

The aim of the course is to provide the students a background in Special and General Relativity, cultivating students’ ability to discuss about an active research field. The course will promote the formulation of questions from the students and discussions between students and teacher in order to cultivate students’ critical spirit and improve their oral communication skills.

教学方式

Lectures (with the use of the blackboard and projector), class exercises, discussion

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Galilean Relativity; Euclidean Space; Principle of Least Action; Newtonian Gravity Week 2: Problems between Galilean Relativity and Maxwell’s Equations; Michelson-Morley Experiment Week 3: Einstein’s Principle of Relativity Week 4: Lorentz Transformations; concept of proper time; cosmic ray muons as an example of time dilation Week 5: Action for a free particle in Special Relativity; the case of massless particles; particle collisions and applications Week 6: Motion of a charge particle in Special Relativity Week 7: Maxwell’s Equations in covariant form; gauge invariance Week 8: Energy-momentum tensor of the electromagnetic field Week 9: Mid-term test Week 10: Riemannian geometry; covariant derivative Week 11: General covariance; Einstein’s Equivalence Principle Week 12: Einstein’s Equations; Einstein-Hilbert action Week 13: Schwarzschild spacetime; Birkhoff’s theorem; classical tests of General Relativity Week 14: Reissner-Nordstrom black holes; Kerr black holes, no-hair theorem ; gravitational collapse; Penrose diagrams Week 15: Friedmann-Robertson-Walker metric; cosmological models Week 16: Gravitational waves in linearized gravity; quadrupole formula; energy of gravitational waves Week 17: Astrophysical sources of gravitational waves; gravitational wave detectors Week 18: Final test

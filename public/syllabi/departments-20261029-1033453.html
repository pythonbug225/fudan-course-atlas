课程简介（中文）

《保险经济学前沿专题》是运用一般经济理论研究保险领域问题的一门核心的研究型课程。本课程的教学目标是通过教学使学生掌握风险与危险理论、预期效用理论、保险需求理论、保险供给理论和保险市场均衡理论等保险经济理论，并能初步学会运用微观经济学、数理经济学、信息经济学和公共经济学等现代经济理论分析保险经济活动中的不确定性问题，试图解释保险经济运行的内在本质，为保险资源的最优配置提供经济学分析。 本课程的基本内容由导论、广义保险理论、保险效用理论、保险消费与储蓄理论、保险经济运行和保险市场均衡六部分构成。其中，保险效用理论、保险消费与储蓄理论是教学重点。在教学和专题研讨过程中始终贯彻理论联系实际，既注重现代西方经济理论在保险学与不确定经济学中的应用分析和定量研究，又要注重与我国保险业发展现状相结合。

课程简介（英文）

Advanced Topics in Insurance Economics is a core research-oriented course that applies general economic theories to study issues in the insurance field. The teaching objective of this course is to enable students to master insurance economic theories, including risk and hazard theory, expected utility theory, insurance demand theory, insurance supply theory, and insurance market equilibrium theory. Students will also learn to preliminarily apply modern economic theories such as microeconomics, mathematical economics, information economics, and public economics to analyze the uncertainties in insurance economic activities. The course aims to explain the intrinsic nature of insurance economic operations and provide economic analysis for the optimal allocation of insurance resources. The basic content of this course consists of six parts: Introduction, Generalized Insurance Theory, Insurance Utility Theory, Insurance Consumption and Savings Theory, Insurance Economic Operations, and Insurance Market Equilibrium. Among these, Insurance Utility Theory and Insurance Consumption and Savings Theory are the key teaching focuses. Throughout the teaching and seminar process, the principle of integrating theory with practice is consistently emphasized. This includes not only focusing on the application analysis and quantitative research of modern Western economic theories in insurance and uncertainty economics but also emphasizing the combination with the current development status of China's insurance industry.

2025秋 · BIOL10002.07 · 杨鲜梅

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程涵盖的内容广泛，从生命的基本化学组成到细胞生物学，从生物化学到遗传学，以及从DNA到RNA再到蛋白质的遗传信息传递过程；其他讲授主题还包括基因表达调控、生物技术、若干精选生物类群、免疫系统及神经系统等。

课程简介（英文）

This course covers topics from general chemical building blocks of life to cellular biology, from biochemistry to genetics, and from DNA to RNA then to protein; Other topics lectured include control of gene expression, biotechnology, several picked kingdoms, immune system, and nerve system.

教学目标

Understand and master basic scientific theories in biology, and be prepared well in studying other courses as cell biology, biochemistry, molecular biology, etc.

教学方式

The teaching mainly involves lecturing by the teacher. And group discussion and presentation are also included at certain stage.

教学内容与进度安排

1st week: Introduction to life science 2nd week: The chemical composition of life 3rd week: The chemical building blocks of life 4th week: Biology of the cell 5th week: National Holiday 6th week: Biology of the cell /Cell cycle control 7th week: Cell cycle control 8th week: Respiration 9th week: Photosynthesis 10th week: Genetics and DNA 11th week: DNA replication/Transcription and translation 12th week: Transcription and translation /Control of gene expression 13th week: Control of gene expression 14th week: Biotechnology 15th week: Prokaryotes/Fungi/ Student Presentation 16th week: Virus / Student Presentation 17th week: Final exam 18th week: Grading

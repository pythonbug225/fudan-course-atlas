课程简介（中文）

本课程为全英文课程。除对合同法的一般介绍外，本课程内容还包括中国合同法、英美合同法以及国际合同公约之间的主要异同及其对国际交易的影响。

课程简介（英文）

This is a 2-credit courst taught in English. In addition to the contract law in general, understanding the main differences between the Chinese contract law and the common law of contracts is an important component of the course. The CISG and its effect on transnational transactions will also be discussed if time allows.

教学目标

This is a 2-credit seminar course designed to introduce the international/comparative contract law systems and the use of commercial contracts in international transactions.

教学方式

Students are expected to read and familiarize themselves with the relevant materials and then present and discuss them in class.

教学内容与进度安排

Topic 1: Contract Law in General Topic 2: Formation of Contract I: offer; Case Analysis Topic 3: Formation of Contract II: acceptance; Case Analysis Topic 4: Formation of Contract III: consideration Topic 5: Defenses; Case Analysis Topic 6: Performance and Breach; Case analysis Topic 7: Remedies; Case analysis Topic 8: CISG and International Transactions

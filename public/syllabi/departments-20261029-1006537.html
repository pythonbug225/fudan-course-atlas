本课程为经济学院博士班基础课，课程涵盖高等概率与统计、微观计量、宏观计量和金融计量

课程简介（英文）

This course is a foundational class for the doctoral program at the School of Economics, covering advanced probability and statistics, micro-econometrics, macro-econometrics, and financial econometrics.

教学目标

This course aims to present you a general econometric frame- work for doing empirical research. We will first review advanced probability theories and statistical theories. Then we will cover topics in Microecono- metrics, Time Series, and Financial Econometrics. After this class, you are expected to be able to (1) self-study advanced econometrics courses and do nontrivial mathematical derivations; (2) know how to build an econometric model for economic theories.

教学内容与进度安排

Course Contents: • Review of Advanced Probability and Statistics – Probability Space, Random Variables, and Conditional Expecta- tion – Convergence Modes – Law of Large Numbers and Central Limit Theorem – Maximum Likelihood Estimation • Standard Regression Models – Ordinary least Squares (OLS) – Nonlinear Least Square (NLS) – Generalized Least Square (GLS) • Endogeneity – IV Estimation – Generalized Methods of Moments (GMM) • Panel Data Analysis – Random Effects – Fixed Effects – Interactive Fixed Effects • Limit Dependent Variable Models – Binary Response Models: Probit/Logit Models – Tobit Model (Censored Models, Truncated Modes) and Sample Selection – Multiple Choice Models • DID • Time Series Analysis – Stationary ARMA Models – Stochastic Processes, Itˆo Process, Itˆo Formula – Non-stationary Processes – GARCH and Stochastic Volatility – Vector Autogression (VAR) and Vector Error Correction Models (VECM)

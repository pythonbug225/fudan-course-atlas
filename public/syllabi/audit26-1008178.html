课程简介（中文）

有机结构分析是有机专业学生的专业必修课程，其中最重要的内容是波谱综合解析，为有机合成结构的确认、化合物性质的鉴定以及产品纯度提供了必要的理论知识和实操技能。它对有机化学、分析化学、生物化学、药物化学、物理化学等均起着积极的推动作用。因此该课程既有很强的理论性，又有很高的应用价值。

课程内容包括UV、IR、MS、NMR、2D-NMR、波谱综合解析等。通过学习，使学生较好地掌握波谱分析方面重要方法和和实际结构分析的能力。在介绍波谱分析方法的新技术、新应用、新进展的同时，采用教学相长的方式，提高学生的独立研究能力。培养学生用波谱学方法解决科研实际问题的能力，从逻辑贯通和发散思维两方面拓展学生利用有机波谱学方法在确定有机化合物的结构、构型、构象上的思辨和实操能力。

课程简介（英文）

Organic Structural Analysis is a compulsory course for students majoring in organic chemistry, with the most important content being comprehensive spectroscopic analysis. It provides essential theoretical knowledge and practical skills for confirming the structures of organic synthesis, identifying compound properties, and assessing product purity. This course plays an active role in promoting the development of organic chemistry, analytical chemistry, biochemistry, medicinal chemistry, physical chemistry, and other related fields. Thus, it possesses both a strong theoretical foundation and high application value.

The course content includes UV, IR, MS, NMR, 2D-NMR, EPR, comprehensive spectroscopic analysis, and more. Through studying this course, students will acquire a solid grasp of important methods in spectroscopic analysis and the ability to perform practical structural analysis. While introducing new techniques, applications, and advancements in spectroscopic analysis methods, the course adopts an approach of teaching and learning from each other to enhance students' independent research capabilities. It aims to cultivate students' ability to solve practical research problems using spectroscopic methods, expanding their logical coherence and divergent thinking in determining the structure, configuration, and conformation of organic compounds through both theoretical and practical approaches.

教学目标

培养学生用波谱学方法解决科研实际问题的能力，从逻辑贯通和发散思维两方面拓展学生利用有机波谱学方法在确定有机化合物的结构、构型、构象上的思辨和实操能力。通过课程的学习，学生可以熟练掌握有机化合物结构鉴定的常规方法和手段。

教学方式

线下课堂，课件教学，实题讲解。

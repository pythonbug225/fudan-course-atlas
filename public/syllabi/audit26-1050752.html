课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍暴露科学的基本概念、原理与方法，帮助学生建立对暴露科学学科发展脉络的系统认识。课程内容涵盖外暴露监测、个体化暴露监测与内暴露监测三大核心内容。同时以专题讲座的形式，介绍暴露组学研究专项领域的最新研究进展、核心技术手段及其在环境健康领域的应用。通过本课程学习，将掌握从外暴露到内暴露的暴露评估方法，为后续开展环境健康及相关研究奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

Exposure Science and Exposomics

Designed for graduate students in environmental health, toxicology, epidemiology, or related fields with basic knowledge of environmental sciences, this course provides a comprehensive introduction to exposure science and its recent evolution into exposomics. It bridges traditional exposure monitoring approaches with cutting-edge technologies to characterize the totality of environmental exposures from external sources to internal doses.

Objectives

To understand the fundamental concepts, principles, and methods of exposure science, and to gain knowledge of state-of-the-art exposomics technologies and their applications in environmental health research.

Content

Introduces core concepts in exposure science, covering external exposure monitoring (e.g., drinking water and other environmental media), personal exposure monitoring, and internal exposure monitoring with an emphasis on biomarkers and study design. The course also covers the emerging field of exposomics, including high-throughput technologies, multi-omics integration, and their applications in identifying exposure–disease associations and understanding the health effects of chemical mixtures.

教学目标

本课程旨在系统介绍暴露科学的基本概念、核心原理与研究方法，内容涵盖外暴露监测、个体化暴露评估及内暴露监测的关键技术与实际应用。通过理论教学与实践训练，学生将掌握内外暴露的监测方法，能够初步设计并实施内暴露研究方案。课程还将聚焦暴露组学的前沿进展，使学生了解该领域最新技术手段及其在环境健康与公共卫生中的创新应用，培养具备跨学科视角与实践能力的专业人才。

教学方式

通过课堂教学学习基础知识和理论，针对学习内容开展课堂教学讨论。在基础学习和专题讨论结束后，通过案例分析学习暴露科学与暴露组的研究方法。

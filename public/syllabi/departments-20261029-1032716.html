课程简介（中文）

本课程深入探讨金融学中两个至关重要且相互关联的领域：金融科技的应用以及个人、公司和市场的金融心理学。在六次课程中，我们将探讨金融的数字化转型——从区块链和人工智能到文本分析；同时探索经济决策的行为基础，如启发式和选择架构。通过弥合前沿算法与人类认知之间的鸿沟，本课程为学生提供了理解现代市场异常现象和金融中介未来所需的多维视角。

课程简介（英文）

This course examines two important and related areas in finance: applications of technology in finance (Fintech) and the financial psychology of individuals, companies, and markets. Over six sessions, we navigate the digital transformation of finance - from Blockchain and AI to Textual Analysis - while concurrently exploring the behavioral underpinnings of economic decisions, such as Heuristics and Choice Architecture. By bridging the gap between cutting-edge algorithms and human cognition, this course equips students with the multi-dimensional insights necessary to understand modern market anomalies and the future of financial intermediation.

教学目标

The primary objective of this course is to foster a rigorous academic foundation at the intersection of technological innovation and behavioral finance. This course examines two important and related areas in finance: applications of technology in finance (Fintech) and the financial psychology of individuals, companies and markets, with an emphasis on developing students’ capacity to formulate original research hypotheses. Beyond conceptual understanding, students will develop the methodological proficiency to utilize machine learning and textual analysis in empirical settings, while critically synthesizing literature on heuristics and neurofinance to explain market inefficiencies. Ultimately, the course aims to transition students from passive learners to active contributors who can scrutinize the co-evolution of algorithmic finance and human cognition within the global financial architecture.

教学方式

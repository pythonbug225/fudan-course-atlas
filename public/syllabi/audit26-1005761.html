课程简介（中文）

该课程主要由各位授课老师以讲座形式进行，讲解肿瘤、泌尿系统、消化系统、神经系统、免疫系统等领域的科研进展，介绍科研思路，并结合分子病理常用的研究方法，介绍实验结果判断，目的是培养学生科研思维，如何确定课题研究方向，选定研究内容，在实验过程中发现并挖掘创新点，以及客观总结科研结果。

课程简介（英文）

This course is mainly carried out by teachers in the form of giving lectures. The lecturers will introduce the progress of scientific research in the fields of tumor, urinary system, digestive system, nervous system, 
immune system, etc. Lecturers also introduce their scientific research ideas, and explain how to analysis experimental results combining with the research methods commonly used in molecular pathology. The purpose of this course is to cultivate students' scientific thinking, how to determine the research direction of the subject, select the research content, dig out innovative points during the experiment, and objectively summarize the scientific research results.  

教学目标

我们期望通过本课程的讲授及学生相关文献的学习，学习到当前研究领域的研究思路、研究方法，结
果判断等内容。学生通过课程学习，了解如何做科研，如何挖掘科研创新点，如何客观总结科研结果，
提升自己的科研素质，有利于自己的科研任务的顺利完成。 
We hope that through the study of those lectures and some relevant literatures, we will let 
students to learn some research methods, get some common concepts about reseach,which is 
helpful for their scientific research tasks.

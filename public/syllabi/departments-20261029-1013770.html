商法与商业道德

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course provides a comprehensive examination of key aspects of corporate law, business ethics, and securities regulation within the Chinese context.  Topics covered encompass corporate finance, ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) considerations, corporate governance, shareholder rights, equity issuance and initial public offerings ("going public"), mergers and acquisitions, and the regulatory framework governing China's capital markets.  The primary focus is to equip students with a foundational legal understanding of establishing business entities in China and navigating the complexities of accessing China's capital markets for financial resources.  Discussions will delve into significant issues pertaining to corporate law and securities regulation, situating them within the evolving legal, business, and policy environments of China.

教学目标

This course employs a multifaceted pedagogical approach, integrating lectures, case studies, interactive discussions, and the Socratic method of inquiry. The primary objective is to cultivate students' ability to critically analyze legal and policy issues prevalent in business decision-making.  Students are expected to engage actively by diligently reviewing assigned materials in advance and demonstrating preparedness to participate in critical discussions. To enhance the learning experience, the instructor may invite seasoned practitioners to share their expertise and insights derived from real-world business transactions, fostering a dynamic exchange of perspectives with students.

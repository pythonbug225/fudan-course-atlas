公共经济学研究

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

《公共经济学研究》课程内容聚焦于中国社会保障、医疗体制、养老服务体制等议题，涵盖五大核心方面：（1）与复旦大学医疗养老前沿学术讨论班结合授课；（2）学习公共经济学、健康经济学领域的经典文献，介绍学术前沿动态；（3）掌握公共经济学领域基本模型和实证研究常用方法，介绍研究中国问题涉及的资料与数据；（4）了解中国近年来对社会经济发展具有广泛影响的公共政策，特别聚焦于医疗、养老等领域；（5）鼓励学生讲述感兴趣的研究设想及其研究设计，并进行深入讨论。通过理论讲解与案例分析，学生将掌握公共经济学的分析框架，学会运用经济工具评估公共政策的效果，培养解决实际经济问题的能力。 课程目标是帮助学生系统掌握公共经济学的理论体系，具备分析公共政策与市场机制关系的综合能力，增强批判性思维与政策制定中的实践应用能力。教学方法结合课堂讲授、案例讨论与小组项目，鼓励学生积极参与讨论，深入理解理论与现实的联系。 通过本课程，学生将全面了解公共经济学的理论体系与应用场景，特别对中国医疗、养老等关键领域的公共政策问题形成深刻认识，为未来从事相关研究或政策分析工作打下坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on key issues in China's public economics, including social security, healthcare systems, and elderly care systems. It covers five core components: Integration with Fudan University’s Advanced Seminar on Healthcare and Elderly Care – Collaborative lectures with leading academic discussions. Classic and Contemporary Literature – Study foundational texts in public economics and health economics, with an emphasis on cutting-edge research. Analytical Methods and Empirical Tools – Master fundamental models in public economics and common empirical techniques, including data sources for studying China-specific issues. Analysis of Major Public Policies – Examine recent Chinese public policies with broad socioeconomic impacts, particularly in healthcare and elderly care. Student-Led Research Proposals – Encourage students to present and refine their research ideas, followed by in-depth discussions. Through theoretical frameworks and case studies, students will develop the ability to analyze public economics issues, evaluate policy effectiveness using economic tools, and cultivate problem-solving skills for real-world challenges. Course Objectives Systematically grasp the theoretical foundations of public economics. Develop analytical skills to assess the interplay between public policy and market mechanisms. Strengthen critical thinking and practical application in policy design. Teaching Methods The course combines lectures, case discussions, and group projects, fostering active participation to bridge theory and practice. Learning Outcomes Upon completion, students will: Gain a comprehensive understanding of public economics theories and applications. Acquire specialized knowledge of China’s public policies in healthcare and elderly care. Build a solid foundation for future research or policy analysis in related fields.

教学目标

通过学习，系统了解现代公共经济学发展趋势及研究前沿，了解经典文献及其贡献，掌握研究公共经济学的经典理论和方法，考察中国公共政策的发展及其影响，由此促进学生积极主动思考问题，提升分析能力和研究能力。核心在于培养学生学术科研能力。

教学方式

教师进行授课引导并启发学生兴趣，学生组成小组进行讨论。

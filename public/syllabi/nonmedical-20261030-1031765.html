数字营销

2026春 · MJC70025.01 · 胥正川

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

元宇宙等新兴数字技术是一个让人兴奋，非常性感的一个新技术时代宣言，它宣告一个新的时代的开启。从消费互联网到产业互联网，从PC互联网到移动互联网，每一轮数字经济的大潮，都在推动着互联网往更高级的形态发展。“高级”的表征是，物理世界的束缚被一条条挣脱，PC互联网挣脱了空间的束缚，移动互联网挣脱了时间的束缚，终于元宇宙要挣脱物理世界的束缚。在元宇宙里，物理世界与数字世界的界限日趋模糊，人机交互无限接近甚至超越人与人的交互体验，数字孪生在工业与社会场景中逐步普及推广，数字产品、数字劳动力及数字财富成为人类财富构成的一部分。 人们在元宇宙空间中创造的障碍比现实世界更低，创造的动力就比现实世界更大，它会给社会带来几何级数的创新增长，这是元宇宙的社会意义。元宇宙不但不会让社会沉迷于虚拟空间“躺平”，相反它的目的是激发人们的创造精神，用虚拟空间低成本的创造探索，去推动现实世界的创新发明，在虚拟空间打造星际战舰，去追求现实宇宙的星辰大海。 要想让人们投入元宇宙中投入精力和资源进行创造发明，就必须让他的创造发明属于他自己而不是某个企业或平台，所以元宇宙世界必须首先构建一套“共同价值”的宪法规则，包括永恒的独立数字身份，以及基于数字身份的数字资产的私人属性，和数字资产的不可侵犯性，以及自愿、公平的交易原则等等 在数字经济迅猛发展的时代背景下，传统营销理论与实践正经历深刻重构。本课程立足于前沿科技与商业融合的交叉点，系统讲授以区块链、数字货币、元宇宙及人工智能（AI）为核心的下一代数字营销体系。课程旨在帮助新媒体专业研究生理解技术变革如何重塑消费者行为、品牌互动、价值创造与营销策略，并培养其在Web3.0时代的战略思维与实操能力。

课程简介（英文）

The advent of emerging digital technologies like the metaverse is an exhilarating declaration of a new technological era, one that heralds the dawn of a new age. From the consumer internet to the industrial internet, and from PC-based internet to mobile internet, each wave of the digital economy has propelled the internet towards more advanced forms. The hallmark of "advancement" is seen in how it progressively liberates us from the constraints of the physical world; PC internet freed us from spatial limitations, mobile internet untethered us from temporal constraints, and now the metaverse promises to break free from the bounds of the physical world altogether. In the metaverse, the lines between the physical and digital worlds are increasingly blurred, human-computer interaction approaches and may even surpass the experience of human-to-human interactions, and digital twins are gradually becoming widespread across industrial and societal contexts. Digital products, digital labor, and digital wealth are becoming integral components of human wealth. The barriers to creation in the metaverse are lower than those in the real world, thus the motivation for creation is greater, leading to exponential growth in innovation for society. This is the social significance of the metaverse. Rather than luring society into a state of complacency within virtual spaces, the metaverse aims to ignite creativity by leveraging the low-cost environment of virtual spaces to foster innovations that can be applied in the real world. It's about crafting interstellar ships in virtual space to pursue the vastness of the real universe. For individuals to invest their efforts and resources into creating and inventing within the metaverse, it is essential that their creations belong to them rather than to corporations or platforms. Therefore, the metaverse must first establish a set of constitutional rules based on "shared values", including eternal independent digital identities, the private nature of digital assets tied to these identities, the inviolability of digital assets, as well as principles of voluntary and fair transactions, among others. Against the backdrop of the rapid development of the digital economy, traditional marketing theories and practices are undergoing profound restructuring. Positioned at the intersection of cutting-edge technology and business integration, this course systematically covers the next generation of digital marketing systems centered around blockchain, digital currencies, the metaverse, and artificial intelligence (AI). Aimed at graduate students specializing in new media, the course seeks to elucidate how technological changes reshape consumer behavior, brand interactions, value creation, and marketing strategies, while fostering strategic thinking and practical skills in the Web3.0 era.

教学目标

通过本课程，学生将能够： 理解区块链、NFT、数字货币等去中心化技术对营销生态的影响； 掌握元宇宙中虚拟身份、沉浸式体验与社群经济的营销逻辑； 运用AI工具进行消费者洞察、内容生成、个性化推荐与营销自动化； 设计融合Web2.0与Web3.0元素的整合数字营销方案； 评估新兴技术应用中的伦理、隐私与监管挑战。在了解消费者决策行为机理后，本课程将展开讨论网络中的营销方法，并探讨如何基于大数据建立客户关系管理和智慧营销的技术与手段。

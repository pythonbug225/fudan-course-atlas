营销管理理论

2025秋 · BA60004.01 · 范秀成

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程聚焦于营销战略的理论基础。主要目标是使学生理解源自相关学科的理论及其在营销战略中的应用，并使学生熟悉问题导向的学术营销研究的主要领域。课程将介绍营销理论的发展，探讨各类营销学派的演变，审视营销战略的理论基础。涵盖的主题包括市场导向、营销战略与绩效、市场进入、品牌价值、产品开发、定价与广告、渠道、销售管理、客户关系管理以及在线营销问题。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the theoretical foundations of marketing strategy. The primary objective is to provide the students with an understanding of the theories drawn from related disciplines and its application in marketing strategy, and familiarize students with various areas of investigation for problem-oriented academic marketing research. The course will introduce the evolution of marketing theories and explore the development of various marketing schools, examining the theoretical foundations of marketing strategies. Topics included are market orientation, marketing strategy and performance, market entry, brand equity, product development, pricing and advertising, channel, sales management, customer relationship management, and online marketing issues

教学目标

to understand the marketing strategies and relevant theoretical explanations to be familiar with the evolution of marketing theories and development of marketing schools to develop the capability of investigation for problem-oriented academic marketing research to improve English reading and writing skills

教学方式

讲授 讨论

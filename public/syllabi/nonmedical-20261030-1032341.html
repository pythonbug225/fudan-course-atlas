社会保障制度与政策研究

2026春 · MANA50010.01 · 梁鸿

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

以研讨型课程设计，加深社会保障理论的理解和把握，系统分析社会保障的历史、变迁、制度设计逻辑、发展趋势，了解国际、国内社会保障前沿问题的研究动态，应用社会保障理论和研究方法，探讨中国社会保障建立与完善，学习公共政策分析方法在国内实践中的应用，使得学生具备独立完成（社会保障相关）政策应用分析的能力。

课程简介（英文）

Through the design of seminar-style courses, students will deepen their understanding and mastery of social security theories, systematically analyze the history, evolution, institutional design logic, and development trends of social security, and stay informed about cutting-edge research issues in social security both domestically and internationally. They will apply social security theories and research methods to explore the establishment and improvement of social security in China, learn how public policy analysis methods are applied in domestic practice, and develop the ability to independently complete policy application analysis related to social security.

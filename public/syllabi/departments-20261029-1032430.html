课程简介（中文）

理论模型研究是一门将实际问题与数学模型结合的艺术。它被广泛应用于经济学、市场营销学，管理科学与信息系统等学科。这门课将主要涵盖价格竞争、消费者搜索、互联网广告和行为经济学等方面；旨在介绍微观经济学与市场营销学中的经典理论模型。学生们将学习如何搭建理论模型以及将经济学模型运用到市场营销问题。

课程简介（英文）

Theoretical modelling is a art of connecting practical issues and math models. It has been widely applied into different disciplines such as economics, marketing, operations management, information system and etc. The course focuses primarily on topics: price competition, consumer search, online markets and behavioral economics. The goal of the course is to acquaint students with advanced analytical methods used in Economics and Marketing literature. Students should learn how to build models, analyze models and how to apply microeconomic models to Marketing.

教学目标

掌握经典理论营销模型 The purpose of this doctoral/master seminar is to acquaint students with the basic research techniques, approaches and key findings in the stream of theoretical models. 发现研究问题并搭建模型 Identify a significant research question, and map the research question to models.

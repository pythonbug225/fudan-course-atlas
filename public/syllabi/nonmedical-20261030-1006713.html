课程简介（中文）

代数几何研究多项式系统解的几何表现形式。从仿射和射影变的经典概念开始，我们将学习像希尔伯特的Nullstellensatz定理和bsamzout定理这样的基本定理。然后，我们将介绍代数几何的现代语言，包括方案，束和态射。

课程简介（英文）

Algebraic geometry studies the geometric manifestations of solutions to polynomial systems. Starting with classical notions of affine and projective varieties, we will study fundamental theorems like Hilbert’s Nullstellensatz and Bézout’s Theorem. We will then introduce modern language of algebraic geometry including schemes, sheaves, and morphisms.

教学目标

Upon completion of this course, the student * is able to define and use fundamental notions and constructions and knows results in algebraic geometry connected to the Zariski topology, affine and projective varieties, schemes, and their regular functions, rational functions, Zariski tangent space and singularities, divisors, line bundles, and quasi-coherent sheaves, morphisms between schemes and their properties such as separatedness and properness * is able to perform an elementary analysis of simple schemes, in particular answer questions on irreducible components and singularieties * knows fundamental intersection theory and Bezout's theorem * has insight into important examples like fiber product of schemes, the diagonal morphism, blowing up of a subscheme, the Segre embedding and the Veronese embedding * is able to produce the main ideas in the proofs of the most important results connected to the notions above.

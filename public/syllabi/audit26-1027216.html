课程简介（中文）

本课程为复旦大学中文系本科语言学专业专业课，旨在系统介绍音系学的基本理论与分析方法。课程内容涵盖音位分析、音系规则、音节结构、韵律特征、界面研究等核心议题，结合汉语及跨语言案例，探讨音系在不同语言中的共性与差异。学生将学习生成音系学、优选论等现代理论框架，并运用音系规则解释语音现象。

课程简介（英文）

This course is a core course for undergraduate linguistics majors in the Department of Chinese Language and Literature at Fudan University. It aims to systematically introduce the fundamental theories and analytical methods of phonology. The curriculum covers core topics such as phonemic analysis, phonological rules, syllable structure, and prosodic features, integrating case studies from Chinese and cross-linguistic contexts to explore universal patterns and language-specific variations in phonological systems. Students will engage with modern theoretical frameworks, including generative phonology and Optimality Theory, and apply phonological rules to explain phonetic phenomena. Through comparative analysis and practical exercises, the course emphasizes the development of critical thinking and research skills, equipping students with a robust foundation for advanced studies in linguistics and related interdisciplinary fields.

教学目标

1、知识传授：系统讲授音系学理论体系，涵盖音位、音节、韵律层级分析及生成音系学、优选论等前沿范式，贯通共时描写与历史演变视角，夯实学科核心知识结构。

2、能力培养：提升学生逻辑思辨、独立研究与解决复杂语言问题的能力。

3、价值引领：强化语言学研究的科学精神与人文关怀，引导学生关注汉语音系特征的文化价值，增强文化自信与学术使命感。

教学方式

以老师讲授为主，授课时穿插提问与互动，辅以作业。

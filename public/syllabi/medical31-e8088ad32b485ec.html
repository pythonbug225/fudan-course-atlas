1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 课程内容包括：外科常见病、外科与手术。手术是基于无菌操作、休克、感染、营养以及多器官衰竭等基本理论和技能。介绍了外科疾病的病因、病理、临床表现、诊断与鉴别诊断、防治原则及手术指征等。操作是培养具有严格无菌观念和基本操作训练的学生。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The course content includes : surgical common disease, surgery and operation.Surgery is based on the sterile operation, shock, infection, nutrition, and the basic theory and skills such as multiple organ failure. Surgical diseases is introduced the etiology, pathology, clinical manifestation, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, prevention and cure principles and the operation indication, etc. Operation is to cultivate students with strict aseptic concept and basic operation training.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

This course is designed to improve students’ understanding of general surgery. The carefully chosen material is intended to sensitize students to anatomy, physiology, pathophysiology, and medical principle of relevant treatments of general surgery.

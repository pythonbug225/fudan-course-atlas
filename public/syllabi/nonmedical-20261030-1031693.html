传播政治经济学前沿

2026春 · JCM70009.01 · 姚建华

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

传播政治经济学是传播学的重要研究流派之一，其学术发展与资本主义体系在全球范围内的建立、急剧扩张以及与之相抗争的社会运动的发展密切相关。这一学派不仅弥补了侧重行政导向与市场导向的美国主流传播研究之缺失，而且解构了主流传播研究的知识生产与社会权力关系。传播政治经济学对当前资本主义高度商业化、集中化、去管制化、国家化的传播制度与传播现象进行了深刻的反思，体现出全球传播政治经济学者们在不同层面与场域所进行的超越资本主义的社会转型的各种努力。本课程的主要授课内容，包括传播政治经济学的理论起源与社会关怀、全球传播产业的商品化、空间化和结构化、信息社会的知识劳工、广告的权力与受众商品的塑造等。 通过本课程的学习，使学生对在全球化背景下，传播政治经济学者对网络新媒体的讨论有所了解，以期在洞察全球信息社会的新媒体的政治经济学脉络和理论取向的基础上，让他们对传播政治经济学及其视野下的全球社会的公正传播这一议题有更深刻与全面的理解。

课程简介（英文）

The political economy of communication is one of the important research paradigms in the field of communication studies. Its academic development is closely linked with the establishment and rapid expansion of the capitalist system on a global scale, as well as with the development of social movements that challenge it. This school of thought not only addresses the shortcomings of the administratively and market-oriented focus of mainstream American communication research but also deconstructs the relationship between knowledge production and social power within mainstream communication studies. The political economy of communication offers a profound critique of the highly commercialized, centralized, deregulated, and nationalized communication systems and phenomena under current capitalism, reflecting the diverse efforts by global scholars in this field to seek social transformation beyond capitalism at different levels and domains. The main content of this course includes the theoretical origins and social concerns of the political economy of communication, the commodification, spatialization, and structuration of the global communication industry, knowledge labor in the information society, and the power of advertising and the shaping of audience commodities. Through this course, students will gain an understanding of how scholars in the political economy of communication discuss the role of new media in the context of globalization. The aim is to provide them with a deeper and more comprehensive understanding of the political economy of communication and the issue of just communication in global society, based on insights into the political-economic context and theoretical orientations of new media in the global information society.

教学目标

课程通过权威、系统的课程讲授，围绕全球化语境中，新媒体与信息的公正传播等中心议题展开讨论，旨在为同学们勾勒出一个清晰、完整的传播政治经济学学术脉络，为参与课程的同学奠定坚实的理论基础。本课程的核心内容包括：传播政治经济学理论、传播政治经济学与平台、社会性别、社会边缘群体、数字劳动、数字游民之间的勾连关系。

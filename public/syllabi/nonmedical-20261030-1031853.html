课程简介（中文）

《量子色动力学导论》是一门面向理论物理或高能物理方向研究生或高年级本可生的进阶课程，旨在系统阐述描述强相互作用的量子色动力学（QCD）理论及其应用。课程以SU(3)非阿贝尔规范理论为核心，深入解析QCD的基本框架（如色荷、胶子场、拉格朗日量构造），结合微扰计算与非微扰方法，揭示夸克禁闭、渐近自由、强子结构等核心物理现象的本质。通过理论推导和前沿文献研讨，课程注重培养学生解决实际问题的能力，衔接高能实验数据分析，为学生从事高能物理理论、实验或跨学科研究奠定扎实基础。

课程简介（英文）

Introduction to Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) is an advanced course designed for graduate students or senior undergraduates specializing in theoretical or high-energy physics. It systematically explores the theory and applications of Quantum Chromodynamics, the framework governing strong interactions. Centered on the SU(3) non-Abelian gauge theory, the course delves into the foundational structure of QCD—including concepts like color charge, gluon fields, and Lagrangian formulation—while integrating both perturbative calculations (e.g., parton models, deep inelastic scattering) and non-perturbative approaches (e.g., lattice QCD, chiral perturbation theory) to elucidate the nature of core physical phenomena such as quark confinement, asymptotic freedom, and hadron structure. Through theoretical derivations and critical analysis of cutting-edge literature, the course emphasizes developing students' problem-solving skills in real-world contexts, bridges the gap to high-energy experimental data analysis, and lays a robust foundation for careers in high-energy physics theory, experimentation, or interdisciplinary research.

教学目标

本课程通过系统讲解强相互作用的基本理论框架（如SU(3)规范对称性），帮助学生掌握量子色动力学（QCD）的核心思想与数学工具（如路径积分、微扰展开及重整化群方法），并深入理解夸克禁闭、渐近自由、强子结构与高能物理现象（如喷注形成、夸克-胶子等离子体）的内在联系。课程强调理论与实验的结合，培养学生运用QCD分析实际问题的能力（如部分子分布函数计算、深度非弹性散射分析），同时引入前沿课题，为学生从事粒子物理理论和高能核物理等研究奠定基础，使其能够独立开展与QCD相关的科学探索，衔接标准模型扩展、核物理或极端天体环境等研究方向。

教学方式

《量子色动力学导论》通常采用理论讲授和问题研讨相结合的教学方式，具体包括以下要点： 核心理论框架的课堂讲授: 通过板书或幻灯片系统梳理QCD的数学结构（如SU(3)规范对称性、拉格朗日量构造），辅以经典论文或教材推导示例（如β函数计算），帮助学生建立理论直觉。 前沿文献研读与课题研究: 分配经典或前沿论文，要求学生撰写综述或复现部分结果，培养独立研究能力。 教学过程中常通过阶段性课题和开放性问题驱动学习，兼顾理论深度与实践灵活性。

教学内容与进度安排

16 weeks excluding 17th and 18th weeks for final exam: Week 1-10: Foundations of QCD Review of QED and Gauge Theories(general aspects of QFT) (week 1-2) Abelian vs. non-Abelian gauge symmetry QED Lagrangian and photon propagator Color Charge and SU(3) Symmetry (week 3-4) Quark model and color quantum number SU(3) group theory basics (generators, structure constants) QCD Lagrangian (week 5-6) Gluon fields and covariant derivative Quantization of quark field, gluon field (path integral method), ghosts Feynman rules for QCD Regularization and renormalization (asymptotic freedom as an example in the next chapter) (week 7-8) Regularization scheme Some general discussion of renormalization Anomaly and instanton (week 9-10) Triangular anomaly QCD topological vacuum and instanton Week 11-12: Asymptotic Freedom and Confinement Running Coupling Constant (week 11) Renormalization group equations β-function calculation (1-loop) Deep Inelastic Scattering (DIS) (week 12) Parton model and Bjorken scaling Structure functions and scaling violations Week 13-14: Perturbative QCD and Jets Week 13-14: Perturbative QCD and Jets QCD at High Energies (week 13) e⁺e⁻ → hadrons (R-ratio) DGLAP equations Jet Physics (week 13) Jet algorithms (kT, anti-kT) Lund string model Factorization Theorems (week 14) Hard vs. soft processes, SCET 4 Future Colliders (EIC, FCC) (week 14) Week 15-16: Non-perturbative QCD (Hadron Structure and Spectroscopy) Quark-Gluon Bound States (week 15) Mesons and baryons Heavy quarkonia (J/ψ, Υ) Lattice QCD (week 15) Wilson action, Monte Carlo simulations Mass spectra and decay constants QCD phase transition: Chiral Symmetry Breaking, deconfinement (effective theory of QCD) (week 16) Pions as Goldstone bosons Chiral perturbation theory NRQCD HQET

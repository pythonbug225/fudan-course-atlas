Advanced Financial Accounting

课程简介（中文）

The course covers five aspects of accounting knowledge: the value of accounting information, revenue recognition, assets, liabilities and cash. On the basis of understanding the basic knowledge, two application scenarios of accounting information are introduced to the students: performance evaluation and valuation.

课程简介（英文）

The course covers five aspects of accounting knowledge: the value of accounting information, revenue recognition, assets, liabilities and cash. On the basis of understanding the basic knowledge, two application scenarios of accounting information are introduced to the students: performance evaluation and valuation.

教学目标

The course mainly takes the form of case study, and we will discuss the literature and cases provided in class. Students are required to read classic literature carefully, think about and discuss pre-class assignments with group members, and complete the case assignment after the course. The purpose is to enable students to have a deeper understanding of the production of accounting information and how to use it in the capital market. This course is an extension and real world application of the basic accounting knowledge learned in M1 financial accounting course.

教学方式

Students are required to read classic literature carefully, think about and discuss pre-class assignments with group members, actively participate in class discussions during the course, and complete the case assignment after the course.

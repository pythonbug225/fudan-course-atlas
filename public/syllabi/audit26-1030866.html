课程简介（中文）

绪论：精神医学的概念，精神医学的发展概况，精神医学与相关学科，精神医学的临床前沿问题，怎样学好精神医学。病因学：精神障碍的生物学因素包括：遗传因素、神经递质、神经内分泌、脑结构及脑功能改变等，环境因素包括：移民、城市化、童年创伤、物质使用等。病因学研究面临的挑战。精神障碍的诊断和分类系统。精神症状学：包括感知障碍，思维障碍，情感障碍，意志障碍，注意障碍，动作行为障碍，记忆障碍，智能障碍，意识障碍，自我意识障碍；常见的精神科综合征；接触精神障碍患者面谈的基本原则与技能。精神障碍诊断方法。双相障碍和抑郁障碍：包括双相障碍和抑郁障碍的概述、流行病学、病因和发病机制、临床表现与类别、诊断和鉴别诊断、治疗与预防。精神分裂症：包括概述、流行病学、病因和发病机制、临床表现与类别、诊断和鉴别诊断、治疗与预防。脑和躯体疾病所致精神障碍：阿尔茨海默病和血管性痴呆概述、流行病学、病因和发病机制、临床表现与类别、诊断和鉴别诊断、治疗与预防。其他脑部疾病和躯体疾病所致精神障碍概述。焦虑障碍：包括焦虑障碍概述，惊恐障碍、广泛性焦虑障碍、社交焦虑障碍的流行病学、病因和发病机制、临床表现与类别、诊断和鉴别诊断、治疗与预防。强迫症和创伤与应激相关障碍：包括概述、流行病学、病因和发病机制、临床表现与类别、诊断和鉴别诊断、治疗与预防。孤独症、ADHD和精神发育迟滞：包括孤独症、ADHD和精神发育迟滞的概述、流行病学、病因和发病机制、临床表现与类别、诊断和鉴别诊断、治疗与预防。人格障碍与性心理障碍，包括人格障碍、性心理障碍的病因、临床表现、分型、诊断和鉴别诊断、治疗与预防。精神障碍治疗，包括心理治疗、药物治疗、物理治疗、康复治疗四个部分，以及抗精神病药物、抗焦虑药无、抗抑郁药物、心境稳定剂、益智药物等临床应用

课程简介（英文）

Introduction: the concept of psychiatry, the development of psychiatry, psychiatry and related disciplines, the clinical frontier problems of psychiatry, and how to learn psychiatry well. Etiology: Biological factors of mental disorder include: genetic factors, neurotransmitters, neuroendocrine, brain structure and brain function changes, and environmental factors include: immigration, urbanization, childhood trauma, substance use, etc. Challenges faced by etiological research. Diagnosis and classification system of mental disorders. Psychiatry: including perception disorder, thinking disorder, emotion disorder, will disorder, attention disorder, action and behavior disorder, memory disorder, intelligence disorder, consciousness disorder and self-awareness disorder; Common psychiatric syndrome; Basic principles and skills of interviewing patients with mental disorders. Diagnostic methods of mental disorders. Bipolar disorder and depressive disorder: including the overview, epidemiology, etiology and pathogenesis, clinical manifestations and categories, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment and prevention of bipolar disorder and depressive disorder. Schizophrenia: including overview, epidemiology, etiology and pathogenesis, clinical manifestations and categories, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment and prevention. Mental disorders caused by brain and body diseases: overview, epidemiology, etiology and pathogenesis, clinical manifestations and categories, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment and prevention of Alzheimer's disease and vascular dementia. Overview of mental disorders caused by other brain diseases and physical diseases. Anxiety disorder: including an overview of anxiety disorder, epidemiology, etiology and pathogenesis, clinical manifestations and categories, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment and prevention of panic disorder, generalized anxiety disorder and social anxiety disorder. Obsessive-compulsive disorder and trauma and stress-related disorders: including overview, epidemiology, etiology and pathogenesis, clinical manifestations and categories, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment and prevention. Autism, ADHD and mental retardation: including the overview, epidemiology, etiology and pathogenesis, clinical manifestations and categories, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment and prevention of autism, ADHD and mental retardation. Personality disorder and sexual psychological disorder, including the etiology, clinical manifestations, classification, diagnosis and differential diagnosis, treatment and prevention of personality disorder and sexual psychological disorder. The treatment of mental disorders includes four parts: psychotherapy, drug therapy, physical therapy and rehabilitation therapy, as well as clinical applications of antipsychotics, anti-anxiety drugs, antidepressants, mood stabilizers, and educational drugs.

教学目标

精神医学是临床医学的一个重要分支，通过学习使学生了解精神医学在今后临床工作的重要性，掌握常见精神障碍的临床表现、诊断与鉴别诊断、治疗原则和常用方法，熟悉精神障碍的患病率、病因、发病机制、预后等相关知识，了解我国精神医学发展（思政）,以便学生在毕业后的临床和研究工作中有所帮助。

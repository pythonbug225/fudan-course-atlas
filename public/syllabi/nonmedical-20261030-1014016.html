课程简介（中文）

因为课程创新性内容学员们的参与性需要非常集中，前期有情商EQ测试，企业创新理论课程（共演战略）- 课程中分小组参与商业模拟与理念实验，参访时小组需现场咨询与课程核心有关的问题，最终考核需小组路演发表所学习的核心理念。

课程简介（英文）

Due to the innovative content of the course, students' participation needs to be very focused. In the early stage, there are emotional intelligence and EQ tests. In the course of Enterprise Innovation Theory (Collaborative Strategy), small groups participate in business simulations and concept experiments. During visits, the group needs to consult on-site on issues related to the core of the course. The final assessment requires the group to present the core concepts learned during a roadshow

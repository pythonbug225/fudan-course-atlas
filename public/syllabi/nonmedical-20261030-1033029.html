课程简介（中文）

这是2025年秋季课程“弦理论导论”的延续。它将涵盖微扰超弦，弦紧化，弦对偶性，超对称d膜，以及相关的东西。

课程简介（英文）

This is a continuation of the course “Introduction to String Theory” offered in the Fall of 2025. It will cover perturbative superstrings, string compactifications, string dualities, supersymmetric D-branes, and related stuff.

教学目标

Make students familiar with the principles, methods and techniques of superstring theory, familiarize them with the known perturbative superstring theories and M-theory.

教学方式

讲授

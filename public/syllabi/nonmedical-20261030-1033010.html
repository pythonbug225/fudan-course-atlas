课程简介（中文）

传感器在基础科学研究、航空航天、生物医学、工业生产、移动通信、智能家居等各个领域都有着广泛的应用。材料科学技术是推动新型传感器发展的核心推动力，新材料的发明可以极大地促进传感器性能的提升。因此，本课程旨在深入探讨传感材料在现代信息产业中的关键作用，特别是在传感器设计过程中的重要性。通过学习和掌握传感材料与传感器原理，可以有效提高材料领域研究生在新技术领域的创新能力。 在本课程中，学生将深入了解各类传感材料的物理化学特性，以及相应的器件工作原理与设计准则，以培养学生的创新思维和解决实际问题的能力。通过本课程，学生将掌握多种传感器的底层研究逻辑，提高在材料领域的创新能力，为未来的科学研究和工程实践打下坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

Sensors are widely applied in various fields such as basic scientific research, aerospace engineering, biomedicine, industrial production, mobile communications, and smart homes. Materials science and technology serve as the core driving force for the development of new-type sensors; the invention of advanced materials can significantly boost the performance of sensors. Therefore, this course aims to thoroughly explore the pivotal role of sensing materials in the modern information industry, particularly their importance in the sensor design process. By learning and mastering the principles of sensing materials and sensors, postgraduate students in the materials field can effectively enhance their innovation capabilities in emerging technological domains. In this course, students will gain an in-depth understanding of the physicochemical properties of various types of sensing materials, as well as the corresponding device operating principles and design criteria, so as to cultivate their innovative thinking and ability to solve practical problems. Through this course, students will grasp the fundamental research logic underlying a variety of sensors, improve their innovation capabilities in the materials field, and lay a solid foundation for future scientific research and engineering practice.

教学目标

1.熟悉掌握各类传感材料。 2.理解各类传感器的基本结构和核心工作原理。 3.掌握计算传感器各类关键参数的能力。 4.具备评估传感器性能和合理选择应用传感器的能力。 5.具备拓展新材料体系潜在传感价值的能力。

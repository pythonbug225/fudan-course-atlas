课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养学生运用数学知识与方法，解决理论与应用经济学问题的实践技能。课程内容分为以下四个部分： 1）无穷级数、幂级数与泰勒级数； 2）多元函数微分学及其应用； 3）多元函数积分学及其应用； 4）常微分方程。

课程简介（英文）

The course gives students’ skills of implementation of mathematical knowledge and practice to economic problems both theoretical and applied ones. There will be four parts in the course: 1）infinite series, power series, Taylor series; 2) multivariable derivative with applications; 3) multivariable integrals with applications; 4) ordinary differential equations.

教学目标

By the end of the semester a student has to: Know about the principal concepts and results of infinite series, power series and Taylor series, multivariable derivative, multivariable integral and applications; Be able to apply calculus to solve problems; Know about the basic concepts and results of ordinary differential equations.

教学方式

Lecture, presentation, group discussion

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule Week 1: infinite series Week 2: power series Week 3: Taylor series Week 4: vectors and the geometry of space Week 5: equation of lines and planes Week 6: vector functions Week 7: limit and continuity of multivariable funtions Week 8: derivative of multivariable functions I Week 9: derivative of multivariable functions II Week 10: double integral I Week 11: triple integral Week 12: line integral Week 13: surface integral I Week 14: surface integral II Week 15: ordinary differential equations I Week 16: ordinary differential equations II

教学参考资料

References: 1.  Calculus, by James Stewart. 2. Thomas’ Calculus.

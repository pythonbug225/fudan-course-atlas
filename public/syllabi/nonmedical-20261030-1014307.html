Principles of Finance and Valuation

2025秋 · MF60020.04 · 童国士

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

This course provides an introduction to the principles of finance and valuation. Specifically, we will discuss: as corporate managers, how to evaluate prospective projects; as investors, how to evaluate firms. The course has two main topics. The first is capital budgeting. We will start with the time value of money, then discuss how various factors (e.g., capital structure) affect the required rate of return, and at last how to make investment decisions. The second topic focuses on the firm valuation. The basic principles follow the first topic naturally. However, we will discuss the procedure in more detail, which includes measuring earnings and cash flows, estimating growth, estimating discount rate, estimating terminal value, firm and equity valuation, and relative valuation using comparable companies and multiples.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to understand the basic principles in finance and to learn how to evaluate projects and companies from both a conceptual and practical framework. Topics include: capital budgeting, DCF valuation, and relative valuation using comparable companies and multiples.

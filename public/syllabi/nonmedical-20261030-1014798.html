艺术品投资与管理

2025秋 · MBA60040.01 · 黄文叡

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程涵盖3大主题：1. 艺术市场概论－让学生了解艺术品不同的交易平台和每个平台对艺术品不同的定价标准；2. 艺术品的收藏规划－教学生如何使用精确且有效的方法来建构艺术收藏，且探讨世界几大收藏家的收藏之道；3. 艺术投资的实践与管理－带领学生建构投资组合，从实操案例中，了解艺术品作为一种投资组合的优势和方法，进而探讨投资管理人要面对的风险和风控。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the relevant theories and knowledge of enterprise crisis management and crisis communication, with three main characteristics: firstly, the integration of theory with practice. Emphasize the application of basic theories of risk management, crisis management, and communication in the changing media ecology and public opinion environment; Secondly, emphasize practical orientation. By analyzing a series of influential and representative cases of enterprise crisis management and communication, interactive communication and group discussions are conducted with students to stimulate their participation and thinking; Thirdly, rooted in complex situations. The course emphasizes professionalism, complexity, and transcendence, focusing on specific contexts of politics, economy, society, and culture for more efficient crisis management and communication. This course covers three main topics: 1 Introduction to the Art Market - Enable students to understand the different trading platforms for art and the pricing standards for art on each platform; 2. Art Collection Planning - Teach students how to use precise and effective methods to construct art collections, and explore the collection methods of several major collectors around the world; 3. Practice and Management of Art Investment - Lead students to construct investment portfolios, understand the advantages and methods of art as an investment portfolio through practical cases, and then explore the risks and risk control that investment managers need to face.

教学目标

本课程主要针对目标: 把艺术品作为一种金融工具，掌握艺术品交易的特殊属性，透过有系统的研究方法，培养学生如何正确选择标的，把艺术品作为一种可资收藏的资产或一种投资组合，以期达到提高生活品味、活化有价资产和分散投资风险的目的。 本课程间接针对目标: 在现有的金融体系内，透过私行或家族办公室的运作，把艺术品作为资产配置的选项，将艺术与金融做一完美的结合。

课程简介（中文）

化学实验室需要跟很多仪器打交道：包括分析仪器（四大谱学仪器、扫描/透射电镜、衍射仪以及光电子能谱等），测量仪器（分析天平、温控仪、酸度计以及压力/真空计等），操纵和执行仪器设备（各种真空泵、烘箱、加热炉、球磨机以及超速离心机等）。对这些种类繁多，形形色色的仪器，除了能够正确操作和使用以外，能够进行日常维护以及简单的维修和故障排除是科研工作顺利和高效进行的前提和保证。目前国内实验室用的一些商用仪器，尤其是大型仪器和高端分析测试仪器大部分都是从国外公司购买。随着科学的发展，新的研究方向不断涌现，不同学科之间交叉程度越来越高，这些变化对科学仪器的种类和功能不断提出新的要求。但是商业仪器需要一定研发周期，另外，商业仪器公司更加关注的是产品的市场规模和利润等，市场上现有的商业仪器不可能完全满足所有的科研需求，越来越多的课题组需要自己动手设计，加工和安装仪器，在一些世界著名的实验室和课题组这已经成为一种趋势和潮流。从化学类专业的教学课程体系来看，化学类专业的学生在仪器的结构，工作原理以及应用（例如：波谱解析）等方面普遍受过比较好的训练，为仪器的高效使用奠定了比较扎实的基础。而科学仪器的维修、故障排除、以至仪器的设计和开发则更多需要的是电力与电子、光学与光电、机械加工与制造、结构与功能材料以及计算机软件和硬件等相关知识，相对而言，化学类专业的学生在这些方面的基础普遍略显薄弱。

本课程主要介绍与科学仪器的维修以及研制相关的一些机械加工与制造，电子与电力，光学与激光，结构与功能材料等方面的最基本的基础知识，

课程简介（英文）

Chemical laboratories involve extensive work with various instruments. These include analytical instruments (such as the four major spectroscopy instruments, scanning/transmission electron microscopes, diffractometers, and photoelectron spectrometers like XPS), measurement instruments (such as analytical balances, temperature controllers, pH meters, and pressure/vacuum gauges), and manipulation/processing equipment (such as various vacuum pumps, ovens, furnaces, ball mills, and ultracentrifuges).

For these diverse instruments, beyond correct operation and usage, the ability to perform daily maintenance, simple repairs, and troubleshooting is essential for ensuring smooth and efficient scientific research. Currently, many commercial instruments used in domestic laboratories, especially large-scale and high-end analytical instruments, are predominantly purchased from foreign companies.

As science advances, new research directions continuously emerge, and interdisciplinary integration deepens. These developments constantly raise new demands for the types and functionalities of scientific instruments. However, commercial instrument development requires significant R&D cycles. Furthermore, commercial instrument companies primarily focus on market size, profitability, and other business considerations. Existing commercial instruments on the market cannot fully satisfy all research needs. Consequently, an increasing number of research groups need to design, fabricate, and assemble instruments themselves. This has become a trend and common practice in some world-renowned laboratories and research groups.

Examining the chemistry curriculum system, chemistry students generally receive solid training in instrument structure, working principles, and applications (e.g., spectral interpretation), laying a relatively strong foundation for the efficient use of instruments. However, instrument maintenance, troubleshooting, and even the design and development of instruments require knowledge in related fields such as electrical and electronic engineering, optics and photonics, machining and manufacturing, structural and functional materials, and computer software/hardware. Relatively speaking, chemistry students often have a weaker foundational background in these areas.

This course primarily introduces the most fundamental foundational knowledge related to the maintenance and development of scientific instruments, covering areas such as machining and manufacturing, electronics and electrical engineering, optics and lasers, and structural and functional materials.

教学目标

通过介绍与科学仪器的维修以及研制相关的一些机械加工与制造，电子与电力，光学与激光，结构与功能材料等方面的最基本的基础知识，以及本人在学习和研究工作中的一些体会和经验，激发学生对科学仪器相关知识学习的兴趣，使学生对相关学科和知识内容不再感到畏惧和毫无头绪，并引导和培养学生对相关知识的自学能力，为研究生今后的研究工作打好一定的基础，以及为研究生对未来的研究方向和职业的选择提供参考。

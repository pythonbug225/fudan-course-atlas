1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 本课程介绍并阐明健康生态模型的概念，并概述与预防三级（一级、二级、三级预防）相关的策略与原则。基于健康生态模型，课程分为三大组成部分：个体层面的临床预防医学、社区层面的群体性公共卫生服务以及系统层面的卫生体系管理。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | This course introduces and explains the concepts of the Health Ecological Model and outlines the strategies and principles associated with the three levels of prevention: primary, secondary, and tertiary. Based on the Health Ecological Model, the curriculum is divided into three key components: clinical preventive medicine at the individual level, community-based public health services at the community level, and health system management at the systemic level.

2. 教学目标 /Course Objectives （结合本校办学定位、学生情况、专业人才培养要求，应体现价值引领、知识传授、能力培养等方面的内容。）

Clinicians play a critical role not only in delivering clinical treatments but also in providing preventive services at both individual and community levels. Preventive Medicine I serves as an introductory course designed to equip undergraduate medical students, in particular those majoring in non-preventive medicine, with essential knowledge and skills in this field. By the end of this course, students are expected to: 1. Grasp fundamental concepts in preventive medicine, 2. Master key skills necessary for implementing clinical preventive services, 3. Identify underlying causes of population health issues within clinical settings, and 4. Advocate effectively for public health within their communities. This comprehensive approach aims to empower future clinicians to integrate preventive medicine into their practice, ultimately enhancing health outcomes for individuals and populations alike.

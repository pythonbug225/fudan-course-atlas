课程简介（英文）

It is a honor class for aquatic environmental chemistry. It includes two parts: lectures and experiments,

教学目标

It will be an advanced course focusing on examination of processes that affect the behavior and fate of organic/inorganic contaminants in aquatic environments. From this class, students will learn to predict chemical properties and to apply the knowledge of chemical properties and transformation reactions to assess the environmental fate of contaminants.

教学方式

The course will utilize a combination of lectures, student presentations, and discussions, alongside dedicated instrument practice sessions. This multifaceted approach encourages active engagement, fosters collaborative learning, and enhances students' practical skills." Beside lectures, We will ask students to visit the research lab, to familiar the experimental detials for analyzing trace emerging contaminants in surfacewater and wastewaters.

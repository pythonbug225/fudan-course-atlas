课程简介（中文）

应用无机化学、配位化学、生物化学、化学生物学的理论概念和实验技术研究生物体系中无机金属离子的行为和规律。在分子水平上探讨无机元素及其化合物与生物体的相互作用； 研究金属离子与各种生物活性配体（包括蛋白质、核酸、氨基酸、核苷酸）的作用规律及这些规律的应用；以无机金属离子为探针来了解纷繁的生物过程和基本途径。阐明金属离子与生物大分子形成配合物的结构与功能的关系，揭示金属离子在生命过程中的奥妙，为生理、病理和相关药物研究提供理论基础。

课程简介（英文）

Apply the theoretical concepts and experimental techniques of inorganic chemistry, coordination chemistry, biochemistry, and chemical biology to study the behavior and regulations of inorganic metal ions in biological systems. Discuss the interaction of inorganic elements and their compounds with organisms at the molecular level; study the rules of metal ions and various biologically active ligands (including proteins, nucleic acids, amino acids, nucleotides) and their application; Metal ions are used as probes to understand the numerous biological processes and basic approaches. To clarify the relationship between the structure and function of the complex formed by metal ions and biological macromolecules, to reveal the mystery of metal ions in the life process, and to provide a theoretical basis for physiological, pathological and related drug research.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，使学生认识无机元素在生命进化过程中的存在及其作用规律，如何应用无机化学、配位化学、生物化学、化学生物学的理论概念和实验技术研究生物体系中无机金属离子的行为和规律。理解无机元素及其化合物与生物体的相互作用；金属离子与各种生物活性配体的作用规律及这些规律的应用。揭示金属离子在生命过程中的奥妙，为生理、病理和相关药物研究提供理论基础。

 

Through the study of this course, students can understand the existence and rules of inorganic elements in the evolution of life, how to apply the theoretical concepts and experimental techniques of inorganic chemistry, coordination chemistry, biochemistry, chemical biology to study inorganic metals in biological systems. Understand the interaction of inorganic elements and their compounds with organisms; the rules of action of metal ions and various biologically active ligands and their application. The mystery of metal ions in the life process will be understood, and a theoretical basis for physiological, pathological and related drug research will be played.

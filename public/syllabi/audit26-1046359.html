课程简介（中文）

目录学是研究目录形成和发展的传统学问，是了解历代传统学术成果的重要入门门径。校雠学是校勘中国古籍的实践性学问，是了解中国传统文化与研究国学的必备基础。本课程介绍传统典籍由古到今发展的脉络，说明简帛、写卷、刻本等不同载体对书籍体例的影响。本课从目录学和文献学角度介绍中国历史上思想文献的形成、流变、篇目分合、篇章变化，为中国哲学史研究提供文献基础。

课程简介（英文）

Bibliology is a traditional discipline that studies the formation and evolution of catalogues, serving as a core introductory pathway to grasp the traditional academic achievements of successive dynasties.
The study of Chinese classical collation is a practical scholarship dedicated to collating ancient Chinese texts, which constitutes an indispensable foundation for understanding traditional Chinese culture and conducting Sinology research.
This course introduces the evolutionary context of traditional Chinese classics from antiquity to the present, and elaborates on the influences of different carriers including bamboo-slip and silk manuscripts, handwritten scrolls, and block-printed editions on the stylistic and structural norms of books.
From the perspectives of bibliography and philology, this course introduces the formation, transmission, division and integration of entries, as well as textual variations of intellectual documents in Chinese history, so as to lay a solid documentary foundation for the research of Chinese philosophical history.

教学目标

使学生了解传统文献学的基本理论和基本方法，重点学习其分支之一中的目录学，熟悉历史上重要的四部文献，重点了解经、子两部文献。对古典文献的版本和收藏做初步了解。从实践的角度学习校雠学，掌握古籍校勘的基本方法和程序，了解古籍校勘中的难点，练习校勘技术，为阅读古典打好文献学基础。

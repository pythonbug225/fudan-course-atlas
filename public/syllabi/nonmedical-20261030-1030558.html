课程简介（中文）

教学内容包括：本土裔美国文学概论（10课时），文学作品选读和研讨（26课时）。具体周课时计划：1-2：本土裔美国文学概述；3-4：19-20世纪英语小说：Mathews：Sundown；5-6：19-20世纪英语小说：McNickle：The Surrounded；7：19-20世纪英语诗歌与戏剧；8：印第安文艺复兴概述；9-10：Momady：The Way to Rain Mountains和House Made of Dawn；11-14：Erdrich：Tracks和Four Souls；15-16现代诗人与诗歌；17-18、当代本土裔美国文学发展 选读主要包括Mathews, McNickle, Momady,和Erdrich等重要小说家的4部长篇小说，Otiz、Harjo、Glancy、Hogan和Rose等当代诗人的作品，以及其他重要作家的作品概述。

课程简介（英文）

The teaching content includes: Introduction to Native American Literature (10 class hours), and Selected Readings and Seminars on Literary Works (26 class hours). Specific weekly schedule: Weeks 1-2: Overview of Native American Literature; Weeks 3-4: 19th-20th Century English Novels: Mathews: Sundown; Weeks 5-6: 19th-20th Century English Novels: McNickle: The Surrounded; Week 7: 19th-20th Century English Poetry and Drama; Week 8: Overview of the Native American Renaissance; Weeks 9-10: Momady: The Way to Rainy Mountain and House Made of Dawn; Weeks 11-14: Erdrich: Tracks and Four Souls; Weeks 15-16: Modern Poets and Poetry; Weeks 17-18: Development of Contemporary Native American Literature. Selected readings primarily include four full-length novels by important novelists such as Mathews, McNickle, Momady, and Erdrich; works by contemporary poets such as Ortiz, Harjo, Glancy, Hogan, and Rose; and an overview of works by other significant writers.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习研讨，初步了解本土裔美国文学的起源和发展，了解其主要作家和作品，丰富对美国文学总体的认识，并为开拓研究领域和方向提供基础。并落实在两个目标上：一是美国本土裔文学批评理论的学习，并对其理论特质有针对性的把握。二是对相关本土裔文学作品的族裔性书写策略和文本性感知。

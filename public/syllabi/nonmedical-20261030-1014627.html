课程简介（中文）

机器学习是一门多领域交叉学科，涉及概率论、线性代数、统计学、凸分析、算法复杂度理论等多门学科。本课程主要教授传统机器学习与深度学习的经典理论与方法及通过编程解决实际工程问题的能力。本课程结合当前科技热点，探索认知以机器学习为基础的人工智能前沿研究方向和激发学生对当前科技热点研究兴趣。

课程简介（英文）

Machine learning is an interdisciplinary field that integrates knowledge from multiple domains, including probability theory, linear algebra, statistics, convex analysis, and algorithm complexity theory. This course primarily teaches the fundamental theories and classical methods of both traditional machine learning and deep learning, while cultivating students' ability to solve real-world engineering problems through programming. By aligning with current technological frontiers, the course explores cutting-edge research directions in artificial intelligence based on machine learning and aims to inspire students' interest in contemporary scientific and technological advancements.

教学目标

通过本课程学习，了解机器学习最新技术的进展。熟练掌握以下经典算法的理论方法包括经典回归、经典分类、弱监督与无监督学习、聚类、数据降维、概率图模型、神经网络等方面；熟练掌握机器学习的主流算法的原理和典型应用的开发。

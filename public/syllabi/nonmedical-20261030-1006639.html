Financial Reporting and Securities Analysis

课程简介（中文）

The course is about handling accounting information in value investing. It attempts to tackle these questions: how to gather relevant accounting information for evaluation? What drives the difference between market and book values of equity, and how to estimate it? Does higher earnings growth always imply higher value? How does a firm’s financial policy impact its profitability measures? How to judge firms’ disclosure quality? How to infer firms’ credit risk? To answer, we need to understand i) the structure and integrity of information disclosed by financial statements; and ii) how to fit relevant accounting information into a proper finance model in practice. With an understanding of how accounting connects to value and risk, financial statement analysis can become powerful aids to evaluate a business and help us avoid investment pitfalls.

教学目标

The objective of this course is to introduce the practice of value investing. In particular, students will learn the residual income-type models to value a publicly traded security.

Case: Kimberly-Clark Reading: Penman Chap 1-2 Week 2 Topic: The basics of financial statements Contents: Forms and relations of financial statements, accounting manipulation, income shifting Reading: Penman Chap 2, Chap 18(optional) Week 3 Topic: Accounting and valuation approaches Contents: Equity statement and balance sheet Reading: Penman Chap 4-6 Week 4 Topic: Reformulation of financial statements (part II) Contents: Income and cash flow statements Reading: Penman Chap 10-11 Week 5 Topic: Analysis of profitability Contents: Analysis of profitability, growth and sustainable earnings Reading: Penman Chap 12-13 Week 6 Topic: Forecasting and equity valuation Contents: Forecasting and valuation Case: Coca-Cola (HBS case if time permits) Reading: Penman Chap 14-15, Chap 20(optional)

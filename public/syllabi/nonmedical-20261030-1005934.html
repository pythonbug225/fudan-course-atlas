课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提升学生在医学语境的学术英语综合能力。设计基于三点核心理念：(1) 中国高校英语教学正从通用英语转向学术/专门用途英语；(2)通用英语与学术英语差异显著，需专门训练以掌握学术语境中的语言运用能力；(3)学术英语需扎实的语言技能和学科基础。故课程提供机会，使学生融入医学内容，发展学术交流所需的语言技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to help students improve their English proficiency in academic contexts where medicine is the subject matter. The course design is based on the following three rationales: 1) Shift from English for General Purposes (EGP) to English for Academic/Specific Purposes (EAP/ESP) is the trend of English teaching and learning at tertiary institutions in China; 2) EAP differs from EGP in many respects and deserves special attention so that learners are able to use English proficiently and properly in academic contexts; 3) EAP entails a vast array of skills and a good knowledge of the subject matter. This course, therefore, is framed to provide students with opportunities to expose themselves to the subject matter related to medicine, to develop language proficiency and skills as needed for communication in academic contexts.

教学目标

At the end of the course, students are expected to expose themselves to the subject matter related to medicine to develop language proficiency and skills as needed for communication in academic contexts use English language in simulated or real situations where English language serves as the medium for communication enrich themselves with specific medical knowledge such as treatment and prevention of diseases, alternative and supplementary medicine, emerging and reemerging diseases, etc. expand their vocabulary by a large margin get familiar with the most commonly used medical terms in the current medical literature and medical practice be able to prepare and deliver presentation on topics related to the texts in the textbook with/without the aid of PPT be proficient in communicating in academic contexts

教学方式

Text analysis: Critical analysis of articles with topics related to medicine. Student presentation: Academic oral presentation (2-3 minutes) based on topics related to Text A in the textbook. Team report: Group presentation (10 minutes) on one topic as listed in the Researching Section in each unit.

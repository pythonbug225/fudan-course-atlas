课程简介（中文）

这门博士课程涵盖国际经济学的高级主题，侧重于理论和实证视角。它包括许多关于国际贸易、国际投资和国际金融（或国际宏观经济学）的问题。具体而言，本课程由两部分组成。第一部分包括两位教授的12次授课，有12节课。程教授专注于全球价值链、网络和生产/贸易/金融/价值链网络、企业异质性、颗粒度与国际化以及FDI和跨国公司；沈教授关注于世界经济与中美贸易前沿、知识产权保护与创新、互联网化、高标准贸易协定与产业链供应链等。第二部分是博士生报告和教授评论，有4节课。

课程简介（英文）

This PhD-level course covers advanced topics in international economics, with a focus on both theoretical and empirical perspectives. It includes myriad issues on international trade, international investment, and international finance (or international macroeconomics). Specifically, this course is comprised of two sections. Section I includes 12 lectures given by two professors. Prof. Cheng focuses on global value chains, economic network, firm granularity and internationalization, FDI and MNCs; Professor Shen focuses on the forefront of the world economy and Sino-US trade, internetization, intellectual property protection and innovation, as well as industrial and supply chains. Section II is devoted to students’ reports and Professors’ comments, which consists of 4 parts.

教学目标

本课程以习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想为指导，讲授国际经济学前沿理论和方法，结合中国具体事例进行世情国情教育，使得研究生学习和掌握使用国际经济学一般原理来分析和解决中国具体事例问题。为此： The course is designed to help PhD students to develop their own research on international economics. This course has two main objectives: First, it offers the PhD students a brief roadmap of cutting-edge international economics topics and questions highlighted in the various sections. Second, it helps them upgrade their capabilities of academic thinking and writing.

教学方式

本课程是由两位教授主讲、博士生汇报论文和撰写一篇课程论文形式等组成： 第一部分由两位教授专门授课12次，共12节课。 第二部分由博士生报告和教授评论，有4节课。 最后，选课博士生需要撰写提交一篇与本课程内容相关的课程论文。

课程简介（中文）

本课程内容涉及汉字、词语、课文、语法及文化等方面，话题涉及日常生活交际的基本层面。通过一个学期的学习，学生需要掌握430个词汇，能理解并运用汉语基本关联词语等语法知识，能阅读并理解26篇长对话、13篇短文，能围绕所给话题写中文短文，能围绕城市、休闲生活、传统服装、中国绘画、中国节日等话题用中文表达自己的观点。

课程简介（英文）

The specific teaching content of this course involves aspects such as Chinese characters, vocabulary, texts, grammar, and culture, with topics covering the basic aspects of daily life communication. Through one semester of study, students are required to master 430 vocabulary words, understand and apply basic Chinese correlative words and other grammatical knowledge, read and comprehend 26 long dialogues and 13 short passages, write Chinese short essays on given topics, and express their views in Chinese on topics such as cities, leisure life, traditional clothing, Chinese painting, and Chinese festivals.

教学目标

此课程针对汉语非母语的医学专业一年级本科生开设，本学期教学旨在帮助学生掌握汉语常用词汇和基本语法，通过听、说、读、写的全面训练使学生具有基本的听说读写能力、言语交际能力, 能满足日常在中国生活、学习和一般社交场合的交际需要。本学期课程结束后学生汉语水平达到HSK三级或以上。

教学方式

词汇教学：PowerPoint辅助讲解与练习，近义词辨析，写故事，“你来比划我来猜”等游戏。 句子教学：学生看图说句子，改错句等。 语法教学：创设生活、学习等现实情景，帮助学生理解语法知识，然后运用该语法知识完成实际的语言任务。 对话教学：角色扮演。 课文教学：朗读课文，复述课文，表演课文，讨论与发表观点等。 阅读教学：阅读拓展课文，回答问题，引导学生围绕一些话题发表自己的观点。 写作教学：课堂上规定时间写一段话，课后每个作业中都有一篇150-200字的写作练习。 Vocabulary teaching: PowerPoint-assisted explanation and practice, analysis of near-synonyms, story writing, games like "You Act and I Guess", etc. Sentence teaching: Students describe sentences based on pictures, correct wrong sentences, etc. Grammar teaching: Create real-life scenarios (such as daily life and study) to help students understand grammatical knowledge, and then use this knowledge to complete practical language tasks. Dialogue teaching: Role-playing. Text teaching: Reading texts aloud, retelling texts, performing texts, discussing and expressing opinions, etc. Reading teaching: Reading extended texts, answering questions, and guiding students to express their opinions on relevant topics. Writing teaching: Writing a paragraph within a specified time in class, and each after-class assignment includes a 150-200-word writing exercise.

考核构成

  • 出勤Attendance 10.0% 缺勤1/3及以上的学生不可以参加考试，“病假”“事假”都算作“缺勤”，“迟到”三次等于一次“缺勤”。Students who are absent for 1/3 or more of the classes are not allowed to take the exam. Both "sick leave" and "personal leave" are considered "absences", and three "late arrivals" are equivalent to one "absence".
  • 课堂表现Participation 15.0% 学生应认真上课，积极参加课堂活动。每个学生都要围绕所给话题，用中文做展示。Students should attend classes carefully and actively participate in classroom activities.Each student is required to make a presentation in Chinese on the given topic.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 15.0% 学生应按时提交作业。Students should submit their assignments on time.
  • 期中考试Midterm Examination 30.0% 第8周 The 8th week
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 30.0% 第16周 The 16th week

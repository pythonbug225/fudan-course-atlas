投资银行与私募股权

2025秋 · MIB50001.01 · 张新

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

Investment banks and private equity funds are important participants in the capital markets. This course introduces the history and status quo of investment banks and private equity funds, their core business areas, and organizational structure. It covers topics of IPOs, leveraged buyouts, and mergers and acquisitions. The course also covers analytical tools such as leveraged buyout models and post-merger consequence models, and uses case discussions and business simulations to help students understand the related decision-making basis and processes.

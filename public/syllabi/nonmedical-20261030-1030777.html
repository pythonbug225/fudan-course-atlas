中国金融市场

2026春 · MF30002.01 · 钱军、马畅

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在通过与其他发达及新兴经济体的比较，系统分析中国金融体系的架构及其主要组成部分，包括银行业、房地产业与资本市场。课程还将深入探讨相关的宏观经济政策，以及当前全球金融体系中的政策争议。课程内容将作为理解全球化现状的基础。

课程简介（英文）

Through comparisons with other developed and emerging economies, this course aims to analyze the structure of China’s financial system and its major components, including the banking sector, real estate sector, and capital markets. It also covers the related macroeconomic policies and ongoing policy debates in the global financial system. The course materials will serve as the basis to understand the current status of globalization.

教学目标

Understand the structure of the financial system and its relationship with economic growth in China and other developed and emerging economies; Through the comparisons with other economies, better understand the current status, risks, and further development of the major segments of the Chinese financial system; Understand macroeconomic policies and related debates in the global financial system.

教学方式

Lectures + Case Studies + Class Discussion & Presentations

教学内容与进度安排

The first five weeks will be taught by Prof. Jun Qian. Week #1: Introduction: structure of financial systems; the Chinese stock market Week #2: Comparing China’s stock market (continued) Week #3: China’s banking sector; case discussion (bank IPO) Week #4: China’s banking sector (continued); shadow banking sector Week #5: Real estate market: status, risks, and debt restructuring; presentations of term projects. The following weeks will be taught by Prof. Chang Ma. Week #6: Chinese bond market Week #7: Foreign exchange market Week #8: Mid-term Exam (covering materials of the first five weeks) Week #9: Exchange rate theory Week #10: Central banks Week #11: National and international accounts Week #12: Open economy framework Week #13: ‘Dilemma’ or ‘trilemma’ Week #14: Globalization and policy responses Week #15: Economic growth and finance Week #16: Presentations (‘debates’) of group assignments Week #17: Final Exam (coving all materials taught by Prof. Ma, ‘cheat sheets’ allowed)

财务报表分析

2026春 · MF30010.01 · 施文韵

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

财务报表分析是面向经济与管理专业本科生的核心课程，旨在从信息范式的视角帮助学生掌握相关原理、知识与技能。课程系统阐述财务会计的本质、财务报告体系及发展历程、会计准则的演进逻辑、概念框架、核心财务报表（包括资产负债表、利润表、现金流量表、所有者权益变动表及报表附注），并深入解析关键会计要素的确认、计量、列报与披露规则。本课程教学目标包括：引导学生认知会计实务中的确认、计量、列报与披露操作，理解实务背后的理论逻辑及其与其他学科知识的关联，掌握会计准则变革的理论渊源，并培养学生形成正确、扎实且具有实践性的应对策略，以应对各类新型交易与事项中会计准则不完善带来的挑战。

课程简介（英文）

Financial Statement Analysis is a core course for undergraduate student of economics and management majors, who want to grasp the principle, knowledge and skills from the standpoint of information paradigm. It teaches nature, financial reporting system, and development of financial accounting, evolution of accounting standards, conceptual framework, and core statements of financial reporting (including balance sheet, income statement, statement of cash flow, statement of changes of equity owners, and footnotes to financial statement), recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure of lots of core accounts. The objectives of this course include: to help students to know the accounting practices of recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure, to help students to know the principles behind these practices, and know the interaction with other discipline knowledge, and grasp the theoretical source of accounting standards change, and help students to get the correct, strong, and practical coping strategy towards the accounting standard’s incompleteness against all kinds of new transaction and events.

教学目标

(1) Stand on the practice of China accounting practice, be familiar with frontier of international accounting development and the tendency of accounting subject development, and achieve harmonization with other discipline knowledge and other administrative practices (2) Grasp the nature of financial accounting, financial reporting system, the historical development and primary principles of accounting, conceptual framework, and specific accounting practices about core accounts, and shortcoming of financial accounting and breaking out of accounting encirclement from the standpoint of information paradigm (3) Shape critical thinking on accounting standards and practices, grasp China accounting practices and international financial reporting language, and effectively communicate accounting information with professionals in other fields, and devote to self enhancement and lifelong learning of accounting (4) Shape professional ethics of faithfulness, and steady and sure style of professional lifetime, and form the professional habit of having strategic vision when they pay close attention to the details of accounting practices

教学方式

Classroom teaching

教学内容与进度安排

Week1：Financial accounting and accounting standards Week2-4：Financial statements Week5-6：Cash and inventories Week7: Investment Week8-9：Long-term assets Week10-11：Liabilities Week12：Income tax Week13：Financial reporting quality Week14-15：Financial analysis Week16：Forecast Week17-18：Review and final project presentation

课程简介（中文）

本课程系统讲解量子多体体系，架设统计力学与现代凝聚态物理之间的桥梁。在回顾《统计力学I》基础知识后，我们将深入探讨密度矩阵表述和朗道费米液体理论等高级概念，继而聚焦两大核心主题： 1. 超导电性（BCS理论）——从库珀对和平均场近似出发，建立超导微观理论，推导博戈留波夫-德热纳方程和能隙方程。重点分析迈斯纳效应、磁通量子化等宏观量子现象，以及比热、准粒子激发等热力学性质。 2. 量子自旋系统——研究低维磁性体系，特别是一维反铁磁自旋链和Haldane猜想。内容包括：贝特拟设的精确解、价键态、Lieb-Schultz-Mattis定理，以及整数与半整数自旋链的本质区别。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers a comprehensive introduction to quantum many-body systems, serving as a bridge between statistical mechanics and modern condensed matter physics.  After reviewing the basics of Statistical Mechanics I, we delve into advanced concepts such as the density matrix formalism and Landau’s Fermi liquid theory, before focusing on two central topics: 1. Superconductivity (BCS Theory) – We develop the microscopic theory of superconductivity, starting from Cooper pairing and mean-field approximation to derive the Bogoliubov-de Gennes equations and gap equation. Key phenomena such as the Meissner effect, flux quantization, and thermodynamic properties (specific heat, quasiparticle excitations) are analyzed in depth. 2. Quantum Spin Systems – We explore low-dimensional magnetism, particularly 1D antiferromagnetic spin chains and the Haldane conjecture. Topics include exact solutions via the Bethe ansatz, valence-bond states, the Lieb-Schultz-Mattis theorem, and the profound distinction between integer and half-odd-integer spin chains.

教学目标

1. Develop a solid understanding of quantum statistical mechanics - Apply the density matrix formalism to analyze mixed states in quantum many-body systems. - Utilize second quantization and mean-field approximations to simplify interacting systems. - Derive key results from Landau’s Fermi liquid theory and contrast them with non-Fermi-liquid behavior. 2. Understand Superconductivity (BCS Theory) - Explain the origin of Cooper pairing and its role in the BCS ground state. - Solve the Bogoliubov-de Gennes equations for homogeneous systems and interpret quasiparticle excitations. - Relate the gap equation to thermodynamic observables (e.g., specific heat, superfluid density). - Connect microscopic theory to macroscopic phenomena (Meissner effect, flux quantization). 3. Analyze Quantum Spin Systems - Construct exact solutions for 1D spin chains using the Bethe ansatz. - Distinguish between integer and half-odd-integer spin chains using the Haldane conjecture and Lieb-Schultz-Mattis theorem. - Describe valence-bond solids (VBS) and resonating valence bond (RVB) states as paradigms of quantum magnetism.

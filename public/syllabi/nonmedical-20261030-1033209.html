课程简介（中文）

本课程为在复旦大学接受研究生教育的留学生开设。教学时间为一个学期，共16周。每周三个课时。教学语言为英语。本课程拟结合现状与历史的介绍，帮助在华的留学生们更好地了解中国的方方面面，尤其是历史以及哲学等知识。通过教学，促使学生在了解的基础上，理解中国和中国人，能与中国和中国人展开对话，从而培养开阔的视角和开放、包容的心态与全球化视角。

课程简介（英文）

This is a general education course offered for international postgraduate students studying at Fudan, with English as the medium of instruction. It is designed to help international students gain a systematic understanding of China’s basic national conditions, history and culture, social features, economic development and diplomatic philosophy, and build a bridge for cross-cultural cognition. Breaking the single mode of knowledge indoctrination, the course balances historical depth with contemporary development and integrates cultural traditions with realistic national conditions. It not only covers core fundamental content such as geography, history and culture, but also focuses on hot topics in China’s new era, including social changes, scientific and technological progress, and ecological civilization construction.

教学目标

Master China’s basic geographical overview, historical development context, and core cultural symbols (such as traditional festivals, etiquette and customs, and schools of thought), and understand the distribution and integration of various ethnic groups in China. Enhance cross-cultural communication skills, accurately understand the behavioral norms and values of Chinese society, avoid cross-cultural misunderstandings, and conduct daily communication and social activities smoothly. 1. 掌握中国的基本地理概况、历史发展脉络、核心文化符号（如传统节日、礼仪习俗、思想流派等），了解中国各民族的分布与融合情况。 2. 提升跨文化沟通能力，能够准确理解中国社会的行为规范、价值观念，避免跨文化误解，顺畅开展日常交流与社交活动。

教学方式

This course adopts a diversified teaching mode combining "classroom teaching + Chaoxing Course + interactive discussion", which balances knowledge transmission and ability training, and adapts to the learning characteristics and cognitive rules of international students. 本课程采用“课堂教学+chaoxing课程+互动研讨”相结合的多元化教学模式，兼顾知识传递与能力培养，适配留学生的学习特点与认知规律

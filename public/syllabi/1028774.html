课程简介（中文）

星际介质是宇宙中重子物质总质量约一半的弥散气体，在宇宙演化中扮演关键角色。它为恒星形成提供原料，并通过超新星反馈、活动星系核反馈等过程调控星系生长。大尺度的星系周介质作为星系与星系际介质之间物质交换的枢纽，参与宇宙中的重子循环。本课程将系统介绍宇宙弥散介质的辐射、热力学和动力学性质，以及相关的磁流体力学、等离子体物理和数值模拟基础。

课程简介（英文）

The interstellar medium (ISM), a diffuse gas accounting for roughly half of the total baryonic mass in the universe, plays a critical role in cosmic evolution. It supplies the fuel for star formation and regulates galaxy growth through processes such as supernova feedback and active galactic nuclei feedback. On larger scales, the circumgalactic medium (CGM) serves as a key hub for matter exchange between galaxies and the intergalactic medium (IGM), participating in the cosmic baryon cycle. This course will provide a systematic introduction to the radiative, thermodynamic, and dynamical properties of diffuse cosmic media, as well as the fundamentals of magnetohydrodynamics, plasma physics, and numerical simulation.

教学目标

全面了解星际介质的物理性质，如其辐射、热力学、动力学等；深入学习星系介质中的重要天体物理过程，如超新星遗迹、宇宙射线等；系统理解宇宙弥散介质与宇宙演化、星系生长、恒星形成的物理联系；能够运用数值模拟工具对天体物理流体力学问题进行初步的探究。 Gain a comprehensive understanding of the physical properties of the interstellar medium, including its radiative, thermodynamic, and dynamical characteristics; develop in-depth knowledge of key astrophysical processes in the galactic medium, such as supernova remnants and cosmic rays; achieve a systematic understanding of the physical connections between diffuse cosmic media and cosmic evolution, galaxy growth, and star formation; and acquire the ability to conduct preliminary investigations of astrophysical fluid dynamics using numerical simulation tools.

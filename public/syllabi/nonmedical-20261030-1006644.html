Application of Blockchain in Finance

2025秋 · MF70011.01 · 唐敦哲、徐文文

来源：复旦教务系统教学大纲；正文整理。

课程简介（中文）

Blockchain is an emerging and highly disruptive technology. This course introduces the fundamental mechanism behind this technology and introduces its applications in Finance. The applications include cryptocurrency like Bitcoin, cryptocurrency with smart contract functions like Ethereum, advanced blockchain applications like DeFi, NFT, and others. The course also discusses current blockchain related policy of different countries around the world.

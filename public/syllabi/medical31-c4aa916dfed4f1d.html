1. 课程简介 /Course Description

课程简介（中文） Course Description ( in Chinese ) | 主要内容包括口腔颌面放射生物学，口腔放射防护，口腔颌面医学影像检查技术和对牙及牙周组织病变，颌面骨炎症，颌骨囊肿，肿瘤和瘤样病变，外伤，唾液腺疾病，颞下颌关节疾病，系统病在口腔、颅、颌面骨的医学影像学表现以及口腔颌面部介入放射学和口腔种植学等方面的内容。

课程简介（英文） Course Description ( in English ) | The main content includes oral and maxillofacial radiobiology, oral radiation protection, medical imaging techniques for the oral and maxillofacial region, as well as imaging manifestations of dental and periodontal diseases, inflammatory conditions of the jawbones, jaw cysts, tumors and tumor-like lesions, trauma, salivary gland disorders, temporomandibular joint diseases, systemic diseases affecting the oral cavity, skull, and maxillofacial bones, and topics in interventional radiology and dental implantology.

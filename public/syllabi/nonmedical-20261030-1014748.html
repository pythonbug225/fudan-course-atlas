专业英语与科技写作

课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提升学生在专业领域的英语应用与科技写作能力。涵盖专业文献查询、整理及阅读、学术论文撰写、科研成果展示等核心内容。通过系统学习，学生能够熟练掌握专业英语词汇与表达，了解科技写作规范与技巧，为未来的学术研究与国际交流打下坚实基础，助力科研成果的有效传播与分享。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to boost graduate students' English-language proficiency in their majors and science-and-technology-writing skills. The course focuses on key aspects like professional-literature reading, academic-paper writing, and research-outcome presentation. By studying this course, students can master professional English vocabulary and expressions, get familiar with science-and-technology-writing standards and techniques. This will equip them for future academic and international exchange work, promoting effective research-outcome communication and sharing.

教学目标

Integrating academic integrity education with cross-cultural communication to develop students' rigorous academic attitudes and global perspectives, and shape them into high-quality talents with international competitiveness. Focusing on specialized English vocabulary, terminology, and scientific writing standards, covering various text types such as academic papers and research reports, to strengthen students' theoretical foundation in specialized English and scientific writing. Emphasizing the integrated training of listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, particularly enhancing practical scientific writing abilities to meet the requirements of training well-rounded talents and boost students' future academic and career development. 融合学术诚信教育与跨文化交流，培养学生严谨治学态度和全球视野，塑造其成为具备国际竞争力的高素质人才。 聚焦专业英语词汇、术语及科技写作规范，涵盖学术论文、科研报告等多元文体知识讲解，夯实学生专业英语与科技写作的理论基础。 注重听说读写综合技能训练，尤其强化科技写作实践能力，满足复合型人才培养要求，为学生未来学术研究与职业发展助力。

教学方式

Primarily lecture-based teaching, combined with multimedia instruction and case analysis. Group discussions and project presentations to cultivate students' innovation and teamwork. 课堂讲授为主，结合多媒体教学与案例分析。 小组讨论与项目汇报，培养学生的创新能力和团队合作精神。

教学内容与进度安排

Teaching contents (18 teaching weeks, 36 class hours in total): Week 1: Course introduction and retrieval of English-language scientific literature Teaching contents: Course goals, contents, and assessment methods are introduced. The importance of professional English and scientific writing is emphasized. Types of literature, commonly-used databases for literature search, and main methods for retrieving English-language scientific literature are presented. After-class assignment: Students are required to consult 5-10 English and Chinese literature articles from different fields (including Chinese and English degree theses, patents, journal reviews, and research articles). They should also familiarize themselves with the result-analysis functions of Web of Science (WOS). Week 2: Management of English-language scientific literature Teaching contents: Functions of reference-management software for professional English literature (e.g. EndNote) are explained. After-class assignment: Students need to download and install reference-management software. They should learn to export literature from databases to the management software, insert references into Word documents using EndNote, customize the export format of cited references, and properly format the reference list of the literature they have read. Week 3: Extensive reading of professional English literature (I) Teaching contents: Extensive-reading skills and methods for professional English literature are taught. The structure and writing features of literature are introduced. Students are guided to identify the innovation points, shortcomings of papers, and collect useful words and sentences. After-class assignment: Students are to extensively read around 20 English literature articles in their field and write a 50-100-word Chinese summary for each. For 3-5 articles, they should copy excellent expressions and words and sentences. For another 3-5 articles, they need to describe the strengths and weaknesses of the articles and share their reading experiences. Week 4: Extensive reading of professional English literature (II) Teaching contents: Intensive-reading skills for professional English literature, methods for accumulating and memorizing professional English vocabulary, and analysis of sentence structures in professional literature. Students are taught techniques for understanding long and complex sentences. After-class assignment: Students are required to intensively read 3-5 English literature articles in their field and take notes on vocabulary and analysis of long and complex sentences. Week 5: Academic writing standards (I) Teaching contents: The overall structure of academic papers is introduced. Requirements and techniques for writing titles, authors, introductions, and acknowledgments are explained. After-class assignment: Students are to come up with a title for an academic paper and write the introduction (200-300 words) and the acknowledgment section based on the title they created. Weeks 6-7: Academic writing standards (II) Teaching contents: Key points of writing paper abstracts (purpose, methods, results, and conclusions) are covered. Principles for selecting keywords and requirements and techniques for writing the experimental section and the results-and-discussion section are explained. After-class assignment: Students need to write an abstract and keywords for the paper title they previously created. Week 8: Creating and using charts and graphs in scientific writing Teaching contents: Common methods for creating charts and graphs (bar charts, line charts, pie charts, etc.) are taught. Standards for reasonably inserting and citing charts and graphs in scientific writing are explained. After-class assignment: Based on existing data, students are to create 2-3 charts and graphs and write related results analysis and discussion. Weeks 9-10: Grammar and expression in scientific English Teaching contents: Differences in word usage between scientific English and general English writing are taught. Common independent structures and other grammatical features and expressions in scientific English writing are introduced. After-class assignment: Students are to write 20 professional English sentences using different types of independent structures. Week 11: Writing literature reviews Teaching contents: The writing process of literature reviews (literature collection, organization, analysis, and summary) is explained. Excellent literature-review cases are analyzed. After-class assignment: Students are required to write a literature review of 1,000-1,500 words. Weeks 12-13: Writing research reports Teaching contents: Types and formats of research reports are introduced. Key points of writing research reports(problem statement, method description, results analysis, etc.) are explained. After-class assignment: Students need to write a research report of 1,500-2,000 words. Week 14: Creating English-language academic-presentation PPTs and communication Teaching contents: How to create English-language PPTs for academic presentations and how to give oral presentations in English are explained. Relevant techniques and methods are covered. After-class assignment: Students are to create a simple PPT for an academic presentation. Week 15: Writing master's/doctoral dissertations Teaching contents: Writing techniques and precautions for dissertations are explained. After-class assignment: Students are required to write a table of contents or a mind map of the dissertation framework along with a corresponding schedule. Week 16: Discussion on AI-empowered professional English and scientific writing Teaching contents: Group discussions are held on how artificial intelligence can empower scientific English writing. After-class assignment: Groups are to submit their assignments and innovative suggestions. Week 17: Course review Teaching contents: Key course contents are reviewed. Course assessment-related contents (including professional English translation or scientific-writing practice and oral presentations) are introduced and assigned. After-class assignment: Students need to prepare their course-assessment projects. Week 18: Course assessment Teaching contents: Course assessment is conducted (including professional English translation, scientific-writing practice, and oral presentations). After-class assignment: Students are to submit their course-assessment projects. Notes on course design for in-class and out-of-class discussions and exercises: Regular discussions: Mainly in-class discussions, usually within 20 minutes, focusing on reviewing the previous class. Mid-term discussion: One group discussion in mid-semester to address students' questions based on teaching progress and their understanding. Homework exercises: Post-class exercises after each session. Final project presentation: One project presentation at the end of the term on a topic in the professional field. 教学内容（18教学周，共36学时）： 第 1 周：课程导入及英文科技文献检索 教学内容：介绍课程目标、内容、考核方式；阐释专业英语与科技写作的重要性，介绍文献类型、文献查找常用数据库和英文科技文献检索的主要方法。 课后作业：查阅5-10篇不同类型专业领域中英文文献（中英文学位论文、中英文专利、期刊综述、期刊论文等），熟悉WOS的结果分析功能。 第 2 周：英文科技文献管理 教学内容：讲解专业英语文献管理软件（比如：Endnote等）及其功能； 课后作业：下载安装文献管理软件，熟悉文献从数据库到管理软件的导出，学会利用Endnote在word文档中插入参考文献，学会自定义导出引用文献格式，整理并规范标注所阅读文献的参考文献列表。 第 3 周：专业英语文献阅读基础（一） 教学内容：讲解专业英语文献泛读技巧与方法；介绍文献结构与写作特点，学会凝练论文创新点、不足及整理好词好句。 课后作业：完成20篇左右专业领域英文文献泛读，每篇撰写50-100字的中文小结。其中3-5篇摘抄好的表述及词句，3-5篇描述文章优缺点及心得体会。 第 4 周：专业英语文献阅读基础（二） 教学内容：讲解专业英语文献精读技巧与专业英语词汇积累与记忆方法；分析专业文献句子结构，掌握长难句理解技巧。 课后作业：精读 3-5 篇专业英文文献，整理词汇与长难句分析笔记。 第 5 周：学术论文写作规范（一） 教学内容：介绍学术论文整体结构；讲解标题、作者、引言、致谢撰写要求与技巧。 课后作业：拟定 1 篇学术论文标题，撰写 200-300 字的引言部分及致谢部分。 第 6-7 周：学术论文写作规范（二） 教学内容：阐述论文摘要写作要点（目的、方法、结果、结论）；讲解关键词选取原则；讲解实验部分及结果与讨论部分写作要求与技巧。 课后作业：为之前拟定的论文标题撰写摘要与关键词。 第 8 周：科技写作中的图表制作与运用 教学内容：教授常用图表（柱状图、折线图、饼图等）制作方法；讲解图表在科技写作中的合理插入与引用规范。 课后作业：根据已有数据制作 2-3 个图表，并撰写相关结果分析与讨论内容。 第 9-10 周：科技英语的文法与表达 教学内容：讲授科技英语与普通英语写作用词的区别；介绍科技英语写作常用独立结构等文法与表达。 课后作业：用不同类型的独立结构写20个专业英语句子。 第 11 周：文献综述写作 教学内容：讲解文献综述的写作流程（文献收集、整理、分析与总结）；分析优秀文献综述案例。 课后作业：撰写 1 篇 1000-1500 字的文献综述。 第 12-13 周：科研报告写作 教学内容：介绍科研报告类型与格式；讲解科研报告写作要点（问题陈述、方法描述、结果分析等）。 课后作业：撰写 1 篇 1500-2000 字的科研报告。 第 14 周：学术报告英文PPT制作及交流 教学内容：讲解如何制作学术报告英文PPT以及如何进行口头英文汇报，讲述相关技巧及方法。 课后作业：制作一份简单的学术报告PPT。 第 15 周：硕士/博士学位论文写作 教学内容：讲解学位论文的写作技巧及其注意事项。 课后作业：撰写学位论文的目录或者论文框架思维导图及相应的进度安排。 第 16 周：AI赋能专业英语与科技写作研讨 教学内容：分组研讨人工智能如何赋能科技英语写作 课后作业：分组提交作业及创新建议。 第 17 周：课程总结 教学内容：回顾课程重点内容；介绍和布置课程考核相关内容（包括专业英语翻译或科技写作实践与口头汇报等）。 课后作业：准备课程考核作品。 第 18 周：课程考核 教学内容：进行课程考核（包括专业英语翻译、科技写作实践与口头汇报等）。 课后作业：提交课程考核作品。 备注：课内外讨论或练习等环节设计： 日常讨论：主要是课内讨论，一般安排在上课二十分钟以内，主要是复习讨论上堂课的内容； 期中讨论：根据教学内容进度及学生对内容理解的程度，在学期中拟安排一次讨论，让学生对所学内容疑点按小组进行解读和分析； 作业练习：每次课安排课后习题练习； 期末项目汇报：针对专业领域，进行项目汇报。

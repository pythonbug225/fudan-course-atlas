课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在培养学生的英语创意写作能力，重点涵盖诗歌与小说两大文体。在为期15周的教学中，学生将通过作品研读、创作实践与工作坊研讨，在反复修改与同行反馈中提升写作技巧。 课程前八至九周聚焦诗歌创作，系统讲授意象、声韵、节奏、隐喻及语感等核心诗歌元素。通过细读伊丽莎白·毕肖普、艾米莉·狄金森、露易丝·格丽克、西尔维娅·普拉斯、方商羊等诗人的作品，学生将深入理解语言如何超越字面意义，实现情感传递、叙事构建与意境营造。每周设置针对性写作练习以探索不同诗歌形式，并通过工作坊进行同行评议以打磨作品。该单元最终需提交诗歌作品集，并举办诗歌朗诵会。 随后四周转入小说创作单元，指导学生构建引人入胜的叙事文本。从人物塑造、对话设计到场景描写，深入剖析细节刻画与矛盾冲突如何赋予故事生命力。通过研读詹姆斯·乔伊斯、露西·考德威尔、克莱尔·吉根等作家的作品，学生将习得创作技法，并系统学习故事架构、节奏把控与修改润色，最终完成成熟的短篇小说。该单元以小说作品集及公开朗读会收官。 学期中另安排3-4节学术写作专题课程，由外籍教授主讲。 通过本课程学习，学生将奠定创意写作基础，增强英语表达自信，掌握重要的修改技巧。更重要的是，他们将在创作实践中发现并锤炼独特的写作者声音。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to develop students’ creative writing skills in English, focusing on both poetry and fiction. Over 15 weeks, students will engage in reading, analyzing, and producing their own creative work while participating in workshops that encourage revision and constructive feedback. In eight or nine weeks, the course will explore poetry, introducing students to key poetic elements such as imagery, sound, rhythm, metaphor, and voice. Through close readings of poets like Elizabeth Bishop, Emily Dickinson, Louise Gluck, Sylvia Plath, Fang Shangyang and others, students will learn how language can evoke emotion, tell a story, and create meaning beyond the literal. Weekly exercises will encourage experimentation with different poetic forms, and workshops will help students refine their work through peer feedback. The poetry unit will culminate in a final poetry portfolio and a performance reading. The next four weeks will shift to fiction writing, guiding students through the process of crafting compelling narratives. Beginning with character development, dialogue, and setting, students will explore how stories come to life through details and conflict. Readings from authors such as James Joyce, Lucy Caldwell, Claire Keegn and others will provide inspiration and technique. Students will also examine story structure, pacing, and revision, leading to the creation of a polished short story. The course will conclude with a fiction portfolio and a public reading session. There will also be 3-4 academic writing sessions spanning over the semester, taught by foreign professor(s). By the end of this course, students will have developed a foundation in creative writing, gained confidence in their ability to express ideas in English, and learned valuable revision skills. Most importantly, they will discover and refine their unique voices as writers.

教学目标

Develop skills in writing poetry and fiction in English Explore different forms, styles, and techniques Engage in peer critique and revision Understand the cultural and literary influences on creative writing

教学方式

This course is primarily workshop-based, combining reading, discussion, writing exercises, and peer feedback to help students develop their creative writing skills.

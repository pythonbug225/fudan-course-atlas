课程简介（中文）

本课程针对《中国英语能力等级量表》四至六级（CEFR B1-B2）学习者，精选TED、新闻报道等真实语料，涵盖教育、科技、文化等多元主题。通过"预测-策略-反思"训练模式，培养：1）核心听力技能：抓取信息、归纳主旨、听记要点；2）思维能力：逻辑推理、细节分析；3）跨文化理解能力：把握语境含义。重点突破自然语速、多口音及术语障碍，助力学习者完成从"被动接收"到"主动理解"的能力跃升。

课程简介（英文）

Designed for students at China Standards of English Level 4–6 (CEFR B1–B2), this course will help enhance their real-world listening proficiency through authentic materials such as TED Talks and news reports. Through the "Predict–Strategize–Reflect" training model, students are expected to build key listening skills including summarizing key ideas, pinpointing critical information, and taking notes in a structured way while simultaneously developing analytical abilities such as logical reasoning, detail analysis, and cross-cultural understanding. The course also addresses challenges of natural speech speed, varied accents, and technical terms, empowering learners to progress from passive listening to active and purposeful understanding.

教学目标

This course aims to develop listening comprehension abilities aligned with China Standards of English (CSE) Level 4-6 (CEFR B1+–B2), supporting the university's mission to cultivate globally competent professionals with strong practical English abilities. Through authentic materials, students will refine their ability to decode natural speech (reductions, stress patterns, varied accents), analyze and summarize key ideas in professional, academic, and social contexts, apply critical thinking to evaluate content ethically and logically, and strengthen intercultural awareness by engaging with global perspectives. Guided by the "Predict-Strategize-Reflect" methodology, the course systematically enhances students’ ability to process standard-speed English, with measurable outcomes including accurate comprehension of central messages and supporting details, structured note-taking for academic and workplace applications, and adaptive listening strategies for technical and non-technical discourse. Consistent with the university's holistic approach to education, the course integrates value cultivation through content emphasizing innovation and scientific spirit, ethical reasoning and humanistic values, and global engagement and professional excellence. Upon completion, students are expected to demonstrate CEFR B1+–B2 competencies, equipping them to thrive in multilingual academic, professional, and social settings—advancing their roles asglobally aware, ethically grounded contributors to national rejuvenation.

教学方式

The course is lecture-based, with the teacher providing in-class instruction with multi-media assistance and guiding activities in a task-based approach, supplemented by out-of-class exercises.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 Orientation * Get an overview of the course Week 2 & 3 Education: East & West * Understand and use the language related to education, schooling; * Learn about different educational systems, teaching and learning; * Look into some cultural differences and ways to deal with them; * Distinguish confusable sounds/words in a sentence or a paragraph. Week 4 & 5 Career Development/ After-class practice 1 * Understand and use the language related to career development; * Learn about the process of job seeking and the current job market; * Prepare students for career change if necessary; * Improve the comprehension of numbers and number-related words. Week 6 & 7 Trade & Business / After-class practice 2&3 * Understand and use the language related to business and trade; * Learn about issues concerning business and trade; * Catch a glimpse of current affairs in the business world; * Practice the listening skill of tackling new words. Week 8 & 9 Cutting-edge Science &Technology/ After-class practice 4&5 * Understand and use the language related to cutting-edge science and technology; * Get a general picture of some of the most promising science and technology; * Look into some possible breathtaking changes the advanced technologies would bring to our lives; * Practice the listening skill of identifying main idea. Week 10 Public Health/ After-class practice 6 * Understand and use the language related to public health and infectious diseases; * Learn about the healthy diet and personal hygiene; * Look into the relationship of antibiotics and superbugs; * Practice the listening skill of identifying the main points. Week 11 Social Value/ After-class practice 7 * Understand and use the language related to social values in life; * Learn about social values in different forms around the world; * Look into roles social values play in people's daily life; * Practice the listening skill of identifying structure. Week 12 Animals & Nature/ After-class practice 8 * Understand and use the language related to animals; * Learn about animal conservation, animal welfare and animal communication; * Explore the relationship between animals and human beings; * Practice the listening skill of identifying supporting details. Week 13 Energy & Environment/ After-class practice 9 * Understand and use the language related to energy and environment; * Learn about the renewable energy sources and their development after fossil fuels; * Look into some environmental problems and explore ways to solve them; * Practice the listening skill of identifying linking words and making predictions. Week 14 Gender Equality/ After-class practice 10 * Understand and use the language related to gender equality; * Look into the current issues about gender equality; * Learn about the progress made in gender equality; * Practice the listening skill of taking notes. Week 15 Love & Marriage * Understand and use the language related to love and marriage; * Learn about the traditional marriage and the trend of marriage development; * Look into the factors in happy and harmonious loving relations and marriages; * Practice in an integral way the listening skills learned previously. Week 16 Q&A * Clarify the difficult and unclear points; * Get a clear picture of final exam format; * Get advice on how to further enhance listening skills. Week 17 Final Examination Week 18 Exam Grading and Grade Entry
